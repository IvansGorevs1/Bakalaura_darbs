Main financial indicators:

• The Group's unaudited consolidated net turnover for the fourth quarter (Q4) of the 2024/2025 financial year was EUR 5.18 million, representing a 13% decrease compared to the same period in FY 2023/2024.
• For the 12 months of the 2024/2025 financial year, the Group's unaudited consolidated turnover was EUR 26.11 million, a 4% decline from the previous financial year’s revenue.
• The Group's expenses did not exceed the amounts planned in the budget.
• The Group closed the fourth quarter of the 2024/2025 financial year with a loss of EUR 913 thousand (unaudited).
• For the twelve months of the 2024/2025 financial year, the Group reported a loss of EUR 820 thousand.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:

• The turnover in the North America and Latin America region decreased by 40% compared to the same quarter of the previous financial year.
• The European region accounted for 36% of turnover, an increase of 24% compared with the fourth quarter of the previous financial year.
• The Asia, Africa, and Middle East region doubled its share of total quarterly turnover compared with the corresponding quarter of the previous financial year.
• The Group's products were sold in 75 countries during the reporting quarter.

Main factors affecting performance:

• Fluctuations in quarterly turnover are influenced by the completion of individual projects.
• The global shortage of various electronic components affected the company's operations.
• The Group continues to invest in new product development and product enhancements, as well as in sales promotion across both existing and new market segments.
• The company regularly reviews procurement volumes and timelines, ensuring sufficient material reserves to fulfill most orders within standard lead times.

Overall summary:

• The Group's turnover decreased by 4% over the 12-month period compared to the previous financial year.
• The Group reported a loss of EUR 820 thousand for the twelve months of the 2024/2025 financial year, which is an improvement from the loss of EUR 2.37 million in the previous financial year.
• The company continues to invest in new product development and sales promotion, but faces challenges due to the global shortage of electronic components.
