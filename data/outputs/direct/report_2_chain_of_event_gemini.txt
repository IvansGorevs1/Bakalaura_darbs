Main financial indicators:

* The Group generated EUR 168.5 million in revenue for the 2025 financial year.
* The annual net loss for the period was EUR 1.17 million.
* Total assets for the Group amounted to EUR 43.38 million as of 31 December 2025.
* Total liabilities reached EUR 36.52 million at the end of the reporting period.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:

* Revenue declined by 16.1% compared to the 2024 financial year.
* The number of travellers served decreased by 20% year-on-year.
* Adjusted EBITDA improved to a positive EUR 420 thousand from a negative EUR 449 thousand in 2024.
* The annual net loss decreased by 84.6% compared to the 2024 net loss of EUR 7.6 million.

Main factors affecting performance:

* The Group intentionally reduced travel volumes and optimized its program to prioritize profitability over market share.
* Intense market competition and excess travel supply during the autumn season exerted downward pressure on pricing.
* Disruptions at Vilnius Airport caused by illegal cross-border activity involving unmanned balloons negatively impacted last-minute travel demand.
* Operational efficiency improved as evidenced by the aircraft load factor increasing by 1.5 percentage points to 97.2%.

Overall summary:

* Novaturas Group successfully shifted its focus toward profitability in 2025, resulting in a positive annual EBITDA and a significant reduction in net losses despite lower revenue and traveller volumes.
* The company improved its operational efficiency and margins by strictly managing supply, reducing capacity on loss-making routes, and focusing on higher-margin destinations.
* Management plans to maintain this focus on consistent profitability growth in 2026 by optimizing the travel program and expanding the hotel portfolio to strengthen market competitiveness.
