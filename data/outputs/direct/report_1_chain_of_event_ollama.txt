Most significant changes compared with the previous period:

• Revenue decreased by EUR 20.5 million
• Net income decreased by EUR 22.9 million
• EBITDA decreased by EUR 44.1 million
• Gross profit decreased by EUR 10.4 million

Main factors affecting performance:

• Economic uncertainty in Finland, particularly low consumer and business confidence
• High inflation and geopolitical tensions
• Changes in tax environments in Estonia
• Sale of three vessels to optimize cost base
• Alignment of estimated useful lives of cruise and passenger vessels to 45 years, reducing depreciation expense

Overall summary:

• Revenue decreased due to economic uncertainty and changes in tax environments
• Net income decreased significantly due to lower revenue and higher expenses
• EBITDA decreased due to lower revenue and higher expenses
• Gross profit decreased due to lower revenue and higher expenses
