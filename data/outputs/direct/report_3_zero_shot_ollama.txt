* Total revenue: EUR 53.2 million (2024: EUR 18.2 million), total revenue in the fourth quarter amounted to EUR 18.9 million (fourth quarter of 2024: EUR 7.6 million)
* Gross profit for 2025: EUR 18.8 million (2024: EUR 5.4 million), gross profit for the fourth quarter was EUR 6.9 million (fourth quarter of 2024: EUR 2.6 million)
* Operating profit for 2025: EUR 14.7 million (2024: EUR 0.1 million), operating profit for the fourth quarter amounted to EUR 7.1 million (fourth quarter of 2024: EUR 1.9 million)
* Net profit for the twelve months of 2025: EUR 12.0 million (2024: EUR 3.9 million loss), net profit for the fourth quarter was EUR 6.0 million (fourth quarter of 2024: EUR 0.2 million)

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:

* Total revenue increased by 191% to EUR 53.2 million (2024: EUR 18.2 million)
* Gross profit for 2025 increased by 253% to EUR 18.8 million (2024: EUR 5.4 million), gross profit for the fourth quarter increased by 133% to EUR 6.9 million (fourth quarter of 2024: EUR 2.6 million)
* Operating profit for 2025 increased by 13,636% to EUR 14.7 million (2024: EUR 0.1 million), operating profit for the fourth quarter increased by 368% to EUR 7.1 million (fourth quarter of 2024: EUR 1.9 million)
* Net profit for the twelve months of 2025 increased by 207% to EUR 12.0 million (2024: EUR 3.9 million loss), net profit for the fourth quarter increased by 300% to EUR 6.0 million (fourth quarter of 2024: EUR 0.2 million)

Main factors affecting performance:

* Increased revenue from real estate sales, primarily due to the handover of completed apartments in the Kalaranna and Uus-Kindrali developments in Tallinn, as well as in the City Villas project in Vilnius
* Improved hotel operations, with a 65% occupancy rate compared to 67% in the same period last year
* Increased sales of real estate commissions on real estate sales
* Strong performance in maintenance services

Overall summary:

* The Group's revenue increased significantly due to the handover of completed apartments and improved hotel operations
* Gross profit for 2025 was higher than expected, driven by increased revenue from real estate sales and improved hotel operations
* Operating profit for 2025 was higher than expected, driven by improved hotel operations and increased sales of real estate commissions on real estate sales
* Net profit for the twelve months of 2025 was higher than expected, driven by improved hotel operations and increased sales of real estate commissions on real estate sales
