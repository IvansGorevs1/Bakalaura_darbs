Main financial indicators:

* The Group reported a net loss of EUR 820 thousand for the 12-month period ending June 30, 2025.
* Consolidated net turnover for the 12-month period reached EUR 26.11 million.
* The Group’s cash balance at the end of the reporting period stood at EUR 4.8 million.
* Earnings per share for the 12-month period were (0.28) EUR.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:

* The annual net loss decreased significantly from EUR 2.37 million in the previous financial year to EUR 820 thousand.
* Annual net turnover experienced a 4% decline compared to the previous financial year.
* Turnover in the North and Latin American region fell by 14%, while European revenue grew by 17%.
* The Group’s total assets increased to EUR 25.92 million from EUR 23.02 million in the prior year.

Main factors affecting performance:

* Quarterly turnover fluctuations were driven by the completion and timing of individual projects with varying scopes.
* The Group’s operations were impacted by the global shortage of electronic components, necessitating the accumulation of material reserves.
* Revenue performance was influenced by regional shifts, specifically a 40% decline in the Americas during the fourth quarter.
* The Group continued to invest in new product development and sales promotion across both existing and new market segments.

Overall summary:

* The Group recorded a reduced annual loss compared to the previous year despite a slight decline in total net sales.
* Management remains cautious regarding future performance due to ongoing business environment uncertainty and the lack of specific sales forecasts.
* The company is focused on stabilizing turnover and diversifying its product portfolio to ensure a positive net result in the long term.
