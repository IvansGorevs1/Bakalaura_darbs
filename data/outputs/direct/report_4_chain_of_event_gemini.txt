Main financial indicators:

* The Group reported an annual net turnover of EUR 26.11 million for the 2024/2025 financial year.
* The net loss for the 12-month reporting period amounted to EUR 820 thousand.
* Total assets as of June 30, 2025, were valued at EUR 25.92 million.
* Total liabilities reached EUR 10.34 million by the end of the financial year.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:

* Annual net turnover declined by 4% compared to the previous financial year.
* The net loss improved significantly, decreasing from EUR 2.37 million in the prior year to EUR 820 thousand.
* Quarterly turnover for Q4 decreased by 13% year-over-year to EUR 5.18 million.
* Total liabilities increased from EUR 6.59 million in the previous year to EUR 10.34 million.

Main factors affecting performance:

* Revenue fluctuations were primarily driven by the completion and timing of individual projects with varying scopes.
* Regional performance was mixed, with a 14% decline in the Americas offset by a 17% increase in European market revenue.
* Operational results were impacted by the global shortage of electronic components, necessitating the accumulation of material reserves.
* The Group continued to prioritize investments in new product development and sales promotion despite the challenging market environment.

Overall summary:

* The Group experienced a moderate decline in annual revenue while successfully reducing its net loss compared to the previous financial year.
* Management maintains a cautious outlook, focusing on product diversification and market research to stabilize turnover and achieve long-term profitability.
* The company remains financially stable with sufficient liquidity, supported by an unutilized credit line and a strong cash position.
