Main financial indicators:
- Q4 2024/2025 net turnover was EUR 5.18 million, down 13% year-on-year (Q4 2023/2024: EUR 5.98 million).  
- Q4 2024/2025 net profit/(loss) was a loss of EUR 0.91 million, compared with a loss of EUR 0.50 million in Q4 2023/2024.  
- Full-year 2024/2025 turnover was EUR 26.11 million, down 4% year-on-year (FY 2023/2024: EUR 27.09 million).  
- Full-year 2024/2025 net loss was EUR 0.82 million versus a net loss of EUR 2.37 million in FY 2023/2024.  

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:
- Q4 2024/2025 turnover declined by EUR 0.80 million (13%) versus Q4 2023/2024, despite higher Europe sales (up 24%).  
- Q4 2024/2025 net loss widened to EUR 0.91 million from EUR 0.50 million due to weaker results in Q4.  
- Full-year net loss improved to EUR 0.82 million from EUR 2.37 million, while turnover fell 4% year-on-year.  
- Full-year cash flow turned positive, with net cash flow of EUR 2.6 million versus a net cash outflow of EUR 1.25 million in FY 2023/2024.  

Main factors affecting performance:
- Quarterly revenue fluctuations were driven by project completion timing, where dispatch and revenue recognition often occurred in subsequent quarters.  
- The Group faced a global shortage of electronic components, although delivery times improved after having built material reserves in the previous period.  
- North and Latin America revenue decreased to EUR 14.28 million for the full year (down 14%) and Q4 turnover in that region fell 40%.  
- Expenses remained within budget plans while the Group continued investing in product development and sales promotion.  

Overall summary:
- SAF Tehnika reported lower revenue in Q4 and a full-year turnover decline of 4% to EUR 26.11 million.  
- Losses persisted, with a Q4 loss of EUR 0.91 million and a full-year net loss of EUR 0.82 million, though the full-year loss narrowed versus FY 2023/2024.  
- Liquidity improved with positive full-year cash flow of EUR 2.6 million and an end-of-period cash balance of EUR 4.8 million.
