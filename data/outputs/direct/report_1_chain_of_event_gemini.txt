Main financial indicators:

* The Group reported annual revenue of EUR 765.3 million for the 2025 financial year.
* Annual EBITDA reached EUR 130.1 million compared to EUR 175.2 million in 2024.
* Net profit for the 12-month period was EUR 17.3 million, down from EUR 40.3 million in the previous year.
* Total assets at year-end 2025 stood at EUR 1,330.8 million, with total liabilities amounting to EUR 580.7 million.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:

* Annual revenue declined by EUR 20.5 million, representing a decrease of approximately 2.6% year-on-year.
* Net profit experienced a significant decline of EUR 23.0 million, falling by approximately 57.1% compared to 2024.
* The number of cargo units transported dropped by 19.2% to 245,004 units, while passenger numbers saw a minor decrease of 0.9%.
* Interest-bearing liabilities were reduced to EUR 445.9 million from EUR 556.4 million at the end of 2024, reflecting a successful debt reduction strategy.

Main factors affecting performance:

* Economic uncertainty and low consumer confidence in core markets, particularly Finland, constrained demand and cargo volumes.
* Increased operating costs were driven by higher taxes in Estonia and the requirement to purchase CO₂ emission allowances.
* Profitability was supported by a reduction in depreciation expenses following the alignment of vessel useful lives to 45 years.
* Strategic management decisions, including the sale of three vessels and the chartering out of others, helped optimize the cost base and maintain liquidity.

Overall summary:

* The Group faced a challenging 2025 financial year characterized by reduced revenue and profitability due to adverse macroeconomic conditions and rising operational costs.
* Management successfully navigated the difficult environment by optimizing the fleet through vessel sales and charters while maintaining a focus on debt reduction and dividend payments.
* Despite the annual decline, the Group demonstrated resilience in the fourth quarter with improved net profit and remains optimistic about recovery trends entering 2026.
