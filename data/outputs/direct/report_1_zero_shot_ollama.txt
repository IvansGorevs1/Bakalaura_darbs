Main financial indicators:

• Revenue: EUR 765.3 million (down from EUR 785.8 million in the previous period)
• Gross profit: EUR 143.2 million (down from EUR 153.6 million in the previous period)
• EBITDA: EUR 130.1 million (down from EUR 175.2 million in the previous period)
• Net profit: EUR 17.3 million (up from a net loss of EUR 5.2 million in the previous period)

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:

• Revenue decreased by EUR 20.5 million
• Gross profit decreased by EUR 10.4 million
• EBITDA decreased by EUR 44.9 million
• Net profit increased by EUR 22.5 million

Main factors affecting performance:

• Economic uncertainty in Finland, particularly low consumer and business confidence
• High inflation and geopolitical tensions
• Changes in tax environments in Estonia
• Increased fuel prices and interest rates
• Impact of high inflation on consumer habits

Overall summary:

• Revenue decreased due to economic uncertainty and changes in tax environments
• Gross profit and EBITDA also decreased, but net profit increased
• The Group's operating environment was influenced by previous fiscal policy changes and ongoing cost pressures in its main markets
• The management is continuously looking for ways to manage risks for the low season
