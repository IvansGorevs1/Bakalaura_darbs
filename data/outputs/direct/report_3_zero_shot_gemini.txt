Main financial indicators:

* The Group’s total revenue for 2025 reached EUR 53.2 million, a significant increase from EUR 18.2 million in 2024.
* Operating profit for 2025 rose to EUR 14.7 million, compared to EUR 0.1 million in the previous year.
* The Group achieved a net profit of EUR 12.0 million in 2025, reversing the EUR 3.9 million loss recorded in 2024.
* Net assets per share improved to EUR 1.12 as of 31 December 2025, up from EUR 0.89 at the end of 2024.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:

* Revenue growth was primarily driven by the successful handover of completed apartments in the Kalaranna and Uus-Kindrali developments in Tallinn and the City Villas project in Vilnius.
* Cash flow from operating activities turned positive at EUR 10.8 million in 2025, compared to a cash outflow of EUR 10.4 million in 2024.
* The Group significantly reduced its debt burden by partially redeeming EUR 9.4 million of secured non-convertible bonds during the year.
* The Group recognized a full impairment loss of EUR 0.86 million on the goodwill associated with the Italian subsidiary, Preatoni Nuda Proprietà S.r.l.

Main factors affecting performance:

* Revenue recognition is strictly tied to the construction cycle and the timing of notarial sales agreements for completed real estate projects.
* The completion and handover of large-scale residential projects in Tallinn and Vilnius were the primary catalysts for the Group's strong financial results.
* The Group’s strategic focus on residential development led to the decision to sell the Brīvības commercial property in Riga to prioritize core activities.
* Performance was influenced by the ongoing management of financing risks, including the successful amendment of bond terms and the securing of new construction loans.

Overall summary:

* Pro Kapital Grupp delivered a strong and profitable performance in 2025, characterized by consistent project execution and significant growth in revenue and net profit.
* The Group successfully transitioned from a period of development to the handover phase for major residential projects, resulting in a robust improvement in liquidity and financial position.
* Management remains focused on long-term strategy, maintaining a disciplined approach to capital allocation and risk management while advancing its development pipeline across the Baltic capitals.
