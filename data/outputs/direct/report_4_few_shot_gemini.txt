Main financial indicators:

* The Group reported an annual consolidated net turnover of EUR 26.11 million for the 2024/2025 financial year.
* The annual net loss for the reporting period was EUR 820 thousand.
* Total assets at the end of the period reached EUR 25.92 million.
* The Group maintained a cash balance of EUR 4.81 million as of June 30, 2025.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:

* Annual net turnover declined by 4% compared to the previous financial year.
* The annual net loss improved significantly from EUR 2.37 million in the prior year to EUR 820 thousand.
* Turnover in the North and Latin American region decreased by 14%, while European revenue grew by 17%.
* Total liabilities increased to EUR 10.34 million, largely driven by a rise in customer prepayments.

Main factors affecting performance:

* Quarterly turnover fluctuations were primarily driven by the completion and timing of individual project deliveries.
* The Group continued to invest in new product development and enhancements across its microwave and IoT portfolios.
* Global supply chain constraints for electronic components necessitated the accumulation of material reserves to ensure delivery timelines.
* The overall business environment remained characterized by uncertainty, though the Group observed no direct impact from regional armed conflicts.

Overall summary:

* The Group experienced a modest decline in annual revenue but achieved a notable reduction in net losses compared to the previous financial year.
* Regional performance was mixed, with strong growth in Europe offset by a contraction in the North and Latin American markets.
* Management remains focused on stabilizing turnover and diversifying the product line to ensure long-term profitability despite ongoing market uncertainties.
