Main financial indicators:

* The company generated EUR 168.5 million in revenue during the 2025 reporting period.
* Adjusted EBITDA reached EUR 420 thousand, marking a recovery from the negative EUR 449 thousand reported in 2024.
* The annual net loss was reduced by 84.6% to EUR 1.17 million compared to the previous year.
* The aircraft load factor improved to 97.2% from 95.7% in 2024.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:

* Total revenue declined by 16.1% year-over-year due to intentional volume reductions.
* The total number of travellers served decreased by 20.3% compared to 2024.
* Operating profit improved significantly, moving from a loss of EUR 6.56 million to a loss of EUR 0.27 million.
* Early bookings for the 2026 summer season increased by 59% by the end of 2025.

Main factors affecting performance:

* Management implemented a strategy of stricter supply discipline and reduced volumes on loss-making routes to prioritize profitability.
* Intense market competition and excess travel supply throughout the year put downward pressure on pricing.
* Disruptions at Vilnius Airport caused by illegal cross-border activity reduced demand for last-minute travel.
* The company successfully optimized its travel portfolio by focusing on higher-margin destinations and improving operational efficiency.

Overall summary:

* The company focused on improving profitability and operational efficiency by intentionally reducing travel volumes and optimizing its destination portfolio.
* Despite a decline in total revenue and traveller numbers, the group achieved positive EBITDA for the full year.
* Management plans to continue its focus on cost efficiency and expanding the hotel portfolio to drive consistent profitability growth in 2026.
