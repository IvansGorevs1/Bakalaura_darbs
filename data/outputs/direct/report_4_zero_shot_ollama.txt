Main financial indicators:

• The Group’s unaudited consolidated net turnover for the fourth quarter (Q4) of the 2024/2025 financial year was EUR 5.18 million, representing a 13% decrease compared to the same period in FY 2023/2024.
• For the 12 months of the 2024/2025 financial year, the Group’s unaudited consolidated turnover was EUR 26.11 million, a 4% decline from the previous financial year’s revenue.
• The Group's expenses did not exceed the amounts planned in the budget, and the company continues to invest in new product development and sales promotion across both existing and new market segments.
• The Group closed the fourth quarter of the 2024/2025 financial year with a loss of EUR 913 thousand (unaudited), while the consolidated unaudited result for the twelve months of the 2024/2025 financial year is a loss of EUR 820 thousand.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:

• Total assets decreased from EUR 25.916.954 to EUR 23.024.704.
• Total liabilities increased from EUR 10.335.629 to EUR 6.588.753.
• The Group's net sales were EUR 26.109.370, with a decrease of 4% compared to the previous financial year.
• The Group's operating expenses decreased by 8%, while salaries and social expenses increased by 8%.

Main factors affecting performance:

• The Group has no clients or suppliers in the region affected by armed conflict (Russia, Ukraine, or Belarus), so there is no impact on order volumes.
• The company regularly reviews procurement volumes and timelines to ensure sufficient material reserves to fulfill most orders within standard lead times.
• The Group continues to actively research the market and identify key issues to propose necessary product modifications and develop prototypes for next-generation technologies.
• The Group's turnover was affected by fluctuations in quarterly sales volumes due to project completion and lead times for material procurement.

Overall summary:

• The Group maintains a certain level of raw materials and consumables to deliver products within competitive deadlines.
• Provisions for doubtful and bad accounts receivable were calculated according to the Group’s provision calculation policy, with additional provisions made for insecure debts.
