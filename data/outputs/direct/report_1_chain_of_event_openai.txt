Main financial indicators:
- Revenue decreased to EUR 765.3 million in 2025 from EUR 785.8 million in 2024.
- EBITDA decreased to EUR 130.1 million in 2025 from EUR 175.2 million in 2024.
- Net profit decreased to EUR 17.3 million in 2025 from EUR 40.3 million in 2024.
- Total assets decreased to EUR 1,330.8 million at 31 December 2025 from EUR 1,463.9 million at 31 December 2024.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:
- Revenue declined by EUR 20.5 million year-on-year to EUR 765.3 million.
- EBITDA declined by EUR 45.1 million year-on-year to EUR 130.1 million.
- Net profit fell by EUR 23.0 million year-on-year to EUR 17.3 million.
- Total assets fell by EUR 133.1 million year-on-year to EUR 1,330.8 million.

Main factors affecting performance:
- Demand was impacted by low consumer and business confidence in core markets (especially Finland) and heightened geopolitical tensions, with cargo volumes down 19.2% to 245,004 units.
- Depreciation expense decreased by EUR 18.0 million to EUR 79.8 million due to alignment of estimated useful lives to 45 years (for cruise and passenger vessels except shuttle vessels) and the sale of three vessels.
- Net finance costs decreased by EUR 6.8 million year-on-year to EUR 21.4 million due to a lower outstanding loan balance and declining interest rates.
- The sale of three vessels (Star I in April, Regal Star in August, and Sailor in October) and cost-efficiency actions (including chartering vessels out and reducing the cost base) supported results despite weaker cargo demand.

Overall summary:
- For 2025, Tallink reported lower top-line revenue (EUR 765.3 million) and materially reduced earnings with EBITDA at EUR 130.1 million and net profit at EUR 17.3 million, both down versus 2024.
- Profitability was squeezed mainly by weaker demand and cargo volumes while partially cushioned by lower depreciation and net finance costs following useful-life alignment, vessel sales, and lower debt/interest costs.
- The balance sheet contracted year-on-year with total assets down to EUR 1,330.8 million at 31 December 2025, while net debt remained broadly stable at EUR 432.4 million and liquidity was EUR 110.4 million as of 31 December 2025.
