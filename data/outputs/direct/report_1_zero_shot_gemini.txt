Main financial indicators:

* The Group’s consolidated revenue for the 2025 financial year amounted to EUR 765.3 million.
* Unaudited EBITDA for the 2025 financial year totaled EUR 130.1 million.
* The unaudited net profit for the 2025 financial year was EUR 17.3 million.
* Earnings per share for the 2025 financial year were EUR 0.023.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:

* Total revenue decreased by EUR 20.5 million compared to the 2024 financial year.
* EBITDA declined from EUR 175.2 million in 2024 to EUR 130.1 million in 2025.
* Net profit decreased significantly from EUR 40.3 million in 2024 to EUR 17.3 million in 2025.
* The number of cargo units transported fell by 19.2% to 245,004 units compared to the previous year.

Main factors affecting performance:

* Economic uncertainty and low consumer confidence in core markets, particularly Finland, constrained demand and cargo flows.
* The Group completed the sale of three vessels during the year to optimize the cost base and reduce debt.
* Profitability was impacted by the absence of state support measures in Estonia and the requirement to purchase CO₂ emission allowances in full.
* Depreciation expenses decreased due to the alignment of the estimated useful lives of cruise and passenger vessels to 45 years.

Overall summary:

* Tallink Grupp navigated a challenging 2025 financial year marked by economic headwinds and reduced cargo volumes, resulting in lower profitability compared to 2024.
* The company focused on operational adaptation through vessel sales, chartering agreements, and consistent debt reduction to maintain financial stability.
* Despite the difficult environment, the Group remains committed to its dividend policy and intends to propose a dividend of 6 euro cents per share for 2026.
