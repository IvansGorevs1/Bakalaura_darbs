Main financial indicators:
- Q4 2024/2025 net sales were EUR 5.18 million, down 13% year-over-year.  
- Q4 2024/2025 net loss was EUR 913 thousand, compared with a loss of EUR 498 thousand in Q4 2023/2024.  
- Full-year 2024/2025 unaudited net sales were EUR 26.11 million, down 4% from the previous financial year.  
- Full-year 2024/2025 unaudited net loss was EUR 820 thousand, compared with a net loss of EUR 2.37 million in 2023/2024.  

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:
- Q4 2024/2025 net sales decreased by EUR 799 thousand (from EUR 5.98 million to EUR 5.18 million).  
- Q4 2024/2025 EBIT fell to a loss of EUR 863 thousand from a loss of EUR 495 thousand.  
- Full-year 2024/2025 net sales decreased by EUR 983 thousand (from EUR 27.09 million to EUR 26.11 million).  
- Full-year 2024/2025 total assets increased to EUR 25.92 million from EUR 23.02 million.  

Main factors affecting performance:
- The report attributes quarterly turnover fluctuations to the completion of individual projects and timing of material procurement and revenue recognition.  
- The Group’s Q4 turnover declined in North America and Latin America by 40% year-over-year to EUR 2.5 million.  
- The Group’s Q4 loss widened amid a lower net sales level while expenses remained within the planned budget.  
- Ongoing global shortages of electronic components affected operations, though delivery times have recently continued to improve.  

Overall summary:
- SAF Tehnika reported lower revenues in both Q4 and the full fiscal year, alongside widening losses in Q4 but a significantly smaller full-year loss versus 2023/2024.  
- Despite reduced turnover, the Group maintained planned expense levels and continued investment in product development, product enhancements, and sales promotion.  
- Liquidity improved over the 12 months with net cash flow of EUR 2.6 million and cash of EUR 4.8 million at period end, supported by an unused EUR 4.95 million credit line.
