Main financial indicators:
- Revenue for the twelve-month period ended 31 December 2025 was EUR 168.483 million, down 16.1% from EUR 200.878 million in 2024.  
- Adjusted EBITDA for 2025 was EUR 420 thousand, improved from negative EUR 449 thousand in 2024.  
- Net loss for 2025 decreased by 39% to EUR 1.17 million from EUR 1.92 million in 2024 (excluding the one-off non-cash goodwill impairment of EUR 5.7 million recorded in 2024).  
- Total assets were EUR 43.380 million at 31 December 2025, up from EUR 39.450 million at 31 December 2024.  

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:
- Revenue declined 16.1% in 2025 to EUR 168.483 million from EUR 200.878 million in 2024.  
- Adjusted EBITDA increased by EUR 869 thousand, moving from negative EUR 449 thousand in 2024 to positive EUR 420 thousand in 2025.  
- Net profit improved (loss reduced) by EUR 6.435 million, with net loss narrowing to EUR 1.169 million in 2025 from EUR 7.604 million in 2024.  
- Operating profit improved by EUR 6.281 million, rising to negative EUR 0.274 million in 2025 from negative EUR 6.555 million in 2024.  

Main factors affecting performance:
- The company intentionally reduced volumes and optimised its travel programme to increase profitability, which lowered revenue and travellers but improved margins and operational efficiency.  
- Market-wide excess travel supply and price pressure in the autumn season limited revenue recovery despite continued positive EBITDA in the second half and for the full year.  
- Disruptions at Vilnius Airport related to illegal cross-border activity involving unmanned balloons reduced last-minute travel demand.  
- Efficiency improvements included a rise in aircraft load factor by 1.5 percentage points to 97.2% (from 95.7%), and load factor gains on key long-haul and ski destinations.  

Overall summary:
- Despite a 16.1% revenue decline in 2025 to EUR 168.483 million, Novaturas materially improved profitability, delivering positive adjusted EBITDA of EUR 420 thousand versus negative EUR 449 thousand in 2024.  
- Net loss narrowed by 39% to EUR 1.17 million (improving versus EUR 1.92 million in 2024, excluding the one-off non-cash goodwill impairment of EUR 5.7 million recorded in 2024).  
- Management attributes the improvement primarily to stricter supply discipline, programme optimization, and higher load factors, partially offset by intense competition, excess supply-driven price pressure, and reduced last-minute demand from Vilnius Airport disruptions.
