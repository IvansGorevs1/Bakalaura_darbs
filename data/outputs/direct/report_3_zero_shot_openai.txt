Main financial indicators:
- Total revenue for 2025 was EUR 53.2 million (2024: EUR 18.2 million), including EUR 18.9 million in Q4.  
- Gross profit in 2025 increased to EUR 18.8 million (2024: EUR 5.4 million), with Q4 gross profit of EUR 6.9 million.  
- Operating profit rose to EUR 14.7 million in 2025 (2024: EUR 0.1 million), while Q4 operating profit was EUR 7.1 million.  
- Net profit was EUR 12.0 million for 2025 (2024: EUR 3.9 million loss) and cash generated from operating activities was EUR 10.8 million (2024: cash used EUR 10.4 million).  

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:
- Revenue for 2025 grew by EUR 35.0 million to EUR 53.2 million, with Q4 revenue rising by EUR 11.3 million to EUR 18.9 million.  
- Gross profit improved by EUR 13.4 million to EUR 18.8 million in 2025, and Q4 gross profit increased by EUR 4.3 million to EUR 6.9 million.  
- Operating profit increased sharply from EUR 0.1 million to EUR 14.7 million in 2025, and from EUR 1.9 million to EUR 7.1 million in Q4.  
- Net profit turned from a EUR 3.9 million loss in 2024 to a EUR 12.0 million profit in 2025, and operating cash flow moved from a EUR 10.4 million use in 2024 to a EUR 10.8 million generation in 2025.  

Main factors affecting performance:
- Revenue from real estate sales increased in 2025 mainly due to handovers of completed apartments in the Kalaranna and Uus–Kindrali developments in Tallinn and the City Villas project in Vilnius.  
- 2025 operating income benefitted from EUR 3.5 million fair value gains on investment property recognized as other operating income (2024: EUR 1.1 million).  
- Finance costs increased year-on-year, with finance cost at EUR 2.6 million in 2025 versus EUR 4.3 million in 2024 and interest paid included in operating cash flow generation dynamics.  
- A goodwill impairment of EUR 863 thousand was recognized in 2025 as part of the impairment test of goodwill from PNP.  

Overall summary:
- Pro Kapital delivered a strong 2025 turnaround with revenue of EUR 53.2 million and net profit of EUR 12.0 million, versus 2024’s EUR 18.2 million revenue and net loss of EUR 3.9 million.  
- Profitability improved substantially across gross profit and operating profit, supported by real estate handovers and higher investment property fair value gains.  
- Operating cash flow strengthened to EUR 10.8 million generated in 2025, while Q4 results remained strong with EUR 18.9 million revenue and EUR 6.0 million net profit.
