Main financial indicators:
- Unaudited revenue for 12 months 2025 was EUR 765.3 million, down from EUR 785.8 million in 2024.  
- Unaudited EBITDA for 12 months 2025 was EUR 130.1 million, down from EUR 175.2 million in 2024.  
- Unaudited net profit for 12 months 2025 was EUR 17.3 million (EPS EUR 0.023), down from EUR 40.3 million (EPS EUR 0.054) in 2024.  
- Net debt at 31 December 2025 was EUR 432.4 million and net debt to EBITDA was 3.3 (3.4 at 30 September 2025).  

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:
- Revenue decreased by EUR 20.5 million year-on-year to EUR 765.3 million.  
- EBITDA fell by EUR 45.1 million year-on-year to EUR 130.1 million and gross profit declined by EUR 10.3 million to EUR 143.2 million.  
- Net profit decreased by EUR 23.0 million year-on-year to EUR 17.3 million.  
- Depreciation and amortisation decreased by EUR 18.0 million year-on-year to EUR 79.8 million, while net finance costs decreased by EUR 6.8 million year-on-year to EUR 21.4 million.  

Main factors affecting performance:
- Economic uncertainty in Finland and weaker consumer and business confidence constrained cargo flows, with a stabilization and moderate growth noted towards the end of the year into 2026.  
- Cargo volumes declined materially year-on-year (cargo units down 19.2% to 245,004) alongside a smaller passenger decline (passengers down 0.9% to 5,531,132).  
- Results were supported by internal cost actions including the sale of three vessels, chartering out vessels, and reduced depreciation due to aligning estimated useful lives of cruise and passenger vessels (excluding shuttle vessels) to 45 years.  
- Financial costs improved as net finance costs fell due to a lower outstanding loan balance and declining interest rates, while cash was affected by dividend payments of EUR 44.6 million plus related income tax of EUR 11.3 million.  

Overall summary:
- Tallink’s 2025 results were pressured by weaker demand in core markets, leading to lower revenue, gross profit, EBITDA, and net profit versus 2024.  
- EBITDA declined to EUR 130.1 million and net profit to EUR 17.3 million, despite lower depreciation and lower net finance costs.  
- Management highlights fleet and cost optimization (including vessel sales and chartering) and ongoing debt reduction, with a proposed 2026 dividend of EUR 0.06 per share.
