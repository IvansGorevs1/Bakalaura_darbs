Main financial indicators:

* The Group reported total revenue of EUR 53.2 million for 2025, a significant increase from EUR 18.2 million in 2024.
* Operating profit reached EUR 14.7 million for the year, compared to EUR 0.1 million in the previous period.
* Net profit for 2025 was EUR 12.0 million, marking a recovery from the EUR 3.9 million loss recorded in 2024.
* Net assets per share improved to EUR 1.12 as of 31 December 2025, up from EUR 0.89 at the end of 2024.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:

* Revenue growth was primarily driven by the successful handover of completed residential units in Tallinn and Vilnius.
* Cash generated from operating activities turned positive at EUR 10.8 million, contrasting with the EUR 10.4 million used in 2024.
* The Group’s total equity increased to EUR 63.3 million, supported by the year's profitable performance.
* Total liabilities decreased to EUR 61.2 million as the company continued to reduce its debt burden through partial bond redemptions.

Main factors affecting performance:

* The completion and handover of major projects, including the Kalaranna and Uus-Kindrali developments, significantly boosted annual revenue.
* The Group successfully amended bond terms and partially redeemed EUR 9.4 million of its senior secured callable fixed rate bonds.
* A full impairment loss of EUR 0.86 million was recognized regarding the goodwill of the Italian subsidiary, Preatoni Nuda Proprietà S.r.l.
* Management focused on core residential development, leading to the decision to divest the Brivības commercial property in Riga.

Overall summary:

* The company achieved a strong and profitable year in 2025, characterized by consistent project execution and successful apartment handovers across its Baltic markets.
* Financial stability was reinforced through disciplined debt management, including the partial repayment of secured bonds and improved operating cash flows.
* The Group remains committed to its long-term strategy of developing premium residential areas while maintaining a conservative financing approach.
