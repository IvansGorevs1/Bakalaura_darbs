Main financial indicators:
- Q4 2024/2025 net sales were EUR 5.18 million, down 13% from Q4 2023/2024.  
- FY 2024/2025 net sales were EUR 26.11 million, down 4% from the previous financial year.  
- Q4 2024/2025 net profit was a loss of EUR 913 thousand, compared with a loss of EUR 498 thousand in Q4 2023/2024.  
- FY 2024/2025 total assets were EUR 25.92 million and total owners’ equity was EUR 15.58 million as of 30.06.2025.  

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:
- Q4 net sales declined by EUR 0.80 million (from EUR 5.98 million to EUR 5.18 million), representing a 13% decrease.  
- FY net sales declined by EUR 0.98 million (from EUR 27.09 million to EUR 26.11 million), representing a 4% decrease.  
- Q4 net loss widened by EUR 415 thousand (from EUR 498 thousand to EUR 913 thousand).  
- FY net loss widened by EUR 1.55 million (from EUR 2.37 million to EUR 0.82 million, i.e., a smaller loss), while total assets increased from EUR 23.02 million to EUR 25.92 million.  

Main factors affecting performance:
- Turnover was impacted by quarterly project execution and revenue recognition timing following material procurement and production lead times.  
- The group’s operations were affected by a global shortage of electronic components, though delivery times had continued to improve versus the prior period.  
- North and Latin America turnover fell by 40% in Q4 (to EUR 2.5 million) while Europe increased by 24% (to EUR 1.9 million) and Asia, Africa and the Middle East doubled (to EUR 846 thousand).  
- Profitability was pressured by the continued large operating cost base, resulting in Q4 EBITDA of EUR (460) thousand and EBIT of EUR (863) thousand.  

Overall summary:
- Revenue decreased year-on-year in both Q4 (-13%) and FY (-4%), driven mainly by a sharp drop in North and Latin America that outweighed Europe growth and Asia, Africa and Middle East recovery.  
- Losses persisted, with the Q4 net loss worsening to EUR (913) thousand from EUR (498) thousand, while FY net loss improved to EUR (820) thousand from EUR (2.37) million.  
- Despite weaker earnings, liquidity strengthened with FY net cash flow of EUR 2.6 million and cash balance rising to EUR 4.8 million at 30.06.2025, alongside higher total assets to EUR 25.92 million.
