Main financial indicators:
- Total revenue increased to EUR 53.2 million in 2025 from EUR 18.2 million in 2024, with EUR 18.9 million reported in the fourth quarter versus EUR 7.6 million a year earlier.
- Gross profit rose to EUR 18.8 million in 2025 from EUR 5.4 million in 2024, and gross profit was EUR 6.9 million in the fourth quarter versus EUR 2.6 million in the fourth quarter of 2024.
- Operating profit improved to EUR 14.7 million in 2025 from EUR 0.1 million in 2024, and operating profit was EUR 7.1 million in the fourth quarter versus EUR 1.9 million in the fourth quarter of 2024.
- Net profit was EUR 12.0 million in 2025 (2024: EUR 3.9 million loss) and EUR 6.0 million in the fourth quarter (2024: EUR 0.2 million), with earnings per share of EUR 0.22 for 2025 (2024: EUR -0.06).

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:
- Total revenue grew by EUR 35.0 million year-over-year to EUR 53.2 million in 2025 from EUR 18.2 million in 2024.
- Net profit increased by EUR 15.9 million year-over-year to EUR 12.0 million in 2025 from a EUR 3.9 million loss in 2024.
- Operating profit increased by EUR 14.6 million year-over-year to EUR 14.7 million in 2025 from EUR 0.1 million in 2024.
- Net assets per share rose to EUR 1.12 at 31 December 2025 from EUR 0.89 at 31 December 2024.

Main factors affecting performance:
- Revenue from real estate sales increased in 2025 due to handover of completed apartments in Kalaranna and Uus-Kindrali developments in Tallinn and in the City Villas project in Vilnius.
- Profitability improved as gross profit increased to EUR 18.8 million in 2025 from EUR 5.4 million in 2024.
- Operating profit increased to EUR 14.7 million in 2025 from EUR 0.1 million in 2024 alongside higher other operating income of EUR 3.8 million in 2025.
- A EUR 863 thousand goodwill impairment loss was recognized in 2025, while cash generated from operating activities turned to EUR 10.8 million from EUR used of EUR 10.4 million in 2024.

Overall summary:
- The report shows a strong turnaround in profitability in 2025, with revenue rising to EUR 53.2 million and net profit reaching EUR 12.0 million.
- Higher real estate handovers drove the increase in revenue and gross profit, leading to a substantial improvement in operating profit.
- The results also reflect financing and capital-market actions during the year, including partial bond redemptions and continued development progress across key projects.
