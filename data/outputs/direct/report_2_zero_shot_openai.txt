Main financial indicators:
- Revenue decreased 16.1% to EUR 168,483 thousand in 12M 2025 (from EUR 200,878 thousand in 12M 2024).
- Gross profit fell 5.3% to EUR 19,650 thousand in 12M 2025 (from EUR 20,742 thousand in 12M 2024).
- Operating profit improved to a loss of EUR 274 thousand in 12M 2025 (from a loss of EUR 6,556 thousand in 12M 2024).
- Adjusted EBITDA turned to positive EUR 420 thousand in 12M 2025 (from negative EUR 449 thousand in 12M 2024) and net loss narrowed 84.6% to EUR 1.17 million in 12M 2025 (from EUR 7.60 million in 12M 2024).

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:
- Revenue declined 16.1% in 2025 alongside a decrease in travellers by 20% to 191 thousand.
- Adjusted EBITDA improved by EUR 869 thousand to EUR 420 thousand after being negative EUR 449 thousand in 2024.
- Net loss decreased by 39% to EUR 1.17 million in 2025 (vs EUR 1.92 million referenced for the year before last, excluding a 2024 goodwill impairment of EUR 5.7 million).
- General and administrative expenses decreased to EUR 4,867 thousand in 12M 2025 from EUR 10,375 thousand in 12M 2024 (a 53.1% reduction).

Main factors affecting performance:
- Management intentionally reduced volumes and optimized the travel programme to improve margins and operational efficiency despite lower demand.
- Aircraft load factor increased by 1.5 pp to 97.2% in 2025 (from 95.7% in 2024), including stronger growth in Estonia and long-haul destinations.
- Market-wide excess travel supply in autumn increased price pressure and disruptions at Vilnius Airport due to illegal cross-border activity reduced last-minute travel demand.
- Higher early sales supported demand, with bookings for the 2026 summer season 59% higher than the previous year by December 2025.

Overall summary:
- In 2025, AB Novaturas generated EUR 168.5 million revenue on 191 thousand travellers, with profitability improving materially through efficiency measures and a focus on margins.
- The Group delivered positive adjusted EBITDA of EUR 420 thousand, while net loss narrowed to EUR 1.17 million.
- Performance was constrained by autumn price pressure from excess supply and by reduced last-minute demand linked to Vilnius Airport disruptions.
