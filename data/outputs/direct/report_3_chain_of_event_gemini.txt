Main financial indicators:

* The Group reported total revenue of EUR 53.2 million for the 2025 financial year.
* Net profit for the twelve-month period reached EUR 12.0 million.
* Total assets as of 31 December 2025 were valued at EUR 124.5 million.
* Total liabilities were recorded at EUR 61.2 million at the end of the reporting period.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:

* Annual revenue increased significantly from EUR 18.2 million in 2024 to EUR 53.2 million in 2025.
* The Group transitioned from a net loss of EUR 3.9 million in 2024 to a net profit of EUR 12.0 million in 2025.
* Operating profit improved substantially to EUR 14.7 million, compared to EUR 0.1 million in the previous year.
* Cash flow from operating activities turned positive at EUR 10.8 million, compared to a cash outflow of EUR 10.4 million in 2024.

Main factors affecting performance:

* Revenue growth was primarily driven by the successful handover of completed residential apartments in Tallinn and Vilnius.
* The completion of major development projects, such as the Kalaranna District and Uus-Kindrali, enabled the recognition of significant real estate sales.
* A fair value adjustment of investment property contributed EUR 3.5 million to other operating income.
* The Group recognized a one-time impairment loss of EUR 0.9 million regarding the goodwill of its Italian subsidiary.

Overall summary:

* The Group achieved a strong and profitable performance in 2025, characterized by successful project completions and increased sales volumes.
* Financial stability was reinforced through disciplined execution of development projects and a reduction in total liabilities.
* Management remains focused on long-term strategy, maintaining a robust pipeline of residential developments across the Baltic capitals.
