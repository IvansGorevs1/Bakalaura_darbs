Main financial indicators:

* The Group generated EUR 168.5 million in revenue for the 2025 financial year.
* Adjusted EBITDA for the period reached EUR 420 thousand, marking a recovery from the negative EUR 449 thousand recorded in 2024.
* The annual net loss for 2025 was EUR 1.17 million, representing a 39% improvement compared to the previous year's loss excluding one-off impairments.
* The aircraft load factor improved to 97.2% in 2025, up from 95.7% in 2024.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:

* Total revenue declined by 16.1% compared to 2024 as the company intentionally reduced volumes to prioritize profitability.
* The total number of travellers served decreased by 20% to 191 thousand clients.
* Operating profit improved significantly to a loss of EUR 274 thousand, compared to a loss of EUR 6.56 million in the prior year.
* Early bookings for the 2026 summer season showed strong momentum, tracking 59% higher by December 2025 than at the same time in the previous year.

Main factors affecting performance:

* Intense market competition and excess travel supply throughout the year exerted downward pressure on pricing.
* The company implemented a strategic shift toward stricter supply discipline and the reduction of volumes on loss-making routes to improve margins.
* Disruptions at Vilnius Airport caused by illegal cross-border activity involving unmanned balloons negatively impacted last-minute travel demand.
* Operational efficiency was bolstered by a focused review of the travel programme and an increased emphasis on higher-margin destinations.

Overall summary:

* Novaturas Group successfully transitioned to positive annual EBITDA in 2025 by prioritizing profitability over volume growth in a challenging competitive environment.
* Despite a decline in total revenue and passenger numbers, the company significantly reduced its net loss and improved its operational efficiency through stricter supply management.
* The company is entering the next period with a focus on consistent profitability growth, expanded hotel portfolio offerings, and strong early booking trends for the upcoming summer season.
