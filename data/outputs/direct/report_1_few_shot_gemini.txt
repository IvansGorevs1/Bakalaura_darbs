Main financial indicators:

* The company reported annual revenue of EUR 765.3 million, representing a decrease from EUR 785.8 million in the previous year.
* Annual EBITDA totaled EUR 130.1 million, down from EUR 175.2 million in 2024.
* Net profit for the financial year was EUR 17.3 million, compared to EUR 40.3 million in the prior year.
* Earnings per share declined to EUR 0.023 from EUR 0.054 in the previous period.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:

* Total passenger numbers decreased by 0.9% to 5.5 million, while cargo units transported fell by 19.2%.
* The company completed the sale of three vessels during the year to optimize its cost base.
* Net finance costs decreased by EUR 6.8 million due to a lower outstanding loan balance and declining interest rates.
* Depreciation expenses fell by EUR 18.0 million following the alignment of estimated useful lives for cruise and passenger vessels to 45 years.

Main factors affecting performance:

* Economic uncertainty and low consumer confidence in core markets, particularly Finland, constrained private consumption and cargo flows.
* The operating environment in Estonia was negatively impacted by the absence of maritime sector support measures and increased tax burdens.
* Profitability was influenced by the requirement to purchase CO₂ emission allowances in full starting in 2025.
* Geopolitical tensions and volatile global trade conditions continued to exert pressure on business operations and demand.

Overall summary:

* The company faced a challenging year characterized by economic headwinds in its core markets and a decline in both revenue and net profit.
* Management focused on adapting to the environment through fleet optimization, debt reduction, and the strategic chartering of vessels.
* Despite the difficult results, the company maintained its commitment to shareholders by proposing a dividend of 6 euro cents per share for 2026.
