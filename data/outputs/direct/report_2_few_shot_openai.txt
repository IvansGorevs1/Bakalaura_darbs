Main financial indicators:
- Revenue for the 12M period fell to EUR 168,483 thousand, down 16.1% year-over-year.  
- Net loss for 12M narrowed to EUR 1.17 million, down 84.6% year-over-year.  
- EBITDA improved to EUR 420 thousand versus negative EUR 6,132 thousand in 12M 2024.  
- Aircraft load factor increased by 1.5 percentage points in 2025 to 97.2% (from 95.7% in 2024).  

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:
- 12M operating profit improved to a loss of EUR 274 thousand from a loss of EUR 6,556 thousand in 12M 2024.  
- 12M profit before tax improved by 85.1% to a loss of EUR 1,162 thousand from EUR 7,786 thousand.  
- Adjusted EBITDA turned from negative EUR 449 thousand in 12M 2024 to positive EUR 420 thousand in 12M 2025.  
- Number of travellers decreased 20% year-over-year to 191 thousand.  

Main factors affecting performance:
- The Group reduced volumes and optimised its travel programme to improve margins and operational efficiency, despite lower revenue and traveller numbers.  
- Increased market-wide travel supply in autumn pressured prices, and Vilnius Airport disruptions related to illegal cross-border unmanned balloon activity reduced last-minute demand.  
- A focused review of the programme, stricter supply discipline, and higher emphasis on higher-margin destinations supported profitability.  
- Early bookings for the 2026 summer season increased by 59% by December 2025, with load factor rising to 97.2%.  

Overall summary:
- In 2025, Novaturas delivered lower revenue and fewer travellers but achieved positive EBITDA and a substantially reduced net loss.  
- The main improvement came from margin and efficiency gains driven by programme optimisation and supply discipline, despite adverse pricing and demand pressures.  
- Operational results were supported by higher load factors and stronger early sales, while financial results reflected the profitability focus and removal of the prior-year goodwill impairment effect when considering adjusted figures.
