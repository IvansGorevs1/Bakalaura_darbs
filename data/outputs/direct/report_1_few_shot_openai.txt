Main financial indicators:
- The Group reported revenue of EUR 765.3 million for 2025, down from EUR 785.8 million in 2024.
- EBITDA totalled EUR 130.1 million in 2025, compared with EUR 175.2 million in 2024.
- Net profit for 2025 was EUR 17.3 million versus EUR 40.3 million in 2024.
- Net debt at 31 December 2025 was EUR 432.4 million with a net debt to EBITDA ratio of 3.3.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:
- Revenue decreased by EUR 20.5 million to EUR 765.3 million year-on-year.
- EBITDA declined by EUR 45.1 million to EUR 130.1 million year-on-year.
- Net profit fell by EUR 23.0 million to EUR 17.3 million year-on-year.
- Depreciation and amortisation decreased by EUR 18.0 million to EUR 79.8 million year-on-year.

Main factors affecting performance:
- Demand was impacted by low consumer and business confidence, ongoing economic challenges in core markets, and heightened geopolitical tensions.
- Gross profit declined by EUR 10.3 million to EUR 143.2 million, while net finance costs decreased by EUR 6.8 million to EUR 21.4 million.
- Depreciation expense decreased due to aligning estimated useful lives of cruise and passenger vessels (except shuttle vessels) to 45 years and due to the sale of three vessels.
- Cost base optimisation through the sale of three vessels and chartering out several vessels supported cost efficiency.

Overall summary:
- Tallink’s 2025 results were pressured by an unfavourable regional economic environment, leading to lower revenue, EBITDA, and net profit compared with 2024.
- Despite the decline, depreciation and finance costs improved year-on-year, and cargo volume decline eased towards the end of the year with moderate growth at the beginning of 2026.
- The Group paid dividends of EUR 44.6 million in 2025 and intends to propose a 2026 dividend of 6 euro cents per share.
