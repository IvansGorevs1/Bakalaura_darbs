Main financial indicators:
- Total revenue increased to EUR 53.2 million in 2025 (2024: EUR 18.2 million) and was EUR 18.9 million in Q4 2025 (Q4 2024: EUR 7.6 million).
- Gross profit rose to EUR 18.8 million in 2025 (2024: EUR 5.4 million) and was EUR 6.9 million in Q4 2025 (Q4 2024: EUR 2.6 million).
- Net profit for 2025 turned to a profit of EUR 12.0 million (2024: EUR 3.9 million loss) and was EUR 6.0 million in Q4 2025 (Q4 2024: EUR 0.2 million).
- Total assets were EUR 124.5 million at 31 December 2025 (2024: EUR 118.8 million) and total liabilities were EUR 61.2 million (2024: EUR 67.5 million).

Most significant changes compared with the previous period:
- Revenue increased by EUR 35.0 million year-on-year to EUR 53.2 million in 2025, compared with EUR 18.2 million in 2024.
- Operating profit increased by EUR 14.6 million to EUR 14.7 million in 2025, compared with EUR 0.1 million in 2024.
- Net profit improved by EUR 15.9 million to EUR 12.0 million in 2025, compared with a EUR 3.9 million loss in 2024.
- Total liabilities decreased by EUR 6.4 million to EUR 61.2 million at 31 December 2025 from EUR 67.5 million at 31 December 2024.

Main factors affecting performance:
- Revenue from real estate sales increased primarily due to handovers of completed apartments in Kalaranna and Uus-Kindrali developments in Tallinn and in the City Villas project in Vilnius.
- Gross and operating profitability improved alongside the higher real estate and commission revenue, reflected in gross profit rising from EUR 5.4 million to EUR 18.8 million and operating profit rising from EUR 0.1 million to EUR 14.7 million.
- Other operating income increased, driven by gain from fair value adjustments of EUR 3.5 million in 2025 (2024: EUR 1.1 million).
- Finance cost burden eased with finance cost decreasing to EUR 2.6 million in 2025 (2024: EUR 4.3 million), helped by lower debt levels following loan and bond repayments and redemptions reported during the year.

Overall summary:
- Pro Kapital delivered a strong turnaround in 2025, with revenue at EUR 53.2 million and net profit of EUR 12.0 million versus a 2024 loss.
- Q4 2025 also showed improved results, with revenue of EUR 18.9 million and net profit of EUR 6.0 million compared with Q4 2024’s EUR 7.6 million revenue and EUR 0.2 million net profit.
- The balance sheet improved in leverage terms as liabilities fell to EUR 61.2 million and net assets per share increased to EUR 1.12 (31 December 2024: EUR 0.89).
