Main financial indicators
* The group’s revenue for 2024 was 7,477 thousand euros, a significant decrease from 18,339 thousand euros in 2023.
* The group reported an operating profit of 68 thousand euros and a net loss of 624 thousand euros for 2024.
* Total liabilities increased to 15,215 thousand euros in interest-bearing debt as of 31 December 2024, compared to 10,080 thousand euros at the end of 2023.
* Basic and diluted earnings per share fell to -0.06 euros in 2024 from 0.34 euros in the previous year.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
* Revenue declined by approximately 2.5 times compared to the 18,339 thousand euros recorded in 2023.
* The company shifted from a net profit of 3,550 thousand euros in 2023 to a net loss of 624 thousand euros in 2024.
* Total assets grew to 39,568 thousand euros by year-end 2024, up from 35,190 thousand euros in 2023.
* The group’s equity decreased to 19,739 thousand euros as of 31 December 2024, down from 21,017 thousand euros at the end of 2023.

Main factors affecting performance
* High interest rates and increased borrowing costs, with a weighted average interest rate of 8.7%, significantly pressured profitability.
* The long development cycle of real estate projects caused volatility in annual financial results as profit is recognized only upon final sale.
* Revenue was constrained by challenges in selling completed inventory and the postponement of the Kodulahe (Soodi 6) project due to construction permit processing.
* The group’s financial performance was impacted by the transition in management and the liquidation of its Latvian subsidiary, marking an exit from that market.

Overall summary
* Arco Vara navigated a challenging 2024 characterized by high interest rates, low consumer confidence, and a significant decline in residential sales.
* The company remains committed to its long-term development strategy, focusing on energy-efficient residential projects and the integration of ESG principles.
* Primary goals for 2025 include completing the first stage of the Bulgarian development, selling existing inventory, and initiating new construction projects.
