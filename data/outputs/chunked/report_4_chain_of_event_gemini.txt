Main financial indicators
* Total consolidated assets reached EUR 10.23 billion, representing a 17% year-on-year increase.
* The net loan portfolio grew by 20% to EUR 5.47 billion, driven by strong growth in corporate and private lending.
* Total deposits increased by 18% to EUR 8.13 billion, supported by significant inflows in demand and term deposits.
* The Group’s capital adequacy ratio stood at 22.85% as of 31 December 2025, remaining well above regulatory requirements.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
* Net profit declined by 22% year-on-year, falling from EUR 150.3 million in 2024 to EUR 117.0 million in 2025.
* Total operating income decreased to EUR 304.8 million from EUR 338.3 million in the previous year.
* Net interest income fell to EUR 235.1 million compared to EUR 273.3 million in 2024.
* Total operating expenses rose to EUR 159.3 million from EUR 146.9 million in the previous year, driven by higher staff costs and depreciation.

Main factors affecting performance
* A decline in interest rates and intense market competition squeezed interest income margins, leading to lower net interest income.
* The Group recognized an impairment loss of EUR 1.6 million on the LHV Paytech cash-generating unit.
* Higher personnel costs and increased administrative expenses contributed to rising operating costs.
* The Group faced a higher corporate income tax burden for credit institutions, with the advance payment rate increasing to 18% starting from 1 January 2025.

Overall summary
* LHV Group experienced a challenging year in 2025, with net profit declining by 22% compared to the previous year.
* Despite the profit decline, the Group achieved significant growth in business volumes, with total assets exceeding EUR 10 billion.
* The Group maintained strong capital and liquidity buffers while successfully navigating a shifting interest rate environment and increased regulatory requirements.
