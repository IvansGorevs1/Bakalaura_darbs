Main financial indicators
* The Group reported a net profit of EUR 117.0 million for 2025.
* Total consolidated assets reached EUR 10.23 billion, representing a 17% year-over-year increase.
* The loan portfolio grew to EUR 5.51 billion, while total customer deposits increased to EUR 8.13 billion.
* Basic earnings per share for the period were EUR 0.35.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
* Net profit decreased by 22% compared to the 2024 result of EUR 150.3 million.
* Net interest income declined to EUR 235.1 million from EUR 273.3 million in the previous year.
* Total operating expenses increased to EUR 159.3 million from EUR 146.9 million in the prior year.
* Expected credit losses on loans and advances decreased significantly to EUR 1.54 million from EUR 16.25 million in 2024.

Main factors affecting performance
* The Group recognized an impairment loss of EUR 1.6 million on the LHV Paytech cash-generating unit.
* LHV Bank successfully issued EUR 300 million in covered bonds and EUR 80 million in Tier 2 bonds during 2025.
* Moody's upgraded the rating of the covered bond program to Aaa and the Group’s long-term issuer rating to Baa2.
* The Group implemented changes in financial statement presentation regarding interest income and cash flow reporting to improve transparency.

Overall summary
* LHV Group maintained strong capital and liquidity buffers throughout 2025, ensuring full compliance with all regulatory and internal requirements.
* The Group prioritized operational resilience and digital transformation while navigating a complex regulatory environment and economic headwinds.
* Future focus remains on long-term scalability, robust governance, and the proactive management of credit, financial crime, and sustainability-related risks.
