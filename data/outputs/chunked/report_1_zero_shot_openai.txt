Main financial indicators
- Revenue decreased to 7,477 thousand euros in 2024 from 18,339 thousand euros in 2023.  
- EBIT fell to 68 thousand euros in 2024 from 3,940 thousand euros in 2023.  
- Net profit/loss changed to a net loss of 624 thousand euros in 2024 from net profit of 3,550 thousand euros in 2023.  
- Cash and cash equivalents increased to 1,720 thousand euros as of 31 December 2024 from 1,472 thousand euros as of 31 December 2023.  

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
- Revenue declined by 10,862 thousand euros (from 18,339 thousand euros to 7,477 thousand euros).  
- EBIT dropped by 3,872 thousand euros (from 3,940 thousand euros to 68 thousand euros).  
- Net profit shifted by 4,174 thousand euros (from net profit of 3,550 thousand euros to net loss of 624 thousand euros).  
- Cash and cash equivalents increased by 248 thousand euros (from 1,472 thousand euros to 1,720 thousand euros).  

Main factors affecting performance
- Low consumer confidence and high interest rates negatively impacted the real estate market activity and the company’s financial results.  
- Profitability faced pressure from volatility in the economic environment and high borrowing costs.  
- In the first half of 2024, selling completed apartments in Kodulahe and Kuldlehe (market value nearly 14 million euros) was a major challenge, and construction of the next stage of Kodulahe (Soodi 6) was postponed due to construction permit processing.  
- Stabilisation in the second half of 2024, including a decrease of Euribor and increased apartment transaction volume in Q4 2024 versus prior periods, supported improved demand alongside adjustments of pricing policies and occupancy rates.  

Overall summary
- Arco Vara ended 2024 with sharply lower revenue and a net loss, compared with 2023 net profit.  
- The decline was driven by challenging market conditions (low confidence and high interest rates), difficulties in selling completed apartments early in the year, and delays affecting project schedules.  
- Despite the overall deterioration, the company improved liquidity as cash and cash equivalents rose to 1,720 thousand euros at year-end 2024.
