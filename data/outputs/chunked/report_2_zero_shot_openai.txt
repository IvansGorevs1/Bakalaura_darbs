Main financial indicators
- Net profit was €28.7 million in 2025 versus €32.2 million in 2024.  
- Net interest income was €73.3 million in 2025 versus €77.6 million in 2024.  
- Total net operating income was €79.4 million in 2025 versus €81.9 million in 2024.  
- ROE fell to 12.9% in 2025 from 16.2% in 2024 while NIM declined to 3.0% from 3.8%.  

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
- Net profit decreased by 11% year-on-year in 2025 (from €32.2 million to €28.7 million).  
- Credit loss allowance improved to -€3.3 million in 2025 from -€4.6 million in 2024.  
- Net interest income decreased by €4.3 million year-on-year in 2025 (from €77.6 million to €73.3 million).  
- ROE declined to 12.9% in 2025 from 16.2% in 2024 as the cost-to-income ratio increased to 52.3% from 49.6%.  

Main factors affecting performance
- Net profit was pressured by a lower-interest economic environment that could not be offset by 19% business-volume growth.  
- Net loan portfolio increased by €340 million (+19%) in 2025, reaching over €2 billion for the first time.  
- Credit risk stayed at a low-risk level during the year, supported by improved credit loss allowance versus 2024.  
- Funding actions included issuing covered bonds (€250 million) to add long-term stable funding and enable a reduction of more expensive deposits.  

Overall summary
- Coop Pank reported 2025 net profit of €28.7 million, down 11% year-on-year, alongside lower net interest income and reduced ROE.  
- Despite profitability pressure, the bank continued to expand, with net loan portfolio up €340 million (+19%) to above €2 billion.  
- Performance was mainly driven by the lower-interest environment, partially offset by strong business growth and an improved credit loss allowance.
