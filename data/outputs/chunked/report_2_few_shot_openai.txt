Main financial indicators
- Net interest income decreased to €73.3 million in 2025 from €77.6 million in 2024.  
- Net fee and commission income increased to €4.5 million in 2025 from €4.3 million in 2024.  
- Net profit decreased to €28.7 million in 2025 from €32.2 million in 2024.  
- Total assets, average, were €2,446 million in 2025 and net interest margin (NIM) was 3.0% in 2025.  

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
- Net interest income fell by €4.3 million (-5.5%) year-on-year to €73.3 million in 2025.  
- Total net operating income decreased to €79.4 million in 2025 from €81.9 million in 2024.  
- Credit loss allowance reduced to -€3.3 million in 2025 from -€4.6 million in 2024.  
- Net profit declined to €28.7 million in 2025 from €32.2 million in 2024 (-11%).  

Main factors affecting performance
- Net profit decline was mainly caused by the lower-interest economic environment that the 19% growth in business volumes could not offset.  
- The synthetic securitisation transaction with EIB Group is stated as expected to become effective in 2026 and to have no impact on 2025 financial results.  
- The loan portfolio increased by €340 million (+19%) and first time exceeded €2 billion.  
- Improved confidence and strong loan demand were attributed to receding and stabilizing interest rates during 2025.  

Overall summary
- Coop Pank reported lower profitability in 2025, with net profit falling 11% year-on-year to €28.7 million.  
- Growth continued, including a 19% increase in the loan portfolio to €2,114 million and a +9% rise in customers to 227,000.  
- Funding and capital-related initiatives included a €250 million covered bond tranche and EIB synthetic securitisation expected to become effective in 2026.
