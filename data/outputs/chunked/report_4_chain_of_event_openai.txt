Main financial indicators
- Total consolidated assets reached EUR 10.2 billion (+17% YoY).
- The loan portfolio increased by EUR 913 million to EUR 5.5 billion (+20% YoY).
- Deposits increased to EUR 8.1 billion (+18% YoY).
- Net profit was EUR 117.0 million (-22% YoY) and basic earnings per share was EUR 0.35 (EUR 0.46 in 2024*).

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
- Net profit declined to EUR 117.0 million from EUR 150.3 million.
- Net interest income decreased 14% to EUR 235.1 million from EUR 273.3 million.
- Expected credit losses on loans and advances decreased to EUR 1,542 thousand in 2025 from EUR 16,246 thousand in 2024.
- Staff costs increased to EUR 91.0 million from EUR 82.3 million and administrative and other operating expenses increased to EUR 68.3 million from EUR 64.6 million.

Main factors affecting performance
- The profitability miss was attributed to declining interest rates combined with intense competition that squeezed interest income, leaving net interest income smaller than expected.
- LHV Pank’s results were pressured by lower net interest income despite higher customer activity and deposits, including a smaller positive effect from deposit repricing.
- Expected credit losses on loans and advances improved versus 2024, decreasing from EUR 16,246 thousand to EUR 1,542 thousand.
- Regulatory complexity increased the operational burden, including 1,200 regulator reports in 2025 alongside increased bureaucracy and internal reporting requirements.

Overall summary
- 2025 showed strong balance-sheet and volume growth (assets +17% YoY, loans +20% YoY, deposits +18% YoY) but weaker profitability with net profit of EUR 117.0 million (-22% YoY).
- Performance missed financial targets with profitability 6.5% below plan despite meeting business volume targets and expanding the customer base.
- Management emphasized resilience in core business volumes and customer growth while highlighting interest-income pressure, competitive dynamics, and increased regulatory/reporting complexity as key headwinds.
