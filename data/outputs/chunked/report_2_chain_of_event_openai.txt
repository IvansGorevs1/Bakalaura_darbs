Main financial indicators
- Net profit was €28.7 million in 2025, down from €32.2 million in 2024.  
- Net interest income was €73.3 million in 2025, down from €77.6 million in 2024.  
- Total net operating income was €79.4 million in 2025, down from €81.9 million in 2024.  
- The net loan portfolio was €2,114 million at year-end 2025, up from €1,774 million at year-end 2024.  

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
- Net profit declined by 11% year-on-year to €28.7 million in 2025.  
- Net interest income decreased by €4.3 million (from €77.6 million to €73.3 million) in 2025.  
- Total net operating income decreased by €2.5 million (from €81.9 million to €79.4 million) in 2025.  
- The net loan portfolio increased by €340 million (from €1,774 million to €2,114 million) in 2025.  

Main factors affecting performance
- The decline in net profit was mainly attributed to the lower-interest economic environment that the 19% growth in business volumes could not offset.  
- The 2025 macro environment included receding inflation and declining interest rates, with base rates declining until summer 2025 and remaining stable thereafter, affecting interest-related earnings.  
- Growth in customer numbers by 19,000 (+9%) to 227,000 and loan portfolio growth to above €2 billion supported higher business volumes during 2025.  
- The value-in-use test for goodwill at 31.12.2025 indicated that the recoverable value of the cash-generating unit exceeded the carrying amount and no impairment losses were recognised.  

Overall summary
- In 2025, Coop Pank grew its loan portfolio to €2,114 million (+€340 million, +19%) and increased customers to 227,000 (+9%).  
- Despite business growth, net profit decreased to €28.7 million (-11% year-on-year) as lower interest rates reduced earnings.  
- Earnings headwinds were reflected in lower net interest income (€73.3 million vs. €77.6 million) and lower total net operating income (€79.4 million vs. €81.9 million).
