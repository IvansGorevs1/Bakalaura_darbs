Main financial indicators
* The group’s revenue for 2024 was 7,477 thousand euros.
* The group reported an operating profit of 68 thousand euros and a net loss of 624 thousand euros.
* Total interest-bearing liabilities amounted to 15,215 thousand euros as of 31 December 2024.
* Cash and cash equivalents totaled 1,720 thousand euros at the end of 2024.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
* Revenue declined from 18,339 thousand euros in 2023 to 7,477 thousand euros in 2024.
* Performance shifted from a net profit of 3,550 thousand euros in 2023 to a net loss of 624 thousand euros in 2024.
* Total interest-bearing liabilities increased from 10,080 thousand euros in 2023 to 15,215 thousand euros in 2024.
* The group’s equity decreased from 21,017 thousand euros at the end of 2023 to 19,739 thousand euros at the end of 2024.

Main factors affecting performance
* The decline in revenue was primarily driven by a significant reduction in the sale of own real estate, which fell from 17,581 thousand euros in 2023 to 7,145 thousand euros in 2024.
* Financial costs increased to 640 thousand euros in 2024, reflecting higher interest expenses on borrowings and a weighted average interest rate of 8.7%.
* Profitability was impacted by a reversal of a 487 thousand euro inventory write-down in 2024, compared to a 528 thousand euro write-down in 2023.
* The group’s performance remains sensitive to real estate development cycles and the timing of apartment sales, which are the primary drivers of revenue recognition.

Overall summary
* The company faced a challenging 2024 economic environment characterized by reduced transaction volumes and higher borrowing costs.
* Management continues to prioritize the completion of existing residential developments and the optimization of its operational structure across Estonia and Bulgaria.
* The group maintains a disciplined approach to risk management, including the monitoring of liquidity and interest rate exposures, while preparing for future regulatory changes.
