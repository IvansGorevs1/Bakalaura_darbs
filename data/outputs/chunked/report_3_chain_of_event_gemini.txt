Main financial indicators
* The Group’s consolidated revenue for 2025 was 35.4 million euros.
* The Group’s net profit for 2025 amounted to 1.0 million euros.
* Total assets as of 31 December 2025 were 91.8 million euros.
* Total interest-bearing loan liabilities were 47.7 million euros at the end of the reporting period.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
* Consolidated revenue decreased from 38.4 million euros in 2024 to 35.4 million euros in 2025.
* Operating profit fell to 3.4 million euros in 2025 from 4.3 million euros in the previous year.
* Contingent liabilities arising from embedded derivatives increased significantly to 14.0 million euros from 5.9 million euros in 2024.
* Revenue from the sale of real estate declined to 33.2 million euros in 2025 compared to 36.2 million euros in 2024.

Main factors affecting performance
* Profitability was negatively impacted by a lower number of apartment handovers and reduced gross profit margins.
* Marketing expenses rose to 1.3 million euros from 0.9 million euros due to the increased scale and number of projects on sale.
* The Group successfully completed a bond offering in November 2025, raising 8 million euros to support operations.
* Financial performance was influenced by the reclassification of several development projects from inventories to investment property.

Overall summary
* Hepsor Group experienced a decline in both annual revenue and net profit compared to the previous year due to project development cycles and lower delivery volumes.
* Despite lower annual profitability, the Group successfully secured additional funding through a bond issuance and maintained a diversified portfolio of projects.
* The Group continues to focus on residential and commercial development across Estonia, Latvia, and Canada, while managing risks related to contingent liabilities and ongoing construction projects.
