Main financial indicators
* The group reported annual revenue of 7,477 thousand euros.
* The company recorded a net loss of 624 thousand euros.
* Total interest-bearing liabilities amounted to 15,215 thousand euros as of 31 December 2024.
* Total inventories reached 29,170 thousand euros at the end of the period.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
* Revenue decreased from 18,339 thousand euros in 2023 to 7,477 thousand euros in 2024.
* Net profit declined from 3,550 thousand euros in 2023 to a net loss of 624 thousand euros in 2024.
* Total liabilities increased to 19,829 thousand euros from 14,173 thousand euros in the previous year.
* The group's total assets grew to 39,568 thousand euros, up from 35,190 thousand euros at the end of 2023.

Main factors affecting performance
* Operating results were impacted by a significant reduction in the sale of own real estate compared to the prior year.
* The group reversed a previous inventory write-down of 487 thousand euros for the Botanica Lozen project.
* Financial costs rose to 640 thousand euros, driven by higher interest expenses on development loans.
* Administrative expenses decreased to 1,127 thousand euros due to lower personnel costs and reduced headcount.

Overall summary
* The company navigated a challenging economic environment in 2024 by focusing on long-term sustainability and project completion.
* Management is prioritizing risk mitigation through geographic diversification and the integration of ESG principles into development projects.
* The group remains committed to its strategic direction, including the development of energy-efficient residential properties for 2025.
