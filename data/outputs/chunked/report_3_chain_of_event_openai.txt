Main financial indicators
- 2025 consolidated revenue was 35.4 million euros, down from 38.4 million euros in 2024.  
- 2025 net profit was 1.0 million euros, down from 2.1 million euros in 2024.  
- Q4 2025 consolidated revenue was 4.5 million euros, down from 10.5 million euros in Q4 2024.  
- Q4 2025 net result was a net loss of 0.5 million euros, compared with a net profit of 1.0 million euros in Q4 2024.  

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
- Full-year consolidated revenue decreased by 3.0 million euros (from 38.4 million euros to 35.4 million euros).  
- Full-year net profit declined by 1.1 million euros (from 2.1 million euros to 1.0 million euros).  
- Q4 2025 revenue fell by 6.0 million euros (from 10.5 million euros to 4.5 million euros).  
- Q4 2025 shifted from net profit of 1.0 million euros to a net loss of 0.5 million euros (a deterioration of 1.5 million euros).  

Main factors affecting performance
- Revenue and profitability declined due to fewer apartments being handed over to customers in 2025 (141 vs 194 in 2024).  
- Profitability was negatively impacted by lower profit margins on development projects sold (15.9% gross margin in 2025 vs 17.8% in 2024).  
- Marketing expenses increased to 1.3 million euros in 2025 from 0.9 million euros in 2024, driven by more and larger projects on sale requiring more intensive sales and marketing.  
- Q4 2025 results were further pressured as the net profit turned into a net loss (net loss of 0.5 million euros in Q4 2025 vs net profit of 1.0 million euros in Q4 2024).  

Overall summary
- Hepsor’s 2025 performance weakened versus 2024, with revenue down to 35.4 million euros and net profit down to 1.0 million euros.  
- The decline was especially pronounced in Q4 2025, where revenue dropped to 4.5 million euros and the Group reported a net loss of 0.5 million euros.  
- Management attributed the weaker profitability mainly to fewer apartment deliveries, lower margins on sold projects, and higher marketing expenses tied to expanded project activity.
