Main financial indicators
* The bank reported a net profit of 28.7 million euros for the 2025 financial year.
* The net loan portfolio grew to 2.114 billion euros by the end of the period.
* Total assets reached 2.703 billion euros as of 31 December 2025.
* Shareholders' equity increased to 234.5 million euros by the end of the reporting period.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
* Net profit decreased by 11% year-on-year, falling from 32.2 million euros in 2024 to 28.7 million euros in 2025.
* The net loan portfolio increased by 19%, rising from 1.774 billion euros to 2.114 billion euros.
* Net interest income declined from 77.6 million euros in 2024 to 73.3 million euros in 2025.
* Cash and cash equivalents rose significantly to 469.7 million euros, compared to 325.4 million euros at the end of 2024.

Main factors affecting performance
* A lower-interest economic environment negatively impacted net profit despite continued growth in business volumes.
* The bank successfully issued 250 million euros in covered bonds to diversify funding sources.
* Management successfully tested goodwill for impairment, confirming that no impairment losses were necessary for the consumer financing segment.
* The bank increased its advance income tax payments for credit institutions, with the effective tax rate rising to 17% in 2025.

Overall summary
* Coop Pank experienced a year of business volume expansion, with the loan portfolio growing by 19% and total assets increasing to 2.703 billion euros.
* Profitability was pressured by the changing interest rate environment and higher tax obligations, leading to an 11% decline in net profit.
* The bank maintained strong capitalization and liquidity, supported by new international funding sources, strategic hedging, and comprehensive risk management frameworks.
