Main financial indicators
- Total revenue was 7,477 thousand euros in 2024 versus 18,339 thousand euros in 2023.
- Operating profit (Operating profit) was 68 thousand euros in 2024 versus 3,940 thousand euros in 2023.
- Net profit/(loss) was a net loss of 624 thousand euros in 2024 versus net profit of 3,550 thousand euros in 2023.
- Total assets at period end were 39,568 thousand euros in 2024 versus 35,190 thousand euros in 2023.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
- Total revenue decreased by 10,862 thousand euros (from 18,339 thousand euros to 7,477 thousand euros).
- Operating profit declined by 3,872 thousand euros (from 3,940 thousand euros to 68 thousand euros).
- Net profit shifted to a loss, declining by 4,174 thousand euros (from a profit of 3,550 thousand euros to a loss of 624 thousand euros).
- Total assets increased by 4,378 thousand euros (from 35,190 thousand euros to 39,568 thousand euros).

Main factors affecting performance
- Revenue in 2024 was driven by sale of own real estate of 7,145 thousand euros and rental of real estate of 52 thousand euros, compared with sale of own real estate of 17,488 thousand euros and rental of real estate of 336 thousand euros in 2023.
- Total cost of sold real estate and services was -5,707 thousand euros in 2024 versus -12,740 thousand euros in 2023, including cost of real estate sold of -5,500 thousand euros versus -11,880 thousand euros.
- Total marketing and distribution expenses were -551 thousand euros in 2024 versus -387 thousand euros in 2023.
- In 2024, the group’s weighted average interest rate of interest-bearing liabilities was 8.7%, up from 8.2% at 31 December 2023.

Overall summary
- Arco Vara Group reported a sharp contraction in 2024 total revenue to 7,477 thousand euros and a major deterioration in profitability, resulting in a net loss of 624 thousand euros.
- Operating profit fell to 68 thousand euros from 3,940 thousand euros and net profit turned from 3,550 thousand euros profit in 2023 to a loss in 2024.
- The company faced headwinds from low consumer confidence and high borrowing costs, compounded by delayed construction permitting, though stabilisation was observed in Q4 with improved transaction volumes.
- Balance sheet scale increased, with total assets rising to 39,568 thousand euros at 31 December 2024.
