Main financial indicators
- Net profit was EUR 117.0 million in 2025, down 22% YoY (EUR 33.3 million decrease).  
- Net income was EUR 304.8 million in 2025, down 10% YoY.  
- Net interest income was EUR 235.1 million in 2025, down 14% YoY, while net fee income was EUR 63.3 million, up 5% YoY.  
- ROE was 16.0% in 2025, which is 1.6% below the forecasted level.  

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
- Net interest margin (NIM) fell to 2.49% in 2025 from 3.48% in 2024.  
- Operating expenses increased to EUR 159.3 million (+8% YoY), with most of the increase related to higher employee numbers.  
- Total assets rose to EUR 10,233.0 million at 31 Dec 2025 from EUR 8,736.3 million at 31 Dec 2024.  
- Expected credit losses on loans and advances decreased to EUR 1,542 thousand in 2025 from EUR 16,246 thousand in 2024.  

Main factors affecting performance
- A decline in interest rates combined with intense competition squeezed interest income more than interest expense.  
- Deposit dynamics and pricing pressure affected earnings, including a higher share of demand deposits (65%) as term deposits became less attractive.  
- The Group recognised an impairment loss of EUR 1.6 million related to AS LHV Paytech while no impairment loss was recognised for AS LHV Varahaldus.  
- Regulatory complexity increased, including 1,200 regulator reports in 2025, alongside ongoing operational and system investment needs.  

Overall summary
- In 2025, LHV Group grew business volumes (assets EUR 10.2 billion, +17% YoY; loans EUR 5.5 billion, +20% YoY; deposits EUR 8.1 billion, +18% YoY) but delivered lower profitability, with net profit EUR 117.0 million (-22% YoY).  
- Total net income in 2025 was EUR 304.8 million (-10% YoY), driven by weaker net interest income (EUR 235.1 million, -14% YoY) despite higher net fee income (EUR 63.3 million, +5% YoY).  
- Performance was impacted by expense growth (EUR 159.3 million, +8% YoY) and missed key profitability targets, with both net income and net profit below plan and ROE below forecast.
