Main financial indicators
- Revenue was 7,477 thousand euros in 2024 versus 18,339 thousand euros in 2023.  
- Operating profit (EBIT) was 68 thousand euros in 2024 versus 3,940 thousand euros in 2023.  
- Net profit (loss) was -624 thousand euros in 2024 versus 3,550 thousand euros in 2023.  
- Total assets were 39,568 thousand euros at 31 December 2024 versus 35,190 thousand euros at 31 December 2023.  

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
- Revenue decreased by 10,862 thousand euros (from 18,339 thousand euros to 7,477 thousand euros).  
- Operating profit (EBIT) declined by 3,872 thousand euros (from 3,940 thousand euros to 68 thousand euros).  
- Net profit shifted to a net loss of 624 thousand euros (from net profit of 3,550 thousand euros to -624 thousand euros).  
- Net loans increased by 4,887 thousand euros (from 8,608 thousand euros to 13,495 thousand euros).  

Main factors affecting performance
- Low consumer confidence and high interest rates pressured the real estate market and the company’s financial results in 2024.  
- The first-half challenge included selling completed apartments in Kodulahe and Kuldlehe with market value of nearly 14 million euros, alongside modest overall purchasing interest.  
- Construction of the next stage of Kodulahe (Soodi 6) was postponed due to processing of the construction permit, impacting the project schedule and future revenue.  
- Stabilization in the second half was linked to a decrease of Euribor, supporting market activity and increased apartment transaction volume in Q4 2024.  

Overall summary
- Arco Vara reported a significant decline in revenue and profitability in 2024, resulting in operating profit of 68 thousand euros and a net loss of 624 thousand euros.  
- Revenue fell to 7,477 thousand euros from 18,339 thousand euros while total assets rose to 39,568 thousand euros.  
- Net loans increased to 13,495 thousand euros and the weighted average annual interest rate on interest-bearing liabilities increased to 8.7%.
