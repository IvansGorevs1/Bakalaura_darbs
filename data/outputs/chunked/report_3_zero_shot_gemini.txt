Main financial indicators
* Consolidated revenue for 2025 was 35.4 million euros, compared to 38.4 million euros in 2024.
* The Group recorded a net profit of 1.0 million euros for 2025, down from 2.1 million euros in 2024.
* Consolidated revenue for the fourth quarter of 2025 was 4.5 million euros, compared to 10.5 million euros in the same period of 2024.
* The Group recorded a net loss of 0.5 million euros in the fourth quarter of 2025, compared to a net profit of 1.0 million euros in the fourth quarter of 2024.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
* Total equity increased to 27.1 million euros as of 31 December 2025, up from 22.0 million euros at the end of 2024.
* Marketing expenses rose to 1.3 million euros in 2025 from 0.9 million euros in 2024 due to intensified sales activities.
* Contingent liabilities arising from embedded derivatives increased to 13.98 million euros as of 31 December 2025 from 5.92 million euros at the end of 2024.
* Cash and cash equivalents decreased to 3.8 million euros at year-end 2025 from 6.2 million euros at the end of 2024.

Main factors affecting performance
* Revenue and profit are significantly influenced by the 24–48 month project development cycle, which causes fluctuations between quarters and years.
* Profitability was negatively impacted by lower profit margins on development projects sold during the year and higher marketing expenditures.
* The Group reclassified several properties from inventories to investment property to focus on long-term rental income generation.
* Financial performance was supported by a stable economic environment, though net profit was affected by increased interest expenses and corporate income tax.

Overall summary
* Hepsor experienced a decline in annual revenue and net profit in 2025 primarily due to a lower volume of apartment handovers.
* Despite lower annual results, the Group strengthened its capital structure through a bond issuance and increased its total assets to 91.8 million euros.
* The company continues to advance its strategic development portfolio in Estonia, Latvia, and Canada while managing its debt obligations and project-specific financing.
