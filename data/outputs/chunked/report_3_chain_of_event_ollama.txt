Main financial indicators
• Revenue: 35.4 million euros 
• Net profit: 1.0 million euros 
• Gross profit: 5.6 million euros 
• Operating profit: 3.4 million euros 
• EBITDA: 3.5 million euros 

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
• Revenue decreased by 8.7% to 35.4 million euros (2024: 38.4 million euros) 
• Net profit declined by 95.2% to 1.0 million euros (2024: 2.1 million euros) 
• Gross profit margin decreased from 17.6% to 15.9% 
• Operating profit margin fell from 11.3% to 9.5% 

Main factors affecting performance
• Decrease in the number of apartments handed over to customers compared to the previous year (141 apartments vs. 194) 
• Lower gross margin on sold projects (16.9% vs. 17.8%) 
• Increased marketing expenses (1.3 million euros vs. 0.9 million euros) 

Overall summary
• Revenue decreased due to lower sales of apartments and other real estate development activities 
• Net profit declined significantly due to increased marketing expenses and lower gross margin on sold projects 
• Gross profit margin and operating profit margin decreased compared to the previous year
