Main financial indicators
* The net profit for 2025 amounted to 28.7 million euros.
* The net loan portfolio grew by 19% to reach 2.114 billion euros.
* The return on equity (ROE) for the period was 12.9%.
* The cost-to-income ratio stood at 52.3%.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
* Net profit decreased by 11% compared to the 32.2 million euros reported in 2024.
* Total net operating income declined from 81.9 million euros in 2024 to 79.4 million euros in 2025.
* The net interest margin (NIM) fell to 3.0% from 3.8% in the previous year.
* The number of customers increased by 19,000, reaching a total of 227,000 by the end of 2025.

Main factors affecting performance
* The lower-interest economic environment negatively impacted net profit despite a 19% growth in business volumes.
* The bank issued 250 million euros in covered bonds in 2025 to diversify funding sources and hedge interest rate risk.
* Management successfully tested goodwill for impairment, confirming that no write-downs were necessary for the consumer financing segment.
* The bank increased its advance income tax payments following a rise in the applicable tax rate for credit institutions to 18%.

Overall summary
* Coop Pank achieved significant growth in loan volumes while managing a challenging interest rate environment that resulted in a year-on-year decline in net profit.
* The bank continues to strengthen its capital and liquidity position through strategic bond issuances and prudent management of its loan portfolio.
* Strategic objectives remain focused on long-term market share expansion and sustainable profitability through 2030.
