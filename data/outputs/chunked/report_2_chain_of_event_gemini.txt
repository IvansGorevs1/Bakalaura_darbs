Main financial indicators
* The net profit for 2025 was 28.7 million euros.
* The net loan portfolio reached 2,114 million euros by the end of 2025.
* Total net operating income for the period was 73.3 million euros.
* Shareholders' equity stood at 234.5 million euros as of 31.12.2025.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
* Net profit declined by 10.7% compared to the 32.2 million euros reported in 2024.
* The net loan portfolio increased by 340 million euros, representing a 19% growth over the previous year.
* Total net operating income decreased from 77.6 million euros in 2024 to 73.3 million euros in 2025.
* Basic earnings per share fell to 0.28 euros in 2025 from 0.31 euros in the prior year.

Main factors affecting performance
* The decline in net profit was primarily driven by a reduction in net interest income and higher credit loss allowances.
* The bank successfully issued 250 million euros in covered bonds in 2025 to diversify funding sources.
* Operating expenses decreased to 10.8 million euros in 2025 from 11.7 million euros in the previous year.
* The bank maintained a stable goodwill value of 6.8 million euros, with impairment tests confirming no losses were necessary.

Overall summary
* Coop Pank achieved significant growth in its loan portfolio during 2025 while maintaining a prudent approach to liquidity and market risk management.
* Despite a year-on-year decrease in net profit, the bank successfully executed its growth strategy and utilized hedging instruments to mitigate interest rate volatility.
* The bank remains focused on long-term strategic goals, including the optimization of regulatory capital and the integration of ESG risk management into its core operations.
