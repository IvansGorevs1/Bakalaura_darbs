Main financial indicators
- Consolidated revenue in 2025 was €35.4 million (2024: €38.4 million), including revenue from sale of real estate of €33.2 million (2024: €36.2 million).  
- 2025 net profit was €1.0 million (2024: €2.1 million), including €0.4 million attributable to owners of the parent (2024: €0.4 million).  
- Q4 2025 revenue was €4.5 million (Q4 2024: €10.5 million) with a net loss of €0.5 million (Q4 2024: net profit of €1.0 million).  
- 2025 gross profit margin was 15.9% (2024: 17.6%) and net profit margin was 2.9% (2024: 5.6%).  

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
- Full-year revenue decreased by €3.0 million from €38.4 million to €35.4 million, while full-year net profit fell from €2.1 million to €1.0 million.  
- Q4 2025 revenue dropped from €10.5 million to €4.5 million and Q4 2025 moved from net profit of €1.0 million to a net loss of €0.5 million.  
- 2025 gross profit margin declined from 17.6% to 15.9%, alongside the Q4 gross profit margin drop from 24.0% to 15.3%.  
- Marketing expenses increased to €1.3 million in 2025 from €0.9 million in 2024.  

Main factors affecting performance
- Profitability was negatively affected by a decrease in apartments handed over to customers to 141 in 2025 from 194 in 2024.  
- Profit margins on sold development projects were lower in 2025, with gross margin at 16.9% (2024: 17.8%).  
- Marketing expenses increased to €1.3 million in 2025 (2024: €0.9 million) due to growth in the number and scale of projects on sale.  
- In Q4 2025, properties were reclassified from inventories to investment property, resulting in other operating income of €0.8 million after fair value revaluation.  

Overall summary
- Hepsor’s 2025 performance weakened year-on-year with lower revenue (€35.4 million vs €38.4 million) and lower net profit (€1.0 million vs €2.1 million).  
- The fourth quarter was particularly weak, with revenue decreasing to €4.5 million and the Group recording a net loss of €0.5 million versus net profit of €1.0 million in Q4 2024.  
- The decline was mainly driven by fewer apartment handovers and reduced gross margins, partially offset by €0.8 million other operating income from inventory-to-investment-property reclassification in Q4.
