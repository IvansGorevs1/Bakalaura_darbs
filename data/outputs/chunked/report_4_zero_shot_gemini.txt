Main financial indicators
* The Group’s net profit for 2025 was EUR 117.0 million, representing a 22% decrease compared to the previous year.
* Total consolidated assets reached EUR 10.2 billion, an increase of 17% year-on-year.
* The loan portfolio grew by 20% to EUR 5.5 billion, while total customer deposits increased by 18% to EUR 8.1 billion.
* Basic earnings per share decreased to EUR 0.35 in 2025 from EUR 0.46 in the previous year.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
* Net profit fell by EUR 33.3 million compared to 2024, primarily due to a decline in net interest income.
* Net interest income decreased by 14% to EUR 235.1 million, driven by higher interest expenses and lower interest income.
* Expected credit losses on loans and advances dropped significantly to EUR 1.5 million in 2025 from EUR 16.2 million in 2024.
* Subordinated debt increased to EUR 206.9 million, supported by the issuance of new Tier 2 and Additional Tier 1 bonds during the year.

Main factors affecting performance
* A decline in interest rates and intense market competition squeezed interest margins, negatively impacting net interest income.
* The Group’s profitability was influenced by a shift in the corporate income tax rate for credit institutions to 18% starting 1 January 2025.
* The Group successfully managed liquidity through the issuance of new subordinated debt and covered bonds, with the covered bond program increased to EUR 1.5 billion.
* Moody's upgraded the rating of the covered bond program to Aaa and the Group’s long-term issuer rating to Baa2 with a positive outlook.

Overall summary
* Despite missing profitability targets, the Group successfully grew its business volumes and customer base throughout 2025.
* The Group maintains a long-term goal to achieve steady income growth and significantly increase net profit by 2030.
* Future performance will be supported by a robust risk management framework and continued compliance with evolving IFRS reporting standards.
