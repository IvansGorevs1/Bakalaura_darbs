Main financial indicators
* The Group reported consolidated revenue of 35.4 million euros for 2025, compared to 38.4 million euros in 2024.
* Net profit for the year amounted to 1.0 million euros, down from 2.1 million euros in the previous year.
* Total assets increased to 91.8 million euros as of 31 December 2025, compared to 88.8 million euros at the end of 2024.
* Contingent liabilities arising from embedded derivatives rose to 13.98 million euros from 5.92 million euros in the previous year.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
* Marketing expenses increased to 1.33 million euros from 0.90 million euros due to higher project-related costs.
* Current liabilities decreased to 14.1 million euros from 30.7 million euros, primarily driven by the repayment of bank loans.
* Non-current liabilities rose to 50.6 million euros from 36.1 million euros, reflecting the issuance of new bonds and increased long-term borrowings.
* Dividends totaling 1.0 million euros were paid to shareholders during the 2025 reporting period.

Main factors affecting performance
* Revenue and profitability were impacted by a lower volume of completed apartment handovers compared to the prior year.
* Financial results were influenced by the reclassification of inventories and the successful raising of 8 million euros through a public bond programme.
* The Group expanded its portfolio through new joint ventures, including the establishment of Hepsor SOF OÜ and Hepsor PHX5 OÜ.
* A legal claim for 467 thousand euros regarding construction defects at Manufaktuuri 18 was brought against a subsidiary, though management considers the claim unsubstantiated.

Overall summary
* Hepsor Group experienced a decline in annual revenue and net profit primarily due to a lower volume of completed apartment handovers.
* Despite lower annual earnings, the Group strengthened its balance sheet by reducing current debt and securing new long-term financing.
* The company continues to focus on residential and commercial development across Estonia, Latvia, and Canada, maintaining a stable long-term outlook.
