Main financial indicators
• Revenue: 7,477 thousand euros (2024), 18,339 thousand euros (2023)
• Operating profit (EBIT): 68 thousand euros (2024), 3,940 thousand euros (2023)
• Net loss: -624 thousand euros (2024), 3,550 thousand euros (2023)

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
• Revenue decreased by 58.8% from 18,339 thousand euros in 2023 to 7,477 thousand euros in 2024.
• Operating profit (EBIT) decreased by 98.6% from 3,940 thousand euros in 2023 to 68 thousand euros in 2024.
• Net loss increased by 17.9% from 3,550 thousand euros in 2023 to -624 thousand euros in 2024.

Main factors affecting performance
• High borrowing costs and volatility of the economic environment placed pressure on corporate profitability.
• Low consumer confidence and high interest rates impacted the activity of the real estate market and the financial results of the company.
• The start of construction of the next stage of Kodulahe was postponed due to processing the construction permit, which impacted the project schedule and future revenue.

Overall summary
• The group's revenue decreased significantly in 2024 compared to 2023, mainly due to low consumer confidence and high interest rates.
• The operating profit (EBIT) also decreased significantly, indicating a decline in profitability.
• Despite these challenges, the company maintained a steady path towards increasing future development volumes and continued development in line with its strategy.
• The group's focus on sustainability and long-term management of investments ensured its financial stability.
• The main risk for the Group is investing equity into a development product for which there is no or too little demand in the future, but measures have been taken to mitigate this risk.
