Main financial indicators
- Net profit for 2025 was EUR 117.0 million (-22% YoY), with net income of EUR 304.8 million (-10% YoY).  
- Total consolidated assets reached EUR 10.2 billion (+17% YoY), including a loan portfolio of EUR 5.5 billion (+20% YoY).  
- Deposits increased to EUR 8.1 billion (+18% YoY), while net interest income decreased to EUR 235.1 million from EUR 273.3 million.  
- Basic earnings per share were EUR 0.35 (2024: EUR 0.46).  

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
- Profitability ended 6.5% below financial plan and net profit decreased 22% YoY despite resilient credit quality.  
- Net interest income fell 14% YoY as interest income and competition dynamics pressured interest income, with impairments down to EUR 1.5 million.  
- Operating expenses increased to EUR 159.3 million (+8% YoY), including staff costs of EUR 91.0 million and administrative and other operating expenses of EUR 68.3 million.  
- Total comprehensive income declined to EUR 112.0 million from EUR 153.7 million, including a decline in other comprehensive income items to -4.969 million on exchange differences.  

Main factors affecting performance
- Lower interest rates and intense competition squeezed interest income, reducing net interest income and contributing to missing profit targets.  
- Fee and commission income grew to EUR 63.3 million (+5% YoY), and the report shows fee and commission income of EUR 85.2 million and fee and commission expense of EUR 22.0 million.  
- Expected credit losses on financial assets measured at amortised cost decreased to EUR 1.518 million in 2025 from EUR 16.256 million in 2024.  
- Results were pressured by EUR 24.0 million deferred tax expense for 2025 payable on expected future dividend payment.  

Overall summary
- The Group reported strong balance-sheet and volume growth in 2025, with assets up to EUR 10.2 billion, loans up to EUR 5.5 billion, and deposits up to EUR 8.1 billion.  
- Profitability declined in 2025, with net profit at EUR 117.0 million (-22% YoY) and performance 6.5% below plan mainly driven by weaker net interest income.  
- The Group faced higher operating expenses (+8% YoY) and increasing regulatory/reporting demands, while maintaining resilient regulatory compliance and strong credit quality.
