Main financial indicators
- Hepsor Group reported consolidated revenue of 35.4 million euros for 2025 versus 38.4 million euros in 2024.
- The Group recorded net profit of 1.0 million euros in 2025 versus 2.1 million euros in 2024.
- Fourth-quarter 2025 revenue was 4.5 million euros versus 10.5 million euros in Q4 2024.
- Fourth-quarter 2025 net loss was 0.5 million euros versus net profit of 1.0 million euros in Q4 2024.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
- Full-year revenue decreased by 3.0 million euros year-over-year (from 38.4 million euros to 35.4 million euros).
- Full-year net profit decreased by 1.1 million euros year-over-year (from 2.1 million euros to 1.0 million euros).
- Fourth-quarter revenue declined by 6.0 million euros year-over-year (from 10.5 million euros to 4.5 million euros).
- Fourth-quarter results swung from net profit of 1.0 million euros to a net loss of 0.5 million euros year-over-year.

Main factors affecting performance
- Revenue and profitability were affected by a lower number of apartments handed over to customers, with 141 apartments delivered in 2025 versus 194 in 2024.
- Profitability was negatively impacted by lower gross margins on development projects sold, with gross margin of 16.9% in 2025 versus 17.8% in 2024.
- Marketing expenses increased to 1.3 million euros in 2025 from 0.9 million euros in 2024, driven by growth in the number and scale of projects on sale and more intensive sales and marketing activities.
- In Q4 2025, properties (Narva mnt 150b, Alvari 1, Alvari 1a and Alvari 5) were reclassified from inventories to investment property, generating 0.8 million euros as other operating income after fair value revaluation.

Overall summary
- In 2025, Hepsor delivered fewer apartments and faced margin pressure and higher marketing costs, leading to lower revenue and reduced net profit compared with 2024.
- In Q4 2025 specifically, revenue dropped sharply and the Group recorded a net loss, reversing the prior-year quarter’s net profit.
- Despite weaker earnings, other operating income increased in Q4 2025 due to the reclassification of inventories to investment property.
- The report also notes that equity items changed during 2025, including a share capital increase by 57,821 euros and dividend payments of 1,002 thousand euros.
