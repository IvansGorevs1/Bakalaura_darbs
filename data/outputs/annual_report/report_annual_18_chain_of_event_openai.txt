OODA AI reported a major shift from build-up to commercialization and growth in 2025. Group net sales increased to SEK 31.880 million from SEK 1.056 million in 2024, driven by invoicing several major transactions under previously communicated plans. Revenue consisted entirely of consulting services, with sales primarily generated in Switzerland and other international markets. Total revenue, including capitalized development work and other operating income, was SEK 40.774 million, compared with SEK 9.540 million.

Operating performance improved substantially. Operating loss narrowed to SEK 29.270 million from SEK 51.781 million, while EBITDA turned positive at SEK 2.957 million compared with a loss of SEK 28.229 million in 2024. The Group incurred approximately SEK 10.238 million in one-off costs related to liquidating older company structures; excluding these costs, adjusted EBITDA was approximately SEK 13.195 million, corresponding to an adjusted EBITDA margin of about 32%. Personnel costs increased to SEK 9.210 million from SEK 7.432 million, while the company maintained a low cost base and made no major recruitment.

Despite the stronger operating result, the Group’s net loss widened to SEK 125.799 million from SEK 83.355 million. Financial items were negative by SEK 95.853 million, compared with SEK 29.900 million negative in 2024, mainly because of impairments and results related to associated companies and other financial holdings. The company’s review and streamlining of its investment portfolio resulted in substantial write-downs, in some cases reducing holdings to a book value of zero. Consequently, financial assets fell to SEK 8.387 million from SEK 96.036 million, and equity declined to SEK 23.859 million from SEK 119.371 million. Earnings per share decreased to negative SEK 35.29 from negative SEK 29.00.

Cash flow from operating activities improved to negative SEK 16.662 million from negative SEK 29.680 million, and was positive by SEK 5.098 million during the second half of the year. However, investing cash flow became more negative at SEK 49.413 million, primarily reflecting investments in intangible and financial assets. Rights issues contributed SEK 44.574 million, but year-end cash declined to SEK 3.335 million from SEK 25.108 million.

Commercial progress included expanded agreements with Win.Rocks AG, bringing total contract value to approximately SEK 21 million, followed by a new agreement of at least SEK 15 million over 24 months. Agreements were also signed with HMM Deutschland and Sergel Kredittjänster, while partnerships with Allgeier Inovar and TIM Solutions supported broader distribution. The platform, partner program, self-service model and decentralized AI infrastructure are intended to support further scaling. Following additional capital raisings, including a planned SEK 10 million issue after year-end, management assessed that the company could continue as a going concern, although continued commercialization, cost control and access to capital remain important.
