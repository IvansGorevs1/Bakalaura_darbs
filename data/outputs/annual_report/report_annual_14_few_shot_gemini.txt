Lyko Group AB reported continued revenue growth alongside declining profitability and a net loss for the 2025 financial year, driven by strategic investments in infrastructure, organization, and retail expansion. 

Total net revenue for 2025 increased by 11.3% to 3,962.6 million Swedish kronor compared with 3,561.7 million Swedish kronor in the previous year. Sales growth was primarily generated by the Nordic operating segment, which grew by 12.0% to 3,699.8 million Swedish kronor and accounted for 93.4% of total net sales. Annual revenue in Norway surpassed one billion Swedish kronor. Conversely, revenue in the Europe segment declined slightly by 1.3% to 130.7 million Swedish kronor. 

Gross profit rose to 1,658.7 million Swedish kronor compared with 1,568.8 million Swedish kronor in 2024. However, the gross margin contracted to 41.9% from 44.0%. Operating profit, or EBIT, weakened to 78.2 million Swedish kronor from 117.4 million Swedish kronor, resulting in an EBIT margin of 2.0% compared with 3.3% in the prior year. The net result for the year deteriorated to a loss of 3.0 million Swedish kronor, down from a profit of 21.0 million Swedish kronor in 2024. Consequently, earnings per share dropped to -0.20 Swedish kronor from 1.37 Swedish kronor. 

The company's financial results were significantly influenced by heavy investments for future expansion, notably the commissioning of the new automated warehouse in Vansbro. Representing the largest project in the company's history with an investment of approximately 500 million Swedish kronor, the automated warehouse facility went live during the year and is designed to handle up to 100,000 orders per day. While laying the groundwork for long-term scalability and cost-efficient logistics, the implementation and ramp-up phases affected financial results during parts of the year. Additional strategic initiatives included the launch of the first 500-square-meter Fab Store concept store in Täby Centrum, the relaunch of the Make Up Store brand, and store openings in Norway and Finland, bringing the total store network to 34 locations by year-end. 

Cash flow from operating activities strengthened substantially, increasing to 527.5 million Swedish kronor compared with 209.7 million Swedish kronor in 2024. Cash flow from investing activities amounted to -351.2 million Swedish kronor, reflecting high capital expenditures directed toward warehouse automation, digital development, and retail expansion. Financing cash flow was 150.9 million Swedish kronor, driven by new borrowings of 270.3 million Swedish kronor offset by lease liability amortizations of 119.4 million Swedish kronor. Total cash and cash equivalents increased to 452.5 million Swedish kronor at the end of 2025, up from 140.5 million Swedish kronor. 

Interest-bearing liabilities including leases totaled 1,416.3 million Swedish kronor, consisting of 745.0 million Swedish kronor in credit institution loans and 671.3 million Swedish kronor in lease liabilities. Net debt excluding IFRS 16 stood at 292.5 million Swedish kronor compared with 333.1 million Swedish kronor at the end of 2024. Equity totaled 456.6 million Swedish kronor, yielding an equity ratio of 14.8%. Reflecting its strategy to prioritize generated cash flows for continued expansion, the Board of Directors proposed no dividend for the 2025 financial year.
