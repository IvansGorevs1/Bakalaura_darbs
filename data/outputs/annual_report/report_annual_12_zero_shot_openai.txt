Konsolidator delivered materially improved performance in 2025, combining stronger growth with substantially lower losses and improved cash generation. Consolidated revenue rose 25% to DKK 25.3m from DKK 20.3m in 2024. Subscription revenue increased to DKK 20.6m from DKK 18.3m, while onboarding and consulting revenue more than doubled, rising 134% to DKK 4.7m following higher onboarding prices introduced in the first quarter.

The EBITDA loss narrowed to DKK 2.0m from DKK 9.3m, an improvement of DKK 7.3m. EBIT loss improved to DKK 5.1m from DKK 12.1m, and the net loss fell to DKK 6.6m from DKK 14.9m. Results benefited from increased revenue, reduced marketing and external costs, lower staff costs, and DKK 2.0m of other operating income. Operating cash flow improved significantly, from negative DKK 9.2m to negative DKK 1.0m, while net cash flow was slightly positive at DKK 11,000 compared with negative DKK 1.5m in 2024. H2 2025 was particularly stronger, producing EBITDA of DKK 0.5m versus a loss of DKK 3.6m in H2 2024 and positive operating cash flow of DKK 0.1m.

Recurring-revenue indicators also strengthened. Contracted ARR increased 15% to DKK 24.4m, above the company’s guidance of DKK 23–24m. New sales rose 45% to DKK 4.5m, average customer size increased from TDKK 70 to TDKK 87, and the CAC payback period improved from 43 to 23 months. The CLV/CAC ratio increased from 2.7 to 4.1, and the Rule of 40 score improved from negative 40% to 17%, reflecting 25% revenue growth and an improved EBITDA margin.

Growth was supported by 52 new customers, compared with 45 in 2024, expansion into four new markets, and a stronger partner model, with 38% of new customers acquired through partners versus 22% previously. The company launched its Financial Data Management solution and expanded development of its FP&A product as part of the new Konsolidator Suite. Iberia became the third-largest market, reaching 30 customers.

Customer retention remained a weakness: ARR churn increased to 12.6% from 10.5%, while net retention stayed at 94%. The Group ended the year with negative equity of DKK 3.6m, despite raising DKK 4.8m through private placements. Management’s 2026 guidance is CARR and revenue of DKK 27–29m and positive EBITDA of DKK 1–2m.
