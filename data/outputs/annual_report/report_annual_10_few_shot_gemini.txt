During the 2025 financial year, InCoax experienced a weakening in its financial performance, primarily driven by timing shifts in customer orders and a more cautious market environment. Net sales for the year declined by 35 percent to SEK 48,055,007 compared with SEK 74,497,775 in the previous year. Despite the lower revenue, gross profit amounted to SEK 29,919,750 compared with SEK 42,179,123 in 2024, while the gross margin improved to 62 percent from 57 percent. 

The company's earnings were impacted by the lower sales volumes combined with continued investments in product development and commercial expansion. Operating loss deteriorated to SEK -32,380,175 compared with an operating loss of SEK -19,378,406 in the prior year. Net loss after tax similarly increased to SEK -32,559,663, or SEK -0.25 per share, compared with a net loss of SEK -19,150,134, or SEK -0.18 per share, in 2024. Total operating expenses decreased to SEK 105,896,000 from SEK 121,245,000, reflecting lower costs for goods for resale and reduced external expenses, although personnel costs increased slightly. 

Throughout the period, the company maintained active investments in its technology platform, with capitalized development costs amounting to SEK 25,269,745 compared with SEK 25,649,493 in the preceding year. To address financial pressures and lower its ongoing cost base, the company initiated structural cost reductions projected to decrease annual expenses by approximately 40 percent, corresponding to about SEK 25 million starting in 2026. 

Cash flow from operating activities improved to SEK -20,011,617 from SEK -27,117,256 in 2024. Total cash flow including financing activities turned positive at SEK 6,516,117 compared with SEK -12,758,133 in the previous year, supported by completed financing activities that included a directed share issue, loan facilities, and convertible instruments which strengthened the company's liquidity position. At the end of the period, cash and cash equivalents stood at SEK 8,748,046 compared with SEK 2,231,929 at the close of 2024, while equity ratio stood at 71.8 percent compared with 80.8 percent previously. The company's auditor highlighted that short-term financing and going concern depend on the successful exercise of outstanding warrant programs or alternative financing solutions.
