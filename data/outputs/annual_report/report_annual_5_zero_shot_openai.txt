Eleving Group delivered record financial performance in 2025, supported by strong portfolio growth, higher demand and continued geographic and product expansion. Revenue reached EUR 250.0 million, up 15.4% year on year from EUR 216.6 million. Adjusted EBITDA increased 13.5% to EUR 101.9 million, while net portfolio grew 20.2% to EUR 446.3 million from EUR 371.2 million. Loan issuances reached an all-time high of EUR 458.0 million, 24.3% above 2024, with vehicle and device finance issuances up 34.0% and consumer finance issuances up 15.7%.

Profit from continuing operations rose modestly to EUR 29.2 million from EUR 28.8 million. Total net profit was EUR 29.2 million, broadly stable compared with EUR 29.6 million in 2024, partly reflecting a EUR 5.0 million foreign-currency translation loss in other comprehensive income and a lower net foreign exchange result. Net profit before foreign exchange effects and discontinued operations increased 25.6% to EUR 40.8 million. Interest revenue grew to EUR 241.6 million from EUR 203.7 million, while interest expense increased to EUR 46.0 million from EUR 41.5 million. Impairment expense was EUR 42.1 million, down from EUR 54.2 million; higher loan write-offs were largely offset by releases of existing impairment allowances. The Group also recorded EUR 1.2 million of bond refinancing expense.

Consumer lending was the largest revenue contributor at EUR 114.8 million, up 18.0%. Flexible vehicle finance revenue rose 25.9% to EUR 60.3 million, while traditional vehicle finance declined 6.0% to EUR 67.0 million. Device financing, launched in 2025, generated EUR 7.9 million. Installment loans were introduced in several European markets, smartphone financing was launched in Kenya and Uganda, and the smartphone portfolio reached EUR 13.5 million by year-end. Tanzania became the Group’s 17th market, focused initially on motorcycle financing.

Capital-market activity strengthened funding capacity. The Group completed a EUR 40 million bond tap and issued EUR 275 million of new five-year bonds while refinancing EUR 150 million of existing debt. Total borrowings reached EUR 441.6 million. The capitalization ratio declined to 23.7% from 29.3%, net leverage increased to 3.8 from 3.3, and interest coverage was 2.3 versus 2.4. Dividends paid to parent shareholders totaled EUR 19.65 million. Fitch affirmed the Group’s B rating and upgraded its outlook from stable to positive. Management expects further growth in 2026, supported by the expanded funding base, new products, local-currency financing and continued efficiency initiatives.
