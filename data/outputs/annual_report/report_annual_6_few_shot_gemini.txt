During the 2025 financial year, Gigasun AB achieved solid operational growth alongside a strategic refinancing process within its Chinese operations. Total revenue for the group increased by 9.8 percent to SEK 239,655 thousand compared with SEK 218,264 thousand in the previous year. Net revenue rose by 9.9 percent to SEK 203,179 thousand, up from SEK 184,795 thousand, supported by higher electricity production. Gross profit improved to SEK 195,829 thousand from SEK 179,219 thousand, maintaining a stable gross margin of 82 percent. Total operating expenses increased by 5.5 percent to SEK 166,177 thousand, driven primarily by scheduled depreciation and increased maintenance costs associated with a larger installed asset base. Operating profit, or EBIT, grew by 21.1 percent to SEK 73,478 thousand compared with SEK 60,677 thousand in the prior year, reflecting improved underlying profitability and stable operations. The operating margin improved to 31 percent from 28 percent. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization reached SEK 163,733 thousand, yielding an EBITDA margin of 68 percent. Net financial items resulted in a total cost of SEK 85,679 thousand, which included interest expenses and similar charges of SEK 81,976 thousand and negative exchange rate differences of SEK 3,843 thousand. Profit after tax for the period amounted to SEK -7,206 thousand, representing a considerable improvement compared with SEK -32,944 thousand reported in the preceding year.

Operationally, the company expanded its portfolio of roof-mounted solar energy installations in China, adding 55.7 MW of new capacity during the year. By the end of 2025, total revenue-generating capacity reached 395.8 MW across 216 installations, compared with 340.1 MW across 200 installations at the end of 2024. Total electricity generated during the year increased by approximately 30 percent to 327.8 million kWh, up from 252.6 million kWh, which contributed to an estimated reduction of approximately 161,000 tonnes in CO2 emissions. During the year, the company signed 17 new contracts representing 41.7 MW of installed capacity, and closed the period with 16.4 MW in signed orders. 

The company’s balance sheet at year-end reflected total assets of SEK 2,296,782 thousand, down from SEK 2,528,093 thousand at the close of 2024. Tangible fixed assets accounted for SEK 1,914,134 thousand, primarily comprising operational solar PV installations. Total equity stood at SEK 488,201 thousand compared with SEK 577,336 thousand, resulting in an equity ratio of 21 percent, down from 23 percent. Interest-bearing debt was reduced to SEK 1,382,613 thousand from SEK 1,483,061 thousand, while net interest-bearing debt decreased to SEK 1,337,465 thousand. Cash and bank balances ended the period at SEK 45,149 thousand, compared with SEK 89,889 thousand at the previous year-end. Cash flow from operating activities reached SEK 101,058 thousand, down from SEK 133,785 thousand. Investing activities generated a cash flow of SEK -208,101 thousand, driven predominantly by capital expenditures of SEK 169,877 thousand directed toward ongoing and new solar installations. Financing activities provided a cash flow of SEK 72,141 thousand, as new loan proceeds in China were utilized to fund projects and repay existing debt. 

A notable financial development during the year was the continuation of the company's strategic refinancing initiatives in China. Gigasun entered into agreements with Agricultural Bank Financial Leasing Co., Ltd to refinance approximately SEK 1,242 million in total, which included an additional SEK 372 million secured during 2025 to repay existing sale and leaseback facilities with JiangSu Financial Leasing Co., Ltd. The new financing carries a reduced interest rate of 3.60 percent and extends amortization to 10 years, which is expected to support future cash flows and earnings through lower financing costs.
