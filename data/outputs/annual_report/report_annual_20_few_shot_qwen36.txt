AS Tallink Grupp reported a decline in financial performance during the 2025 financial year, characterized by lower revenue and reduced profitability compared to the previous period. The Group’s audited consolidated revenue amounted to EUR 765.3 million, representing a decrease of 2.6% from EUR 785.8 million in 2024. This contraction was primarily driven by a 19.2% drop in cargo units transported to 245,004, alongside a modest 0.9% reduction in passenger numbers to 5.5 million. The operating environment was challenging, marked by cautious consumer behavior, geopolitical tensions, and weak economic conditions in the Group’s core markets of Finland, Estonia, and Sweden. Revenue from route operations, the Group’s core business, fell by EUR 13.7 million to EUR 625.2 million. Segment results varied across routes, with the Estonia-Finland route maintaining strong passenger growth but seeing a decline in cargo revenue, while the Estonia-Sweden and Finland-Sweden routes experienced significant drops in both passenger and cargo volumes.

Profitability metrics weakened substantially. EBITDA declined by EUR 45.1 million, or 25.7%, to EUR 130.1 million, down from EUR 175.2 million in 2024. The net profit for the period was EUR 17.3 million, a sharp decrease from EUR 40.3 million in the prior year. The reduction in earnings was influenced by lower onboard sales due to fewer passengers and weak consumer confidence, as well as higher personnel expenses which rose by 4.3% to EUR 201.0 million. However, these negative factors were partially offset by a EUR 18.0 million decrease in depreciation and amortization expenses. This cost saving resulted from an alignment of the estimated useful lives of cruise and passenger vessels to 45 years, as well as the sale of three vessels during the year. Net finance costs also improved, declining by EUR 6.8 million to EUR 21.4 million, benefiting from lower outstanding loan balances and declining interest rates.

Significant balance sheet and liquidity developments occurred during the period. The Group sold three vessels, generating proceeds of EUR 78.8 million, which contributed to a reduction in net debt from EUR 537.7 million at the end of 2024 to EUR 432.4 million at the end of 2025. Total interest-bearing liabilities decreased by 19.9% to EUR 445.9 million. Despite the profit decline, the Group maintained its dividend policy, paying EUR 44.6 million in dividends during the year, which impacted the cash position. Cash and cash equivalents decreased to EUR 13.5 million, though the Group maintained a total liquidity buffer of EUR 110.4 million, including unused credit facilities. The equity ratio improved to 56.4% from 53.4% in the previous year, reflecting a strong capital position despite the payout of dividends. The Group’s net debt to EBITDA ratio increased slightly to 3.3 from 3.1, remaining within management’s target range. Overall, the period was defined by operational headwinds and reduced demand, mitigated by effective cost management, asset optimization, and debt reduction.
