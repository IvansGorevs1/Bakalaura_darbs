Konsolidator A/S reported significant financial improvement during the 2025 fiscal year, characterized by strong revenue growth, substantial cost reductions, and a transition toward operational profitability. The company’s revenue increased by 25% to DKK 25.3 million, up from DKK 20.3 million in 2024. This growth was driven by a 13% increase in subscription fees to DKK 20.6 million and a sharp 134% rise in onboarding and consulting fees, which reached DKK 4.7 million. The expansion in non-recurring revenue was primarily attributed to price adjustments for onboarding services implemented in the first quarter of 2025.

Profitability metrics showed marked improvement, although the company remained in a loss position on a consolidated basis. The EBITDA loss narrowed significantly by DKK 7.3 million, settling at DKK 2.0 million compared to a loss of DKK 9.3 million in the previous year. This improvement was supported by a decrease in staff costs from DKK 22.0 million to DKK 21.0 million and a reduction in external expenses by 11% to DKK 6.3 million, largely due to lower marketing and advertising spend. The loss for the year also improved, decreasing to DKK 6.6 million from DKK 14.9 million in 2024, aided by a positive deferred tax adjustment of DKK 1.0 million.

Key SaaS metrics reflected the company’s strategic shift toward efficient, partner-driven growth. Contracted Annual Recurring Revenue (CARR) grew by 15% to DKK 24.4 million. The company acquired 52 new customers, with 38% coming through partner channels, up from 22% in 2024. Operational efficiency improved notably, with the Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Payback Period dropping by 50% to 23 months from 43 months in 2024. Consequently, the Rule of 40 score improved dramatically from -40% to 17%. However, churn remained a challenge, with the ARR churn rate increasing to 12.6% from 10.5% in 2024, although management expects this to decline to between 6% and 9% in 2026 following onboarding process improvements.

The company’s cash flow position strengthened considerably. Cash flow from operating activities improved from a negative DKK 9.2 million to a negative DKK 1.0 million, driven by better working capital management, including earlier invoicing and a shift toward annual subscription contracts. Net cash flow for the year was marginally positive at DKK 11,000. To support operations and product development, Konsolidator secured DKK 4.8 million through two private placements. Despite these gains, consolidated equity remained negative at DKK 3.6 million, primarily due to losses in the Iberian subsidiary, while the parent company maintained a positive equity of DKK 0.7 million.

Strategically, 2025 marked the launch of the Konsolidator Suite, expanding the product offering beyond consolidation to include Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) and data management solutions. The company also strengthened its partner ecosystem, notably with Exsitec AB in the Nordics. Looking ahead to 2026, Konsolidator forecasts revenue of DKK 27–29 million and aims for positive EBITDA between DKK 1 and 2 million, signaling a continued trajectory toward sustainable profitability.
