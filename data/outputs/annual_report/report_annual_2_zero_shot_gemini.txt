During the 2025 financial year, the Infortar Group achieved strong overall financial performance characterized by substantial revenue growth, improved operational profitability, and a strengthened equity base. Group revenue reached EUR 1,837,000 thousand, representing a significant increase compared to EUR 1,371,775 thousand in the previous financial year. Gross profit rose to EUR 239,842 thousand up from EUR 128,628 thousand, and EBITDA increased to EUR 227,919 thousand compared to EUR 145,275 thousand in 2024. The EBITDA margin improved to 12.4% from 10.6%. Operating profit for the financial year reached EUR 109,008 thousand, compared to EUR 77,024 thousand in 2024. Net profit for the financial year was EUR 69,857 thousand, of which EUR 70,550 thousand was attributable to the owners of the parent, reflecting a decrease compared to the net profit of EUR 193,670 thousand reported in 2024. This reduction in net profit is primarily attributable to one-off effects related to the initial consolidation of Tallink Group recognized in the comparative period. Basic earnings per share stood at EUR 3.43, down from EUR 9.36 in 2024. Total assets at the end of the reporting period stood at EUR 2,596,607 thousand, compared to EUR 2,715,234 thousand as of 31 December 2024, while total equity increased to EUR 1,175,404 thousand from EUR 1,166,221 thousand. Interest-bearing liabilities decreased to EUR 1,071,353 thousand from EUR 1,223,287 thousand, and net debt declined to EUR 851,582 thousand compared to EUR 1,055,708 thousand at the end of the previous period. The equity ratio improved to 45% from 43.0%, and net debt to EBITDA decreased to 3.7 from 4.0. 

The significant changes compared with the previous financial year were primarily driven by the full twelve-month consolidation of AS Tallink Grupp following the acquisition of controlling influence in August 2024, whereas the comparative figures for 2024 included Tallink’s results only from August onwards. In addition, the Group expanded its operations through business combinations, notably the acquisition of Estonia Farmid OÜ in July 2025 and OÜ Oisu Biogaas in November 2025, alongside the full-year inclusion of Elenger Polska and Tallinna Raamatutrükikoja. These corporate developments contributed to higher labour expenses, which reached EUR 280,961 thousand compared to EUR 141,137 thousand in 2024, and increased operational scale across the energy and agricultural segments. Loan liabilities decreased as a result of scheduled repayments across several Group entities, including the Tallink Group level, and adjustments to align the financial position with prevailing market conditions. 

The company's results were shaped by developments across its core business segments, namely maritime transport, energy, real estate, and supporting sectors including agriculture. The maritime transport segment, anchored by Tallink Grupp, was influenced by persistent economic uncertainty, geopolitical tensions affecting customer demand from specific regions, and strict regulatory requirements including the EU Emissions Trading System and FuelEU Maritime. Despite a slight 0.9% year-on-year decrease in passenger numbers to 5,531,132, maritime revenues and seasonal fluctuations were supported by vessel chartering activities. In the energy segment, operated by Elenger Grupp, regional gas demand declined amidst price volatility, yet Elenger maintained a strong market position, delivering 18 TWh of LNG cargoes through Finnish and Baltic terminals and expanding its renewable portfolio with new solar and battery storage projects in Latvia. The real estate and construction segments advanced key developments, including the completion of the Rimi logistics centre in Laagri and the commencement of the Depo commercial project in Lasnamäe and the Rail Baltica mainline construction. Furthermore, the expansion into the agricultural sector through Estonia Farmid integrated nearly 10,000 hectares of farmland and large-scale dairy operations, reinforcing the Group’s long-term strategy to build a diversified, crisis-resilient portfolio. 

Overall, Infortar’s performance during the reporting period demonstrated enhanced operational resilience and financial stability.
