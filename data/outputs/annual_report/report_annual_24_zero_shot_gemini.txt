Trophy Games Development A/S delivered a strong financial performance during the 2025 reporting period, characterized by the successful scaling of its core Transport Game Series and a strategic shift in its monetization and marketing mix. Group revenue increased by 40 percent to DKK 126,523 thousand, up from DKK 90,070 thousand in 2024. EBITDA grew by 26 percent to DKK 31,117 thousand, compared to DKK 24,671 thousand in the previous year, while the EBITDA-margin stood at 25 percent. Profit before tax reached DKK 19,013 thousand, representing a 109 percent increase over DKK 9,068 thousand in 2024, resulting in an improved EBT-margin of 15 percent. Net profit for the year rose to DKK 12,539 thousand compared to DKK 7,471 thousand in 2024. Throughout 2025, underlying performance consistently exceeded initial expectations, prompting management to revise its full-year guidance upward four times.

The top-line growth was primarily driven by the expansion of the Transport Series and the Big Three titles, which collectively represented 67 percent of total revenue at DKK 85 million. Airline Manager contributed DKK 37,787 thousand, reflecting a 28 percent increase. Truck Manager emerged as a major growth engine, scaling rapidly with revenue surging to DKK 25,181 thousand following its late 2024 release. Farm Manager, launched in July 2025 following five million pre-registrations, contributed DKK 22,002 thousand in its first six months. In contrast, other transport titles declined by 46 percent to DKK 20,955 thousand as marketing resources were reallocated toward the high-velocity Big Three. Legacy games remained stable, contributing DKK 20,598 thousand in revenue. A significant business development was the optimization of in-app advertising, which surged 100 percent to DKK 18,476 thousand, accounting for 14.6 percent of total revenue and improving direct cost efficiency due to the absence of platform fees. Marketing expenses were expanded by 76 percent to DKK 40,486 thousand to fuel user acquisition.

Financial results were impacted by an extraordinary tax repayment of DKK 4.7 million following a Danish tax authorities' reassessment concluding that game development activities do not qualify as R&D under current tax credit definitions, which included DKK 990 thousand in interest and surcharges recognized as financial expenses. Total assets grew by 24 percent to DKK 84,597 thousand. The company maintained a robust capital structure with a solvency ratio of 78 percent and zero interest-bearing external debt. Cash at bank and in hand ended at DKK 23,049 thousand, affected by the record marketing budget, an increase in other receivables including a DKK 14 million VAT receivable, a share buy-back program totaling DKK 4,894 thousand, and the tax credit repayment. Overall, the reporting period demonstrated strong operational scalability, robust profitability, and successful portfolio expansion that positioned the company favorably for continued growth entering 2026.
