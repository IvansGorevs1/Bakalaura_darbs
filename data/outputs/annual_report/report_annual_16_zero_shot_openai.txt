Nordic Fibreboard AS reported a challenging 2025, with revenue declining 3.4% to €7.328 million from €7.592 million in 2024. Fibreboard production and sales accounted for virtually all revenue at €7.325 million, while real estate management revenue fell to €3 thousand from €25 thousand. Sales within the European Union, representing 92% of total revenue, remained relatively stable, but sales to Africa, Asia and the Middle East declined.

Profitability deteriorated substantially. EBITDA was negative €420 thousand, compared with negative €25 thousand in 2024, reducing the EBITDA margin to negative 6% from 0%. The fibreboard segment moved from positive EBITDA of €26 thousand to negative €296 thousand, primarily because of underutilized production capacity and the December factory shutdown. The shutdown was planned in 2025 to facilitate the transition to a new gas boiler house; personnel, maintenance and repair costs continued during the stoppage and had an approximately €0.2 million negative effect on fibreboard EBITDA. The real estate segment’s EBITDA loss also widened from €48 thousand to €86 thousand.

Gross profit declined to €603 thousand from €771 thousand, while distribution costs increased to €982 thousand from €935 thousand and administrative expenses rose to €502 thousand from €381 thousand. Consequently, the operating loss widened to €940 thousand from €547 thousand, and the operating margin declined to negative 13% from negative 7%. After net financial costs of €214 thousand, the Group recorded a net loss of €1.154 million, compared with €786 thousand in 2024. Basic and diluted loss per share improved to €0.14 from €0.17, reflecting the increase in shares following the October 2025 issue, although book value per share declined to €0.53 from €0.80.

Total assets increased to €9.323 million from €8.252 million, mainly due to investment in production assets and higher inventories. Equity rose to €4.462 million from €3.616 million after a €2 million share issue, partly offset by the annual loss. Borrowings declined to €3.168 million from €3.724 million, but year-end cash fell to €5 thousand from €53 thousand. Operating cash flow was negative €503 thousand, compared with negative €174 thousand in 2024, while investing outflows increased to €989 thousand, including €1.367 million invested in production assets. The Group ended the year with negative working capital of €2.7 million.

Key developments included completion of the merger with Pärnu Riverside Development OÜ, continued development of the Admirali Quarter project, and major investment in a new gas boiler house, completed in January 2026. A preliminary agreement to sell ten Admirali Quarter properties was signed in January 2026.
