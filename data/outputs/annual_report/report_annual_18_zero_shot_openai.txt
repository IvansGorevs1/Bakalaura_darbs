OODA AI reported a major shift toward commercialization and growth in 2025. Group revenue increased to SEK 32.1 million from SEK 1.1 million in 2024, with net sales rising to SEK 31.9 million from SEK 1.1 million. The increase reflected invoicing from several major transactions. Sales consisted entirely of consulting services and were generated mainly outside Sweden, including SEK 26.7 million in Switzerland, SEK 2.4 million elsewhere in the EU and SEK 2.8 million in other countries.

Operating performance improved substantially. Operating loss narrowed to SEK 29.3 million from SEK 51.8 million, while EBITDA improved to a positive SEK 3.0 million from negative SEK 28.2 million. EBITDA included approximately SEK 10.2 million of one-off costs related to liquidating older company structures; excluding these costs, adjusted EBITDA was approximately SEK 13.2 million, corresponding to an adjusted margin of about 32% of revenue. Personnel costs increased to SEK 9.2 million from SEK 7.4 million, while the Group continued to maintain a low cost base and had an average of four employees.

Despite the improved operating result, the Group recorded a net loss of SEK 125.8 million, compared with SEK 83.4 million in 2024. Financial items deteriorated to a loss of SEK 95.9 million from SEK 29.9 million, primarily because of impairments and results related to associated companies and other financial holdings. As part of its strategic refocusing on AI and decentralized infrastructure, the Company wrote down several holdings, in some cases to zero. Financial assets declined to SEK 8.4 million from SEK 96.0 million, and consolidated equity fell to SEK 23.9 million from SEK 119.4 million. Earnings per share were negative SEK 35.29, compared with negative SEK 29.00.

Cash flow remained negative but improved from operations. Operating cash flow was negative SEK 16.7 million, compared with negative SEK 29.7 million, and was positive by SEK 5.1 million during the second half of the year. Investing cash flow was negative SEK 49.4 million, including SEK 8.7 million invested in platform and AI development and SEK 40.7 million in financial fixed assets. Financing activities contributed SEK 44.3 million through share issues and debt repayment. Year-end cash was SEK 3.3 million, down from SEK 25.1 million.

Commercial developments included expanded agreements with Win.Rocks AG totaling approximately SEK 21 million, a new Win.Rocks agreement worth at least SEK 15 million over 24 months, an agreement with Sergel Kredittjänster, and partnerships supporting broader distribution. The Company also advanced its decentralized AI infrastructure from concept to MVP and operational testnet. Following year-end, OODA AI launched a new platform and decided on an approximately SEK 10 million cash issue, while planning broader platform commercialization in the second quarter of 2026.
