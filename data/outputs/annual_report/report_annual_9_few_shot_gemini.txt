During the financial year 2025, Impero A/S achieved a resilient performance despite macroeconomic and geopolitical headwinds, ending the period with annual recurring revenue reaching 46.2 million Danish kroner, which represented a 19 percent year-on-year growth rate. This performance fell in the upper end of the company's revised ARR guidance range of 11 to 19 percent. Recognized revenue for the 12-month period increased by 19 percent to 42.0 million Danish kroner compared with 35.4 million Danish kroner in 2024. Growth was driven by platform expansion within the existing customer base, new product features, and solid performances in core markets, with Denmark and the DACH region growing net new ARR by 22 percent and 26 percent, respectively. 

The company demonstrated solid SaaS metrics throughout the year, highlighted by an exceptionally low churn rate of 1 percent and a net revenue retention rate of 106 percent. Average ARR per account reached a record high level of 228 thousand Danish kroner, representing an 8 percent increase over the previous year, supported by a gross uplift of 12 percent. Impero also surpassed the milestone of 200 customers during the period and improved customer concentration among its top 10 customers, which decreased to 20 percent of total ARR by year-end. 

Profitability indicators showed significant improvements compared with the previous financial year. EBITDA ended at negative 3.1 million Danish kroner, representing a substantial improvement from negative 8.7 million Danish kroner in 2024 and landing in the better end of the revised guidance range of negative 5 million to negative 3 million Danish kroner. Operating loss narrowed to 6.7 million Danish kroner from 11.7 million Danish kroner, while the net loss for the period was 6.8 million Danish kroner compared with 11.4 million Danish kroner in the prior year. The improved financial result was primarily driven by the 19 percent revenue growth outpacing a more moderate 6 percent increase in both staff costs and other external expenses. Own work capitalized increased to 5.2 million Danish kroner from 3.6 million Danish kroner due to a heightened focus on developing new platform features such as the control testing module and entity management. 

Cash flow from ordinary operating activities improved to a negative 1.3 million Danish kroner from a negative 4.3 million Danish kroner in 2024. Total cash and cash equivalents increased significantly to 18.9 million Danish kroner as of 31 December 2025, up from 9.8 million Danish kroner at the end of 2024. This was primarily bolstered by a directed share issue that raised 15 million Danish kroner in December 2025, alongside proceeds from warrant exercises. Total equity stood at 15.4 million Danish kroner at year-end. 

Following the capital injection and continued solid SaaS metrics, the Board of Directors concluded that growth investments would create greater long-term shareholder value than pursuing the prior goal of achieving recurring positive cash flow before the end of 2026. Consequently, the company deferred this cash flow ambition and announced plans to accelerate commercial investments in 2026. For the full year 2026, Impero expects ARR to reach between 53 million and 57 million Danish kroner, reflecting an anticipated growth rate of approximately 15 percent to 23 percent, and projects EBITDA to be in the range of negative 2 million to positive 1 million Danish kroner.
