Impero A/S delivered improved financial performance in 2025 despite geopolitical and macroeconomic uncertainty, particularly during the first half of the year. Revenue increased 19% to DKK 42.0M from DKK 35.4M in 2024, supported by continued commercial focus, platform investments, and growth in the company’s main markets. Cost of sales decreased 16%, raising the contribution margin to 98% from 97%. Other external expenses and staff costs each increased 6%, reflecting continued investment in commercial activities, new hires, salary adjustments, and organizational changes.

Profitability improved substantially. EBITDA was negative DKK 3.1M, compared with negative DKK 8.7M in 2024, while operating loss narrowed to DKK 6.7M from DKK 11.7M. The improvement reflected revenue growth outpacing increases in operating expenses. Own work capitalized increased to DKK 5.2M from DKK 3.6M, primarily due to greater development activity. Net loss for the year was DKK 6.8M, compared with DKK 11.4M in 2024.

Recurring revenue remained the key growth indicator. ARR reached DKK 46.2M at year-end, up 19% from DKK 38.7M in 2024 and at the upper end of the revised guidance range. Net new ARR was DKK 7.5M for the full year, below DKK 8.3M in 2024, but second-half momentum strengthened considerably, with fourth-quarter net new ARR rising to DKK 3.1M from DKK 1.8M in the corresponding quarter. Customer retention remained strong: churn was only 1%, compared with 2% in 2024, while net revenue retention was 106%, down slightly from 108%. Average ARR per account increased 8% to DKK 228T, supported by a 12% gross uplift from existing customers.

Growth was led by Denmark and the DACH region, where ARR increased 22% and 26%, respectively. Each region represented approximately 44% of total ARR at year-end. The company surpassed 200 customers after adding 26 during the year, while top-10 customer concentration declined to 20% from 24%, strengthening revenue diversification. Product expansion, including the Control Testing module and initial AI functionality, created additional upselling opportunities and supported platform stickiness. Partner activity was affected by delayed customer decisions, although Impero expanded its German partner network to five revenue-generating partners.

Cash flow also improved. Cash flow from ordinary operating activities was negative DKK 1.3M, compared with negative DKK 4.3M in 2024, although DKK 5.6M was invested in intangible assets. A DKK 15M directed issue in December helped increase year-end cash to DKK 18.9M and equity to DKK 15.4M. With this stronger capital position, Impero deferred its ambition of recurring positive cash flow before the end of 2026 to prioritize commercial investment. For 2026, the company expects ARR of DKK 53M–57M and EBITDA between negative DKK 2M and positive DKK 1M.
