InCoax Networks’ 2025 performance was weaker than the previous year, although the company made substantial progress in product development and financing. Net sales fell 35% to SEK 48.1 million from SEK 74.5 million, primarily because customer orders and projects were delayed and market conditions were more cautious. Product sales represented 85% of revenue, compared with 93% in 2024, while service sales increased to SEK 7.0 million from SEK 5.3 million. North America remained the main market, generating SEK 39.6 million of sales versus SEK 69.6 million in 2024.

Gross profit declined to SEK 29.9 million from SEK 42.2 million, but the gross margin improved to 62% from 57%, reflecting the lower proportion of goods-related costs. The operating loss widened to SEK 32.4 million from SEK 19.4 million, as lower sales were combined with continued investment in product development, strategic collaborations, and commercial expansion. Operating expenses decreased to SEK 105.9 million from SEK 121.2 million, mainly due to lower resale-goods and external costs, partly offset by higher personnel expenses. The net loss increased to SEK 32.6 million from SEK 19.2 million, and earnings per share declined to SEK –0.25 from SEK –0.18.

Operating cash flow remained negative at SEK –20.0 million but improved from SEK –27.1 million in 2024. Investing cash flow was SEK –25.3 million, largely due to capitalized development costs. Financing activities generated SEK 51.8 million, including a directed share issue of approximately SEK 33.5 million and SEK 20 million in borrowing. Total cash flow consequently turned positive at SEK 6.5 million, compared with SEK –12.8 million in 2024, and cash at year-end increased to SEK 8.7 million from SEK 2.2 million. The equity ratio decreased to 71.8% from 80.8%, while shares outstanding increased to 131.3 million from 108.2 million.

The year marked a transition from intensive development and integration to commercial execution. The D2501 product was fully integrated into Nokia’s Gigabit Connect platform, and initial Nokia-linked installations were completed in North America. InCoax also launched the D2508 product through its own channels, with planned Nokia integration and distribution in 2026. The company reported customer wins and continued strategic partnerships, but sales ramp-up was slower than expected.

Management initiated structural cost reductions expected to lower ongoing costs by more than 40%, or over SEK 25 million annually from 2026. The objective is to achieve positive or neutral cash flow within the coming twelve months. However, the auditor highlighted a material uncertainty regarding going concern because short-term financing depends partly on sufficient exercise of outstanding warrants or alternative financing.
