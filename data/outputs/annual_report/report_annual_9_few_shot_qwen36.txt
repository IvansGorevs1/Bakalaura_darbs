Impero A/S reported a resilient financial performance for the fiscal year 2025, characterized by strong revenue growth and significant improvement in profitability metrics, despite initial macroeconomic headwinds. Total recognized revenue increased by 19% to DKK 42.0 million, up from DKK 35.4 million in 2024. This growth was primarily driven by commercial momentum in the second half of the year and continued expansion within the company’s core markets of Denmark and the DACH region. The cost of sales decreased by 16% to DKK 1.0 million, contributing to an improved gross profit of DKK 41.0 million.

Profitability saw substantial enhancement, with EBITDA improving to a loss of DKK 3.1 million, a significant positive swing of DKK 5.6 million compared to the DKK 8.7 million loss recorded in 2024. Operating profit also improved, narrowing the deficit to DKK 6.8 million from DKK 11.4 million in the prior year. This improvement was supported by a 6% increase in staff costs and a 6% rise in other external expenses, which were more than offset by the 19% revenue growth. The company capitalized DKK 5.2 million in development costs, reflecting ongoing investment in product innovation, including the launch of the Control Testing module and AI-integrated features.

The company’s recurring revenue base strengthened considerably, with Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) reaching DKK 46.2 million at the end of 2025, representing a 19% year-on-year growth. This performance landed at the upper end of the revised guidance range. SaaS metrics remained robust, highlighted by an exceptionally low churn rate of 1% and a Net Revenue Retention Rate (NRR) of 106%. The average revenue per account (ARPA) for new customers reached DKK 204,000, demonstrating the effectiveness of the land-and-expand strategy. The customer base expanded to over 200 clients, with notable diversification across industries and a reduction in top-10 customer concentration from 37% in 2020 to 20% in 2025.

Strategically, Impero shifted its focus toward accelerating growth investments rather than pursuing immediate recurring cash flow positivity. Consequently, the Board deferred the ambition of achieving positive recurring cash flow before the end of 2026. To support this strategy and strengthen the balance sheet, the company raised DKK 15 million through a directed equity issue in December 2025. This capital injection, combined with existing cash reserves, resulted in a cash position of DKK 18.9 million and equity of DKK 15.4 million at year-end, with no interest-bearing debt. Looking ahead to 2026, the company guides for ARR between DKK 53 million and DKK 57 million, reflecting expected growth of 15% to 23%, while anticipating an EBITDA range between a DKK 2 million loss and a DKK 1 million profit.
