Talkpool reported its financial results for the year ended December 31, 2025, marking the end of its restructuring phase and the transition into organic growth. For the full year 2025, net sales rose by 12.5 percent to EUR 17 756 thousand compared to EUR 15 782 thousand in 2024, representing the first year of double-digit growth since the consolidation phase began. Profitability indicators remained relatively stable despite strategic investments in future growth, with EBITDA reaching EUR 1 828 thousand compared to EUR 1 702 thousand in the previous year, yielding an EBITDA margin of 10.3 percent down slightly from 10.8 percent. EBIT for the period was EUR 1 620 thousand compared to EUR 1 527 thousand, with an EBIT margin of 9.1 percent compared to 9.7 percent in 2024. Net earnings after tax increased significantly by 42 percent to EUR 862 thousand compared to EUR 605 thousand, resulting in an EAT margin of 4.8 percent up from 3.8 percent. The financial position also strengthened, with equity improving to EUR 2.1 million and net cash reaching a positive position of EUR 0.3 million by the end of 2025, reflecting a EUR 6.0 million improvement since 2021. The operational and financial developments were driven by several key factors and business events described in the report. The Americas remained Talkpool's largest market, delivering growth despite US dollar weakness and challenges in Haiti, alongside re-entering the US market through a service contract with MasTec and supporting Digicel in Jamaica following Hurricane Melissa. In Europe, the German fiber expansion under Deutsche Telekom provided a steady operational contribution, supported by approximately EUR 30 billion in committed operator investments through 2030, though a shift in rollout priorities caused a temporary flattening of planning assignment volumes. Independent valuation by BDO assessed the German subsidiary at approximately EUR 20.5 million. Furthermore, Talkpool Pakistan achieved record revenues in local currency, benefiting from renegotiated commercial terms that improved gross profitability, and successfully developed a Vision AI-based site security and compliance monitoring solution. The company also launched a new Swedish affiliated company, Talkpool AB, in June 2025 to deliver advanced communication solutions for the defence sector. Strategic initiatives included the launch of the Vision 2030 growth plan in April, targeting an average annual revenue growth rate of 16.8 percent and a 14.0 percent EBITDA margin by 2030, a target that organic growth already reached by year-end.
