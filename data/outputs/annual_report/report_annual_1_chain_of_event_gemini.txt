For the 2025 financial year, AAC Clyde Space reported net sales of SEK 295 M, representing a 16 percent decline compared to SEK 352 M in 2024. Total revenue for the group amounted to SEK 338 M compared with SEK 409 M in the previous year. The company achieved a positive EBITDA of SEK 17 M, which represents a decline compared to EBITDA of SEK 47 M reported in 2024, marking the third consecutive year of positive EBITDA. The operating result before depreciation and impairment declined to SEK -40 M from SEK -4 M in 2024, while the loss after tax widened to SEK -47 M compared to a loss of SEK -6 M in the previous year. Earnings per share based on profit for the period attributable to parent company shareholders were SEK -7.63, compared with SEK -1.00 in 2024. Operating cash flow declined to SEK -73 M from SEK 57 M in the preceding year. Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the period stood at SEK 30 M, down from SEK 50 M at the end of 2024, while the equity ratio improved to 80 percent from 71 percent. The order backlog at the end of the period was SEK 352 M, compared with SEK 641 M reported at the end of 2024. 

The financial performance was influenced by specific business developments and project dynamics described in the report. The business was structured into two segments: Products and Missions, and Data and Services. Net sales in Data and Services increased to SEK 80 M from SEK 45 M, with EBITDA rising to SEK 28 M from SEK 21 M, supported by operational milestones such as the Sedna-1 and Sedna-2 satellites becoming fully operational. Conversely, net sales in Products and Missions decreased to SEK 245 M from SEK 346 M, and EBITDA declined to SEK 17 M from SEK 52 M. The report explicitly connects financial and operational variances to specific programme events and delays. Two programme-related delays materially affected revenue, cash flow, and order intake during the year. In one customer project, technical discussions between the end customer and a subcontractor delayed milestone recognition, shifting approximately SEK 32 M of revenue into 2026. In the EPS-Sterna weather constellation programme, political and procurement developments postponed project initiation, which contributed to an updated full-year guidance during the year. Additionally, commissioning of the Sedna satellites took longer than planned, and the launch of the first two VIREON earth observation satellites was delayed due to rescheduling by the launch provider. 

To support strategic investments in satellite constellations and data services, the company strengthened its capital base through share issues and credit facilities. A directed share issue in June 2025 raised approximately SEK 64.5 M before transaction costs, and in December 2025, the company welcomed Bonnier Capital as a strategic investor through a directed share issue that raised approximately SEK 50.3 M before transaction costs, as part of an investment of up to SEK 140 M. Furthermore, the company expanded its overdraft bank facility from SEK 30 M to SEK 50 M. Total available cash, including the bank overdraft facility, amounted to SEK 80.1 M at year-end. Subsequent events after the reporting period included an extraordinary general meeting in January 2026 approving the second tranche of the directed share issue to Bonnier Capital corresponding to approximately SEK 49.6 M, and the receipt of an order valued at EUR 76.3 M, approximately SEK 820 M, from OHB Sweden for the delivery of 20 microwave radiometers for the EPS-Sterna satellite programme, which contributed to an increase in the order backlog to approximately SEK 1.1 billion by the end of March 2026.
