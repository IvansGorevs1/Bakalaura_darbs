Heimstaden AB reported a robust financial performance for the fiscal year 2025, characterized by strong operational fundamentals, disciplined cost management, and significant value creation through its privatisation programme. The company’s total rental income remained stable at SEK 15,916 million, compared to SEK 15,919 million in the prior year. This stability was driven by positive like-for-like rental growth of 4.9%, which was primarily supported by indexation effects contributing 3.3%, alongside gains from tenant improvements and occupancy improvements. These revenue gains were partially offset by negative impacts from foreign exchange fluctuations and the divestment of properties through the privatisation and portfolio sales programmes.

Profitability metrics showed marked improvement. Net operating income increased by 2.6% to SEK 11,475 million, resulting in an expanded net operating income margin of 72.1% compared to 70.3% in the previous year. This margin expansion was largely attributable to a 6.1% decrease in property expenses to SEK 4,378 million, driven by cost control measures, digitalisation efficiencies, and a shrinking portfolio size due to ongoing sales. Corporate administrative expenses also declined significantly to SEK 373 million from SEK 621 million, further boosting operating results.

The company’s balance sheet and credit metrics strengthened considerably during the period. Net loan-to-value decreased from 56.4% to 52.2%, and the interest coverage ratio improved to 2.0 times from 1.9 times. This deleveraging was supported by the deployment of cash proceeds from the privatisation programme, which generated total sales of SEK 10,664 million for 2,466 residential units at a premium of 28.9% to book value. Since the programme's inception, Heimstaden has sold 4,443 units for a total value of SEK 19,451 million. The fair value of investment properties stood at SEK 324,429 million, reflecting a 2.9% year-on-year increase driven by net operating income growth, value-accretive capital expenditures, and strong performance in the home-owner market in select countries.

Strategic developments included a successful liability management exercise, with the subsidiary Heimstaden Bostad raising approximately SEK 59,500 million through refinancings and new issuances. In January 2025, Fitch Ratings affirmed Heimstaden’s ‘B-‘ rating but revised the outlook to Negative. Additionally, the company received a favorable arbitration award in December 2025 in a dispute with Alecta, confirming no breach of the Shareholders Agreement. A goodwill impairment loss of SEK 3,707 million was recorded, primarily linked to the Akelius acquisition. Overall, the period was defined by efficient capital allocation, resilient occupancy rates of 98.5%, and continued progress toward sustainability targets, including a 14.2% reduction in greenhouse gas emissions against the 2020 baseline.
