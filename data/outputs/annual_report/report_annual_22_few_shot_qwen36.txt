Tallinna Vesi reported robust financial performance for 2025, characterized by significant revenue growth and improved profitability. Total sales increased by 11.4% to €72.48 million, up from €65.08 million in the previous year. This growth was driven primarily by an 18.3% rise in sales to private customers in the main service area, reaching €31.84 million, which was supported by increased consumption and tariff adjustments implemented throughout 2024 and 2025. Conversely, sales to business customers declined by 2.7% to €16.76 million, reflecting a statutory reduction in water tariff rates for this segment aimed at harmonizing prices with private customers. Additionally, construction services revenue surged by 62.0% to €8.46 million, contributing to the overall top-line expansion.

Profitability metrics strengthened considerably during the period. Gross profit rose by 10.5% to €29.52 million, while operating profit increased by 11.8% to €21.22 million. The improvement in operating margins was largely attributable to cost optimizations, particularly the reduction in electricity expenses following the full operational capacity of the combined heat and power plant at the Paljassaare Wastewater Treatment Plant. This facility now generates all the heat and more than half of the electricity required for treatment processes, significantly lowering purchased energy costs. Net profit grew by 7.2% to €14.24 million, resulting in earnings per share of €0.71. Profit before taxes increased by 18.0% to €17.43 million, although net profit growth was moderated by a higher income tax rate on dividend payments.

The company maintained a strong balance sheet while pursuing aggressive investment strategies. Investments in fixed assets reached €55.74 million, exceeding the previous year’s €49.53 million, focusing on infrastructure continuity, environmental protection, and smart meter installations. Consequently, non-current tangible assets grew to €338.65 million. The company’s debt levels increased, with outstanding loans rising to €160 million from €118 million at the end of 2024, leading to a debt-to-total-assets ratio of 66.02%. Despite the increased leverage, the company maintained a healthy liquidity position with cash balances growing to €11.29 million.

Operationally, the company achieved key sustainability and service quality targets. The water loss rate in the distribution network improved to 13.01%, and the number of sewer blockages decreased significantly. Customer satisfaction metrics showed positive trends, with the number of complaints falling to 531 from 832 in the prior year. The company also continued its commitment to shareholder returns, having paid a dividend of €0.53 per share in 2025, representing 80% of the 2024 net profit. Overall, the reporting period demonstrated Tallinna Vesi’s ability to enhance financial results through operational efficiency and strategic investments while maintaining high service standards and regulatory compliance.
