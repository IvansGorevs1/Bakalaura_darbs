During the financial year ended December 31, 2025, Coop Pank achieved solid business volume growth supported by an improving post-recession economic environment in Estonia, despite facing a lower-interest rate environment that impacted profitability. Net profit for 2025 reached 28.7 million euros, representing an 11 percent decrease compared to 32.2 million euros in 2024. Total net operating income also experienced a slight decline, decreasing by 3 percent from 81.9 million euros in 2024 to 79.4 million euros in 2025, primarily driven by a reduction in net interest income from 77.6 million euros down to 73.3 million euros. Net fee and commission income showed a positive development, rising by 4 percent from 4.3 million euros to 4.5 million euros. Operating expenses increased by 2 percent to 41.5 million euros compared to 40.6 million euros in the previous period, while credit loss allowances improved, decreasing from 4.6 million euros in 2024 to 3.3 million euros in 2025. Income tax expense increased to 5.8 million euros from 4.5 million euros due to a rise in the advance income tax rate for credit institutions from 14 percent to 18 percent. Basic earnings per share were 0.28 euros in 2025 compared to 0.31 euros in 2024, and diluted earnings per share were 0.27 euros compared to 0.31 euros. Return on equity stood at 12.9 percent, and return on assets was 1.2 percent. The cost-to-income ratio was reported at 52.3 percent.

The bank experienced substantial growth in key business volumes during 2025. The net loan portfolio grew by 19 percent, exceeding 2 billion euros for the first time in the bank's history to reach 2.11 billion euros at the end of 2025 compared to 1.77 billion euros at the end of 2024. Growth was driven across all financing lines, led by the business loan portfolio which surged by 27 percent to 968.6 million euros, and the home loan portfolio which increased by 17 percent. Customer deposits expanded by 9 percent to reach 2.05 billion euros, consisting of an 11 percent increase in demand deposits and an 8 percent increase in term deposits. Total assets grew by 24 percent to 2.70 billion euros. Shareholders equity increased by 11 percent to 234.5 million euros. Capital adequacy stood at 18.54 percent, with a Common Equity Tier 1 ratio of 14.15 percent, maintaining full compliance with all regulatory capital requirements. The total customer base expanded by 19,000 customers, or 9 percent, reaching 227,000 clients by year-end.

The company's performance was significantly influenced by macroeconomic factors, most notably falling euro area interest rates as the 6-month Euribor declined to 2.1 percent by the end of 2025, down from previous highs. While lower interest rates reduced net interest margins and net interest income, they helped improve confidence among businesses and households, stimulating strong loan demand across the Estonian market. Strategic business developments included the successful issuance in March 2025 of 250 million euros in covered bonds listed on the Irish Stock Exchange, providing a stable long-term funding source that allowed the bank to intentionally reduce more expensive term deposits. Additionally, Coop Pank entered into a synthetic securitization transaction with the European Investment Bank Group to transfer credit risk and obtain capital relief for issuing up to 249 million euros in new sustainable loans, and secured a 5 million euro subordinated loan from the European Energy Efficiency Fund. Fulfilling its dividend policy, Coop Pank paid out 7.2 million euros in dividends in May 2025, corresponding to 0.07 euros per share from 2024 earnings. For the 2025 financial year, the Management Board has proposed a dividend distribution of 0.065 euros per share, totaling 6.7 million euros.
