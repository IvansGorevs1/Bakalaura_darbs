Impero A/S reported a resilient financial performance for the fiscal year 2025, characterized by strong revenue growth and significant improvements in profitability metrics despite macroeconomic headwinds. The company achieved Annual Recurring Revenue of DKK 46.2 million, representing a 19% year-on-year increase from DKK 38.7 million in 2024. This growth placed Impero at the upper end of its revised guidance range and was driven by a robust second half of the year, where net new ARR reached DKK 3.1 million in Q4, compared to DKK 1.8 million in the same period of 2024. Total recognized revenue for the year increased by 19% to DKK 42.0 million, while cost of sales decreased by 16% to DKK 1.0 million, resulting in a gross profit margin of 98%.

The company’s operational efficiency improved markedly, with EBITDA rising to a loss of DKK 3.1 million, a substantial improvement from the DKK 8.7 million loss recorded in 2024. This positive shift was supported by a DKK 5.2 million capitalization of development costs, up from DKK 3.6 million in the previous year, and balanced cost management in staff and external expenses. Operating profit improved by DKK 4.6 million to a loss of DKK 6.8 million. The net loss for the period stood at DKK 6.8 million, compared to DKK 11.4 million in 2024.

Key SaaS metrics demonstrated high customer retention and satisfaction. The churn rate remained exceptionally low at 1%, and the Net Revenue Retention rate was 106%. The Average ARR per Account reached a record high of DKK 228 thousand, reflecting a successful land-and-expand strategy. The customer base expanded to over 200 clients, with significant contributions from the Danish and DACH regions, which grew net new ARR by 22% and 26% respectively. Customer concentration risk decreased, with the top ten customers accounting for 20% of ARR, down from 37% in 2020.

Strategically, Impero deferred its ambition to achieve recurring positive cash flow before the end of 2026. The Board determined that accelerating commercial investments would generate greater long-term value. This decision was enabled by a strong capital position following a DKK 15 million directed issue in December 2025. Cash and cash equivalents increased to DKK 18.9 million, with no interest-bearing debt. Looking ahead, Impero guides for 2026 ARR between DKK 53 million and 57 million, with EBITDA expected to range from a DKK 2 million loss to a DKK 1 million profit. The company continues to invest in AI-driven capabilities and product innovations, such as the Control Testing module, to strengthen its competitive position in the Governance, Risk, and Compliance market.
