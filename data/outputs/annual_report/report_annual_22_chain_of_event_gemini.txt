For the financial year ended December 31, 2025, AS Tallinna Vesi and its subsidiaries reported robust overall financial performance, characterized by significant growth in sales, operating profit, and net profit compared to the previous year. Group sales reached 72.48 million euros, representing an increase of 11.4 percent, or 7.40 million euros, compared to 65.08 million euros in 2024. Water services revenue grew by 6.9 percent to 62.31 million euros, driven by increased private customer consumption and tariff adjustments implemented across mid-2024 and 2025. Construction services revenue experienced substantial growth of 62.0 percent, rising to 8.46 million euros from 5.22 million euros, supported by higher turnover from pipe construction and external road construction services.

Profitability indicators improved across the board. Gross profit for 2025 was 29.52 million euros, up 10.5 percent from 26.71 million euros in 2024. Operating profit before depreciation and amortisation (EBITDA) increased by 12.5 percent to 30.95 million euros compared to 27.51 million euros in the prior year, yielding an EBITDA margin of 42.70 percent. Operating profit rose by 11.8 percent to 21.22 million euros from 18.98 million euros. Profit before taxes grew by 18.0 percent to 17.43 million euros, up from 14.78 million euros, supported by lower Euribor rates reducing net financial expenses to 3.78 million euros. Net profit for the period reached 14.24 million euros, representing a 7.2 percent increase compared to 13.28 million euros in 2024, despite being impacted by an increase in the income tax rate on dividend payments. Earnings per share rose to 0.71 euros from 0.66 euros.

The financial performance was significantly influenced by operational efficiency measures, notably the full-capacity operation of the combined heat and power plant at the Paljassaare Wastewater Treatment Plant. This facility successfully upcycled sewage sludge into biogas, generating 9,987 MWh of electricity and 6,933 MWh of heat during 2025. This self-generated renewable energy covered all heat demands and more than half of the Paljassaare plant's electricity requirements, directly contributing to a 6.9 percent decrease in electricity purchase costs to 4.37 million euros. These savings helped offset cost pressures from a 5.8 percent increase in chemical expenses due to higher methanol prices and a 21.4 percent rise in pollution tax expenses resulting from higher tax rates and usage volumes.

Total cost of goods and services sold increased by 12.0 percent to 42.96 million euros, driven by higher construction service costs and a 6.8 percent increase in staff costs to 11.45 million euros. The group continued heavy investments in infrastructure, investing 55.74 million euros in fixed assets during 2025 compared to 49.53 million euros in 2024. These investments targeted environmental protection, sustainable infrastructure continuity, stormwater systems, smart water meter rollouts, and treatment plant upgrades. To support these capital expenditures and ongoing operations, outstanding loans increased to 160 million euros as of December 31, 2025, up from 118 million euros, including a new 25 million euro facility and drawdowns under a syndicated loan agreement. Consequently, the total debt-to-assets ratio adjusted to 66.02 percent from 61.71 percent, while cash and cash equivalents increased to 11.29 million euros from 3.59 million euros. The provision for possible third-party claims relating to historical tariff disputes remained unchanged at 6.02 million euros.
