Tallinna Sadam Group reported a strong financial performance for the year ended 31 December 2025, characterized by increased profitability and operational efficiency despite a slight decline in total revenue. The Group’s consolidated revenue decreased by 0.8% to EUR 118.7 million, primarily due to a significant drop in charter fees from the multifunctional icebreaker Botnica, which fell by 34% to EUR 7.9 million as the vessel’s utilization rate dropped to 49%. However, this negative impact was offset by growth in other segments. Revenue from vessel dues increased by 8% to EUR 34.1 million, driven by calls from larger tankers and cruise ships. Lease income grew by 3% to EUR 14.6 million, and passenger fees rose by 3% to EUR 12.2 million, supported by a 1% increase in passenger numbers to 8.3 million. Cargo volumes also recovered, growing by 5.1% to 13.8 million tonnes, with liquid bulk seeing a substantial 47% increase.

Operating profit improved significantly, rising by 12.2% to EUR 32.7 million, while adjusted EBITDA grew by 6.4% to EUR 56.5 million. The adjusted EBITDA margin expanded from 44.4% to 47.6%, reflecting better cost control and operational leverage. Operating expenses decreased by 10% to EUR 36.4 million, largely due to lower technical maintenance and repair costs, particularly in the Other segment where the icebreaker Botnica required fewer repairs in 2025 compared to the previous year. Personnel expenses increased by 3% to EUR 26.6 million, and depreciation charges rose slightly by 1%.

Net profit for the period increased by 17.3% to EUR 22.5 million, up from EUR 19.2 million in 2024. This growth was driven by higher operating profits and a 30% reduction in net finance costs to EUR 4.8 million, resulting from lower interest rates and reduced loan balances. However, the growth in net profit was partially moderated by an increase in income tax expense, which rose by 73.3% to EUR 5.4 million due to a higher dividend tax rate. The Group paid dividends of EUR 19.2 million, equivalent to EUR 0.073 per share, representing 100% of the previous year’s profit.

Capital investments totaled EUR 33.2 million, a 14.4% decrease from the prior year, with major expenditures focused on infrastructure in Muuga Harbour and the completion of the new multifunctional quay at Paldiski South Harbour. The Group’s financial position remained robust, with net debt decreasing by 15.5% to EUR 141.7 million and equity increasing by 0.9% to EUR 380.9 million. The equity to assets ratio stood at 61%. The stock total return for shareholders was 29%, reflecting positive market sentiment. The company continues to focus on strategic developments, including the Rail Baltica cargo station connection and the implementation of shore power solutions to support sustainable growth.
