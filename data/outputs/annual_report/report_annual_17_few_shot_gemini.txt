The group reported a decline in financial performance during the year ended December 31, 2024, amid an intensely competitive market environment and oversupply of travel services in the Baltic States. Revenue from contracts with customers decreased by 3.6% to EUR 200.9 million compared with EUR 208.3 million in the previous year. The total number of travelers served across Lithuania, Latvia, and Estonia declined by 7.6% to 239.5 thousand passengers compared with 259.3 thousand passengers in 2023. 

Gross profit fell by 20.9% to EUR 20.7 million, down from EUR 26.2 million in 2023, while gross profit margin narrowed by 2.3 percentage points to 10.3%. Operating expenses reached EUR 27.5 million, representing a 25% increase compared with the prior year. This figure includes a one-off, non-cash goodwill impairment loss of EUR 5.7 million related to the Estonian cash-generating unit. Excluding this impairment charge, operating expenses amounted to EUR 21.8 million, marking a 1% decrease from 2023. 

The group recorded a negative EBITDA of EUR 6.1 million, compared to a positive EBITDA of EUR 5.2 million in the previous period. Adjusted EBITDA, which excludes the goodwill impairment, stood at EUR 449 thousand negative, compared with positive EUR 5.2 million in 2023. Operating loss totaled EUR 6.7 million, compared with an operating profit of EUR 4.9 million in the prior year. Net loss for 2024 widened significantly to EUR 7.6 million from a net profit of EUR 3.4 million in 2023, resulting in basic and diluted earnings per share of EUR 0.97 negative, compared with EUR 0.43 positive in the preceding year. 

Despite weaker performance during the first half of the year, operating results showed a material improvement starting in September 2024, driven by travel program optimization, reduced dependence on last-minute sales, and a higher seat capacity utilization load factor of 98.9% in the fourth quarter compared to 96.7% in the final quarter of 2023. 

Total assets stood at EUR 39.5 million at the end of 2024, down from EUR 47.6 million at the end of 2023. Total equity decreased to EUR 8.0 million from EUR 15.7 million, affected by the net loss incurred during the period. Borrowings increased to EUR 9.3 million, up from EUR 8.7 million in the prior year. The group experienced non-compliance with certain financial and non-financial loan covenants as of December 31, 2024, but subsequently secured waivers from lenders and obtained additional financing, including a credit line and loans from a new strategic investor, Neset Kockar, who acquired a 23.2% stake in the company during the first stage of a planned transaction in early 2025. Management concluded that the going concern assumption remains appropriate based on improving operational efficiency, expected profitability recovery, ongoing restructuring initiatives, and available financing support.
