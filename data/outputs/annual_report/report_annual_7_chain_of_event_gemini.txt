For the financial year 2025, Heimstaden reported rental income of SEK 15,916 million, remaining virtually flat compared to SEK 15,919 million in the previous year. This stable topline performance was driven by indexation and tenant improvement investments, which were offset by negative foreign exchange effects, privatisation sales, and portfolio divestments. Like-for-like rental income growth for the year was 4.9%, compared to 5.6% in 2024, supported by indexation contributions of 3.3%, tenant improvements of 0.7%, occupancy improvements of 0.7%, and rent reversion of 0.2%. Real economic occupancy for residential units remained flat at 98.5%.

Net operating income (NOI) improved to SEK 11,475 million from SEK 11,188 million in 2024, resulting in a last-twelve-months NOI margin of 72.1%, up from 70.3%. Property expenses decreased by 6.1% to SEK 4,378 million, down from SEK 4,661 million, driven by management cost control measures, digitalisation, and a shrinking portfolio size resulting from privatisation and portfolio sales programmes. 

The fair value of investment properties was SEK 324,429 million at year-end, compared to SEK 335,422 million in 2024. Fair value adjustments on investment properties generated a gain of SEK 9,498 million, or 3.0%, compared to SEK 8,553 million or 2.6% in the previous year. Privatisation sales achieved SEK 10,664 million, with 2,466 residential units sold at a 28.9% premium to book value. Realised gains from the divestment of properties rose to SEK 2,207 million compared to SEK 1,687 million in 2024. 

Operating profit reached SEK 22,484 million, compared to SEK 19,608 million in the prior year. The company recorded a goodwill impairment loss of SEK 3,707 million related to Akelius cash-generating units due to an increase in weighted average cost of capital discount rates. Net financial items included interest income of SEK 136 million, interest expenses of SEK 6,536 million, foreign exchange gains of SEK 3,996 million, and a fair value adjustment on derivative financial instruments of SEK 70 million. Profit before tax increased to SEK 16,012 million from SEK 10,403 million in 2024. After income tax expenses of SEK 2,955 million, profit for the period reached SEK 13,056 million, compared to SEK 7,621 million in the previous year. 

Total equity increased to SEK 150,557 million at the end of 2025 from SEK 147,803 million at the end of 2024, supported by total comprehensive income of SEK 4,199 million. Net loan-to-value improved to 52.2% from 56.4% in 2024, and the net debt to total assets ratio improved to 48.0% from 51.0%. The interest coverage ratio was 2.0x compared to 1.9x in the prior year. Total interest-bearing liabilities stood at SEK 175,227 million, down from SEK 195,816 million in 2024. Subsidiary Heimstaden Bostad completed approximately SEK 59,500 million in refinancings and new financings during the year across unsecured capital markets and bank financing.
