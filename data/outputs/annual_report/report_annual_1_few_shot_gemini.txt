AAC Clyde Space experienced a mixed financial performance during the 2025 financial year, marked by revenue contraction and a wider net loss, alongside continued operational restructuring and strategic investments. Net sales for the Group declined by 16% to SEK 295.3 million compared with SEK 352.9 million in the previous year. Total revenue, including work performed by the company for its own use and capitalised, as well as other operating income, amounted to SEK 337.8 million down from SEK 408.8 million. The top-line decline was partly influenced by programme-related delays, including technical discussions between an end customer and a subcontractor that delayed milestone recognition and shifted approximately SEK 32 million into 2026, as well as political and procurement postponements in the EPS-Sterna programme.

Profitability weakened during the period. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortisation decreased to SEK 17.5 million from SEK 46.7 million in 2024. The operating result after depreciation, amortisation, and impairment of intangible non-current assets deteriorated to a loss of SEK 40.0 million compared with a loss of SEK 4.0 million in the previous year. Net financial items resulted in an expense of SEK 10.7 million, widening from an expense of SEK 1.3 million, primarily impacted by remeasurement of additional purchase considerations and foreign exchange differences. Consequently, the loss after tax for the period expanded to SEK 46.6 million from SEK 5.7 million. Earnings per share based on profit attributable to parent company shareholders stood at SEK -7.63 compared with SEK -1.00 previously.

Segment performance varied across the newly structured business units. The Data & Services segment generated net sales of SEK 80.1 million, up from SEK 44.9 million in 2024, supported by an 80 percent increase driven by the expansion of proprietary satellite systems and recurring revenue streams. EBITDA for Data & Services improved to SEK 27.8 million from SEK 21.1 million. Conversely, the Products & Missions segment reported net sales of SEK 245.0 million down from SEK 346.0 million, with EBITDA decreasing to SEK 16.8 million from SEK 51.6 million, reflecting project timing effects and lower delivery volumes.

Cash flow from operating activities was negative at SEK 72.6 million, compared with a positive cash flow of SEK 56.8 million in 2024, affected by working capital changes and operating results. Investments in fixed assets totaled SEK 46.7 million, comprising SEK 43.0 million in tangible assets and SEK 2.8 million in intangible assets. Available cash and cash equivalents at year-end amounted to SEK 30.1 million down from SEK 49.7 million, while total available cash including the bank overdraft facility stood at SEK 80.1 million compared with SEK 70.8 million. The Group strengthened its capital base through directed share issues during the year, including a strategic investment agreement with Bonnier Capital. The equity to assets ratio strengthened to 80% at the end of 2025 compared with 71% at the end of 2024. Total shareholders' equity stood at SEK 710.8 million. The order backlog at the end of the period stood at SEK 352 million, which was subsequently reinforced in early 2026 by a landmark EUR 76.3 million order for the EPS-Sterna programme, lifting the total order backlog to approximately SEK 1.1 billion.
