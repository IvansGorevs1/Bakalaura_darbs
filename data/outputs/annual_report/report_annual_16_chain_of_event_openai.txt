Nordic Fibreboard AS’s 2025 performance weakened materially despite relatively stable fibreboard sales. Consolidated revenue declined 3.4% to €7.328 million from €7.592 million in 2024, with fibreboard revenue falling 3.2% to €7.325 million. Sales within the European Union, representing 92% of revenue, were broadly stable, while sales to Africa, Asia and the Middle East declined. Real-estate management revenue fell to €3 thousand from €25 thousand following the termination of rental agreements in August 2024 because communal costs could not be covered by rental income.

Profitability deteriorated significantly. EBITDA declined to a loss of €420 thousand from a loss of €25 thousand, reducing the EBITDA margin to negative 6% from 0%. The fibreboard segment moved from positive EBITDA of €26 thousand to negative €296 thousand, primarily because of underutilized production capacity, which increased the cost of internally produced goods. EBITDA was also negatively affected by approximately €0.2 million of personnel, maintenance and repair costs during the December factory shutdown. The 2025 shutdown was planned to facilitate the transition to a new gas boiler house, whereas the 2024 shutdown was unplanned and resulted from weather-related structural damage. The boiler-system upgrade was completed in January 2026.

The operating loss increased to €940 thousand from €547 thousand, with operating margin worsening to negative 13% from negative 7%. Distribution costs rose to €982 thousand from €935 thousand and administrative expenses to €502 thousand from €381 thousand. Finance costs remained broadly stable at €255 thousand, compared with €247 thousand, while finance income increased to €41 thousand from €8 thousand, including a €40 thousand gain on the sale of Trigon Property Development shares. Nevertheless, net loss widened to €1.154 million from €786 thousand, although loss per share improved to €0.14 from €0.17 because the number of shares increased substantially.

The Group invested €1.367 million in production assets, mainly the new gas boiler house, and €161 thousand in real estate development. A €2 million share issue completed in October supported financing activities and increased equity to €4.462 million from €3.616 million. Borrowings declined to €3.168 million from €3.724 million, but operating cash flow remained negative at €503 thousand, compared with negative €174 thousand in 2024, and cash ended the year at only €5 thousand. Working capital was negative €2.7 million, compared with negative €0.7 million, and all three borrowing covenants were unfulfilled at year-end. Management plans to address this position through the planned sale of ten Admirali Quarter properties, for which a preliminary sale agreement was signed on 8 January 2026.
