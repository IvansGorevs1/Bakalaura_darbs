Eleving Group S.A. reported its financial results for the 2024 reporting period, highlighting a year of record growth, strategic progress, and improved profitability. Total revenue reached EUR 216.6 million, representing an increase of nearly 15 percent compared to EUR 189.3 million reported in 2023. The Group achieved an all-time best annual net profit of EUR 29.6 million, which is an increase of approximately 21 percent compared to EUR 24.5 million in the previous year. When adjusted for a one-off tax expense of EUR 2.6 million resulting from additional VAT liabilities determined by the Romanian Tax Authority for prior periods, the adjusted total net profit for 2024 amounted to EUR 32.2 million. Adjusted EBITDA reached EUR 89.8 million, marking an increase of over 16 percent compared to EUR 77.5 million in 2023. Basic and diluted earnings per share from continuing operations were EUR 0.25, compared to EUR 0.22 in the previous period, while total earnings per share were EUR 0.25 compared to EUR 0.24 in 2023. 

The vehicle and consumer financing net portfolio grew to EUR 371.2 million at the end of 2024, reflecting an increase of 16 percent compared to EUR 320.3 million as of December 31, 2023. Interest revenue rose to EUR 203.7 million from EUR 176.3 million in the prior year, while net interest income increased to EUR 162.3 million from EUR 138.8 million. Impairment expenses for the period totaled EUR 42.1 million, compared to EUR 39.8 million in 2023. Selling expenses were EUR 6.4 million, down from EUR 7.2 million, whereas administrative expenses increased to EUR 74.7 million from EUR 63.2 million, largely driven by the full-year inclusion of the EC Finance Group acquired in 2023. Profit before tax from continuing operations reached EUR 37.7 million, up from EUR 28.5 million in 2023. Total assets increased to EUR 476.3 million as of December 31, 2024, compared to EUR 421.3 million at the end of 2023. Total equity increased significantly to EUR 108.1 million from EUR 65.4 million, supported by the successful initial public offering and positive operational results. Total liabilities stood at EUR 355.9 million, down from EUR 368.2 million in the previous year, with total borrowings amounting to EUR 339.6 million. 

The most significant changes compared to the previous period included the successful initial public offering completed in October 2024 on Nasdaq Riga and the Frankfurt Stock Exchange, which raised EUR 29.0 million in equity through the issuance of 17,058,824 new shares and strengthened the Group's capital base. The proceeds were used to redeem subordinated bonds and selectively repay higher-cost platform loans. Operationally, the Group completed the integration of its Sub-Saharan operations acquired in 2023, tripling their combined portfolio size, and finalized its digitalization efforts across European markets with a new customer self-service platform. Furthermore, the Group continued to scale its green mobility initiatives, financing approximately 2,000 electric motorcycles in Eastern Africa and reaching its 2025 environmental targets a full year ahead of schedule. 

Main business events and market factors affecting results included stable macroeconomic conditions in core European markets, absence of major macroeconomic disruptions compared to previous years, and proactive risk management that improved portfolio quality and lowered non-performing loan rates. However, results were impacted by regulatory developments, including a tax audit in Romania that resulted in additional VAT liabilities of EUR 3.0 million for the period between 2017 and 2022, which reduced net profit by EUR 2.6 million. 

Overall, Eleving Group demonstrated robust operational maturity, resilience, and efficiency throughout 2024. The Company expanded its market share, advanced its digitalization agenda, successfully entered the public equity markets, and reinforced its financial standing, laying a solid foundation for future growth and its strategic objective to nearly double the size of the business within the next two years.
