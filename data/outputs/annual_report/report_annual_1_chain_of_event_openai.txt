AAC Clyde Space’s 2025 performance was affected by programme delays and timing shifts. Net sales declined 16% to SEK 295.3 million from SEK 352.9 million, while total revenue fell to SEK 337.8 million from SEK 408.8 million. EBITDA decreased to SEK 17.5 million from SEK 46.7 million, although the Group reported positive EBITDA for the third consecutive year. Operating loss widened substantially to SEK 40.0 million from SEK 4.0 million, and the loss after tax increased to SEK 46.6 million from SEK 5.7 million. Earnings per share declined to SEK 7.63 negative from SEK 1.00 negative.

The main deterioration occurred in Products & Missions, where sales fell to SEK 245.0 million from SEK 346.0 million and EBITDA to SEK 16.8 million from SEK 51.6 million. The report states that two programme-related delays materially affected revenue, cash flow and order intake. Technical discussions between an end customer and a subcontractor delayed milestone recognition, shifting approximately SEK 32 million of revenue into 2026. Political and procurement developments also postponed initiation of the EPS-Sterna programme. These factors contributed to revised 2025 guidance and the expectation that around SEK 30 million from a customer project would be recognised in 2026.

Data & Services developed positively. Sales increased 80% to SEK 80.1 million from SEK 44.9 million, while EBITDA rose to SEK 27.8 million from SEK 21.1 million. Sedna-1 and Sedna-2 became operational in November, expanding AIS maritime-data capacity, coverage and reliability and supporting multi-year customer agreements and recurring maritime revenues. Assembly of the VIREON Earth Observation satellites also began, supporting the Group’s strategy of building recurring data-service income.

Operating cash flow deteriorated to negative SEK 72.6 million from positive SEK 56.8 million, reflecting the weaker earnings and timing of programme activity. Cash and cash equivalents declined to SEK 30.1 million from SEK 49.7 million, but the undrawn overdraft facility increased to SEK 50 million from SEK 30 million. The equity ratio improved to 80% from 71%, supported by directed share issues that raised approximately SEK 114.8 million before issue costs. No dividend was proposed.

Despite the weak reported year, the post-year-end outlook strengthened. OHB Sweden awarded a EUR 76.3 million contract, approximately SEK 820 million, for 20 EPS-Sterna microwave radiometers, increasing the order backlog to approximately SEK 1.1 billion by the end of March 2026. Sedna-3 and Sedna-4 were initiated, VIREON-1 and VIREON-2 were launched, and INFLECION Phase 1 was completed.
