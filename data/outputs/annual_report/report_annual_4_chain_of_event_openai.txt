Eleving Group delivered its strongest annual performance in 2024, with revenue increasing 14.4% to EUR 216.6 million from EUR 189.3 million in 2023. Adjusted EBITDA rose 15.9% to EUR 89.8 million, while profit from continuing operations increased 31.4% to EUR 28.8 million. Total net profit reached a record EUR 29.6 million, up 20.9% from EUR 24.5 million. This included EUR 0.8 million from discontinued operations, compared with EUR 2.5 million in 2023. The reported result was reduced by a EUR 2.6 million net one-off tax expense following a Romanian VAT audit; excluding this item, net profit was EUR 32.2 million, more than 31% above the prior year.

Growth was supported by higher lending activity and portfolio expansion. Loan issuance increased 26.8% to EUR 368.6 million, and the net loan and vehicle-rental portfolio grew 15.9% to EUR 371.2 million from EUR 320.3 million. Interest revenue rose 15.6% to EUR 203.7 million, and fee and commission income increased 12.4% to EUR 10.1 million. Despite portfolio growth, management reported improved portfolio quality: vehicle-finance non-performing loans declined from 7.5% to 6.1%, while consumer-finance NPLs decreased from 4.5% to 4.3%. The ratio of impairment expense to interest revenue also fell by two percentage points, which the Group attributed to disciplined underwriting and risk management.

Operating developments included the completion of the integration of Sub-Saharan businesses acquired in 2023; their combined portfolio reportedly tripled during the year. Digitalization progressed through the rollout of the customer self-service platform across established European vehicle-finance markets, while applications nearly doubled year over year. The Group also financed more than 2,000 electric motorcycles in East Africa, supporting approximately 20 million electric kilometers and estimated savings of 1,000 tonnes of CO2.

Capitalization strengthened materially following the October IPO, which raised EUR 29.0 million and increased total equity to EUR 108.1 million from EUR 65.4 million. The proceeds enabled early repayment of subordinated bonds and selected higher-cost borrowings. The capitalization ratio improved to 29.3% from 26.1%, net leverage declined to 3.3x from 3.7x, and interest coverage improved to 2.4x from 2.3x. The Group’s outlook emphasizes continued portfolio growth, new products and markets, further local-currency funding, electric-motorcycle expansion, and refinancing preparation for Eurobonds maturing in 2026.
