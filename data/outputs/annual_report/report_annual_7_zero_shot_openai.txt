Heimstaden reported stronger operating performance in 2025 despite broadly flat rental income and a smaller portfolio. Rental income was SEK 15,916 million, essentially unchanged from SEK 15,919 million in 2024. Like-for-like rental income growth was 4.9%, down from 5.6%, driven mainly by indexation, tenant improvements, occupancy gains, and rent reversion. Real economic occupancy remained high at 98.5%, while fourth-quarter occupancy improved to 98.7%.

Profitability increased as property expenses fell 6.1% to SEK 4,378 million, reflecting cost controls, digitalisation, and the effects of portfolio reductions through privatisations and other sales. Net operating income rose 2.6% to SEK 11,475 million, and the NOI margin improved substantially to 72.1% from 70.3%. Capital expenditure was SEK 4,439 million, compared with SEK 5,765 million in the broader prior-year KPI, while spending under the four main investment categories increased to approximately SEK 3,893 million from SEK 3,824 million. Tenant improvement investments generated SEK 94 million of additional rental income at an average yield on cost of 10.8%.

Investment property values increased 3.0% on a fair-value basis, producing a gain of SEK 9,498 million compared with SEK 8,554 million in 2024, although year-end property values declined to SEK 324,429 million from SEK 335,422 million after disposals and currency effects. Value growth was particularly strong in Denmark, Czechia, the Netherlands, and Finland. Privatisation sales reached SEK 10,664 million, with 2,466 units sold at a 28.9% premium to book value. Total realised gains from property divestments increased to SEK 2,207 million from SEK 1,687 million.

Profit before tax rose to SEK 16,012 million from SEK 10,403 million, supported by higher property gains, fair-value gains, and a SEK 3,996 million foreign-exchange gain, compared with a SEK 2,038 million loss in 2024. This was partly offset by a SEK 3,707 million goodwill impairment charge. Net profit increased to SEK 13,056 million from SEK 7,621 million, while total comprehensive income fell to SEK 4,199 million because of SEK 8,857 million in negative currency translation differences.

The balance sheet strengthened through debt reduction and asset sales. Net LTV improved to 52.2% from 56.4%, net debt to total assets declined to 48.0% from 51.0%, and ICR increased to 2.0x from 1.9x. Heimstaden Bostad completed approximately SEK 59.5 billion of financing transactions, while Heimstaden executed liability-management measures and maintained access to substantial financing markets. Fitch affirmed the company’s B- rating but revised the outlook to Negative. In December, arbitration proceedings initiated by Alecta concluded in Heimstaden’s favour. No dividend was proposed.
