Eleving Group S.A. reported its strongest financial performance to date for the year ended December 31, 2025, driven by robust portfolio growth and successful product expansion across its European and African markets. The Group achieved record-high revenue of EUR 250.0 million, representing a 15.5% increase compared to EUR 216.6 million in 2024. This top-line growth was supported by a 20.2% expansion in the net loan portfolio, which reached EUR 446.3 million at year-end, up from EUR 371.2 million in the previous period. The growth was primarily fueled by strong performance in Latvia, Romania, and Kenya, with significant contributions from new product lines. Consumer lending products, the largest revenue segment, grew by 18.0% to EUR 114.8 million. Flexible vehicle financing also saw substantial growth, increasing by 25.9% to EUR 60.3 million, while the newly launched device financing products contributed EUR 7.9 million in their first full year. Traditional vehicle financing experienced a slight decline of 6.0%, contributing EUR 67.0 million to revenue.

Profitability metrics also improved significantly. Adjusted EBITDA reached a twelve-month record high of EUR 101.9 million, an increase of 13.5% from EUR 89.8 million in 2024. Net profit from continuing operations amounted to EUR 29.2 million, showing stability compared to EUR 28.8 million in the prior year, although net profit before foreign exchange effects rose by 25.6% to EUR 40.8 million. The Group maintained disciplined credit quality, with impairment expenses remaining at a similar level relative to interest revenue despite the larger portfolio. The capitalization ratio stood at 23.7% at year-end, down from 29.3% in 2024, reflecting the increased loan book size, while the interest coverage ratio remained healthy at 2.3.

Strategic developments played a crucial role in the Group’s performance. In 2025, Eleving Group entered new markets, including Tanzania, and expanded its smartphone financing offerings in Kenya and Uganda, which reached a portfolio value of EUR 13.5 million by year-end. The Group also deepened its presence in European markets by introducing installment loans. Capital management was a key highlight, with the completion of two major bond issuances. In March, a EUR 40 million bond tap was executed, and in October, the Group raised EUR 275 million through a refinancing and new issuance, the largest in the company’s history. These actions strengthened the funding base and led Fitch Ratings to upgrade the Group’s outlook from stable to positive, while affirming its B credit rating. Additionally, the Group paid total dividends of EUR 19.65 million in 2025, delivering a 10% cash yield based on the IPO price. Looking ahead, the Group is well-positioned for continued growth, supported by a secured funding base and a strategy focused on operational efficiency, including the planned rollout of AI voice agents in call centers.
