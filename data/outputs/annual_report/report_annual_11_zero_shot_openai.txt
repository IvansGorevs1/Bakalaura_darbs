JLT Mobile Computers delivered a recovery in 2025, with stronger sales and order intake despite weak market demand, geopolitical uncertainty, tariff concerns and substantial currency pressure. Group net sales rose 11.1% to SEK 131.5 million from SEK 118.4 million, while order intake increased 37.5% to SEK 141.6 million. The year-end order backlog grew to SEK 24.1 million from SEK 13.9 million, including SEK 15.0 million scheduled for delivery in the first quarter of 2026.

Profitability remained negative but improved substantially. Gross profit declined to SEK 50.4 million from SEK 54.2 million, and the gross margin fell to 38.3% from 45.8%. The company attributed the margin reduction to currency movements, particularly the US dollar’s almost 20% decline against the Swedish krona. Dollar-denominated sales represented 74% of turnover, and invoicing at weaker exchange rates than those used in quotations reduced margins. EBITDA improved to negative SEK 1.7 million from negative SEK 2.1 million, while operating loss narrowed to SEK 1.9 million from SEK 9.7 million. Profit after tax improved to a loss of SEK 2.5 million from a loss of SEK 7.7 million.

Cost-reduction measures supported the improvement. Operating expenses fell to SEK 52.4 million from SEK 57.3 million, including a SEK 6.3 million reduction in organizational and R&D costs. The discontinuation of JLT Software Solutions AB and consolidation of software development reduced overhead costs by approximately SEK 5 million and materially lowered amortization. A defense-market evaluation project incurred approximately SEK 1 million during the year.

Market conditions were mixed. Demand remained subdued in warehousing, logistics, transportation and manufacturing, while the port and mining segments developed positively. Sales in the Americas increased to SEK 92.2 million from SEK 69.8 million and represented 70.1% of total sales, whereas sales declined in EMEA and the Nordics. A SEK 22 million order from an American food producer was a significant contributor to growth.

Cash flow remained positive at SEK 3.7 million, although below SEK 6.8 million in 2024. Inventory reduction contributed materially: inventories fell by SEK 12.7 million to SEK 25.5 million. Cash and cash equivalents increased to SEK 16.9 million from SEK 13.3 million, and solidity improved to 58% from 54%, although equity declined to SEK 41.8 million from SEK 47.6 million. The company launched a 15-inch Full HD rugged computer and an AI-based screen-blanking safety solution. No dividend is proposed for 2025.
