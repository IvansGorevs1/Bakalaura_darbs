A/S SAF Tehnika reported a decline in consolidated net turnover for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2025, compared to the previous period. The Group’s net turnover decreased by 4%, or 1.1 million EUR, to reach 25.99 million EUR. The parent company’s net turnover saw a sharper decline of 6%, amounting to 21.92 million EUR. This reduction in revenue was primarily driven by a significant 14% drop in the Americas region, where turnover fell by 2.3 million EUR to 14.17 million EUR. The decrease in the Americas was attributed to client caution regarding investment decisions and tariff changes, as well as the deferral of large-volume project realizations to future periods. Conversely, the European region, including Latvia, demonstrated strong growth, with turnover increasing by 17% to 10.0 million EUR. Revenue in the Asia, Middle East, and Africa regions decreased slightly by 5% to 1.8 million EUR.

Despite the top-line contraction, the Group’s gross profit improved significantly. Consolidated gross profit rose to 9.50 million EUR from 7.53 million EUR in the prior year, reflecting better cost management and lower cost of sales. However, the company reported a net loss of 643 thousand EUR for the period, a substantial improvement from the net loss of 2.37 million EUR recorded in the previous year. The parent company also posted a reduced net loss of 552 thousand EUR, compared to 1.34 million EUR previously. The narrowing of losses was supported by higher gross margins, although operating expenses, including sales, marketing, and administrative costs, remained elevated relative to the lower revenue base.

Financial liquidity strengthened considerably during the reporting period. Cash and cash equivalents increased by 2.6 million EUR to 4.81 million EUR at the end of the fiscal year. The parent company maintained a credit line facility with Luminor Bank AS totaling 4.95 million EUR, which remained entirely unused at the reporting date. The Group’s current ratio stood at 2.05, indicating sufficient short-term liquidity to meet obligations. Inventory levels decreased by 1.24 million EUR, partly due to the release of provisions for slow-moving stock accumulated during previous supply chain disruptions. Management noted that while supply chain stability has improved, the company continues to hold strategic reserves to mitigate potential future risks.

Business developments included the establishment of a new branch in Canada to mitigate risks associated with cross-border trade uncertainties and tariffs. The Group continued its focus on product innovation, particularly in the Internet of Things (IoT) segment with its Aranet brand, and received 570 thousand EUR in co-financing for research and development projects. Geopolitical tensions and trade relations between the US and China did not have a material negative impact on sales volumes during the period. Management views the current net loss as temporary and expressed a cautious but positive outlook for future periods, citing stabilization in market activity and ongoing product diversification efforts.
