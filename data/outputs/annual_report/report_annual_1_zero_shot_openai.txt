AAC Clyde Space’s 2025 performance was affected by programme delays, resulting in lower revenue, profitability and operating cash flow despite continued strategic progress and a stronger capital base. Net sales decreased 16% to SEK 295.3 million from SEK 352.9 million, while total revenue fell to SEK 337.8 million from SEK 408.8 million. EBITDA declined to SEK 17.5 million from SEK 46.7 million, although the Group reported positive EBITDA for the third consecutive year. EBIT deteriorated to SEK -40.0 million from SEK -4.0 million, and the loss after tax widened to SEK 46.6 million from SEK 5.7 million. Earnings per share decreased to SEK -7.63 from SEK -1.00.

The main adverse effects were timing-related. A customer programme delay shifted approximately SEK 32 million of milestone revenue into 2026, while political and procurement developments postponed the start of the EPS-Sterna weather programme. Commissioning of the Sedna satellites also took longer than planned, and the launch of the first VIREON Earth Observation satellites was delayed by the launch provider. These developments materially affected revenue, cash flow and order intake and led to revised 2025 guidance.

Performance varied significantly between segments. Data & Services grew 80% to SEK 80.1 million in sales, and EBITDA increased to SEK 27.8 million from SEK 21.1 million. Sedna-1 and Sedna-2 became operational, expanding maritime AIS coverage and supporting recurring data revenues. Products & Missions sales declined to SEK 245.0 million from SEK 346.0 million, while EBITDA fell to SEK 16.8 million from SEK 51.6 million, reflecting project timing and delays. The Group reorganised its operations into these two segments during the year.

Operating cash flow turned negative at SEK 72.6 million, compared with positive cash flow of SEK 56.8 million in 2024. Cash and cash equivalents declined to SEK 30.1 million from SEK 49.7 million, but the available overdraft facility increased to SEK 50 million, leaving total available liquidity of SEK 80.1 million. The equity ratio improved to 80% from 71%, supported by directed share issues that raised approximately SEK 114.8 million before issue costs.

Strategically, the company advanced INFLECION, CubeCAT laser terminals and the VIREON constellation. After year-end, it received an EUR 76.3 million order for 20 EPS-Sterna microwave radiometers, increasing the order backlog to approximately SEK 1.1 billion by the end of March 2026. No dividend was proposed.
