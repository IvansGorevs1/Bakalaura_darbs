During the financial year ended 31 December 2025, AS Tallinna Sadam Group achieved improved profitability despite a slight decline in consolidated revenue. Total revenue decreased by 0.8% compared with the previous year to EUR 118.7 million, primarily due to a lower utilisation rate and fewer charter days for the multifunctional icebreaker Botnica in the Other segment. Conversely, revenue increased across all other operating segments, supported by higher vessel dues from larger vessel capacities and indexation of fee rates. Operating expenses decreased significantly by 10% to EUR 36.4 million, driven by lower technical maintenance and repair costs, reduced operating lease expenses, and the receipt of insurance indemnity for a previous technical failure. Consequently, operating profit grew by 12.2% to EUR 32.7 million, and adjusted EBITDA increased by 6.4% to EUR 56.5 million, raising the adjusted EBITDA margin from 44.4% to 47.6%. Net profit experienced even stronger growth, rising by 17.3% to EUR 22.5 million, supported by a 30% reduction in finance costs due to lower market interest rates and reduced loan volumes, although partially offset by higher dividend income tax expenses resulting from an increased tax rate. 

Operational indicators showed positive developments across core business lines. Total cargo volume grew by 5.1% to 13.8 million tonnes, led by a significant recovery in liquid bulk volumes at Paldiski South Harbour and record shipments of steel and grain at Muuga Harbour. The number of passengers across the harbours increased by 1.0% to 8.3 million, driven by growth on the Tallinn–Helsinki route and an increase in cruise passenger traffic. The ferry service operated by TS Laevad served 2.5 million passengers and 1.2 million vehicles across 23,050 trips, achieving lower fuel consumption per voyage following the implementation of energy monitoring systems and renewable electricity usage. Meanwhile, the icebreaker Botnica recorded a utilisation rate of 49% after participating in rescue and support operations in northern Canada during the autumn. Capital investments totalled EUR 33.2 million for the year, representing a 14.4% decrease from the prior period, with major projects including the completion of the new multifunctional quay at Paldiski South Harbour and infrastructure preparations for future shore power installations. The Group maintained a strong financial position, reducing net debt by 15.5% to EUR 141.7 million while preserving an equity-to-assets ratio of 61%. Aligning with its established dividend policy and mirroring the prior year, the management board proposed distributing a dividend of EUR 0.073 per share, amounting to a total dividend payout of EUR 19.2 million.
