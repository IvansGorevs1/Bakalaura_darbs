TKM Group reported consolidated sales revenue of 919.6 million euros for the 2025 financial year, representing a decrease of 2.6% compared to 944.6 million euros in 2024. Profit before tax declined by 31.4% to 24.3 million euros, while net profit decreased by 36.1% to 17.5 million euros from 27.5 million euros in the previous year. Basic and diluted earnings per share fell to 0.43 euros compared to 0.67 euros in 2024. 

The financial performance was shaped by a challenging economic environment characterized by low consumer confidence due to uncertain geopolitical conditions and domestic tax changes, notably the introduction of Estonia's motor vehicle tax. This tax contributed to a sharp contraction in the Estonian automotive retail sector and a broader decline in consumer demand, while intense retail competition and price sensitivity persisted. 

Among business segments, the car trade segment experienced the most significant revenue drop, falling 11.9% to 176.9 million euros, with segment net profit decreasing by 54.4% to 4.3 million euros. Supermarket revenue remained relatively stable at 611.9 million euros, up 0.3%, though segment net profit declined by 38.6% to 9.5 million euros due to margin pressure and a higher share of promotional products. The department stores segment saw a slight revenue decline of 1.1% to 103.0 million euros and recorded a net loss of 0.5 million euros. The security segment experienced an 8.1% drop in external revenue to 20.1 million euros, posting a nominal net profit. Conversely, the real estate segment grew external revenue by 5.1% to 7.7 million euros, driven by rental income from a newly launched logistics centre, while segment net profit rose 20.5% to 6.3 million euros, though comparative prior-period profits had benefited from one-off property sales and revaluation gains. 

Operating expenses were kept largely under control through supply chain optimization and process improvements, with annual staff costs increasing by only 3.7% and other operating expenses rising by 0.2%. Net profit also benefited from a 1.5 million euro reduction in borrowing interest expenses and a 1.2 million euro decrease in income tax expense. Total group investments amounted to 25.5 million euros in 2025, directed toward store refurbishments, logistics enhancements, digital platform upgrades, and the opening of new car showrooms in Vilnius and Peetri. Subsequent to the balance sheet date, in January 2026, the Group agreed to acquire Škoda distribution rights in Tallinn and Harju County to further strengthen its car segment. As of December 31, 2025, the Group held 29.5 million euros in cash and cash equivalents, and total borrowings stood at 320.5 million euros, with working capital temporarily negative at 14.2 million euros due to long-term loan instalments maturing in 2026 that are planned for refinancing.
