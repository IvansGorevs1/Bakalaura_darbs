Novaturas Group’s 2024 performance weakened overall, despite a material improvement in the final quarter. Revenue declined 3.6% to EUR 200.9 million from EUR 208.3 million in 2023, while the number of travelers fell 7.6% to 239.5 thousand. Gross profit decreased 20.9% to EUR 20.7 million, reducing the gross margin from 12.6% to 10.3%.

Profitability deteriorated substantially. EBITDA declined from a positive EUR 5.2 million in 2023 to negative EUR 6.1 million, while adjusted EBITDA, excluding the goodwill impairment, fell from EUR 5.2 million to negative EUR 449 thousand. Operating profit shifted from EUR 4.3 million to a loss of EUR 6.6 million, and the Group reported a net loss of EUR 7.6 million compared with restated net profit of EUR 3.4 million in 2023. Earnings per share decreased from EUR 0.43 to a loss of EUR 0.98.

The reported loss includes a one-off, non-cash goodwill impairment charge of EUR 5.7 million relating to the Estonian cash-generating unit. Excluding this impairment, the Group’s net loss was EUR 1.9 million. Operating expenses increased 25% to EUR 27.5 million because of the impairment; excluding that item, expenses were EUR 21.8 million, 1% below 2023. Net finance expenses also increased from EUR 488 thousand to EUR 1.2 million, reflecting higher foreign-exchange and realized derivative losses.

Management attributed the annual deterioration primarily to intensified competition and oversupply of travel in the Baltic States, which caused significant price competition, particularly for Turkey and the Greek islands. In response, Novaturas optimized its travel program and diversified its offering toward other destinations. Flight-package revenue, the Group’s principal product, fell to EUR 170.7 million from EUR 182.8 million, while other sales increased to EUR 25.4 million from EUR 20.9 million. Demand for Spain and long-haul destinations grew strongly, including a 149% increase in passengers to Malaga, Barcelona and Mallorca and an 8% increase in long-haul travelers.

Results improved in the final months: adjusted EBITDA was negative EUR 1.6 million in January–June but positive EUR 2.3 million in September–December. In the fourth quarter, the load factor increased to 98.9% from 96.7%, and reduced last-minute sales contributed to profitable operations.

The Group ended the year with EUR 394 thousand in cash and current liabilities exceeding current assets by EUR 12.5 million. It breached financial and non-financial covenants, although waivers were obtained. Management expects 2025 EBITDA of EUR 2–3.3 million and is pursuing cost optimization, financing and strategic benefits from investor Neset Kockar, who acquired 23.2% of the Company after year-end.
