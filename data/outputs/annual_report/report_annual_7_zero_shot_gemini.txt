During the reporting period ending December 31, 2025, Heimstaden demonstrated stable operational performance alongside continued portfolio optimisation and liability management. Rental income remained flat at SEK 15,916 million compared to SEK 15,919 million in the previous year, supported by like-for-like rental growth of 4.9% driven primarily by indexation and tenant improvements. Residential real economic occupancy remained stable at 98.5%. Property expenses decreased by 6.1% to SEK 4,378 million from SEK 4,661 million, driven by cost control measures, digitalisation, and a shrinking portfolio size resulting from divestment programmes. Net operating income improved to SEK 11,475 million from SEK 11,188 million, resulting in an annualized net operating income margin of 72.1% compared to 70.3% in 2024. Profit before tax rose to SEK 16,012 million from SEK 10,403 million, and profit for the period increased to SEK 13,056 million from SEK 7,621 million, driven by higher fair value adjustments of investment properties of SEK 9,498 million compared to SEK 8,554 million, along with net foreign exchange gains of SEK 3,996 million compared to losses of SEK 2,038 million. These positive effects were partially offset by a goodwill impairment loss of SEK 3,707 million recognized on Akelius cash-generating units. Total equity slightly increased to SEK 150,557 million from SEK 147,803 million at year-end 2024. Credit metrics improved, with net loan-to-value decreasing to 52.2% from 56.4%, net debt to total assets declining to 48.0% from 51.0%, and the interest coverage ratio improving to 2.0x from 1.9x. The average interest rate ended the year at 3.43% compared to 3.01%. Privatisation sales contributed SEK 10,664 million through the sale of 2,466 residential units at a 28.9% premium to book value. Cumulative privatisation sales since inception reached a sales value of SEK 19,451 million. Subsidiary Heimstaden Bostad completed approximately SEK 59,500 million in refinancings and new financings, and issued SEK 13,767 million in green bonds backed by SEK 75,920 million in EU Taxonomy-aligned green assets. Overall, the company’s performance reflects disciplined execution of its capital recycling and decarbonisation strategies, resilient rental demand, and strengthened balance sheet metrics despite broader macroeconomic volatility.
