Talkpool delivered a stronger financial performance in 2025, ending its multi-year restructuring and cost-reduction phase and returning to organic growth. Net sales increased 12.5% to EUR 17.756 million from EUR 15.782 million in 2024, marking the first year of double-digit growth since the consolidation phase began. EBITDA rose to EUR 1.828 million from EUR 1.702 million, although the EBITDA margin declined slightly to 10.3% from 10.8% as the company began investing in personnel, technology, and future growth. EBIT increased to EUR 1.620 million from EUR 1.527 million, with the EBIT margin declining to 9.1% from 9.7%.

Net earnings after tax improved substantially by 42%, reaching EUR 862 thousand compared with EUR 605 thousand in 2024. The EAT margin consequently increased to 4.8% from 3.8%. Gross margin was reported at 26.4%. Management stated that the business generated approximately EUR 1.83 million in EBITDA and EUR 0.86 million in net earnings despite initial investments and temporary margin pressure associated with the new growth strategy.

The Americas remained the largest market and continued to grow, supported by the long-standing Digicel relationship in the Caribbean and activity in Haiti, although the US ramp-up was slower than planned. Talkpool re-entered the US market through a new MasTec service contract and completed projects from its Miami office, while a partnership with Nokia USA on a 5G testing project was announced in November. In Germany, fiber-network planning for Deutsche Telekom remained a major contributor, but assignment volumes temporarily flattened as rollout priorities shifted toward homes passed rather than connected. Pakistan achieved its highest revenue in local-currency terms, renegotiated customer terms, and improved gross profitability, while becoming the Group’s main production base.

Currency headwinds affected reported results: the US dollar weakened 11% and the Pakistani rupee 14% during the first half of 2025, reducing revenues translated into euros. Despite these pressures, Talkpool strengthened its financial position. Equity improved to EUR 2.1 million from negative EUR 2.5 million in 2021, while net debt declined to a positive net cash position of EUR 0.3 million at year-end, compared with net debt of EUR 5.7 million in 2021.

Talkpool launched its Vision 2030 plan, targeting EUR 40 million in revenue, a 14% EBITDA margin, 16.8% annual revenue growth, and zero net debt by 2030. Management reported that organic growth reached the long-term target by year-end, while AI-enabled solutions and remote-service investments laid the foundation for further expansion.
