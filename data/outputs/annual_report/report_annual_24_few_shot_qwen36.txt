Trophy Games reported a landmark fiscal year in 2025, characterized by accelerated growth and successful strategic execution. The company achieved total revenue of DKK 126.5 million, representing a 40% increase from DKK 90.1 million in the previous year. This top-line expansion was primarily driven by the scaling of the Transport Series, particularly the new flagship titles Truck Manager and Farm Manager, alongside continued strength from the legacy title Airline Manager. These three core titles, referred to as the "Big 3," collectively contributed DKK 85 million, or 67% of total revenue, marking a significant shift from 2024 when they accounted for only 35% of revenue.

Profitability metrics also improved substantially. EBITDA rose by 26% to DKK 31.1 million, while EBIT increased by 109% to DKK 19.0 million. The EBITDA margin stood at 25%, compared to 27% in 2024, reflecting a deliberate increase in marketing spend to capture market share. The EBT margin improved to 15%, up from 10% in the prior year. Management noted that the 2024 EBT margin was depressed by non-recurring impairment costs of DKK 4.9 million; adjusted for these items, the underlying profitability remained stable across both periods.

A key operational development was the strategic optimization of in-app advertising (IAA) revenue. Total IAA revenue doubled to DKK 18.5 million, accounting for 14.6% of total revenue. This shift improved direct cost efficiency, as IAA revenue is not subject to the platform fees that typically apply to in-app purchases. Consequently, direct costs increased by 34% to DKK 30.5 million, largely due to higher volume and platform fees on IAP, while marketing expenses surged by 76% to DKK 40.5 million to support user acquisition for the new titles.

The company’s financial position remained robust, with a solvency ratio of 78% and no interest-bearing external debt. Total assets grew by 24% to DKK 84.6 million, driven by increased receivables. Cash at bank decreased by 9% to DKK 23.0 million due to record marketing investments, a DKK 4.7 million repayment of previously received tax credits, and a share buyback program totaling DKK 4.9 million. However, the cash position improved significantly to DKK 36.4 million by the end of January 2026 following year-end receivable collections.

Looking ahead, the company provided guidance for 2026 projecting revenue between DKK 130 million and DKK 153 million, representing 3% to 21% growth. EBITDA is expected to range from DKK 28 million to DKK 36 million, and EBT from DKK 18 million to DKK 24 million. This outlook assumes a marketing budget of DKK 45–50 million and ad revenue growth to DKK 22–27 million. The pipeline includes the pre-production of Train Manager 2 and Space Manager, aiming to further diversify the portfolio and sustain long-term growth.
