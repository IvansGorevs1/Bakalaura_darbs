Nordic Fibreboard AS reported a decline in financial performance for the 2025 financial year, driven by challenging market conditions, underutilized production capacity, and planned factory shutdowns. Consolidated net sales for 2025 decreased by 3.4% to 7.33 million euros, compared with 7.59 million euros in 2024. Fibreboard production and sales remained the primary revenue source at 7.33 million euros, declining slightly by 3% from 7.57 million euros in the previous year. Real estate management revenue dropped significantly to 3 thousand euros, down from 25 thousand euros in 2024, following the termination of rental agreements in August 2024 to prepare for development activities. Geographically, European Union sales remained relatively flat at 6.70 million euros, representing 92 percent of total net sales, while sales to Africa, Asia, and the Middle East experienced declines.

Profitability metrics weakened further during the period. The consolidated EBITDA was negative 0.42 million euros, compared with negative 0.03 million euros in 2024, resulting in a negative EBITDA margin of 6 percent. The negative EBITDA was primarily caused by underutilized production capacity and a planned factory shutdown in December 2025 to transition to a new gas boiler house, during which personnel, maintenance, and repair costs continued to accrue. Operating loss widened to 0.94 million euros from 0.54 million euros in 2024, while net financial costs remained relatively stable at 0.21 million euros. Consequently, the Group reported a consolidated net loss of 1.15 million euros for 2025, compared with a net loss of 0.79 million euros in the previous year. Basic and diluted loss per share improved slightly to 0.14 euros, compared with 0.17 euros in 2024.

The statement of financial position showed total assets of 9.32 million euros as of December 31, 2025, compared with 8.25 million euros at the end of 2024. Total liabilities increased to 4.86 million euros from 4.63 million euros, consisting of 1.55 million euros in payables and prepayments and 3.17 million euros in borrowings and lease liabilities. Total equity rose to 4.46 million euros, up from 3.61 million euros, bolstered by a share capital increase completed in October 2025 that generated 2.00 million euros through the issuance of 4,000,000 new shares. Cash flow from operating activities was a negative 0.50 million euros, cash outflows from investing activities totaled 0.99 million euros due to production and real estate investments, and cash generated from financing activities reached 1.44 million euros. The Group ended the year with negative net working capital of 2.7 million euros. Subsequent to the balance sheet date, the company entered into a preliminary agreement on January 8, 2026, to sell the ten properties comprising the Admirali Quarter development project in Pärnu, with the final sale agreement expected by June 30, 2026.
