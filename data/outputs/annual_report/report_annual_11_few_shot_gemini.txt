During the financial year 2025, JLT Mobile Computers AB reported net sales of SEK 131.5 million, representing an increase of 11.1 percent compared to SEK 118.4 million in the previous year. Order intake grew significantly by 37.5 percent to SEK 142.0 million compared to SEK 103.0 million previously, supported by strategic deals including a major SEK 22 million order from an American food producer. Despite the top-line growth, profitability remained negative, though losses narrowed considerably. The operating loss improved to SEK -1.9 million from SEK -9.7 million in 2024, while EBITDA stood at SEK -1.7 million compared to SEK -2.1 million. Profit after tax improved to SEK -2.5 million from SEK -7.7 million. Earnings per share were SEK -0.09 compared to SEK -0.27 in the prior year. The Board of Directors has proposed that no dividend be paid for the 2025 fiscal year.

The company’s financial performance was heavily impacted by external macroeconomic factors, specifically currency fluctuations and international trade uncertainties. The US dollar declined by nearly 20 percent against the Swedish krona during the year. Since 74 percent of JLT’s sales were invoiced in dollars at weaker exchange rates than when quotes were provided, and inventory was revalued at current dollar purchase rates, the gross margin dropped by 7.5 percentage points to 38.3 percent compared to 45.8 percent in 2024. Gross profit decreased to SEK 50.4 million from SEK 54.2 million. Furthermore, geopolitical uncertainty and unclear customs and tariff regulations caused a wait-and-see approach among end customers in the primary warehousing and logistics segment, while increasing internal administrative burdens and investigation costs. 

To counteract weaknesses in core markets, JLT focused on strategic portfolio expansion and operational efficiency. The company shifted attention toward more stable sectors such as ports, construction, and mining equipment, yielding positive results. Product innovations included the launch of the JLT6015, the industry’s first rugged on-board computer with a 15-inch Full HD display, and an AI-powered screen blanking solution to enhance industrial safety. Additionally, structural cost-reduction measures implemented previously, including the consolidation of product development and the discontinuation of a subsidiary, reduced overhead costs by SEK 5 million and lowered depreciation charges. Operating expenses for the group decreased to SEK 52.4 million from SEK 57.3 million.

Cash flow management and balance sheet strengthening were major priorities during the period. Focused efforts to reduce safety stocks built up during prior component shortages successfully lowered inventories by SEK 12.7 million to SEK 25.5 million at year-end, down from SEK 38.3 million. Consequently, cash flow from operating activities after changes in working capital improved to SEK 5.9 million, and total cash flow for the year was SEK 3.7 million compared to SEK 6.8 million in 2024. The group closed the year with cash and cash equivalents of SEK 16.9 million, up from SEK 13.3 million. Solidity strengthened to 59 percent from 54 percent, and total equity stood at SEK 41.8 million compared to SEK 47.6 million at the end of 2024. Overall, despite challenging external currency and market headwinds, JLT achieved notable sales growth, lowered its cost base, and significantly improved operating results and capital efficiency.
