During the financial year 2025, Lyko Group AB reported net revenue of SEK 3,962.6 million, representing an increase of 11.3 percent compared to SEK 3,561.7 million in 2024. Total revenue reached SEK 3,975.7 million, up from SEK 3,579.1 million in the previous year. Sales growth was primarily driven by the Nordic segment, which grew by 12.0 percent to SEK 3,699.8 million, while sales in the Europe segment decreased slightly by 1.3 percent to SEK 130.7 million. Gross profit for the year rose to SEK 1,658.7 million compared to SEK 1,568.8 million in 2024, although the gross profit margin contracted from 44.0 percent to 41.9 percent. Operating profit (EBIT) declined to SEK 78.2 million from SEK 117.4 million, resulting in an EBIT margin of 2.0 percent compared to 3.3 percent in the prior year. The company recorded a net loss of SEK 3.0 million for the year, down from a profit of SEK 21.0 million in 2024, corresponding to earnings per share of SEK -0.20 compared to SEK 1.37 previously. 

Significant changes in the company’s financial position and cash flows were also reported. Cash flow from operating activities improved substantially to SEK 527.5 million from SEK 209.7 million in 2024. Cash flow used in investing activities increased to SEK -351.2 million from SEK -204.0 million, driven heavily by capital expenditures for the new warehouse automation in Vansbro and store expansions. Cash flow from financing activities was SEK 150.9 million compared to SEK 65.9 million, reflecting new borrowings of SEK 270.3 million offset by lease liability repayments of SEK -119.4 million. Total cash and cash equivalents increased significantly to SEK 452.5 million at year-end from SEK 140.5 million. The balance sheet total expanded to SEK 3,086.4 million from SEK 2,313.3 million, driven by investments in property, plant, and equipment, higher borrowing, and increased current liabilities. Consequently, the equity ratio decreased from 20.3 percent to 14.8 percent, with total equity standing at SEK 456.6 million. Net debt excluding leasing was reported at SEK 292.5 million compared to SEK 333.1 million in the previous year. 

The company's performance was influenced by several major business events and market factors. A key operational milestone was the commissioning and ramp-up of the new warehouse automation project in Vansbro, the largest investment in the company's history, designed to scale capacity up to approximately 100,000 orders per day and improve long-term logistical efficiency. Implementation and ramp-up effects impacted results during parts of the year. In retail expansion, Lyko opened its first Fab Store concept in Täby Centrum, Stockholm, along with new stores in Norway and Finland, bringing the total store count to 34. The company also progressed its own brands portfolio, notably through the strategic relaunch of the Make Up Store and the introduction of new concepts, while initiating a reorganization of the Europe segment to centralize operations and reduce overhead costs. 

Overall, Lyko continued to pursue its strategic growth trajectory, outperforming the broader beauty market and welcoming hundreds of thousands of new customers, with annual revenue in Norway surpassing SEK 1 billion and total group revenue approaching SEK 4 billion. While profitability margins faced pressure from intense competition, operational scaling, and implementation costs associated with major infrastructure projects, the business successfully enhanced its logistical and organizational platform, positioning itself for future volume growth and efficiency. In line with its strategy to prioritize expansion over near-term distributions, the Board of Directors proposed that no dividend be paid for the 2025 financial year.
