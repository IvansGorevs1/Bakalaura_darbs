OODA AI AB reported a significant transformation in 2025, shifting from a build-up phase to a growth-oriented commercialization strategy. The company’s most notable financial development was a dramatic increase in revenue, with net sales reaching SEK 31,880 thousand compared to SEK 1,056 thousand in the previous year. This surge was driven by the invoicing of several major transactions, including expanded agreements with Win.Rocks AG and new contracts with Sergel Kredittjänster and HMM Deutschland. Despite this strong top-line growth, the Group reported a net loss for the year of SEK 125,799 thousand, resulting in earnings per share of SEK -35.29, an improvement from the SEK -29.00 loss per share in 2024. The negative earnings trend was primarily attributable to substantial impairments and losses from financial items rather than underlying operational activities.

The Group’s operating profit improved significantly to -29,270 thousand SEK from -51,781 thousand SEK in the prior year. EBITDA turned positive at 2,957 thousand SEK, a stark contrast to the -28,229 thousand SEK reported in 2024. When adjusted for one-off costs of approximately 10,238 thousand SEK related to the liquidation of older company structures, the adjusted EBITDA amounted to approximately 13,195 thousand SEK, representing an adjusted EBITDA margin of approximately 32 percent of revenue. This indicates that the core operational business is generating positive cash flow, supported by a low cost base and no major recruitment during the period. Personnel costs remained stable at -9,210 thousand SEK.

The substantial net loss and reduction in equity were largely due to a comprehensive review and streamlining of the company’s financial holdings. The Group recognized significant impairment losses, particularly in participations in associated companies and other securities, which contributed to a total result from financial items of -95,853 thousand SEK. Consequently, financial assets decreased from 96,036 thousand SEK to 8,387 thousand SEK, and total equity fell from 119,371 thousand SEK to 23,859 thousand SEK. Goodwill was also fully impaired, reducing its carrying amount to zero.

To strengthen its financial position, the Company completed several capital raisings throughout 2025, raising approximately SEK 44.6 million through rights issues. This, combined with reduced debt, improved the liquidity position despite a negative cash flow from operating activities of -16,662 thousand SEK for the full year. However, the second half of the year saw a positive operating cash flow of 5,098 thousand SEK, signaling improved operational momentum. The Board of Directors confirms that the company has the conditions to continue operations on a going concern basis, focusing on scaling the platform, expanding distribution through partners, and commercializing its decentralized AI infrastructure in 2026.
