For the financial year ended 31 December 2025, AS Tallinna Sadam Group reported revenue of EUR 118.7 million, representing a slight decrease of 0.8 percent or EUR 0.9 million compared to EUR 119.6 million in 2024. Despite the minor drop in revenue, the Group achieved an operating profit of EUR 32.7 million, which is an increase of 12.2 percent or EUR 3.6 million compared to EUR 29.1 million in the previous year. Adjusted EBITDA grew by 6.4 percent or EUR 3.4 million to EUR 56.5 million, up from EUR 53.1 million in 2024, resulting in an improved adjusted EBITDA margin of 47.6 percent compared to 44.4 percent previously. Profit for the period rose by 17.3 percent or EUR 3.3 million to EUR 22.5 million, compared to EUR 19.2 million in 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share increased from EUR 0.07 to EUR 0.09. Total assets at period-end stood at EUR 622.1 million, down by 1.2 percent from EUR 629.9 million, while total equity increased by 0.9 percent to EUR 380.9 million. Net debt decreased significantly by 15.5 percent to EUR 141.7 million, down from EUR 167.6 million at the end of 2024. Capital investments for the year were EUR 33.2 million, representing a 14.4 percent decrease compared to EUR 38.8 million in the prior year. Operating volumes showed positive developments in several areas, with cargo volumes increasing by 5.1 percent to 13.8 million tonnes and the number of passengers rising by 1.0 percent to 8.3 million. Vessel calls remained stable at 7,127. The financial results were influenced by contrasting developments across business segments. Revenue growth was achieved in the passenger harbours, cargo harbours, and ferry segments, driven by an increased number of tanker and container ship calls, higher passenger volumes on the Tallinn-Helsinki route, fee indexing, and a notable recovery in liquid bulk cargo volumes at Paldiski South Harbour and Muuga Harbour. Conversely, segment Other experienced a revenue decline due to a lower utilisation rate and fewer charter days for the icebreaker Botnica, whose charter days decreased from 240 to 198 days. However, operating profit and adjusted EBITDA in the cargo and passenger segments improved due to higher revenue and a decrease in operating expenses. Operating expenses across the Group decreased by 10.0 percent or EUR 4.0 million to EUR 36.4 million, largely due to lower technical maintenance and repair costs, reduced fuel consumption, and the receipt of an insurance indemnity of EUR 0.9 million related to a prior-period technical failure on the icebreaker Botnica. Finance costs decreased by 30.2 percent to EUR 5.8 million due to falling Euribor interest rates and reduced loan balances, which positively impacted profit before tax, although net profit growth was partially offset by a higher dividend income tax expense. Based on the financial results and dividend policy, the management board proposed a dividend distribution of EUR 0.073 per share, totaling EUR 19.2 million, matching the total dividend paid out in the previous year.
