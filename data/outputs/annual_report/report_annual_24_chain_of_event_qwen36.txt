Trophy Games reported a landmark fiscal year 2025, characterized by accelerated growth and the successful scaling of its Transport Series. Total revenue increased by 40% to DKK 126.5 million, significantly surpassing the initial guidance range of DKK 85–106 million. This top-line expansion was primarily driven by the strong performance of the company’s three largest titles, collectively referred to as the Big 3, which generated DKK 85 million in revenue, representing 67% of the total. Airline Manager contributed DKK 37.8 million, reflecting a 28% increase from the prior year. Truck Manager emerged as a major growth engine, with revenue surging to DKK 25.2 million from a base of DKK 1.2 million in 2024, following its late 2024 launch. Farm Manager, released in July 2025, added DKK 22.0 million in revenue during its first six months. Conversely, revenue from Other Transport Titles declined by 46% to DKK 21.0 million, as the company strategically reallocated marketing resources toward the higher-performing Big 3 titles.

Profitability metrics also improved substantially. EBITDA rose by 26% to DKK 31.1 million, although the EBITDA margin slightly contracted to 25% from 27% in 2024 due to increased marketing spend aimed at capturing market share. Profit before tax (EBT) more than doubled, increasing by 109% to DKK 19.0 million. The report notes that adjusting for non-recurring impairment costs of DKK 4.9 million recognized in 2024, the underlying profitability remained stable, with an adjusted EBT margin of 15% for both years. A one-time tax repayment of DKK 4.7 million related to previous R&D tax credits impacted liquidity but resulted in a carryforward tax loss.

The company’s operational strategy shifted to include a hybrid monetization model, with in-app advertising revenue doubling to DKK 18.5 million. This shift improved direct cost efficiency, as advertising revenue is not subject to the platform fees applied to in-app purchases. Marketing expenses increased by 76% to DKK 40.5 million to support user acquisition for the scaling titles. Despite these investments, the company maintained a strong balance sheet with a solvency ratio of 78% and no interest-bearing debt. Cash at year-end was DKK 23.0 million, temporarily affected by a large VAT receivable and share buybacks, but improved to DKK 36.4 million in early January 2026. Looking ahead to 2026, the company guides for revenue between DKK 130 million and DKK 153 million, supported by continued optimization of ad revenue and the upcoming release of Train Manager 2.
