Gigasun delivered stronger operating performance in 2025, supported by continued expansion of its Chinese rooftop solar portfolio. Total revenue increased 9.8% to SEK 239.7 million, while net revenue from electricity sales rose 9.9% to SEK 203.2 million. Other operating income increased to SEK 36.5 million, including SEK 32.7 million in subsidies. Revenue growth was driven by higher electricity production, although it was partly offset by lower average prices caused by a greater share of electricity sold to the grid, revised tariffs and currency effects. Reported currency effects reduced total revenue by SEK 18.0 million.

Operating expenses rose 5.5% to SEK 166.2 million, mainly reflecting the larger installed base, higher depreciation and increased maintenance costs. Despite this, EBITDA increased from SEK 145.8 million to SEK 163.7 million, with the margin improving from 67% to 68%. EBIT rose 21.1% to SEK 73.5 million, and the EBIT margin increased from 28% to 31%, demonstrating improved underlying operating profitability. Interest expenses and similar charges declined from SEK 88.7 million to SEK 82.0 million as refinancing in China progressed. However, a SEK 3.8 million negative currency difference and a one-off cost of SEK 3.5 million affected the bottom line. Profit after tax improved to a loss of SEK 7.2 million from a loss of SEK 32.9 million in 2024, while earnings per share improved from SEK -0.58 to SEK -0.13.

Operationally, Gigasun installed 55.7 MW during the year, bringing revenue-generating capacity to 395.8 MW at year-end, up from 340.1 MW. The portfolio comprised 216 installations, compared with 200 previously. Electricity generation increased 30% to 327.8 million kWh, reducing CO2-related emissions by approximately 161,000 tonnes. The company signed 17 contracts representing approximately 41.8 MW, although signed orders at year-end declined to 18.9 MW from 38.8 MW.

Financing remained a central focus. Gigasun secured additional refinancing of approximately SEK 372 million, in addition to the SEK 870 million agreed in 2024, at an interest rate of 3.60% and with a ten-year amortization period. The SOLT4 bond was extended to 8 November 2026. Interest-bearing debt declined to SEK 1,382.6 million from SEK 1,483.1 million, but the equity ratio fell to 21% from 23%, and cash decreased to SEK 45.1 million from SEK 89.9 million. The Group’s cash flow was negative at SEK 34.9 million, reflecting continued investment in solar facilities. Management states that securing further financing in 2026 remains important to support the business plan and continued operations.
