InCoax Networks AB reported a significant decline in financial performance for the year 2025, marking a transition from intensive product development to commercial execution. Net sales amounted to SEK 48,055,007, representing a 35 percent decrease compared to SEK 74,497,775 in the previous year. This reduction was primarily attributed to timing shifts in customer orders and a more cautious market environment, particularly within the North American segment which contributed the majority of revenue. Despite the lower top line, the gross margin improved to 62 percent from 57 percent in 2024, reflecting a more efficient cost structure for goods sold.

The company recorded an operating loss of SEK 32,380,175, a substantial widening from the operating loss of SEK 19,378,406 in 2024. This deterioration in earnings was driven by the drop in revenue, while the company maintained high levels of investment in product development and commercial expansion to strengthen its long-term growth platform. Capitalized development costs remained stable at SEK 25,269,745, largely related to the next-generation MoCA Access platform. The net loss after tax reached SEK 32,559,663, corresponding to a loss per share of SEK 0.25, compared to SEK 0.18 in the prior year.

A critical aspect of the 2025 performance was the company’s cash flow position. While operating cash flow remained negative at SEK 20,011,617, the total cash flow including financing activities turned positive at SEK 6,516,117, a sharp reversal from the negative SEK 12,758,133 in 2024. This positive outcome was the result of successful financing activities, including a directed share issue that raised approximately SEK 33.5 million and the issuance of loans. These activities strengthened the company’s financial position, resulting in cash and cash equivalents of SEK 8,748,046 at year-end, up from SEK 2,231,929 in 2024.

Strategically, 2025 concluded the most development-intensive phase in the company’s history. Key milestones included the launch of the D2501 product within the Nokia Gigabit Connect platform, providing access to Nokia’s global operator base, and the launch of the D2508 product through InCoax’s own channels. The CEO highlighted a shift in focus toward commercial execution, noting that structural cost reductions of more than 40 percent have been implemented to lower the ongoing cost base. The company aims to reach positive cash flow within the coming twelve months by intensifying commercial efforts and converting its pipeline into business. The equity ratio decreased to 71.8 percent from 80.8 percent, reflecting the dilution from new share issues and accumulated losses, yet the company maintains a robust balance sheet with total assets of SEK 152,839,491. The overall development indicates a company stabilizing its operations after a period of heavy investment, now positioning itself for scalable commercial rollout in multi-dwelling buildings where existing coaxial infrastructure can be leveraged for high-speed broadband.
