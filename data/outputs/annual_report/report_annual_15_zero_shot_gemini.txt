AS Merko Ehitus published its consolidated annual report for the financial year ended December 31, 2025. During the reporting period, the company achieved total consolidated revenue of EUR 310.9 million, representing a significant decrease of 42.3% compared to EUR 539.0 million in 2024. Gross profit for 2025 stood at EUR 55.9 million, down from EUR 95.9 million in the previous year, while the gross margin remained stable at 18.0% compared to 17.8% in 2024. Operating profit declined to EUR 34.3 million from EUR 72.5 million, resulting in an operating profit margin of 11.0% versus 13.4% in 2024. Pre-tax profit amounted to EUR 44.8 million compared to EUR 76.4 million in 2024. Net profit attributable to equity holders of the parent was EUR 39.9 million, compared to EUR 64.7 million in the previous reporting period. Basic and diluted earnings per share decreased to EUR 2.26 from EUR 3.65 in 2024. 

The most significant changes compared to the previous financial year were driven by lower revenue generation in the construction service segment. This decrease occurred as several large-scale infrastructure and energy projects initiated in earlier years were completed during 2025, and the signing of new contracts had been slower during 2024 and the first half of 2025. Conversely, the real estate development segment exhibited growth, with revenue rising by 13.8% to EUR 73.4 million, supported by the handover of 358 apartments compared to 323 apartments in 2024. Geographically, Estonia generated 54.9% of the group's revenue, Lithuania contributed 28.5%, and Latvia accounted for 16.6%. The company maintained a strong balance sheet with total assets of EUR 415.0 million as of December 31, 2025, compared to EUR 447.1 million a year earlier. Equity attributable to equity holders of the parent rose to EUR 260.6 million from EUR 254.3 million. The equity ratio strengthened to 62.8% from 56.9%, while the debt ratio remained moderate at 8.0% compared to 7.5% at the end of 2024. Return on equity was 15.6%, down from 28.4% in 2024, but continued to fulfill the company's long-term financial objective of at least 10%. 

The company’s results were shaped by a persistent uncertain global political and economic environment, high inflation, and cautious private investment. European central banks maintained higher interest rates, and in the Baltics, recovery was uneven, marked by consumer caution in Estonia and Latvia, contrasted with greater economic activity in Lithuania. The construction market faced a decline in private investments, leading to a higher share of public sector orders, particularly in defense and infrastructure. Despite initial delays in signing new contracts, the second half of 2025 showed a clear improvement. Notably, preparatory efforts in Lithuania resulted in the signing of major contracts at the beginning of 2026 for the construction of the Rūdninkai defence campus phases. Furthermore, strategic investments in joint ventures like Connecto contributed significantly, accounting for one-quarter of the group's net profit. The group's secured order book increased notably to EUR 466.9 million as of December 31, 2025, up from EUR 340.6 million a year earlier, providing a stable workload for future periods. 

Overall, company performance during the reporting period reflected a transition following the completion of major prior-year projects and a return to more traditional sector margins amidst competitive market conditions. Despite the contraction in revenue and net profit, Merko Ehitus successfully preserved its robust capitalisation, improved its equity ratio, and expanded its secured order book. In line with the long-term financial objective to distribute 50-70% of annual profit as dividends, the Management Board, in coordination with the Supervisory Board, has proposed a dividend of EUR 1.25 per share from net profits brought forward, totalling EUR 22.1 million for the 2025 financial year, representing a 55% dividend rate.
