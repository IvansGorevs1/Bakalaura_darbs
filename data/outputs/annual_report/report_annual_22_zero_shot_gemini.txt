During the 2025 financial year, AS Tallinna Vesi and its subsidiaries demonstrated solid overall financial and operational performance, underscored by revenue growth, profitability gains, and strategic advancements in sustainability and infrastructure investments. The group’s total sales for 2025 reached 72.48 million euros, representing an increase of 11.4% or 7.40 million euros compared to 65.08 million euros in 2024. Water services revenue accounted for 62.31 million euros, marking a 6.9% year-on-year increase driven by higher sales to private customers within the main service area and outside service area customers, alongside tariff adjustments. Gross profit for the year rose by 10.5% to 29.52 million euros compared to 26.71 million euros in the previous year. Operating profit before depreciation and amortisation (EBITDA) improved by 12.5% to 30.95 million euros from 27.51 million euros, yielding an EBITDA margin of 42.70%. Operating profit grew by 11.8% to 21.22 million euros compared to 18.98 million euros in 2024, supported by the optimisation of electricity costs following the full commissioning of the combined heat and power plant at the Paljassaare Wastewater Treatment Plant. Profit before taxes increased by 18.0% to 17.43 million euros, while net profit for the reporting period rose by 7.2% to 14.24 million euros compared to 13.28 million euros in 2024, with net profit growth partially moderated by a higher income tax rate on dividend payments. Earnings per share increased to 0.71 euros from 0.66 euros in 2024. The group's financial position remained robust, with total assets expanding to 364.57 million euros as of 31 December 2025, compared to 313.84 million euros at the end of 2024. Total equity stood at 123.79 million euros. Non-current assets increased to 341.35 million euros, largely driven by significant fixed asset investments totaling 55.74 million euros in 2025, up from 49.53 million euros in 2024, focused on maintaining asset reliability, infrastructure modernization, and environmental compliance. Outstanding borrowings increased to 160.0 million euros from 118.0 million euros at the close of 2024, reflecting long-term financing drawdowns under agreements with the Nordic Investment Bank and a syndicated loan facility to support ongoing capital expenditure programs. The group's debt-to-total-assets ratio was 66.02%. The economic environment during 2025 was characterized by signs of recovery in the wider Estonian economy, accompanied by a consumer price index increase of 4.8% and a construction price index increase of 1.5%. Business operations were significantly influenced by strategic investments in renewable energy production, smart water meter rollouts reaching over 80% of customers, and trenchless pipeline construction methods. Key environmental achievements included the full operational capacity of the combined heat and power plant, which generated all heating and over half of the electricity required for wastewater treatment processes, and recognition as the Green Company of the Year. Overall, the company maintained high service quality standards, with 99.5% of tap water samples complying with drinking water standards and treated effluent outperforming legal requirements, successfully navigating operational challenges while advancing its long-term strategic and sustainability objectives.
