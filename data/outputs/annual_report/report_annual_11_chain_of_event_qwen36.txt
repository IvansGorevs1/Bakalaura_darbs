JLT Mobile Computers reported net sales of SEK 131.5 million for the fiscal year 2025, representing an 11.1 percent increase from the SEK 118.4 million recorded in the previous year. This top-line growth was driven by a robust order intake of SEK 141.6 million, a 37.5 percent rise compared to the prior period. The strong commercial momentum was supported by a major deal with an American food producer valued at SEK 22 million and successful expansion into new market segments such as ports, construction, and mining equipment. However, the company’s profitability was negatively impacted by significant currency fluctuations. The US dollar depreciated by nearly 20 percent against the Swedish krona during the year. Since 74 percent of JLT’s sales were invoiced in dollars, this devaluation reduced the gross margin to 38.3 percent from 45.8 percent in 2024, a decrease of 7.5 percentage points. Despite the margin compression, operating expenses were effectively managed. Organizational and research and development costs decreased by SEK 6.3 million due to structural changes, including the coordination of software development and the discontinuation of the subsidiary JLT Software Solutions AB. These measures reduced overhead costs by SEK 5 million and lowered depreciation on development projects. Consequently, the operating loss narrowed significantly to SEK -1.9 million, a substantial improvement from the SEK -9.7 million loss reported in 2024. EBITDA also improved to SEK -1.7 million from SEK -2.1 million. The company’s cash flow from operating activities turned positive at SEK 3.7 million, compared to SEK 6.8 million in the previous year, largely due to a strategic reduction in inventory. Inventories decreased by SEK 12.7 million to SEK 25.5 million as the company worked to reduce safety stocks built up during previous component shortages. This focus on working capital efficiency helped offset the negative impact of currency translation differences on equity. The group reported a net loss after tax of SEK -2.5 million, an improvement from the SEK -7.7 million loss in 2024. The Board of Directors proposed no dividend payment for the year. Looking ahead, JLT entered 2026 with a strengthened product portfolio, including the launch of the industry’s first rugged 15-inch Full HD computer and an AI-powered screen blanking solution. The company’s strategic diversification away from its largest segment, warehousing and logistics, which remained subdued due to geopolitical uncertainty, positions it to capture growth in markets with larger investment budgets. The combination of a lower cost base, broader market exposure, and strong order intake provides a solid platform for sustainable growth as the broader market recovers.
