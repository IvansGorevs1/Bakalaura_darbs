Konsolidator A/S reported a significant improvement in financial performance for the year ended December 31, 2025, marking the first full year of execution under its Resilient Growth strategy. Total revenue increased by 25% to DKK 25.3 million, up from DKK 20.3 million in 2024. This growth was driven by a 13% increase in subscription fees to DKK 20.6 million and a substantial 134% rise in onboarding and consulting fees, which reached DKK 4.7 million. The company added 52 new customers during the period, with 38% acquired through partners, reflecting a successful shift toward a partner-driven growth model. Contracted Annual Recurring Revenue (CARR) grew by 15% to DKK 24.4 million, exceeding the guidance range of DKK 23–24 million. The average contract value per customer also increased to DKK 87,000 from DKK 70,000 in the previous year.

Profitability metrics showed marked enhancement. The EBITDA loss narrowed significantly by 77% to DKK 2.0 million, compared to a loss of DKK 9.3 million in 2024. This improvement was supported by cost optimizations, particularly in marketing, and a strategic agreement with Exsitec AB that transferred customer success activities in Sweden and Norway, positively impacting the EBITDA margin. The EBIT loss also improved, decreasing to DKK 5.1 million from DKK 12.1 million in 2024. The net loss for the year stood at DKK 6.6 million, a considerable reduction from the DKK 14.9 million loss recorded in 2024.

Key SaaS metrics demonstrated improved operational efficiency and unit economics. The Rule of 40 score improved dramatically from -40% in 2024 to 17% in 2025, reflecting the balance between revenue growth and profitability. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) payback period shortened to 23 months from 43 months, driven by reduced marketing spend and higher revenue per customer. The Customer Lifetime Value to CAC ratio increased to 4.1 from 2.7. However, the ARR churn rate rose to 12.6% from 10.5% in 2024, attributed to increased competition and changing customer needs regarding FP&A tools. Management expects churn to decline to between 6% and 9% in 2026 following onboarding improvements. Net retention remained stable at 94%.

Financial position changes included a negative consolidated equity of DKK 3.6 million, though the parent company, Konsolidator A/S, maintained positive equity of DKK 0.7 million. The company secured DKK 4.8 million through two private placements in 2025 to support operations and product development. Cash flow from operating activities improved to a negative DKK 1.0 million from a negative DKK 9.2 million in 2024, aided by better working capital management and a shift toward annual invoicing. Intangible assets, primarily development costs for the Konsolidator Suite, increased to DKK 15.5 million. The company launched new components of the Konsolidator Suite, including a Financial Data Management solution and an FP&A tool, positioning itself as a broader financial reporting platform. For 2026, the company guides for revenue between DKK 27–29 million and expects positive EBITDA of DKK 1–2 million.
