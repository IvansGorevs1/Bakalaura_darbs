Talkpool reported a strong financial improvement in 2025, marking a transition from restructuring to organic growth. Net sales increased 12.5% to EUR 17.756 million from EUR 15.782 million in 2024, the first year of double-digit growth since the consolidation phase began. EBITDA rose to EUR 1.828 million from EUR 1.702 million, although the EBITDA margin declined slightly to 10.3% from 10.8%. EBIT increased to EUR 1.620 million from EUR 1.527 million, while the EBIT margin decreased to 9.1% from 9.7%. Net earnings after tax grew substantially, by 42%, to EUR 862 thousand from EUR 605 thousand, raising the EAT margin to 4.8% from 3.8%.

The company attributed the modest margin pressure to initial investments in personnel, technology, and growth initiatives following the end of its cost-reduction phase. Management said the new business model is expected to generate higher margins over time, while acknowledging that investments may temporarily weigh on cash flow and profitability. Currency movements also affected reported results: the US dollar weakened by 11% and the Pakistani rupee by 14% during the first half of the year, reducing revenues translated into euros.

Growth was organic and accelerated during the year, reaching the company’s long-term target of 16.8% annual growth by year-end, according to management. The Americas remained the largest market and continued to grow despite currency weakness, supported by the longstanding Digicel relationship and ongoing Caribbean activity. Talkpool re-entered the US market through a MasTec service contract, although the US ramp-up was slower than planned and order volumes remained low after the company shifted away from fibre rollout work toward remote wireless services. Germany remained a significant and stable contributor, but planning volumes temporarily flattened as Deutsche Telekom prioritized homes passed over homes connected. Pakistan achieved its highest revenue in local currency, and renegotiated customer terms in the second half improved gross profitability.

Financial resilience also strengthened. Equity improved from negative EUR 2.5 million in 2021 to EUR 2.1 million at the end of 2025, while net debt declined from EUR 5.7 million in 2021 to net cash of EUR 0.3 million. BDO valued the German subsidiary at approximately EUR 20.5 million, offsetting a reduction in the carrying value of the Pakistani asset following an extraordinary audit.

Talkpool launched its Vision 2030 strategy, targeting EUR 40 million in revenue, a 14% EBITDA margin, and zero net debt by 2030. Technology and AI trials began in 2025, while further growth initiatives include expanded remote services, a new Swedish defence-sector business, and planned US and Saudi Arabian development.
