A/S SAF Tehnika reported consolidated net revenue of 25.99 million EUR for the fiscal year ended June 30, 2025, representing a decrease of 1.1 million EUR or 4% compared to the previous year. The parent company’s net revenue was 21.92 million EUR, a decline of 1.33 million EUR or 6%. The Group recorded a net loss of 643 thousand EUR, an improvement from the previous year’s loss of 2.37 million EUR. The parent company also reduced its net loss to 552 thousand EUR from 1.34 million EUR. The primary driver of the revenue decline was a 14% drop in the Americas region, which accounts for 55% of total revenue. Sales in this region fell by 2.3 million EUR to 14.17 million EUR due to client caution regarding investment decisions and tariff changes, as well as the deferral of large project realizations to future periods. Conversely, European revenue grew by 17% to 10.0 million EUR, while the Asia, Middle East, and Africa region saw a slight 5% decrease to 1.8 million EUR. Gross profit increased significantly to 9.50 million EUR from 7.53 million EUR, driven by a 16% reduction in cost of goods sold to 16.49 million EUR, largely due to lower procurement and subcontractor service costs. Administrative expenses rose to 3.36 million EUR from 2.91 million EUR, partly due to increased IT service costs and salaries, while selling and marketing expenses decreased slightly to 7.43 million EUR. The Group improved its liquidity position, with cash and cash equivalents rising by 2.6 million EUR to 4.81 million EUR. Inventory levels remained high at 13.99 million EUR due to precautionary stockpiling against supply chain risks, although slow-moving inventory provisions decreased by 1.24 million EUR to 3.86 million EUR. The company maintained a strong balance sheet with no utilization of its 4.95 million EUR credit line with Luminor Bank. Management views the net loss as temporary, noting positive operating cash flow, and continues to invest in R&D, receiving 570 thousand EUR in co-financing for product development projects. The company established a branch in Canada to mitigate supply chain and customs risks associated with trade uncertainties. Despite geopolitical tensions and global electronic component shortages, the Group stabilized its operations and maintained its strategic focus on wireless data transmission and IoT solutions. The Board proposes covering the year’s loss from retained earnings of previous years. No significant events occurred after the reporting period that would materially affect the financial position. The auditor issued an unqualified opinion, confirming the financial statements present a true and fair view in accordance with IFRS.
