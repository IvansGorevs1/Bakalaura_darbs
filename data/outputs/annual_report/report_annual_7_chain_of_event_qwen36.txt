Heimstaden AB reported a strong financial performance for the full year 2025, characterized by improved operational efficiency, significant asset value growth, and successful deleveraging. The company achieved a Net Operating Income of SEK 11,475 million, representing a 2.6% increase from SEK 11,188 million in 2024. This growth was driven by a 4.9% like-for-like rental income increase, supported by indexation and tenant improvements, which offset the negative impact of portfolio sales and foreign exchange effects that kept total rental income flat at SEK 15,916 million. Property expenses decreased by 6.1% to SEK 4,378 million, primarily due to cost control measures, digitalization, and a shrinking portfolio size resulting from the ongoing privatisation program. Consequently, the Net Operating Income margin expanded to 72.1% from 70.3% in the prior year.

The fair value of investment properties increased by 2.9% year-on-year to SEK 324,429 million, reflecting a SEK 9,498 million gain. This appreciation was supported by strong operating fundamentals, accretive capital expenditures, and favorable market conditions in key regions such as Denmark, the Netherlands, and Poland. The average valuation yield remained stable at 3.69%. The company recorded a goodwill impairment loss of SEK 3,707 million, attributed to an increase in weighted average cost of capital affecting the Akelius cash-generating units. Despite this non-cash charge, the profit before tax rose significantly to SEK 16,012 million from SEK 10,403 million in 2024, largely due to the fair value adjustments and realized gains.

A central theme of the 2025 results was the execution of the privatisation program, which facilitated substantial deleveraging. The company sold 2,466 residential units for SEK 10,664 million, achieving a 28.9% premium to book value. Proceeds from these sales, along with portfolio sales, were deployed to reduce outstanding debt. This strategy resulted in a marked improvement in credit metrics. The Net Loan-to-Value ratio decreased to 52.2% from 56.4%, and the Net Debt to Total Assets ratio fell to 48.0% from 51.0%. The Interest Coverage Ratio improved to 2.0 times from 1.9 times, indicating enhanced financial stability and capacity to service debt.

Liability management activities included the issuance of new senior unsecured notes and the refinancing of existing maturities, ensuring a diversified funding profile with an interest rate hedge ratio of 87%. The company also maintained strong liquidity with unutilized credit facilities of SEK 23,449 million. In the governance and legal sphere, Heimstaden received a favorable arbitration award in December 2025, confirming no breach of the Shareholders Agreement with Alecta. Looking ahead, the company expects continued value growth driven by NOI expansion and strategic capital allocation, while maintaining a disciplined approach to risk management and sustainability integration. The Board proposed no dividend payment for 2025, opting to carry forward accumulated profits to strengthen the equity base.
