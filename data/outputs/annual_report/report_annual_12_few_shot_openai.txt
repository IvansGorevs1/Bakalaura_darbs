Konsolidator delivered materially improved financial performance in 2025, combining stronger growth with lower costs and a substantially reduced operating loss. Revenue increased 25% to DKK 25.3m from DKK 20.3m in 2024. Subscription revenue rose to DKK 20.6m from DKK 18.3m, while onboarding and consulting revenue increased 134% to DKK 4.7m, supported by higher onboarding fees introduced for new customers. The company reached 283 active customers, signed 51–52 new customers during the year, and expanded into Portugal, Chile, France, and Malaysia.

Profitability improved significantly. EBITDA loss narrowed to DKK 2.0m from DKK 9.3m, while EBIT loss decreased to DKK 5.1m from DKK 12.1m. The net loss was reduced to DKK 6.6m from DKK 14.9m, partly supported by a DKK 1.0m positive change in deferred tax. Cost reductions were an important contributor: external expenses fell 11% to DKK 6.3m, and staff costs after capitalized wages declined to DKK 21.1m from DKK 22.0m despite a higher average workforce. Outsourcing marketing activities, reduced advertising and consulting expenditure, and the transfer of customer-success activities in Sweden and Norway to Exsitec supported operating efficiency. Other operating income of DKK 2.0m also positively affected EBITDA.

The second half showed an important operating improvement, with EBITDA of DKK 0.5m compared with a DKK 3.6m loss in the corresponding period of 2024. Operating cash flow for the full year improved from an outflow of DKK 9.2m to DKK 1.0m, while net cash flow was slightly positive at DKK 11,000. The company raised DKK 4.8m through two private placements, but cash remained limited at DKK 0.4m at year-end. Debt repayments began in July, and total borrowings were DKK 14.1m. Consolidated equity remained negative at DKK 3.6m, although the parent company reported positive equity of DKK 0.7m.

Recurring-revenue indicators also strengthened. Contracted ARR increased 15% to DKK 24.4m, new sales rose 45% to DKK 4.5m, and the CAC payback period improved from 43 to 23 months. The Rule of 40 score improved from negative 40% to 17%, and CLV/CAC increased from 2.7 to 4.1. However, ARR churn worsened to 12.6% from 10.5%, while net retention remained 94%. The launch of the Konsolidator Suite, including financial data management, analytics, and FP&A capabilities, was the central strategic development. For 2026, management guides for CARR and revenue of DKK 27–29m and positive EBITDA of DKK 1–2m, while targeting churn of 6–9%.
