Eleving Group S.A. delivered its strongest financial performance to date in 2025, recording a record-high revenue of EUR 250.0 million, which represents a 15.5% increase compared to EUR 216.6 million in 2024. The Group achieved an adjusted EBITDA of EUR 101.9 million, up 13.5% from EUR 89.8 million in the prior year. Net profit from continuing operations remained stable at EUR 29.2 million, compared to EUR 28.8 million in 2024, while net profit before foreign exchange effects and discontinued operations increased by 25.6% to EUR 40.8 million. The net loan portfolio grew significantly by 20.2% to EUR 446.3 million, driven by strong demand across the Group’s diversified product lines.

Revenue growth was primarily fueled by an expansion in consumer lending and flexible vehicle financing. Consumer lending products contributed EUR 114.8 million, reflecting an 18.0% increase year-on-year, while flexible vehicle financing products rose by 25.9% to EUR 60.3 million. In contrast, traditional vehicle financing experienced a 6.0% decline to EUR 67.0 million. A new product line, device financing, launched in 2025, contributed EUR 7.9 million to revenue. The Group’s active customer base exceeded 620,000, and total loan issuances reached a record EUR 458.0 million, a 24.3% increase from 2024. This growth was supported by the launch of smartphone financing in Kenya and Uganda and the introduction of installment loans in several European markets, including Latvia, Estonia, and Romania.

The financial results were influenced by significant operational and capital market developments. The Group successfully executed two major bond issuances, including a EUR 275 million refinancing and new issuance, which was the largest in the company’s history. This strengthened the funding base and supported Fitch Ratings’ decision to upgrade the Group’s outlook from stable to positive, while affirming its B credit rating. Operational efficiency initiatives, such as the pilot of AI voice agents in call centers, were introduced to manage rising cost pressures. Additionally, the Group expanded its geographical footprint by entering Tanzania with motorcycle financing and optimizing its portfolio in Moldova by selling a vehicle finance unit to focus on personal loans.

Despite the revenue growth, the Group faced higher impairment expenses, which increased to EUR 54.2 million from EUR 42.1 million in 2024. This rise was largely due to the formal derecognition of long-overdue balances deemed unrecoverable, resulting in a significant increase in written-off loans to EUR 49.5 million from EUR 23.3 million in the previous year. The Group maintained a disciplined approach to risk management, with the capitalization ratio standing at 23.7% and net leverage at 3.8. Total dividends paid to shareholders amounted to EUR 19.65 million, representing a 10% cash yield based on the IPO price. The Group concluded the year with a solid foundation for continued double-digit growth in 2026, supported by a diversified portfolio, strong liquidity, and expanded market presence.
