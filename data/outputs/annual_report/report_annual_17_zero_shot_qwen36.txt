Novaturas AB reported a significant deterioration in financial performance for the year ended 31 December 2024, driven by intense price competition and an oversupply of travel services in the Baltic States. Consolidated revenues decreased by 3.6% to EUR 200.9 million from EUR 208.3 million in 2023. The number of passengers served also declined by 7.6% to 239,500 from 259,300 in the prior year. Gross profit fell sharply by 20.9% to EUR 20.7 million, reflecting a compression in gross profit margins from 12.6% to 10.3%. Operating expenses increased by 25% to EUR 27.5 million, a rise primarily attributable to a one-off, non-cash goodwill impairment of EUR 5.7 million related to the Estonian cash-generating unit. Excluding this impairment, operating expenses were only 1% lower than in 2023.

The Group recorded an EBITDA loss of EUR 6.1 million in 2024, compared to an EBITDA profit of EUR 5.2 million in 2023. Adjusted EBITDA, which excludes the goodwill impairment, also turned negative, amounting to EUR -0.4 million against a positive EUR 5.2 million in the previous year. Consequently, the Group incurred a net loss of EUR 7.6 million, reversing a net profit of EUR 3.4 million in 2023. Earnings per share dropped to EUR -0.97 from EUR 0.43. The poor first-half performance was partially offset by a material improvement in the fourth quarter, where adjusted EBITDA reached EUR 2.3 million. This recovery was supported by a higher load factor of 98.9% and a strategic reduction in last-minute trips, which typically depress profitability.

The competitive environment, particularly in Turkey and Greek island destinations, forced the Group to optimize its travel program and adjust offerings to maintain customer satisfaction while managing costs. Despite the annual loss, the company noted growth in trips to Spain and long-haul destinations, with passenger numbers to Spain increasing by 149% compared to 2022. The Group’s liquidity position faced pressure, with total liquidity ratios declining to 80.0% from 63.0% in 2023, and the debt-to-equity ratio rising significantly to 121.1% from 58.0%. As of the reporting date, the Group did not comply with all financial covenants under its financing agreements, although waivers were subsequently obtained from key lenders.

Looking ahead, the Group announced strategic developments to strengthen its market position. In early 2025, Novaturas signed an agreement with Turkish investor Neset Kockar to acquire a 23.2% stake, with a final stage expected to bring his holding to 33.19%. This investment is anticipated to provide synergies, new growth paths, and enhanced liquidity. The Group also secured additional financing, including a EUR 2.5 million credit line and loans from the new investor, to support its operational recovery and strategic initiatives for 2025.
