AS Tallink Grupp’s financial performance weakened in 2025 amid subdued consumer and business confidence, economic challenges in its core markets and heightened geopolitical tensions. Revenue decreased by 2.6% to EUR 765.3 million from EUR 785.8 million in 2024. Core route revenue declined by EUR 13.7 million to EUR 625.2 million, while route operations’ segment result fell to EUR 70.5 million from EUR 81.5 million. Restaurant and shop sales remained the largest revenue source at EUR 366.4 million, although they declined from EUR 376.0 million. Ticket sales decreased slightly to EUR 222.7 million, while cargo transport revenue fell to EUR 77.9 million and charter income to EUR 54.9 million.

Passenger volumes declined modestly by 0.9% to 5.5 million, but cargo units fell significantly by 19.2% to 245,004 and passenger vehicles decreased by 2.2% to 760,473. Passenger numbers increased 1.8% on the Estonia–Finland route but declined on the Finland–Sweden and Estonia–Sweden routes. The Estonia–Finland segment generated EUR 314.5 million of revenue and remained the Group’s most profitable route, although its result fell by EUR 10.9 million to EUR 70.4 million. The Finland–Sweden result declined to EUR 1.3 million, partly reflecting 68 days of vessel maintenance, while Estonia–Sweden remained loss-making with a segment loss of EUR 1.2 million.

Profitability deteriorated substantially. Gross profit decreased to EUR 143.2 million from EUR 153.6 million, EBITDA fell 25.7% to EUR 130.1 million, and EBIT declined to EUR 50.3 million from EUR 77.4 million. Net profit dropped 57.1% to EUR 17.3 million, reducing earnings per share to EUR 0.023 from EUR 0.054. Personnel expenses increased 4.3% to EUR 201.0 million, while fuel and emission costs were broadly stable at EUR 96.5 million. Lower depreciation, resulting mainly from extending the estimated useful lives of most cruise and passenger vessels to 45 years, reduced costs by EUR 18.0 million and partly offset weaker operating performance. Net finance costs also decreased to EUR 21.4 million from EUR 28.3 million.

Cash flow from operations remained positive at EUR 127.1 million, although below EUR 153.4 million in 2024. Proceeds from selling three vessels contributed to EUR 46.1 million of net investing cash flow. Debt reduction continued, with interest-bearing liabilities falling 19.9% to EUR 445.9 million and net debt declining to EUR 432.4 million. However, net debt to EBITDA increased to 3.3 because EBITDA declined. Liquidity at year-end was EUR 110.4 million, comprising EUR 13.5 million in cash and EUR 96.9 million of unused credit facilities. Equity amounted to EUR 750.1 million, and the equity ratio improved to 56.4%. The Group paid a EUR 0.06-per-share dividend totalling EUR 44.6 million and plans to propose the same dividend for 2026. Overall, 2025 reflected lower demand and weaker profitability, alongside active fleet rationalisation, debt reduction and continued cost-efficiency measures.
