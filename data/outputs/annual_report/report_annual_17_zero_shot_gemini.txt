During the financial year ended 31 December 2024, the Novaturas Group recorded revenue from contracts with customers of EUR 200.9 million, representing a 3.6% decrease compared to EUR 208.3 million in 2023. The total number of travelers served across Lithuania, Latvia, and Estonia was 239 thousand, compared to 259 thousand in the previous period. Gross profit decreased by 20.9% to EUR 20.7 million, down from EUR 26.2 million in 2023. The gross profit margin was 10.3%, compared to 12.6% in the prior year. Operating profit was negative at EUR 6.6 million, contrasting with an operating profit of EUR 4.3 million in 2023. EBITDA was negative at EUR 6.1 million, compared to positive EUR 5.2 million in 2023. Adjusted EBITDA stood at EUR -449 thousand, compared to positive EUR 5.2 million in 2023. The Group incurred a net loss of EUR 7.6 million, compared to a net profit of EUR 3.4 million in 2023. The net loss for 2024 includes a one-off, non-cash goodwill impairment of EUR 5.7 million related to the Estonian cash-generating unit. Excluding this impairment, the Group’s net loss was EUR 1.9 million. Basic earnings per share were EUR -0.98, compared to EUR 0.43 in 2023. 

The overall financial results were impacted by an increased competitive environment and travel oversupply in the Baltic States, which triggered intense price competition, particularly in Turkey and the Greek islands. Despite a weaker performance in the first half of the year, when adjusted EBITDA reached EUR -1.6 million, operating results showed material improvement starting in September 2024, with adjusted EBITDA for the September to December period reaching EUR 2.3 million. This recovery was driven by travel program optimization and lower participation in last-minute sales, leading to a high load factor of 98.9% in the fourth quarter compared to 96.7% in the fourth quarter of 2023. 

Corporate developments during and after the reporting period included strategic changes in shareholding. In April 2025, the Group announced the implementation of the first stage of a transaction with Turkish tourism investor Neset Kockar, who acquired a 23.2% stake in the company. The final stage of the transaction is expected by autumn 2025, after which the investor will hold 33.19% of the company's shares. Additionally, the company secured credit facilities and loans, including a EUR 2.5 million credit line with SME Bank in January 2025 and loans totaling EUR 2.0 million from Neset Kockar in 2025 with maturity in June 2026.
