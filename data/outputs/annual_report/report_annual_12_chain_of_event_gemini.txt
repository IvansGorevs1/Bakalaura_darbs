Konsolidator reported total revenue of DKK 25.3 million for the 2025 financial year, representing a 25% increase compared to DKK 20.3 million in 2024. Subscription fees rose by 13% to DKK 20.6 million from DKK 18.3 million, while onboarding and consulting fees increased by 134% to DKK 4.7 million from DKK 2.0 million. Contracted Annual Recurring Revenue grew by 15% year-over-year to reach DKK 24.4 million at year-end, exceeding the guidance range of DKK 23 million to DKK 24 million. 

Profitability metrics showed significant improvements during the reporting period. The EBITDA loss for 2025 narrowed by 77% to DKK 2.0 million, compared to an EBITDA loss of DKK 9.3 million in 2024. The net loss for the year was reduced to DKK 6.6 million from DKK 14.9 million in the previous year. Operating cash flow improved substantially, registering a negative DKK 1.0 million compared to a negative DKK 9.2 million in 2024. Total assets amounted to DKK 25.7 million at year-end, while consolidated equity stood at a negative DKK 3.6 million, compared to a negative DKK 2.4 million at the end of 2024, whereas the parent company Konsolidator A/S reported a positive equity of DKK 0.7 million. 

SaaS metrics reflected positive operational momentum. The Rule of 40 score improved by 57 percentage points to 17% at year-end 2025, compared to -40% in 2024. The Customer Acquisition Cost payback period shortened to 23 months from 43 months, and the Customer Lifetime Value to CAC ratio strengthened to 4.1 from 3.1. Net retention remained unchanged at 94%, while the ARR churn rate increased slightly to 12.6% from 10.5%. 

The financial performance was influenced by several strategic and operational developments. Throughout 2025, Konsolidator advanced its Resilient Growth strategy by expanding from a single consolidation tool into the broader Konsolidator Suite, which now incorporates Financial Planning and Analysis capabilities, a financial data management solution built on Microsoft Fabric, and an analytics platform named The Hub. The company also expanded its partner ecosystem, notably entering a strategic partnership with Exsitec AB in Sweden to handle sales, onboarding, and customer success, which contributed to partner-driven sales reaching 38% of new customers compared to 22% in 2024. Furthermore, cost optimizations initiated in 2024, including the restructuring and outsourcing of marketing activities, took full effect in 2025 and lowered external expenses by 11% to DKK 6.3 million. Additional liquidity of DKK 4.8 million in net proceeds was secured through two private placements of shares during the year. 

For the 2026 financial year, Konsolidator has issued financial guidance targeting Contracted Annual Recurring Revenue of DKK 27 million to DKK 29 million, revenue of DKK 27 million to DKK 29 million, and a positive EBITDA of DKK 1 million to DKK 2 million, assuming stable cost developments and a decline in churn to between 6% and 9%.
