The Infortar Group reported a significant expansion in revenue and profitability during the 2025 financial year, driven primarily by the full-year consolidation of Tallink Grupp and continued growth in the energy segment. Total revenue increased by 33.9% to EUR 1,837 million, compared to EUR 1,372 million in the previous year. This substantial growth was supported by strong performance in the maritime transport and energy sectors, which offset seasonal fluctuations and broader macroeconomic uncertainties. EBITDA rose by 56.9% to EUR 228 million, reflecting an improved EBITDA margin of 12.4% from 10.6% in 2024. The increase in EBITDA is attributable to the inclusion of Tallink’s full-year results, as Tallink was consolidated from August 2024 in the prior period, as well as an approximately EUR 12 million improvement in Elenger’s EBITDA and contributions from newly acquired agricultural entities.

Despite the top-line growth, net profit attributable to the owners of the parent company decreased significantly to EUR 70.6 million from EUR 191.3 million in 2024. This decline was mainly due to one-off effects related to the initial consolidation of Tallink Grupp in 2024, which included a gain from bargain purchase and remeasurement of previously held interests that are not recurring in 2025. Operating profit increased by 41.5% to EUR 109 million, indicating strong underlying operational performance. The group maintained a robust financial position with total assets of EUR 2,597 million and equity growing to EUR 1,175 million. The equity ratio improved to 45% from 43%, and net debt decreased to EUR 852 million from EUR 1,056 million, resulting in a lower net debt to EBITDA ratio of 3.7 compared to 4.0 in the previous year.

Strategic developments in 2025 included the completion of the acquisition of Estonia Farmid OÜ, enhancing the group’s presence in agriculture and biomethane production. In the energy segment, Elenger expanded its renewable portfolio with new solar and battery storage facilities in Latvia and continued to strengthen its gas distribution network in Poland. The maritime segment saw Tallink carry 5.5 million passengers, a slight decrease of 0.9% year-on-year, while managing seasonal risks through vessel chartering activities. The group also initiated a share buyback program in October 2025 and maintained its dividend policy, proposing a dividend of EUR 3 per share for 2025, totaling EUR 62 million. Overall, the period was characterized by successful integration of major acquisitions, diversified revenue streams, and improved financial resilience.
