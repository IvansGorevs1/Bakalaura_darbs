Eleving Group delivered its strongest financial performance to date in 2025, characterized by significant revenue growth, expanded portfolio size, and improved profitability metrics. The company reported total revenue of EUR 250.0 million, marking a 15.5% increase compared to the previous year. This growth was driven by a diversified product mix, with consumer lending products contributing EUR 114.8 million (up 18.0%), flexible vehicle financing contributing EUR 60.3 million (up 25.9%), and device financing adding EUR 7.9 million as a new revenue stream in 2025. Traditional vehicle financing experienced a slight decline of 6.0% to EUR 67.0 million. The net loan portfolio grew by 20.2% to EUR 446.3 million, supported by record-high loan issuances totaling EUR 458.0 million.

Profitability improved substantially, with adjusted EBITDA reaching a record EUR 101.9 million, an increase of 13.5% year-on-year. Net profit from continuing operations stood at EUR 29.2 million, while net profit before foreign exchange effects and discontinued operations rose by 25.6% to EUR 40.8 million. The group maintained disciplined credit quality, with impairment expenses remaining proportionate to revenue despite the portfolio expansion. Total net profit for the period was EUR 29.2 million, consistent with the prior year when excluding foreign exchange fluctuations.

Strategic developments played a crucial role in the period’s performance. The Group successfully executed two major bond issuances, including a EUR 275 million refinancing transaction in October, which was the largest in the company’s history. This strengthened the capital base and led Fitch Ratings to upgrade the Group’s outlook from stable to positive while affirming its B credit rating. Geographically, the Group expanded into Tanzania and launched smartphone financing in Kenya and Uganda, contributing to strong demand in African markets. In Europe, the introduction of installment loans in several markets further diversified the product offering.

Operational efficiency remained a priority, with the Group reducing its cost-to-income ratio and piloting AI voice agents in call centers to enhance service capacity. The company also maintained a strong liquidity position, with cash and cash equivalents increasing to EUR 39.1 million. Dividends totaling EUR 19.65 million were distributed to shareholders, representing a 10% cash yield based on the IPO price. Looking ahead, the Group is positioned for continued double-digit growth, focusing on portfolio expansion, operational excellence, and further integration of technology to support sustainable profitability.
