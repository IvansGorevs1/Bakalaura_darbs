During the financial year ended December 31, 2024, AS LHV Group achieved the highest business volumes and net profit in its history, despite challenging economic conditions and declining interest rates in the second half of the year. The group reported a net profit of EUR 150.3 million, representing a 7% increase compared to EUR 140.9 million in the previous year. Total net income grew by 11% year-on-year to reach EUR 338.3 million, driven by an 8% increase in net interest income to EUR 273.3 million and a 24% increase in net fee and commission income to EUR 60.3 million. Operating expenses increased by 14% to EUR 146.9 million, largely due to an increased number of employees, resulting in a cost-to-income ratio of 43.4%. Return on equity stood at 24.5%, and return on assets was 1.9%. Expected credit losses on financial instruments measured at amortised cost amounted to EUR 16.3 million, compared to EUR 11.5 million in 2023. Total consolidated assets expanded by 23% year-on-year to reach EUR 8.7 billion. The growth in the balance sheet was supported by a 21% increase in total customer deposits, which totaled EUR 6.9 billion at year-end, and a 28% expansion in the net loan portfolio, which increased by EUR 990 million to EUR 4.6 billion. Growth in the loan portfolio was broad-based, with Estonian corporate loans increasing by 21% to EUR 2.3 billion, retail loans increasing by 21% to EUR 1.9 billion, and UK SME loans growing by 338% to EUR 348 million. Asset quality remained strong overall, supported by proactive risk management. Capital adequacy ratios remained robust and well above regulatory requirements, with total capital adequacy at 20.68%, Tier 1 capital ratio at 17.96%, and Common Equity Tier 1 capital ratio at 16.89%. The leverage ratio stood at 6.71%, while the Liquidity Coverage Ratio and Net Stable Funding Ratio were 187.5% and 154.4%, respectively. The company successfully executed capital-related transactions during the year, including issuing EUR 20 million of Tier 2 bonds and EUR 300 million of MREL-eligible bonds while redeeming EUR 20 million of Additional Tier 1 bonds. Business developments were marked by strong customer acquisition, with the number of LHV Pank customers increasing by 9% to 456,000, expansion of lending and payment services in the UK following a full year of operations under a banking license, and steady performance across asset management and non-life insurance subsidiaries. Looking forward, LHV Group enters 2025 in a strong position, anticipating short-term profit moderation due to declining benchmark interest rates, which will be partially offset by ongoing business volume growth and enhanced operational efficiencies.
