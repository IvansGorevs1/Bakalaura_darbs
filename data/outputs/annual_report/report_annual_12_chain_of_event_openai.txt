Konsolidator delivered materially improved financial performance in 2025, although the Group remained loss-making and financially constrained. Revenue rose 25% to DKK 25.3m from DKK 20.3m in 2024, driven by subscription revenue increasing from DKK 18.3m to DKK 20.6m and onboarding and consulting fees increasing 134% to DKK 4.7m. The higher onboarding revenue followed price increases for new customers introduced in the first quarter. The company ended the year with 283 customers, 52 new customers, and Contracted Annual Recurring Revenue (CARR) of DKK 24.4m, up 15% from DKK 21.3m and above its guidance of DKK 23–24m. Average CARR per customer increased from TDKK 70 to TDKK 87.

Profitability improved significantly. The EBITDA loss narrowed to DKK 2.0m from DKK 9.3m, while the net loss declined to DKK 6.6m from DKK 14.9m. The improvement reflected stronger revenue, lower marketing and external costs, and the impact of outsourcing marketing activities, which management states reduced the EBIT loss by DKK 3m. Other operating income also increased to DKK 2.0m from DKK 0.7m, including income associated with a commercial partner agreement. In the second half, EBITDA turned positive at DKK 0.5m, compared with a DKK 3.6m loss in H2 2024, and operating cash flow improved to positive DKK 0.1m from negative DKK 3.6m.

SaaS efficiency indicators improved overall. The Rule of 40 score increased to 17% from negative 40%, reflecting 25% revenue growth and an EBITDA margin of negative 8%. CAC payback improved to 23 months from 43 months, with customer acquisition costs down 32%, primarily because of lower marketing expenditure. CLV/CAC rose to 4.1 from 2.7. However, ARR churn worsened to 12.6% from 10.5%, while net retention remained 94%. The report attributes increased churn among established customers partly to growing demand for FP&A capabilities and ERP systems meeting the needs of smaller groups.

Strategically, Konsolidator transitioned from a single consolidation product to the broader Konsolidator Suite, launching a financial data management solution and developing an FP&A tool, which attracted its first customers before launch. Partner-led sales accounted for 38% of new customers versus 22% in 2024, supporting the company’s scalable growth model. The Iberian subsidiary expanded to 30 customers and became the third-largest market.

Despite improved operations, consolidated equity remained negative at DKK 3.6m, compared with negative DKK 2.4m, and cash was only DKK 0.4m at year-end. Two private placements raised DKK 4.8m, while loan repayments began during the year. For 2026, management guides to CARR and revenue of DKK 27–29m and positive EBITDA of DKK 1–2m, with expected churn of 6–9%.
