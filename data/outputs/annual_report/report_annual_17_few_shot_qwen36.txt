Novaturas AB reported a significant deterioration in financial performance for the year ended 31 December 2024, transitioning from a profit in the prior year to a substantial net loss. Revenue from contracts with customers decreased by 3.6% to EUR 200.9 million, down from EUR 208.3 million in 2023. This decline was driven by a 7.6% reduction in the number of passengers served, falling from 259,300 to 239,500. The Group operated in a highly competitive environment characterized by an oversupply of travel in the Baltic States, which intensified price competition, particularly for destinations in Turkey and Greece. Consequently, gross profit fell by 20.9% to EUR 20.7 million, and the gross profit margin contracted from 12.6% to 10.3%.

Operating expenses increased by 25% to EUR 27.5 million compared to the previous year. This rise was primarily attributable to a one-off, non-cash goodwill impairment loss of EUR 5.7 million related to the Estonian cash-generating unit. Excluding this impairment, operating expenses were effectively flat, showing only a 1% decrease to EUR 21.8 million. As a result, the Group recorded an operating loss (EBIT) of EUR 6.7 million, compared to an operating profit of EUR 4.9 million in 2023. EBITDA turned negative, amounting to EUR 6.1 million, a stark reversal from the positive EUR 5.2 million reported in the prior year. Adjusted EBITDA, which excludes the goodwill impairment, was also negative at EUR 449 thousand, compared to a positive EUR 5.2 million in 2023.

The Group incurred a net loss of EUR 7.6 million for 2024, resulting in earnings per share of EUR (0.97), compared to a net profit of EUR 3.4 million and earnings per share of EUR 0.43 in 2023. The financial results were negatively impacted by higher finance expenses, which totaled EUR 2.6 million, up from EUR 1.7 million in 2023. The Group did not comply with all financial and non-financial covenants under its financing agreements as of 31 December 2024. However, waivers were obtained from key lenders, including “Pagalbos verslui fondas” and Luminor Bank AS, to maintain compliance.

Despite the challenging full-year results, the Group demonstrated operational improvement in the second half of the year. Adjusted EBITDA was positive in the fourth quarter, driven by a more efficient travel program, a higher load factor of 98.9%, and a significant decrease in last-minute trips. In response to the competitive landscape, Novaturas optimized its destination portfolio, increasing sales to long-haul destinations such as Spain and exotic locations in Asia and Africa. Looking forward, the Group secured a strategic investment from Turkish businessman Neset Kockar, who acquired 23.2% of shares in April 2025, with plans to increase his stake to 33.19% by autumn. This investment is expected to provide synergies, additional liquidity through new loans, and support for future growth and operational optimization.
