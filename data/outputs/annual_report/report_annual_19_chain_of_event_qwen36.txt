Talkpool AG reported a significant financial turnaround for the year ended December 31, 2025, marking the end of a multi-year restructuring phase and the beginning of organic growth. The company achieved net sales of EUR 17.8 million, representing a 12.5 percent increase from EUR 15.8 million in 2024. This revenue growth was driven by strong performance in the Americas, which remained the largest market, and stabilization in Pakistan, the group’s main production base. Despite currency headwinds from a weakening US dollar and Pakistani Rupee in the first half of the year, the company delivered double-digit growth, the first since its consolidation phase began.

Profitability metrics showed mixed but generally positive trends. EBITDA increased slightly to EUR 1.8 million from EUR 1.7 million in the prior year, resulting in an EBITDA margin of 10.3 percent, a slight decline from 10.8 percent in 2024. Similarly, EBIT rose to EUR 1.6 million from EUR 1.5 million, with an EBIT margin of 9.1 percent compared to 9.7 percent previously. However, net earnings after tax improved substantially, rising 42 percent to EUR 0.9 million from EUR 0.6 million, leading to an EAT margin of 4.8 percent versus 3.8 percent in the previous period. The improvement in net earnings occurred despite management noting that initial investments in future growth created temporary pressure on margins. The company prioritizes recurring engagements to generate predictable cash flows, a strategy that supported this financial resilience.

Several key business events influenced these results. Talkpool ended its "reduce to the MAX" cost-cutting focus and launched the Vision 2030 Strategic Growth Plan in April, targeting EUR 40 million in revenues and a 14 percent EBITDA margin by 2030. The company shifted its geographic focus toward developed markets, particularly Western Europe, while maintaining a strong presence in the Americas. In Germany, Talkpool continued to support Deutsche Telekom’s fiber rollout, although a shift in the operator’s priorities led to a temporary flattening of planning assignment volumes. In Pakistan, the company renegotiated commercial terms with major customers in the second half of the year, resulting in improved gross profitability. Additionally, Talkpool supported Digicel in restoring communications infrastructure in Jamaica following Hurricane Melissa, highlighting the strength of its long-term client relationships.

The balance sheet strengthened significantly during the period. Equity improved by EUR 4.6 million to EUR 2.1 million, moving from a negative position in 2021. Net cash position also turned positive, reaching EUR 0.3 million by year-end, an improvement of EUR 6.0 million from 2021. This financial stability allowed the company to receive an unqualified audit opinion regarding going concern, a major milestone. Management expects near-term pressure on cash flow and margins due to strategic investments in technology and personnel, particularly in AI-powered remote services and the US market entry. However, organic growth accelerated throughout the year, exceeding 20 percent by year-end, signaling a robust foundation for future expansion. The company is now positioned to leverage its low-cost delivery model from Pakistan and emerging AI solutions to capture value in the growing telecom services market.
