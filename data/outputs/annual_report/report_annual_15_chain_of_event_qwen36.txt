AS Merko Ehitus reported consolidated revenue of EUR 310.9 million for the year ended 31 December 2025, a significant decline of 42.3 percent compared to EUR 539.0 million in 2024. This decrease was primarily driven by the completion of several large construction contracts in Lithuania and Estonia that had generated substantial revenue in the prior year, combined with a slower pace of signing new major contracts in the first half of 2025. The construction services segment, which accounted for 76.4 percent of total revenue, saw its sales drop by 49.9 percent to EUR 237.6 million. Conversely, the real estate development segment demonstrated resilience, with revenue increasing by 13.8 percent to EUR 73.4 million, supported by the sale of 358 apartments.

The group’s operating profit decreased to EUR 34.3 million from EUR 72.5 million in 2024, resulting in an operating profit margin of 11.0 percent, down from 13.4 percent. The net profit attributable to equity holders of the parent was EUR 39.9 million, compared to EUR 64.7 million in the previous year. Despite the lower absolute profit figures, the net margin improved slightly to 12.8 percent from 12.0 percent. The decline in profitability was largely attributed to the expiration of highly profitable contracts highlighted in 2024 and a return to more typical sector margins in a competitive environment. However, the group maintained strong financial discipline, with gross profit margin stabilizing at 18.0 percent.

A key positive development for the reporting period was the contribution from joint ventures, particularly the Connecto network companies. The profit from joint ventures amounted to EUR 10.4 million, with the investment in Connecto accounting for approximately one quarter of the consolidated net profit. This strategic investment proved valuable amidst the turbulence in the core construction market. The secured order book grew substantially to EUR 466.9 million from EUR 340.6 million at the end of 2024, indicating a strong pipeline for future revenue. This growth was fueled by new major contracts signed in the second half of 2025 and early 2026, including significant defense infrastructure projects in Lithuania.

The group’s financial position remained robust, with equity increasing to EUR 260.6 million and the equity ratio rising to 62.8 percent from 56.9 percent. The debt ratio remained low at 8.0 percent. Cash and cash equivalents decreased to EUR 41.4 million from EUR 91.9 million, primarily due to dividend payments of EUR 33.6 million and increased investments in inventories related to residential development projects. The management proposed a dividend of EUR 1.25 per share for 2025, maintaining a focus on shareholder returns while preserving capital for future growth. The outlook for 2026 remains positive, with expectations for stable housing markets and continued demand for defense and infrastructure construction.
