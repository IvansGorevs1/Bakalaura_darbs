OODA AI AB (publ) experienced a breakthrough financial year in 2025, successfully shifting its strategic focus from corporate structure and stabilization to commercial scaling and operational growth within artificial intelligence and software-as-a-service solutions. 

The Group reported a sharp increase in revenue for the financial year 2025, with total revenue rising to SEK 32,058 thousand compared with SEK 1,061 thousand in 2024. Net sales similarly grew to SEK 31,880 thousand from SEK 1,056 thousand in the previous period. This significant sales expansion was primarily driven by the invoicing of major commercial transactions executed according to plan, notably including substantial agreements with Win.Rocks AG and HMM Deutschland within the Swiss and European markets. 

Profitability indicators showed a mixed performance driven by strong operational progress offset by heavy non-operating write-downs. Operating profit for the Group improved to negative SEK 29,270 thousand compared with negative SEK 51,781 thousand in 2024. Furthermore, the Group generated a positive EBITDA of SEK 2,957 thousand, marking a strong recovery from an EBITDA of negative SEK 28,229 thousand the previous year. When adjusted for approximately SEK 10,238 thousand in one-off liquidation costs associated with older corporate structures, the adjusted EBITDA reached approximately SEK 13,195 thousand, representing an adjusted EBITDA margin of approximately 32 percent of revenue. 

Despite operational improvements, the Group’s bottom line was heavily burdened by financial items and asset impairments resulting from a strategic review and streamlining of financial holdings and associated companies. Results from financial items deteriorated to negative SEK 95,853 thousand compared with negative SEK 29,900 thousand in 2024, largely due to SEK 63,243 thousand in impairments from associated companies and SEK 32,227 thousand in impairments from other fixed-asset securities and receivables. Consequently, the Group reported a net loss for the year of SEK 125,799 thousand, compared with a net loss of SEK 83,355 thousand in the preceding year. Earnings per share worsened to SEK -35.29 from SEK -29.00 in 2024. 

The balance sheet underwent substantial restructuring during 2025. Total assets for the Group decreased to SEK 43,640 thousand at the end of the year from SEK 145,033 thousand at the end of 2024, primarily reflecting impairments and revaluations of financial fixed assets. Financial fixed assets dropped sharply from SEK 96,036 thousand to SEK 8,387 thousand. Total equity declined from SEK 119,370 thousand to SEK 23,859 thousand at year-end, yielding an equity ratio of 55 percent compared with 82 percent previously. The number of shares increased from 3,385,254 to 3,731,465 through capital acquisitions and rights issues, which contributed SEK 44,574 thousand positively to financing activities. Cash flow from operating activities remained negative at SEK 16,662 thousand, an improvement from negative SEK 29,680 thousand in 2024, supported notably by a positive operational cash flow of SEK 5,098 thousand during the second half of the year. Cash and cash equivalents closed at SEK 3,335 thousand, down from SEK 25,108 thousand at the close of 2024. 

Overall, the 2025 financial period was characterized by a successful commercial breakthrough and robust top-line growth in core AI operations, alongside decisive structural cleanups, debt reduction, and portfolio streamlining that heavily impacted book equity but positioned the company for scalable growth entering 2026.
