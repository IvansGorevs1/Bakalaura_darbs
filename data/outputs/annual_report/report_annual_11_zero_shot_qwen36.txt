JLT Mobile Computers reported a challenging financial year in 2025, marked by significant headwinds from currency fluctuations and geopolitical uncertainty, yet demonstrating operational resilience and strategic progress. The company achieved net sales of SEK 131.5 million, representing an 11 percent increase from the previous year’s SEK 118.4 million. This revenue growth was supported by a strong commercial performance, with order intake rising 37 percent to SEK 141.6 million from SEK 103.0 million. The increase in order intake was notably driven by a major deal worth SEK 22 million with an American food producer. Despite the top-line growth, the company reported a net loss after tax of SEK -2.5 million, a substantial improvement from the SEK -7.7 million loss recorded in 2024.

The deterioration in profitability was primarily attributable to a sharp decline in gross margin, which fell to 38.3 percent from 45.8 percent in the prior year. This 7.5 percentage point decrease was a temporary effect caused by the US dollar losing almost 20 percent against the Swedish krona during the year. Since 74 percent of sales were invoiced in dollars, the weaker currency at the time of invoicing compared to quotation significantly compressed margins. Additionally, inventory revaluation based on current dollar purchase rates further impacted gross profit. Operating expenses decreased by SEK 6.3 million compared to the previous year, totaling SEK 52.4 million, due to structural cost-saving measures. These included the coordination of software development with product development and the discontinuation of the subsidiary JLT Software Solutions AB, which reduced overhead costs by SEK 5 million and lowered depreciation on development projects. Consequently, the operating loss narrowed to SEK -1.9 million from SEK -9.7 million, and EBITDA improved to SEK -1.7 million from SEK -2.1 million.

Market dynamics played a crucial role in the company’s performance. Demand in the largest segment, warehousing and logistics, remained subdued due to a wait-and-see approach by end customers driven by geopolitical uncertainty. To mitigate this risk, JLT strategically broadened its market exposure, focusing on ports, construction, and mining equipment, where positive trends were emerging. The company also launched innovative products, including the industry’s first rugged on-board computer with a 15-inch Full HD display and an AI-powered screen blanking solution, strengthening its premium position. Financially, the company improved its cash flow to SEK +3.7 million from SEK +6.8 million, aided by a deliberate reduction in inventories by SEK 12.7 million to SEK 25.5 million. This inventory drawdown enhanced capital efficiency and liquidity, with cash and cash equivalents rising to SEK 16.9 million. The company entered 2026 with a stronger cost base, a diversified product portfolio, and reduced dependence on a single market segment, positioning itself for sustainable growth as market conditions stabilize.
