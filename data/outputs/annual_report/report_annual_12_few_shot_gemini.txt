During the 2025 financial year, Konsolidator A/S demonstrated improved financial performance and progress in executing its Resilient Growth strategy. The company reported revenue of DKK 25.3m, representing a 25% increase compared with DKK 20.3m in 2024. Subscription fees grew to DKK 20.6m from DKK 18.3m, while onboarding and consulting fees rose significantly to DKK 4.7m from DKK 2.0m, largely driven by initial price adjustments implemented in early 2025. 

Profitability indicators improved substantially during the period. The EBITDA loss for 2025 was reduced to DKK 2.0m, marking a 77% improvement compared with an EBITDA loss of DKK 9.3m in 2024. Furthermore, the second half of 2025 achieved a positive EBITDA of DKK 0.5m. The EBIT loss narrowed to DKK 5.1m from DKK 12.1m in the previous year, and the net loss for the year decreased to DKK 6.6m compared with DKK 14.9m in 2024. The bottom-line results were supported by higher subscription and onboarding revenue, reduced marketing costs following previous restructuring activities, and operating income from a strategic commercial partner agreement. 

Operating cash flows also improved, registering a negative DKK 1.0m in 2025 compared with a negative DKK 9.2m in 2024. This improvement was supported by a smaller EBIT loss, a DKK 2.4m increase in deferred income resulting from customers shifting to annual invoicing cycles, and disciplined working capital management. Net cash flow for the year was positive at DKK 0.1m. Total assets at year-end stood at DKK 25.7m, up from DKK 23.0m in 2024. Consolidated equity remained negative at DKK 3.6m, compared with a negative equity of DKK 2.4m at the end of 2024, primarily due to accumulated losses in the Spanish subsidiary Konsolidator Iberia. However, the parent company, Konsolidator A/S, maintained a positive equity of DKK 0.7m. Additional equity of DKK 4.8m was secured through two private placements during the year. 

Key SaaS metrics reflected positive operational development. Contracted Annual Recurring Revenue increased by 15% year-over-year to DKK 24.4m, exceeding management guidance of DKK 23m to DKK 24m. New sales rose by 45% to DKK 4.5m, and the company added 52 new customers during the year, bringing the total active customer count to 283. Average contract value per customer increased to TDKK 87 from TDKK 70 in 2024. The Customer Acquisition Cost payback period improved significantly to 23 months from 43 months, and the Rule of 40 score strengthened to 17% from -40% in the previous year. The ARR churn rate increased to 12.6% from 10.5%, while net retention remained unchanged at 94%. 

The period was marked by the transition from a single consolidation tool to the broader Konsolidator Suite, which now incorporates financial data management solutions and financial planning and analysis capabilities. Strategic partnerships, notably with Exsitec AB, expanded market reach and supported scalable customer acquisition and onboarding. 

For the 2026 financial year, Konsolidator expects continued growth and profitability, issuing financial guidance of DKK 27m to DKK 29m for Contracted Annual Recurring Revenue, DKK 27m to DKK 29m for revenue, and a positive EBITDA of DKK 1m to DKK 2m.
