During the financial year ended 31 December 2025, AS Tallink Grupp and its subsidiaries generated audited consolidated revenue of EUR 765.3 million, representing a 2.6% decrease compared to EUR 785.8 million in 2024. Revenue from the Group's core route operations declined by EUR 13.7 million to EUR 625.2 million. Onboard and onshore restaurant and shop sales reached EUR 366.4 million, accounting for approximately half of total revenue, while ticket sales amounted to EUR 222.7 million, cargo transport sales to EUR 77.9 million, and vessel charter income to EUR 54.9 million. Geographically, revenue was distributed as EUR 314.5 million on the Estonia-Finland route, EUR 225.5 million on Finland-Sweden routes, EUR 85.3 million on Estonia-Sweden routes, and EUR 145.5 million in the Other segment.

The Group’s gross profit for 2025 decreased to EUR 143.2 million compared to EUR 153.6 million in 2024. EBITDA declined to EUR 130.1 million from EUR 175.2 million, while the audited net profit for the period dropped to EUR 17.3 million, down 57.1% from EUR 40.3 million in 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share stood at EUR 0.023 compared to EUR 0.054 in the previous year. Profitability was influenced by low consumer confidence and geopolitical tensions in core markets, offset by cost efficiency measures, ongoing vessel chartering, and a decline in depreciation expense. Amortisation and depreciation expenses decreased by EUR 18.0 million to EUR 79.8 million, primarily due to the alignment of estimated useful lives of cruise and passenger vessels (excluding shuttle vessels) to 45 years and the sale of three vessels during the year. Net finance costs improved to EUR 21.4 million compared to EUR 28.3 million in 2024.

Operating performance indicators showed mixed results. The Group carried 5.53 million passengers, representing a minor 0.9% decrease year-on-year. However, transported cargo units fell significantly by 19.2% to 245,004 units, and passenger vehicles decreased by 2.2% to 760,473 vehicles. Business operations were conducted with an active fleet of 11 vessels at year-end, including 2 shuttle vessels, 6 passenger cruise ferries, and 3 chartered-out vessels. During the year, the Group completed the sales of three idle vessels: the passenger vessel Star I in April, the cargo vessel Regal Star in August, and the cargo vessel Sailor in October.

The Group maintained a solid liquidity and financial position. Net operating cash flow remained positive at EUR 127.1 million, while net cash flow from investing activities was positive at EUR 46.1 million, supported by EUR 78.8 million in proceeds from the disposal of fixed assets. Total capital expenditures were EUR 33.0 million. Total liquidity buffer, consisting of cash, cash equivalents of EUR 13.5 million, and unused overdraft credit facilities of EUR 96.9 million, stood at EUR 110.4 million as of 31 December 2025. Interest-bearing liabilities decreased by 19.9% to EUR 445.9 million, driven by total loan and lease repayments alongside interest payments amounting to EUR 113.5 million. Net debt decreased to EUR 432.4 million compared to EUR 537.7 million at the end of 2024, resulting in a net debt to EBITDA ratio of 3.3. Total equity stood at EUR 750.1 million, providing a strong equity ratio of 56.4%. Cash outflows during the period included dividend payments of EUR 44.6 million and related income tax of EUR 11.3 million. The Management Board will propose a dividend payment of EUR 0.06 per share for 2026.
