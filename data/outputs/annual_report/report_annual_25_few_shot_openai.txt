WindowMaster International’s financial performance weakened in 2025, although margins at the gross-profit level improved and operating cash generation remained solid. Revenue declined 8.8% to DKK 268.6 million from DKK 294.5 million in 2024. Management attributed the reduction primarily to a slow start to the year, lower sales to a major Nordic customer, weaker performance in Climatic in the Nordic region, and lower-than-expected growth in North America. Revenue was nevertheless in line with the latest guidance of DKK 269–270 million, although DKK 21–41 million below the original budget.

Gross profit decreased to DKK 131.1 million from DKK 137.9 million, but the gross margin increased to 48.8% from 46.9%. The improvement reflected a business mix with less revenue from key accounts and more direct project revenue. EBITDA fell to DKK 28.0 million from DKK 37.3 million, reducing the EBITDA margin to 10.4% from 12.7%. Fixed costs were maintained to support future growth and included DKK 2.5 million of extraordinary costs related to the ransomware attack that affected the Group’s IT infrastructure in May and June. Earnings before tax declined to DKK 2.9 million from DKK 12.2 million, while profit after tax fell sharply to DKK 0.9 million from DKK 10.6 million. The effective tax rate was high at 70%, partly because tax assets and tax-loss carryforwards were not utilized.

Operating cash flow remained broadly stable at DKK 28.5 million, compared with DKK 29.4 million in 2024, supported by improved working capital, particularly lower inventories and work in progress. Investment cash outflows declined to DKK 9.4 million from DKK 17.6 million, despite continued spending on digitalization, software development, process automation, and IT infrastructure. Year-end cash increased to DKK 4.0 million, while net interest-bearing debt rose to DKK 38.7 million from DKK 36.7 million. Financial gearing increased from 1.2 to 1.7 times EBITDA but remained below the target of 2.0. Equity declined to DKK 32.5 million, and the net-cash equity ratio was 23.5%, below the target of more than 25%.

Market activity improved during the second half of 2025 as interest rates stabilized, particularly in non-residential construction. Demand continued to benefit from investment in energy-efficient buildings and environmental regulation in Europe and North America, although project timing, economic uncertainty, geopolitical developments, and supply-chain disruption remained risks. For 2026, management expects organic revenue growth of 7–14% to DKK 290–310 million and EBITDA of DKK 45–55 million, corresponding to an EBITDA margin of approximately 8–10%.
