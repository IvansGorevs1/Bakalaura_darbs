Eleving Group delivered its strongest financial performance to date in 2024, characterized by robust revenue growth, improved profitability, and significant strategic milestones. The Group reported total revenue of EUR 216.6 million for the twelve months ended December 31, 2024, representing a 14.4% increase from EUR 189.3 million in 2023. This growth was driven by strong demand across both vehicle and consumer finance segments, with vehicle finance applications increasing by 80.9% year-over-year. The Group’s net loan portfolio expanded by 16% to reach EUR 371.2 million, reflecting successful organic growth and the integration of Sub-Saharan operations acquired in 2023.

Profitability metrics showed substantial improvement. Total net profit reached EUR 29.6 million, a 20.8% increase from EUR 24.5 million in 2023. Adjusted for a one-off Romanian VAT liability of EUR 2.6 million, the net profit would have been EUR 32.2 million, indicating underlying growth of over 31%. EBITDA rose by 16% to EUR 89.8 million from EUR 77.5 million in the prior year. The Group maintained disciplined risk management, evidenced by a reduction in non-performing loan rates for vehicle finance from 7.5% to 6.1% and for consumer finance from 4.5% to 4.3%. The capitalization ratio improved to 29.3% from 26.1%, and net leverage decreased to 3.3 from 3.7, strengthening the Group’s balance sheet and covenant compliance.

A defining event in 2024 was the successful initial public offering in October, which raised EUR 29.0 million in equity. This was the largest IPO in Nasdaq Riga’s history and significantly strengthened the Group’s capital base. The proceeds were primarily used to repay subordinated bonds and high-cost debt, optimizing the funding structure. The Group also received a credit rating upgrade from Fitch Ratings from B- to B with a stable outlook, citing improved performance and lower leverage.

Operational efficiency and digital transformation contributed to the positive results. The implementation of a new digital self-service platform in European markets enhanced customer engagement and operational efficiency. In Africa, the Group accelerated its sustainability agenda by financing nearly 2,000 electric motorcycles, saving an estimated 1,000 metric tonnes of CO2 and achieving its 2025 environmental target one year ahead of schedule. The Group exited its Belarus and Balkan vehicle finance operations, classifying them as discontinued operations, which generated a net profit from discontinued operations of EUR 0.8 million in 2024. Overall, Eleving Group demonstrated resilience and growth, laying a solid foundation for its strategy to nearly double the business size within the next two years.
