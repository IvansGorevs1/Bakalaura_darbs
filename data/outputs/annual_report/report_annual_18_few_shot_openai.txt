OODA AI reported a substantial commercial expansion in 2025, while the Group remained loss-making because of significant impairments and valuation-related financial charges. Revenue increased to SEK 32,058 thousand from SEK 1,061 thousand, with net sales rising to SEK 31,880 thousand from SEK 1,056 thousand. The increase reflected invoicing from several major transactions and was generated entirely from consulting services, primarily in Switzerland and other international markets.

Underlying operating performance improved considerably. Operating loss narrowed to SEK 29,270 thousand from SEK 51,781 thousand, while EBITDA improved to SEK 2,957 thousand from a negative SEK 28,229 thousand. EBITDA was affected by approximately SEK 10,238 thousand in non-recurring costs related to the liquidation of older company structures; excluding these costs, adjusted EBITDA was approximately SEK 13,195 thousand, corresponding to an adjusted margin of about 32% of revenue. Personnel costs increased to SEK 9,210 thousand from SEK 7,432 thousand, although the Group continued to operate with a low cost base and only four average employees.

Despite the stronger core business, the Group recorded a net loss of SEK 125,799 thousand, compared with SEK 83,355 thousand in 2024. Financial items deteriorated to a loss of SEK 95,853 thousand, mainly due to impairments and results from associated companies. Impairments included SEK 63,243 thousand on associated-company holdings and SEK 32,227 thousand on other securities and receivables. As a result, earnings per share declined to negative SEK 35.29 from negative SEK 29.00.

The company made important commercial and strategic progress during the year. The Win.Rocks AG relationship reached a total contract value of approximately SEK 21 million, followed by a new agreement worth at least SEK 15 million over 24 months in December. Agreements were also established with HMM Deutschland and Sergel Kredittjänster, while partnerships with Allgeier Inovar and TIM Solutions supported broader distribution. OODA AI further developed its Universal AI Platform, decentralized AI infrastructure, MVP and test network, with broader platform commercialization planned for the second quarter of 2026.

The financial position weakened substantially because of portfolio restructuring and impairments. Equity fell to SEK 23,859 thousand from SEK 119,371 thousand, and financial assets declined to SEK 8,387 thousand from SEK 96,036 thousand. Cash flow from operating activities improved to negative SEK 16,662 thousand from negative SEK 29,680 thousand, and was positive by SEK 5,098 thousand in the second half. However, investing activities consumed SEK 49,413 thousand, while rights issues generated SEK 44,574 thousand, leaving cash at SEK 3,335 thousand at year-end. The Board considers continued commercialization, cost control and access to additional capital essential to maintaining operations in 2026.
