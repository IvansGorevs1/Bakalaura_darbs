TKM Grupp AS reported its financial results for the 2025 financial year, operating within a challenging economic environment characterised by low consumer confidence, ongoing economic uncertainty, high price sensitivity, and new domestic tax regulations, most notably the motor vehicle tax introduced in Estonia. During the reporting period, the Group achieved consolidated sales revenue of 919.6 million euros, representing a 2.6% decrease compared to 944.6 million euros in 2024. Profit before tax declined by 31.4% year on year to 24.3 million euros, compared to 35.5 million euros in the previous year. Net profit for 2025 amounted to 17.5 million euros, marking a 36.1% decrease from 27.5 million euros in 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share decreased to 0.43 euros from 0.67 euros in the prior year. Operating profit for the year stood at 34.9 million euros, down from 47.6 million euros in 2024, while EBITDA decreased by 14.7% to 77.4 million euros. The Group’s financial results were primarily impacted by a substantial contraction in the car trade segment, driven by a sharp drop in new passenger car sales in Estonia following the introduction of the motor vehicle tax. Car segment revenue fell by 11.9% to 176.9 million euros, and its net profit dropped by 54.4% to 4.3 million euros. The supermarkets segment demonstrated stability, with revenue increasing by 0.3% to 611.9 million euros, although net profit in this segment decreased by 38.6% to 9.5 million euros due to higher promotional shares and cost pressures. The department stores segment recorded revenue of 103.0 million euros, down 1.1%, and a net loss of 0.5 million euros. The security segment experienced an 8.1% revenue decrease to 20.1 million euros, with net profit close to zero. Conversely, the real estate segment saw external revenue grow by 5.1% to 7.7 million euros, supported by rental income from a newly leased logistics centre, while net profit for the segment reached 6.3 million euros. Across the Group, effective cost control measures and careful inventory management helped maintain stability, with annual growth in other operating expenses limited to 0.2% and labour costs increasing by 3.7% to 116.4 million euros. Net profit was positively influenced by a reduction in borrowing interest costs and lower income tax expenses. Total investments made by the Group during 2025 amounted to 25.5 million euros, directed towards modernising sales environments, expanding showroom facilities in the Baltic region, and enhancing digital and logistics infrastructure. As of 31 December 2025, the Group held cash and cash equivalents of 29.5 million euros, compared to 45.5 million euros at the end of 2024, and total assets stood at 697.9 million euros. Working capital was negative at 14.2 million euros due to the classification of several long-term loans maturing in 2026 as current liabilities, which the company plans to refinance. The equity ratio remained stable at 38%. The Management Board proposed to pay dividends of 0.60 euros per share, totalling 24.4 million euros, from retained earnings.
