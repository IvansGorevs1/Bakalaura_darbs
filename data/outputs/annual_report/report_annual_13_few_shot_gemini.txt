AS LHV Group delivered strong financial performance in 2024, achieving its highest business volumes and net profit in the company’s history. Consolidated net profit reached EUR 150.3 million, representing a 7% increase compared to the previous year. This growth was supported by robust business expansion across key segments, effective cost management, and sustained credit portfolio quality. Total consolidated assets grew by 23% year-over-year to reach EUR 8.7 billion by the end of December 2024, driven by growth in deposit bases across all customer segments. 

The group's net loan portfolio expanded significantly, increasing by 28% to EUR 4.6 billion. This growth was led by retail loans, which grew by 21%, and corporate loans, which increased by 33.3%. Total customer deposits rose by 21% to reach EUR 6.9 billion. Regular customer deposits in LHV Pank increased by 14% to EUR 4.6 billion, while UK-based LHV Bank experienced substantial deposit growth, contributing to its independent financing. Total net income for the group grew by 11% to EUR 338.3 million, supported by an 8% increase in net interest income to EUR 273.3 million and a 24% rise in net fee and commission income to EUR 60.3 million. Operating expenses increased by 14% to EUR 146.9 million, primarily driven by a higher headcount. The cost-to-income ratio remained stable at 43.4%. 

Throughout 2024, the operating environment was characterized by slowing global inflation, shifting monetary policies, and decreasing base interest rates during the second half of the year. Despite negative Estonian GDP growth and increased competition in the banking sector, LHV fulfilled all its financial profitability and volume targets, leading to an update of its financial plan in September that raised targets by 25%. Credit quality remained strong with a very low share of non-performing loans, although expected credit losses increased to EUR 16.3 million due to portfolio expansion. Capital adequacy and liquidity ratios remained robust and well above regulatory requirements, with common equity tier 1 standing at 16.89% and total capital adequacy at 20.68%. Looking forward to 2025, the company anticipates a short-term decline in profits resulting from lower interest rate environments, but expects to offset part of the negative impact through continued business growth and operational efficiency.
