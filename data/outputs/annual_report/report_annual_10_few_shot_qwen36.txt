InCoax Networks AB reported a significant decline in financial performance during the 2025 fiscal year, characterized by a sharp drop in revenue and a widening net loss. Net sales decreased by 35 percent to SEK 48,055,007, down from SEK 74,497,775 in the previous year. This contraction was primarily driven by timing shifts in customer orders and a more cautious market environment, particularly in North America, which accounted for the majority of sales. The operating result deteriorated to a loss of SEK 32,380,175, compared to an operating loss of SEK 19,378,406 in 2024. The increased loss reflects the lower revenue base while the company maintained high levels of investment in product development and commercial expansion to strengthen its long-term growth platform. Gross profit declined to SEK 29,919,750, although the gross margin improved to 62 percent from 57 percent, indicating better cost efficiency in goods for resale despite lower volumes.

The net loss after tax amounted to SEK 32,559,663, resulting in a loss per share of SEK 0.25. Despite the operational losses, the company’s cash flow position improved significantly due to financing activities. Cash flow including financing activities turned positive at SEK 6,516,117, compared to a negative SEK 12,758,133 in the prior year. This positive outcome was largely attributable to a directed share issue that raised approximately SEK 33.5 million and the issuance of loans, which strengthened the company’s liquidity. Cash and cash equivalents at year-end stood at SEK 8.7 million. The equity ratio decreased to 71.8 percent from 80.8 percent, reflecting the dilution from the new share issue and accumulated losses.

Strategically, 2025 marked a transition from a development-intensive phase to commercial execution. The company completed the development of its D2501 and D2508 products, with the D2501 fully integrated into Nokia’s Gigabit Connect platform, providing access to Nokia’s global operator base. Initial installations via Nokia have begun in North America. Concurrently, the company initiated a structural cost reduction program, aiming to lower ongoing costs by more than 40 percent, or approximately SEK 25 million annually, starting in 2026. This reduction focuses on lower development investments and a more efficient organizational structure. The CEO emphasized that the highest priority is reaching positive cash flow within the coming twelve months through increased sales conversion and operational efficiency. The company continues to invest in capitalizing development costs, which amounted to SEK 25,269,745, supporting the next generation of its MoCA Access platform. Overall, the period was defined by financial challenges due to delayed orders, offset by strategic product launches, financing to secure liquidity, and decisive measures to prepare for scalable commercial growth in 2026.
