During the financial year 2025, WindowMaster International A/S experienced a decline in revenue compared to the previous year, primarily due to a slow start in 2025, reduced sales to a key Nordic customer, and lower growth in North America. Consolidated revenue for 2025 amounted to DKK 268,588,654, representing an 8 percent decrease compared to DKK 294,481,472 in 2024. Gross profit reached DKK 131,055,764, down from DKK 137,949,145 in the previous period. Despite the lower top-line figures, the gross margin improved to 48.8 percent compared to 46.9 percent in 2024, driven by a business mix featuring less revenue from key accounts and more direct project revenue under the Accelerate Core strategy. Operating profit in terms of EBITDA decreased to DKK 27,969,000 from DKK 37,305,000 in 2024, resulting in an EBITDA margin of 10.4 percent compared to 12.7 percent in the prior year. This decrease was affected by maintained fixed cost levels to support future growth alongside extraordinary one-off costs of DKK 2.5 million incurred from a ransomware attack in May and June 2025. Profit before tax declined to DKK 2,907,507 from DKK 12,183,769 in 2024, and profit after tax fell to DKK 869,196 compared to DKK 10,601,388 in the previous period, impacted by unutilized tax assets. Cash flow from operating activities remained strong at DKK 28,460,663 compared to Dkk 29,430,925 in 2024, supported by improvements in net working capital, particularly regarding inventories and work in progress. Cash flow from investing activities amounted to Dkk -9,374,141, down from DKK -17,593,686 in 2024, driven by investments in digitalization and IT network updates. Net interest-bearing debt stood at DKK 38.7 million at the end of 2025, resulting in a financial gearing ratio of 1.7, which remains below the company target of 2.0. Total equity at the end of the reporting period was DKK 32,472,860, corresponding to an equity ratio of 23.5 percent. Overall market activity stabilized during the second half of 2025 as interest rates leveled off, supporting increased project execution within the non-residential construction segment. For the full year 2026, management expects organic revenue growth in the range of 7 to 14 percent to reach between DKK 290 million and DKK 310 million, with EBITDA anticipated to increase to between DKK 45 million and DKK 55 million, corresponding to an EBITDA margin of around 8 to 10 percent.
