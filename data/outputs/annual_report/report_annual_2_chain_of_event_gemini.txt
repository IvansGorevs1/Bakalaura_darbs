The consolidated financial report of AS Infortar for the year ended 31 December 2025 details the financial performance and operational developments of the investment company and its 109 group companies operating primarily in maritime transport, energy, real estate, and agricultural sectors. For the financial year 2025, the Group reported total revenue of approximately 1.837 billion euros compared to 1.372 billion euros in the previous year. Gross profit reached 239.842 million euros, up from 128.628 million euros in 2024. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA) amounted to 227.919 million euros, compared to 145.275 million euros in 2024, representing an EBITDA margin of 12.4%. Operating profit for the financial year was 109.008 million euros, compared to 77.024 million euros in the prior year. Net profit for the financial year was 69.857 million euros, of which 70.550 million euros was attributable to the owners of the parent company, reflecting a decrease from the 2024 net profit of 193.670 million euros, which was heavily influenced by one-off gains from the initial consolidation of Tallink Grupp recognized in the previous period. Earnings per share for 2025 stood at 3.43 euros basic and 3.37 euros diluted, compared to 9.36 euros basic and 9.12 euros diluted in 2024. Total assets at the end of 2025 were 2.597 billion euros, down slightly from 2.715 billion euros at the end of 2024, while total equity increased to 1.175 billion euros from 1.166 billion euros. Interest-bearing liabilities decreased to 1.071 billion euros from 1.223 billion euros, reflecting loan repayments across several group entities. Cash and cash equivalents at year-end totaled 219.771 million euros, up from 167.579 million euros. The financial results were significantly influenced by the full twelve-month consolidation of AS Tallink Grupp following the acquisition of a controlling stake in August 2024, alongside expansion in the energy sector through Elenger Grupp and acquisitions in the agricultural sector, notably Estonia Farmid OÜ. Seasonal fluctuations affected performance, with the energy segment generating higher profits during colder winter periods and the maritime transport segment peaking during summer months. In line with its dividend policy, Infortar paid dividends of 3 euros per share in 2025, totalling 62 million euros, and the management has proposed a dividend of 3.02 euros per share for the 2025 financial results.
