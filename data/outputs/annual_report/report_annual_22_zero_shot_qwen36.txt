Tallinna Vesi reported strong financial performance for the year ended 31 December 2025, driven by increased sales and operational efficiencies. Total sales reached €72.48 million, representing an 11.4% increase or €7.40 million compared to €65.08 million in 2024. This growth was primarily fueled by an 18.3% rise in sales to private customers in the main service area to €31.84 million, attributable to higher consumption and tariff adjustments implemented in July 2024, November 2024, and May 2025. Conversely, sales to business customers decreased by 2.7% to €16.76 million due to tariff reductions aimed at harmonizing prices with private customers. Sales from construction services also surged by 62.0% to €8.46 million, reflecting increased turnover in pipe and road construction.

Profitability metrics improved significantly. Gross profit rose by 10.5% to €29.52 million, while operating profit increased by 11.8% to €21.22 million. The growth in operating profit was largely attributed to the optimization of electricity costs following the full operation of the combined heat and power plant at the Paljassaare Wastewater Treatment Plant, which now produces all required heat and over half of the electricity for treatment processes. Profit before taxes grew by 18.0% to €17.43 million, and net profit increased by 7.2% to €14.24 million. Earnings per share stood at €0.71, up from €0.66 in the previous year.

The company maintained a robust balance sheet with total assets increasing to €364.57 million. Investments in fixed assets totaled €55.74 million, supporting infrastructure continuity and sustainability initiatives. Outstanding loans increased to €160 million, with an average interest rate of 3.24%. The debt-to-total-assets ratio rose to 66.02% from 61.71% in 2024. Cash balances improved substantially, ending the year at €11.29 million compared to €3.59 million in 2024.

Operational and sustainability performance remained high. The company achieved its target for reducing sewer blockages, with a 39% decrease compared to the 2020–2022 average. Water loss rates were maintained at 13.01%, below the 14% target. The quality of treated effluent continued to outperform regulatory standards, and 99.5% of drinking water samples met strict quality requirements. The company was awarded the title of Green Company of the Year, recognizing its environmental efforts. Additionally, Tallinna Vesi declared a dividend of €0.53 per share for 2024, distributed in June 2025, aligning with its policy to distribute 50–80% of annual profit. The share price closed at €11.00 on 31 December 2025, reflecting a 7.84% increase from the beginning of the year.
