InCoax Networks’ 2025 financial performance weakened materially compared with 2024. Net sales fell 35% to SEK 48.1 million from SEK 74.5 million, mainly due to timing shifts and delays in customer orders, weaker sales development, and a more cautious market. Product sales declined particularly sharply, accounting for SEK 41.0 million compared with SEK 69.2 million previously, while service sales increased to SEK 7.0 million from SEK 5.3 million. North America remained the largest market, but regional sales there decreased to SEK 39.6 million from SEK 69.6 million.

Gross profit decreased to SEK 29.9 million from SEK 42.2 million, although the gross margin improved to 62% from 57%, reflecting the lower proportion of goods-related costs. Operating expenses fell to SEK 105.9 million from SEK 121.2 million, primarily because of lower resale-goods and external costs, partly offset by higher personnel expenses. Despite these reductions, the operating loss widened to SEK 32.4 million from SEK 19.4 million, as lower revenue coincided with continued substantial investment in product development, strategic collaborations, and commercial expansion. Capitalized development costs were SEK 25.3 million, broadly unchanged from SEK 25.6 million in 2024. The net loss increased to SEK 32.6 million from SEK 19.2 million, and loss per share worsened to SEK 0.25 from SEK 0.18.

Cash flow including financing activities improved to a positive SEK 6.5 million from negative SEK 12.8 million. Operating cash flow remained negative at SEK 20.0 million but improved from negative SEK 27.1 million, while financing activities generated SEK 51.8 million. Financing included a directed share issue, convertible financing, and loans, providing approximately SEK 53.5 million before issue costs. Cash and cash equivalents increased to SEK 8.7 million at year-end from SEK 2.2 million, although the company reported that its credit facility was fully utilized. The equity ratio declined to 71.8% from 80.8%, and shares outstanding increased by 21% to 131.3 million, causing dilution.

The year marked a transition from intensive development and integration toward commercial execution. D2501 was fully integrated into Nokia’s Gigabit Connect platform, and initial North American installations were completed through Nokia. InCoax also launched the D2508 product through its own channels. However, delayed customer projects and slower commercial ramp-up within certain partnerships constrained 2025 revenue. Management initiated structural cost reductions expected to lower ongoing costs by more than 40%, or over SEK 25 million annually, from 2026, with an objective of reaching cash-flow neutrality within twelve months. The auditor highlighted material uncertainty regarding going concern because future financing depends partly on warrant exercises or alternative funding.
