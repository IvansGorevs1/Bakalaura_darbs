During the financial year ended 31 December 2025, AS Tallinna Sadam Group achieved consolidated revenue of EUR 118,687 thousand, representing a slight decrease of 0.8% or EUR 901 thousand compared to EUR 119,587 thousand in 2024. Despite the minor drop in revenue, the Group improved its profitability indicators. Operating profit rose by 12.2% or EUR 3,555 thousand to EUR 32,704 thousand, up from EUR 29,148 thousand in the previous year. Adjusted EBITDA increased by 6.4% or EUR 3,375 thousand to EUR 56,510 thousand, compared to EUR 53,134 thousand in 2024, pushing the adjusted EBITDA margin up to 47.6% from 44.4%. Profit for the period grew by 17.3% or EUR 3,310 thousand to EUR 22,463 thousand, compared to EUR 19,153 thousand in 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share increased to EUR 0.09 from EUR 0.07. Total assets at period-end stood at EUR 622,063 thousand, down 1.2% from EUR 629,861 thousand, while total equity rose by 0.9% to EUR 380,877 thousand from EUR 377,613 thousand. Net debt decreased significantly by 15.5% to EUR 141,708 thousand compared to EUR 167,622 thousand at the end of 2024. Capital investments were reduced by 14.4% to EUR 33,245 thousand from EUR 38,831 thousand. 

The overall performance was supported by a strong recovery in cargo volumes and passenger numbers. Total cargo volume grew by 5.1% to 13.799 million tonnes, driven by significant increases in liquid bulk and dry bulk handling, alongside record shipments of steel and grain at Muuga Harbour. The number of passengers across the Group's harbours increased by 1.0% to 8.3 million passengers, supported by growth on the Tallinn-Helsinki route and a higher number of cruise passengers and winter cruise calls. In contrast, the segment Other experienced a decline in revenue due to a lower utilisation rate of 49% for the icebreaker Botnica, which recorded 42 fewer charter days compared to 2024. Operating expenses decreased by 10.0% to EUR 36,412 thousand, primarily due to lower non-current asset repair costs and the receipt of an insurance indemnity of EUR 900 thousand related to a technical failure of the icebreaker Botnica in the prior year. Finance costs decreased by 30.0% to EUR 5,773 thousand due to falling Euribor rates and reduced loan volumes. However, income tax expense increased by 73.3% to EUR 5,415 thousand, driven by changes in dividend taxation rules and higher tax rates. 

The Group maintained its commitment to shareholder value by paying a dividend of EUR 0.073 per share, totalling EUR 19,199 million, which represented 100% of the previous year's profit. For the upcoming financial year, the management board has proposed distributing the same dividend amount of EUR 0.073 per share, totalling EUR 19,199,000. Overall, the Group’s balanced business model across passenger harbours, cargo harbours, ferries, and shipping, combined with a strong balance sheet and an equity-to-assets ratio of 61%, enabled the company to navigate geopolitical uncertainties and input price pressures while advancing strategic infrastructure projects such as the new multifunctional quay at Paldiski South Harbour and preparations for shore power facilities.
