During the 2025 financial year, OODA AI AB (publ) experienced a significant operational breakthrough, shifting its focus from structural build-up to commercial scaling within artificial intelligence and software-as-a-service. Group net sales surged substantially to SEK 31,880 thousand, compared to SEK 1,056 thousand in the previous year, driven primarily by major transaction invoicing, including agreements with Win.Rocks AG and HMM Deutschland. The Group's total revenue reached SEK 32,058 thousand, up from SEK 1,061 thousand in 2024. 

Operating profitability showed marked improvement. The Group reported an operating loss of SEK -29,270 thousand for 2025, which represents an improvement compared to the operating loss of SEK -51,781 thousand recorded in 2024. Furthermore, the EBITDA result turned positive at SEK 2,957 thousand, compared to SEK -28,229 thousand in the prior year. When adjusted for approximately SEK 10,238 thousand in non-recurring one-off costs related to the liquidation of older corporate structures, adjusted EBITDA stood at approximately SEK 13,195 thousand, corresponding to an adjusted EBITDA margin of approximately 32 percent of revenue. Personnel costs for the Group increased moderately to SEK -9,210 thousand from SEK -7,432 thousand, while the average number of employees remained low at 4 compared to 3 in 2024. 

Despite operational progress, the Group's bottom line was heavily burdened by financial items, which resulted in a loss from financial items of SEK -95,853 thousand, compared to SEK -29,900 thousand in 2024. This was largely driven by impairments, divestments, and revaluations of financial assets and associated companies, including SEK -63,243 thousand in impairment losses from participations in associated companies and SEK -32,227 thousand in impairments from other securities and fixed asset receivables. Consequently, the Group's result for the year was a loss of SEK -125,799 thousand, compared to a loss of SEK -83,355 thousand in 2024. Basic earnings per share were SEK -35.29, down from SEK -29.00 in the previous year. 

The balance sheet reflected considerable restructuring and streamlining. Total assets for the Group decreased to SEK 43,640 thousand at the end of 2025 from SEK 145,033 thousand at the end of 2024, largely due to the write-downs and revaluations of financial holdings. Financial fixed assets dropped sharply to SEK 8,387 thousand from SEK 96,036 thousand. Group equity declined from SEK 119,370 thousand to SEK 23,859 thousand at year-end, while the equity ratio stood at 55 percent compared to 82 percent previously. Cash and cash equivalents decreased to SEK 3,335 thousand from SEK 25,108 thousand. The total number of shares increased from 3,385,254 to 3,731,465 through capital acquisitions and share issues carried out in January, March, and August 2025, which raised additional capital and reduced indebtedness. Cash flow from operating activities was SEK -16,662 thousand, an improvement from SEK -29,680 thousand in 2024, supported by a positive operational cash flow of SEK 5,098 thousand during the second half of the year. Investing activities used SEK -49,413 thousand, while financing activities contributed SEK 44,302 thousand. 

Significant events during the period included multiple corporate mergers to simplify the group structure, the acquisition of remaining shares in EO Capital 1 AB, and the expansion of key commercial partnerships and contracts, such as the Win.Rocks AG agreement totaling SEK 21 million and an additional SEK 15 million agreement signed in December 2025. After the financial year end, the company resolved on a further cash issue of approximately SEK 10 million in March 2026 and advanced plans for a broader platform launch scheduled for the second quarter of 2026.
