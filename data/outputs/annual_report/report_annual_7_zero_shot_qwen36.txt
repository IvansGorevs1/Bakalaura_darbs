Heimstaden AB reported a strong financial performance for the full year 2025, characterized by margin expansion, disciplined capital allocation, and solid operational fundamentals. The company’s net operating income increased to SEK 11,475 million from SEK 11,188 million in 2024, resulting in an improved net operating income margin of 72.1 percent compared to 70.3 percent in the prior year. This growth was driven by a 4.9 percent like-for-like rental income growth, supported by indexation, tenant improvements, and optimal price setting, which offset the negative impact from portfolio sales and foreign exchange effects. Total rental income remained flat at SEK 15,916 million, while property expenses decreased by 6.1 percent to SEK 4,378 million due to cost control measures, digitalization, and a shrinking portfolio size resulting from the ongoing privatization program.

The fair value of investment properties stood at SEK 324,429 million at year-end, representing a 2.9 percent increase from SEK 335,422 million in 2024. This appreciation was driven by net operating income growth, value-accretive capital expenditures, and strong performance in the home-owner market in select countries. Realized gains from the divestment of properties rose to SEK 2,207 million from SEK 1,687 million in 2024. The privatization program continued to generate significant value, with SEK 10,664 million in sales achieved in 2025 for 2,466 residential units at a 28.9 percent premium to book value. Since the program's inception, total sales value has reached SEK 19,451 million for 4,443 units at a 27.9 percent premium.

Financially, the company maintained a robust balance sheet with a net loan-to-value ratio of 52.2 percent, down from 56.4 percent in 2024, reflecting deleveraging through the deployment of privatization proceeds. The interest coverage ratio improved to 2.0 times from 1.9 times. Interest expenses were stable at SEK 6,536 million, with an average interest rate of 3.4 percent. Liability management activities included the issuance of SEK 750 million in senior unsecured floating rate notes and EUR 430 million in fixed rate notes by Heimstaden AB, alongside significant refinancing by its subsidiary Heimstaden Bostad.

The year ended with a profit for the period of SEK 13,056 million, compared to SEK 7,621 million in 2024, driven by fair value adjustments and realized gains. However, a goodwill impairment loss of SEK 3,707 million was recorded in the fourth quarter. The company’s equity increased to SEK 150,557 million. Real economic occupancy remained stable at 98.5 percent for the year. In governance developments, Fitch affirmed the company’s rating at B- with a negative outlook in January 2025, and an arbitration award in December 2025 confirmed no breach of the Shareholders Agreement by Heimstaden Bostad. No dividends were proposed for the year, with all funds carried forward.
