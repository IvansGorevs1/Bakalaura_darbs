During the financial year ended 31 December 2024, Novaturas Group recorded revenues of 200.9 million euros, representing a 3.6% decrease compared to 208.3 million euros in 2023. The group served 239.5 thousand travelers in Lithuania, Latvia, and Estonia during 2024, reflecting a 7.6% decrease from 259.3 thousand travelers served in 2023. Gross profit for 2024 was 20.7 million euros, marking a 20.9% decline compared to 26.2 million euros in the previous year. Operating expenses reached 27.5 million euros, which is 25% higher than in 2023, primarily due to a one-off, non-cash goodwill impairment loss of 5.7 million euros related to the Estonian cash-generating unit. Excluding this goodwill impairment, operating expenses for 2024 amounted to 21.8 million euros, representing a 1% decrease compared to 2023. The group reported a negative EBITDA of 6.1 million euros for 2024, compared to positive 5.2 million euros in 2023. Adjusted EBITDA, which excludes the goodwill impairment, was negative 449 thousand euros for 2024, compared to positive 5.2 million euros in 2023. The audited net loss for 2024 totaled 7.6 million euros, compared to a net profit of 3.4 million euros in 2023. Excluding the goodwill impairment, the group's net loss amounted to 1.9 million euros. Basic and diluted earnings per share were negative 0.97 euros in 2024, compared to positive 0.43 euros in 2023. 

The financial performance was influenced by an increased competitive environment and an oversupply of travel in the Baltic States, which triggered intense price competition particularly in Turkey and the Greek islands. In response, the group optimized its travel program and adjusted its offerings, leading to improved operating results starting from September 2024, with an adjusted EBITDA of 2.3 million euros recorded between September and December 2024. The fourth quarter load factor reached 98.9%, compared to 96.7% in the fourth quarter of 2023. Furthermore, the share of last-minute trips in sales decreased significantly during the final quarter. 

Geographically, sales in Lithuania were 110.7 million euros, in Latvia 42.8 million euros, and in Estonia 47.4 million euros. Flight packages remained the primary product, generating 170.7 million euros in revenue and serving 183.3 thousand passengers. Sales for sightseeing tours by coach reached 2.0 million euros, while sightseeing tours by plane generated 2.8 million euros, and other sales amounted to 25.4 million euros. Turkey remained the primary summer destination, accounting for approximately 34.3% of bookings. 

As of 31 December 2024, the group’s total assets stood at 39.5 million euros, down from 47.6 million euros at the end of 2023. Total equity decreased to 8.0 million euros from 15.7 million euros. Total borrowings increased to 9.3 million euros at the end of 2024 from 8.7 million euros at the end of 2023. As of 31 December 2024, the group did not comply with certain financial and non-financial covenants under its financing agreements, but obtained waivers from Pagalbos Verslui Fondas for ordinary bonds and from Luminor Bank AS for its credit line. Cash and cash equivalents decreased to 394 thousand euros at the end of 2024 from 3.35 million euros at the end of 2023. Net cash flows from operating activities were negative 1.6 million euros, net cash flows used in investing activities were 532 thousand euros, and net cash flows used in financing activities were 807 thousand euros. Subsequent to the reporting period, the group secured additional credit facilities and entered into agreements for a strategic investment by Neset Kockar, who acquired a 23.2% shareholding in the company with further stages of the transaction planned.
