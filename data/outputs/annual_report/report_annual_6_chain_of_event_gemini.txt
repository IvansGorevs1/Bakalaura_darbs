Gigasun AB reported total revenues of 239,655 thousand Swedish kronor for the 2025 financial year, representing an increase compared to 218,264 thousand Swedish kronor in the previous year. Net revenue rose by 9.9 percent to 203,179 thousand Swedish kronor from 184,795 thousand Swedish kronor previously, while other operating income amounted to 36,476 thousand Swedish kronor compared with 33,469 thousand Swedish kronor in the preceding year. Gross profit improved to 195,829 thousand Swedish kronor from 179,219 thousand Swedish kronor, maintaining a gross margin of 82 percent. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization increased to 163,733 thousand Swedish kronor compared to 145,760 thousand Swedish kronor, with the EBITDA margin rising slightly to 68 percent from 67 percent. Operating profit improved by 21.1 percent to 73,478 thousand Swedish kronor from 60,677 thousand Swedish kronor, and the operating margin increased to 31 percent compared to 28 percent in the previous period. Profit after tax improved to negative 7,206 thousand Swedish kronor compared to negative 32,944 thousand Swedish kronor in the preceding year, resulting in earnings per share of negative 0.13 Swedish kronor compared to negative 0.58 Swedish kronor previously.

The financial performance was influenced by operational expansion and portfolio growth. During 2025, the company increased its total installed capacity of roof-mounted solar energy installations to 395.8 megawatts across 216 installations, up from 340.1 megawatts across 200 installations at the end of 2024. The company installed 55.7 megawatts of roof-based solar energy facilities during the year, while signing 17 contracts representing 41.7 megawatts of capacity, leaving 16.4 megawatts in signed orders at year-end. Electricity generation rose to 327.8 million kilowatt-hours from 252.6 million kilowatt-hours in the previous period, driving the revenue growth. However, revenues were partially offset by negative currency effects of 18.0 million Swedish kronor and lower average prices resulting from a higher share of electricity sold to the grid and revised tariffs in the second half of the year. Operating expenses increased by 5.5 percent to 166,177 thousand Swedish kronor, primarily driven by higher scheduled depreciation and increased maintenance associated with the larger installed base. 

Financially, Gigasun entered into agreements with Agricultural Bank Financial Leasing Co., Ltd to refinance approximately 1,242 million Swedish kronor to repay sale and leaseback facilities with JiangSu Financial Leasing Co., Ltd, which contributed to lower interest expenses of 76,944 thousand Swedish kronor compared to 84,165 thousand Swedish kronor. The remaining SOLT4 bond was extended to November 8, 2026. Total interest-bearing debt decreased to 1,382,613 thousand Swedish kronor at the end of 2025 from 1,483,061 thousand Swedish kronor, and net interest-bearing debt decreased to 1,337,465 thousand Swedish kronor from 1,393,172 thousand Swedish kronor. Total assets stood at 2,296,782 thousand Swedish kronor compared to 2,528,093 thousand Swedish kronor, with cash and bank balances amounting to 45,149 thousand Swedish kronor compared to 89,889 thousand Swedish kronor. Equity at year-end was 488,201 thousand Swedish kronor, resulting in an equity ratio of 21 percent compared to 23 percent in the previous year. Cash flow from operating activities was 101,058 thousand Swedish kronor compared to 133,785 thousand Swedish kronor, while investing activities used 208,101 thousand Swedish kronor primarily for investments in solar panel installations, and financing activities generated 72,141 thousand Swedish kronor.
