SAF Tehnika Group reported a mixed but materially improved performance for the financial year ended 30 June 2025. Consolidated revenue decreased by EUR 1.1 million, or 4%, to EUR 25.99 million, while the parent company’s revenue fell by 6% to EUR 21.92 million. The decline was concentrated in the Americas, where revenue decreased by EUR 2.3 million, or 14%, to EUR 14.17 million, representing 55% of Group revenue. Management attributed this largely to the timing of large projects being delivered in later periods and temporarily cautious customer investment decisions linked to tariff announcements. European revenue, including Latvia, increased by EUR 1.4 million, or 17%, to EUR 10.0 million, partly offsetting the Americas decline. Revenue in Asia, Africa and the Middle East fell by 5% to EUR 1.8 million.

Despite lower sales, profitability improved significantly. Consolidated gross profit rose from EUR 7.53 million to EUR 9.50 million, and the gross margin increased from approximately 28% to 37%. This improvement was supported by a EUR 3.1 million reduction in production costs, primarily lower procurement and subcontracting costs. Selling and marketing expenses declined by 5% to EUR 7.43 million, although administrative expenses increased by 16% to EUR 3.36 million. Consequently, operating losses narrowed from EUR 3.22 million to EUR 1.29 million. Consolidated net loss improved from EUR 2.37 million to EUR 643 thousand, while loss per share improved from EUR 0.798 to EUR 0.216. The parent company’s net loss decreased from EUR 1.34 million to EUR 552 thousand.

Cash generation strengthened considerably. Group operating cash flow increased to EUR 3.87 million from EUR 1.71 million, and year-end cash rose by EUR 2.6 million to EUR 4.81 million. The Group invested EUR 812 thousand in IT infrastructure, production and research equipment, software, licences, certification and renovations. Slow-moving inventory provisions declined by EUR 1.24 million to EUR 3.86 million, although inventories increased to EUR 14.0 million.

The Group continued product development across microwave radio, Spectrum Compact and Aranet IoT solutions and received EUR 570 thousand in co-financing for development projects. Market uncertainty remained due to US-China trade tensions, tariffs on Aranet products in the United States and Canada-US customs uncertainty. In response, the Group established a Canadian branch to reduce supply-chain and customs-cost risks. Management considers the losses temporary, but remains cautious and has made no specific forecasts for future sales or financial results. The auditors issued an unqualified opinion.
