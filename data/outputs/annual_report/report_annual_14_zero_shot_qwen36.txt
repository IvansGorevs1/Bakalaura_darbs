Lyko Group AB reported net revenue of SEK 3,962.6 million for the full year 2025, representing an 11.3 percent increase compared to SEK 3,561.7 million in 2024. This growth was primarily driven by the Nordic segment, where revenue rose by 12.0 percent to SEK 3,699.8 million, accounting for 93.4 percent of total sales. The European segment experienced a slight decline of 1.3 percent, with revenue totaling SEK 130.7 million. The company welcomed over 350,000 new customers in the fourth quarter alone, reinforcing its position as the leading beauty specialist in the Nordics.

Despite the top-line growth, profitability metrics deteriorated significantly. Gross profit increased modestly to SEK 1,658.7 million, but the gross profit margin contracted from 44.0 percent to 41.9 percent. Operating profit (EBIT) fell sharply to SEK 78.2 million from SEK 117.4 million in the prior year, resulting in an EBIT margin of 2.0 percent compared to 3.3 percent previously. The decline in operating profit was largely attributable to substantial investments in infrastructure, particularly the new automated warehouse in Vansbro, as well as costs associated with store expansions and organizational development. Consequently, the net result for the year was a loss of SEK 3.0 million, a reversal from the net profit of SEK 21.0 million reported in 2024. Earnings per share declined to negative SEK 0.20 from SEK 1.37.

Cash flow from operating activities improved substantially, reaching SEK 527.5 million compared to SEK 209.7 million in 2024. This positive operational performance allowed the company to fund significant investing activities, which totaled SEK 351.2 million. The primary investment was the commissioning of the new warehouse automation in Vansbro, the largest project in the company’s history, which is expected to be fully operational in early 2026. Additionally, Lyko opened new stores in Norway and Finland and launched its first Fab Store concept in Täby. Financing activities generated SEK 150.9 million, mainly through new loans taken to support expansion plans.

The financial position saw an increase in total assets to SEK 3,086.4 million, driven by higher property, plant, and equipment values and increased inventory. Equity decreased slightly to SEK 456.6 million, leading to an equity ratio of 14.8 percent, down from 20.3 percent. Net debt, excluding IFRS 16 lease liabilities, stood at SEK 292.5 million, a decrease from SEK 333.1 million in the previous year. The Board of Directors proposed no dividend for 2025, prioritizing the use of cash flows for continued expansion. Following the reporting period, Lyko announced a cost savings program aimed at generating annual savings of approximately SEK 100 million to strengthen future earnings.
