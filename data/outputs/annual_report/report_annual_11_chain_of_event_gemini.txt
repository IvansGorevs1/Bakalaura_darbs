For the full year 2025, JLT Mobile Computers AB reported net sales of SEK 131.5 million, representing an 11.1 percent increase compared to SEK 118.4 million in the previous year. Order intake grew by 37.5 percent to SEK 141.6 million, up from SEK 103.0 million, supported by a major deal of SEK 22 million with an American food producer. Despite the top-line growth, gross profit decreased to SEK 50.4 million from SEK 54.2 million, and the gross margin declined by 7.5 percentage points to 38.3 percent compared to 45.8 percent last year. The reduction in gross margin and earnings was directly influenced by external factors, notably the US dollar losing nearly 20 percent against the Swedish krona during a period when 74 percent of sales were invoiced in dollars, combined with revaluing inventory based on current dollar purchase rates. Operating profit improved significantly to SEK -1.9 million from SEK -9.7 million in 2024, aided by structural cost savings that reduced overhead and R&D costs by SEK 6.3 million. Profit after tax improved to SEK -2.5 million compared to SEK -7.7 million in the previous year. Earnings per share amounted to SEK -0.09 compared to SEK -0.27 in 2024. The company’s financial position reflected focused capital management, with inventories decreasing by SEK 12.7 million to SEK 25.5 million down from SEK 38.3 million at the end of the previous year, which helped generate a cash flow from operating activities of SEK 5.9 million and a cash flow for the year of SEK 5.6 million. Cash and cash equivalents increased to SEK 16.9 million from SEK 13.3 million, and solidity strengthened to 59 percent compared to 54 percent. The Board of Directors proposed that no dividend be paid for the 2025 financial year, matching the prior year's dividend of SEK 0.00.
