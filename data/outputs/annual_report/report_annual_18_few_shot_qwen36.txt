OODA AI AB reported a significant transformation in its financial performance during 2025, characterized by a substantial increase in revenue and a strategic shift from a build-up phase to commercial scaling. The group’s net sales surged to SEK 31,880 thousand, a dramatic rise from SEK 1,056 thousand in the previous year. This growth was driven by the invoicing of major transactions, including expanded contracts with key customers such as Win.Rocks AG and Sergel Kredittjänster, as well as new partnerships in the banking, health, and staffing sectors. The operating profit improved to a loss of SEK 29,270 thousand, compared to a loss of SEK 51,781 thousand in 2024. This improvement was largely due to the exclusion of one-off liquidation costs amounting to approximately SEK 10,238 thousand. Adjusted for these non-recurring items, the EBITDA was positive at approximately SEK 13,195 thousand, representing a margin of roughly 32 percent of revenue.

Despite the operational improvement, the group recorded a net loss of SEK 125,799 thousand, resulting in earnings per share of SEK -35.29. This negative result was primarily attributable to substantial impairments and losses from financial items, totaling SEK 95,853 thousand. These financial losses stemmed from the review and streamlining of the company’s portfolio holdings, where certain investments were written down to zero based on conservative valuations. Consequently, financial assets decreased significantly from SEK 96,036 thousand to SEK 8,387 thousand. The group’s equity also declined sharply to SEK 23,859 thousand from SEK 119,371 thousand the previous year, reflecting these impairment charges.

The company’s liquidity position was strengthened through multiple capital raises during the year, contributing SEK 44,302 thousand to financing activities. These capital injections, along with reduced debt, improved the company’s financial structure. Cash flow from operating activities improved to a loss of SEK 16,662 thousand from a loss of SEK 29,680 thousand in 2024, and notably turned positive in the second half of the year at SEK 5,098 thousand. The board of directors confirmed that the company meets the going concern requirements, citing strong operational growth, positive adjusted EBITDA, and the completion of several capital issues as evidence of financial stability.

Looking forward, OODA AI plans to focus on scaling its AI platform, expanding distribution through partner channels, and commercializing its decentralized AI infrastructure. The company aims to broaden its market presence with a planned public launch of its platform in the second quarter of 2026. The strategic focus remains on increasing platform usage and leveraging a scalable business model that minimizes cost increases relative to revenue growth. The company continues to invest in research and development, capitalizing approximately SEK 8,716 thousand in development costs during the year to support its technical infrastructure and AI product offerings.
