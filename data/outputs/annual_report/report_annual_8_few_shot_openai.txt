SAF Tehnika Group’s financial performance improved materially in the 2024/2025 financial year ended 30 June 2025, although the Group remained loss-making. Consolidated revenue declined by 4% to EUR 25.99 million from EUR 27.09 million in the previous year. The decline was concentrated in the Americas, where revenue fell by EUR 2.3 million, or 14%, to EUR 14.17 million, representing 55% of Group revenue. Management attributed the reduction largely to the timing of large projects and temporary customer caution related to investment decisions and tariff announcements. European revenue increased by EUR 1.4 million, or 17%, to EUR 10.0 million, while revenue from Asia, Africa and the Middle East decreased by 5% to EUR 1.8 million.

Despite lower sales, gross profit increased significantly to EUR 9.50 million from EUR 7.53 million. Production costs declined by 16% to EUR 16.49 million, mainly reflecting lower procurement and subcontracting costs. However, sales and marketing expenses remained substantial at EUR 7.43 million, while administrative expenses increased by 16% to EUR 3.36 million. The Group’s operating loss therefore narrowed to EUR 1.29 million from EUR 3.22 million. After other income, financial income and financial costs, the loss before tax was EUR 652,629, compared with EUR 2.39 million previously. Net loss improved to EUR 642,642 from EUR 2.37 million, and loss per share decreased to EUR 0.216 from EUR 0.798.

The Group’s financial position strengthened in several respects. Cash and cash equivalents increased by EUR 2.6 million to EUR 4.81 million, supported by positive operating cash flow of EUR 3.87 million. Total assets rose to EUR 26.08 million from EUR 23.02 million, although equity declined to EUR 15.76 million as a result of the annual loss. Total liabilities increased to EUR 10.33 million, primarily because customer advances and other contract liabilities rose to EUR 5.15 million from EUR 1.97 million. The available EUR 4.95 million Luminor credit line remained unused at year-end.

Inventory increased to EUR 14.00 million, but the provision for slow-moving inventory fell by EUR 1.24 million to EUR 3.86 million. The Group invested EUR 812,000 in IT infrastructure, production and research equipment, software, licences, certification and premises renovation. Research and development activity continued across microwave radio, Spectrum Compact and Aranet products, supported by EUR 570,000 of co-financing. The Group also established a Canadian branch to mitigate potential supply-chain and customs-cost risks. Management considers the loss temporary but remains cautious because of tariff uncertainty, geopolitical conditions and continued dependence on international markets. The auditors issued an unmodified opinion on the financial statements.
