AS LHV Group reported record financial performance for the year 2024, driven by robust growth in its loan portfolio and favorable interest rate environments, although the company anticipates a short-term decline in profitability due to shifting macroeconomic conditions. The Group achieved its highest-ever net profit of EUR 150.3 million, representing a 7% increase from the previous year. This growth was supported by a 28% expansion in the loan portfolio, which reached EUR 4.6 billion, fueled by strong demand in both corporate and retail segments. Total consolidated assets also grew significantly by 23% to EUR 8.7 billion, underpinned by a 21% increase in customer deposits to EUR 6.9 billion. The strong balance sheet growth was accompanied by improved efficiency, with the cost-to-income ratio standing at a solid 43.4%, despite higher operating expenses linked to workforce expansion and investments in the UK subsidiary.

The primary driver of the increased profitability was the high base interest rate environment, which boosted net interest income by 8% to EUR 273.3 million. Net fee and commission income also saw substantial growth, rising 24% to EUR 60.3 million, reflecting increased customer activity and successful cross-selling initiatives. Operating expenses increased by 14% to EUR 146.9 million, primarily due to a rise in personnel costs as the Group expanded its staff to support business growth and digital transformation. Despite these cost increases, the Group maintained strong capitalization, with a total capital adequacy ratio of 20.68% and a return on equity of 24.5%. The Group also successfully issued EUR 20 million in Tier 2 bonds and redeemed EUR 20 million in Additional Tier 1 bonds, optimizing its capital structure.

Geographically, the Estonian banking operations remained the cornerstone of the Group, contributing the majority of net income. The UK subsidiary, LHV Bank Ltd, continued its rapid expansion, with its loan portfolio growing by 338% to EUR 348 million and deposits increasing by 195%. The UK business launched retail banking services and achieved record volumes in payment services for financial intermediaries. In the asset management segment, assets under management grew by 3% to EUR 1.56 billion, although the subsidiary did not earn success fees due to benchmark index performance. The insurance subsidiary, LHV Kindlustus, returned to profitability with a net profit of EUR 1.2 million, supported by stable premium growth and controlled loss ratios.

Looking ahead, the Group faces a changing interest rate landscape, with the ECB and Bank of England implementing rate cuts in the second half of 2024. Management has updated its financial plan, hiking profitability targets by 25% during the year but acknowledging that a short-term decline in profits is expected in 2025 due to the lower interest rate environment. To mitigate this impact, the Group plans to rely on continued business growth, particularly in the UK and through increased cross-selling, alongside ongoing efforts to improve operational efficiency. The Group remains well-capitalized and liquid, with strong regulatory ratios, positioning it to sustain long-term growth and deliver returns to shareholders despite the anticipated macroeconomic headwinds.
