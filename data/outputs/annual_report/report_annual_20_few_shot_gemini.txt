AS Tallink Grupp reported lower financial results for the 2025 financial year, reflecting a challenging macroeconomic environment characterized by subdued consumer and business confidence, geopolitical tensions, and regional tax changes. Consolidated revenue declined by 2.6% to EUR 765.3 million compared with EUR 785.8 million in the previous year. Revenue from core route operations decreased by EUR 13.7 million to EUR 625.2 million, while restaurant and shop sales onboard and on shore fell to EUR 366.4 million from EUR 376.0 million. Ticket sales edged down to EUR 222.7 million, cargo transport sales decreased to EUR 77.9 million, and charter income declined to EUR 54.9 million. 

Profitability measures weakened significantly during the period. Gross profit dropped to EUR 143.2 million from EUR 153.6 million, and gross margin contracted to 18.7%. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization fell to EUR 130.1 million compared with EUR 175.2 million in 2024, with the EBITDA margin declining to 17.0%. Audited net profit for the year decreased to EUR 17.3 million, down from EUR 40.3 million in the prior year, resulting in basic and diluted earnings per share of EUR 0.023 compared with EUR 0.054 previously. Net profit margin stood at 2.3%. Profitability was negatively affected by weak consumer spending in core markets, yet received some positive impact from ongoing cost-efficient operations, vessel chartering, the sale of idle vessels, and a decrease in depreciation expense resulting from the alignment of estimated useful lives of cruise and passenger vessels to 45 years. 

Operational indicators showed mixed volumes. The Group carried 5.5 million passengers, representing a minor decrease of 0.9% year-on-year. Transported cargo units dropped significantly by 19.2% to 245,004 units, while passenger vehicles declined by 2.2% to 760,473 units. The active fleet comprised 11 vessels at year-end, including two shuttle vessels, six passenger vessels, and three chartered-out vessels, with all laid-up vessels either redeployed or sold during the year. Capital expenditures totaled EUR 33.0 million, directed primarily toward vessel maintenance, technical upgrades, and IT system improvements. 

The Group maintained a solid liquidity and financial position despite market headwinds. Net operating cash flow remained positive at EUR 127.1 million, while net cash from investing activities reached EUR 46.1 million, supported by EUR 78.8 million in proceeds from the disposal of three vessels. Total interest-bearing liabilities were reduced by 19.9% to EUR 445.9 million at year-end. Net debt decreased to EUR 432.4 million from EUR 537.7 million, while the net debt-to-EBITDA ratio moved to 3.3. Total liquidity buffer, comprising cash, cash equivalents, and unused credit facilities, stood at EUR 110.4 million. Total equity closed at EUR 750.1 million, yielding an improved equity ratio of 56.4%. Total loan and related interest payments amounted to EUR 113.5 million. Reflecting commitment to shareholder returns, the Group paid total dividends of EUR 44.6 million, corresponding to EUR 0.06 per share, and the Management Board will propose an identical dividend payout for 2026.
