Tallinna Vesi reported improved financial performance in 2025. Group revenue increased 11.4% to €72.48 million, supported by 6.9% growth in water-services revenue to €62.31 million and a 62.0% increase in construction-services revenue to €8.46 million. Private-customer revenue in the main service area rose 18.3% to €31.84 million, driven by higher consumption and tariff changes. Business-customer revenue declined 2.7% to €16.76 million following reductions in business tariffs introduced to harmonise private and business prices. Revenue from customers outside the main service area increased 13.8% to €7.71 million.

Costs of goods and services sold grew 12.0% to €42.96 million, mainly because construction-services costs increased 74.5% to €7.13 million, staff costs rose 6.8% to €11.45 million, and depreciation and amortisation increased 11.9% to €9.08 million. Direct production costs were broadly unchanged at €9.90 million. Electricity costs fell 6.9% to €4.37 million, primarily because the combined heat and power plant at Paljassaare produced electricity for internal consumption. Chemicals costs increased 5.8%, while pollution taxes rose 21.4% because of higher rates and usage volumes.

Gross profit increased 10.5% to €29.52 million, and operating profit rose 11.8% to €21.22 million. The operating margin was broadly stable at 29.27%, compared with 29.17% in 2024. Net financial expense declined to €3.78 million from €4.20 million, mainly due to lower Euribor rates. Profit before tax increased 18.0% to €17.43 million, while net profit rose 7.2% to €14.24 million. Net profit growth was moderated by the higher income tax rate on dividend payments. Earnings per share increased from €0.66 to €0.71.

Investment accelerated substantially. Fixed-asset investment reached €55.74 million, compared with €49.53 million in 2024, and total assets increased to €364.57 million from €313.84 million. The investment programme was supported by additional borrowing: outstanding loans rose to €160 million from €118 million, increasing the debt-to-assets ratio from 61.71% to 66.02%. Operating cash flow improved to €30.83 million from €26.25 million, while year-end cash increased to €11.29 million from €3.59 million after €45.15 million of investing outflows and €10.6 million of dividends paid.

Operational developments included commissioning the combined heat and power plant, which produced all treatment-plant heat and 43.8% of electricity consumption at the wastewater plant and main pumping station. The company laid or reconstructed 45.1 kilometres of pipes, expanded smart-meter installation beyond 80% of customers, and reported fewer sewer blockages and water emergencies. Watercom also achieved record turnover and profits as Estonia’s construction market recovered. The group retained a €6.02 million provision for tariff-related third-party claims, with five actions totalling €14.67 million still unresolved.
