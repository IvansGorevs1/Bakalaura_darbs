AS LHV Group delivered record financial results in 2024 despite falling interest rates, severe competition for deposits and lending, increased regulation, and continued economic contraction in Estonia. Consolidated net profit rose 7% to EUR 150.3 million, while profit attributable to the parent’s owners reached EUR 149.0 million. Net income increased 11% to EUR 338.3 million, supported by 8% growth in net interest income to EUR 273.3 million and a 24% increase in net fee and commission income to EUR 60.3 million. Profit before tax was EUR 175.1 million. Operating expenses grew 14% to EUR 146.9 million, mainly because of higher employee numbers, and expected credit losses increased to EUR 16.3 million from EUR 11.5 million. Nevertheless, the cost-to-income ratio remained 43.4%, and return on equity was 24.5%, compared with 29.0% in 2023.

Balance-sheet growth was substantial. Total assets increased 23% to EUR 8.7 billion, net loans rose 28% to EUR 4.6 billion, and customer deposits increased 21% to EUR 6.9 billion. Loan growth reflected 21% increases in both Estonian corporate and retail lending, while UK SME loans expanded 338% to EUR 348 million. Deposit growth was supported particularly by term deposits, although LHV Pank reduced platform deposits by one-third. Loan quality remained strong overall, but the prolonged Estonian recession led to higher overdue and forborne exposures, more restrictive consumer-finance lending, increased impairment allowances, and a rise in Stage 2 and Stage 3 loans.

LHV Pank remained the principal earnings contributor, generating EUR 140.5 million of net profit, down 1% despite 6% higher income. Its results were negatively affected by deposit repricing as interest rates declined, while customer activity drove a 34% increase in fee income. LHV Bank’s net profit increased to EUR 5.8 million from EUR 5.3 million as deposits rose 195% to EUR 705 million and loans grew 338% to EUR 348 million, although profitability remained below plan because of slower early-year lending and fluctuating intermediary deposits. LHV Kindlustus reported its second profitable year, with net profit rising 300% to EUR 1.2 million on 21% premium growth. LHV Paytech’s profit increased 40% to EUR 0.7 million, while LHV Varahaldus’s profit declined 2% to EUR 1.6 million because no success fee was earned.

Capital and liquidity remained strong. The total capital adequacy ratio was 20.68%, down from 23.45%, while CET1 was 16.89%. LCR and NSFR were 187.5% and 154.4%, respectively, both well above regulatory minima. LHV issued EUR 20 million of Tier 2 bonds, redeemed EUR 20 million of Additional Tier 1 bonds, and issued EUR 300 million of MREL-eligible bonds. The share price fell 7% to EUR 3.245. Management expects lower 2025 profits because of the interest-rate environment, partly offset by continued business growth, cost control, and efficiency improvements.
