AS LHV Group delivered record financial results in 2024 despite a difficult operating environment marked by a continuing Estonian recession, intense competition for deposits and loans, increased regulatory requirements and falling interest rates during the second half of the year. Consolidated net income increased 11% to EUR 338.3 million, supported by strong balance-sheet growth, while net interest income rose 8% to EUR 273.3 million and net fee and commission income increased 24% to EUR 60.3 million. Operating expenses grew 14% to EUR 146.9 million, primarily because of higher employee numbers, and expected credit losses increased to EUR 16.3 million from EUR 11.5 million. Nevertheless, pre-tax profit rose to EUR 175.1 million and net profit reached a record EUR 150.3 million, up 7% year on year. Profit attributable to owners of the parent was EUR 149.0 million, compared with EUR 139.6 million in 2023, while basic earnings per share increased to EUR 0.46 from EUR 0.44.

Growth was concentrated in lending and deposits. Total assets increased 23% to EUR 8.7 billion, the net loan portfolio expanded 28% to EUR 4.6 billion, and customer deposits grew 21% to EUR 6.9 billion. Estonian corporate and retail loan portfolios each increased 21%, while UK SME lending expanded 338% to EUR 348 million. Credit quality remained strong overall, although forborne and overdue exposures increased in consumer finance and corporate lending as the economy weakened. The Group’s loan-loss allowance rose to EUR 39.8 million from EUR 29.7 million, and a EUR 3.7 million model overlay was recorded for anticipated changes to the ECL methodology.

LHV Pank remained the main contributor, generating EUR 140.5 million of net profit, slightly below 2023, as higher income was offset by increased operating expenses, credit losses and a EUR 24.4 million deferred tax expense related to anticipated dividends. LHV Bank increased net income to EUR 44.6 million and profit to EUR 5.8 million as deposits and lending expanded rapidly, while LHV Kindlustus achieved its second profitable year, with net profit rising to EUR 1.2 million on 21% premium growth. Asset management profit declined 2% to EUR 1.6 million because no performance fee was earned, while Paytech profit increased 40% to EUR 0.7 million.

Profitability remained strong, with ROE at 24.5%, although down from 29.0%, and the cost-to-income ratio at 43.4%. Capital and liquidity remained well buffered: CET1, Tier 1 and total capital ratios were 16.89%, 17.96% and 20.68%, respectively, while LCR and NSFR were 187.5% and 154.4%. The Group issued EUR 300 million of MREL-eligible bonds and EUR 20 million of Tier 2 bonds, while redeeming EUR 20 million of Additional Tier 1 bonds. The share price fell 7% to EUR 3.245, but the proposed 2024 dividend was EUR 0.09 per share. For 2025, management expects lower short-term profit because of declining interest rates, partly offset by continued loan growth and efficiency improvements.
