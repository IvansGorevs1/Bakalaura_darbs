A/S SAF Tehnika reported a consolidated net turnover of 25.99 million EUR for the financial year ended June 30, 2025, representing a decrease of 1.1 million EUR or 4% compared to the previous year. The parent company’s net turnover was 21.92 million EUR, down by 1.33 million EUR or 6%. Despite the revenue decline, the Group achieved a gross profit of 9.50 million EUR, an increase from 7.53 million EUR in the prior period, driven by a significant reduction in cost of sales which fell to 16.49 million EUR from 19.56 million EUR. However, operating losses widened to 1.29 million EUR from 3.22 million EUR in the previous year, primarily due to higher administrative and selling expenses relative to the lower revenue base. The Group reported a net loss for the period of 643 thousand EUR, a substantial improvement from the net loss of 2.37 million EUR recorded in the previous year. On a standalone basis, the parent company recorded a net loss of 552 thousand EUR compared to 1.34 million EUR previously.

Geographically, the Americas region, which accounts for 55% of total turnover, saw a 14% decline in revenue to 14.17 million EUR. This decrease was attributed to client caution regarding investment decisions and tariff changes, as well as the realization of large-volume projects in future periods. Conversely, the European region, including Latvia, demonstrated strong growth with revenue increasing by 17% to 10.0 million EUR. Revenue from the Asia, Middle East, and Africa regions decreased slightly by 5% to 1.8 million EUR. The company noted that while global geopolitical tensions and trade relations between the US and China impact the electronics sector, they have not yet caused significant negative effects on SAF Tehnika’s sales volumes. To mitigate supply chain and customs risks, particularly regarding tariffs on Aranet products and uncertainty in Canada-US trade, the Group established a branch in Canada during the reporting year.

Inventory management remained a critical focus area. Although the global situation regarding component availability has stabilized, the Group continues to hold higher levels of inventory purchased in previous years to ensure delivery timelines. Slow-moving inventory provisions decreased by 1.24 million EUR to 3.9 million EUR, reflecting improved inventory turnover. The Group’s cash position strengthened significantly, with cash and cash equivalents rising by 2.6 million EUR to 4.81 million EUR at year-end. The parent company maintained a credit line of 4.95 million EUR with Luminor Bank, which remained undrawn. Management views the current net loss as temporary, noting that operating cash flows remained positive. The Board proposes covering the accumulated losses from retained earnings of previous years. The company continues to invest in product innovation, particularly in the IoT segment with the Aranet brand and microwave radio equipment, supported by state grants and R&D funding.
