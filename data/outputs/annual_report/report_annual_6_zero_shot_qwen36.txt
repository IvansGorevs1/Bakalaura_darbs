Gigasun AB reported a strong operational performance for the 2025 financial year, characterized by significant growth in installed capacity and electricity production, despite a net loss due to non-cash currency effects. The company’s total revenue increased by 9.8 percent to SEK 239.7 million, driven by higher production volumes. Net revenue, which constitutes 85 percent of total revenue, rose by 9.9 percent to SEK 203.2 million. The increase in revenue was partially offset by lower average electricity prices resulting from a higher share of power sold to the grid, revised tariffs in the second half of the year, and unfavorable currency translation effects, which negatively impacted revenues by SEK 18.0 million. Operating expenses grew by 5.5 percent to SEK 166.2 million, primarily due to increased depreciation and maintenance costs associated with the expanding portfolio. Consequently, operating profit (EBIT) improved by 21.1 percent to SEK 73.5 million, reflecting stable operations and improved underlying profitability.

Electricity generation reached 327.8 million kWh, a 30 percent increase from the previous year, reducing CO2 emissions by approximately 161,000 tonnes. The company expanded its portfolio to 216 installations with a total installed capacity of 395.8 MW, up from 340.1 MW at the end of 2024. This growth was supported by the installation of 55.7 MW of new capacity during the year. Gigasun signed 17 new contracts representing 41.7 MW of capacity, although the backlog of signed orders decreased to 16.4 MW from 38.8 MW in the prior year. The average remaining contract time for the portfolio stood at 15.9 years.

Financially, the company recorded a loss for the period of SEK -7.2 million, compared to a loss of SEK -32.9 million in 2024. The improved result was impacted by a non-cash currency loss of SEK -3.8 million and a one-off cost of SEK 3.5 million. Adjusted for these items, the profit amounted to SEK 0.1 million, demonstrating a clear improvement in core operational profitability. Interest expenses decreased by 8.5 percent to SEK 76.9 million, benefiting from ongoing refinancing activities in China. Gigasun entered into agreements with Agricultural Bank Financial Leasing Co., Ltd to refinance approximately SEK 1,242 million, extending the amortization period to 10 years and securing a lower interest rate of 3.60 percent. This refinancing is expected to further reduce financing costs and improve cash flow.

The equity ratio declined to 21 percent from 23 percent, and interest-bearing debt decreased to SEK 1,382.6 million from SEK 1,483.1 million. The company maintains its long-term target of reaching 1,000 MW of installed capacity. The SOLT4 bond was extended to November 2026, providing additional financial flexibility. The Board of Directors proposes no dividend payment for 2025, prioritizing growth and capital structure stability.
