Eleving Group reported strong financial performance during the year ended December 31, 2024, characterized by revenue growth, record profitability, and portfolio expansion across its core markets. Total revenue increased by 14.4% to EUR 216.6 million, compared with EUR 189.3 million in the previous year, driven primarily by higher interest revenue and increased fee and commission income related to financing activities. Profitability improved significantly, with net profit before foreign exchange results rising by 14.9% to EUR 32.5 million, and total net profit reaching EUR 29.6 million, representing a 21.0% increase compared with EUR 24.5 million reported in the previous year. Adjusted for a one-off tax expense related to a Romanian tax audit, total net profit amounted to EUR 32.2 million. The company achieved an all-time high adjusted EBITDA of EUR 89.8 million, up from EUR 77.5 million in the prior year. The financial results were supported by steady macroeconomic conditions in core regions, stable operational execution, and the successful integration of previously acquired operations, particularly in Sub-Saharan Africa. The net portfolio of vehicle and consumer financing grew by 15.9% to EUR 371.2 million at the end of the period, compared with EUR 320.3 million a year earlier. Impairment expenses for loans and advances to customers totaled EUR 42.1 million, reflecting active management of bad debt cessions alongside portfolio growth. A major strategic development during the reporting period was the successful initial public offering of the company in October, which raised EUR 29.0 million in equity proceeds and strengthened its capital position while expanding its presence on public capital markets. Total equity increased to EUR 108.1 million as of December 31, 2024, compared with EUR 65.4 million at the end of the previous period, resulting in a stronger capitalization ratio of 29.3%. Overall, the reporting period reflected successful strategic execution, continued business expansion, and improved financial resilience across the group.
