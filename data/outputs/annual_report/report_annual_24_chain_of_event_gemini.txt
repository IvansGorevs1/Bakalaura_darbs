For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, Trophy Games Development A/S reported significant financial growth and operational scaling, driven primarily by the successful expansion of its Transport Games Series. Total group revenue and other operating income increased by 40% to DKK 126,523 thousand, up from DKK 90,070 thousand in 2024. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization grew by 26% to DKK 31,117 thousand compared to DKK 24,671 thousand in the previous year, resulting in an EBITDA margin of 25%. Profit before tax reached DKK 19,013 thousand, representing a 109% increase over the DKK 9,068 thousand reported in 2024, yielding an improved EBT margin of 15%. Net profit for the year was DKK 12,539 thousand compared to DKK 7,471 thousand in 2024, and basic earnings per share rose to DKK 0.47 from DKK 0.28. 

The financial performance was heavily influenced by the strong commercial execution and scaling of core titles within the Transport Series, specifically Airline Manager, Truck Manager, and Farm Manager. Airline Manager contributed revenue of DKK 37,787 thousand, reflecting a 28% increase. Truck Manager experienced substantial growth following foundational improvements, with revenue surging by 1,971% to DKK 25,181 thousand. Farm Manager, successfully launched in July 2025 following five million pre-registrations, generated DKK 22,002 thousand during its first six months. Together, these three flagship titles formed the Big 3, generating 67% of total 2025 revenue and 65% of EBITDA. In contrast, revenue from smaller transport titles such as Energy Manager declined by 46% to DKK 20,955 thousand as marketing resources were strategically reallocated toward the higher-velocity Big 3. Legacy titles remained relatively stable, generating DKK 20,598 thousand in revenue. 

A pivotal strategic development in late 2025 was the optimisation of in-app advertising across the portfolio. Total in-app advertising revenue doubled to DKK 18,476 thousand, representing 14.6% of total revenue. Because in-app advertising carries zero platform fees compared to traditional in-app purchases which face commission rates of up to 30% from providers like Apple and Google, this shift enhanced direct cost efficiency and supported margin stability. To fuel user acquisition, the company expanded its marketing budget by 76% to DKK 40,486 thousand, focusing on titles with projected short payback periods. Direct costs rose 34% to DKK 30,460 thousand to support higher transaction volumes. 

The balance sheet remained robust with total assets growing by 24% to DKK 84,597 thousand, supported by a strong solvency ratio of 78% and an absence of interest-bearing external debt. Total equity increased to DKK 66,120 thousand, delivering a return on equity of 20%. Cash at bank and in hand ended the year at DKK 23,049 thousand, down 9% due to record marketing investments, a DKK 4,894 thousand share buy-back program, a temporary increase in other receivables including a DKK 14 million VAT receivable, and an extraordinary DKK 4.7 million repayment of historical tax credits following a reassessment by Danish tax authorities regarding game development R&D classifications. The company looks ahead to 2026 with revenue guidance projected between DKK 130 million and DKK 153 million, EBITDA expected between DKK 28 million and DKK 36 million, and EBT expected between DKK 18 million and DKK 24 million.
