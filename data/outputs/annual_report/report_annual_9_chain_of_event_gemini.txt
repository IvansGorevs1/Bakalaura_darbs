For the financial year 2025, Impero A/S reported recognized revenue of DKK 42,014 thousand, representing a 19% increase compared to DKK 35,431 thousand in 2024. This top-line growth was supported by continued commercial focus and platform development across core markets. Gross profit reached DKK 25,900 thousand compared to DKK 18,785 thousand in the previous year. Operating profit improved to negative DKK 6,718 thousand from negative DKK 11,653 thousand in 2024. EBITDA also improved significantly, ending at negative DKK 3,113 thousand compared to negative DKK 8,714 thousand in 2024. The net loss for the period was DKK 6,835 thousand, marking an improvement from the net loss of DKK 11,447 thousand reported in 2024. Earnings per share were negative DKK 0.29, compared to negative DKK 0.49 in the prior year. 

Annual Recurring Revenue reached DKK 46.2M at the end of 2025, corresponding to a 19% year-on-year growth rate from DKK 38.7M on 31 December 2024. This ARR growth rate was in the upper end of the revised guidance range of 11% to 19%. SaaS metrics remained solid, highlighted by an exceptionally low churn rate of 1% and a Net Revenue Retention rate of 106%, compared to 108% in 2024. Average ARR per account rose to a record high of DKK 228 thousand, up 8% from the previous year, supported by a land-and-expand strategy and a gross uplift of 12%. 

Financial performance was influenced by market headwinds and geopolitical and macroeconomic uncertainty during the first half of the year, which constrained net new ARR generation early on. However, market conditions improved in the second half, leading to the company's best second half ever for net new ARR. Own work capitalized increased to DKK 5,190 thousand from DKK 3,621 thousand in 2024 due to an increased focus on developing new features. Cost of sales decreased by 16% to DKK 990 thousand, while other external expenses and staff costs increased by 6% to DKK 20,314 thousand and DKK 29,013 thousand, respectively, driven by commercial investments, new hires, and salary adjustments. 

In terms of capital position and cash flows, Impero strengthened its financial flexibility through a directed issue raising DKK 15M in December 2025, alongside warrant exercises. Consequently, cash and cash equivalents increased to DKK 18,900 thousand as of 31 December 2025, compared to DKK 9,798 thousand at the end of 2024. Cash flow from ordinary operating activities improved to negative DKK 1,279 thousand from negative DKK 4,252 thousand in 2024. Cash flow from investing activities was negative DKK 5,640 thousand, primarily impacted by additions of intangible assets related to software development. Cash flow from financing activities was DKK 16,089 thousand, driven by the capital increase. Total equity increased to DKK 15,413 thousand at year-end 2025 from DKK 6,159 thousand in 2024. 

Following the improved capital position and solid SaaS metrics, the Board of Directors concluded that growth investments will create more long-term shareholder value than pursuing cash flow positivity before the end of 2026. Consequently, Impero deferred its ambition of achieving recurring positive cash flow by the end of 2026 and plans to accelerate commercial investments. For the full year 2026, Impero expects ARR to reach between DKK 53M and 57M, reflecting a growth rate of approximately 15% to 23%, and anticipates EBITDA to be in the range of negative DKK 2M to positive DKK 1M.
