Lyko delivered continued growth in 2025, although profitability weakened as investments and implementation costs increased. Net sales rose 11.3% to SEK 3,962.6 million, compared with SEK 3,561.7 million in 2024. Growth was driven primarily by the Nordic segment, where sales increased 12.0% to SEK 3,699.8 million and accounted for 93.4% of group sales. Norway surpassed SEK 1 billion in annual revenue. Europe remained broadly stagnant, with sales declining 1.3% to SEK 130.7 million; the company began reorganizing the segment by closing local offices in Germany and the Netherlands and centralizing operations.

Gross profit increased to SEK 1,658.7 million from SEK 1,568.8 million, but the gross margin declined from 44.0% to 41.9%. EBIT fell to SEK 78.2 million from SEK 117.4 million, reducing the EBIT margin from 3.3% to 2.0%. EBITDA declined to SEK 252.3 million from SEK 287.4 million. The weaker operating result, together with financial expenses of SEK 82.7 million, resulted in a loss before tax of SEK 0.9 million, compared with a profit of SEK 37.6 million in 2024. Net loss was SEK 3.0 million, compared with net profit of SEK 21.0 million, and earnings per share decreased to negative SEK 0.20 from SEK 1.37.

The main factors affecting performance were substantial investments in infrastructure, stores, technology, and brand development. The new automated warehouse expansion in Vansbro, an investment of approximately SEK 500 million, began operating during the year and is expected to be fully operational in early 2026. The system is designed to handle approximately 100,000 orders per day and affected results during its implementation. Lyko also opened stores in Espoo and Sandnes/Stavanger and launched its first 500-square-meter Fab Store in Täby. The company relaunched Make Up Store and continued expanding its own-brand portfolio, while customer engagement remained strong through Lyko Community.

Cash flow from operating activities improved substantially to SEK 527.5 million from SEK 209.7 million. Investing cash flow increased to a negative SEK 351.2 million, mainly reflecting warehouse automation, store openings, and digital development. Cash and cash equivalents rose to SEK 452.5 million from SEK 140.5 million, while net debt excluding IFRS 16 declined to SEK 292.5 million from SEK 333.1 million. However, the equity ratio fell to 14.8% from 20.3% as assets and borrowing increased. No dividend is proposed for 2025. After year-end, Lyko announced a cost-savings program expected to generate approximately SEK 100 million in annual savings, excluding restructuring costs.
