Trophy Games delivered a landmark performance in fiscal year 2025, characterized by significant strategic execution and operational validation. The company achieved a 40% year-over-year increase in revenue, reaching DKK 126.5 million, compared to DKK 90.1 million in 2024. This growth was primarily driven by the successful scaling of its Transport Series, particularly the flagship titles Airline Manager, Truck Manager, and Farm Manager, which collectively accounted for 67% of total revenue. EBITDA grew by 26% to DKK 31.1 million, while Profit Before Tax (EBT) surged by 109% to DKK 19.0 million. The company materially exceeded its initial guidance, which had projected revenue between DKK 85 million and DKK 106 million, revising its outlook upward four times throughout the year as performance consistently outpaced expectations.

The revenue expansion was fueled by the launch of Farm Manager in July 2025, which contributed DKK 22.0 million in its first six months, and the continued scaling of Truck Manager, which saw revenue jump from DKK 1.2 million in 2024 to DKK 25.2 million in 2025. Airline Manager remained a stable cornerstone, generating DKK 37.8 million. Conversely, revenue from other transport titles declined by 46% to DKK 21.0 million as marketing resources were reallocated to the higher-performing core titles. A key driver of profitability was the strategic optimization of in-app advertising, which doubled to DKK 18.5 million. This shift improved direct cost efficiency, as advertising revenue is not subject to the platform fees that apply to in-app purchases. Consequently, direct costs rose by 34% to DKK 30.5 million, while marketing expenses increased by 76% to DKK 40.5 million to support user acquisition.

Despite the increased investment in marketing and development, the EBITDA margin decreased slightly to 25% from 27% in 2024, reflecting the targeted spend to capture market share. The EBT margin improved to 15%, largely because 2024 included significant non-recurring impairment costs of DKK 4.9 million. Adjusting for these items, the underlying profitability remained consistent. The company maintained a robust balance sheet with a solvency ratio of 78% and no interest-bearing debt. Cash at bank decreased by 9% to DKK 23.0 million due to strategic investments, including a DKK 4.9 million share buyback program and a one-time repayment of DKK 4.7 million in previously received tax credits following a reassessment by Danish tax authorities.

Looking ahead to 2026, the company guides for revenue between DKK 130 million and DKK 153 million, representing growth of 3% to 21%. EBITDA is expected to range from DKK 28 million to DKK 36 million. This outlook assumes a marketing budget of DKK 45 million to DKK 50 million and ad revenue growth to DKK 22 million–DKK 27 million. The company continues to invest in its product pipeline, with pre-production underway for Train Manager 2 and Space Manager, aiming to expand its ecosystem of simulation titles. The overall development in 2025 demonstrates a successful transition to a scalable, data-driven growth model with strong user engagement and diversified revenue streams.
