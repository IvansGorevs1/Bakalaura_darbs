Lyko Group reported net revenue of SEK 3,962.6 million for the full year 2025, representing an 11.3 percent increase from SEK 3,561.7 million in 2024. This growth was primarily driven by the Nordic segment, where sales rose 12.0 percent to SEK 3,699.8 million, accounting for 93.4 percent of total revenue. In contrast, the Europe segment experienced a slight decline of 1.3 percent, with net sales decreasing to SEK 130.7 million. The company welcomed over 350,000 new customers during the fourth quarter, reinforcing its position as the leading beauty destination in the Nordics.

Despite the top-line growth, profitability metrics deteriorated compared to the previous year. Gross profit increased to SEK 1,658.7 million, but the gross profit margin contracted to 41.9 percent from 44.0 percent in 2024. Operating profit (EBIT) fell significantly to SEK 78.2 million from SEK 117.4 million, resulting in an EBIT margin of 2.0 percent compared to 3.3 percent previously. This decline in operating performance was attributed to investments in infrastructure, store expansion, and brand development, as well as the ramp-up of new warehouse automation in Vansbro. The implementation of this automation, the company’s largest historical investment, affected results during parts of the year but is expected to provide structural cost advantages and higher capacity for future growth.

The net result for the year was a loss of SEK 3.0 million, a sharp reversal from the profit of SEK 21.0 million recorded in 2024. Earnings per share decreased to negative SEK 0.20 from SEK 1.37. Depreciation and amortization expenses increased to SEK 174.1 million, and tax expenses amounted to SEK 2.1 million. Financial items, including interest expenses, contributed to the negative net result. However, cash flow from operating activities improved substantially to SEK 527.5 million from SEK 209.7 million in the prior year, driven by strong working capital management.

Strategic developments during the period included the opening of Lyko’s first Fab Store in Täby Centrum, a scalable retail concept designed to enhance customer experience. The company also continued expanding its physical store network, opening new locations in Norway and Finland. Additionally, Lyko relaunched its iconic Make Up Store brand and launched new own brands such as Scandinavian Soap Factory and PLY Skin. The Lyko Community platform saw high engagement, with 21 million views in November alone. Looking ahead, the company announced a cost savings program in January 2026 aimed at generating annual savings of approximately SEK 100 million, following a restructuring cost of SEK 22 million in the first quarter. The Board of Directors proposed no dividend for the 2025 financial year, prioritizing cash flows for continued expansion.
