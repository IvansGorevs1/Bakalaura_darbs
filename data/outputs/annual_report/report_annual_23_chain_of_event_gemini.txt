For the financial year ended December 31, 2025, TKM Grupp AS reported audited consolidated sales revenue of 919.6 million euros, representing a decrease of 2.6% compared to 944.6 million euros in 2024. Profit before tax declined by 31.4% year-on-year to 24.3 million euros, while net profit decreased by 36.1% to 17.5 million euros from 27.5 million euros in the previous year. Basic and diluted earnings per share dropped to 0.43 euros from 0.67 euros in 2024. Operating profit for the period stood at 34.9 million euros compared to 47.5 million euros a year earlier, and EBITDA decreased by 14.7% to 77.4 million euros. Gross profit reached 254.6 million euros, down from 259.8 million euros in 2024, maintaining a stable gross margin of 27.7%. Total assets decreased to 697.9 million euros as of December 31, 2025, from 706.6 million euros at the end of 2024, and total equity slightly increased to 263.7 million euros from 261.5 million euros. Cash and cash equivalents stood at 29.5 million euros at year-end, down from 45.4 million euros at the close of 2024. Total investments made during 2025 amounted to 25.5 million euros, compared to 36.6 million euros in the prior year.

The performance decline was primarily driven by a challenging economic environment characterized by low consumer confidence due to uncertain geopolitical conditions and domestic tax changes, notably the introduction of the motor vehicle tax in Estonia. This tax led to a sharp decrease in demand and a contraction in the Estonian automotive retail sector, causing sales in the Group's car trade segment to fall by 11.9% to 176.9 million euros and segment net profit to drop by 54.4% to 4.3 million euros. Total new passenger car sales in Estonia declined by 48.6% year-on-year. The supermarkets segment, representing the largest share of revenue, proved resilient with a slight revenue increase of 0.3% to 611.9 million euros, although net profit in the segment decreased by 38.6% to 9.5 million euros due to increased share of promotional products and cost pressures. The department stores segment experienced a 1.1% revenue decrease to 103.0 million euros and recorded a net loss of 0.5 million euros. The security segment saw external revenue decline by 8.1% to 20.1 million euros, resulting in a nominal net profit. Conversely, the real estate segment grew external revenue by 5.1% to 7.7 million euros, supported by rental income from a newly leased logistics centre, though segment net profit increased by 20.5% to 6.3 million euros despite the prior year's comparative base including one-off property sale gains and fair value revaluations. Overall financial results were positively influenced by a reduction in borrowing costs, saving 1.5 million euros in interest expenses, and lower income tax expense due to deferred tax recalculations, while labor costs increased by 3.7% and other operating expenses remained stable with a 0.2% annual growth.
