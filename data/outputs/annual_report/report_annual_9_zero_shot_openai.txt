Impero delivered resilient growth in 2025 despite geopolitical and macroeconomic uncertainty, particularly in the first half of the year. Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) increased 19% year on year to DKK 46.2 million at 31 December 2025, at the upper end of the revised guidance range of DKK 43–46 million. Revenue rose 19% to DKK 42.0 million from DKK 35.4 million in 2024. Growth regained momentum in the second half, with fourth-quarter net new ARR of DKK 3.1 million compared with DKK 1.8 million in the corresponding quarter of 2024, although full-year net new ARR declined to DKK 7.5 million from DKK 8.3 million.

Profitability improved substantially. EBITDA was negative DKK 3.1 million, compared with negative DKK 8.7 million in 2024, and operating loss narrowed to DKK 6.7 million from DKK 11.7 million. The improvement reflected higher revenue alongside more limited increases in staff costs and other external expenses, which rose 6% each. Cost of sales decreased 16%, lifting the contribution margin to 98% from 97%. The net loss was DKK 6.8 million, compared with DKK 11.4 million in 2024. Development costs capitalized increased to DKK 5.2 million from DKK 3.6 million, supporting continued platform investment.

SaaS metrics remained strong. Churn fell to 1% from 2%, while Net Revenue Retention decreased modestly to 106% from 108%. Gross uplift from existing customers was 12%, and Average ARR per Account reached a record DKK 228,000, up 8% from DKK 212,000. The customer base exceeded 200 after 26 new customer wins, and the top-10 customer concentration declined to 20% from 24% in 2024, indicating greater diversification. Denmark and the DACH region were the main growth markets, with ARR growth of 22% and 26%, respectively; ARR from other markets declined 6%.

Cash flow also improved. Cash flow from ordinary operating activities was negative DKK 1.3 million, compared with negative DKK 4.3 million in 2024, while year-end cash increased to DKK 18.9 million from DKK 9.8 million. The improvement was supported by a DKK 15 million directed issue in December, alongside warrant exercises. Impero launched its Control Testing module, introduced initial AI functionality, expanded its German partner network, and continued investing in its platform. For 2026, the company expects ARR of DKK 53–57 million and EBITDA between negative DKK 2 million and positive DKK 1 million. Commercial investments will accelerate, and the ambition of recurring positive cash flow before the end of 2026 has been deferred.
