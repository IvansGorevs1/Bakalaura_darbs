Infortar reported strong operating growth in 2025, although net profit declined because 2024 included significant one-off gains related to the initial consolidation of Tallink. Group revenue increased 33.9% to EUR 1.837 billion from EUR 1.372 billion, while EBITDA rose 56.9% to EUR 227.9 million from EUR 145.3 million. The EBITDA margin improved to 12.4% from 10.6%, and operating profit increased 41.5% to EUR 109.0 million. The results benefited from Tallink being consolidated for the full year in 2025, compared with only five months in 2024, as well as an approximately EUR 12 million improvement in Elenger’s EBITDA and contributions from the newly consolidated Estonia Farmid and Halinga businesses.

Net profit fell 63.9% to EUR 69.9 million, while profit attributable to the parent decreased to EUR 70.6 million from EUR 191.3 million. The prior-year result included a EUR 93.7 million gain from the bargain purchase of Tallink and related initial-consolidation effects. Earnings per share declined to EUR 3.43 from EUR 9.36. Profitability excluding these one-off effects improved, as reflected in the higher operating profit and EBITDA.

Energy remained the largest revenue contributor, generating EUR 980.2 million, compared with EUR 966.2 million in 2024, while maritime transport revenue reached EUR 761.7 million, compared with EUR 316.8 million in the consolidated period of 2024. Tallink carried 5.53 million passengers, down 0.9% year on year; cargo volumes declined 19.2% and passenger vehicles 2.2%. Demand was affected by economic uncertainty and geopolitical tensions. In energy, regional gas consumption fell 9% to 39.7 TWh, although Elenger delivered 18 TWh of LNG cargoes, maintaining an approximately one-quarter market share in the Finland–Baltic region. Gas prices remained volatile.

The Group strengthened its financial position. Total assets decreased 4.4% to EUR 2.597 billion, while equity increased to EUR 1.175 billion from EUR 1.166 billion, lifting the equity ratio to 45% from 43%. Interest-bearing liabilities declined 12.4% to EUR 1.071 billion and net debt fell 19.3% to EUR 851.6 million; net debt to EBITDA improved to 3.7 from 4.0. Operating cash flow increased substantially to EUR 384.0 million from EUR 89.3 million, and cash rose to EUR 219.8 million. The Group paid EUR 62 million in dividends, or EUR 3 per share, and proposed a 2025 dividend of EUR 3.02 per share.
