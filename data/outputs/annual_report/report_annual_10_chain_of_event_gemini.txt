During the 2025 financial year, InCoax Networks AB reported net sales of SEK 48,055,007, representing a 35 percent decrease compared to SEK 74,497,775 in 2024. This decline in revenue was primarily driven by timing shifts in customer orders and a more cautious market. Geographically, net sales in North America amounted to SEK 39,578,479 compared to SEK 69,556,761 in the previous year, while EU sales were SEK 3,172,469 compared to SEK 4,559,094, and other regions accounted for SEK 5,304,059 compared to SEK 381,920.

Despite the lower top-line revenue, the company achieved a gross profit of SEK 29,919,750, down from SEK 42,179,123 in 2024, while the gross margin improved to 62 percent from 57 percent. Operating expenses decreased to SEK 105,896,000 from SEK 121,245,000 in the previous year. Costs for goods for resale fell to SEK 18,135,257 from SEK 32,318,652 due to lower sales volumes, and other external costs decreased to SEK 42,755,237 from SEK 49,840,502. Conversely, personnel costs increased to SEK 40,427,773 compared to SEK 38,201,669 in 2024. 

The company recorded an operating loss (EBIT) of SEK -32,380,175, reflecting both the reduced revenue and continued high investments in product development and commercial expansion. This compared to an operating loss of SEK -19,378,406 in 2024. The net result after tax for 2025 amounted to a loss of SEK -32,559,663, compared to a loss of SEK -19,150,134 in the preceding year. Earnings per share worsened to SEK -0.25 from SEK -0.18, and diluted earnings per share were SEK -0.24 compared to SEK -0.17. 

Cash flow from operating activities improved to SEK -20,011,617 from SEK -27,117,256 in 2024. Investing activities resulted in a cash flow of SEK -25,269,745 compared to SEK -25,706,339, driven entirely by capitalized development costs for next-generation products. Financing activities contributed a positive cash flow of SEK 51,797,480 compared to SEK 40,065,462 in 2024, supported by completed financing arrangements including share issues that provided SEK 33,509,996 before issue costs, a new borrowing of SEK 20,000,000, and loan amortization. Total cash flow including financing activities was positive at SEK 6,516,117, an improvement from SEK -12,758,133 in 2024. Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the period stood at SEK 8,748,046, up from SEK 2,231,929 at the start of the year. 

Total assets as of December 31, 2025, increased to SEK 152,839,491 from SEK 136,815,074, driven by higher capitalized development expenditures, which reached SEK 117,992,681 compared to SEK 92,722,936. Total equity stood at SEK 109,778,755, down slightly from SEK 110,540,939, resulting in an equity ratio of 71.8 percent compared to 80.8 percent in 2024. Total liabilities increased to SEK 43,060,736 from SEK 26,274,135.
