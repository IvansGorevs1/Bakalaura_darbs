The company reported strong financial and operational performance during the reporting period, driven by record-high revenue generation and sustained portfolio expansion across European and African markets. Total revenue increased by 15.5% to reach EUR 250.0 million compared to the previous year. This top-line growth was supported by well-balanced contributions across core business lines, including traditional vehicle financing at EUR 67.0 million, flexible vehicle financing at EUR 60.3 million, smartphone financing at EUR 7.9 million following a new product launch, and consumer lending products at EUR 114.8 million. 

Profitability indicators also demonstrated positive development. Adjusted EBITDA reached EUR 101.9 million, representing a 13.5% increase year-on-year. Profit before tax rose to EUR 41.9 million compared with EUR 37.7 million in the prior period, while profit from continuing operations stood at EUR 29.2 million. Total net profit for the period was EUR 29.2 million, remaining stable relative to the previous year. The net loan portfolio grew by 20.3% to EUR 446.3 million at the end of the year, supported by all-time high twelve-month loan issuances totaling EUR 458.0 million. 

The financial results were significantly influenced by strategic expansion initiatives, product diversification, and active capital management. The company entered the Tanzanian market with motorcycle financing, expanded its product offering by introducing installment loans in several European markets, and successfully launched smartphone financing in Kenya and Uganda, which achieved a loan portfolio of EUR 13.5 million by year-end. Operational efficiency improvements also contributed to results, reflected in a reduced cost-to-income ratio and ongoing tests of automated solutions such as artificial intelligence voice agents. 

During the period, the company was active in capital markets, marking the first anniversary of its initial public offering and successfully completing two major bond transactions. In March, a EUR 40 million bond tap was executed, followed in October by the refinancing of EUR 150 million in maturing bonds alongside the issuance of a new EUR 275 million senior secured bond, representing the largest bond transaction in the company’s history. In parallel, the company distributed EUR 19.65 million in dividends during 2025, representing a 10% cash yield based on the initial public offering price. Overall, the reporting period was characterized by robust business growth, successful execution of strategic expansion, and strengthened capital and funding structures.
