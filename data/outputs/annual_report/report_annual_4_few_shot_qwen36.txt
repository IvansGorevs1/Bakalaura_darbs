Eleving Group delivered its strongest financial performance to date in 2024, characterized by robust revenue growth, improved profitability, and significant strategic milestones. The company reported total revenue of EUR 216.6 million, representing a 14.4% increase compared to EUR 189.3 million in 2023. This growth was driven by strong demand across its vehicle and consumer finance segments, supported by successful digitalization efforts that nearly doubled the number of loan applications received. The net loan portfolio expanded by 16.0% to reach EUR 371.2 million, reflecting continued market expansion and effective scaling of operations, particularly in Sub-Saharan Africa and Eastern Europe.

Profitability metrics showed marked improvement. Total net profit reached EUR 29.6 million, an increase of 21% from EUR 24.5 million in the previous year. Adjusted for a one-off Romanian VAT liability of EUR 2.6 million, the underlying net profit amounted to EUR 32.2 million, indicating growth of more than 31%. The Group’s adjusted EBITDA rose by 16% to EUR 89.8 million, demonstrating enhanced operational efficiency and margin expansion despite the rapid scaling of the business. The capitalization ratio improved to 29.3% from 26.1% in 2023, strengthening the Group’s balance sheet and providing ample headroom for debt covenants.

A defining event of the reporting period was the successful initial public offering (IPO) in October 2024, which raised EUR 29.0 million in equity. This transaction, the largest in Nasdaq Riga’s history, allowed the Group to repay high-cost subordinated bonds and reduce overall leverage. The IPO also marked a transition to public equity markets, enhancing the Group’s capital base and access to diverse funding sources. The Group continued to diversify its funding profile, increasing local currency financing in key markets such as Kenya and Botswana, and optimizing costs on the Mintos peer-to-peer platform.

Operationally, the Group maintained strict underwriting discipline, which contributed to an improvement in portfolio quality. The non-performing loan (NPL) rate for vehicle finance decreased from 7.5% to 6.1%, while the consumer finance NPL rate fell from 4.5% to 4.3%. The Group also completed the integration of its Sub-Saharan operations acquired in 2023, tripling the portfolio size in the region. Furthermore, Eleving Group accelerated its sustainability agenda, financing nearly 2,000 electric motorcycles in East Africa, which saved an estimated 1,000 metric tonnes of CO2 and allowed the company to meet its 2025 electric vehicle deployment target a year early.

Looking ahead, the Group aims to nearly double its business size within the next two years through organic growth, product innovation, and geographic expansion. The strategy focuses on deepening presence in existing markets, launching new consumer loan products, and entering new geographies, while maintaining a disciplined approach to risk management and capital allocation. The successful IPO and strong financial results in 2024 have positioned Eleving Group to capitalize on these opportunities with a reinforced balance sheet and enhanced market credibility.
