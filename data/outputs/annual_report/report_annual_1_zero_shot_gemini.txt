During the 2025 reporting period, AAC Clyde Space experienced mixed financial results characterized by a decrease in net sales alongside continued positive EBITDA. Consolidated net sales for the group decreased by 16 percent to SEK 295.3 million compared to SEK 352.9 million in the previous year. Total revenue for the group amounted to SEK 337.8 million down from SEK 408.8 million in 2024. The company generated a positive EBITDA of SEK 17.5 million, marking three consecutive years of positive EBITDA, though down from SEK 46.7 million in 2024. The operating result (EBIT) declined to SEK -40.0 million compared to SEK -4.0 million in the prior year. The net result after tax for the period worsened to a loss of SEK -46.6 million compared to a loss of SEK -5.7 million in 2024, resulting in earnings per share of SEK -7.63 compared to SEK -1.00 in the previous reporting period. 

The financial performance was significantly affected by specific business events and operational factors, notably programme-related delays that impacted revenues and cash flow. Specifically, technical discussions between an end customer and a subcontractor delayed milestone recognition, shifting approximately SEK 32 million of revenue into 2026. Furthermore, political and procurement developments postponed the initiation of the EPS-Sterna programme, the commissioning of two Sedna satellites took longer than planned, and the launch of the first two VIREON satellites was rescheduled by the launch provider. Despite these hurdles, the Data & Services segment grew by 80 percent, with net sales reaching SEK 80.1 million compared to SEK 44.9 million in 2024, and segment EBITDA increasing to SEK 27.8 million from SEK 21.1 million. Conversely, the Products & Missions segment recorded net sales of SEK 245.0 million down from SEK 346.0 million, and a segment EBITDA of SEK 16.8 million compared to SEK 51.6 million in the previous year. 

Cash flow and liquidity indicators showed negative movements during the year. Operating cash flow dropped to SEK -73.0 million compared to positive cash flow of SEK 57.0 million in 2024. Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the period stood at SEK 30.1 million down from SEK 49.7 million at the end of 2024. Total available cash, including an expanded bank overdraft facility of SEK 50.0 million where SEK 0 was utilized, amounted to SEK 80.1 million compared to SEK 70.8 million previously. Contract liabilities decreased from SEK 97.9 million to SEK 51.0 million as customer prepayments were converted to net sales. The group maintained a strong equity ratio of 80 percent compared to 71 percent at the end of 2024, supported by capital-raising activities including directed share issues to strengthen the capital base and invest in satellite constellations. 

Overall, while the company faced temporary delays and reduced net sales and operating results during 2025, its strategic transition toward recurring data revenues advanced successfully, supported by institutional investments and major milestones achieved after year-end, including a record order from OHB Sweden valued at EUR 76.3 million for the EPS-Sterna weather constellation.
