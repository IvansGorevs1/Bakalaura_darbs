Konsolidator A/S delivered improved financial and operational performance during the financial year ended December 31, 2025, driven by the execution of its Resilient Growth strategy. The company advanced its transition from a single consolidation tool to a comprehensive financial reporting platform, the Konsolidator Suite, which now integrates financial consolidation, a financial data warehouse, analytics and reporting via The Hub, and a newly developed financial planning and analysis tool. 

For the full year 2025, consolidated revenue increased by 25 percent to DKK 25.28 million, compared to DKK 20.27 million in 2024. Subscription fees rose to DKK 20.62 million from DKK 18.28 million, while onboarding and consulting fees grew significantly by 134 percent to DKK 4.66 million, up from DKK 1.99 million, driven by price adjustments initiated in early 2025. Total contribution reached DKK 23.39 million compared to DKK 19.03 million in the previous year. 

Profitability metrics demonstrated substantial improvement. The EBITDA loss narrowed by 77 percent to DKK 1.99 million in 2025 from DKK 9.29 million in 2024. Notably, the second half of 2025 achieved a positive EBITDA of DKK 0.54 million, compared to an EBITDA loss of DKK 3.63 million in the second half of 2024. The EBIT loss for 2025 was DKK 5.08 million, showing an improvement from the DKK 12.06 million loss reported in 2024. The loss for the year was DKK 6.62 million, down from DKK 14.87 million in 2024. 

Key SaaS metrics reflected positive momentum across the business. Contracted Annual Recurring Revenue (CARR) increased by 15 percent year-over-year to reach DKK 24.39 million at year-end 2025, exceeding guidance of DKK 23 million to DKK 24 million. The Rule of 40 score improved by 57 percentage points to 17 percent at the end of 2025, compared to negative 40 percent in 2024. Operational efficiency strengthened as the Customer Acquisition Cost payback period dropped from 43 months in 2024 to 23 months in 2025, and the Customer Lifetime Value to Customer Acquisition Cost ratio improved to 4.1 from 2.7. Net retention remained stable at 94 percent, while the ARR churn rate increased to 12.6 percent from 10.5 percent. Konsolidator added 52 new customers during the year, bringing the total active customer base to 283, and increased the average ARR per customer to DKK 87,000 from DKK 70,000. Partner-driven sales accounted for 38 percent of new customer acquisitions, up from 22 percent in 2024. 

Cash flow generation also improved over the reporting period. Cash flow from operating activities was negative DKK 1.05 million in 2025, representing a substantial improvement from negative DKK 9.17 million in 2024, supported by lower EBIT losses, increased deferred income from annual invoicing shifts, and improved working capital management. Net cash flow for the year was positive at DKK 11,000, compared to negative DKK 1.48 million in 2024. During 2025, the company secured DKK 4.8 million in net proceeds through two successful private share placements. Total assets at year-end 2025 stood at DKK 25.70 million, including cash and cash equivalents of DKK 0.37 million. Consolidated equity was negative DKK 3.58 million, primarily impacted by accumulated losses in the Spanish subsidiary Konsolidator Iberia, whereas the Danish parent company, Konsolidator A/S, reported a positive equity of DKK 0.70 million. Interest-bearing debt included credit institutions and other loans totaling DKK 12.72 million, of which DKK 3.29 million is current, following the commencement of fixed quarterly installments of DKK 1.2 million in July 2025.
