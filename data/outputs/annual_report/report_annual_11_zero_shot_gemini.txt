During the financial year 2025, JLT Mobile Computers AB reported net sales of SEK 131.5 million, representing an increase of 11.1 percent compared to SEK 118.4 million in the previous year. Order intake grew by 37.5 percent to SEK 142.0 million compared to SEK 103.0 million in 2024, supported in part by a major SEK 22 million deal with an American food producer. Gross profit reached SEK 50.4 million compared to SEK 54.2 million in 2024, while the gross margin declined by 7.5 percentage points to 38.3 percent down from 45.8 percent. The lower gross margin and earnings were heavily impacted by the US dollar losing nearly 20 percent against the Swedish krona during the year, alongside inventory revaluations based on current dollar purchase rates, despite 74 percent of sales being invoiced in dollars. Operating expenses decreased to SEK 52.4 million from SEK 57.3 million, driven by structural cost-saving measures including the discontinuation of subsidiary JLT Software Solutions AB and coordinated product development, which lowered overhead costs by SEK 5 million. Consequently, EBITDA improved to SEK -1.7 million from SEK -2.1 million, and operating profit strengthened significantly to SEK -1.9 million compared to SEK -9.7 million in the previous reporting period. Profit after tax improved to SEK -2.5 million compared to SEK -7.7 million, with earnings per share reported at SEK -0.09 compared to SEK -0.27 in 2024. The financial position reflected a reduction in inventories, which fell by SEK 12.7 million to SEK 25.5 million at year-end compared to SEK 38.3 million previously, improving capital efficiency and cash flow. Cash flow from operating activities was SEK 5.9 million compared to SEK 7.5 million, and total cash and cash equivalents increased to SEK 16.9 million from SEK 13.3 million. Total equity at the end of the period stood at SEK 41.8 million compared to SEK 47.6 million, with equity per share at SEK 1.45 compared to SEK 1.66, and solidity strengthened to 59 percent from 54 percent. The Board of Directors proposed to the Annual General Meeting that no dividend be paid for the fiscal year 2025, matching the SEK 0.00 dividend of the prior year. Business performance was affected by challenging external factors, including geopolitical uncertainty and subdued demand in the warehousing and logistics segment, which were partly counterbalanced by positive momentum in the port and mining segments as well as strategic product launches such as the industry-first 15-inch Full HD rugged vehicle-mount computer and AI-powered screen blanking safety solutions.
