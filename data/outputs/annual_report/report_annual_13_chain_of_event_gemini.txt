AS LHV Group reported strong financial performance for the year ended 31 December 2024, achieving its highest business volumes and operating profit in the company's history. Consolidated net profit reached EUR 150.3 million, representing a 7% increase compared to EUR 140.9 million reported for 2023. This profit growth was driven by a robust combination of asset expansion, effective cost management, and sustained credit quality. Total net income increased by 11% to EUR 338.3 million, supported by an 8% increase in net interest income to EUR 273.3 million, a 24% increase in net fee and commission income to EUR 60.3 million, and net other income rising to EUR 4.7 million. Operating expenses increased by 14% to EUR 146.9 million, primarily due to an increased number of employees to support business expansion. Profit before credit losses rose by 9% to EUR 191.4 million. Impairment losses on financial instruments measured at amortised cost increased to EUR 16.3 million compared to EUR 11.5 million in the previous period. Income tax expense rose to EUR 24.8 million from EUR 23.7 million. 

The financial position of the Group expanded significantly during 2024. Total consolidated assets reached EUR 8.7 billion, representing a 23% increase compared to EUR 7.1 billion at the end of 2023. The expansion in the balance sheet was driven by growth across all customer deposit segments and loan portfolios. Total deposits from customers increased by 21% to EUR 6.9 billion, compared to EUR 5.7 billion at the end of the previous year. This included financial intermediaries deposits of EUR 1.5 billion, while regular customer deposits in LHV Pank increased by 14% to EUR 5.6 billion. The net loan portfolio grew by 28% to EUR 4.6 billion, up from EUR 3.6 billion at the end of 2023. Retail loans expanded by 21% to EUR 1.9 billion, corporate loans in Estonia grew by 21% to EUR 2.3 billion, and SME loans in the United Kingdom grew by 338% to EUR 0.3 billion. Total shareholders equity increased to EUR 678.7 million from EUR 556.4 million. 

The financial performance was influenced by several key business events, developments, and market conditions. Following a supervisory transition, LHV managed to restore historically ambitious business growth, supported by a strong EUR 1 billion or 28% growth in the loan portfolio and a high base interest rate environment during the first half of the year. LHV Bank completed its first full year of operations under a full banking license acquired in 2023, expanding its UK-based corporate portfolio beyond the EUR 300 million mark and launching retail banking solutions late in the year. LHV Pank increased its customer base by 38,000 customers or 9% year-over-year to 456,000 customers, and active everyday payment users increased by 12% to 22,800. Assets under management by LHV Varahaldus increased by 3% to EUR 1.56 billion primarily due to positive fund returns. LHV Kindlustus achieved consistent growth in market share and profitability, recording its second consecutive profitable year with gross written premiums increasing by 21% to EUR 38.0 million. During the second half of 2024, interest rates experienced a swift decline as central banks, including the European Central Bank and the Bank of England, implemented rate cuts. To mitigate risks associated with the changing interest rate cycle and increased competition, the company implemented strategic cost control measures, including department restructurings and redundancies at the end of the year, while maintaining strong capital and liquidity buffers with a Common Equity Tier 1 ratio of 16.89% and a total capital adequacy ratio of 20.68%.
