Infortar Group reported a significant increase in revenue for the financial year 2025, reaching EUR 1,837 million, compared to EUR 1,372 million in 2024. This growth was primarily driven by the full-year consolidation of Tallink Grupp, which Infortar gained controlling influence over in August 2024, and improved performance in the energy segment. EBITDA also rose substantially to EUR 228 million from EUR 145 million in the previous year, reflecting the inclusion of Tallink’s full-year results and an approximate EUR 12 million improvement in Elenger’s EBITDA. The EBITDA margin expanded to 12.4% from 10.6% in 2024. Operating profit increased to EUR 109 million from EUR 77 million. However, net profit attributable to the owners of the parent decreased sharply to EUR 70.6 million from EUR 191.3 million in 2024. The report explicitly attributes this decline in net profit to one-off effects related to the initial consolidation of Tallink Group in 2024, which included a gain from bargain purchase and remeasurement of previously held interests that did not recur in 2025.

Total assets amounted to EUR 2,597 million, a decrease from EUR 2,715 million in 2024, while total equity increased to EUR 1,175 million from EUR 1,166 million. The equity ratio improved to 45% from 43%. Interest-bearing liabilities decreased to EUR 1,071 million from EUR 1,223 million, and net debt fell to EUR 852 million from EUR 1,056 million. The net debt to EBITDA ratio improved to 3.7 from 4.0. The group maintained a current ratio of 0.8. The decrease in loan liabilities reflects loan repayments across several group entities, including at the Tallink level.

In the maritime transport segment, Tallink carried 5.53 million passengers, a 0.9% decrease year-on-year, while cargo units transported fell by 19.2%. Revenue from vessel chartering helped balance seasonal fluctuations. In the energy segment, Elenger delivered 18 TWh of LNG cargoes, maintaining a market share of approximately one quarter in the Finland-Baltic region. Elenger expanded its renewable portfolio by commissioning a new solar power plant in Latvia and completing its first large-scale battery storage facility. In the real estate segment, a logistics center in Laagri was completed, and construction began on a commercial space for Depo. Supporting sector developments included the acquisition of Estonia Farmid and Oisu Biogaas, strengthening the group's agriculture and biomethane production capabilities. The group paid dividends of EUR 3 per share, totaling EUR 62 million.
