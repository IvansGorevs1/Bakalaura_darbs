Nordic Fibreboard AS reported a deterioration in financial performance during the 2025 fiscal year, characterized by a decline in revenue and a significant widening of net losses. Consolidated net sales decreased by 3.4% to €7.33 million, down from €7.59 million in 2024. This reduction was driven primarily by lower sales volumes in non-EU markets, particularly in Africa and Asia, while sales within the European Union, which constitutes 92% of total revenue, remained relatively stable. The fiberboard production segment, the Group’s core business, saw its sales drop slightly by 3% to €7.33 million. Additionally, revenue from real estate management fell sharply to €3,000 from €25,000 in the prior year, following the termination of rental agreements in August 2024 due to high operational costs that could not be covered by rental income.

Profitability metrics weakened considerably. The Group recorded a consolidated EBITDA of negative €0.42 million, compared to a negative €0.03 million in 2024, resulting in an EBITDA margin of negative 6%, a decrease of 6 percentage points. The negative EBITDA in both periods was largely attributable to factory shutdowns in December. In 2025, the shutdown was a planned transition to a new gas boiler house, whereas in 2024, it was an unplanned suspension due to structural damage from weather conditions. During these shutdowns, personnel costs, maintenance, and repair expenses continued to accrue, negatively impacting operating results. The consolidated net loss expanded to €1.15 million from €0.79 million in 2024. Operating loss increased to €0.94 million from €0.55 million, reflecting higher distribution and administrative expenses alongside the lower gross profit.

Despite the operating losses, the Group’s balance sheet showed growth in total assets, which rose to €9.3 million from €8.3 million, primarily due to investments in property, plant, and equipment and real estate. However, the working capital position deteriorated significantly, with negative working capital widening to €2.7 million from €0.7 million in 2024, a situation the management attributes to the negative financial results and difficult market conditions. To address liquidity and support ongoing operations, the Group completed a share issue in October 2025, raising €2.0 million. This capital injection increased total equity to €4.46 million and reduced the debt-to-equity ratio to 52% from 56% in the previous year. Total borrowings decreased to €3.2 million from €3.7 million, although covenant non-compliance with Coop Pank AS resulted in the reclassification of long-term debt as short-term.

Business developments included the merger of the subsidiary Pärnu Riverside Development OÜ into Nordic Fibreboard AS in June 2025 to simplify the corporate structure and consolidate ownership of the Admirali Quarter real estate development project. The Group also continued its investment in modern energy solutions, completing the upgrade of the boiler system in January 2026. Looking ahead, the management anticipates stable demand for fiberboards and plans to liquidate negative working capital through the sale of properties in the Admirali Quarter, for which a preliminary sale agreement was entered into in January 2026. The overall development of the company was marked by operational challenges and cost pressures, offset by strategic capital raising and structural simplification efforts.
