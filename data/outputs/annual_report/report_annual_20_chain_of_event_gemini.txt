For the financial year ended 31 December 2025, AS Tallink Grupp reported consolidated revenue of EUR 765.3 million, representing a 2.6% decline compared to EUR 785.8 million in 2024. Operating results also softened year-on-year, with EBITDA decreasing from EUR 175.2 million in 2024 to EUR 130.1 million in 2025, and net profit declining from EUR 40.3 million to EUR 17.3 million. Earnings per share dropped to EUR 0.023 from EUR 0.054 in the previous period. Gross profit contracted from EUR 153.6 million to EUR 143.2 million, and the gross margin moved from 19.5% to 18.7%. Operating performance was impacted by subdued consumer and business confidence across core markets, persistent geopolitical tensions, and inflationary pressures, alongside reduced transport volumes. Total passenger numbers decreased slightly by 0.9% to 5.5 million, while transported cargo units fell significantly by 19.2% to 245,004, and passenger vehicles declined by 2.2% to 760,473. Route operations generated EUR 625.2 million, down EUR 13.7 million from 2024, yielding a segment result of EUR 70.5 million compared to EUR 81.5 million previously. The Estonia-Finland route generated EUR 314.5 million in revenue with a segment result of EUR 70.4 million, Finland-Sweden routes recorded EUR 225.5 million in revenue with a segment result of EUR 1.3 million, and Estonia-Sweden routes achieved EUR 85.3 million in revenue with a segment result of a EUR 1.2 million loss. The Other segment contributed EUR 145.5 million in revenue and EUR 28.5 million in segment result. Operating expenses were influenced by personnel costs of EUR 201.0 million, shop and restaurant cost of goods sold of EUR 160.5 million, and fuel and emission costs of EUR 96.5 million. Depreciation and amortisation expenses decreased significantly by EUR 18.0 million to EUR 79.8 million, driven by the alignment of estimated useful lives of cruise and passenger vessels to 45 years and the sale of three vessels during the year, namely the passenger vessel Star I, and cargo vessels Regal Star and Sailor. Net finance costs declined to EUR 21.4 million due to reduced loan balances and falling interest rates. Cash flow from operating activities remained positive at EUR 127.1 million. Net cash flow from investing activities was EUR 46.1 million, bolstered by EUR 78.8 million in proceeds from the disposal of property, plant, and equipment, while capital expenditures totaled EUR 33.0 million. Net debt decreased from EUR 537.7 million at the end of 2024 to EUR 432.4 million as of 31 December 2025, driven by a reduction in interest-bearing liabilities to EUR 445.9 million. The net debt to EBITDA ratio stood at 3.3, compared to 3.1 at the end of 2024. Total liquidity at year-end was EUR 110.4 million, comprising EUR 13.5 million in cash and cash equivalents and EUR 96.9 million in unused overdraft facilities. Total equity stood at EUR 750.1 million, reflecting a strong equity ratio of 56.4%. During the year, the Group paid total dividends of EUR 44.6 million, representing EUR 0.06 per share, along with related income tax of EUR 11.3 million. Management will propose a dividend of EUR 0.06 per share for 2026. Subsequent to the reporting period, Paavo Nõgene resigned as Chairman of the Management Board effective 23 May 2026, and Peep Jalakas was elected as the new Chairman starting 6 April 2026, while Margus Schults was removed from the Management Board on 27 February 2026 to continue as Managing Director of Tallink Silja Oy.
