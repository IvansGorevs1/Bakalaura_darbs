AS LHV Group reported record financial results for the year ended 31 December 2024, achieving a net profit of EUR 150.3 million, which represents a 7% increase or EUR 9.4 million growth compared to the prior year. This performance was driven by strong asset growth, efficient cost management, and sustained credit quality, despite a challenging macroeconomic environment characterized by a recession in Estonia and declining interest rates in the second half of the year. The Group’s total assets expanded by 23% year-over-year to EUR 8.7 billion, while the loan portfolio grew significantly by 28% to EUR 4.6 billion. This lending expansion was broad-based, with corporate loans increasing by 33.3% and retail loans rising by 21.0%. Deposits also grew robustly, totaling EUR 6.9 billion, an increase of 21% from the previous year, supported by higher interest rates that attracted term deposits.

The Group’s net income reached EUR 338.3 million, an 11% increase, fueled by an 8% rise in net interest income to EUR 273.3 million and a substantial 24% jump in net fee and commission income to EUR 60.3 million. Operating expenses increased by 14% to EUR 146.9 million, primarily due to a rise in employee numbers, yet the cost-to-income ratio remained healthy at 43.4%. The return on equity stood at 24.5%, and the return on assets was 1.9%. Impairment losses on financial instruments increased to EUR 16.3 million, reflecting the cautious approach taken amid economic uncertainty, but the credit portfolio quality remained strong with a low share of non-performing loans.

Key business developments included significant growth in the UK subsidiary, LHV Bank Ltd, where the loan portfolio surged by 338% to EUR 348 million and deposits grew by 195% to EUR 705 million. In Estonia, LHV Pank strengthened its market position, becoming the third-largest bank by customer base, which grew by 9% to 456,000 customers. The insurance subsidiary, LHV Kindlustus, posted its second profitable year with net profit of EUR 1.2 million, and the asset management arm, LHV Varahaldus, saw assets under management increase by 3% to EUR 1.56 billion.

Capital adequacy remained robust, with the Common Equity Tier 1 ratio at 16.89% and the total capital adequacy ratio at 20.68%, well above regulatory requirements. The Group maintained strong liquidity metrics, with a Liquidity Coverage Ratio of 187.5% and a Net Stable Funding Ratio of 154.4%. During the year, the Group issued EUR 20 million in Tier 2 bonds and EUR 300 million in MREL-eligible bonds, while redeeming EUR 20 million in Additional Tier 1 bonds. The Management Board proposed a dividend of EUR 0.13 per share for 2024, reflecting the company's strong profitability and commitment to shareholder returns. Looking ahead, the Group anticipates a short-term decline in profits due to the interest rate environment but expects to offset this through continued business growth and operational efficiency.
