JLT Mobile Computers reported improved sales and a substantial recovery in profitability during 2025, although the Group remained loss-making. Net sales increased 11.1% to SEK 131.5 million from SEK 118.4 million, while order intake rose 37.5% to SEK 141.6 million. The year-end order backlog increased to SEK 24.1 million from SEK 13.9 million, including SEK 15.0 million scheduled for delivery in the first quarter of 2026.

Profitability was affected by a sharp decline in the US dollar, which weakened by almost 20% against the Swedish krona. With 74% of sales invoiced in dollars, the currency effect reduced the gross margin to 38.3%, compared with 45.8% in 2024, and gross profit declined to SEK 50.4 million from SEK 54.2 million despite higher revenue. Customs uncertainty also contributed to cautious customer behavior and increased administrative costs. Demand was particularly weak in warehousing and logistics, transportation, and manufacturing, while port and mining markets developed positively. The Americas accounted for SEK 92.2 million, or 70.1% of sales, compared with 59.0% a year earlier.

Cost-reduction measures partly offset the margin pressure. Operating expenses fell to SEK 52.4 million from SEK 57.3 million, including a SEK 6.3 million reduction in organizational and R&D costs. The discontinued operations of JLT Software Solutions AB and the consolidation of software and product development reduced overhead costs by approximately SEK 5 million and significantly lowered amortization. EBITDA improved to negative SEK 1.7 million from negative SEK 2.1 million, while operating loss narrowed to SEK 1.9 million from SEK 9.7 million. After financial items, the loss was SEK 1.8 million compared with SEK 9.6 million, and the net loss decreased to SEK 2.5 million from SEK 7.7 million. A defense-market evaluation initiated in the second half of the year cost approximately SEK 1 million.

Cash flow remained positive at SEK 3.7 million, although below SEK 6.8 million in 2024. Inventory reduction was a major contributor, with inventories declining by SEK 12.7 million to SEK 25.5 million. Cash and cash equivalents increased to SEK 16.9 million, while equity declined to SEK 41.8 million; solvency nevertheless improved to 59% from 54%.

JLT expanded its product portfolio with the JLT6015, featuring a 15-inch Full HD display, and introduced an AI-based Screen Blanking safety solution. These developments, together with stronger exposure to ports, construction, and mining, are intended to support growth as market conditions recover. No dividend is proposed for 2025.
