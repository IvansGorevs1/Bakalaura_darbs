InCoax Networks AB reported a significant decline in financial performance for the fiscal year 2025, marking a transition from a development-heavy phase to a focus on commercial execution. Net sales decreased by 35 percent to SEK 48,055,007, down from SEK 74,497,775 in the previous year. The report explicitly attributes this revenue drop to timing shifts in customer orders and a more cautious market environment, particularly in North America where sales fell from SEK 69,556,761 to SEK 39,578,479. Despite the lower revenue, the gross margin improved to 62 percent from 57 percent in 2024, reflecting a structural shift in the cost base and lower costs for goods for resale.

The operating result deteriorated significantly, with an operating loss of SEK -32,380,175 compared to an operating loss of SEK -19,378,406 in 2024. This widening loss was driven by the reduced top-line revenue while the company maintained high levels of investment in product development and commercial expansion. Capitalized development costs remained substantial at SEK 25,269,745, indicating continued strategic investment in the MoCA Access platform. The net loss after tax amounted to SEK -32,559,663, resulting in a loss per share of SEK -0.25, compared to SEK -0.18 per share in the prior year.

Cash flow dynamics shifted positively due to financing activities rather than operational improvements. Cash flow from operating activities improved slightly to SEK -20,011,617 from SEK -27,117,256 in 2024, but remained negative. Investing activities consumed SEK -25,269,745, primarily for capitalized development costs. However, total cash flow including financing activities turned positive at SEK 6,516,117, up from a negative SEK -12,758,133 in 2024. This positive outcome was largely due to completed financing activities, including a directed share issue that raised approximately SEK 33.5 million and the issuance of a SEK 20 million loan facility from the principal shareholder. Consequently, cash and cash equivalents increased to SEK 8,748,046 at year-end from SEK 2,231,929 at the beginning of the period.

The company’s equity ratio declined to 71.8 percent from 80.8 percent, primarily due to the accumulated losses and the dilution from new share issuance. The number of shares outstanding increased to 131,267,435 from 108,157,093. Management has implemented structural cost reductions, aiming to lower the ongoing cost base by more than 40 percent, or approximately SEK 25 million annually, starting in 2026. The CEO highlighted that the company has concluded its most development-intensive phase and is now focusing on scaling trials and rollouts through partnerships, such as the integration of the D2501 product into Nokia’s platform. The auditor’s report noted a material uncertainty related to the going concern assumption, citing the company’s dependence on the exercise of warrants for future financing. Despite these challenges, the company aims to reach positive cash flow within the coming twelve months by intensifying commercial efforts and improving operational efficiency.
