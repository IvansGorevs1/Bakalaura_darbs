Eleving Group S.A. reported its strongest financial performance to date for the year ended 31 December 2024, driven by robust operational growth, strategic market expansion, and successful capital market activities. The Group achieved a record total net profit of EUR 29.6 million, representing a significant increase of approximately 21% compared to EUR 24.5 million in 2023. When adjusted for a one-off Romanian VAT liability of EUR 2.6 million arising from a prior period tax audit, the underlying net profit reached EUR 32.2 million, reflecting a year-over-year growth of more than 31%. Revenue also demonstrated strong momentum, totaling EUR 216.6 million, an increase of nearly 15% from EUR 189.3 million in the previous year. This top-line growth was supported by a diversified revenue stream, with consumer lending products contributing EUR 95.5 million, traditional vehicle financing generating EUR 73.1 million, and flexible and subscription-based products adding EUR 48.0 million.

The Group’s loan portfolio expanded substantially, reaching a new all-time high of EUR 371.2 million at the end of 2024, up by 16% from EUR 320.3 million in 2023. This growth was primarily fueled by increased demand in key markets, including Romania, Uganda, Albania, and the acquired Sub-Saharan regions. Loan issuance volumes surged by 26.8% to EUR 368.6 million, highlighting the effectiveness of the Group’s digitalization efforts and expanded sales channels. Operational efficiency improved alongside growth, as evidenced by a reduction in non-performing loan rates for vehicle finance from 7.5% to 6.1% and for consumer finance from 4.5% to 4.3%. The Group’s adjusted EBITDA hit a record high of EUR 89.8 million, up over 16% from EUR 77.5 million in 2023, indicating enhanced profitability and cost management.

A defining milestone for Eleving Group in 2024 was the successful completion of its initial public offering in October, which raised EUR 29.0 million in equity. This was the largest IPO in Nasdaq Riga’s history and significantly strengthened the Group’s capital base, increasing total equity to EUR 108.1 million. The proceeds were strategically utilized to redeem expensive subordinated bonds and repay higher-cost loans, thereby optimizing the debt profile and reducing interest expenses. The capitalization ratio improved to 29.3% from 26.1%, and net leverage decreased to 3.3 from 3.7, providing ample headroom for financial covenants and supporting future growth initiatives. The Group also received a credit rating upgrade from Fitch Ratings, citing improved performance, lower leverage, and stable business model history.

Strategically, the Group continued to deepen its presence in existing markets while expanding its product portfolio. In Africa, Eleving Group accelerated its sustainability agenda by financing nearly 2,000 electric motorcycles, surpassing its 2025 target a year early and saving an estimated 1,000 metric tonnes of CO2 emissions. The integration of Sub-Saharan operations acquired in 2023 contributed significantly to growth, tripling the combined portfolio size in the region. Looking ahead, the Group remains focused on doubling its business size within the next two years through organic growth, product innovation, and geographic expansion, while maintaining a disciplined approach to risk management and capital allocation.
