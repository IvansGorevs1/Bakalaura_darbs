Novaturas AB reported a significant deterioration in financial performance for the year ended 31 December 2024, recording a net loss of EUR 7.6 million compared to a net profit of EUR 3.4 million in the prior year. Total revenue from contracts with customers decreased by 3.6% to EUR 200.9 million, down from EUR 208.3 million in 2023. This decline was driven by a reduction in the number of passengers served, which fell by 7.6% to 239,500 from 259,300 in 2023. The Group’s gross profit also declined sharply by 20.9% to EUR 20.7 million, resulting in a gross profit margin of 10.3% compared to 12.6% in the previous year.

The primary factor contributing to the substantial drop in profitability was a one-off, non-cash impairment loss on goodwill amounting to EUR 5.7 million. This impairment was recognized in the Estonian cash-generating unit following an impairment test that reflected a revised outlook for the region. Excluding this impairment, the adjusted EBITDA was negative EUR 449 thousand, a stark contrast to the positive EUR 5.2 million reported in 2023. Consequently, operating profit turned into a loss of EUR 6.7 million, whereas it was a profit of EUR 4.9 million in 2023. The increase in operating expenses to EUR 27.5 million was largely attributable to the goodwill impairment; excluding this item, operating expenses were effectively flat, showing only a 1% decrease from the previous year.

Management attributed the challenging operating environment to an increased competitive landscape and an oversupply of travel options in the Baltic States, which triggered intense price competition, particularly in popular destinations such as Turkey and the Greek islands. Despite the full-year loss, the Group demonstrated material operational improvement in the second half of the year. Adjusted EBITDA for the period from September to December 2024 was positive EUR 2.3 million, compared to a loss of EUR 1.6 million in the first half. This recovery was driven by effective optimization of the travel program, which improved the load factor to 98.9% in the fourth quarter and reduced the share of last-minute trips that typically negatively impact financial results.

The financial strain led to covenant breaches with lenders, although waivers were subsequently obtained. To address liquidity and strategic needs, the Group secured additional financing, including a EUR 2.5 million credit line and a EUR 1 million loan in early 2025. Furthermore, the Group entered into a transaction with strategic investor Neset Kockar, who acquired 23.2% of the company’s shares in April 2025, with an additional 9.99% expected to be acquired later. The arrival of the new investor is expected to provide synergies and support the Group’s return to profitability, with management forecasting EBITDA between EUR 2 million and EUR 3.3 million for 2025.
