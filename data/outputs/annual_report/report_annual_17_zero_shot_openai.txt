Novaturas Group’s 2024 performance weakened materially. Revenue declined 3.6% to EUR 200.9 million from EUR 208.3 million in 2023, while the number of passengers served fell 7.6%, from 259.3 thousand to 239.5 thousand. Gross profit decreased 20.9% to EUR 20.7 million, reducing the gross margin from 12.6% to 10.3%.

The Group reported a net loss of EUR 7.6 million, compared with a restated net profit of EUR 3.4 million in 2023. EBITDA declined from positive EUR 5.2 million to negative EUR 6.1 million, while adjusted EBITDA, excluding the goodwill impairment, fell from positive EUR 5.2 million to negative EUR 0.45 million. Operating loss was EUR 6.6 million, compared with operating profit of EUR 4.3 million in the restated prior year, and the loss before tax was EUR 7.8 million versus profit before tax of EUR 3.9 million. Basic loss per share was EUR 0.98, compared with earnings of EUR 0.43 in 2023.

Results included a one-off, non-cash goodwill impairment charge of EUR 5.7 million relating to the Estonian cash-generating unit. Excluding this charge, the Group’s net loss was EUR 1.9 million. Operating expenses increased to EUR 27.5 million, primarily because of the impairment; excluding it, operating expenses were EUR 21.8 million, 1% below 2023. Net finance expenses also increased to EUR 1.2 million from EUR 0.5 million, reflecting higher foreign-exchange and realised derivative losses.

Management attributed the overall deterioration to increased competition and oversupply of travel in the Baltic States, which led to intense price competition, particularly for Turkey and the Greek islands. Flight-package revenue, the Group’s main product, fell to EUR 170.7 million from EUR 182.8 million, while other sales increased to EUR 25.4 million from EUR 20.9 million. Destination diversification included strong growth in Spain and long-haul travel, with long-haul passengers increasing 8% and the fourth-quarter load factor improving to 98.9% from 96.7%.

Performance improved significantly late in the year: adjusted EBITDA was negative EUR 1.6 million in January–June but positive EUR 2.3 million in September–December. The Group’s optimization of its travel program, reduced last-minute sales and improved capacity utilization supported profitable fourth-quarter operations.

Financial pressure increased, with year-end cash falling to EUR 0.4 million from EUR 3.3 million, equity declining to EUR 8.0 million from EUR 15.7 million, and the debt-to-equity ratio rising to 121.1% from 58.0%. The Group breached financing covenants but received waivers. Subsequent financing and the acquisition of a 23.2% stake by investor Neset Kockar were identified as support for the going-concern assessment.
