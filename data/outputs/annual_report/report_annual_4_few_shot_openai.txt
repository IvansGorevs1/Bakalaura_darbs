Eleving Group delivered record financial performance in 2024, supported by strong portfolio expansion, higher demand, improved risk performance, and successful access to equity markets. Revenue increased nearly 15% to EUR 216.6 million from EUR 189.3 million in 2023, while adjusted EBITDA rose more than 16% to EUR 89.8 million from EUR 77.5 million. Profit from continuing operations increased to EUR 28.8 million from EUR 21.9 million, and total net profit reached an all-time high of EUR 29.6 million, up approximately 21% from EUR 24.5 million. The reported result included a EUR 2.6 million net profit impact from additional Romanian VAT liabilities relating to prior periods; excluding this one-off tax expense, net profit was EUR 32.2 million, representing growth of more than 31%.

Business volumes expanded materially. Loan issuance increased 26.8% to EUR 368.6 million, while the net loan and pre-owned vehicle rental portfolio grew 16% to EUR 371.2 million from EUR 320.3 million. Active customers totaled approximately 326,900. Demand was supported by digitalization, broader sales channels, and growth in Romania, Uganda, Albania, and the Sub-Saharan markets acquired in 2023. Loan applications in vehicle finance increased 80.9%, and the completed integration of the Sub-Saharan operations was accompanied by substantial portfolio growth. Portfolio quality also improved, with non-performing loan rates declining from 7.5% to 6.1% in vehicle finance and from 4.5% to 4.3% in consumer finance. The impairment-to-interest-revenue ratio decreased by two percentage points despite portfolio growth.

Interest revenue increased to EUR 203.7 million from EUR 176.3 million, although interest expense also rose to EUR 41.5 million from EUR 37.5 million. Administrative expenses increased to EUR 74.7 million from EUR 63.2 million, and other operating expenses rose to EUR 13.8 million, partly reflecting the Romanian VAT assessment. Foreign exchange movements remained material, with a net foreign exchange loss of EUR 3.7 million, although this improved from EUR 6.4 million in 2023.

The Group strengthened its capital position through its October IPO, which raised EUR 29.0 million and was the largest listing in Nasdaq Riga’s history. Equity increased to EUR 108.1 million from EUR 65.4 million, the capitalization ratio improved to 29.3% from 26.1%, and net leverage declined to 3.3 from 3.7. IPO proceeds were used partly to repay subordinated bonds and higher-cost funding, while the Group continued diversifying financing through local-currency programs and debt markets. Cash flow from operating activities remained positive at EUR 18.4 million, and cash at year-end increased to EUR 34.5 million from EUR 27.5 million.

Overall, 2024 marked significant growth and improved financial resilience. Management’s 2025 priorities are continued portfolio expansion, new products and markets, further local funding diversification, refinancing of 2026 Eurobonds, and continued investment in electric mobility and digital operations.
