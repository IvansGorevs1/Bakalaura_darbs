OODA AI AB reported a significant transformation in its financial performance for the year 2025, marking a decisive shift from a build-up phase to a growth-oriented business model. The most prominent development was the substantial increase in revenue, driven by the commercialization of its AI platform and the invoicing of major transactions. Group net sales surged to SEK 31,880 thousand, a dramatic increase from SEK 1,056 thousand in 2024. This growth was primarily attributable to new and expanded agreements with key customers, including a collaboration with Win.Rocks AG valued at approximately SEK 21 million during the year, followed by a new agreement of at least SEK 15 million over 24 months in December. Additional contracts were secured with Sergel Kredittjänster and HMM Deutschland, establishing a growing customer base in sectors such as banking, health, and staffing.

Despite the revenue growth, the Group reported a net loss for the year amounting to SEK -125,799 thousand, compared to a loss of SEK -83,355 thousand in 2024. Earnings per share deteriorated to SEK -35.29 from SEK -29.00 in the previous period. The Board explicitly stated that this negative earnings trend was not reflective of underlying operational activities but was mainly driven by impairments and results from financial items. Specifically, the Group recorded significant impairment losses related to the review and streamlining of its financial holdings. Financial assets decreased sharply from SEK 96,036 thousand to SEK 8,387 thousand, largely due to write-downs of portfolio holdings to a book value of zero as the company focused resources on its core AI business. Consequently, total equity fell from SEK 119,371 thousand to SEK 23,859 thousand.

Operationally, the company demonstrated improved efficiency. The Group’s EBITDA improved significantly to SEK 2,957 thousand from a loss of SEK -28,229 thousand in 2024. When adjusted for one-off costs of approximately SEK 10,238 thousand related to the liquidation of older company structures, the adjusted EBITDA amounted to approximately SEK 13,195 thousand, representing an adjusted EBITDA margin of approximately 32 percent of revenue. Personnel costs remained relatively stable at SEK -9,210 thousand, reflecting a low cost base and limited recruitment. The company also reduced its interest expenses drastically, from SEK -17,032 thousand in 2024 to SEK -384 thousand in 2025, due to lower indebtedness.

Liquidity was strengthened through multiple capital raises. The Group received SEK 44,302 thousand from financing activities, including cash issues in January, March, and August. Cash flow from operating activities improved to SEK -16,662 thousand from SEK -29,680 thousand in the prior year, with the second half of 2025 showing positive operating cash flow of SEK 5,098 thousand. The company entered 2026 with a clear focus on scaling the platform, expanding distribution through partners, and continuing the development of its decentralized AI infrastructure. The Board assessed that the company has sufficient conditions to continue operations on a going concern basis, supported by completed capital raisings and a strengthened operational position.
