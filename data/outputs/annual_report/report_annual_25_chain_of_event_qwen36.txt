WindowMaster International A/S reported a decline in financial performance for the fiscal year 2025, characterized by reduced revenue and profitability compared to the previous year. Total revenue amounted to DKK 268.6 million, representing an 8% decrease from DKK 294.5 million in 2024. This contraction was primarily attributed to a slow start to the year and reduced sales to a key customer in the Nordic region, specifically Climatic. Despite the lower top-line, the company’s gross profit margin improved to 48.8% from 46.9% in 2024, reaching DKK 131.1 million. Management attributes this margin expansion to a favorable business mix, with less revenue from key accounts and a higher proportion of direct project sales, which aligns with the "Accelerate Core" strategy.

Operating profit, measured as EBITDA, fell to DKK 28.0 million from DKK 37.3 million in 2024, resulting in an EBITDA margin of 10.4% compared to 12.7% previously. The decline in operating profitability was driven by maintained fixed cost levels despite lower revenue, as well as one-time costs of DKK 2.5 million incurred due to a ransomware attack on the company’s IT infrastructure in May and June 2025. Consequently, earnings before tax dropped to DKK 2.9 million from DKK 12.2 million in the prior year. Profit after tax was significantly lower at DKK 0.9 million, down from DKK 10.6 million in 2024. The high effective tax rate of 70% was largely due to the non-utilization of tax loss carryforwards.

Cash flow from operating activities decreased slightly to DKK 28.5 million from DKK 29.4 million in 2024, primarily reflecting the lower level of business activity. However, the company successfully improved net working capital, reducing it from DKK 35.0 million to DKK 31.9 million, mainly through better management of inventories and work in progress. Cash flow from investing activities was DKK -9.4 million, focused on digitalization initiatives and IT security upgrades. Net interest-bearing debt increased to DKK 38.7 million from DKK 36.7 million, leading to a financial gearing ratio of 1.7, up from 1.2 in 2024, though still within the target of below 2.0. Equity decreased to DKK 32.5 million, resulting in an equity ratio of 23.5%, slightly below the 25% target.

Looking ahead to 2026, WindowMaster anticipates organic revenue growth between 7% and 14%, with EBITDA expected to reach DKK 45-55 million. The outlook remains subject to uncertainty regarding global economic conditions and geopolitical tensions, but management expects market activity to improve, particularly in the non-residential segment, supported by stabilized interest rates and regulatory drivers for energy-efficient buildings.
