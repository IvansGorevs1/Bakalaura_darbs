JLT Mobile Computers reported a period of mixed financial performance in 2025, characterized by significant top-line growth offset by substantial margin compression due to adverse currency movements. Net sales increased by 11.1 percent to SEK 131.5 million, compared with SEK 118.4 million in the previous year. This revenue growth was supported by a strong commercial momentum, evidenced by a 37.5 percent increase in order intake to SEK 141.6 million. The sales expansion was largely driven by the Americas region, where turnover rose to SEK 92.2 million from SEK 69.8 million, aided by a major deal with an American food producer. However, demand in the core warehousing and logistics segment remained subdued due to geopolitical uncertainty and customer caution.

Despite the revenue growth, profitability deteriorated significantly. The gross margin decreased by 7.5 percentage points to 38.3 percent, down from 45.8 percent in 2024. This decline was primarily attributed to the US dollar losing nearly 20 percent against the Swedish krona during the year. Since 74 percent of sales were invoiced in dollars, the weaker exchange rate at the time of invoicing compared to quotation, along with inventory revaluation, severely impacted margins. Consequently, the operating profit improved only modestly in absolute terms but remained negative, reporting a loss of SEK 1.9 million compared to a loss of SEK 9.7 million in the prior year. The improvement in operating results was largely due to a reduction in depreciation and amortization expenses, which fell to SEK 1.3 million from SEK 9.0 million, following the write-down of capitalized development costs for the discontinued software subsidiary in the previous year. Operating expenses also decreased by SEK 6.3 million, reflecting structural cost savings and the consolidation of product development efforts.

The company’s cash flow situation strengthened considerably, with cash flow from operating activities reaching SEK 3.7 million, up from SEK 6.8 million in the previous year when adjusted for non-cash items and working capital changes. This positive cash flow was largely driven by a deliberate reduction in inventory levels, which decreased by SEK 12.7 million to SEK 25.5 million. This action released tied-up capital that had accumulated during the component shortage period. The group’s equity decreased to SEK 41.8 million from SEK 47.6 million, primarily due to the net loss and translation differences. The company ended the year with a net cash position of SEK 16.9 million.

Looking ahead, the company has diversified its market exposure into ports, construction, and mining segments to reduce reliance on the volatile logistics sector. Strategic product innovations, including a new 15-inch Full HD rugged computer and an AI-powered safety feature, were launched to strengthen the premium product portfolio. The Board of Directors proposed no dividend for the 2025 fiscal year, citing the need to preserve capital and focus on balance sheet strength amid external economic uncertainties. The overall development indicates a company transitioning to a more efficient cost base and broader market presence, aiming for sustainable growth as market conditions stabilize in 2026.
