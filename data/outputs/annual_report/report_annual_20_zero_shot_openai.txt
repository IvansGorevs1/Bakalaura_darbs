AS Tallink Grupp’s financial performance weakened in 2025 amid low consumer and business confidence, economic challenges in its core markets and heightened geopolitical tensions. Revenue declined 2.6% to EUR 765.3 million from EUR 785.8 million in 2024, while EBITDA fell 25.7% to EUR 130.1 million from EUR 175.2 million. Net profit decreased 57.1% to EUR 17.3 million, or EUR 0.023 per share, compared with EUR 40.3 million, or EUR 0.054 per share, in the previous year. Accordingly, the EBITDA margin fell from 22.3% to 17.0% and the net profit margin from 5.1% to 2.3%.

Passenger volumes were broadly stable, with 5.5 million passengers carried, down 0.9%. Cargo volumes declined more sharply, falling 19.2% to 245,004 units, while passenger vehicles decreased 2.2% to 760,473. Estonia-Finland passenger numbers increased 1.8%, supporting a EUR 1.7 million increase in route revenue to EUR 314.5 million, although the segment result declined by EUR 10.9 million to EUR 70.4 million. Finland-Sweden revenue fell EUR 3.3 million to EUR 225.5 million, with the segment result decreasing to EUR 1.3 million, partly reflecting 68 days of vessel maintenance. Estonia-Sweden revenue declined EUR 12.1 million to EUR 85.3 million, while its loss narrowed to EUR 1.2 million.

Restaurant and shop sales, nearly half of total revenue, decreased to EUR 366.4 million from EUR 376.0 million. Cargo transportation revenue fell to EUR 77.9 million from EUR 88.6 million, and charter revenue declined to EUR 54.9 million from EUR 62.0 million. Personnel expenses increased 4.3% to EUR 201.0 million, while fuel and emission costs were broadly stable at EUR 96.5 million. Depreciation and amortisation fell EUR 18.0 million to EUR 79.8 million, primarily because vessel useful lives were extended to 45 years and three vessels were sold. Net finance costs also declined to EUR 21.4 million from EUR 28.3 million.

The Group sold Star I, Regal Star and Sailor and ended the year with no vessels in lay-up. Operating cash flow remained positive at EUR 127.1 million, although below EUR 153.4 million in 2024. Proceeds from vessel disposals contributed EUR 78.8 million to investing cash flow. Interest-bearing liabilities declined 19.9% to EUR 445.9 million and net debt to EUR 432.4 million, while the equity ratio improved to 56.4%. Cash and cash equivalents were EUR 13.5 million, supplemented by EUR 96.9 million of unused overdraft facilities. A dividend of EUR 0.06 per share, totalling EUR 44.6 million, was paid, and the same dividend is proposed for 2026.
