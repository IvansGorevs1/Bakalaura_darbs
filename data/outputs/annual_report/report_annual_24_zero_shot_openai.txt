Trophy Games delivered substantial growth in 2025, driven primarily by the rapid scaling of its Transport Series, particularly Truck Manager and the July launch of Farm Manager. Revenue increased 40% to DKK 126.5 million from DKK 90.1 million in 2024. EBITDA rose 26% to DKK 31.1 million, while profit before tax more than doubled to DKK 19.0 million, up 109% from DKK 9.1 million. Profit for the year increased 68% to DKK 12.5 million, compared with DKK 7.5 million in 2024. The EBITDA margin declined to 25% from 27%, reflecting substantially higher marketing investment, while the EBT margin improved to 15% from 10%. The 2024 EBT included DKK 4.9 million of non-recurring impairment costs; excluding these, the company reported that underlying EBT margins were 15% in both years.

The Transport Series generated DKK 106 million of revenue, up from DKK 69 million, and DKK 27 million of EBITDA, compared with DKK 17 million in 2024. Airline Manager revenue increased 28% to DKK 37.8 million, while Truck Manager revenue rose to DKK 25.2 million from DKK 1.2 million. Farm Manager contributed DKK 22.0 million during its first six months. Together, these three titles accounted for 67% of group revenue, or DKK 85 million, and 65% of EBITDA, or DKK 20 million, compared with 35% of revenue and 25% of EBITDA in 2024. Revenue from other Transport titles declined 46% to DKK 21.0 million as marketing resources were redirected toward the higher-growth titles. Legacy Games revenue was broadly stable at DKK 20.6 million, although EBITDA fell to DKK 11.0 million from DKK 12.8 million.

The company expanded marketing expenditure 76% to DKK 40.5 million to support user acquisition and portfolio scaling. In-app advertising revenue doubled to DKK 18.5 million and represented 14.6% of total revenue, up from 10.2% in 2024. Q4 was the strongest quarter in company history, with revenue up 106% to DKK 41.0 million and EBT up 574% to DKK 10.1 million, despite a 296% increase in quarterly marketing expenditure.

Operating cash flow was DKK 12.9 million, down from DKK 19.3 million, while year-end cash declined 9% to DKK 23.0 million, affected by higher marketing investment, increased receivables, a DKK 4.7 million tax-credit repayment and DKK 4.9 million of share buybacks. The group remained debt-free, with a 78% solvency ratio. For 2026, management guides for revenue of DKK 130–153 million, EBITDA of DKK 28–36 million and EBT of DKK 18–24 million, excluding potential acquisitions and The Ranchers.
