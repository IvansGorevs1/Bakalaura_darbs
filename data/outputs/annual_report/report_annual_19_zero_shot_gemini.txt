During the 2025 financial period, Talkpool transitioned from a multi-year restructuring and cost-minimization phase into organic growth under its new Vision 2030 strategic plan. For the full year spanning January 1st to December 31st, 2025, the company reported net sales of EUR 17,756 thousand, compared to EUR 15,782 thousand in 2024, representing a year-over-year increase of 12.5 percent and marking the first double-digit growth since the consolidation phase began. Profitability indicators remained stable despite initial investments for future expansion. EBITDA reached EUR 1,828 thousand, compared to EUR 1,702 thousand in the previous year, resulting in an EBITDA margin of 10.3 percent versus 10.8 percent in 2024. EBIT stood at EUR 1,620 thousand, compared to EUR 1,527 thousand in 2024, with an EBIT margin of 9.1 percent compared to 9.7 percent. Net earnings after tax showed a significant increase of 42 percent, rising to EUR 862 thousand from EUR 605 thousand in 2024, yielding a net earnings after tax margin of 4.8 percent compared to 3.8 percent in the prior period. 

The company's financial structure strengthened notably over the period. Equity improved to EUR 2.1 million from negative levels in prior comparative evaluations, and net debt was further reduced, reaching a positive net cash position of EUR 0.3 million by the end of 2025, calculated as cash minus interest-bearing liabilities. 

Operationally, the results were shaped by the conclusion of restructuring, an acceleration of organic growth, and strategic re-entry into developed markets alongside expansion in remote service delivery. The Americas remained Talkpool’s largest market, driven by longstanding relationships in the Caribbean and contract support for Digicel, alongside initial re-entry into the U.S. market through a service contract with MasTec and project work handled via the Miami office. In Europe, the German subsidiary maintained a steady contribution through fiber optical network planning partnerships with Deutsche Telekom, supported by an independent valuation by BDO that assessed the German business at approximately EUR 20.5 million. Talkpool Pakistan operated as a key production and engineering hub, achieving record revenue in local currency and renegotiating commercial terms to improve gross profitability. Strategic developments also included trials of bespoke AI-powered technology solutions, the launch of a Swedish affiliated company for the defense sector, and the initiation of Vision 2030 targets aiming for an average annual revenue growth rate of 16.8 percent and a 14.0 percent EBITDA margin by 2030. Overall, the company’s performance reflected successful balance sheet repair, positive earnings expansion, and a solid operational foundation established for future growth.
