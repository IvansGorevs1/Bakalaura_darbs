For the 2024 financial year, Eleving Group S.A. reported a total revenue of EUR 216.6 million, representing an increase compared to the previous period's revenue of EUR 189.3 million. Total interest revenue reached EUR 203.7 million, up from EUR 176.3 million in 2023. Operating expenses included administrative expenses of EUR 74.7 million compared to EUR 63.2 million, and impairment expenses of EUR 42.1 million compared to EUR 39.8 million in the prior year. Profit before tax for continuing operations rose to EUR 37.7 million from EUR 28.5 million. The net profit from continuing operations was EUR 28.8 million compared to EUR 21.9 million, while profit from discontinued operations contributed EUR 0.8 million compared to EUR 2.5 million. The Group achieved an all-time best annual net profit of EUR 29.6 million, representing a 21% increase over the EUR 24.5 million reported in 2023. Total equity increased significantly to EUR 108.1 million as of December 31, 2024, up from EUR 65.4 million at the end of 2023. Total assets grew to EUR 476.3 million from EUR 421.3 million, and total liabilities decreased to EUR 355.9 million from EUR 368.2 million. The vehicle and consumer financing net portfolio expanded to EUR 371.2 million from EUR 320.3 million. Adjusted EBITDA reached EUR 89.8 million, compared to EUR 77.5 million in the prior period. The financial results were influenced by business developments, including the successful execution of an initial public offering in October 2024 on Nasdaq Riga, which secured EUR 29.0 million in equity and contributed to an increase in share premium and share capital. Additional factors affecting the results included the expansion of digital sales channels, the full integration and portfolio scaling of Sub-Saharan operations acquired in 2023, continued scaling of electric vehicle and motorcycle financing, and portfolio quality enhancements demonstrated by reduced non-performing loan rates. Profitability was also affected by a tax audit by the Romanian Tax Authority that resulted in additional VAT liabilities for prior periods, reducing net profit by EUR 2.6 million. Debt management activities during the period included the optimization of funding structures, local currency financing programs, and the premature repayment of subordinated bonds utilizing IPO proceeds.
