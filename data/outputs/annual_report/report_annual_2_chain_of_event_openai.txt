Infortar reported strong operating growth in 2025, although net profit declined because 2024 included substantial one-off gains from the initial consolidation of Tallink. Revenue increased 33.9% to EUR 1.837 billion from EUR 1.372 billion, while EBITDA rose 57.0% to EUR 227.9 million and the EBITDA margin improved from 10.6% to 12.4%. Operating profit increased 41.5% to EUR 109.0 million. However, profit for the year fell 63.9% to EUR 69.9 million, and profit attributable to the parent declined to EUR 70.6 million from EUR 191.3 million. Earnings per share decreased to EUR 3.43 from EUR 9.36. Management attributes the lower net profit mainly to the 2024 bargain-purchase and other one-off effects associated with acquiring control of Tallink.

The improvement in revenue and EBITDA primarily reflects the full-year consolidation of Tallink in 2025, compared with consolidation from August 2024 onward. Maritime transport generated EUR 761.7 million of revenue and EUR 119.5 million of EBITDA, while energy contributed EUR 980.2 million of revenue and EUR 89.3 million of EBITDA. Energy EBITDA improved by approximately EUR 12 million. Supporting segments also benefited from the consolidation of Estonia Farmid and Halinga. Tallink’s passenger volumes declined 0.9% to 5.53 million, cargo units fell 19.2% to 245,004, and passenger vehicles decreased 2.2%, but vessel chartering helped offset seasonal and demand-related pressures. The segment continued to face economic uncertainty, the effects of the war in Ukraine, and higher regulatory and fuel-related costs.

The energy segment operated amid lower regional gas demand, which fell 9% to 39.7 TWh, and volatile gas prices. Elenger delivered 18 TWh of LNG, maintaining approximately one-quarter of the Finland-Baltic market. Its results were supported by infrastructure expansion and renewable-energy development, including a 4.9 MW solar plant, an 8.0 MWh battery facility in Latvia, bioLNG bunkering activity, and continued investment in biomethane.

The Group’s financial position strengthened. Equity rose 0.8% to EUR 1.175 billion, while total assets declined 4.4% to EUR 2.597 billion. Interest-bearing liabilities decreased 12.4% to EUR 1.071 billion, net debt fell 19.3% to EUR 851.6 million, and net debt/EBITDA improved from 4.0 to 3.7. Operating cash flow increased substantially to EUR 384.0 million from EUR 89.3 million. Infortar paid EUR 62 million in dividends, or EUR 3 per share, and proposed a 2025 dividend of EUR 3.02 per share. The Group completed acquisitions in agriculture and biogas, including 96.6% of Estonia Farmid and 100% of Oisu Biogaas, supporting its strategy of portfolio diversification and renewable-energy expansion.
