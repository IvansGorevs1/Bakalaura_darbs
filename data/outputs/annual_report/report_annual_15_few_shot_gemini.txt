During the financial year ended December 31, 2025, AS Merko Ehitus reported consolidated revenue of EUR 310.9 million, representing a significant decline of 42.3% compared to EUR 539.0 million in the previous year. The decrease in top-line performance was primarily driven by lower activity in the construction service segment as several major projects completed in 2024 were finalized, coupled with a slower pace of signing new contracts during 2024 and the first half of 2025. By geographic region, 54.9% of revenue was generated in Estonia, 28.5% in Lithuania, and 16.6% in Latvia. 

Profitability also adjusted downward from the record levels of the prior year, reflecting a return to more traditional operating margins within a highly competitive sector. Gross profit fell from EUR 95.9 million to EUR 55.9 million, while the gross margin remained relatively stable at 18.0%, compared with 17.8% in 2024. Operating profit declined to EUR 34.3 million from EUR 72.5 million, resulting in an operating profit margin of 11.0% versus 13.4% in the prior year. Net profit attributable to equity holders of the parent decreased to EUR 39.9 million compared to EUR 64.7 million in 2024, yielding a net margin of 12.8% compared to 12.0% previously. 

The construction service segment generated revenue of EUR 237.6 million, down 49.9% from EUR 474.6 million in 2024, and contributed 76.4% of total group revenue. Operating profit for this segment decreased by 56.8% to EUR 25.7 million, with an operating profit margin of 10.8%. Conversely, the real estate development segment experienced revenue growth of 13.8%, reaching EUR 73.4 million, supported by the handover of 358 apartments to customers compared to 323 apartments in 2024. Real estate operating profit stood at EUR 13.4 million, down 18.8% from EUR 16.5 million in the prior year. 

The group maintained a solid financial position and strong capitalization. Total assets decreased to EUR 415.0 million as of December 31, 2025, down from EUR 447.1 million a year earlier. Equity attributable to equity holders of the parent rose to EUR 260.6 million from EUR 254.3 million. The equity ratio strengthened to 62.8% compared to 56.9% at the end of 2024, comfortably exceeding the strategic target of at least 40%. The debt ratio remained moderate at 8.0%, compared with 7.5% at the end of 2024. Cash and cash equivalents decreased from EUR 91.9 million to EUR 41.4 million, while short-term bank deposits increased to EUR 18.0 million from EUR 10.0 million. Net debt stood at negative EUR 8.3 million due to substantial liquid reserves exceeding borrowings. 

Market conditions during the period were shaped by persistent economic uncertainty, high global turbulence, and restrictive monetary policies, with the European Central Bank maintaining key interest rates at higher levels. While private investments remained subdued, public sector procurement actively supported activity, particularly in infrastructure and national defence. Notably, the second half of 2025 marked a clear operational improvement, highlighted by the preparation for major contracts, including significant defense campus projects in Lithuania secured subsequently. The group’s secured order book (excluding own developments) increased significantly by 37.1% to EUR 466.9 million as of December 31, 2025, compared to EUR 340.6 million at the end of 2024, providing a stable workload for future periods. 

Reflecting the group's strong capitalization and profitability, the Management Board, in coordination with the Supervisory Board, has proposed to distribute EUR 22.1 million in dividends from retained earnings, amounting to EUR 1.25 per share. This represents a 55% dividend rate and a 4.0% dividend yield based on the year-end share price of EUR 31.30.
