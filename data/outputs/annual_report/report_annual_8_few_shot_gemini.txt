AS SAF Tehnika and its subsidiaries reported a consolidated net turnover of 25.99 million euros for the 2024 to 2025 financial year, representing a decrease of 4% or 1.1 million euros compared with the previous financial year. The parent company reported a net turnover of 21.92 million euros, which was 6% or 1.33 million euros lower than in the prior year. Sales to countries outside Latvia accounted for 97% of the group's total turnover, with products sold across 87 countries. 

Geographically, the Americas region generated 14.17 million euros, accounting for 55% of the group's total turnover, but experienced a 14% or 2.3 million euro decline due to large-scale projects being realized in subsequent periods and temporary customer caution surrounding investment decisions and tariff announcements. Conversely, revenue in the European region, including Latvia, increased by 17% or 1.4 million euros to reach 10.0 million euros. Revenue in the Asia, Middle East, and Africa regions decreased by 5% or 90 thousand euros to 1.8 million euros. 

The group closed the 2024 to 2025 financial year with a consolidated net loss of 643 thousand euros, which represents an improvement compared with the net loss of 2.37 million euros recorded in the previous financial year. The parent company reported a net loss of 552 thousand euros for the reporting period, compared with a loss of 1.34 million euros in the preceding year. Gross profit for the group improved significantly to 9.50 million euros from 7.53 million euros previously. Operating losses also narrowed to 1.29 million euros from 3.22 million euros in the prior financial year. 

Total assets at the end of the reporting period stood at 26.08 million euros, up from 23.02 million euros at the close of the previous financial year. Inventories increased to 14.00 million euros compared with 12.79 million euros, while slow-moving inventory reserves decreased by 1.24 million euros. Cash and cash equivalents rose notably to 4.81 million euros from 2.21 million euros. Total equity stood at 15.76 million euros at the end of the period, compared with 16.44 million euros previously, while total liabilities increased to 10.33 million euros from 6.59 million euros, largely driven by higher contract liabilities and trade payables. 

The group continued its investments in IT infrastructure, production and research equipment, software, licenses, product certification, and premises renovation, totaling 812 thousand euros during the year. Management views the reporting period loss as transitory, noting that operating cash flows remained positive, and the group intends to maintain its strategy of developing competitive wireless data transmission products and specialized niche solutions while exercising caution regarding future sales forecasts.
