AS LHV Group reported record financial results for the year ended 31 December 2024, driven by robust growth in its loan portfolio, high interest rates, and efficient cost management. The Group achieved a net profit of EUR 150.3 million, representing a 7% increase from the prior year’s EUR 140.9 million. This performance marked the highest business volumes and operating profit in the company’s history. Total consolidated assets grew by 23% to EUR 8.7 billion, while the loan portfolio expanded significantly by 28% to EUR 4.6 billion. This lending growth was broad-based, with corporate loans increasing by 33.3% and retail loans rising by 21.0%. The deposit base also strengthened, growing by 21% to EUR 6.9 billion, supported by higher interest rates that attracted term deposits.

Revenue streams reflected this expansion, with net interest income rising 8% to EUR 273.3 million and net fee and commission income increasing 24% to EUR 60.3 million. Total net income reached EUR 338.3 million, an 11% year-over-year increase. Operating expenses grew by 14% to EUR 146.9 million, primarily due to an increase in employee numbers and associated costs. Despite this rise, the Group maintained a strong cost-to-income ratio of 43.4%. Impairment losses on financial instruments increased to EUR 16.3 million, up from EUR 11.5 million in 2023, reflecting the larger loan book and cautious provisioning amid an uncertain economic outlook. The effective tax rate remained stable at 17%.

The Group’s capital position remained robust, with the Common Equity Tier 1 ratio at 16.89% and the Total Capital Adequacy Ratio at 20.68%, well above regulatory minimums. Liquidity metrics were also strong, with the Liquidity Coverage Ratio at 187.5% and the Net Stable Funding Ratio at 154.4%. These ratios indicate a resilient liquidity profile capable of withstanding stress scenarios.

Key business developments contributed to these results. In Estonia, LHV Pank increased its customer base by 38,000, reaching over 456,000 customers, and strengthened its position as the third-largest bank in the country. The UK subsidiary, LHV Bank, capitalized on its full year of operations with a banking license, achieving significant growth in both deposits and loans, particularly in the SME segment. Additionally, the Group acquired a profitable loan portfolio from AS TBB Pank. Management updated the financial plan in September 2024, raising profitability targets by 25% due to the strong performance. Looking ahead, the Group anticipates a short-term decline in profits due to falling interest rates but expects to offset this through continued business growth and operational efficiency improvements. The Management Board proposed a dividend of EUR 0.09 per share, totaling EUR 29.2 million, reflecting the company’s commitment to returning value to shareholders while maintaining strong capital buffers.
