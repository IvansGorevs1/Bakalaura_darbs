For the year ended 30 June 2025, SAF Tehnika Group reported revenue of EUR 25.99 million, down EUR 1.10 million, or 4%, from EUR 27.09 million in the previous financial year. The parent company’s revenue declined 6% to EUR 21.92 million. International sales remained dominant, representing 97% of Group revenue, with products sold in 87 countries.

The Group’s gross profit increased substantially to EUR 9.50 million from EUR 7.53 million, as cost of goods sold fell 16% to EUR 16.49 million. This improvement was mainly reflected in lower procurement and subcontracting costs. Selling and marketing expenses decreased 5% to EUR 7.43 million, while administrative expenses rose 16% to EUR 3.36 million. As a result, operating losses narrowed to EUR 1.29 million from EUR 3.22 million. Including other income of EUR 703 thousand, financial income of EUR 50 thousand, financial expenses of EUR 112 thousand and a EUR 10 thousand tax benefit, the Group’s net loss improved materially to EUR 643 thousand from EUR 2.37 million. Loss per share decreased to EUR 0.216 from EUR 0.798. The parent company’s net loss narrowed to EUR 552 thousand from EUR 1.34 million.

Regional performance was mixed. Revenue in the Americas fell 14% to EUR 14.17 million, partly because large projects were scheduled for delivery in later periods and customers temporarily adopted a more cautious approach to investment decisions amid tariff announcements. European revenue increased 17% to EUR 10.0 million, while revenue from Asia, Africa and the Middle East declined 5% to EUR 1.8 million. Management reported signs of stabilization and gradually recovering market activity.

The main product segment, comprising CFIP, Integra, Spectrum Compact and Aranet, generated EUR 24.20 million in revenue, compared with EUR 25.10 million previously. Revenue from other products declined to EUR 1.80 million from EUR 2.00 million. Research and development continued across microwave transmission and Aranet IoT solutions, with EUR 3.43 million of related costs recognized and EUR 570 thousand of project co-financing received.

The Group generated positive operating cash flow of EUR 3.87 million and ended the year with EUR 4.81 million in cash, up EUR 2.60 million. It invested EUR 812 thousand in infrastructure, equipment, software, licences, certification and premises. Inventory provisions for slow-moving stock fell to EUR 3.86 million from EUR 5.10 million. A Canadian branch was established to reduce potential supply-chain and customs risks, while the EUR 4.95 million credit line remained unused. Management considers the loss temporary but remains cautious about geopolitical, tariff and market uncertainties. The auditors issued an unmodified opinion.
