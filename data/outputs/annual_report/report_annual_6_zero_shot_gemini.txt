Gigasun AB published its financial report for the 2025 financial year, reflecting a period of continued operational growth and improved profitability amid a transformative market environment in China. Total revenue for the group increased by 9.8 percent to SEK 239,655 thousand, compared to SEK 218,264 thousand in the previous year. Net revenue rose by 9.9 percent to SEK 203,179 thousand, up from SEK 184,795 thousand, while other operating income, primarily consisting of state, regional, and local subsidies, stood at SEK 36,476 thousand compared to SEK 33,469 thousand in 2024. Gross profit improved to SEK 195,829 thousand from SEK 179,219 thousand, maintaining a stable gross margin of 82 percent. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization increased to SEK 163,733 thousand from SEK 145,760 thousand, yielding an EBITDA margin of 68 percent compared to 67 percent previously. Operating profit experienced a notable 21.1 percent increase, reaching SEK 73,478 thousand compared to SEK 60,677 thousand, resulting in an improved EBIT margin of 31 percent against 28 percent in the preceding year.

Profit after financial items amounted to SEK -12,201 thousand compared to SEK -25,927 thousand. Interest expenses and similar charges decreased to SEK 81,976 thousand from SEK 88,658 thousand, supported by ongoing refinancing activities in China with Agricultural Bank Financial Leasing Co., Ltd. Currency differences impacted the results with an exchange rate loss of SEK 3,843 thousand compared to a gain of SEK 1,758 thousand in 2024. After tax effects and a positive tax contribution of SEK 4,996 thousand, the net result for the year was SEK -7,206 thousand, representing an improvement compared to SEK -32,944 thousand reported for 2024. Earnings per share before and after dilution improved to SEK -0.13 from SEK -0.58.

The group’s financial position at year-end comprised total assets of SEK 2,296,782 thousand, down from SEK 2,528,093 thousand in 2024. Tangible fixed assets, largely consisting of solar energy installations, amounted to SEK 1,914,134 thousand compared to SEK 2,123,241 thousand. Interest-bearing debt decreased to SEK 1,382,613 thousand from SEK 1,483,061 thousand, and net interest-bearing debt decreased to SEK 1,337,465 thousand from SEK 1,393,172 thousand. Cash and bank balances stood at SEK 45,149 thousand compared to SEK 89,889 thousand. Total equity decreased to SEK 488,201 thousand from SEK 577,336 thousand, resulting in an equity ratio of 21 percent compared to 23 percent at the end of 2024. Cash flow from operating activities amounted to SEK 101,058 thousand compared to SEK 133,785 thousand, while investing activities generated a cash flow of SEK -208,101 thousand, driven mainly by investments in solar panels of SEK 169,877 thousand. Financing activities generated a cash flow of SEK 72,141 thousand through new loan proceeds and repayments.

Operationally, Gigasun expanded its portfolio by installing 55.7 MW of roof-mounted solar PV capacity during the year, reaching a total installed, revenue-generating capacity of 395.8 MW across 216 installations and 197 customers at the end of 2025, compared to 340.1 MW across 200 installations at the end of 2024. Produced electricity rose by 30 percent to 327.8 million kWh, up from 252.6 million kWh, contributing to an estimated reduction of approximately 161,000 tonnes in CO2 emissions compared to 124,000 tonnes in the previous year.
