OODA AI AB experienced a breakthrough financial year in 2025, successfully transitioning from a build-up and stabilization phase into commercialization and growth within enterprise artificial intelligence and decentralized infrastructure. For the financial year 2025, the Group reported a sharp increase in revenue, with total revenue rising to SEK 32,058 thousand compared to SEK 1,061 thousand in the previous year, and net sales surging to SEK 31,880 thousand from SEK 1,056 thousand. This growth was primarily driven by the invoicing of several major transactions, notably significant customer agreements with Win.Rocks AG, which grew to a total contract value of approximately SEK 21 million alongside a new 24-month agreement worth at least SEK 15 million, as well as commercial progress with HMM Deutschland and Sergel Kredittjänster. The Group achieved an operating profit of SEK -29,270 thousand, representing an improvement compared to SEK -51,781 thousand in 2024. Furthermore, the Group generated a positive EBITDA of SEK 2,957 thousand compared to SEK -28,229 thousand in the prior year. When adjusted for one-off costs of approximately SEK 10,238 thousand related to the liquidation of older company structures, the adjusted EBITDA amounted to approximately SEK 13,195 thousand, corresponding to an adjusted EBITDA margin of approximately 32 percent of revenue. Despite operational improvements, the Group recorded a net result for the year of SEK -125,799 thousand compared to SEK -83,355 thousand in 2024, and earnings per share of SEK -35.29 compared to SEK -29.00 in the previous year. This negative bottom-line trend was primarily attributable to financial items and substantial impairments rather than underlying operational activities, including results from associated companies and financial holdings of SEK -95,853 thousand resulting from a strategic review and conservative revaluation of holdings. Cash flow from operating activities improved to SEK -16,662 thousand compared to SEK -29,680 thousand in 2024, with the Group achieving a positive operational cash flow of SEK 5,098 thousand during the second half of 2025. Cash flow from investing activities was SEK -49,413 thousand compared to SEK -19,093 thousand, while financing activities contributed positively with SEK 44,302 thousand compared to SEK 68,922 thousand in the prior period, supported by capital injections from share issues that increased the number of shares from 3,385,254 to 3,731,465 and brought total equity down to SEK 23,859 thousand at year-end compared to SEK 119,371 thousand in 2024. Cash and cash equivalents stood at SEK 3,335 thousand at the end of 2025, down from SEK 25,108 thousand at the end of 2024. Throughout the reporting period, the company focused on scaling its Universal AI Platform, advancing decentralized Web3 initiatives such as the MVP and test network for the SOODA token, streamlining its corporate and portfolio structure through multiple mergers, and establishing strategic partnerships to drive wider market penetration and recurring revenue streams as it enters 2026.
