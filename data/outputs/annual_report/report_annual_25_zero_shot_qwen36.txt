WindowMaster International A/S reported a decline in financial performance for the fiscal year 2025 compared to 2024, primarily driven by a slowdown in revenue generation. Total revenue amounted to DKK 268.6 million, representing an 8% decrease from DKK 294.5 million in the previous year. This contraction was attributed to a slow start to the year and reduced sales to a key customer in the Nordic region, as well as lower growth in North America. Despite the lower top line, the company achieved an improved gross profit margin of 48.8% in 2025, up from 46.9% in 2024. The margin expansion was linked to a favorable business mix, characterized by less revenue from key accounts and more direct project revenue, which aligns with the company’s "Accelerate Core" strategy.

Operating profit (EBITDA) fell to DKK 28.0 million from DKK 37.3 million in 2024, resulting in an EBITDA margin of 10.4% compared to 12.7% previously. The decline in operating profitability was primarily due to maintained fixed cost levels intended to support future growth, coupled with a DKK 2.5 million one-off expense incurred from a ransomware attack on the company’s IT infrastructure in May and June 2025. Consequently, profit before tax dropped to DKK 2.9 million from DKK 12.2 million, and profit after tax decreased significantly to DKK 0.9 million from DKK 10.6 million. The high effective tax rate of 70% in 2025 was driven by the non-utilization of tax loss carryforwards.

Cash flow from operating activities remained robust at DKK 28.5 million, slightly down from DKK 29.4 million in 2024. The company successfully improved net working capital, which decreased to DKK 31.9 million from DKK 35.0 million, primarily through better management of inventories and work in progress. Cash flow from investing activities was DKK -9.4 million, reflecting investments in digitalization, software development, and IT security upgrades, including the firewall enhancements necessitated by the ransomware incident. Net interest-bearing debt increased to DKK 38.7 million from DKK 36.7 million, leading to a financial gearing ratio of 1.7, up from 1.2 in 2024, though still within the target of below 2.0. Equity decreased to DKK 32.5 million, resulting in an equity ratio of 23.5%, slightly below the 25% target.

Looking ahead, WindowMaster expects market conditions to improve in 2026, driven by stabilized interest rates and ongoing demand for energy-efficient building solutions. The company has set a revenue growth target of 7% to 14% for 2026, aiming for revenue between DKK 290 million and DKK 310 million. Management anticipates that EBITDA will increase to between DKK 45 million and DKK 55 million, corresponding to an EBITDA margin of 8% to 10%. This projected profitability improvement is expected to be driven by operating leverage, as top-line growth requires only minor increases in the fixed cost base. The company remains confident in its long-term growth opportunities despite short-term uncertainties related to geopolitical developments and supply chain constraints.
