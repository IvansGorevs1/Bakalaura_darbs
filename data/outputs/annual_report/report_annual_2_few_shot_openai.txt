Infortar reported substantial growth in revenue and operating performance in 2025, although net profit declined from the exceptionally high level recorded in 2024. Revenue increased 33.9% to EUR 1.837 billion, while EBITDA rose 56.9% to EUR 227.9 million and the EBITDA margin improved from 10.6% to 12.4%. Operating profit increased 41.5% to EUR 109.0 million. The results benefited mainly from the full-year consolidation of Tallink Grupp, whose results were included only from August 2024, as well as a roughly EUR 12 million improvement in Elenger’s EBITDA and contributions from the newly consolidated agriculture businesses.

Net profit fell 63.9% to EUR 69.9 million, with profit attributable to Infortar’s shareholders declining to EUR 70.6 million from EUR 191.3 million. The comparison was distorted by one-off effects in 2024, including gains associated with the initial consolidation of Tallink. In 2025, financial income and expenses moved from a EUR 73.8 million net gain to a EUR 26.0 million net expense, as the prior-year bargain-purchase gain was not repeated. Earnings per share decreased to EUR 3.43 from EUR 9.36.

Energy remained the largest revenue contributor, generating EUR 980.2 million, compared with EUR 966.2 million in 2024, while maritime transport revenue increased to EUR 761.7 million from EUR 316.8 million on the longer consolidation period. Tallink carried 5.53 million passengers, down 0.9% year-on-year, while cargo volumes decreased 19.2% and passenger vehicles declined 2.2%. The energy business delivered 18 TWh of LNG cargoes, unchanged from 2024, despite a 9% decline in regional gas consumption and continued price volatility linked to weather and geopolitical developments.

The Group expanded its portfolio through the acquisition of 96.6% of Estonia Farmid and 100% of Oisu Biogaas, while continuing investments in biomethane, solar generation, battery storage and real estate. These developments supported Infortar’s strategy of diversification and more stable cash flows.

The financial position strengthened despite continued investment and dividend payments. Total assets decreased 4.4% to EUR 2.597 billion, while equity increased to EUR 1.175 billion from EUR 1.166 billion and the equity ratio improved to 45% from 43%. Interest-bearing liabilities declined 12.4% to EUR 1.071 billion, net debt fell to EUR 851.6 million, and net debt to EBITDA improved from 4.0 to 3.7. Operating cash flow increased significantly to EUR 384.0 million from EUR 89.3 million, and year-end cash rose to EUR 219.8 million. Infortar paid EUR 62 million in dividends during the year and proposed a 2025 dividend of EUR 3.02 per share.
