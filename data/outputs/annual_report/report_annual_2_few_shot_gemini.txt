The Infortar Group reported a strong financial performance for the 2025 financial year, supported by business expansion, strategic portfolio diversification, and the full twelve-month consolidation of its maritime transport subsidiary, Tallink Grupp. Group revenue reached approximately EUR 1.84 billion, representing a significant increase compared with EUR 1.37 billion reported in 2024. Gross profit improved markedly to EUR 239.8 million from EUR 128.6 million, while EBITDA rose to EUR 227.9 million compared with EUR 145.3 million in the previous period. Operating profit increased to EUR 109.0 million from EUR 77.0 million. Net profit for the financial year was EUR 69.9 million, compared with EUR 193.7 million in 2024. The higher net profit in the comparative period was mainly attributable to one-off effects related to the initial consolidation of Tallink Group, including a substantial gain from a bargain purchase recognized in 2024. Total assets stood at EUR 2.60 billion as of 31 December 2025, compared with EUR 2.72 billion a year earlier. Total equity strengthened to EUR 1.18 billion from EUR 1.17 billion, reflecting the Group's continued ability to build its own funds. Net debt decreased to EUR 851.6 million from EUR 1.06 million, and the net debt to EBITDA ratio improved to 3.7 from 4.0. Interest-bearing liabilities decreased to EUR 1.07 billion from EUR 1.22 billion, reflecting loan repayments across several Group entities and alignment with market conditions. 

The Group operates primarily in the maritime transport, energy, and real estate sectors, supplemented by agriculture and other supporting activities. In 2025, strategic developments included the acquisition of a 96.57% interest in Estonia Farmid OÜ in July and the full acquisition of Oisu Biogaas OÜ in November, which further integrated agricultural operations with renewable energy and biomethane production. The energy segment, anchored by Elenger Grupp, expanded its activities across the Baltic-Finnish region and Poland, investing in solar power and battery storage facilities while managing natural gas distribution networks and LNG imports. In the real estate segment, the completion of major logistics and commercial space developments contributed to the Group’s asset base. Seasonal fluctuations continued to impact results, with maritime transport generating peak profitability during the summer months and the energy segment experiencing peak demand during the winter heating season. The Group maintained its commitment to shareholder returns, paying total dividends of EUR 62 million, or EUR 3 per share, in 2025, supported by strong liquidity and a prudent leverage profile. Overall, the reporting period demonstrated the resilience of Infortar’s diversified investment model against ongoing macroeconomic uncertainties and geopolitical tensions.
