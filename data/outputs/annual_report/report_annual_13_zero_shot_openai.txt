AS LHV Group reported record consolidated net profit of EUR 150.3 million in 2024, up 7% from EUR 140.9 million in 2023. Profit before tax increased to EUR 175.1 million, while total net income rose 11% to EUR 338.3 million. Net interest income grew 8% to EUR 273.3 million and net fee and commission income increased 24% to EUR 60.3 million. These gains reflected strong balance-sheet growth, higher customer activity and effective cost management, although the rapid decline in interest rates during the second half reduced interest income momentum and deposit repricing weighed on margins. Net interest margin declined from 3.88% to 3.48%.

Operating expenses increased 14% to EUR 146.9 million, mainly because of higher employee numbers and continued investment in building the UK retail bank. The cost-to-income ratio was 43.4%, compared with 42.3% in 2023. Expected credit losses increased to EUR 16.3 million from EUR 11.5 million, reflecting a cautious approach amid Estonia’s continuing recession and a modest weakening in some consumer and corporate exposures, although overall loan quality remained strong. Return on equity was 24.5%, down from 29.0%, while return on assets declined from 2.1% to 1.9%.

The Group’s growth was driven primarily by lending and deposits. Total assets reached EUR 8.7 billion, up 23%. Net loans increased EUR 990 million, or 28%, to EUR 4.6 billion. Estonian corporate and retail portfolios each grew 21%, while UK SME lending expanded 338% to EUR 348 million. Deposits increased 21% to EUR 6.9 billion, supported mainly by term deposits and strong growth in LHV Bank’s platform deposits. LHV Pank added 38,000 customers, bringing its total to 456,000.

LHV Pank remained the main earnings contributor, with net profit of EUR 140.5 million, down 1% despite 6% higher income, as expenses and credit losses rose. LHV Bank delivered EUR 5.8 million of profit, supported by rapid loan and deposit growth, while LHV Varahaldus earned EUR 1.6 million and assets under management increased 3% to EUR 1.56 billion. LHV Kindlustus achieved its second profitable year, with net profit rising to EUR 1.2 million from EUR 0.3 million, and gross written premiums increasing 21% to EUR 38.0 million. LHV Paytech’s profit rose 40% to EUR 0.7 million.

Capital and liquidity remained strong, although capital ratios declined as risk-weighted assets expanded. The total capital adequacy ratio was 20.68%, compared with 23.45% in 2023, and the CET1 ratio was 16.89%. The Group complied with all regulatory and internal requirements and issued EUR 300 million of MREL bonds and EUR 20 million of Tier 2 bonds. Management expects lower short-term profits in 2025 as interest rates decline, partly offset by continued business growth and efficiency measures.
