Gigasun AB reported a strong operational performance for the 2025 financial year, characterized by significant growth in electricity production and improved underlying profitability. Total revenue increased by 9.8 percent to SEK 239.7 million, while net revenue rose by 9.9 percent to SEK 203.2 million. The primary driver of this top-line growth was a 30 percent increase in produced electricity, which reached 327.8 million kWh, up from 252.6 million kWh in the previous year. This operational expansion contributed to a reduction in CO2-related emissions by approximately 161,000 tonnes.

The company’s operating profit, or EBIT, improved substantially by 21.1 percent to SEK 73.5 million, compared to SEK 60.7 million in 2024. This increase reflects the successful execution of the company’s strategy to build a portfolio of high-quality solar installations with stable revenue streams. The EBIT margin expanded to 31 percent from 28 percent in the prior year. Gross profit also grew to SEK 195.8 million, maintaining a stable gross margin of 82 percent. EBITDA increased to SEK 163.7 million, with an EBITDA margin of 68 percent.

Despite the strong operational results, the company reported a net loss for the period of SEK -7.2 million, a significant improvement from the net loss of SEK -32.9 million in 2024. The reported loss was negatively impacted by a non-cash currency effect of SEK -3.8 million and a one-off cost of SEK 3.5 million. When adjusted for these items, the profit amounted to SEK 0.1 million, demonstrating a clear improvement in core operational profitability. The earnings per share improved to -0.13 SEK from -0.58 SEK in the previous year.

Several strategic and market events influenced these financial outcomes. The company continued a refinancing program with Agricultural Bank Financial Leasing Co., Ltd, which secured new financing of SEK 372 million in addition to previous agreements. This refinancing, featuring an interest rate of 3.60 percent and an extended amortization period, has begun to reduce interest expenses, which totaled SEK 76.9 million, down from SEK 84.2 million in 2024. Furthermore, the SOLT4 bond was extended to November 2026, providing greater financial flexibility.

Market conditions in China also played a role. Electricity tariffs in the Jiangsu and Anhui provinces were adjusted in the second half of the year, reducing the realized price per kWh by approximately 8 percent. The company assesses that the impact on its existing project portfolio is manageable, amounting to a 3–4 percent reduction in revenue. However, structural cost improvements, including lower financing costs and declining construction costs for new projects, are expected to offset the lower average revenue per kWh. The company installed 55.7 MW of new capacity during the year, bringing the total installed capacity to 395.8 MW. Signed orders stood at 16.4 MW at year-end. The equity ratio decreased to 21 percent from 23 percent, primarily due to the accumulated losses and currency translation effects, though the rolling twelve-month equity ratio remained at 22 percent. The company maintains its long-term target of reaching 1,000 MW of installed capacity.
