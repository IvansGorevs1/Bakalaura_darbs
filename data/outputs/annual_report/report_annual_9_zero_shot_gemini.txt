During the financial year 2025, Impero A/S achieved an annual recurring revenue of DKK 46.2 million at the end of the period, representing a 19 percent year-on-year growth rate compared to DKK 38.7 million at the end of 2024. Total revenue for 2025 reached DKK 42.0 million, marking a 19 percent increase from DKK 35.4 million in the previous year. Gross profit improved to DKK 41.0 million compared to DKK 34.2 million in 2024. EBITDA for 2025 was negative DKK 3.1 million, showing a significant improvement of DKK 5.6 million compared to an EBITDA of negative DKK 8.7 million in 2024. Operating loss improved to negative DKK 6.7 million from negative DKK 11.6 million in 2024, and the net loss for the period was DKK 6.8 million compared to DKK 11.4 million in 2024. 

The company demonstrated solid SaaS performance indicators, highlighted by an exceptionally low churn rate of 1 percent in 2025, down from 2 percent in 2024. Net revenue retention was 106 percent compared to 108 percent in the previous period, while net uplift stood at 7 percent against 10 percent in 2024. The average ARR per account reached a record high of DKK 228 thousand, up 8 percent from DKK 212 thousand in 2024. Customer acquisition costs were DKK 371 thousand with a payback period of 22 months, compared to DKK 222 thousand and 15 months in 2024. 

Business operations were affected by macroeconomic volatility and geopolitical uncertainty early in the year, leading to a temporary slowdown in the first half followed by a strong recovery and a record second half in net new ARR generation. Key operational milestones included surpassing the milestone of 200 customers, welcoming 26 new clients during the year, and expanding total customer coverage to 40 percent of the Danish C25 index and 22.5 percent of the German DAX index. Geographic growth was led by the DACH region and Denmark, which grew net new ARR by 26 percent and 22 percent year-on-year, respectively. Product advancements included the launch of the Control Testing module and the implementation of AI-augmented engineering practices and initial AI features. 

To strengthen its capital position, Impero completed a directed issue raising DKK 15 million in December 2025. Total equity stood at DKK 15.4 million as of 31 December 2025, compared to DKK 6.2 million at year-end 2024. Cash and cash equivalents increased to DKK 18.9 million from DKK 9.8 million, with no interest-bearing debt. Because of the improved capital position, the Board of Directors decided to defer its ambition of achieving recurring positive cash flow before the end of 2026, choosing instead to accelerate investments in commercial activities to drive long-term growth. For the full year 2026, Impero expects ARR to reach between DKK 53 million and 57 million, representing a growth rate of approximately 15 percent to 23 percent, and anticipates EBITDA to be in the range of negative DKK 2 million to positive DKK 1 million.
