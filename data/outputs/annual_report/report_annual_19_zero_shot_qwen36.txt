Talkpool AG reported a strong financial performance for the fiscal year 2025, marking a significant transition from a multi-year restructuring phase to a period of organic growth. The company achieved net sales of EUR 17.756 million, representing a 12.5 percent increase compared to EUR 15.782 million in 2024. This revenue growth was driven by sustained momentum in the Americas, which remained the largest market, and stable contributions from Germany, despite temporary fluctuations in planning assignment volumes due to shifts in Deutsche Telekom’s rollout priorities.

Profitability metrics showed mixed but generally positive trends. EBITDA increased to EUR 1.828 million from EUR 1.702 million in the prior year, resulting in an EBITDA margin of 10.3 percent, a slight decline from the 10.8 percent recorded in 2024. Similarly, EBIT rose to EUR 1.620 million from EUR 1.527 million, with the EBIT margin decreasing marginally to 9.1 percent from 9.7 percent. Management attributed these slight margin compressions to temporary pressures caused by initial investments required to generate higher-margin future business, including personnel hiring and technological innovation. However, net earnings after tax improved significantly, rising by 42 percent to EUR 862 thousand from EUR 605 thousand in 2024. The net earnings after tax margin expanded to 4.8 percent from 3.8 percent, reflecting improved bottom-line performance despite the strategic investments.

The company’s balance sheet strengthened considerably during the reporting period. Equity improved by EUR 4.6 million, moving from a deficit of EUR 2.5 million in 2021 to a positive position of EUR 2.1 million by the end of 2025. Net cash position also improved by EUR 6.0 million, reaching EUR 0.3 million, up from negative EUR 5.7 million in 2021. This financial stability was further reinforced by an extraordinary audit that increased the valuation of the German subsidiary to approximately EUR 20.5 million, offsetting reductions in the carrying value of Pakistani assets. Currency headwinds, including an 11 percent weakening of the US dollar and a 14 percent decline in the Pakistani Rupee, negatively impacted EUR-reported revenues in the first half of the year, but the company maintained resilience throughout.

Strategically, Talkpool ended its "reduce to the MAX" cost-cutting focus and launched the "Vision 2030" growth plan in April 2025. This plan sets long-term targets including a 16.8 percent compound annual growth rate, a 14.0 percent EBITDA margin, and zero net debt by 2030. The company shifted its geographic focus toward developed markets, particularly Western Europe and the United States, while leveraging Pakistan as a cost-efficient production base for remote services. Key business developments included supporting Digicel in Jamaica after Hurricane Melissa, launching a new Swedish affiliated company for defense communications, and re-entering the US market through a contract with MasTec. Additionally, the company initiated trials of AI-powered technology solutions, with commercialization expected in the coming years. The first phase of the share options scheme delivered strong results, with share value increasing significantly. Management anticipates near-term pressure on cash flow and margins due to growth investments but expects these to translate into higher profitability within the next 12 months.
