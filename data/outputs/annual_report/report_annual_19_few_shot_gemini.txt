Talkpool reported positive financial results for the 2025 reporting period, marked by double-digit revenue growth and improved profitability as the company transitioned from its multi-year restructuring phase into organic business expansion. Net sales for the full year amounted to EUR 17,756 thousand, representing a 12.5 percent increase compared to EUR 15,782 thousand reported in 2024. Profitability also improved across major indicators, supported by cost control and operational efficiency initiatives. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization reached EUR 1,828 thousand, compared to EUR 1,702 thousand in the previous year, resulting in an EBITDA margin of 10.3 percent versus 10.8 percent in 2024. Operating profit stood at EUR 1,620 thousand, compared to EUR 1,527 thousand in 2024, with an EBIT margin of 9.1 percent compared to 9.7 percent. Net earnings after tax increased significantly by 42 percent to EUR 862 thousand, up from EUR 605 thousand in the prior year, yielding a net earnings after tax margin of 4.8 percent compared to 3.8 percent previously. 

The financial performance was influenced by several business developments and market factors across the company's operating regions. The Americas remained Talkpool's largest market, delivering strong revenue growth despite currency headwinds from a weakening US dollar and operational challenges in Haiti. In Pakistan, operations achieved record local currency revenue and improved gross profitability following commercial renegotiations with major customers. In Germany, fiber network planning activities continued at a high pace in cooperation with Deutsche Telekom, though assignment volumes experienced a temporary flattening due to a shift in the operator's rollout priorities. Additionally, an independent valuation by BDO assessed the fair value of Talkpool's German subsidiary at EUR 20.5 million. 

The company's balance sheet and financial position strengthened substantially during 2025. Equity improved to EUR 2.1 million from negative EUR 2.5 million in 2021, while net cash improved significantly over the same multi-year period to reach a positive position of EUR 0.3 million by the end of 2025. Management also introduced the Vision 2030 strategic growth plan in April 2025, setting long-term targets including an average annual revenue growth rate of 16.8 percent, an EBITDA margin of 14 percent, and zero net debt by 2030. Overall, the reporting period reflected a successful conclusion of restructuring activities, accelerating organic growth, and strengthening foundational profitability and cash generation capabilities.
