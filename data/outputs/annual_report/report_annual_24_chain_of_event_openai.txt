Trophy Games delivered a significantly stronger performance in 2025, driven primarily by the scaling of its Transport Series. Revenue increased 40% to DKK 126.5 million from DKK 90.1 million, while EBITDA rose 26% to DKK 31.1 million. Profit before tax more than doubled, increasing 109% to DKK 19.0 million from DKK 9.1 million, and profit for the year rose to DKK 12.5 million from DKK 7.5 million. The EBITDA margin declined to 25% from 27%, reflecting substantially higher marketing investment, while the EBT margin improved to 15% from 10%. The company exceeded its initial 2025 guidance and raised its full-year expectations four times during the year.

Growth was concentrated in the three largest Transport titles. Airline Manager revenue increased 28% to DKK 37.8 million, Truck Manager revenue rose from DKK 1.2 million to DKK 25.2 million, and Farm Manager generated DKK 22.0 million during its first six months following its July launch. Together, these “Big 3” titles accounted for 67% of group revenue, or DKK 85 million, compared with 35% in 2024, and generated 65% of EBITDA, or DKK 20 million. The shift reduced reliance on older titles, although revenue from other Transport games fell 46% to DKK 21.0 million as marketing resources were redirected toward the faster-growing core titles.

The company expanded marketing expenditure 76% to DKK 40.5 million to support user acquisition and capture market share. This contributed to increased installs, unique paying users and transactions, which rose 48%, 69% and 58%, respectively. A strategic advertising optimisation programme launched in late September also contributed to results: in-app advertising revenue doubled to DKK 18.5 million and represented 14.6% of revenue, compared with 10.2% in 2024. Management noted that advertising carries no platform fees, supporting direct-cost efficiency and margins.

The first half was affected by a weakening USD, which reduced reported revenue and EBITDA, but the lower real cost of marketing in key territories enabled higher acquisition spending. Q4 became the strongest quarter in the company’s history, with revenue up 106% to DKK 41.0 million and EBT up 574% to DKK 10.1 million, despite marketing expenditure rising 296%.

Cash flow from operating activities declined to DKK 12.9 million from DKK 19.3 million, while year-end cash fell 9% to DKK 23.0 million, reflecting increased marketing, a DKK 14 million VAT receivable, share buybacks of DKK 4.9 million and repayment of DKK 4.7 million in previously received tax credits. The group remained debt-free with a 78% solvency ratio. For 2026, management guides for revenue of DKK 130–153 million, EBITDA of DKK 28–36 million and EBT of DKK 18–24 million.
