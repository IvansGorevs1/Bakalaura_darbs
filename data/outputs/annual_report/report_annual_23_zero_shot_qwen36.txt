TKM Grupp AS reported consolidated revenue of 919.6 million euros for the fiscal year 2025, representing a decrease of 2.6 percent compared to 944.6 million euros in the previous year. The Group’s net profit amounted to 17.5 million euros, a significant decline of 36.1 percent from the 27.5 million euros recorded in 2024. This reduction in profitability was primarily driven by a substantial contraction in the car trade segment, which was heavily impacted by the introduction of the motor vehicle tax in Estonia and resulting declines in consumer demand. The car segment’s revenue fell by 11.9 percent to 176.9 million euros, and its net profit dropped by 54.4 percent to 4.3 million euros. The supermarket segment, the Group’s largest revenue contributor, showed resilience with a slight revenue increase of 0.3 percent to 611.9 million euros, although its net profit decreased by 38.6 percent to 9.5 million euros due to lower sales volumes and increased promotional activity. The department stores segment recorded a net loss of 0.5 million euros, a deterioration from the small profit in the prior year, while the security services segment saw its net profit decline by 117.8 percent to nearly zero. Conversely, the real estate segment contributed positively to the bottom line, with net profit rising by 20.5 percent to 6.3 million euros, supported by rental income from a new logistics centre. The Group’s operating profit decreased to 34.9 million euros from 47.6 million euros, and earnings per share fell to 43 cents from 67 cents. Despite the challenging economic environment characterized by low consumer confidence and high price sensitivity, the Group maintained stable operations with a workforce of 4,743 employees. Total investments in 2025 amounted to 25.5 million euros, focusing on store refurbishments, digital platform upgrades, and the expansion of the car dealership network in the Baltic States. The Group continued to strengthen its market position through strategic developments, including the opening of new KIA and Škoda showrooms in Vilnius and the establishment of a new dealership in Peetri, Estonia. Furthermore, the Group announced a post-balance-sheet event in January 2026 to acquire Rohe Auto AS and SKO Motors OÜ, aiming to expand its Škoda distribution rights in Tallinn and Harju County. The company’s share price closed at 9.50 euros at the end of the year, with a market capitalization of 386.9 million euros. The Management Board proposed a dividend of 0.60 euros per share for the 2025 financial year, to be paid out of accumulated retained earnings. Overall, the Group’s performance in 2025 reflected the pressures of a volatile economic landscape, yet the company demonstrated operational stability and continued strategic investment in its core business segments to support long-term growth.
