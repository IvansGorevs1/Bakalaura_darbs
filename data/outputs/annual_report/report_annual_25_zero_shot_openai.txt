WindowMaster International A/S reported a weaker financial performance in 2025, although operating cash flow remained solid and market activity improved during the second half of the year. Revenue declined 8% to DKK 268.6m from DKK 294.5m in 2024. Management attributed the decrease primarily to a slow start to the year and lower sales to a key customer, particularly in the Nordic market. Revenue was DKK 21–41m below the original budget but in line with the latest guidance of DKK 269–270m. The decline reflected lower Climatic sales in the Nordic region and less growth in North America.

Gross profit decreased to DKK 131.1m from DKK 137.9m, but the gross margin improved to 48.8% from 46.9%. Management linked the margin improvement to a more favorable business mix, with less key-account revenue and more direct project revenue. EBITDA fell 25% to DKK 28.0m from DKK 37.3m, reducing the EBITDA margin to 10.4% from 12.7%. Fixed costs were maintained to support future growth and included DKK 2.5m of extraordinary costs related to a ransomware attack in May and June, including infrastructure rebuilding, enhanced security and external consultants. Earnings before tax declined to DKK 2.9m from DKK 12.2m, while profit after tax fell to DKK 0.9m from DKK 10.6m. The effective tax rate was 70%, partly because tax assets and tax loss carryforwards were not fully utilized.

Cash flow from operating activities remained broadly stable at DKK 28.5m, compared with DKK 29.4m in 2024, supported by improved net working capital, particularly inventories and work in progress. Investing cash outflow decreased to DKK 9.4m from DKK 17.6m, as the prior year included significant investments. Investments in 2025 focused on digitalization, software development, process automation and IT infrastructure. Cash increased by DKK 0.6m to DKK 4.0m at year-end.

Net interest-bearing debt increased to DKK 38.7m from DKK 36.7m, and gearing rose to 1.7 times EBITDA from 1.2, remaining below the target of 2.0. Equity declined to DKK 32.5m from DKK 36.6m, with the net-cash equity ratio at 23.5%, below the target of more than 25%.

Market conditions improved as interest rates stabilized, especially in the non-residential construction sector during the second half of 2025. Demand continued to benefit from energy-efficiency investments and environmental regulation in Europe and North America. For 2026, management expects organic revenue growth of 7–14% to DKK 290–310m and EBITDA of DKK 45–55m, corresponding to an EBITDA margin of approximately 8–10%, while noting substantial uncertainty in project timing and global economic and geopolitical conditions.
