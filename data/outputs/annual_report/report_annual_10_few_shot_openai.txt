InCoax Networks’ financial performance weakened in 2025, primarily because delayed customer orders and a more cautious market reduced sales. Net sales declined 35% to SEK 48.1 million from SEK 74.5 million in 2024, with product revenue falling to SEK 41.0 million from SEK 69.2 million. North America remained the main market, generating SEK 39.6 million of sales compared with SEK 69.6 million previously. Despite the lower revenue, gross margin improved to 62% from 57%, as goods-for-resale costs decreased to SEK 18.1 million from SEK 32.3 million.

The lower sales level, together with continued investments in product development, strategic collaborations, and market development, led to a deterioration in profitability. Operating loss increased to SEK 32.4 million from SEK 19.4 million, while the loss after tax widened to SEK 32.6 million from SEK 19.2 million. Earnings per share decreased to SEK –0.25 from SEK –0.18. Total operating expenses declined to SEK 105.9 million from SEK 121.2 million, mainly due to lower external costs and goods-for-resale expenses, although personnel costs increased. The company capitalized SEK 25.3 million of development costs, broadly in line with SEK 25.6 million in 2024, bringing capitalized development assets to SEK 118.0 million at year-end.

Cash flow improved because of financing activities, although the underlying cash position remained pressured. Cash flow from operating activities was negative SEK 20.0 million, an improvement from negative SEK 27.1 million, while investing activities used SEK 25.3 million. A directed share issue, convertible financing, and loans generated approximately SEK 53.5 million before issue costs, resulting in total cash flow of SEK 6.5 million compared with negative SEK 12.8 million in 2024. Cash and cash equivalents increased to SEK 8.7 million at year-end, but the credit facility was fully utilized. The equity ratio declined to 71.8% from 80.8%, and long-term liabilities totaled SEK 23.2 million.

Strategically, 2025 marked a transition from intensive development toward commercialization. The company completed key product and integration work, including the D2501 product’s integration into Nokia’s Gigabit Connect platform, and began initial North American installations through the partnership. The D2508 product was also launched through InCoax’s own channels. Management has initiated structural cost reductions expected to lower ongoing costs by more than 40%, or over SEK 25 million annually from 2026, with the objective of achieving cash-flow neutrality within the coming year. However, the auditor highlighted material uncertainty regarding going concern because future financing depends partly on the exercise of warrants or obtaining alternative funding.
