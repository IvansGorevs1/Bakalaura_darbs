Talkpool delivered improved financial performance in 2025 as it completed its restructuring phase and returned to organic growth. Net sales increased 12.5% to EUR 17.756 million from EUR 15.782 million in 2024, marking the company’s first year of double-digit growth since the consolidation period began. Growth was organic, although reported revenue was adversely affected by currency weakness, including an 11% decline in the US dollar and a 14% decline in the Pakistani rupee during the first half of the year.

Profitability remained solid despite investments in personnel, technology, and future growth. EBITDA rose to EUR 1.828 million from EUR 1.702 million, although the EBITDA margin decreased slightly to 10.3% from 10.8%. EBIT increased to EUR 1.620 million from EUR 1.527 million, while the EBIT margin declined to 9.1% from 9.7%. Net earnings after tax increased substantially by 42% to EUR 862 thousand from EUR 605 thousand, raising the net earnings margin to 4.8% from 3.8%. Management expects temporary pressure on margins and cash flow as investments made under the new growth strategy take approximately six to twelve months to generate revenue.

The Americas remained the largest market, supported by continued momentum in the Caribbean and long-standing customer relationships, particularly with Digicel. Talkpool also supported the restoration of Jamaica’s communications infrastructure following Hurricane Melissa. The German business continued to make a substantial contribution, although planning volumes temporarily flattened as Deutsche Telekom shifted emphasis from homes connected to homes passed. Pakistan achieved its highest revenue in local currency, renegotiated customer terms, and improved gross profitability, while also expanding its services portfolio. The US market entry progressed more slowly than planned, with low order volumes after the company discontinued fiber rollout work to focus on remote wireless services.

Talkpool continued shifting toward technology-enabled remote services and AI-supported solutions, with initial trials and proof-of-concept projects completed during the year. The company launched its Vision 2030 plan, targeting EUR 40 million in revenue, a 14% EBITDA margin, 16.8% average annual revenue growth, and zero net debt by 2030.

The balance sheet strengthened materially. Equity improved to EUR 2.1 million from negative EUR 2.5 million in 2021, while net debt improved by EUR 6.0 million over the same period to a positive net cash position of EUR 0.3 million at year-end 2025. An independent BDO valuation placed the German subsidiary’s market value at approximately EUR 20.5 million. Subsequent to year-end, a EUR 1.65 million share issue in March 2026 was expected to move the group to approximately EUR 2 million of net cash.
