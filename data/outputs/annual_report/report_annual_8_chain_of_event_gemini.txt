For the financial year ended June 30, 2025, AS SAF Tehnika and its subsidiaries reported a consolidated net turnover of 25.99 million euros, representing a decrease of 1.1 million euros or 4% compared to the previous financial year 2023/2024. The parent company reported a net turnover of 21.92 million euros, which is 1.33 million euros or 6% lower than the 23.25 million euros reported in the preceding financial year. Group sales outside Latvia accounted for 97% of the total turnover, reaching 25.24 million euros, with products sold across 87 countries worldwide.

Regionally, turnover in the Americas, encompassing North, South, and Central America, was 14.17 million euros, representing 55% of the total group turnover and marking a decrease of 2.3 million euros or 14% compared to the 2023/2024 financial year. This decline was largely influenced by the realization of large-scale projects in subsequent periods and temporary caution among customers regarding investment decisions and tariff announcements, although the market is showing stabilization and a gradual recovery of activity. Conversely, turnover in the European region, including Latvia, increased by 1.4 million euros or 17%, reaching 10.0 million euros. Revenue in the Asia, Middle East, and Africa regions decreased by 90 thousand euros or 5%, amounting to 1.8 million euros.

The group concluded the 2024/2025 financial year with a consolidated net loss of 642.6 thousand euros, and basic and diluted loss per share of 0.216 euros. This represents an improvement compared to the previous financial year, when the group reported a net loss of 2.37 million euros and a loss per share of 0.798 euros. The parent company reported a net loss of 551.8 thousand euros for the reporting period, compared to a net loss of 1.34 million euros in the previous financial year. Gross profit for the group increased significantly to 9.50 million euros, up from 7.53 million euros in the 2023/2024 financial year, driven by a reduction in cost of goods sold from 19.56 million euros down to 16.49 million euros.

Operating loss for the group also improved to 1.29 million euros from 3.22 million euros in the previous year. Selling and marketing expenses decreased to 7.43 million euros from 7.84 million euros, while administration expenses increased to 3.36 million euros from 2.91 million euros. Other income decreased to 703.4 thousand euros from 919.3 thousand euros, including 664.6 thousand euros in state grants received. Finance income was 49.9 thousand euros, and finance costs were 111.7 thousand euros.

As of June 30, 2025, total consolidated assets stood at 26.08 million euros, compared to 23.02 million euros at the end of the previous financial year. Current assets increased to 21.16 million euros from 17.54 million euros, driven largely by an increase in cash and cash equivalents to 4.81 million euros from 2.21 million euros, and inventories of 14.00 million euros compared to 12.79 million euros. Total equity decreased to 15.76 million euros from 16.44 million euros as a result of the reporting year losses. Total liabilities increased to 10.33 million euros from 6.59 million euros, primarily due to an increase in contract liabilities from 1.23 million euros to 4.45 million euros and trade payables and other liabilities. The group's net cash flow from operating activities was positive at 3.87 million euros, compared to 1.71 million euros in the previous financial year. Investing activities resulted in a net cash outflow of 755.0 thousand euros, while financing activities resulted in a net cash outflow of 418.9 thousand euros.
