WindowMaster International A/S reported a decline in financial performance for the 2025 fiscal year compared to the previous period. Total revenue decreased by 8% to DKK 269 million, down from DKK 294 million in 2024. This contraction was primarily driven by a slow start to the year and reduced sales to a key customer in the Nordic region, as well as lower-than-expected growth in North America. Despite the top-line pressure, the company achieved an improvement in gross profit margins, which rose to 48.8% from 46.9% in the prior year. This margin expansion was attributed to a favorable business mix characterized by less revenue from key accounts and a higher proportion of direct project sales, consistent with the company’s "Accelerate Core" strategy.

Operating profitability weakened significantly. EBITDA fell to DKK 28.0 million from DKK 37.3 million, resulting in an EBITDA margin of 10.4% compared to 12.7% previously. The decrease in operating profit was linked to maintained fixed cost levels intended to support future growth, as well as DKK 2.5 million in one-off costs incurred due to a ransomware attack on the company’s IT infrastructure in May and June 2025. Consequently, earnings before tax dropped to DKK 2.9 million from DKK 12.2 million. The profit after tax amounted to DKK 0.9 million, a sharp decline from DKK 10.6 million in 2024, partly due to the non-utilization of tax loss carryforwards.

Cash flow from operating activities remained robust at DKK 28.5 million, slightly below the DKK 29.4 million generated in 2024. The company successfully improved net working capital, reducing it to DKK 31.9 million from DKK 35.0 million, primarily through better management of inventories and work in progress. Cash flow from investment activities was DKK -9.4 million, reflecting investments in digitalization and IT security upgrades. Net interest-bearing debt increased to DKK 38.7 million from DKK 36.7 million, leading to a financial gearing ratio of 1.7, up from 1.2 in the previous year. Equity decreased to DKK 32.5 million, resulting in an equity ratio of 23.5%.

Looking ahead, the company’s outlook for 2026 is characterized by uncertainty regarding the timing of market improvement. Management expects organic revenue growth of 7% to 14%, targeting DKK 290–310 million, with EBITDA expected to reach DKK 45–55 million. The stabilization of interest rates during 2025 supported increased project execution, particularly in the second half of the year, and the company anticipates continued positive underlying market trends driven by energy efficiency investments and environmental regulations.
