Gigasun AB reported a year of robust operational growth and improved profitability in 2025, despite a net loss driven by non-cash currency fluctuations and one-off costs. The company’s core business, which involves financing, owning, and operating roof-mounted solar energy installations in China, demonstrated strong underlying performance. Total revenue increased by 9.8 percent to SEK 239.7 million, up from SEK 218.3 million in the previous year. This growth was primarily supported by a 30 percent increase in electricity production, which reached 328 million kWh, compared to 253 million kWh in 2024. The expansion of the installed capacity base, which grew by 16 percent to 395.8 MW from 340.1 MW, was a key driver of this higher production volume.

Operating profit, or EBIT, showed significant improvement, rising by 21.1 percent to SEK 73.5 million from SEK 60.7 million in the prior period. This increase reflects stable operations and improved underlying profitability, with the EBIT margin expanding to 31 percent from 28 percent. Gross profit also grew to SEK 195.8 million, maintaining a stable gross margin of 82 percent. The company’s ability to generate cash from operations remained strong, with cash flow from operating activities amounting to SEK 101.1 million, although this was a decrease from the SEK 133.8 million generated in 2024, largely due to changes in working capital.

The reported net loss for the period was SEK -7.2 million, a substantial improvement from the SEK -32.9 million loss in 2024. However, this result was negatively impacted by a non-cash currency effect of SEK -3.8 million and a one-off cost of SEK 3.5 million. Adjusted for these items, the profit amounted to SEK 0.1 million, demonstrating that the core operations are now profitable. Interest expenses decreased to SEK 76.9 million from SEK 84.2 million, aided by ongoing refinancing activities in China with Agricultural Bank Financial Leasing Co., Ltd. These refinancing measures, which include extending amortization periods and securing lower interest rates, are expected to further improve cash flow and earnings in the coming years.

Strategically, Gigasun continued to expand its portfolio, signing 17 contracts representing 41.7 MW of new capacity. At year-end, the company had 16.4 MW in signed orders. The company maintains its long-term target of reaching 1,000 MW of installed capacity. The market environment in China remains favorable, with solar capacity surpassing coal-fired power capacity for the first time. Although electricity tariffs in some provinces were adjusted, reducing realized prices by approximately 8 percent in the second half of the year, the company assesses the impact on its existing portfolio as manageable. Gigasun remains focused on securing equity for continued expansion, as access to capital is identified as the primary bottleneck for growth. The company does not intend to pay a dividend in the foreseeable future, prioritizing reinvestment into its growing portfolio.
