Lyko Group delivered continued revenue growth in 2025 but with weaker profitability. Net sales rose 11.3% to SEK 3,962.6 million from SEK 3,561.7 million in 2024, approaching SEK 4 billion. Growth was primarily driven by the Nordics, where sales increased 12.0% to SEK 3,699.8 million and represented 93.4% of group sales. Norway exceeded SEK 1 billion in annual revenue. Europe remained comparatively weak, with sales declining 1.3% to SEK 130.7 million; a reorganization was initiated in the fourth quarter, including closure of local offices in Germany and the Netherlands and centralized management from the Nordic headquarters.

Gross profit increased to SEK 1,658.7 million from SEK 1,568.8 million, but the gross margin fell from 44.0% to 41.9%. EBIT declined to SEK 78.2 million from SEK 117.4 million, reducing the EBIT margin from 3.3% to 2.0%. EBITDA decreased to SEK 252.3 million from SEK 287.4 million. The company reported a loss before tax of SEK 0.9 million, compared with profit of SEK 37.6 million in 2024, and a net loss of SEK 3.0 million versus profit of SEK 21.0 million. Earnings per share fell to SEK -0.20 from SEK 1.37. The report states that implementation of the new warehouse automation affected results during parts of the year, while the company also operated in an industry characterized by intense competition, profit warnings and structural shifts.

Cash generation improved substantially. Cash flow from operating activities increased to SEK 527.5 million from SEK 209.7 million, while total cash flow rose to SEK 327.3 million from SEK 71.7 million. Investing cash flow increased to SEK -351.2 million, mainly reflecting the new warehouse automation, store openings and digital development. The Vansbro automation investment was approximately SEK 500 million and, once fully operational in early 2026, is designed to handle up to approximately 100,000 orders per day.

The company ended the year with SEK 452.5 million in cash, compared with SEK 140.5 million previously. Net debt excluding IFRS 16 declined to SEK 292.5 million from SEK 333.1 million, although total interest-bearing liabilities including leases increased to SEK 1,416.3 million from SEK 1,169.3 million. The equity ratio decreased to 14.8% from 20.3%, reflecting significant balance-sheet expansion and increased borrowing.

Major developments included the opening of two stores, Lyko’s first Fab Store in Täby, the relaunch of Make Up Store, the launch of Scandinavian Soap Factory, and continued investment in own brands, logistics and customer engagement. No dividend is proposed for 2025.
