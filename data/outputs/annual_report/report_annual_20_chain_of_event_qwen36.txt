AS Tallink Grupp reported a decline in financial performance for the year ended 31 December 2025, driven by challenging market conditions and reduced operational volumes. Consolidated revenue decreased by 2.6% to EUR 765.3 million from EUR 785.8 million in the prior year. This contraction was primarily attributable to a 19.2% drop in cargo units transported to 245,004 and a 2.2% decrease in passenger vehicles to 760,473. Although the number of passengers carried remained relatively stable at 5.5 million, showing a minor 0.9% decline, the mix of traffic shifted, with passenger numbers on the Estonia-Finland route increasing by 1.8% while those on Finland-Sweden and Estonia-Sweden routes fell by 2.4% and 12.5% respectively. The segment result from core route operations declined to EUR 70.5 million from EUR 81.5 million in 2024.

The Group’s EBITDA fell significantly by 25.7% to EUR 130.1 million, down from EUR 175.2 million in 2024. Consequently, net profit for the period decreased by 57.1% to EUR 17.3 million, or EUR 0.023 per share, compared to EUR 40.3 million or EUR 0.054 per share in the previous year. The deterioration in profitability was linked to several specific factors. Revenue from cargo transport dropped sharply, and onboard sales declined due to lower passenger numbers and weak consumer confidence. Personnel expenses increased by 4.3% to EUR 201.0 million, largely due to collective agreement adjustments for maritime workers. Fuel and emission costs rose slightly to EUR 96.5 million.

However, these negative pressures were partially offset by cost-saving measures and accounting adjustments. The Group aligned the estimated useful lives of its cruise and passenger vessels to 45 years, which reduced depreciation expense by EUR 18.0 million to EUR 79.8 million. Additionally, the sale of three vessels contributed to lower depreciation and generated proceeds of EUR 78.8 million from investing activities. Net finance costs also improved, declining by EUR 6.8 million to EUR 21.4 million, aided by a decreased outstanding loan balance and lower interest rates.

The Group maintained a strong liquidity position with a total liquidity buffer of EUR 110.4 million at year-end, comprising cash, cash equivalents, and unused credit facilities. Net debt decreased by 19.6% to EUR 432.4 million from EUR 537.7 million in 2024, resulting in a net debt to EBITDA ratio of 3.3. Interest-bearing liabilities fell to EUR 445.9 million. The Group paid dividends totaling EUR 44.6 million, supported by its dividend policy. Capital expenditures amounted to EUR 33.0 million, focused on vessel maintenance and IT system improvements. The economic environment remained volatile, characterized by low consumer and business confidence, geopolitical tensions, and inflationary pressures in core markets, which continued to impact travel demand and purchasing behavior. Despite these headwinds, the Group preserved its market-leading position on key routes and continued to optimize its fleet through vessel sales and chartering arrangements.
