Aktsiaselts Infortar reported strong financial performance for the full year 2025, characterized by significant revenue growth and improved operational efficiency. The Group’s total revenue reached EUR 1,837 million, a substantial increase from EUR 1,371.8 million in 2024. This growth was primarily driven by the full-year consolidation of Tallink Grupp, which contributed significantly to the maritime transport segment, alongside improved results in the energy segment and the addition of new agricultural operations. Gross profit expanded to EUR 239.8 million from EUR 128.6 million in the prior year. Operating profit also saw a notable rise, increasing to EUR 109 million compared to EUR 77 million in 2024. EBITDA grew by approximately 57% to EUR 228 million, with the EBITDA margin improving from 10.6% to 12.4%. The net profit attributable to equity holders of the parent company was EUR 70.6 million, a decrease from EUR 191.3 million in 2024. The report attributes this decline in net profit to one-off effects related to the initial consolidation of Tallink Grupp in 2024, which included a gain from bargain purchase that did not recur in 2025.

The balance sheet reflects a strengthened financial position with total assets amounting to EUR 2,597 million and total equity increasing to EUR 1,175 million. The equity ratio improved to 45% from 43% in the previous year. Interest-bearing liabilities decreased to EUR 1,071 million from EUR 1,223 million, resulting in a reduction of net debt to EUR 852 million from EUR 1,056 million. The net debt to EBITDA ratio improved to 3.7 from 4.0. The Group maintained strong liquidity with cash and cash equivalents totaling EUR 219.8 million.

Strategic developments during the year included the acquisition of Estonia Farmid OÜ in July, enhancing the Group’s presence in agriculture and biomethane production. In the energy segment, Elenger Grupp expanded its renewable portfolio by commissioning a new solar power plant in Latvia and completing its first large-scale battery storage facility. The maritime segment benefited from vessel chartering, which helped balance seasonal fluctuations. The Group continued to focus on sustainability, with 7% of turnover and 13% of capital expenditure classified as taxonomy-aligned. Infortar paid dividends of EUR 3 per share, totaling EUR 62 million, consistent with its dividend aristocrat policy. The share price increased by 5.5% to EUR 45.8 at year-end. The Group’s workforce grew to 6,466 employees, reflecting the expansion of its consolidated subsidiaries. Overall, Infortar demonstrated resilience and strategic execution, delivering stable cash flows and strengthening its asset base despite a challenging macroeconomic environment marked by geopolitical tensions and energy market volatility.
