Konsolidator A/S reported a significant improvement in financial performance for the year ended December 31, 2025, driven by its Resilient Growth strategy and the expansion of its product portfolio. Total revenue increased by 25% to DKK 25.3 million, up from DKK 20.3 million in 2024. This growth was supported by a 13% increase in subscription fees to DKK 20.6 million and a substantial 134% rise in onboarding and consulting fees to DKK 4.7 million, resulting from price changes initiated in the first quarter of 2025. The company added 52 new customers during the year, bringing the total active customer base to 283.

Contracted Annual Recurring Revenue (CARR) grew by 15% to DKK 24.4 million, exceeding the guidance range of DKK 23–24 million. This increase was driven by new sales of DKK 4.5 million and net upsells of DKK 1.1 million, partially offset by churn of DKK 2.5 million. The average contract value per customer increased to DKK 87,000 from DKK 70,000 in the previous year. The Rule of 40 score improved dramatically from negative 40% in 2024 to 17% in 2025, reflecting the combination of 25% revenue growth and an EBITDA margin of negative 8%.

Operating profitability showed marked improvement, with the EBITDA loss narrowing significantly to DKK 2.0 million from a loss of DKK 9.3 million in 2024. This improvement was primarily due to reduced marketing and external expenses following the outsourcing of marketing activities in 2024, as well as higher revenue. Staff costs decreased slightly to DKK 21.0 million from DKK 22.0 million in 2024, despite an increase in the average number of employees from 30 to 32. The net loss for the year was DKK 6.6 million, a substantial improvement from the DKK 14.9 million loss in 2024.

Cash flow from operating activities improved to a negative DKK 1.0 million from a negative DKK 9.2 million in 2024, aided by better working capital management, including a shift toward annual invoicing cycles and longer vendor payment terms. Net cash flow for the year was slightly positive at DKK 11,000. To support operations and product development, the company raised DKK 4.8 million through two private placements in February and November 2025. Consequently, the parent company’s equity turned positive to DKK 0.7 million, although the consolidated equity remained negative at DKK 3.6 million due to losses in the Iberian subsidiary.

Key operational metrics highlighted both successes and challenges. The Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) payback period improved by 50% to 23 months from 43 months in 2024, reflecting lower marketing costs. However, the ARR churn rate increased to 12.6% from 10.5%, attributed to competition from ERP systems and the evolving needs of customers for FP&A tools. In response, Konsolidator launched new modules, including a Financial Data Management solution and an FP&A tool, to enhance retention and value. The company expects churn to decline to between 6% and 9% in 2026 as these new features are adopted. Looking ahead, Konsolidator projects revenue and CARR between DKK 27 and 29 million for 2026, with a target of achieving positive EBITDA between DKK 1 and 2 million.
