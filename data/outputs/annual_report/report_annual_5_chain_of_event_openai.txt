Eleving Group delivered record financial results in 2025, supported by strong portfolio expansion, higher demand, and product diversification. Revenue increased 15.4% year on year to EUR 250.0 million, while adjusted EBITDA rose 13.5% to EUR 101.9 million. Net profit from continuing operations increased to EUR 29.2 million from EUR 28.8 million, although total net profit was broadly stable at EUR 29.2 million compared with EUR 29.6 million in 2024, partly reflecting a EUR 5.0 million foreign-currency translation loss in other comprehensive income. Net profit before foreign exchange and discontinued operations increased 25.6% to EUR 40.8 million.

The net portfolio grew 20.2% to EUR 446.3 million, an increase of EUR 75.1 million, and total loan issuances reached a record EUR 458.0 million, up 24.3% from EUR 368.6 million in 2024. Vehicle and device finance issuances increased 34.0%, while consumer finance issuances grew 15.7%. Revenue growth was led by consumer lending, which contributed EUR 114.8 million, up 18.0%, and flexible vehicle finance, which contributed EUR 60.3 million, up 25.9%. Traditional vehicle finance revenue declined 6.0% to EUR 67.0 million, while device financing contributed EUR 7.9 million following its launch in 2025.

The Group attributed growth to strong demand, customer retention, broader digital and partner channels, and new products. Installment loans were introduced in Latvia, Estonia, Romania, and Armenia, generating EUR 14.7 million in issuances. Smartphone financing launched in Kenya and Uganda, reaching a EUR 13.5 million portfolio and 260,000 customers by year-end. Tanzania became the Group’s 17th market through motorcycle financing. Latvia, Romania, and Kenya were identified as major contributors to growth. The Group also sold a EUR 12.4 million vehicle-finance portfolio in Moldova and plans to focus there on personal loans amid regulatory uncertainty.

Funding capacity strengthened materially. The Group completed a EUR 40 million bond tap and issued EUR 275 million of new five-year bonds while refinancing EUR 150 million of existing debt. Total borrowings principal reached EUR 441.6 million. However, capitalization declined to 23.7% from 29.3%, and net leverage increased to 3.8 from 3.3. The Group paid EUR 19.65 million in dividends, representing approximately a 10% cash yield based on the IPO price. Fitch affirmed its B rating and upgraded the outlook from stable to positive.
