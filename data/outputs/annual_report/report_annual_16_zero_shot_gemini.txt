During the 2025 financial year, Nordic Fibreboard AS reported consolidated net sales of 7.33 million euros, representing a decrease of 3.4% compared to the 2024 sales of 7.59 million euros. Sales revenue from the production and wholesale of fibreboards was 7.33 million euros in 2025 compared to 7.57 million euros in 2024, while European Union sales remained relatively flat at 92% of total sales. Revenue from real estate management dropped to 3 thousand euros from 25 thousand euros in the previous year. The company recorded a consolidated EBITDA of negative 0.42 million euros for 2025, compared to negative 0.03 million euros in 2024, resulting in an EBITDA margin of negative 6%. The negative EBITDA in both periods was attributed to scheduled and unscheduled factory shutdowns in December for transitioning to a new gas boiler house and addressing weather-related structural damage, respectively, which incurred ongoing personnel, maintenance, and repair costs while production was suspended. The consolidated net loss for 2025 widened to 1.15 million euros from a net loss of 0.79 million euros in 2024, resulting in a basic loss per share of 0.14 euros compared to 0.17 euros in 2024. As of 31 December 2025, total assets stood at 9.3 million euros compared to 8.3 million euros at the end of 2024, while total liabilities increased to 4.9 million euros from 4.6 million euros, consisting of 1.3 million euros in payables and 3.2 million euros in borrowings. Operating cash flow for 2025 resulted in a negative cash flow of 503 thousand euros compared to a negative cash flow of 174 thousand euros in 2024. Cash outflows from investing activities totaled 989 thousand euros, driven by 1,367 thousand euros in production asset investments and 161 thousand euros in real estate investments, partially offset by 539 thousand euros from the sale of shares in TPD. Financing activities generated a cash inflow of 1,444 thousand euros, boosted by a share capital increase completed in October 2025 that brought in 2.0 million euros through the issuance of 4 million new shares at an issue price of 0.50 euros per share, raising total share capital to 850 thousand euros. Business developments during the period included the corporate merger of Nordic Fibreboard AS and its wholly owned subsidiary Pärnu Riverside Development OÜ on 30 July 2025, consolidating ownership and management of the Admirali Quarter real estate development project. Furthermore, the company faced a challenging macroeconomic environment characterized by weak domestic demand and slow economic growth in Estonia, alongside rising taxes and inflation reaching approximately 6%. Overall, the company experienced a wider net loss and negative operational cash flows underutilized production capacity and restructuring expenses, though it strengthened its equity base through a successful share capital increase and advanced plans for its real estate portfolio, subsequently entering into a preliminary sales agreement in January 2026 to dispose of the Admirali Quarter development properties by mid-2026.
