Tallinna Sadam Group reported a year of resilient financial performance in 2025, characterized by stable revenue and significant improvements in profitability and operational efficiency. Consolidated revenue amounted to EUR 118.7 million, representing a slight decline of 0.8% compared to the previous year. This minor decrease was primarily driven by a sharp reduction in charter fees from the icebreaker Botnica, which fell by 34% to EUR 7.9 million due to a lower utilization rate of 49%. However, this negative impact was more than offset by growth in the core port operations. Revenue from vessel dues increased by 8% to EUR 34.1 million, supported by calls from larger tankers and cruise ships. Passenger fees grew by 3% to EUR 12.2 million, while cargo charges rose by 3% to EUR 6.9 million, reflecting increased handling volumes. Lease income also expanded by 3% to EUR 14.6 million due to fee indexing.

Operational metrics demonstrated a strong recovery in cargo and passenger flows. Cargo volume grew by 5.1% to 13.8 million tonnes, with liquid bulk seeing a substantial 47% increase, partly recovering from previous sanction-related declines. The number of passengers reached 8.3 million, a 1.0% increase, bolstered by a 25% surge in cruise passengers and a 10% rise in cruise ship calls. Ferry services maintained steady performance, serving 2.5 million passengers and 1.2 million vehicles across domestic routes, despite a slight 0.6% decrease in the number of voyages.

The Group’s ability to manage costs effectively led to marked improvements in profitability. Operating expenses decreased by 10% to EUR 36.4 million, largely due to lower technical maintenance and repair costs for the icebreaker Botnica, which had been elevated in the prior year due to a technical failure. Consequently, operating profit rose by 12.2% to EUR 32.7 million, and the operating profit margin improved from 24.4% to 27.6%. Adjusted EBITDA increased by 6.4% to EUR 56.5 million, with the adjusted EBITDA margin expanding to 47.6%. Net profit grew by 17.3% to EUR 22.5 million, although this growth was partially tempered by a higher dividend income tax expense resulting from a change in Estonian tax regulations.

Financial position remained robust, with equity increasing by 0.9% to EUR 380.9 million and net debt decreasing by 15.5% to EUR 141.7 million. The Group maintained a strong liquidity position, with cash and cash equivalents rising to EUR 32.0 million. Capital investments totaled EUR 33.2 million, a 14.4% decrease from the previous year, focusing on infrastructure upgrades at Paldiski South Harbour and Muuga Harbour, as well as ferry maintenance. The company continued its shareholder-friendly policy, distributing a dividend of EUR 19.2 million, which represented 100% of the prior year’s profit. Overall, the period was defined by successful cost management, operational recovery in core port activities, and strengthened financial metrics.
