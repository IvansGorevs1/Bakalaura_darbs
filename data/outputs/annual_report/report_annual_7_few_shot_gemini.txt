During the reporting period, Heimstaden AB experienced stable underlying operational performance alongside ongoing balance sheet strengthening and portfolio optimization. Rental income for the full year remained flat at SEK 15,916 million compared to SEK 15,919 million in the previous year, supported by indexation and tenant improvement investments, though offset by foreign exchange effects, privatisations, and portfolio sales. For the fourth quarter isolated, rental income decreased by 3.4% to SEK 3,945 million from SEK 4,083 million in the corresponding period the year before. Annualised like-for-like rental income growth reached 4.9%, driven primarily by indexation contributing 3.3%, alongside tenant improvements, occupancy enhancements, and rent reversion. Real economic occupancy remained flat at 98.5% for the full year and increased to 98.7% for the fourth quarter. 

Property expenses decreased by 6.1% for the year to SEK 4,378 million from SEK 4,661 million, driven by cost control measures, digitalisation in management companies, and a shrinking portfolio size resulting from sales programmes. This cost discipline contributed to an improvement in net operating income to SEK 11,475 million from SEK 11,188 million, resulting in a last twelve months net operating income margin of 72.1% compared to 70.3% previously. 

The fair value of investment properties was SEK 324,429 million at year-end, down from SEK 335,422 million, reflecting portfolio divestments and foreign currency effects. Changes in the fair value of investment properties produced a gain of SEK 9,498 million, representing 3.0% of fair value, supported by operating fundamentals and strong residential housing markets in select regions. Realized gains from property divestments increased to SEK 2,207 million from SEK 1,687 million, driven by the privatisation programme and portfolio sales. Cumulative privatisation sales since inception reached SEK 19,451 million across 4,443 residential units, achieving a 27.9% premium to book value. During the fourth quarter, 798 residential units were sold for a total sales value of SEK 2,890 million at a 32.5% premium to book value. Profit before tax for the year rose significantly to SEK 16,012 million from SEK 10,403 million, while profit for the period reached SEK 13,056 million compared to SEK 7,621 million, influenced by valuation gains and foreign exchange movements. 

On the capital and financing side, the group engaged in active liability management. Subsidiary Heimstaden Bostad completed approximately SEK 59,500 million in refinancings and new financings during the year across debt capital markets and bank financing. Interest expenses for the year were SEK 6,536 million, slightly down from SEK 6,554 million, with the average interest rate ending the year at 3.43% compared to 3.01% previously. The interest coverage ratio improved to 2.0x from 1.9x. Net loan-to-value improved significantly to 52.2% from 56.4%, driven by debt repayment funded by cash proceeds from divestments. Total equity increased to SEK 150,557 million from SEK 147,803 million. Total assets at year-end stood at SEK 354,849 million, down from SEK 373,416 million, reflecting asset reductions from the privatisation strategy and foreign exchange translation effects.
