For the financial year 2025, Lyko reported a net revenue of 3,962.6 million Swedish kronor, representing a revenue growth of 11.3 percent compared to the previous year when net revenue was 3,561.7 million Swedish kronor. The gross profit for 2025 amounted to 1,658.7 million Swedish kronor, resulting in a gross profit margin of 41.9 percent, which declined from 44.0 percent in 2024. Operating profit, or EBIT, for the period decreased to 78.2 million Swedish kronor compared to 117.4 million Swedish kronor in the prior year, with the EBIT margin dropping from 3.3 percent to 2.0 percent. The net result for the year declined to a loss of 3.0 million Swedish kronor, down from a profit of 21.0 million Swedish kronor in 2024, leading to earnings per share of minus 0.20 Swedish kronor compared to 1.37 Swedish kronor in the previous year. Conversely, cash flow from operating activities showed a significant improvement, rising to 527.5 million Swedish kronor from 209.7 million Swedish kronor in 2024. Net debt excluding IFRS 16 stood at 292.5 million Swedish kronor at the end of the period, compared to 333.1 million Swedish kronor in the prior year.

The financial performance was influenced by several business events, developments, and strategic investments described in the report. During 2025, the company continued its growth trajectory by expanding its physical and digital presence, which included opening new retail locations such as its first Fab Store in Täby Centrum, a third store in Finland, and a fifth store in Norway, where annual revenue surpassed one billion Swedish kronor. A primary operational development was the commissioning and ramp-up of the new automated warehouse expansion in Vansbro, an investment designed to handle up to 100,000 orders per day and improve long-term logistical efficiency. The report notes that the implementation and ramp-up of this warehouse automation affected results during parts of the year. Additionally, the group initiated a reorganization of its Europe segment in December, which included closing local offices in the Netherlands and Germany to manage operations centrally from the Nordic headquarters and lower overhead costs. Due to the prioritization of generated cash flows for continued expansion, the Board of Directors proposed no dividend for the 2025 financial year.
