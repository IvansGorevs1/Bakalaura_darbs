WindowMaster International A/S reported a weaker financial performance in 2025, although operating cash flow remained resilient and market conditions improved during the second half of the year. Revenue declined 8% to DKK 268.6m from DKK 294.5m in 2024. Management attributed the reduction primarily to a slow start to the year and lower sales to a key Nordic customer, whose revenue fell to DKK 30m from DKK 43m. Revenue was also below the original budget by DKK 21–41m, mainly because of lower Climatic sales in the Nordic region and less growth in North America, but it was in line with the latest guidance of DKK 269–270m.

Gross profit decreased to DKK 131.1m from DKK 137.9m, but the gross margin improved to 48.8% from 46.9%. The company linked the higher margin to a more favorable business mix, with less revenue from key accounts and more direct project revenue, consistent with its “Accelerate Core” strategy. EBITDA declined 25% to DKK 28.0m from DKK 37.3m, and the EBITDA margin fell to 10.4% from 12.7%. Fixed costs were maintained to support future growth, while EBITDA was negatively affected by DKK 2.5m of extraordinary costs following a ransomware attack in May and June, including infrastructure rebuilding, enhanced firewall security, and external consultants.

Profit before tax fell to DKK 2.9m from DKK 12.2m, and profit after tax declined to DKK 0.9m from DKK 10.6m. The effective tax burden was unusually high because tax assets, including tax loss carryforwards, were not utilized.

Cash flow from operating activities remained strong at DKK 28.5m, compared with DKK 29.4m in 2024, supported by improved net working capital, particularly lower inventories and work in progress. Investing cash outflow improved to DKK 9.4m from DKK 17.6m, despite investments in digitalization and IT infrastructure. Cash increased by DKK 0.6m to DKK 4.0m. Net interest-bearing debt rose to DKK 38.7m from DKK 36.7m, increasing gearing from 1.2 to 1.7 times EBITDA, but remaining below the target of 2.0. Equity declined to DKK 32.5m from DKK 36.6m.

Management expects further market improvement in 2026, supported by stabilized interest rates, non-residential construction activity, demand for energy-efficient buildings, service contracts, geographical expansion, and refurbishments. Revenue is forecast at DKK 290–310m, representing organic growth of 7–14%, while EBITDA is expected to rise to DKK 45–55m, with an EBITDA margin of approximately 8–10%.
