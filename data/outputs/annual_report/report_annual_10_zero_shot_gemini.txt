During the reporting period of 2025, InCoax Networks AB reported net sales of SEK 48,055,007, representing a 35 percent decrease compared to SEK 74,497,775 in the previous year. This revenue decline was primarily attributed to timing shifts in customer orders. Goods and products accounted for 85 percent of net sales, while services made up the remainder. Geographically, North America generated SEK 39,578,479, the EU accounted for SEK 3,172,469, and other markets contributed SEK 5,304,059. 

The company recorded a gross profit of SEK 29,919,750, down from SEK 42,179,123 in 2024, resulting in an improved gross margin of 62 percent compared to 57 percent previously. Operating loss (EBIT) widened to SEK -32,380,175, compared with SEK -19,378,406 in the prior year, reflecting the lower revenue alongside sustained investments in product development and commercial expansion. Loss after financial items and loss after tax both stood at SEK -32,559,663, compared to SEK -19,150,134 in 2024. Earnings per share amounted to SEK -0.25, compared to SEK -0.18 previously, or SEK -0.24 after dilution compared to SEK -0.17. 

Total operating expenses decreased to SEK 105,896 thousand from SEK 121,245 thousand in the previous year. Costs for goods for resale fell to SEK 18,135,257 from SEK 32,318,652 due to lower sales volumes, and other external costs decreased to SEK 42,755,237 from SEK 49,840,502. Conversely, personnel costs rose to SEK 40,427,773 from SEK 38,201,669, while the average number of employees remained stable at 31. Capitalized development costs amounted to SEK 25,269,745, virtually unchanged from SEK 25,649,493 in 2024. 

Cash flow from operating activities improved to SEK -20,011,617 from SEK -27,117,256. Investing activities resulted in a cash flow of SEK -25,269,745, driven by capitalized development expenditures. Financing activities generated SEK 51,797,480, bolstered by a directed share issue yielding approximately SEK 33.5 million before issue costs and a new loan facility of SEK 20 million. Total cash flow including financing activities turned positive at SEK 6,516,117, compared to SEK -12,758,133 in 2024. Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the period stood at SEK 8,748,046, up from SEK 2,231,929. Total equity was reported at SEK 109,778,755 with an equity ratio of 71.8 percent, compared to SEK 110,540,939 and 80.8 percent at the end of 2024. Total non-current liabilities amounted to SEK 23,245,786, which included convertible debt instruments of SEK 3,245,786 and other non-current liabilities of SEK 20,000,000. 

Throughout the reporting period, InCoax experienced a transitional phase marked by weaker-than-expected sales and delayed customer projects, yet concluded its most intensive development phase by launching key products including the D2501—fully integrated into Nokia's platform—and the D2508 for multi-dwelling units. To address financial performance and position the company for commercial execution, management initiated structural cost reductions projected to lower ongoing costs by more than 40 percent, representing annual savings of approximately SEK 25 million heading into 2026, with the objective of achieving positive cash flow within the coming twelve months.
