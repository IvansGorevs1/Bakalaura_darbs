Eleving Group S.A. reported strong financial and operational performance for the twelve-month period ending December 31, 2025. The company achieved record-high revenues of 250.0 million euros, representing a 15.4% increase compared to 2016.6 million euros reported in 2024. The net portfolio grew by 20.2% to 446.3 million euros, up from 371.2 million euros at the end of the previous period. Adjusted EBITDA reached a record 101.9 million euros, compared to 89.8 million euros in 2024. Profit from continuing operations was 29.2 million euros, slightly higher than the 28.8 million euros reported in the previous period, while total net profit for the period stood at 29.2 million euros compared to 29.6 million euros in 2024. Net profit before foreign exchange results and discontinued operations amounted to 40.8 million euros, compared to 32.5 million euros in 2024. Total loan issuances reached an all-time high of 458.0 million euros, a 24.3% increase year-on-year. Active customers exceeded 620,000. Revenue was well-balanced across core business lines, with traditional vehicle financing contributing 67.0 million euros, flexible vehicle financing generating 60.3 million euros, device financing contributing 7.9 million euros, and consumer lending products generating 114.8 million euros. Geographically, Latvia, Romania, and Kenya were the main contributors to growth. The Group expanded its product offerings by introducing installment loans in several European markets, launching smartphone financing in Kenya and Uganda, and entering Tanzania with motorcycle financing. Capital management highlights included the first anniversary of the company's IPO, the distribution of 19.65 million euros in dividends representing a 10% cash yield based on the IPO price, and the successful execution of two major bond issuances. These included a 40 million euro bond tap in March 2025 and the largest bond issuance in the company's history in October 2025, raising 275 million euros to refinance existing notes maturing in 2026. The capitalization ratio stood at 23.7%, the interest coverage ratio was 2.3, and net leverage was recorded at 3.8. Overall, the company maintained robust growth, operational efficiency, and solid liquidity throughout the reporting period.
