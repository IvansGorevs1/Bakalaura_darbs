JLT Mobile Computers reported a clear commercial recovery in 2025, although profitability remained negative. Group net sales increased 11.1% to SEK 131.5 million from SEK 118.4 million, while order intake rose 37.5% to SEK 141.6 million from SEK 103.0 million. Year-end order backlog increased to SEK 24.1 million from SEK 13.9 million, including SEK 15.0 million scheduled for delivery in the first quarter of 2026. A major SEK 22 million order from an American food producer contributed significantly to the stronger order intake.

Despite higher sales, gross profit declined to SEK 50.4 million from SEK 54.2 million and the gross margin fell to 38.3% from 45.8%. The report attributes the margin reduction to currency effects: the US dollar weakened by almost 20% against the Swedish krona, while 74% of sales were invoiced in dollars. Invoicing therefore occurred at weaker exchange rates than those used when quotations were prepared, while inventory was revalued using current dollar purchase rates. Customs uncertainty also reduced customer willingness to invest and increased administrative costs.

Cost reductions partially offset the margin pressure. Operating expenses decreased to SEK 52.4 million from SEK 57.3 million, with other external costs falling to SEK 15.8 million from SEK 19.3 million and personnel costs to SEK 36.6 million from SEK 38.0 million. The company’s consolidation of software and product development and discontinuation of JLT Software Solutions AB reduced overhead costs by approximately SEK 5 million in 2025 and lowered depreciation on development projects. EBITDA improved to negative SEK 1.7 million from negative SEK 2.1 million, while operating loss narrowed substantially to SEK 1.9 million from SEK 9.7 million. Net loss improved to SEK 2.5 million from SEK 7.7 million, though the group remained loss-making.

Market conditions were mixed. Demand weakened in warehousing and logistics, transportation, and manufacturing amid macroeconomic and geopolitical uncertainty, while activity was positive in ports and mining. JLT broadened its market focus toward ports, construction, and mining equipment. It launched the JLT6015, a rugged vehicle-mounted computer with a 15-inch Full HD display, and introduced an AI-based Screen Blanking safety solution.

Cash flow remained positive at SEK 3.7 million, though below SEK 6.8 million in 2024. Inventory reduction was a major contributor, with inventories falling SEK 12.7 million to SEK 25.5 million. Cash and cash equivalents increased to SEK 16.9 million, and solidity improved to 59% from 54%. The Board proposed no dividend for 2025.
