- 3. Main factors affecting performance
  - Revenue decline driven by project timing and regional demand shifts
    - Q4 net turnover decreased (EUR 5.184 million), with:
      - North & Latin America turnover EUR 2.5 million, down 40% vs same quarter prior year
      - Europe turnover EUR 1.9 million, up 24% vs same quarter prior year
      - Asia/Africa/Middle East turnover EUR 846 thousand, doubled vs the corresponding quarter prior year (16% of total Q4 turnover)
    - The report states quarterly turnover fluctuations are influenced by completion of individual projects and timing of production/dispatch and revenue recognition.
  - Profitability deterioration in Q4 despite regional improvements
    - Q4 operating result weakened (EBIT and net loss worsened), while:
      - Direct cost of goods/services decreased (Q4), but EBIT still declined (losses grew), with overall operating expenses remaining high.
  - Component supply constraints and inventory actions
    - Group operations were affected by a global shortage of electronic components.
    - The Group accumulated material reserves previously to fulfill most orders; delivery times continued to improve recently.
    - Inventory increased by 8% as of June 30, 2025 (EUR 13.939 million).
  - Liquidity supported by cash generation and customer advances
    - Net cash flow for the 12 months was EUR 2.6 million and cash balance rose to EUR 4.8 million.
    - Advances received from customers for goods totaled EUR 3.9 million.
    - The Parent has a credit line of EUR 4.95 million that was not utilized at period end.

- 4. Overall summary
  - Q4 2024/2025
    - Net sales were EUR 5.184 million, down 13% year-on-year, and the Group recorded a larger loss: net loss EUR 913 thousand (vs EUR 498 thousand loss in Q4 prior year).
    - Despite improved turnover in Europe (+24%) and strong growth in Asia/Africa/Middle East (doubled; EUR 846 thousand), North & Latin America declined significantly (down 40%), contributing to the overall Q4 revenue fall.
  - Full year 2024/2025 (12 months)
    - Net sales declined 4% to EUR 26.109 million, but the loss narrowed substantially: net loss EUR 820 thousand (vs EUR 2.369 million loss in the prior year).
    - Cash generation improved materially:
      - Net cash flow EUR 2.599 million (vs (EUR 1.251) million)
      - Cash and cash equivalents increased to EUR 4.812 million (from EUR 2.213 million)
    - Balance sheet:
      - Total assets increased to EUR 25.917 million, while owners’ equity decreased to EUR 15.581 million.
      - Inventory increased to EUR 13.939 million (up 8%), consistent with the Group’s approach to mitigate electronic component shortages.
