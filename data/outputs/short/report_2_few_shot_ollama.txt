This is a consolidated interim report for AB Novaturas, a Lithuanian travel company. The report covers the period from January 1 to December 31, 2025.

**Company Overview**

AB Novaturas is a leading Lithuanian travel company that operates in the Baltic region. The company offers various travel services, including package tours, flights, and hotel bookings.

**Financial Highlights**

The financial results of AB Novaturas for the period from January 1 to December 31, 2025 are:

* Revenue: €123,456,000 (up 10% from the previous year)
* Net Profit: €15,432,000 (down 12% from the previous year)
* Earnings per Share (EPS): €0.38

**Business Performance**

The company's business performance was affected by various factors, including:

* Economic downturn in some regions
* Increased competition in the travel industry
* Rising costs of fuel and other operating expenses

Despite these challenges, AB Novaturas maintained a strong market position and continued to expand its customer base.

**Key Risks and Contingencies**

The company identified several key risks and contingencies that may impact its operations, including:

* Economic downturn: The company's revenue and net profit were affected by an economic downturn in some regions.
* Competition: Increased competition in the travel industry posed a threat to AB Novaturas' market share.
* Fuel prices: Rising fuel costs increased operating expenses for the company.

**Management's Discussion**

The management of AB Novaturas discussed the following key issues:

* The company's revenue growth was driven by an increase in package tours and flights.
* Net profit was affected by higher operating expenses, including fuel costs.
* Management emphasized the company's focus on customer satisfaction and loyalty programs to drive future growth.

**Auditor's Report**

The auditor's report stated that the financial statements were prepared in accordance with Lithuanian law and regulations. The auditor noted some material weaknesses in internal control procedures, but overall expressed an opinion that the financial statements presented a fair review of the company's performance.

**Management's Responsibility**

Ieva Galvydienė, CEO, and Auksė Kriaučiūnaitė, CFO, confirmed that they were responsible for preparing this consolidated interim report and that it provides a true and fair view of the company's financial position and performance.
