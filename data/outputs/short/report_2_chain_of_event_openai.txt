3. Main factors affecting performance
- Reduced volumes and optimized travel programme (management commentary)
  - Group reduced volumes and optimized the travel programme, leading to lower revenue and fewer travellers, but improved margins and operational efficiency.
- Profitability supported by efficiency improvements
  - Aircraft load factor increased by **1.5 pp** to **97.2%** (from **95.7%** in 2024).
  - Strongest load factor growth in the Estonian market (**+2 pp**).
  - Load factor on long-haul destinations increased from **97.4%** to **98.5%** (and exceeded **99%** in the second half); ski destinations rose from **90.5%** to **97.2%**.
- Market conditions and disruptions limited the final result (management commentary)
  - Environment of **intense competition and excess travel supply**, which increased price pressure in autumn.
  - **Vilnius Airport disruptions** related to illegal cross-border activity involving unmanned balloons reduced last-minute travel demand.
- Revenue contraction across segments (segment information, 12M 2025 vs 12M 2024)
  - Flight package tours sales: **145,120** vs **170,697** (**-15.0%**)
  - Sightseeing tours by plane sales: **2,178** vs **2,840** (**-23.3%**)
  - Other sales: **19,149** vs **25,360** (**-24.5%**)
- Expense behavior (consolidated income statement, 12M 2025 vs 12M 2024)
  - General and administrative expenses decreased materially in a positive direction:
    - **(4,867)** vs **(10,375)** (improvement; **53%** change reported)
  - Selling expenses decreased:
    - **(15,170)** vs **(17,102)** (**11%** reported improvement)
- Financial line items
  - Finance income decreased:
    - **308** vs **1,412** (**-78%**)
  - Finance expenses decreased:
    - **(1,196)** vs **(2,643)** (**55%** improvement)

4. Overall summary
- AB Novaturas’ 2025 consolidated results show a decline in top-line performance but a major improvement in profitability:
  - Sales fell **16.1%** to **EUR 168.5 million**, with operating and net losses narrowing substantially.
  - EBITDA turned from **negative** in 2024 to **positive EUR 0.42 million** in 2025 (and adjusted EBITDA similarly at **EUR 0.42 million**).
  - Net loss improved from **(EUR 7.6 million)** in 2024 to **(EUR 1.17 million)** in 2025 (an **84.6%** improvement).
- The improvement was primarily driven by management actions and operational efficiency despite lower volumes:
  - Load factor increased to **97.2%** (+**1.5 pp**) and the company reduced volumes on loss-making routes while emphasizing higher-margin destinations.
- Performance was constrained by external conditions:
  - Intense competition and excess travel supply pressured prices, and Vilnius Airport disruptions reduced last-minute demand.
- Financial position at year-end:
  - Total assets increased to **EUR 43.38 million** (from **EUR 39.45 million**), while equity decreased to **EUR 6.86 million** (from **EUR 8.02 million**), resulting in higher leverage (debt/equity **191.42%**, up **70.3 pp**).
  - Liquidity remained supported with a total liquidity ratio of **53.77** (up **3.2 pp**).
