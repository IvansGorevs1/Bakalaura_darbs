1.	**Main financial indicators**  
- **Q4 2024/25 net sales:** **EUR 5.18m** (**-13% YoY**)  
- **FY 2024/25 net turnover:** **EUR 26.11m** (**-4% YoY**)  
- **Q4 2024/25 loss:** **EUR 0.91m** (vs **EUR 0.50m** loss in Q4 prior year)  
- **FY 2024/25 net loss:** **EUR 0.82m** (vs **EUR 2.37m** loss last year)  
- **EBITDA (Q4):** **-EUR 0.46m** (EBITDA margin **-9%**)  
- **Total assets (30.06.2025):** **EUR 25.92m** (vs **EUR 23.02m** at 30.06.2024)  
- **Cash & cash equivalents (30.06.2025):** **EUR 4.81m** (vs **EUR 2.21m**)  
- **Net cash flow (FY):** **EUR 2.60m**  
- **Liquidity:** Quick ratio **57%**, current ratio **78%**  

2.	**Most significant changes compared with the previous period**  
- **Sales declined in Q4:** revenue **-13% YoY** (and down further in the Americas region: **-40% YoY**).  
- **FY performance improved despite lower revenue:** net loss narrowed to **EUR 0.82m** from **EUR 2.37m**.  
- **Balance sheet strengthened:** **total assets rose** to **EUR 25.92m**, while **cash increased sharply** to **EUR 4.81m**.  
- **Working capital mix improved:** inventories **increased by 8%** (to **EUR 13.94m**) and customer prepayments increased to **EUR 3.92m**.  
- **Profitability remained negative:** EBIT and EBITDA were still negative in Q4 (EBIT **-EUR 0.86m**).  

3.	Main factors affecting performance**  
- **Project timing and delivery cycles:** turnover fluctuated because revenue recognition depends on project completion and shipment, often shifting into later quarters.  
- **Global electronic components shortage (ongoing impact):** the Group built/maintained material reserves to meet order lead times; delivery times have been improving recently.  
- **Regional demand divergence:**  
  - **Americas:** declined in Q4 and in FY (Americas revenue **-14% YoY** in FY).  
  - **Europe:** increased in Q4 (**+24% YoY**) and in FY (**+17% YoY**).  
  - **Asia/Africa/Middle East:** weaker overall (FY **-5% YoY**; Q4 increased but at a smaller base).  
- **Higher operating pressures in Q4:** costs remained high versus income, keeping EBIT negative.  
- **Liquidity support:** the Group maintained financing flexibility via an **unused EUR 4.95m credit line**; cash generation remained positive for FY.  

4.	**Overall summary**  
SAF Tehnika reported **lower sales** in **Q4 2024/25** (EUR **5.18m**, **-13% YoY**) and a **larger Q4 loss** (EUR **0.91m**). For the full year, despite turnover declining to **EUR 26.11m** (**-4% YoY**), the Group **significantly reduced its annual net loss** to **EUR 0.82m** (from **EUR 2.37m**), supported by improved liquidity (**FY net cash flow: EUR 2.60m**, cash **EUR 4.81m** at year-end). Performance was mainly driven by **project execution timing**, **regional revenue shifts**, and the **continuing global component supply constraints**, which the Group managed through maintained inventory and procurement planning.
