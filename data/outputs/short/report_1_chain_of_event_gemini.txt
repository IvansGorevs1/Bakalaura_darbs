**3. Main factors affecting performance**
*   Fluctuations in quarterly turnover were influenced by the completion of individual projects, affecting the timing of goods dispatch and revenue recognition.
*   Turnover in the North America and Latin America region decreased by 40% in Q4 and 14% for the 12-month period.
*   The European region's turnover increased by 24% in Q4 and 17% for the 12-month period.
*   Turnover in the Asia, Africa and Middle East region doubled in Q4 but declined by 5% for the 12-month period.
*   The Group continued to invest in new product development, product enhancements, and sales promotion.
*   Operations in the previous period were affected by a global shortage of electronic components, leading the company to accumulate material reserves, though delivery times have recently improved.
*   A net cash flow of EUR 2.6 million for the 12 months contributed to the increased cash balance.
*   The significant increase in customer prepayments for goods (to EUR 3.9 million) indicates a strong backlog of orders for future revenue recognition.
*   EUR 239 thousand were invested in fixed assets during Q4, primarily to support production, testing processes, and acquire office equipment.
*   The increase in salary-related accrued expenses is due to fluctuations in vacation accruals and bonuses.
*   The Group continuously monitors changes in the global trade environment, including potential tariff adjustments, and market demand for radio systems.

**4. Overall summary**
*   SAF Tehnika JSC reported a 4% decrease in consolidated net turnover for the 12 months of financial year 2024/2025, reaching EUR 26.11 million, and a more significant 13% decline in Q4 turnover to EUR 5.18 million.
*   Despite the reduction in overall sales, the Group substantially decreased its consolidated net loss for the 12 months by EUR 1.55 million, settling at EUR (820) thousand, which represents an improvement in annual profitability. However, the Q4 net loss worsened, increasing from EUR (498) thousand to EUR (913) thousand.
*   The company's financial position strengthened, with Total Assets growing by EUR 2.89 million to EUR 25.92 million, largely driven by a EUR 2.60 million increase in cash and bank balances and an 8% increase in inventories.
*   Total Liabilities increased by EUR 3.75 million to EUR 10.34 million, primarily due to a substantial increase in customer prepayments for goods and services, which rose by EUR 3.16 million to EUR 3.92 million, indicating a healthy future order book.
*   Regional sales performance was mixed, with North and Latin America experiencing a 40% decrease in Q4 turnover and a 14% decrease for the 12-month period, while Europe's turnover increased by 24% in Q4 and 17% for the full year, and Asia, Africa, and the Middle East doubled its Q4 turnover.
*   The Group continues to invest in new product development and sales promotion, managing material reserves to ensure competitive delivery times, and adapting to ongoing demand for high-data-rate radio systems despite market shifts.
