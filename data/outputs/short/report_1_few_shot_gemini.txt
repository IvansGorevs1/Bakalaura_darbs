Here is a summary of the financial report for SAF Tehnika JSC:

**1. Main financial indicators**
The Group reported a consolidated net turnover of EUR 26.11 million for the financial year 2024/2025 and EUR 5.18 million for Q4. The net loss for the full financial year was EUR 820 thousand, while the net loss for Q4 was EUR 913 thousand. Total assets stood at EUR 25.92 million, and total owner's equity at EUR 15.58 million, as of June 30, 2025. Net cash flow from operations for the 12 months was EUR 2.6 million.

**2. Most significant changes compared with the previous period**
Annual net turnover declined by 4%, and Q4 turnover decreased by 13% compared to the previous year's respective periods. The full-year net loss significantly improved by 65% (from EUR 2.37 million to EUR 0.82 million). However, the Q4 net loss widened by 83% (from EUR 0.50 million to EUR 0.91 million). Sales in North and Latin America saw a 14% decrease annually and a 40% decrease in Q4, while European sales increased by 17% annually and 24% in Q4. Total assets increased by 12.6% (EUR 2.9 million), while total owner's equity decreased by 5.2% (EUR 0.85 million). Inventories grew by 8%, and salaries and social costs increased by 8% year-over-year.

**3. Main factors affecting performance**
Fluctuations in quarterly turnover were primarily influenced by the completion timing of individual projects and lead times for material procurement. The company continued strategic investments in new product development, product enhancements, and sales promotion in existing and new market segments. Improved delivery times for electronic components positively impacted operations, following previous global shortages. The company is actively diversifying its product portfolio with IoT solutions and developing next-generation technologies to meet evolving market demands.

**4. Overall summary**
SAF Tehnika JSC reported a decrease in annual revenue but significantly reduced its overall net loss for the financial year 2024/2025. Regional performance varied, with strong growth in Europe partially offsetting declines in North and Latin America. Key factors included project completion variability, ongoing investments in R&D and diversification, and improving supply chain conditions. The company maintains a cautious outlook amid a persistent uncertain business environment.
