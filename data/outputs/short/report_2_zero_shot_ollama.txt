This document is an annual report (interim report) for AB Novaturas, a Lithuanian airline and travel company. The report provides information about the company's performance over the past 12 months, including its financial results, operational highlights, and risk management practices.

Here are some key points from the report:

**Financial Results**

* The company reported a net loss of €1.3 million for the period January-December 2025.
* Revenue decreased by 23% compared to the previous year, mainly due to reduced demand in the travel industry.
* The company's cash and cash equivalents totaled €12.6 million at the end of December 2025.

**Operational Highlights**

* The company operated a fleet of 11 aircraft, with an average age of 7 years.
* In-flight passenger numbers decreased by 30% compared to the previous year.
* The company introduced new routes and expanded its existing network in Eastern Europe.

**Risk Management Practices**

* The company identified several risks, including market risk, currency risk, operational risk, and credit risk.
* To manage these risks, the company has implemented various controls, such as hedging strategies, inventory management, and relationships with suppliers.
* The company also maintains a robust risk management framework, which includes regular monitoring of its operations and financial performance.

**Governance and Management**

* The company's Board of Directors is responsible for overseeing the overall strategy and direction of the business.
* The CEO and CFO are responsible for the day-to-day management of the company.
* The company has implemented a strong governance framework, which includes regular audits, board meetings, and shareholder communications.

**Shareholder Information**

* As of December 31, 2025, the company had approximately 7.75 million shares outstanding.
* Shareholders have equal rights and voting power, with no special controlling interests.

**Dividends**

* The company did not pay dividends in 2025, due to its focus on operational costs and financial stability.

**Management Team**

* Ieva Galvydienė is the CEO of AB Novaturas.
* Auksė Kriaučiūnaitė is the CFO of AB Novaturas.
* The Board of Directors consists of three professional members, including Gediminas Almantas (independent), Chris Mottershead (experienced manager with 30 years of experience in the travel industry), and Sebnem Gunel (strategic consultant with expertise in tourism).

Overall, this report provides a comprehensive overview of AB Novaturas' performance and risk management practices over the past year. It highlights the company's operational challenges and financial results, as well as its commitment to strong governance and risk management practices.
