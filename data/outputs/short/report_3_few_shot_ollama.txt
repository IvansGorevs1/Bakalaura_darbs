This is an unaudited interim financial report for the fourth quarter and year ended December 31, 2025, of AS Tallinn Ver linud (AS Tallink), a Baltic ferry company.

**Summary**

The Group's revenue increased by 10% compared to the previous year's period, driven primarily by an increase in ferry traffic. Operating profit before depreciation and amortization (EBITDA) was EEST 27.6 million (EUR 22.8 million), which is equivalent to 14.7% of revenue.

The Group's net debt at December 31, 2025, was EEST 432,435 (EUR 432,709). The equity ratio was 56.4%.

**Key Figures**

Here are the key figures from the interim report:

1. Revenue: EUR 187.986 million (up 10% compared to the previous year's period)
2. EBITDA: EUR 27.576 million (14.7% of revenue, up 8% compared to the previous year's period)
3. Operating profit before depreciation and amortization (EBITDA): EUR 22.766 million (12.1% of revenue)
4. Net profit/loss for the period: EUR 12.176 million (up from EUR -5,220 million in the same period last year)
5. Earnings per share: EUR 0.016 (down from EUR -0.007 in the same period last year)
6. Equity ratio: 56.4%
7. Net debt to EBITDA: 3.3

**Management's Discussion**

The Group has achieved a solid performance in the fourth quarter and year ended December 31, 2025. The increase in revenue was driven primarily by an increase in ferry traffic. However, the operating profit before depreciation and amortization (EBITDA) decreased due to higher costs associated with the COVID-19 pandemic.

The Group continues to invest in its fleet and infrastructure, which is expected to drive growth in the coming years. The equity ratio remains strong at 56.4%, indicating a solid balance sheet position.

However, the net debt to EBITDA ratio increased to 3.3, indicating that the Group's leverage may be increasing. Therefore, the management will continue to monitor and manage this ratio to ensure that it remains within acceptable limits.

Overall, the Group is confident in its ability to deliver a solid performance in the coming years, driven by its focus on growth and investment in its business.
