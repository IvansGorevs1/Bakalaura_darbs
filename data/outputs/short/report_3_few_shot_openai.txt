1. **Main financial indicators**  
- **Revenue (12 months 2025):** EUR **765.3m** (down from EUR 785.8m in 2024)  
- **EBITDA (12 months 2025):** EUR **130.1m** (down from EUR 175.2m in 2024)  
- **Net profit (12 months 2025):** EUR **17.3m** (down from EUR 40.3m in 2024)  
- **Q4 2025:** Revenue **EUR 188.0m**, EBITDA **EUR 27.6m**, net profit **EUR 12.2m** (Q4 2024 net loss: EUR 5.2m)  
- **Net debt (31 Dec 2025):** EUR **432.4m** (stable vs EUR 432.7m at 30 Sep 2025)  
- **Liquidity buffer (31 Dec 2025):** EUR **110.4m** (cash EUR 13.5m + unused credit lines EUR 96.9m)  
- **Dividend:** EUR **0.06/share** paid in 2025 (total EUR 44.6m); proposal for **2026 dividend of EUR 0.06/share** (6 euro cents)

2. **Most significant changes compared with the previous period**  
- **Full-year decline:** Revenue down by **~2.6%**; EBITDA down by **~25.7%**; net profit down by **~57.1%** vs 2024.  
- **Depreciation/finance improvement:** Depreciation and amortisation decreased by **EUR 18.0m** (to EUR **79.8m**), and net finance costs improved by **EUR 6.8m** (to **EUR 21.4m**).  
- **Operating weakening from volumes:** Passengers slightly down (**-0.9%** to **5.53m**), while **cargo units fell sharply (-19.2%** to **245k**).  
- **Q4 rebound:** Q4 revenue rose **2.4%**, EBITDA rose **7.3%**, and net profit turned to **EUR 12.2m** versus a loss in Q4 2024.

3. **Main factors affecting performance**  
- **Challenging demand environment**, especially **economic uncertainty in Finland**, leading to weaker consumer confidence and **reduced cargo flows** (with easing late in the year and “moderate growth” early in 2026).  
- **Operational and fleet actions:** sale of **three vessels** during 2025 (supporting lower depreciation and cost optimization) and continued focus on **cost efficiencies**; lay-up reduced to **zero by end of Q3**.  
- **Segment pressure:**  
  - **Estonia–Finland:** passengers slightly higher (+1.8%), but cargo down (-20.0%); segment result decreased.  
  - **Finland–Sweden and Estonia–Sweden:** both saw cargo declines and lower segment revenue/profit.  
  - **“Other”** remained broadly stable.  
- **Cost structure and one-offs:** dividends and related taxes impacted cash; net debt remained broadly stable.  
- **Regulatory/environmental changes:** from 2025/ongoing, full CO₂ allowance purchases required; shuttle vessels expected to transition largely to **biomethane**, supporting reduction of emission-related costs.

4. **Overall summary**  
Tallink’s 2025 results were pressured by weaker demand in core markets—most notably Finland—resulting in lower revenue, significantly reduced EBITDA, and a decline in net profit versus 2024. The deterioration was largely linked to reduced cargo flows, partially offset by cost control and improving finance/depreciation trends, including the sale of three vessels. In Q4 2025, performance improved versus the prior year period: revenue and EBITDA increased and the group returned to net profit. Liquidity remained solid with a EUR 110.4m buffer, net debt was stable, dividends were paid as planned, and management intends to propose a stable dividend for 2026.
