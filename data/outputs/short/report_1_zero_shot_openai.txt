- **2. Most significant changes compared with the previous period**
  - **Profitability deteriorated in Q4:**  
    - Q4 net loss increased to **EUR -0.91m** from **EUR -0.50m** (worse by **EUR 0.41m**).
    - **Net sales fell 13%** in Q4 (**EUR 5.18m** vs **EUR 5.98m**).
  - **Full-year performance improved vs prior year:**  
    - **Net loss reduced** to **EUR -0.82m** from **EUR -2.37m**.
    - **EBIT improved** to **EUR -0.75m** from **EUR -2.26m**.
    - **Net sales declined 4%** for the year (**EUR 26.11m** vs **EUR 27.09m**).
  - **Balance sheet strengthened on cash:**  
    - **Cash increased** to **EUR 4.81m** from **EUR 2.21m**.
    - **Total assets increased** to **EUR 25.92m** from **EUR 23.02m**.
  - **Equity decreased:**  
    - Owners’ equity declined to **EUR 15.58m** from **EUR 16.44m** (net loss impact).
  - **Liquidity:**  
    - **Quick ratio increased** to **57%** from **49%**, but **current ratio decreased** to **78%** from **94%**.

- **3. Main factors affecting performance**
  - **Project/timing effects on quarterly revenue recognition**
    - Management notes quarterly turnover fluctuations due to **project completion**, **production duration**, and **material procurement lead times**; **dispatch and revenue recognition** may occur in subsequent quarters.
  - **Global shortage of electronic components**
    - The Group was affected by the shortage; it previously accumulated material reserves to fulfill orders and **delivery times have continued to improve** recently.
  - **Regional revenue mix changes**
    - Q4: **Americas** (North & Latin) **-40%** vs prior-year Q4; **Europe** **+24%**; **Asia/Africa/Middle East** **doubled** (now **16%** of Q4 sales).
    - Full year: **Americas** revenue **-14%**; **Europe** revenue **+17%**; **Asia/Africa/Middle East** **-5%**.
  - **Bad receivables / receivables risk**
    - Annual “bad receivables” expense/impact changed to **EUR (5,887)** from **EUR 1,874**; note states additional provisions were made for debts assessed as insecure despite agreed payment terms not yet expiring.
  - **Labor costs**
    - Full year wages and related social expenses increased by **8%** (to total **EUR 12.41m** from **EUR 11.48m**), driven by salaries/bonuses/social expenses.
  - **Liquidity measures**
    - Parent has an unused **Credit Line Agreement** with Luminor Bank for **EUR 4.95m** at period end.

- **4. Overall summary**
  - **SAF Tehnika closed FY 2024/25 with a reduced loss:** **net loss EUR -0.82m** (vs **-2.37m** in FY 2023/24) despite **net sales declining 4%** to **EUR 26.11m**.
  - **Q4 specifically worsened:** **Q4 net loss EUR -0.91m** (vs **-0.50m**) on **Q4 net sales EUR 5.18m** (**-13%**).
  - **Revenue volatility is attributed to project execution and supply lead times**, alongside historical effects of **global electronic component shortages** (with **improving delivery times**).
  - **Financial position improved in cash:** **cash rose to EUR 4.81m** and the Group generated **positive net cash flow EUR 2.60m** over the year; however, **owners’ equity fell** to **EUR 15.58m** due to the ongoing net loss.
