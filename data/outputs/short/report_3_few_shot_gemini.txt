**1. Main financial indicators**
For the financial year 2025, the Group reported revenue of EUR 765.3 million, a net profit of EUR 17.3 million, and EBITDA of EUR 130.1 million. The company carried 5.5 million passengers and transported 245,004 cargo units. Net debt stood at EUR 432.4 million, and a dividend of EUR 0.06 per share was proposed for 2026, consistent with the 2025 payment.

**2. Most significant changes compared with the previous period**
Revenue decreased by 2.6% year-over-year. EBITDA declined significantly by 25.8%, and net profit fell by 57.1%. Passenger numbers slightly decreased by 0.9%, while cargo units transported saw a substantial decrease of 19.2%. Net debt was successfully reduced by 19.6%. Depreciation and amortization expense decreased by 18.4%, and net finance costs declined by 24.2%.

**3. Main factors affecting performance**
The results were primarily affected by low consumer and business confidence, ongoing economic challenges, and heightened geopolitical tensions, leading to reduced demand and cargo flows. Strategic operational decisions, including the sale of three vessels and the chartering out of others, contributed to cost optimization and debt reduction. Positive impacts included a decrease in depreciation expense due to vessel useful life alignment and lower net finance costs. Conversely, increased costs from CO2 emission allowances and adverse changes in the tax environment negatively impacted profitability. Significant capital investments were directed towards vessel maintenance and IT system improvements.

**4. Overall summary**
The year 2025 was challenging, marked by a decline in revenue and profit, mainly attributable to a tough economic climate and decreased cargo volumes. Despite these difficulties, the company undertook significant operational adjustments, optimized its asset base, reduced debt, and maintained a stable dividend policy.
