1. Main financial indicators
**Revenue**: EUR 765.3 million for the 2025 financial year (EUR 785.8 million in 2024).
**Net Profit**: EUR 17.3 million for the period (EUR 40.3 million in 2024).
**EBITDA**: EUR 130.1 million (EUR 175.2 million in 2024).
***Total Assets**: EUR 1,330.8 million as of 31 December 2025 (EUR 1,463.9 million as of 31 December 2024).
**Total Liabilities**: EUR 580.7 million as of 31 December 2025 (EUR 681.6 million as of 31 December 2024).
**Net Debt**: EUR 432.4 million as of 31 December 2025 (EUR 537.7 million as of 31 December 2024).

2. Most significant changes compared with the previous period
**Revenue Decline**: Total revenue decreased by 2.6%, falling from EUR 785.8 million to EUR 765.3 million.
**Profitability Drop**: Net profit declined by 57.1%, dropping from EUR 40.3 million to EUR 17.3 million.
**EBITDA Reduction**: EBITDA fell by 25.7%, from EUR 175.2 million to EUR 130.1 million.
**Debt Improvement**: Net debt was reduced by EUR 105.3 million, a significant decrease from the previous year's EUR 537.7 million.
**Lower Operating Costs**: Depreciation and amortisation expenses decreased by EUR 18.0 million (18.4%) due to the alignment of vessel useful lives to 45 years and the sale of three ships.

3. Main factors affecting performance
**Market Conditions**: Low consumer and business confidence, particularly in Finland, along with economic uncertainty and geopolitical tensions, constrained private consumption and cargo flows.
**Vessel Sales**: The Group optimized its cost base by selling three vessels: the passenger vessel Star I and cargo vessels Regal Star and Sailor.
**Chartering Strategy**: Profitability was supported by chartering out three vessels (Romantika, Galaxy I, and Silja Europa) to external parties.
**Regulatory Costs**: Performance was impacted by the new requirement to purchase CO₂ emission allowances in full starting in 2025.
**Maintenance and Repair**: Planned maintenance works totaled 73 days in 2025, a significant increase compared to 20 days in 2024, affecting vessel availability and increasing investment costs.

4. Overall summary
Tallink Grupp faced a challenging financial year in 2025 characterized by a decline in revenue and net profit due to unfavorable economic conditions in core markets and high geopolitical uncertainty.
Despite these pressures, the Group successfully reduced its debt burden and optimized its fleet through the sale and chartering of vessels, which helped manage operating costs.
Strategic adjustments, such as aligning vessel depreciation periods and focusing on fuel efficiency via biomethane, were implemented to improve long-term sustainability and cost efficiency.
The Group maintained its commitment to shareholders by paying EUR 44.6 million in dividends during 2025 and proposing a similar dividend for 2026, signaling confidence in its operational stability.
