Here is the summary of the financial report:

**1. Main financial indicators**
*   Revenue: EUR 168,483 thousand
*   Net loss: EUR 1,169 thousand
*   Adjusted EBITDA: EUR 420 thousand
*   Operating loss: EUR 274 thousand
*   Gross profit: EUR 19,650 thousand
*   Total assets (as of 31 December 2025): EUR 43,380 thousand
*   Total equity (as of 31 December 2025): EUR 6,863 thousand
*   Number of travellers served: 191 thousand
*   Aircraft load factor: 97.2%
*   Gross profit margin: 11.66%
*   Debt/equity ratio: 191.42%

**2. Most significant changes compared with the previous period**
*   Revenue decreased by 16.1% from EUR 200,878 thousand in 2024 to EUR 168,483 thousand in 2025.
*   Number of travellers decreased by 20.3% from 239.5 thousand in 2024 to 191 thousand in 2025.
*   Net loss significantly narrowed by 84.6% from EUR 7,604 thousand in 2024 to EUR 1,169 thousand in 2025.
*   Adjusted EBITDA improved from a loss of EUR 449 thousand in 2024 to a profit of EUR 420 thousand in 2025, an improvement of 193.6%.
*   Operating loss narrowed by 95.8% from EUR 6,556 thousand in 2024 to EUR 274 thousand in 2025.
*   Gross profit decreased by 5.3% from EUR 20,742 thousand in 2024 to EUR 19,650 thousand in 2025.
*   Gross profit margin increased by 1.3 percentage points to 11.66% in 2025 from 10.33% in 2024.
*   Operating margin increased by 3.1 percentage points to (0.16)% in 2025 from (3.26)% in 2024.
*   EBITDA margin increased by 3.3 percentage points to 0.25% in 2025 from (3.05)% in 2024.
*   General and administrative expenses decreased by 53% from EUR 10,375 thousand in 2024 to EUR 4,867 thousand in 2025.
*   Selling expenses decreased by 11% from EUR 17,102 thousand in 2024 to EUR 15,170 thousand in 2025.
*   Aircraft load factor increased by 1.5 percentage points to 97.2% in 2025 from 95.7% in 2024.
*   Return on assets (ROA) improved by 16.4 percentage points to (2.89)% in 2025 from (19.27)% in 2024.
*   Return on equity (ROE) improved by 77.8 percentage points to (17.03)% in 2025 from (94.81)% in 2024.
*   Debt/equity ratio increased by 70.3 percentage points to 191.42% in 2025 from 121.16% in 2024.
*   Total equity decreased by EUR 1,157 thousand from EUR 8,020 thousand in 2024 to EUR 6,863 thousand in 2025.

**3. Main factors affecting performance**
*   **Profitability improvement:** Achieved by a focused review of the travel programme, stricter supply discipline, and enhanced operational efficiency.
*   **Reduced volumes and revenue:** Resulted from an intentional strategy to reduce volumes on loss-making routes and optimize the travel programme to prioritize profitability.
*   **Emphasis on higher-margin destinations:** Contributed to improved margins despite the overall decline in gross profit.
*   **Increased efficiency:** Demonstrated by a rise in the aircraft load factor to 97.2%, including stronger growth in the Estonian market and long-haul/ski destinations.
*   **Effective cost management:** Evidenced by significant reductions in general and administrative expenses and selling expenses.
*   **Market challenges in H2 2025:** Increased market-wide supply put pressure on prices, and disruptions at Vilnius Airport reduced last-minute travel demand.
*   **Growing destinations:** Strong demand for destinations like Calabria (Italy), Barcelona, Madeira, Egypt, ski trips to France, Zanzibar, Vietnam, Sri Lanka, and Gambia.
*   **Stronger early sales for 2026:** Bookings for the 2026 summer season were 59% higher than the previous year by December 2025.
*   **Increased borrowings:** Total borrowings rose from EUR 9,250 thousand in 2024 to EUR 12,867 thousand in 2025, contributing to the higher debt/equity ratio.

**4. Overall summary**
*   AB Novaturas successfully shifted its focus to profitability in 2025, achieving a positive Adjusted EBITDA of EUR 420 thousand and significantly reducing its net loss to EUR 1,169 thousand, despite operating in a highly competitive market.
*   This improvement in financial performance came at the expense of volume, with revenue declining by 16.1% to EUR 168,483 thousand and the number of travellers decreasing by 20.3% to 191 thousand, due to strategic programme optimization.
*   Operational efficiency gains were evident in a 1.5 percentage point increase in the aircraft load factor to 97.2% and a 1.3 percentage point rise in the gross profit margin.
*   The company also effectively managed costs, leading to a 53% reduction in general and administrative expenses and an 11% decrease in selling expenses.
*   Challenges during the second half of the year, such as increased market supply and disruptions at Vilnius Airport, impacted results.
*   Looking ahead, strong early bookings for the 2026 summer season (up 59%) and the introduction of new destinations indicate a continued strategy focused on operational and cost efficiency, and an expanded hotel portfolio to drive consistent profitability growth.
*   The company's debt/equity ratio increased by 70.3 percentage points to 191.42% due to increased borrowings.
