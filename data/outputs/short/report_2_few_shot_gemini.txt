The company reported EUR 168 million in revenue, serving 191 thousand travelers. It achieved a positive Adjusted EBITDA of EUR 420 thousand, though it still posted a net loss of EUR 1.17 million. Revenue declined by 16.1% and traveler numbers decreased by 20% year-over-year. Despite this, Adjusted EBITDA significantly improved from a negative EUR 449 thousand to a positive EUR 420 thousand. Profitability margins improved, with the gross profit margin increasing by 1.3 percentage points, and the operating margin improving by 3.1 percentage points. Cash and cash equivalents increased by 329%, while the debt-to-equity ratio rose by 70.3 percentage points.

Performance was primarily driven by a strategic decision to intentionally reduce travel volumes and optimize the travel program to prioritize profitability, which led to lower sales but improved margins. Positive factors included enhanced operational efficiency, with the aircraft load factor rising by 1.5 percentage points to 97.2%, and strong early bookings for the 2026 summer season, up 59%. The company also introduced new destinations. Notably, the group breached some financial covenants.

**4. Overall summary**
The company successfully executed a strategy focused on profitability and operational efficiency in a challenging market. Despite a decline in revenue and traveler numbers, it achieved a significant turnaround in Adjusted EBITDA and substantially reduced its net loss, driven by improved margins and efficiency. Strategic volume reduction and new destination introductions position the company for future growth, although increased leverage and covenant breaches require attention.
