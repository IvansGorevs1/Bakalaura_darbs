1. Main financial indicators
- Revenue (12M 2025): EUR 168,483 thousand (down 16.1% vs 12M 2024: EUR 200,878 thousand)
- Gross profit (12M 2025): EUR 19,650 thousand (down 5.3% vs 12M 2024: EUR 20,742 thousand)
- Operating profit (12M 2025): loss of EUR 274 thousand (vs loss of EUR 6,556 thousand in 12M 2024)
- EBITDA (12M 2025): EUR 420 thousand (vs EBITDA loss of EUR 6,132 thousand in 12M 2024); change +106.8%
- Adjusted EBITDA (12M 2025): EUR 420 thousand (vs EUR -449 thousand in 12M 2024); change +193.6%
- Net profit (12M 2025): loss of EUR 1,169 thousand (vs loss of EUR 7,604 thousand in 12M 2024); change +84.6%
- Profit before tax (12M 2025): loss of EUR 1,162 thousand (vs loss of EUR 7,786 thousand in 12M 2024); change +85.1%
- Liquidity ratio (12M 2025): 53.77% (vs 50.61% in 12M 2024)
- Equity ratio (12M 2025): 16.96% (vs 20.33% in 12M 2024)
- Debt/equity ratio (12M 2025): 191.42% (vs 121.16% in 12M 2024)
- EPS (12M 2025): EUR (0.15) (vs EUR (0.97) in 12M 2024)

2. Most significant changes compared with the previous period
- Revenue decreased by 16.1% (EUR 168.5m vs EUR 200.9m in 2024)
- EBITDA improved sharply from a loss to positive:
  - EBITDA: EUR (6,132) thousand in 12M 2024 → EUR 420 thousand in 12M 2025
  - Adjusted EBITDA: EUR (449) thousand in 12M 2024 → EUR 420 thousand in 12M 2025
- Loss narrowed materially:
  - Net loss: EUR (7,604) thousand in 12M 2024 → EUR (1,169) thousand in 12M 2025 (+84.6% improvement)
- Segment result turnaround vs prior year is visible in margins:
  - Flight package tours: segment profit increased to EUR 12,162 thousand (from EUR 10,311 thousand)
  - Other sales remained loss-making but improved: segment profit EUR (2,515) thousand (vs EUR (2,237) thousand)
- Total assets increased to EUR 43,380 thousand (from EUR 39,450 thousand in 2024)
- Cash and cash equivalents increased to EUR 1,691 thousand (from EUR 394 thousand at 31/12/2024)

3. Main factors affecting performance
- Profitability improvement despite lower volumes:
  - Focused review of travel programme, stricter supply discipline, and enhanced operational efficiency
  - Reduced volumes on loss-making routes; more emphasis on higher-margin destinations
- Higher load factor:
  - Overall aircraft load factor increased by 1.5 pp to 97.2% (from 95.7%)
  - Long-haul load factor increased from 97.4% to 98.5%; exceeded 99% in the second half of the year
  - Ski destinations load factor increased from 90.5% to 97.2%
- Market conditions and disruptions that limited results:
  - Intense competition and excess travel supply; autumn supply increase pressured prices
  - Vilnius Airport disruptions related to illegal cross-border activity involving unmanned balloons reduced last-minute travel demand
- Early sales strengthening (positive trend):
  - Bookings for 2026 summer season by December 2025 were 59% higher than the previous year
- Impairment reference (mentioned as a comparison factor):
  - 2024 included a one-off non-cash goodwill impairment of EUR 5.7 million; 2025 excluded this item

4. Overall summary
- In 2025, AB Novaturas delivered lower revenue (EUR 168.5m, -16.1%) and fewer travellers, but achieved a strong profitability turnaround with positive EBITDA of EUR 420 thousand (vs EBITDA loss of EUR 6.1m in 2024).
- The net loss narrowed to EUR 1.17m (from EUR 7.60m in 2024), with the report citing improvements from programme optimization, stricter supply discipline, and higher aircraft load factors.
- Performance was still constrained by increased market-wide supply and Vilnius Airport disruptions affecting last-minute demand, which reduced the final outcome relative to expectations.
- Liquidity improved (total liquidity ratio 53.77% vs 50.61%), while the equity ratio decreased to 16.96% (from 20.33%).
