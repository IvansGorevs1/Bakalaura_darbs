1.	Main financial indicators  
- Sales: **EUR 168.5m** (**-16.1%** vs 12M 2024)  
- Gross profit: **EUR 19.7m** (**-5.3%**); gross margin **11.66%** (**+1.3 pp**)  
- Operating result: **Operating profit of -EUR 0.3m** (vs **-EUR 6.6m** in 2024)  
- EBITDA: **EUR 0.42m** (vs **-EUR 6.13m** in 2024); EBITDA margin **0.25%** (**+3.3 pp**)  
- Adjusted EBITDA: **EUR 0.42m** (vs **-EUR 0.45m** in 2024)  
- Net loss: **-EUR 1.17m** (**-84.6%** improvement) ; net margin **-0.69%** (**+3.1 pp**)  
- Balance sheet (year-end): **Total assets EUR 43.4m**; **Total equity EUR 6.9m** (equity ratio **16.96%**)  
- Liquidity (total liquidity ratio): **53.77** (**+3.2 pp**)  
- Cash flow: **Net cash from operating activities -EUR 0.66m**; cash and cash equivalents **EUR 1.69m** (up from **EUR 0.39m**)

2.	Most significant changes compared with the previous period  
- The Group **reduced revenue and volumes** sharply, but **improved profitability**: EBITDA turned **positive** in 2025 (from **negative** in 2024).  
- **Net loss narrowed materially** to **-EUR 1.17m** (from **-EUR 7.60m**), reflecting better operating performance and excluding a prior-year **one-off non-cash goodwill impairment** (EUR **5.7m** in 2024).  
- Costs were better controlled: gross profit fell less than sales, and expense pressure eased versus 2024 (notably operating expenses in comparison to the prior year loss period).  
- Financial position: **assets increased** to **EUR 43.4m** from **EUR 39.5m**, while **equity decreased** to **EUR 6.9m** from **EUR 8.0m**, despite operating improvement.

3.	Main factors affecting performance  
- Management intentionally **reduced travel volumes** and **optimized the travel programme** (especially **reduced loss-making routes** and increased focus on **higher-margin destinations**), which lowered revenue but improved margins and operational efficiency.  
- **Higher load factor**: aircraft load factor rose **+1.5 pp** to **97.2%** (from **95.7%**), supporting profitability even with fewer passengers.  
- Market headwinds limited the final result in the second half:  
  - **Increased market-wide travel supply** during autumn pressured **prices**.  
  - **Vilnius Airport disruptions** linked to illegal cross-border activity (unmanned balloons) reduced **last-minute demand**.  
- Operational mix impact: while **flight package tours** remained the largest segment, profitability improved overall; “other sales” remained negative on segment gross profit, although less negative than before.

4.	Overall summary  
In 2025, AB Novaturas reported **lower sales and fewer travellers** (revenue **-16.1%**; travellers **-20%**), mainly due to deliberate volume reduction and weaker market conditions in late 2025. However, the Group **materially improved operating performance**, achieving **positive EBITDA (EUR 0.42m)** and reducing the **net loss to -EUR 1.17m** versus -EUR 7.60m in 2024—supported by programme optimization, stricter supply discipline, and higher aircraft load factors. Despite these improvements, the Group continued to face challenging market pricing pressure and disruptions affecting demand.
