3. Main factors affecting performance
- Demand and market conditions (FY 2025 and Q4 impacts)
  - Economic uncertainty in Finland constrained private consumer confidence and adversely affected cargo flows.
  - Low consumer and business confidence and heightened global geopolitical tensions reduced demand.
  - Cargo volumes declined substantially: cargo units down 19.2% to 245,004 in FY 2025.
- Operational adjustments
  - Sale of three vessels during FY 2025: passenger vessel Star I (Apr), cargo vessel Regal Star (Aug), cargo vessel Sailor (Oct), contributing to cost base optimization.
  - Several vessels chartered out; lay-up vessels reduced to zero by end of Q3 2025 (from four at end of Q1 2025).
- Cost and accounting effects
  - Depreciation and amortisation decreased by EUR 18.0 million, mainly due to alignment of estimated useful lives of cruise and passenger vessels (except shuttle vessels) to 45 years.
  - Further depreciation decline impacted by the sale of three vessels.
- Finance and cash flow items
  - Debt burden reduction and declining interest rates contributed to a EUR 6.8 million improvement in net finance costs (to EUR 21.4 million).
  - Dividend payments affected cash: dividends of EUR 44.6 million and related income tax of EUR 11.3 million; loan and interest payments of EUR 113.5 million in FY 2025.
- Q4 2025 specific drivers
  - Low confidence and geopolitical tensions continued to affect results.
  - Sale of cargo vessel Sailor.
  - Dividends and related income tax in Q4: EUR 22.3 million dividends and EUR 6.3 million income tax.
  - Repayment of long-term loans and interest expense in Q4: EUR 18.7 million (vs EUR 34.0 million in Q4 2024).
  - FuelEU Maritime compliance units sales generated excess non-core revenue in Q4.

4. Overall summary
- FY 2025 was difficult due to adverse macroeconomic conditions, especially economic uncertainty in Finland, which constrained consumer/business confidence and drove a sharp decline in cargo volumes.
- Despite lower revenue, profitability benefits came from cost base optimization (vessel sales and chartering), continued debt reduction, and lower depreciation expense from useful-life alignment; however, these were not enough to offset the demand-driven decline.
- Reported FY 2025 financial results:
  - Revenue EUR 765.3 million (down from EUR 785.8 million)
  - EBITDA EUR 130.1 million (down from EUR 175.2 million)
  - Net profit EUR 17.3 million (down from EUR 40.3 million)
- Q4 2025 improved year-on-year, with revenue, EBITDA, and net profit all higher than Q4 2024 (net profit of EUR 12.2 million vs net loss of EUR 5.2 million), supported by factors including reduced depreciation expense and non-core FuelEU Maritime compliance unit sales, alongside continuing market pressure and vessel-related actions.
- Financial position strengthened on leverage:
  - Net debt reduced to EUR 432.4 million (from EUR 537.7 million at 31 Dec 2024), while total assets and liabilities declined; liquidity decreased due to dividends, related tax, and loan/interest repayments.
