3.  **Main factors affecting performance**
    *   Operating in an environment of intense competition and excess travel supply.
    *   Intentional reduction of volumes and optimization of the travel programme to increase profitability.
    *   Improved margins and operational efficiency due to a focused review of the programme, stricter supply discipline, and reduced volumes on loss-making routes.
    *   Increased emphasis on higher-margin destinations and leveraging Novaturas’ broad portfolio.
    *   Higher expectations for the second half of the year were limited by an increase in market-wide supply during the autumn season putting pressure on prices.
    *   Disruptions at Vilnius Airport related to illegal cross-border activity involving unmanned balloons reduced last-minute travel demand.
    *   Aircraft load factor improvement, particularly in the Estonian market and for long-haul and ski destinations.
    *   Significant increase in early bookings for the 2026 summer season, with strongest growth for Greece, Bulgaria, Tunisia, and Turkey.

4.  **Overall summary**
    *   AB Novaturas served 191 thousand travelers in 2025, a 20% decrease from 2024, and revenue declined by 16.1% to EUR 168.5 million.
    *   Despite lower volumes and revenue, the Group achieved a positive Adjusted EBITDA of EUR 420 thousand in 2025, a significant improvement from a negative EUR 449 thousand in 2024.
    *   The annual net loss decreased substantially by 84.6% to EUR 1.17 million, compared to EUR 7.6 million in 2024 (excluding the one-off goodwill impairment in 2024).
    *   Improved profitability was driven by strategic decisions to reduce volumes on loss-making routes, optimize the travel programme, and focus on higher-margin destinations.
    *   Operational efficiency improved, marked by a 1.5 percentage point increase in aircraft load factor to 97.2%.
    *   Market challenges included intense competition, excess travel supply, and disruptions at Vilnius Airport.
    *   Positive trends for the future include a 59% increase in early bookings for the 2026 summer season and the introduction of new destinations.
