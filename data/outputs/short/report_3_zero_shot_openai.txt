- **2. Most significant changes compared with the previous period**
  - **FY 2025 vs FY 2024**
    - **Revenue:** down **EUR 20.5m** to **EUR 765.3m**
    - **EBITDA:** down **EUR 45.1m** to **EUR 130.1m**
    - **Net profit:** down **EUR 23.0m** to **EUR 17.3m**
    - **Depreciation & amortisation:** down **EUR 18.0m** to **EUR 79.8m**
    - **Net finance costs:** down **EUR 6.8m** to **EUR 21.4m**
    - **Net debt:** improved to **EUR 432.4m** (from **EUR 537.7m** at 31 Dec 2024)
    - **Cash & cash equivalents:** down to **EUR 13.5m** (from **EUR 31.9m** at 30 Sep 2025) mainly reflecting dividends and debt repayments
  - **Q4 2025 vs Q4 2024**
    - **Revenue:** up **2.4%** to **EUR 188.0m**
    - **EBITDA:** up to **EUR 27.6m** (from **EUR 25.7m**)
    - **Net result:** improved to **net profit EUR 12.2m** (from **net loss EUR 5.2m**)

- **3. Main factors affecting performance**
  - **Demand and macro conditions**
    - **Economic uncertainty in Finland** reduced private consumer confidence and **adversely affected cargo flows**; cargo volume decline eased toward year-end.
    - **Low consumer and business confidence** and **heightened geopolitical tensions** also affected results.
  - **Traffic volumes**
    - **Passengers (FY 2025):** **5,531,132** (**-0.9%** YoY)
    - **Cargo units (FY 2025):** **245,004** (**-19.2%** YoY)
    - **Passenger vehicles (FY 2025):** **760,473** (**-2.2%** YoY)
  - **Cost and accounting impacts**
    - **Sale of three vessels** in 2025 (Star I in April; Regal Star in August; Sailor in October).
    - **Lower depreciation expense** due to alignment of estimated useful lives of **cruise and passenger vessels (except shuttle vessels)** to **45 years**, resulting in reduced depreciation rates.
  - **Financial costs and debt**
    - **Net finance costs decreased by EUR 6.8m** due to **declining interest rates** and a **decline in outstanding loan balance**.
    - **Loan repayment and interest payments in FY 2025:** **EUR 113.5m**.
  - **Operational actions**
    - **Chartered out vessels:** Group operated **11 vessels** (including **2 shuttle vessels**) with **3 vessels chartered out**.
    - **Lay-up reduced** from **four vessels at end of Q1 2025** to **zero by end of Q3 2025** (via new employment or vessel sales).
  - **Investments**
    - **Capex increased** to **EUR 33.0m** (from **EUR 22.4m**), including maintenance/refurbishment (Baltic Princess, Silja Serenade) and IT improvements.
    - Planned maintenance and repair works: **73 days** (FY24: **20 days**).
  - **Q4 2025 specific items cited**
    - Dividend payment in Q4: **EUR 22.3m** plus related income tax **EUR 6.3m**
    - Loan repayment and interest expense in Q4: **EUR 18.7m**
    - **FuelEU Maritime compliance units sold** generated excess revenue outside core operations
    - Sale of surplus/other items and **depreciation alignment reduced depreciation expense**  
    - Charter extension: **Silja Europa** charter extended until **31 Jan 2027** (with option to extend another year)

- **4. Overall summary**
  - **FY 2025 results were lower year-on-year:** revenue **EUR 765.3m**; EBITDA **EUR 130.1m**; net profit **EUR 17.3m** (all down vs FY 2024), driven primarily by weaker demand (notably Finland cargo) and lower profitability, partially offset by **lower depreciation**, **lower net finance costs**, **vessel sales**, and **cost efficiency actions**.
  - **Q4 performance improved strongly:** revenue rose to **EUR 188.0m**, EBITDA to **EUR 27.6m**, and shifted to **net profit EUR 12.2m** vs Q4 2024’s net loss.
  - **Balance sheet and liquidity:** net debt was stable in late 2025 at **EUR 432.4m** with **net debt/EBITDA of 3.3**; cash decreased to **EUR 13.5m** at year-end due to **dividends (EUR 44.6m)** and **debt repayments/interest (EUR 113.5m)**, while the group maintained a **liquidity buffer of EUR 110.4m**.
  - **Strategic/operational steps:** completed vessel sales, reduced lay-up, chartered out vessels, and increased maintenance/capex to support fleet performance and route sustainability; additionally, **Silja Europa** charter was extended in December 2025.
