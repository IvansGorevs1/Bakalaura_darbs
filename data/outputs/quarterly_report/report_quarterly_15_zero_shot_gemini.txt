During the twelve-month period ended 31 December 2025, Nordecon AS recorded consolidated revenue of 208,281 thousand euros, representing a decrease of approximately 7% compared to 223,925 thousand euros in 2024. The revenue decline was primarily driven by the Buildings segment, which fell by approximately 10% to 168,302 thousand euros and accounted for 81% of total revenue. Conversely, revenue in the Infrastructure segment grew by 10% to 39,902 thousand euros. Geographically, Estonia remained the dominant market, generating 98% of total revenue, while Ukraine accounted for 2%. 

Profitability indicators showed a decrease compared to the previous period. Gross profit for 2025 was 13,535 thousand euros, resulting in a gross margin of 6.5%, down from 7.5% in 2024. Operating profit stood at 5,651 thousand euros, yielding an operating margin of 2.7% compared to 3.6% in the prior year. EBITDA reached 8,250 thousand euros with an EBITDA margin of 4.0%, down from 11,025 thousand euros and 4.9% in 2024. Administrative expenses decreased by about 14% to 6,814 thousand euros, largely due to lower staff costs and reduced performance-based pay liabilities. Net finance costs rose to 2,965 thousand euros, heavily impacted by a foreign exchange loss of 750 thousand euros resulting from the 12% weakening of the Ukrainian hryvnia against the euro during the translation of loans provided to Ukrainian subsidiaries. Consequently, the group ended the reporting period with a net profit of 2,545 thousand euros, compared to 5,165 thousand euros in 2024. Net profit attributable to owners of the parent was 1,439 thousand euros, down from 3,827 thousand euros in the previous year. 

Cash flow from operating activities produced a net inflow of 1,204 thousand euros in 2025, compared to 1,075 thousand euros in 2024. Investing activities resulted in a net cash outflow of 2,767 thousand euros, which included 2,898 thousand euros placed in long-term deposits to secure guarantee limits. Financing activities resulted in a net cash outflow of 1,194 thousand euros, driven mainly by lease and loan repayments. Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the period stood at 5,266 thousand euros, compared to 8,195 thousand euros at 31 December 2024. 

The Estonian construction market stabilized during 2025, experiencing modest overall economic growth of 1% and an increase in the construction price index of 1.5% driven largely by rising material prices. Despite the lower annual revenue, Nordecon substantially strengthened its future outlook by increasing its order book by approximately 30% year on year to 273,060 thousand euros at 31 December 2025, supported by 234,994 thousand euros in new contracts secured during the year. This growth was propelled by a nearly threefold increase in the Infrastructure segment order book, driven by major contracts such as the Rail Baltica railway infrastructure and road construction projects for the Estonian Transport Administration. Approximately 69% of the total order book is scheduled for execution in 2026. Backed by this robust order backlog and an improved market outlook, management expects business volumes to increase in the coming year.
