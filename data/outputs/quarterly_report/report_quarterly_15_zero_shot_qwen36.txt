Nordecon AS reported a consolidated revenue of €208.3 million for the twelve months ended December 31, 2025, representing a 7% decrease from €223.9 million in the prior year. This decline was primarily driven by a roughly 10% drop in revenue from the Buildings segment, which accounted for 81% of total revenue. Conversely, the Infrastructure segment saw a 10% growth in revenue, reaching €39.9 million. The construction price index in Estonia increased by 1.5% year-on-year, largely due to rising material prices, while the broader Estonian economy experienced modest growth of 1%.

Profitability metrics weakened compared to the previous period. The group’s gross margin fell by one percentage point to 6.5% from 7.5% in 2024, with the Buildings segment margin decreasing to 7.7% and the Infrastructure segment margin improving slightly to 4.8%. Administrative expenses declined by approximately 14% to €6.8 million, reflecting lower staff costs due to reduced performance-based pay liabilities. Consequently, operating profit decreased to €5.7 million from €8.1 million in 2024, resulting in an operating margin of 2.7% against a target of above 3%. EBITDA stood at €8.3 million, with an EBITDA margin of 4.0%.

Net profit for the period was €2.5 million, down significantly from €5.2 million in 2024. This reduction was heavily impacted by a €750 thousand foreign exchange loss arising from the weakening of the Ukrainian hryvnia against the euro, which affected the translation of loans provided to Ukrainian subsidiaries. Finance costs totaled €3.5 million, including this exchange loss and interest expenses of €982 thousand. Net profit attributable to owners of the parent was €1.4 million, or €0.05 per share.

Despite lower current-year revenues, the company’s order book grew substantially by 30% to €273.1 million at year-end, up from €209.5 million in 2024. This growth was driven by a nearly threefold increase in the Infrastructure segment’s order book, fueled by major contracts such as Rail Baltica and national road projects. Approximately 69% of the order book is scheduled for execution in 2026. The group secured new contracts worth €235 million during the year.

Liquidity remained a focus area, with the current ratio slightly declining to 0.93 from 0.94. Cash and cash equivalents decreased to €5.3 million from €8.2 million, influenced by cash placed in long-term deposits to secure guarantee limits and lease payments. No dividends were paid in 2025, whereas €1.3 million was distributed in the previous year. The group maintains a balanced approach between building and infrastructure construction, aiming to mitigate market risks through efficient resource allocation and digital process improvements. Management anticipates business volume growth in 2026, supported by the strengthened order book and optimistic economic forecasts for the Estonian construction market.
