The company reported a significant increase in full-year revenue, driven by the inclusion of Tallink Grupp results for the full twelve-month period compared to the previous year when results were consolidated starting from August. Total revenue for the twelve months of 2025 reached approximately 1.84 billion euros, compared with 1.37 billion euros in 2024. Gross profit improved to 240.85 million euros from 128.63 million euros, and EBITDA rose to 232.58 million euros from 145.42 million euros. Operating profit for the year increased to 111.06 million euros from 77.02 million euros. Net profit for the period declined to 71.97 million euros compared with 193.67 million euros in the prior year, primarily due to one-off effects related to the initial consolidation of Tallink Group recognized in 2024. For the fourth quarter of 2025, the company recorded revenue of 417.44 million euros, gross profit of 63.28 million euros, EBITDA of 42.60 million euros, operating profit of 19.84 million euros, and net profit of 14.14 million euros, recovering from a net loss of 11.99 million euros in the fourth quarter of 2024.

The financial performance was supported by strong operational cash flows and deliberate inventory management. Total assets at the end of December 2025 stood at 2.59 billion euros, down from 2.72 billion euros a year earlier, while total equity increased slightly to 1.18 billion euros from 1.17 billion euros. Interest-bearing liabilities decreased significantly to 1.06 billion euros from 1.22 billion euros, reflecting loan repayments across several group entities and adjustments to financial positions in line with market conditions. Net debt decreased to 840.68 million euros from 1.05 billion euros, and the equity ratio improved to 45.5% from 43.0%. Cash and cash equivalents rose to 219.77 million euros at the end of 2025 from 167.58 million euros. Total cash flows from operating activities surged to 388.63 million euros, compared with 89.29 million euros in 2024, aided by a substantial decrease in natural gas inventories and higher operating liabilities resulting from the full-year consolidation of Tallink Grupp. 

The group's main business segments experienced varying market conditions. The maritime transport segment, represented by Tallink Grupp, faced lower consumer confidence and geopolitical tensions, leading to a slight drop in passenger numbers and lower yearly revenue and net profit for Tallink, though fourth-quarter results showed improved profitability. The energy segment, led by Elenger Grupp, benefited from a 7% increase in gas and electricity sales volumes despite an overall 2% decline in regional gas consumption, while also expanding its infrastructure through new battery-based energy storage systems and LNG supply agreements. The real estate and supporting segments continued steady operations, including ongoing infrastructure construction such as the Rail Baltica mainline section. Overall, the reporting period was characterized by strengthened cash flows, debt reduction, and strategic portfolio diversification across maritime, energy, real estate, and agricultural activities.
