During the first three months of 2026, AB Pieno Žvaigždės reported total revenue of 52,064 thousand euros, showing a slight decrease compared to 52,324 thousand euros in the same period of 2025. Despite the minor decline in sales, the company achieved a significant improvement in profitability. Gross profit increased substantially to 13,986 thousand euros from 9,794 thousand euros in the prior year period. This growth was supported by a reduction in the cost of sales, which decreased to 38,078 thousand euros from 42,530 thousand euros. Operating profit before finance costs rose significantly to 3,366 thousand euros compared to 535 thousand euros for the first three months of 2025, while EBITDA improved to 4,730 thousand euros from 2,001 thousand euros. Net profit for the three-month period ending March 31, 2026, surged to 3,068 thousand euros, marking a major increase from 227 thousand euros reported in the corresponding period of 2025. Consequently, earnings per share and net profit per share rose to 0.07 euros, up from 0.01 euros in the previous year. Total assets increased to 85,305 thousand euros as of March 31, 2026, compared to 83,475 thousand euros at the end of December 2025. Total equity also grew to 41,164 thousand euros from 38,238 thousand euros over the same timeframe. Net cash flow from operating activities showed a positive turnaround, reaching 1,625 thousand euros compared to a negative 183 thousand euros for the first three months of 2025. Book value per share at the end of the period stood at 0.91 euros, compared to 0.89 euros at the end of the comparative period, and the current ratio improved to 1.51 from 1.27.
