SAF Tehnika reported a consolidated net turnover of EUR 26.11 million for the twelve months ended June 30, 2025, representing a 4% decline from the previous financial year. The fourth quarter of the 2024/2025 financial year saw a sharper contraction, with net sales dropping 13% to EUR 5.18 million compared to EUR 5.98 million in the same period last year. This quarterly decrease was primarily driven by a 40% drop in turnover from the North and Latin American regions, which fell to EUR 2.5 million. Conversely, the European region demonstrated resilience and growth, with turnover increasing by 24% to EUR 1.9 million. The Asia, Africa, and Middle East region also performed well, doubling its quarterly turnover to EUR 846 thousand. Over the full year, the Americas accounted for 55% of total revenue but declined by 14%, while Europe contributed 38% of revenue with a 17% increase. The Asia, Africa, and Middle East region saw a modest 5% decline in annual revenue.

Profitability deteriorated significantly during the reporting period. The Group recorded a net loss of EUR 820 thousand for the full year, a substantial improvement from the EUR 2.37 million loss in the prior year, largely due to lower direct costs of goods sold. However, the fourth quarter alone resulted in a net loss of EUR 913 thousand, worsening from the EUR 498 thousand loss in Q4 of the previous year. Earnings before interest, taxes, and depreciation (EBITDA) for the quarter were negative EUR 459 thousand, compared to negative EUR 96 thousand in the prior year period. The loss before interest and taxes (EBIT) for the full year was EUR 754 thousand, significantly better than the EUR 2.26 million loss recorded previously. Operating expenses remained relatively stable, with salaries and social expenses increasing by 8% to EUR 10.58 million, while bonuses and social expenses more than doubled to EUR 1.82 million.

The company faced challenges related to the global shortage of electronic components, which previously impacted production. To mitigate this, SAF Tehnika accumulated material reserves, ensuring short delivery times. By the end of the reporting period, delivery times had continued to improve. The company’s balance sheet shows a strong liquidity position, with cash and bank balances rising to EUR 4.81 million from EUR 2.21 million the previous year. Total current assets increased to EUR 20.93 million, supported by a significant rise in customer prepayments, which jumped to EUR 3.92 million from EUR 759 thousand. Inventories also increased by 8% to EUR 13.94 million. The Group maintained a EUR 4.95 million credit line with Luminor Bank AS, which remained entirely unused. The number of employees increased slightly to 273 from 265 in the previous year. Management remains cautious regarding future forecasts, citing ongoing uncertainty in the business environment, although no direct impact from armed conflicts in neighboring regions was observed.
