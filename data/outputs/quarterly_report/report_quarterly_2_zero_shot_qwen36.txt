AB Amber Grid reported a significant improvement in financial performance for the three-month period ended 31 March 2026. The company achieved total revenue of EUR 32,821 thousand, representing a substantial 58% increase compared to EUR 20,763 thousand in the same period of 2025. This growth was primarily driven by a 35% increase in the volume of natural gas transmitted and higher transmission prices set for 2026. Conversely, revenue from system balancing products decreased by 99% to EUR 24 thousand, a change attributed to a new transit agreement with Kaliningrad that significantly reduced the need for balancing gas. Operating expenses excluding depreciation, impairment, and write-offs amounted to EUR 9,570 thousand, a decrease from EUR 11,867 thousand in the prior year period. This reduction was largely due to a 68% drop in natural gas costs, which fell by 99% for balancing products, although costs for technological needs increased by 118% due to higher gas demand. Consequently, EBITDA surged to EUR 23,304 thousand from EUR 8,918 thousand in the previous year. After accounting for depreciation and amortization of EUR 3,677 thousand, operating profit (EBIT) reached EUR 19,627 thousand, compared to EUR 5,185 thousand in the prior period. Finance costs increased to EUR 626 thousand from EUR 408 thousand, resulting in a profit before tax of EUR 19,001 thousand, up from EUR 4,987 thousand. After income tax expenses of EUR 3,238 thousand, the company reported a net profit of EUR 15,763 thousand, a marked improvement from EUR 4,239 thousand in the corresponding period of 2025. Basic and diluted earnings per share rose to EUR 0.09 from EUR 0.02. The balance sheet reflects total assets of EUR 324,064 thousand, with total equity increasing to EUR 182,039 thousand from EUR 166,276 thousand at the end of 2025. Net debt stood at EUR 106,610 thousand, an increase from EUR 76,052 thousand at the end of March 2025, primarily due to changes in borrowings. During the period, the company reclassified long-term borrowings from financial institutions and parent companies to non-current liabilities following the expiration of a loan agreement with the Nordic Investment Bank and the assumption of remaining loan amounts by EPSO-G. Cash and cash equivalents decreased to EUR 832 thousand from EUR 1,942 thousand at the end of 2025, driven by operating cash flows and financing activities. The company also recognized a provision for the potential repayment of EUR 5,815 thousand in guarantee funds received from a construction contractor amidst ongoing legal disputes. Overall, the reporting period demonstrated strong operational profitability and revenue growth, despite increased net debt and ongoing legal contingencies.
