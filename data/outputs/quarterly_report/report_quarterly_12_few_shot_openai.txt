AS LHV Group’s Q2 2026 performance improved substantially from the previous quarter, although profitability remained below the level of Q2 2025. Consolidated net profit rose 25% quarter-on-quarter to EUR 24.7 million, including EUR 24.1 million attributable to the parent’s shareholders, but declined 20% year-on-year from EUR 30.8 million. Earnings per share increased to EUR 0.07 from EUR 0.06 in Q1, while remaining below EUR 0.09 a year earlier. For the first half of 2026, net profit was EUR 44.4 million, down 26% from EUR 59.9 million in the first half of 2025.

Revenue growth was driven mainly by net interest income, which increased 5% from Q1 to EUR 62.5 million and 8% year-on-year. Total net operating income rose 7% quarter-on-quarter and 7% year-on-year to EUR 79.0 million. Net fee and commission income was broadly unchanged from Q1 at EUR 14.6 million but declined 6% year-on-year, reflecting lower client activity. Other financial income increased to EUR 1.2 million, partly supported by the revaluation of the bond portfolio, while net insurance income recovered to EUR 0.7 million from a loss in Q1.

Operating expenses decreased 4% from Q1 to EUR 43.6 million, but were 8% higher than a year earlier. Despite the quarterly reduction, higher personnel and technology-related costs contributed to a cost-to-income ratio of 61.9%, compared with 53.9% in Q1 and 47.3% in Q2 2025. Operating profit increased to EUR 35.4 million from EUR 28.1 million in Q1. However, loan and bond impairment charges rose to EUR 5.8 million, compared with EUR 1.3 million in Q1 and a EUR 4.2 million reversal in Q2 2025. Management attributed the charges primarily to forward-looking model provisions and a limited number of individual cases, while stating that overall loan portfolio quality remained strong.

Business volumes continued to expand. The loan portfolio grew 3% during the quarter and 15% year-on-year to EUR 5.8 billion. Customer deposits declined 3% quarter-on-quarter to EUR 7.6 billion, mainly because of lower platform and financial-intermediary deposits, although ordinary customer deposits increased. Total managed funds rose 4% during the quarter and 15% year-on-year to EUR 1.8 billion.

LHV Pank generated EUR 24.3 million of quarterly profit, up 20% from Q1 but down 18% year-on-year. LHV Bank earned EUR 1.4 million, LHV Varahaldus EUR 0.4 million and LHV Kindlustus EUR 0.8 million, compared with a loss in Q1. The quarter also included strategic developments, including the migration of the Bank’s core systems to the cloud, a product-based management model, a crypto-assets service provider licence and preparations for greater international and cross-border operations.

Return on equity improved to 13.1% from 10.7% in Q1 but remained below 17.4% a year earlier. Capital adequacy was 20.8%, down from 21.7% in Q1 and 23.0% in Q2 2025, while liquidity remained strong with an LCR of 170.5%. Overall, Q2 showed improved quarterly earnings and continued loan growth, but higher credit provisions, elevated expenses, weaker fee income and reduced deposits constrained profitability and returns compared with the prior year.
