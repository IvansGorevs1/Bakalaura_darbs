AB KN Energies reported a significant improvement in financial performance for the first quarter of 2026 compared to the same period in 2025. The Group achieved total revenue from contracts with customers of EUR 29,823 thousand, representing a 17.7 percent increase from EUR 25,326 thousand in the prior year. This growth was primarily driven by the LNG terminal segment, where regasification services revenue rose to EUR 19,162 thousand from EUR 14,593 thousand. Additionally, sales of liquid energy products transshipment services increased to EUR 7,596 thousand from EUR 6,615 thousand, while consulting services revenue grew substantially to EUR 2,749 thousand from EUR 1,932 thousand.

Operating profit strengthened markedly, reaching EUR 9,608 thousand, up from EUR 7,141 thousand in the first quarter of 2025. This improvement was supported by a gross profit increase to EUR 12,907 thousand from EUR 9,793 thousand. Although distribution costs and administrative expenses rose to EUR 3,316 thousand from EUR 3,002 thousand, the overall operating margin expanded. The net finance cost decreased significantly to EUR 784 thousand from EUR 1,777 thousand in the previous year, largely due to lower interest expenses on borrowings and lease liabilities, which fell from EUR 2,287 thousand to EUR 1,860 thousand. This reduction in finance costs contributed to a substantial rise in profit before income tax, which totaled EUR 8,824 thousand compared to EUR 5,364 thousand in 2025.

The Group recorded a net profit for the period of EUR 7,247 thousand, a 63.7 percent increase from EUR 4,426 thousand in the corresponding period of the previous year. Earnings per share also improved, rising to EUR 0.02 from EUR 0.01. The LNG segment was the primary contributor to this profit growth, generating a segment net profit of EUR 4,529 thousand compared to EUR 1,328 thousand in 2025. The Liquid Energy Terminals segment saw a decline in segment net profit to EUR 1,590 thousand from EUR 2,332 thousand, while the commercial LNG segment improved to EUR 1,128 thousand from EUR 766 thousand.

Cash flow from operating activities increased robustly to EUR 27,917 thousand from EUR 13,758 thousand, driven by higher net profit and favorable changes in working capital, including a decrease in trade receivables. The Group’s cash and cash equivalents rose to EUR 17,986 thousand from EUR 12,361 thousand at the end of 2025. Total assets increased slightly to EUR 556,798 thousand from EUR 554,958 thousand, while total equity grew to EUR 179,866 thousand from EUR 172,592 thousand. Borrowings and lease liabilities remained stable, with total borrowings decreasing slightly to EUR 302,069 thousand from EUR 311,194 thousand. No impairment indicators were identified for the Klaipėda liquid energy terminal assets. Subsequent to the reporting period, the Board of Directors was re-elected on April 30, 2026, replacing the previous two-tier governance structure with a single-tier Board.
