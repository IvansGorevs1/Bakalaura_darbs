AS Tallink Grupp reported a challenging financial year 2025, characterized by a decline in revenue and profitability driven by economic uncertainty and reduced consumer confidence in its core markets, particularly Finland. For the twelve months ended 31 December 2025, the Group’s consolidated revenue decreased by 2.6% to EUR 765.3 million from EUR 785.8 million in the previous year. This contraction was primarily attributed to a significant drop in cargo volumes, which fell by 19.2% to 245,004 units, and a modest 0.9% decrease in passenger numbers to 5,531,132. The Estonia-Sweden route experienced the sharpest decline in passenger traffic, down 12.5%, while the Estonia-Finland route saw a slight increase of 1.8%.

Profitability metrics reflected the adverse operating environment. Unaudited EBITDA totaled EUR 130.1 million, a substantial decrease of 25.8% from EUR 175.2 million in 2024. The EBITDA margin contracted to 17.0% from 22.3% in the prior year. The Group’s net profit for the period was EUR 17.3 million, or EUR 0.023 per share, compared to a net profit of EUR 40.3 million, or EUR 0.054 per share, in 2024. Despite the revenue decline, gross profit decreased by a smaller margin of 6.7% to EUR 143.2 million, supported by a significant reduction in depreciation expenses. Depreciation and amortization costs fell by 18.4% to EUR 79.8 million, largely due to the alignment of estimated useful lives for cruise and passenger vessels to 45 years and the sale of three vessels during the year.

The Group’s financial position remained stable with a net debt of EUR 432.4 million at year-end, broadly unchanged from the previous quarter. The net debt to EBITDA ratio improved slightly to 3.3 from 3.4 at the end of the third quarter. Total loan repayments and interest payments amounted to EUR 113.5 million, helping to reduce the interest-bearing liabilities. The Group maintained a liquidity buffer of EUR 110.4 million, comprising cash equivalents and unused credit facilities. Capital investments increased to EUR 33.0 million, focused on maintenance and refurbishment of key vessels.

In the fourth quarter of 2025, performance showed signs of stabilization. Revenue increased by 2.4% to EUR 188.0 million, and EBITDA rose to EUR 27.6 million from EUR 25.7 million in the same period last year. The Group returned to net profitability in Q4 with EUR 12.2 million, reversing a net loss of EUR 5.2 million in Q4 2024. The Management Board proposed a dividend of EUR 0.06 per share for 2025, totaling EUR 44.6 million, which was paid out in two installments during the year. Looking ahead, the Group aims to navigate ongoing geopolitical tensions and tax changes by optimizing its fleet and maintaining cost efficiencies.
