During the second quarter and first six months of 2026, Liven AS experienced a strong recovery in residential real estate demand, supported by improving purchasing power and stable economic conditions. For the second quarter of 2026, the company reported revenue of EUR 4,503 thousand, compared with EUR 7,388 thousand in the second quarter of 2025. Profit before income tax for the second quarter was EUR -234 thousand, down from EUR 1,002 thousand in the prior-year period, and the net loss for the quarter reached EUR 618 thousand compared to a net profit of EUR 974 thousand a year earlier. For the 6-month period ended 30 June 2026, revenue increased to EUR 11,439 thousand from EUR 9,319 thousand in the first six months of 2025. Profit before income tax for the 6-month period stood at EUR 186 thousand compared to EUR 296 thousand previously, while the net result showed a loss of EUR 198 thousand compared to a net profit of EUR 268 thousand in the first half of 2025. Basic earnings per share were EUR -0.049 for the second quarter and EUR -0.016 for the six-month period.

The second quarter financial results were impacted by approximately EUR 90 thousand in one-off costs related to the public offering of shares that were not recognised as a reduction of share premium, as well as an income tax expense of EUR 384 thousand connected with dividend payments. During the six-month reporting period, the company handed over a total of 26 homes and commercial units, compared with 41 units in the first half of 2025. Sales activity remained robust, with 57 sales contracts signed under the law of obligations during the second quarter, bringing the 6-month total to 111 sales contracts, which is nearly double the 56 contracts signed in the first half of 2025. The company entered the third quarter of 2026 with 171 contracts under the law of obligations and a presale volume of EUR 48.6 million, of which EUR 39.8 million relates to buildings scheduled for completion during 2026. 

Total assets increased by EUR 8,505 thousand during the second quarter to reach EUR 100,186 thousand as of 30 June 2026, driven primarily by continued construction activity across development projects. Total loan liabilities grew to EUR 57,341 thousand at the end of the second quarter, reflecting an increase in construction loans to EUR 16,561 thousand alongside development bank loans, bonds, and investor loans. Equity at the end of the period stood at EUR 26,746 thousand, resulting in an equity ratio of 26.7% and an adjusted equity ratio excluding construction loans of 32.0%. Cash and cash equivalents increased by EUR 1,979 thousand during the second quarter to EUR 10,033 thousand, bolstered by net proceeds of EUR 4,687 thousand from the issuance of new shares through an initial public offering, which led to the listing of Liven AS shares on the Baltic Main List of the Nasdaq Tallinn Stock Exchange on 15 May 2026. 

Business developments during the period included the expansion of the development portfolio with new property acquisitions in Berlin, specifically Plantagenstraße 19 and Reichstraße 101, alongside the post-reporting-period acquisition of Pärnu maantee 35, 37, and 41 in Tallinn. Presales were launched for phase II of the Regati project, the new phase of Iseära, and phase I of Kadakadabra. Overall performance during the reporting period demonstrated a strong rebound in market demand and sales contracting pace, positioning the company with nearly 90% of its 2026 revenue target realised or covered by contracts as it shifts focus toward project completions and home handovers in the second half of the year.
