Arco Vara AS reported a significant improvement in its financial performance for the first quarter of 2025, driven by higher sales revenue and a return to profitability compared with the same period of the previous year. Sales revenue for the reporting period reached 1,706 thousand euros, representing an increase from 990 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2024. Revenue from the sale of own real estate rose to 1,589 thousand euros from 927 thousand euros, while service and franchise revenues also experienced modest growth. 

The company recorded an operating profit of 259 thousand euros for the first quarter of 2025, contrasting with an operating loss of 208 thousand euros in the corresponding period of 2024. Net profit for the period improved to 103 thousand euros, compared with a net loss of 349 thousand euros reported in the first quarter of the previous year. Basic and diluted earnings per share both increased to 0.01 euros, up from negative 0.03 euros in the prior year period. 

Financial results were influenced by ongoing activities across the company's real estate development portfolio in Estonia and Bulgaria. During the first quarter of 2025, the group sold 4 apartments, compared with 3 apartments and 1 commercial space sold in the first quarter of 2024. The company also expanded its project pipeline by acquiring a new development site on Spordi Street in Tallinn, supported by a bank loan of 2,190 thousand euros. 

Total assets increased to 42,728 thousand euros as of 31 March 2025, compared with 39,568 thousand euros at the end of December 2024. Inventories grew to 32,329 thousand euros from 29,170 thousand euros. Interest-bearing liabilities rose to 16,568 thousand euros at the end of March 2025, up from 15,215 thousand euros at the close of 2024, primarily due to new financing for development projects. Net loans stood at 15,455 thousand euros, reflecting an increase of 1,960 thousand euros during the first three months of the year. The weighted average annual interest rate of interest-bearing liabilities decreased by 0.5 percentage points from the end of the previous year to reach 8.2% as of 31 March 2025, supported by a decline in the Euribor. Equity attributable to owners of the parent remained stable at 19,842 thousand euros, compared with 19,739 thousand euros at the end of December 2024. 

Cash flows used in operating activities amounted to 2,838 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2025, compared with 2,217 thousand euros used in the first quarter of 2024. Cash flows from financing activities were positive at 2,231 thousand euros, reflecting new loan proceeds that exceeded loan settlements and interest payments. Overall, net cash flows decreased by 607 thousand euros during the quarter, leaving the group with cash and cash equivalents of 1,113 thousand euros at the end of March 2025.
