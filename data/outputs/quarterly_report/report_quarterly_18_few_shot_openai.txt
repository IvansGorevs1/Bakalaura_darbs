AB Pieno Žvaigždės delivered a substantially stronger financial performance in the first quarter of 2026, despite broadly stable revenue. Sales amounted to EUR 52.064 million, compared with EUR 52.324 million in the first quarter of 2025, a decline of approximately 0.5%. Sales in Lithuania decreased to EUR 31.405 million from EUR 32.112 million, while sales to other EU countries increased to EUR 11.341 million from EUR 10.801 million. By product category, fresh milk products remained the largest contributor at EUR 42.497 million, while dry milk product sales declined to EUR 1.397 million. Cheese and other product sales increased.

Profitability improved significantly. Cost of sales fell to EUR 38.078 million from EUR 42.530 million, resulting in gross profit of EUR 13.986 million, up from EUR 9.794 million. Gross margin therefore increased materially. Other operating income also rose to EUR 363 thousand from EUR 28 thousand. These improvements more than offset higher sales and administrative expenses, which increased to EUR 10.983 million from EUR 9.287 million. Operating profit before finance costs rose to EUR 3.366 million from EUR 535 thousand.

Net finance expenses were broadly stable at EUR 298 thousand, compared with EUR 308 thousand a year earlier. As a result, profit before tax and net profit both increased to EUR 3.068 million from EUR 227 thousand. Earnings per share rose to EUR 0.07 from EUR 0.01. EBITDA increased to EUR 4.730 million from EUR 2.001 million, confirming the substantial improvement in operating performance.

Cash generation also strengthened. Net cash flow from operating activities was EUR 1.625 million, compared with an outflow of EUR 183 thousand in the first quarter of 2025. Operating cash flow benefited from higher profitability and a EUR 998 thousand reduction in inventories, although this was partly offset by a EUR 2.536 million increase in receivables. Investing activities used EUR 551 thousand, mainly for property, plant and equipment, compared with EUR 1.058 million in the prior-year period. Financing activities resulted in a net outflow of EUR 723 thousand, primarily reflecting repayment of borrowings and finance lease liabilities, whereas the previous year included EUR 8.5 million of loan proceeds.

Total assets increased to EUR 85.305 million at 31 March 2026 from EUR 83.475 million at year-end. Equity rose to EUR 41.164 million, supported by first-quarter profit, while total liabilities declined to EUR 44.141 million. Cash increased during the quarter to EUR 2.157 million, although it remained below the EUR 4.902 million reported at 31 March 2025. Overall, the period was characterized by stable sales, sharply improved margins and profitability, stronger operating cash generation, and a modest strengthening of the company’s equity position.
