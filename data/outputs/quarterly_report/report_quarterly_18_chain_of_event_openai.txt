AB Pieno Žvaigždės reported substantially improved profitability in the first three months of 2026 despite broadly stable revenue. Sales were EUR 52.064 million, down marginally from EUR 52.324 million in the same period of 2025. However, cost of sales fell to EUR 38.078 million from EUR 42.530 million, increasing gross profit by 42.8% to EUR 13.986 million and materially improving the gross margin.

Other operating income increased to EUR 363 thousand from EUR 28 thousand, while sales and administrative expenses rose 18.3% to EUR 10.983 million. Despite the higher operating expenses, operating profit before finance costs increased more than sixfold, from EUR 535 thousand to EUR 3.366 million. Net finance expenses improved slightly to EUR 298 thousand from EUR 308 thousand, resulting in profit before tax and net profit of EUR 3.068 million, compared with EUR 227 thousand a year earlier. Earnings per share rose to EUR 0.07 from EUR 0.01. EBITDA increased to EUR 4.730 million from EUR 2.001 million.

The report does not identify specific market or operational events explaining the sharp reduction in cost of sales or the improved profitability. It does show that revenue remained relatively stable across the company’s main product categories and markets. Fresh milk products generated EUR 42.497 million in sales, cheese sales increased to EUR 5.276 million from EUR 5.192 million, and other products rose to EUR 2.894 million from EUR 2.621 million. These increases were offset by lower sales of dry milk products, which declined to EUR 1.397 million from EUR 1.912 million. Sales in Lithuania decreased, while sales in other EU countries increased.

Operating cash flow improved significantly. Net cash flow from operating activities was positive at EUR 1.625 million, compared with an outflow of EUR 183 thousand in the first quarter of 2025. Cash flow benefited from the higher net profit and a EUR 998 thousand reduction in inventories, partly offset by a EUR 2.536 million increase in receivables and EUR 516 thousand of income tax paid. Investing activities used EUR 551 thousand, compared with EUR 1.058 million previously, while financing activities used EUR 723 thousand versus a EUR 4.133 million inflow in 2025, when the company received EUR 8.5 million in loans.

Cash increased by EUR 351 thousand during the quarter to EUR 2.157 million. Total assets rose to EUR 85.305 million from EUR 83.475 million at year-end 2025, while total equity increased to EUR 41.164 million, primarily reflecting first-quarter profit. The current ratio improved to 1.51 from 1.27.
