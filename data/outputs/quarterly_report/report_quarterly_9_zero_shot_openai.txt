Hepsor’s 2025 performance weakened compared with 2024, reflecting fewer apartment handovers, lower project margins and higher marketing costs. Consolidated revenue declined 7.8% to €35.4 million from €38.4 million, while net profit fell 51.3% to €1.0 million. Profit attributable to the parent’s owners was €0.4 million, broadly unchanged from €0.4 million in 2024, while profit attributable to non-controlling interests declined to €0.6 million from €1.7 million. Gross profit decreased to €5.6 million from €6.8 million, and the gross margin narrowed to 15.9% from 17.6%. Operating profit fell to €3.4 million from €4.3 million, with the operating margin declining to 9.5% from 11.3%. EBITDA decreased to €3.5 million from €4.5 million.

Fourth-quarter results were particularly weak. Revenue fell to €4.5 million from €10.5 million, and the Group recorded a net loss of €0.5 million compared with a €1.0 million profit in the comparable quarter. The quarterly gross margin declined to 15.3% from 24.0%, while the net margin was -10.7%, compared with 9.1% in Q4 2024. An €0.8 million gain from revaluing properties transferred to investment property supported fourth-quarter operating profit, which was €0.3 million versus €2.0 million a year earlier.

The main operating factor was the reduction in apartments handed over, to 141 from 194, including 22 in the fourth quarter compared with 52 previously. The gross margin on sold projects also declined to 16.9% from 17.8%, while marketing expenses rose to €1.3 million from €0.9 million because more projects were offered for sale and required greater sales activity. Conversely, primary sales agreements increased substantially to 185 homes worth €36.1 million, compared with 109 homes worth €19.8 million in 2024. Revenue generated in Latvia increased to €9.3 million from €5.9 million, partly offsetting the decline in Estonia, where revenue fell to €26.1 million from €32.5 million.

The development pipeline expanded materially. Active projects increased to 33 from 24, with sellable area rising to approximately 196,650 square metres from 172,500 square metres. Homes under construction increased to 428 from 192, supported by four new project starts in Estonia and Latvia. The Group also expanded its Canadian portfolio and received building rights for the Weston Road project in Toronto.

At year-end, total assets rose 3.4% to €91.8 million. Interest-bearing liabilities declined to €47.7 million from €54.7 million, and the debt ratio improved to 52.0% from 61.6%. Equity increased 23.0% to €27.1 million, lifting the equity ratio to 29.5% from 24.8%. Cash decreased to €3.8 million from €6.2 million. In November, Hepsor raised €8.0 million through its first bond issue, carrying a fixed annual interest rate of 9.5%, strengthening funding for its expanded development programme.
