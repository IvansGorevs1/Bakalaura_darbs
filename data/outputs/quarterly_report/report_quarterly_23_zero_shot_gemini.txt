During the first nine months of 2025, AB KN Energies and its subsidiaries demonstrated overall positive financial and operational development. For the reporting period ending September 30, 2025, the Group reported revenue from contracts with customers of EUR 76,664 thousand, representing an increase compared to EUR 67,977 thousand reported for the same period in 2024. The company level revenue for the first nine months of 2025 reached EUR 72,848 thousand, up from EUR 65,009 thousand in the previous year. Gross profit for the Group stood at EUR 31,549 thousand, compared to EUR 29,237 thousand for the first nine months of 2024. Operating profit for the Group increased to EUR 21,282 thousand from EUR 18,942 thousand. Profit before tax for the Group was EUR 16,186 thousand, compared to EUR 14,910 thousand in the prior year period. Net profit attributable to the shareholders of the Company for the first nine months of 2025 increased to EUR 13,158 thousand, compared to EUR 11,862 thousand for the corresponding period in 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share for the Group rose to EUR 0.035 for the first nine months of 2025, compared to EUR 0.031 for the first nine months of 2024. For the Company alone, net profit for the reporting period was EUR 11,613 thousand, compared to EUR 10,702 thousand in the prior year period. 

The primary business factors and developments that affected the company’s results during the reporting period included increased income from liquefied natural gas terminal regasification services collected directly from LNG terminal users, which reached EUR 43,640 thousand compared to EUR 31,401 thousand in the first nine months of 2024. This growth was driven by higher LNG regasification volumes, bonuses paid by LNG terminal clients for booked capacity, and the absence of operational downtime which had occurred in the previous year when the terminal was out of operation for one and a half months due to dry-dock repair works. Additionally, sales of liquid energy products transshipment services increased to EUR 21,375 thousand from EUR 20,241 thousand, and sales of consulting services rose to EUR 7,507 thousand from EUR 3,961 thousand. Conversely, other sales related to LNG terminal activity decreased, primarily due to lower payments collected from clients for unused allocated capacities, and revenue was also impacted by a reduction in LNG security supplement income resulting from the proportionally recognized repayment of excess returns from regulated activities during the 2022 to 2023 period. 

Significant changes in the financial position and statement of financial position items included total assets of the Group amounting to EUR 530,364 thousand as at September 30, 2025, compared to EUR 535,174 thousand as at December 31, 2024. Total equity for the Group increased to EUR 167,721 thousand as at September 30, 2025, from EUR 163,128 thousand as at December 31, 2024. Non-current liabilities of the Group were EUR 327,356 thousand as at September 30, 2025, down from EUR 331,801 thousand at the end of 2024, while current liabilities decreased to EUR 35,287 thousand from EUR 40,245 thousand. Cash and cash equivalents for the Group increased substantially to EUR 54,398 thousand as at September 30, 2025, compared to EUR 17,203 thousand as at December 31, 2024, supported by net cash flows from operating activities of EUR 76,331 thousand for the nine-month period. Borrowings stood at EUR 311,443 thousand in total for the Group and Company as at September 30, 2025, consisting of loans from the Nordic Investment Bank and the European Investment Bank along with guarantee payments to the Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Lithuania, compared to EUR 320,352 thousand as at December 31, 2024.
