Arco Vara AS reported its financial results for the first quarter of 2025, demonstrating improved financial performance compared to the same period in 2024. For the reporting period ending March 31, 2025, the group generated total revenue of 1,706 thousand euros, representing an increase of 716 thousand euros compared to 990 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2024. Revenue from the sale of own real estate rose to 1,589 thousand euros from 927 thousand euros, while revenue from rendering services increased to 117 thousand euros from 63 thousand euros. Gross profit improved to 598 thousand euros compared to 173 thousand euros in the prior year period. Operating profit or EBIT reached 259 thousand euros, marking a recovery from the operating loss of 208 thousand euros reported in the first quarter of 2024. Net profit for the period was 103 thousand euros, an improvement from the net loss of 349 thousand euros in the corresponding period of 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share both stood at 0.01 euros, compared to -0.03 euros in the first quarter of 2024. The financial results were influenced by the moderate recovery in the real estate markets in Estonia and Bulgaria, characterized by stabilizing price levels, declining Euribor rates, and increased transaction and sales activity within residential developments such as Rannakalda and Botanica Lozen. Net cash flows used in operating activities were 2,838 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2025, compared to 2,217 thousand euros used in the first quarter of 2024. Net cash flows from financing activities were 2,231 thousand euros, compared to 1,479 thousand euros in the previous year. Total cash and cash equivalents stood at 1,113 thousand euros as of March 31, 2025, down from 1,720 thousand euros at the beginning of the year. Total assets increased to 42,728 thousand euros at the end of the reporting period from 39,568 thousand euros as of December 31, 2024. Total liabilities rose to 22,886 thousand euros from 19,829 thousand euros, driven by an increase in interest-bearing loans and borrowings, which reached 16,568 thousand euros compared to 15,215 thousand euros at the end of 2024. Net loans increased by 1,960 thousand euros during the first three months of the year to reach 15,455 thousand euros as of March 31, 2025. The weighted average annual interest rate of interest-bearing liabilities was 8.2% at the end of the period, representing a decrease of 0.5 percentage points compared to December 31, 2024. Total equity attributable to owners of the parent was 19,842 thousand euros as of March 31, 2025, showing a slight increase from 19,739 thousand euros at the end of 2024.
