During the financial year 2025, AS Tallink Grupp reported unaudited consolidated revenue of EUR 765.3 million, representing a decrease compared to EUR 785.8 million in the financial year 2024. The company generated an unaudited EBITDA of EUR 130.1 million, down from EUR 175.2 million in the previous year. Unaudited net profit for 2025 stood at EUR 17.3 million, or EUR 0.023 per share, compared to EUR 40.3 million, or EUR 0.054 per share, reported for 2024. Gross profit decreased to EUR 143.2 million from EUR 153.6 million. For the fourth quarter of 2025 specifically, revenue rose by 2.4% year-on-year to EUR 188.0 million, EBITDA reached EUR 27.6 million compared to EUR 25.7 million in the fourth quarter of 2024, and net profit was EUR 12.2 million, marking an improvement from a net loss of EUR 5.2 million in the same period of the previous year.

The company’s financial and operational results were influenced by several significant changes compared to the previous period. Amortisation and depreciation expenses decreased by EUR 18.0 million to EUR 79.8 million, primarily driven by the alignment of estimated useful lives for cruise and passenger vessels to 45 years and the sale of three vessels during the year. Net finance costs declined by EUR 6.8 million to EUR 21.4 million due to lower outstanding loan balances and declining interest rates. Total passenger numbers across the group decreased slightly by 0.9% to 5,531,132 passengers, while transported cargo units experienced a notable decline of 19.2% to 245,004 units. Passenger vehicles decreased by 2.2% to 760,473 units. As of 31 December 2025, the group’s net debt remained virtually unchanged from the end of the third quarter at EUR 432.4 million, though significantly lower than the EUR 537.7 million reported at the end of 2024, bringing the net debt to EBITDA ratio to 3.3. The total liquidity buffer at year-end was EUR 110.4 million, consisting of EUR 13.5 million in cash and cash equivalents and EUR 96.9 million in unused credit lines. Total loan and interest payments during 2025 amounted to EUR 113.5 million. 

The company's performance was affected by several main business events, developments, and market factors. Operations remained under pressure due to low private consumer and business confidence, ongoing economic challenges, and inflation in core markets, particularly Finland, which constrained consumer spending and cargo flows. To optimize its cost base and adapt to the environment, the group completed the sale of three vessels during the year, namely the passenger vessel Star I in April, the cargo vessel Regal Star in August, and the cargo vessel Sailor in October. Additionally, the group maintained three vessels on charter agreements throughout the year, including the cruise ferries Romantika, Galaxy I, and Silja Europa, with the latter two having their charters extended toward the end of the year. Capital investments totaled EUR 33.0 million, directed mainly toward maintenance, technical upgrades, and refurbishments of vessels such as the Baltic Princess and Silja Serenade, which resulted in 73 days of planned maintenance works. Furthermore, the company distributed dividends of EUR 0.06 per share, totaling EUR 44.6 million alongside EUR 11.3 million in related income tax, and the Management Board intends to propose a dividend of 6 euro cents per share for 2026.

Overall, the reporting period was characterized as challenging due to unfavourable macroeconomic conditions and economic uncertainty in the region's core markets. Despite these headwinds, internal adjustments such as vessel sales, chartering arrangements, cost management, and reduced debt and depreciation burdens enabled Tallink Grupp to achieve satisfactory results under the circumstances, maintaining stable profitability, positive net results, and a consistent commitment to shareholder returns.
