Telia Lietuva, AB reported a strong financial performance for the first half of 2026, characterized by significant growth in revenue, profitability, and cash flow generation. The company’s interim service revenue increased by 6.7 percent, while equipment sales revenue grew by 7.2 percent, contributing to a total revenue increase of 7.2 percent to EUR 259.1 million, compared to EUR 241.7 million in the same period of the previous year. This growth was driven by rising demand for IT services, which saw a substantial 23.9 percent increase, and strong performance in mobile services, which grew by 8.3 percent. Fixed service revenue also expanded by 5.3 percent, supported by higher internet and TV service revenues.

Profitability metrics improved markedly across the board. Adjusted EBITDA rose by 10.2 percent to EUR 106.9 million, with the adjusted EBITDA margin expanding to 41.3 percent from 40.1 percent in the prior year. Reported EBITDA increased by 9.5 percent to EUR 105.5 million, maintaining a robust margin of 40.7 percent. Operating profit (EBIT) excluding non-recurring items grew by 16.3 percent to EUR 63.9 million, reflecting an EBIT margin of 24.1 percent. Consequently, the profit for the period increased by 14.7 percent to EUR 51.3 million, or EUR 0.088 per share, up from EUR 44.7 million in 2025. The profit before income tax margin also improved to 23.7 percent from 21.8 percent.

The company’s financial position remained solid, with free cash flow increasing by 12.5 percent to EUR 64.1 million. Operating cash flow generated from activities rose by 15.0 percent to EUR 103.1 million. Capital investments surged by 57.9 percent to EUR 37.5 million, primarily due to significant spending on mobile network expansion and the acquisition of 5G spectrum licenses. Specifically, the company acquired two 1,500 MHz frequency blocks for nearly EUR 8 million and extended rights to the 2,100 MHz band for EUR 2 million, totaling EUR 9.7 million in spectrum investments. These investments are aimed at enhancing network capacity to meet the 25 percent annual growth in mobile data traffic in Lithuania.

Shareholder returns were substantial, with dividends of EUR 81.6 million paid for the 2025 financial year, equating to EUR 0.14 per share. This payout represented a record high dividend per share and was funded by the company’s strong cash generation. The net debt position improved significantly, turning from a net debt of EUR 27.5 million in June 2025 to a net cash position of EUR 4.3 million by the end of June 2026. The gearing ratio decreased to negative 1.1 percent, indicating a strengthened balance sheet.

Despite the positive financial results, operating expenses increased by 11.3 percent to EUR 68.8 million. This rise was largely attributable to non-recurring redundancy expenses of EUR 1.4 million related to the restructuring of the Digital & Technology unit, as well as higher costs for energy, external IT services, and network interconnection. Employee headcount decreased by 6.9 percent to 1,601, reflecting ongoing efficiency measures. Overall, the first half of 2026 demonstrated Telia Lietuva’s ability to drive top-line growth and margin expansion through strategic investments in network infrastructure and a focus on high-growth service segments, while maintaining strong liquidity and returning significant value to shareholders.
