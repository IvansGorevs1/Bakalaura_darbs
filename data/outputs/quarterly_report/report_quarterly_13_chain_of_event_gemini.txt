During the second quarter and first six months of 2026, Liven AS experienced a strong recovery in residential real estate demand, accompanied by key corporate milestones including the initial public offering of its shares and their admission to the Baltic Main List of the Nasdaq Tallinn Stock Exchange on May 15, 2026. For the six-month period ending June 30, 2026, the group reported consolidated revenue of 11,439 thousand euros, representing an increase compared to 9,319 thousand euros for the first six months of 2025. Operating profit for the six-month period was 341 thousand euros, compared with 325 thousand euros a year earlier, while profit before tax reached 186 thousand euros compared to 296 thousand euros in the first half of 2025. Due to income tax expenses of 384 thousand euros incurred in connection with dividends paid during the quarter, the group recorded a net loss of 198 thousand euros for the six-month period, which compares to a net profit of 268 thousand euros for the first six months of 2025. Basic and diluted earnings per share for the six-month period were both minus 0.016 euros, compared with 0.022 euros for the same period in 2025. 

For the second quarter of 2026 specifically, revenue stood at 4,503 thousand euros, declining from 7,388 thousand euros in the second quarter of 2025. Operating performance shifted to an operating loss of 177 thousand euros for the second quarter, down from an operating profit of 1,012 thousand euros in the second quarter of 2025, influenced by one-off administrative and distribution costs of approximately 90 thousand euros related to the public offering of shares that were not recognized as a reduction of share premium. Profit before tax for the second quarter was minus 234 thousand euros compared to 1,002 thousand euros in the prior year period. Following income tax expenses of 384 thousand euros on dividends, the net loss for the second quarter of 2026 was 618 thousand euros, reflecting a decline from a net profit of 974 thousand euros in the second quarter of 2025. Basic earnings per share for the quarter were minus 0.049 euros and diluted earnings per share were minus 0.047 euros, compared with 0.081 euros and 0.080 euros respectively in the second quarter of 2025. 

Sales activity expanded significantly during the reporting period. During the second quarter of 2026, the group signed 57 sales contracts under the law of obligations compared to 31 contracts in the second quarter of 2025. Over the first six months of 2026, the group signed 111 sales contracts, nearly doubling the 56 contracts signed during the first six months of 2025. Demand recovery drove the weekly sales ratio above the long-term normal range of 1.5 to 2.0 percent to nearly 3.1 percent as a four-week average, supported by presales in projects such as Regati phase II, Iseära, and Kadakadabra phase I. The group entered the third quarter of 2026 with 171 contracts under the law of obligations and a presale volume of 48.6 million euros, of which 39.8 million euros relate to buildings scheduled for completion in 2026. Conversely, the handover of completed homes slowed during the second quarter, with only 12 homes handed over compared to 35 homes in the second quarter of 2025, bringing the total number of handovers for the first six months of 2026 to 26 homes compared to 41 homes in the first six months of 2025. 

The group's total assets grew by 8,505 thousand euros during the second quarter to reach 100,186 thousand euros as of June 30, 2026, driven primarily by continued construction in development projects. Total loan liabilities reached 57,341 thousand euros at the end of the quarter, consisting of 16,561 thousand euros in construction bank loans, 19,542 thousand euros in development bank loans, 14,885 thousand euros in bonds, and 6,353 thousand euros in investor loans.
