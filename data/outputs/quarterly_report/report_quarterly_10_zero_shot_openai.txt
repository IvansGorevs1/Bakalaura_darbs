Infortar Group reported a strong 2025, although net profit was materially lower than in 2024 because the prior-year result included one-off effects associated with the initial consolidation of Tallink Grupp. Revenue increased to EUR 1.837 billion from EUR 1.372 billion, while EBITDA rose 60.0% to EUR 232.6 million from EUR 145.4 million. EBITDA margin improved to 13.4% from 10.6%. Operating profit increased 44.2% to EUR 111.1 million, but net profit fell to EUR 72.0 million from EUR 193.7 million; profit attributable to the parent declined to EUR 72.7 million from EUR 191.3 million. Earnings per share decreased to EUR 3.54 from EUR 9.36.

The improvement in operating performance reflected the full-year consolidation of Tallink in 2025, compared with consolidation from August 2024 onward, an approximately EUR 12 million improvement in Elenger’s EBITDA, and contributions from Estonia Farmid and Halinga. Energy generated the largest external revenue, EUR 980.2 million, followed by maritime transport at EUR 761.7 million. Full-year maritime EBITDA was EUR 124.2 million, down from EUR 175.2 million, while energy EBITDA increased to EUR 89.3 million from EUR 77.2 million. Real estate EBITDA was EUR 9.3 million, compared with EUR 10.3 million, and supporting-segment EBITDA increased to EUR 9.8 million from EUR 2.7 million.

Fourth-quarter performance improved significantly year on year. Revenue was EUR 417.4 million, down from EUR 446.2 million, but EBITDA increased to EUR 42.6 million from EUR 27.9 million and operating profit moved from a EUR 6.8 million loss to a EUR 19.8 million profit. Net profit was EUR 14.1 million, compared with a EUR 12.0 million loss in Q4 2024. Maritime revenue increased 2.4% to EUR 188.0 million, EBITDA rose to EUR 27.6 million from EUR 25.7 million, and net profit improved from a EUR 5.2 million loss to EUR 12.2 million. Tallink passenger volumes declined 0.9% for the year, cargo units fell 19.2%, and passenger vehicles decreased 2.2%. Energy sales volumes nevertheless increased 7% in the quarter to 4.8 TWh despite a 2% decline in regional gas consumption.

The Group strengthened its financial position. Equity rose to EUR 1.178 billion, the equity ratio improved to 45.5% from 43.0%, and interest-bearing liabilities declined to EUR 1.060 billion from EUR 1.223 billion. Net debt fell to EUR 840.7 million, and net debt to EBITDA improved to 3.6 from 4.0. Operating cash flow increased to EUR 388.6 million from EUR 89.3 million, while year-end cash rose to EUR 219.8 million. Major developments included the acquisition of Oisu Biogaas, continued Rail Baltica and Depo construction projects, a multi-year agreement for 12 LNG cargoes, and further investment in energy storage and maritime assets.
