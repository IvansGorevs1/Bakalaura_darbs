Telia Lietuva, AB delivered strong financial performance in the first half of 2026, characterized by robust revenue growth, expanded profitability, and significant strategic investments in network infrastructure. The company reported total revenue of EUR 259.1 million for the January-June period, representing a 7.2 percent increase compared to EUR 241.7 million in the same period of 2025. This growth was driven by a 6.7 percent rise in interim service revenue and a 7.2 percent increase in equipment sales revenue. The revenue composition reflected a strong demand for IT services, which grew by 23.9 percent, and mobile services, which increased by 8.3 percent. Fixed service revenue also grew by 5.3 percent, supported by a 4.2 percent rise in internet services.

Profitability metrics improved significantly across the board. Adjusted EBITDA excluding non-recurring items rose by 10.2 percent to EUR 106.9 million, with the adjusted EBITDA margin expanding to 41.3 percent from 40.1 percent in the prior year period. Reported EBITDA increased by 9.5 percent to EUR 105.5 million, achieving an EBITDA margin of 40.7 percent. Operating profit (EBIT) excluding non-recurring items grew by 16.3 percent to EUR 63.9 million, resulting in an EBIT margin of 24.1 percent. Consequently, profit for the period increased by 14.7 percent to EUR 51.3 million, up from EUR 44.7 million in 2025. The profit margin stood at 19.8 percent, compared to 18.5 percent in the previous year. Earnings per share rose by 14.7 percent to EUR 0.088.

The company’s cash generation capabilities remained solid, with net cash generated by operating activities increasing by 15.0 percent to EUR 103.1 million. Free cash flow grew by 12.5 percent to EUR 64.1 million, up from EUR 57.0 million in 2025. This strong cash position enabled the company to distribute record dividends of EUR 81.6 million to shareholders for the year 2025, equivalent to EUR 0.14 per share. The dividend payout ratio was approximately 90.3 percent of the 2025 net profit.

Capital investments surged by 57.9 percent to EUR 37.5 million, reflecting the company’s commitment to network expansion and modernization. A key driver of this increase was the acquisition of 5G spectrum licenses, which accounted for EUR 9.7 million of the total investment. Specifically, in June 2026, Telia Lietuva acquired two 1,500 MHz frequency blocks for nearly EUR 8 million and extended rights to the 2,100 MHz band for EUR 2 million. These frequencies are critical for enhancing 5G network capacity and data transmission speeds. Additionally, the company invested in expanding its mobile network with new base stations and modernizing existing sites. The net debt position improved dramatically, shifting from a net debt of EUR 27.5 million in June 2025 to a net cash position of EUR 4.3 million in June 2026. The share price increased by 34.2 percent to EUR 2.18 by the end of the period, boosting market capitalization to EUR 1,270 million. Overall, Telia Lietuva demonstrated resilient operational performance, strategic infrastructure development, and healthy financial returns during the first half of 2026.
