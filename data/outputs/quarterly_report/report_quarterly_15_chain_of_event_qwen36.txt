Nordecon AS reported a consolidated revenue of 208.3 million euros for the twelve months ended December 31, 2025, representing a seven percent decrease from the 223.9 million euros recorded in 2024. This decline was primarily driven by a ten percent drop in the Buildings segment, which accounted for 81 percent of total revenue, while the Infrastructure segment saw a ten percent growth. The group’s gross margin contracted by one percentage point to 6.5 percent, down from 7.5 percent in the prior year, with the Buildings segment margin falling from 8.9 percent to 7.7 percent. Administrative expenses decreased by approximately 14 percent to 6.8 million euros, largely due to lower staff costs resulting from reduced performance-based pay liabilities. Consequently, operating profit declined to 5.7 million euros from 8.1 million euros in 2024, leading to an operating margin of 2.7 percent compared to 3.6 percent previously. Net profit for the period was 2.5 million euros, a significant reduction from 5.2 million euros in 2024, largely impacted by a 750 thousand euro foreign exchange loss caused by the weakening of the Ukrainian hryvnia against the euro. The exchange rate of the hryvnia weakened by approximately 12 percent during the year, contributing to higher finance costs that totaled 3.5 million euros. Despite the lower profitability, the group’s order book grew substantially by 30 percent to 273.1 million euros at year-end, up from 209.5 million euros in 2024. This growth was driven by a nearly threefold increase in the Infrastructure segment’s order book, which now comprises 42 percent of the total backlog, compared to 18 percent in the previous year. Major new contracts included significant projects for Rail Baltica and national road construction, alongside various commercial and public building projects. The Buildings segment order book decreased by 7 percent. Approximately 69 percent of the current order book is scheduled for execution in 2026, providing visibility for future revenue. Cash flows from operating activities generated a net inflow of 1.2 million euros, while investing activities used 2.8 million euros, largely due to cash placed in long-term deposits to secure guarantee limits. Financing activities resulted in a net outflow of 1.2 million euros, reflecting loan repayments and lease payments. The group ended the period with cash and cash equivalents of 5.3 million euros, down from 8.2 million euros at the end of 2024. The current ratio stood at 0.93, indicating a slightly higher liquidity risk than in the prior year, influenced by the classification of loans to Ukrainian associates as non-current. No dividends were paid in 2025, whereas 1.3 million euros were distributed in 2024. The company maintained a stable workforce with an average of 431 employees, and nominal labor cost efficiency improved by 5.3 percent due to the disproportionate reduction in staff costs relative to revenue.
