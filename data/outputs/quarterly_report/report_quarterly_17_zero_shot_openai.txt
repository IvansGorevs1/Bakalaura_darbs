AB Novaturas’ 2025 performance reflected a deliberate reduction in travel volumes amid intense competition and excess market supply, with profitability improving despite lower revenue. The Group served 190.9 thousand travellers, down 20.3% from 239.5 thousand in 2024, while revenue declined 16.1% to EUR 168.5 million. Flight package tours remained the core business, generating EUR 145.1 million of sales, 15.0% below the prior year. However, segment sales profit increased 18.0% to EUR 12.2 million, supported by stricter supply discipline, reduced loss-making routes and a higher focus on better-margin destinations.

Gross profit decreased 5.3% to EUR 19.7 million, but the gross margin improved from 10.33% to 11.66%. Selling expenses fell 11.3% to EUR 15.2 million, primarily due to lower commissions, while general and administrative expenses declined 53.1% to EUR 4.9 million, largely reflecting significantly lower other administrative costs. As a result, the operating loss narrowed to EUR 274 thousand from EUR 6.6 million in 2024. EBITDA improved to positive EUR 420 thousand from negative EUR 6.1 million, and adjusted EBITDA increased from negative EUR 449 thousand to EUR 420 thousand. The Group nevertheless reported a net loss of EUR 1.17 million, an 84.6% improvement from the EUR 7.60 million loss in 2024; the prior-year result included a EUR 5.7 million non-cash goodwill impairment. Earnings per share improved to negative EUR 0.15 from negative EUR 0.97.

Operational efficiency improved, with the aircraft load factor rising 1.5 percentage points to 97.2%. Long-haul load factors increased to 98.5%, while ski-destination load factors rose from 90.5% to 97.2%. Nevertheless, additional market supply in the autumn pressured prices, and disruptions at Vilnius Airport related to illegal cross-border unmanned-balloon activity reduced last-minute demand, limiting the second-half result. In the second half, revenue fell 13.9% to EUR 94.3 million, but EBITDA remained positive at EUR 674 thousand compared with negative EUR 4.6 million in the same period of 2024.

The Group ended the year with EUR 1.7 million in cash, compared with EUR 394 thousand a year earlier, while total borrowings increased to EUR 12.9 million from EUR 9.3 million and equity declined to EUR 6.9 million from EUR 8.0 million. Early bookings for summer 2026 were 59% higher by December 2025, and the Group plans a similar or slightly larger travel programme, continued cost and operational efficiency measures, and an expanded hotel portfolio.
