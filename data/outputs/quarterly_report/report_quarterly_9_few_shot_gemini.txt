Hepsor AS reported a weakening of its financial performance for the twelve-month period ending December 31, 2025, compared with the previous year. Consolidated revenue decreased to 35.4 million euros from 38.4 million euros in 2024. Net profit declined to 1.0 million euros from 2.1 million euros, while net profit attributable to the owners of the parent company remained relatively stable at 0.4 million euros. For the fourth quarter of 2025, consolidated revenue dropped to 4.5 million euros from 10.5 million euros in the fourth quarter of 2024, and the company recorded a net loss of 0.5 million euros compared with a net profit of 1.0 million euros in the prior-year period. Net loss attributable to the owners of the parent company for the fourth quarter was 0.5 million euros, compared with a net profit of 0.6 million euros a year earlier. 

The primary factor affecting the Group's revenue and profitability during 2025 was a decrease in the number of apartments handed over to customers, totaling 141 units compared with 194 units in 2024. Profitability was further impacted by lower gross profit margins on development projects sold and an increase in marketing expenses, which rose to 1.3 million euros from 0.9 million euros due to more intensive sales activities driven by a larger number and scale of projects on sale. Gross profit for 2025 decreased to 5.6 million euros with a gross profit margin of 15.9%, compared with 6.8 million euros and 17.6% in 2024. Operating profit for the year fell to 3.4 million euros, representing an operating profit margin of 9.5%, down from 4.3 million euros and 11.3% in the previous year. 

Geographically, revenue generated in Estonia accounted for 26.1 million euros, or 73.7% of total sales revenue, while revenue generated in Latvia increased to 9.3 million euros, representing 26.3% of the total. In addition to residential sales, the Group generated other revenue of 2.2 million euros. 

Total assets increased by 3.4% over the year to 91.8 million euros as at December 31, 2025. Inventories stood at 58.9 million euros, accounting for 64.2% of total assets, and decreased from 64.1 million euros at the end of 2024 due to apartment handovers, land plot sales, and the reclassification of certain properties to investment property. Cash and cash equivalents decreased to 3.8 million euros from 6.2 million euros. Total interest-bearing loan liabilities decreased to 47.7 million euros from 54.7 million euros, bringing the debt ratio down to 52.0% from 61.6%. The adjusted debt ratio stood at 37.0%. During November 2025, the company successfully completed a public bond offering, raising 8.0 million euros at a fixed annual interest rate of 9.50%. Total equity grew by 23.0% to 27.1 million euros, resulting in an equity ratio of 29.5%, primarily driven by non-cash contributions to the voluntary reserve of a subsidiary. Operating cash flow for the twelve-month period was positive at 14.3 million euros.
