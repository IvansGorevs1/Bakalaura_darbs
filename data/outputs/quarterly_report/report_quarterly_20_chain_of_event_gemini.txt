For the twelve months of the 2024/2025 financial year ending June 30, 2025, SAF Tehnika reported an unaudited consolidated net turnover of EUR 26.11 million, representing a 4% decline compared to EUR 27.09 million in the previous financial year. The Group closed the twelve-month period with a net loss of EUR 820 thousand, which shows an improvement compared to the net loss of EUR 2.37 million reported for the 2023/2024 financial year. For the fourth quarter of the 2024/2025 financial year, the Group generated an unaudited net turnover of EUR 5.18 million, down 13% compared to EUR 5.98 million in the corresponding period of the prior year, and recorded a quarterly net loss of EUR 913 thousand compared to a loss of EUR 498 thousand in the fourth quarter of the 2023/2024 financial year. 

Geographically, revenues from North and Latin America accounted for 55% of the total 12-month turnover, amounting to EUR 14.28 million and decreasing by 14% compared to the previous financial year. European revenues grew by 17% to EUR 10.04 million, representing 38% of the total turnover, while revenues from Asia, Africa, and the Middle East declined by 5% to EUR 1.78 million, accounting for 7% of total turnover. During the fourth quarter specifically, regional performance showed a 40% decrease in the Americas to EUR 2.5 million, a 24% increase in Europe to EUR 1.9 million, and a doubling of turnover in Asia, Africa, and the Middle East to EUR 846 thousand compared to the fourth quarter of the prior year. The Group noted that quarterly turnover fluctuations are influenced by the completion, scope, complexity, production duration, and material procurement lead times of individual projects, which frequently result in revenue recognition occurring in subsequent quarters. 

Total assets at the end of the reporting period stood at EUR 25.92 million, compared to EUR 23.02 million as of June 30, 2024. Total owners equity decreased to EUR 15.58 million from EUR 16.44 million. The Group maintained a strong cash position with a net cash flow for the 12 months of EUR 2.6 million, bringing the cash and bank balance to EUR 4.81 million at the end of the period, up from EUR 2.21 million a year earlier. Customer prepayments for goods and services increased significantly to EUR 3.92 million from EUR 759 thousand, and total inventories rose by 8% to EUR 13.94 million. To support liquidity, the Parent Company retained an active, unutilized credit line agreement with Luminor Bank AS for EUR 4.95 million. Total investments in fixed assets during the fourth quarter amounted to EUR 239 thousand, directed primarily toward production, testing processes, and office equipment.
