AS Silvano Fashion Group reported consolidated revenue of 28,590 thousand EUR for the first six months of 2026, representing a modest 0.6% increase compared to 28,428 thousand EUR in the same period of 2025. Despite the slight growth in top-line sales, the Group’s profitability metrics declined significantly. Gross profit rose by 5.3% to 15,892 thousand EUR, driven by a 4.8% decrease in the cost of goods sold. This improvement in cost efficiency resulted in an expanded gross margin of 55.6%, up from 53.1% in the prior year. However, operating profit decreased by 3.5% to 5,751 thousand EUR, reflecting higher distribution and administrative expenses that offset the gains in gross margin. Consequently, the operating profit margin contracted to 20.1% from 21.0%. EBITDA also saw a slight decline of 0.8%, settling at 7,508 thousand EUR with a margin of 26.3%.

The most substantial deterioration occurred in net profit. Consolidated net profit for the period amounted to 4,321 thousand EUR, a sharp 31.2% drop from 6,279 thousand EUR in the previous year. Net profit attributable to equity holders of the Parent company fell by 31.5% to 3,961 thousand EUR, leading to a decrease in earnings per share from 0.16 EUR to 0.11 EUR. This decline in net profitability was primarily influenced by changes in net finance income, which dropped from 1,652 thousand EUR to 444 thousand EUR, largely due to lower currency exchange income compared to the prior year.

Geographically, the Group’s performance was mixed. Sales in the key market of Russia decreased by 0.6% to 15,767 thousand EUR, with local currency sales falling by 7.8%. In contrast, sales in Belarus increased by 6.4% to 9,046 thousand EUR, supported by a 2.7% rise in local currency sales. Sales in Ukraine plummeted by 58.3% to 15 thousand EUR, while the Baltics and other markets also experienced declines. Structurally, the retail segment demonstrated strong growth, increasing by 13.6% to 13,548 thousand EUR and accounting for 47.4% of total revenue. Wholesale revenue, however, declined by 9.1% to 14,934 thousand EUR, reducing its share of total sales to 52.2%.

Financially, the Group maintained a robust liquidity position. Cash and cash equivalents nearly doubled, increasing by 87.4% to 12,443 thousand EUR, while operating cash flow surged by 96.6% to 6,842 thousand EUR. Total assets grew by 5.1% to 98,603 thousand EUR, and equity attributable to equity holders increased by 6.1% to 81,479 thousand EUR. Return on assets and return on equity declined to 7.8% and 9.5% respectively, reflecting the lower net income relative to the asset and equity base. The Group ended the period with 1,594 employees and operated 135 own stores, a slight reduction from 137 at the end of 2025.
