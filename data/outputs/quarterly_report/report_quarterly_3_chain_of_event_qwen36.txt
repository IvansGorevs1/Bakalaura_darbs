AS Amber Latvijas balzams reported a significant deterioration in financial performance for the first three months of 2026, resulting in a net loss of EUR 1.4 million compared to a net profit of EUR 0.9 million in the same period of 2025. The company’s unaudited net turnover decreased by 29.6% to EUR 11.1 million, driven primarily by a sharp decline in sales volume, which fell 36.1% to 517,600 nine-liter cases. Gross profit also contracted substantially, dropping 66.5% by EUR 2.3 million to EUR 1.2 million, a decline attributed to changes in product mix and pricing methodology adjustments that offset cost reductions. Consequently, the operating result shifted from a profit of EUR 0.6 million in 2025 to an operating loss of EUR 1.6 million in 2026, with the operating margin turning negative at -14.5%.

The severe financial decline was precipitated by a combination of geopolitical and operational crises. The company lost control over its largest customer, Stoli Group (USA), LLC, following the appointment of a trustee during the latter’s Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceedings in the United States. This event critically impacted the company’s solvency and cash flow. Additionally, the company faced aggressive actions from Russian authorities, including the confiscation of the Group-owned Talvis distillery, following public support for Ukraine. These external shocks, combined with a global downturn in the spirits market characterized by declining sales volumes and inventory adjustments, created significant cash flow difficulties. The situation escalated when the State Revenue Service froze the company’s bank accounts due to delayed excise tax payments, forcing AS Amber Latvijas balzams to file for legal protection proceedings in the Riga City Court in February 2026. This legal protection aims to restructure liabilities and stabilize operations, with a court-approved deadline of October 2026 for submitting an action plan.

The initiation of legal protection proceedings also triggered an event of default for the principal shareholder, Amber Beverage Group Holding S.à r.l., under its bond documentation, subsequently affecting its financing arrangements. In response to these challenges, the company is implementing measures to increase production efficiency and reduce costs. While operational quality metrics improved, with the Right First Time rate rising to 99.5%, the On Time In Full delivery rate dropped significantly to 70.3% from 97.5% in the prior year. The company continues to pay substantial taxes, including EUR 6.4 million in excise duties, while navigating a complex restructuring environment involving its parent company’s judicial reorganization in Luxembourg. Management remains focused on maintaining business continuity and improving financial stability through governance enhancements and internal process audits.
