Infortar Group reported significant improvements in profitability for the first half of 2026, despite a decline in total revenue. The group’s consolidated revenue decreased by 0.6% to EUR 945.9 million, down from EUR 951.9 million in the same period of 2025. This reduction was primarily driven by the energy segment, where Elenger Grupp’s gas and electricity sales volumes fell by 34% to 3.3 TWh, largely due to a decrease in wholesale sales. However, the group’s operational performance improved markedly. EBITDA increased by 30.8% to EUR 111.2 million, up from EUR 85.1 million in the first half of 2025. Operating profit, or EBIT, nearly doubled, rising by 93.5% to EUR 51.1 million from EUR 26.4 million. This turnaround was supported by stronger performance across the energy segment, the addition of new agricultural units, and the closure of unprofitable operations. The most significant improvement occurred in the supporting businesses segment, which shifted from a loss of EUR 4.4 million to a profit of EUR 5.6 million in EBITDA terms.

In the maritime transport segment, revenue remained stable at EUR 207.0 million in the second quarter, unchanged from the prior year. However, EBITDA declined slightly to EUR 34.3 million from EUR 37.4 million. The segment faced headwinds from heightened geopolitical tensions in the Middle East, which increased fuel costs by EUR 8.4 million year-on-year. Additionally, the share of emissions to be surrendered under the EU Emissions Trading System increased to 100% in 2026. Passenger numbers decreased by 2.2% to 1,454,725, while cargo units increased by 2.9%. The energy segment’s revenue dropped significantly in the second quarter due to lower consumption and wholesale sales, with natural gas consumption in the Finnish-Baltic region falling by 7%. Despite this, the energy segment contributed to the overall EBITDA growth through improved operational efficiency and the stabilization provided by network operations, which are less seasonal than trading activities.

The group’s financial position remained robust. Total equity increased to EUR 1,200.5 million, and the equity ratio stood at 44.2%. Net debt decreased to EUR 829.9 million, resulting in a net debt to EBITDA ratio of 3.3, down from 3.7 at the end of 2025. Cash and cash equivalents rose to EUR 253.2 million, reflecting effective cash flow management and the maintenance of a liquidity buffer to finance new investments. The group invested approximately EUR 50 million in the first half of the year, including EUR 7.3 million in the maritime transport segment. Key developments included the completion of the Halinga biomethane plant, which integrates agriculture and energy production, and the continued construction of major infrastructure projects such as the Rail Baltica mainline. Infortar also increased its ownership in Tallink Grupp to 68.76% and announced a share buyback program. The group’s diversified portfolio across maritime, energy, real estate, and supporting sectors helped mitigate risks associated with market volatility and geopolitical uncertainty.
