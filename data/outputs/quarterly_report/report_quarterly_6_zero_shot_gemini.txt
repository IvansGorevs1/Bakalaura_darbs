During the first six months of 2026, Aktsiaselts Infortar and its subsidiaries demonstrated improved profitability despite stable revenue levels. For the six months ended June 30, 2026, the group reported total revenue of EUR 945,917 thousand, representing a slight decrease compared to EUR 951,869 thousand for the same period in 2025. Gross profit increased significantly to EUR 114,132 thousand from EUR 81,736 thousand. EBITDA rose by 31% to EUR 111,228 thousand, up from EUR 85,051 thousand in the first half of 2025, driven by contributions across all business segments and a notable turnaround in supporting businesses where EBITDA improved from a loss of EUR 4,400 thousand to a profit of EUR 5,600 thousand. Operating profit reached EUR 51,068 thousand, nearly doubling the EUR 26,383 thousand reported in the previous year. Profit before tax grew to EUR 27,195 thousand compared to EUR 2,695 thousand, while net profit for the six-month period turned positive at EUR 7,201 thousand, recovering from a net loss of EUR 14,411 thousand in the first half of 2025. Net profit attributable to the owners of the parent company amounted to EUR 15,163 thousand, compared to a net loss of EUR 2,549 thousand a year earlier. Basic earnings per share for the six months stood at EUR 0.74, compared to negative EUR 0.12 in the prior period. For the second quarter of 2026 specifically, revenue was EUR 440,921 thousand compared to EUR 504,512 thousand in the second quarter of 2025, while net profit for the quarter was EUR 1,883 thousand compared to EUR 150 thousand a year earlier. The group financial position as of June 30, 2026, showed total assets of EUR 2,714,309 thousand, up from EUR 2,596,607 thousand at the end of 2025. Total equity reached EUR 1,200,533 thousand compared to EUR 1,175,404 thousand, with an equity ratio of 44.2%. Interest-bearing liabilities stood at EUR 1,083,091 thousand, and cash and cash equivalents increased to EUR 253,187 thousand from EUR 146,368 thousand at June 30, 2025. Significant changes compared to the previous period included a reduction in revenue within the energy segment, where natural gas and electricity sales volumes decreased due to lower wholesale sales, alongside an increase in natural gas inventory stored to EUR 108,719 thousand from EUR 26,027 thousand at the end of 2025. Business performance was affected by several external factors, including heightened geopolitical tensions in the Middle East that increased fuel costs and energy market volatility, weak market demand driven by low consumer and business confidence, and regulatory changes such as the European Union Emissions Trading System increasing the share of CO2 emissions to be surrendered to 100%. Operational developments included Tallink Grupp carrying 1,454,725 passengers in the second quarter, a 2.2% decrease year-on-year, and chartering out the passenger vessel Superfast IX. Elenger Group delivered five LNG cargoes in the second quarter, and advanced its battery energy storage systems with facilities in Latvia entering commercial operation or grid testing. Infortar also increased its ownership in Tallink Grupp to 68.76% and completed the construction of the Halinga biomethane plant. Overall, the group maintained a strong balance sheet and stable core operations, allowing it to navigate volatile economic and geopolitical conditions while advancing strategic investments across its maritime transport, energy, real estate, and supporting business segments.
