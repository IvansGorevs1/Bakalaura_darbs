Arco Vara reported a marked improvement in financial performance in the first quarter of 2025 as Estonia’s residential real estate market showed moderate signs of recovery. Revenue increased to €1.706 million from €0.990 million in Q1 2024, an increase of €716 thousand, or approximately 72%. Revenue from the sale of own real estate rose to €1.589 million from €927 thousand, while service revenue increased to €117 thousand from €63 thousand, supported mainly by higher franchise fees and rental income.

The group returned to profitability. Operating profit was €259 thousand, compared with an operating loss of €208 thousand in the prior-year quarter. Net profit was €103 thousand, or €0.01 per share, versus a net loss of €349 thousand and a loss per share of €0.03 in Q1 2024. The improvement was primarily reflected in the Estonian development segment, which generated €1.589 million of revenue and €320 thousand of operating profit, compared with an operating loss of €128 thousand a year earlier. Bulgarian development remained loss-making, with an operating loss of €76 thousand, while the Estonian construction segment recorded a €46 thousand operating loss.

Gross profit increased to €598 thousand from €173 thousand despite higher cost of sales of €1.108 million. Marketing expenses were broadly stable at €69 thousand, and administrative expenses declined to €270 thousand from €297 thousand. Finance costs remained high at €143 thousand, compared with €141 thousand in Q1 2024, including €147 thousand of interest expense.

Operating cash flow remained negative at €2.838 million, compared with negative €2.217 million in the previous year, reflecting cash investment in development activities. Financing activities generated €2.231 million, mainly through €3.090 million of new borrowings, resulting in a net cash outflow of €607 thousand. Cash and cash equivalents decreased from €1.720 million at year-end 2024 to €1.113 million at 31 March 2025.

Total assets rose to €42.728 million from €39.568 million at year-end, largely because inventories increased to €32.329 million following property acquisitions and development investment. Total interest-bearing liabilities increased to €16.568 million from €15.215 million, and net loans rose to €15.455 million. The weighted average interest rate declined to 8.2% as Euribor decreased. Equity increased slightly to €19.842 million, although the equity ratio declined to 46.4% from 49.9% at year-end.

Operationally, four apartments were sold during the quarter. Rannakalda reached 91 of 113 apartments sold, while Botanica Lozen was 95% complete, with nine of 16 houses pre-sold. Arco Vara also acquired the Spordi Street development site in Tallinn, a planned 56-apartment project with total investment of €15 million. Pre-sales are expected to begin in Q2 2025, alongside planned construction starts for Spordi Street and Soodi 6. Overall, the quarter combined improved profitability with continued investment and higher leverage to support the group’s development pipeline.
