AS Trigon Property Development reported its financial results for the fourth quarter and the 12-month period ending December 31, 2025. During the 12-month period of 2025, the company experienced a decline in financial performance, recording a net loss of 99,507 euros compared to a net profit of 167,409 euros reported for the 12 months of 2024. Consequently, basic and diluted earnings per share dropped to -0.02212 euros for the 12-month period of 2025, down from 0.03721 euros in the previous year. For the fourth quarter of 2025 alone, the company posted a net loss of 23,333 euros, representing a decline compared to the net profit of 154,215 euros generated in the fourth quarter of 2024.

The primary driver of the weaker financial results was the absence of real estate sales during the 12-month period of 2025. In contrast, the performance in the comparative 2024 period was supported by real estate transactions, including the sale of a 0.09-hectare transportation purpose land for 31,920 euros in the third quarter of 2024 and the sale of a 0.70-hectare property for 300,000 euros in the fourth quarter of 2024. As a result, gross profit for the 12-month period fell from 220,299 euros in 2024 to zero in 2025. 

Operating expenses also contributed to the overall loss for the period. Administrative and general expenses increased to 108,021 euros for the 12 months of 2025, compared to 63,650 euros for the 12 months of 2024. This increase was partly influenced by personnel expenses of 41,452 euros paid as remuneration to members of the Management Board and Supervisory Board, whereas zero remuneration was paid to board members in 2024. Financial income partially offset expenses, rising to 8,513 euros for the 12-month period of 2025 from 5,760 euros in the prior year.

The company's total assets decreased to 1,804,656 euros as of December 31, 2025, compared to 1,873,680 euros at the end of 2024. Cash balances reduced from 370,856 euros at the close of 2024 to 227,911 euros as of December 31, 2025, with total cash flows used in operating activities amounting to 142,945 euros for the 12-month period of 2025. Conversely, inventories increased from 1,471,238 euros to 1,562,500 euros over the same timeframe, driven by investments totaling 91,262 euros made into the company's real estate development project in Pärnu, Estonia. Total liabilities increased to 49,389 euros at the end of 2025 from 18,906 euros at the end of 2024, leading to a debt ratio of 2.74 percent compared to 1.01 percent previously. Total equity decreased from 1,854,774 euros to 1,755,267 euros at the end of 2025, and the book value of the share declined from 0.41 euros to 0.39 euros. The closing price of the company's share on the Nasdaq Tallinn stock exchange rose slightly from 0.620 euros at the end of 2024 to 0.634 euros at the end of 2025.
