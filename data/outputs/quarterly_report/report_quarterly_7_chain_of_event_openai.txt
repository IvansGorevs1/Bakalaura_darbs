AS Silvano Fashion Group’s revenue for the six months ended 30 June 2026 increased 0.6% to €28.590 million from €28.428 million a year earlier. Performance was supported by a significant shift toward retail: retail revenue rose 13.6% to €13.548 million and represented 47.4% of total sales, while wholesale revenue declined 9.1% to €14.934 million. Gross profit increased 5.3% to €15.892 million, and gross margin improved to 55.6% from 53.1%, partly reflecting a 4.8% reduction in cost of goods sold.

Despite the stronger gross result, operating profit decreased 3.5% to €5.751 million, with operating margin falling to 20.1% from 21.0%. EBITDA declined 0.8% to €7.508 million, and its margin edged down to 26.3% from 26.6%. The largest deterioration was in net earnings: consolidated net profit fell 31.2% to €4.321 million, while profit attributable to the parent’s equity holders decreased 31.5% to €3.961 million. Earnings per share declined from €0.16 to €0.11, and the attributable net profit margin fell from 20.3% to 13.9%.

The decline in net profit occurred despite profit before tax decreasing less sharply, from €7.601 million to €6.191 million. Net finance income fell to €444 thousand from €1.652 million in the prior-year period, mainly because the previous period included substantially higher currency exchange income. Income tax expense also increased to €1.870 million from €1.322 million. In the second quarter alone, revenue rose 2.2% to €16.774 million, but operating profit fell 10.9% to €4.060 million and net profit attributable to parent shareholders was broadly stable at €3.048 million.

Russia remained the largest market, accounting for 55.1% of sales, although revenue declined 0.6% in euros and 7.8% in local currency. Belarus sales increased 6.4% to €9.046 million, or 2.7% in local currency, partly offsetting the Russian decline. The Group continued to focus on improving Russian retail profitability and operated 67 own stores there at period-end, compared with 69 at year-end 2025. Across the network, directly operated stores decreased from 137 to 135, while total own and franchise stores numbered 547.

Operating cash flow nearly doubled to €6.842 million from €3.481 million. Cash and cash equivalents rose 87.4% to €12.443 million, while total assets increased 5.1% to €98.603 million and parent-attributable equity rose 6.1% to €81.479 million. The 2025 net profit of €9.229 million was retained rather than distributed as dividends.
