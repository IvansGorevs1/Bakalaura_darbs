AB KN Energies reported a significant improvement in financial performance for the three months ended 31 March 2026, driven by robust growth in revenue and substantial gains in profitability compared to the same period in 2025. The Group’s revenue from contracts with customers increased by approximately 17.7%, rising from EUR 25,326 thousand in the prior year period to EUR 29,823 thousand. This growth was primarily attributable to a strong increase in income from liquefied natural gas terminal regasification services, which climbed to EUR 19,162 thousand from EUR 14,593 thousand. Additionally, sales of liquid energy products transshipment services grew to EUR 7,596 thousand, up from EUR 6,615 thousand, while consulting services also saw an increase.

Profitability metrics improved markedly. Gross profit expanded by 31.8% to EUR 12,907 thousand, compared to EUR 9,793 thousand in the previous year. Operating profit surged by 34.6% to EUR 9,608 thousand, up from EUR 7,141 thousand. This acceleration in operating performance was supported by higher revenues, although operating expenses also rose slightly. Distribution costs and administrative expenses increased by 10.5% to EUR 3,316 thousand, largely due to higher wages, salaries, and social security costs, as well as increased depreciation and amortization. Cost of sales also grew by 8.9% to EUR 16,916 thousand, reflecting higher environmental taxes and repair and maintenance expenses.

A key driver of the improved bottom line was a significant reduction in net finance costs. Net finance costs decreased by 55.9% to EUR 784 thousand, down from EUR 1,777 thousand in the prior period. This improvement was primarily due to a EUR 458 thousand net gain on derivatives recognized in the current period, which had no equivalent in the previous year, alongside lower interest expenses on borrowings. Consequently, profit before income tax increased by 64.5% to EUR 8,824 thousand from EUR 5,364 thousand. After accounting for income tax expenses of EUR 1,577 thousand, the Group’s net profit for the period reached EUR 7,247 thousand, a substantial increase from EUR 4,426 thousand in the prior year. Earnings per share rose to EUR 0.02 from EUR 0.01.

Cash flow from operating activities strengthened considerably, reaching EUR 27,917 thousand compared to EUR 13,758 thousand in the prior period. This was driven by higher net profit and favorable changes in working capital, particularly a decrease in trade receivables. The Group’s total assets stood at EUR 556,798 thousand, with total equity increasing to EUR 179,866 thousand. Total borrowings decreased slightly to EUR 302,069 thousand, reflecting ongoing repayment of loans from the Nordic Investment Bank and the European Investment Bank. The company also maintained a healthy cash position, with cash and cash equivalents totaling EUR 17,986 thousand. Overall, the reporting period was characterized by strong operational growth, improved cost management, and a favorable financial environment that significantly boosted net income.
