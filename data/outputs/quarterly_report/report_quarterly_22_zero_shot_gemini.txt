During the first six months of 2026, Telia Lietuva demonstrated solid financial and operational performance. Total revenue for H1 2026 grew by 7.2 per cent to reach EUR 259.1 million, up from EUR 241.7 million in the corresponding period of 2025. Revenue growth was supported by increases across all major segments, with fixed services rising by 5.3 per cent to EUR 112.9 million, mobile services increasing by 8.3 per cent to EUR 100.0 million, and equipment sales growing by 9.6 per cent to EUR 46.1 million. For the second quarter alone, total revenue rose by 6.9 per cent to EUR 130.3 million. Profitability indicators showed strong double-digit growth. Operating profit (EBIT) for the first half of 2026 increased by 15.1 per cent to EUR 62.4 million, while adjusted EBIT excluding non-recurring items grew by 16.3 per cent to EUR 63.9 million. EBITDA for H1 2026 increased by 9.5 per cent to EUR 105.5 million, and adjusted EBITDA excluding non-recurring items rose by 10.2 per cent to EUR 106.9 million, surpassing the EUR 100 million threshold for the first time with an adjusted EBITDA margin of 41.3 per cent compared to 40.1 per cent a year ago. Profit before income tax for the first six months of 2026 went up by 16.6 per cent to EUR 61.3 million, and net profit for the period increased by 14.7 per cent to EUR 51.3 million. Basic and diluted earnings per share for H1 2026 were EUR 0.088, compared to EUR 0.077 in H1 2025. 

Operating expenses excluding non-recurring items for H1 2026 increased by 10.2 per cent to EUR 67.3 million, driven primarily by a 22.3 per cent increase in other operating expenses due to higher energy costs, external IT services, and common Group function expenses. Employee-related operating expenses remained virtually flat at EUR 33.3 million. Total non-recurring restructuring expenses for the first half of the year amounted to EUR 1.4 million. Cash flow generation remained robust, with net cash generated by operating activities increasing by 15.0 per cent to EUR 103.1 million, and free cash flow growing by 12.5 per cent to EUR 64.1 million. Capital investments (Capex) surged by 57.9 per cent to EUR 37.5 million for H1 2026, up from EUR 23.7 million in H1 2025, driven by investments in IT systems and infrastructure, mobile network expansion, and the acquisition of 5G spectrum licenses. 

Operationally, the company recorded growth in mobile subscriptions, which totaled 1.73 million at the end of June 2026, representing a 3.3 per cent increase year-on-year, driven by a 4.2 per cent growth in post-paid subscriptions. Broadband internet connections stood at 405 thousand, TV service customers reached 254 thousand, and fixed telephone lines decreased by 10.9 per cent to 131 thousand. The total number of full-time employees decreased by 6.5 per cent to 1,524. Following the Annual General Meeting held on 24 April 2026, the company distributed a record-high dividend of EUR 0.14 per share, totaling EUR 81.6 million for the financial year 2025, paid out in May 2026. As of 30 June 2026, total assets stood at EUR 623.2 million, shareholders equity amounted to EUR 372.0 million, and the company had a negative net debt of EUR 4.3 million with no outstanding bank loans.
