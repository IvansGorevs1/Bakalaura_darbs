AB Pieno Žvaigždės reported a significant improvement in profitability for the first three months of 2026 compared with the same period in 2025, despite a slight decrease in overall revenue. Total sales for the three-month period ended March 31, 2026, were 52,064 thousand euros, representing a minor decline of approximately 0.5% from 52,324 thousand euros reported in the first quarter of 2025. 

In contrast to the stable top-line figures, profitability metrics experienced substantial growth. Gross profit surged by nearly 43% to 13,986 thousand euros, up from 9,794 thousand euros in the previous year, driven by a reduction in the cost of sales from 42,530 thousand euros down to 38,078 thousand euros. Operating performance also strengthened considerably, with EBITDA more than doubling to 4,730 thousand euros compared with 2,001 thousand euros in the prior-year period. Operating profit before finance costs rose sharply to 3,366 thousand euros from 535 thousand euros. 

Consequently, the company's net profit for the first three months of 2026 reached 3,068 thousand euros, showing a dramatic increase from 227 thousand euros reported for the first three months of 2025. Basic earnings per share correspondingly improved to 0.07 euros, compared with 0.01 euros in the previous year. 

Geographically and by product lines, revenue remained relatively steady. Domestic sales in Lithuania decreased slightly to 31,405 thousand euros from 32,112 thousand euros, while sales in other European Union countries increased to 11,341 thousand euros from 10,801 thousand euros. Sales in non-European Union countries edged down slightly to 9,318 thousand euros from 9,410 thousand euros. Among product categories, fresh milk products generated 42,497 thousand euros, cheese contributed 5,276 thousand euros, dry milk products accounted for 1,397 thousand euros, and other products totaled 2,894 thousand euros. 

The company's financial position remained robust at the end of the reporting period. Total assets increased to 85,305 thousand euros as of March 31, 2026, compared with 83,475 thousand euros at the end of 2025. Total equity grew to 41,164 thousand euros from 38,238 thousand euros over the same timeframe, supported by the strong quarterly net earnings. Total liabilities decreased slightly to 44,141 thousand euros from 45,237 thousand euros. Net cash flows from operating activities also showed a positive development, rising to 1,625 thousand euros compared with a negative cash flow of 183 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2025.
