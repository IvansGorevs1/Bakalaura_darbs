Infortar Group reported mixed financial results for the first half and second quarter of 2026. For the six months ended 30 June 2026, consolidated revenue reached 945.9 million euros, compared with 951.9 million euros in the corresponding period of the previous year. For the second quarter of 2026, revenue was 440.9 million euros, down from 504.5 million euros a year earlier. The lower revenue figures were primarily driven by a decline in the energy segment, where lower wholesale sales affected overall energy sales volumes. Despite the slight decrease in revenue, the company achieved improved profitability across several key indicators. Gross profit for the six-month period rose significantly to 114.1 million euros from 81.7 million euros in the prior-year period. Second-quarter gross profit also improved to 59.4 million euros compared with 55.7 million euros a year ago. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization for the first six months increased to 111.2 million euros, up from 85.1 million euros in the first half of 2025. Second-quarter EBITDA rose to 64.2 million euros from 57.4 million euros previously, lifting the EBITDA margin to 14.6% from 11.4%. Operating profit for the six-month period doubled to 51.1 million euros compared with 26.4 million euros in the prior year, while second-quarter operating profit reached 29.7 million euros versus 27.0 million euros in the second quarter of 2025. Profit before tax for the first six months improved substantially to 27.2 million euros from 2.7 million euros a year earlier. Net profit for the six-month period turned positive at 7.2 million euros, recovering from a net loss of 14.4 million euros in the first half of 2025. Net profit attributable to the owners of the parent for the six-month period reached 15.2 million euros compared with a net loss of 2.5 million euros previously. For the second quarter alone, net profit stood at 1.9 million euros, with 2.3 million euros attributable to the owners of the parent. Corporate income tax expenses remained high at 20.0 million euros for the six-month period, reflecting income tax on dividends recorded during the reporting window. As of 30 June 2026, Infortar Group held total assets of 2,714.3 million euros and total equity of 1,200.5 million euros, with an equity ratio of 44.2%. Interest-bearing liabilities stood at 1,083.1 million euros, and cash and cash equivalents totaled 253.2 million euros. The group's performance during the period was supported by stronger results in the energy segment, the expansion of agricultural units, the closure of unprofitable operations, and a notable turnaround in supporting businesses.
