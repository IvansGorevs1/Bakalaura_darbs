Hepsor AS reported consolidated revenue of 35.4 million euros for the twelve months of 2025, representing a decrease compared to 38.4 million euros in 2024. Consolidated net profit for 2025 amounted to 1.0 million euros, down from 2.1 million euros in the previous year, with net profit attributable to the owners of the parent company remaining steady at 0.4 million euros for both 2025 and 2024. For the fourth quarter of 2025, consolidated revenue reached 4.5 million euros, declining from 10.5 million euros in the fourth quarter of 2024, and the Group recorded a net loss of 0.5 million euros compared to a net profit of 1.0 million euros in the same period of the prior year. The net loss attributable to the owners of the parent company in the fourth quarter of 2025 was 0.5 million euros, compared to a net profit of 0.6 million euros in the fourth quarter of 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share were 0.10 euros for the full year 2025, compared to 0.11 euros in 2024, and stood at -0.14 euros for the fourth quarter of 2025, compared to 0.15 euros for the fourth quarter of 2024.

Operating profit for 2025 was 3.4 million euros, down from 4.3 million euros in 2024, while gross profit declined to 5.6 million euros from 6.8 million euros. The gross profit margin was 15.9% in 2025 compared to 17.6% in 2024, and the operating profit margin decreased to 9.5% from 11.3%. Total assets as of 31 December 2025 increased to 91.8 million euros from 88.8 million euros at the end of 2024. Total liabilities decreased to 64.8 million euros from 66.8 million euros, while total interest-bearing loan liabilities declined to 47.7 million euros from 54.7 million euros. Total equity rose by 23.0% to 27.1 million euros as of 31 December 2025, up from 22.0 million euros at the end of 2024.

The financial performance and changes during the reporting period were directly influenced by several business developments and operational factors explicitly reported by the company. The decrease in revenue and profitability for 2025 was driven by a lower number of apartments handed over to customers, totaling 141 homes compared to 194 homes in 2024. Profitability was further impacted by lower gross profit margins on development projects sold and an increase in marketing expenses to 1.3 million euros from 0.9 million euros, which resulted from growth in the number and scale of projects on sale. Conversely, performance was supported by other operating income of 1.2 million euros compared to 0.4 million euros in 2024, driven by the reclassification and fair value revaluation of properties transferred to investment property, as well as an increase in interest income.

Significant corporate and financial events during 2025 included the successful completion of the first series of a public bond offering in November 2025, where Hepsor raised 8.0 million euros with a fixed annual interest rate of 9.50% after strong investor demand led to an increase from the initial 6 million euro volume. The Group also expanded its development portfolio by adding six residential projects, including the Manufaktuuri 3 property in Tallinn, and commenced construction on four new residential development projects. Additionally, strategic partnerships were formed, such as the establishment of a joint venture with the EfTEN Special Opportunities Fund for a development project in Lasnamäe and a cooperation agreement with Tolaram Group for the Manufaktuuri Quarter development phase.
