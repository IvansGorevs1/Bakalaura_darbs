AS Pro Kapital Grupp reported a significant turnaround in financial performance for the twelve months of 2025, transitioning from a net loss in the previous year to a robust net profit. Total revenue surged to EUR 53.2 million, a substantial increase from EUR 18.2 million in 2024. This growth was primarily driven by the completion and handover of residential units in key development projects, including the Kalaranna and Uus-Kindrali districts in Tallinn, as well as the City Villas project in Vilnius. Revenue recognition is closely tied to the construction cycle, with the timing of these completions marking a decisive shift from the lower sales activity of 2024, which was limited to remaining inventory in Latvia and Lithuania.

Profitability metrics improved markedly across the board. Gross profit rose to EUR 18.8 million from EUR 5.4 million the prior year, reflecting a gross margin expansion from 30% to 35%. Operating profit increased dramatically to EUR 14.7 million, compared to a negligible EUR 0.1 million in 2024, resulting in an operating margin of 28%. The Group’s net profit for the full year was EUR 12.0 million, reversing the EUR 3.9 million loss recorded in 2024. Earnings per share improved to EUR 0.22 from EUR -0.06. The fourth quarter of 2025 also demonstrated strong momentum, with revenue of EUR 18.9 million and net profit of EUR 6.0 million.

Operational cash flow generation strengthened considerably, with cash generated from operating activities reaching EUR 10.8 million in 2025, compared to cash used of EUR 10.4 million in the previous year. This positive shift was supported by the conversion of customer advances into revenue upon property handovers. The Group’s balance sheet position also improved, with total equity increasing to EUR 63.3 million from EUR 51.2 million. Net assets per share rose to EUR 1.12, and the debt-to-equity ratio decreased from 1.32 to 0.97, indicating a more conservative and stable capital structure. Return on equity improved to 21.0% from -7.0%.

Strategic developments during the period included the partial redemption of senior secured callable fixed-rate bonds by EUR 9.4 million in August, reducing the outstanding nominal amount to EUR 10.5 million. The Group also initiated construction on the Blue Marine residential project in Riga’s Klīversala Quarter and advanced planning for new developments in Tallinn’s Kristne City. While hotel operations in Germany performed slightly below budget and Italian brokerage activities faced a subdued market, the core real estate development segment in the Baltics drove the overall success. The company’s focus on high-quality residential projects in premium locations has reinforced its market position and delivered sustainable growth.
