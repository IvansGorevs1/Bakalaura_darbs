VILVI Group reported strong revenue growth in the first quarter of 2026, although operating profitability weakened substantially. Consolidated revenue increased 22.4% year on year to EUR 86.97 million, supported by the acquisition of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB, higher sales of dry milk and fresh milk products, and growth across key markets. Revenue from the European Union rose 0.6% to EUR 42.52 million, while sales in Lithuania increased 72.9% to EUR 14.63 million and sales in other countries rose 46.6% to EUR 29.82 million. The European Union remained the largest market, but its share of total revenue declined from 59% to 49%.

EBITDA fell 50.2% to EUR 3.00 million, reducing the EBITDA margin from 8.5% to 3.5%. The report states that Q1 2026 EBITDA excludes EUR 5.5 million of preliminary negative goodwill related to the Marijampolės pieno konservai acquisition. Despite the lower EBITDA, net profit increased 25.7% to EUR 5.09 million, compared with EUR 4.05 million in Q1 2025, and the net profit margin improved slightly from 5.7% to 5.8%. Earnings per share rose 26.5% to EUR 0.43.

Performance varied significantly by operating segment. Cheese, cheese products and other sales declined 28.8% to EUR 37.64 million and represented 43% of revenue, compared with 75% a year earlier. Fresh milk product revenue increased 73.4% to EUR 12.75 million, while dry milk product revenue more than doubled, rising 126.9% to EUR 24.66 million. Canned milk contributed EUR 11.92 million, reflecting the consolidation of Marijampolės pieno konservai from 1 January 2026.

The Group purchased 155,954 tons of basic-indicator milk during the quarter, 54.2% more than in Q1 2025. The acquisition of Marijampolės pieno konservai was completed on 16 January, adding canned milk, butter and milk powder operations and plans for further whey-processing capacity expansion. VILVI also began commercial cheese production at its new Baltic Dairy Board factory in Latvia, which is planned to produce up to 18,000 tons annually for export to more than 40 countries.

Financial expansion increased leverage. Net financial debt rose 89.7% to EUR 83.43 million, while total assets increased from EUR 188.98 million at year-end 2025 to EUR 220.31 million at 31 March 2026. Operating cash flow remained negative at EUR 9.63 million, and cash declined to EUR 0.95 million, partly offset by EUR 15.84 million of net financing inflows.
