During the first six months of 2026, AS Merko Ehitus recorded consolidated revenue of EUR 149.9 million, representing a 10.7% decrease compared to EUR 167.9 million in the first six months of 2025. Revenue for the second quarter of 2026 was EUR 92.7 million, up from EUR 82.6 million in the second quarter of the previous year. Profitability declined across the reporting period. Pre-tax profit for the first six months of 2026 stood at EUR 10.8 million, down from EUR 23.5 million in the first half of 2025, bringing the six-month pre-tax profit margin to 7.2% compared to 14.0% in the prior-year period. Net profit attributable to shareholders for the first six months of 2026 was EUR 12.8 million, or EUR 0.73 per share, compared to EUR 21.7 million, or EUR 1.22 per share, in the first six months of 2025. For the second quarter of 2026, net profit attributable to shareholders was EUR 8.5 million compared to EUR 11.2 million in the second quarter of 2025. The six-month net profit margin was 8.6%, down from 12.9% a year earlier. Operating profit for the first six months of 2026 was EUR 8.4 million compared to EUR 18.6 million in the first six months of 2025. 

The company operates across two main business segments: construction service and real estate development. The construction service segment generated EUR 118.3 million in revenue during the first six months of 2026, a 1.5% decrease compared to EUR 120.1 million in the first half of 2025, and recorded an operating profit of EUR 3.0 million compared to EUR 8.8 million in the previous year. The real estate development segment generated EUR 31.6 million in revenue for the first six months of 2026, representing a 33.9% decrease from EUR 47.8 million in the first six months of 2025, and posted an operating profit of EUR 7.0 million compared to EUR 11.9 million in the prior-year period. During the first six months of 2026, the group sold 120 apartments and 5 commercial premises, earning EUR 27.6 million from the sale of own-developed apartments, compared to 222 apartments and 2 commercial premises generating EUR 44.9 million in the first six months of 2025. In the second quarter of 2026, apartment sales reached 84 units with EUR 20.6 million in revenue, compared to 101 units and EUR 20.2 million in revenue during the second quarter of 2025. 

A significant positive development during the period was the group's secured order book, which reached an all-time high of EUR 835.7 million as of June 30, 2026, marking an approximate 88% increase compared to EUR 443.8 million as of June 30, 2025. Group companies signed new construction contracts worth EUR 486.4 million in the first six months of 2026, compared to EUR 223.1 million in the first six months of 2025. The growth was largely driven by public sector projects, including Rail Baltica and defence-related infrastructure, such as the Rūdninkai defence force campus project whose contract entered into full force in early July with construction costs exceeding EUR 370 million across two phases. 

As of June 30, 2026, total assets stood at EUR 437.9 million, compared to EUR 403.5 million as of June 30, 2025. The group maintained a strong equity position of EUR 251.1 million, representing 57.4% of total assets, compared to EUR 242.3 million or 60.1% of total assets a year earlier. Cash and cash equivalents amounted to EUR 39.6 million at the end of June 2026, compared to EUR 25.9 million at the end of June 2026.
