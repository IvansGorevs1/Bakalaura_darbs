During the first six months of 2026, AS Tallinna Sadam recorded mixed financial results characterized by modest revenue growth accompanied by a decline in profitability. Total revenue increased by 2.4% to EUR 59.3 million, supported primarily by higher charter fee revenue from the icebreaker Botnica, increased electricity sales, and growth in ferry services. However, the Group experienced lower operating performance due to rising operating expenses, decreased cargo volumes, and fewer vessel calls. Operating profit fell by 25.7% to EUR 13.6 million, and profit for the period dropped by 41.7% to EUR 6.0 million. Adjusted EBITDA decreased by 13.5% to EUR 25.8 million compared with the same period of the previous year. 

The financial performance of the comparative period in 2025 was positively affected by one-off items, specifically the profit from the disposal of land for the Rail Baltica project and an insurance indemnity received for the icebreaker Botnica. These items did not recur in 2026, contributing to the year-on-year decline in profitability. Operating expenses rose significantly by 24.0% to EUR 20.2 million, driven largely by higher fuel costs due to increased prices and severe ice conditions, higher electricity costs resulting from new network tariffs and a security of supply fee, and increased maintenance expenses for non-current assets. Depreciation, amortisation, and impairment also increased by 6.0% to EUR 12.9 million, while personnel expenses grew by 4.7% to EUR 13.6 million. 

Operational volumes reflected a slight contraction across core segments. Total cargo volume handled by the Group harbours decreased by 5.0% to 6.5 million tonnes, driven primarily by a 33.8% decline in liquid bulk. The total number of passengers fell by 2.0% to 3.6 million, with decreases recorded on both the Tallinn-Helsinki and Tallinn-Stockholm routes, although cruise passenger numbers grew strongly by 47.8%. Vessel calls decreased by 4.8% to 3,376 calls. In contrast, the icebreaker Botnica achieved 130 contractual charter days, representing an 18.2% increase and a utility rate of 72%, supported by project-based work in the North Sea. 

Segment reporting indicated that revenue increased in all business areas except cargo harbours. Passenger harbours revenue rose by 2.9% to EUR 18.9 million, ferry segment revenue increased by 1.7% to EUR 18.6 million, and segment other revenue grew by nearly one fifth to EUR 5.9 million. Conversely, cargo harbours revenue declined by 2.5% to EUR 15.8 million. Adjusted EBITDA decreased across all segments compared with the previous year, with the largest reductions occurring in the cargo harbours and ferry segments. 

The Group maintained a solid financial position during the reporting period. Net finance costs decreased by 11.2% to EUR 2.3 million due to lower interest expenses resulting from reduced debt levels. Total loans and borrowings stood at EUR 173.4 million at the end of June 2026, virtually unchanged from the end of 2025. Investments totaled EUR 3.5 million for the six-month period, representing a significant decrease of 70.6% compared with EUR 12.0 million in the prior-year period. Total assets at the end of the period were EUR 615.0 million, and total equity stood at EUR 367.7 million. In May 2026, the company paid out dividends totaling EUR 19.2 million, translating to EUR 0.073 per share, unchanged from the previous year.
