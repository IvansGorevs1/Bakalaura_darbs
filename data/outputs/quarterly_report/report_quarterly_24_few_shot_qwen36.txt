AS Trigon Property Development reported a net loss for the twelve months ended December 31, 2025, marking a significant deterioration in financial performance compared to the previous year. The company recorded a net loss of 99,507 euros, reversing the net profit of 167,409 euros achieved in 2024. This shift was driven primarily by the absence of property sales in 2025, whereas the prior period benefited from the sale of two land plots. Consequently, the company generated zero gross profit in 2025, down from 220,299 euros in 2024.

Operating expenses increased substantially during the reporting period, reaching 108,021 euros compared to 63,650 euros in the previous year. The rise in administrative and general expenses was largely attributable to the introduction of personnel costs, which amounted to 41,452 euros in 2025. These costs represent remuneration for the Management Board and Supervisory Board, as the company had no other employees and incurred no labor costs in 2024. Additionally, auditing fees increased to 23,922 euros from 20,570 euros, and accounting service costs rose to 8,400 euros from 3,240 euros. Other general expenses also contributed to the increase, totaling 3,600 euros in 2025, whereas none were recorded in the prior year.

The company’s financial position remained stable despite the operating loss. Total assets decreased slightly to 1,804,656 euros from 1,873,680 euros in 2024. Inventories, which constitute the majority of the company's assets, increased to 1,562,500 euros from 1,471,238 euros, reflecting investments of 91,262 euros made into the development project in Pärnu. Equity declined to 1,755,267 euros from 1,854,774 euros, primarily due to the accumulation of the net loss. The debt ratio remained low at 2.74%, up from 1.01% in the previous year, with total liabilities standing at 49,389 euros.

Cash flows from operating activities used 142,945 euros in 2025, a stark contrast to the positive cash flow of 194,723 euros in 2024. This outflow was driven by changes in working capital, including an increase in inventories and a decrease in receivables. The cash balance at year-end was 227,911 euros, down from 370,856 euros. Earnings per share turned negative, recording a loss of 0.02212 euros per share, compared to a profit of 0.03721 euros in 2024. The company’s share price closed at 0.634 euros at the end of 2025, showing a modest increase from 0.620 euros in the previous year. The company continues to hold a single development project in Pärnu, focusing on selling land plots rather than developing rental properties, with no plans to change its business activity.
