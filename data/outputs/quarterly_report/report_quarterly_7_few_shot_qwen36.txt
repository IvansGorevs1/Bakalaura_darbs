AS Silvano Fashion Group reported a mixed financial performance for the first half of 2026, characterized by modest revenue growth and improved gross margins, contrasted by a significant decline in net profitability. Total revenue for the six-month period amounted to 28,590 thousand EUR, representing a slight increase of 0.6% compared to the same period in the previous year. This top-line stability was underpinned by a strong shift in sales channels, with retail revenue growing by 13.6% to 13,548 thousand EUR, while wholesale revenue declined by 9.1% to 14,934 thousand EUR. Consequently, the retail segment’s contribution to total sales rose to 47.4% from 41.9% in the prior year.

Profitability metrics presented a divergent picture. Gross profit increased by 5.3% to 15,892 thousand EUR, driven by a 4.8% reduction in the cost of goods sold. The gross margin expanded significantly to 55.6% from 53.1% in the previous year. However, operating profit decreased by 3.5% to 5,751 thousand EUR, resulting in an operating margin of 20.1%, down from 21.0%. Consolidated EBITDA also saw a marginal decline of 0.8% to 7,508 thousand EUR. The most substantial deterioration occurred in net profit, which fell by 31.2% to 4,321 thousand EUR. Net profit attributable to equity holders of the Parent company dropped to 3,961 thousand EUR, a decrease of 31.5% from the prior year’s 5,781 thousand EUR. Earnings per share decreased correspondingly to 0.11 EUR from 0.16 EUR. The decline in net profit was partly influenced by a significant drop in net finance income, which fell to 444 thousand EUR from 1,652 thousand EUR in the previous period, largely due to reduced currency exchange gains.

Geographically, the Group’s performance was heavily reliant on its two largest markets, Russia and Belarus, which together accounted for 86.7% of total sales. Sales in Russia totaled 15,767 thousand EUR, a decrease of 0.6% in EUR terms, although local currency sales declined by 7.8%. In Belarus, sales grew by 6.4% to 9,046 thousand EUR, with local currency sales increasing by 2.7%. Other markets showed weakness, with sales in Ukraine plummeting by 58.3% to 15 thousand EUR, and sales in the Baltics and other regions declining by 16.7% and 5.1% respectively.

The Group’s financial position remained robust, with total assets increasing by 5.1% to 98,603 thousand EUR. Equity attributable to equity holders of the Parent company grew by 6.1% to 81,479 thousand EUR. Liquidity improved markedly, with cash and cash equivalents nearly doubling to 12,443 thousand EUR from 6,639 thousand EUR at the end of 2025. Operating cash flow surged by 96.6% to 6,842 thousand EUR, reflecting efficient working capital management, evidenced by a decrease in inventories and current liabilities. Despite the drop in net income, the company maintained strong liquidity ratios, with a current ratio of 10.3 and a quick ratio of 6.7. The Group employed 1,594 people as of June 30, 2026, and continued to invest in its retail infrastructure, with capital expenditures on property, plant, and equipment totaling 115 thousand EUR for the period.
