Hepsor AS reported a decline in financial performance for the full year 2025, with consolidated revenue decreasing to 35.4 million euros from 38.4 million euros in the previous year. Net profit also fell to 1.0 million euros, down from 2.1 million euros in 2024, with net profit attributable to the owners of the parent company remaining flat at 0.4 million euros. The fourth quarter of 2025 was particularly weak, recording revenue of 4.5 million euros compared to 10.5 million euros in the same period of the prior year, and resulting in a net loss of 0.5 million euros. This deterioration was primarily driven by a reduction in the number of apartments handed over to customers, which dropped to 141 from 194 in 2024. Additionally, profitability was negatively impacted by lower gross profit margins, which decreased to 15.9% from 17.6%, and a significant increase in marketing expenses to 1.3 million euros, up from 0.9 million euros, reflecting more intensive sales activities as the number and scale of projects on sale grew.

Despite the lower top-line results, the Group’s balance sheet strengthened, with total assets increasing by 3.4% to 91.8 million euros. Total equity rose by 23.0% to 27.1 million euros, largely due to a non-cash contribution from a non-controlling interest in a subsidiary. The Group’s debt position improved, with total interest-bearing loan liabilities decreasing to 47.7 million euros from 54.7 million euros, reducing the debt ratio to 52.0% from 61.6%. The adjusted debt ratio also improved to 37.0% from 45.0%. Cash flow from operating activities was positive at 14.3 million euros, significantly higher than the 8.9 million euros recorded in 2024, although the overall net cash flow for the period was negative at 2.4 million euros due to investing and financing activities.

Operationally, Hepsor expanded its development pipeline substantially. The number of residential apartments under construction more than doubled to 428 by the end of 2025, up from 192 in the previous year. The company commenced construction on four new residential projects in Tallinn and Riga, including significant developments such as Manufaktuuri Vabrik and Starta 17. In the commercial sector, the StokOfiss U34 building in Riga received a partial occupancy permit, with 74% of its leasable area already leased. The Group also strengthened its financing profile by successfully completing a public bond offering in November 2025, raising 8.0 million euros with strong investor oversubscription. Looking ahead, Hepsor plans to launch construction on five new projects in 2026 across Estonia and Latvia, signaling continued expansion despite the current year's revenue contraction.
