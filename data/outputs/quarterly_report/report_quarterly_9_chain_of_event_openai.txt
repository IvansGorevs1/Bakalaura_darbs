Hepsor’s 2025 revenue declined 7.8% to €35.4 million from €38.4 million in 2024, while net profit fell 51.3% to €1.0 million. Profit attributable to the parent’s owners was €0.4 million, broadly unchanged from €0.4 million in 2024, although non-controlling interests’ profit decreased from €1.7 million to €0.6 million. Fourth-quarter performance weakened substantially: revenue fell 57.1% to €4.5 million, and the Group reported a €0.5 million net loss compared with a €1.0 million profit in Q4 2024. Parent-attributable loss was €0.5 million, against a €0.6 million profit a year earlier.

The decline primarily reflected fewer apartment handovers, at 141 compared with 194 in 2024, and lower margins on projects sold. Gross profit decreased to €5.6 million from €6.8 million, with the gross margin falling from 17.6% to 15.9%; the reported gross margin on sold projects declined from 17.8% to 16.9%. Operating profit fell 22.5% to €3.4 million, and the operating margin declined from 11.3% to 9.5%. Marketing expenses increased 48.6% to €1.3 million from €0.9 million because the larger number and scale of projects on sale required more intensive sales and marketing activity. These pressures were partly offset by €0.8 million of other operating income from revaluing properties transferred from inventories to investment property and by a €0.3 million increase in interest income. Finance and personnel expenses remained at broadly similar levels.

Despite weaker completed sales, forward sales improved. The Group concluded agreements for 185 homes worth €36.1 million, nearly double 2024’s 109 homes and €19.8 million. The four-quarter rolling average of initial sales reached €9 million in Q4 2025, up 182% from €5 million a year earlier. The development pipeline expanded to 33 active projects with approximately 196,650 m² of sellable area, versus 24 projects and 172,500 m² at year-end 2024. Homes under construction increased 223% to 428, driven particularly by new projects in Latvia and the start of additional Tallinn developments.

The balance sheet strengthened overall. Total equity rose 23.0% to €27.1 million, mainly because of a €5.8 million non-cash contribution by a non-controlling shareholder to Hepsor PHX5. Interest-bearing loan liabilities declined to €47.7 million from €54.7 million, reducing the debt ratio from 61.6% to 52.0%. However, cash decreased from €6.2 million to €3.8 million, reflecting negative annual cash flow of €2.6 million. The Group also raised €8.0 million through its first bond issue, carrying a fixed 9.50% annual interest rate, after the offering was 1.4 times oversubscribed.
