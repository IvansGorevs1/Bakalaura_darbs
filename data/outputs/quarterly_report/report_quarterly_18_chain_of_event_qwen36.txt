AB Pieno Žvaigždės reported a significant improvement in financial performance for the first quarter of 2026 compared to the same period in 2025. The company achieved a net profit of 3,068 thousand EUR, a substantial increase from the 227 thousand EUR recorded in the first quarter of 2025. This positive result was driven by a notable expansion in gross profit, which rose to 13,986 thousand EUR from 9,794 thousand EUR in the prior year period. The improvement in profitability was primarily due to a decrease in the cost of sales, which fell to 38,078 thousand EUR from 42,530 thousand EUR, despite revenue remaining relatively stable. Revenue for the first quarter of 2026 was 52,064 thousand EUR, a slight decline from 52,324 thousand EUR in the previous year.

Operating profit before finance costs showed marked growth, reaching 3,366 thousand EUR in the first quarter of 2026, compared to just 535 thousand EUR in the same period of 2025. This increase was supported by higher gross margins and a rise in other operating income, which totaled 363 thousand EUR versus only 28 thousand EUR in the prior year. Although sales and administrative expenses increased to 10,983 thousand EUR from 9,287 thousand EUR, the overall operating performance improved significantly. Finance costs remained relatively consistent, with net finance expenses of 298 thousand EUR in 2026 compared to 308 thousand EUR in 2025. Consequently, profit before tax increased to 3,068 thousand EUR from 227 thousand EUR. Earnings per share also improved, rising to 0.07 EUR from 0.01 EUR in the previous year.

The company’s financial position strengthened during the period. Total assets increased to 85,305 thousand EUR from 83,475 thousand EUR at the end of 2025. Total equity grew to 41,164 thousand EUR from 38,238 thousand EUR, driven by the retention of earnings. The current ratio improved to 1.51 from 1.27, indicating better short-term liquidity. Cash and cash equivalents increased to 2,157 thousand EUR from 1,806 thousand EUR. Operating cash flow improved significantly, with net cash flow from operating activities reaching 1,625 thousand EUR, compared to a negative 183 thousand EUR in the first quarter of 2025. This positive cash flow was supported by changes in working capital, although receivables increased by 2,536 thousand EUR. Investing activities used 551 thousand EUR in cash, primarily for the acquisition of property, plant, and equipment. Financing activities resulted in a net outflow of 723 thousand EUR, mainly due to loan repayments and finance lease liabilities. The company continues to focus on its core business of producing and selling milk products in Lithuania and other EU markets, with fresh milk products remaining the largest sales segment.
