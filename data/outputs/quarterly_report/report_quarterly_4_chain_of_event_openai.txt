Ambu reported a solid start to Q1 2025/26, although profitability and cash generation declined against an exceptionally strong prior-year quarter. Revenue increased to DKK 1,558m from DKK 1,510m, representing 8.6% organic growth and 3.2% reported growth. Endoscopy Solutions remained the main growth engine, delivering 14.4% organic growth and accounting for 63% of revenue. Within this segment, Urology, ENT, & GI grew 21.0%, primarily supported by continued adoption of the aScope 4 portfolio and strong momentum for aScope 5 Uretero, while Respiratory grew 8.3%, driven by bronchoscopy solutions, aScope 5 Broncho adoption, and the SureSight video laryngoscope launch. Anesthesia & Patient Monitoring was essentially flat, with organic growth of -0.1%, reflecting an extraordinarily high comparable revenue base from Q1 2024/25.

Growth was strongest in North America and Europe, with organic increases of 9.8% and 9.7%, respectively. Rest of World declined 1.6% organically because of order fluctuations and high comparable revenue from the previous year. Foreign-exchange effects reduced total reported growth by 5.4%.

EBIT before special items fell to DKK 164m from DKK 243m, and the EBIT margin declined to 10.5% from 16.1%. The report attributes the margin pressure to tariff costs and foreign-exchange headwinds; tariff costs exceeded DKK 50m. Excluding these external factors, the EBIT margin was 15.2%, indicating continued underlying operating leverage. The reported gross margin decreased modestly to 60.8% from 61.3%, while the operating-expense ratio increased to 50.3% from 45.2%, partly because Ambu continued investing in commercial infrastructure and tariff costs were included in operating expenses.

Net profit declined to DKK 118m from DKK 183m, while earnings per share fell to DKK 0.44 from DKK 0.68. Operating cash flow decreased to DKK 106m from DKK 144m, and free cash flow before acquisitions fell to DKK 13m from DKK 69m. Cash flow was negatively affected by lower EBITDA, elevated net working capital, foreign-exchange effects, and tariff costs. Net working capital remained elevated at DKK 1,321m, or 22% of revenue, mainly because of higher inventories.

Ambu introduced the portable SureSight Mobile video laryngoscope and expanded its Recircle recycling programme to include SureSight blades during 2026. The company maintained its full-year guidance for 10–13% organic revenue growth, a 12–14% EBIT margin before special items, and cash conversion above 40%, while noting that growth and margins are expected to be higher in the second half than in the first.
