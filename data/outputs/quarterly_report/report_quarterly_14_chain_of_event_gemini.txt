During the first six months of 2026, AS Merko Ehitus generated consolidated revenue of EUR 149.9 million, representing a 10.7% decrease compared to EUR 167.9 million reported for the first six months of 2025. For the second quarter of 2026, revenue reached EUR 92.7 million, compared to EUR 82.6 million in the second quarter of the previous year. The net profit attributable to equity holders of the parent for the six-month period was EUR 12.8 million, down from EUR 21.7 million in the same period of 2025. For the second quarter of 2026, net profit attributable to shareholders was EUR 8.5 million, compared to EUR 11.2 million for the second quarter of 2025. Pre-tax profit for the first six months of 2026 stood at EUR 10.8 million, down from EUR 23.5 million for the first six months of 2025, bringing the pre-tax profit margin to 7.2% compared to 14.0% in the prior-year period. 

The decline in six-month revenue and profitability was primarily driven by the construction service segment, where operating profit decreased to EUR 3.0 million from EUR 8.8 million in the first six months of 2025, and the real estate development segment, where operating profit dropped to EUR 7.0 million from EUR 11.9 million. The real estate segment experienced a 33.9% decrease in revenue to EUR 31.6 million, with 120 apartments sold during the first six months of 2026 compared to 222 apartments in the corresponding period of 2025. Conversely, the construction service segment saw its second-quarter revenue rise 14.8% to EUR 70.1 million due to large-scale projects entering more active construction phases, while second-quarter real estate revenue increased by 4.5% to EUR 22.6 million. 

Despite lower half-year financial results, the group achieved an all-time high secured order-book of EUR 835.7 million as of 30 June 2026, representing an approximate 88% increase compared to EUR 443.8 million as of 30 June 2025. Group companies signed EUR 486.4 million in new contracts during the first six months of 2026, compared to EUR 223.1 million in the first six months of 2025. The growth in the secured order book and public sector share was driven by major infrastructure and defense-related contracts, including the Rūdninkai military campus project. 

As of 30 June 2026, total assets were EUR 437.8 million, compared to EUR 403.5 million as of 30 June 2025. Total equity stood at EUR 251.1 million, accounting for 57.4% of total assets. Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the reporting period were EUR 39.6 million, compared to EUR 25.9 million as of 30 June 2025. Net debt was EUR 29.6 million at the end of June 2026, compared to negative EUR 1.1 million a year earlier. Operating cash flow for the first six months of 2026 was negative at EUR 31.6 million, influenced by increases in inventories related to apartment construction, while investing cash flow was positive at EUR 16.4 million and financing cash flow was positive at EUR 13.4 million. During the annual general meeting on 28 April 2026, shareholders approved a dividend payout of EUR 22.1 million, or EUR 1.25 per share, from retained earnings.
