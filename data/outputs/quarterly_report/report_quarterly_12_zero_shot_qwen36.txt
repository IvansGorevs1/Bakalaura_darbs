AS LHV Group reported a net profit of EUR 24.7 million for the second quarter of 2026, an increase of EUR 5 million from the EUR 19.7 million recorded in the first quarter of 2026. However, this represents a decline of EUR 6.1 million, or approximately 20%, compared to the EUR 30.8 million net profit achieved in the second quarter of 2025. The profit attributable to owners of the parent company was EUR 24.1 million. Earnings per share stood at EUR 0.07, up from EUR 0.06 in Q1 2026 but down from EUR 0.09 in Q2 2025.

The Group’s total net income grew by 7.3% quarter-over-quarter to EUR 79.0 million, driven primarily by an 8% increase in net interest income to EUR 62.5 million. Net service fee income remained stable at EUR 14.6 million. Operating expenses decreased by 4% to EUR 43.6 million, compared to EUR 45.6 million in the previous quarter, although they were higher than the EUR 40.5 million incurred in Q2 2025. A significant factor impacting profitability was the rise in loan and bond provisions, which amounted to EUR 5.8 million in Q2 2026, a substantial increase from the EUR 1.3 million provision in Q1 2026 and a reversal from the EUR -4.2 million gain (negative provision) recorded in Q2 2025. Income tax expenses decreased to EUR 5.0 million from EUR 7.1 million in the prior quarter.

Return on equity for the period was 13.1%, improving from 10.7% in Q1 2026 but falling from 17.4% in Q2 2025. The capital adequacy ratio decreased to 20.8% from 21.7% in the first quarter and 23.0% in the second quarter of the previous year. The Group’s loan volume expanded by 3% to EUR 5,768 million, with growth in both household and corporate segments. Conversely, customer deposits decreased by EUR 245 million to EUR 7,557 million, largely due to a reduction in platform deposits and deposits from financial intermediaries.

Management noted that the quarter was slightly weaker than planned, with expenses running higher than anticipated despite business volumes tracking the plan. The increase in impairment charges was attributed to model-based, forward-looking provisions for specific clients, although the company does not anticipate these losses materializing. The core banking subsidiary, AS LHV Pank, contributed the majority of the profit with EUR 24.3 million, while subsidiaries LHV Varahaldus and LHV Kindlustus reported modest profits of EUR 0.4 million and EUR 0.8 million, respectively. LHV Bank Limited earned EUR 1.4 million, and LHV Paytech incurred a net loss of EUR 0.3 million. The Group successfully transitioned its core systems to a cloud environment and implemented a product-based management model during the quarter.
