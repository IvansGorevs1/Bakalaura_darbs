Ambu had a solid start to the 2025/26 financial year, delivering total organic revenue growth of 8.6% and a reported revenue increase of 3.2% to DKK 1,558m, compared to DKK 1,510m in the first quarter of the previous financial year. This growth momentum was primarily driven by Endoscopy Solutions, which achieved organic growth of 14.4% and contributed 63% of total revenue. Within Endoscopy Solutions, the Urology, ENT, and GI business group posted organic growth of 21.0%, driven by the aScope 4 portfolio and aScope 5 Uretero, while the Respiratory business group delivered 8.3% organic growth supported by aScope 5 Broncho adoption and the launch of the SureSight video laryngoscope solution. Conversely, Anesthesia and Patient Monitoring posted flat organic growth of -0.1%, impacted by extraordinarily high comparable revenue in the same quarter last year. Geographically, organic revenue grew by 9.8% in North America and 9.7% in Europe, whereas the Rest of World declined by 1.6% due to order fluctuations and high comparative baselines. 

Financial results for the quarter showed a gross profit of DKK 947m, yielding a gross margin of 60.8% compared to 61.3% in the prior-year period. Operating profit, or EBIT before special items, was DKK 164m, resulting in an EBIT margin before special items of 10.5%, down from 16.1% in the first quarter of 2024/25. This margin contraction was negatively impacted by more than DKK 50m in tariff costs and foreign exchange headwinds; adjusting for these external factors, the underlying EBIT margin was 15.2%. Net profit for the period was DKK 118m, compared to DKK 183m previously, with earnings per share dropping to DKK 0.44 from DKK 0.68. Free cash flow before acquisitions decreased to DKK 13m from DKK 69m, primarily weighed down by changes in net working capital, which remained elevated at DKK 1,321m, or 22% of revenue, to mitigate geopolitical uncertainties and support growth. During the quarter, Ambu paid out DKK 90m in dividends, corresponding to DKK 0.41 per share, and used DKK 38m to acquire treasury shares under its share buyback program. 

Business developments in the reporting period featured the introduction of the SureSight Mobile portable video laryngoscope and the planned expansion of the Ambu Recircle Program to include SureSight blades in 2026. Furthermore, Ambu maintained its full-year 2025/26 financial guidance, continuing to expect organic revenue growth of 10% to 13% and an EBIT margin before special items of 12% to 14%, alongside a cash conversion of plus 40%.
