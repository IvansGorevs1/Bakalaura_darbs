During the first three months of 2026, AB Pieno Žvaigždės reported total revenue of 52,064 thousand euros, showing a slight decrease compared to 52,324 thousand euros in the corresponding period of 2025. Despite the minor change in revenue, the company achieved a significant improvement in its profitability and overall financial performance during the reporting period. Gross profit increased substantially to 13,986 thousand euros compared with 9,794 thousand euros in the first three months of 2025. This was supported by a notable decrease in the cost of sales, which dropped to 38,078 thousand euros from 42,530 thousand euros. Operating profit before finance costs rose sharply to 3,366 thousand euros, up from 535 thousand euros in the previous year. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization reached 4,730 thousand euros, compared to 2,001 thousand euros reported for the first three months of 2025. The net profit for the reporting period experienced a dramatic increase, reaching 3,068 thousand euros compared to just 227 thousand euros in the same period of 2025. Consequently, net profit per share rose to 0.07 euros from 0.01 euros, and the book value per share at the end of the period increased to 0.91 euros from 0.89 euros. Regarding the statement of financial position, total assets stood at 85,305 thousand euros as of March 31, 2026, compared to 83,475 thousand euros at the end of December 2025. Total equity improved to 41,164 thousand euros from 38,238 thousand euros, driven by higher retained earnings. Total liabilities decreased to 44,141 thousand euros from 45,237 thousand euros. Cash flows from operating activities also strengthened considerably, yielding a net cash flow of 1,625 thousand euros compared to a negative net cash flow of 183 thousand euros in the first three months of 2025. The company’s primary business activities involve the production and sales of milk products, operating across domestic and international markets, with geographic sales distribution including Lithuania at 31,405 thousand euros, other European Union countries at 11,341 thousand euros, and non-European Union countries at 9,318 thousand euros. Product group sales were led by fresh milk products at 42,497 thousand euros, cheese at 5,276 thousand euros, other products at 2,894 thousand euros, and dry milk products at 1,397 thousand euros. There were no material post-statement events reported after the balance sheet date.
