AS Silvano Fashion Group reported consolidated revenue of 28,590 thousand EUR for the first six months of 2026, representing a modest 0.6% increase compared to 28,428 thousand EUR in the same period of 2025. Despite the slight top-line growth, the company experienced a significant decline in profitability. Consolidated net profit attributable to equity holders of the Parent company fell by 31.5% to 3,961 thousand EUR, down from 5,781 thousand EUR in the prior year. Consequently, earnings per share decreased by 31.3% to 0.11 EUR from 0.16 EUR. The net profit margin attributable to parent company equity holders contracted sharply from 20.3% to 13.9%.

Gross profit improved by 5.3% to 15,892 thousand EUR, driven by a 4.8% decrease in the cost of goods sold. This resulted in an expanded gross margin of 55.6%, up from 53.1% in the previous year. However, operating profit declined by 3.5% to 5,751 thousand EUR, with the operating profit margin narrowing from 21.0% to 20.1%. EBITDA also saw a slight reduction of 0.8%, settling at 7,508 thousand EUR with a margin of 26.3%.

The shift in sales structure contributed to the mixed financial results. Retail revenue grew significantly by 13.6% to 13,548 thousand EUR, accounting for 47.4% of total sales. In contrast, wholesale revenue dropped by 9.1% to 14,934 thousand EUR, representing 52.2% of the total. Geographically, the Group’s largest market, Russia, saw sales decrease by 0.6% to 15,767 thousand EUR, with local currency sales falling by 7.8%. Belarus remained a strong performer, with sales increasing by 6.4% to 9,046 thousand EUR and local currency sales rising by 2.7%. Sales in Ukraine plummeted by 58.3% to just 15 thousand EUR, while the Baltics and other markets also experienced declines of 16.7% and 5.1% respectively.

Financial position remained robust with total assets increasing by 5.1% to 98,603 thousand EUR. Equity attributable to equity holders of the Parent company grew by 6.1% to 81,479 thousand EUR. The Group maintained strong liquidity, with cash and cash equivalents nearly doubling by 87.4% to 12,443 thousand EUR. Operating cash flow surged by 96.6% to 6,842 thousand EUR, reflecting improved cash generation despite lower net profits. The current ratio improved to 10.3 from 8.6, and the quick ratio rose to 6.7 from 5.2. Investments in property, plant, and equipment decreased to 115 thousand EUR from 368 thousand EUR in the prior year. The Group employed 1,594 people as of the end of the reporting period, a slight reduction from 1,639 at the end of 2025.
