During the 2025 financial year, AS Pro Kapital Grupp reported total revenue of EUR 53.2 million, marking a substantial increase compared to EUR 18.2 million in 2024. Fourth quarter revenue also grew significantly to EUR 18.9 million from EUR 7.6 million in the final quarter of 2024. The strong revenue growth was driven by the handover of completed apartments in the Kalaranna and Uus-Kindrali developments in Tallinn, as well as the City Villas project in Vilnius, following the signing of notarial sales agreements and the transfer of legal titles. Conversely, revenue in the prior year remained lower because apartment handovers in the Kalaranna District only commenced in December 2024, with earlier sales restricted to remaining inventory in Latvia and Lithuania. Gross profit for the twelve months of 2025 rose to EUR 18.8 million compared to EUR 5.4 million in 2024, while fourth quarter gross profit reached EUR 6.9 million up from EUR 2.6 million. Operating profit for 2025 improved dramatically to EUR 14.7 million from EUR 0.1 million in 2024, and the fourth quarter operating result increased to EUR 7.1 million compared to EUR 1.9 million in the corresponding period of the previous year. Net profit for the twelve months of 2025 turned positive at EUR 12.0 million, representing a sharp improvement from the net loss of EUR 3.9 million reported in 2024. Fourth quarter net profit similarly increased to EUR 6.0 million from EUR 0.2 million in the final quarter of 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share for 2025 were EUR 0.22, compared to EUR -0.06 in 2024, while fourth quarter earnings per share rose to EUR 0.11 from EUR 0.01. Operating cash flows improved to a positive EUR 10.8 million for 2025, recovering from cash used of EUR 10.4 million in 2024. Total assets stood at EUR 124.5 million at the end of December 2025 compared to EUR 118.8 million a year earlier, while total liabilities decreased to EUR 61.2 million from EUR 67.5 million. Total equity increased to EUR 63.3 million from EUR 51.2 million, and net asset value per share grew to EUR 1.12 from EUR 0.89 as of 31 December 2024. Financial performance was further impacted by the full impairment of goodwill associated with the Italian subsidiary Preatoni Nuda Proprietà S.r.l., resulting in an impairment loss of EUR 863 thousand recognized in 2025. Financing activities during the year included the partial redemption of secured non-convertible bonds in the amount of EUR 9.405 million in August 2025, which reduced the total outstanding nominal amount of the bonds from EUR 19.95 million to EUR 10.545 million.
