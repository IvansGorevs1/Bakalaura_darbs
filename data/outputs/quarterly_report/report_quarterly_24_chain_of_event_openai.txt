AS Trigon Property Development reported a significant deterioration in 2025, moving from a net profit of €167,409 in 2024 to a net loss of €99,507. Fourth-quarter performance also weakened, with a net loss of €23,333 compared with a profit of €154,215 in the fourth quarter of 2024. Earnings per share consequently declined from €0.03721 to negative €0.02212 for the full year and from €0.03428 to negative €0.00519 in the fourth quarter.

The main factor behind the decline was the absence of real estate sales during 2025. Gain on sales was €0, compared with €331,920 in 2024, including a €300,000 property sale in the fourth quarter of 2024 and a €31,920 land sale in the third quarter. As a result, gross profit fell from €220,299 to zero and operating performance changed from a €161,649 profit to a €108,021 operating loss. Administrative and general expenses increased to €108,021 from €63,650, a rise of approximately 70%. The report identifies €41,452 of remuneration paid to management and supervisory board members in 2025, compared with no such payments in 2024, contributing to the higher expense level. Financial income increased to €8,513 from €5,760 but was insufficient to offset the operating loss.

The company continued to own a single 13.2-hectare development project in Pärnu, intended for a commercial, industrial and logistics park, and stated that it planned to continue selling land plots without changing its business activity. The company invested €91,262 in the development project during 2025, increasing inventories to €1.56 million from €1.47 million. No inventory write-downs were recorded.

Operating cash flow changed from an inflow of €194,723 in 2024 to an outflow of €142,945 in 2025, reflecting the operating loss, investment in inventories and the lack of property-sale proceeds. Cash declined to €227,911 from €370,856. Total assets decreased to €1.80 million from €1.87 million, while equity fell to €1.76 million from €1.85 million as a result of the annual loss. Liabilities rose to €49,389 from €18,906, although the company remained lightly leveraged, with a debt ratio of 2.74% compared with 1.01%.

The share price increased modestly from €0.620 to €0.634, raising market capitalization from €2.79 million to €2.85 million, despite the decline in profitability and book value per share from €0.41 to €0.39.
