AB KN Energies reported robust financial performance for the nine months of 2025, characterized by significant revenue growth and improved profitability compared to the same period in 2024. The Group’s revenue from contracts with customers increased to EUR 76,664 thousand, up from EUR 67,977 thousand in the prior year. This growth was primarily driven by higher volumes and favorable conditions in the liquefied natural gas (LNG) segment. Specifically, income from LNG terminal regasification services rose to EUR 43,640 thousand from EUR 31,401 thousand. The increase was attributed to the LNG terminal operating at full capacity in 2025, whereas it was out of service for one and a half months in 2024 due to dry-dock repairs. Additionally, higher regasification volumes and bonuses paid by clients for booked capacity contributed to the revenue uplift. Revenue from liquid energy terminal transshipment services also grew slightly to EUR 21,375 thousand from EUR 20,241 thousand.

Gross profit improved substantially, reaching EUR 31,549 thousand compared to EUR 29,237 thousand in the previous year. Operating profit saw an even more pronounced increase, rising to EUR 21,282 thousand from EUR 18,942 thousand. This improvement was supported by a decrease in operating expenses, which fell to EUR 10,640 thousand from EUR 10,419 thousand, despite a slight increase in wage costs. The reduction in operating expenses was partly due to the normalization of depreciation charges; in 2024, significant depreciation related to the LNG terminal’s dry-dock period was classified as an operating expense rather than cost of sales.

Profit before tax increased to EUR 16,186 thousand from EUR 14,910 thousand. However, finance costs remained high at EUR 6,616 thousand, up from EUR 6,692 thousand in 2024, though interest expenses on loans increased to EUR 6,086 thousand from EUR 4,181 thousand, reflecting the debt structure associated with the Floating Storage and Regasification Unit (FSRU). The FSRU, acquired in December 2024, is now fully integrated, with its depreciation included in cost of sales. Net profit for the period amounted to EUR 13,158 thousand, up from EUR 11,862 thousand, resulting in basic earnings per share of EUR 0.035 compared to EUR 0.031 in the prior year.

Cash flow from operating activities strengthened significantly, generating EUR 76,331 thousand compared to EUR 35,637 thousand in the previous period. This surge was largely driven by a reclassification of EUR 31,900 thousand of financial assets to cash and cash equivalents. The company’s cash and cash equivalents balance grew to EUR 54,398 thousand from EUR 17,203 thousand at the end of 2024. Total assets decreased slightly to EUR 530,364 thousand from EUR 535,174 thousand, primarily due to a reduction in other financial assets and inventories. Equity increased to EUR 167,721 thousand, supported by retained earnings and dividends declared totaling EUR 8,622 thousand. The company maintained a stable capital structure with total borrowings and lease liabilities remaining relatively consistent with the previous year.
