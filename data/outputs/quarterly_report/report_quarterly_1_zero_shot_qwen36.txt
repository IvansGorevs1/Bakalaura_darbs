AB KN Energies reported strong financial performance for the three months ended March 31, 2026, characterized by significant growth in revenue and profitability compared to the same period in 2025. The Group achieved total revenue from contracts with customers of EUR 29,823 thousand, representing a substantial increase from EUR 25,326 thousand in the first quarter of 2025. This growth was primarily driven by the LNG terminal segment, where revenue from regasification services rose to EUR 19,162 thousand from EUR 14,593 thousand, and consulting services increased to EUR 2,749 thousand from EUR 1,932 thousand. Liquid energy terminal revenues remained relatively stable at EUR 7,779 thousand compared to EUR 7,864 thousand in the prior year period.

Profitability metrics showed marked improvement. Gross profit increased to EUR 12,907 thousand from EUR 9,793 thousand, reflecting a gross margin expansion. Operating profit rose significantly to EUR 9,608 thousand, up from EUR 7,141 thousand in the previous year. This improvement was supported by higher revenues, although cost of sales also increased to EUR 16,916 thousand from EUR 15,533 thousand, largely due to higher environmental taxes and FSRU expenses. Distribution costs and administrative expenses grew to EUR 3,316 thousand from EUR 3,002 thousand.

The net profit for the period reached EUR 7,247 thousand, a substantial increase from EUR 4,426 thousand in the corresponding period of 2025. Earnings per share improved to EUR 0.02 from EUR 0.01. The improvement in net profit was aided by a significant reduction in net finance costs, which decreased to EUR 784 thousand from EUR 1,777 thousand in the prior year. This reduction was primarily due to lower interest expenses on borrowings, which fell to EUR 1,729 thousand from EUR 2,160 thousand, partially offset by reduced interest income from financial assets. Income tax expenses increased to EUR 1,577 thousand from EUR 938 thousand, aligning with the higher pre-tax profits.

Cash flow from operating activities strengthened considerably, reaching EUR 27,917 thousand compared to EUR 13,758 thousand in the previous year. This was driven by higher net profits and favorable changes in working capital, including a decrease in trade receivables and an increase in trade payables. Net cash used in investing activities was EUR 10,771 thousand, reflecting capital expenditures on property, plant, and equipment. Net cash used in financing activities was EUR 11,521 thousand, mainly due to repayment of borrowings and interest payments. The Group’s cash and cash equivalents increased to EUR 17,986 thousand at the end of the period from EUR 12,361 thousand at the end of 2025. Total equity grew to EUR 179,866 thousand from EUR 172,592 thousand, driven by the retained earnings from the period's profits. The company maintained a stable capital structure with total borrowings decreasing slightly to EUR 302,069 thousand from EUR 311,194 thousand. No impairment indicators were identified for the Klaipėda liquid energy terminal assets during the reporting period.
