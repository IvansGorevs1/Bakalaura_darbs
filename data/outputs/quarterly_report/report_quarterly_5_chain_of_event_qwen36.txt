Arco Vara AS reported a significant improvement in financial performance for the first quarter of 2025, transitioning from a net loss in the prior year to a net profit. The group’s total sales revenue increased by 72.3 percent to 1,706 thousand euros, up from 990 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2024. This growth was primarily driven by the sale of own real estate, which generated 1,589 thousand euros compared to 927 thousand euros in the same period last year. Additionally, franchise fees from real estate agencies contributed 65 thousand euros to revenue, a slight increase from 55 thousand euros in Q1 2024. The operating profit turned positive, reaching 259 thousand euros, a substantial recovery from the operating loss of 208 thousand euros recorded in Q1 2024. Consequently, the net profit for the quarter was 103 thousand euros, reversing the net loss of 349 thousand euros experienced in the previous year.

The improvement in profitability was supported by a 22.6 percent increase in gross profit to 598 thousand euros, while operating expenses remained relatively stable. Administrative expenses decreased by 9.1 percent to 270 thousand euros, and marketing and distribution expenses were nearly flat at 69 thousand euros. However, the group’s debt burden increased, with net loans rising by 1,960 thousand euros to reach 15,455 thousand euros as of March 31, 2025. This increase was attributed to the refinancing of completed developments with bank loans during 2024 and new borrowings for ongoing projects. Despite the higher debt levels, the weighted average annual interest rate on interest-bearing liabilities decreased by 0.5 percentage points to 8.2 percent, reflecting the continued decline in the Euribor and stabilizing inflation, which the CEO’s review cited as factors contributing to increased transaction activity and consumer interest in the residential segment.

Total assets grew to 42,728 thousand euros from 35,079 thousand euros at the end of the previous year, largely due to an increase in inventories to 32,329 thousand euros. Equity stood at 19,842 thousand euros, resulting in an equity ratio of 46.4 percent. The group sold four apartments in Q1 2025, bringing the total units sold in the Rannakalda development to 91 out of 113. In Bulgaria, the Botanica Lozen project reached 95 percent readiness with nine of sixteen houses pre-sold. The company also acquired a new development site in Tallinn on Spordi Street, with pre-sales planned for the second quarter of 2025. Cash and cash equivalents decreased to 1,113 thousand euros from 1,720 thousand euros at the start of the year, resulting in net cash outflows from operating activities of 2,838 thousand euros. The financial leverage ratio increased to 2.15 from 1.70 in the prior year period, indicating a higher reliance on debt financing relative to equity.
