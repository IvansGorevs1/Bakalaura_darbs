AS LHV Group reported a stronger second quarter than the first, but weaker results than a year earlier. Q2 2026 net profit was EUR 24.7 million, up 25% from EUR 19.7 million in Q1 but down 20% from EUR 30.8 million in Q2 2025. Profit attributable to shareholders was EUR 24.1 million, compared with EUR 19.8 million in Q1 and EUR 30.0 million a year earlier. Basic earnings per share increased to EUR 0.07 from EUR 0.06 in Q1 but declined from EUR 0.09 in Q2 2025. For the first half, net profit was EUR 44.4 million, 26% below the EUR 59.9 million reported for the first half of 2025.

Net operating income rose 7% year on year and quarter on quarter to EUR 79.0 million. Net interest income was the main contributor, increasing 5% from Q1 to EUR 62.5 million and 8% from Q2 2025. Net fee and commission income was broadly unchanged from Q1 at EUR 14.6 million but declined 6% year on year, reflecting lower client activity. Other financial income improved to EUR 1.2 million, while net insurance income recovered to EUR 0.7 million from a loss of EUR 0.7 million in Q1.

Operating expenses fell 4% from Q1 to EUR 43.6 million but were 8% higher than a year earlier, primarily reflecting increased personnel and IT costs. Operating profit rose to EUR 35.4 million from EUR 28.1 million in Q1. This improvement was partly offset by EUR 5.8 million of loan and bond impairment charges, compared with EUR 1.3 million in Q1 and a EUR 4.2 million reversal in Q2 2025. Management said the charges included forward-looking, model-based provisions for specific clients, while overall loan portfolio quality remained strong. Income tax expense decreased to EUR 5.0 million from EUR 7.1 million in Q1 and EUR 6.8 million a year earlier.

The loan portfolio grew 3% during the quarter to EUR 5.77 billion and was 15% above Q2 2025. Customer deposits declined 3% from Q1 to EUR 7.56 billion, mainly because of lower platform and financial-intermediary deposits. Return on equity improved to 13.1% from 10.7% in Q1 but remained below 17.4% a year earlier. Capital adequacy was 20.8%, down from 21.7% in Q1 and 23.0% in Q2 2025.

LHV Pank remained the dominant contributor, earning EUR 24.3 million. LHV Bank earned EUR 1.4 million, Varahaldus EUR 0.4 million and Kindlustus EUR 0.8 million. Key developments included the completion of LHV Pank’s migration to a cloud-based core system, a new product-based management model, Estonia’s first bank crypto-assets licence under MiCA, and continued preparations for international expansion. The operating environment remained affected by geopolitical tensions, energy-market uncertainty and volatile financial markets.
