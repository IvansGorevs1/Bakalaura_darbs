SAF Tehnika’s financial performance improved on a full-year basis in 2024/2025, although the Group remained loss-making and quarterly results weakened. Full-year turnover declined 4% to EUR 26.11 million from EUR 27.09 million. The decline was driven primarily by a 14% reduction in sales in North and Latin America to EUR 14.28 million, which represented 55% of total revenue. This was partly offset by 17% growth in Europe to EUR 10.04 million, while sales in Asia, Africa and the Middle East decreased 5%.

Profitability improved substantially for the year. The net loss narrowed to EUR 820 thousand from EUR 2.37 million in 2023/2024, while the operating loss improved to EUR 754 thousand from EUR 2.26 million. Lower direct production costs were the main favorable development, falling to EUR 9.33 million from EUR 13.12 million. However, personnel-related costs increased 8% to EUR 12.41 million, including a significant rise in bonuses and related social expenses. Depreciation and amortization also increased. The Group’s full-year EPS improved to a loss of EUR 0.28 from EUR 0.80.

The fourth quarter was materially weaker than the comparable period. Revenue decreased 13% to EUR 5.18 million, and the net loss widened to EUR 913 thousand from EUR 498 thousand. EBITDA declined to negative EUR 460 thousand from negative EUR 96 thousand, while EBIT fell to negative EUR 863 thousand from negative EUR 495 thousand. North and Latin American quarterly turnover declined 40% to EUR 2.5 million, partially offset by 24% growth in Europe to EUR 1.9 million and a doubling of turnover in Asia, Africa and the Middle East to EUR 846 thousand. Management noted that quarterly revenue can fluctuate significantly depending on the timing and completion of individual projects, production cycles and material procurement lead times.

The Group continued investing in product development, product enhancements and sales promotion, while expenses remained within budget. Operations were affected by the global shortage of electronic components, although delivery times had recently improved. The Group maintained material reserves to support customer delivery requirements and continued developing microwave radio, spectrum analyzer and IoT products.

Cash generation strengthened considerably. Net cash flow for the year was EUR 2.60 million, compared with an outflow of EUR 1.25 million in the prior year, raising cash and bank balances to EUR 4.81 million from EUR 2.21 million. The unused EUR 4.95 million Luminor credit line provided additional liquidity capacity. Total assets increased to EUR 25.92 million, while equity declined to EUR 15.58 million, reflecting the annual loss and currency translation effects. Total liabilities rose to EUR 10.34 million, mainly because customer prepayments increased to EUR 3.92 million from EUR 759 thousand.
