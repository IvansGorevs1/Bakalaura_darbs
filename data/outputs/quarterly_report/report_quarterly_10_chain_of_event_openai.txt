Infortar Group reported a strong 2025 operating performance, although net profit declined because the 2024 result included one-off effects from the initial consolidation of Tallink Grupp. Revenue increased 33.9% to EUR 1.837 billion from EUR 1.372 billion, while EBITDA rose 60.1% to EUR 232.6 million from EUR 145.4 million. The EBITDA margin improved to 13.4% from 10.6%, and operating profit increased 44.2% to EUR 111.1 million. Net profit, however, fell to EUR 72.0 million from EUR 193.7 million, while profit attributable to the parent decreased to EUR 72.7 million from EUR 191.3 million. Earnings per share declined to EUR 3.54 from EUR 9.36. The report attributes the lower comparative net profit mainly to one-off effects related to Tallink’s initial consolidation in 2024.

Fourth-quarter performance also improved operationally. Revenue was EUR 417.4 million, down 6.4% year-on-year, but EBITDA increased 52.7% to EUR 42.6 million and the EBITDA margin rose to 10.2% from 6.3%. Operating profit improved from a EUR 6.8 million loss to a EUR 19.8 million profit, while net profit changed from a EUR 12.0 million loss to EUR 14.1 million profit. Maritime transport revenue increased 2.4% to EUR 188.0 million, EBITDA rose to EUR 27.6 million from EUR 25.7 million, and net profit reached EUR 12.2 million compared with a EUR 5.2 million loss. Tallink’s full-year revenue declined 2.6% to EUR 765.3 million, EBITDA fell to EUR 130.1 million from EUR 175.2 million, and passenger volumes decreased 0.9%; cargo units fell 19.2%.

Energy sales volumes increased 7% in the fourth quarter to 4.8 TWh despite a 2% decline in regional gas consumption. Elenger’s full-year EBITDA improved by approximately EUR 12 million. The group also benefited from consolidating Tallink for the full 12 months in 2025, compared with consolidation from August 2024, and from contributions by Estonia Farmid and Halinga.

The balance sheet strengthened: equity rose to EUR 1.178 billion, the equity ratio improved to 45.5% from 43.0%, and net debt fell to EUR 840.7 million from EUR 1.056 billion, reducing net debt/EBITDA to 3.6 from 4.0. Operating cash flow increased substantially to EUR 388.6 million from EUR 89.3 million, and year-end cash rose to EUR 219.8 million. Loan and lease liabilities decreased to EUR 1.060 billion from EUR 1.223 billion, reflecting repayments across several Group entities. Infortar acquired 100% of Oisu Biogaas in the fourth quarter and proposed a dividend consistent with its stated dividend policy.
