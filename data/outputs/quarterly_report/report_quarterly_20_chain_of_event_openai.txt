SAF Tehnika’s unaudited 2024/2025 financial year ended 30 June 2025 with consolidated turnover of EUR 26.11 million, down 4% from EUR 27.09 million in the previous year. Regional performance was mixed: revenue from the Americas declined 14% to EUR 14.28 million, while European revenue increased 17% to EUR 10.04 million. Revenue from Asia, Africa and the Middle East fell 5%. The Group continued selling in 75 countries.

Despite lower revenue, the full-year net loss narrowed substantially to EUR 820 thousand from EUR 2.37 million, while EBIT improved to a loss of EUR 754 thousand from a loss of EUR 2.26 million. This improvement was supported by a reduction in direct costs of goods sold to EUR 9.33 million from EUR 13.12 million and lower total operating expenses of EUR 27.56 million compared with EUR 30.27 million. However, salary, bonus and related social expenses increased 8% to EUR 12.41 million, including bonuses of EUR 1.82 million versus EUR 943 thousand. Foreign-exchange losses of EUR 47 thousand also contrasted with a EUR 45 thousand gain in the prior year.

Fourth-quarter performance deteriorated. Q4 turnover fell 13% to EUR 5.18 million, and the net loss widened to EUR 913 thousand from EUR 498 thousand. Q4 EBIT declined to a loss of EUR 863 thousand from EUR 495 thousand. North and Latin American quarterly turnover decreased 40% to EUR 2.5 million, while European turnover increased 24% to EUR 1.9 million and turnover in Asia, Africa and the Middle East doubled to EUR 846 thousand. Management noted that quarterly results are affected by the timing and complexity of individual projects, production processes, material procurement and the subsequent recognition of revenue.

Operations continued to be affected by the global shortage of electronic components. The Group had accumulated material reserves to meet orders and maintain short delivery times, and reported that delivery times had recently improved. Inventories increased 8% to EUR 13.94 million, reflecting the need to maintain product and service-related component reserves. The Group continued investing in product development, product enhancements, sales promotion and IoT solutions; Q4 fixed-asset investment amounted to EUR 239 thousand.

Cash performance strengthened materially. Net cash flow for the year was EUR 2.60 million, compared with an outflow of EUR 1.25 million in the prior year, and period-end cash increased to EUR 4.81 million from EUR 2.21 million. The EUR 4.95 million Luminor credit line remained unused. Total assets rose to EUR 25.92 million, while equity declined to EUR 15.58 million. The Board remains cautious and aims to stabilize turnover and achieve a positive long-term net result, but provided no specific forecast.
