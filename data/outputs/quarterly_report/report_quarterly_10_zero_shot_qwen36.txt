Infortar Group reported strong financial performance for the full year 2025, characterized by significant revenue growth and improved profitability compared to the previous period. The Group’s consolidated revenue reached approximately EUR 1.8 billion, a substantial increase from EUR 1.37 billion in 2024. This growth was primarily driven by the full-year consolidation of Tallink Grupp, which was acquired in August 2024, and improved results from the energy segment. EBITDA rose to EUR 233 million, up from EUR 145 million in 2024, with the EBITDA margin expanding to 13.4% from 10.6%. Operating profit for the twelve-month period amounted to EUR 111 million, compared to EUR 77 million in the prior year. However, net profit decreased to EUR 72 million from EUR 194 million in 2024. Management attributes this decline mainly to one-off effects related to the initial consolidation of Tallink Group in 2024, rather than operational deterioration.

The maritime transport segment, represented by Tallink Grupp, generated revenue of EUR 765 million for the full year, a slight decrease from EUR 786 million in 2024. Tallink carried 5.5 million passengers, a 0.9% year-on-year decline, while cargo units transported fell by 19.2%. Tallink’s EBITDA decreased to EUR 130 million from EUR 175 million in 2024, and net profit dropped to EUR 17 million from EUR 40 million. Despite lower volumes, the segment maintained stable operations, with investments totaling EUR 33 million directed toward vessel maintenance and refurbishment. In the fourth quarter of 2025, Tallink returned to profitability with a net profit of EUR 12.2 million, reversing a net loss of EUR 5.2 million in the same period of 2024.

The energy segment, led by Elenger Grupp, contributed significantly to the Group’s top-line growth. Energy sales volumes increased by 7% in the fourth quarter, totaling 4.8 TWh, despite a 2% decline in overall regional natural gas consumption. Elenger maintained a 21% market share in the Finnish-Baltic region. The segment benefited from strategic initiatives, including a multi-year LNG supply agreement and the commissioning of battery-based energy storage systems in Latvia. The Group’s total assets stood at EUR 2.59 billion, with total equity increasing to EUR 1.18 billion. The equity ratio improved to 45.5% from 43.0% in the previous year. Interest-bearing liabilities decreased to EUR 1.06 billion from EUR 1.22 billion, reflecting loan repayments across several Group entities. Net debt declined to EUR 841 million, and the net debt to EBITDA ratio improved to 3.6 from 4.0. The Group employed 6,466 people as of the end of 2025. The CEO emphasized that diversification into agriculture and infrastructure has strengthened the Group’s resilience, allowing for stable cash flows and the proposal of a dividend worthy of a dividend aristocrat. The share price closed at EUR 45.8 per share at the end of the reporting period.
