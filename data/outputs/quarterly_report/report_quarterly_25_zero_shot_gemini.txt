During the first three months of 2026, VILVI Group experienced significant growth in consolidated sales revenue, reaching 86,970 thousand euros, which represents a 22.4% increase compared to 71,072 thousand euros in the same period of 2025. This performance was supported by a 54.2% increase in purchased basic indicators milk, totaling 155,954 tons compared to 101,118 tons in the prior-year period. Despite higher revenue, the group reported an EBITDA of 3,003 thousand euros for Q1 2026, reflecting a 50.2% decrease from 6,031 thousand euros in Q1 2025. The EBITDA margin dropped from 8.5% to 3.5%. The EBITDA figure for Q1 2026 excludes preliminary negative goodwill of 5,542 thousand euros related to the acquisition of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB. Conversely, the group's net profit for the first three months of 2026 increased by 25.7% to 5,085 thousand euros, up from 4,045 thousand euros in Q1 2025. Basic and diluted earnings per share rose correspondingly from 0.34 euros to 0.43 euros, a 26.5% increase. Net financial debt saw a substantial rise, growing by 89.7% to 83,427 thousand euros compared to 43,971 thousand euros at the end of Q1 2025. 

Significant changes in the financial results and operations were largely driven by corporate acquisitions and structural developments. On 16 January 2026, Vilkyškių pieninė AB completed the acquisition of 100% of the shares of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB, a major dairy processing and canned milk producer in the Baltic States. Consequently, canned milk products were consolidated starting 1 January 2026, contributing 11,923 thousand euros or 14% of total sales revenue during Q1 2026. Furthermore, commercial cheese production officially commenced at the new VILVI Group cheese factory, Baltic Dairy Board SIA, in Bauska, Latvia, following completed construction, equipment installation, and testing. Sales revenue by operating segments showed that cheese, cheese products, and other segments decreased by 28.8% to 37,641 thousand euros, accounting for 43% of total revenue. Fresh milk products grew by 73.4% to 12,748 thousand euros, representing 15% of revenue, and dry milk products surged by 126.9% to 24,659 thousand euros, making up 28% of total revenue. Regarding sales markets, revenue in the European Union was 42,518 thousand euros or 49% of the total, Lithuania accounted for 14,633 thousand euros or 17%, and other countries generated 29,818 thousand euros or 34% of total revenue. Overall, the reporting period was characterized by expanded production capacity, strategic subsidiary integration, revenue expansion, and an increase in net profit despite contracting EBITDA margins and higher financial debt.
