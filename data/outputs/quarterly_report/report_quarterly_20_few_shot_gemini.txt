The company reported a weakening of financial performance during the fourth quarter of the 2024 and 2025 financial year, alongside a net loss for the twelve-month reporting period ending June 30, 2025. For the fourth quarter, unaudited consolidated net turnover reached 5.18 million euros, representing a 13 percent decrease compared to 5.98 million euros reported in the same period of the previous financial year. The decline in quarterly revenue was heavily influenced by a 40 percent drop in the North America and Latin America region, where turnover fell to 2.5 million euros, though this was partially offset by a 24 percent increase in European turnover to 1.9 million euros and a doubling of revenue in the Asia, Africa, and Middle East region to 846 thousand euros. For the full twelve-month period of the 2024 and 2025 financial year, the Group generated a consolidated turnover of 26.11 million euros, marking a 4 percent decline from 27.09 million euros in the preceding financial year. Full-year revenues in the Americas decreased by 14 percent to 14.28 million euros, while European revenues grew by 17 percent to 10.04 million euros, and sales in Asia, Africa, and the Middle East decreased by 5 percent to 1.78 million euros. 

Profitability indicators remained negative across the reporting periods, although full-year losses narrowed significantly compared to the prior year. The Group closed the fourth quarter with an unaudited net loss of 913 thousand euros, worsening from a net loss of 498 thousand euros in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. For the entire twelve-month financial year, the Group recorded an unaudited net loss of 820 thousand euros, which represents an improvement compared with the net loss of 2.37 million euros reported for the 2023 and 2024 financial year. Loss per share for the twelve months improved to negative 0.28 euros from negative 0.80 euros in the prior year. Total operating expenses for the year decreased as direct costs of goods sold fell to 9.33 million euros from 13.12 million euros, while salaries, bonuses, and related social expenses increased to 12.41 million euros from 11.48 million euros. 

The company maintained a stable financial position with a total net cash flow of 2.60 million euros for the twelve-month period, resulting in a cash and bank balance of 4.81 million euros at the end of the reporting period. Customer prepayments for goods and services increased substantially to 3.92 million euros compared to 759 thousand euros previously. To ensure ongoing liquidity, the Parent Company maintained an active credit line agreement with Luminor Bank AS for a total facility of 4.95 million euros, which remained completely unutilized at the end of the financial year. Total assets increased to 25.92 million euros as of June 30, 2025, up from 23.02 million euros a year earlier, driven largely by increases in inventories and cash balances, while total owners equity decreased to 15.58 million euros from 16.44 million euros following the recognition of the annual loss.
