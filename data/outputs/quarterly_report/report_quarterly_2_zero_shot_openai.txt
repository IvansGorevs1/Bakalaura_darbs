For the three months ended 31 March 2026, Amber Grid AB reported a substantial improvement in financial performance. Revenue increased to EUR 32.821 million from EUR 20.763 million in the corresponding period of 2025, while total income rose to EUR 32.874 million from EUR 20.785 million. Revenue from natural gas transmission and related services increased by 58%, supported by a 35% increase in transmitted gas volumes and higher transmission prices for 2026. Revenue from system balancing products fell by 99%, following a new transit agreement to Kaliningrad that significantly reduced the need for balancing gas.

Operating expenses excluding depreciation declined to EUR 9.570 million from EUR 11.867 million. Natural gas and related service purchases fell by 68% to EUR 1.477 million, mainly because balancing-gas costs decreased by 99%, partly offset by higher gas consumption for technological needs. Wage and related costs increased to EUR 4.367 million from EUR 3.659 million, while other expenses rose to EUR 3.231 million from EUR 3.015 million.

EBITDA increased to EUR 23.304 million from EUR 8.918 million, and operating profit rose to EUR 19.627 million from EUR 5.185 million. Net finance costs increased to EUR 626 thousand from EUR 408 thousand, while the prior-year results included EUR 210 thousand of income from associates and a EUR 42 thousand loss on options. Profit before tax consequently reached EUR 19.001 million, compared with EUR 4.987 million previously. Income tax expense increased to EUR 3.238 million from EUR 748 thousand, but net profit still rose to EUR 15.763 million from EUR 4.239 million. Earnings per share increased to EUR 0.09 from EUR 0.02.

Operating cash flow improved to EUR 22.927 million from EUR 8.264 million. Investments in property, plant and equipment and intangible assets increased to EUR 2.837 million from EUR 1.405 million. Financing cash outflows reached EUR 20.467 million, primarily reflecting a EUR 17.845 million reduction in overdraft, compared with EUR 6.981 million in the prior-year period. Cash and cash equivalents decreased from EUR 1.942 million at year-end 2025 to EUR 832 thousand at 31 March 2026.

Total assets were broadly stable at EUR 324.064 million. Equity increased to EUR 182.039 million from EUR 166.276 million, mainly due to the period’s net profit. Total borrowings declined to EUR 104.174 million from EUR 123.666 million, and net debt decreased to EUR 106.610 million from EUR 125.224 million at 31 December 2025. The company also reclassified significant borrowings from current to non-current liabilities after the expiry of the Nordic Investment Bank agreement and the related lapse of covenant and cross-default consequences.
