AB KN Energies reported improved financial performance for the first nine months of 2025. Group revenue increased 12.8% to EUR 76.664 million from EUR 67.977 million in the same period of 2024. Growth was driven mainly by LNG terminal services, whose revenue rose to EUR 43.640 million from EUR 31.401 million. The report attributes this increase to higher regasification volumes, bonuses from LNG clients for booked capacity, and the absence of the one-and-a-half-month dry-dock shutdown that affected operations in 2024. Revenue from liquid energy product transshipment also increased to EUR 21.375 million, while consulting services revenue rose to EUR 7.507 million. These gains were partly offset by lower revenue from other LNG terminal activities and the reduction of LNG security supplement income following the repayment of excess returns from regulated activities in 2022–2023.

Gross profit increased 7.9% to EUR 31.549 million, although the gross margin declined as cost of sales rose 16.5% to EUR 45.115 million. The increase in costs was primarily reflected in substantially higher depreciation and amortisation following the acquisition of the floating storage and regasification unit, as well as higher wages, FSRU operating and maintenance costs, and environmental taxes. Operating expenses increased only 2.1% to EUR 10.640 million. Consequently, operating profit improved 12.4% to EUR 21.282 million.

Profit before tax rose 8.6% to EUR 16.186 million. Finance income declined from EUR 2.644 million to EUR 1.520 million, but finance costs were broadly stable at EUR 6.616 million compared with EUR 6.692 million previously. Net profit increased 10.9% to EUR 13.158 million, and earnings per share rose from EUR 0.031 to EUR 0.035. Total comprehensive income was EUR 13.215 million, compared with EUR 11.615 million in 2024.

The Group generated EUR 76.331 million in operating cash flow, more than double the EUR 35.637 million recorded in the prior-year period. Investing activities used EUR 13.804 million, including EUR 9.751 million for property, plant and equipment and intangible assets. Financing activities used EUR 25.332 million, including EUR 8.622 million in dividends and EUR 10.451 million of loan repayments. Cash and cash equivalents increased to EUR 54.398 million from EUR 17.203 million at year-end 2024, while borrowings declined to EUR 311.443 million from EUR 320.352 million. Total equity increased to EUR 167.721 million despite the dividend distribution.
