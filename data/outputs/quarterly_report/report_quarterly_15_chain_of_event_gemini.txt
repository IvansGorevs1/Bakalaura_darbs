Nordecon AS reported consolidated revenue of 208 million euros for the twelve months of 2025, representing a decrease of approximately 7% compared to 224 million euros in the previous year. This revenue decline was primarily driven by a roughly 10% decrease in the Buildings segment, which generated 168 million euros and accounted for 81% of total revenue. Conversely, revenue in the Infrastructure segment grew by 10% to 40 million euros. Geographically, Estonia accounted for 98% of total revenue, amounting to 204 million euros, while Ukraine contributed 2% or 5 million euros. 

The group achieved a gross profit of 13.5 million euros for 2025, down from 16.8 million euros in 2024, resulting in a gross margin of 6.5% compared to 7.5% previously. Administrative expenses decreased by 14% to 6.8 million euros, primarily due to lower staff costs resulting from weaker financial performance and smaller provisions for performance-based pay. Operating profit for the period was 5.7 million euros, compared to 8.1 million euros in 2024, representing an operating margin of 2.7% versus 3.6%. EBITDA reached 8.3 million euros with a margin of 4.0%, down from 11.0 million euros and 4.9% in the prior year. Net finance costs increased to 3.0 million euros from 2.3 million euros, significantly impacted by a foreign exchange loss of 750 thousand euros caused by the approximately 12% weakening of the Ukrainian hryvnia against the euro during the translation of loans provided to Ukrainian subsidiaries. Consequently, the group reported a net profit of 2.5 million euros for 2025, compared to 5.2 million euros in 2024. Net profit attributable to owners of the parent was 1.4 million euros, down from 3.8 million euros, resulting in basic and diluted earnings per share of 0.05 euros compared to 0.12 euros in 2024. 

Operating activities generated a net cash inflow of 1.2 million euros in 2025, closely matching the 1.1 million euros recorded in 2024. Investing activities resulted in a net cash outflow of 2.8 million euros, influenced by 2.9 million euros placed in long-term deposits to secure guarantee limits. Financing activities resulted in a net cash outflow of 1.2 million euros, compared to an outflow of 4.2 million euros in the previous year, with no dividends paid during 2025. Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the period stood at 5.3 million euros, down from 8.2 million euros at the end of 2024. Total assets increased to 126.7 million euros from 113.8 million euros, while total liabilities rose to 97.7 million euros from 87.1 million euros. Total equity increased to 29.0 million euros from 26.6 million euros, yielding an equity ratio of 22.9%. 

A notable positive development during the period was the expansion of the group's order book, which grew by 30% to 273.1 million euros at 31 December 2025, up from 209.5 million euros a year earlier. This growth was driven by new contracts secured totaling 235.0 million euros and a nearly threefold expansion in the Infrastructure segment order book, supported by major wins including a Rail Baltica main line railway infrastructure contract and road construction projects with the Estonian Transport Administration. Management anticipates increased business volumes for 2026 based on this stronger order book and improved market conditions.
