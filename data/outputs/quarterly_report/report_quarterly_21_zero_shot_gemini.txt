During the first six months of 2026, AS Tallinna Sadam reported revenue of EUR 59,262 thousand, reflecting an increase of EUR 1,399 thousand or 2.4% compared to EUR 57,862 thousand in the corresponding period of 2025. Despite the revenue growth, operating profit decreased by EUR 4,708 thousand or 25.7% to EUR 13,578 thousand down from EUR 18,286 thousand. Adjusted EBITDA declined by EUR 4,036 thousand or 13.5% to EUR 25,769 thousand compared to EUR 29,805 thousand in the previous year. Profit for the period fell by EUR 4,287 thousand or 41.7% to EUR 5,999 thousand down from EUR 10,286 thousand. Basic and diluted earnings per share dropped from EUR 0.04 to EUR 0.02. Total assets at period-end stood at EUR 615,034 thousand representing a minor decrease of 1.1% compared to EUR 622,079 thousand at 30 June 2025. Net debt increased by EUR 2,100 thousand or 1.5% to EUR 145,764 thousand compared to EUR 143,664 thousand. Equity at period-end was EUR 367,677 thousand compared to EUR 368,700 thousand. Investments made during the first six months dropped significantly by 70.6% or EUR 8,491 thousand to EUR 3,529 thousand down from EUR 12,020 thousand. 

The overall financial performance was adversely impacted by higher operating expenses, primarily driven by increases in fuel and electricity costs, higher depreciation, amortisation, and impairment, as well as a decline in vessel calls and cargo volumes. Operating expenses rose by EUR 3,907 thousand or 24.0% to EUR 20,184 thousand compared to EUR 16,277 thousand. Furthermore, the results for the comparative period in 2025 benefited positively from one-off items including the profit from the disposal of land for the Rail Baltica project and insurance indemnities received. Revenue growth was supported by a higher number of charter days for the multifunctional icebreaker Botnica alongside increases in the sale of electricity and ferry services. Cargo volumes handled across the Group harbours decreased by 5.0% or 344 thousand tonnes to 6,481 thousand tonnes, driven mainly by a 33.8% decline in liquid bulk. The number of passengers fell by 2.0% or 73 thousand to 3,586 thousand due to declines on the Tallinn-Helsinki and Tallinn-Stockholm routes, although cruise passengers grew by nearly 48%. Vessel calls decreased by 4.8% to 3,376 calls. 

For the second quarter of 2026, revenue increased by EUR 1,584 thousand or 5.4% to EUR 31,092 thousand compared to EUR 29,508 thousand in the second quarter of 2025. Operating profit for the second quarter declined by EUR 2,145 thousand or 21.4% to EUR 7,883 thousand down from EUR 10,028 thousand, and profit for the period dropped by EUR 2,049 thousand or 59.0% to EUR 1,426 thousand compared to EUR 3,475 thousand. Adjusted EBITDA for the second quarter decreased by EUR 1,693 thousand or 10.7% to EUR 14,196 thousand. During the quarter, passenger numbers decreased by 1.1% to 2,221 thousand and cargo volumes declined by 2.3% to 3,407 thousand tonnes.
