Aktsiaselts Infortar released its consolidated unaudited financial report for the fourth quarter and the full year ended December 31, 2025. For the 12-month period, the Group reported total revenue of EUR 1,837,000 thousand, compared to EUR 1,371,775 thousand in 2024. Gross profit for the year reached EUR 240,846 thousand, up from EUR 128,628 thousand in the previous year. Operating profit for 2025 was EUR 111,061 thousand, compared to EUR 77,024 thousand in 2024, while EBITDA reached EUR 232,581 thousand, compared to EUR 145,415 thousand reported for 2024. Net profit for the 12-month period stood at EUR 71,969 thousand, down from EUR 193,670 thousand in 2024, with profit attributable to owners of the parent at EUR 72,662 thousand compared to EUR 191,253 thousand in the prior year. Earnings per share for 2025 were EUR 3.54 compared to EUR 9.36 in 2024. For the fourth quarter alone, revenue was EUR 417,438 thousand, gross profit was EUR 63,282 thousand, EBITDA was EUR 42,604 thousand, operating profit was EUR 19,842 thousand, and net profit was EUR 14,141 thousand, marking an improvement over the net loss of EUR 11,988 thousand recorded in the fourth quarter of 2024. 

The financial changes were influenced by several key developments and business events. The increase in full-year revenue, gross profit, and EBITDA was primarily driven by the full twelve-month consolidation of Tallink Grupp, whereas Tallink Grupp’s results in 2024 were only consolidated from August onwards. Additionally, Elenger’s EBITDA improved by approximately EUR 12 million, and Infortar's consolidated figures incorporated contributions from Estonia Farmid and Halinga. The decline in net profit compared to the previous year was mainly attributable to one-off effects related to the initial consolidation of Tallink Group recognised in 2024. On the balance sheet, total assets as of December 31, 2025, were EUR 2,587,818 thousand, compared to EUR 2,715,234 thousand at the end of 2024. Total equity stood at EUR 1,177,516 thousand, compared to EUR 1,166,221 thousand at the end of the previous year, resulting in an improved equity ratio of 45.5 percent compared to 43.0 percent. Interest-bearing liabilities decreased to EUR 1,060,453 thousand from EUR 1,223,287 thousand, reflecting loan repayments across several Group entities, including the Tallink Group level, and adjustments to financial positions in line with market conditions. Net debt decreased to EUR 840,682 thousand from EUR 1,055,708 thousand, and net debt to EBITDA improved to 3.6 from 4.0. Operating cash flow for the 12 months of 2025 was positively affected by a decrease in inventories related to deliberate and market-driven inventory management, an increase in operating liabilities resulting from the full 12-month consolidation of Tallink Group, and depreciation and impairment of property, plant, and equipment, while reflecting more stable operational performance without extensive one-off financial and fair value impacts seen in 2024.
