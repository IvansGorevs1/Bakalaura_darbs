AB “Pieno Žvaigždės” reported a significantly improved financial performance in the first quarter of 2026 despite broadly stable revenue. Revenue decreased slightly to EUR 52.064 million from EUR 52.324 million in the first quarter of 2025, a reduction of approximately 0.5%. However, cost of sales fell more substantially, to EUR 38.078 million from EUR 42.530 million, increasing gross profit by 42.8% to EUR 13.986 million.

Operating performance strengthened considerably. Other operating income, net, increased to EUR 363 thousand from EUR 28 thousand, while sales and administrative expenses rose to EUR 10.983 million from EUR 9.287 million. Despite the higher operating expenses, operating profit before finance costs increased to EUR 3.366 million, compared with EUR 535 thousand a year earlier. Net finance expenses remained broadly stable at EUR 298 thousand, versus EUR 308 thousand in the prior-year period. As a result, profit before tax and net profit rose to EUR 3.068 million from EUR 227 thousand. Earnings per share increased to EUR 0.07 from EUR 0.01. EBITDA more than doubled, reaching EUR 4.730 million compared with EUR 2.001 million.

Cash generation also improved. Net cash flow from operating activities was positive at EUR 1.625 million, compared with an outflow of EUR 183 thousand in the first quarter of 2025. Operating cash flow before working-capital changes increased to EUR 4.336 million from EUR 1.367 million. Investing activities used EUR 551 thousand, an improvement from EUR 1.058 million in the comparable period, mainly reflecting lower property, plant and equipment acquisitions and higher proceeds from asset disposals. Financing activities resulted in a net outflow of EUR 723 thousand, compared with a EUR 4.133 million inflow in the prior year, when the company received EUR 8.5 million in loans. Ending cash increased during the quarter by EUR 351 thousand to EUR 2.157 million, although it remained below the EUR 4.902 million reported at 31 March 2025.

At 31 March 2026, total assets were EUR 85.305 million, up from EUR 83.475 million at year-end 2025. Equity increased to EUR 41.164 million from EUR 38.238 million, primarily reflecting first-quarter profit, while total liabilities declined to EUR 44.141 million from EUR 45.237 million. The current ratio improved from 1.27 at 31 March 2025 to 1.51. Sales were mainly generated in Lithuania, with additional contributions from other EU and non-EU markets. The report states that there were no material post-balance-sheet events.
