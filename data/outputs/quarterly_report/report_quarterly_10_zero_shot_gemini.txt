During the financial year ended December 31, 2025, Infortar Group achieved consolidated revenue of 1,837,000 thousand euros, compared to 1,371,775 thousand euros in 2024. Gross profit for 2025 rose to 240,846 thousand euros from 128,628 thousand euros in the previous year. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization reached 232,581 thousand euros, up from 145,415 thousand euros in 2024, yielding an EBITDA margin of 13.4% compared to 10.6% previously. Operating profit for the 12-month period stood at 111,061 thousand euros, compared to 77,024 thousand euros in 2024. Net profit for the financial year was 71,969 thousand euros, down from 193,670 thousand euros reported in 2024, with net profit attributable to the owners of the parent amounting to 72,662 thousand euros compared to 191,253 thousand euros in the prior year. Earnings per share for 2025 were 3.54 euros basic and 3.47 euros diluted, reflecting a decrease from 9.36 euros basic and 9.12 euros diluted in 2024. For the fourth quarter of 2025 specifically, revenue was 417,438 thousand euros, gross profit was 63,282 thousand euros, EBITDA was 42,604 thousand euros, operating profit was 19,842 thousand euros, and net profit was 14,141 thousand euros. 

The most significant financial changes compared to the previous period included a reduction in total assets to 2,587,818 thousand euros from 2,715,234 thousand euros and a decrease in interest-bearing liabilities to 1,060,453 thousand euros from 1,223,287 thousand euros. Net debt improved to 840,682 thousand euros from 1,055,708 thousand euros, bringing the net debt to EBITDA ratio down to 3.6 from 4.0. Total equity increased slightly to 1,177,516 thousand euros from 1,166,221 thousand euros, and the equity ratio strengthened to 45.5% from 43.0%. The lower net profit compared to 2024 was primarily attributable to one-off effects related to the initial consolidation of Tallink Grupp recognized in the prior year. Furthermore, natural gas inventory decreased significantly to 26,027 thousand euros from 152,075 thousand euros due to deliberate and market-driven inventory management. 

The company’s results were affected by several business events and market factors across its core segments. Maritime transport operations through Tallink Grupp carried 5,531,132 passengers in 2025, representing a 0.9% decrease year-on-year, with Tallink reporting revenue of 765.3 million euros and net profit of 17.3 million euros. In the energy segment, Elenger Grupp increased its gas and electricity sales volumes by 7% in the fourth quarter to 4.8 TWh, despite regional consumption declining by 2%, and maintained a Finnish-Baltic market share of 21%. Energy operations were impacted by volatility in European gas prices, which declined sharply toward the end of the year before rising due to cold weather at the start of 2026. The real estate and infrastructure segments progressed with major projects such as Rail Baltica mainline construction and commercial space development. Additionally, the group expanded its agricultural footprint by acquiring a 100% stake in OÜ Oisu Biogaas in November 2025. 

Overall, the group demonstrated stable operational performance and strengthened its balance sheet resilience despite an economic environment marked by geopolitical tensions and uneven market cycles. Prudent leverage reduction, robust liquidity with cash and cash equivalents ending the year at 219,771 thousand euros, and portfolio diversification across maritime transport, energy, real estate, and agriculture contributed to steady cash flows and an improved financial footing for the future.
