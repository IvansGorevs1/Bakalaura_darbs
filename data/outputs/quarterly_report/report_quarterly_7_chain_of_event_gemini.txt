AS Silvano Fashion Group reported a consolidated revenue of 28,590 thousand euros for the first six months of 2026, representing a slight increase of 0.6% compared to 28,428 thousand euros for the same period in 2025. Gross profit improved by 5.3% to 15,892 thousand euros, up from 15,091 thousand euros, resulting in an increased gross margin of 55.6% compared to 53.1% in the previous year. Operating profit declined by 3.5% to 5,751 thousand euros from 5,961 thousand euros, and consolidated EBITDA decreased by 0.8% to 7,508 thousand euros from 7,566 thousand euros. Reported consolidated net profit for the six-month period experienced a significant decline of 31.2%, dropping to 4,321 thousand euros from 6,279 thousand euros, while net profit attributable to equity holders of the parent company decreased by 31.5% to 3,961 thousand euros from 5,781 thousand euros. Consequently, basic and diluted earnings per share fell by 31.3% to 0.11 euros from 0.16 euros.

The financial performance was influenced by diverging trends across business segments and geographic markets. Retail revenue demonstrated solid growth, increasing by 13.6% to 13,548 thousand euros and accounting for 47.4% of total sales, driven by an expansion in retail contributions. Conversely, wholesale revenue contracted by 9.1% to 14,934 thousand euros, representing 52.2% of total revenue. Geographically, Russia remained the Group's primary market with sales of 15,767 thousand euros, down 0.6% in euros and 7.8% in local currency, accounting for 55.1% of total sales. Sales in Belarus grew by 6.4% to 9,046 thousand euros, representing 31.6% of total revenue, with a 2.7% increase in local currency. Operations in Ukraine saw a sharp decline of 58.3% to 15 thousand euros, while the Baltics and other markets also experienced decreases. Operating cash flow for the period showed a substantial improvement, rising by 96.6% to 6,842 thousand euros compared to 3,481 thousand euros in the prior year.

As of 30 June 2026, the Group’s total assets stood at 98,603 thousand euros, reflecting a 5.1% increase from 93,847 thousand euros at the end of 2025. Total current assets rose by 7.2% to 85,377 thousand euros, supported by an increase in cash and cash equivalents to 12,443 thousand euros and short-term deposits of 38,707 thousand euros. Total equity attributable to equity holders of the parent company increased by 6.1% to 81,479 thousand euros. During the reporting period, the Group operated a network of 135 directly managed stores and 412 franchise stores across its markets, with capital expenditures focused primarily on store openings, renovations, and production equipment totaling 115 thousand euros.
