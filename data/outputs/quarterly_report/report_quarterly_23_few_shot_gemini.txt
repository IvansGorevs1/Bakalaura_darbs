The group reported an improvement in financial performance and profitability for the first nine months of 2025 compared with the same period of the previous year. Group revenue from contracts with customers increased by 13% to EUR 76,664 thousand, up from EUR 67,977 thousand during the first nine months of 2024. This top-line growth was supported by higher income from liquefied natural gas terminal regasification services collected directly from users, increased sales of liquid energy products transshipment services, and higher revenue from consulting services. Group operating profit rose to EUR 21,282 thousand compared with EUR 18,942 thousand in the prior-year period. Profit before tax for the group increased to EUR 16,186 thousand from EUR 14,910 thousand, while net profit attributable to the shareholders of the company grew to EUR 13,158 thousand compared with EUR 11,862 thousand. Basic and diluted earnings per share for the group increased to EUR 0.035 from EUR 0.031. For the parent company specifically, revenue from contracts with customers increased to EUR 72,848 thousand from EUR 65,009 thousand, operating profit grew to EUR 19,277 thousand from EUR 17,408 thousand, and profit for the period increased to EUR 11,613 thousand from EUR 10,702 thousand. The financial results were influenced by several operational and market factors. Revenue from liquefied natural gas terminal services benefited from higher regasification volumes, client bonuses for booked capacity, and the absence of the operational interruptions that occurred in the previous year, when the terminal underwent dry-dock repair works resulting in one and a half months of inactivity. Conversely, certain revenue streams were affected by reduced payments collected for unused allocated capacities and a decrease in LNG security supplement income driven by the proportionally recognized repayment of excess returns from regulated activities during earlier periods. Cost of sales for the group increased to EUR 45,115 thousand from EUR 38,740 thousand, primarily due to changes in asset classification following the acquisition of the floating storage and regasification unit in December 2024, which shifted expenses from right-of-use asset depreciation to property, plant, and equipment depreciation. Distribution costs and administrative expenses for the group increased moderately to EUR 10,640 thousand from EUR 10,419 thousand, driven largely by higher wages, salaries, social security, and consulting costs. Finance income for the group decreased to EUR 1,520 thousand from EUR 2,644 thousand, mainly because of lower interest income and the absence of currency exchange income recorded in the previous period, while finance costs remained relatively stable at EUR 6,616 thousand compared with EUR 6,692 thousand, though interest expenses on loans increased following financing adjustments. Overall, the reporting period was characterized by revenue expansion, higher operational activity, and improved net profitability for the group.
