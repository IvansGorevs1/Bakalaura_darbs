Amber Grid AB reported financial results for the three-month period ended 31 March 2026, showing a significant improvement in profitability and overall financial performance compared to the same period in 2025. 

For the three months ended 31 March 2026, the company generated total revenue of EUR 32,821 thousand, reflecting an increase compared to EUR 20,763 thousand for the three months ended 31 March 2025. Revenue from contracts with customers grew to EUR 32,787 thousand from EUR 20,735 thousand, primarily driven by a 58 percent increase in revenue from natural gas transmission and related services, which reached EUR 32,700 thousand compared to EUR 16,297 thousand in the prior-year period. This growth was influenced by a 35 percent increase in the volume of natural gas transmitted and higher transmission prices set for 2026. Conversely, revenue from system balancing products decreased to EUR 24 thousand from EUR 4,403 thousand due to a new transit agreement signed in 2026 that reduced the need for balancing gas. 

Total operating expenses, excluding depreciation, write-off, and impairment, decreased to EUR 9,570 thousand for the period ended 31 March 2026 from EUR 11,867 thousand for the period ended 31 March 2025. This decrease was largely driven by a 68 percent reduction in purchases of natural gas, which fell to EUR 1,477 thousand from EUR 4,622 thousand, primarily resulting from lower demand for balancing gas. Meanwhile, wages, salaries, and related expenses increased to EUR 4,367 thousand from EUR 3,659 thousand. 

Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA) rose significantly to EUR 23,304 thousand for the first quarter of 2026, compared to EUR 8,918 thousand for the first quarter of 2025. Operating profit (EBIT) increased to EUR 19,627 thousand from EUR 5,185 thousand. Net profit for the period ended 31 March 2026 surged to EUR 15,763 thousand, compared to EUR 4,239 thousand for the corresponding period in 2025. Consequently, basic and diluted earnings per share increased to EUR 0.09 for the period ended 31 March 2026, up from EUR 0.02 for the period ended 31 March 2025. 

As of 31 March 2026, the company’s total assets stood at EUR 324,064 thousand, compared to EUR 324,739 thousand as of 31 December 2025. Total equity increased to EUR 182,039 thousand from EUR 166,276 thousand. Net debt stood at EUR 106,610 thousand at the end of March 2026, compared to EUR 76,052 thousand at the end of March 2025. Net cash flows from operating activities improved to EUR 22,927 thousand for the three months ended 31 March 2026, compared to EUR 8,264 thousand for the same period in 2025. Net cash flows used in investing activities were EUR 3,570 thousand, while net cash flows used in financing activities amounted to EUR 20,467 thousand.
