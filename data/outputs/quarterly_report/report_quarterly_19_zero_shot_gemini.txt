During the twelve months of 2025, AS Pro Kapital Grupp achieved strong financial results characterized by a return to profitability and a significant increase in revenue compared to the previous year. Total revenue for 2025 reached 53.16 million euros, compared to 18.16 million euros in 2024. For the fourth quarter of 2025, revenue amounted to 18.90 million euros, compared to 7.55 million euros in the fourth quarter of 2024. The substantial growth in real estate sales was driven primarily by the handover of completed apartments in the Kalaranna and Uus Kindrali developments in Tallinn, as well as the City Villas project in Vilnius, whereas apartment handovers in the Kalaranna District only commenced in December 2024. 

Gross profit for 2025 rose to 18.80 million euros, yielding a gross profit margin of 35 percent, compared with 5.42 million euros and 30 percent in 2024. Operating profit for the twelve-month period improved markedly to 14.73 million euros, representing an operating result of 28 percent, up from 0.12 million euros and 1 percent in the prior year. The company recorded a net profit of 12.04 million euros for 2025, reversing the net loss of 3.88 million euros reported in 2024. Fourth-quarter net profit stood at 6.02 million euros, compared to 0.19 million euros in the final quarter of 2024. Basic earnings per share for 2025 were positive at 0.22 euros, compared to negative 0.06 euros in 2024. Cash generated from operating activities reached 10.81 million euros in 2025, marking a strong recovery from cash used of 10.36 million euros in 2024. Net asset value per share increased to 1.12 euros as of 31 December 2025, up from 0.89 euros a year earlier. 

The financial position of the company strengthened during the reporting period. Total assets increased to 124.49 million euros as of 31 December 2025, compared to 118.76 million euros at the end of 2024. Total liabilities decreased to 61.16 million euros from 67.54 million euros, while total equity rose to 63.33 million euros from 51.22 million euros. The debt-to-equity ratio improved to 0.97 from 1.32. Financing activities during the year included the partial redemption of secured non-convertible bonds by 9.41 million euros, reducing the outstanding nominal amount to 10.55 million euros, alongside bank loan transactions resulting in total bank loan debt of 23.60 million euros at year-end. 

Business developments during 2025 featured continued execution across key residential projects in the Baltic capitals. In Tallinn, construction of the white building in the Uus Kindrali project was completed, with 39 residential units closed by year-end, and construction advanced on a second residential building. In Riga, construction commenced on the Blue Marine project within the Klīversala Quarter, and a construction loan was secured with BluOr Bank. In Vilnius, the final stage of Šaltinių Namai Attico neared completion, with initial residential units handed over to customers in December 2025. Additionally, the company recorded a fair value gain on investment property of 3.47 million euros, which contributed positively to operating income, while recognizing a full impairment loss of 0.86 million euros on goodwill associated with its Italian subsidiary Preatoni Nuda Proprietà.
