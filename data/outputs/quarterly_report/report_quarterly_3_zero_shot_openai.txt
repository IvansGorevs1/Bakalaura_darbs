AS Amber Latvijas balzams reported a significant deterioration in performance in the first three months of 2026. Sales volume fell 36.1% year on year to 517,600 9-liter cases, while net turnover decreased 29.6% to EUR 11.1 million. Management attributed the revenue decline primarily to lower sales volumes and a high comparison base in the first quarter of 2025, when contractual revenue adjustments had positively affected sales.

Gross profit declined 66.5% to EUR 1.2 million from EUR 3.5 million. The reduction reflected changes in product mix and pricing methodology; cost reductions achieved during the period largely offset the impact of these factors. The company recorded an operating loss of EUR 1.6 million, compared with an operating profit of EUR 0.6 million in the prior-year period, reducing the operating margin to -14.5% from 3.7%. Net finance income was EUR 0.2 million, but the company still reported a net loss of EUR 1.4 million, compared with a profit of EUR 0.9 million in the first quarter of 2025. Earnings per share consequently declined to a loss of EUR 0.19 from earnings of EUR 0.11.

Results were affected by several major external and financial pressures. In January 2026, Stoli Group (USA), LLC, the company’s largest customer, entered Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceedings and a trustee was appointed, resulting in the group losing control of its U.S. operations. The company also cited the downturn in the global spirits market, weaker export demand, changing consumer behavior, economic pressure on discretionary spending, and inventory adjustments in distribution channels. Geopolitical developments following the group’s support for Ukraine also led Russian authorities to designate related entities as extremist and confiscate the group-owned Talvis distillery.

These factors created substantial cash-flow difficulties and delayed excise tax payments. After discussions concerning overdue excise liabilities, Latvia’s State Revenue Service froze the company’s bank accounts. On 30 January 2026, the company applied for legal protection proceedings, which the Riga City Court approved on 5 February. The proceedings are intended to restructure liabilities and stabilize operations; the action-plan deadline was later extended to 5 October 2026. The filing also triggered defaults under the principal shareholder’s bond and bank financing arrangements.

Operating cash flow was negative at EUR 1.8 million, and cash decreased to EUR 38,318 at 31 March 2026. Management is reviewing operations and continues measures to improve production efficiency, reduce costs, strengthen financial controls, and restore profitability.
