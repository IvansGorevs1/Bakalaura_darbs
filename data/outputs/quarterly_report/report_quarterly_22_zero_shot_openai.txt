Telia Lietuva reported strong growth in the first half of 2026. Revenue increased 7.2% year on year to EUR 259.1 million, supported by higher mobile services, fixed services and equipment sales. Mobile services revenue rose 8.3% to EUR 100.0 million, fixed services increased 5.3% to EUR 112.9 million, and equipment sales grew 9.6% to EUR 46.1 million. Within fixed services, IT services were particularly strong, increasing 23.9%.

Profitability improved faster than revenue. EBITDA rose 9.5% to EUR 105.5 million, with the margin increasing to 40.7% from 39.9%. Adjusted EBITDA excluding non-recurring items increased 10.2% to EUR 106.9 million, and the adjusted margin reached 41.3%. Operating profit grew 15.1% to EUR 62.4 million, while profit before tax increased 16.6% to EUR 61.3 million. Profit for the period rose 14.7% to EUR 51.3 million, increasing the profit margin from 18.5% to 19.8%; earnings per share rose from EUR 0.077 to EUR 0.088.

Cost of goods and services increased 2.3%, reflecting higher equipment sales volumes and increased network interconnection and roaming costs. Operating expenses rose 11.3%, mainly because of higher energy costs, external IT services and common Group-function expenses. The company also recorded EUR 1.4 million of non-recurring redundancy expenses related to restructuring its Digital & Technology unit. Headcount declined by 6.9% to 1,601.

Cash generation strengthened, with net cash from operating activities up 15.0% to EUR 103.1 million and free cash flow up 12.5% to EUR 64.1 million. Capital investments increased 57.9% to EUR 37.5 million, including EUR 9.7 million for 5G spectrum licenses, higher mobile-network and IT investment, and continued infrastructure development. As a result, the company ended June with net cash of EUR 4.3 million, compared with net debt of EUR 27.5 million a year earlier.

Major developments included the acquisition of two 1,500 MHz frequency blocks for almost EUR 8 million and extension of rights to use the 2,100 MHz band for EUR 2 million. The company also announced EUR 7.1 million of 2026 mobile-network investment and began construction of a new data center near Vilnius, with planned investment of up to EUR 26 million. In May, it paid EUR 81.6 million in dividends for 2025, or EUR 0.14 per share.
