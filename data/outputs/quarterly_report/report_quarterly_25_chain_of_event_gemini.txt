During the first three months of 2026, VILVI Group reported consolidated sales revenue of 86.97 million euros, representing an increase of 22.4 percent compared to the same period in 2025 when revenue was 71.07 million euros. EBITDA for the first three months of 2026 reached 3.00 million euros, marking a decrease of 50.2 percent compared to 6.03 million euros reported in the first three months of 2025. This EBITDA figure for the first three months of 2026 excludes preliminary negative goodwill of 5.54 million euros related to the acquisition of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB. The Group's net profit for the first three months of 2026 amounted to 5.085 million euros, which is a 25.7 percent increase compared to 4.045 million euros in the corresponding period of 2025. Basic and diluted earnings per share rose by 26.5 percent to 0.43 euros from 0.34 euros. Net financial debt increased by 89.7 percent to 83.43 million euros as of March 31, 2026, up from 43.97 million euros on March 31, 2025. The volume of basic milk purchased during the first three months of 2026 grew by 54.2 percent to 155,954 tons, compared to 101,118 tons in the first three months of 2025.

Revenue growth was distributed across various markets and operating segments. Sales revenue in the European Union reached 42.52 million euros, accounting for 49 percent of total revenue. Revenue in Lithuania increased by 72.9 percent to 14.63 million euros, representing 17 percent of the total, while revenue in other countries grew by 46.6 percent to 29.82 million euros, representing 34 percent of total revenue. By operating segment, revenue from cheese, cheese products, and other decreased by 28.8 percent to 37.64 million euros. Revenue from fresh milk products increased by 73.4 percent to 12.75 million euros. Dry milk products generated 24.66 million euros, reflecting a 126.9 percent increase. Canned milk, which was consolidated starting January 1, 2026, following the acquisition of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB, contributed 11.92 million euros in revenue.

Significant business developments during the period included Vilkyškių pieninė AB completing the acquisition of 100 percent of the shares of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB on January 16, 2026. Additionally, VILVI Group officially commenced commercial cheese production at its new cheese factory operated by Baltic Dairy Board SIA in Bauska, Latvia, which has a planned production volume of 18 thousand tons per year.
