Ambu delivered a solid start to 2025/26, with Q1 revenue of DKK 1,558m, up 3.2% on a reported basis and 8.6% organically from DKK 1,510m in Q1 2024/25. Organic growth was lower than the previous year’s 19.5%, reflecting high comparables, while foreign exchange reduced reported growth by 5.4 percentage points.

Endoscopy Solutions remained the main growth engine, generating 63% of revenue and 14.4% organic growth, compared with 20.6% in the prior-year quarter. Respiratory grew organically by 8.3%, supported by continued adoption of aScope 5 Broncho and the launch of the SureSight video laryngoscope solution. Urology, ENT, & GI recorded particularly strong organic growth of 21.0%, driven mainly by the aScope 4 portfolio and continued momentum for aScope 5 Uretero; growth also benefited from some orders typically expected in Q2. Anesthesia & Patient Monitoring was broadly flat, with organic growth of -0.1% versus 17.8% last year, reflecting an exceptionally high prior-year revenue base. North America and Europe grew organically by 9.8% and 9.7%, respectively, while Rest of World declined 1.6% because of order fluctuations and high comparables.

Profitability declined year on year. EBIT before special items fell to DKK 164m from DKK 243m, and the EBIT margin decreased to 10.5% from 16.1%. Tariff costs of more than DKK 50m and foreign-exchange headwinds were the principal reported pressures; excluding these effects, the EBIT margin was 15.2%. The gross margin declined slightly to 60.8% from 61.3%, while the operating-expense ratio increased to 50.3% from 45.2%, partly because of continued investment in commercial infrastructure and the inclusion of tariff costs in operating expenses. Net profit decreased to DKK 118m from DKK 183m, and EPS fell to DKK 0.44 from DKK 0.68.

Cash generation was weaker. Operating cash flow declined to DKK 106m from DKK 144m, and free cash flow before acquisitions fell to DKK 13m from DKK 69m. The main drag was an adverse DKK 107m change in working capital, as inventories and working capital remained elevated to support growth and mitigate geopolitical uncertainties. Ambu paid DKK 110m in dividends and repurchased DKK 38m of treasury shares during the quarter.

The company maintained its full-year guidance for 10–13% organic revenue growth, a 12–14% EBIT margin before special items, and cash conversion above 40%. Growth and margin are expected to be higher in the second half of the financial year, while tariffs are expected to reduce the EBIT margin by approximately two percentage points under current assumptions.
