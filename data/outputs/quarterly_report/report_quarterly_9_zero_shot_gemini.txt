Hepsor AS reported consolidated revenue of 35.4 million euros for the twelve months ended 31 December 2025, compared with 38.4 million euros in the previous year. Group net profit for 2025 amounted to 1.0 million euros, down from 2.1 million euros in 2024. Net profit attributable to the owners of the parent company was 0.399 million euros in 2025, compared with 0.423 million euros in 2024. For the fourth quarter of 2025, consolidated revenue stood at 4.5 million euros, compared with 10.5 million euros in the fourth quarter of 2024, resulting in a net loss of 0.486 million euros against a net profit of 0.957 million euros in the prior-year period. The net loss attributable to the owners of the parent for the fourth quarter of 2025 was 0.538 million euros, compared with a net profit of 0.578 million euros in the fourth quarter of 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share for 2025 were 0.10 euros, compared with 0.11 euros in 2024.

The financial results were primarily affected by a decrease in the number of apartments handed over to customers, totaling 141 apartments in 2025 compared with 194 apartments in 2024. Profitability was further impacted by lower gross profit margins on sold development projects, which stood at 15.9% in 2025 compared with 17.6% in 2024, and higher marketing expenses of 1.3 million euros compared with 0.9 million euros in 2024, driven by increased sales and marketing activities across a growing number of projects on sale. Operating profit for 2025 reached 3.4 million euros, down from 4.3 million euros in 2024, while EBITDA was 3.5 million euros compared with 4.5 million euros in the previous year. 

Total assets of the Group increased by 3.4% to 91.8 million euros as at 31 December 2025, up from 88.8 million euros a year earlier. Inventories decreased to 58.9 million euros from 64.1 million euros, representing 64.2% of total assets, influenced by apartment deliveries, land plot sales, and reclassifications to investment property, offset by the addition of three new residential development projects. Total interest-bearing loan liabilities decreased to 47.7 million euros from 54.7 million euros, with the debt ratio improving to 52.0% from 61.6%. Total equity increased by 23.0% to 27.1 million euros, primarily driven by non-cash contributions from non-controlling interests to the voluntary reserve of a subsidiary, resulting in an equity ratio of 29.5% compared with 24.8% at the end of 2024. Cash and cash equivalents stood at 3.8 million euros as at 31 December 2025, down from 6.2 million euros at the beginning of the year. Operating cash flow for 2025 was positive at 14.3 million euros, supported by working capital changes and non-cash transfers, while cash flows from investing and financing activities were negative at 7.5 million euros and 9.5 million euros, respectively. In November 2025, the Group successfully completed a public bond issue raising 8.0 million euros at a fixed annual interest rate of 9.50%.

Overall, the company experienced a contraction in revenue and net profitability due to lower apartment handover volumes and reduced project margins, while expanding its active development portfolio and securing long-term bond financing to support future operations.
