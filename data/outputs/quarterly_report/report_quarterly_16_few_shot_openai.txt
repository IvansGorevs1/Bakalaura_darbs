Nordic Fibreboard AS increased revenue in the first quarter of 2026, but profitability deteriorated substantially. Consolidated revenue rose 7.3% to €1.861 million from €1.735 million in Q1 2025, with almost all revenue generated by fibreboard sales. Growth was driven mainly by Finnish DIY stores, where order volume increased by €241 thousand to €855 thousand. Sales to European Union markets rose to €1.781 million from €1.639 million, while sales to Africa declined and limited sales began in Asia.

Despite higher sales, the Group’s gross result shifted to a €105 thousand loss from a €127 thousand profit. Cost of goods sold increased to €1.966 million from €1.608 million, primarily because electricity, heat and water costs rose to €1.048 million from €725 thousand. Results were affected by the scheduled factory shutdown in January during the transition to a new boiler house, which required approximately two months for calibration and start-up. Cold weather also increased energy consumption, while gas prices nearly doubled in February following a US military attack on Iran and remained approximately 50% above their beginning-of-year level thereafter.

EBITDA declined to negative €395 thousand from negative €85 thousand, reducing the EBITDA margin to negative 21% from negative 5%. Operating loss widened to €530 thousand from €217 thousand, while the operating margin deteriorated to negative 28% from negative 13%. The fibreboard segment recorded EBITDA of negative €321 thousand and a net loss of €496 thousand, compared with negative €62 thousand and a €204 thousand loss, respectively, in the prior-year quarter. Group net loss increased to €570 thousand from €228 thousand. Financial expenses decreased to €41 thousand from €51 thousand, but Q1 2025 had included €40 thousand of financial income from the sale of Trigon Property Development shares, which was absent in 2026.

The balance sheet expanded, with total assets rising to €9.694 million from €9.323 million at year-end 2025 and €8.722 million a year earlier. Equity fell to €3.892 million from €4.462 million at year-end, reflecting the quarterly loss, while total borrowings increased to €3.320 million from €3.168 million. The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 60% from 52%. Cash remained limited at €1 thousand at quarter-end. Operating activities generated €13 thousand of cash, compared with an outflow of €528 thousand a year earlier, but €169 thousand was invested in production assets and net cash decreased by €4 thousand.

The Group completed its boiler-system upgrade and continues developing interior finishing panels and Nordic-market sales. It also signed a preliminary agreement to sell ten Pärnu properties by 30 June 2026, subject to the agreement’s conditions. Management expects fibreboard demand and sales volumes to remain stable in the coming months.
