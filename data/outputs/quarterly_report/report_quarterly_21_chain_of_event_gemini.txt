For the six-month period ended 30 June 2026, AS Tallinna Sadam reported revenue of EUR 59,262 thousand, reflecting an increase of EUR 1,399 thousand or 2.4% compared to EUR 57,862 thousand in the same period of 2025. Despite the revenue growth, the Group experienced a decline in profitability. Operating profit dropped by EUR 4,708 thousand or 25.7% to EUR 13,578 thousand compared to EUR 18,286 thousand in the prior-year period. Adjusted EBITDA decreased by EUR 4,036 thousand or 13.5% to EUR 25,769 thousand from EUR 29,805 thousand. Profit for the period fell by EUR 4,287 thousand or 41.7% to EUR 5,999 thousand down from EUR 10,286 thousand, resulting in basic and diluted earnings per share of EUR 0.02 compared to EUR 0.04 in the previous year. Total assets at period-end stood at EUR 615,034 thousand, showing a 1.1% decrease from EUR 622,079 thousand at the end of the comparative period, while equity at period-end was EUR 367,677 thousand, and net debt was EUR 145,764 thousand.

The decline in financial performance was primarily driven by higher operating expenses, which rose by EUR 3,907 thousand or 24.0% to EUR 20,184 thousand, influenced significantly by increased fuel costs and electricity expenses, alongside higher depreciation, amortisation and impairment charges of EUR 12,867 thousand compared to EUR 12,140 thousand previously. Furthermore, the financial results for the comparative period in 2025 had benefited from one-off items, specifically the profit gained from the disposal of land for the Rail Baltica project and insurance indemnities received. Operational volumes also contracted during the period, with total cargo volume decreasing by 5.0% to 6,481 thousand tonnes primarily due to a 33.8% drop in liquid bulk, passenger numbers falling by 2.0% to 3,586 thousand, and vessel calls declining by 4.8% to 3,376 calls. These negative factors were partially offset by positive drivers such as an increased number of charter days and a higher utility rate for the icebreaker Botnica, higher revenue from the sale of electricity, and indexation-driven increases in ferry service revenues. Investments during the period totalled EUR 3,529 thousand, representing a 70.6% decrease compared to EUR 12,020 thousand in the first six months of 2025.
