AS Tallink Grupp reported a challenging but profitable 2025, with performance affected by economic uncertainty, weak consumer and business confidence, geopolitical tensions and reduced cargo demand in its core markets. Revenue declined 2.6% to EUR 765.3 million from EUR 785.8 million in 2024. Passengers carried decreased 0.9% to 5.53 million, cargo units fell 19.2% to 245,004, and passenger vehicles declined 2.2% to 760,473.

Route operations revenue decreased by EUR 13.7 million to EUR 625.2 million, while the segment result fell to EUR 70.5 million from EUR 81.5 million. Estonia–Finland passenger numbers increased 1.8% and revenue rose to EUR 314.5 million, but the segment result declined by EUR 10.9 million to EUR 70.4 million. Finland–Sweden revenue fell to EUR 225.5 million and the segment result decreased to EUR 1.3 million, partly reflecting 68 days of vessel maintenance. Estonia–Sweden revenue declined to EUR 85.3 million, while its segment loss improved to EUR 1.2 million. The Other segment generated EUR 145.5 million of revenue and a EUR 28.5 million result, broadly in line with 2024.

EBITDA decreased substantially to EUR 130.1 million from EUR 175.2 million, and net profit fell to EUR 17.3 million from EUR 40.3 million. Earnings per share declined to EUR 0.023 from EUR 0.054. The reduction in depreciation and amortisation expense, down 18.4% to EUR 79.8 million, supported operating profitability after vessel useful lives were aligned to 45 years and three vessels were sold. Net finance costs also declined 24.2% to EUR 21.4 million due to lower loan balances and interest rates. However, operating costs increased 1.4%, including a 5.1% rise in staff costs and a 0.8% increase in fuel costs.

Fourth-quarter performance improved year-on-year. Revenue rose 2.4% to EUR 188.0 million, EBITDA increased to EUR 27.6 million from EUR 25.7 million, and the Group recorded a EUR 12.2 million net profit compared with a EUR 5.2 million loss in Q4 2024. Route operations revenue increased to EUR 152.0 million and its result rose to EUR 16.1 million.

The Group sold three vessels, chartered out three vessels, and reduced vessels in lay-up to zero by the third quarter. Capital expenditure increased to EUR 33.0 million, mainly for vessel maintenance and refurbishment. Net debt remained broadly unchanged at EUR 432.4 million, while the net debt-to-EBITDA ratio was 3.3. The Group paid EUR 44.6 million in dividends and intends to propose another EUR 0.06-per-share dividend for 2026.
