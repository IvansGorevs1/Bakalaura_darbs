Infortar Group’s financial performance improved substantially in the first half of 2026 despite broadly stable revenue and weaker demand in several markets. Six-month revenue decreased by 0.6% year on year to EUR 945.9 million, primarily because lower wholesale gas and electricity sales reduced energy-segment revenue. However, gross profit increased from EUR 81.7 million to EUR 114.1 million, while EBITDA rose 30.8% to EUR 111.2 million and the EBITDA margin improved from 8.9% to 11.8%. Operating profit nearly doubled to EUR 51.1 million from EUR 26.4 million.

The Group returned to profitability. Profit before tax increased to EUR 27.2 million from EUR 2.7 million, and net profit was EUR 7.2 million compared with a loss of EUR 14.4 million in the prior-year period. Profit attributable to the parent’s owners was EUR 15.2 million, versus a loss of EUR 2.5 million. Basic earnings per share improved to EUR 0.74 from negative EUR 0.12. The results benefited from stronger energy performance, the addition of agricultural units, closure of unprofitable operations and a major turnaround in supporting businesses, whose EBITDA improved from a EUR 4.4 million loss to a EUR 5.6 million profit.

Energy sales volumes fell 34% to 3.3 TWh, mainly due to reduced wholesale activity, while regional natural-gas consumption declined 7%. Nevertheless, the energy segment’s six-month EBITDA increased to EUR 59.9 million from EUR 51.7 million. Maritime transport EBITDA rose to EUR 39.3 million from EUR 33.3 million, although second-quarter EBITDA declined to EUR 34.3 million from EUR 37.4 million. Tallink carried 2.2% fewer passengers, while cargo volumes increased 2.9%. Higher fuel prices raised costs by EUR 8.4 million, and the EU emissions requirement increased to 100% of emissions from 70% in 2025. Real estate and supporting activities also improved.

The Group invested approximately EUR 50 million during the first half, including EUR 48.5 million in property, plant and equipment. Operating cash flow decreased to EUR 99.5 million from EUR 256.6 million, partly reflecting an EUR 88.4 million increase in inventories, while investing cash outflow was EUR 45.4 million. Financing cash outflow declined sharply to EUR 20.6 million from EUR 307.6 million, and cash increased to EUR 253.2 million from EUR 219.8 million at year-end.

Total assets rose to EUR 2.71 billion and equity to EUR 1.20 billion, while the equity ratio decreased to 44.2% from 45.3%. Net debt declined to EUR 829.9 million, and net debt to EBITDA improved from 3.7 to 3.3. Infortar also increased its Tallink ownership to 68.76%. Geopolitical tensions, energy and fuel-price volatility, interest rates and demand conditions remain important risks.
