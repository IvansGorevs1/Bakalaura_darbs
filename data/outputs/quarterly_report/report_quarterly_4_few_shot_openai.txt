Ambu reported a solid start to fiscal 2025/26, with revenue growth continuing despite a high comparative base and significant foreign-exchange and tariff headwinds. Revenue increased to DKK 1,558m from DKK 1,510m, representing reported growth of 3.2% and organic growth of 8.6%, compared with 19.5% organic growth in Q1 2024/25. Growth was led by Endoscopy Solutions, while Anesthesia & Patient Monitoring was broadly unchanged.

Endoscopy Solutions remained the main growth engine, producing 14.4% organic growth and accounting for 63% of group revenue. Respiratory grew organically by 8.3%, supported by continued adoption of aScope 5 Broncho and the launch of the SureSight video laryngoscope portfolio. Urology, ENT, & GI delivered particularly strong organic growth of 21.0%, driven mainly by continued penetration of the aScope 4 portfolio and strong demand for aScope 5 Uretero. Anesthesia & Patient Monitoring recorded organic growth of -0.1%, reflecting exceptionally high revenue in the same quarter of the prior year; Anesthesia declined 1.2%, while Patient Monitoring increased 1.1%.

Geographically, organic growth remained solid in North America and Europe, at 9.8% and 9.7%, respectively. Rest of World declined by 1.6%, affected by order fluctuations and a high prior-year comparison. Currency movements reduced total reported growth by 5.4%.

Profitability declined compared with the prior-year quarter. EBIT before special items fell to DKK 164m from DKK 243m, and the EBIT margin decreased to 10.5% from 16.1%. The margin was affected by tariff costs exceeding DKK 50m and foreign-exchange headwinds. Excluding these factors, the EBIT margin was 15.2%, indicating continued underlying operating leverage. The gross margin declined slightly to 60.8% from 61.3%, while the operating expense ratio increased to 50.3% from 45.2%, partly reflecting continued investment in commercial infrastructure and the inclusion of tariff costs in operating expenses. Net profit decreased to DKK 118m from DKK 183m, and earnings per share fell to DKK 0.44 from DKK 0.68.

Cash generation weakened, with operating cash flow declining to DKK 106m from DKK 144m and free cash flow before acquisitions falling to DKK 13m from DKK 69m. The reduction was mainly caused by lower EBITDA and elevated working capital, maintained to support growth and mitigate geopolitical uncertainties. Ambu paid DKK 110m in dividends and spent DKK 38m on treasury shares during the quarter.

Ambu maintained its full-year guidance for 10–13% organic revenue growth, an EBIT margin before special items of 12–14%, and cash conversion above 40%. Growth and profitability are expected to be higher in the second half of the year.
