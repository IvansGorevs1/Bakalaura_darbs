SAF Tehnika reported a consolidated net turnover of EUR 26.11 million for the twelve months ended June 30, 2025, representing a 4% decline compared to EUR 27.09 million in the previous financial year. For the fourth quarter alone, net turnover decreased by 13% to EUR 5.18 million from EUR 5.98 million in the prior year period. The company recorded a net loss of EUR 820 thousand for the full year, a significant improvement from the EUR 2.37 million loss reported in the 2023/2024 financial year. However, the fourth quarter saw an increased loss of EUR 913 thousand, compared to a EUR 498 thousand loss in the same quarter of the previous year. Earnings before interest and taxes for the twelve months were a loss of EUR 754 thousand, while the quarterly EBIT loss widened to EUR 863 thousand.

Regional performance varied significantly. In North and Latin America, which accounted for 55% of annual turnover, revenue fell by 14% to EUR 14.28 million. In contrast, European revenues grew by 17% to EUR 10.04 million, representing 38% of total turnover. The Asia, Africa, and Middle East region saw a slight 5% decline to EUR 1.78 million. Quarterly analysis showed a 40% drop in North and Latin American sales, while European sales increased by 24% and Asia, Africa, and Middle East sales doubled. The management attributed quarterly fluctuations to the timing of project completions and revenue recognition, noting that dispatch of goods often occurs in subsequent quarters.

Operating expenses remained substantial, with salaries and social expenses increasing by 8% to EUR 10.58 million, and bonuses rising sharply to EUR 1.82 million from EUR 942 thousand. The Group continued to invest in product development and sales promotion, with expenses not exceeding budgeted amounts. The global shortage of electronic components affected operations, but the company had accumulated material reserves to maintain delivery times, which have recently improved.

Financial position remained strong with a net cash flow of EUR 2.6 million for the year and a cash balance of EUR 4.8 million at period end. Customer advances increased substantially to EUR 3.9 million from EUR 759 thousand the previous year. Total assets grew to EUR 25.9 million, driven by an 8% increase in inventories to EUR 13.9 million. The company maintained liquidity through an unused EUR 4.95 million credit line with Luminor Bank. Employee count increased slightly to 273 from 265. The Board remains cautious regarding future forecasts, aiming to stabilize turnover to ensure long-term positive net results amidst ongoing global business uncertainty.
