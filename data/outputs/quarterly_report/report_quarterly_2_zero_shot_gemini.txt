During the three-month period ended 31 March 2026, Amber Grid AB experienced a strong overall improvement in its financial performance compared to the same period in 2025. The company reported total revenue of EUR 32,821 thousand, representing an increase from EUR 20,763 thousand for the three-month period ended 31 March 2025. Operating profit (EBIT) grew significantly to EUR 19,627 thousand, compared to EUR 5,185 thousand in the previous period. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortisation (EBITDA) reached EUR 23,304 thousand, up from EUR 8,918 thousand. Net profit for the reporting period rose to EUR 15,763 thousand, compared to EUR 4,239 thousand for the period ended 31 March 2025. Consequently, basic and diluted earnings per share increased to EUR 0.09 from EUR 0.02. Net cash flows from operating activities also improved to EUR 22,927 thousand, compared to EUR 8,264 thousand in the prior-year period.

The company's results were primarily driven by developments in the natural gas transmission sector. Revenue from the transmission of natural gas within Lithuania rose to EUR 32,700 thousand from EUR 16,297 thousand, which was influenced by a 35 percent increase in transmitted natural gas volumes and higher transmission prices set for 2026. Conversely, revenue from system balancing products declined sharply to EUR 24 thousand from EUR 4,403 thousand, driven by a new transit agreement to Kaliningrad signed in 2026 that substantially reduced the need for balancing gas. Correspondingly, purchases of natural gas and related services decreased to EUR 1,477 thousand from EUR 4,622 thousand, largely due to a 99 percent drop in balancing product expenses, although costs for technological needs increased due to a 2.7-fold rise in gas demand for technological purposes. Wages and salaries, and related expenses increased to EUR 4,367 thousand from EUR 3,659 thousand, and other expenses rose slightly to EUR 3,231 thousand from EUR 3,015 thousand. Total finance costs, net, increased to EUR 626 thousand from EUR 408 thousand, and current year income tax expense rose to EUR 3,276 thousand from EUR 827 thousand.

Regarding the company’s financial position and structure, total assets stood at EUR 324,064 thousand as at 31 March 2026, remaining stable compared to EUR 324,739 thousand as at 31 December 2025. Total equity increased to EUR 182,039 thousand from EUR 166,276 thousand. Non-current liabilities rose to EUR 103,374 thousand from EUR 55,503 thousand, while current liabilities decreased to EUR 38,651 thousand from EUR 102,960 thousand. These shifts in borrowings were largely affected by the expiration and transfer of specific loan agreements, including the Nordic Investment Bank loan agreement expiring on 25 February 2026, which led to the reclassification of certain borrowings between current and non-current liabilities. Net debt stood at EUR 106,610 thousand as at 31 March 2026, compared to EUR 76,052 thousand as at 31 March 2025. Subsequent to the reporting period, on 17 April 2026, the General Meeting of Shareholders approved a total allocation of EUR 9,989 thousand for dividend payments, equating to EUR 0.0560 per share.
