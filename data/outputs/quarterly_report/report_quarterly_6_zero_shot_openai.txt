Infortar Group reported a significant improvement in profitability in the first half of 2026 despite broadly stable revenue. Six-month revenue decreased 0.6% year on year to EUR 945.9 million, while EBITDA increased 30.8% to EUR 111.2 million and the EBITDA margin rose from 8.9% to 11.8%. Operating profit almost doubled, increasing 93.6% to EUR 51.1 million. Profit before tax rose to EUR 27.2 million from EUR 2.7 million, and the Group generated net profit of EUR 7.2 million compared with a EUR 14.4 million loss in the prior-year period. Net profit attributable to the parent’s owners was EUR 15.2 million, versus a EUR 2.5 million loss, resulting in basic earnings per share of EUR 0.74 compared with negative EUR 0.12.

Second-quarter revenue declined 12.6% to EUR 440.9 million, but gross profit increased to EUR 59.4 million from EUR 55.7 million. EBITDA rose 11.8% to EUR 64.2 million, operating profit increased 9.8% to EUR 29.7 million, and net profit reached EUR 1.9 million compared with EUR 0.2 million in Q2 2025. The improvement reflected stronger performance across all segments, particularly supporting businesses, which moved from a EUR 4.4 million EBITDA loss in the first half of 2025 to EUR 5.6 million of EBITDA in 2026. Energy EBITDA increased to EUR 59.9 million from EUR 51.7 million, while maritime transport EBITDA rose to EUR 39.3 million from EUR 33.3 million. Real estate EBITDA increased to EUR 6.5 million from EUR 4.4 million.

Energy revenue declined as Elenger’s gas and electricity sales volumes fell 34% to 3.3 TWh, mainly because of lower wholesale sales. Regional gas consumption decreased 7% to 7.0 TWh. Results in maritime transport were affected by weaker demand, higher fuel prices, increased EU ETS emissions costs and geopolitical volatility. Tallink carried 1.45 million passengers in Q2, down 2.2% year on year, while cargo units increased 2.9%. Fuel costs rose by EUR 8.4 million, and Tallink’s EBITDA declined to EUR 34.3 million from EUR 37.4 million.

Total assets increased to EUR 2.71 billion from EUR 2.60 billion at year-end, while equity rose to EUR 1.20 billion. Cash increased to EUR 253.2 million, and net debt declined to EUR 829.9 million from EUR 851.6 million. The Group invested approximately EUR 50 million during the first half. Infortar increased its Tallink ownership to 68.76%, and no business combinations were completed in Q2.
