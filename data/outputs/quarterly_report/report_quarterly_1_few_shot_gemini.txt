The group demonstrated stronger financial performance during the three months ended March 31, 2026, compared with the same period in 2025. Consolidated revenue from contracts with customers grew to 29,823 thousand euros from 25,326 thousand euros reported previously. This top-line expansion was primarily driven by higher revenue from liquefied natural gas terminal regasification services collected directly from users, which increased to 19,162 thousand euros compared with 14,593 thousand euros in the prior-year period, alongside growth in consulting services and liquid energy products transshipment services. 

Cost of sales for the group rose to 16,916 thousand euros from 15,533 thousand euros, influenced by increases in wages, salaries, and social security, as well as higher expenses related to taxes on environmental pollution and emission allowances. Despite the higher cost of sales, gross profit improved significantly to 12,907 thousand euros compared with 9,793 thousand euros. Operating profit increased to 9,608 thousand euros from 7,141 thousand euros in the previous period. Net finance costs narrowed to 784 thousand euros from 1,777 thousand euros, largely due to a combination of higher finance income from a net gain on derivatives and lower interest expenses on borrowings. 

Profit before income tax for the group rose notably to 8,824 thousand euros from 5,364 thousand euros. After accounting for income tax expenses of 1,577 thousand euros, the group reported a net profit of 7,247 thousand euros for the period, compared with 4,426 thousand euros in the first three months of 2025. Basic and diluted earnings per share increased to 0.02 euros from 0.01 euros. Total comprehensive income for the period reached 7,274 thousand euros compared with 4,495 thousand euros previously. 

The company also reported positive results on a standalone basis, with revenue reaching 28,681 thousand euros and net profit amounting to 6,762 thousand euros, compared with 24,283 thousand euros and 4,016 thousand euros respectively for the corresponding period in 2025. Cash flows from operating activities for the group improved strongly to 27,917 thousand euros from 13,758 thousand euros, while cash and cash equivalents stood at 17,986 thousand euros as at March 31, 2026, up from 12,361 thousand euros at the end of December 2025. Total equity for the group increased to 179,866 thousand euros from 172,592 thousand euros over the same timeframe. Overall, the reporting period was characterized by revenue expansion, improved profitability margins, and strengthened cash generation across the group's operations.
