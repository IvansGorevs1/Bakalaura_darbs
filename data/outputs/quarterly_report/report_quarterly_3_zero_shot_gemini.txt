During the first three months of 2026, AS Amber Latvijas balzams reported an unaudited net turnover of EUR 11.1 million, which represents a decrease of 29.6 percent compared to EUR 15.8 million reported in the corresponding period of 2025. Total sales volume reached 517,600 units in 9-liter cases, reflecting a decline of 36.1 percent from 810,079 units in the prior-year period. The reduction in net revenue was driven by lower sales volumes and a high comparison base from the previous year, which had benefited from a contractual revenue adjustment. Gross profit for the period fell by 66.5 percent to EUR 1.2 million, down from EUR 3.5 million in the first three months of 2025, primarily influenced by product mix changes and pricing methodology adjustments. The company incurred an operating loss of EUR 1.6 million, contrasting with an operating profit of EUR 0.6 million in the previous year, resulting in an operating profit margin of negative 14.5 percent compared to 3.7 percent in 2025. The net result for the reporting period was a loss of EUR 1.4 million, compared with a net profit of EUR 0.9 million for the same period in 2025. Earnings per share stood at negative EUR 0.19, compared to EUR 0.11 in the first quarter of 2025.

The financial performance and business operations were significantly affected by severe external challenges. In January 2026, bankruptcy proceedings under Chapter 11 were initiated for related group company Stoli Group USA, leading to the complete loss of control over its operations and impacting the solvency of the company's largest customer. This situation, combined with a broader downturn in the global spirits market, shifting consumer habits, and repercussions from Russian authorities confiscating the Talvis distillery following Amber Beverage Group's support for Ukraine, resulted in acute cash flow difficulties and delayed excise tax payments. Consequently, the State Revenue Service froze the company's bank accounts. To ensure business continuity and protect creditors, AS Amber Latvijas balzams applied for and received approval from the Riga City Court on February 5, 2026, to initiate legal protection proceedings to restructure liabilities and stabilize operations. Furthermore, the filing triggered an event of default for principal shareholder Amber Beverage Group Holding S.à r.l. under its bond documentation and bank financing arrangements, though the principal shareholder subsequently obtained a four-month moratorium until August 24, 2026, under Luxembourg reorganization proceedings.

Regarding operational indicators, the Right First Time production quality rate improved to 99.5 percent from 98.4 percent in the first three months of 2025, while the On Time In Full delivery rate decreased to 70.3 percent compared to 97.5 percent in the prior-year period. The company remained a major taxpayer, contributing EUR 8.4 million to the state budget during the reporting period, which included EUR 6.4 million in excise tax. Total assets as of March 31, 2026, were EUR 191.3 million, consisting of EUR 43.6 million in non-current assets and EUR 147.7 million in current assets, while total equity stood at EUR 126.0 million and total liabilities reached EUR 65.3 million. Current assets exceeded current liabilities by EUR 82.9 million, and the current ratio was 2.28. Management continues to evaluate operational efficiency, cost optimization, and production improvements to restore profitability and navigate the ongoing restructuring process.
