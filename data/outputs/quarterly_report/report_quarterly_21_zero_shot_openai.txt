AS Tallinna Sadam reported mixed performance for the six months ended 30 June 2026. Revenue increased 2.4% year on year to EUR 59.3 million, supported mainly by higher charter income from the icebreaker Botnica, increased electricity sales and ferry-service revenue. However, profitability weakened materially as operating expenses rose 24.0% to EUR 20.2 million, driven by higher fuel, electricity, maintenance and repair costs, while depreciation, amortisation and impairment increased 6.0% to EUR 12.9 million.

Operating profit fell 25.7% to EUR 13.6 million, reducing the operating margin from 31.6% to 22.9%. Adjusted EBITDA declined 13.5% to EUR 25.8 million, with the margin decreasing from 51.5% to 43.5%. Profit before tax fell 27.3% to EUR 11.4 million. Despite lower net finance costs, profit for the period dropped 41.7% to EUR 6.0 million, and earnings per share decreased from EUR 0.04 to EUR 0.02. The comparison was also affected by EUR 0.9 million of profit from the Rail Baltica land disposal and a EUR 0.9 million insurance indemnity recognised in 2025, neither of which recurred in 2026.

Operating activity was weaker overall. Cargo volume decreased 5.0% to 6.5 million tonnes, mainly because liquid bulk fell 33.8%, while vessel calls declined 4.8%. Passenger numbers decreased 2.0% to 3.6 million, primarily due to lower traffic on the Tallinn–Helsinki and Tallinn–Stockholm routes. This was partly offset by a 47.8% increase in cruise passengers, reflecting 12 additional cruise ship calls. Botnica’s charter days increased from 110 to 130, supported by project work in the North Sea, but higher repair costs limited the contribution to earnings.

By segment, revenue increased in Passenger harbours, Ferry and Other, but declined 2.5% in Cargo harbours. Adjusted EBITDA decreased in every segment, with the largest reductions in Cargo harbours, down EUR 1.9 million, and Ferry, down EUR 1.8 million. Ferry profitability was pressured by higher fuel costs associated with higher diesel prices and more severe ice conditions.

The Group invested EUR 3.5 million, 70.6% less than in the prior-year period. Operating cash flow decreased to EUR 18.2 million from EUR 26.3 million, and cash ended the period at EUR 27.6 million. A EUR 19.2 million dividend was paid in May. Total assets were EUR 615.0 million, equity EUR 367.7 million and net debt EUR 145.8 million at period-end. Significant developments included new Terminal A and Terminal D detailed plans, a new potential EUR 32 million loan facility, additional summer ferry trips and Botnica’s North Sea charter.
