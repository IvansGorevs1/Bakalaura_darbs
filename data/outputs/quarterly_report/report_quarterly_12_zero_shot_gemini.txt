During the second quarter of 2026, AS LHV Group reported a consolidated net profit of EUR 24.7 million, reflecting an increase of EUR 5 million compared to the EUR 19.7 million recorded in the first quarter of 2026, but a decrease compared to the EUR 30.8 million posted in the second quarter of 2025. Of the total Q2 2026 net profit, EUR 24.1 million was attributable to the owners of the parent, compared to EUR 19.8 million in Q1 2026 and EUR 30.0 million in Q2 2025. Basic and diluted earnings per share for Q2 2026 stood at EUR 0.07, up from EUR 0.06 in Q1 2026 but down from EUR 0.09 in Q2 2025. For the first six months of 2026, the Group achieved a net profit of EUR 44.4 million, with EUR 44.0 million attributable to owners of the parent, and earnings per share of EUR 0.13. 

The Group’s consolidated net income for Q2 2026 reached EUR 79.0 million, representing a 7.3% increase from EUR 73.7 million in Q1 2026 and a growth from EUR 73.9 million in Q2 2025. Net interest income for the quarter grew by 5% to EUR 62.5 million compared to EUR 59.3 million in Q1 2026, and was 8% higher than the EUR 57.6 million reported in Q2 2025. Net service fee income remained stable at EUR 14.6 million in Q2 2026 compared to EUR 14.7 million in Q1 2026, though it marked a 6% decrease from EUR 15.6 million in Q2 2025. Operating expenses for Q2 2026 decreased by 4% to EUR 43.6 million from EUR 45.6 million in the previous quarter, but increased by 8% compared to EUR 40.5 million in Q2 2025. Loan and bond provisions increased significantly to EUR 5.8 million in Q2 2026 from EUR 1.3 million in Q1 2026. Income tax expenses for the quarter were EUR 5.0 million, down from EUR 7.1 million in Q1 2026 and EUR 6.8 million in Q2 2025. 

Regarding balance sheet developments, the Group's total loan volume grew by 3% during the quarter to EUR 5,768 million by the end of June 2026, up from EUR 5,585 million at the end of Q1 2026, and recorded a 15% increase compared to EUR 4,999 million at the end of Q2 2025. Conversely, total customer deposits decreased by 3% during the quarter to EUR 7,557 million from EUR 7,803 million at the end of Q1 2026. Within deposits, ordinary client deposits increased by EUR 105 million, while financial intermediary deposits decreased by EUR 74 million and platform deposits decreased by EUR 276 million. Total assets at the end of Q2 2026 stood at EUR 9,652 million, compared to EUR 10,233 million at the end of 2025. Total equity reached EUR 758.2 million at the end of June 2026, with EUR 750.7 million attributable to the owners of the parent. 

Key financial ratios for Q2 2026 included an annualized return on equity attributable to owners of the parent of 13.1%, which was 2.4 percentage points higher than the 10.7% recorded in Q1 2026, but lower than the 17.4% in Q2 2025. Capital adequacy stood at 20.8% at the end of Q2 2026, compared to 21.7% in Q1 2026 and 23.0% in Q2 2025.
