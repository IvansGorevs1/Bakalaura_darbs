During the second quarter of 2026, AS LHV Group reported a consolidated net profit of EUR 24.7 million, representing an increase of EUR 5 million compared to EUR 19.7 million in the first quarter of 2026, but a decrease compared to EUR 30.8 million in the second quarter of 2025. Of the total Q2 2026 net profit, EUR 24.1 million was attributable to the owners of the parent, up from EUR 19.8 million in the previous quarter and down from EUR 30.0 million in the same period of the prior year. Basic and diluted earnings per share for Q2 2026 stood at EUR 0.07, compared to EUR 0.06 in Q1 2026 and EUR 0.09 in Q2 2025. For the first six months of 2026, total net profit reached EUR 44.4 million, with basic and diluted earnings per share at EUR 0.13. Consolidated net income for Q2 2026 amounted to EUR 79.0 million, growing by 7.3% from EUR 73.7 million in Q1 2026 and rising slightly from EUR 73.9 million in Q2 2025. Net interest income for the quarter reached EUR 62.5 million, marking a 5% increase from EUR 59.3 million in Q1 2026 and an 8% growth compared to EUR 57.6 million in Q2 2025. Net fee and commission income remained stable at EUR 14.6 million compared to EUR 14.7 million in Q1 2026, but decreased by 6% from EUR 15.6 million in Q2 2025. Operating expenses for Q2 2026 were EUR 43.6 million, reflecting a 4% decrease from EUR 45.6 million in the previous quarter and an 8% increase from EUR 40.5 million in Q2 2025. Loan and bond provisions increased to EUR 5.8 million in Q2 2026, compared to EUR 1.3 million in Q1 2026 and a negative EUR 4.2 million in Q2 2025. Income tax expenses decreased to EUR 5.0 million from EUR 7.1 million in Q1 2026 and EUR 6.8 million in Q2 2025. Return on equity attributable to shareholders rose to 13.1% in Q2 2026 from 10.7% in Q1 2026, though it declined from 17.4% in Q2 2025. Capital adequacy stood at 20.8% at the end of Q2 2026, compared to 21.7% in Q1 2026 and 23.0% in Q2 2025. Total assets at the end of June 2026 were EUR 9,652.0 million, down from EUR 10,233.0 million at the end of 2025. The Group's loan volume grew by 3% during the quarter to EUR 5,768 million, representing a 15% increase compared to Q2 2025. Customer deposits decreased by 3% during the quarter to EUR 7,557 million, driven by a reduction in platform deposits and financial intermediary deposits, while ordinary client deposits increased. Among the group's business units, AS LHV Pank reported a net profit of EUR 24.3 million for Q2 2026, LHV Bank Limited earned EUR 1.4 million, AS LHV Kindlustus recorded a net profit of EUR 0.8 million, and AS LHV Varahaldus posted a net profit of EUR 0.4 million, while LHV Paytech registered a net loss of EUR 0.3 million and LHV Group recorded a separate net loss of EUR 1.2 million.
