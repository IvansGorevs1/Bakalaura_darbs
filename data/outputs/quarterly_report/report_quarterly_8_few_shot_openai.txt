AS Tallink Grupp’s 2025 financial performance weakened compared with 2024, although results improved substantially in the fourth quarter. Full-year revenue decreased by 2.6% to EUR 765.3 million from EUR 785.8 million, while EBITDA fell by 25.7% to EUR 130.1 million. Net profit declined to EUR 17.3 million from EUR 40.3 million, reducing earnings per share from EUR 0.054 to EUR 0.023. Gross profit decreased to EUR 143.2 million, and the EBITDA margin narrowed to 17.0% from 22.3%.

The decline reflected low consumer and business confidence, persistent economic challenges in Finland and other core markets, geopolitical tensions, and weaker cargo demand. Passenger volumes decreased by 0.9% to 5.5 million, while cargo units fell significantly by 19.2% to 245,004 and passenger vehicles declined by 2.2% to 760,473. Route operations revenue decreased by EUR 13.7 million to EUR 625.2 million, and its segment result fell to EUR 70.5 million from EUR 81.5 million. Estonia–Finland revenue increased modestly to EUR 314.5 million, but its segment result declined by EUR 10.9 million to EUR 70.4 million. Finland–Sweden revenue decreased to EUR 225.5 million and its segment result fell to EUR 1.3 million, partly due to 68 days of vessel maintenance. Estonia–Sweden revenue declined to EUR 85.3 million, while its segment loss improved to EUR 1.2 million. The Other segment generated EUR 145.5 million of revenue and a broadly stable EUR 28.5 million result, supported in part by charter activities.

Cost developments provided some offset. Depreciation and amortisation declined by EUR 18.0 million to EUR 79.8 million, mainly because vessel useful lives were aligned to 45 years and three vessels were sold. Net finance costs also decreased by EUR 6.8 million to EUR 21.4 million as debt balances and interest rates declined. During the year, the Group sold the vessels Star I, Regal Star and Sailor, reduced vessels in lay-up to zero, and maintained three vessels under charter agreements.

Fourth-quarter results were notably stronger. Revenue increased 2.4% to EUR 188.0 million, EBITDA rose to EUR 27.6 million from EUR 25.7 million, and the Group generated EUR 12.2 million of net profit compared with a EUR 5.2 million loss a year earlier. This improvement was supported by higher route-operation and Other-segment results, lower depreciation, vessel disposals, charter income, and the sale of surplus FuelEU Maritime compliance units.

The Group continued to reduce borrowings, with net debt broadly stable at EUR 432.4 million and the net debt-to-EBITDA ratio improving slightly to 3.3. Cash and unused credit facilities provided a EUR 110.4 million liquidity buffer at year-end. Capital expenditure increased to EUR 33.0 million, mainly for vessel maintenance, refurbishment, and IT systems. Despite the challenging year, the Board intends to propose a EUR 0.06 per share dividend for 2026.
