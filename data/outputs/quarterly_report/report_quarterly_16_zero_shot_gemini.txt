During the first quarter of 2026, Nordic Fibreboard AS reported consolidated net revenue of €1.86 million, representing a 7.3% increase compared to €1.74 million in the first quarter of 2025. The core driver of this revenue growth was fibreboard sales, which rose by €127 thousand, or 7.3%, supported by Finnish DIY stores where order volumes increased by €241 thousand from €614 thousand in the first quarter of 2025 to €855 thousand in the first quarter of 2026. Geographically, European Union sales reached €1.78 million, compared with €1.64 million in the prior-year period. Property management revenue remained immaterial following the termination of lease agreements for the Suur-Jõe property in August 2024.

Despite the increase in revenue, the company's profitability deteriorated significantly during the reporting period. Consolidated EBITDA dropped to a negative €395 thousand, yielding an EBITDA margin of negative 21%, compared with an EBITDA of negative €85 thousand and an EBITDA margin of negative 5% in the first quarter of 2025. The company recorded an operating loss of €530 thousand, compared to an operating loss of €217 thousand in the previous year, and the consolidated net loss expanded to €570 thousand from €228 thousand in the first quarter of 2025. Basic and diluted earnings per share both stood at negative €0.07, compared to negative €0.05 in the first quarter of 2025. 

The weaker financial results were primarily caused by operational disruptions and external market factors. Specifically, the company's results were negatively impacted by the scheduled factory shutdown in early January for the transition to a new boiler house, the subsequent two-month calibration and start-up period of the new gas boiler house, and abnormally cold weather conditions that increased energy consumption. Furthermore, in late February, geopolitical tensions caused a sharp spike in energy prices, nearly doubling the market price of gas. Although gas prices subsequently fell, they remained approximately 50% higher than at the beginning of the year. 

Total assets as of March 31, 2026, stood at €9.7 million, compared with €8.7 million as of March 31, 2025. Total liabilities increased to €5.8 million from €5.3 million over the same timeframe, consisting of €1.5 million in payables and €3.3 million in borrowings. Operating activities generated a negative cash flow of €13 thousand, which was an improvement compared to a negative cash flow of €528 thousand in the first quarter of 2025. Investing activities resulted in a cash outflow of €169 thousand due to investments in production assets, contrasting with a cash inflow of €471 thousand in the prior-year period. Financing activities resulted in a cash inflow of €152 thousand, compared to an inflow of €84 thousand in the first quarter of 2025. The net cash flow for the period was a negative €4 thousand, leaving the company with cash and cash equivalents of €1 thousand at the end of March 2026. 

In real estate developments, the company completed design works for the Admirali Quarter project at Suur-Jõe 48 in Pärnu, securing building permits for internal roads, utility networks, multiple apartment buildings, and the reconstruction of the existing office building. Additionally, on January 8, 2026, Nordic Fibreboard AS entered into a preliminary sale-purchase agreement for ten properties on Suur-Jõe and Admirali streets, with the final agreement expected to be concluded upon fulfillment of contract conditions no later than June 30, 2026.
