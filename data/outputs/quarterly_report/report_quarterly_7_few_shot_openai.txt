AS Silvano Fashion Group’s performance in the first six months of 2026 was mixed. Revenue increased slightly by 0.6% year on year to EUR 28.590 million, while gross profit rose 5.3% to EUR 15.892 million. The gross margin improved to 55.6% from 53.1%, supported by a 4.8% reduction in the cost of goods sold. However, higher distribution and administrative expenses contributed to a 3.5% decline in operating profit to EUR 5.751 million. EBITDA decreased marginally by 0.8% to EUR 7.508 million, with the EBITDA margin easing to 26.3% from 26.6%.

Net profitability weakened substantially. Consolidated net profit fell 31.2% to EUR 4.321 million, while profit attributable to the parent company’s shareholders declined 31.5% to EUR 3.961 million. Earnings per share decreased to EUR 0.11 from EUR 0.16, and the attributable net profit margin declined to 13.9% from 20.3%. The reduction in profit reflected lower operating earnings, a significant decrease in net finance income to EUR 0.444 million from EUR 1.652 million, and higher income tax expense of EUR 1.870 million compared with EUR 1.322 million in the prior-year period. Currency exchange results nevertheless improved, producing EUR 0.152 million of exchange income compared with EUR 1.438 million in the previous year.

Revenue trends differed by business and market. Retail revenue increased 13.6% to EUR 13.548 million, raising its share of total sales to 47.4%, while wholesale revenue declined 9.1% to EUR 14.934 million. Russia remained the largest market, generating EUR 15.767 million, down 0.6% in euros and 7.8% in local currency. Belarus sales increased 6.4% to EUR 9.046 million, or 2.7% in local currency. Sales in Ukraine and the Baltics also declined. The Group and its franchise partners operated 547 stores at period-end, including 135 directly operated stores.

Cash generation improved considerably, with operating cash flow rising 96.6% to EUR 6.842 million. Cash and cash equivalents increased 87.4% from year-end to EUR 12.443 million, while short-term deposits stood at EUR 38.707 million. Total assets increased 5.1% to EUR 98.603 million, and equity attributable to parent-company shareholders rose 6.1% to EUR 81.479 million. Inventory decreased to EUR 29.702 million, and current liabilities declined. Overall, the Group maintained strong cash and equity positions and improved gross profitability, but weaker financing results and higher operating costs led to a marked decline in net profit.
