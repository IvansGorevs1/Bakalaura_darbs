AB Pieno Žvaigždės reported a significant improvement in financial performance for the first quarter of 2026, characterized by a substantial increase in profitability despite a marginal decline in total revenue. The company generated revenue of 52,064 thousand EUR, representing a slight decrease of 0.5% compared to 52,324 thousand EUR in the same period of the previous year. This minor contraction in top-line sales was primarily driven by a reduction in the Lithuanian domestic market, where sales fell from 32,112 thousand EUR to 31,405 thousand EUR. Conversely, the company successfully expanded its international footprint, with sales to other EU countries rising to 11,341 thousand EUR from 10,801 thousand EUR, while sales to non-EU markets remained relatively stable at 9,318 thousand EUR.

The most notable development in the reporting period was the dramatic improvement in gross profit and operating margins. Gross profit increased by 42.8%, rising from 9,794 thousand EUR in the first quarter of 2025 to 13,986 thousand EUR in the first quarter of 2026. This improvement was largely attributable to a significant reduction in the cost of sales, which decreased by 10.5% to 38,078 thousand EUR from 42,530 thousand EUR in the prior year period. The company’s ability to lower production costs while maintaining sales volume indicates effective operational efficiency and cost management strategies.

Operating profit before finance costs surged from 535 thousand EUR to 3,366 thousand EUR, reflecting the positive impact of improved gross margins. Although sales and administrative expenses increased by 18.2% to 10,983 thousand EUR from 9,287 thousand EUR, the growth in operating profit was sufficient to offset this rise. Consequently, EBITDA more than doubled, increasing from 2,001 thousand EUR to 4,730 thousand EUR. Finance expenses remained relatively stable, totaling 298 thousand EUR in net costs, compared to 308 thousand EUR in the previous year. As a result, profit before tax jumped from 227 thousand EUR to 3,068 thousand EUR, leading to a net profit of 3,068 thousand EUR. This represents a more than thirteen-fold increase in net earnings, with earnings per share rising from 0.01 EUR to 0.07 EUR.

On the balance sheet, total assets grew by 2.2% to 85,305 thousand EUR, supported by an increase in receivables and inventories. Total equity increased to 41,164 thousand EUR, driven by the accumulation of retained earnings. The company’s liquidity position strengthened, with the current ratio improving to 1.51 from 1.27 in the previous year. Cash and cash equivalents increased to 2,157 thousand EUR, resulting in a positive net cash flow from operating activities of 1,625 thousand EUR, a sharp reversal from the negative cash flow of 183 thousand EUR recorded in the same period of 2025. Overall, the first quarter of 2026 demonstrated robust financial health, marked by enhanced profitability, improved cash generation, and a solid balance sheet position.
