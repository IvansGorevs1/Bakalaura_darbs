Liven AS reported a significant shift in financial performance for the first half of 2026, transitioning from a net profit in the prior year to a net loss, driven primarily by the timing of project completions and one-off costs associated with its initial public offering. Revenue for the six-month period ended June 30, 2026, totaled EUR 11,439 thousand, representing a 22.8% increase compared to EUR 9,319 thousand in the same period of 2025. This growth was largely attributable to higher sales volumes in the first quarter and strong presales activity. However, the profit before income tax declined to EUR 186 thousand from EUR 296 thousand in the prior year period. The second quarter specifically showed a profit before tax of negative EUR 234 thousand, compared to a positive EUR 1,002 thousand in the second quarter of 2025. Consequently, the group recorded a net loss of EUR 198 thousand for the first half of 2026, reversing the net profit of EUR 268 thousand reported in the same period last year. The quarterly net loss was EUR 618 thousand, influenced by a EUR 384 thousand income tax expense related to dividends and approximately EUR 90 thousand in one-off costs for the public offering of shares that were not recognized as a reduction of share premium.

Despite the earnings decline, the company demonstrated robust sales momentum. Liven signed 111 sales contracts under the law of obligations in the first half of 2026, nearly doubling the 56 contracts signed in the same period of 2025. The weekly sales ratio, a key indicator of demand, strengthened significantly, reaching a four-week average of nearly 3.1% in the second quarter, well above the long-term normal range of 1.5% to 2.0%. This surge in demand was driven by presales in the Regati phase II, the new phase of Iseära, and Kadakadabra phase I. The presale portfolio grew by EUR 14.9 million since the beginning of the year, reaching EUR 48.6 million as of June 30, 2026. This strong pipeline ensures that nearly 90% of the annual revenue target of EUR 59 million is either realized or covered by contracts.

The company’s capital structure improved markedly due to the completion of its initial public offering in May 2026. Liven listed its shares on the Nasdaq Tallinn Main List, raising EUR 4,932 thousand from the issuance of 1,053,936 new shares. This capital injection, combined with operating activities, increased total assets to EUR 100,186 thousand and equity to EUR 26,746 thousand. The equity ratio rose to 26.7% from 19.4% a year earlier, and the adjusted equity ratio excluding construction loans improved to 32.0%. Total loan liabilities reached EUR 57,341 thousand, with construction loans increasing to EUR 16,561 thousand due to ongoing development activities. The group also expanded its development portfolio, acquiring properties in Berlin and Tallinn, including a significant plot at Pärnu maantee for EUR 10 million. These strategic moves position Liven for continued growth, with expectations that net profit will increase and exceed the 20% return on equity target in the second half of the year as more projects reach completion.
