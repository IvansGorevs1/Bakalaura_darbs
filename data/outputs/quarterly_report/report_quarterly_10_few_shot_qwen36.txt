Infortar Group reported a significant recovery in financial performance for the full year 2025, driven by the consolidation of Tallink Grupp for the entire twelve-month period and improved operational results in the energy segment. Total revenue increased by 33.9% to EUR 1.837 billion, compared to EUR 1.372 billion in 2024. This growth was supported by higher passenger and freight transport volumes in maritime services and increased energy sales, despite a general decline in regional natural gas consumption. EBITDA rose substantially by 60.0% to EUR 232.6 million, with the EBITDA margin expanding to 13.4% from 10.6% in the previous year. Operating profit more than doubled to EUR 111.1 million, reflecting the stabilization of the maritime segment and the seasonal strength of the energy business in the fourth quarter.

Net profit for the period amounted to EUR 72.0 million, a decrease from EUR 193.7 million in 2024. This decline was primarily attributable to one-off accounting effects in 2024 related to the initial consolidation of Tallink Grupp, which included significant deferred tax liabilities and fair value adjustments not repeated in 2025. In the fourth quarter specifically, net profit turned positive at EUR 14.1 million, reversing a net loss of EUR 12.0 million in the same period of 2024. The maritime transport segment contributed EUR 130.1 million in EBITDA for the full year, while the energy segment generated EUR 89.3 million in EBITDA, an improvement of approximately EUR 12 million year-over-year.

The group’s balance sheet remained conservative, with total equity increasing to EUR 1.178 billion and the equity ratio improving to 45.5% from 43.0%. Net debt decreased to EUR 840.7 million from EUR 1.056 billion, resulting in a net debt-to-EBITDA ratio of 3.6, down from 4.0. Interest-bearing liabilities were reduced through active repayment of long-term loans across several group entities, including Tallink. Cash and cash equivalents grew to EUR 220 million, providing strong liquidity. The group employed 6,466 people at year-end, reflecting the expanded scope of operations following the inclusion of Tallink and agricultural assets.

Strategic developments included the acquisition of OÜ Oisu Biogaas in November, further integrating agriculture and biomethane production into the portfolio. In the energy sector, Elenger Grupp secured multi-year LNG supply agreements and commissioned battery-based energy storage systems in Latvia. In maritime transport, Tallink continued fleet maintenance and refurbishment, while extending the charter of the Silja Europa. The CEO highlighted that the group’s diversified portfolio across maritime, energy, real estate, and agriculture has enhanced resilience against economic volatility and geopolitical tensions, positioning the company for stable cash flow generation and sustainable dividend distribution.
