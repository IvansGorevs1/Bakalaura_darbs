During the twelve-month period ended 31 December 2025, AB Novaturas and its subsidiaries experienced a decline in operational volumes driven by an intentional strategy to prioritize profitability over scale amidst intense market competition and excess travel supply. For the full year 2025, the Group generated EUR 168.48 million in revenue, representing a 16.1 percent decrease compared to EUR 200.88 million reported in 2024. The total number of travellers served decreased by 20.3 percent year-on-year to 190.9 thousand clients, down from 239.5 thousand clients in 2024. Geographically, customer volumes declined across all operating markets, with Lithuania dropping 22.2 percent to 106.1 thousand clients, Latvia decreasing 25.0 percent to 38.0 thousand clients, and Estonia declining 10.6 percent to 46.8 thousand clients. 

Despite the lower revenue and reduced traveller volumes, the company successfully improved its operational margins and achieved positive profitability metrics. Gross profit for the twelve-month period amounted to EUR 19.65 million, reflecting a slight 5.3 percent decrease from EUR 20.74 million in 2024, though the gross profit margin improved by 1.3 percentage points to 11.66 percent. Operating profit stood at negative EUR 274 thousand, which marked a 95.8 percent improvement compared to the operating loss of EUR 6.56 million recorded in 2024. Adjusted EBITDA for 2025 turned positive at EUR 420 thousand, compared to negative EUR 449 thousand in 2024, which excludes the one-off, non-cash impairment of goodwill amounting to EUR 5.7 million recorded in the previous year. Net profit for the period was a loss of EUR 1.17 million, representing an 84.6 percent reduction in net loss compared to EUR 7.60 million in 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share improved to negative EUR 0.15, compared to negative EUR 0.97 in the prior year. 

The company's financial performance was affected by several operational factors and market developments. Management intentionally optimized the travel programme and reduced volumes on loss-making routes to enhance margins. This focus contributed to an increase in the aircraft load factor, which rose by 1.5 percentage points to 97.2 percent for the full year, compared to 95.7 percent in 2024. Load factors on long-haul destinations grew to 98.5 percent, and ski destinations reached 97.2 percent. However, financial results in the second half of the year were constrained by market-wide supply increases during the autumn season that pressured pricing, as well as disruptions at Vilnius Airport related to illegal cross-border activity involving unmanned balloons that negatively impacted last-minute travel demand. Positive developments included strong early sales momentum, with bookings for the 2026 summer season tracking 59 percent higher by December 2025 compared to the same point in the previous year. 

The statement of financial position as of 31 December 2025 recorded total assets of EUR 43.38 million, compared to EUR 39.45 million at the end of 2024. Total equity stood at EUR 6.86 million, down from EUR 8.02 million at year-end 2024, resulting in an equity ratio of 16.96 percent. Total borrowings increased to EUR 12.87 million at the end of December 2025 from EUR 9.25 million at the end of the previous year, consisting of long-term borrowings of EUR 7.50 million and current borrowings of EUR 5.37 million. Cash and cash equivalents increased to EUR 1.69 million at the end of the reporting period from EUR 394 thousand at the close of 2024, while other current financial assets, representing cash deposits placed for travel guarantees, stood at EUR 4.50 million. Net cash flows from operating activities were negative EUR 656 thousand, net cash flows from investing activities were negative EUR 246 thousand due to acquisitions of non-current assets, and net cash flows from financing activities were positive EUR 2.20 million, resulting in a net increase in cash and cash equivalents of EUR 1.30 million.
