Amber Grid AB reported substantially stronger financial performance in the three months ended 31 March 2026. Revenue increased 58% to EUR 32.821 million from EUR 20.763 million in the corresponding period of 2025, while total income rose to EUR 32.874 million from EUR 20.785 million. Revenue from natural gas transmission and related services increased 58%, supported by a 35% increase in transmitted gas volumes and higher transmission prices for 2026. Revenue from system balancing products declined 99%, as a new transit agreement with Kaliningrad significantly reduced the need for balancing gas.

Operating expenses excluding depreciation fell 19% to EUR 9.570 million from EUR 11.867 million. Natural gas and related-service costs declined 68% to EUR 1.477 million, mainly because balancing-gas demand and related costs fell 99%. This more than offset the 118% increase in gas costs for technological needs. Wages and related expenses increased to EUR 4.367 million from EUR 3.659 million, while other expenses rose to EUR 3.231 million from EUR 3.015 million.

EBITDA increased 161% to EUR 23.304 million from EUR 8.918 million. Operating profit rose to EUR 19.627 million from EUR 5.185 million, despite broadly stable depreciation and amortisation of EUR 3.677 million. Net finance costs increased to EUR 626 thousand from EUR 408 thousand, and income tax expense rose to EUR 3.238 million from EUR 748 thousand, reflecting the higher profit. Consequently, profit before tax increased to EUR 19.001 million from EUR 4.987 million, while net profit rose 272% to EUR 15.763 million from EUR 4.239 million. Earnings per share increased to EUR 0.09 from EUR 0.02.

Net cash generated from operating activities improved to EUR 22.927 million from EUR 8.264 million. However, investing activities used EUR 3.570 million, including EUR 2.837 million for property, plant and equipment and intangible assets, and financing activities used EUR 20.467 million, primarily due to a EUR 17.845 million reduction in overdraft and EUR 1.956 million of borrowing repayments. Cash therefore declined to EUR 832 thousand from EUR 1.942 million at year-end 2025.

Total equity increased to EUR 182.039 million from EUR 166.276 million, mainly through the period’s net profit. Total borrowings decreased to EUR 104.174 million from EUR 123.666 million, while net debt increased from EUR 76.052 million at 31 March 2025 to EUR 106.610 million at 31 March 2026. The maturity profile improved after loan arrangements became effective in February 2026, eliminating the Company’s financial covenants under the former Nordic Investment Bank agreement and allowing certain borrowings to be reclassified from current to non-current liabilities.
