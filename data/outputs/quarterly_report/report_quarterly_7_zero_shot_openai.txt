AS Silvano Fashion Group delivered broadly stable revenue but lower profitability in the six months ended 30 June 2026. Revenue increased 0.6% year on year to EUR 28.590 million, while gross profit rose 5.3% to EUR 15.892 million. Gross margin improved to 55.6% from 53.1%, supported by a 4.8% reduction in the cost of goods sold.

The improvement in gross profit did not translate into higher operating earnings. Operating profit declined 3.5% to EUR 5.751 million, reducing the operating margin to 20.1% from 21.0%. EBITDA decreased 0.8% to EUR 7.508 million, with the EBITDA margin broadly stable at 26.3% versus 26.6% in the prior-year period. Distribution expenses increased to EUR 6.912 million from EUR 6.230 million, and administrative expenses rose to EUR 3.084 million from EUR 2.672 million.

Net profit fell 31.2% to EUR 4.321 million, while profit attributable to the parent company’s shareholders declined 31.5% to EUR 3.961 million. Earnings per share decreased to EUR 0.11 from EUR 0.16. The lower result reflected the reduction in net finance income, which fell to EUR 0.444 million from EUR 1.652 million, as well as higher income tax expense of EUR 1.870 million compared with EUR 1.322 million. Net profit attributable to parent-company shareholders represented 13.9% of revenue, down from 20.3%.

Performance was affected by a shift in sales mix. Retail revenue increased 13.6% to EUR 13.548 million and accounted for 47.4% of total sales, compared with 41.9% a year earlier. Wholesale revenue decreased 9.1% to EUR 14.934 million. Russia remained the largest market, generating EUR 15.767 million, down 0.6% in euros and 7.8% in local currency. Belarus sales increased 6.4% to EUR 9.046 million, while Ukraine and the Baltics recorded declines. Russia and Belarus together represented 86.7% of total sales.

Operating cash flow almost doubled to EUR 6.842 million from EUR 3.481 million. Cash and cash equivalents increased 87.4% from year-end 2025 to EUR 12.443 million, while total assets rose 5.1% to EUR 98.603 million. Parent-attributable equity increased 6.1% to EUR 81.479 million. The Group operated 135 own stores and 412 franchise stores at period-end, and employed 1,594 people, down from 1,639 at 31 December 2025.
