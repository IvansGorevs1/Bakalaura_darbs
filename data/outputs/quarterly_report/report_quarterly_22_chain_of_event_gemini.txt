During the first half of 2026, Telia Lietuva, AB reported total revenue of EUR 259.1 million, representing a 7.2 per cent increase compared to EUR 241.7 million in the first half of 2025. This growth was supported by increases across all major revenue categories, with fixed services revenue growing by 5.3 per cent to EUR 112.9 million, mobile services revenue growing by 8.3 per cent to EUR 100 million, and equipment sales increasing by 9.6 per cent to EUR 46.1 million. 

Earnings for the period also improved significantly. Operating profit (EBIT) rose by 15.1 per cent to EUR 62.4 million, up from EUR 54.2 million a year earlier. Adjusted EBITDA excluding non-recurring items increased by 10.2 per cent to EUR 106.9 million compared to EUR 97 million in the first half of 2025, with the adjusted EBITDA margin improving from 40.1 per cent to 41.3 per cent. Profit before income tax grew by 16.6 per cent to EUR 61.3 million, while net profit for the period increased by 14.7 per cent to EUR 51.3 million, up from EUR 44.7 million. Basic and diluted earnings per share rose correspondingly from EUR 0.077 to EUR 0.088. 

Operating expenses excluding non-recurring items increased by 10.2 per cent to EUR 67.3 million, driven by higher costs for energy, external IT services, and common Group functions. Total operating expenses reached EUR 68.8 million, including EUR 1.4 million in non-recurring redundancy expenses associated with the restructuring of the Digital and Technology unit. Cost of goods and services increased by 2.3 per cent to EUR 85.5 million, influenced by higher equipment sales volumes and increased network interconnection and roaming costs. 

Cash flows and capital investments demonstrated strong performance during the first half of 2026. Net cash generated by operating activities grew by 15.0 per cent to EUR 103.1 million, and free cash flow increased by 12.5 per cent to EUR 64.1 million. Capital investments (Capex) surged by 57.9 per cent to EUR 37.5 million, compared to EUR 23.7 million in the first half of 2025. This increase was driven by investments of EUR 9.7 million in 5G spectrum licenses, following the acquisition of 40 MHz of 150 MHz frequency blocks for almost EUR 8 million and the extension of 2,100 MHz frequency usage rights for EUR 2 million in an auction organized by the Communications Regulatory Authority. 

In accordance with the Annual General Meeting decision in April 2026, the company distributed a record high dividend of EUR 0.14 per share, totaling EUR 81.6 million, to shareholders for the financial year 2025. As of 30 June 2026, total assets stood at EUR 623.2 million, shareholders equity amounted to EUR 372.0 million, and the company held a net debt position of negative EUR 4.3 million with no outstanding bank loans.
