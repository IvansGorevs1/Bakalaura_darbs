AS LHV Group reported a consolidated net profit of EUR 24.7 million for the second quarter of 2026, representing an increase of EUR 5 million compared with the first quarter of 2026, but a decrease of EUR 6.1 million compared with the second quarter of 2025. For the first six months of 2026, the Group's net profit reached EUR 44.4 million, compared with EUR 59.3 million for the same period in 2025. Net profit attributable to owners of the parent was EUR 24.1 million in Q2 2026, resulting in basic and diluted earnings per share of EUR 0.07. 

The Group's consolidated net income for Q2 2026 amounted to EUR 79.0 million, reflecting a 7.3% increase over Q1 2026 and a slight increase compared with EUR 73.9 million in Q2 2025. Net interest income grew by 5% quarter-over-quarter to EUR 62.5 million, supported by strong loan portfolio expansion and higher interest income. Compared to Q2 2025, net interest income rose by 8%. Net service fee income remained stable at EUR 14.6 million in Q2 2026, showing a 6% decline year-over-year. Operating expenses for the quarter were EUR 43.6 million, decreasing by EUR 2 million from Q1 2026 but increasing by EUR 3.1 million compared with Q2 2025. The Group recorded higher loan and bond provisions of EUR 5.8 million in Q2 2026, up from EUR 1.3 million in the previous quarter, driven by model-based, forward-looking impairment charges. Income tax expenses stood at EUR 5.0 million for the quarter. 

The Group's total assets reached EUR 9,652.0 million at the end of June 2026, down from EUR 10,233.0 million at the end of 2025. The loan portfolio continued to expand, reaching EUR 5,768.0 million at the end of Q2 2026, representing a 3% quarterly increase and a 15% growth year-over-year. Conversely, customer deposits decreased by 3% during the quarter to EUR 7,557.3 million, mainly due to reductions in platform deposits and deposits from financial intermediaries, while ordinary client deposits increased. Total equity attributable to owners of the parent was EUR 750.7 million. Capital adequacy stood at 20.8% at the end of Q2 2026, remaining well above internal and regulatory requirements. Return on equity attributable to shareholders was 10.7% for the quarter, recovering from Q1 2026 levels but remaining below the 17.4% reported in Q2 2025. 

Performance across the Group's main subsidiaries varied during the period. AS LHV Pank posted a consolidated net profit of EUR 24.3 million in Q2 2026, benefiting from loan growth and cloud-infrastructure migration, although earnings were impacted by higher impairment charges. LHV Bank Limited reported a Q2 net profit of EUR 1.4 million, with total income remaining slightly ahead of plan. AS LHV Varahaldus contributed a net profit of EUR 0.4 million, supported by growth in assets under management to nearly EUR 1.8 billion, despite a slight decrease in active second-pillar clients. AS LHV Kindlustus recorded a net profit of EUR 0.8 million, marking a considerable operational improvement driven by lower loss frequency and strong sales results. Overall, the period was characterized by steady core revenue growth and strategic business advancements, balanced against higher provisioning costs and shifting deposit volumes.
