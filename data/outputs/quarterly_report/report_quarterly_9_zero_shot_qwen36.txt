Hepsor AS reported consolidated revenue of 35.4 million euros for the twelve months ended 31 December 2025, a decrease from 38.4 million euros in the previous year. The Group recorded a net profit of 1.0 million euros, compared to 2.1 million euros in 2024. Net profit attributable to the owners of the parent company remained stable at 0.4 million euros. In the fourth quarter of 2025, revenue fell to 4.5 million euros from 10.5 million euros in the same period of 2024, resulting in a net loss of 0.5 million euros, whereas the Group had posted a net profit of 1.0 million euros in Q4 2024. The decline in full-year profitability was primarily driven by a reduction in the number of apartments handed over to customers, which dropped to 141 from 194 in 2024. Additionally, gross profit margins decreased to 15.9% from 17.6%, and marketing expenses increased to 1.3 million euros from 0.9 million euros due to intensified sales activities for a larger portfolio of projects on sale.

Despite lower sales volumes, the Group significantly expanded its development pipeline. The number of residential apartments under construction rose by 223% to 428 units at year-end, with substantial growth in Latvia where 227 homes were under construction compared to 40 in the prior year. The Group concluded primary sales agreements for 185 homes valued at 36.1 million euros, nearly double the volume of the previous year. This growth in pre-sales was supported by stabilized interest rates and positive economic outlooks in Estonia and Latvia. The Group also strengthened its balance sheet by increasing total equity by 23% to 27.1 million euros, largely due to non-cash contributions from non-controlling interests. Total interest-bearing loan liabilities decreased to 47.7 million euros from 54.7 million euros, improving the debt ratio to 52.0% from 61.6%.

A key financial development was the successful public offering of bonds in November 2025. The initial issue volume of 6 million euros was oversubscribed by 1.4 times, allowing the Group to increase the total offering to 8 million euros. The bonds carry a fixed annual interest rate of 9.50%. Operating cash flow remained positive at 14.3 million euros, providing liquidity for ongoing construction projects. The Group’s commercial real estate portfolio achieved an occupancy rate of 84% of leasable area in completed projects. Looking ahead, Hepsor plans to launch construction on five new development projects in 2026, including residential and commercial properties in Estonia and Latvia, further expanding its operational footprint and future revenue potential.
