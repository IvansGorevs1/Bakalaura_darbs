AS Pro Kapital Grupp reported a substantial improvement in performance during 2025, driven primarily by increased real estate handovers in Tallinn and Vilnius. Revenue rose to EUR 53.2 million from EUR 18.2 million in 2024, while fourth-quarter revenue increased to EUR 18.9 million from EUR 7.6 million. Real estate sales generated EUR 45.4 million, compared with EUR 10.7 million a year earlier, reflecting handovers in the Kalaranna and Uus-Kindrali developments in Tallinn and City Villas in Vilnius. Estonia accounted for 75% of total revenue, compared with 27% in 2024.

Gross profit increased to EUR 18.8 million from EUR 5.4 million, with the gross margin improving from 30% to 35%. Operating profit reached EUR 14.7 million, compared with EUR 0.1 million in 2024, producing an operating margin of 28% versus 1%. Net profit was EUR 12.0 million, reversing a EUR 3.9 million net loss in the previous year. Earnings per share improved to EUR 0.22 from a loss of EUR 0.06. Fourth-quarter results also strengthened materially, with gross profit of EUR 6.9 million, operating profit of EUR 7.1 million and net profit of EUR 6.0 million, compared with EUR 2.6 million, EUR 1.9 million and EUR 0.2 million, respectively, in the fourth quarter of 2024.

Other operating income included a EUR 3.5 million fair-value gain on investment property, compared with EUR 1.1 million in 2024. This was partly offset by an EUR 0.9 million impairment of goodwill related to the Italian Preatoni Nuda Proprietà business. Hotel operations remained profitable, but performance was slightly below budget: occupancy declined to 65% from 67%, and net operating profit decreased to EUR 0.4 million from EUR 0.5 million. Italian operations faced subdued real estate market activity and recorded a loss before tax of EUR 1.2 million.

Operating activities generated EUR 10.8 million of cash, compared with EUR 10.4 million used in 2024. Cash increased to EUR 5.1 million at year-end. Total assets rose to EUR 124.5 million from EUR 118.8 million, while liabilities declined to EUR 61.2 million from EUR 67.5 million. Equity increased to EUR 63.3 million from EUR 51.2 million, and the debt-to-equity ratio improved to 0.97 from 1.32. Net assets per share rose to EUR 1.12 from EUR 0.89.

Construction progressed at Uus-Kindrali and Blue Marine, while Kalaranna was completed and Šaltinių Namai Attico neared completion. The Group also partially repaid EUR 9.4 million of secured bonds and began construction of Blue Marine in Riga.
