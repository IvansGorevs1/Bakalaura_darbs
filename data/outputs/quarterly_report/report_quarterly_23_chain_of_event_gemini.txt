For the nine months ended 30 September 2025, the AB KN Energies Group reported revenue from contracts with customers of EUR 76,664 thousand, representing an increase compared to EUR 67,977 thousand for the same period in 2024. Gross profit for the nine months of 2025 was EUR 31,549 thousand, compared to EUR 29,237 thousand in the prior-year period. Operating profit rose to EUR 21,282 thousand from EUR 18,942 thousand. Profit before tax for the period reached EUR 16,186 thousand, compared to EUR 14,910 thousand in the first nine months of 2024. Net profit for the group amounted to EUR 13,158 thousand, up from EUR 11,862 thousand reported for the first nine months of 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share for the group increased to EUR 0.035 for the nine months of 2025, compared to EUR 0.031 for the corresponding period in 2024. For the company alone, revenue from contracts with customers reached EUR 72,848 thousand for the nine months of 2025 compared to EUR 65,009 thousand in 2024, gross profit was EUR 29,058 thousand compared to EUR 26,957 thousand, operating profit was EUR 19,277 thousand compared to EUR 17,408 thousand, and profit for the period was EUR 11,613 thousand compared to EUR 10,702 thousand. 

The financial performance improvements observed during the reporting period were influenced by several operational and business factors explicitly detailed in the report. Revenue growth in liquefied natural gas terminal activities was driven by the fact that the terminal was fully operational during the first nine months of 2025, whereas in the comparable period of 2024 it was out of operation for one and a half months due to dry-dock repair works. Additional contributing factors included higher LNG regasification volumes and bonuses paid by LNGT clients for booked capacity. Income from LNGT regasification services collected directly from users rose to EUR 43,640 thousand from EUR 31,401 thousand. These increases occurred despite offsetting factors, such as a reduction in LNG security supplement income caused by the proportionally recognized repayment of excess returns from regulated activities during 2022 through 2023, and lower payments collected from clients for unused allocated capacities. 

Total assets for the group stood at EUR 530,364 thousand as at 30 September 2025, compared to EUR 535,174 thousand as at 31 December 2024. Total equity for the group increased to EUR 167,721 thousand at the end of September 2025 from EUR 163,128 thousand at the end of 2024, primarily impacted by net profit generated during the period and offset by declared dividends of EUR 8,622 thousand. Total non-current liabilities for the group were EUR 327,356 thousand as at 30 September 2025, down from EUR 331,801 thousand at year-end 2024, including borrowings of EUR 294,351 thousand compared to EUR 301,400 thousand. Total current liabilities decreased to EUR 35,287 thousand from EUR 40,245 thousand. Cash and cash equivalents for the group significantly increased to EUR 54,398 thousand as at 30 September 2025 from EUR 17,203 thousand as at 31 December 2024, alongside a decrease in other financial assets, which include short-term deposits, to EUR 21,074 thousand from EUR 50,399 thousand over the same period.
