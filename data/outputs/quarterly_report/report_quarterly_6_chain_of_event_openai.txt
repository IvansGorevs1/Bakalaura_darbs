Infortar Group’s first-half 2026 performance improved substantially despite broadly stable revenue. Revenue declined 0.6% year on year to EUR 945.9 million, mainly because lower wholesale gas and electricity sales reduced energy-segment revenue. Gross profit increased 39.7% to EUR 114.1 million, while EBITDA rose 30.8% to EUR 111.2 million and the EBITDA margin improved from 8.9% to 11.8%. EBIT nearly doubled, increasing 93.6% to EUR 51.1 million. Profit before tax rose to EUR 27.2 million from EUR 2.7 million, and net profit improved to EUR 7.2 million from a EUR 14.4 million loss. Profit attributable to the parent’s shareholders increased to EUR 15.2 million from a EUR 2.5 million loss, resulting in basic earnings per share of EUR 0.74 compared with negative EUR 0.12.

All business segments contributed to the EBITDA improvement. Energy EBITDA increased to EUR 59.9 million from EUR 51.7 million, real estate EBITDA rose to EUR 6.5 million from EUR 4.4 million, and maritime transport EBITDA increased to EUR 39.3 million from EUR 33.3 million. Supporting-segment EBITDA turned positive, rising from a EUR 4.4 million loss to EUR 5.6 million. Management attributed the stronger profitability to improved energy performance, the addition of new agricultural units, and the closure of unprofitable operations.

In the second quarter, consolidated revenue fell 12.6% to EUR 440.9 million, but EBITDA increased 11.8% to EUR 64.2 million and EBIT rose 9.8% to EUR 29.7 million. Tallink’s quarterly revenue was unchanged at EUR 207.0 million, while EBITDA declined to EUR 34.3 million from EUR 37.4 million. Passenger volumes fell 2.2%, and higher fuel costs, increased EU ETS emissions obligations, weak demand, and geopolitical-related fuel volatility affected results. Fuel costs were EUR 8.4 million higher year on year.

Elenger’s second-quarter gas and electricity sales volumes declined 34% to 3.3 TWh, primarily because of lower wholesale sales, while regional gas consumption fell 7%. The group delivered five LNG cargoes and continued developing battery-storage facilities. Its Polish subsidiaries also signed a EUR 60 million loan agreement to expand the Polish gas business.

Total assets increased to EUR 2.71 billion from EUR 2.60 billion, and equity rose to EUR 1.20 billion from EUR 1.18 billion. Cash increased to EUR 253.2 million, while net debt declined to EUR 829.9 million from EUR 851.6 million and net debt to EBITDA improved from 3.7 to 3.3. Investments were approximately EUR 50 million. Infortar increased its Tallink ownership to 68.76%, completed the Halinga biomethane plant, continued Rail Baltica and other construction projects, and shareholders approved the 2026 dividend.
