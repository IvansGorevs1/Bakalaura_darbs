AS Pro Kapital Grupp reported a substantially stronger performance in 2025, driven mainly by completed residential developments in Tallinn and Vilnius. Revenue increased to EUR 53.2 million from EUR 18.2 million in 2024, while fourth-quarter revenue rose to EUR 18.9 million from EUR 7.6 million. Real estate sales revenue grew to EUR 45.4 million from EUR 10.7 million, reflecting apartment handovers in the Kalaranna and Uus-Kindrali developments in Tallinn and City Villas in Vilnius. In 2024, handovers were limited, with Kalaranna deliveries starting only in December.

Gross profit rose to EUR 18.8 million from EUR 5.4 million, with the margin improving from 30% to 35%. Operating profit increased to EUR 14.7 million from EUR 0.1 million, producing a 28% operating margin compared with 1% previously. Net profit was EUR 12.0 million, reversing a EUR 3.9 million loss in 2024. Fourth-quarter net profit increased to EUR 6.0 million from EUR 0.2 million, and earnings per share improved to EUR 0.22 from a loss of EUR 0.06. The improvement was supported by higher real estate sales and a EUR 3.5 million fair-value gain on investment property, compared with EUR 1.1 million in 2024. These gains were partly offset by an EUR 0.9 million impairment of goodwill related to the Italian Preatoni Nuda Proprietà business, whose projected profitability was deferred and depended on additional financing. Finance costs declined to EUR 2.6 million from EUR 4.3 million, partly because 2024 included a EUR 1.4 million loss from modification of a financial liability.

Operating cash flow improved markedly, with EUR 10.8 million generated in 2025 compared with EUR 10.4 million used in 2024. Cash at year-end increased to EUR 5.1 million from EUR 4.3 million. Total equity rose to EUR 63.3 million from EUR 51.2 million, while liabilities declined to EUR 61.2 million from EUR 67.5 million. Consequently, debt-to-equity improved to 0.97 from 1.32, and net assets per share increased to EUR 1.12 from EUR 0.89.

Operationally, construction of the Blue Marine residential project in Riga began in the third quarter and reached the zero-cycle stage by year-end, with construction financing obtained from BluOr Bank. Kalaranna was completed, with 64% of sellable area sold, while 39 Uus-Kindrali units were handed over by year-end and the remaining presold units were scheduled for early 2026. The Group also decided to sell the Brīvības commercial property in Riga and repaid EUR 9.4 million of secured bonds, reducing their outstanding nominal amount to EUR 10.5 million. Hotel performance remained slightly below budget as occupancy fell to 65% from 67%, while Italian operations were affected by subdued real estate market activity.
