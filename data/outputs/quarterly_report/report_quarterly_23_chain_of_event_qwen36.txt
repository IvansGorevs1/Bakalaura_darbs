AB KN Energies reported consolidated revenue of EUR 76,664 thousand for the nine months ended September 30, 2025, representing a significant increase from EUR 67,977 thousand in the same period of 2024. This growth was primarily driven by higher liquefied natural gas terminal regasification volumes, bonuses paid by clients for booked capacity, and the fact that the terminal was fully operational in 2025 compared to a one-and-a-half-month outage due to dry-dock repairs in 2024. Gross profit rose to EUR 31,549 thousand from EUR 29,237 thousand, reflecting improved operational efficiency despite higher depreciation costs associated with the Floating Storage and Regasification Unit acquired in late 2024. Operating profit increased to EUR 21,282 thousand from EUR 18,942 thousand, demonstrating strong core business performance. The profit before tax grew to EUR 16,186 thousand from EUR 14,910 thousand, while the net profit for the period reached EUR 13,158 thousand, up from EUR 11,862 thousand in 2024. Earnings per share increased to EUR 0.035 from EUR 0.031.

The financial results were influenced by several key factors. The reclassification of the Floating Storage and Regasification Unit from right-of-use assets to property, plant, and equipment resulted in EUR 11,369 thousand of depreciation expenses being recognized in cost of sales for 2025, compared to lease-related depreciation in prior periods. Although cost of sales increased to EUR 45,115 thousand from EUR 38,740 thousand, this was largely due to higher depreciation and environmental taxes. Operating expenses remained relatively stable at EUR 10,640 thousand compared to EUR 10,419 thousand. Finance costs decreased slightly to EUR 6,616 thousand from EUR 6,692 thousand, partially offsetting the gains from higher revenue.

The company’s financial position strengthened during the period. Total assets decreased marginally to EUR 530,364 thousand from EUR 535,174 thousand, primarily due to a reduction in other financial assets and cash equivalents being utilized for operations and investments. However, cash and cash equivalents increased substantially to EUR 54,398 thousand from EUR 17,203 thousand at the end of 2024, driven by strong operating cash flows of EUR 76,331 thousand. Total equity increased to EUR 167,721 thousand from EUR 163,128 thousand, supported by the net profit and transfers between reserves. Borrowings decreased to EUR 311,443 thousand from EUR 320,352 thousand, as loan repayments exceeded new borrowings. The company declared dividends of EUR 8,622 thousand, which were paid out during the period. The operating segments showed robust performance, with the LNG terminal segment contributing the majority of revenue and profit, while the liquid energy terminal segment also saw revenue growth. No impairment indicators were identified for the Klaipėda liquid energy terminal assets.
