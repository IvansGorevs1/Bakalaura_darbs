AS Amber Latvijas balzams reported a significant deterioration in financial performance during the first three months of 2026, marked by substantial declines in revenue, profitability, and operational stability. The company’s net turnover decreased by 29.6% to EUR 11.1 million compared to EUR 15.8 million in the same period of 2025. This contraction was primarily driven by a 36.1% drop in total sales volume, which fell to 517,600 nine-liter cases from 810,079 cases in the prior year. The decline in sales was attributed to lower market demand, a high comparison base in 2025 that included a positive revenue adjustment, and unfavorable changes in product mix and pricing methodology.

The impact on profitability was severe. Gross profit plummeted by 66.5% to EUR 1.2 million, down from EUR 3.5 million in the previous year. Consequently, the company recorded an operating loss of EUR 1.6 million, a sharp reversal from the operating profit of EUR 0.6 million reported in the first quarter of 2025. The operating margin turned negative, reaching -14.5% compared to 3.7% in the prior period. The net result for the quarter was a loss of EUR 1.4 million, contrasting with a net profit of EUR 0.9 million in 2025. While the company achieved some cost reductions, these savings were largely offset by the decline in production volumes and structural changes in its cost structure.

The financial downturn was exacerbated by a series of external challenges that severely impacted the company’s cash flow and solvency. A critical factor was the initiation of Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceedings for Stoli Group (USA), LLC, a related group company and the firm’s largest customer, which resulted in the loss of control over U.S. operations. Additionally, geopolitical tensions led to aggressive actions by Russian authorities against the Amber Beverage Group, including the confiscation of the Talvis distillery. These events, combined with a global downturn in the spirits market, created significant liquidity pressures. The situation culminated in the freezing of the company’s bank accounts by the State Revenue Service due to delayed excise tax payments.

To address these existential threats, AS Amber Latvijas balzams filed for legal protection proceedings (LPP) in the Riga City Court, which was approved on February 5, 2026. The LPP provides a framework for restructuring liabilities and stabilizing operations, with a deadline of October 5, 2026, for submitting an action plan for creditor and court approval. The filing also triggered events of default for the principal shareholder, Amber Beverage Group Holding S.à r.l., leading to broader restructuring efforts within the group, including judicial reorganization proceedings in Luxembourg. Despite the financial distress, the company maintained high production quality, with its Right First Time rate improving to 99.5%, although order fulfillment efficiency declined, with the On Time In Full rate dropping to 70.3%. Management continues to implement cost-cutting measures and efficiency improvements to navigate the challenging economic and geopolitical environment.
