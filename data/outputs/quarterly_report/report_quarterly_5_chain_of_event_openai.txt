Arco Vara reported a significant improvement in first-quarter 2025 performance compared with Q1 2024. Revenue increased 72% to €1.706 million from €0.990 million, primarily because revenue from the sale of development properties rose to €1.589 million from €0.927 million. Franchise revenue also increased to €65 thousand from €55 thousand, while €44 thousand of rental revenue was recorded compared with none in the prior-year quarter.

The group returned to profitability, reporting operating profit of €259 thousand versus an operating loss of €208 thousand in Q1 2024. Gross profit rose to €598 thousand from €173 thousand, despite higher cost of sales of €1.108 million compared with €817 thousand, reflecting the higher volume of property sales. Administrative expenses declined to €270 thousand from €297 thousand, and marketing and distribution expenses were broadly unchanged at €69 thousand. Finance costs remained similar at €143 thousand versus €141 thousand. After €13 thousand of income tax, net profit was €103 thousand, compared with a net loss of €349 thousand. Basic and diluted earnings per share improved to €0.01 from negative €0.03.

The improved results were supported by stronger property sales activity. Four apartments were sold during the quarter, compared with three apartments and one commercial premise in Q1 2024. In Rannakalda, four additional apartments were sold, bringing cumulative sales to 91 of 113 units. Botanica Lozen reached 95% construction readiness, with nine of 16 houses pre-sold, while sales at Soodi 6 continued with 10 of 66 apartments pre-sold by quarter-end. Management attributed the broader improvement in market conditions to declining Euribor, stabilizing inflation, stable or slightly higher transaction activity, and renewed consumer and investor interest, particularly in residential real estate.

Cash flow remained negative, with €2.838 million used in operating activities versus €2.217 million in Q1 2024. Financing activities provided €2.231 million, including €3.090 million of new loans, resulting in net cash outflow of €607 thousand compared with €738 thousand previously. Cash ended at €1.113 million. Net loans increased to €15.455 million, while total interest-bearing liabilities reached €16.568 million, partly due to a €2.190 million loan for the newly acquired Spordi 3a and 3b site. The average loan interest rate declined to 8.2% as Euribor fell.

Total assets rose to €42.728 million from €39.568 million at year-end 2024, mainly as inventories increased to €32.329 million. Equity rose modestly to €19.842 million, but the equity ratio declined to 46.4% from 58.9% a year earlier. The group also expanded its portfolio by acquiring the permitted Spordi Street development in Tallinn, with 56 planned apartments and total investment of €15 million.
