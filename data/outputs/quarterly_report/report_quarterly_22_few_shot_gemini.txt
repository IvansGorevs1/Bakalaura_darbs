Telia Lietuva reported a strong financial and operational performance during the first half of 2026, supported by growth across key revenue streams and improved profitability. Total revenue for the six-month period ended June 30, 2026, increased by 7.2 percent to 259.1 million euros compared with 241.7 million euros in the same period of the previous year. Revenue growth was driven by increases in fixed services, mobile services, and equipment sales. Fixed services revenue rose by 5.3 percent to 112.9 million euros, mobile services revenue grew by 8.3 percent to 100 million euros, and equipment sales increased by 9.6 percent to 46.1 million euros. 

Profitability indicators showed significant gains during the reporting period. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization increased by 9.5 percent to 105.5 million euros, while adjusted EBITDA excluding non-recurring items grew by 10.2 percent to 106.9 million euros. The adjusted EBITDA margin improved to 41.3 percent from 40.1 percent a year earlier. Operating profit rose by 15.1 percent to 62.4 million euros, and operating profit excluding non-recurring items increased by 16.3 percent to 63.9 million euros. Profit before income tax reached 61.3 million euros, representing a 16.6 percent increase, while net profit for the period went up by 14.7 percent to 51.3 million euros. Basic and diluted earnings per share increased correspondingly from 0.077 euros to 0.088 euros. 

The company experienced higher operating expenses, which grew by 11.3 percent to 68.8 million euros for the first half of the year, largely due to increases in energy costs, external IT services, and non-recurring redundancy expenses of 1.4 million euros related to the restructuring of the Digital and Technology unit. Capital investments surged by 57.9 percent to 37.5 million euros, driven by mobile network expansion, IT infrastructure, and the acquisition of 5G spectrum licenses, including two 1,500 MHz frequency blocks for nearly 8 million euros. Free cash flow improved by 12.5 percent to 64.1 million euros, and net cash generated by operating activities rose by 15 percent to 103.1 million euros. 

The financial position of the company remained solid, with total assets standing at 623.2 million euros as of June 30, 2026. Shareholders equity totaled 372 million euros, representing 59.7 percent of total assets. The company held no outstanding bank or third-party loans at the end of the period, though it maintained liabilities under reverse factoring agreements amounting to 60.1 million euros and participated in a cash pool arrangement with Telia Company AB. Following the Annual General Meeting decision in April 2026, the company distributed a record high dividend payout of 81.6 million euros, or 0.14 euros per share, for the year 2025. Overall, the period was characterized by robust revenue expansion, strategic mobile network investments, and strong cash generation.
