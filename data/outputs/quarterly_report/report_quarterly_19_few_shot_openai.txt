AS Pro Kapital Grupp reported a substantial improvement in financial performance in 2025, driven primarily by the completion and handover of residential real estate in Tallinn and Vilnius. Revenue increased to EUR 53.2 million from EUR 18.2 million in 2024, while fourth-quarter revenue rose to EUR 18.9 million from EUR 7.6 million. Real estate sales generated EUR 45.4 million, compared with EUR 10.7 million a year earlier, reflecting apartment handovers in the Kalaranna and Uus-Kindrali developments in Tallinn and City Villas in Vilnius. Estonia accounted for 75% of total revenue, compared with 27% in 2024.

Gross profit rose to EUR 18.8 million from EUR 5.4 million, and the gross margin improved to 35% from 30%. Operating profit increased sharply to EUR 14.7 million, compared with EUR 0.1 million in 2024. The operating result benefited from higher real estate sales and a EUR 3.5 million increase in the fair value of investment property, compared with a EUR 1.1 million increase in the prior year. Other operating expenses included an EUR 863 thousand impairment of goodwill related to the Italian Preatoni Nuda Proprietà business.

The Group recorded net profit of EUR 12.0 million, reversing a EUR 3.9 million net loss in 2024. Profit attributable to the owners of the parent was EUR 12.3 million, and earnings per share improved to EUR 0.22 from a loss of EUR 0.06. Fourth-quarter net profit was EUR 6.0 million, compared with EUR 0.2 million in the same quarter of 2024. Finance costs decreased to EUR 2.6 million from EUR 4.3 million, partly because the previous year included a EUR 1.4 million loss from modification of a financial liability.

Cash generated from operating activities improved significantly to EUR 10.8 million from cash used of EUR 10.4 million in 2024. However, financing activities resulted in a net cash outflow of EUR 9.6 million, reflecting bond redemptions, loan repayments, and interest payments. Cash and cash equivalents increased to EUR 5.1 million at year-end from EUR 4.3 million.

The financial position strengthened during the year. Total equity increased to EUR 63.3 million from EUR 51.2 million, while liabilities declined to EUR 61.2 million from EUR 67.5 million. The debt-to-equity ratio improved to 0.97 from 1.32, and net asset value per share rose to EUR 1.12 from EUR 0.89. The Group redeemed EUR 9.4 million of secured bonds, leaving EUR 10.5 million outstanding, alongside EUR 8.2 million of unsecured bonds. Operationally, Kalaranna was completed, Uus-Kindrali handovers began, and construction started on the Blue Marine project in Riga. Hotel occupancy declined to 65% from 67%, while Italian operations remained loss-making in a subdued real estate market.
