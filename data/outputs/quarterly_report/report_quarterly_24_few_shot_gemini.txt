AS Trigon Property Development reported a downturn in its financial performance for the 12 months of 2025 compared with the previous year, primarily driven by the absence of real estate sales and increased operating expenses. The company operates in a single business segment focused on real estate development, centered on a 13.2-hectare property in Pärnu, Estonia, and maintains its ongoing strategy of selling existing land plots. 

During the 12-month period ended December 31, 2025, the company generated zero gain from sales compared with 331,920 euros in the prior year, during which land plots were sold. Consequently, gross profit dropped from 220,299 euros in 2024 to zero in 2025. Administrative and general expenses increased significantly to 108,021 euros from 63,650 euros, largely due to newly incurred personnel expenses of 41,452 euros paid as remuneration to members of the Management Board and Supervisory Board, whereas no such remuneration was paid in 2024. Other operating income also decreased from 5,000 euros in 2024 to zero in 2025. 

Reflecting these developments, the company recorded an operating loss of 108,021 euros for 2025, contrasting with an operating profit of 161,649 euros in 2024. Financial income improved slightly to 8,513 euros from 5,760 euros. Overall, AS Trigon Property Development closed the 12-month reporting period with a net loss of 99,507 euros, a reversal from the net profit of 167,409 euros reported for the 12 months of 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share worsened correspondingly to -0.02212 euros from 0.03721 euros. For the fourth quarter of 2025 specifically, the company posted a net loss of 23,333 euros compared with a net profit of 154,215 euros in the fourth quarter of 2024, when property sales of 300,000 euros had been realized. 

Total assets decreased to 1,804,656 euros as of December 31, 2025, down from 1,873,680 euros at the end of 2024. Cash and cash equivalents declined to 227,911 euros from 370,856 euros, driven by 142,945 euros of cash flows used in operating activities, which included 91,262 euros of investments made into the property development project that increased inventories to 1,562,500 euros from 1,471,238 euros. Total liabilities rose to 49,389 euros from 18,906 euros. Total equity decreased to 1,755,267 euros from 1,854,774 euros at the close of 2024, resulting in a book value per share of 0.39 euros compared with 0.41 euros previously. Return on assets and return on equity both moved into negative territory at -5.51% and -5.67%, respectively, compared with positive returns of 8.93% and 9.03% in the prior year. The company’s shares closed the period at 0.634 euros on the Nasdaq Tallinn stock exchange, up slightly from 0.620 euros at the end of 2024, resulting in a market capitalization of 2,852,405 euros.
