Telia Lietuva, AB reported strong financial performance for the first half of 2026, driven by robust revenue growth and improved profitability margins. Total revenue increased by 7.2 percent to EUR 259.1 million, up from EUR 241.7 million in the same period of 2025. This growth was supported by a 6.7 percent rise in interim service revenue and a 7.2 percent increase in equipment sales revenue. Service revenue breakdown shows that mobile services grew by 8.3 percent to EUR 100.0 million, while fixed services increased by 5.3 percent to EUR 112.9 million. IT services within the fixed segment saw significant growth of 23.9 percent.

Profitability metrics improved substantially across the board. Adjusted EBITDA excluding non-recurring items rose by 10.2 percent to EUR 106.9 million, resulting in an adjusted EBITDA margin of 41.3 percent, compared to 40.1 percent in the prior year period. Reported EBITDA also increased by 9.5 percent to EUR 105.5 million, with a margin of 40.7 percent. Operating profit (EBIT) excluding non-recurring items grew by 16.3 percent to EUR 63.9 million, reflecting an EBIT margin of 24.1 percent. Net profit for the period increased by 14.7 percent to EUR 51.3 million, with earnings per share rising to EUR 0.088 from EUR 0.077 in 2025. The profit before income tax increased by 16.6 percent to EUR 61.3 million.

The company’s financial position strengthened, with free cash flow from operations increasing by 12.5 percent to EUR 64.1 million. Cash generated by operating activities rose by 15.0 percent to EUR 103.1 million. Capital investments surged by 57.9 percent to EUR 37.5 million, primarily due to significant spending on mobile network expansion and spectrum licenses. Specifically, EUR 9.7 million was invested in 5G spectrum licenses, including the acquisition of two 1,500 MHz frequency blocks for nearly EUR 8 million in June 2026. This investment supports the company’s strategy to enhance mobile network capacity and 5G coverage, responding to a 25 percent annual growth in mobile data traffic in Lithuania.

Despite higher capital expenditures, the company maintained a strong liquidity position. Net debt turned negative to EUR -4.3 million, indicating a net cash position, compared to net debt of EUR 27.5 million in the previous year. The gearing ratio improved to -1.1 percent from 7.7 percent. The company paid dividends of EUR 81.6 million for the year 2025, equivalent to EUR 0.14 per share, which was a record high payout. Employee-related expenses rose slightly due to non-recurring redundancy expenses of EUR 1.4 million associated with the restructuring of the Digital & Technology unit. The total number of employees decreased by 6.9 percent to 1,601 headcounts. Overall, Telia Lietuva demonstrated improved operational efficiency and financial resilience, successfully balancing increased investment in network infrastructure with strong cash generation and profitability.
