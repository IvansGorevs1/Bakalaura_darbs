AS Tallinna Sadam’s financial performance weakened in the first six months of 2026 despite modest revenue growth. Revenue increased by EUR 1.4 million, or 2.4%, to EUR 59.3 million, supported mainly by higher charter-fee revenue from the icebreaker Botnica, increased electricity sales, and growth in ferry-service revenue. Botnica recorded 130 charter days, compared with 110 a year earlier, with its utilization rate rising from 61% to 72%.

Profitability declined substantially as operating expenses rose faster than revenue. Operating expenses increased by EUR 3.9 million, or 24.0%, to EUR 20.2 million. The largest increases were fuel costs, up 47.6% to EUR 4.4 million, electricity costs, up 28.8% to EUR 3.1 million, and technical maintenance and repair costs, up 80.0% to EUR 2.8 million. Higher fuel consumption caused by more severe ice conditions, increased diesel and electricity prices, additional repair work, higher personnel expenses, and increased depreciation, amortisation and impairment all weighed on results. Depreciation, amortisation and impairment rose 6.0% to EUR 12.9 million.

Operating profit fell by EUR 4.7 million, or 25.7%, to EUR 13.6 million, reducing the operating margin from 31.6% to 22.9%. Adjusted EBITDA decreased by EUR 4.0 million, or 13.5%, to EUR 25.8 million, while its margin declined from 51.5% to 43.5%. The comparison was also adversely affected by the absence of EUR 0.9 million of insurance compensation received in 2025 and the EUR 0.9 million profit from the disposal of land for the Rail Baltica project. Lower net finance costs partially offset the operating decline, but profit for the period still decreased by EUR 4.3 million, or 41.7%, to EUR 6.0 million. Earnings per share fell from EUR 0.04 to EUR 0.02.

Operating volumes were generally lower. Cargo volumes declined 5.0% to 6.5 million tonnes, mainly because liquid bulk fell 33.8%. Passenger numbers decreased 2.0% to 3.6 million, reflecting lower traffic on the Tallinn–Helsinki and Tallinn–Stockholm routes, although cruise passengers increased 47.8%. Vessel calls declined 4.8%. These declines were partly offset by stronger ferry activity and higher Botnica utilization.

Investment fell sharply to EUR 3.5 million from EUR 12.0 million. Operating cash flow decreased to EUR 18.2 million from EUR 26.3 million, while the EUR 19.2 million dividend payment contributed to a EUR 4.4 million net cash outflow. Cash ended the period at EUR 27.6 million. Total assets were EUR 615.0 million, net debt rose 1.5% to EUR 145.8 million, and equity declined to EUR 367.7 million.
