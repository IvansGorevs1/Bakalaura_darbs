Infortar Group reported consolidated revenue of EUR 1.8 billion for the full year 2025, representing a significant increase from EUR 1.37 billion in 2024. This growth was primarily driven by the consolidation of Tallink Grupp for the entire twelve-month period, whereas Tallink’s results were only included from August 2024 in the previous year. EBITDA rose to EUR 233 million from EUR 145 million in 2024, with the EBITDA margin expanding to 13.4% from 10.6%. Operating profit for the year reached EUR 111 million, up from EUR 77 million in 2024. However, net profit declined sharply to EUR 72 million from EUR 194 million in the prior year. The report attributes this decrease in net profit mainly to one-off effects related to the initial consolidation of Tallink Group recognized in 2024. Earnings per share decreased to EUR 3.5 from EUR 9.10.

In the maritime transport segment, Tallink Grupp generated revenue of EUR 765.3 million, a decrease from EUR 785.8 million in 2024. EBITDA fell to EUR 130.1 million from EUR 175.2 million, and net profit dropped to EUR 17.3 million from EUR 40.3 million. The decline in maritime performance was impacted by low consumer and business confidence in home markets and mounting geopolitical tensions. Passenger numbers decreased by 0.9% to 5.53 million, while cargo units transported fell by 19.2%.

The energy segment contributed significantly to the group's improved profitability. Elenger Grupp’s EBITDA improved by approximately EUR 12 million compared to the previous year. In the fourth quarter, despite a 2% decrease in regional natural gas consumption, Elenger’s sales volumes increased by 7% to 4.8 TWh, maintaining a 21% market share in the Finnish-Baltic market. The company signed a multi-year agreement for LNG supplies and continued expanding its battery-based energy storage systems.

Financial position metrics showed improved leverage. Net debt decreased to EUR 841 million from EUR 1.06 billion, and the net debt to EBITDA ratio improved to 3.6 from 4.0. Interest-bearing liabilities fell to EUR 1.06 billion from EUR 1.22 billion, reflecting loan repayments across several group entities, including Tallink. Total equity increased slightly to EUR 1.18 billion from EUR 1.17 billion, resulting in an equity ratio of 45.5% compared to 43.0% in 2024. Cash and cash equivalents rose to EUR 220 million from EUR 168 million. The group maintained a conservative balance sheet with strong liquidity, supporting its strategy of stable cash flow generation and long-term investment in sectors like agriculture and energy infrastructure.
