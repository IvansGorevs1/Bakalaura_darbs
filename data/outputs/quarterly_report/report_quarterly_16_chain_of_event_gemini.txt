Nordic Fibreboard AS published its consolidated interim report for the first quarter and three months of 2026, detailing financial performance, business developments, and market conditions. For the first quarter of 2026, the Group reported consolidated net revenue of 1.86 million euros, representing a 7.3% increase compared to 1.74 million euros recorded in the first quarter of 2025. This top-line improvement was driven by the fibreboard production and sales segment, which grew by 127 thousand euros, or 7.3%, from 1.73 million euros in the first quarter of 2025 to 1.86 million euros in the first quarter of 2026. Within geographical performance, sales to European Union customers rose from 1.639 million euros to 1.781 million euros, supported primarily by Finnish DIY stores, whose order volume increased by 241 thousand euros from 614 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2025 to 855 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2026. Sales to African markets stood at 62 thousand euros compared to 87 thousand euros in the prior-year period, while sales to Asia were 13 thousand euros compared to zero in the first quarter of 2025. 

Despite the growth in revenue, profitability indicators declined significantly during the reporting period. The Group recorded a consolidated EBITDA of negative 395 thousand euros, with an EBITDA margin of negative 21%, worsening from an EBITDA of negative 85 thousand euros and an EBITDA margin of negative 5% in the first quarter of 2025. The fibreboard segment recorded an EBITDA of negative 321 thousand euros compared to negative 62 thousand euros in the previous year, and a net loss of 496 thousand euros compared to a net loss of 204 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2025. Total operating loss for the Group expanded to 530 thousand euros from 217 thousand euros, and the consolidated net loss for the first quarter of 2026 worsened to 570 thousand euros compared to a net loss of 228 thousand euros in the same period of 2025. Basic and diluted earnings per share both stood at negative 0.07 euros, compared to negative 0.05 euros in the first quarter of 2025. 

The report explicitly connects these adverse financial results to specific operational and market events during the period. The financial performance was negatively impacted by the scheduled shutdown of the factory in early January 2026 for the transition to a new boiler house, as well as the subsequent commissioning, calibration, and start-up of the new gas boiler house, which took approximately two months. Additionally, cold weather conditions increased normal energy consumption. Market conditions also deteriorated when geopolitical tensions in late February, specifically a US military attack on Iran, caused a sharp increase in energy prices that nearly doubled the market price of gas. Although gas prices subsequently fell, they remained approximately 50% higher than at the beginning of the year. 

In the real estate management segment, revenue remained immaterial following the termination of lease agreements for the Pärnu property in August 2024. Development activities continued with the completion of design works for the Admirali Quarter development project at Suur-Jõe 48, for which building permits have been issued for internal roads, utility networks, specific apartment buildings, and the reconstruction of the existing office building. Furthermore, on January 8, 2026, Nordic Fibreboard AS entered into a preliminary sale-purchase agreement for the sale of ten properties located on Suur-Jõe Street and Admirali Street in Pärnu, with the final agreement to be concluded upon the fulfillment of conditions no later than June 30, 2026. 

As of March 31, 2026, the Group reported total assets of 9.7 million euros compared to 8.7 million euros as of March 31, 2025, and total liabilities of 5.8 million euros compared to 5.3 million euros. Total borrowings stood at 3.32 million euros at the end of the period, consisting of 2.51 million euros in current borrowings and 809 thousand euros in non-current borrowings, compared to 3.81 million euros in total borrowings as of March 31, 2025.
