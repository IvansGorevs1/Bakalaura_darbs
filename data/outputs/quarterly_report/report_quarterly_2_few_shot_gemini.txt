Amber Grid AB reported strong financial results for the three months ended 31 March 2026. Total revenue for the period reached 32.8 million euros, representing an increase of 58 percent compared to 20.8 million euros for the same period in 2025. The growth in revenue was driven primarily by a 35 percent increase in transmitted natural gas volumes within Lithuania and higher transmission prices set for 2026. 

Operating expenses decreased to 9.6 million euros during the first quarter of 2026, down from 11.9 million euros in the previous year. This reduction was mainly due to lower natural gas costs, which fell by 68 percent to 1.5 million euros, driven by a sharp decline in balancing product expenses following the signing of a new transit agreement to Kaliningrad. Conversely, wages and salaries and related expenses increased to 4.4 million euros compared to 3.7 million euros in the prior-year period. 

Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization surged to 23.3 million euros, compared with 8.9 million euros for the first three months of 2025. Operating profit reached 19.6 million euros, up from 5.2 million euros previously. Net profit for the reporting period grew significantly to 15.8 million euros, compared with 4.2 million euros in the prior year. Basic and diluted earnings per share rose to 0.09 euros, up from 0.02 euros in the corresponding period of 2025. 

Total assets stood at 324.1 million euros as at 31 March 2026, remaining stable compared to 324.7 million euros at the end of December 2025. Total equity increased to 182.0 million euros from 166.3 million euros, supported by the strong net profit generated during the period. Total borrowings decreased to 104.2 million euros as at 31 March 2026 from 123.7 million euros at the end of 2025. Net debt stood at 106.6 million euros at the end of the reporting period. Net cash flows from operating activities improved substantially to 22.9 million euros, compared to 8.3 million euros in the first quarter of 2025.
