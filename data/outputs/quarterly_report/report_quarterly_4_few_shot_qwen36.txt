Ambu A/S reported a solid start to the 2025/26 financial year, characterized by continued strong growth momentum in its core business segments despite external headwinds. Total revenue increased organically by 8.6% to DKK 1,558 million, compared to DKK 1,510 million in the same period last year. On a reported basis, revenue grew by 3.2%, negatively impacted by foreign exchange fluctuations which reduced revenue by 5.4%. The organic growth was primarily driven by the Endoscopy Solutions segment, which posted a robust 14.4% increase. Within this segment, the Urology, ENT, & GI business group was the main contributor, achieving 21.0% organic growth fueled by the continued adoption of the aScope 4 portfolio and strong momentum for the newly launched aScope 5 Uretero. The Respiratory business group also delivered positive results with 8.3% organic growth, supported by steady adoption of aScope 5 Broncho and the recent launch of the SureSight Mobile video laryngoscope solution. In contrast, the Anesthesia & Patient Monitoring segment reported flat organic growth of -0.1%, a result heavily influenced by an extraordinarily high revenue baseline in the first quarter of the previous year. Geographically, North America and Europe both contributed to growth with 9.8% and 9.7% organic increases, respectively, while the Rest of World region saw a slight decline of 1.6% due to order fluctuations and high comparables.

Profitability metrics reflected the impact of external costs, with EBIT before special items decreasing to DKK 164 million from DKK 243 million in the prior year period. Consequently, the EBIT margin before special items narrowed to 10.5% from 16.1%. Management noted that this decline was significantly affected by tariff costs exceeding DKK 50 million and foreign exchange headwinds. When adjusting for these specific external factors, the underlying EBIT margin was calculated at 15.2%, demonstrating continued operational leverage from the revenue growth. Operating expenses as a percentage of revenue rose to 50.3% from 45.2%, largely due to the inclusion of tariff costs and ongoing investments in commercial infrastructure to support long-term growth.

Cash flow performance was constrained by working capital management and external costs. Free cash flow before acquisitions amounted to DKK 13 million, down from DKK 69 million in the previous year. This decrease was primarily driven by an increase in net working capital, which remained elevated to mitigate global geopolitical uncertainties and support business growth. Additionally, foreign exchange impacts and tariff costs further pressured cash generation. Despite these operational cash flow challenges, the company maintained a strong balance sheet with net interest-bearing debt of minus DKK 178 million. Shareholder returns continued, with DKK 90 million paid out in dividends and DKK 38 million utilized for treasury share acquisitions as part of a broader buyback program. Ambu has maintained its full-year financial guidance, expecting organic revenue growth of 10-13% and an EBIT margin before special items of 12-14%, although excluding tariff impacts, the margin is expected to be higher. The company remains focused on executing its ZOOM AHEAD strategy to drive high, long-term growth across its portfolio.
