Liven AS reported a mixed second quarter and first half of 2026. Demand improved substantially, but quarterly profitability declined because few homes were handed over and the company incurred offering-related and dividend tax expenses. Second-quarter revenue was EUR 4.503 million, down from EUR 7.388 million in Q2 2025 and EUR 6.937 million in Q1 2026. EBITDA fell to EUR 884 thousand from EUR 2.390 million a year earlier, reducing the EBITDA margin to 19.6% from 32.3%. Operating profit was EUR 177 thousand negative, compared with EUR 1.012 million positive in Q2 2025, while the net loss was EUR 618 thousand versus net profit of EUR 974 thousand. The quarter included approximately EUR 90 thousand of one-off public-offering costs and EUR 384 thousand of income tax related to dividends. Basic loss per share was EUR 0.049.

For the six months, however, revenue increased 22.8% to EUR 11.439 million from EUR 9.319 million, despite handovers declining to 26 homes and commercial units from 41. Operating profit rose modestly to EUR 341 thousand from EUR 325 thousand, but profit before tax decreased to EUR 186 thousand from EUR 296 thousand. After the dividend-related tax expense, the group reported a net loss of EUR 198 thousand, compared with net profit of EUR 268 thousand in the first half of 2025. Six-month EBITDA fell to EUR 2.425 million from EUR 2.777 million, and the margin declined to 21.2% from 29.8%.

Sales momentum strengthened materially. Liven signed 57 sales contracts in Q2, compared with 31 a year earlier, and 111 in the first half, nearly twice the 56 signed in the same period of 2025. The weekly sales ratio reached nearly 3.1% at its peak and averaged approximately 2.4% in the quarter, above the long-term normal range of 1.5–2.0%. The presale portfolio grew to EUR 48.6 million at the start of Q3 from EUR 33.7 million at the beginning of 2026, with EUR 39.8 million relating to projects scheduled for completion in 2026.

Continued construction expanded assets by EUR 8.505 million during the quarter to EUR 100.186 million. New construction loans of EUR 9.335 million increased total construction loans to EUR 16.561 million, while total borrowings reached EUR 57.341 million. Cash increased to EUR 10.033 million, supported by EUR 4.932 million raised in Liven’s initial public offering. The shares began trading on Nasdaq Tallinn’s Baltic Main List on 15 May 2026.

The development portfolio expanded through new Berlin acquisitions and, after the reporting date, the EUR 10 million acquisition of Tallinn properties at Pärnu maantee 35, 37 and 41. Total portfolio forecast revenue rose to EUR 497.2 million. Management expects most 2026 handovers, revenue and profit in the final quarter and forecasts revenue of EUR 59 million for the year, with nearly 90% already earned or covered by contracts at the half-year end.
