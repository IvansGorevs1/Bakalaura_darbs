Nordic Fibreboard AS increased consolidated revenue in Q1 2026 by 7.3% to €1.861 million from €1.735 million in Q1 2025. The increase of €126 thousand was driven primarily by fibreboard sales to Finnish DIY stores, where order volume rose by €241 thousand to €855 thousand. Revenue from the European Union increased to €1.781 million from €1.639 million, while sales to Africa declined to €62 thousand from €87 thousand.

Despite higher sales, profitability deteriorated substantially. EBITDA worsened to negative €395 thousand from negative €85 thousand, and the EBITDA margin fell to negative 21% from negative 5%. The operating loss increased to €530 thousand from €217 thousand, reducing the operating margin to negative 28% from negative 13%. The Group reported a net loss of €570 thousand, compared with €228 thousand in Q1 2025, while net margin declined to negative 31% from negative 13%. Basic and diluted earnings per share were negative €0.07 for the quarter, compared with negative €0.05 previously.

The report explicitly attributes the weaker result mainly to the scheduled factory shutdown in early January during the transition to a new gas boiler house. Commissioning, calibration and start-up of the new system took approximately two months. Cold weather also increased normal energy consumption. In addition, gas prices rose sharply in February following the reported US military attack on Iran; although prices later declined, they remained approximately 50% above the beginning-of-year level. These factors contributed to cost of goods sold rising to €1.966 million from €1.608 million, with electricity, heat and water costs increasing to €1.048 million from €725 thousand. Consequently, gross profit of €127 thousand in Q1 2025 turned into a gross loss of €105 thousand.

Financial costs declined to €41 thousand from €51 thousand, but Q1 2025 also included €40 thousand of financial income from the revaluation and sale of Trigon Property Development shares, which did not recur in 2026. Labour costs decreased to €411 thousand from €471 thousand, alongside a reduction in average personnel from 69 to 59.

Operating cash flow improved to an inflow of €13 thousand from an outflow of €528 thousand, mainly because higher payables offset operating losses and working-capital requirements. However, €169 thousand was invested in production assets, and financing provided €152 thousand, including a €343 thousand related-party loan. Net cash decreased by €4 thousand to €1 thousand at quarter-end.

At 31 March 2026, total assets were €9.694 million, up from €9.323 million at year-end 2025, while equity fell to €3.892 million from €4.462 million due to the quarterly loss. Total borrowings increased to €3.320 million from €3.168 million. The Group expects stable fibreboard demand and is continuing energy-efficiency investments, market development and product development for interior panels.
