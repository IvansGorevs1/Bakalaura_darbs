Arco Vara AS reported a significant improvement in financial performance during the first quarter of 2025, transitioning from a net loss to a net profit while benefiting from a recovering real estate market in Estonia and Bulgaria. The group’s total revenue increased by 72% to 1,706 thousand euros, up from 990 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2024. This growth was primarily driven by higher sales of real estate from the company’s own development projects, which accounted for 1,589 thousand euros of the total revenue, compared to 927 thousand euros in the prior year period. The remaining revenue consisted of franchise fees and other services.

Profitability metrics showed marked enhancement. The group recorded an operating profit (EBIT) of 259 thousand euros, a substantial turnaround from the operating loss of 208 thousand euros reported in the same period last year. Consequently, net profit turned positive at 103 thousand euros, reversing the net loss of 349 thousand euros seen in Q1 2024. Earnings per share also improved, rising to 0.01 euros from -0.03 euros. The improvement in operating results was supported by stable gross margins and controlled administrative expenses, despite a slight increase in marketing and distribution costs.

Financial position and liquidity remained robust, although the company increased its debt burden to support ongoing development activities. Net loans rose by 1,960 thousand euros to 15,455 thousand euros as of 31 March 2025. This increase was largely due to refinancing completed developments and securing new loans for upcoming projects, including the Spordi 3a/3b development in Tallinn. The weighted average annual interest rate on interest-bearing liabilities decreased by 0.5 percentage points to 8.2%, reflecting the broader decline in Euribor rates. Total assets grew to 42,728 thousand euros, driven by an increase in inventories related to properties under development and in the planning stages. Equity stood at 19,842 thousand euros, resulting in an equity ratio of 46.4%.

Business operations were characterized by steady progress across multiple projects. In Estonia, the Rannakalda development continued to sell units, with 91 out of 113 apartments sold. The Soodi 6 project saw 10 out of 66 apartments pre-sold as construction preparation advanced. The group also acquired a new development site on Spordi Street, with pre-sales scheduled for the second quarter. In Bulgaria, the Botanica Lozen project reached 95% completion, with 9 out of 16 houses sold. The company maintained a cautious but optimistic outlook, citing stabilizing inflation and declining interest rates as key factors supporting market revitalization. The primary focus for the remainder of the year remains on generating cash flow from completed units to fund the launch of larger-scale projects such as Arcojärve.
