Arco Vara AS reported a significant turnaround in financial performance for the first quarter of 2025, transitioning from a loss to a profit. The group’s total revenue increased by 72 percent to 1,706 thousand euros, up from 990 thousand euros in the same period of 2024. This growth was primarily driven by higher sales of the company’s own real estate developments, which reached 1,589 thousand euros compared to 927 thousand euros in Q1 2024. Franchise and other service revenues also contributed modestly to the top line. The improved sales volume resulted in a gross profit of 598 thousand euros, a substantial improvement from the previous year’s figures.

Operating performance showed marked improvement, with the group recording an operating profit (EBIT) of 259 thousand euros, reversing the operating loss of 208 thousand euros reported in Q1 2024. After accounting for financial expenses of 143 thousand euros and income taxes of 13 thousand euros, the group achieved a net profit of 103 thousand euros. This stands in stark contrast to the net loss of 349 thousand euros recorded in the first quarter of 2024. Earnings per share turned positive at 0.01 euros, compared to a negative 0.03 euros in the prior year period.

The company’s balance sheet reflects continued investment in its development pipeline. Total assets grew to 42,728 thousand euros, while total equity increased slightly to 19,842 thousand euros. Inventory levels rose to 32,329 thousand euros, indicating active development projects. The group’s net debt position increased by 1,960 thousand euros to 15,455 thousand euros as of March 31, 2025. Despite the increase in debt, the weighted average annual interest rate on interest-bearing liabilities decreased by 0.5 percentage points to 8.2 percent, benefiting from the declining Euribor environment.

Business operations remained stable with four residential units sold during the quarter. The company continues to advance multiple development projects in Estonia and Bulgaria. In Tallinn, the Rannakalda project saw continued sales, while the Soodi 6 project moved toward construction commencement. The group also acquired a new site on Spordi Street, with pre-sales planned for the second quarter. In Bulgaria, the Botanica Lozen project is nearing completion, with sales activity remaining steady. The broader real estate market in Estonia showed signs of revitalization, supported by stabilizing inflation and lower interest rates, which contributed to increased transaction activity and consumer interest. The company’s strategic focus remains on completing current developments to generate cash flow for future projects while navigating a cautiously recovering market.
