Telia Lietuva reported strong financial performance for the first half of 2026. Revenue increased 7.2% year on year to EUR 259.1 million, driven by growth in mobile services of 8.3%, fixed services of 5.3%, and equipment sales of 9.6%. Within fixed services, IT services were particularly strong, rising 23.9%. Mobile subscriptions increased 3.3% to 1.731 million, mainly due to 4.2% growth in post-paid customers, although broadband, TV and fixed telephone connections declined.

Profitability improved faster than revenue. EBITDA rose 9.5% to EUR 105.5 million, exceeding EUR 100 million for the first time, while the EBITDA margin increased to 40.7% from 39.9%. Adjusted EBITDA grew 10.2% to EUR 106.9 million, with the adjusted margin reaching 41.3%. Operating profit increased 15.1% to EUR 62.4 million, and profit for the period rose 14.7% to EUR 51.3 million, lifting the net margin from 18.5% to 19.8% and earnings per share from EUR 0.077 to EUR 0.088.

Cash generation also strengthened. Net cash from operating activities increased 15.0% to EUR 103.1 million, while free cash flow rose 12.5% to EUR 64.1 million. Despite substantially higher investment, the Company ended June with negative net debt of EUR 4.3 million, compared with net debt of EUR 27.5 million at the end of 2025 and EUR 27.5 million a year earlier. Capital investments increased 57.9% to EUR 37.5 million, primarily reflecting investment in IT systems and infrastructure, mobile network expansion, and EUR 9.7 million spent on 5G spectrum licenses.

The higher investment reflects strategic network and infrastructure development. In June, Telia Lietuva acquired two 1,500 MHz frequency blocks for almost EUR 8 million and extended its right to use the 2,100 MHz band for EUR 2 million. The new spectrum will support further 5G development amid annual mobile data traffic growth of approximately 25%. The Company also plans to invest EUR 7.1 million in mobile network expansion during 2026 and up to EUR 26 million in a new data center near Vilnius, expected to be completed in 2027.

Operating expenses excluding non-recurring items increased 10.2%, partly due to higher energy, external IT and Group-function costs. Restructuring of the Digital & Technology unit generated EUR 1.4 million of redundancy expenses. The workforce decreased by 6.9% to 1,601 employees. In May, the Company paid EUR 81.6 million in dividends for 2025, or EUR 0.14 per share, a record payout.
