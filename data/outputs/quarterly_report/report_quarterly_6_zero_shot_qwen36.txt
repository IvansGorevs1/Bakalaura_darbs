Infortar Group reported a significant improvement in profitability for the first half of 2026, despite a slight decline in consolidated revenue. The group, an Estonian investment company active in maritime transport, energy, and real estate, generated EBITDA of EUR 111 million for the six-month period, representing a 31% increase compared to EUR 85 million in the same period of 2025. Operating profit (EBIT) surged by 94% to EUR 51 million, up from EUR 26 million in the prior year. This substantial growth in earnings was driven by improved performance across all business segments, particularly a dramatic turnaround in the supporting businesses segment, which shifted from a loss of EUR 4.4 million to a profit of EUR 5.6 million. The energy segment also contributed positively, while the maritime transport segment remained stable with unchanged revenue and net loss figures compared to the previous year.

Revenue for the first half of 2026 totaled EUR 946 million, a marginal decrease from EUR 952 million in 2025. This decline was primarily attributable to the energy segment, where Elenger Grupp’s gas and electricity sales volumes dropped by 34% due to reduced wholesale sales. Conversely, the maritime transport segment maintained its revenue levels at EUR 350 million, although it faced headwinds from higher fuel costs, which increased by EUR 8.4 million year-on-year due to geopolitical tensions in the Middle East. The maritime segment recorded a net loss of EUR 2.5 million in the second quarter, consistent with the prior year, while generating EBITDA of EUR 34.3 million, down slightly from EUR 37.4 million.

The company’s financial position remained robust, with total assets reaching EUR 2.71 billion and equity increasing to EUR 1.20 billion. Net debt decreased to EUR 830 million from EUR 852 million at the end of 2025, and the net debt to EBITDA ratio improved to 3.3 times from 3.7 times. Cash and cash equivalents grew to EUR 253 million, reflecting effective cash flow management. The group employed 6,890 people as of June 30, 2026. Key developments included the completion of the Halinga biomethane plant, which integrates agriculture and energy production, and the continuation of major construction projects, including the Rail Baltica mainline. Infortar increased its ownership in Tallink Grupp to 68.76% and initiated a share buyback program. The CEO emphasized that the results demonstrate sustainable value creation driven by core operations rather than one-off transactions. Geopolitical risks and energy price volatility remain key factors influencing the outlook, but the diversified portfolio and strong balance sheet provide resilience. The Annual General Meeting approved the dividend for the year, signaling confidence in the company’s long-term development.
