For the twelve-month period ended 31 December 2025, AB Novaturas reported consolidated revenues of EUR 168.48 million, representing a 16.1 percent decline compared to EUR 200.88 million for the twelve-month period ended 31 December 2024. The total number of travellers served by the group decreased by 20 percent year on year to 190.9 thousand clients. Gross profit for the twelve-month period decreased by 5.3 percent to EUR 19.65 million compared with EUR 20.74 million in 2024. Operating profit improved significantly by 95.8 percent, amounting to a negative EUR 274 thousand compared to a negative EUR 6.56 million in the previous year. EBITDA was positive at EUR 420 thousand, contrasting with a negative EUR 6.13 million reported for the twelve-month period ended 31 December 2024. Adjusted EBITDA, which excludes the one-off non-cash impairment of goodwill amounting to EUR 5.7 million recorded in 2024, stood at EUR 420 thousand compared to a negative EUR 449 thousand in the previous year. Profit before tax was a negative EUR 1.16 million compared to a negative EUR 7.79 million in 2024, and the annual net loss for the period decreased by 84.6 percent to EUR 1.17 million compared to EUR 7.60 million for the prior year. Basic and diluted earnings per share improved to a negative EUR 0.15 compared to a negative EUR 0.97 in 2024. Total assets as of 31 December 2025 were EUR 43.38 million compared to EUR 39.45 million at the end of 2024, while total equity stood at EUR 6.86 million compared to EUR 8.02 million. Total borrowings increased to EUR 12.87 million as of 31 December 2025 from EUR 9.25 million as of 31 December 2024. The financial results were influenced by management's strategic decision to prioritize profitability over volume in an environment of intense competition and excess travel supply. By intentionally reducing volumes on loss-making routes and optimizing the travel programme, the company improved its aircraft load factor by 1.5 percentage points to 97.2 percent and achieved positive annual EBITDA despite the decline in revenue and traveller numbers. Additional market-wide supply increases during the autumn season and disruptions at Vilnius Airport related to illegal cross-border activity involving unmanned balloons also impacted late-season demand.
