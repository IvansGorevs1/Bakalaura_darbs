During the twelve months of 2025, AS Trigon Property Development recorded a net loss of 99,507 euros, representing a decline compared to the net profit of 167,409 euros reported for the twelve months of 2024. For the fourth quarter of 2025, the company posted a net loss of 23,333 euros, down from a net profit of 154,215 euros in the fourth quarter of 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share for the 12-month period of 2025 stood at -0.02212 euros, compared to 0.03721 euros in the previous year. For the fourth quarter alone, basic and diluted earnings per share were -0.00519 euros, compared to 0.03428 euros in the fourth quarter of 2024. 

The company generated no gain on sales or gross profit during the 12 months of 2025 or the fourth quarter of 2025, as no property sales occurred during the reporting period. In contrast, the company recorded a gross profit of 220,299 euros for the 12 months of 2024, which included gains from the sale of a 0.09-hectare transportation purpose land for 31,920 euros and a 0.70-hectare property for 300,000 euros. Administrative and general expenses increased to 108,021 euros for the 12-month period of 2025, up from 63,650 euros in 2024, driven notably by 41,452 euros in personnel expenses paid as remuneration to members of the Management Board and Supervisory Board, whereas no such remuneration was paid in 2024. Financial income for the 12-month period of 2025 was 8,513 euros, compared to 5,760 euros in 2024. 

Total assets as of December 31, 2025, decreased to 1,804,656 euros from 1,873,680 euros at the end of 2024. Cash and cash equivalents declined to 227,911 euros at the close of 2025 from 370,856 euros at the end of 2024, with total cash flows used in operating activities amounting to 142,945 euros for the 12-month period, compared to positive cash flows of 194,723 euros in the prior year. Inventories increased to 1,562,500 euros as of December 31, 2025, up from 1,471,238 euros at the end of 2024, reflecting 91,262 euros of investments made into the company's single real estate development project located in Pärnu, Estonia. Total liabilities rose to 49,389 euros at the end of 2025 from 18,906 euros a year earlier. Total equity decreased to 1,755,267 euros as of December 31, 2025, compared to 1,854,774 euros at the end of 2024. Financial ratios showed a return on assets of -5.51% and a return on equity of -5.67% for 2025, compared to 8.93% and 9.03% respectively in 2024. The debt ratio was 2.74% at the end of 2025, up from 1.01% at the end of 2024. The closing share price on the Nasdaq Tallinn stock exchange increased to 0.634 euros at the end of 2025 from 0.620 euros at the end of 2024, resulting in a market capitalization of 2,852,405 euros.
