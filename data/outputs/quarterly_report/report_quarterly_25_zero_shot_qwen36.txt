VILVI Group reported consolidated revenue of EUR 86.97 million for the first quarter of 2026, representing a significant 22.4% increase compared to EUR 71.07 million in the same period of 2025. This growth continues the upward trend observed in 2025, which saw a 26.5% rise in revenue. The primary driver of this expansion was the acquisition of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB, completed on January 16, 2026. Consequently, the Group’s sales structure shifted, with the canned milk segment contributing EUR 11.92 million to the total revenue, a category that did not exist in previous reporting periods due to consolidation starting from 2026.

Despite the strong top-line growth, profitability metrics weakened significantly. EBITDA fell to EUR 3.0 million, a sharp decrease of 50.2% from EUR 6.03 million in Q1 2025. The EBITDA margin contracted to 3.5% from 8.5% in the prior year, a decline of 5.0 percentage points. This deterioration in operating profitability was largely attributable to a preliminary negative goodwill charge of EUR 5.5 million recorded in relation to the acquisition of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB. Excluding this non-cash item, the underlying operational performance would appear different, but the reported EBITDA reflects the immediate financial impact of the transaction.

Net profit, however, increased by 25.7% to EUR 5.085 million, up from EUR 4.045 million in Q1 2025. The profit margin remained relatively stable at 5.8%, compared to 5.7% in the previous year. Earnings per share rose to EUR 0.43 from EUR 0.34. The increase in net profit despite lower EBITDA suggests that other factors, such as tax benefits or non-operating items, mitigated the impact of the goodwill charge on the bottom line, although the income statement shows a slight increase in profit before tax from EUR 4.361 million to EUR 5.148 million.

Operational activity expanded substantially, with the volume of purchased basic indicator milk reaching 155,954 tons, a 54.2% increase from 101,118 tons in Q1 2025. This surge in raw material procurement aligns with the integration of new production capacities and the acquisition of additional facilities. Net financial debt more than doubled, rising by 89.7% to EUR 83.427 million from EUR 43.971 million in Q1 2025, reflecting the financing activities associated with the acquisition and expansion projects.

Business developments during the period included the commencement of commercial cheese production at the new Baltic Dairy Board SIA factory in Latvia, which is expected to produce 18,000 tons annually and create over 100 jobs. The factory also received an honorable third place in the Latvian Construction Awards 2025. Additionally, the Group participated in Gulfood, the world’s largest food and beverage exhibition, to showcase its products. These strategic moves indicate a period of aggressive expansion and integration for VILVI Group, prioritizing market share and capacity growth over short-term margin preservation.
