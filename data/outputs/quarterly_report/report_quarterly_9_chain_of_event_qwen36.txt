Hepsor AS reported consolidated revenue of 35.4 million euros for the full year 2025, representing a decline from 38.4 million euros in 2024. The Group recorded a net profit of 1.0 million euros for the year, a significant decrease from the 2.1 million euros reported in 2024. Profit attributable to the owners of the parent company remained stable at 0.4 million euros, while non-controlling interests contributed 0.6 million euros compared to 1.7 million euros in the previous year. In the fourth quarter of 2025, revenue fell to 4.5 million euros from 10.5 million euros in the same period of 2024. The Group posted a net loss of 0.5 million euros in Q4 2025, reversing the 1.0 million euro net profit recorded in Q4 2024.

The deterioration in financial performance was primarily driven by a reduction in the number of apartments handed over to customers, which dropped to 141 units in 2025 from 194 units in 2024. This volume decrease, combined with lower profit margins on sold projects, negatively impacted profitability. The gross profit margin declined to 15.9% in 2025 from 17.6% in 2024. Operating expenses also increased, with marketing expenses rising to 1.3 million euros from 0.9 million euros in the prior year. The report attributes this increase to more intensive sales and marketing activities required by the growth in the number and scale of projects on sale. Finance costs and personnel expenses remained at levels similar to the previous year.

Despite lower annual revenue, the Group’s development pipeline expanded significantly. The number of residential apartments under construction more than doubled to 428 units at the end of 2025, up from 192 units at the end of 2024. The Group concluded primary sales agreements for 185 homes in 2025, valued at 36.1 million euros, nearly double the volume of the previous year. This growth in pre-sales was supported by stabilized interest rates and positive economic outlooks in Estonia and Latvia. The Group also strengthened its balance sheet, with total equity increasing by 23.0% to 27.1 million euros. The debt ratio improved to 52.0% from 61.6% in the previous year, aided by the successful issuance of 8.0 million euros in bonds in November 2025. Total assets grew by 3.4% to 91.8 million euros, while total liabilities decreased by 24.2% to 50.6 million euros. Cash and cash equivalents declined to 3.8 million euros from 6.2 million euros at the start of the year, resulting in a negative net cash flow of 2.4 million euros for the period.
