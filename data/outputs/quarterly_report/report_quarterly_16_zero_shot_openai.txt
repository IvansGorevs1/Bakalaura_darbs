Nordic Fibreboard AS increased consolidated revenue in Q1 2026 by 7.3% to €1.861 million from €1.735 million in Q1 2025, driven primarily by stronger fibreboard sales to Finnish DIY stores, whose order volume rose by €241 thousand to €855 thousand. EU revenue increased to €1.781 million from €1.639 million, while sales to Africa declined to €62 thousand from €87 thousand.

Despite the revenue growth, profitability deteriorated substantially. EBITDA fell to negative €395 thousand from negative €85 thousand, reducing the EBITDA margin to negative 21% from negative 5%. The operating loss widened to €530 thousand from €217 thousand, and the net loss increased to €570 thousand from €228 thousand. The net margin consequently declined to negative 31% from negative 13%. Basic and diluted earnings per share were negative €0.07 in the quarter, compared with negative €0.05 in Q1 2025.

The main pressure came from production costs, particularly energy. Cost of goods sold increased to €1.966 million from €1.608 million, exceeding revenue and producing a gross loss of €105 thousand compared with a gross profit of €127 thousand a year earlier. Electricity, heat and water costs increased to €1.048 million from €725 thousand. Results were affected by the scheduled factory shutdown in early January during the transition to a new gas boiler house, which required approximately two months for calibration and start-up. Cold weather increased energy consumption, and gas prices nearly doubled in late February following the US military attack on Iran; although prices subsequently declined, they remained approximately 50% above the beginning-of-year level. Distribution costs also rose to €261 thousand from €228 thousand, while administrative expenses increased to €154 thousand from €111 thousand. Labour costs decreased to €411 thousand from €471 thousand.

Financial expenses declined to €41 thousand from €51 thousand, but Q1 2025 had included €40 thousand of financial income from the sale of Trigon Property Development shares, which did not recur.

Operating cash flow improved to an inflow of €13 thousand from an outflow of €528 thousand, but €169 thousand was invested in production assets. Financing provided €152 thousand, including a €343 thousand related-party loan, resulting in a net cash outflow of €4 thousand and closing cash of only €1 thousand.

At 31 March 2026, total assets were €9.694 million, up from €9.323 million at year-end 2025, while equity fell to €3.892 million from €4.462 million because of the quarterly loss. Total borrowings increased to €3.320 million from €3.168 million. Management expects stable demand and sales volumes and continues to develop Nordic markets and interior finishing panels.
