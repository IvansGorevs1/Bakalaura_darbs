Ambu delivered a solid start to the 2025/26 financial year, driven by continued growth momentum across its core business areas. Total revenue for the first quarter reached 1,558 million crowns, representing organic growth of 8.6% and reported growth of 3.2% compared to the same period in the previous year. The performance was supported by strong demand in key markets, although growth was partially offset by negative currency exchange headwinds of 5.4%. 

The primary driver of the company’s performance was the Endoscopy Solutions segment, which generated revenue of 986 million crowns and accounted for 63% of total sales. Endoscopy Solutions achieved organic growth of 14.4%, continuing its upward trajectory from high comparative figures. This expansion was led by the Urology, ENT, and GI business group, which posted organic growth of 21.0% due to strong momentum in urology and the continued adoption of aScope portfolios. The Respiratory business group also contributed positively with organic growth of 8.3%, supported by the adoption of bronchoscopy solutions and the introduction of the SureSight Mobile video laryngoscope. Conversely, the Anesthesia and Patient Monitoring segment posted flat organic growth of -0.1%, yielding 572 million crowns in revenue, primarily due to extraordinarily high comparable revenue recorded in the same quarter last year. 

Geographically, revenue growth was solid in core regions, with North America and Europe posting organic growth of 9.8% and 9.7%, respectively. Meanwhile, the Rest of World region declined organically by 1.6% due to order fluctuations and high comparative baselines. 

Profitability for the quarter was impacted by external cost pressures. Operating profit before special items reached 164 million crowns, down from 243 million crowns previously. The resulting EBIT margin before special items was 10.5%, compared to 16.1% in the first quarter of the prior year. This margin contraction was influenced by tariff costs exceeding 50 million crowns and negative foreign exchange headwinds. Adjusting for these external factors, the underlying EBIT margin stood at 15.2%, reflecting continued operational leverage. Net profit for the period decreased to 118 million crowns, compared with 183 million crowns in the corresponding period last year, resulting in earnings per share of 0.44 crowns. 

Cash flow generation was subdued during the quarter, with cash flow from operating activities totaling 106 million crowns and free cash flow before acquisitions dropping to 13 million crowns from 69 million crowns. This was mainly impacted by elevated net working capital levels maintained to navigate global uncertainties and support growth, alongside foreign exchange headwinds and tariffs. Net interest-bearing debt stood at -178 million crowns at the end of the period. During the quarter, the company distributed 90 million crowns in dividends and allocated 38 million crowns to treasury shares under its share buyback program. 

Looking ahead, Ambu maintained its financial guidance for the 2025/26 financial year, continuing to expect total organic revenue growth of 10% to 13% and an EBIT margin before special items of 12% to 14%. Both growth and profitability are anticipated to be higher in the second half of the financial year compared to the first.
