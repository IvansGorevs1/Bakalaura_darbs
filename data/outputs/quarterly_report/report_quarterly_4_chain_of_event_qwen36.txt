Ambu A/S reported a solid start to the 2025/26 financial year with total revenue of DKK 1,558 million in the first quarter. This represents an organic growth of 8.6 percent compared to DKK 1,510 million in the same period last year, while reported revenue growth stood at 3.2 percent. The decline in reported growth relative to the prior year’s strong performance was primarily driven by unfavorable foreign exchange effects, which reduced revenue by 5.4 percent. Endoscopy Solutions remained the primary growth engine, contributing 63 percent of total revenue and achieving an organic increase of 14.4 percent. Within this segment, the Urology, ENT, and GI business group posted robust organic growth of 21.0 percent, largely fueled by the continued adoption of the aScope 4 portfolio and strong momentum for the newly launched aScope 5 Uretero. The Respiratory business group also delivered positive results with 8.3 percent organic growth, supported by steady adoption of the aScope 5 Broncho and the recent introduction of the SureSight Mobile video laryngoscope solution. In contrast, the Anesthesia and Patient Monitoring segment experienced flat organic growth of negative 0.1 percent, a result heavily impacted by an extraordinarily high revenue baseline in the first quarter of the previous year.

Operating profitability was negatively affected by external headwinds, specifically tariff costs and foreign exchange pressures. The EBIT margin before special items decreased to 10.5 percent from 16.1 percent in the prior year period. However, when adjusting for tariff costs exceeding DKK 50 million and foreign exchange headwinds, the underlying EBIT margin was 15.2 percent, indicating that the company maintained operational leverage despite these challenges. Operating expenses as a percentage of revenue increased to 50.3 percent from 45.2 percent, partly due to the inclusion of tariff costs and continued investments in commercial infrastructure to drive future growth.

Cash flow performance reflected these operational dynamics and working capital changes. Free cash flow before acquisitions amounted to DKK 13 million, a significant decrease from DKK 69 million in the previous year. This reduction was mainly attributed to an increase in net working capital, which remained elevated at DKK 1,321 million or 22 percent of revenue, as the company sought to mitigate global geopolitical uncertainties and support growth. Additionally, foreign exchange and tariff costs further impacted cash generation. On the balance sheet, Ambu maintained a strong financial position with net interest-bearing debt of negative DKK 178 million, providing a net cash position. The company returned capital to shareholders by paying DKK 90 million in dividends and utilizing DKK 38 million for treasury share acquisitions as part of its buyback program. Ambu maintained its full-year financial guidance, expecting organic revenue growth of 10 to 13 percent and an EBIT margin before special items of 12 to 14 percent, noting that growth and margins are anticipated to be higher in the second half of the year.
