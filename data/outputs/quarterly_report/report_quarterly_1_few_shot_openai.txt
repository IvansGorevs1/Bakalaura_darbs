AB KN Energies reported significantly improved financial performance in the first quarter of 2026. Group revenue increased to EUR 29.8 million from EUR 25.3 million in the same period of 2025, an 18% rise. Growth was driven mainly by higher LNG terminal regasification revenue, which increased to EUR 19.2 million from EUR 14.6 million, and stronger liquid energy product transshipment revenue of EUR 7.6 million compared with EUR 6.6 million. Consulting services revenue also increased to EUR 2.7 million from EUR 1.9 million. These improvements more than offset lower revenue from other LNG terminal-related sales and the continued reduction in income included in the LNG security supplement.

Cost of sales rose 9% to EUR 16.9 million, reflecting higher personnel costs, environmental and emissions-related taxes, repair and maintenance expenses, electricity costs, and other operating expenses. Distribution and administrative expenses increased 10% to EUR 3.3 million. Despite the higher cost base, gross profit increased 32% to EUR 12.9 million, and the gross margin improved from approximately 39% to 43%. Operating profit rose 35% to EUR 9.6 million from EUR 7.1 million.

Net finance costs declined substantially to EUR 0.8 million from EUR 1.8 million. Finance income increased to EUR 1.1 million, including a EUR 0.5 million net gain on derivatives, while finance costs fell to EUR 1.9 million from EUR 2.3 million, mainly because of lower interest expenses on borrowings and the absence of comparable currency-exchange and derivative losses. As a result, profit before tax increased 65% to EUR 8.8 million. After income tax expense of EUR 1.6 million, group net profit rose 64% to EUR 7.2 million from EUR 4.4 million. Earnings per share increased to EUR 0.02 from EUR 0.01.

The LNG terminal segment remained the main contributor to results, generating EUR 18.7 million of revenue and EUR 4.5 million of net profit. Its profit before tax increased to EUR 5.9 million from EUR 2.2 million, while liquid energy terminals generated EUR 1.6 million of net profit, down from EUR 2.3 million. The commercial LNG segment’s net profit increased to EUR 1.1 million from EUR 0.8 million.

Operating cash flow more than doubled to EUR 27.9 million from EUR 13.8 million, supported particularly by a reduction in receivables. Investing and financing outflows increased because of higher asset investment, debt repayments, and interest payments. Nevertheless, cash and cash equivalents increased to EUR 18.0 million at 31 March 2026 from EUR 12.4 million at year-end, while borrowings declined to EUR 302.1 million from EUR 311.2 million. Total equity increased to EUR 179.9 million, reflecting the period’s comprehensive income.
