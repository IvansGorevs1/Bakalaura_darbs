AS Amber Latvijas balzams reported a significant deterioration in financial performance for the first three months of 2026, driven by a challenging geopolitical and economic environment. The Company’s net turnover decreased by 29.6% to EUR 11.1 million compared to EUR 15.8 million in the same period of 2025. This decline was primarily attributed to a substantial drop in sales volume, which fell by 36.1% to 517,600 nine-liter cases from 810,079 cases in the prior year. The Company noted that the previous year’s results were positively influenced by a revenue adjustment, creating a high comparison base. Consequently, gross profit contracted by 66.5% to EUR 1.2 million, as cost reductions were offset by unfavorable changes in product mix and pricing adjustments.

The Company’s operating result shifted from a profit of EUR 0.6 million in 2025 to a loss of EUR 1.6 million in 2026, resulting in an operating margin of negative 14.5%. The net result for the period was a loss of EUR 1.4 million, compared to a net profit of EUR 0.9 million in 2025. Management identified several critical external factors contributing to these results. Following the appointment of a trustee for the related group company Stoli Group (USA), LLC, under U.S. Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceedings in January 2026, the Group lost control over U.S. operations. This event severely impacted the solvency of the Company’s largest customer and disrupted cash flows. Additionally, Russian authorities designated related entities as extremist and confiscated the Group-owned Talvis distillery after Amber Beverage Group expressed support for Ukraine. These actions, combined with a global downturn in spirits sales and inventory adjustments in distribution channels, created severe liquidity pressures.

The cash flow difficulties led to delayed excise tax payments, resulting in the State Revenue Service freezing the Company’s bank accounts. To ensure business continuity and protect creditors, AS Amber Latvijas balzams filed for legal protection proceedings (LPP) in the Riga City Court, which was approved on February 5, 2026. The LPP provides a framework for restructuring liabilities, including tax debts, with a court-approved action plan due by October 5, 2026. The filing also triggered events of default for the principal shareholder, Amber Beverage Group Holding S.à r.l., under its bond documentation and financing arrangements. In response to the crisis, the Company is implementing measures to increase production efficiency and reduce costs. While production quality improved, with the Right First Time rate rising to 99.5%, order fulfillment efficiency declined, with the On Time In Full rate dropping to 70.3% from 97.5% in the previous year. The Company continues to pay taxes, having contributed EUR 8.4 million to the state budget during the period, including EUR 6.4 million in excise taxes.
