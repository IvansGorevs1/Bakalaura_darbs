Liven AS reported mixed financial results for the second quarter and first six months of 2026, characterized by strong demand and contract growth alongside lower quarterly revenue and profitability due to the timing of property completions. Revenue for the second quarter decreased to 4,503 thousand euros compared with 7,388 thousand euros in the same period of 2025. For the first six months of the year, revenue increased to 11,439 thousand euros from 9,319 thousand euros a year earlier. The group recorded a net loss of 618 thousand euros for the second quarter, down from a net profit of 974 thousand euros in the second quarter of 2025, and a net loss of 198 thousand euros for the six-month period compared with a net profit of 268 thousand euros in the first half of the previous year. Results for the second quarter were impacted by one-off costs of approximately 90 thousand euros related to the public offering of shares and an income tax expense of 384 thousand euros associated with dividend payments. 

Operational activity in the residential real estate market remained robust, supported by recovering household purchasing power and declining inflation. The company signed 57 sales contracts under the law of obligations during the second quarter, bringing the total for the first six months of 2026 to 111 contracts, nearly double the 56 contracts signed in the first half of 2025. The total presale volume reached 48.6 million euros by the end of June, comprising 171 contracts. Only 12 homes were handed over during the second quarter, and 26 homes over the first six months, reflecting the cyclical nature of construction completions where most handovers and revenue recognitions are scheduled for the final quarter of the year. 

Total assets grew by 8,505 thousand euros during the second quarter to reach 100,186 thousand euros as of 30 June 2026, primarily driven by continued construction in development projects and property acquisitions. Total loan liabilities reached 57,341 thousand euros at the end of the period, including construction loans of 16,561 thousand euros following the issuance of new bank construction loans totaling 9,335 thousand euros during the quarter. Cash and cash equivalents increased by 1,979 thousand euros during the quarter to 10,033 thousand euros. Equity stood at 26,746 thousand euros, supported by an initial public offering in April and May 2026 in which the company issued 1,053,936 new shares, raising net proceeds of 4,687 thousand euros and admitting its shares to the Baltic Main List of the Nasdaq Tallinn Stock Exchange. The equity ratio improved to 26.7%, and the adjusted equity ratio excluding construction loans reached 32.0%. 

Looking ahead, management expects strong demand for new homes to persist and anticipates that net profit and return on equity will exceed the 20% target for the full year, supported by a robust presale portfolio covering nearly 90% of the 2026 revenue target of 59 million euros. The company expanded its development portfolio through new property acquisitions in Berlin and Tallinn and plans to initiate construction on several new project phases in the second half of 2026 to support medium-term growth.
