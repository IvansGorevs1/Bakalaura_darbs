AS Trigon Property Development’s financial performance weakened substantially in 2025, as the company recorded no real estate sales and moved from a profit to a loss. Revenue from property sales was zero, compared with €331,920 in 2024, when the company sold two land plots. As a result, gross profit also fell to zero from €220,299. The company’s operating result was a loss of €108,021, compared with operating profit of €161,649 in the previous year.

Net loss for 2025 amounted to €99,507, reversing the €167,409 net profit reported for 2024. Financial income increased to €8,513 from €5,760 but was insufficient to offset the operating loss. Fourth-quarter results followed the same trend: the company reported a net loss of €23,333, compared with a net profit of €154,215 in the fourth quarter of 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share declined to a loss of €0.02212 from earnings of €0.03721.

Administrative and general expenses increased significantly to €108,021 from €63,650, mainly reflecting €41,452 in board remuneration, compared with no personnel expenses in 2024. The company had no employees during either year. Other notable expenses included auditing, stock exchange and security fees, accounting services, and land-related costs.

At year-end, total assets stood at €1.80 million, down from €1.87 million. Inventories increased to €1.56 million from €1.47 million because €91,262 was invested in the Pärnu property development project. No inventory write-downs were recorded. Cash declined to €227,911 from €370,856, and operating activities used €142,945 of cash, compared with a €194,723 inflow in 2024. Total liabilities increased to €49,389 from €18,906, while equity decreased to €1.76 million from €1.85 million following the annual loss.

The company continued to focus on selling land from its 13.2-hectare Pärnu development project, planned for commercial, industrial, and logistics use, and reported no plans to begin a new business activity in 2026. Despite the weaker financial results, management stated that the company remains a going concern. The share price increased modestly to €0.634 from €0.620, while market capitalization rose to approximately €2.85 million.
