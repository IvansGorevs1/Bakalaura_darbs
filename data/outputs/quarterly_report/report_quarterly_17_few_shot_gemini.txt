During the twelve-month period ended 31 December 2025, AB Novaturas reported a decline in overall revenue and traveler volumes as the company prioritized profitability and operational efficiency in a competitive market environment with excess travel supply. Total revenue for the twelve months of 2025 decreased by 16.1 percent to 168.48 million euros compared with 200.88 million euros in 2024. The total number of travelers served by the group declined by 20.3 percent to 190.9 thousand clients from 239.5 thousand clients in the previous year. 

Gross profit for the twelve-month period amounted to 19.65 million euros, marking a 5.3 percent decrease from 20.74 million euros reported in 2024. Despite lower sales volumes, the group successfully achieved a positive annual EBITDA of 420 thousand euros, improving from negative 6.13 million euros in 2024 and negative 449 thousand euros on an adjusted basis for that prior year. Operating performance also improved significantly, with an operating loss of 274 thousand euros for the full year 2025, compared with an operating loss of 6.56 million euros in 2024. The net loss for the twelve-month period decreased by 84.6 percent to 1.17 million euros, compared with a net loss of 7.60 million euros in the previous year, with earnings per share standing at negative 0.15 euros. 

The financial results were supported by stricter supply discipline, optimization of travel programs, and a reduction in volumes on loss-making routes. This operational focus led to an increase in the aircraft load factor, which rose by 1.5 percentage points to 97.2 percent for the full year 2025 compared with 95.7 percent in 2024. Early booking trends also showed positive development, with bookings for the 2026 summer season increasing by 59 percent by December 2025 compared with the same period a year earlier. 

Segment data for the twelve-month period indicated that flight package tours generated 145.12 million euros in sales, down 15.0 percent year-on-year, while segment sales profit increased by 18.0 percent to 12.16 million euros. Sightseeing tours by coach generated 2.04 million euros in sales with a sales profit of 63 thousand euros. Sightseeing tours by plane recorded 2.18 million euros in sales, representing a 23.3 percent decline, and a sales profit of 135 thousand euros. Other sales contributed 19.15 million euros in revenue with a negative segment sales profit of 2.52 million euros. Geographically, customer volumes declined across all Baltic markets, with Lithuania recording 106.1 thousand clients, Latvia 38.0 thousand clients, and Estonia 46.8 thousand clients. 

As of 31 December 2025, the company reported total assets of 43.38 million euros, up from 39.45 million euros at the end of 2024. Total equity stood at 6.86 million euros compared with 8.02 million euros a year earlier. Total borrowings increased to 12.87 million euros as of 31 December 2025, from 9.25 million euros at the close of 2024, consisting of non-current borrowings of 7.50 million euros and current borrowings of 5.37 million euros. The group held 1.69 million euros in cash and cash equivalents at the end of the reporting period.
