AS Merko Ehitus’ financial performance weakened in the first half of 2026 despite a stronger second quarter and a substantially larger order book. Six-month revenue declined 10.7% year on year to EUR 149.9 million, although second-quarter revenue increased 12.1% to EUR 92.7 million as major projects moved into more active construction phases. Pre-tax profit fell to EUR 10.8 million from EUR 23.5 million, while net profit attributable to shareholders decreased to EUR 12.8 million from EUR 21.7 million. Consequently, the net profit margin declined to 8.6% from 12.9%, and earnings per share fell to EUR 0.73 from EUR 1.22. In the second quarter, net profit was EUR 8.5 million, compared with EUR 11.2 million a year earlier.

Profitability was affected by lower margins in construction services and reduced real estate development volumes. Group gross profit decreased to EUR 17.4 million from EUR 29.1 million, and operating profit fell 55.1% to EUR 8.4 million. The construction services segment generated EUR 118.3 million of revenue, down 1.5%, but operating profit dropped 65.5% to EUR 3.0 million. Its operating margin declined to 2.6% from 7.3%, reflecting the expiration of particularly successful earlier contracts and a return to more typical margins in a highly competitive market. Real estate development revenue decreased 33.9% to EUR 31.6 million, while operating profit fell 40.8% to EUR 7.0 million. Apartment sales declined to 120 from 222, and revenue from apartment sales fell to EUR 27.6 million from EUR 44.9 million. However, second-quarter development revenue and operating profit increased modestly year on year.

The main positive development was the secured order book, which reached a record EUR 835.7 million at 30 June 2026, up approximately 88% from EUR 443.8 million a year earlier. New contracts signed during the first half totaled EUR 486.4 million, more than double the prior-year amount. Growth was driven largely by public-sector, Rail Baltica, defence-related and energy-infrastructure projects. The Rūdninkai defence force campus entered full-scale construction after the reporting date; its two phases have a combined construction cost exceeding EUR 370 million.

Cash and cash equivalents rose to EUR 39.6 million from EUR 25.9 million, but operating cash flow was negative at EUR 31.6 million, primarily because of increased inventories and construction-contract balances. Residential development investment increased to EUR 42.6 million from EUR 22.5 million, supported by additional borrowing. Net debt rose to EUR 29.6 million, while the equity ratio remained strong at 57.4%. Overall, Merko entered the second half with weaker earnings and cash conversion but a significantly stronger project pipeline and increased construction activity.
