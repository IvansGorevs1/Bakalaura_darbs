Ambu A/S reported a solid start to the 2025/26 financial year, characterized by continued strong growth momentum in its Endoscopy Solutions segment. Total revenue increased organically by 8.6% to DKK 1,558 million, compared to DKK 1,510 million in the same quarter of the previous year. On a reported basis, revenue growth was 3.2%, negatively impacted by foreign exchange headwinds of 5.4%. The Endoscopy Solutions business group, which accounted for 63% of total revenue, delivered robust organic growth of 14.4%, driven primarily by the Urology, ENT, & GI group, which grew by 21.0%. This growth was fueled by the continued penetration of the aScope 4 portfolio and strong momentum for the newly launched aScope 5 Uretero. The Respiratory business group also contributed to this success with 8.3% organic growth, supported by the adoption of aScope 5 Broncho and the recent launch of the SureSight Mobile video laryngoscope. In contrast, the Anesthesia & Patient Monitoring segment posted flat organic growth of -0.1%, reflecting an extraordinarily high revenue baseline in the prior year period. Geographically, North America and Europe both contributed positively with organic growth of 9.8% and 9.7%, respectively, while the Rest of World region declined by 1.6% due to order fluctuations and high comparables.

Profitability metrics showed pressure from external factors. EBIT before special items amounted to DKK 164 million, down from DKK 243 million in Q1 2024/25, resulting in an EBIT margin of 10.5%, a decrease of 5.6 percentage points. The report notes that this margin was negatively impacted by tariff costs exceeding DKK 50 million and foreign exchange headwinds. However, adjusting for these external factors, the underlying EBIT margin was 15.2%, demonstrating continued operational leverage from organic growth. Operating expenses to revenue increased to 50.3% from 45.2% in the prior year, partly due to the inclusion of tariff costs and ongoing investments in commercial infrastructure to drive future growth. Gross margin declined slightly to 60.8% from 61.3%, primarily due to the high comparable gross margin in the previous year.

Cash flow performance was impacted by working capital changes and external costs. Free cash flow before acquisitions totaled DKK 13 million, down from DKK 69 million in the prior year. This decline was mainly driven by an increase in net working capital, which remained elevated to mitigate global geopolitical uncertainties and support growth. Cash flow from operating activities was DKK 106 million, compared to DKK 144 million previously. The company maintained its financial guidance for the full year, expecting organic revenue growth of 10-13% and an EBIT margin before special items of 12-14%. Ambu also announced the initiation of a share buyback program with DKK 38 million spent on treasury shares in Q1 and paid a dividend of DKK 90 million. The company’s net interest-bearing debt stood at a net cash position of DKK 178 million, indicating a strong balance sheet despite the temporary headwinds. Overall, Ambu demonstrated resilience and strategic execution, with the Endoscopy Solutions segment continuing to be the primary engine for growth, while management remains committed to long-term investments and sustainability initiatives, including the expansion of its recycling program.
