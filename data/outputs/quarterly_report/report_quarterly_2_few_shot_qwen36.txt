Amber Grid AB reported a significant improvement in financial performance for the three months ended 31 March 2026. The company’s revenue increased by 58% to EUR 32,821 thousand, compared to EUR 20,763 thousand in the same period of the previous year. This growth was primarily driven by a 35% increase in the volume of natural gas transmitted and higher transmission prices set for 2026. Conversely, revenue from system balancing products decreased by 99% due to a new transit agreement with Kaliningrad that reduced the need for balancing gas.

Operating profitability improved substantially. EBITDA rose to EUR 23,304 thousand from EUR 8,918 thousand in the prior year period. Operating expenses decreased by 19% to EUR 9,570 thousand, largely due to a 68% reduction in purchases of natural gas and related services, which fell to EUR 1,477 thousand from EUR 4,622 thousand. Although wages and salaries increased by 19% to EUR 4,367 thousand, the overall cost structure became more efficient. Net profit surged to EUR 15,763 thousand, up from EUR 4,239 thousand in the previous year, resulting in basic earnings per share of EUR 0.09 compared to EUR 0.02.

The company’s balance sheet reflects changes in its capital structure and liquidity. Total assets remained relatively stable at EUR 324,064 thousand. Equity increased to EUR 182,039 thousand, supported by the accumulated net profit. A key development was the restructuring of borrowings. The company reclassified loans from the Nordic Investment Bank and the European Investment Bank from current to non-current liabilities after entering into a tripartite loan transfer agreement effective 25 February 2026. This agreement transferred the remaining loan amount of EUR 9,783 thousand to the parent company EPSO-G, removing the company from financial covenants. Consequently, total borrowings decreased to EUR 104,174 thousand from EUR 123,666 thousand at the end of 2025. Net debt stood at EUR 106,610 thousand, an increase from EUR 76,052 thousand in the previous year, primarily due to higher non-current borrowings and lower cash reserves.

Cash flow from operating activities increased significantly to EUR 22,927 thousand from EUR 8,264 thousand, reflecting higher profitability and changes in working capital. However, net cash used in financing activities was EUR 20,467 thousand, driven by repayments of borrowings and changes in overdrafts. The company’s cash and cash equivalents decreased to EUR 832 thousand from EUR 1,942 thousand at the end of 2025. The board approved a dividend of EUR 9,989 thousand for the 2025 financial year, to be distributed following the shareholder meeting in April 2026. The company continues to operate as a natural gas transmission system operator in Lithuania, with its results influenced by regulatory decisions and market dynamics in gas transit and balancing.
