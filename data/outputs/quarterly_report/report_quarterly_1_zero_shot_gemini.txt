During the three months ended March 31, 2026, AB KN Energies and its subsidiaries demonstrated overall improved financial performance compared to the same period in 2025. For the reporting period, the Group reported revenue from contracts with customers of EUR 29,823 thousand, reflecting an increase compared to EUR 25,326 thousand for the first three months of 2025. The Company similarly generated revenue of EUR 28,681 thousand, up from EUR 24,283 thousand in the previous year.

The Group achieved a gross profit of EUR 12,907 thousand for the three months of 2026, marking an increase from EUR 9,793 thousand in the comparative period of 2025. Operating profit for the Group rose to EUR 9,608 thousand from EUR 7,141 thousand. Net finance costs for the Group improved, amounting to a negative EUR 784 thousand compared to a negative EUR 1,777 thousand in the prior year period, aided by a net gain on derivatives of EUR 458 thousand and reduced interest expenses on borrowings. Profit before income tax for the Group increased to EUR 8,824 thousand from EUR 5,364 thousand. Net profit for the period reached EUR 7,247 thousand for the Group, compared to EUR 4,426 thousand in the first three months of 2025. The Company recorded a net profit of EUR 6,762 thousand, compared to EUR 4,016 thousand previously. Basic and diluted earnings per share for both the Group and the Company stood at EUR 0.02 for the three months of 2026, up from EUR 0.01 in the prior year period.

The financial results were driven by developments across operating segments, primarily the Liquefied Natural Gas Terminal activities and liquid energy terminals. Segment performance was affected by categories such as wages, salaries, and social security expenses of EUR 4,967 thousand for the Group, expenses related to the Floating Storage Regasification Unit of EUR 2,095 thousand, and taxes on environmental pollution and emission allowances of EUR 2,225 thousand. 

As of March 31, 2026, the Group reported total assets of EUR 556,798 thousand and total equity of EUR 179,866 thousand, compared to total assets of EUR 554,958 thousand and total equity of EUR 172,592 thousand as of December 31, 2025. Borrowings stood at EUR 302,069 thousand at the end of March 2026, down from EUR 311,194 thousand at the end of December 2025, consisting of loans from the Nordic Investment Bank and the European Investment Bank along with payable interest and capitalised fees. Cash and cash equivalents increased to EUR 17,986 thousand as of March 31, 2026, from EUR 12,361 thousand as of December 31, 2025. Net cash flows from operating activities for the Group were EUR 27,917 thousand for the three months of 2026, compared to EUR 13,758 thousand in the prior year period. Net cash flows used in investing activities were EUR 10,771 thousand, and net cash flows used in financing activities were EUR 11,521 thousand. 

Significant business events during the period included the expiration of the term of office of the Board on April 25, 2026, and the election of a new Board on April 30, 2026, consisting of four independent members and two representatives delegated by the Ministry of Energy, which replaced the previous two-tier governance structure.
