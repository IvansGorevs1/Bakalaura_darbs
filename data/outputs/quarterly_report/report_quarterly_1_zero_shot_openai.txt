AB KN Energies reported a stronger first quarter in 2026, with consolidated revenue increasing 17.8% to EUR 29.823 million from EUR 25.326 million in the same period of 2025. Gross profit rose 31.8% to EUR 12.907 million, despite cost of sales increasing 8.9% to EUR 16.916 million. Operating expenses increased 10.5% to EUR 3.316 million, while operating profit grew 34.5% to EUR 9.608 million.

Net finance costs improved substantially to EUR 0.784 million from EUR 1.777 million a year earlier. Finance income increased to EUR 1.076 million, including a EUR 458 thousand net gain on derivatives, while finance costs declined to EUR 1.860 million from EUR 2.345 million. Consequently, profit before tax increased 64.5% to EUR 8.824 million. After income tax expense of EUR 1.577 million, consolidated net profit rose 63.8% to EUR 7.247 million, compared with EUR 4.426 million in the first quarter of 2025. Earnings per share increased from EUR 0.01 to EUR 0.02. Total comprehensive income was EUR 7.274 million, including EUR 27 thousand of foreign-currency translation gains.

The LNG terminal segment was the main contributor to the improvement. LNG terminal revenue increased to EUR 18.687 million from EUR 14.989 million, and segment net profit rose to EUR 4.529 million from EUR 1.328 million. Commercial LNG revenue increased to EUR 3.357 million from EUR 2.473 million, with segment net profit reaching EUR 1.128 million versus EUR 0.766 million. Liquid energy terminals revenue was broadly stable at EUR 7.779 million compared with EUR 7.864 million, but segment net profit declined to EUR 1.590 million from EUR 2.332 million. Group EBITDA increased 18.8% to EUR 16.153 million, although liquid energy terminals EBITDA fell to EUR 3.002 million from EUR 3.724 million.

Operating cash flow more than doubled to EUR 27.917 million from EUR 13.758 million, supported principally by a EUR 7.010 million decrease in receivables, compared with an increase of EUR 2.184 million in 2025. Investing cash outflow increased to EUR 10.771 million, including EUR 4.906 million of capital expenditure, while financing outflow rose to EUR 11.521 million due mainly to higher debt repayments and interest paid. Cash and cash equivalents increased to EUR 17.986 million from EUR 12.361 million at year-end, while total borrowings declined to EUR 302.069 million from EUR 311.194 million. Total assets were EUR 556.798 million and equity increased to EUR 179.866 million from EUR 172.592 million.
