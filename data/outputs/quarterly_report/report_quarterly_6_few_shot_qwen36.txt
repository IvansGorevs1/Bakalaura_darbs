Infortar Group reported a significant improvement in profitability during the first half of 2026, despite a modest decline in total revenue. The group’s consolidated revenue decreased by 0.6% to EUR 945.9 million, compared to EUR 951.9 million in the same period last year. This reduction was primarily driven by the energy segment, where Elenger Grupp’s gas and electricity sales volumes fell by 34% due to lower wholesale sales and reduced consumption in the Finnish-Baltic region. Conversely, the maritime transport segment maintained stable revenue at EUR 350.1 million, supported by a 2.9% increase in cargo units, although passenger numbers declined slightly.

Profitability metrics showed strong positive momentum. EBITDA increased by 30.8% to EUR 111.2 million, with the EBITDA margin expanding from 8.9% to 11.8%. Operating profit (EBIT) nearly doubled, rising by 93.5% to EUR 51.1 million. This substantial improvement in earnings was not driven by a single segment but was broadly supported by performance across the group. The most notable turnaround occurred in the supporting businesses segment, which shifted from a loss of EUR 4.4 million to a profit of EUR 5.6 million, aided by the addition of new agricultural units and the closure of unprofitable operations. The energy segment also contributed positively, with operating profit increasing to EUR 45.5 million from EUR 38.0 million, despite lower sales volumes. The maritime segment saw its operating loss narrow significantly to EUR 3.0 million from a loss of EUR 12.9 million, although it remained impacted by higher fuel costs and geopolitical tensions affecting energy markets.

The group’s net profit attributable to owners of the parent company turned positive, reaching EUR 15.2 million, compared to a net loss of EUR 2.5 million in the prior year period. This reversal was influenced by a change in income tax recognition, including a significant tax charge on dividends recorded in the maritime segment. Cash and cash equivalents increased to EUR 253.2 million, reflecting effective cash flow management and the maintenance of a liquidity buffer for new investments. Total assets grew to EUR 2.71 billion, while total equity increased to EUR 1.20 billion. The equity ratio decreased slightly to 44.2% from 45.3% at the end of 2025, and net debt to EBITDA improved to 3.3x from 3.7x.

Key operational developments included the completion of the Halinga biomethane plant, which integrates agriculture and energy production, and the continued construction of major infrastructure projects such as the Rail Baltica mainline. Infortar also increased its ownership stake in Tallink Grupp to 68.76% during the quarter. The company maintained a stable balance sheet with moderate debt levels, enabling it to focus on long-term value creation through sustainable core business operations and strategic investments in renewable energy and infrastructure.
