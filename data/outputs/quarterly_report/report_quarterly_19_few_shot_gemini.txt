AS Pro Kapital Grupp reported a significant improvement in financial performance for the twelve months ended 31 December 2025, driven primarily by strong real estate sales and the completion and handover of key residential development projects. Total revenue for the year surged by 193% to EUR 53.2 million, compared with EUR 18.2 million in 2024. Fourth quarter revenue also increased notably to EUR 18.9 million from EUR 7.6 million in the same period of the previous year. 

The significant growth in revenue was directly linked to the construction cycle and the timing of project completions, particularly the handover of completed apartments in the Kalaranna and Uus-Kindrali developments in Tallinn, as well as the City Villas project in Vilnius. Consequently, gross profit for 2025 rose significantly to EUR 18.8 million, up from EUR 5.4 million in 2024, with the gross profit margin improving to 35% from 30%. Operating profit experienced a dramatic turnaround, reaching EUR 14.7 million compared with just EUR 0.1 million in the prior year, while operating profit margin rose to 28% from 1%. 

Net profit for the twelve-month period turned positive at EUR 12.0 million, recovering sharply from a net loss of EUR 3.9 million in 2024. Fourth quarter net profit also improved to EUR 6.0 million compared with EUR 0.2 million in the final quarter of 2024. Basic earnings per share for 2025 were EUR 0.22, compared with a loss per share of EUR 0.06 in the previous year. 

Cash flows from operating activities improved substantially, generating EUR 10.8 million in 2025 compared with cash used of EUR 10.4 million in 2024. Total assets increased to EUR 124.5 million as at 31 December 2025 from EUR 118.8 million a year earlier. Total liabilities decreased to EUR 61.2 million from EUR 67.5 million, supported by the partial redemption of secured non-convertible bonds and active management of bank borrowings. Total equity increased to EUR 63.3 million from EUR 51.2 million, and net asset value per share rose to EUR 1.12 from EUR 0.89. 

The financial results were achieved through disciplined execution across the Group's main markets in the Baltics, Germany, and Italy. In Tallinn, operations contributed 75% of total revenues, bolstered by handovers in the Kalaranna District and the Uus-Kindrali residential project. In Riga, construction commenced on the Blue Marine project within the Klīversala Quarter, and inventory was fully sold out. In Vilnius, final-stage construction near completion at Šaltinių Namai Attico contributed to the Lithuanian segment representing 12% of total revenues. The German hotel operations and Italian bare ownership brokerage activities provided stable, albeit subdued, contributions amid challenging broader market conditions. Overall, the reporting period reflected robust operational momentum, successful project delivery, and a return to strong profitability for the Group.
