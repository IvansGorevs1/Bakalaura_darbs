Hepsor’s financial performance weakened in 2025 compared with 2024, primarily because fewer apartments were handed over and project margins declined. Consolidated revenue decreased by 7.8% to €35.4 million from €38.4 million, while gross profit fell to €5.6 million from €6.8 million. The gross profit margin declined to 15.9% from 17.6%, reflecting a lower gross margin on sold projects, which decreased to 16.9% from 17.8%. Marketing expenses increased substantially to €1.3 million from €0.9 million as the Group expanded the number and scale of projects on sale.

Operating profit declined by 22.5% to €3.4 million, and the operating margin decreased to 9.5% from 11.3%. Net profit fell by 51.3% to €1.0 million, although profit attributable to the owners of the parent company remained broadly unchanged at €0.4 million. The larger reduction in total net profit was mainly reflected in lower profit attributable to non-controlling interests, which decreased to €0.6 million from €1.7 million. Earnings per share declined to €0.10 from €0.11.

Fourth-quarter results were particularly weak. Revenue fell to €4.5 million from €10.5 million, and the Group recorded a net loss of €0.5 million compared with a €1.0 million profit in the fourth quarter of 2024. Fourth-quarter gross profit declined to €0.7 million, with the margin falling to 15.3% from 24.0%. Results included €0.8 million of other operating income from revaluing properties transferred from inventories to investment property, which partly supported operating performance.

The number of apartments delivered declined to 141 from 194, but sales activity strengthened. The Group concluded primary sales agreements for 185 homes worth €36.1 million, compared with 109 homes worth €19.8 million in 2024. The development portfolio expanded to 33 active projects from 24, while apartments under construction increased to 428 from 192, supported by new projects in Estonia and Latvia. Revenue also became more geographically balanced, with Estonia contributing €26.1 million and Latvia €9.3 million.

The balance sheet strengthened despite lower profitability. Total assets increased to €91.8 million, equity rose 23.0% to €27.1 million, and the equity ratio improved to 29.5% from 24.8%. Interest-bearing liabilities declined to €47.7 million from €54.7 million, reducing the debt ratio to 52.0% from 61.6%. However, cash decreased to €3.8 million from €6.2 million. In November, Hepsor raised €8.0 million through a bond issue carrying a fixed annual interest rate of 9.50%. The Group also paid €1.0 million in dividends. Overall, 2025 combined weaker reported earnings and cash generation with significant expansion of the development pipeline and construction activity.
