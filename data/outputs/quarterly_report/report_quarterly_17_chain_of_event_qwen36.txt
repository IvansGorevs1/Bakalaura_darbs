AB Novaturas reported consolidated revenue of EUR 168.5 million for the twelve-month period ended 31 December 2025, representing a 16.1 percent decline from EUR 200.9 million in the prior year. The number of travelers served decreased by 20.3 percent to 191 thousand, down from 239.5 thousand in 2024. Despite the reduction in volume, the Group achieved a positive Adjusted EBITDA of EUR 420 thousand, a significant improvement from the negative EUR 449 thousand recorded in 2024. This turnaround was driven by a strategic decision to prioritize profitability over volume, resulting in the optimization of the travel program and the reduction of volumes on loss-making routes. Gross profit margin improved by 1.3 percentage points to 11.66 percent, reflecting better operational efficiency and stricter supply discipline.

The financial performance was influenced by intense market competition and an excess of travel supply, which pressured prices, particularly during the autumn season. Additionally, disruptions at Vilnius Airport caused by illegal cross-border activity involving unmanned balloons negatively impacted last-minute travel demand, limiting the final results for the second half of the year. However, the company managed to maintain high aircraft load factors, which increased by 1.5 percentage points to 97.2 percent for the full year. Load factors on long-haul destinations rose to 98.5 percent, and ski destinations saw a notable increase to 97.2 percent.

Operating profit for the period was a loss of EUR 274 thousand, an improvement of 95.8 percent compared to the EUR 6.6 million loss in 2024. The net loss decreased by 39 percent to EUR 1.2 million, excluding a one-off non-cash impairment of goodwill amounting to EUR 5.7 million recorded in the previous year. Earnings per share improved to negative EUR 0.15 from negative EUR 0.97 in 2024. The return on equity improved significantly, rising by 77.8 percentage points to negative 17.03 percent.

Segment analysis indicates that flight package tours, the core business, generated EUR 145.1 million in sales, a 15.0 percent decrease, but achieved a sales profit margin of 8 percent, up from 1 percent in the prior year. Other sales segments, including sightseeing tours and other products, experienced sharper declines in revenue but also showed margin improvements or reduced losses. Early bookings for the 2026 summer season increased by 59 percent by December 2025, signaling strong demand for specific destinations such as Greece, Bulgaria, Tunisia, and Turkey. The company plans to maintain a similar or slightly higher travel program for 2026, focusing on cost efficiency and expanding its hotel portfolio to enhance competitiveness. Total borrowings increased to EUR 12.9 million from EUR 9.3 million in 2024, while total assets stood at EUR 43.4 million. The company’s market capitalization was EUR 8.82 million at the end of the period.
