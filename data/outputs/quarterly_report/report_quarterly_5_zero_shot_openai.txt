Arco Vara reported a marked improvement in first-quarter 2025 performance as Estonia’s real estate market showed moderate recovery, supported by declining Euribor, stabilizing inflation and increased residential-market activity. Revenue rose to €1.706 million from €0.990 million in Q1 2024, an increase of €716 thousand, or approximately 72%. Real estate sales contributed €1.589 million, compared with €927 thousand a year earlier, while service revenue increased to €117 thousand from €63 thousand. Four apartments were sold during the quarter, compared with three apartments and one commercial premise in Q1 2024.

The group returned to profitability. Operating profit was €259 thousand, versus an operating loss of €208 thousand in Q1 2024, while net profit reached €103 thousand compared with a net loss of €349 thousand. Gross profit increased to €598 thousand from €173 thousand, despite cost of sales rising to €1.108 million from €817 thousand. Marketing expenses were broadly stable at €69 thousand, and administrative expenses declined to €270 thousand from €297 thousand. Finance costs remained similar at €143 thousand, compared with €141 thousand, while income tax expense was €13 thousand. Basic and diluted earnings per share improved to €0.01 from negative €0.03.

Cash flow remained negative because of development investment. Operating cash flow was negative €2.838 million, compared with negative €2.217 million in Q1 2024, while financing activities generated €2.231 million, including €3.090 million of new borrowings. Net cash outflow was €607 thousand, improving from €738 thousand, and cash at quarter-end was €1.113 million versus €1.720 million at year-end 2024.

Total assets increased to €42.728 million from €39.568 million at 31 December 2024, mainly reflecting higher inventories, which rose to €32.329 million from €29.170 million. The group acquired the Spordi 3a and 3b development site in Tallinn, financed partly through a €2.190 million bank loan; the planned €15 million project will comprise 56 apartments, with presales scheduled for Q2 2025. Net loans increased by €1.960 million to €15.455 million, while total interest-bearing liabilities reached €16.568 million. The weighted average loan interest rate declined to 8.2% from 8.7% at year-end, reflecting lower Euribor.

Operationally, Rannakalda sold four additional apartments, bringing cumulative sales to 91 of 113 units. Botanica Lozen reached 95% completion, with 9 of 16 houses sold, while Soodi 6 had 10 of 66 apartments presold. Preparatory and planning work progressed on Kodulahe, Arcojärve and other projects. Overall, the quarter was characterized by improved profitability, continued portfolio expansion and preparation for larger development launches, although cash generation and sales execution remained key challenges.
