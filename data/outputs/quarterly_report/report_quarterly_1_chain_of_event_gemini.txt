For the three months ended 31 March 2026, AB KN Energies reported significant improvements in its financial performance compared to the same period in 2025. The Group recorded consolidated revenue from contracts with customers of EUR 29,823 thousand, marking an increase from EUR 25,326 thousand for the first three months of 2025. This growth was accompanied by a higher gross profit of EUR 12,907 thousand, compared to EUR 9,793 thousand in the previous year. Operating profit for the Group rose to EUR 9,608 thousand from EUR 7,141 thousand, driven by higher revenues and improved operational results. Net finance costs showed a favorable change, decreasing from a negative EUR 1,777 thousand in the three months of 2025 to a negative EUR 784 thousand in the three months of 2026, supported by an increase in finance income to EUR 1,076 thousand from EUR 568 thousand and a reduction in finance costs to EUR 1,860 thousand from EUR 2,345 thousand. Consequently, the Group's profit before income tax grew to EUR 8,824 thousand compared to EUR 5,364 thousand, and net profit for the period increased to EUR 7,247 thousand from EUR 4,426 thousand. Basic and diluted earnings per share for the Group improved to EUR 0.02 from EUR 0.01. Similar positive trends were observed in the Company-only results, where revenue increased to EUR 28,681 thousand from EUR 24,283 thousand, gross profit rose to EUR 12,150 thousand from EUR 9,050 thousand, operating profit reached EUR 9,159 thousand compared to EUR 6,652 thousand, and net profit for the period advanced to EUR 6,762 thousand from EUR 4,016 thousand, with basic and diluted earnings per share also rising to EUR 0.02 from EUR 0.01. The financial position of the Group remained solid, with total assets of EUR 556,798 thousand as at 31 March 2026 compared to EUR 554,958 thousand as at 31 December 2025, and total equity increasing to EUR 179,866 thousand from EUR 172,592 thousand. The Group generated robust net cash flows from operating activities amounting to EUR 27,917 thousand for the three months of 2026, up from EUR 13,758 thousand in the comparable 2025 period. These financial results were influenced by higher revenues collected directly from liquefied natural gas terminal users for regasification services and increased sales of consulting services, alongside changes in finance income and reduced borrowing-related finance costs.
