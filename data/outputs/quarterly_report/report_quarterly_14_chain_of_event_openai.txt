AS Merko Ehitus reported revenue of EUR 149.9 million for the first six months of 2026, down 10.7% from EUR 167.9 million a year earlier. However, second-quarter revenue increased 12.1% to EUR 92.7 million from EUR 82.6 million, reflecting higher activity in both construction services and real estate development. Profitability weakened substantially in the half-year: operating profit fell 55.1% to EUR 8.4 million, pre-tax profit declined 54.1% to EUR 10.8 million, and net profit attributable to shareholders decreased 40.8% to EUR 12.8 million. The net profit margin fell to 8.6% from 12.9%, while earnings per share declined to EUR 0.73 from EUR 1.22. Second-quarter net profit was EUR 8.5 million, compared with EUR 11.2 million in the prior-year quarter.

The reduction in earnings was mainly reflected in the construction services segment. Six-month segment revenue decreased 1.5% to EUR 118.3 million, but operating profit dropped 65.5% to EUR 3.0 million and the operating margin declined to 2.6% from 7.3%. Management attributed the lower margin to the expiration of successful contracts highlighted in previous periods and a return to more typical margins in a highly competitive market, with first-half results also affected by first-quarter seasonality. Construction input prices, including materials and equipment, increased during the second quarter, and management expects continuing pressure from labour and energy costs.

Real estate development revenue fell 33.9% to EUR 31.6 million and operating profit declined 40.8% to EUR 7.0 million. The group sold 120 apartments in the first half, compared with 222 a year earlier, although second-quarter apartment revenue was broadly stable at EUR 20.6 million versus EUR 20.2 million. The group launched construction of 204 apartments, down from 713, while investing EUR 42.6 million in ongoing development projects, compared with EUR 22.5 million.

The most significant positive development was the secured order book, which reached a record EUR 835.7 million at 30 June 2026, up approximately 88% from EUR 443.8 million a year earlier. New contracts signed in the first half totalled EUR 486.4 million, more than double the prior-year amount. Growth was driven especially by public-sector, Rail Baltica, defence-related and energy-independence projects. Preparatory stages for the Rūdninkai defence campus were completed after the reporting date, allowing full-scale construction to begin; the two phases have a combined construction cost exceeding EUR 370 million.

Cash and cash equivalents increased to EUR 39.6 million from EUR 25.9 million, but operating cash flow deteriorated to negative EUR 31.6 million, primarily because inventories rose by EUR 24.1 million amid increased apartment construction. Additional borrowing supported development activity, raising net debt to EUR 29.6 million from net cash of EUR 1.1 million a year earlier. Equity remained strong at EUR 251.1 million, although the equity ratio declined to 57.4% from 60.1%, partly following payment of EUR 22.1 million in dividends.
