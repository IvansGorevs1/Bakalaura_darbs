AB Pieno Žvaigždės reported a significant improvement in financial performance for the first quarter of 2026 compared to the same period in the previous year. The company achieved total revenue of 52,064 thousand EUR, which represents a slight decrease of approximately 0.5 percent from the 52,324 thousand EUR recorded in the first quarter of 2025. Despite the marginal decline in top-line sales, the company demonstrated enhanced operational efficiency and profitability. The cost of sales decreased substantially to 38,078 thousand EUR from 42,530 thousand EUR in the prior year period. This reduction in costs drove a substantial increase in gross profit, which rose to 13,986 thousand EUR, up from 9,794 thousand EUR in the first quarter of 2025. This represents a growth of approximately 42.8 percent in gross profit.

Operating performance also showed marked improvement. Operating profit before finance costs reached 3,366 thousand EUR, a significant increase from the 535 thousand EUR reported in the same period last year. This improvement was supported by higher gross margins, partially offset by an increase in sales and administrative expenses, which rose to 10,983 thousand EUR from 9,287 thousand EUR. Finance costs remained relatively stable, with net finance expenses of 298 thousand EUR compared to 308 thousand EUR in the previous year. Consequently, profit before tax surged to 3,068 thousand EUR from just 227 thousand EUR in the first quarter of 2025. The company recorded a net profit of 3,068 thousand EUR, resulting in earnings per share of 0.07 EUR, compared to 0.01 EUR in the prior year period. EBITDA also more than doubled, reaching 4,730 thousand EUR from 2,001 thousand EUR.

Regarding the financial position, total assets increased to 85,305 thousand EUR at the end of March 2026 from 83,475 thousand EUR at the end of 2025. Total equity grew to 41,164 thousand EUR, up from 38,238 thousand EUR, primarily due to the retention of earnings. The current ratio improved to 1.51 from 1.27, indicating stronger short-term liquidity. Cash and cash equivalents increased to 2,157 thousand EUR from 1,806 thousand EUR at the end of the previous year. Net cash flow from operating activities was positive at 1,625 thousand EUR, a stark contrast to the negative cash flow of negative 183 thousand EUR in the first quarter of 2025. Investing activities used 551 thousand EUR, while financing activities used 723 thousand EUR, largely due to loan repayments. The company continues to operate primarily in the domestic Lithuanian market and other EU countries, with fresh milk products remaining the largest sales category. No material post-balance sheet events occurred during the reporting period.
