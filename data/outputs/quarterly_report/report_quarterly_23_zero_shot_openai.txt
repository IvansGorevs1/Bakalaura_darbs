For the nine months of 2025, AB KN Energies reported stronger consolidated performance than in the same period of 2024. Group revenue increased 12.8% to EUR 76.664 million, while gross profit rose 7.9% to EUR 31.549 million. Operating profit increased 12.4% to EUR 21.282 million, despite higher distribution and administrative expenses of EUR 10.640 million, compared with EUR 10.419 million a year earlier.

Profit before tax grew 8.6% to EUR 16.186 million. Net profit increased 10.9% to EUR 13.158 million, raising basic and diluted earnings per share from EUR 0.031 to EUR 0.035. Total comprehensive income was EUR 13.215 million, compared with EUR 11.615 million in the prior-year period. The company’s separate net profit rose 8.5%, from EUR 10.702 million to EUR 11.613 million.

The principal contributor to revenue growth was LNG terminal activity. LNGT revenue increased to EUR 45.568 million from EUR 38.426 million, while liquid energy terminal revenue rose to EUR 21.856 million from EUR 21.066 million. Commercial LNG revenue increased to EUR 9.240 million from EUR 8.485 million. Management attributed the higher LNGT revenue to the terminal’s 2024 shutdown for one and a half months of dry-dock repairs, higher LNG regasification volumes and bonuses paid by clients for booked capacity. These developments were partly offset by lower payments for unused allocated capacity and the reduction of LNG security supplement income, including recognition of repayments related to excess returns from regulated activities in 2022–2023.

Cost of sales increased to EUR 45.115 million from EUR 38.740 million. Depreciation and amortisation rose substantially following the acquisition of the floating storage and regasification unit in December 2024; EUR 11.369 million of related depreciation was recorded in cost of sales during 2025. Finance income declined to EUR 1.520 million from EUR 2.644 million, while finance costs were broadly stable at EUR 6.616 million compared with EUR 6.692 million.

Operating cash flow more than doubled to EUR 76.331 million from EUR 35.637 million. After investing and financing outflows, net cash increased by EUR 37.195 million, and cash and cash equivalents reached EUR 54.398 million at 30 September 2025, compared with EUR 17.203 million at year-end 2024. Total assets decreased slightly to EUR 530.364 million, while total equity increased to EUR 167.721 million. Borrowings declined to EUR 311.443 million from EUR 320.352 million, and EUR 8.622 million of dividends were paid during the period.
