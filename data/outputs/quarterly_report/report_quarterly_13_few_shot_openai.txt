Liven AS’s operating performance improved in the first half of 2026, although profitability weakened because revenue recognition remained uneven and the second quarter was affected by lower handovers, dividend-related income tax and one-off IPO costs. The group signed 57 sales contracts in the second quarter, compared with 31 a year earlier, bringing the six-month total to 111, nearly double the 56 contracts signed in the first half of 2025. The weekly sales ratio rose to almost 3.1% at quarter-end, with the quarterly average increasing to approximately 2.4% from 1.5% in the first quarter. Presales reached EUR 48.6 million at the start of the third quarter, including EUR 39.8 million for projects scheduled for completion in 2026.

Revenue for the second quarter declined 39% year on year to EUR 4.503 million, as only 12 homes were handed over compared with 35 in the second quarter of 2025 and no new project was completed. Profit before tax fell to a loss of EUR 234 thousand from a profit of EUR 1.002 million. Net loss was EUR 618 thousand, compared with net profit of EUR 974 thousand a year earlier. The result included approximately EUR 90 thousand of IPO-related distribution and administrative costs and EUR 384 thousand of income tax associated with dividends.

For the six months, revenue increased 23% to EUR 11.439 million, despite handovers declining to 26 homes and commercial units from 41. Profit before tax decreased to EUR 186 thousand from EUR 296 thousand, while net profit turned into a EUR 198 thousand loss from a EUR 268 thousand profit. EBITDA declined to EUR 2.425 million from EUR 2.777 million, and the EBITDA margin fell to 21.2% from 29.8%. Operating profit, however, increased slightly to EUR 341 thousand from EUR 325 thousand. Basic earnings per share were negative EUR 0.016, compared with positive EUR 0.022 in the prior-year period.

The balance sheet expanded as construction activity continued. Assets increased to EUR 100.186 million from EUR 86.457 million at year-end 2025, primarily reflecting higher inventories and investment properties. Equity rose to EUR 26.746 million, and the equity ratio improved to 26.7% from 19.4% a year earlier. Total borrowings increased to EUR 57.341 million, including EUR 16.561 million of construction loans. Cash reached EUR 10.033 million after the group raised EUR 4.932 million through its initial public offering, paid EUR 1.362 million in dividends and expanded project financing.

The company listed its shares on Nasdaq Tallinn’s Baltic Main List in May and added properties in Berlin and Tallinn to its development portfolio. Portfolio forecast revenue increased to EUR 497.2 million, supported by new acquisitions. Management expects most 2026 handovers, revenue and profit to occur in the final quarter and forecasts revenue of EUR 59 million for the year, with nearly 90% already earned or covered by contracts at the half-year.
