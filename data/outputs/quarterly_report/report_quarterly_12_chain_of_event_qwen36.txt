AS LHV Group reported a consolidated net profit of EUR 24.7 million for the second quarter of 2026, representing a 25 percent increase from EUR 19.7 million in the first quarter of 2026. However, this figure marks a 20 percent decline compared to EUR 30.8 million in the same period of the previous year. Earnings per share stood at EUR 0.07, up from EUR 0.06 in Q1 2026 but down from EUR 0.09 in Q2 2025. The Group’s total net income for the quarter reached EUR 79.0 million, an increase of 7.3 percent compared to Q1 2026 and 7 percent compared to Q2 2025.

The primary driver of the improved quarter-over-quarter performance was a 5 percent growth in net interest income to EUR 62.5 million, supported by higher market interest rates and increased loan volumes. Net service fee income remained stable at EUR 14.6 million. Operating expenses decreased by 4 percent to EUR 43.6 million, contributing to the rise in operating profit, which grew to EUR 35.4 million from EUR 28.1 million in the prior quarter. Conversely, the year-over-year decline in net profit was primarily attributable to a significant increase in loan and bond provisions. Provisions amounted to EUR 5.8 million in Q2 2026, compared to a reversal of EUR 4.2 million in Q2 2025. The Management Board noted that these higher provisions were due to model-based, forward-looking impairment charges for specific clients, despite maintaining that the overall loan portfolio quality remains strong and actual losses are not anticipated to materialize.

Asset growth continued, with the loan portfolio expanding by 3 percent to EUR 5,768 million, driven by increases in home loans and corporate lending. The volume of deposits decreased by EUR 245 million to EUR 7,557 million, largely due to a reduction in platform deposits and financial intermediary deposits. The Group’s return on equity for the quarter was 13.1 percent, improving from 10.7 percent in Q1 2026 but falling short of the 17.4 percent recorded in Q2 2025. Capital adequacy stood at 20.8 percent, down from 21.7 percent in the first quarter and 23.0 percent in the previous year, yet remaining above internal and regulatory targets.

Strategic developments during the quarter included the successful migration of the bank’s core systems to a cloud-based architecture, which aims to enhance scalability and support international growth. The bank also transitioned to a product-based management model and received a crypto-assets service provider license. In the insurance segment, LHV Kindlustus returned to profitability with a net profit of EUR 0.8 million, aided by favorable weather conditions and lower loss frequency. LHV Varahaldus reported a modest net profit of EUR 0.4 million, while the UK subsidiary, LHV Bank, earned EUR 1.4 million. The Group’s financial performance was slightly weaker than planned, primarily due to higher-than-expected operating expenses and lower service fee income resulting from reduced client activity.
