Nordic Fibreboard AS reported a consolidated net revenue of 1.86 million euros for the first quarter of 2026, representing a 7.3 percent increase compared to 1.74 million euros in the same period of the previous year. This growth was primarily driven by the fibreboard production segment, where sales rose by 127 thousand euros to 1.86 million euros. The improvement in sales performance was largely supported by increased order volumes from Finnish DIY stores, which contributed an additional 241 thousand euros in revenue. Revenue from the secondary real estate management segment remained immaterial following the termination of lease agreements in August 2024. Despite the top-line growth, the company’s profitability deteriorated significantly. Consolidated EBITDA for the quarter was negative 395 thousand euros, a sharp decline from the negative 85 thousand euros reported in Q1 2025, resulting in an EBITDA margin of negative 21 percent compared to negative 5 percent in the prior year. The operating loss widened to 530 thousand euros from 217 thousand euros in the previous period, leading to a net loss of 570 thousand euros, which contrasts with the net loss of 228 thousand euros recorded in Q1 2025. The deterioration in financial results was explicitly attributed to operational disruptions and rising energy costs. The factory shutdown in early January 2026 for the transition to a new boiler house, along with a two-month calibration period, negatively impacted production efficiency. Furthermore, a significant increase in gas prices in February 2026, triggered by geopolitical tensions including a US military attack on Iran, nearly doubled the market price of gas. Although prices subsequently fell, they remained approximately 50 percent higher than at the beginning of the year, heavily impacting the cost of goods sold, which rose to 1.97 million euros from 1.61 million euros in the prior year. On the balance sheet, total assets increased to 9.7 million euros from 8.7 million euros in the previous year, while total liabilities rose to 5.8 million euros from 5.3 million euros. Equity decreased to 3.9 million euros from 4.5 million euros at the end of 2025, reflecting the accumulated net loss. The company employed 57 people as of March 31, 2026, down from 69 employees in the same period of the previous year. Looking ahead, the company expects demand for fibreboard to remain stable and anticipates holding sales volumes at current levels, with a strategic focus on developing existing clients and expanding into the Nordic market.
