During the 2025 financial year, AS Tallink Grupp reported consolidated revenue of EUR 765.3 million, representing a decrease compared to EUR 785.8 million in the 2024 financial year. Unaudited EBITDA declined to EUR 130.1 million from EUR 175.2 million, and unaudited net profit for the period fell to EUR 17.3 million, or EUR 0.023 per share, down from EUR 40.3 million, or EUR 0.054 per share, in the previous year. Gross profit decreased to EUR 143.2 million from EUR 153.6 million. For the fourth quarter of 2025, revenue was EUR 188.0 million compared to EUR 183.5 million in the fourth quarter of 2024, EBITDA was EUR 27.6 million compared to EUR 25.7 million, and net profit was EUR 12.2 million compared to a net loss of EUR 5.2 million in the prior-year period. Total net debt stood at EUR 432.4 million at the end of 2025, showing a reduction from EUR 537.7 million at the end of 2024, while the net debt to EBITDA ratio was 3.3 compared to 3.1 previously.

The financial performance was influenced by challenging macroeconomic conditions, particularly economic uncertainty and low consumer and business confidence in Finland, Sweden, and Estonia, which constrained consumer spending and cargo flows. Total passenger numbers for the year decreased by 0.9% to 5,531,132, while transported cargo units dropped by 19.2% to 245,004, and passenger vehicles declined by 2.2% to 760,473. Route operations generated EUR 625.2 million in revenue with a segment result of EUR 70.5 million, compared to EUR 638.9 million and EUR 81.5 million in 2024. Profitability was further impacted by tax increases in home markets, such as higher VAT and income tax rates in Estonia and Finland. 

Mitigating factors included internal decisions and operational adaptations. The Group completed the sale of three vessels during the year, specifically the passenger vessel Star I in April, the cargo vessel Regal Star in August, and the cargo vessel Sailor in October. Additionally, the Group maintained several vessels on charter contracts, including the Silja Europa, Galaxy I, and Romantika. Amortisation and depreciation expenses decreased by EUR 18.0 million to EUR 79.8 million, influenced by the alignment of the estimated useful lives of cruise and passenger vessels to 45 years and the vessel sales. Net finance costs declined by EUR 6.8 million to EUR 21.4 million due to lower outstanding loan balances and declining interest rates. Total loan repayments and interest payments during the year amounted to EUR 113.5 million, and capital investments totaled EUR 33.0 million. The Group paid dividends of EUR 44.6 million, equivalent to EUR 0.06 per share, in two instalments during 2025, and the Management Board intends to propose a dividend of 6 euro cents per share for 2026.
