Infortar Group reported strong operating growth in 2025, although net profit declined substantially because the 2024 comparison included significant one-off effects from the initial consolidation of Tallink. Revenue increased 34% to EUR 1.837 billion, while EBITDA rose 60% to EUR 232.6 million. EBITDA margin improved from 10.6% to 13.4%, and operating profit increased 44% to EUR 111.1 million. However, net profit fell 63% to EUR 72.0 million, from EUR 193.7 million, while profit attributable to the parent declined to EUR 72.7 million from EUR 191.3 million. The report attributes the reduction primarily to one-off effects associated with the initial consolidation of Tallink in 2024. Earnings per share decreased from EUR 9.36 to EUR 3.54.

The energy segment remained the largest contributor to revenue, generating EUR 1.013 billion, up from EUR 980.8 million, while EBITDA increased from EUR 77.2 million to EUR 89.3 million. Elenger’s EBITDA improved by approximately EUR 12 million. Despite a 2% decline in regional natural-gas consumption, Elenger’s gas and electricity sales volumes increased 7% to 4.8 TWh. The company also signed a multiyear agreement covering 12 LNG cargoes and continued investments in energy storage and bioLNG bunkering.

Maritime transport revenue declined 3% to EUR 765.3 million, and EBITDA fell 29% to EUR 124.2 million, reflecting weaker passenger, cargo and vehicle volumes, lower consumer and business confidence, geopolitical tensions and higher operating pressures. Tallink carried 5.53 million passengers, down 0.9%, while cargo units decreased 19.2%. In contrast, the fourth quarter improved: maritime revenue rose 2.4% to EUR 188.0 million, EBITDA increased to EUR 27.6 million, and the segment moved from a EUR 5.2 million loss to EUR 12.2 million net profit. Cruise vessel chartering and the sale of the cargo vessel Sailor were among the notable developments.

The group strengthened its financial position during the year. Total equity increased to EUR 1.178 billion, the equity ratio improved from 43.0% to 45.5%, and net debt declined 20% to EUR 840.7 million. Net debt to EBITDA improved from 4.0 to 3.6. Operating cash flow increased substantially to EUR 388.6 million from EUR 89.3 million, supporting debt repayments, investment and EUR 76.5 million of dividends. Cash rose to EUR 219.8 million, while interest-bearing liabilities decreased to EUR 1.060 billion. Infortar also expanded its agricultural and bioenergy activities through the acquisition of Oisu Biogaas and continued major infrastructure and real-estate projects.
