AB Novaturas’ financial performance improved in 2025 despite a significant decline in activity. The Group served 190.9 thousand travellers, 20.3% fewer than in 2024, while revenue fell 16.1% to EUR 168.5 million. The decrease reflected intense competition, excess travel supply and the Group’s deliberate reduction of loss-making routes and overall volumes. Demand was also affected late in the year by increased autumn market supply and disruptions at Vilnius Airport related to illegal cross-border activity involving unmanned balloons.

Profitability nevertheless strengthened substantially. Gross profit declined only 5.3% to EUR 19.7 million, and the gross margin improved from 10.33% to 11.66%. Adjusted EBITDA increased to EUR 420 thousand from negative EUR 449 thousand in 2024, producing a positive EBITDA margin of 0.25% compared with negative 3.05% previously. Operating loss narrowed to EUR 274 thousand from EUR 6.6 million, while the loss before tax decreased to EUR 1.2 million from EUR 7.8 million. Net loss improved by 84.6% to EUR 1.17 million, compared with EUR 7.60 million in 2024, and loss per share decreased from EUR 0.97 to EUR 0.15. The 2024 result included a one-off, non-cash goodwill impairment of EUR 5.7 million; excluding that impairment, the annual net loss declined by 39% from EUR 1.92 million.

The main business remained flight package tours. Their sales decreased 15.0% to EUR 145.1 million, but segment sales profit rose 18.0% to EUR 12.2 million as the margin increased from approximately 1% to 8%. Coach sightseeing tours also improved modestly, while plane sightseeing tours weakened sharply, with segment sales profit falling 84.4% to EUR 135 thousand. Other sales remained loss-making, generating a EUR 2.5 million segment loss, compared with EUR 2.2 million in 2024.

Operational efficiency improved, with the aircraft load factor rising 1.5 percentage points to 97.2%. Long-haul and ski-trip load factors also increased materially. Turkey remained the largest destination, although its share declined, while Egypt, Italy, Spain and long-haul destinations gained share. Early bookings for the 2026 summer season were 59% higher by December 2025 than a year earlier.

The balance sheet strengthened in total assets and cash, but financial leverage increased. Cash rose to EUR 1.7 million from EUR 394 thousand, while total borrowings increased to EUR 12.9 million and the debt-to-equity ratio rose to 191.42%. Equity declined to EUR 6.9 million, and no dividends were paid. For 2026, management plans a similar or slightly higher programme, with continued emphasis on profitability, cost efficiency and expansion of the hotel portfolio.
