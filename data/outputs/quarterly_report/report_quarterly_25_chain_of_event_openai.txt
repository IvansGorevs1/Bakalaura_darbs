VILVI Group reported strong revenue growth but sharply lower operating profitability in the first quarter of 2026. Consolidated revenue increased 22.4% year over year to EUR 86.97 million, supported by higher sales in Lithuania and other countries. Revenue from Lithuania rose 72.9% to EUR 14.63 million, while sales to other countries increased 46.6% to EUR 29.82 million; European Union revenue was broadly stable at EUR 42.52 million. The market mix shifted, with the European Union accounting for 49% of revenue, Lithuania 17%, and other countries 34%.

EBITDA fell 50.2% to EUR 3.00 million, reducing the EBITDA margin to 3.5% from 8.5% a year earlier. Despite the decline in EBITDA, net profit increased 25.7% to EUR 5.09 million, and earnings per share rose 26.5% to EUR 0.43. The income statement shows that other operating income increased to EUR 5.66 million from EUR 0.08 million, while gross profit declined to EUR 5.42 million from EUR 7.46 million. Distribution and administrative expenses also increased substantially. The report states that Q1 2026 EBITDA excludes preliminary negative goodwill of EUR 5.5 million related to the acquisition of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB.

The largest change in the operating portfolio came from the acquisition of 100% of Marijampolės pieno konservai, completed on 16 January 2026. Canned milk revenue contributed EUR 11.92 million in the quarter and was consolidated from 1 January 2026. Dry milk products revenue more than doubled to EUR 24.66 million, up 126.9%, and fresh milk products increased 73.4% to EUR 12.75 million. In contrast, cheese, cheese products, and other revenue declined 28.8% to EUR 37.64 million compared with an unusually strong Q1 2025.

Milk procurement increased 54.2% to 155,954 tons, reflecting significantly higher processing volumes. The Group also commenced commercial cheese production at its new Baltic Dairy Board factory in Latvia, which is planned to produce up to 18,000 tons annually for export to more than 40 countries.

Expansion and acquisition activity increased financial leverage. Net financial debt rose 89.7% year over year to EUR 83.43 million, while total assets increased to EUR 220.31 million from EUR 188.98 million at year-end 2025. Cash flow remained negative: operating activities used EUR 9.63 million and investing activities used EUR 8.04 million, partly offset by EUR 15.84 million of financing inflows. Cash declined to EUR 0.95 million at 31 March 2026.
