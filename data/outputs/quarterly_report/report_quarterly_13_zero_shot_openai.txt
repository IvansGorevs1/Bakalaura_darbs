Liven AS reported a strong recovery in residential demand during the second quarter of 2026, although financial results were weakened by the timing of home handovers and one-off public-offering costs. The group signed 57 sales contracts in the quarter, compared with 31 a year earlier, bringing the six-month total to 111, nearly double the 56 contracts signed in the first half of 2025. The weekly sales ratio rose to nearly 3.1% at quarter-end, with the quarterly average increasing to approximately 2.4% from 1.5% in the first quarter. Presales reached EUR 48.6 million at the beginning of the third quarter, including EUR 39.8 million for projects scheduled for completion in 2026.

Revenue for the second quarter was EUR 4.503 million, down from EUR 7.388 million in the same period of 2025, while EBITDA declined to EUR 0.884 million from EUR 2.390 million and operating profit fell to a loss of EUR 0.177 million from a profit of EUR 1.012 million. Profit before tax was a loss of EUR 0.234 million, compared with a profit of EUR 1.002 million. The quarter ended with a net loss of EUR 0.618 million, versus net profit of EUR 0.974 million a year earlier. Results included approximately EUR 90 thousand in share-offering costs and EUR 384 thousand of income tax related to dividends.

For the six months, revenue increased 22.8% to EUR 11.439 million from EUR 9.319 million, despite fewer handovers: 26 homes and commercial units were delivered compared with 41 in the prior-year period. EBITDA decreased to EUR 2.425 million from EUR 2.777 million, while operating profit rose modestly to EUR 0.341 million from EUR 0.325 million. Profit before tax declined to EUR 0.186 million from EUR 0.296 million, and the group recorded a net loss of EUR 0.198 million compared with net profit of EUR 0.268 million. Basic earnings per share were negative EUR 0.016, compared with EUR 0.022 previously.

Assets increased to EUR 100.186 million, primarily due to continued construction, while equity rose to EUR 26.746 million and the equity ratio improved to 26.7% from 19.4% a year earlier. Total borrowings reached EUR 57.341 million as construction financing increased. Cash rose to EUR 10.033 million following the IPO, which raised EUR 4.932 million from more than 2,400 investors. The shares began trading on Nasdaq Tallinn’s Main List in May.

The development portfolio expanded to 136,506 square metres with forecast revenue of EUR 497.2 million. New Berlin acquisitions and the post-period acquisition of Tallinn properties at Pärnu maantee 35/37/41 strengthened future growth prospects. Management expects most 2026 handovers, revenue and profit to occur in the fourth quarter and forecasts revenue of EUR 59 million for the year, with nearly 90% already earned or covered by contracts.
