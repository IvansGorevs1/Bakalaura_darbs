AS Pro Kapital Grupp reported a significant turnaround in financial performance for the twelve months ended December 31, 2025, transitioning from a net loss in the prior year to a substantial net profit. Total revenue for the period surged to EUR 53.2 million, compared to EUR 18.2 million in 2024. This increase was primarily driven by the handover of completed residential units in the Kalaranna and Uus-Kindrali developments in Tallinn, as well as the City Villas project in Vilnius. Revenue recognition is closely tied to the timing of notarial sales agreements and legal title transfers, meaning the Group’s results are heavily influenced by construction cycle completions. In the fourth quarter of 2025, revenue amounted to EUR 18.9 million, up from EUR 7.6 million in the same period of the previous year.

The Group’s profitability metrics improved markedly. Gross profit reached EUR 18.8 million for the year, representing a gross margin of 35%, up from EUR 5.4 million and a 30% margin in 2024. Operating profit rose sharply to EUR 14.7 million, or 28% of revenue, compared to a negligible EUR 0.1 million in 2024. Consequently, the net profit for the twelve months was EUR 12.0 million, reversing a net loss of EUR 3.9 million recorded in 2024. Earnings per share increased to EUR 0.22 from EUR -0.06. Return on equity improved to 21.0% from -7.0%, and return on assets rose to 10.1% from -3.4%. Net assets per share increased to EUR 1.12 from EUR 0.89.

Cash flow from operating activities turned positive, generating EUR 10.8 million in 2025, whereas it had used EUR 10.4 million in 2024. The Group maintained a conservative financing policy, resulting in a debt-to-equity ratio of 0.97, down from 1.32 in the previous year. Total debt decreased, with secured bonds partially repaid in August 2025, reducing the outstanding nominal amount from EUR 19.95 million to EUR 10.545 million. The Group also amended the terms of its senior secured callable fixed rate bonds in February 2025 to include an option to repay bonds pro rata instead of divesting hotel operations in Germany.

Operationally, the Estonian segment dominated revenue, accounting for 75% of the total, up from 27% in 2024, due to major project completions in Tallinn. The Latvian segment’s contribution fell to 0.3% as remaining inventory was sold, while the Lithuanian segment contributed 12%. The German hotel segment, which had previously been a major revenue source, contributed 11% of total revenue, with occupancy rates softening towards year-end. The Italian segment, focused on bare ownership brokerage, contributed 2% amidst a challenging market environment. The Group also initiated construction on the Blue Marine project in Riga and acquired an additional 10% stake in its Italian subsidiary, Preatoni Nuda Proprietà. Goodwill from the Italian acquisition was fully impaired in 2025 due to revised profitability projections. Overall, the reporting period was characterized by strong execution, tangible progress in key development projects, and a return to robust profitability.
