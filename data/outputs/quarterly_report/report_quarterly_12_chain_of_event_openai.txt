LHV Group’s Q2 2026 net profit was EUR 24.7 million, up 25% from EUR 19.7 million in Q1 but down 20% from EUR 30.8 million in Q2 2025. Profit attributable to the parent’s shareholders was EUR 24.1 million, and earnings per share were EUR 0.07, compared with EUR 0.09 a year earlier. First-half net profit reached EUR 44.4 million, 26% below the EUR 59.9 million reported for the first half of 2025.

Revenue performance improved during the quarter. Net income rose 7.3% quarter-on-quarter and 7% year-on-year to EUR 79.0 million, supported primarily by net interest income, which increased 5% from Q1 to EUR 62.5 million and 8% from Q2 2025. Net fee and commission income was broadly unchanged from Q1 at EUR 14.6 million but declined 6% year-on-year, reflecting lower client activity. Net insurance income recovered to EUR 0.7 million from a EUR 0.7 million loss in Q1. Operating expenses fell 4% quarter-on-quarter to EUR 43.6 million, but were 8% higher year-on-year, contributing to a cost/income ratio of 61.9%, compared with 47.3% a year earlier.

The main factor limiting profitability was a sharp increase in impairment charges. Loan and bond portfolio losses were EUR 5.8 million in Q2, compared with EUR 1.3 million in Q1 and a EUR 4.2 million gain in Q2 2025. Management said the charges reflected forward-looking model provisions and a limited number of specific client cases, while overall loan portfolio quality remained strong and some losses were not expected to materialize. Income tax expense declined to EUR 5.0 million from EUR 7.1 million in Q1 and EUR 6.8 million a year earlier.

Business volumes continued to expand. The Group’s loan portfolio grew 3% quarter-on-quarter and 15% year-on-year to EUR 5.8 billion, while customer deposits fell 3% from Q1 to EUR 7.6 billion, mainly because of reduced platform and financial-intermediary deposits. LHV Pank generated EUR 24.3 million of quarterly profit, up 20% from Q1 but down 18% year-on-year. Its loan book increased by EUR 114 million, while deposits declined by EUR 312 million. LHV Bank’s profit rose to EUR 1.4 million, and LHV Kindlustus returned to a EUR 0.8 million profit from a Q1 loss, aided by fewer major losses and lower loss frequency. LHV Varahaldus earned EUR 0.4 million.

Return on equity improved to 13.1% from 10.7% in Q1 but remained below 17.4% a year earlier. Capital adequacy was 20.8%, down from 23.0% year-on-year, as risk-weighted assets increased. The quarter also included strategic changes at LHV Pank, including migration of core systems to Amazon Web Services, adoption of product-based management, and receipt of Estonia’s first bank licence for regulated crypto-asset services under MiCA.
