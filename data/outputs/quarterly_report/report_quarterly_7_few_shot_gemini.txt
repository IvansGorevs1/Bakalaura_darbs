During the first six months of 2026, AS Silvano Fashion Group recorded total revenue of 28,590 thousand euros, representing a slight increase of 0.6% compared with 28,428 thousand euros reported in the same period of 2025. Gross profit improved by 5.3% to reach 15,892 thousand euros, driven by a lower cost of sold goods and an increase in the gross margin to 55.6% from 53.1%. In contrast, operating profit declined by 3.5% to 5,751 thousand euros, and consolidated EBITDA decreased marginally by 0.8% to 7,508 thousand euros. Net profit for the six-month period experienced a more pronounced decline, dropping by 31.2% to 4,321 thousand euros compared with 6,279 thousand euros in the prior-year period. Net profit attributable to equity holders of the parent company amounted to 3,961 thousand euros, down 31.5% from 5,781 thousand euros, resulting in basic and diluted earnings per share of 0.11 euros compared with 0.16 euros in the previous year.

The company's revenue performance varied across its business segments and geographic markets. Retail revenue grew significantly by 13.6% to reach 13,548 thousand euros, accounting for 47.4% of total sales, whereas wholesale revenue decreased by 9.1% to 14,934 thousand euros, representing 52.2% of total revenue. Geographically, Russia remained the primary market with sales of 15,767 thousand euros, down 0.6% in euros and 7.8% in local currency, representing 55.1% of total sales. Sales in Belarus increased by 6.4% in euros and 2.7% in local currency to 9,046 thousand euros, comprising 31.6% of total revenue. Operations in Ukraine, the Baltics, and other markets experienced declines, with Ukrainian sales dropping by 58.3% to 15 thousand euros and Baltic sales falling by 16.7% to 484 thousand euros.

As of 30 June 2026, the company's financial position strengthened, with total assets increasing by 5.1% to 98,603 thousand euros compared with 93,847 thousand euros at the end of December 2025. Current assets rose by 7.2% to 85,377 thousand euros, supported by an increase in cash and cash equivalents to 12,443 thousand euros and short-term bank deposits of 38,707 thousand euros. Total equity attributable to equity holders of the parent company grew by 6.1% to 81,479 thousand euros. Operating cash flow improved substantially, surging by 96.6% to 6,842 thousand euros compared with 3,481 thousand euros in the first six months of 2025. Capital expenditures for the period totalled 115 thousand euros, directed primarily toward store openings, renovations, and maintenance of production equipment, down from 368 thousand euros in the previous year. Overall, the reporting period was characterized by stable overall revenue and improved retail performance and gross margins, which were offset by higher expenses and lower net profitability.
