AS Trigon Property Development reported a significant deterioration in performance in 2025 compared with 2024. The company generated no property sales revenue during the year, compared with €331,920 in 2024, when two land transactions were completed. As a result, gross profit fell from €220,299 to zero and the operating result changed from a profit of €161,649 to a loss of €108,021. Administrative and general expenses increased 70% to €108,021 from €63,650, including €41,452 of remuneration paid to management and supervisory board members, compared with no such expense in 2024. Financial income rose to €8,513 from €5,760, but this was insufficient to offset operating costs.

The company recorded a net loss of €99,507 for 2025, compared with a net profit of €167,409 in 2024. Fourth-quarter results also weakened sharply: the company posted a net loss of €23,333, versus a net profit of €154,215 in the fourth quarter of 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share declined from €0.03721 to negative €0.02212.

At year-end, total assets decreased 3.7% to €1.805 million from €1.874 million. Cash declined to €227,911 from €370,856, reflecting operating cash outflows of €142,945, compared with positive operating cash flow of €194,723 in 2024. Inventories increased to €1.563 million from €1.471 million, primarily because €91,262 was invested in the Pärnu property development project. No inventory impairment was recorded. Total liabilities increased to €49,389 from €18,906, while equity fell 5.4% to €1.755 million as a result of the annual loss.

The company continues to own a single 13.2-hectare development project in Pärnu, Estonia, intended for commercial, industrial and logistics uses. Its stated strategy remains the sale of existing land plots, with no plans to begin a new business activity in the following year. The company did not complete any land sales in 2025, in contrast to the 0.09-hectare sale for €31,920 in the third quarter of 2024 and the 0.70-hectare sale for €300,000 in the fourth quarter.

The share price increased modestly from €0.620 to €0.634 during 2025, raising market capitalisation from €2.789 million to €2.852 million. However, book value per share declined from €0.41 to €0.39, and return on assets and return on equity changed from positive 8.93% and 9.03% to negative 5.51% and 5.67%, respectively.
