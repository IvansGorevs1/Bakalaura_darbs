Liven AS reported a mixed financial performance for the first half of 2026, characterized by a significant increase in sales activity and a strong expansion of its development portfolio, offset by a net loss in the second quarter due to one-off costs associated with its initial public offering. The company’s underlying business dynamics showed robust recovery, with the number of sales contracts signed under the law of obligations doubling to 111 in the first six months compared to 56 in the same period of 2025. This surge in demand was driven by presales in key projects such as Regati phase II, the new phase of Iseära, and Kadakadabra phase I. Consequently, the weekly sales ratio, a key indicator of demand-supply balance, exceeded the long-term normal range, reaching nearly 3.1% in the second quarter, up from approximately 1.5% in the first quarter.

Despite the strong sales momentum, revenue recognition remained constrained by the cyclical nature of construction. Revenue for the first half of 2026 was EUR 11,439 thousand, an increase from EUR 9,319 thousand in the prior year period. However, only 26 homes and commercial units were handed over in the first half, compared to 41 in 2025, limiting the volume of recognized sales. The second quarter specifically saw revenue decline to EUR 4,503 thousand from EUR 7,388 thousand in the second quarter of 2025, reflecting the timing of completions. As a result, the company reported a net loss of EUR 198 thousand for the six-month period, a reversal from the net profit of EUR 268 thousand in 2025. The second quarter alone recorded a net loss of EUR 618 thousand, primarily due to EUR 90 thousand in one-off costs related to the share offering and a significant income tax expense of EUR 384 thousand linked to dividend payments.

Financially, the company strengthened its capital structure through the successful initial public offering in May 2026, which raised EUR 4,932 thousand from the issuance of over 1 million shares at EUR 4.68 each. Total assets grew by EUR 8,505 thousand during the second quarter to EUR 100,186 thousand, driven by continued construction activities and the acquisition of new development properties. The equity ratio improved to 26.7% at the end of the quarter, up from 19.4% a year earlier, indicating enhanced financial stability. The development portfolio expanded significantly, with the total saleable area reaching 136,506 square meters and forecast revenue potential of EUR 497.2 million. This growth was fueled by acquisitions in Berlin and a major property purchase in central Tallinn after the reporting period.

Looking ahead, management expects the recovery in demand to continue, supported by improving household purchasing power and stable interest rates. The company has secured nearly 90% of its 2026 revenue target through existing contracts and presales, with a presale portfolio of EUR 48.6 million. The focus is now shifting toward completing ongoing projects and starting construction on new phases, including Kadakadabra and Juhkentali, to support growth in 2027 and beyond. The company anticipates net profit to increase in the latter half of the year as more homes are handed over, aiming to exceed its 20% return on equity target.
