During the first six months of 2026, AS Merko Ehitus reported consolidated revenue of EUR 149.9 million, representing a 10.7% decrease compared to the EUR 167.9 million recorded in the same period of 2025. Revenue for the second quarter of 2026 reached EUR 92.7 million, showing an increase from EUR 82.6 million in the second quarter of the previous year. Profitability declined across the board during the reporting period. Pre-tax profit for the first six months of 2026 dropped to EUR 10.8 million from EUR 23.5 million in the prior-year period, resulting in a pre-tax profit margin of 7.2% compared with 14.0% previously. Net profit attributable to shareholders for the first half of the year fell to EUR 12.8 million from EUR 21.7 million, with the net profit margin decreasing to 8.6% from 12.9%. For the second quarter of 2026, net profit attributable to shareholders was EUR 8.5 million compared to EUR 11.2 million in the second quarter of 2025. Basic and diluted earnings per share for the six-month period were EUR 0.73, down from EUR 1.22 in the first half of 2025. 

The decline in financial results was primarily influenced by lower performance in the real estate development segment and normalization of construction margins following the expiration of successful contracts from previous periods. In the construction service segment, revenue remained relatively stable at EUR 118.3 million for the first six months of 2026 compared to EUR 120.1 million a year earlier, while operating profit for this segment fell significantly to EUR 3.0 million from EUR 8.8 million. In the real estate development segment, six-month revenue dropped by 33.9% to EUR 31.6 million, and revenue from the sale of own-developed apartments declined to EUR 27.6 million from EUR 44.9 million, accompanied by a decrease in segment operating profit to EUR 7.0 million from EUR 11.9 million. During the first half of the year, the group sold a total of 120 apartments and 5 commercial premises, compared to 222 apartments and 2 commercial premises in the corresponding period of 2025. 

A major positive development during the period was the expansion of the group's secured order book, which reached an all-time high of EUR 835.7 million as of June 30, 2026, representing an increase of approximately 88% compared to EUR 443.8 million as of June 30, 2025. Group companies signed new construction contracts worth EUR 486.4 million during the first six months of 2026, substantially higher than the EUR 223.1 million signed in the first half of 2025, driven heavily by public sector and defense-related projects such as the Rūdninkai military campus. 

The company maintained a solid financial and liquidity position. As of June 30, 2026, cash and cash equivalents stood at EUR 39.6 million, compared to EUR 25.9 million a year earlier. Total assets increased to EUR 437.9 million at the end of the first half of 2026 from EUR 403.5 million as of June 30, 2025, while total equity rose to EUR 251.1 million from EUR 242.3 million. The equity ratio remained strong at 57.4%, and the debt ratio was 15.8%. Operating cash flow for the first six months of 2026 was negative at EUR 31.6 million, largely driven by investments in inventories and construction contracts, while investing and financing cash flows were positive at EUR 16.4 million and EUR 13.4 million, respectively. During the annual general meeting held in April 2026, shareholders approved a dividend payout of EUR 22.1 million, or EUR 1.25 per share, from retained earnings.
