AS LHV Group reported a net profit of EUR 24.7 million for the second quarter of 2026, representing a 25% increase from EUR 19.7 million in the first quarter of 2026. However, this figure marks a 20% decline compared to EUR 30.8 million in the same period of the previous year. Earnings per share stood at EUR 0.07, up from EUR 0.06 in Q1 2026 but down from EUR 0.09 in Q2 2025. The Group’s consolidated net income, a broader measure of operational performance, reached EUR 79.0 million, reflecting a 7.3% growth from the previous quarter and a 7% increase year-over-year.

The primary driver of the improved quarterly results was a 5% rise in net interest income to EUR 62.5 million, supported by higher market interest rates and growth in the loan portfolio. Net interest income grew by 8% compared to Q2 2025. Conversely, net service fee income remained largely flat at EUR 14.6 million, showing a 6% decrease from the prior year, which the Management Board attributed to lower client activity. Operating expenses decreased by 4% to EUR 43.6 million in the quarter, though they were 8% higher than in Q2 2025. The cost-to-income ratio for the quarter was 61.9%, an increase from 53.9% in Q1 2026 and 47.3% in Q2 2025.

A significant factor impacting profitability was the increase in loan and bond provisions, which amounted to EUR 5.8 million in Q2 2026, compared to EUR 1.3 million in Q1 2026 and a reversal of EUR 4.2 million in Q2 2025. The Management Board explained that these charges reflected forward-looking model provisions for specific clients, despite the overall loan portfolio quality remaining strong. Income tax expenses decreased to EUR 5.0 million from EUR 7.1 million in the previous quarter.

The Group’s loan volume grew by 3% to EUR 5,768 million by the end of Q2 2026, with a 15% increase compared to Q2 2025. This growth was driven by home loans and corporate lending, particularly in the real estate sector. In contrast, total customer deposits decreased by 3% to EUR 7,557 million, primarily due to a reduction in platform deposits and deposits from financial intermediaries. The return on equity for the quarter was 13.1%, higher than the 10.7% in Q1 2026 but lower than the 17.4% recorded in Q2 2025.

Capital adequacy stood at 20.8% at the end of the quarter, down from 21.7% in Q1 2026 and 23.0% in Q2 2025, though it remained above the Group’s internal target of 20.0%. The liquidity coverage ratio was 170.5%. During the quarter, the Group implemented strategic changes, including a transition to a cloud-based core system architecture and a shift to a product-based management model. Additionally, LHV Pank received a crypto-assets service provider license, marking the first such license for a bank in Estonia. Despite these developments, the Management Board noted that the quarter was slightly weaker than planned, primarily due to higher-than-expected expenses and lower service fee income, although interest income performance exceeded forecasts.
