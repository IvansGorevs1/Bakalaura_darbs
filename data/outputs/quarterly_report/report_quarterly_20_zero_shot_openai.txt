SAF Tehnika’s unaudited 2024/2025 financial year was characterized by lower revenue but a significant reduction in losses. Consolidated turnover declined 4% to EUR 26.11 million from EUR 27.09 million in the previous financial year. Revenue from North and Latin America fell 14% to EUR 14.28 million and represented 55% of total turnover, while European revenue increased 17% to EUR 10.04 million, accounting for 38%. Revenue from Asia, Africa and the Middle East decreased 5% to EUR 1.78 million.

Despite the revenue decline, profitability improved substantially. The annual net loss narrowed to EUR 820 thousand from EUR 2.37 million, while EBIT improved to a loss of EUR 754 thousand from a loss of EUR 2.26 million. The improvement reflected lower total operating expenses, which decreased to EUR 27.56 million from EUR 30.27 million, mainly due to a reduction in direct costs of goods sold. However, salary, bonus and related social expenses increased 8% to EUR 12.41 million. The annual loss per share improved to EUR 0.28 from EUR 0.80.

The fourth quarter was materially weaker than the prior-year quarter. Q4 turnover decreased 13% to EUR 5.18 million. North and Latin American sales fell 40% to EUR 2.5 million, while European turnover increased 24% to EUR 1.9 million and sales in Asia, Africa and the Middle East doubled to EUR 846 thousand. The Group recorded a Q4 net loss of EUR 913 thousand, compared with a EUR 498 thousand loss in Q4 2023/2024. Q4 EBITDA deteriorated to negative EUR 460 thousand from negative EUR 96 thousand, and EBIT declined to negative EUR 863 thousand from negative EUR 495 thousand.

Cash generation improved considerably. Operating cash flow increased to EUR 2.13 million from EUR 939 thousand, and total cash flow was positive at EUR 2.60 million compared with negative EUR 1.25 million previously. Cash and cash equivalents rose to EUR 4.81 million from EUR 2.21 million. Customer advances increased to EUR 3.92 million, and the Group had an unused EUR 4.95 million credit line available for liquidity support.

Operations continued to be affected by shortages of electronic components, although delivery times were improving. The Group maintained material reserves, continued investment in product development, product enhancements and sales promotion, and invested EUR 239 thousand in fixed assets during Q4. Management reported no major changes in the microwave radio market, while monitoring global trade conditions, including potential US tariffs. The Group ended the period with 273 employees, compared with 265 a year earlier.
