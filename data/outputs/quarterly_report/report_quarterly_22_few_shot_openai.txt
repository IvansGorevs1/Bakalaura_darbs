Telia Lietuva reported stronger financial performance in the first half of 2026, with growth across revenue, profitability and cash generation. Revenue increased 7.2% year on year to EUR 259.1 million. Mobile services grew 8.3% to EUR 100.0 million, fixed services rose 5.3% to EUR 112.9 million, and equipment sales increased 9.6% to EUR 46.1 million. Within fixed services, IT services were particularly strong, increasing 23.9% to EUR 19.8 million. In the second quarter, revenue grew 6.9% to EUR 130.3 million.

Profitability improved despite higher costs. EBITDA increased 9.5% to EUR 105.5 million, while adjusted EBITDA excluding non-recurring items rose 10.2% to EUR 106.9 million. The adjusted EBITDA margin increased from 40.1% to 41.3%, and the reported EBITDA margin reached 40.7%. Operating profit grew 15.1% to EUR 62.4 million, with the EBIT margin rising to 24.1% from 22.4%. Profit before income tax increased 16.6% to EUR 61.3 million, while profit for the period rose 14.7% to EUR 51.3 million. Earnings per share increased from EUR 0.077 to EUR 0.088.

Cost pressures included higher equipment volumes, network interconnection and roaming costs, energy expenses, external IT services and common Group-function costs. Operating expenses excluding non-recurring items rose 10.2%. The company also recorded EUR 1.4 million of non-recurring redundancy expenses connected with restructuring its Digital & Technology unit. Employee numbers declined 6.9% to 1,601, or 1,524 on a full-time-equivalent basis.

Cash generation remained strong. Cash flow from operations increased 15.0% to EUR 103.1 million, and free cash flow rose 12.5% to EUR 64.1 million. Capital investments increased 57.9% to EUR 37.5 million, driven by mobile-network expansion, IT infrastructure and EUR 9.7 million invested in 5G spectrum licenses. The company acquired 40 MHz of 1,500 MHz spectrum for almost EUR 8 million and extended its right to use the 2,100 MHz band for EUR 2 million. It also began construction of a new data center near Vilnius, with up to EUR 26 million planned for the first two modules.

The financial position remained solid. Net debt was negative EUR 4.3 million, compared with EUR 27.5 million of net debt a year earlier. In May, the company paid EUR 81.6 million in dividends for 2025, equivalent to EUR 0.14 per share. Overall, the period showed sustained revenue growth, stronger margins and earnings, robust cash generation, and significantly increased investment in network and technology infrastructure.
