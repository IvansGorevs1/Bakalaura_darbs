Read and summarize the following financial report.

Before producing the final summary, analyze the report by following this sequence:

Identify the most important financial results and indicators reported for the period.
Determine the most significant changes compared with the relevant previous period reported in the document and identify whether performance improved or declined.
Identify the main business events, developments, decisions, or market conditions described in the report that affected these results.
Connect the identified financial changes with the relevant events or factors when such a relationship is explicitly supported by the document.
Use this sequence of financial results, changes, and influencing events to determine the most important developments of the reporting period.

Then create a concise summary covering the most important financial performance, changes, and factors identified through this analysis.

Prioritize information that is material for understanding the company’s financial performance. Include numerical values and percentage changes when they are important and explicitly reported in the document.

Use only information contained in the financial report. Preserve numerical accuracy, do not invent missing information, and do not infer causal relationships that are not supported by the document. Do not use Markdown formatting, headings, bullet points, or bold text.

Keep the summary concise and focused on the most important results. Do not include secondary balance-sheet, liquidity, operational, regional, or other details unless they are important for understanding the company’s overall performance.

**Aim for approximately 350–450 words. The summary may be shorter when the report contains less material information, but it should provide sufficient detail to cover the main financial developments.**

Output only the final coherent financial summary and do not include the intermediate analysis or the individual steps.
