You are an expert evaluator preparing DRAFT completeness criteria for summaries of a financial report.

You are given:
1. SOURCE FINANCIAL REPORT — the primary source.
2. REFERENCE SUMMARY — a human-prepared example indicating information considered important.

This step is performed BEFORE any candidate model summary is evaluated.

Your task is to produce a draft list of material information units that a concise financial-report summary should reasonably cover.

IMPORTANT:
- You are NOT given any candidate model summary.
- Do not use external knowledge.
- SOURCE is primary.
- REFERENCE is only a salience aid and is not exhaustive ground truth.
- Every factual or numerical detail in a draft unit must be supportable by SOURCE.
- Do not invent causal relationships.
- Do not combine separate events merely because they concern the same programme or company.

The evaluated summaries are concise. Therefore key-information units must represent material summary-level content, not every detail available in the report.

PRIORITIZE:
- headline financial performance;
- major period-over-period changes;
- major explicitly stated performance drivers;
- important segment/business developments;
- material liquidity, cash-flow, financing or capital developments;
- major operational or strategic developments;
- materially important post-reporting-period events.

AVOID ROUTINE BOILERPLATE:
- corporate-governance composition;
- remuneration;
- generic risk lists;
- accounting-policy descriptions;
- auditor information;
- generic market descriptions;
- minor technical/operational details.

GRANULARITY AND NON-DUPLICATION:
- Each draft unit should represent one independently omittable material theme or event.
- If a candidate summary could reasonably mention one event without mentioning another, treat them as separate units unless one is only context for the other.
- Do not create one unit for every individual number.
- Do not intentionally repeat the same material fact in multiple draft units.
- Keep "information" focused on CORE CONTENT that determines coverage.
- Put supporting technical details, dates, secondary figures and background mainly in source_evidence rather than making every one of them a mandatory part of the criterion.
- Aim for approximately 6–10 units.
- Never return more than 12.

IMPORTANCE:
HIGH — central to understanding financial performance, major drivers, liquidity/capital position, or a major strategic development.
MEDIUM — materially relevant but less central.

Return only valid JSON.

Use exactly:

  "draft_key_information_units": [
    {
      "id": "D1",
      "information": "...",
      "importance": "HIGH",
      "source_evidence": "...",
      "reference_support": true
    }
  ],
  "selection_rationale": "..."
}

Use sequential IDs D1, D2, D3...
