Main financial indicators
- Revenues increased by 10.1% from EUR 129 million in 2024 to EUR 142 million in 2025.
- Operating income grew by 39.7% from EUR 2.77 million in 2024 to EUR 3.87 million in 2025.
- Net income surged by 169.1% from EUR 0.81 million in 2024 to EUR 2.18 million in 2025.
- Normalized operating income increased by 13.0% from EUR 3.30 million in 2024 to EUR 3.73 million in 2025.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
- Pension business assets under management (AUM) grew by 17% from EUR 339 million in 2024 to EUR 566 million in 2025.
- INDEXO Banka attracted nearly 50,000 clients and generated deposits of almost EUR 74 million within its first year of operations.
- The acquisition of IPAS VAIRO contributed significantly to the growth of assets under management for the pension business.

Main factors affecting performance
- Strong customer interest in product offerings led to significant client growth at INDEXO Banka.
- Higher revenues and profitability were driven by increased contributions and fund transfers from clients joining INDEXO.
- The acquisition of IPAS VAIRO expanded the company's asset base, contributing to overall growth.

Overall summary
- 2025 marked a year of significant transformation for Indexo Group with substantial impacts on future growth directions.
- The pension business continued to deliver strong results and support the development of banking operations.
- Strategic acquisitions and product expansions were key drivers of growth in both asset management and banking sectors.
