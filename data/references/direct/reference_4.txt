Main financial indicators
- Net turnover for Q4 2024/2025 was EUR 5.18 million, down 13% from the same period in FY 2023/2024.
- Consolidated unaudited result for the twelve months of 2024/2025 is a loss of EUR 820 thousand.
- The Group's cash balance at the end of the period was EUR 4.8 million.
- Advances received from customers for goods totaled EUR 3.9 million.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
- Turnover in the North America and Latin America region decreased by 40% to 47% or EUR 2.5 million.
- The European region turnover increased by 24% to EUR 1.9 million.
- Asia, Africa, and Middle East region turnover doubled to 16% of total quarterly turnover (EUR 846 thousand).
- No major changes in the microwave radio market were observed during the quarter.

Main factors affecting performance
- Fluctuations in quarterly turnover are influenced by project completion, complexity, duration, and material procurement lead times.
- The Group’s costs did not exceed planned levels.
- Hostilities in Ukraine do not have a direct impact on the Group's activities.
- General uncertainty in the business environment remains.

Overall summary
- The company aims to stabilize turnover to ensure a positive net result in the long term.
- SAF Tehnika continues to invest in product development and modifications.
- The Group monitors changes in the global trade environment, including potential tariff adjustments.
