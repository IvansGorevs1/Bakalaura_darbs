Main financial indicators
- Revenue for 2025 was EUR 53.2 million, up from EUR 18.2 million in 2024.
- Gross profit for 2025 was EUR 18.8 million, compared to EUR 5.4 million in 2024.
- Operating profit for 2025 was EUR 14.7 million, up from a loss of EUR 0.1 million in 2024.
- Net profit for 2025 was EUR 12.0 million, compared to a loss of EUR 3.9 million in 2024.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
- Revenue increased significantly from EUR 18.2 million in 2024 to EUR 53.2 million in 2025.
- Gross profit more than tripled, from EUR 5.4 million in 2024 to EUR 18.8 million in 2025.
- Operating profit turned positive and increased significantly, from a loss of EUR 0.1 million in 2024 to EUR 14.7 million in 2025.
- Net profit improved substantially, moving from a loss of EUR 3.9 million in 2024 to a profit of EUR 12.0 million in 2025.

Main factors affecting performance
- Increased revenue and gross profit due to the completion and sale of more apartments across various projects.
- Positive operating and net profits driven by higher sales volumes and improved cost management.
- Strong market positioning and trust from clients, evidenced by record sales prices in flagship developments.
- Continued execution of long-term strategy and disciplined project management.

Overall summary
- 2025 was a strong and profitable year for the Group with consistent execution and tangible progress across all markets.
- Revenue and profitability significantly improved compared to the previous year.
- The Group maintained discipline, creativity, and a clear sense of purpose in executing its long-term strategy.
