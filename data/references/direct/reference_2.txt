Main financial indicators
- Revenues: EUR 168,483 thousand (down 16.1% from EUR 200,878 thousand in 2024)
- Gross profit: EUR 19,650 thousand (down 5.3% from EUR 20,742 thousand in 2024)
- EBITDA: EUR 420 thousand (up 106.8% from EUR -6,132 thousand in 2024)
- Adjusted EBITDA: EUR 420 thousand (up 193.6% from EUR 449 thousand in 2024)

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
- Revenue decreased by 16.1%
- Gross profit margin declined by 5.3%
- EBITDA turned positive and increased significantly to EUR 420 thousand
- Net loss reduced by 84.6% to EUR -1,169 thousand

Main factors affecting performance
- Intense competition and excess travel supply led to lower volumes
- Optimization of the travel program improved margins and operational efficiency
- Reduction in loss-making routes and focus on higher-margin destinations
- Disruptions at Vilnius Airport due to illegal cross-border activities reduced last-minute travel demand

Overall summary
- Despite lower revenue, profitability metrics improved significantly
- Strategic decisions led to positive EBITDA despite market pressures
- Net loss decreased substantially compared to the previous year
