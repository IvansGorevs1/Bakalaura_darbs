Main financial indicators
- Revenue for the year 2025 was EUR 765.3 million, down from EUR 785.8 million in 2024.
- Unaudited EBITDA totalled EUR 130.1 million in 2025, compared to EUR 175.2 million in 2024.
- The unaudited net profit for the period was EUR 17.3 million in 2025, down from EUR 40.3 million in 2024.
- Number of passengers carried in 2025 decreased by 0.9% year-on-year to 5,531,132.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
- Revenue and operating results were impacted by low consumer and business confidence, ongoing economic challenges, and geopolitical tensions.
- The number of vessels in lay-up decreased from four at the end of Q1 2025 to zero by the end of Q3 2025.
- Three vessels were sold during the financial year: Star I, Regal Star, and Sailor.
- Net debt remained broadly unchanged at EUR 432.4 million as of December 31, 2025.

Main factors affecting performance
- Demand was impacted by low consumer and business confidence in core markets.
- The sale of surplus FuelEU Maritime compliance units positively affected financial results.
- Alignment of the estimated useful lives of cruise and passenger vessels to 45 years reduced depreciation expense.
- Payment of dividends and related income tax, as well as loan repayments, impacted cash flow.

Overall summary
- Group continued to focus on cost efficiencies and maintaining profitable operations on core routes.
- The Group operated three hotels in Tallinn and one in Riga.
- The Group's liquidity buffer remained strong at EUR 110.4 million as of December 31, 2025.
