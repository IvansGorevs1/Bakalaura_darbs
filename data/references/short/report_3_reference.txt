Main financial indicators

* In financial year 2025, the Group carried 5,531,132 passengers, which was 0.9% less than in 2024.
* Cargo units transported decreased by 19.2% to 245,004 and passenger vehicles decreased by 2.2% to 760,473.
* Unaudited consolidated revenue amounted to EUR 765.3 million compared with EUR 785.8 million in 2024.
* EBITDA totalled EUR 130.1 million compared with EUR 175.2 million in 2024.
* Unaudited net profit for the period was EUR 17.3 million compared with EUR 40.3 million in 2024.
* As at 31 December 2025, net debt amounted to EUR 432.4 million and the net debt to EBITDA ratio stood at 3.3.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period

* Total revenue decreased by EUR 20.5 million year-on-year.
* Revenue from route operations decreased by EUR 13.7 million to EUR 625.2 million, while the segment result declined from EUR 81.5 million to EUR 70.5 million.
* Gross profit decreased to EUR 143.2 million from EUR 153.6 million.
* EBITDA and net profit both declined significantly compared with 2024.
* In contrast, the fourth quarter of 2025 showed improvement: revenue increased to EUR 188.0 million, EBITDA to EUR 27.6 million, and net profit reached EUR 12.2 million, compared with a net loss of EUR 5.2 million in Q4 2024.

Main factors affecting performance

* The Group’s results were negatively affected by low consumer and business confidence in its home markets.
* Ongoing economic difficulties in core markets and heightened geopolitical tensions also affected demand.
* Some route results were influenced by maintenance works on vessels during the year.
* During 2025, the Group sold three vessels and reduced the number of vessels in lay-up to zero by the end of the third quarter.
* The cash position was significantly affected by dividend payments of EUR 44.6 million and related income tax of EUR 11.3 million.
* Profitability was positively supported by lower depreciation expense following the alignment of estimated useful lives of cruise and passenger vessels to 45 years.

Overall summary

* Overall, the Group’s full-year 2025 results were weaker than in 2024, with lower revenue, EBITDA, and net profit.
* At the same time, the fourth quarter showed an improvement in revenue and profitability compared with the same period of the previous year.
* The Group continued to focus on cost efficiency, profitable core route operations, and chartering opportunities for vessels not used on the main routes.
* The debt position remained broadly stable at the end of the year.
