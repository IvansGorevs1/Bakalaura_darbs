Main financial indicators
- Total consolidated assets reached EUR 10.2 billion (+17% YoY).
- The loan portfolio increased by EUR 913 million to EUR 5.5 billion (+20% YoY).
- Net profit was at EUR 117.0 million (-22% YoY), a decrease of EUR 33.3 million.
- The number of LHV Pank customers increased by 36,000 (+8% YoY).

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
- Interest income decreased 14%, while net fee income increased 5%.
- Net profit was 6.5% below plan and ROE was 1.6% below forecasted level.
- The loan portfolio grew by 20% to EUR 5.5 billion, with retail loans increasing by 17%.
- Total deposits increased by 18% to EUR 8.1 billion.

Main factors affecting performance
- Lower net interest income due to lower interest rates on corporate and home loan portfolios.
- Increased competition in the deposit market.
- Economic headwinds and intensified regulatory requirements.
- Changes in customer behavior towards digital channels and security concerns.

Overall summary
- LHV Group faced a challenging year with profitability falling short of financial targets.
- The bank continued to grow its customer base, but net income was impacted by lower interest rates and increased competition.
- Strategic investments in the UK and technology are creating a solid foundation for future growth.
