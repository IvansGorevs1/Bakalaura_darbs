Main financial indicators
- Revenue: 7,477 thousand euros (2.5 times less than in 2023)
- Operating profit (EBIT): 68 thousand euros (compared to 3,940 thousand euros in 2023)
- Net loss: 624 thousand euros (compared to net profit of 3,550 thousand euros in 2023)
- Number of apartments sold: 23 (in projects developed by the group) in Estonia

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
- Revenue decreased significantly from 18,339 thousand euros in 2023 to 7,477 thousand euros in 2024
- Operating profit dropped sharply from 3,940 thousand euros in 2023 to 68 thousand euros in 2024
- Net loss increased significantly from 3,550 thousand euros in 2023 to 624 thousand euros in 2024
- Number of apartments sold decreased from 66 in 2023 to 23 in 2024

Main factors affecting performance
- Decrease in revenue due to lower sales volume and market conditions
- Increase in debt burden (net loans) up to 13,495 thousand euros as of 31 December 2024
- High interest rates leading to higher financial costs
- Delayed start of construction at Soodi 6 due to processing the construction permit

Overall summary
- The company faced significant challenges in 2024 due to low consumer confidence and high interest rates
- Signs of stabilisation were seen in the second half of the year, with an increase in apartment market transactions
- Strategic direction and management are expected to form a strong basis for future developments and sustainable growth
