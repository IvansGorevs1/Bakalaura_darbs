Main financial indicators
- Revenue for 2025 was €35,414 thousand.
- Gross profit for 2025 was €5,636 thousand.
- EBITDA for 2025 was €3,458 thousand.
- Net profit for 2025 was €1,039 thousand.

Most significant changes compared with the previous period
- Hepsor AS sold a 50% stake in Hepsor SOF OÜ to EfTEN Special Opportunities Fund.
- The share capital of Hepsor AS increased by €57,821 due to the acquisition of a 20% holding in Hepsor Latvia OÜ.
- Hepsor AS acquired a 100% stake in Starta 17 SIA and established Hepsor PHX5 OÜ with AS Phoenix Land.
- The Group's total assets increased by 3.4% to €91,835 thousand.

Main factors affecting performance
- Decrease in the number of apartments delivered compared to the previous year (141 vs. 194).
- Lower gross margin on sold projects (16.9% vs. 17.8%).
- Increased marketing expenses.
- Sales revenue fluctuations due to the real estate development cycle.

Overall summary
- The Group's profitability was negatively impacted by lower sales and increased marketing costs.
- Key financial ratios showed a decline in margins but an increase in equity ratio.
- Significant investments were made in new projects, impacting asset composition.
