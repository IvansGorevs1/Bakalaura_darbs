The Board of Directors and CEO of OODA AI AB (publ) submit the following annual report and consolidated financial statements for the financial year 2025. The group's legal parent company is OODA AI AB (publ), corporate registration number 556737-5489. 

The annual report is prepared in Swedish kronor, SEK. Unless otherwise stated, all amounts are reported in thousands of kronor (kSEK). Information in parentheses refers to the previous year. 

## **CEO Statement** 

Dear shareholders, 

OODA AI enters 2026 in a new phase. Over the past years, we have built, structured and positioned the Company. The focus is now clear: to scale the business and increase usage of our platform. 

## **A shift from build-up to growth** 

The work carried out over the past 12 months has fundamentally changed the Companyʼs position. We have established a functioning technical platform, secured commercial agreements with recurring customers, and strengthened our financial structure through debt reduction and capital injections. This means we are now in a position to shift focus from structure and stabilization to growth and scaling. 

## **A scalable business model with clear leverage** 

Our platform is designed to scale without a corresponding increase in the cost base. This means increased usage has a direct impact on profitability. As availability expands through self-service and partner distribution during 2026, the conditions for broader distribution, increased usage and a more scalable business model are strengthened. This creates a solid foundation for improved predictability and long-term profitability. 

## **AI evolving from tool to infrastructure** 

We are in a shift where AI is no longer a complement, but an integrated part of companiesʼ operational core. Our solutions are used in processes where efficiency, cost reduction and data quality have a direct impact on customer outcomes. Once implemented, usage tends to grow over time, creating value expansion within existing customer relationships. This strengthens both the revenue base and our long-term position. 

## **Positioning within next-generation AI infrastructure** 

Alongside our commercial progress, we continue to build our decentralized AI initiative. We have moved from concept to operational infrastructure with an MVP and testnet, where both technical functionality and economic flows are validated. The next step is to take this model towards commercial deployment. Tokenization is a central part of this, enabling the creation of an economic layer where computation, verification and incentives interact. This opens up new revenue streams and a business model that extends beyond traditional software. 

## **Distribution and ecosystem as growth drivers** 

To accelerate growth, we are building a partner structure that enables distribution at scale. Through partners, we can reach new markets and customer segments without carrying the full sales effort ourselves. Combined with self-service, this creates a model where both direct sales and indirect channels contribute to growth. Our ambition is to establish an ecosystem where customers, developers and partners build on top of our platform.

## **Management report Information about the business** 

OODA AI AB (publ) is a group of companies focused on artificial intelligence (AI) and software-as-a-service (SaaS). The group of companies provides AI services in the form of decentralized and distributed AI computing, AI tools, and AI consulting, training, and implementation. The group of companies operates in a global market, with headquarters in Stockholm (Sweden) and Munich (Germany). Listed on Nasdaq Stockholm First North, also traded on Börse Frankfurt, Börse Stuttgart, and Börse Munich. 

The Group has had a breakthrough year in 2025, with a focus on commercialization, scaling and further development of the Company's technical platform. During the year, OODA AI has moved from a build-up to a clearer growth phase, where the platform has developed into a more complete and scalable infrastructure for enterprise AI. The platform brings together AI models, applications and workflows in an integrated infrastructure that enables companies to implement and use AI at scale. The platform provides access to over 150 AI models and a broad ecosystem of integrations via a unified interface and API. In parallel, the Company has taken decisive steps in decentralized AI, with the completion of litepaper, the launch of MVP, the operational test network and the continued development of the SOODA token as an economic layer in the network. 

During the year, the Parent Company has established and further developed several important customer agreements. During the year, the collaboration with Win.Rocks AG has grown to a total contract value of approximately SEK 21 million, and in December 2025, a new agreement of at least SEK 15 million over 24 months was signed. During the year, an agreement was also signed with Sergel Kredittjänster regarding AI services for document interpretation. OODA AI has also established a growing customer base in several sectors, including banking and debt collection, the health sector, staffing and industrial applications. During 2025, the Company has also developed its partner strategy through a dedicated partner program and strategic collaborations with, among others, Allgeier Inovar GmbH and TIM Solutions GmbH, which creates the conditions for broader distribution and faster market penetration. 

During 2025, the Group continued its work to streamline the portfolio structure and reduce the complexity of the group of companies. During the first half of the year, the companies EO Capital 2, EO Capital 3, EPLIK 202306 and EPLIK 202312 merged into oodash Portfolio AB. During the second half of the year, the merger of oodash Sweden AB into oodash Portfolio AB was also registered, and after the end of the financial year, the merger of EO Capital 1 AB into OODA AI AB (publ) was registered.

In parallel, the Company has conducted a review of its financial holdings with the aim of focusing resources on the core business within AI and decentralized infrastructure. The review has included both an assessment of the strategic relevance of the holdings and their valuation according to applicable accounting principles. In connection with this, certain holdings have been written down, in some cases to a book value of SEK 0, based on a conservative valuation. 

The Board's current focus is to gradually sell, spin off, merge or otherwise liquidate remaining portfolio holdings as appropriate strategic alternatives arise, rather than building a broad investment portfolio within the oodash Portfolio. Companies may be added to the portfolio if they are deemed to be in line with the overall strategic direction within AI. 

The Group's revenue in 2025 amounted to SEK 32,058 thousand (SEK 1,061 thousand), of which net sales amounted to SEK 31,880 thousand (SEK 1,056 thousand). The sharp increase in sales is a direct consequence of the invoicing of several major transactions taking place according to previously communicated plan. Earnings per share amounted to SEK -35.29 per share compared to SEK -29.00 per share in 2024. The negative earnings trend is mainly attributable to impairments and results from financial items, rather than the underlying operational activities. 

The operating profit for the Group in 2025 amounted to -29,270 thousand SEK (-51,781 thousand SEK), where the EBITDA result was 2,957 thousand SEK (-28,229 thousand SEK). During the year, the result was burdened by one-off costs of approximately 10,238 thousand SEK attributable to the liquidation of older company structures, which are not considered to be recurring. Adjusted for these, EBITDA amounted to approximately 13,195 thousand SEK, corresponding to an adjusted EBITDA margin of approximately 32 percent of the Group's revenue. Personnel costs in the Group amounted to -9,210 thousand SEK (-7,432 thousand SEK). The Company has continued to have a low cost base and, in line with previous communication, has not carried out any major recruitment during the year. Result from financial items in the Group in 2025 amounted to -95,853 thousand SEK, mainly due to impairments and results from associated companies in connection with the continued streamlining of operations. 

The Group's financial assets amounted to 8,387 thousand SEK at the end of 2025, compared to 96,036 thousand SEK at the end of 2024. The change is mainly attributable to impairments, divestments and revaluations of financial assets, including results from associated companies. During the year, the Company has conducted a review of its financial holdings with the aim of streamlining the operations and focusing on the core business within AI and decentralized infrastructure. In connection with this, certain holdings have been written down, in some cases to a book value of 0 SEK, based on a conservative valuation. 

The Group's equity at the end of 2025 amounted to 23,859 thousand SEK, compared to 119,371 thousand SEK at the end of 2024. The number of shares increased during the year from 3,385,254 to 3,731,465 shares as a result of capital acquisitions. Cash flow from operating activities amounted to -16,662 thousand SEK (-29,680 thousand SEK), cash flow from investing activities amounted to -49,413 thousand SEK (-19,093 thousand SEK) and financing activities contributed positively with 44,302 thousand SEK (68,922 thousand SEK). During the second half of 2025, the Group simultaneously reported a positive cash flow from operating activities of 5,098 thousand SEK, which indicates a strengthened operational development. 

The company is headquartered in Stockholm.

## **Development costs and research** 

According to the Company's accounting principles, development costs are capitalized when all criteria in K3 Chapter 18.6 are met, such as identifiable assets, technical and commercial feasibility and the probability that future economic benefits will flow to the Company. Expenses that do not meet the criteria are expensed directly. The Parent Company has capitalized development costs of approximately SEK 8.7 million in 2025, compared to approximately SEK 8.5 million in 2024. 

The capitalizations in 2025 are mainly attributable to the continued development of the Company's platform and AI products. This includes the further development of OODA AI's Universal AI Platform, where AI models, applications and workflows are brought together in an integrated infrastructure, as well as the underlying infrastructure for decentralized, verifiable and scalable AI computing. During the year, development work has also included work related to the Company's Web3 infrastructure, including MVP, test network and technical components that underpin tokenization and the network's economic layer. 

The company does not conduct basic research but focuses on applied development of AI platforms, AI products and decentralized infrastructure with the aim of strengthening competitiveness and creating scalable services for existing and future customers. The company continuously monitors capitalized projects to ensure that the carrying amounts of the assets do not exceed the recoverable amount. 

## **Activities requiring a permit or notification according to the Environmental Code** 

The parent company does not conduct any activities that require permits or notification according to the Environmental Code. 

## **Sustainability information** 

As a public limited company, the Company is subject to high requirements for financial, operational and sustainability-related transparency. The Company's sustainability work is based on an annually updated policy that describes how environmental, social and corporate governance aspects are integrated into the operations and forms the basis for the non-financial disclosures. 

The company operates primarily digitally, which limits its direct environmental footprint. Environmental work therefore focuses on reducing paper usage, sustainable travel choices and digital working methods that can reduce emissions from daily commuting. The business also prioritizes recycling and responsible resource consumption. 

The company's international profile contributes to a business characterized by diversity and inclusion. The group strives for a work environment where employees are treated equally regardless of background and where fundamental rights and freedoms are respected. Forced labor and child labor are not accepted, and the company is based on fundamental principles of human rights in its internal work and in its relationship with partners and other stakeholders. 

Business ethics are a central part of the Company's governance. Fairness, integrity and transparency shall characterize the operations, which means zero tolerance for bribery and corruption and compliance with applicable rules and competition law principles. The Board determines the overall direction for the sustainability work and the operational management is responsible for implementation in day-to-day operations.

The Company believes that the combination of a scalable product, growing distribution, low cost base and continued technical differentiation provides good conditions for continued growth with improved profitability.

## _**Going concern**_ 

The Board of Directors notes that the reported financial position as of December 31, 2025 has been significantly affected by the review of financial holdings and results from associated companies and other items affecting valuation. The Group's equity at the end of the year amounted to SEK 23,858 thousand, compared with SEK 119,371 thousand the previous year. However, the Board of Directors believes that these items do not reflect the development in the core operational activities, where the Company has shown strong growth during the year, positive EBITDA, significantly lower financing costs and positive cash flow from operating activities during the second half of the year. 

The Company's financial position has been strengthened during the year through completed capital raisings and lower indebtedness. During 2025, the Company was provided with capital through issues in January, March and August, and after the end of the financial year, it was also decided to issue another cash issue of approximately SEK 10 million. These capital contributions have improved the Company's scope for action while reducing the risk level in the balance sheet. In parallel, the Board and management have continued their work to keep the cost base low, simplify the structure and focus resources on operations with clear strategic importance. 

The Board of Directors assesses that the conditions for continued operation in 2026 primarily rest on continued commercialization of the platform, expanded distribution through partner channels and self-service, continued cost control and the ability to provide additional capital if necessary. The broader launch of the platform, which has been communicated for the beginning of the second quarter of 2026, is considered an important step in this development. In light of the operational development in 2025, the capital raisings that have been completed and decided upon, and the continued streamlining of the Group, the Board of Directors assesses that the Company has the conditions to continue operating and that the annual report can therefore be prepared on the going concern basis.

## **Risks** 

## _Competent personnel_ 

The company's continued development is dependent on being able to attract, retain and develop qualified personnel in artificial intelligence, product development, decentralized infrastructure and business development. Competition for experienced AI profiles remains high. At the same time, the group believes that its technology profile, international focus and efficient organizational structure provide good conditions for attracting and retaining key competence. The company uses AI to a large extent in its own operations, which reduces the need for extensive staff expansion, but the operations remain dependent on a limited number of key individuals. 

## _Market risks_ 

The Company operates in markets characterized by rapid technological development, high levels of innovation and changing customer behavior. OODA AI's continued growth is dependent on the Company successfully translating its technical platform into increased usage, broader distribution and long-term recurring revenue. In 2025, the Company has shown strong revenue growth, expanded customer agreements and established a partner program for broader distribution, but there is a risk that commercialization will develop more slowly than planned, that major customer projects will be delayed or that the use of the platform will not reach the expected scale. To mitigate this risk, the Company is continuously working on product development, customer validation, strategic partnerships and expansion of partner channels. 

Macro factors such as inflation, interest rate changes, currency movements and a weaker investment climate can affect both customers' willingness to invest and the capital market's risk appetite. This in turn can affect demand, sales cycles and the Company's ability to raise future capital on attractive terms. The Board therefore closely monitors market developments and is continuously working to adapt the Company's financing and growth plans to prevailing market conditions. 

Regulatory changes can affect both the Parent Company and the Group's operations. As the Company operates in areas such as AI, data protection, cybersecurity and tokenization, changing requirements regarding, for example, data management, AI security, regulatory compliance and digital assets may result in increased costs, longer lead times or the need for adaptations in products and processes. The Company therefore monitors regulatory initiatives on an ongoing basis and develops its platform with a focus on safe, verifiable and compliant AI use.

## _Financial risks_ 

In 2025, the Group strengthened its operational activities through significantly increased net sales, positive EBITDA and significantly lower interest expenses compared to the previous year. At the same time, the year's profit and equity were significantly affected by impairments, revaluations and results from previous financial holdings. At the end of 2025, the Group's equity amounted to SEK 23,858 thousand and cash and cash equivalents to SEK 3,335 thousand. This means that the Company continues to be dependent on good cost control, functioning cash flows from operational activities and the ability to provide additional capital if necessary. 

The Board of Directors believes that continued low debt is important for a healthy capital structure. During the year, the Company has carried out several issues that have strengthened liquidity and reduced financial risk, and after the end of the financial year, a further issue has been decided. Despite this, a liquidity risk remains if revenue development deviates significantly from plan or if capital market conditions deteriorate. Cash flow forecasts are therefore updated continuously and include scenarios for delayed customer payments, lower sales rates and changed financing conditions. 

The Group continuously invests in intangible assets, primarily related to the AI  platform and related infrastructure. If commercial development does not meet expectations, or if market conditions change rapidly, further impairment needs may arise, which could negatively affect earnings and equity. The Board therefore monitors developments on an ongoing basis and evaluates business plans, cost structure and investments in relation to the Company's development. 

## _Risks in holdings_ 

There is a risk that values  in remaining holdings will not be realized to the extent expected, or that divestments, mergers or other structural measures cannot be implemented on favorable terms. 

The Board of Directors continuously evaluates the strategic importance of the holdings and may reduce exposure further if it is deemed to create value for the Company and its shareholders.

## **Note 1 Accounting and valuation principles** 

## **General information** 

The annual report and consolidated accounts have been prepared in accordance with the Annual Accounts Act and BFNAR 2012:1 Annual Report and Consolidated Accounts (K3). The accounting principles are unchanged compared to the previous year. 

## **Revenue recognition** 

Revenue has been recognized at the fair value of what has been received or will be received and is reported to the extent that it is probable that the economic benefits will flow to the company and the revenue can be calculated reliably. 

The company's revenue consists exclusively of consulting services. Revenue is reported in accordance with K3 Chapter 23 and is recognized as revenue as the services are performed. Revenue primarily relates to consulting assignments that are performed continuously over time. For assignments on a recurring basis, revenue is recognized as the work is performed based on time spent and a chargeable hourly rate. For fixed-price assignments, percentage-of-completion accounting is applied, where revenue is recognized in relation to the degree of completion of the assignment. The degree of completion is normally determined based on time spent in relation to the total estimated time for the assignment. 

Work performed but not yet invoiced is reported as accrued income (interim receivables). Advance billing is reported as prepaid income until the service has been performed. 

If there is uncertainty about the possibility of receiving payment, revenue is reduced and an expense is recognized corresponding to the amount that is deemed uncertain. 

Compensation in the form of interest or dividends is recognized as income when it is probable that the company will receive the economic benefits associated with the transaction and when the income can be measured reliably. 

Interest is recognized as income according to the so-called effective interest method. Dividends are recognized as income when the company's right to payment is secured. 

## **Ongoing service assignments** 

Income from assignments on a recurring basis is recognized as income as the work is performed and materials are delivered or consumed, meaning that the profit from the assignments is settled successively. 

If it is not probable that payment will be received for amounts that have already been recognized as revenue, the amount that is not likely to be received is recognized as an expense. 

In the balance sheet, reported revenue is compared with the amounts invoiced to the customer during the same period. If the invoiced amounts exceed the reported revenue, the difference constitutes a liability, which is reported as invoiced but not accrued revenue. If the revenue exceeds the invoiced amounts, the difference constitutes a receivable, which is reported as accrued but not invoiced revenue.

## **Consolidated financial statements** 

## _**Consolidation method**_ 

The consolidated financial statements have been prepared according to the acquisition method. This means that the identifiable assets and liabilities of acquired businesses are reported at market value according to the acquisition analysis. If the acquisition value of the business exceeds the estimated market value of the expected net assets according to the acquisition analysis, the difference is reported as goodwill. 

## _**Subsidiary**_ 

In oodash Portfolio AB there are a number of portfolio holdings, some of which are majority owned but are not consolidated in the consolidated accounts, as they are not considered to be material to the group. The consolidated accounts otherwise include, in addition to the parent company, all companies in which the parent company directly or indirectly holds more than 50% of the voting rights or otherwise has a controlling influence and thereby has the right to shape the company's financial and operational strategies with the aim of obtaining economic benefits. 

A subsidiary's income and expenses are included in the consolidated financial statements from the date of acquisition until the date when the parent company no longer has a controlling influence over the subsidiary. 

Minority interest is the portion of the subsidiary's results and net assets in the consolidated financial statements attributable to equity instruments that are not, directly or indirectly through subsidiaries, owned by the parent company. 

Minority interests are reported in the consolidated balance sheet as a separate item within the consolidated equity. The accounting is based on the group as a single entity according to the so-called unit theory. All assets over which the group has a controlling influence are included in the consolidated balance sheet, even those that are partly owned by other owners. The minority's share of the profit after tax is reported separately as a minority interest. 

Acquisitions and divestments of minority interests are reported within equity. 

## _**Associated companies and Jointly controlled entities**_ 

Associated companies are companies in which the company has significant but not controlling influence. Significant influence is normally considered to exist when the company owns at least 20% but not more than 50% of the votes in another company. 

## _**Accounting for associated companies**_ 

Holdings in associated companies are reported in the consolidated accounts using the equity method. This means that an acquisition analysis is prepared and any surplus or deficit values  are identified. The consolidated value of the share is affected during the holding period by amortization of surplus values or reversal of deficit values. The share value is also affected by the share of profit in the associated company during the holding period, adjusted for internal profits and other consolidated adjustments. Dividends received reduce the consolidated value. 

The share of the associated company's profit after tax is reported on a separate line and affects the Group's operating profit. If there is a reliable and lasting increase in value and the value significantly exceeds the book value, it is written up to a maximum of this value. The revaluation amount is used for allocation to a revaluation fund.

## _**Transactions between group companies**_ 

Intra-group receivables and liabilities, transactions between group companies and unrealized profits are eliminated in full. Unrealized losses are also eliminated unless the transaction corresponds to an impairment requirement.

## _**Goodwill**_ 

Goodwill is the difference that arises if the acquisition value of the acquired entity is higher than the value of the acquired entity's net assets. At the time of acquisition, the goodwill arising is recognized as an asset in the balance sheet. 

## **Intangible assets** 

The company reports internally generated intangible assets according to the capitalization model. This means that all expenses related to the development of an internally generated intangible asset are capitalized and amortized over the asset's estimated useful life, provided that the criteria in BFNAR 2012:1 are met. 

When accounting for development expenses, the capitalization model is applied. This means that expenses incurred during the development phase are recognized as assets when all of the following conditions are met: 

- It is technically possible to complete the intangible asset so that it can be used or sold. 

- The intention is to complete the intangible asset so that it can be used or sold. 

- Prerequisites exist to use or sell the intangible asset. 

- There are necessary and adequate technical, financial and other resources to complete the development and to use or sell the intangible asset. 

- The expenses attributable to the intangible asset can be measured reliably. 

Internally generated intangible assets are reported at cost less accumulated depreciation and impairment losses. 

The cost of an internally generated intangible asset consists of all directly attributable expenses (e.g. materials and salaries).

## **Financial instruments** 

Financial instruments are measured at cost. The instrument is recognized in the balance sheet when the company becomes a party to the contractual terms of the instrument. Financial assets are derecognized when the right to receive cash flows from the instrument has expired or has been transferred and the company has transferred substantially all the risks and rewards of ownership. Financial liabilities are derecognized when the obligations are settled or otherwise extinguished. 

Financial assets and liabilities are reported in accordance with Chapter 11 (Financial instruments measured at cost) in BFNAR 2012:1. 

## _**Shares in subsidiaries**_ 

Shares in subsidiaries are reported at cost less any impairment losses. Cost includes the purchase price paid for the shares and acquisition costs. Any capital contributions are added to cost when they arise. 

## _**Other long-term securities holdings**_ 

Investments in securities acquired with the intention of being held long-term have been reported at their acquisition value. An assessment is made on each balance sheet date for any impairment.

Accounts receivable and short-term receivables are reported as current assets at the amount expected to be collected after deduction of individually assessed doubtful receivables. 

## _**Loans and accounts payable**_ 

Loan liabilities and accounts payable are initially recognized at cost less transaction costs. If the reported amount differs from the amount to be repaid at maturity, the difference is accrued as interest expense over the term of the loan using the instrument's effective interest rate. This ensures that the reported amount and the amount to be repaid at maturity are the same. 

## _**Offsetting of financial receivable and financial liability**_ 

A financial asset and a financial liability are offset and reported with a net amount in the balance sheet only when there is a legal right of set-off and when a settlement with a net amount is intended to occur or when a simultaneous disposal of the asset and settlement of the liability is intended to occur.

## **Income taxes** 

Total tax consists of current tax and deferred tax. Taxes are recognized in the income statement, except when the underlying transaction is recognized directly against equity, in which case the related tax effects are recognized in equity. 

## _**Current tax**_ 

Current tax refers to income tax for the current financial year and the portion of income tax from previous financial years that has not yet been reported. Current tax is calculated based on the tax rate in effect on the balance sheet date. 

## _**Deferred tax**_ 

Deferred tax is income tax that relates to future financial years as a result of past events. Accounting is carried out according to the balance sheet method. Under this method, deferred tax liabilities and deferred tax assets are recognised on temporary differences that arise between the book and tax values  of assets and liabilities, as well as for other tax deductions or deficits. 

Deferred tax assets are reported net of deferred tax liabilities only if they can be paid in a net amount. Deferred tax is calculated based on the tax rate in effect on the balance sheet date. The effects of changes in applicable tax rates are recognized in the income statement in the period in which the change is enacted. Deferred tax assets are reported as financial assets and deferred tax liabilities as provisions. 

Deferred tax assets relating to loss carryforwards or other future tax deductions are recognized to the extent that it is probable that the deductions can be offset against future taxable profits. 

Due to the connection between accounting and taxation, the deferred tax liability attributable to untaxed reserves is not reported separately.

Employee benefits refer to all forms of compensation that the company provides to employees. Short-term benefits include salaries, paid vacation, paid absence, bonuses and post-employment benefits (pension).
