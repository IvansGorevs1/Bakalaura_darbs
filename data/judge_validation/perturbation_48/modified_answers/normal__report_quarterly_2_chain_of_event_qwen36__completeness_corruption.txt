AB Amber Grid reported a significant improvement in financial performance for the three-month period ended 31 March 2026. Revenue increased by 58% to EUR 32,821 thousand, driven by higher transmitted gas volumes and transmission prices. Operating expenses decreased to EUR 9,570 thousand as natural gas costs fell. EBITDA reached EUR 23,304 thousand, operating profit was EUR 19,627 thousand, and net profit was EUR 15,763 thousand. Total assets stood at EUR 324,064 thousand, while total equity increased to EUR 182,039 thousand.
