VILVI Group reported strong revenue growth but significantly lower operating profitability in the first quarter of 2026. Consolidated revenue increased 22.4% year on year to EUR 86.97 million, supported by higher sales in Lithuania and other countries and the consolidation of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB from 1 January 2026. Revenue from Lithuania rose 72.9% to EUR 14.63 million, while revenue from other countries increased 46.6% to EUR 29.82 million. European Union revenue was broadly stable at EUR 42.52 million, representing 49% of total sales compared with 59% a year earlier.

The sales mix changed substantially. Dry milk product revenue more than doubled to EUR 24.66 million, while fresh milk product revenue increased to EUR 12.75 million.

EBITDA fell 50.2% to EUR 3.00 million, and the EBITDA margin decreased to 3.5%. Despite the lower EBITDA, net profit increased 25.7% to EUR 5.09 million.

Milk purchases increased during the quarter. The Group completed the acquisition of Marijampolės pieno konservai and began commercial cheese production at its new factory in Latvia.

Net financial debt increased to EUR 83.43 million, while total assets rose to EUR 220.31 million.
