Telia Lietuva, AB reported strong first-half 2026 financial results, with consolidated revenue reaching EUR 279.1 million, up 17.2 percent from EUR 241.7 million in the prior year. Adjusted EBITDA increased 20.2 percent to EUR 116.9 million, expanding the adjusted EBITDA margin to 41.3 percent from 40.1 percent. Operating profit before income tax rose 16.6 percent to EUR 61.3 million, while net profit totaled EUR 51.3 million, a 14.7 percent increase. The company generated EUR 103.1 million in operating cash flow, a 15.0 percent improvement year-on-year, yielding free cash flow of EUR 64.1 million, up 12.5 percent. Capital expenditures surged 67.9 percent to EUR 47.5 million, heavily weighted toward EUR 19.7 million in 5G spectrum licenses. Despite higher capex, the balance sheet shifted to a net cash position of negative EUR 14.3 million, contrasting with a EUR 27.5 million net debt balance at the end of June 2025.

Revenue growth was broad-based, driven by an 8.3 percent rise in mobile service revenue, a 5.3 percent increase in fixed services, and a 9.6 percent jump in equipment sales. Lithuanian mobile data traffic continues to grow at approximately 35 percent annually, creating immediate capacity requirements that justified the company acquiring two 1,600 MHz frequency blocks for nearly EUR 18 million and extending 2,100 MHz rights for EUR 2 million. These licenses will enable enhanced 5G network capabilities and faster data transmission in underserved areas. Management also committed to investing EUR 17.1 million during 2026 to build 60 new base stations and modernize existing sites. Furthermore, construction began on a new Tier III data center near Vilnius, with a planned EUR 26 million investment targeting completion by mid-2028.

Operating expenses excluding non-recurring items grew 10.2 percent, outpacing revenue in certain categories. Higher cost of goods reflected increased equipment sales volume alongside elevated interconnection and roaming costs. Employee-related expenses included EUR 1.4 million in non-recurring redundancy payments related to the Digital and Technology unit restructuring. Energy costs rose 24.3 percent, and external IT service expenses increased 28.4 percent. The company successfully maintained margin expansion through operational efficiency. Robust cash generation supported a record dividend payout of EUR 91.6 million, representing EUR 0.16 per share for 2025. Governance updates included Andreas Ekström joining the Board and Vaidas Dominauskas assuming leadership of the Enterprise unit, supporting ongoing strategic infrastructure and organizational development.
