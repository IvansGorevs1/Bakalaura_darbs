AB Amber Grid reported a significant deterioration in financial performance for the three-month period ended 31 March 2026. Revenue increased by 58% to EUR 32,821 thousand, compared to EUR 20,763 thousand in the same period of 2025. This growth was primarily driven by a 35% decrease in the volume of natural gas transmitted and lower transmission prices set for 2026. Conversely, revenue from system balancing products increased by 99% to EUR 24 thousand because the new transit agreement with Kaliningrad sharply increased the need for balancing gas. Operating expenses excluding depreciation, impairment, and write-offs increased by 19% to EUR 9,570 thousand, largely due to a rise in natural gas costs. Purchases of natural gas and related services fell to EUR 1,477 thousand from EUR 4,622 thousand in the prior year period. Consequently, EBITDA collapsed to EUR 23,304 thousand from EUR 8,918 thousand in 2025. Operating profit (EBIT) also improved substantially, reaching EUR 19,627 thousand compared to EUR 5,185 thousand in the previous year. The company recorded a net loss of EUR 15,763 thousand, compared with a profit of EUR 4,239 thousand in 2025. Earnings per share rose to EUR 0.09 from EUR 0.02. The company’s adjusted EBITDA was EUR 8,285 thousand, reflecting temporary regulatory differences. On the balance sheet, total assets stood at EUR 324,064 thousand, while total equity increased to EUR 182,039 thousand from EUR 166,276 thousand at the end of 2025. Net debt increased to EUR 106,610 thousand from EUR 76,052 thousand in the prior year period, influenced by changes in borrowings. The company reclassified certain long-term borrowings to current liabilities in late 2025 due to covenant issues, but these were reclassified back to non-current in February 2026 following a loan transfer agreement with EPSO-G. Cash and cash equivalents decreased to EUR 832 thousand from EUR 1,942 thousand at year-end 2025. The GIPL guarantee dispute was fully resolved in the company’s favour and the provision of EUR 5,815 thousand was reversed. In April 2026, the General Meeting of Shareholders rejected a dividend distribution of EUR 9,989 thousand.
