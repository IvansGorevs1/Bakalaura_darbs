During the first three months of 2026, VILVI Group reported significant revenue growth accompanied by higher net profit, though EBITDA declined. Consolidated sales revenue increased by 22.4% to 86.97 million euros. Dry and fresh milk product revenue rose strongly, while the newly consolidated canned milk segment contributed 11.92 million euros. 

Despite the revenue growth, EBITDA dropped by 50.2% to 3.00 million euros and the margin fell to 3.5%. Net profit nevertheless increased by 25.7% to 5.09 million euros. 

The financial position expanded substantially, largely because of acquisition activity. Total assets reached 220.31 million euros, while net financial debt climbed to 83.43 million euros. The reporting period included the consolidation of new subsidiaries and the commencement of commercial production at new facilities.
