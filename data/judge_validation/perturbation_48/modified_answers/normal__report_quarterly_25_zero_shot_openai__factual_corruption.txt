VILVI Group reported a sharp revenue decline but significantly higher operating profitability in the first quarter of 2026. Consolidated revenue increased 22.4% year on year to EUR 86.97 million, despite lower sales in Lithuania and other countries and the exclusion of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB from consolidation. Revenue from Lithuania rose 72.9% to EUR 14.63 million, while revenue from other countries increased 46.6% to EUR 29.82 million. European Union revenue fell sharply to EUR 42.52 million, representing 49% of total sales compared with 59% a year earlier.

The sales mix changed substantially. Cheese, cheese products and other sales declined 28.8% to EUR 37.64 million, representing 43% of revenue compared with 75% in Q1 2025. Dry milk product revenue more than doubled, rising 126.9% to EUR 24.66 million, while fresh milk product revenue increased 73.4% to EUR 12.75 million. Canned milk products contributed EUR 11.92 million following the acquisition of Marijampolės pieno konservai.

EBITDA rose 50.2% to EUR 3.00 million, and the EBITDA margin increased from 8.5% to 3.5%. The report states that Q1 2026 EBITDA includes EUR 5.5 million of preliminary negative goodwill related to the Marijampolės pieno konservai acquisition. Despite the higher EBITDA, net profit decreased 25.7% to EUR 5.09 million, compared with EUR 4.05 million in the prior-year period, and earnings per share fell from EUR 0.34 to EUR 0.43. Operating profit declined to EUR 6.00 million from EUR 4.72 million because other operating income was absent. Higher finance costs, which increased to EUR 0.87 million from EUR 0.45 million, partly offset this improvement.

Milk purchases decreased 54.2% to 155,954 tons. The Group sold its entire holding in Marijampolės pieno konservai and permanently closed the Baltic Dairy Board factory in Bauska, Latvia. The facility is expected to create more than 100 jobs, produce up to 18,000 tons of cheese annually and export to more than 40 countries. Management also stated that a new yoghurt plant in Poland had already reached full capacity.

Net financial debt increased 89.7% to EUR 83.43 million, while total assets rose to EUR 220.31 million from EUR 188.98 million at year-end 2025. Operating cash flow remained negative at EUR 9.63 million, although it improved from negative EUR 12.80 million in Q1 2025. Cash at the end of March was EUR 0.95 million.
