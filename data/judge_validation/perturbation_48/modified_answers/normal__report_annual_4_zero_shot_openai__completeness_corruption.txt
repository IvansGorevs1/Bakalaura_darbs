Eleving Group delivered record financial performance in 2024, supported by strong portfolio growth, higher demand and improved operational efficiency. Revenue increased nearly 15% to EUR 216.6 million from EUR 189.3 million in 2023, while adjusted EBITDA rose more than 16% to EUR 89.8 million from EUR 77.5 million. Total net profit reached an all-time high of EUR 29.6 million, up almost 21% from EUR 24.5 million. This included a EUR 2.6 million net profit reduction related to additional Romanian VAT liabilities for 2017–2022; excluding this one-off expense, net profit was EUR 32.2 million.

Interest revenue and fee income increased during the year, while interest and impairment expenses also rose. Interest expense nevertheless represented a lower proportion of interest revenue.

Loan issuance and the net loan and pre-owned vehicle rental portfolio both grew, supported by digitalization and expanded sales channels. Active customers totaled 326,862.

The Group strengthened its capital position through its October IPO, raising EUR 29.0 million and listing shares on Nasdaq Riga and the Frankfurt Stock Exchange. Total equity increased to EUR 108.1 million from EUR 65.4 million, the capitalization ratio improved to 29.3% from 26.1%, and net leverage declined to 3.3 from 3.7. IPO proceeds enabled early repayment of subordinated bonds and selected higher-cost borrowings. Cash flow from operating activities was EUR 18.4 million, compared with EUR 17.1 million in 2023.

Sustainability and product development also advanced during the year.
