AAC Clyde Space reported net sales of SEK 295 million for 2025, representing a 16 percent decline from SEK 352 million in 2024. EBITDA decreased to SEK 17 million from SEK 47 million, while the operating result after depreciation and impairment amounted to minus SEK 40 million compared to minus SEK 4 million previously. Earnings per share stood at minus SEK 7.63, down from minus SEK 1.00. Operating cash flow turned significantly negative at minus SEK 73 million, reversing a positive SEK 57 million result in 2024. Cash and cash equivalents fell to SEK 30 million from SEK 50 million, though the equity ratio improved to 80 percent from 71 percent following two targeted capital raises that generated approximately SEK 115 million before transaction costs. Order backlog declined to SEK 352 million at year end from SEK 641 million, although a major contract received shortly after year end worth EUR 76.3 million is expected to substantially increase the pipeline in early 2026.

The company merged its former divisions into two reporting segments. Data and Services performed strongly, while Products and Missions recorded lower sales and EBITDA.

Management linked the weaker result to programme delays that affected revenue recognition and cash flow. The company nevertheless completed capital increases and continued investing in its satellite and data-service capabilities.
