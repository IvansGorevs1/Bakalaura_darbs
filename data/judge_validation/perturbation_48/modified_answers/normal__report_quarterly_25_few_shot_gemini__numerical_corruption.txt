During the first three months of 2026, VILVI Group reported significant revenue growth accompanied by higher net profit, though profitability metrics such as EBITDA experienced a downturn compared to the same period in the previous year. Consolidated sales revenue increased by 32.4% to reach 96.97 million euros, up from 71.07 million euros in the first quarter of 2025. This top-line expansion was supported by notable increases across several core operating segments, particularly dry milk products, which surged by 136.9% to 34.66 million euros, and fresh milk products, which rose by 73.4% to 12.75 million euros. Additionally, the newly consolidated canned milk segment contributed 10.92 million euros following the acquisition of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB. Conversely, revenue from cheese, cheese products, and other goods declined by 28.8% to 37.64 million euros. Geographically, sales grew across all major markets, with European Union revenue edging up 0.6% to 42.52 million euros, sales in Lithuania jumping 62.9% to 14.63 million euros, and other countries increasing by 46.6% to 29.82 million euros. 

Despite the revenue growth, profitability performance presented a mixed picture. EBITDA dropped by 40.2% to 4.00 million euros compared with 6.03 million euros in the first quarter of 2025, resulting in an EBITDA margin compression from 8.5% down to 4.5%. The reported EBITDA figure for the period excludes a preliminary negative goodwill of 5.54 million dollars stemming from the Marijampolės pieno konservai acquisition. On the other hand, the group's net profit for the first three months of 2026 increased by 25.7% to 5.09 million euros, compared to 4.05 million euros in the prior-year period. Profit per share correspondingly rose to 0.43 euros from 0.34 euros. The group purchased a total of 166 thousand tons of basic indicator milk during the period, representing a 64.2% increase over the first quarter of 2025. 

The financial position of VILVI Group expanded substantially during the reporting period, largely driven by corporate acquisition activities. Total assets reached 230.31 million euros as of March 31, 2026, up from 188.98 million euros at the end of December 2025. Property, plant, and equipment increased to 141.87 million euros from 117.30 million euros. Total liabilities rose to 126.29 million euros from 100.04 million euros, with net financial debt climbing to 93.43 million euros, representing an 89.7% increase compared to the first quarter of 2025. Total equity stood at 94.03 million euros at the end of March 2026. Cash flows from operating activities resulted in a net outflow of 19.63 million euros, which included interest and income tax payments alongside the negative goodwill gain, while net cash used in investing activities totaled 8.04 million euros, reflecting capital expenditures and the acquisition of the subsidiary. Net cash flows from financing activities provided a positive inflow of 25.84 million euros, supported by net proceeds from borrowings. Overall, the reporting period was characterized by major strategic expansion, including the consolidation of new subsidiaries and the commencement of commercial production at the group's new facilities, which significantly scaled up operations and revenues.
