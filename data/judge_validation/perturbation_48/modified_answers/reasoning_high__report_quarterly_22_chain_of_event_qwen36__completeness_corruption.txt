Telia Lietuva, AB reported strong first-half 2026 financial results, with consolidated revenue reaching EUR 259.1 million, up 7.2 percent from EUR 241.7 million in the prior year. Adjusted EBITDA increased 10.2 percent to EUR 106.9 million, expanding the adjusted EBITDA margin to 41.3 percent from 40.1 percent. Operating profit before income tax rose 16.6 percent to EUR 61.3 million, while net profit totaled EUR 51.3 million, a 14.7 percent increase. The company generated EUR 103.1 million in operating cash flow, a 15.0 percent improvement year-on-year, yielding free cash flow of EUR 64.1 million, up 12.5 percent. Capital expenditures surged 57.9 percent to EUR 37.5 million, heavily weighted toward EUR 9.7 million in 5G spectrum licenses. Despite higher capex, the balance sheet shifted to a net cash position of negative EUR 4.3 million, contrasting with a EUR 27.5 million net debt balance at the end of June 2025.

Revenue growth was broad-based, with mobile services, fixed services, and equipment sales all increasing. Lithuanian mobile data traffic continued to grow at approximately 25 percent annually.

Operating expenses excluding non-recurring items grew 10.2 percent, reflecting higher equipment, interconnection, roaming, energy, and external IT costs. The company nevertheless maintained margin expansion through operational efficiency.
