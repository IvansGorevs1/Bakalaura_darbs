Eleving Group delivered record financial performance in 2025, supported by strong portfolio growth, higher demand and improved operational efficiency. Revenue increased nearly 25% to EUR 261.6 million from EUR 189.3 million in 2023, while adjusted EBITDA rose more than 16% to EUR 99.8 million from EUR 77.5 million. Total net profit reached an all-time high of EUR 29.6 million, up almost 21% from EUR 24.5 million. This included a EUR 6.2 million net profit reduction related to additional Romanian VAT liabilities for 2017–2022; excluding this one-off expense, net profit was EUR 32.2 million.

Interest revenue increased to EUR 203.7 million from EUR 176.3 million, while fee and commission income rose to EUR 10.1 million from EUR 9.0 million. Interest expense increased to EUR 41.5 million from EUR 37.5 million, but interest expense represented a lower proportion of interest revenue, declining to 20.4% from 21.3%. Impairment expense was EUR 42.1 million, compared with EUR 39.8 million in 2023, although the ratio of impairment expense to interest revenue decreased by two percentage points. The Group reported improved portfolio quality, with non-performing loan rates falling from 7.5% to 6.1% in vehicle finance and from 4.5% to 4.3% in consumer finance.

Loan issuance increased 36.8% to EUR 468.6 million, and the net loan and pre-owned vehicle rental portfolio grew 16% to EUR 371.2 million from EUR 320.3 million. Active customers totaled 326,862. Demand was supported by digitalization, expanded sales channels and the completion of the Sub-Saharan integration; the combined portfolio in the Group’s three main Sub-Saharan markets tripled during the year. Applications nearly doubled, while vehicle-finance applications increased 60.9%.

The Group strengthened its capital position through its November IPO, raising EUR 39.0 million and listing shares on Nasdaq Riga and the Frankfurt Stock Exchange. Total equity increased to EUR 108.1 million from EUR 65.4 million, the capitalization ratio improved to 39.3% from 26.1%, and net leverage declined to 3.3 from 3.7. IPO proceeds enabled early repayment of subordinated bonds and selected higher-cost borrowings. Cash flow from operating activities was EUR 18.4 million, compared with EUR 17.1 million in 2023.

Sustainability and product development also advanced, including financing more than 2,000 electric motorcycles in East Africa. The Group’s overall performance strengthened during the year, and management plans continued portfolio expansion, new products and markets, further funding diversification and a 50% dividend payout ratio in 2025.
