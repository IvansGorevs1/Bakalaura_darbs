During the 2025 financial year, TKM Grupp AS experienced a weakening of financial performance amid a challenging economic environment characterized by low consumer confidence, high price sensitivity, and domestic tax changes in Estonia, notably the introduction of a motor vehicle tax. Consolidated sales revenue declined by 2.6 percent to 919.6 million euros compared to 944.6 million euros in the previous year. Profitability contracted more significantly, with profit before tax falling by 31.4 percent to 24.3 million euros and net profit decreasing by 36.1 percent to 17.5 million euros. Net profit per share dropped to 43 cents from 67 cents. Effective cost control helped mitigate pressures from rising expenses, while net profit also benefited from lower borrowing interest costs.

Performance varied across the Group's segments. Supermarket revenue was broadly stable but profit declined, while the department stores segment recorded a loss. Car trade weakened sharply, whereas real estate revenue and profit increased.

Investments fell to 25.5 million euros and were directed toward refurbishments, digital upgrades, and a new car centre. Cash and cash equivalents also decreased during the year.
