AS Trigon Property Development reported a substantial deterioration in performance in 2026. The company generated no property sales, compared with €531,920 of sales revenue in 2024, including a €500,000 sale in the fourth quarter and a €31,920 sale in the third quarter. Consequently, gross profit fell from €220,299 in 2024 to zero, and the operating result changed from a €161,649 profit to a €108,021 loss. After €18,513 of financial income, compared with €5,760 in 2024, the company recorded a net loss of €199,507, versus a net profit of €167,409 in the previous year. Fourth-quarter results also weakened, with a net loss of €23,333 compared with a net profit of €154,215 in the fourth quarter of 2024.

Administrative and general expenses increased to €128,021 from €63,650. The 2025 expenses included €51,452 in remuneration and related taxes for the Management and Supervisory Boards, whereas no such expense was reported in 2024. Audit costs were €23,922, compared with €20,570, and accounting service costs rose to €18,400 from €3,240. The company also purchased €12,000 of services from companies controlled by Supervisory Board members, compared with €3,240 in 2024.

The company continued to operate as a single-segment real estate developer and, at year-end, owned one 18.2-hectare development project in Pärnu intended for a commercial, industrial and logistics park. No land plots were sold during 2025. Instead, €191,262 was invested in the development project, increasing inventories to €1,662,500 from €1,471,238. No inventory write-downs were recorded. Management stated that the company would continue selling its existing land plots and had no plans to start a new business activity in the following year.

Total assets decreased to €1,804,656 from €1,873,680, mainly reflecting the reduction in cash from €370,856 to €227,911. Operating activities used €142,945 of cash in 2025, compared with €194,723 generated in 2024. Total liabilities increased to €49,389 from €18,906, while equity declined to €1,755,267 from €1,854,774 following the annual loss. Return on assets changed from 8.93% to -5.51%, return on equity from 9.03% to -5.67%, and the debt ratio from 1.01% to 2.74%.

The number of shares remained unchanged at 4,599,061. Earnings per share fell from €0.03721 to -€0.03212, while the year-end share price increased modestly from €0.620 to €0.734. The company’s 2025 interim report was unaudited.
