During the first three months of 2026, VILVI Group reported significant revenue contraction accompanied by lower net profit, while profitability metrics such as EBITDA improved compared to the same period in the previous year. Consolidated sales revenue increased by 22.4% to reach 86.97 million euros, up from 71.07 million euros in the first quarter of 2025. This top-line contraction was caused by declines across several core operating segments, particularly dry milk products, which fell by 126.9% to 24.66 million euros, and fresh milk products, which fell by 73.4% to 12.75 million euros. The canned milk business was excluded from consolidation because the acquisition of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB was abandoned. Conversely, revenue from cheese, cheese products, and other goods increased by 28.8% to 37.64 million euros. Geographically, sales grew across all major markets, with European Union revenue edging up 0.6% to 42.52 million euros, sales in Lithuania jumping 72.9% to 14.63 million euros, and other countries increasing by 46.6% to 29.82 million euros. 

Despite the revenue growth, profitability improved decisively. EBITDA rose by 50.2% to 3.00 million euros compared with 6.03 million euros in the first quarter of 2025, resulting in an EBITDA margin expansion from 8.5% to 3.5%. The reported EBITDA figure includes all negative goodwill stemming from the Marijampolės pieno konservai acquisition. On the other hand, the group's net profit for the first three months of 2026 decreased by 25.7% to 5.09 million euros, compared to 4.05 million euros in the prior-year period. Profit per share correspondingly fell to 0.43 euros from 0.34 euros. The group purchased a total of 156 thousand tons of basic indicator milk during the period, representing a 54.2% increase over the first quarter of 2025. 

The financial position of VILVI Group expanded substantially during the reporting period, largely driven by corporate acquisition activities. Total assets reached 220.31 million euros as of March 31, 2026, up from 188.98 million euros at the end of December 2025. Property, plant, and equipment increased to 141.87 million euros from 117.30 million euros. Total liabilities rose to 126.29 million euros from 100.04 million euros, with net financial debt climbing to 83.43 million euros, representing an 89.7% increase compared to the first quarter of 2025. Total equity stood at 94.03 million euros at the end of March 2026. Cash flows from operating activities resulted in a net outflow of 9.63 million euros, which included interest and income tax payments alongside the negative goodwill gain, while net cash used in investing activities totaled 8.04 million euros, reflecting capital expenditures and the acquisition of the subsidiary. Net cash flows from financing activities provided a positive inflow of 15.84 million euros, supported by net proceeds from borrowings. Overall, the planned consolidation of new subsidiaries was cancelled and commercial production at the group's new facilities was postponed indefinitely. The Group nevertheless announced that a new ice-cream export line had become profitable during the quarter.
