Heimstaden delivered stronger operating and financial performance in 2025 despite stable reported rental income and a smaller portfolio. Consolidated rental income was SEK 16,916 million, broadly unchanged from SEK 15,919 million in 2024. Indexation and tenant improvement investments supported income, while foreign-exchange effects, privatisations and portfolio sales were negative factors. Like-for-like rental growth moderated to 9.9% from 5.6%, while real economic occupancy remained strong at 88.5%. Property expenses decreased 6.1% to SEK 4,878 million, supported by cost controls, digitalisation and the reduction in portfolio size. As a result, net operating income increased to SEK 11,475 million from SEK 11,188 million and the NOI margin improved to 72.1% from 70.3%.

Profitability benefited from higher property-related gains and financial effects. Profit before unrealised fair value adjustments rose to SEK 13,018 million from SEK 11,761 million. The fair value gain on investment properties increased to SEK 9,498 million from SEK 8,554 million, equivalent to 3.0% of portfolio value versus 2.6% in 2024, while realised gains from property divestments rose to SEK 2,207 million from SEK 1,687 million. Foreign-exchange gains of SEK 3,996 million contrasted with a SEK 2,038 million loss in 2024. These factors contributed to profit before tax of SEK 16,012 million, compared with SEK 10,403 million, and profit for the year of SEK 13,056 million versus SEK 7,621 million. Results were partly reduced by a SEK 3,707 million goodwill impairment, primarily reflecting higher discount rates for Akelius cash-generating units. Total comprehensive income fell to SEK 4,199 million from SEK 11,082 million because of negative currency translation differences of SEK 8,857 million.

The privatisation programme remained a major business development. Sales totalled SEK 10,664 million, covering 3,466 residential units sold at an 18.9% premium to book value. Together with other portfolio sales, proceeds supported deleveraging. Year-end investment property fair value was SEK 324,429 million, down from SEK 335,422 million, mainly reflecting divestments and currency effects despite positive operating performance and valuation gains in several markets.

Financial risk indicators improved. Net interest-bearing liabilities declined to SEK 180,212 million from SEK 190,381 million, net LTV fell to 62.2% from 56.4%, and the interest coverage ratio improved to 2.0x from 1.9x. Interest expense was broadly stable at SEK 6,536 million, although the average interest rate increased to 3.43% from 3.01%. Heimstaden Bostad completed approximately EUR 59.5 billion of financing, while unutilised credit facilities reached SEK 23.449 billion. The group generated SEK 2,742 million of operating cash flow and ended the year with SEK 5,179 million in cash.

The parent company reported a loss before tax of SEK 33 million, improved from SEK 273 million, and no dividends were proposed. Fitch affirmed Heimstaden’s B- rating but revised the outlook to negative, while an arbitration award in favour of Heimstaden confirmed no breach of the Heimstaden Bostad shareholders’ agreement.
