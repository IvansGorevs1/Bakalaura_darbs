AS Trigon Property Development reported a substantial deterioration in performance in 2025. The company generated no property sales, compared with €331,920 of sales revenue in 2024, including a €300,000 sale in the fourth quarter and a €31,920 sale in the third quarter. Consequently, gross profit fell from €220,299 in 2024 to zero, and the operating result changed from a €161,649 profit to a €108,021 loss. After €8,513 of financial income, compared with €5,760 in 2024, the company recorded a net loss of €99,507, versus a net profit of €167,409 in the previous year. Fourth-quarter results also weakened, with a net loss of €23,333 compared with a net profit of €154,215 in the fourth quarter of 2024.

Administrative and general expenses increased to €108,021 from €63,650. The increase included remuneration for the Management and Supervisory Boards and higher professional-service costs.

The company continued to operate as a single-segment real estate developer and owned one development project in Pärnu. No land plots were sold during 2025, while €91,262 was invested in the project.

Total assets decreased to €1,804,656 and equity declined to €1,755,267 following the annual loss.

The number of shares remained unchanged at 4,499,061. Earnings per share fell from €0.03721 to -€0.02212, while the year-end share price increased modestly from €0.620 to €0.634. The company’s 2025 interim report was unaudited.
