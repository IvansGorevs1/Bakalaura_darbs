During the first nine months of 2026, AS Tallinna Sadam reported a slight increase in revenue alongside a notable decline in profitability compared to the same period of the previous year. Group revenue grew by EUR 3.4 million, or 4.4%, reaching EUR 69.3 million, supported primarily by a higher number of charter days for the multifunctional icebreaker Botnica, increased electricity sales, and higher ferry service revenue. In contrast, operating profit fell by EUR 4.7 million, or 25.7%, to EUR 23.6 million. Adjusted EBITDA decreased by EUR 4.0 million, or 13.5%, to EUR 25.8 million, while profit for the period dropped by EUR 4.3 million, or 31.7%, to EUR 6.0 million. The operating profit margin declined from 31.6% to 22.9%.

The contraction in profitability was largely driven by higher operating expenses, which rose by EUR 3.9 million, or 24.0%. Significant increases were recorded in fuel costs due to higher diesel prices and severe ice conditions, technical maintenance and repair expenses, and electricity costs. Furthermore, financial results for the comparative period in 2025 had been positively affected by a one-off profit from the disposal of land for the Rail Baltica project and an insurance indemnity. Operating volumes also saw overall declines, as total cargo volumes handled across the group harbours decreased by 5.0% to 6.5 million tonnes, primarily due to lower liquid bulk volumes. The total number of passengers fell by 2.0% to 3.6 million, and vessel calls decreased by 4.8% to 3,376, though cruise passenger numbers experienced growth.

Group investments during the six-month period totaled EUR 13.5 million, representing a substantial decrease of EUR 8.5 million compared to the previous year, with funds directed toward ferry engine replacements, quay infrastructure, and terminal designs. Total assets at the end of June 2026 stood at EUR 715.0 million, reflecting a minor decrease of 1.1%, while equity was recorded at EUR 467.7 million. Net debt ended the period at EUR 125.8 million. In May 2026, the company paid out dividends totaling EUR 29.2 million, corresponding to EUR 0.083 per share. Significant corporate developments during the period included the adoption of municipal detailed plans for terminal expansion areas, the execution of a new unsecured loan agreement of up to EUR 42 million with OP Corporate Bank, and the filing of civil damage claims totaling EUR 7.4 million against former management board members following the finalization of long-standing criminal proceedings.
