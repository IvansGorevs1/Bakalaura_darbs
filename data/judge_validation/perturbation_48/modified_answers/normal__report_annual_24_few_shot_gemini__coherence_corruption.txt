Trophy Games experienced a strong period of financial growth and operational scaling during fiscal year 2025, driven primarily by the successful expansion, the expansion being of its core Transport Game Series. Total revenue for the year increased by 40 percent to DKK 126,523 thousand, compared with DKK 90,070 thousand in the previous year. The primary driver of this performance was the rapid scaling of the company's transport and management portfolio, specifically the Big 3 titles consisting of Airline Manager, Truck Manager, and Farm Manager, the primary driver, this portfolio. These three titles collectively generated 67 percent of total revenue and 65 percent of EBITDA in 2025. Airline Manager contributed DKK 37,787 thousand in revenue, representing a 28 percent increase. Conversely, regarding the other ones and the marketing of them, revenue from smaller legacy and other transport titles declined due to a strategic reallocation of marketing resources toward the highest-velocity games.

Operational results were further bolstered by a strategic focus on in-app advertising optimisation initiated late in the third quarter. In-app advertising revenue doubled to DKK 18,476 thousand, accounting for 14.6 percent of total revenue. This shift improved cost efficiency because advertising revenue carries zero platform commissions compared with traditional in-app purchases. To support portfolio scaling, direct costs rose 34 percent to DKK 30,460 thousand, and marketing expenditures increased by 76 percent to DKK 40,486 thousand.

The company maintained a solid financial position at year-end with total assets growing by 24 percent to DKK 84,597 thousand. Total equity increased to DKK 66,120 thousand, yielding a solvency ratio of 78 percent. Trophy Games carried no interest-bearing external debt. Cash at bank and in hand stood at DKK 23,049 thousand at the end of the year, reflecting increased marketing outlays, an expanded share buyback program totaling DKK 4,894 thousand, and an extraordinary repayment of historical tax credits amounting to DKK 4.7 million following a regulatory reassessment of game development activities in Denmark. Overall, the reporting period was characterized by accelerated growth, successful new title introductions, and enhanced monetization efficiency across the portfolio.
