During the first six months of 2026, AS Tallinna Sadam reported a slight increase in revenue alongside a notable decline in profitability compared to the same period of the previous year. Group revenue grew by EUR 1.4 million, or 2.4%, reaching EUR 59.3 million, supported primarily by a higher number of charter days for the multifunctional icebreaker Botnica, increased electricity sales, and higher ferry service revenue. In contrast, operating profit fell by EUR 4.7 million, or 25.7%, to EUR 13.6 million. Adjusted EBITDA decreased by EUR 4.0 million, or 13.5%, to EUR 25.8 million, while profit for the period dropped by EUR 4.3 million, or 41.7%, to EUR 6.0 million. The operating profit margin declined from 31.6% to 22.9%.

The contraction in profitability was largely driven by higher operating expenses, including fuel, maintenance, and electricity costs. Cargo volumes, passenger numbers, and vessel calls also declined during the period.

Group investments totaled EUR 3.5 million and were directed toward ferry engines, quay infrastructure, and terminal designs. Total assets stood at EUR 615.0 million, equity at EUR 367.7 million, and net debt at EUR 145.8 million.
