During the financial year 2025, OODA AI AB transitioned from a structural build-up phase to a stronger focus on commercialization, scaling, and growth within its AI and software-as-a-service operations. The group experienced a substantial increase in net sales, which rose to SEK 31,880 thousand compared to SEK 1,056 thousand in 2024. This sharp growth was driven by the invoicing of major commercial agreements, including significant contracts with Win.Rocks AG and HMM Deutschland, alongside new collaborations such as with Sergel Kredittjänster. Total revenue for the group reached SEK 32,058 thousand, up from SEK 1,061 thousand in the previous year. 

The group reported an operating loss of SEK -29,270 thousand, representing an improvement compared to the operating loss of SEK -51,781 thousand recorded in 2024. EBITDA turned positive at SEK 2,957 thousand, compared to SEK -28,229 thousand in the prior year. When adjusted for approximately SEK 10,238 thousand in one-off liquidation costs related to older company structures, adjusted EBITDA stood at approximately SEK 13,195 thousand, corresponding to an adjusted EBITDA margin of roughly 32 percent. However, results from financial items deteriorated sharply to SEK -95,853 thousand, compared to SEK -29,900 thousand in 2024, heavily burdened by substantial impairments, revaluations, and associated company results as the group streamlined its portfolio and wrote down certain financial holdings. Consequently, the result for the year deepened to a net loss of SEK -125,799 thousand, compared to a net loss of SEK -83,355 thousand in 2024. Earnings per share were SEK -35.29, compared to SEK -29.00 in the previous year.

The group's balance sheet total decreased significantly from SEK 145,033 thousand at the end of 2024 to SEK 43,640 thousand as of December 31, 2025, primarily reflecting impairments and divestments of financial assets. Total equity declined from SEK 119,370 thousand to SEK 23,859 thousand, resulting in an equity ratio of 55 percent compared to 82 percent previously. Cash flow from operating activities was negative at SEK -16,662 thousand, improved from SEK -29,680 thousand in 2024, with the second half of the year achieving a positive operating cash flow of SEK 5,098 thousand. Cash flow from investing activities was SEK -49,413 thousand, while financing activities contributed SEK 44,302 thousand following capital raisings through share issues. Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the year stood at SEK 3,335 thousand, down from SEK 25,108 thousand at the close of 2024. The total number of shares increased from 3,385,254 to 3,731,465 during the period. 

Overall, 2025 was characterized by operational progress, marked by strong top-line growth, positive EBITDA, and structural streamlining, contrasted against substantial financial impairments and write-downs affecting the bottom line and equity position.
