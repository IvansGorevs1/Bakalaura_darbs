During the first three months of 2026, AS Amber Latvijas balzams reported an unaudited net turnover of EUR 11.1 million, which is a decrease of 29.6% compared to EUR 15.8 million in the corresponding period of 2025. Total sales volume declined by 36.1% to 517,600 nine-liter cases from 810,079 nine-liter cases in the prior year. Gross profit fell by 66.5% to EUR 1.2 million, down from EUR 3.5 million in the first three months of 2025. The company recorded an operating loss of EUR 1.6 million for the reporting period, representing a significant decline from an operating profit of EUR 0.6 million in the previous year. Consequently, the net result shifted from a profit of EUR 0.9 million in the first three months of 2025 to a net loss of EUR 1.4 million in the first three months of 2026, resulting in a negative earnings per share of EUR 0.19 compared to EUR 0.11 in the prior period. 

The financial performance was heavily impacted by external geopolitical and market conditions. The global spirits market experienced a downturn marked by lower sales volumes and changing consumer habits. Furthermore, following Amber Beverage Group's support for Ukraine, Russian authorities designated related entities as extremist and confiscated the group-owned Talvis distillery. In January 2026, the Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceedings and the appointment of a trustee for Stoli Group USA led to a complete loss of control over operations within the U.S. jurisdiction, directly affecting the solvency of the company's largest customer and its ability to meet creditor obligations. These cash flow difficulties resulted in delayed excise tax payments and subsequent freezing of company bank accounts by the State Revenue Service. 

To ensure business continuity and protect creditors, the company filed an application on 30 January 2026 for legal protection proceedings, which was approved by the Riga City Court on 5 February 2026, allowing the company to restructure liabilities and stabilize operations. This filing automatically triggered events of default for the principal shareholder, Amber Beverage Group Holding S.à r.l., under its bond documentation and bank financing arrangements. Subsequently, the principal shareholder was authorized to initiate judicial reorganization proceedings in Luxembourg with a moratorium granted until 24 August 2026, suspending enforcement actions and events of default under the bonds. In operational efficiency indicators, the Right First Time rate improved to 99.5% from 98.4%, while the On Time In Full rate declined to 70.3% from 97.5%. The company also paid EUR 8.4 million in total taxes during the reporting period, including EUR 6.4 million in excise tax.
