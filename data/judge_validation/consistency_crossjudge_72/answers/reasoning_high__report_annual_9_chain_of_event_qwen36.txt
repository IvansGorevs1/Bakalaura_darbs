Impero A/S delivered resilient financial results for the full year 2025, reporting Annual Recurring Revenue of DKK 46.2 million, a 19% year-on-year increase that achieved the upper bound of the revised guidance range. Revenue similarly grew by 19% to DKK 42.0 million from DKK 35.4 million in 2024. Operating performance improved markedly, with EBITDA rising to negative DKK 3.1 million from negative DKK 8.7 million the previous year, an improvement of DKK 5.6 million that placed results at the favorable end of the DKK -5 million to -3 million guidance interval. This enhancement was primarily driven by revenue growth outpacing operating cost expansion; staff costs and other external expenses each increased by only 6%, allowing contribution margins to benefit from a 16% decline in cost of sales. Furthermore, the activation of development costs rose to DKK 5.2 million from DKK 3.6 million, supporting the bottom line. Cash flow from ordinary operating activities contracted less severely to negative DKK 1.3 million compared to negative DKK 4.3 million in 2024, improving the free cash flow to net new ARR ratio to 0.9 at year-end.

SaaS metrics demonstrated strong product-market fit and customer retention. The churn rate remained exceptionally low at 1%, including zero churn within the DACH region, while the Net Revenue Retention Rate stabilized at 106%. Average Revenue Per Account reached a record DKK 228 thousand, validating the land-and-expand strategy evidenced by a 12% gross uplift from the existing base. The customer roster surpassed 200 accounts, enhancing diversification as the proportion of ARR derived from the top ten customers decreased to 20% from 37% in 2020. Revenue growth was concentrated in core regions, with net new ARR advancing 22% in Denmark and 26% in the DACH region, offsetting a 6% decline in other markets caused by substantial contract contractions in the UK totaling approximately DKK 1.8 million. The partner channel faced temporary delays in customer decision-making, limiting its contribution to new ARR generation in 2025, though network expansion in Germany positioned the company for future acceleration.

Following a successful private placement raising DKK 15 million in December 2025, the Board announced a strategic decision to defer the objective of achieving recurring positive cash flow before the end of 2026. This move prioritizes growth investments to capitalize on market opportunities, supported by an equity position of DKK 15.4 million and a cash balance of DKK 18.9 million. Looking ahead to 2026, the company anticipates ARR growth of 15% to 23%, projecting ARR between DKK 53 million and DKK 57 million. The Board expects EBITDA to fluctuate between negative DKK 2 million and positive DKK 1 million, reflecting planned increases in commercial staffing and marketing expenditures, which are anticipated to have a delayed impact on revenue due to the length of enterprise sales cycles.
