Impero delivered resilient growth in 2025 despite geopolitical and macroeconomic uncertainty, particularly during the first half of the year. Annual recurring revenue (ARR) increased 19% year over year to DKK 46.2 million, reaching the upper end of the revised guidance range. Revenue rose 19% to DKK 42.0 million from DKK 35.4 million in 2024. The company regained momentum in the second half, generating net new ARR of DKK 3.1 million in the fourth quarter versus DKK 1.8 million in the same quarter of 2024, although full-year net new ARR declined to DKK 7.5 million from DKK 8.3 million.

Profitability improved substantially. EBITDA was negative DKK 3.1 million, compared with negative DKK 8.7 million in 2024, while operating loss improved to DKK 6.7 million from DKK 11.7 million. Management attributed the improvement primarily to 19% revenue growth combined with lower increases in staff costs and other external expenses, which rose 6% each. Cost of sales decreased 16%, increasing the contribution margin to 98% from 97%. The reported net loss improved to DKK 6.8 million from DKK 11.4 million. Capitalized development costs increased to DKK 5.2 million from DKK 3.6 million, reflecting greater investment in new platform features and positively affecting EBITDA.

SaaS metrics remained strong, although some efficiency indicators weakened. Churn fell to 1% from 2%, and there was no churn in the DACH region. Net revenue retention was 106%, down from 108%, while net uplift declined to 7% from 10%. Gross uplift reached DKK 4.5 million, or 12%, supported by the company’s land-and-expand strategy. Average revenue per account increased 8% to DKK 228,000, while customer acquisition cost rose to DKK 371,000 from DKK 222,000 and CAC payback increased to 22 months from 15 months because of higher lead-generation spending and fewer new customers.

Growth was led by Denmark and DACH, where net new ARR grew 22% and 26%, respectively. The customer base exceeded 200 after 26 new customer wins, while top-10 customer concentration fell to 20% from 24%, strengthening revenue diversification. Product development included the July launch of the Control Testing module, initial AI features, and preparations for Entity Management, supporting enterprise expansion and upselling opportunities.

Cash flow also improved: cash flow from ordinary operating activities was negative DKK 1.3 million versus negative DKK 4.3 million in 2024. Cash ended at DKK 18.9 million after a DKK 15 million directed issue in December, with equity rising to DKK 15.4 million. Given the stronger capital position, Impero deferred its ambition of recurring positive cash flow before the end of 2026 to prioritize commercial investments. For 2026, it guides for ARR of DKK 53–57 million and EBITDA between negative DKK 2 million and positive DKK 1 million.
