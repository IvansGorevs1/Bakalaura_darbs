Novaturas Group’s financial performance weakened in 2024. Revenue decreased by 3.6% to EUR 200.9 million, while the number of passengers served fell by 7.6% to 239.5 thousand. Gross profit declined by 20.9% to EUR 20.7 million, reducing the gross margin to 10.3% from 12.6% in 2023. The deterioration was driven by increased competition and oversupply in the Baltic travel market, which intensified price competition, particularly for Turkey and the Greek islands.

Profitability also declined substantially. EBITDA fell from a positive EUR 5.2 million in 2023 to a negative EUR 6.1 million, while adjusted EBITDA, excluding the goodwill impairment, decreased from EUR 5.2 million to a negative EUR 449 thousand. Operating expenses rose to EUR 27.5 million, although expenses excluding the EUR 5.7 million goodwill impairment were EUR 21.8 million, approximately 1% below the prior year. The Group reported an operating loss of EUR 6.6 million, a loss before tax of EUR 7.8 million and a net loss of EUR 7.6 million, compared with a restated net profit of EUR 3.4 million in 2023. Excluding the non-cash goodwill impairment related to the Estonian cash-generating unit, the net loss was EUR 1.9 million. The prior-year figures were restated following corrections for previously overstated prepayments and understated expenses.

Flight packages remained the main source of revenue, but sales declined to EUR 170.7 million from EUR 182.8 million. Other sales, including flight tickets and hotel bookings, increased to EUR 25.4 million from EUR 20.9 million. Management’s optimization of the travel program improved performance toward year-end: adjusted EBITDA was negative EUR 1.6 million in the first half but positive EUR 2.3 million from September through December. Fourth-quarter seat utilization increased to 98.9% from 96.7%, while demand grew for Spain and long-haul destinations.

Cash flow and financial stability remained under pressure. Operating activities used EUR 1.6 million of cash, compared with an inflow of EUR 3.0 million in 2023, and cash and cash equivalents declined to EUR 394 thousand from EUR 3.3 million. Group borrowings increased to EUR 9.3 million, current liabilities exceeded current assets by EUR 12.5 million, and the liquidity ratio fell to 0.51 from 0.65. The Group did not meet certain financing covenants at year-end, although waivers were obtained. Subsequent financing included a EUR 2.5 million SME Bank loan and EUR 2 million of loans from strategic investor Neset Kockar, who acquired 23.2% of the company. Management expects further operational improvements, financing support and synergies with the investor to support a return to profitability, forecasting 2025 EBITDA of EUR 2.0–3.3 million.
