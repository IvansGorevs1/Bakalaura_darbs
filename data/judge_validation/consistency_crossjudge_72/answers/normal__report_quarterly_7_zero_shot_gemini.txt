During the first six months of 2026, AS Silvano Fashion Group generated consolidated revenue of 28,590 thousand euros, representing a slight increase of 0.6% compared to 28,428 thousand euros reported in the first six months of 2025. The company achieved a gross profit of 15,892 thousand euros, which is 5.3% higher than the 15,091 thousand euros recorded in the previous year's corresponding period. This improvement contributed to an increase in the gross margin to 55.6% from 53.1%. Operating profit for the six-month period decreased by 3.5% to 5,751 thousand euros compared to 5,961 thousand euros in the first six months of 2025, with the operating profit margin standing at 20.1%. Consolidated EBITDA was 7,508 thousand euros, reflecting a minor decrease of 0.8% compared to 7,566 thousand euros in the prior year period. Reported consolidated net profit for the six months of 2026 amounted to 4,321 thousand euros, down 31.2% from 6,279 thousand euros in the first six months of 2025. Net profit attributable to equity holders of the parent company was 3,961 thousand euros compared to 5,781 thousand euros in the previous year, resulting in basic earnings per share of 0.11 euros compared to 0.16 euros previously.

The primary drivers of the company’s business performance included divergent trends across its core sales channels and geographic markets. Wholesale revenue decreased by 9.1% to 14,934 thousand euros, accounting for 52.2% of total sales, primarily due to lower wholesale volumes. Conversely, retail revenue experienced a robust increase of 13.6% to reach 13,548 thousand euros, representing 47.4% of total sales. Geographically, Russia remained the largest market with sales of 15,767 thousand euros, down 0.6% in euros and down 7.8% in local currency, accounting for 55.1% of total revenue. Belarus, the second largest market, generated 9,046 thousand euros, representing a 6.4% increase in euros and a 2.7% increase in local currency, and accounted for 31.6% of total sales. Combined, Russia and Belarus generated 86.7% of the group's total revenue. Sales in Ukraine dropped by 58.3% to 15 thousand euros, while Baltics sales decreased by 16.7% to 484 thousand euros, and other markets declined by 5.1% to 3,278 thousand euros. By the end of the reporting period, the group and its franchise partners operated a total of 547 brand stores, which included 135 stores operated directly by the group.

The overall financial position of the company strengthened during the reporting period. Total assets as of June 30, 2026, increased by 5.1% to 98,603 thousand euros compared to 93,847 thousand euros at the end of December 2025. Total current assets rose by 7.2% to 85,377 thousand euros, supported by a significant increase in cash and cash equivalents to 12,443 thousand euros from 6,639 thousand euros, alongside short-term deposits over 3 months amounting to 38,707 thousand euros. Total equity attributable to equity holders of the parent company grew by 6.1% to reach 81,479 thousand euros compared to 76,768 thousand euros at the end of 2025. Operating cash flow for the six-month period showed a substantial improvement, rising by 96.6% to 6,842 thousand euros from 3,481 thousand euros in the first six months of 2025. Investments in property, plant, and equipment were reduced to 115 thousand euros during the reporting period compared to 368 thousand euros in the prior year, directed primarily toward opening and renovating own stores and maintaining production facilities.
