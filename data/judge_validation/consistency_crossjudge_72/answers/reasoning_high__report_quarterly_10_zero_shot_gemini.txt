During the financial year 2025, Aktsiaselts Infortar and its subsidiaries achieved consolidated revenue of EUR 1,837,000 thousand, up from EUR 1,371,775 thousand in 2024, largely reflecting the full twelve-month consolidation of Tallink Grupp following its acquisition in August 2024. Gross profit rose to EUR 240,846 thousand from EUR 128,628 thousand, and EBITDA increased to EUR 232,581 thousand compared to EUR 145,415 thousand, yielding an EBITDA margin of 13.4% versus 10.6% previously. Operating profit reached EUR 111,061 thousand, compared to EUR 77,024 thousand in 2024. Net profit for the financial year decreased to EUR 71,969 thousand from EUR 193,670 thousand, primarily due to one-off effects related to the initial consolidation of Tallink Group recognized in 2024. Profit attributable to the owners of the parent was EUR 72,662 thousand, resulting in ordinary earnings per share of EUR 3.54 compared to EUR 9.36 in the prior year.

For the fourth quarter of 2025, Infortar reported revenue of EUR 417,438 thousand, EBITDA of EUR 42,604 thousand, operating profit of EUR 19,842 thousand, and net profit of EUR 14,141 thousand, recovering from a net loss of EUR 11,988 thousand in the fourth quarter of 2024. Total assets at year-end 2025 stood at EUR 2,587,818 thousand, down from EUR 2,715,234 thousand at the end of 2024. Total equity increased slightly to EUR 1,177,516 thousand from EUR 1,166,221 thousand, improving the equity ratio to 45.5% from 43.0%. Interest-bearing liabilities decreased to EUR 1,060,453 thousand from EUR 1,223,287 thousand due to loan repayments across several group entities. Net debt declined to EUR 840,682 thousand, bringing the net debt to EBITDA ratio to 3.6 compared to 4.0 at the end of 2024. Total operating cash flows surged to EUR 388,632 thousand for the twelve months of 2025 from EUR 89,288 thousand, supported by disciplined inventory management, full-year consolidation adjustments, and stable operational performance. Cash and cash equivalents reached EUR 219,771 thousand.

Segment developments highlighted mixed operational trends. In maritime transport, Tallink Grupp carried 5,531,132 passengers, a 0.9% decrease year-on-year, recording annual revenue of EUR 765.3 million and net profit of EUR 17.3 million. In the energy sector, Elenger Grupp increased its fourth-quarter gas and electricity sales volumes by 7% to 4.8 TWh despite a regional consumption decline. Infortar also continued strategic portfolio diversification into agriculture through Infortar Agro, including the fourth-quarter acquisition of Oisu Biogaas, alongside real estate and infrastructure projects such as the Rail Baltica mainline construction. Overall performance demonstrated increased group resilience and stable cash generation amid an uneven macroeconomic environment.
