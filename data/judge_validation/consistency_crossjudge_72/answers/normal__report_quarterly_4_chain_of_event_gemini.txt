Ambu delivered a solid start to the 2025/26 financial year with continued strong growth momentum. Total revenue for the first quarter reached DKK 1,558m compared to DKK 1,510m in the same period last year. This performance translated into total organic revenue growth of 8.6%, while reported revenue growth was 3.2%, hampered by a currency headwind of 5.4%. 

The primary driver of the company performance was Endoscopy Solutions, which generated 63% of total revenue and achieved strong organic growth of 14.4% driven by rising procedure volumes and the transition to single-use technology. Within this segment, the Urology, ENT, and GI business group posted organic growth of 21.0%, propelled by strong momentum in Urology and the aScope 4 and aScope 5 portfolios, alongside orders that typically fall in the second quarter. The Respiratory business group posted 8.3% organic growth against high comparables from the previous year, supported by aScope 5 Broncho adoption and the launch of the SureSight Connect video laryngoscope. Conversely, the Anesthesia and Patient Monitoring business group recorded flat organic growth of -0.1%, weighed down by an extraordinarily high revenue baseline in the previous year, with Anesthesia declining by 1.2% and Patient Monitoring growing by 1.1%. 

Geographically, revenue growth was supported by solid organic gains in North America and Europe, which posted 9.8% and 9.7% growth, respectively. In contrast, the Rest of World region declined by 1.6% due to order fluctuations and high comparative revenue from last year. 

Profitability for the quarter declined compared to the previous year. Operating profit before special items, or EBIT b.s.i., fell to DKK 164m from DKK 243m, resulting in an EBIT margin b.s.i. of 10.5% compared to 16.1% in the prior-year period. This margin contraction was negatively impacted by more than DKK 50m in tariff costs and foreign exchange headwinds. Adjusting for these external factors, the underlying EBIT margin reached 15.2%, reflecting continued operational leverage. Net profit for the period decreased to DKK 118m from DKK 183m, yielding earnings per share of DKK 0.44 compared to DKK 0.68 previously. 

Cash flow from operating activities was DKK 106m compared to DKK 144m, resulting from lower EBITDA. Free cash flow before acquisitions dropped to DKK 13m from DKK 69m, primarily impacted by elevated net working capital maintained to mitigate geopolitical uncertainties and support growth, alongside foreign exchange headwinds and tariffs. During the quarter, Ambu paid out DKK 90m in net dividends and used DKK 38m to acquire treasury shares under its share buyback program. 

Looking ahead, Ambu maintained its full-year financial guidance for 2025/26, expecting total organic revenue growth of 10% to 13% and an EBIT margin before special items of 12% to 14%, or 14% to 16% excluding anticipated tariff impacts.
