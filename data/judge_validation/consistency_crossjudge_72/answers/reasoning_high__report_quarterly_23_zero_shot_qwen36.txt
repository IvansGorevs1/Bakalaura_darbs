AB KN Energies reported improved financial performance for the nine months ended 30 September 2025, driven by full LNG terminal operations, higher regasification volumes, and positive capacity pricing, despite elevated costs and regulatory adjustments. Group revenue rose 12.8 percent to EUR 76,664 thousand from EUR 67,977 thousand in the same period last year. Gross profit increased 7.9 percent to EUR 31,549 thousand, while operating profit grew 12.4 percent to EUR 21,282 thousand against EUR 18,942 thousand prior year. Profit before tax climbed 8.6 percent to EUR 16,186 thousand, and net profit reached EUR 13,158 thousand, up 10.9 percent from EUR 11,862 thousand. Basic earnings per share improved to EUR 0.035 from EUR 0.031.

The primary catalysts for revenue growth included the LNG terminal returning to full commercial service in 2025 after a one-and-a-half-month dry dock closure in late 2024, alongside higher regasification volumes and client capacity booking bonuses. However, revenue was partially offset by a EUR 2,753 thousand reduction in security supplement income, resulting from the proportional repayment of excess returns generated during the 2022 to 2023 regulatory period, as well as lower collections for unused terminal capacities. Cost of sales expanded 16.5 percent to EUR 45,115 thousand, reflecting higher operational expenses, increased wages, environmental levies, and a significant reclassification of depreciation following the acquisition of a floating storage and regasification unit in December 2024. Expenses previously recorded under right-of-use assets are now recognized in cost of sales, adding approximately EUR 11,369 thousand to the current periods depreciation charges. Operating expenses rose modestly to EUR 10,640 thousand, up 2.1 percent, primarily due to wage inflation and expanded IT and consulting costs.

Financing activities remained stable, with finance costs decreasing slightly to EUR 6,616 thousand from EUR 6,692 thousand, though finance income fell sharply to EUR 1,520 thousand due to lower interest earnings on deposits. The company maintained strong liquidity, ending the period with cash and cash equivalents totaling EUR 54,398 thousand, more than tripling the year-end 2024 balance of EUR 17,203 thousand. Operating cash flow surged to EUR 76,331 thousand from EUR 35,637 thousand, supported by higher profitability, improved working capital management, and the recovery of substantial excise duty collateral. Total borrowings and lease liabilities showed a gradual decline, and the board declared dividends amounting to EUR 8,622 thousand for distribution. Overall, the group demonstrated resilient top-line expansion and robust cash generation, successfully navigating transitional accounting adjustments and regulatory refunds while scaling operational output in its core energy infrastructure segments.
