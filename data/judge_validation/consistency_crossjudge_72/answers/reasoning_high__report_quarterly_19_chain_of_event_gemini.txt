During the twelve months of 2025, AS Pro Kapital Grupp reported a significant financial improvement compared to the previous year, driven primarily by real estate project completions and apartment handovers. Total revenue for 2025 reached 53.2 million euros, representing a substantial increase from 18.2 million euros in 2024. Fourth quarter revenue also rose to 18.9 million euros from 7.6 million euros in the final quarter of 2024. The surge in real estate revenue was primarily the result of handing over completed apartments in the Kalaranna and Uus-Kindrali developments in Tallinn, as well as the City Villas project in Vilnius, whereas apartment handovers in 2024 were much more limited as Kalaranna handovers only commenced in December of that year.

Gross profit for the full year 2025 surged to 18.8 million euros with a 35 percent gross margin, compared to 5.4 million euros and a 30 percent margin in 2024. Operating profit experienced a dramatic increase to 14.7 million euros, up from 0.1 million euros in the previous year. The Group successfully shifted from a net loss of 3.9 million euros in 2024 to a net profit of 12.0 million euros for the twelve months of 2025. Fourth quarter net profit stood at 6.0 million euros, compared to 0.2 million euros in the fourth quarter of 2024. Basic earnings per share improved to 0.22 euros for 2025, recovering from negative 0.06 euros in 2024.

Operating cash flow turned positive, generating 10.8 million euros in 2025, contrasting with cash usage of 10.4 million euros during 2024. Other operating income included a gain of 3.5 million euros from fair value adjustments on investment property, compared to 1.1 million euros in 2024. Conversely, the company recognized a full impairment loss of 0.9 million euros on goodwill associated with the Italian subsidiary Preatoni Nuda Proprietà. Total assets increased to 124.5 million euros as of 31 December 2025, up from 118.8 million euros a year earlier. Total equity rose to 63.3 million euros from 51.2 million euros, and net asset value per share increased to 1.12 euros from 0.89 euros.

During the reporting period, the Group engaged in significant debt and operational activities. On 20 August 2025, the Group partially repaid 9.4 million euros of its senior secured callable fixed rate bonds, reducing the outstanding nominal amount to 10.5 million euros. Total bank loan debt stood at 23.6 million euros at the end of the year, following total bank loan receipts of 31.5 million euros and repayments of 25.7 million euros during the period. Operational milestones during the year included completing construction on the Kalaranna District and the white building at Uus-Kindrali in Tallinn, commencing construction on the Blue Marine project in Riga, and completing the City Villas stage in Vilnius with initial customer handovers in December 2025.
