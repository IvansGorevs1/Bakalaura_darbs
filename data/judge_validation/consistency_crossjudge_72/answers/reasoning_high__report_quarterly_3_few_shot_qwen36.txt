AS Amber Latvijas Balzams reported a pronounced deterioration in its financial performance during the first quarter of 2026. Net turnover declined by 29.6% to EUR 11.1 million compared with the same period last year, while total sales volumes fell by 36.1% to 517,600 nine-liter cases. This contraction in commercial activity was accompanied by a sharp erosion in profitability. Gross profit dropped by 66.5% to EUR 1.2 million, primarily due to unfavorable shifts in product mix and adjustments to pricing methodologies, which overshadowed partial cost-reduction efforts. Consequently, the company recorded an operating loss of EUR 1.6 million, reversing an operating profit of EUR 0.6 million in the prior-year quarter and pushing the operating margin into negative territory at minus 14.5 percent. The net result similarly deteriorated, showing a quarterly loss of EUR 1.4 million against a profit of EUR 0.9 million previously. Management noted that the high comparison base in 2025, which benefited from a contractual revenue adjustment, further amplified the year-over-year decline.

The deterioration in earnings and cash generation was driven by a combination of external shocks and structural challenges. A major catalyst was the initiation of U.S. bankruptcy proceedings under Chapter 11 by the affiliated Stoli Group entity in January 2026, resulting in a complete loss of operational control over what had been the company’s largest customer. This event severely disrupted collections and strained working capital. Compounding these pressures were geopolitical repercussions, including aggressive regulatory actions by Russian authorities that culminated in the confiscation of a key distilling asset, alongside a broader global downturn in the spirits sector characterized by weakening consumer demand and distribution channel inventory corrections. These factors collectively triggered acute liquidity constraints, leading to delayed excise tax payments and the freezing of corporate bank accounts by state revenue authorities.

To preserve business continuity and safeguard creditor interests, the company successfully petitioned the Riga City Court to initiate legal protection proceedings on 5 February 2026. This restructuring framework is currently enabling management to negotiate a formal liability resolution plan with a submission deadline of 5 October 2026, while maintaining essential production activities and fulfilling ongoing tax obligations, having remitted EUR 8.4 million to the state budget during the quarter. The principal shareholder concurrently entered judicial reorganization proceedings in Luxembourg, benefiting from a court-granted moratorium that temporarily suspends debt enforcement until late August 2026. Despite the severe operational and financial headwinds, the company maintains a positive net current asset position exceeding EUR 82.9 million. Management remains focused on implementing production efficiency upgrades, comprehensive cost optimization, and internal governance enhancements to stabilize the balance sheet and restore sustainable profitability once the restructuring processes conclude.
