During the first quarter of 2025, Arco Vara AS reported a significant financial improvement compared to the same period in 2024, driven by a moderate recovery in the real estate market, lower Euribor rates, and stabilizing inflation. Total revenue for the reporting period reached 1,706 thousand euros, representing an increase of 716 thousand euros compared to 990 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2024. Revenue from the sale of own real estate rose to 1,589 thousand euros from 927 thousand euros, while service and franchise revenues contributed 117 thousand euros compared to 63 thousand euros previously. 

The company shifted from a loss to a profit during the quarter. Operating profit, or EBIT, stood at 259 thousand euros, marking a substantial turnaround from the operating loss of 208 thousand euros recorded in the first quarter of 2024. Net profit for the first quarter of 2025 was 103 thousand euros, compared to a net loss of 349 thousand euros in the previous year's comparable period. Basic and diluted earnings per share both improved to 0.01 euros, up from negative 0.03 euros in the first quarter of 2024. 

Regarding operational activity, the group sold four apartments during the first quarter of 2025, compared to three apartments and one commercial space in the first quarter of 2024. Total assets at the end of the reporting period increased to 42,728 thousand euros from 39,568 thousand euros at the end of 2024. Inventories rose to 32,329 thousand euros compared to 29,170 thousand euros at December 31, 2024. Net loans increased by 1,960 thousand euros during the first three months of the year, reaching 15,455 thousand euros as of March 31, 2025. Total equity stood at 19,842 thousand euros, reflecting an equity ratio of 46.4 percent. The weighted average annual interest rate of interest-bearing liabilities decreased by 0.5 percentage points compared to the end of 2024, settling at 8.2 percent as of March 31, 2025. 

Business developments during the quarter featured active progress across several residential projects. In Tallinn, four additional apartments were sold in the Rannakalda development, bringing total sales to 91 out of 113 units, while pre-sales continued for the Soodi 6 project. Arco Vara also acquired a new residential development site on Spordi Street in Tallinn with a building permit for 56 apartments. In Bulgaria, the Botanica Lozen project reached 95 percent readiness with 9 out of 16 houses sold by the end of the quarter. Overall, Arco Vara’s performance in the first quarter of 2025 demonstrated operational stabilization and financial recovery, supported by ongoing construction and pre-sales activities across its home markets.
