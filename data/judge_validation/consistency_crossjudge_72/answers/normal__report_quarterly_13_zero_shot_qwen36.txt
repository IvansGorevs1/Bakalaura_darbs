Liven AS reported a mixed financial performance for the first half of 2026, characterized by strong sales momentum and strategic expansion, offset by a net loss in the second quarter due to one-off costs associated with its initial public offering. The company’s core business activity remained robust, with sales contracts signed doubling to 111 in the first six months compared to 56 in the same period of 2025. This surge in demand was reflected in the weekly sales ratio, which exceeded the long-term normal range of 1.5–2.0%, reaching nearly 3.1% as a four-week average in the second quarter. The company’s market share in Greater Tallinn was recorded at over 8% during this period. Despite the high volume of new contracts, revenue recognition lagged behind sales activity due to the timing of project completions. Revenue for the first half of 2026 totaled EUR 11,439 thousand, an increase from EUR 9,319 thousand in the prior year period. However, the second quarter saw a decline in revenue to EUR 4,503 thousand from EUR 7,388 thousand in Q2 2025, primarily because fewer homes were handed over. Only 12 homes were handed over in the second quarter, compared to 35 in the same period last year, with no new completions occurring during the quarter. Consequently, the company reported a net loss of EUR 618 thousand in the second quarter, reversing a net profit of EUR 974 thousand in Q2 2025. For the six-month period, Liven AS recorded a net loss of EUR 198 thousand, compared to a net profit of EUR 268 thousand in the first half of 2025. The second quarter loss was significantly impacted by EUR 90 thousand in one-off costs related to the public offering of shares, which were not recognized as a reduction of share premium. Additionally, income tax expenses increased to EUR 384 thousand in Q2 due to dividends paid. Operating profit for the first half of the year was EUR 341 thousand, up from EUR 325 thousand in the previous year, while EBITDA decreased to EUR 2,425 thousand from EUR 2,777 thousand in the first half of 2025. The equity ratio improved to 26.7% at the end of June 2026, up from 19.4% in June 2025, strengthened by the net proceeds of EUR 4,687 thousand from the IPO. Total assets grew by EUR 8,505 thousand during the quarter to EUR 100,186 thousand. The company entered the third quarter with a presale portfolio valued at EUR 48.6 million, covering nearly 90% of its 2026 revenue target. Strategic developments included the acquisition of two new properties in Berlin and one in Tallinn after the reporting period, expanding the development portfolio to include over 1,700 homes. The company expects net profit to increase in the second half of the year as construction completions accelerate and revenue recognition catches up with the strong sales pipeline.
