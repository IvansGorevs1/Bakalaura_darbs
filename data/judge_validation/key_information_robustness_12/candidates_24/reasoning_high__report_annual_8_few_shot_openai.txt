For the financial year ended June 30, 2025, SAF Tehnika Group’s performance improved substantially in profitability despite lower revenue. Consolidated revenue decreased by 4% to EUR 25.99 million from EUR 27.09 million, while the parent company’s revenue declined by 6% to EUR 21.92 million. The main pressure came from the Americas, where revenue fell by 14% to EUR 14.17 million, representing 55% of Group revenue. Management attributed the decline largely to the timing of large projects, customer caution over investment decisions, and uncertainty surrounding tariff changes. European revenue increased by 17% to EUR 10.0 million, partly offsetting the decline, while revenue in Asia, Africa, and the Middle East decreased by 5% to EUR 1.8 million.

Profitability strengthened as production costs fell more sharply than revenue. Consolidated cost of goods sold declined to EUR 16.49 million from EUR 19.56 million, raising gross profit to EUR 9.50 million from EUR 7.53 million. The operating loss narrowed to EUR 1.29 million from EUR 3.22 million. Although administrative expenses increased to EUR 3.36 million, lower sales and marketing expenses and reduced production costs supported the improvement. The Group reported a net loss of EUR 643 thousand, compared with a loss of EUR 2.37 million in the prior year. Loss per share improved to EUR 0.216 from EUR 0.798. The parent company’s net loss decreased to EUR 552 thousand from EUR 1.34 million.

The Group continued investing in product development and its microwave transmission, Spectrum Compact, and Aranet IoT product lines. Research and development costs reached EUR 3.43 million, and product development projects received EUR 570 thousand of co-financing. The Group also established a Canadian branch to reduce potential supply-chain and customs risks. US tariffs on microwave equipment imported from the European Union were unchanged, while Aranet products were subject to a 15% tariff. Management reported that market conditions were stabilizing, although it remained cautious about future sales and financial results.

Cash generation was a major positive development. Net operating cash flow increased to EUR 3.87 million from EUR 1.71 million, and year-end cash rose to EUR 4.81 million from EUR 2.21 million. The Group invested EUR 812 thousand in IT infrastructure, production and research equipment, software, licenses, certification, and premises renovation. Inventory increased to EUR 14.0 million, but the slow-moving inventory provision declined by EUR 1.24 million to EUR 3.86 million. Total liabilities increased to EUR 10.33 million, mainly reflecting higher customer advances and contract liabilities, while equity decreased to EUR 15.76 million because of the annual loss. The EUR 4.95 million credit line remained unused. No dividends were declared. The auditors issued an unmodified opinion, stating that the financial statements presented a true and fair view and complied with applicable IFRS requirements.
