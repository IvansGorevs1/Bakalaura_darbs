Lyko Group reported a year of strong top-line growth in 2025, characterized by significant strategic investments that temporarily pressured profitability. The company’s net revenue increased by 11.3% to SEK 3,962.6 million, up from SEK 3,561.7 million in the previous year. This growth was primarily driven by the Nordic segment, which accounts for the vast majority of sales, where revenue grew by 12.0% to SEK 3,699.8 million. In contrast, the Europe segment saw a slight decline of 1.3% to SEK 130.7 million, coinciding with a strategic reorganization that involved closing local offices in Germany and the Netherlands to centralize operations and reduce overhead costs.

Despite the robust revenue expansion, profitability metrics weakened compared to the prior year. Gross profit rose moderately to SEK 1,658.7 million, but the gross profit margin contracted from 44.0% to 41.9%. Operating profit (EBIT) declined significantly to SEK 78.2 million, down from SEK 117.4 million in 2024, resulting in an EBIT margin of 2.0% compared to 3.3% previously. This reduction in operating margin was largely attributable to high investment costs associated with the commissioning of a new automated warehouse in Vansbro, a SEK 500 million project representing the largest investment in the company’s history. The ramp-up of this automation, which increases daily order capacity to approximately 100,000 orders, impacted results during parts of the year but is expected to drive long-term cost efficiency and scalability.

The company’s net result for the year turned negative, recording a loss of SEK 3.0 million compared to a profit of SEK 21.0 million in 2024. Consequently, earnings per share fell to SEK -0.20 from SEK 1.37. Financial expenses remained high at SEK 82.7 million, reflecting the increased debt load used to finance expansion. However, the company generated strong cash flow from operating activities, which more than doubled to SEK 527.5 million from SEK 209.7 million in the prior year, supported by efficient working capital management.

Strategic developments during the period included the launch of the first Fab Store in Täby Centrum, a scalable retail concept designed to enhance the customer experience and serve as a blueprint for future expansion. The company also continued to grow its portfolio of own brands, such as the relaunch of Make Up Store and the introduction of Scandinavian Soap Factory, which contribute to margin stability. Additionally, Lyko welcomed over 350,000 new customers in the fourth quarter alone, demonstrating strong brand momentum and market share gains in a competitive industry. Looking ahead, the company has announced a cost savings program aimed at generating annual savings of approximately SEK 100 million, excluding one-off restructuring costs, to improve future profitability. The Board has proposed no dividend for 2025, prioritizing reinvestment in growth and infrastructure.
