SAF Tehnika reported a challenging financial performance for the 2024/2025 fiscal year, characterized by a decline in revenue and a net loss, although the loss was significantly narrower than in the previous period. The Group’s consolidated net turnover for the twelve months ended June 30, 2025, amounted to EUR 26.11 million, representing a 4% decrease compared to EUR 27.09 million in the prior year. This contraction was primarily driven by a 14% decline in the North and Latin American regions, where revenue fell to EUR 14.28 million. Conversely, the European market demonstrated resilience, with turnover increasing by 17% to EUR 10.04 million, while the Asia, Africa, and Middle East region saw a modest 5% decline.

Profitability metrics reflected the pressure on sales. The Group recorded a net loss of EUR 820 thousand for the full year, a substantial improvement from the EUR 2.37 million loss reported in 2023/2024. However, the fourth quarter of the 2024/2025 fiscal year was particularly weak, with quarterly revenue dropping 13% to EUR 5.18 million and a net loss of EUR 913 thousand, compared to a loss of EUR 498 thousand in the same quarter of the previous year. Earnings before interest, taxes, and depreciation (EBITDA) for the full year turned negative, reflecting the impact of fixed costs against lower sales volumes.

Operating expenses remained relatively stable despite the drop in revenue, with salaries and social expenses increasing by 8% to EUR 12.4 million, partly due to bonus accruals and a slight increase in headcount to 273 employees. The company continued to invest in product development and sales promotion, ensuring that expenses did not exceed budgeted amounts. The Group also faced headwinds from the global shortage of electronic components, although it had accumulated material reserves to maintain delivery times, which have recently improved.

Financial position remained solid despite the operational losses. The Group generated a positive net cash flow of EUR 2.6 million for the year, largely supported by strong cash generation from operations and advances received from customers, which totaled EUR 3.9 million. The cash balance at the end of the period reached EUR 4.8 million, up from EUR 2.2 million in the previous year. Total assets increased to EUR 25.9 million, driven by higher inventory levels and cash reserves, while total owners' equity decreased to EUR 15.6 million due to the accumulated losses. The company maintains a EUR 4.95 million credit line with Luminor Bank, which remained undrawn, providing ample liquidity. Looking forward, the Board aims to stabilize turnover to achieve a positive net result in the long term, while continuing to monitor global trade risks and technological shifts in the telecommunications market.
