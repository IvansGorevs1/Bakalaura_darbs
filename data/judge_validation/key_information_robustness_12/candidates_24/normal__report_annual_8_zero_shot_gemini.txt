During the 2024 to 2025 financial year ending on June 30, 2025, AS SAF Tehnika and its subsidiaries experienced a decrease in net revenue, reporting a consolidated net turnover of 25.99 million euros, which represents a decrease of 1.1 million euros or 4% compared to the previous financial year. The parent company reported a net turnover of 21.92 million euros, reflecting a decrease of 1.33 million euros or 6% compared to the 2023 to 2024 financial year. Despite the decrease in revenue, the group significantly reduced its net loss, closing the reporting period with a consolidated net loss of 643 thousand euros, compared to a net loss of 2.37 million euros in the previous financial year. The parent company reported a net loss of 552 thousand euros, compared to 1.34 million euros in the previous financial year. 

Geographically, the Americas region generated 14.17 million euros, accounting for 55% of the group's total turnover and representing a decrease of 2.3 million euros or 14% compared to the previous year. This decrease was largely influenced by the realization of large-scale projects in future periods and temporary customer cautiousness regarding investment decisions and tariff announcements. Conversely, the European region, including Latvia, increased its turnover by 1.4 million euros or 17%, reaching 10.0 million euros. Revenues in the Asia, Middle East, and Africa regions decreased by 90 thousand euros or 5%, totaling 1.8 million euros. Sales outside Latvia accounted for 97% of the group's total turnover, with products sold across 87 countries. 

The group's performance was affected by global electronics market dynamics, trade tensions, and geopolitical uncertainties, though direct negative impacts on sales volumes remained limited. Import tariffs for microwave equipment imported into the US from the European Union remained unchanged, while Aranet products were subject to a 15% tariff. To mitigate supply chain and customs risks, the group established a branch in Canada during the reporting year. The group also continued investing in product development, IT infrastructure, and research equipment, with total capital investments reaching 812 thousand euros in the acquisition of tangible and intangible assets. Slow-moving inventory reserves decreased by 1.24 million euros over the year as supply chain conditions stabilized. 

The group's cash and cash equivalents balance at the end of the reporting period stood at 4.81 million euros for the group and 2.84 million euros for the parent company, showing an increase of 2.6 million euros and 1.35 million euros respectively compared to the previous year-end. To ensure liquidity, the parent company maintained a credit line agreement with Luminor Bank AS for a total amount of 4.95 million euros, which remained unused at the end of the reporting period. The group's equity-to-liabilities structure reflected total assets of 26.08 million euros and total equity of 15.76 million euros at the end of the reporting period. Management views the current financial loss as transitional, supported by positive operating cash flows and stabilizing market demand across its core product segments.
