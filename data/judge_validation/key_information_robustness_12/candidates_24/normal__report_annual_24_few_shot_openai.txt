Trophy Games delivered a strong financial performance in 2025, driven by rapid scaling of its Transport Series and increased advertising monetisation. Revenue rose 40% to DKK 126.5 million from DKK 90.1 million in 2024. EBITDA increased 26% to DKK 31.1 million, although the EBITDA margin decreased to 25% from 27% as the company expanded marketing investment. Profit before tax more than doubled to DKK 19.0 million, up 109%, while profit for the year increased to DKK 12.5 million from DKK 7.5 million. The EBT margin improved to 15% from 10%, although the 2024 comparison was affected by DKK 4.9 million of non-recurring impairment costs.

Growth was concentrated in the three main Transport titles. Airline Manager revenue increased 28% to DKK 37.8 million, while Truck Manager generated DKK 25.2 million compared with DKK 1.2 million in 2024 as it moved into its scaling phase. Farm Manager, launched in July 2025, contributed DKK 22.0 million during its first six months. Together, these “Big 3” titles generated DKK 85 million, or 67% of group revenue, and DKK 20 million of EBITDA, compared with 35% of revenue and 25% of EBITDA in 2024. This reduced the company’s reliance on older titles. Conversely, revenue from other Transport games declined 46% to DKK 21.0 million as marketing resources were redirected toward the higher-growth titles. Legacy Games revenue was broadly stable at DKK 20.6 million, although EBITDA fell from DKK 12.8 million to DKK 11.0 million.

In-app advertising revenue doubled to DKK 18.5 million and represented 14.6% of total revenue, compared with 10.2% in 2024. The company began a phased advertising optimisation programme in late September, with further implementation planned through the second quarter of 2026. Advertising carries no platform fees, unlike in-app purchases, supporting margins.

Marketing expenditure increased 76% to DKK 40.5 million to support user acquisition and market-share expansion. This investment contributed to a record fourth quarter, when revenue rose 106% to DKK 41.0 million and EBT increased 574% to DKK 10.1 million.

Cash flow from operations was DKK 12.9 million, down from DKK 19.3 million, reflecting working-capital movements, the DKK 4.7 million repayment of previously received tax credits, and higher investment. Year-end cash decreased 9% to DKK 23.0 million, while total equity increased to DKK 66.1 million. The group had no interest-bearing external debt and maintained a 78% solvency ratio. For 2026, management expects revenue of DKK 130–153 million, EBITDA of DKK 28–36 million and EBT of DKK 18–24 million, while continuing to invest in Truck Manager, Farm Manager, Train Manager 2 and Space Manager.
