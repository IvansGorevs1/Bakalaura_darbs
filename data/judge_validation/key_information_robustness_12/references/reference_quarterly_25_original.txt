VILVI Group unaudited consolidated
 financial results for the first three
 months of 2026

 According to preliminary unaudited data, VILVI Group
 consolidated sales revenue for the first three months of 2026
 amounted to MEUR 87.0, an increase of 22.4% compared to the
 same period in 2025 (consolidated sales revenue amounted to
 MEUR 71.1 in the first three months of 2025).
 In Q1 2026, the Group earned a consolidated net profit of MEUR
 5.1, which is 25.7% higher than during the same period in 2025
 (the consolidated net profit for Q1 2025 amounted to MEUR 4.0).
 VILVI Group consists of Vilkyškių pieninė AB, Modest AB, Kelmės
 pieninė AB, Kelmės pienas UAB, Pieno logistika AB, Baltic Dairy
 Board SIA and Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB, which joined
 the Group on 16 January 2026.
