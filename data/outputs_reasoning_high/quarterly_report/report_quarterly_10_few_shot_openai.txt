Infortar Group reported a strong improvement in operating performance in 2025, although net profit declined because the 2024 result included significant one-off effects related to the initial consolidation of Tallink Grupp. Revenue increased 33.9% to EUR 1.837 billion from EUR 1.372 billion, while EBITDA rose 60.0% to EUR 232.6 million. Operating profit increased 44.2% to EUR 111.1 million, and the EBITDA margin improved from 10.6% to 13.4%. Net profit, however, fell to EUR 72.0 million from EUR 193.7 million, with profit attributable to the parent’s owners decreasing to EUR 72.7 million from EUR 191.3 million. The annual comparison is also affected by Tallink being consolidated for the full twelve months in 2025, compared with only the period from August in 2024.

Fourth-quarter performance also strengthened operationally. Revenue declined 6.4% to EUR 417.4 million, but EBITDA increased 52.8% to EUR 42.6 million and the EBITDA margin rose to 10.2% from 6.3%. Operating profit improved from a EUR 6.8 million loss to a EUR 19.8 million profit, while net profit increased from a EUR 12.0 million loss to EUR 14.1 million.

Energy was the largest contributor to annual revenue and EBITDA growth. Its revenue increased to EUR 1.013 billion from EUR 980.8 million and EBITDA rose to EUR 89.3 million from EUR 77.2 million. In the fourth quarter, Elenger’s gas and electricity sales volumes increased 7% to 4.8 TWh despite a 2% decline in regional natural-gas consumption. The group also signed a multiyear agreement covering 12 LNG cargoes and continued investments in battery-storage capacity and bioLNG bunkering. Maritime transport revenue declined annually to EUR 765.3 million from EUR 785.8 million, while EBITDA fell to EUR 124.2 million from EUR 175.2 million. Tallink carried 5.5 million passengers, down 0.9%, and transported fewer cargo units and passenger vehicles. In contrast, its fourth-quarter revenue and EBITDA improved year-on-year, and the Silja Europa charter was extended to January 2027, with a further extension option.

The balance sheet strengthened. Total assets decreased to EUR 2.588 billion, while equity increased to EUR 1.178 billion and the equity ratio improved to 45.5% from 43.0%. Interest-bearing liabilities fell to EUR 1.060 billion, net debt to EUR 840.7 million, and net debt-to-EBITDA to 3.6 from 4.0. Operating cash flow rose to EUR 388.6 million from EUR 89.3 million, and year-end cash increased to EUR 219.8 million. The group paid EUR 76.5 million in dividends and acquired Oisu Biogaas during the quarter.
