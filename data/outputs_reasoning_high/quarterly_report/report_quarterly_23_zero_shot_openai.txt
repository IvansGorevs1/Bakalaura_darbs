AB KN Energies reported stronger financial results for the nine months of 2025. Consolidated revenue increased to EUR 76.664 million from EUR 67.977 million in the comparable period of 2024. Gross profit rose to EUR 31.549 million from EUR 29.237 million, while operating profit increased to EUR 21.282 million from EUR 18.942 million. Profit before tax reached EUR 16.186 million, compared with EUR 14.910 million, and net profit increased to EUR 13.158 million from EUR 11.862 million. Total comprehensive income was EUR 13.215 million, versus EUR 11.615 million, and basic and diluted earnings per share rose from EUR 0.031 to EUR 0.035.

Revenue growth was primarily driven by LNG terminal activities. LNG regasification and related services generated EUR 43.640 million, compared with EUR 31.401 million in 2024. The report attributes the increase to the terminal’s one-and-a-half-month dry-dock outage in 2024, higher LNG regasification volumes and bonuses paid by LNG terminal clients for booked capacity. Liquid energy products transshipment revenue increased to EUR 21.375 million from EUR 20.241 million, while consulting services revenue rose to EUR 7.507 million from EUR 3.961 million. These gains were partly offset by lower revenue from other LNG terminal activities, reflecting reduced payments for unused allocated capacity, and by lower LNG security supplement income due to the repayment of excess returns from regulated activities in 2022–2023.

Cost of sales increased to EUR 45.115 million from EUR 38.740 million, and distribution and administrative expenses rose to EUR 10.640 million from EUR 10.419 million. The cost structure was affected by the acquisition of the floating storage and regasification unit on 6 December 2024: EUR 11.369 million of related depreciation was recorded in depreciation and amortisation in 2025, rather than under right-of-use depreciation as in prior periods. Finance income declined to EUR 1.520 million from EUR 2.644 million, while finance costs were broadly stable at EUR 6.616 million compared with EUR 6.692 million.

Operating cash flow more than doubled to EUR 76.331 million from EUR 35.637 million. The increase included EUR 31.900 million from reclassification of financial assets to cash and cash equivalents. Investing activities used EUR 13.804 million, including EUR 9.751 million of capital expenditure, while financing activities used EUR 25.332 million, including EUR 8.622 million of dividends and EUR 10.451 million of loan repayments. Cash and cash equivalents rose to EUR 54.398 million from EUR 17.203 million at year-end 2024. Consolidated equity increased to EUR 167.721 million, while total borrowings declined to EUR 311.443 million from EUR 320.352 million. Despite the stronger nine-month performance, third-quarter revenue was broadly unchanged and quarterly net profit fell to EUR 4.570 million from EUR 10.403 million.
