Amber Grid AB reported its unaudited condensed interim financial statements for the three-month period ended 31 March 2026, demonstrating a significant improvement in overall financial performance compared to the same period in 2025. Total revenue for the reporting period reached EUR 32,821 thousand, reflecting a substantial increase from EUR 20,763 thousand recorded in the first quarter of 2025. This revenue growth was primarily driven by an increase in revenue from natural gas transmission and related services, which totaled EUR 32,700 thousand compared to EUR 16,297 thousand previously. According to the document, this growth was influenced by a 35 percent increase in the volume of natural gas transmitted and higher transmission prices established for 2026.

Operating expenses excluding depreciation, write-offs, and impairment decreased to EUR 9,570 thousand for the three months ended 31 March 2026, down from EUR 11,867 thousand in the corresponding period of 2025. Purchases of natural gas decreased by 68 percent to EUR 1,477 thousand from EUR 4,622 thousand, a change explicitly linked by the report to a decrease in the demand for balancing gas following the signing of a new transit agreement to Kaliningrad in 2026, which significantly reduced the need for balancing gas. Conversely, wages, salaries, and related expenses increased to EUR 4,367 thousand from EUR 3,659 thousand.

Reflecting higher revenue and lower operating expenses, earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization rose sharply to EUR 23,304 thousand for the period ended 31 March 2026, compared to EUR 8,918 thousand for the first quarter of 2025. Operating profit reached EUR 19,627 thousand, up from EUR 5,185 thousand. Profit before tax was reported at EUR 19,001 thousand compared to EUR 4,987 thousand in the prior-year period. Net profit for the three-month period ended 31 March 2026 surged to EUR 15,763 thousand from EUR 4,239 thousand in the corresponding period of 2025. Basic and diluted earnings per share increased correspondingly to EUR 0.09 from EUR 0.02.

Regarding the financial position, total assets as at 31 March 2026 stood at EUR 324,064 thousand, remaining relatively stable compared to EUR 324,739 thousand as at 31 December 2025. Total equity increased to EUR 182,039 thousand as at 31 March 2026 from EUR 166,276 thousand at the end of December 2025, supported by net profit generated during the period. Total borrowings as at 31 March 2026 amounted to EUR 104,174 thousand compared to EUR 123,666 thousand as at 31 December 2025. During the period, a tripartite loan transfer agreement with the Nordic Investment Bank came into force on 25 February 2026, under which parent company EPSO-G assumed the remaining loan amount of EUR 9,783 thousand, releasing the company from specific financial covenants and resulting in the reclassification of the European Investment Bank loan from current to non-current liabilities. Net debt as at 31 March 2026 was reported at EUR 106,610 thousand. Subsequent to the reporting period, on 17 April 2026, the general meeting of shareholders approved the allocation of EUR 9,989 thousand for dividend payments, representing EUR 0.0560 per share.
