Liven AS reported a mixed financial performance for the second quarter and first six months of 2026, characterized by robust sales activity and market demand recovery alongside lower quarterly revenue and a net loss due to the cyclical timing of home handovers and one-off expenses. During the second quarter of 2026, the company signed 57 sales contracts under the law of obligations, compared with 31 contracts in the same period of the previous year. For the first six months, sales contracts reached 111, nearly doubling from 56 contracts in the first half of 2025. This momentum expanded the presale portfolio, with the company entering the third quarter with a presale volume of 48.6 million euros and 171 contracts under the law of obligations.

Despite strong sales, revenue for the second quarter declined to 4.5 million euros from 7.4 million euros in the second quarter of 2025, primarily because only 12 completed homes were handed over compared with 35 homes a year earlier. Cumulative revenue for the first six months increased to 11.4 million euros from 9.3 million euros in the corresponding period of 2025, supported by 26 total home deliveries. Profitability weakened during the second quarter, resulting in a pre-tax loss of 234 thousand euros and a net loss of 618 thousand euros, compared with a net profit of 974 thousand euros in the second quarter of 2025. For the first six months of 2026, the company posted a pre-tax profit of 186 thousand euros and a net loss of 198 thousand euros, impacted by approximately 90 thousand euros in one-off costs related to a public share offering and 384 thousand euros in income tax expenses on dividends.

Total assets grew to 100.2 million euros as of June 30, 2026, up from 86.5 million euros at the end of 2025, driven by ongoing construction across development projects. Total loan liabilities increased to 57.3 million euros by the end of June 2026, including 16.6 million euros in construction loans, while cash and cash equivalents rose by 1.98 million euros during the second quarter to 10.0 million euros.

A major corporate milestone during the period was the initial public offering of Liven AS shares conducted between April 23 and May 7, 2026, generating net proceeds of 4.7 million euros and admitting the company's shares to the Baltic Main List of the Nasdaq Tallinn Stock Exchange on May 15, 2026. The company also distributed 1.36 million euros in dividends from 2025 profits and expanded its international and domestic development pipeline through property acquisitions in Berlin and Tallinn.

Looking ahead, management expects strong support for the full-year revenue target of 59 million euros, with nearly 90 percent already realized or covered by contracts. Performance in the second half of the year is anticipated to benefit from project completions and home handovers, with the company maintaining its target of achieving a return on equity above 20 percent.
