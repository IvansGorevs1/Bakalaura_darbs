SAF Tehnika Group reported a consolidated net turnover of EUR 26.11 million for the twelve months ended June 30, 2025, reflecting a four percent decline compared to the prior financial year. Fourth quarter turnover stood at EUR 5.18 million, a thirteen percent drop year-over-year. Despite the revenue decrease, the group posted a narrower annual loss of EUR 820 thousand, an improvement from the EUR 2.37 million deficit recorded in the previous period, though fourth quarter losses widened to EUR 913 thousand against EUR 498 thousand previously. Regionally, North and Latin American sales accounted for 55 percent of annual revenue at EUR 14.28 million, representing a fourteen percent contraction. European revenue grew by seventeen percent to EUR 10.04 million, while turnover in Asia, Africa, and the Middle East declined slightly. The reduction in quarterly sales was primarily attributed to project-based revenue recognition patterns, where dispatch and accounting often shifted to subsequent periods due to production timelines and procurement lead times. Operational costs remained within budget, but salary and related expenses increased by eight percent annually. The company continues to invest heavily in product development, particularly in its microwave radio systems, Spectrum Compact analyzers, and Aranet wireless sensor network solutions, alongside expanding sales efforts across existing and emerging markets. Macroeconomic conditions influenced results, notably a persistent global shortage of electronic components that required the accumulation of material reserves to maintain delivery schedules, although fulfillment times have recently improved. Market dynamics in the microwave radio sector remained stable, with no immediate expectations for rapid industry shifts. The management team acknowledged broader geopolitical uncertainties and potential tariff adjustments but confirmed no direct operational disruption from conflicts in Eastern Europe. Financially, the group generated a positive net cash flow of EUR 2.6 million for the year, ending with a cash balance of EUR 4.8 million and customer advances totaling EUR 3.9 million. An unutilized EUR 4.95 million credit facility remains available for liquidity support. The workforce expanded to 273 employees from 265 a year earlier. While profitability pressure persists due to higher labor costs and revenue volatility, the board maintains a cautious outlook, emphasizing cost discipline and product diversification as strategies to stabilize turnover and return to sustained profitability over the long term.
