During the six-month period ended 30 June 2026, AS Tallinna Sadam reported a decline in overall financial performance despite a modest increase in revenue. Revenue for the group rose by 2.4 percent or EUR 1,399 thousand to EUR 59,262 thousand, compared to EUR 57,862 thousand in the same period of 2025. This revenue growth was mainly driven by higher charter fee revenue from an increased number of charter days for the multifunctional icebreaker Botnica, higher revenue from the sale of electricity resulting from higher market prices and a new security of supply fee, and the indexation of ferry service tariffs. However, operating profit decreased by 25.7 percent or EUR 4,708 thousand to EUR 13,578 thousand, and profit for the period dropped by 41.7 percent or EUR 4,287 thousand to EUR 5,999 thousand. Adjusted EBITDA also decreased by 13.5 percent to EUR 25,769 thousand. The decline in profitability was primarily caused by significantly higher operating expenses, which grew by 24.0 percent or EUR 3,907 thousand to EUR 20,184 thousand. Fuel costs rose by 47.6 percent due to higher diesel prices and more severe ice conditions, electricity costs increased by 28.8 percent, and expenses for technical maintenance and repair surged by 80.0 percent. Furthermore, financial results in the comparative period of 2025 benefited positively from one-off items, specifically a EUR 0.9 million profit from the disposal of land for the Rail Baltica project and a EUR 0.9 million insurance indemnity for the icebreaker Botnica, which did not recur in 2026. 

Operating volumes across the group saw general declines, with total cargo volumes falling by 5.0 percent to 6,481 thousand tonnes, largely due to a 33.8 percent decrease in liquid bulk. The total number of passengers decreased by 2.0 percent to 3,586 thousand, and vessel calls dropped by 4.8 percent to 3,376 calls. Investments during the first six months of 2026 totaled EUR 3,529 thousand, marking a 70.6 percent decrease compared to EUR 12,020 thousand in the prior year. Net finance costs decreased by 11.2 percent to EUR 2,279 thousand due to lower interest expenses on reduced debt levels. Total assets at the end of the period stood at EUR 615,034 thousand, equity was EUR 367,677 thousand, and net debt was EUR 145,764 thousand. During the annual general meeting on 28 April 2026, shareholders approved a dividend distribution of EUR 0.073 per share, amounting to EUR 19,199 thousand in total, which resulted in an income tax expense of EUR 5,415 thousand paid in the second quarter.
