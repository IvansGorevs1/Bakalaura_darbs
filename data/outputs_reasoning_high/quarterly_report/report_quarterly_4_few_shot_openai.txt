Ambu reported a solid start to fiscal 2025/26, with revenue growth continuing despite difficult comparables and significant foreign-exchange and tariff headwinds. Q1 revenue increased to DKK 1,558m from DKK 1,510m, representing reported growth of 3.2% and organic growth of 8.6%, compared with 19.5% organic growth in the prior-year quarter. Endoscopy Solutions remained the main growth engine, generating organic growth of 14.4% and accounting for 63% of revenue. Urology, ENT, & GI grew organically by 21.0%, primarily driven by the aScope 4 portfolio and continued momentum for aScope 5 Uretero, while Respiratory grew by 8.3%, supported by bronchoscopy solutions, aScope 5 adoption, and the launch of SureSight video laryngoscope solutions.

Anesthesia & Patient Monitoring delivered flat organic growth of -0.1%, reflecting an exceptionally strong comparable quarter last year. Anesthesia declined by 1.2%, while Patient Monitoring grew by 1.1%. North America and Europe both recorded solid organic growth of 9.8% and 9.7%, respectively. Rest of World declined by 1.6% because of order fluctuations and high prior-year revenue.

Profitability weakened year on year. EBIT before special items fell to DKK 164m from DKK 243m, reducing the margin to 10.5% from 16.1%. The reported margin was affected by tariff costs of more than DKK 50m and foreign-exchange headwinds; excluding these factors, the EBIT margin was 15.2%. Continued investment in commercial infrastructure also increased operating expenses, with the OPEX ratio rising to 50.3% from 45.2%. Gross margin declined modestly to 60.8% from 61.3%. EBITDA before special items decreased to DKK 261m from DKK 331m, while net profit declined to DKK 118m from DKK 183m. Earnings per share fell to DKK 0.44 from DKK 0.68.

Cash generation was weaker, with operating cash flow of DKK 106m compared with DKK 144m and free cash flow before acquisitions of DKK 13m compared with DKK 69m. Elevated net working capital, particularly inventories, was maintained to support growth and manage geopolitical uncertainties, while cash flow was also affected by tariffs and foreign exchange. Ambu ended the quarter with DKK 735m in cash and net interest-bearing debt of negative DKK 178m, indicating a net cash position. The company paid dividends and initiated its DKK 150m share buyback program, purchasing DKK 38m of treasury shares.

Ambu maintained its full-year guidance for 10–13% organic revenue growth, an EBIT margin before special items of 12–14%, and cash conversion above 40%. Growth and margins are expected to be stronger in the second half, with Endoscopy Solutions targeted to grow by approximately 15% and Anesthesia & Patient Monitoring by mid-single digits.
