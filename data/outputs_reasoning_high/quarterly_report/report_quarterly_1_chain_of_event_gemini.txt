During the three-month period ended 31 March 2026, AB KN Energies and its subsidiaries reported a significant improvement in financial performance compared to the same period in 2025. Group revenue from contracts with customers reached 29,823 thousand euros, reflecting an increase from 25,326 thousand euros reported for the first three months of 2025. Similarly, the parent company reported revenue from contracts with customers of 28,681 thousand euros compared to 24,283 thousand euros in the previous year. The growth in revenue was largely driven by higher income from liquefied natural gas terminal regasification services collected directly from users, which rose to 19,162 thousand euros from 14,593 thousand euros, alongside an increase in consulting services sales to 2,749 thousand euros from 1,932 thousand euros.

Cost of sales for the group increased to 16,916 thousand euros from 15,533 thousand euros, influenced by higher taxes on environmental pollution, emission allowances, and increased personnel expenses. Despite higher costs, the group generated a gross profit of 12,907 thousand euros, up from 9,793 thousand euros in the prior year period. Operating profit for the group improved to 9,608 thousand euros compared to 7,141 thousand euros for the first three months of 2025. Net finance costs showed a favorable improvement, decreasing to a net expense of 784 thousand euros from 1,777 thousand euros, primarily supported by a net gain on derivatives of 458 thousand euros and lower interest expenses on borrowings.

Profit before income tax for the group increased to 8,824 thousand euros from 5,364 thousand euros. Net profit for the period attributable to shareholders reached 7,247 thousand euros, compared to 4,426 thousand euros in the prior year period. Basic and diluted earnings per share increased to 0.02 euros from 0.01 euros. The company alone posted a net profit of 6,762 thousand euros, up from 4,016 thousand euros. Cash flows from operating activities experienced substantial growth, reaching 27,917 thousand euros compared to 13,758 thousand euros in the first three months of 2025. Total equity of the group increased to 179,866 thousand euros as of 31 March 2026, compared to 172,592 thousand euros at the end of December 2025.

Regarding subsequent governance developments, the term of office of the board expired on 25 April 2026, and a new board was elected on 30 April 2026, replacing the previous two-tier governance structure with a single board elected for a four-year term.
