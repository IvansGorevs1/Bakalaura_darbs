During the nine months ended 30 September 2025, AB KN Energies and its subsidiaries reported a consolidated net profit of 13,158 thousand euros, reflecting an increase compared to 11,862 thousand euros for the same period in 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share attributable to shareholders rose to 0.035 euros from 0.031 euros in the previous year. Consolidated revenue from contracts with customers grew to 76,664 thousand euros for the first nine months of 2025, up from 67,977 thousand euros during the corresponding period of 2024. The group recorded a gross profit of 31,549 thousand euros, compared to 29,237 thousand euros previously, while operating profit increased to 21,282 thousand euros from 18,942 thousand euros. Profit before tax reached 16,186 thousand euros, up from 14,910 thousand euros.

The growth in revenue and profitability was primarily driven by higher income from liquefied natural gas terminal regasification services collected directly from users, which increased to 43,640 thousand euros from 31,401 thousand euros. According to the report, this increase was influenced by the terminal not performing usual activities for one and a half months in 2024 due to dry-dock repair works, higher liquefied natural gas regasification volumes, and bonuses paid by clients for booked capacity. Sales of liquid energy products transshipment services also increased to 21,375 thousand euros from 20,241 thousand euros, and consulting services sales rose to 7,507 thousand euros from 3,961 thousand euros. Conversely, other sales related to liquefied natural gas terminals activity decreased to 6,383 thousand euros from 11,530 thousand euros, mainly due to fewer payments collected from clients for unused allocated capacities. Additionally, revenue from liquefied natural gas terminal services included in the security supplement decreased due to the reduction of income caused by the proportionally recognized repayment of excess returns from regulated activities during the 2022 to 2023 period.

Cost of sales for the nine months of 2025 rose to 45,115 thousand euros from 38,740 thousand euros in the prior year period. Distribution costs and administrative expenses were 10,640 thousand euros, compared to 10,419 thousand euros. Financial performance was also impacted by finance costs of 6,616 thousand euros against finance income of 1,520 thousand euros.

As of 30 September 2025, total consolidated assets stood at 530,364 thousand euros, down slightly from 535,174 thousand euros as of 31 December 2024. Total equity increased to 167,721 thousand euros from 163,128 thousand euros. Net cash flows from operating activities improved significantly, reaching 76,331 thousand euros for the first nine months of 2025 compared to 35,637 thousand euros for the first nine months of 2024. Dividends declared to shareholders during the period amounted to 8,622 thousand euros, compared to 5,000 thousand euros declared in the comparative period of 2024.
