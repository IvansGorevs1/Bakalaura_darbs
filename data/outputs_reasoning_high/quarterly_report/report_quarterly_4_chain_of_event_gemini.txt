During the first quarter of the 2025/26 financial year, Ambu A/S achieved a solid start with continued strong growth momentum, delivering total revenue of DKK 1,558 million compared to DKK 1,510 million in the previous year. This corresponded to a reported revenue growth of 3.2% and an organic growth of 8.6%. Ambu maintained its financial guidance for the full 2025/26 financial year, continuing to expect organic revenue growth of 10% to 13%, an EBIT margin before special items of 12% to 14%, and a cash conversion of plus 40%.

Growth was primarily driven by the Endoscopy Solutions business group, which generated organic revenue growth of 14.4% and accounted for 63% of total revenue at DKK 986 million. This performance was supported by rising procedure volumes and the adoption of single-use endoscopy, featuring strong contributions from Urology, ENT, and GI at 21.0% organic growth and Respiratory at 8.3% organic growth. Conversely, the Anesthesia and Patient Monitoring business group posted flat organic growth of minus 0.1% and generated DKK 572 million, affected by extraordinarily high comparable revenue in the same quarter last year. Geographically, organic revenue grew by 9.8% in North America and 9.7% in Europe, while the Rest of World declined by 1.6% due to order fluctuations and high comparable baselines.

Operating profit as EBIT before special items was DKK 164 million, down from DKK 243 million in the prior year period. The EBIT margin before special items decreased by 5.6 percentage points to 10.5%, compared to 16.1% previously. This decrease was primarily driven by external factors, including tariff costs exceeding DKK 50 million and foreign exchange headwinds. Adjusting for these external factors, the underlying EBIT margin reached 15.2%, demonstrating continued operational leverage. Gross profit rose to DKK 947 million from DKK 925 million, though the gross margin declined slightly by 0.5 percentage points to 60.8%. Net profit for the period decreased to DKK 118 million from DKK 183 million, resulting in earnings per share of DKK 0.44 compared to DKK 0.68 in the prior year period.

Free cash flow before acquisitions dropped to DKK 13 million from DKK 69 million, negatively impacted by changes in net working capital, which remained elevated at DKK 1,321 million to mitigate global geopolitical uncertainties and support growth, alongside foreign exchange headwinds and tariff costs. During the quarter, Ambu paid out DKK 90 million in dividends and utilized DKK 38 million to acquire treasury shares under its share buyback program. Business highlights included launching the SureSight Mobile portable video laryngoscope solution and extending the Ambu Recircle Program.
