During the six-month period ended 30 June 2026, AS Tallinna Sadam generated revenue of 59.262 million euros, representing an increase of 2.4 percent or 1.399 million euros compared to 57.862 million euros in the same period of 2025. The revenue growth was primarily driven by a higher number of charter days for the multifunctional icebreaker Botnica, increased revenue from the sale of electricity, and higher revenue from ferry services. Despite the top-line growth, the company experienced a decline in profitability due to rising operating expenses, higher depreciation, and the absence of one-off gains recorded in the comparative period, such as profit from the disposal of land for the Rail Baltica project and an insurance indemnity received in 2025. Operating expenses increased by 24.0 percent to 20.184 million euros, largely influenced by higher fuel costs due to severe ice conditions and rising prices, increased technical maintenance and repair expenses, and higher electricity costs resulting from new network tariffs, consumption volumes, and a new security of supply fee. Consequently, operating profit decreased by 25.7 percent to 13.578 million euros compared to 18.286 million euros in the previous year. Adjusted EBITDA fell by 13.5 percent to 25.769 million euros down from 29.805 million euros. Profit for the period declined by 41.7 percent to 5.999 million euros compared to 10.286 million euros, while profit before income tax dropped by 27.3 percent to 11.414 million euros. Basic and diluted earnings per share decreased to 0.02 euros from 0.04 euros. Net finance costs decreased by 11.2 percent to 2.279 million euros due to lower interest expenses on reduced debt levels. Income tax expense remained stable at 5.415 million euros, arising from the annual dividend distribution. Operating volumes across the group showed mixed results. Cargo volumes decreased by 5.0 percent to 6.481 million tonnes, primarily driven by a significant decline in liquid bulk volumes. The total number of passengers fell by 2.0 percent to 3.586 million, and vessel calls decreased by 4.8 percent to 3,376. Investments during the six-month period dropped significantly by 70.6 percent to 3.529 million euros compared to 12.020 million euros in the comparative period, reflecting expenditures on engine replacements, ferry box coolers, and terminal developments. Total assets at the end of the period stood at 615.034 million euros, equity reached 367.677 million euros, and net debt was 145.764 million euros. In April 2026, shareholders approved a dividend distribution of 0.073 per share, totaling 19.2 million euros, matching the prior year's payout. Additionally, the company entered into an unsecured loan agreement with OP Corporate Bank for up to 32 million euros to support general business purposes and investments, and advanced real estate planning for the Terminal A and D quarters in Tallinn. Overall performance was constrained by inflationary cost pressures, higher maintenance outlays, and lower cargo volumes, offsetting the revenue gains achieved in charter fees and electricity sales.
