During the second quarter of 2026, Kvika banki hf. reported a pre-tax profit of ISK 1,545 million, representing a 23.7 percent decrease compared to ISK 2,025 million in the second quarter of 2025. For the first six months of 2026, pre-tax profit from continuing operations increased by 23.0 percent to ISK 3,354 million compared to ISK 2,726 million in the prior-year period. After-tax profit for the second quarter of 2026 stood at ISK 903 million, down from ISK 1,439 million in the second quarter of 2025. For the first six months of 2026, after-tax profit reached ISK 2,277 million compared to ISK 3,525 million in the first six months of 2025, which included profit from discontinued operations.

Net interest income for the second quarter of 2026 was ISK 3,013 million, a 1.7 percent increase from ISK 2,962 million in the second quarter of 2025. For the first six months of 2026, net interest income rose 6.8 percent to ISK 6,279 million from ISK 5,879 million, driven by loan book growth. Net fee and commission income decreased by 26.8 percent in the second quarter to ISK 1,416 million compared to ISK 1,935 million in the prior-year period, reflecting softer capital markets activity. For the first six months of 2026, net fee and commission income fell 16.0 percent to ISK 2,902 million from ISK 3,455 million. Administrative expenses for the second quarter of 2026 decreased by 1.1 percent year-on-year to ISK 2,947 million, despite including ISK 112 million in one-off restructuring costs from efficiency measures initiated in June.

Total assets expanded by 9.5 percent from year-end 2025 to reach ISK 375.7 billion at the end of June 2026. Loans to customers grew by 7.6 percent, or ISK 15.8 billion, from year-end 2025 to stand at ISK 223.3 billion. Total deposits increased by 16.5 percent from year-end 2025, surpassing ISK 200 billion for the first time to reach ISK 201.2 billion at the end of the quarter, supported by retail and public entity deposits. Market funding increased following senior unsecured and additional tier 1 capital issuances completed during the first half of the year.

The bank maintained a strong capital position, with a capital adequacy ratio of 23.0 percent at the end of June 2026, reflecting the distribution of a special dividend. Kvika's total capital requirement decreased to 17.7 percent following the 2026 SREP results. Significant business developments during the period included the ongoing sales process for Straumur greiðslumiðlun hf. and the announcement that CEO Ármann Þorvaldsson will step down by the end of 2026. Efficiency measures implemented in June are projected to reduce the annual cost base by ISK 300 to 400 million, while international funding issuances aim to optimize future borrowing costs.
