Nordic Fibreboard AS reported consolidated net revenue of €1.86 million for the first quarter of 2026, representing a 7.3% increase from €1.74 million in Q1 2025. Revenue growth was primarily supported by Finnish DIY stores, where order volumes rose by €241 thousand to €855 thousand. Despite this top-line expansion, profitability deteriorated significantly. Consolidated EBITDA reached negative €395 thousand with a margin of negative 21%, a sharp decline from negative €85 thousand and negative 5% in the prior year. The operating loss widened to €530 thousand from €217 thousand, resulting in a net loss of €570 thousand compared to €228 thousand previously. Earnings per share reflected these challenges, showing a trailing twelve-month loss of €0.18.

The adverse financial performance was driven by specific operational disruptions and market pressures. A scheduled factory shutdown in early January 2026 halted production during the transition to a new gas boiler house, with calibration and startup taking approximately two months. Cold weather conditions further increased energy consumption. Critically, the US military attack on Iran in late February caused a surge in energy markets, with gas prices nearly doubling. Although prices receded afterward, they remained approximately 50% higher than at the start of the year. These cost pressures contributed to a cost of goods sold of €1.97 million, exceeding revenue and producing a gross loss of €105 thousand.

On the balance sheet, total assets increased to €9.7 million from €8.7 million a year earlier. Total liabilities rose to €5.8 million, while total equity decreased to €3.9 million from €4.5 million at the end of December 2025, reflecting accumulated losses. Borrowings stood at €3.3 million, down from €3.8 million in Q1 2025. The Group employed an average of 59 people during the quarter, compared to 69 in Q1 2025, with labour expenses reducing to €0.41 million. Cash flow improved markedly, with operating activities generating a net inflow of €13 thousand versus a cash outflow of €528 thousand in the previous year. Investment activities consumed €169 thousand, mainly for production assets, while financing activities provided €152 thousand, including a €343 thousand loan received from a related party.

Business developments included the merger of Pärnu Riverside Development OÜ into Nordic Fibreboard AS in June 2025 to simplify the corporate structure. The property management segment generates immaterial revenue following the termination of building leases in August 2024. Progress on the Admirali Quarter residential development continues, with design works completed and building permits issued. In January 2026, the company entered a preliminary sale-purchase agreement for ten properties in Pärnu, with the final transaction expected by June 2026. Management anticipates stable demand and will focus on growing the Nordic market and expanding sales of interior finishing panels. The investment in the new boiler system has been completed to improve energy efficiency and production reliability.
