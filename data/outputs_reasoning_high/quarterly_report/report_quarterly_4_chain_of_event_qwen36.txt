Ambu reported total revenue of DKK 1,558 million for Q1 2025/26, reflecting an organic increase of 8.6 percent and a reported growth of 3.2 percent compared to DKK 1,510 million in the prior year. The deceleration in organic growth stemmed primarily from exceptionally high comparable revenues last year, which suppressed performance in the Anesthesia and Patient Monitoring segment where organic growth was flat at minus 0.1 percent. Conversely, Endoscopy Solutions drove top line expansion with 14.4 percent organic growth, accounting for 63 percent of total revenue. This segment benefited from rising procedure volumes and accelerated single use technology adoption. The Urology, ENT, and GI group recorded 21.0 percent organic growth led by the aScope portfolio, while the Respiratory group contributed 8.3 percent organic growth following the SureSight Mobile video laryngoscope launch.

Profitability contracted during the quarter. Earnings before interest and tax before special items fell to DKK 164 million from DKK 243 million a year earlier, reducing the EBIT margin before special items from 16.1 percent to 10.5 percent. Management explicitly linked this margin decline to tariff costs exceeding DKK 50 million and adverse foreign exchange movements. When excluding these external pressures, the underlying EBIT margin reached 15.2 percent, indicating sustained operational leverage from the endoscopy growth. Gross margin decreased marginally to 60.8 percent due to a comparably high baseline in the prior year.

Cash generation weakened accordingly. Free cash flow before acquisitions dropped to DKK 13 million from DKK 69 million last year. The report attributes this decline mainly to an increase in net working capital, which management deliberately maintained at elevated levels to cushion against global geopolitical uncertainties and support expansion. Net working capital represented 22 percent of quarterly revenue. Foreign exchange headwinds and tariff expenses further reduced cash flow, while operating cash flow totaled DKK 106 million and investing outflows reached DKK 93 million, predominantly funding research and development.

Leadership maintained its full year guidance, forecasting total organic revenue growth between 10 and 13 percent and an EBIT margin before special items of 12 to 14 percent, or 14 to 16 percent excluding roughly two percentage points from tariffs. The company expects growth and profitability to improve in the second half of the fiscal year as ongoing tariff mitigation strategies, including strategic investments in American production facilities, take effect. Shareholder returns continued with DKK 90 million in dividends and DKK 38 million dedicated to treasury share purchases under an active buyback program. These financial outcomes underscore a period of strategic capacity building and market adaptation amid external cost pressures.
