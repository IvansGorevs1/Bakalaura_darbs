Telia Lietuva, AB reported a strong financial and operational performance for the first half of 2026, driven by growth across all primary revenue streams and improved profitability metrics. Total revenue for the six-month period grew by 7.2 per cent to reach EUR 259.1 million, compared with EUR 241.7 million in the same period of 2025. Revenue expansion was supported by an 8.3 per cent increase in mobile services revenue to EUR 100.0 million, a 5.3 per cent increase in fixed services revenue to EUR 112.9 million, and a 9.6 per cent growth in equipment sales to EUR 46.1 million. 

Profitability improved significantly during the reporting period. EBITDA increased by 9.5 per cent to EUR 105.5 million, while adjusted EBITDA excluding non-recurring items rose by 10.2 per cent to EUR 106.9 million, resulting in an adjusted EBITDA margin of 41.3 per cent. Operating profit grew by 15.1 per cent to EUR 62.4 million, and profit before income tax increased by 16.6 per cent to EUR 61.3 million. Despite an increase in Lithuania's corporate income tax rate to 17 per cent from 16 per cent, profit for the period grew by 14.7 per cent to EUR 51.3 million, up from EUR 44.7 million in the first half of 2025. Earnings per share similarly rose by 14.7 per cent to EUR 0.088.

The company maintained robust cash generation capabilities. Net cash generated by operating activities rose by 15.0 per cent to EUR 103.1 million, and free cash flow increased by 12.5 per cent to EUR 64.1 million. Capital investments experienced a sharp increase of 57.9 per cent, totaling EUR 37.5 million compared with EUR 23.7 million a year earlier, driven primarily by outlays for IT systems, infrastructure, and 5G spectrum licenses. 

Significant business developments during the period included the acquisition of two 1,500 MHz frequency blocks for approximately EUR 8 million in a regulatory auction to further develop the 5G network, alongside the extension of the 2,100 MHz frequency band right for EUR 2 million. Additionally, the company commenced construction of a new data center near Vilnius with planned investments of up to EUR 26 million. Operational metrics showed that total mobile service subscriptions grew by 3.3 per cent to 1.73 million, while total personnel headcount decreased by 6.9 per cent to 1,601 employees. 

Reflecting its solid financial position, the company distributed a record-high dividend payout of EUR 81.6 million, or EUR 0.14 per share, in May 2026 for the previous financial year. Total assets stood at EUR 623.2 million as of June 30, 2026, while shareholders equity amounted to EUR 372.0 million. Overall, the first half of 2026 was characterized by solid top-line growth, enhanced operating profitability, increased strategic investments in network infrastructure, and substantial returns to shareholders.
