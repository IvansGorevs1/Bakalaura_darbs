Amber Grid AB reported significant improvements in its financial performance for the three months ended 31 March 2026. Revenue rose 58 percent to EUR 32,821 thousand from EUR 20,763 thousand in the first quarter of 2025. This growth stemmed from a 35 percent increase in transmitted gas volume and higher 2026 transmission tariffs. Transmission revenue reached EUR 32,700 thousand, whereas balancing revenue collapsed by 99 percent to EUR 24 thousand. A new transit agreement signed in 2026 drastically reduced balancing requirements. Natural gas purchases correspondingly fell 68 percent to EUR 1,477 thousand, although costs for technological needs rose 118 percent.

EBITDA expanded to EUR 23,304 thousand from EUR 8,918 thousand. Operating profit grew to EUR 19,627 thousand from EUR 5,185 thousand. Net profit surged to EUR 15,763 thousand, or EUR 0.09 per share, compared to EUR 4,239 thousand and EUR 0.02 per share previously. Income tax expense increased to EUR 3,238 thousand. Other expenses included wages and salaries rising to EUR 4,367 thousand from EUR 3,659 thousand. Finance costs net were EUR 626 thousand versus EUR 408 thousand. Adjusted EBITDA remained stable at EUR 8,285 thousand versus EUR 8,260 thousand last year, despite negative management adjustments for temporary regulatory differences totaling EUR 15,019 thousand.

Total assets were EUR 324,064 thousand. Equity rose to EUR 182,039 thousand, supported by retained earnings of EUR 122,439 thousand. Trade receivables climbed 39 percent to EUR 14,146 thousand due to unpaid realized contract assets. Inventories grew 16 percent to EUR 4,794 thousand reflecting higher gas volumes and prices. Operating cash flow improved to EUR 22,927 thousand from EUR 8,264 thousand. Net debt increased to EUR 106,610 thousand from EUR 76,052 thousand at year-end 2025. Non-current borrowings totaled EUR 97,707 thousand, including EUR 40,098 thousand from financial institutions.

Prior to this period, failure to comply with financial covenants as of December 2025 had required reclassifying long-term borrowings to current liabilities. A February 2026 agreement transferred EUR 9,783 thousand of debt to parent company EPSO-G, enabling reclassification to non-current liabilities and resolving the covenant breaches. An ongoing legal dispute concerning the Gas Pipeline Interconnection between Poland and Lithuania persists, with a EUR 5,815 thousand provision maintained for potential repayment of received guarantee funds. Subsequent to the reporting date, the General Meeting approved dividends of EUR 9,989 thousand, or EUR 0.0560 per share.
