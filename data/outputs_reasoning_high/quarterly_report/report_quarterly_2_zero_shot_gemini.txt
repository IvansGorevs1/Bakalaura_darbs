During the three-month period ended 31 March 2026, Amber Grid AB experienced a significant improvement in its financial performance compared to the same period in 2025. The company reported total revenue of EUR 32,821 thousand, representing a 58 percent increase from EUR 20,763 thousand in the first quarter of 2025. This growth was primarily driven by a 35 percent increase in the volume of natural gas transmitted through the territory of Lithuania and higher natural gas transmission prices set for 2026. Conversely, revenue from system balancing products decreased by 99 percent to EUR 24 thousand compared to EUR 4,403 thousand in the previous period, largely due to a new transit agreement to Kaliningrad signed in 2026 that significantly reduced the need for balancing gas.

Operating expenses excluding depreciation, impairment, and write-offs decreased to EUR 9,570 thousand from EUR 11,867 thousand in the prior year period. Within expenses, purchases of natural gas and related services declined by 68 percent to EUR 1,477 thousand down from EUR 4,622 thousand, driven by lower balancing product costs, whereas wages, salaries, and related expenses increased to EUR 4,367 thousand compared to EUR 3,659 thousand. 

The company achieved a substantial increase in profitability during the reporting period. EBITDA reached EUR 23,304 thousand compared to EUR 8,918 thousand in the first quarter of 2025. Operating profit or EBIT rose to EUR 19,627 thousand from EUR 5,185 thousand. Net profit for the period surged to EUR 15,763 thousand, compared to EUR 4,239 thousand reported in the three months ended 31 March 2025. Consequently, basic and diluted earnings per share increased to EUR 0.09 from EUR 0.02. Income tax expenses for the period totalled EUR 3,238 thousand compared to EUR 748 thousand previously.

As of 31 March 2026, the company’s total assets stood at EUR 324,064 thousand, nearly unchanged from EUR 324,739 thousand as at 31 December 2025. Total equity increased to EUR 182,039 thousand from EUR 166,276 thousand at the end of December 2025, supported by the net profit generated during the first quarter. Total borrowings decreased to EUR 104,174 thousand as at 31 March 2026 from EUR 123,666 thousand at the end of 2025. During the period, a tripartite loan transfer agreement with the Nordic Investment Bank came into effect on 25 February 2026, transferring the remaining loan to parent company EPSO-G and releasing the company from prior financial covenants, which also eliminated cross-default enforcement risks and allowed the reclassification of European Investment Bank borrowings back to non-current liabilities. Net debt stood at EUR 106,610 thousand at the end of March 2026. 

Significant ongoing matters include legal disputes, notably involving claims with construction contractor Alvora UAB over warranty obligations for the GIPL pipeline project, for which the company holds a provision of EUR 5,815 thousand for the potential repayment of guarantee funds received. Subsequent to the reporting period, on 17 April 2026, the general meeting of shareholders approved the distribution of 2025 profits, allocating EUR 9,989 thousand, or EUR 0.0560 per share, for dividend payments.
