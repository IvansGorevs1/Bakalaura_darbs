VILVI Group delivered strong revenue growth in the first three months of 2026, although underlying operating profitability weakened. Consolidated revenue increased 22.4% year on year to EUR 86.97 million, supported by higher milk purchases, expansion of product categories and the consolidation of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB from 1 January 2026. The Group purchased 155,954 tons of basic-indicator milk, 54.2% more than in the same period of 2025.

Revenue growth was concentrated outside the traditional cheese business. Cheese, cheese products and other revenue declined 28.8% to EUR 37.64 million, reducing its share of total revenue from 75% to 43%. Fresh milk products increased 73.4% to EUR 12.75 million, while dry milk products more than doubled, rising 126.9% to EUR 24.66 million. Canned milk contributed EUR 11.92 million for the first time following the acquisition of Marijampolės pieno konservai, which produces canned milk, butter and milk powders.

By market, revenue from Lithuania rose 72.9% to EUR 14.63 million, and sales to other countries increased 46.6% to EUR 29.82 million. Revenue from the European Union was broadly stable at EUR 42.52 million, up 0.6%. As a result, Lithuania’s share of revenue increased to 17%, while the European Union’s share fell to 49% from 59% a year earlier.

EBITDA fell 50.2% to EUR 3.00 million, and the EBITDA margin declined from 8.5% to 3.5%. Cost of sales increased to EUR 81.55 million from EUR 63.61 million, outpacing revenue growth, while distribution and administrative expenses also rose. Despite the lower EBITDA, net profit increased 25.7% to EUR 5.09 million, with earnings per share rising to EUR 0.43 from EUR 0.34 and the net profit margin edging up to 5.8%. Results included EUR 5.5 million of preliminary negative goodwill related to the acquisition; this amount was excluded from EBITDA and was reflected through other operating income. Higher finance costs, which increased to EUR 0.87 million from EUR 0.45 million, partly offset the operating performance.

The acquisition was completed on 16 January 2026, with 100% of Marijampolės pieno konservai acquired and plans announced to integrate the company and expand whey-processing capacity. The Group also began commercial cheese production at its new Baltic Dairy Board factory in Latvia, with planned capacity of 18,000 tons annually and exports to more than 40 countries.

The expansion materially increased the Group’s scale and leverage. Total assets rose to EUR 220.31 million from EUR 188.98 million at year-end 2025, while net financial debt increased 89.7% to EUR 83.43 million. Operating cash flow remained negative at EUR 9.63 million, investing cash flow was negative EUR 8.04 million, and financing activities generated EUR 15.84 million, leaving cash of EUR 0.95 million at 31 March 2026.
