AS Trigon Property Development reported a deterioration in financial performance for the twelve months of 2025 compared with the previous year, primarily due to an absence of real estate sales. The company recorded no gain on sales during 2025, contrasting with the 331,920 euros generated from property sales in 2024. Consequently, gross profit dropped to zero from 220,299 euros in the prior year. The lack of revenue generation combined with increased operating expenses pushed the company into an operating loss of 108,021 euros for 2025, a significant reversal from the operating profit of 161,649 euros reported in 2024. After accounting for financial income of 8,513 euros, the net loss for the twelve-month period reached 99,507 euros, down from a net profit of 167,409 euros in 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share worsened accordingly to -0.02212 euros from 0.03721 euros in the previous year.

Administrative and general expenses increased to 108,021 euros in 2025 compared with 63,650 euros in 2024. This rise was driven largely by personnel expenses of 41,452 euros allocated to management and supervisory board remuneration, which had no comparable expense in the prior year, alongside higher costs for accounting and auditing services. The company's total assets decreased to 1,804,656 euros as of December 31, 2025, from 1,873,680 euros at the end of 2024. Cash reserves declined to 227,911 euros from 370,856 euros, with total cash flows used in operating activities amounting to 142,945 euros, influenced by a negative working capital change from inventories as the company invested 91,262 euros into its Pärnu real estate development project. Consequently, inventories increased to 1,562,500 euros from 1,471,238 euros. Total liabilities rose to 49,389 euros from 18,906 euros, lifting the debt ratio to 2.74 percent from 1.01 percent. Total equity declined to 1,755,267 euros from 1,854,774 euros. Return on assets dropped to -5.51 percent from 8.93 percent, while return on equity fell to -5.67 percent from 9.03 percent.

Regarding business operations, the company continued its core activity of real estate development on its 13.2-hectare property in Pärnu, maintaining its strategy to sell existing land plots while remaining open to rental property development or other opportunities if they arise. The company had no employees during either reporting period. On the stock exchange, the closing share price of AS Trigon Property Development increased slightly to 0.634 euros at the end of 2025 from 0.620 euros at the end of 2024, resulting in a higher market capitalization of 2,852,405 euros compared with 2,789,418 euros in the prior year. Meanwhile, the book value of the share decreased to 0.39 euros from 0.41 euros, and a total of 146,813 shares were traded during the year with a total turnover of 96,814 euros.
