Nordecon AS reported its financial results for the twelve months ended December 31, 2025, highlighting a decrease in revenue and profitability alongside a substantial expansion of its order book. Group revenue for the reporting period declined by 7% year on year to 208,281 thousand euros, down from 223,925 thousand euros in 2024. The contraction was primarily driven by the buildings segment, where revenue decreased by approximately 10% to 168,302 thousand euros, whereas revenue generated by the infrastructure segment grew by 10% to 39,902 thousand euros.

Profitability measures weakened compared with the previous year. Gross profit fell to 13,535 thousand euros from 16,770 thousand euros, with the gross margin declining to 6.5% from 7.5%. Operating profit decreased to 5,651 thousand euros from 8,061 thousand euros, resulting in an operating margin of 2.7% down from 3.6%. EBITDA dropped to 8,250 thousand euros with an EBITDA margin of 4.0%, compared with 11,025 thousand euros and 4.9% previously. Administrative expenses decreased by approximately 14% to 6,814 thousand euros, largely reflecting lower staff costs and reduced provisions for performance-based pay.

Net financial performance was impacted by foreign exchange movements. Net finance costs increased to 2,965 thousand euros from 2,333 thousand euros, driven by a foreign exchange loss of 750 thousand euros resulting from the depreciation of the Ukrainian hryvnia by approximately 12% against the euro on loans provided to Ukrainian subsidiaries. Consequently, the group ended the reporting period with a net profit of 2,545 thousand euros, compared with 5,165 thousand euros in 2024. Net profit attributable to owners of the parent company amounted to 1,439 thousand euros, down from 3,827 thousand euros, yielding basic and diluted earnings per share of 0.05 euros compared to 0.12 euros in the prior year.

Cash flows from operating activities generated a net inflow of 1,204 thousand euros, compared with 1,075 thousand euros in 2024. Investing activities resulted in a net cash outflow of 2,767 thousand euros, influenced by the placement of 2,898 thousand euros into long-term deposits to secure guarantee limits. Financing activities recorded a net cash outflow of 1,194 thousand euros. Cash and cash equivalents stood at 5,266 thousand euros at the end of December 2025, down from 8,195 thousand euros at the close of 2024.

A notable positive development was the growth of the group order book, which surged by 30% to 273,060 thousand euros at December 31, 2025, compared with 209,489 thousand euros a year earlier. This expansion was propelled by new contracts secured totaling 234,994 thousand euros, led by a nearly threefold increase in the infrastructure segment order book driven by major railway and road projects. Management expects this larger order book to support higher business volumes in the coming year.
