During the financial year 2025, AS Pro Kapital Grupp achieved significant financial and operational growth, turning a net loss into a strong and profitable performance across its markets. Total revenue for the twelve months of 2025 reached 53.2 million euros, representing a substantial increase compared to 18.2 million euros in 2024. Fourth quarter revenue also grew to 18.9 million euros from 7.5 million euros in the corresponding period of the previous year. The significant rise in revenue was primarily driven by the handover of completed apartments in the Kalaranna and Uus-Kindrali developments in Tallinn, as well as the City Villas project in Vilnius, whereas apartment handovers in 2024 were largely limited due to project timelines. Gross profit for 2025 rose to 18.8 million euros with a gross profit margin of 35 percent, compared to 5.4 million euros and a 30 percent margin in 2024. Operating profit improved sharply to 14.7 million euros, or 28 percent of revenue, compared to just 123 thousand euros in 2024. The Group recorded a net profit of 12.0 million euros for 2025, recovering from a net loss of 3.9 million euros in the previous year. Fourth quarter net profit stood at 6.0 million euros compared to 187 thousand euros in the final quarter of 2024. Basic earnings per share turned positive at 0.22 euros for the twelve months of 2025, compared to negative 0.06 euros in 2024. Cash generated from operating activities improved to 10.8 million euros in 2025, contrasting with cash used of 10.4 million euros during 2024. Total assets increased to 124.5 million euros as of 31 December 2025, up from 118.8 million euros a year earlier, while total liabilities decreased to 61.2 million euros from 67.5 million euros. Total equity rose to 63.3 million euros from 51.2 million euros, and net asset value per share increased to 1.12 euros from 0.89 euros. The debt to equity ratio improved to 0.97 at the end of 2025 from 1.32 at the end of 2024. Return on assets reached 10.1 percent, and return on equity was 21.0 percent. Several key business events marked the reporting period. In August 2025, the Group partially repaid 9.405 million euros of its senior secured callable fixed rate bonds, reducing the outstanding nominal amount to 10.545 million euros. Unsecured bonds remained at an outstanding nominal amount of 8.2 million euros. Total bank loan debt stood at 23.6 million euros at year end, following 31.5 million euros received and 25.7 million euros repaid during the year. Operationally, the Group commenced construction on the Blue Marine residential building in Riga in July 2025 and completed the Kalaranna district and the white building in the Uus-Kindrali project in Tallinn. Additionally, on 1 September 2025, the Group acquired an additional 10 percent ownership interest in Preatoni Nuda Proprietà in Italy, raising its stake to 77.5 percent, though it recognized a full goodwill impairment loss of 863 thousand euros on this Italian subsidiary during the year. Overall, the company demonstrated solid execution, robust financial recovery, and disciplined capital management throughout 2025.
