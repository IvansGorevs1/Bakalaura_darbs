During the twelve-month period ended 31 December 2025, the Novaturas Group operated in an environment of intense competition and excess travel supply, prioritizing profitability enhancement over volume growth. This strategic focus involved intentionally reducing volumes and optimizing the travel program, which led to a decline in activity volumes but an improvement in operational efficiency and margins. 

The Group served 191 thousand travellers in 2025, representing a 20.3 percent decrease compared to 239.5 thousand travellers in 2024. Revenue from contracts with customers declined by 16.1 percent to EUR 168.48 million, down from EUR 200.88 million in the previous year. Despite lower revenues, gross profit decreased by a smaller margin of 5.3 percent to EUR 19.65 million, compared to EUR 20.74 million in 2024, resulting in a gross profit margin of 11.66 percent, which represents an increase of 1.3 percentage points. 

Operating performance improved significantly during the reporting period. The Group recorded an operating loss of EUR 274 thousand, reflecting a 95.8 percent improvement compared to an operating loss of EUR 6.56 million in 2024. EBITDA turned positive at EUR 420 thousand, compared to negative EUR 6.13 million in 2024, and compared to an adjusted EBITDA of negative EUR 449 thousand in the previous period. Profit before taxes amounted to a loss of EUR 1.16 million, compared to a loss of EUR 7.79 million in 2024, an 85.1 percent improvement. Net loss for the period stood at EUR 1.17 million, compared to EUR 7.60 million in 2024, marking an 84.6 percent improvement. Excluding the one-off, non-cash impairment of goodwill amounting to EUR 5.7 million recorded in 2024, the annual net loss decreased by 39 percent. Earnings per share improved to negative EUR 0.15 from negative EUR 0.97 in 2024. 

Operational efficiency was supported by an increase in the aircraft load factor, which rose by 1.5 percentage points to 97.2 percent compared to 95.7 percent in 2024. Additionally, early sales showed positive trends, with bookings for the 2026 summer season reaching levels 59 percent higher by December 2025 compared to the previous year. Results were partially constrained during the autumn season by market-wide supply increases exerting price pressure and by disruptions at Vilnius Airport related to illegal cross-border activity involving unmanned balloons, which reduced last-minute travel demand. 

As of 31 December 2025, total assets stood at EUR 43.38 million, compared to EUR 39.45 million at the end of 2024. Total equity decreased to EUR 6.86 million from EUR 8.02 million. Total borrowings increased to EUR 12.87 million from EUR 9.25 million at the end of 2024. The Group complied with certain financial covenants as of 31 December 2025 while breaching others. No dividends were announced or paid during the reporting period, and the market capitalization of the company reached EUR 8.82 million at the end of December 2025.
