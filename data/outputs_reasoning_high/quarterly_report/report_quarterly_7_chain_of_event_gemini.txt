During the first six months of 2026, AS Silvano Fashion Group recorded consolidated revenue of 28,590 thousand euros, representing a slight increase of 0.6 percent compared to 28,428 thousand euros in the same period of 2025. Gross profit increased by 5.3 percent to 15,892 thousand euros, driven by a lower cost of sold goods, raising the gross profit margin to 55.6 percent from 53.1 percent. Consolidated operating profit decreased by 3.5 percent to 5,751 thousand euros, resulting in an operating profit margin of 20.1 percent compared to 21.0 percent in the previous year. Consolidated EBITDA decreased by 0.8 percent to 7,508 thousand euros with a margin of 26.3 percent. Net profit for the period dropped significantly by 31.2 percent to 4,321 thousand euros compared to 6,279 thousand euros in the prior year period. Net profit attributable to equity holders of the parent company amounted to 3,961 thousand euros, down 31.5 percent from 5,781 thousand euros, while basic and diluted earnings per share decreased to 0.11 euros from 0.16 euros. Operating cash flow for the period experienced a substantial increase of 96.6 percent, reaching 6,842 thousand euros compared to 3,481 thousand euros in the first six months of 2025. Segment results show that wholesale revenue decreased by 9.1 percent to 14,934 thousand euros, accounting for 52.2 percent of total sales, whereas retail revenue grew by 13.6 percent to 13,548 thousand euros, representing 47.4 percent of total revenue. Geographically, sales in Russia, the largest market, decreased by 0.6 percent to 15,767 thousand euros, representing 55.1 percent of total sales, with local currency sales decreasing by 7.8 percent. Sales in Belarus, the second largest market, increased by 6.4 percent in euros to 9,046 thousand euros, representing 31.6 percent of total sales, with local currency sales increasing by 2.7 percent. Sales in Ukraine decreased by 58.3 percent to 15 thousand euros, and Baltics sales declined by 16.7 percent to 484 thousand euros. As of 30 June 2026, the group's total assets amounted to 98,603 thousand euros, reflecting a 5.1 percent increase compared to 31 December 2025. Total equity attributable to equity holders of the parent company increased by 6.1 percent to 81,479 thousand euros. Cash and cash equivalents rose by 87.4 percent to 12,443 thousand euros, and short-term deposits over three months stood at 38,707 thousand euros, representing a 1.0 percent increase. Investments in property, plant and equipment totaled 115 thousand euros during the six-month period, down from 368 thousand euros in the previous year, directed mainly toward opening and renovating own stores and maintaining production equipment. The group employed 1,594 people at the end of the reporting period, compared to 1,639 employees at the end of 2025, operating a total of 135 own stores and 412 franchise stores.
