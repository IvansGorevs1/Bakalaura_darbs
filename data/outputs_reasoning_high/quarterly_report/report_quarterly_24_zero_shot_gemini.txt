During the twelve-month period ending December 31, 2025, AS Trigon Property Development experienced a decline in financial performance compared to the previous year, primarily driven by the absence of real estate sales. The company reported a net loss of 99,507 euros for 2025, marking a negative shift from the net profit of 167,409 euros recorded in 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share dropped to -0.02212 euros from 0.03721 euros in the prior year. The company generated zero gain on sales for 2025, compared to 331,920 euros in 2024, during which time a 0.09-hectare property and a 0.70-hectare property were sold. Consequently, gross profit fell from 220,299 euros in 2024 to zero in 2025. Operating loss for the twelve-month period of 2025 stood at 108,021 euros, contrasting with an operating profit of 161,649 euros in 2024. Administrative and general expenses increased to 108,021 euros in 2025 from 63,650 euros in 2024, largely due to personnel expenses of 41,452 euros paid as remuneration to members of the management and supervisory boards, whereas no such remuneration was paid in 2024. Financial income improved slightly to 8,513 euros in 2025 from 5,760 euros in 2024. Total cash flows used in operating activities amounted to 142,945 euros for 2025, compared to positive cash flows of 194,723 euros generated in 2024, reducing the cash balance from 370,856 euros at the end of 2024 to 227,911 euros as of December 31, 2025. Total assets decreased to 1,804,656 euros at the end of 2025 from 1,873,680 euros at the end of 2024. Inventories increased to 1,562,500 euros from 1,471,238 euros, reflecting 91,262 euros of investments made into the company's sole property development project, which encompasses a 13.2-hectare area in Pärnu, Estonia. Total liabilities rose to 49,389 euros from 18,906 euros, leading to an increase in the debt ratio from 1.01% to 2.74%. Total equity declined from 1,854,774 euros to 1,755,267 euros. Return on assets worsened to -5.51% from 8.93%, and return on equity dropped to -5.67% from 9.03%. The book value per share decreased from 0.41 euros to 0.39 euros, while the total number of issued shares remained unchanged at 4,499,061. Despite the weaker financial results, the closing share price on the Nasdaq Tallinn stock exchange rose slightly from 0.620 euros at the end of 2024 to 0.634 euros at the end of 2025, resulting in an increased market capitalization of 2,852,405 euros compared to 2,789,418 euros in the prior year. The company's primary business objective remains the sale of land plots within its Pärnu development project to entities establishing commercial, industrial, or logistics activities, with no plans to alter its core business activities.
