AB Pieno Žvaigždės reported a substantial improvement in profitability in the first three months of 2026, although revenue remained broadly stable. Revenue was EUR 52.064 million, down slightly from EUR 52.324 million in the same period of 2025. Sales in Lithuania declined to EUR 31.405 million from EUR 32.112 million, while sales in other EU countries increased to EUR 11.341 million from EUR 10.801 million. By product group, fresh milk sales were almost unchanged, dry milk sales decreased, and cheese and other product sales increased.

The main improvement came from a significant reduction in the cost of sales, which fell to EUR 38.078 million from EUR 42.530 million. As a result, gross profit increased 43% to EUR 13.986 million, compared with EUR 9.794 million a year earlier, and the gross margin improved from approximately 18.7% to 26.9%. Other operating income also increased to EUR 363 thousand from EUR 28 thousand. These developments more than offset the 18% increase in sales and administrative expenses, which reached EUR 10.983 million. Operating profit before finance costs consequently rose to EUR 3.366 million from EUR 535 thousand.

Net finance expenses improved slightly to EUR 298 thousand from EUR 308 thousand. Profit before tax and net profit both reached EUR 3.068 million, compared with EUR 227 thousand in the first quarter of 2025. EBITDA increased to EUR 4.730 million from EUR 2.001 million, while earnings per share rose to EUR 0.07 from EUR 0.01. No corporate income tax was recognized in the income statement for either period.

Cash generation also strengthened. Net cash flow from operating activities turned positive at EUR 1.625 million, compared with an outflow of EUR 183 thousand in the prior-year period. Cash flows from ordinary activities before working-capital changes increased to EUR 4.336 million from EUR 1.367 million. Improved inventory-related cash flow supported the result, although receivables increased and used EUR 2.536 million of cash. Investing outflows declined to EUR 551 thousand from EUR 1.058 million. Financing activities resulted in a EUR 723 thousand outflow, compared with a EUR 4.133 million inflow a year earlier, when the company received EUR 8.5 million in loans. Cash therefore increased from EUR 1.806 million at year-end 2025 to EUR 2.157 million, but remained below EUR 4.902 million at 31 March 2025.

Total assets rose to EUR 85.305 million from EUR 83.475 million at year-end, while equity increased to EUR 41.164 million from EUR 38.238 million, mainly reflecting first-quarter profit. Total liabilities declined to EUR 44.141 million from EUR 45.237 million, and the current ratio improved to 1.51 from 1.27. No material post-balance-sheet events were reported.
