During the twelve-month period ended 31 December 2025, AB Novaturas reported a decline in sales and traveller volumes as the company intentionally prioritized profitability and operational efficiency amid an environment of intense market competition and excess travel supply. The group served 190.9 thousand travellers during the year, representing a 20.3 percent decrease compared with 2024. Revenues from contracts with customers declined by 16.1 percent to EUR 168.483 million, down from EUR 200.878 million in the previous year. 

Despite the reduction in sales volume, the company’s profitability indicators showed substantial improvement. Gross profit decreased by 5.3 percent to EUR 19.650 million, compared with EUR 20.742 million in 2024, but the gross profit margin rose by 1.3 percentage points to 11.66 percent. The company achieved a positive EBITDA of EUR 420 thousand, recovering from a negative EBITDA of EUR 6.132 million recorded in the prior year. Operating results improved significantly, registering an operating loss of EUR 274 thousand, which represents a 95.8 percent reduction compared to the operating loss of EUR 6.556 million in 2024. Consequently, the net loss for the period decreased by 84.6 percent to EUR 1.169 million, compared with a net loss of EUR 7.604 million in 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share improved to negative EUR 0.15 from negative EUR 0.97.

The strategic focus on travel programme optimization and stricter supply discipline helped increase the aircraft load factor by 1.5 percentage points to 97.2 percent. However, financial results in the second half of the year were constrained by market-wide supply increases during the autumn season that pressured prices, as well as disruptions at Vilnius Airport related to illegal cross-border activity involving unmanned balloons that reduced last-minute travel demand. On a positive note, early sales momentum strengthened, with bookings for the 2026 summer season increasing by 59 percent by December 2025 compared to the previous year.

As of 31 December 2025, total assets of the group stood at EUR 43.380 million, compared with EUR 39.450 million at the end of 2024. Total equity decreased to EUR 6.863 million from EUR 8.020 million, influenced by the net loss incurred during the period. Total borrowings increased to EUR 12.867 million from EUR 9.250 million at the end of 2024, and the group noted that it complied with certain financial covenants while breaching others as of the reporting date. Travel guarantees amounted to EUR 21 million, backed by insurance policies, bank guarantees, and EUR 4.5 million in cash deposits. The company neither announced nor paid dividends during the twelve-month reporting period.
