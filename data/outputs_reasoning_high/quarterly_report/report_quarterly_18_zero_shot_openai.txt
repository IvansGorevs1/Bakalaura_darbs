AB Pieno Žvaigždės reported a substantially stronger financial performance in the three months ended 31 March 2026, based on unaudited IFRS interim financial statements. Revenue was €52.064 million, slightly below €52.324 million in the same period of 2025. Sales in Lithuania declined to €31.405 million from €32.112 million, while sales to other EU countries increased to €11.341 million from €10.801 million. By product group, fresh milk product sales remained broadly stable at €42.497 million, cheese sales increased to €5.276 million, and dry milk product sales decreased to €1.397 million.

Despite the small decline in revenue, profitability improved significantly. Cost of sales fell to €38.078 million from €42.530 million, increasing gross profit to €13.986 million from €9.794 million. Other operating income rose to €363 thousand from €28 thousand. Sales and administrative expenses increased to €10.983 million from €9.287 million, but operating profit before finance costs still rose to €3.366 million from €535 thousand. Net finance expenses were €298 thousand, compared with €308 thousand a year earlier. Profit before tax and net profit both reached €3.068 million, compared with €227 thousand in the first quarter of 2025, while earnings per share increased to €0.07 from €0.01. EBITDA was €4.730 million, compared with €2.001 million.

Operating cash flow improved materially. Cash generated from operating activities before interest and tax was €2.405 million, compared with €53 thousand in the prior-year period, and net operating cash flow was positive at €1.625 million versus negative €183 thousand. Investing activities used €551 thousand, mainly reflecting €900 thousand of property, plant and equipment acquisitions, partly offset by proceeds from asset disposals. Financing activities used €723 thousand, compared with €4.133 million generated in the prior-year period, when the company received €8.5 million in loans. Ending cash was €2.157 million, down from €4.902 million at 31 March 2025, but up from €1.806 million at 31 December 2025.

At 31 March 2026, total assets were €85.305 million, compared with €83.475 million at year-end 2025. Equity increased to €41.164 million from €38.238 million, primarily reflecting first-quarter net profit and a €142 thousand adjustment related to a 2025 IFRS 15 timing difference. Total liabilities decreased to €44.141 million from €45.237 million. The current ratio improved to 1.51 from 1.27, and book value per share rose to €0.91 from €0.89. The report identifies no material post-balance-sheet events.
