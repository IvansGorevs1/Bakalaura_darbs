AS Pro Kapital Grupp’s 2025 performance improved substantially compared with 2024. Revenue increased to EUR 53.2 million from EUR 18.2 million, while fourth-quarter revenue rose to EUR 18.9 million from EUR 7.6 million. Gross profit reached EUR 18.8 million, compared with EUR 5.4 million in 2024, and the gross margin increased to 35% from 30%. Operating profit rose to EUR 14.7 million from EUR 0.1 million, lifting the operating margin to 28% from 1%. The Group reported net profit of EUR 12.0 million, reversing a EUR 3.9 million loss in 2024. Fourth-quarter net profit was EUR 6.0 million, compared with EUR 0.2 million a year earlier. Earnings per share improved to EUR 0.22 from a loss of EUR 0.06.

The increase in revenue was primarily driven by the handover of completed apartments in Tallinn’s Kalaranna District and Uus-Kindrali developments and in the City Villas project in Vilnius. Real estate sales revenue rose to EUR 45.4 million from EUR 10.7 million. The report explains that real estate revenue is recognised when legal title is transferred, making results dependent on project completion and handover timing. Estonia generated EUR 39.6 million of revenue and accounted for 75% of total revenue, compared with 27% in 2024. During the year, 104 apartments were sold, compared with 14 in 2024. Kalaranna was completed, with 64% of sellable area sold by year-end, while 39 Uus-Kindrali units had been closed and the remaining presold units were scheduled for handover in early 2026. In Vilnius, initial City Villas units were handed over in December.

Operating performance also benefited from a EUR 3.5 million fair-value gain on investment property, compared with EUR 1.1 million in 2024. Finance costs fell to EUR 2.6 million from EUR 4.3 million, partly because the prior year included a EUR 1.4 million loss from modification of a financial liability. These gains were partly offset by an EUR 863,000 impairment of goodwill related to the Italian Preatoni Nuda Proprietà business, reflecting deferred projected profitability and dependence on additional financing.

Cash generated from operating activities was EUR 10.8 million, compared with EUR 10.4 million used in 2024. Cash at year-end increased to EUR 5.1 million from EUR 4.3 million. Total equity rose to EUR 63.3 million from EUR 51.2 million, while liabilities declined to EUR 61.2 million from EUR 67.5 million; the debt-to-equity ratio improved to 0.97 from 1.32. The Group repaid EUR 9.4 million of secured bonds, reducing their outstanding nominal amount to EUR 10.5 million.

Blue Marine construction began in Riga and secured project financing, while Brivibas Business Quarter was classified for sale because of subdued commercial property conditions. Hotel occupancy declined to 65% from 67%, and Italian operations faced subdued real estate market activity.
