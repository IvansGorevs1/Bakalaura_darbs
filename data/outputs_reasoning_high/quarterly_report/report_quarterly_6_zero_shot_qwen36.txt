For the six months ended 30 June 2026, Aktsiaselts Infortar Group reported consolidated revenue of EUR 945.9 million, a marginal decrease from EUR 951.9 million in the comparable period of 2025. This slight decline was primarily attributable to reduced wholesale energy sales and lower gas consumption across the Finnish-Baltic region, where total regional consumption fell by 7 percent year-on-year. Despite the stagnant top line, the Group delivered a robust profitability recovery. EBITDA advanced 31 percent to EUR 111.2 million, expanding the margin to 11.8 percent from 8.9 percent. Operating profit surged 94 percent to EUR 51.1 million, while net profit attributable to the parent company shareholders swung from a loss of EUR 2.5 million to a positive result of EUR 15.2 million. Basic earnings per share amounted to EUR 0.74.

The improvement stemmed from broad-based operational enhancements and strategic portfolio adjustments. The maritime transport division narrowed its losses substantially, though it faced headwinds including EUR 8.4 million higher fuel expenses and expanded EU Emissions Trading System compliance requirements. Passenger volumes dipped by 2.2 percent, reflecting weaker consumer confidence, while cargo movements rose by 2.9 percent. The energy segment stabilized amid normalized natural gas benchmarks averaging EUR 45.64 per megawatt hour, well below earlier peak levels. A notable highlight was the transformation of the supporting businesses segment, which recorded a EUR 5.6 million EBITDA profit compared to a EUR 4.4 million loss previously, driven by new agricultural integrations and the closure of inefficient operations. 

The consolidated balance sheet demonstrates strengthened financial resilience. Total equity increased to EUR 1,200.5 million, and net debt contracted to EUR 829.9 million from EUR 851.6 million at the end of 2025. Liquidity measures improved markedly, with the current ratio rising to 1.0 and cash reserves growing to EUR 253.2 million. The Group maintained moderate leverage, reflected in a net debt to annualized EBITDA ratio of 3.3. External conditions remain characterized by geopolitical volatility and fluctuating commodity markets, which continue to influence supply chain costs and energy pricing dynamics. Nevertheless, Infortar diversified asset base across maritime, energy infrastructure, and real estate sectors provides structural stability. Management continues to prioritize disciplined capital deployment into long-duration projects while preserving financial flexibility to navigate cyclical pressures and sustain shareholder returns.
