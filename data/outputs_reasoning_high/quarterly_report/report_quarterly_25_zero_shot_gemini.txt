During the first three months of 2026, VILVI Group achieved significant business expansion, highlighted by the completion of the acquisition of 100 percent of the shares of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB on January 16, 2026, and the commencement of commercial cheese production at the new Baltic Dairy Board factory in Bauska, Latvia. These strategic milestones substantially shaped the group operations and financial results during the reporting period.

Consolidated sales revenue for the first three months of 2026 reached 86,970 thousand EUR, representing an increase of 22.4 percent compared to 71,072 thousand EUR in the same period of 2025. Revenue distribution across markets showed European Union sales at 42,518 thousand EUR, accounting for 49 percent of total revenue, sales in other countries at 29,818 thousand EUR or 34 percent, and Lithuania at 14,633 thousand EUR or 17 percent.

Analyzing performance by operating segments, revenue from cheese, cheese products, and other categories amounted to 37,641 thousand EUR, decreasing by 28.8 percent compared to the previous year. Conversely, dry milk products grew by 126.9 percent to 24,659 thousand EUR, fresh milk products increased by 73.4 percent to 12,748 thousand EUR, and canned milk, newly consolidated following the acquisition, contributed 11,923 thousand EUR.

The group purchased 155,954 tons of basic indicators milk during the first quarter of 2026, reflecting a sharp increase of 54.2 percent year-over-year. Despite the top-line revenue expansion, earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization decreased by 50.2 percent to 3,003 thousand EUR, resulting in an EBITDA margin of 3.5 percent compared to 8.5 percent in the prior year period. The EBITDA figure excludes preliminary negative goodwill of 5,542 thousand EUR related to the acquisition of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB.

Net profit for the first three months of 2026 amounted to 5,085 thousand EUR, marking an increase of 25.7 percent compared to 4,045 thousand EUR in the first quarter of 2025. Profit per share rose by 26.5 percent to 0.43 EUR from 0.34 EUR. Net financial debt increased significantly by 89.7 percent to 83,427 thousand EUR, reflecting financing activities associated with corporate expansion and acquisitions. Overall, the reporting period demonstrated strong revenue growth, increased milk procurement volumes, and higher net profit driven by strategic corporate developments, despite compressed core EBITDA margins and higher financial leverage.
