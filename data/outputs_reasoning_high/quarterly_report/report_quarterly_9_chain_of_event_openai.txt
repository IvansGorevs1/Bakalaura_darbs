Hepsor’s 2025 consolidated revenue declined to €35.4 million from €38.4 million in 2024, while net profit fell to €1.0 million from €2.1 million. Net profit attributable to the parent’s owners was €0.4 million, compared with €0.4 million in 2024. EBITDA decreased to €3.5 million from €4.5 million, and operating profit fell to €3.4 million from €4.3 million. Margins also weakened: the gross profit margin declined to 15.9% from 17.6%, the operating margin to 9.5% from 11.3%, and the net profit margin to 2.9% from 5.6%.

The weaker annual performance primarily reflected fewer apartment handovers, at 141 compared with 194 in 2024, lower gross margins on development projects, and higher marketing expenses of €1.3 million versus €0.9 million. Marketing costs increased because the Group had more and larger projects on sale, requiring more intensive sales activity. Finance costs and personnel expenses remained broadly unchanged, while interest income increased by €0.3 million. Other operating income also increased, partly because properties were reclassified from inventories to investment property and revalued.

Fourth-quarter performance deteriorated substantially. Revenue fell to €4.5 million from €10.5 million, gross profit to €0.7 million from €2.5 million, and operating profit to €0.3 million from €2.0 million. The Group recorded a net loss of €0.5 million, compared with a €1.0 million profit in the fourth quarter of 2024. The quarter included €0.8 million of other operating income from the fair-value revaluation of properties reclassified to investment property, but this did not offset lower sales and margins.

Despite lower completed sales, market activity improved. The Group concluded primary sales agreements for 185 homes worth €36.1 million, nearly twice the 109 homes and €19.8 million recorded in 2024. Stabilised interest rates and a positive economic outlook in Estonia and Latvia supported demand, while projects entering construction expanded supply. The development portfolio grew to 33 active projects with approximately 196,650 square metres of sellable area. Homes under construction increased to 428 from 192, a 223% increase, following the launch of projects in Estonia and Latvia.

The Group strengthened its project pipeline and financing during the year. It added six residential projects representing approximately 700 planned apartments and 2,500 square metres of commercial space, and established a joint venture with EfTEN for the Lasnamäe development. In November, Hepsor raised €8.0 million through bonds carrying a 9.50% annual interest rate. Year-end assets were €91.8 million, up 3.4%, while equity increased 23.0% to €27.1 million, partly due to a €5.8 million non-cash contribution from a non-controlling interest. Interest-bearing loans fell to €47.7 million from €54.7 million, although cash decreased to €3.8 million from €6.2 million and annual net cash flow was negative at €2.6 million.
