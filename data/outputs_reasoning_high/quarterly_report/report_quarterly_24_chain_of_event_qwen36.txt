AS Trigon Property Development reported a net loss of 99,507 euros for the twelve months ended December 31, 2025, reversing a net profit of 167,409 euros in the prior year. Performance declined sharply due to zero sales activity, resulting in no gross profit compared to 220,299 euros in 2024 when the company generated 331,920 euros in revenue excluding VAT from land disposals. Administrative and general expenses increased to 108,021 euros from 63,650 euros, a rise of approximately 70 percent. This expense growth was primarily caused by personnel costs of 41,452 euros paid as remuneration to members of the Management Board and Supervisory Board, expenditures that were absent in the previous year, along with elevated auditing and accounting fees. The company recorded an operating loss of 108,021 euros versus an operating profit of 161,649 euros in 2024.

Return on assets fell to negative 5.51 percent from positive 8.93 percent, and return on equity decreased to negative 5.67 percent from 9.03 percent. Total assets declined to 1,804,656 euros from 1,873,680 euros, while total equity reduced to 1,755,267 euros from 1,854,774 euros due to the accumulated loss. Inventory values rose to 1,562,500 euros from 1,471,238 euros, reflecting the addition of 91,262 euros in capitalized investments related to the company's development project in Pärnu, Estonia. Cash balances contracted to 227,911 euros from 370,856 euros. Liabilities grew to 49,389 euros from 18,906 euros, increasing the debt ratio to 2.74 percent from 1.01 percent, though the firm retains a strong solvency position with low leverage. Earnings per share dropped to negative 0.02212 euros from positive 0.03721 euros. The share price closed at 0.634 euros, marginally up from 0.620 euros. The company continues its strategy of selling land plots for its single real estate project and has no plans to alter its business model. The number of shareholders decreased to 1,034 from 1,138, with Pärnu Holdings OÜ maintaining a controlling interest of 39.22 percent.
