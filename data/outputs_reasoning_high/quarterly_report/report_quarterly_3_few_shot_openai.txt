AS Amber Latvijas balzams reported a substantial deterioration in financial performance in the first three months of 2026. Sales volume fell 36.1% to 517,600 nine-liter cases from 810,079 cases in the corresponding period of 2025. Net turnover decreased 29.6% to EUR 11.1 million, primarily because of lower sales volumes and a high comparative base in 2025, when revenue was increased by an adjustment recognized under contractual terms.

Gross profit declined 66.5% to EUR 1.2 million from EUR 3.5 million. Management attributed the decline mainly to changes in product mix and pricing methodology. Although the Company reduced certain costs, including cost of sales, distribution expenses, and administrative expenses, these savings did not offset the effect of lower production and sales volumes. The Company consequently recorded an operating loss of EUR 1.6 million, compared with an operating profit of EUR 0.6 million in the prior-year period, and its operating margin fell to negative 14.5% from 3.7%. Net finance income decreased to EUR 0.2 million from EUR 0.3 million, while the Company reported a net loss of EUR 1.4 million compared with a profit of EUR 0.9 million. Earnings per share declined to negative EUR 0.19 from positive EUR 0.11.

The results were affected by difficult market and geopolitical conditions. The appointment of a trustee for related company Stoli Group (USA), LLC, following its Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceedings caused the Group to lose control of its U.S. operations and directly weakened the solvency of the Company’s largest customer. The report also cites a global downturn in the spirits market, changing consumer habits, pressure on discretionary spending, and inventory adjustments in distribution channels. In addition, Russian authorities designated related entities as extremist and confiscated the Group-owned Talvis distillery.

These developments created significant cash-flow pressure and delayed excise tax payments. After discussions concerning overdue liabilities, the State Revenue Service froze the Company’s bank accounts. Amber Latvijas balzams applied for legal protection proceedings on 30 January 2026, and the Riga City Court approved the application on 5 February. The proceedings are intended to restructure liabilities, including tax debts, and stabilize operations. The action-plan deadline was extended to 5 October 2026. The Company paid EUR 8.4 million in taxes during the period, including EUR 6.4 million in excise tax.

Liquidity weakened during the quarter. Operating cash flow was negative EUR 1.8 million, compared with positive EUR 8.3 million in 2025, and cash and cash equivalents fell to EUR 38,000 from EUR 1.0 million at year-end. Total liabilities increased to EUR 65.3 million from EUR 59.9 million, while equity decreased to EUR 126.0 million from EUR 127.4 million. Subsequent events included judicial reorganization proceedings for the principal shareholder in Luxembourg, with a moratorium through 24 August 2026, highlighting continuing financial and restructuring risks across the Group.
