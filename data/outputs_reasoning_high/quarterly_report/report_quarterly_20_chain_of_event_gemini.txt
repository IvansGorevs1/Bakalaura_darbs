During the financial year ended June 30, 2025, SAF Tehnika reported an unaudited consolidated net turnover of 26.11 million euros, representing a 4 percent decline compared to 27.09 million euros in the previous financial year. For the fourth quarter of the 2024/2025 financial year, net turnover reached 5.18 million euros, marking a 13 percent decrease compared to 5.98 million euros in the corresponding period of the previous year. The document explicitly notes that quarterly turnover fluctuations are influenced by the completion of individual projects varying in scope and complexity, production duration, and material procurement lead times, which often result in revenue recognition occurring in subsequent quarters.

Regionally, twelve-month revenues in North and Latin America decreased by 14 percent to 14.28 million euros, accounting for 55 percent of total turnover. Conversely, European revenues increased by 17 percent to 10.04 million euros, representing 38 percent of total turnover, while revenues from Asia, Africa, and the Middle East declined by 5 percent to 1.78 million euros. For the fourth quarter specifically, turnover in the Americas dropped by 40 percent to 2.5 million euros, whereas European turnover grew by 24 percent to 1.9 million euros, and turnover in Asia, Africa, and the Middle East doubled to 846 thousand euros.

The Group closed the 2024/2025 financial year with a net loss of 820 thousand euros, which represents an improved financial result compared to a net loss of 2.37 million euros reported for the 2023/2024 financial year. However, for the fourth quarter alone, the Group recorded a net loss of 913 thousand euros, widening from a net loss of 498 thousand euros in the same quarter of the previous financial year.

Total assets at the end of the reporting period stood at 25.92 million euros, compared to 23.02 million euros as of June 30, 2024. Total owners equity decreased to 15.58 million euros from 16.44 million euros. The Group generated a net cash flow of 2.6 million euros for the twelve-month period, ending the year with a cash balance of 4.81 million euros. Customer prepayments for goods totaled 3.92 million euros, and the parent company maintained an unutilized credit line facility of 4.95 million euros with Luminor Bank AS to ensure liquidity. Operations were previously affected by global electronic component shortages, prompting the company to accumulate material reserves to fulfill orders and secure short delivery times. The Group continued to invest in new product development, product enhancements, and sales promotion across existing and new market segments, maintaining a workforce of 273 employees at the end of the reporting period compared to 265 employees a year earlier.
