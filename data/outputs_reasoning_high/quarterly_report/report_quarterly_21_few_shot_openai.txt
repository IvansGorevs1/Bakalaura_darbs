AS Tallinna Sadam’s financial performance weakened in the first six months of 2026 despite modest revenue growth. Revenue increased by EUR 1.4 million, or 2.4%, to EUR 59.3 million, supported mainly by higher charter income from the icebreaker Botnica, increased electricity sales and growth in ferry service revenue. Botnica recorded 130 charter days, up from 110, including 20 days of project-based work in the North Sea during June. Revenue rose across the Passenger harbours, Ferry and Other segments, but declined 2.5% in Cargo harbours.

Operating profitability deteriorated as expenses grew substantially faster than revenue. Operating expenses increased by EUR 3.9 million, or 24.0%, to EUR 20.2 million. Fuel costs rose 47.6% to EUR 4.4 million because of higher diesel prices and more severe ice conditions, while electricity costs increased 28.8% to EUR 3.1 million. Technical maintenance and repair costs rose 80.0% to EUR 2.8 million, partly reflecting more extensive repairs to Botnica, ferries and harbour infrastructure, while the comparative period benefited from a EUR 0.9 million insurance indemnity. Depreciation, amortisation and impairment increased 6.0% to EUR 12.9 million, partly due to the commissioning of a new quay and capitalised ferry dry-docking projects. The prior-year comparison was also affected by a EUR 0.9 million profit from the sale of land for the Rail Baltica project.

Operating profit fell by EUR 4.7 million, or 25.7%, to EUR 13.6 million, reducing the operating margin from 31.6% to 22.9%. Adjusted EBITDA declined 13.5% to EUR 25.8 million, with the margin decreasing from 51.5% to 43.5%. The largest EBITDA declines occurred in the Cargo harbours and Ferry segments, where lower cargo activity and higher electricity and fuel costs affected results. Net finance costs decreased 11.2% to EUR 2.3 million, partly mitigating the operating decline. Profit before tax fell 27.3% to EUR 11.4 million, while profit for the period decreased 41.7% to EUR 6.0 million. The unchanged EUR 5.4 million income tax expense reflected the EUR 19.2 million dividend paid during the period.

Operating activity was mixed. Cargo volumes declined 5.0% to 6.5 million tonnes, mainly because liquid bulk fell 33.8%. Passenger numbers decreased 2.0% to 3.6 million, although cruise passengers increased 47.8%. Cash generated from operations declined to EUR 18.2 million from EUR 26.3 million, and net cash flow was negative at EUR 4.4 million after the dividend payment. Investments fell 70.6% to EUR 3.5 million. At period-end, cash was EUR 27.6 million, total borrowings were EUR 173.4 million and equity was EUR 367.7 million. The Group also secured an unsecured loan facility of up to EUR 32 million for general business and investment purposes. Overall, higher costs, weaker cargo and passenger volumes, and the absence of prior-year one-off gains outweighed revenue growth.
