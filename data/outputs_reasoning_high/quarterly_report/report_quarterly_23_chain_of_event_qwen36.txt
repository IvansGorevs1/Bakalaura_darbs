AB KN Energies reported consolidated revenue of EUR 76,664 thousand for the nine months ended 30 September 2025, representing a twelve point eight percent increase compared to EUR 67,977 thousand in the same period of 2024. This top line growth translated to an operating profit of EUR 21,282 thousand, up twelve point four percent from EUR 18,942 thousand, and a net profit of EUR 13,158 thousand, which is ten point nine percent higher than the EUR 11,862 thousand recorded previously. Basic earnings per share rose to EUR 0.035 from EUR 0.031. The Group generated robust operating cash flows of EUR 76,331 thousand, more than doubling the prior year figure of EUR 35,637 thousand, ultimately strengthening its cash and cash equivalents position to EUR 54,398 thousand at quarter end.

The improvement in core profitability was primarily driven by the liquefied natural gas terminal operations, which recorded revenues of EUR 45,568 thousand against EUR 38,426 thousand in 2024. Management explicitly attributes this segment expansion to the absence of the one and a half month dry dock maintenance outage that reduced 2024 output, coupled with higher regasification volumes and additional capacity booking bonuses paid by clients. Liquid energy terminal revenues also saw modest growth to EUR 21,856 thousand from EUR 21,066 thousand. These gains were partially offset by a reduction in security supplement income related to regulated excess return repayments, which decreased other LNG terminal sales.

Cost of sales increased to EUR 45,115 thousand from EUR 38,740 thousand, mainly reflecting an accounting change following the acquisition and subsequent reclassification of the Floating Storage and Regasification Unit from right of use assets to property, plant and equipment in December 2024. This adjustment reclassified approximately EUR 11,369 thousand of annualized depreciation into cost of sales, whereas it was previously recognized under operating expenses. Operating expenses remained relatively stable at EUR 10,640 thousand, demonstrating effective cost management despite a slight rise in average headcount and wage costs. Total borrowings declined marginally to EUR 311,443 thousand, resulting in a minor reduction in finance costs to EUR 6,616 thousand. Total equity grew to EUR 167,721 thousand, supported by retained earnings and the declaration of higher dividends amounting to EUR 8,622 thousand. Overall, the reporting period demonstrates enhanced operational throughput, improved cash generation, and stabilized leverage following infrastructure normalization.
