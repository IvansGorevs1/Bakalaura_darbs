During the first six months of 2026, Aktsiaselts Infortar and its subsidiaries reported consolidated revenue of EUR 945,917 thousand, representing a slight decrease compared to EUR 951,869 thousand in the first six months of 2025. Second-quarter 2026 revenue was EUR 440,921 thousand, down from EUR 504,512 thousand in the prior-year period. Despite lower revenue, the group achieved a substantial improvement in overall profitability. Gross profit for the six-month period increased to EUR 114,132 thousand from EUR 81,736 thousand a year earlier. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization rose by 31 percent to EUR 111,228 thousand for the first six months of 2026, compared to EUR 85,051 thousand in the corresponding period of 2025. Operating profit for the six-month period surged by 94 percent to EUR 51,068 thousand, up from EUR 26,383 thousand. The group reported a net profit of EUR 7,201 thousand for the first six months of 2026, recovering from a net loss of EUR 14,411 thousand in the first six months of 2025. Net profit attributable to the owners of the parent for the six-month period was EUR 15,163 thousand, compared to a net loss of EUR 2,549 thousand a year prior. For the second quarter of 2026 specifically, net profit was EUR 1,883 thousand compared to EUR 150 thousand in the second quarter of 2025. As of 30 June 2026, total assets stood at EUR 2,714,309 thousand, up from EUR 2,596,607 thousand at the end of 2025, while total equity increased to EUR 1,200,533 thousand from EUR 1,175,404 thousand, and interest-bearing liabilities reached EUR 1,083,091 thousand. Segment performances varied across the group operations. The maritime transport segment, operating through Tallink Grupp where Infortar ownership increased to 68.76 percent, recorded second-quarter segment external sales of EUR 204,966 thousand. Tallink carried 1,454,725 passengers in the second quarter, down 2.2 percent year-on-year, and generated a net loss of EUR 2.5 million, unchanged from the second quarter of 2025. Maritime results were impacted by heightened geopolitical tensions in the Middle East, which increased fuel costs by EUR 8.4 million, a higher EU Emissions Trading System surrender requirement of 100 percent, and an income tax on dividends of EUR 12.5 million recorded in the second quarter. The energy segment experienced a decrease in revenue and sales volumes, with Elenger Group gas and electricity sales falling 34 percent to 3.3 TWh due to reduced wholesale sales. A major driver of the group overall profitability improvement was the supporting businesses segment, where earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization turned around from a loss of EUR 4.4 million to a profit of EUR 5.6 million over the first six months, supported by agricultural expansion and the completion of the Halinga biomethane plant. The real estate segment continued stable operations, while the group maintained a strong liquidity position with cash and cash equivalents totaling EUR 253,187 thousand as of 30 June 2026.
