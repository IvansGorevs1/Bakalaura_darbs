Hepsor AS reported consolidated financial results for the twelve months of 2025, reflecting the cyclical nature of real estate development. Consolidated sales revenue for 2025 amounted to 35.4 million euros, representing a decrease compared to 38.4 million euros in 2024. Revenue for the fourth quarter of 2025 stood at 4.5 million euros, down from 10.5 million euros in the fourth quarter of 2024. The Group recorded a net profit of 1.0 million euros for 2025, compared to 2.1 million euros in the previous year. Net profit attributable to the owners of the parent company was 0.4 million euros in 2025, matching the 0.4 million euros reported in 2024. For the fourth quarter of 2025, the Group posted a net loss of 0.5 million euros, which included a net loss attributable to the owners of the parent company of 0.5 million euros, compared to a net profit of 1.0 million euros and a net profit attributable to owners of 0.6 million euros in the fourth quarter of 2024.

The financial results for 2025 were primarily impacted by a decrease in the number of completed apartments handed over to customers, totaling 141 homes compared to 194 homes in 2024. Profitability was further affected by lower profit margins on sold development projects, with the gross profit margin declining to 15.9 percent from 17.6 percent, alongside an increase in marketing expenses to 1.3 million euros from 0.9 million euros due to intensified sales activities across a growing portfolio. Gross profit for 2025 totaled 5.6 million euros compared to 6.8 million euros in 2024, while operating profit reached 3.4 million euros down from 4.3 million euros in the previous year.

Despite lower completed handovers, sales activity showed positive development during the reporting year. The Group concluded primary sales agreements for 185 homes with a total value of 36.1 million euros, nearly doubling the 109 homes worth 19.8 million euros recorded in 2024. Construction volumes also expanded significantly, with 428 new homes under construction as at 31 December 2025, representing a 223 percent increase compared to 192 homes at the end of 2024.

A major corporate event during the period was the successful completion of a public bond offering in November 2025. Driven by strong investor demand, the initial offering volume of 6 million euros was oversubscribed 1.4 times, leading to an increase in the total issue size to 8 million euros at a fixed annual interest rate of 9.50 percent, with the bonds admitted to trading on the Nasdaq Baltic Bond List.

As at 31 December 2025, the Group's total assets stood at 91.8 million euros, marking a 3.4 percent increase from 88.8 million euros at the end of 2024. Total loan liabilities decreased to 47.7 million euros from 54.7 million euros, while total equity grew by 23.0 percent to 27.1 million euros, primarily supported by a non-cash contribution to voluntary reserve capital. In August 2025, the company paid dividends totaling 1.0 million euros, equating to 0.26 euros per share.
