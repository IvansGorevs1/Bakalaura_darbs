Nordic Fibreboard AS increased consolidated revenue in Q1 2026 by 7.3% to €1.861 million, compared with €1.735 million in Q1 2025. The growth was generated almost entirely by fibreboard sales and was supported particularly by Finnish DIY stores, where order volume increased by €241 thousand to €855 thousand. Sales to the European Union rose to €1.781 million from €1.639 million, while sales to Africa declined to €62 thousand from €87 thousand.

Despite the revenue increase, profitability deteriorated substantially. EBITDA declined to a negative €395 thousand from negative €85 thousand, reducing the EBITDA margin to negative 21% from negative 5%. The Group reported a gross loss of €105 thousand, compared with a gross profit of €127 thousand a year earlier, as cost of goods sold increased to €1.966 million from €1.608 million. Electricity, heat and water costs rose to €1.048 million from €725 thousand. The operating loss widened to €530 thousand from €217 thousand, and the net loss increased to €570 thousand from €228 thousand. The net margin consequently declined to negative 31% from negative 13%.

Results were negatively affected by the scheduled factory shutdown in early January during the transition to the new boiler house. Commissioning, calibration and start-up of the new system took approximately two months. Cold weather also increased energy consumption, while gas prices nearly doubled in late February following the US military attack on Iran. Although gas prices subsequently declined, they remained approximately 50% above the beginning-of-year level at the time of the report. Distribution costs increased to €261 thousand from €228 thousand and administrative expenses to €154 thousand from €111 thousand, partly offsetting lower labour costs of €411 thousand versus €471 thousand. Financial costs fell to €41 thousand from €51 thousand, but the prior-year period included €40 thousand of finance income from the sale of Trigon Property Development shares.

Cash flow improved operationally, with net cash generated from operating activities of €13 thousand compared with an outflow of €528 thousand in Q1 2025. However, €169 thousand was invested mainly in production assets, while the prior-year period included a €539 thousand inflow from the sale of financial assets. Financing activities generated €152 thousand, including a €343 thousand related-party loan. Net cash decreased by €4 thousand, leaving cash of €1 thousand at quarter-end.

At 31 March 2026, total assets were €9.694 million and liabilities €5.802 million, while equity fell to €3.892 million from €4.462 million at year-end 2025. Total borrowings increased to €3.320 million from €3.168 million, and the debt-to-equity ratio rose to 60% from 52%. Management expects fibreboard demand and sales volumes to remain stable, while focusing on Nordic market growth, new customers and interior finishing panels. The Group also continued development of the Admirali Quarter and signed a preliminary agreement to sell ten Pärnu properties, with completion expected no later than 30 June 2026.
