AS Merko Ehitus consolidated interim report for the first six months of 2026 shows a decrease in revenue and net profit compared to the same period in 2025, alongside an all-time high secured order book. During the six-month period ending June 30, 2026, the group generated revenue of EUR 149.9 million, representing a 10.7% decrease compared to EUR 167.9 million in the first six months of 2025. Revenue for the second quarter reached EUR 92.7 million, up from EUR 82.6 million in the second quarter of the previous year. Net profit attributable to shareholders for the first six months of 2026 was EUR 12.8 million, down from EUR 21.7 million in the corresponding period of 2025. For the second quarter of 2026, net profit attributable to shareholders stood at EUR 8.5 million, compared to EUR 11.2 million in the second quarter of 2025. The pre-tax profit for the six-month period was EUR 10.8 million, yielding a pre-tax profit margin of 7.2%, down from 14.0% in the first half of 2025.

The group's performance was split across its two business segments. The construction service segment recorded EUR 118.3 million in revenue for the first six months of 2026, a 1.5% decrease from EUR 120.1 million in the same period of 2025, while its operating profit dropped by 65.5% to EUR 3.0 million from EUR 8.8 million, reflecting the expiration of successful contracts and a return to more typical sector margins amid high competition. The real estate development segment experienced a larger decline, with revenue falling 33.9% to EUR 31.6 million from EUR 47.8 million, and revenue from apartment sales dropping to EUR 27.6 million from EUR 44.9 million. The group sold 120 apartments during the first six months of 2026, compared to 222 apartments in the first six months of 2025. Operating profit for the real estate segment fell 40.8% to EUR 7.0 million from EUR 11.9 million.

Despite lower financial results for the half-year, the group achieved an all-time high secured order book of EUR 835.7 million as of June 30, 2026, marking an increase of approximately 88% compared to EUR 443.8 million a year earlier. This expansion was driven by major public sector contracts, particularly in defense-related facilities and infrastructure projects like Rail Baltica. New contracts signed in the first six months of 2026 totaled EUR 486.4 million, compared to EUR 223.1 million in the same period of 2025. Market conditions were characterized by persistent inflationary pressures on construction input prices, labor, and energy, while private sector clients remained cautious. 

As of June 30, 2026, the group held EUR 39.6 million in cash and cash equivalents, total assets of EUR 437.9 million, and equity of EUR 251.1 million, which accounted for 57.4% of total assets. Shareholders approved and paid out dividends totaling EUR 22.1 million, or EUR 1.25 per share, during the reporting period.
