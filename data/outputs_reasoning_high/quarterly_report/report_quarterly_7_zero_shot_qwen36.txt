AS Silvano Fashion Group reported consolidated revenue of 28,590 thousand EUR for the first six months of 2026, representing a 0.6% increase compared to 28,428 thousand EUR in the same period of 2025. Revenue growth was driven primarily by a 13.6% expansion in retail sales to 13,548 thousand EUR, which offset a 9.1% decline in wholesale revenue to 14,934 thousand EUR. The largest market, Russia, contributed 55.1% of total sales at 15,767 thousand EUR, while Belarus accounted for 31.6% with sales reaching 9,046 thousand EUR. Sales in Belarus increased by 6.4%, supported by a 2.7% rise in local currency volume, whereas Russian local currency sales decreased by 7.8%. Sales in Ukraine fell 58.3% to 15 thousand EUR, and the Baltics declined 16.7% to 484 thousand EUR.

Gross profit improved to 15,892 thousand EUR, up 5.3% from the prior year, resulting in an expanded gross margin of 55.6% compared to 53.1%. However, operating profit declined by 3.5% to 5,751 thousand EUR as increased distribution and administrative expenses outpaced revenue gains, pushing the operating margin down to 20.1% from 21.0%. Consolidated EBITDA fell slightly by 0.8% to 7,508 thousand EUR. Net profit attributable to equity holders of the Parent company dropped significantly by 31.5% to 3,961 thousand EUR, yielding earnings per share of 0.11 EUR against 0.16 EUR previously. The decline was influenced by higher income tax expenses and a reduction in net finance income to 444 thousand EUR. Despite the lower bottom line, operating cash flow strengthened substantially, rising 96.6% to 6,842 thousand EUR.

The company maintained a robust financial position with total assets increasing by 5.1% to 98,603 thousand EUR. Cash and cash equivalents grew by 87.4% to 12,443 thousand EUR, and equity attributable to parent shareholders rose by 6.1% to 81,479 thousand EUR. Working capital shifts included a decrease in the inventory balance by 1,535 thousand EUR to 29,702 thousand EUR, contrasting with an increase in trade and other receivables by 846 thousand EUR. Capital investments totaled 115 thousand EUR, directed mainly toward opening and renovating own stores. The Group operated 135 owned stores as of June 30, with 67 in Russia and 65 in Belarus, while total employment stood at 1,594 employees. Return on assets declined to 7.8% from 10.0%, and return on equity decreased to 9.5% from 12.5%. Shareholders approved the 2025 results, leaving retained earnings undistributed, and appointed Treetiger OÜ as auditor. Management emphasized a strategy to improve retail profitability in Russia amidst currency challenges.
