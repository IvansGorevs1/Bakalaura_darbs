Telia Lietuva delivered strong financial growth in the first half of 2026. Revenue increased 7.2% year on year to EUR 259.1 million, driven by growth across the main revenue categories. Fixed-services revenue rose 5.3% to EUR 112.9 million, mobile-services revenue increased 8.3% to EUR 100.0 million, and equipment sales grew 9.6% to EUR 46.1 million. IT services were a particularly strong contributor, increasing 23.9% to EUR 19.8 million. In the second quarter, revenue grew 6.9% to EUR 130.3 million.

Profitability improved faster than revenue. Adjusted EBITDA, excluding non-recurring items, increased 10.2% to EUR 106.9 million, with the margin rising to 41.3% from 40.1%. Reported EBITDA grew 9.5% to EUR 105.5 million and its margin improved to 40.7% from 39.9%. Operating profit increased 15.1% to EUR 62.4 million, while profit before tax rose 16.6% to EUR 61.3 million. Profit for the period was EUR 51.3 million, up 14.7% from EUR 44.7 million, and earnings per share increased to EUR 0.088 from EUR 0.077. Second-quarter profit rose 20.6% to EUR 26.7 million.

Cost of goods and services increased 2.3%, reflecting higher equipment sales volumes and increased network interconnection and roaming costs. Operating expenses excluding non-recurring items rose 10.2%. Energy expenses increased 14.3%, external IT-service costs rose 18.4%, and expenses for common Group functions also increased. Non-recurring expenses reached EUR 1.4 million, mainly redundancy costs related to restructuring the Digital & Technology unit. Headcount decreased by 86 to 1,601, while full-time employees declined by 93 to 1,524.

Cash generation strengthened: operating cash flow increased 15.0% to EUR 103.1 million and free cash flow rose 12.5% to EUR 64.1 million. Capital investments increased 57.9% to EUR 37.5 million, including EUR 9.7 million for 5G spectrum licenses, EUR 6.1 million for the mobile network and EUR 14.2 million for IT systems and infrastructure. In June, the company acquired 40 MHz in the 1,500 MHz band for almost EUR 8 million and extended its 2,100 MHz rights for EUR 2 million. These investments support rising mobile data traffic, which the company reports is growing by about 25% annually, as well as planned mobile-network expansion and a new data center investment of up to EUR 26 million.

The company paid EUR 81.6 million in dividends for 2025. Despite this distribution, it had no outstanding loans and reported negative net debt of EUR 4.3 million at June-end, compared with positive net debt of EUR 27.5 million a year earlier. Overall, the first half showed sustained revenue growth, improving margins, stronger profitability and cash generation, alongside significantly higher investment.
