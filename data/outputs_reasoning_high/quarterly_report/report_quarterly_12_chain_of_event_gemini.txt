AS LHV Group reported a consolidated net profit of EUR 24.7 million for the second quarter of 2026, up from EUR 19.7 million in the first quarter of 2026, but down from EUR 30.8 million in Q2 2025. Net profit attributable to owners of the parent was EUR 24.1 million, compared to EUR 19.8 million in Q1 2026 and EUR 30.0 million in Q2 2025. For the first six months of 2026, net profit totaled EUR 44.4 million, compared to EUR 59.9 million in the first half of 2025.

Total net operating income reached EUR 79.0 million in Q2 2026, representing a 7 percent increase over both Q1 2026 and Q2 2025. Net interest income grew 5 percent quarter-over-quarter and 8 percent year-over-year to EUR 62.5 million. Net fee and commission income remained stable at EUR 14.6 million. Operating expenses decreased to EUR 43.6 million from EUR 45.6 million in Q1 2026, though they increased from EUR 40.5 million in Q2 2025. Loan and bond provisions rose to EUR 5.8 million, up from EUR 1.3 million in Q1 2026, due to model-based forward-looking impairment charges and specific client cases despite strong underlying portfolio quality. Income tax expenses were EUR 5.0 million.

Return on equity attributable to shareholders was 13.1 percent in Q2 2026, higher than 10.7 percent in Q1 2026 but lower than 17.4 percent in Q2 2025. Capital adequacy stood at 20.8 percent, compared to 21.7 percent in the previous quarter. Total assets were EUR 9,652.0 million at the end of June 2026. The Group’s loan portfolio grew 3 percent over the quarter and 15 percent year-over-year to EUR 5,768 million. Customer deposits decreased by EUR 245 million during the quarter to EUR 7,557 million, driven by lower financial intermediary and platform deposits, whereas ordinary client deposits increased.

Among business units, AS LHV Pank reported a net profit of EUR 24.3 million, LHV Bank Limited earned EUR 1.4 million, AS LHV Varahaldus posted EUR 0.4 million, and AS LHV Kindlustus generated EUR 0.8 million, while LHV Paytech recorded a net loss of EUR 0.3 million. Key operational developments included AS LHV Pank successfully migrating its core systems to the Amazon Web Services cloud environment, adopting a product-based management model, securing a MiCA crypto-assets service provider license, and initiating preparations for a name change to LHV Bank AS along with cross-border EEA license registration. The broader economic environment was marked by persistent geopolitical tensions, volatile energy prices, and rising Euribor rates resulting from central bank monetary tightening. Management noted that while expenses and loan provisions were higher than planned, net interest income exceeded expectations due to favorable market interest rates.
