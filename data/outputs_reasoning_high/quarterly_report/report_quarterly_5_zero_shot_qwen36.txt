Arco Vara AS delivered a clear financial turnaround in the first quarter of 2025, transitioning from losses to profitability amid a cautiously recovering Baltic real estate market. Group sales revenue reached 1,706 thousand euros, an increase of 716 thousand euros compared to 990 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2024. This top-line growth stemmed primarily from higher domestic development sales, supplemented by franchise fees totaling 65 thousand euros. Supported by stronger margins and controlled overheads, operating profit shifted to a 259 thousand euro gain, reversing a 208 thousand euro operating loss in the prior year period. Net profit likewise improved to 103 thousand euros from a 349 thousand euro loss, lifting basic and diluted earnings per share to 0.01 euros from minus 0.03 euros. 

Despite improved accounting profitability, operational cash consumption rose to 2,838 thousand euros from 2,217 thousand euros, reflecting active investment in ongoing construction and land acquisition. Net financing inflows partially offset this pressure, resulting in a net cash outflow of 607 thousand euros and closing cash reserves at 1,113 thousand euros. To fund pipeline expansion, the groups net loan position grew by 1,960 thousand euros up to 15,455 thousand euros as of March 31, 2025, bringing total interest-bearing debt to 16,568 thousand euros. Importantly, the weighted average annual borrowing cost declined by 0.5 percentage points to 8.2 percent, aligning with broader declines in the Euribor. Total assets expanded to 42,728 thousand euros, with shareholders equity settling at 19,842 thousand euros and maintaining a healthy equity ratio of 46.4 percent.

Commercially, Arco Vara executed targeted sales and advanced its multi-year project roadmap. Four residential units changed hands in the quarter, while pre-sale commitments strengthened across key developments. The Rannakalda portfolio saw cumulative deliveries reach 91 of 113 planned apartments, and the Botanica Lozen estate in Sofia approached 95 percent physical completion with nine of sixteen homes pre-sold. Strategic land acquisitions included a new buildable plot at Spordi Street in Tallinn, earmarked for 56 apartments with construction launching late in the second quarter and full delivery projected for 2026. Broader market conditions remained anchored by stabilizing inflation and gradually improving buyer sentiment, which allowed the company to sustain pricing discipline while prioritizing cash conversion from completed inventory. Management continues to balance near-term liquidity requirements with long-term capacity building, positioning the group for incremental volume recognition as detailed planning approvals finalize and major constructions transition into sales-ready phases.
