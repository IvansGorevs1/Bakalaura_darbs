During the first three months of 2026, AS Amber Latvijas balzams experienced a significant downturn in financial performance amid severe operational and geopolitical challenges. Total sales volume decreased by 36.1 percent to 517,600 nine-liter cases, down from 810,079 cases in the corresponding period of 2025. The unaudited net turnover for the reporting period amounted to EUR 11.1 million, representing a 29.6 percent decrease compared to EUR 15.8 million in the first three months of 2025. This revenue decline was driven by lower sales volumes and a high comparison base from the previous year.

Gross profit dropped by 66.5 percent to EUR 1.2 million, compared to EUR 3.5 million in the first quarter of 2025, primarily influenced by product mix changes and pricing methodology adjustments. The company reported an operating loss of EUR 1.6 million for the first three months of 2026, contrasting with an operating profit of EUR 0.6 million in the prior-year period. The operating profit margin worsened from 3.7 percent to -14.5 percent. Consequently, the unaudited net result for the reporting period was a net loss of EUR 1.4 million, compared with a net profit of EUR 0.9 million in the first three months of 2025. Earnings per share declined to EUR -0.19 from EUR 0.11.

The company’s results and operational continuity were severely affected by major external factors. In January 2026, Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceedings for related group company Stoli Group USA led to a loss of control in the U.S. jurisdiction, directly impacting the solvency of the company's largest customer and its ability to meet creditor obligations. Additional pressures stemmed from Russian government actions against group entities, a global spirits market downturn, changing consumer habits, and inventory adjustments. These combined cash flow difficulties led to delayed excise tax payments and the freezing of bank accounts by the State Revenue Service.

To ensure business continuity and protect creditors, AS Amber Latvijas balzams filed for legal protection proceedings on January 30, 2026, which were approved by the Riga City Court on February 5, 2026. This process provides a structured framework for restructuring liabilities. Furthermore, the filing triggered events of default under the principal shareholder Amber Beverage Group Holding Sarl bond documentation, leading the principal shareholder to initiate judicial reorganization in Luxembourg. Despite these challenges, the company paid EUR 8.4 million in taxes to the state budget, including EUR 6.4 million in excise tax. Operationally, the Right First Time indicator improved to 99.5 percent from 98.4 percent, while the On Time In Full delivery rate declined to 70.3 percent from 97.5 percent. Management is actively implementing cost reduction and efficiency measures to navigate the environment.
