For the three months ended March 31, 2026, AB Pieno Žvaigždės reported revenue of 52,064 thousand euros, remaining virtually unchanged from the prior year period figure of 52,324 thousand euros. Despite flat top-line sales, cost of sales declined substantially from 42,530 thousand euros to 38,078 thousand euros, expanding gross profit to 13,986 thousand euros from 9,794 thousand euros. Operating profit before finance costs surged to 3,366 thousand euros from 535 thousand euros, resulting in a net profit of 3,068 thousand euros compared to 227 thousand euros previously. Earnings per share increased to 0.07 euros from 0.01 euros, while EBITDA more than doubled to 4,730 thousand euros from 2,001 thousand euros. Liquidity and solvency indicators strengthened considerably, with the current ratio improving to 1.51 and book value per share rising to 0.91 euros. Net cash flow from operating activities reversed to a positive 1,625 thousand euros from a negative 183 thousand euros in the comparable period. Cash utilization in investing activities decreased to 551 thousand euros, reflecting lower capital expenditure. Financing activities generated a net outflow of 723 thousand euros due to loan repayments of 469 thousand euros, finance lease payments of 251 thousand euros, and dividend distribution of 3 thousand euros. Changes in working capital supported the improved cash position, driven primarily by a 2,536 thousand euro reduction in trade receivables, which outweighed a 998 thousand euro increase in inventories and a 393 thousand euro decrease in trade payables. The company maintained its primary focus on the Lithuanian domestic market, where sales reached 31,405 thousand euros against a slight decline from 32,112 thousand euros in the prior year. Exports to other European Union countries expanded to 11,341 thousand euros, while shipments to non-EU markets contracted marginally to 9,318 thousand euros. Product performance shifted moderately, with fresh milk product revenues edging down slightly and dry milk product sales declining noticeably, partially offset by gains in cheese and other product categories. The statement notes a 346 thousand euro loss from property, plant, and equipment disposals, alongside depreciation and amortisation charges of 1,427 thousand euros. No material post-statement events were recorded. The report does not attribute the financial improvements to specific external market conditions or strategic announcements, indicating that the enhanced profitability and cash generation stem directly from internal cost efficiencies, favorable working capital management, and steady core operational execution. Consequently, total equity grew to 41,164 thousand euros, bolstered by retained earnings increasing to 18,614 thousand euros, while total liabilities diminished to 44,141 thousand euros, reinforcing a solidified financial position for the quarter.
