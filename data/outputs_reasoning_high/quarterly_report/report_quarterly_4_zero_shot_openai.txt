Ambu reported a solid start to the 2025/26 financial year, with Q1 revenue increasing to DKK 1,558m from DKK 1,510m. Organic growth was 8.6%, compared with 19.5% in Q1 2024/25, while reported growth was 3.2%, reflecting a 5.4% negative currency impact. Growth was led by Endoscopy Solutions, supported by rising procedure volumes, continued adoption of single-use endoscopy and market expansion.

Endoscopy Solutions grew organically by 14.4%, compared with 20.6% in the prior-year quarter, and accounted for 63% of total revenue. Urology, ENT, & GI was the strongest business group, with organic growth of 21.0%, primarily driven by continued penetration of the aScope 4 portfolio and strong momentum for aScope 5 Uretero. Respiratory grew 8.3%, supported by bronchoscopy solutions, aScope 5 adoption and the launch of the SureSight video laryngoscope solution. Anesthesia & Patient Monitoring recorded flat organic growth of -0.1%, compared with 17.8% last year, as both Anesthesia and Patient Monitoring faced an exceptionally high comparable revenue base.

North America and Europe delivered organic growth of 9.8% and 9.7%, respectively. Rest of World declined 1.6% organically because of order fluctuations and high comparable revenue from the prior year.

Profitability declined year on year. EBIT before special items fell to DKK 164m from DKK 243m, reducing the EBIT margin from 16.1% to 10.5%. EBITDA before special items decreased to DKK 261m from DKK 331m, and the EBITDA margin declined from 21.9% to 16.8%. The EBIT margin was negatively affected by tariff costs of more than DKK 50m and foreign-exchange headwinds; excluding these factors, Ambu reported an EBIT margin of 15.2%. The gross margin was broadly stable at 60.8%, down from 61.3%, while the operating-expense ratio increased to 50.3% from 45.2% as Ambu continued investing in commercial infrastructure and absorbed tariff costs.

Profit before tax decreased to DKK 153m from DKK 237m, and net profit fell to DKK 118m from DKK 183m. Free cash flow before acquisitions declined to DKK 13m from DKK 69m, mainly because of lower EBITDA, elevated working capital, foreign-exchange effects and tariffs. Cash conversion fell to 5% from 21%.

Ambu launched its ZOOM AHEAD strategy and introduced the portable SureSight Mobile video laryngoscope. The company maintained its full-year guidance for 10–13% organic revenue growth, a 12–14% EBIT margin before special items and cash conversion above 40%; excluding the expected tariff impact, the EBIT margin target is 14–16%.
