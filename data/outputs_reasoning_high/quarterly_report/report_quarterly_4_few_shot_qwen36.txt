The company reported continued top-line expansion during the first quarter of the 2025/26 fiscal year, though reported profitability was affected by external cost pressures and foreign exchange volatility. Total revenue reached DKK 1,558 million, reflecting an organic increase of 8.6 percent compared with DKK 1,510 million in the same period last year, while currency fluctuations resulted in a reported growth of 3.2 percent. This momentum was primarily sustained by robust performance within the Endoscopy Solutions segment, which grew organically by 14.4 percent. Growth in this division was fueled by rising procedural volumes, continued market penetration of the aScope portfolio, and the commercial introduction of the SureSight Connect video laryngoscope. Conversely, the Anesthesia and Patient Monitoring unit delivered flat organic growth of minus 0.1 percent, largely offset by an exceptionally strong revenue baseline in the prior year. Geographic expansion remained balanced, with organic gains of 9.8 percent in North America and 9.7 percent in Europe partially cushioned by a 1.6 percent contraction in the Rest of World region.

Operating profitability contracted as planned expenses intensified alongside macroeconomic headwinds. Earnings before interest and tax before special items totaled DKK 164 million, yielding an operating margin of 10.5 percent against 16.1 percent previously. The compression was directly attributed to tariff-related expenditures exceeding DKK 50 million and unfavorable foreign exchange movements, which together reduced the adjusted underlying margin to 15.2 percent. Operating expenses rose as a percentage of revenue to 50.3 percent, reflecting deliberate commercial infrastructure investments aimed at securing long-term market position. Gross margin also dipped slightly to 60.8 percent against a historically high baseline in the prior year. Free cash flow before acquisitions declined to DKK 13 million from DKK 69 million in the comparative period, primarily due to elevated inventory levels maintained to buffer against global supply chain uncertainties and negative currency translation effects. Alongside commercial initiatives, the company advanced its circular economy efforts by launching the portable SureSight Mobile video laryngoscope and expanding its recycling program to cover new video equipment components. Despite these liquidity constraints, the company returned capital to shareholders through a DKK 90 million dividend payment and initiated a share repurchase program with DKK 38 million deployed for treasury stock purchases. Management reaffirmed full-year expectations, projecting organic revenue growth between 10 and 13 percent and a targeted EBIT margin range of 12 to 14 percent once tariff impacts are excluded. Overall, the period demonstrated resilient demand and successful product commercialization, even as near-term earnings were temporarily moderated by strategic reinvestment and external cost inflation.
