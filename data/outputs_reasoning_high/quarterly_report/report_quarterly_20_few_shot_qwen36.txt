SAF Tehnika reported a decline in revenue during the fiscal year ending June 30, 2025, while profitability improved substantially compared to the prior year. Consolidated turnover decreased by four percent to EUR 26.11 million for the full year, accompanied by a sharper thirteen percent drop to EUR 5.18 million in the fourth quarter. The revenue contraction was primarily driven by a fourteen percent decline in North and Latin American markets, which generated EUR 14.28 million, while European revenues rose seventeen percent to EUR 10.04 million. Turnover in the Asia, Africa, and Middle East regions fell slightly by five percent. Quarterly fluctuations continued to reflect project-based sales cycles and timing differences between goods dispatch and revenue recognition.

Despite lower sales volumes, the company narrowed its annual net loss to EUR 820 thousand, a marked improvement from the EUR 2.37 million deficit recorded in the previous fiscal year. This improvement stemmed from a significant reduction in direct costs of goods sold, which fell to EUR 9.33 million from EUR 13.12 million, although these savings were partially offset by an eight percent increase in salary and social expenses to EUR 10.58 million alongside higher bonus payouts. The Group maintained operational discipline by keeping total expenses within budget while continuing strategic investments in product development and market expansion.

Liquidity and cash generation strengthened considerably. Operating cash flow reached EUR 2.13 million, supporting a net cash inflow of EUR 2.6 million for the twelve-month period. The cash position improved to EUR 4.81 million at year-end, supported by a substantial rise in customer prepayments to EUR 3.92 million. The company retains an unused EUR 4.95 million credit facility to ensure ongoing financial flexibility. Inventory levels grew by eight percent to EUR 13.94 million as strategic stockpiling addressed global electronic component shortages, enabling faster delivery times and reliable order fulfillment.

Market conditions remained stable, with sustained demand for microwave radio systems despite broader telecommunications modernization trends. The business environment was unaffected by regional conflicts, and management emphasized proactive risk monitoring and supply chain adjustments. Headcount expanded modestly to 273 employees to support production capabilities and innovation initiatives, particularly in Internet of Things and next-generation wireless solutions. Overall, the reporting period reflected a consolidation phase characterized by revenue normalization, improved cost control, enhanced cash flow generation, and foundational preparations for long-term financial stability.
