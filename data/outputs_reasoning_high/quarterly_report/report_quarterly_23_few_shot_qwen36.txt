AB KN Energies delivered strong financial performance for the nine months ended 30 September 2025, with consolidated revenue rising to EUR 76,664 thousand from EUR 67,977 thousand, a growth of approximately 13%. Expansion was driven principally by the Liquefied Natural Gas Terminal segment, where regasification services revenue surged to EUR 43,640 thousand from EUR 31,401 thousand. This increase stems from higher regasification volumes, client bonuses for booked capacity, and the return to normal operations following the prior-year dry-dock maintenance that caused a month and a half of downtime. Revenue from liquid energy products transshipment grew moderately to EUR 21,375 thousand. Segment profitability reflected these trends, with the Liquefied Natural Gas Terminal contributing EUR 7,779 thousand to pre-tax profit and the Liquid Energy Terminals segment yielding EUR 5,293 thousand, while commercial LNG activities added EUR 9,240 thousand in revenue.

Cost of sales increased to EUR 45,115 thousand from EUR 38,740 thousand, accelerating faster than revenue due to structural accounting changes. Following the acquisition of the Floating Storage and Regasification Unit, depreciation expenses associated with this asset were reclassified to property, plant and equipment, embedding EUR 11,369 thousand of depreciation into cost of sales, compared to minimal amounts previously. Furthermore, environmental taxes and emission allowances nearly doubled. Operating expenses held steady at EUR 10,640 thousand. Consequently, gross profit rose to EUR 31,549 thousand, supporting a robust operating profit of EUR 21,282 thousand, up from EUR 18,942 thousand.

Financial results were further supported by a dramatic decline in derivative-related losses, which fell to EUR 16 thousand from EUR 1,260 thousand in the comparable period. Although interest expenses grew to EUR 6,086 thousand from EUR 4,181 thousand, partially reflecting higher borrowing costs against a slightly reduced loan principal, overall finance costs remained manageable near the previous level. Net profit attributable to shareholders reached EUR 13,158 thousand, a 11% increase over the EUR 11,862 thousand recorded in the prior nine months. Earnings per share improved to EUR 0.035, and total comprehensive income totaled EUR 13,215 thousand.

Liquidity and cash generation strengthened markedly. Operating cash flows more than doubled to EUR 76,331 thousand from EUR 35,637 thousand, underpinned by core operational cash generation, working capital improvements, and the reclassification of EUR 31,900 thousand in short-term deposits back to cash equivalents. The cash and cash equivalents balance expanded significantly to EUR 54,398 thousand. Capital expenditures declined to EUR 9,751 thousand from EUR 10,692 thousand. Shareholder distributions increased, with dividends declared at EUR 8,622 thousand against EUR 5,000 thousand previously. Total equity advanced to EUR 167,721 thousand, bolstered by retained earnings and reserve allocations, while the group maintained a prudent capital structure with borrowings at EUR 311,443 thousand and lease liabilities at EUR 25,063 thousand.
