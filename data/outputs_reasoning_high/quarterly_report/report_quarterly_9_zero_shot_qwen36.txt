Hepsor AS reported consolidated revenue of 35.4 million euros for the twelve months ended December 31, 2025, declining from 38.4 million euros in the prior year. Fourth-quarter revenue dropped further to 4.5 million euros from 10.5 million euros in the corresponding period of 2024. The Group recorded a full-year net profit of 1.0 million euros, with 0.4 million euros attributable to parent company owners, compared to 2.1 million euros total and 0.4 million euros attributable in 2024. In the fourth quarter, the Company posted a net loss of 0.5 million euros, contrasting with a 1.0 million euro profit in the previous-year quarter.

Profitability softened due to fewer apartment deliveries, which totaled 141 units in 2025 against 194 in 2024, coupled with compressed project margins and elevated marketing costs. The gross profit margin narrowed to 15.9 percent from 17.6 percent in the prior year. Marketing expenditures rose to 1.3 million euros from 0.9 million euros, driven by more intensive sales campaigns aligned with a growing inventory of available units. Operating profit contracted to 3.4 million euros from 4.3 million euros. These headwinds were partially mitigated by 0.8 million euros in other operating income stemming from the reclassification of specific inventories to investment properties, alongside a rise in interest income.

On an operational level, the development pipeline expanded markedly. The Company added six new residential projects in 2025, bringing total planned apartments to approximately 700. Groundbreaking activity accelerated across Tallinn and Riga, pushing the number of homes under construction to 428 at year-end, a 223 percent increase from 192 at the close of 2024. Commercial assets remain well-positioned, with 84 percent occupancy achieved across the completed commercial portfolio and anchor tenants secured for upcoming centers. In Canada, planning milestones advanced successfully, notably securing council approval to nearly double the approved building volume for the Weston Road site to 62,000 square meters.

Financial structuring supported the operational buildup. A publicly offered bond series closed in November 2025, raising 8 million euros at a fixed 9.5 percent annual coupon after being 1.4 times oversubscribed. The Group also established a 50 percent joint venture with EfTEN Capital to develop a 300-unit residential project in Tallinn. Capitalization strengthened accordingly, with total equity growing 23.0 percent to 27.1 million euros, bolstered by retained earnings and non-cash property contributions from minority partners. Concurrently, leverage improved, lowering the debt ratio to 52.0 percent from 61.6 percent year-ago. While near-term revenue and margin metrics reflect cyclical delivery pacing and higher sales activity, the enlarged construction backlog and healthier balance sheet position the Group for sustained project completions and revenue realization in subsequent development cycles.
