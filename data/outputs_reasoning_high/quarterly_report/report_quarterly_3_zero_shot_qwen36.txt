AS Amber Latvijas balzams reported a substantial deterioration in financial performance during the first three months of 2026, marked by a sharp contraction in sales, significant losses, and severe liquidity pressures that led to the initiation of legal protection proceedings. Net turnover decreased by 29.6 percent to EUR 11.1 million, primarily driven by a 36.1 percent reduction in sales volume, which fell to 517,600 cases expressed in 9-liter boxes compared to 810,079 cases in the same period of 2025. The decline was also influenced by a high comparison base in the prior year, where results benefited from a contractual revenue adjustment. Gross profit collapsed by 66.5 percent to EUR 1.2 million, affected by adverse changes in product mix and pricing methodology adjustments, despite efforts to reduce costs. Consequently, the operating result shifted from a profit of EUR 0.6 million to a loss of EUR 1.6 million, reducing the operating margin to negative 14.5 percent from positive 3.7 percent in 2025. The Company recorded a net loss of EUR 1.4 million against a net profit of EUR 0.9 million in the previous year, with earnings per share deteriorating to a loss of EUR 0.19 from a profit of EUR 0.11.

Several external factors critically impacted business continuity. A global downturn in the spirits market reduced demand, while geopolitical tensions resulted in Russian authorities designating related entities as extremist and confiscating the Amber Beverage Group-owned Talvis distillery. Additionally, the commencement of Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceedings for the related U.S. affiliate Stoli Group in January 2026 caused the Company to lose control over its largest customer, severely affecting cash flows. Accumulated cash flow difficulties and delayed excise tax payments prompted the State Revenue Service to freeze the Company's bank accounts, making it impossible to operate without intervention. On 5 February 2026, the Riga City Court approved the Company's application for legal protection proceedings to restructure liabilities and stabilize operations. The court has extended the deadline for the action plan to 5 October 2026. The filing automatically triggered events of default for principal shareholder Amber Beverage Group, which subsequently obtained authorization for judicial reorganization proceedings in Luxembourg, secured by a four-month moratorium until 24 August 2026.

Operational indicators reflected the strain on the business. While the Right First Time quality metric improved to 99.5 percent from 98.4 percent, the On Time In Full delivery rate dropped significantly to 70.3 percent from 97.5 percent. Liquidity remained constrained, with cash and cash equivalents decreasing to approximately EUR 38,000 by quarter-end. Although current assets exceeded current liabilities by EUR 82.9 million, the ratio weakened compared to 2025. The Company is implementing comprehensive measures to enhance production efficiency, optimize costs, and improve commercial performance within the structured legal protection framework to ensure sustainable development and resolve all outstanding liabilities.
