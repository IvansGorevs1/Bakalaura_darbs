AS Trigon Property Development reported its interim financial results for the fourth quarter and 12 months of 2025, showing a decline in financial performance compared to 2024. For the 12 months ended December 31, 2025, the company recorded a net loss of 99,507 euros, compared to a net profit of 167,409 euros in 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share worsened to negative 0.02212 euros from positive 0.03721 euros in the prior year.

The decline in profitability was primarily driven by the absence of real estate sales during 2025. While the company generated gains on sales of 331,920 euros and a gross profit of 220,299 euros in the 12 months of 2024 from selling a 0.09-hectare transportation land plot and a 0.70-hectare property, no land sales occurred in 2025, resulting in zero gross profit. Additionally, administrative and general expenses increased to 108,021 euros in 2025 from 63,650 euros in 2024. This increase was driven by 41,452 euros in personnel expenses paid to management and supervisory board members and higher accounting costs. Although financial income rose to 8,513 euros from 5,760 euros, it failed to offset the operating loss of 108,021 euros, which contrasted with an operating profit of 161,649 euros in 2024.

As of December 31, 2025, total assets decreased to 1,804,656 euros from 1,873,680 euros, and total equity declined to 1,755,267 euros from 1,854,774 euros at the end of 2024. The company's cash balance decreased by 142,945 euros during 2025 to 227,911 euros compared to 370,856 euros previously. Total cash flows used in operating activities were 142,945 euros in 2025, reversing from cash flows generated of 194,723 euros in 2024. This shift reflected inventory investments of 91,262 euros into the property development project, bringing total inventories to 1,562,500 euros from 1,471,238 euros at the end of 2024. Total liabilities rose to 49,389 euros from 18,906 euros, increasing the debt ratio to 2.74 percent from 1.01 percent.

Despite the net loss, the closing share price on the Nasdaq Tallinn stock exchange rose to 0.634 euros at the end of 2025 from 0.620 euros at the end of 2024, resulting in a market capitalization of 2,852,405 euros compared to 2,789,418 euros. The company had no employees and maintained its focus on developing its 13.2-hectare real estate property in Pärnu, Estonia.
