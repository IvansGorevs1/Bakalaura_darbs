Arco Vara reported a significant improvement in first-quarter 2025 performance. Total revenue increased to €1.706 million from €990 thousand in Q1 2024, including €1.589 million from sales of real estate in its own development projects compared with €927 thousand previously. Four apartments were sold during the quarter, versus three apartments and one commercial premise in Q1 2024. Gross profit rose to €598 thousand from €173 thousand, and operating profit improved to €259 thousand from an operating loss of €208 thousand. The group recorded net profit of €103 thousand, compared with a net loss of €349 thousand a year earlier, while basic and diluted earnings per share improved to €0.01 from negative €0.03.

The improvement was driven primarily by the Estonian development segment, which generated €1.589 million of revenue and €320 thousand of operating profit, compared with €935 thousand of revenue and a €128 thousand operating loss in Q1 2024. The other segment also remained profitable, with operating profit of €61 thousand on €117 thousand of revenue. Bulgarian development produced no revenue and an operating loss of €76 thousand, while Estonian construction had no external revenue and an operating loss of €46 thousand. Finance costs were broadly unchanged at €143 thousand, compared with €141 thousand, and the group recognized €13 thousand of income tax expense.

Operating cash flow remained negative and weakened to an outflow of €2.838 million from €2.217 million. However, financing activities generated €2.231 million, up from €1.479 million, resulting in a smaller net cash outflow of €607 thousand compared with €738 thousand. New loans of €3.090 million included €2.190 million to finance the Spordi 3a and 3b land acquisition and €900 thousand for Rannakalda construction. Cash ended the quarter at €1.113 million, down from €1.720 million at year-end but above the €734 thousand recorded at the end of Q1 2024.

Interest-bearing liabilities increased to €16.568 million from €15.215 million at year-end, and net loans rose by €1.960 million to €15.455 million. The weighted average loan rate nevertheless declined by 0.5 percentage points to 8.2%, reflecting lower Euribor rates. Total assets increased to €42.728 million from €39.568 million at year-end, partly reflecting €3.5 million of property acquired for development, while equity rose slightly to €19.842 million.

Management described the Estonian real estate market as cautiously recovering, supported by declining Euribor, stabilizing inflation and increased residential transaction activity. Rannakalda reached 91 apartments sold out of 113, Botanica Lozen reached 95% construction readiness with 9 of 16 houses sold, and the group acquired the Spordi site for a planned 56-apartment development with total investment of €15 million. Pre-sales and construction for Spordi are planned for Q2 2025.
