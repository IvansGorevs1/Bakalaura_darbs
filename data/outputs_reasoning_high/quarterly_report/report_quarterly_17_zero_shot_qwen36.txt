During the twelve-month period ended 31 December 2025, AB Novaturas generated consolidated revenue of EUR 168,483 thousand, representing a 16.1 percent decline compared to EUR 200,878 thousand in 2024. The Group served approximately 191 thousand travellers, a 20 percent decrease from the prior year. Despite the reduction in passenger volumes and top-line sales, the company successfully pivoted toward a profitability-focused strategy, resulting in a positive adjusted EBITDA of EUR 420 thousand, reversing the negative EUR 449 thousand recorded in 2024 after excluding a one-off EUR 5.7 million goodwill impairment. The annual net loss narrowed significantly to EUR 1.17 million, a 39 percent improvement over the EUR 1.92 million loss in 2024 excluding the aforementioned impairment. Gross profit amounted to EUR 19,650 thousand, decreasing by 5.3 percent year-over-year, while the gross profit margin expanded by 1.3 percentage points to 11.66 percent. Operating profit improved substantially to a loss of EUR 274 thousand from EUR 6,556 thousand in the previous period, reflecting stricter cost discipline and optimized route management.

The deterioration in revenue and passenger numbers was primarily driven by an intentionally reduced flight programme aimed at eliminating loss-making routes amid intense market competition and excess travel supply. This strategic downsizing allowed the Group to reallocate capacity toward higher-margin destinations, leveraging its extensive portfolio across Europe, North Africa, and long-haul markets. Operational efficiency notably strengthened, with the average aircraft load factor increasing by 1.5 percentage points to 97.2 percent, up from 95.7 percent. Load factors on long-haul and ski destinations reached 98.5 percent and 97.2 percent respectively. Market challenges during the latter part of the year included heightened competitive pricing pressure in autumn and operational disruptions at Vilnius Airport caused by unauthorized balloon activity, which suppressed last-minute demand. Conversely, booking momentum improved, with summer 2026 reservations growing by 59 percent compared to the previous year. Segment analysis indicates that flight package tours remained the dominant revenue source, posting a 15.0 percent sales decline but delivering an 18.0 percent increase in segment sales profit to EUR 12,162 thousand, supported by a margin expansion to 15.0 percent. Looking ahead, the company plans to maintain current programme levels while prioritizing operational efficiency, cost control, and hotel portfolio expansion to sustain profitability growth. Overall, the reporting period marked a decisive transition from volume-driven operations to a leaner, margin-oriented model, successfully converting a highly challenging market environment into improved financial stability and positive cash generation.
