During the first quarter of 2026, VILVI Group reported consolidated revenue of 86,970 thousand euros, representing a 22.4 percent year-on-year increase from 71,072 thousand euros in Q1 2025. Despite strong top-line growth, near-term profitability metrics showed mixed results. EBITDA declined by 50.2 percent to 3,003 thousand euros, resulting in an EBITDA margin of 3.5 percent, down from 8.5 percent in the prior year period. This EBITDA figure excludes a preliminary negative goodwill charge of 5,500 thousand euros linked to a recent acquisition. Conversely, net profit increased by 25.7 percent to 5,085 thousand euros, reflecting a profit margin of 5.8 percent and earnings per share of 0.43 euros. The company also significantly expanded its raw material procurement, purchasing 155,954 tons of basic indicators milk, a 54.2 percent rise compared to Q1 2025.

Revenue growth was primarily driven by segment and regional expansions. The newly consolidated canned milk segment contributed 11,923 thousand euros, representing 14 percent of total sales. Dry milk product sales surged by 126.9 percent to 24,659 thousand euros, while fresh milk product sales grew by 73.4 percent to 12,748 thousand euros. Sales in cheese, cheese products, and other categories decreased by 28.8 percent to 37,641 thousand euros. Geographically, Lithuanian domestic sales jumped 72.9 percent to 14,633 thousand euros, and exports to non-European Union markets rose 46.6 percent to 29,818 thousand euros. Sales within the European Union remained largely stable, increasing by just 0.6 percent to 42,518 thousand euros.

Operational developments substantially shaped the quarter. On January 16, 2026, the group acquired 100 percent of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB, a producer of canned milk products and milk powders. Management plans to integrate the subsidiary to capture operational synergies and expand regional whey processing capacity. Additionally, commercial cheese production officially commenced at the new Baltic Dairy Board SIA factory in Bauska, Latvia, which is designed to produce 18,000 tons annually for export to more than forty countries. The facility recently secured an honorable third place in the Latvian Construction Awards 2025 competition. These strategic investments and expanded purchasing volumes drove substantial revenue growth across multiple segments, although higher cost of sales compressed gross profit and operating margins during the reporting period.
