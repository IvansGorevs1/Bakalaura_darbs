During the first six months of 2026, AS Merko Ehitus experienced a decrease in overall revenue and profitability compared with the same period of the previous year, while securing an all-time high order book. Group revenue for the six-month period declined by 10.7% to EUR 149.9 million, down from EUR 167.9 million in the first half of 2025. However, revenue for the second quarter alone increased to EUR 92.7 million compared to EUR 82.6 million in the second quarter of the prior year, driven by higher activity in both construction services and real estate development as several large-scale projects entered more active construction phases and apartment deliveries accelerated. Profitability metrics contracted across the board. Net profit attributable to shareholders for the six months dropped to EUR 12.8 million from EUR 21.7 million in the previous year, and operating profit decreased to EUR 8.4 million from EUR 18.6 million. The pre-tax profit margin for the six-month period stood at 7.2%, compared to 14.0% a year earlier, while the net profit margin was 8.6% versus 12.9%. This normalization of margins was primarily influenced by the expiration of highly successful contracts from previous periods, sector-wide competition, and rising construction input prices fueled by ongoing geopolitical tensions and inflationary pressures. Analyzing the operational segments, the construction service segment generated EUR 118.3 million in revenue, representing a modest 1.5% decrease, and recorded an operating profit of EUR 3.0 million, down from EUR 8.8 million. The real estate development segment saw revenue decline by 33.9% to EUR 31.6 million, with operating profit falling to EUR 7.0 million from EUR 11.9 million. During the first six months of the year, the group sold 120 apartments, compared to 222 apartments in the corresponding period of 2025. Despite the reduction in short-term sales and earnings, the group achieved a significant milestone with its secured order book reaching an all-time high of EUR 835.7 million as of 30 June 2026, marking an increase of approximately 88% compared to EUR 443.8 million a year earlier. This expansion was largely propelled by public sector and defense-related contracts, such as the Rūdninkai military campus in Lithuania and Rail Baltica infrastructure projects in Estonia, which successfully compensated for cautious private sector demand. New construction contracts signed during the six-month period totaled EUR 486.4 million, significantly exceeding the EUR 223.1 million recorded in the first half of 2025. The group maintained a solid financial and liquidity position at the end of the reporting period. Cash and cash equivalents stood at EUR 39.6 million, up from EUR 25.9 million a year prior. Operating cash flow for the six-month period was negative at EUR 31.6 million, primarily affected by investments in residential development inventories and construction working capital changes. Conversely, investing cash flow was positive at EUR 16.4 million, and financing cash flow was positive at EUR 13.4 million, supported by net loan increases for development projects and despite paying EUR 22.1 million in dividends to shareholders. Total assets reached EUR 437.9 million, and equity stood at EUR 251.1 million, resulting in a strong equity ratio of 57.4%. Overall, the reporting period reflected robust long-term backlog growth and active project execution amidst persistent cost inflation and a subdued private construction market.
