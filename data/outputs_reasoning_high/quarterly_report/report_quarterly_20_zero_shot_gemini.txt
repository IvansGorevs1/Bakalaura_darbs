During the 2024/2025 financial year ended June 30, 2025, SAF Tehnika reported an unaudited consolidated net turnover of 26.11 million euros, representing a 4 percent decline compared to the previous financial year when turnover reached 27.09 million euros. For the fourth quarter of the financial year, unaudited consolidated net turnover was 5.18 million euros, marking a 13 percent decrease from the 5.98 million euros recorded in the corresponding period of the previous year. The decline in quarterly turnover was noted as being influenced by the completion, scope, complexity, and varying lead times of individual projects.

Geographically, revenues in the Americas region reached 14.28 million euros over the twelve-month period, accounting for 55 percent of total turnover and showing a 14 percent decrease compared to the prior financial year. European countries generated 10.04 million euros, representing 38 percent of total turnover and reflecting a 17 percent increase year-over-year. Revenues from the Asia, Africa, and Middle East region totaled 1.78 million euros, accounting for 7 percent of total turnover and declining by 5 percent over the twelve-month period.

The Group closed the twelve-month reporting period with a net loss of 820 thousand euros, which represents an improvement compared to the net loss of 2.37 million euros reported for the 2023/2024 financial year. For the fourth quarter alone, the Group recorded a net loss of 913 thousand euros, compared to a net loss of 498 thousand euros in the fourth quarter of the preceding financial year. Operating results were affected by expenses including wages, social costs, and bonuses, with total salary and bonus related expenses increasing to 12.41 million euros from 11.48 million euros in the previous financial year.

Total assets at the end of the reporting period stood at 25.92 million euros, up from 23.02 million euros as of June 30, 2024. Total owners equity decreased to 15.58 million euros from 16.44 million euros at the end of the previous financial year. Total inventories increased by 8 percent to reach 13.94 million euros, as the Group maintains material reserves to ensure competitive delivery times amidst past global component shortages. The Group generated a net cash flow of 2.6 million euros over the twelve-month period, closing the financial year with a cash and bank balance of 4.81 million euros, compared to 2.21 million euros at the beginning of the period. Customer prepayments for goods and services totaled 3.92 million euros at the end of the period. To ensure liquidity, the Parent Company maintained an unutilized credit line agreement with Luminor Bank AS for a total amount of 4.95 million euros. The total number of employees at the end of the reporting period was 273, compared to 265 employees at the end of the previous financial year.
