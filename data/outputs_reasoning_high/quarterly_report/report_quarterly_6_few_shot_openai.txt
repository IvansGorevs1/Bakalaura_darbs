Infortar Group’s profitability improved significantly in the first half of 2026 despite broadly unchanged revenue. Second-quarter revenue declined 12.6% year on year to EUR 440.9 million, mainly because lower wholesale energy sales reduced energy-segment revenue. For the six months, revenue decreased only 0.6% to EUR 945.9 million. Gross profit increased to EUR 59.4 million in the quarter from EUR 55.7 million and to EUR 114.1 million for the six months from EUR 81.7 million.

Second-quarter EBITDA rose 11.8% to EUR 64.2 million, lifting the EBITDA margin to 14.6% from 11.4%. Six-month EBITDA increased 30.8% to EUR 111.2 million, with the margin improving to 11.8% from 8.9%. Operating profit increased 9.8% in the quarter to EUR 29.7 million and 93.6% for the six months to EUR 51.1 million. Profit before tax rose to EUR 17.7 million in the quarter from EUR 15.5 million and to EUR 27.2 million for the six months from EUR 2.7 million. Net profit was EUR 1.9 million in the quarter, compared with EUR 0.2 million a year earlier, while six-month net profit improved from a EUR 14.4 million loss to EUR 7.2 million profit. Profit attributable to the parent’s owners reached EUR 15.2 million for the six months, compared with a EUR 2.5 million loss previously, and basic earnings per share increased to EUR 0.74 from negative EUR 0.12.

All segments contributed to the first-half EBITDA improvement. Energy EBITDA increased to EUR 59.9 million from EUR 51.7 million, real estate EBITDA rose to EUR 6.5 million from EUR 4.4 million, and supporting businesses improved from a EUR 4.4 million EBITDA loss to a EUR 5.6 million profit. Maritime transport EBITDA increased to EUR 39.3 million from EUR 33.3 million for the six months, although Tallink’s second-quarter EBITDA declined to EUR 34.3 million from EUR 37.4 million. Tallink’s quarterly results were affected by weaker demand, a 2.2% fall in passengers, higher fuel costs of EUR 8.4 million, and the EU ETS surrender requirement rising to 100% from 70%. Energy sales volumes fell 34% to 3.3 TWh, primarily due to lower wholesale sales.

The Group acquired additional Tallink shares, increasing ownership to 68.76%, while no business combinations were completed during the quarter. Investments were approximately EUR 50 million, including continued development of the Halinga biomethane plant, infrastructure and construction projects, and energy-storage facilities.

At 30 June, total assets were EUR 2.71 billion and equity EUR 1.20 billion. Cash increased to EUR 253.2 million, while net debt declined to EUR 829.9 million and net debt to EBITDA improved to 3.3 from 3.7 at year-end. Operating cash flow was EUR 99.5 million, and the Group maintained financial buffers despite geopolitical, energy-price, demand and interest-rate risks.
