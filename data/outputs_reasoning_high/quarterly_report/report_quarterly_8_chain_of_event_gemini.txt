During the 2025 financial year, AS Tallink Grupp faced a challenging operating environment marked by economic uncertainty, low consumer and business confidence particularly in its core Finnish market, and geopolitical tensions, which negatively affected financial performance compared to the previous year. Total unaudited consolidated revenue for the twelve-month period ended 31 December 2025 decreased to 765.3 million euros from 785.8 million euros in 2024. The group carried 5,531,132 passengers, representing a 0.9 percent decrease year-on-year, while transported cargo units decreased by 19.2 percent to 245,004 units, and passenger vehicles declined by 2.2 percent to 760,473 vehicles. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization for 2025 totaled 130.1 million euros, down from 175.2 million euros reported in 2024. Net profit for the financial year 2025 dropped to 17.3 million euros, or 0.023 euros per share, compared to 40.3 million euros, or 0.054 euros per share, in the preceding year. Gross profit for 2025 amounted to 143.2 million euros compared to 153.6 million euros a year earlier. Operating results were influenced by internal cost optimization measures, including the sale of three vessels during the year, namely the passenger vessel Star I, and cargo vessels Regal Star and Sailor, as well as ongoing vessel charter arrangements involving the cruise ferries Romantika, Galaxy I, and Silja Europa. Profitability was positively affected by a decrease in depreciation and amortisation expenses down to 79.8 million euros from 97.8 million euros in 2024, which resulted from aligning the estimated useful lives of cruise and passenger vessels to 45 years. Net finance costs also declined to 21.4 million euros from 28.3 million euros due to lower outstanding loan balances and declining interest rates. Total loan repayments and interest payments during 2025 amounted to 113.5 million euros. As of 31 December 2025, the group’s net debt stood at 432.4 million euros, reflecting a reduction from 537.7 million euros at the end of 2024, with a net debt to EBITDA ratio of 3.3. Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the period were 13.5 million euros, alongside 96.9 million euros in unused credit lines. The cash position was affected by the payment of dividends totaling 44.6 million euros, representing 0.06 euros per share, and related income tax payments of 11.3 million euros. For the fourth quarter of 2025 alone, the group reported revenue of 188.0 million euros, up 2.4 percent compared to 183.5 million euros in the fourth quarter of 2024. Fourth-quarter EBITDA rose to 27.6 million euros from 25.7 million euros, and the group achieved a net profit of 12.2 million euros compared to a net loss of 5.2 million euros in the same period of the prior year. Reflecting confidence in ongoing stability, the management board intends to propose a dividend of 6 euro cents per share for 2026.
