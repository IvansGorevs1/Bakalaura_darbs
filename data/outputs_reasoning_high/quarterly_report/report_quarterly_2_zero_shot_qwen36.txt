Amber Grid AB reported a substantial improvement in financial performance for the first quarter of 2026, driven by higher transmission volumes, increased tariffs, and a sharp decline in natural gas balancing costs. Net profit surged to EUR 15,763 thousand compared to EUR 4,239 thousand in the same period of 2025, representing a more than threefold increase. Basic earnings per share rose to EUR 0.09 from EUR 0.02. Operating profit grew to EUR 19,627 thousand from EUR 5,185 thousand, while EBITDA increased significantly to EUR 23,304 thousand versus EUR 8,918 thousand.

Revenue reached EUR 32,821 thousand, an increase of approximately 58 percent over the prior-year period. This growth was primarily attributed to a 35 percent rise in the volume of natural gas transmitted and higher transmission prices implemented for 2026. Conversely, revenue from system balancing products plummeted by 99 percent due to a new transit agreement signed with Kaliningrad in 2026, which substantially reduced the need for balancing services. Correspondingly, purchases of natural gas and related services decreased by 68 percent to EUR 1,477 thousand, largely resulting from a 99 percent drop in balancing product costs, although costs for technological needs rose by 118 percent.

Significant changes occurred in the company's capital structure during the reporting period. On 25 February 2026, a tripartite loan transfer agreement became effective, under which the remaining balance of the Nordic Investment Bank loan was assumed by parent company EPSO-G. Consequently, the Company is no longer subject to financial covenants, and the related cross-default provisions with the European Investment Bank lapsed. This allowed for the reclassification of loan portions from current to non-current liabilities, improving the short-term liquidity profile. Total borrowings decreased to EUR 104,174 thousand from EUR 123,666 thousand at the end of 2025. Net debt stood at EUR 106,610 thousand. The weighted average interest rate on borrowings was 2.30 percent.

The statement of cash flows showed robust operating cash inflows of EUR 22,927 thousand, compared to EUR 8,264 thousand previously. Net cash used in investing activities was EUR 3,570 thousand, and financing activities utilized EUR 20,467 thousand due to borrowings repayments, interest payments, and changes in overdraft facilities. Cash balances declined to EUR 832 thousand. Equity increased to EUR 182,039 thousand, reflecting accumulated profits. Total assets remained stable at EUR 324,064 thousand.

Operational developments included a slight reduction in the workforce to 365 employees. A provision of EUR 5,815 thousand remains recognized for potential repayment of guarantee funds received regarding the construction of the Gas Pipeline Interconnection between Poland and Lithuania, pending the outcome of ongoing litigation with contractor Alvora UAB. Following the reporting period, shareholders approved a dividend distribution of EUR 9,989 thousand. Management adjusted EBITDA was EUR 8,285 thousand, reflecting temporary regulatory differences that impacted the reconciliation to net profit.
