SAF Tehnika’s consolidated turnover for the 2024/2025 financial year was EUR 26.11 million, down 4% from EUR 27.09 million in 2023/2024. Despite the lower revenue, the Group significantly reduced its annual net loss to EUR 820 thousand from EUR 2.37 million, while EBIT improved to a loss of EUR 754 thousand from a loss of EUR 2.26 million. The improvement was supported by lower direct costs of goods sold, which declined to EUR 9.33 million from EUR 13.12 million, and lower total operating expenses of EUR 27.56 million compared with EUR 30.27 million. Financial items also produced a smaller net loss, EUR 62 thousand versus EUR 90 thousand previously, although foreign exchange results changed from a gain to a loss.

The fourth quarter was materially weaker. Net sales fell 13% year on year to EUR 5.18 million, and the net loss widened to EUR 913 thousand from EUR 498 thousand. EBIT deteriorated to a loss of EUR 863 thousand from EUR 495 thousand. Quarterly turnover was affected by the timing of individual projects, whose production, material procurement and delivery schedules can shift revenue recognition into subsequent quarters. Regional performance was mixed: North and Latin American turnover declined 40% to EUR 2.5 million, while European turnover increased 24% to EUR 1.9 million and turnover in Asia, Africa and the Middle East doubled to EUR 846 thousand.

For the full year, the Americas remained the largest market with EUR 14.28 million of revenue, down 14%. European revenue rose 17% to EUR 10.04 million, while Asia, Africa and the Middle East declined 5%. The regional shift toward Europe partly offset the decline in the Americas. The Group continued investing in new and enhanced products, sales promotion, and IoT solutions; management stated that expenses remained within budget.

Operations continued to be affected by the global shortage of electronic components. The Group had accumulated material reserves to meet orders and maintain short delivery times, while component delivery times had recently improved. Inventories increased 8% to EUR 13.94 million, reflecting the Group’s policy of maintaining materials for timely deliveries and repair services.

Cash generation strengthened considerably. Net cash flow was EUR 2.60 million, compared with an outflow of EUR 1.25 million in the prior year, and year-end cash increased to EUR 4.81 million from EUR 2.21 million. Customer advances were EUR 3.92 million, and the unused EUR 4.95 million Luminor Bank credit line provided additional liquidity capacity. Total assets increased to EUR 25.92 million, while owners’ equity declined to EUR 15.58 million, reflecting the year’s loss.
