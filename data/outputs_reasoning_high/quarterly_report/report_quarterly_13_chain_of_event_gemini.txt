During the second quarter and first six months of 2026, Liven AS experienced strong residential real estate market activity and robust growth in sales contracts, alongside lower quarterly revenue and a net loss driven by one-off expenses and dividend-related taxes. For the six months ended June 30, 2026, the group signed 111 sales contracts under the law of obligations, nearly doubling the 56 contracts signed in the same period of 2025. In the second quarter alone, 57 sales contracts were signed compared to 31 in the second quarter of 2025. The group entered the third quarter with 171 contracts under the law of obligations and a presale volume of EUR 48.6 million, of which EUR 39.8 million relates to buildings scheduled for completion in 2026. This presale portfolio grew by EUR 14.9 million since the beginning of the year. 

Despite strong sales, revenue for the second quarter of 2026 decreased to EUR 4,503 thousand from EUR 7,388 thousand in the second quarter of 2025, as only 12 homes were handed over during the quarter compared to 35 in the prior-year period. Cumulative revenue for the first six months of 2026 increased to EUR 11,439 thousand from EUR 9,319 thousand in the first six months of 2025, supported by 26 handed-over homes and commercial units. Profitability declined in the second quarter, resulting in a profit before income tax of EUR -234 thousand and a net loss of EUR 618 thousand, compared to a net profit of EUR 974 thousand in the second quarter of 2025. For the first six months of 2026, the net loss stood at EUR 198 thousand compared to a net profit of EUR 268 thousand a year earlier. 

The second quarter financial results were affected by approximately EUR 90 thousand in one-off costs related to the public offering of shares and an income tax expense of EUR 384 thousand incurred in connection with dividends paid during the quarter. Total assets expanded by EUR 8,505 thousand during the second quarter to reach EUR 100,186 thousand at June 30, 2026, driven primarily by continued construction activity in development projects. Total loan liabilities reached EUR 57,341 thousand, including EUR 16,561 thousand in construction loans, after new bank construction loans of EUR 9,335 thousand were issued during the quarter. Cash and cash equivalents increased by EUR 1,979 thousand to EUR 10,033 thousand. 

A key milestone during the period was the initial public offering of Liven AS shares from April 23 to May 7, 2026, through which 1,053,936 new shares were issued at EUR 4.68 per share, generating net proceeds of EUR 4,687 thousand. Since May 15, 2026, the company shares have traded on the Baltic Main List of the Nasdaq Tallinn Stock Exchange under the ticker LVN1T. Furthermore, the group expanded its development portfolio through property acquisitions in Berlin and Tallinn, bringing its total saleable area to 136,506 square meters and forecast revenue to EUR 497.2 million by the end of June 2026.
