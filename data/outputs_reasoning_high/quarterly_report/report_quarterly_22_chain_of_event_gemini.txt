During the first half of 2026, Telia Lietuva, AB achieved solid financial performance with growth across key revenue and profitability metrics compared to the same period in 2025. Total revenue for the six-month period ended June 30, 2026, increased by 7.2 percent to EUR 259.1 million, up from EUR 241.7 million a year earlier. This growth was driven by increases across all major revenue streams, including fixed services, which rose by 5.3 percent to EUR 112.9 million, mobile services, which grew by 8.3 percent to EUR 100.0 million, and equipment sales, which increased by 9.6 percent to EUR 46.1 million. 

Profitability indicators showed notable improvement. EBITDA increased by 9.5 percent to EUR 105.5 million, compared to EUR 96.3 million in the first half of 2025, while adjusted EBITDA rose by 10.2 percent to EUR 106.9 million. Operating profit grew by 15.1 percent to EUR 62.4 million, and profit for the period increased by 14.7 percent to EUR 51.3 million, up from EUR 44.7 million. Basic earnings per share rose correspondingly by 14.7 percent to EUR 0.088. These earnings improvements occurred despite a corporate profit tax rate increase from 16 to 17 percent in Lithuania for 2026. 

Operating expenses excluding non-recurring items rose by 10.2 percent to EUR 67.3 million. This increase was influenced by a 14.3 percent rise in energy expenses, higher external IT service costs, and EUR 1.4 million in non-recurring redundancy expenses related to the restructuring of the company digital and technology unit. Cost of goods and services also increased by 2.3 percent to EUR 85.5 million due to higher equipment sales volumes and network costs. 

Capital investments surged by 57.9 percent to EUR 37.5 million, up from EUR 23.7 million, driven by EUR 9.7 million invested in 5G spectrum licenses, mobile network expansion, and IT infrastructure. Influencing events included the acquisition of 40 MHz of 1,500 MHz frequency blocks for nearly EUR 8 million and the extension of the 2,100 MHz frequency band for EUR 2 million in regulatory auctions to develop the 5G network, alongside the commencement of construction on a new data center near Vilnius. 

Free cash flow grew by 12.5 percent to EUR 64.1 million, and net cash generated by operating activities rose by 15.0 percent to EUR 103.1 million. Reflecting its strong financial position, the company distributed EUR 81.6 million in dividends, representing a record EUR 0.14 per share, for the year 2025 in May 2026. As of June 30, 2026, the company had no outstanding bank loans.
