During the first quarter of 2026, Nordic Fibreboard AS reported consolidated sales revenue of 1.86 million euros, representing a 7.3 percent increase compared to 1.74 million euros in the first quarter of 2025. This top-line growth was supported by an increase in order volumes from Finnish DIY stores, which grew by 241 thousand euros to 855 thousand euros. Geographically, European Union revenues reached 1.78 million euros, up from 1.64 million euros in the previous year. However, despite the revenue growth, the company's financial performance deteriorated significantly across major profitability indicators. The gross result turned negative at 105 thousand euros, down from a gross profit of 127 thousand euros in the same period of 2025. Consolidated EBITDA worsened to a negative 395 thousand euros with an EBITDA margin of negative 21 percent, compared to a negative 85 thousand euros and a negative 5 percent margin in the first quarter of 2025. The operating loss expanded to 530 thousand euros from 217 thousand euros, and the consolidated net loss for the period widened to 570 thousand euros from 228 thousand euros in the prior year period. Basic and diluted earnings per share both stood at negative 0.07 euros, compared to negative 0.05 euros in the first quarter of 2025. The decline in profitability was directly driven by several operational and macroeconomic factors, including a scheduled factory shutdown in early January for the transition to a new boiler house, the subsequent two-month calibration and start-up period for the new gas boiler house, and exceptionally cold weather conditions that increased normal energy consumption. Furthermore, a late February geopolitical conflict caused a sharp increase in energy prices, nearly doubling the market price of gas, which remained approximately 50 percent higher than at the beginning of the year. Consequently, electricity, heat, and water costs rose to 1.05 million euros from 725 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2025. In terms of financial position, total assets as of March 31, 2026, reached 9.69 million euros, compared to 8.72 million euros a year earlier. Total liabilities increased to 5.80 million euros, while total borrowings decreased slightly to 3.32 million euros from 3.81 million euros. Equity stood at 3.89 million euros at the end of March 2026, compared to 3.39 million euros as of March 31, 2025. The group employed 57 people at the end of the period, down from 69 employees a year prior, leading to lower total labour expenses of 411 thousand euros compared to 471 thousand euros. In real estate management, design works for the Admirali Quarter development project at Suur-Jõe 48 in Pärnu were completed and building permits were issued. Additionally, the company entered into a preliminary sale-purchase agreement on January 8, 2026, to sell ten properties on Suur-Jõe and Admirali streets, with the final transaction scheduled for completion no later than June 30, 2026.
