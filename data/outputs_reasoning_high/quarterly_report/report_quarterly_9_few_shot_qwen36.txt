Hepsor Group reported consolidated revenue of €35.4 million for 2025, a decline from €38.4 million in 2024. Net profit decreased to €1.0 million from €2.1 million, though net profit attributable to owners of the parent company remained stable at €0.4 million. Performance in the fourth quarter reflected typical cyclical fluctuations, with revenue falling to €4.5 million from €10.5 million and resulting in a net loss of €0.5 million compared to a net profit of €1.0 million in the prior year quarter.

The year-over-year deterioration in revenue and profitability was primarily caused by a lower volume of apartment completions, with 141 units handed over versus 194 in 2024. Gross profit margin narrowed to 15.9% from 17.6%, and marketing expenses increased to €1.3 million from €0.9 million, reflecting intensified sales activities associated with a larger portfolio of projects reaching the sales phase. Offsetting these pressures were higher interest income and €0.8 million in other operating income arising from reclassifying certain inventories to investment properties measured at fair value.

Underlying sales momentum improved markedly. The Group concluded primary sales agreements for 185 homes valued at €36.1 million, nearly double the volume recorded in 2024. The four-quarter moving average of initial sales grew 182% to €9 million. Development activity accelerated substantially; the number of residential apartments under construction rose 223% to 428 units by year-end, supported by the commencement of several key projects in Tallinn and Riga, including the Manufaktuuri Vabrik development with a construction contract value of €33.3 million.

Financial position indicators strengthened. Total interest-bearing loan liabilities declined to €47.7 million from €54.7 million, improving the debt ratio to 52.0% from 61.6% and the adjusted debt ratio to 37.0% from 45.0%. Total equity grew 23% to €27.1 million, driven principally by a non-cash contribution to the voluntary reserve of the joint venture Hepsor PHX5 OÜ. Cash flow from operations increased to €14.3 million. Financing activities included the successful public offering of €8.0 million in bonds, which was significantly oversubscribed, while the Group distributed €1.0 million in dividends.

Operational developments extended beyond residential sales. The Group secured long-term leases for the planned Peetri business centre and maintained strong occupancy across its commercial portfolio. In Canada, progress was made in land-use planning, including a decision granting increased building rights for the Weston Road project. For 2026, the Group anticipates launching construction on five new projects in Estonia and Latvia, benefiting from a stabilizing interest rate environment and improving market conditions.
