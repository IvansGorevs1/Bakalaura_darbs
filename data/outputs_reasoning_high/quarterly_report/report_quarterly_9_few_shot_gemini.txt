Hepsor AS reported lower financial performance for the twelve-month period ending December 31, 2025, compared to the previous year. Consolidated sales revenue declined by 7.8 percent to 35.4 million euros, down from 38.4 million euros in 2024. The decrease in revenue and overall profitability was primarily driven by a lower number of apartments handed over to customers, totaling 141 units compared to 194 units in the prior year. Net profit for the reporting period fell to 1.0 million euros from 2.1 million euros in 2024, while net profit attributable to the owners of the parent company remained stable at 0.4 million euros. For the fourth quarter of 2025, the company recorded revenue of 4.5 million euros and a net loss of 0.5 million euros, compared to revenue of 10.5 million euros and a net profit of 1.0 million euros in the corresponding period of the previous year.

Profitability was negatively impacted by reduced gross profit margins on sold development projects, which dropped to 15.9 percent from 17.6 percent in 2024, yielding a gross profit of 5.6 million euros compared to 6.8 million euros previously. Operating expenses increased as marketing expenses rose to 1.3 million euros from 0.9 million euros, driven by more intensive sales and marketing activities due to a larger number and scale of projects on sale. Operating profit for 2025 amounted to 3.4 million euros, down from 4.3 million euros in the previous year. Conversely, financial results benefited from higher other operating income, which reached 1.2 million euros compared to 0.4 million euros in 2024, largely supported by the reclassification of certain properties from inventories to investment property at fair value.

Despite weaker financial results, operational activity expanded significantly during 2025. The company concluded primary sales agreements for 185 homes with a total value of 36.1 million euros, nearly doubling the figures from 2024, supported by a stabilised interest rate environment. At year-end 2025, the group had 428 new homes under construction, representing a substantial increase from 192 homes at the end of 2024. In commercial real estate, the company advanced its StokOfiss U34 project in Riga, which achieved a 74 percent lease rate by the end of the year following a partial occupancy permit issued in July. In financing developments, Hepsor successfully completed a public bond offering in November 2025, raising 8.0 million euros at a fixed annual interest rate of 9.50 percent after strong investor demand led to an increase from the initial six million euro target. Total assets increased to 91.8 million euros at the end of 2025, while total interest-bearing loan liabilities decreased to 47.7 million euros from 54.7 million euros. Total equity rose by 23.0 percent to 27.1 million euros, supported by non-cash contributions to voluntary reserves from non-controlling interests, while the company distributed 1.0 million euros in dividends during the period.
