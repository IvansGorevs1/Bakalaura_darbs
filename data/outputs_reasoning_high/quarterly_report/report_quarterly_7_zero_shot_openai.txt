AS Silvano Fashion Group’s unaudited revenue for the six months ended 30 June 2026 was EUR 28.590 million, up 0.6% from EUR 28.428 million in the same period of 2025. Gross profit increased 5.3% to EUR 15.892 million, as cost of goods sold declined 4.8%. Gross margin improved to 55.6% from 53.1%.

Despite the stronger gross profit, operating profit decreased 3.5% to EUR 5.751 million, with the operating margin declining to 20.1% from 21.0%. EBITDA was broadly stable at EUR 7.508 million, down 0.8%, while the EBITDA margin decreased slightly to 26.3% from 26.6%. Net finance income fell to EUR 444 thousand from EUR 1.652 million, and income tax expense increased to EUR 1.870 million from EUR 1.322 million. As a result, consolidated net profit declined 31.2% to EUR 4.321 million. Net profit attributable to the parent company’s shareholders fell 31.5% to EUR 3.961 million, reducing earnings per share to EUR 0.11 from EUR 0.16 and the attributable net profit margin to 13.9% from 20.3%.

The business mix shifted toward retail. Retail revenue rose 13.6% to EUR 13.548 million and represented 47.4% of total sales, compared with 41.9% a year earlier. Wholesale revenue declined 9.1% to EUR 14.934 million, reducing its share of sales to 52.2% from 57.8%. Russia remained the largest market, generating EUR 15.767 million, down 0.6% in euros and 7.8% in local currency. Belarusian sales increased 6.4% in euros to EUR 9.046 million, or 31.6% of total sales, while sales in Ukraine and the Baltics declined.

Operating cash flow nearly doubled to EUR 6.842 million from EUR 3.481 million. At 30 June 2026, total assets were EUR 98.603 million, up 5.1% from year-end 2025, while equity attributable to parent-company shareholders rose 6.1% to EUR 81.479 million. Cash and cash equivalents increased 87.4% to EUR 12.443 million, in addition to EUR 38.707 million in short-term deposits. The Group operated 547 own and franchised stores, including 135 directly operated stores, two fewer than at year-end, and employed 1,594 people versus 1,639. The 2025 profit of EUR 9.229 million was left undistributed and transferred to retained earnings.
