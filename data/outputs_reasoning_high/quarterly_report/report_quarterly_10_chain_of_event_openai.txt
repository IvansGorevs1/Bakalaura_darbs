Infortar Group reported a strong increase in operating performance in 2025, although net profit declined because of one-off effects recorded in 2024. Full-year revenue rose to EUR 1.837 billion from EUR 1.372 billion, while EBITDA increased to EUR 232.6 million from EUR 145.4 million and the EBITDA margin improved from 10.6% to 13.4%. Operating profit rose to EUR 111.1 million from EUR 77.0 million. Net profit, however, fell to EUR 72.0 million from EUR 193.7 million, and profit attributable to the parent’s shareholders declined to EUR 72.7 million from EUR 191.3 million. Earnings per share decreased to EUR 3.54 from EUR 9.36. The report attributes this decline mainly to one-off effects related to the initial consolidation of Tallink in 2024.

Fourth-quarter performance also improved operationally. Revenue was EUR 417.4 million, compared with EUR 446.2 million in Q4 2024, but gross profit increased to EUR 63.3 million from EUR 34.9 million. EBITDA rose to EUR 42.6 million from EUR 27.9 million, and the Group moved from an operating loss of EUR 6.8 million to operating profit of EUR 19.8 million. Net profit was EUR 14.1 million, compared with a net loss of EUR 12.0 million a year earlier.

The energy segment supported the improvement. Although natural gas consumption in the Finnish-Baltic region fell 2%, Elenger’s gas and electricity sales volumes increased 7% to 4.8 TWh. Its Q4 EBITDA rose to EUR 12.7 million from negative EUR 2.2 million, while full-year EBITDA increased by approximately EUR 12 million to EUR 89.3 million. The maritime segment’s Q4 revenue increased 2.4% to EUR 188.0 million, EBITDA rose to EUR 27.6 million from EUR 25.7 million, and net profit improved to EUR 12.2 million from a EUR 5.2 million loss. Tallink’s Q4 results were affected by weak consumer and business confidence, geopolitical tensions, the sale of the Sailor cargo vessel, dividend and tax payments, and lower loan-related costs. For the full year, Tallink revenue and EBITDA declined to EUR 765.3 million and EUR 130.1 million, respectively, from EUR 785.8 million and EUR 175.2 million.

The Group’s stronger EBITDA partly reflects consolidation of Tallink for the full year in 2025, compared with August onward in 2024, as well as contributions from Estonia Farmid and Halinga. In Q4, Infortar Agro acquired Oisu Biogaas, expanding the agricultural and biomethane portfolio. Elenger also signed an agreement covering 12 LNG cargoes and continued developing renewable-energy storage.

Financial resilience improved: cash increased to EUR 219.8 million, net debt fell to EUR 840.7 million, and net debt to EBITDA improved to 3.6 from 4.0. The equity ratio rose to 45.5% from 43.0%, supported by loan repayments and disciplined inventory management.
