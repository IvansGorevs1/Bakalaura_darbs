Hepsor’s performance weakened in 2025 compared with 2024, although its development pipeline and sales activity strengthened. Consolidated revenue declined to €35.4 million from €38.4 million, while gross profit fell to €5.6 million from €6.8 million. The gross profit margin decreased to 15.9% from 17.6%, reflecting lower margins on projects sold. Operating profit was €3.4 million, compared with €4.3 million, and the operating profit margin fell to 9.5% from 11.3%. Net profit declined to €1.0 million from €2.1 million, with €0.4 million attributable to the owners of the parent, broadly unchanged from 2024. Earnings per share decreased to €0.10 from €0.11.

Fourth-quarter results were substantially weaker. Revenue fell to €4.5 million from €10.5 million, gross profit to €0.7 million from €2.5 million, and operating profit to €0.3 million from €2.0 million. The Group recorded a net loss of €0.5 million, compared with a €1.0 million profit in the fourth quarter of 2024. The quarterly net profit margin was -10.7%, versus 9.1% a year earlier. Fourth-quarter results included €0.8 million of other operating income from revaluing properties transferred from inventories to investment property.

The main pressure on profitability was the reduction in apartments handed over to customers, to 141 from 194, together with lower project margins and higher marketing expenses of €1.3 million versus €0.9 million. Finance and personnel expenses remained at approximately the previous year’s levels, while finance income increased to €0.7 million from €0.4 million. Estonia remained the larger market, generating €26.1 million of revenue versus €32.5 million, while Latvian revenue increased to €9.3 million from €5.9 million. Sales momentum improved: primary sales agreements covered 185 homes worth €36.1 million, compared with 109 homes worth €19.8 million in 2024. The four-quarter rolling average of initial sales increased 182% to €9 million.

The Group expanded its portfolio by six residential projects. Active projects rose to 33 from 24, with sellable area increasing to approximately 196,650 square metres from 172,500 square metres. Homes under construction increased 223% to 428. Major developments included construction starts in Riga and the Manufaktuuri projects in Tallinn. Hepsor also secured building rights for its first Toronto project, increasing planned volume from 27,000 to 62,000 square metres.

At year-end, total assets were €91.8 million and equity €27.1 million, compared with €88.8 million and €22.0 million respectively. Interest-bearing loans decreased to €47.7 million from €54.7 million, reducing the debt ratio to 52.0% from 61.6%, while cash fell to €3.8 million from €6.2 million. In November, Hepsor raised €8.0 million through bonds carrying a 9.50% annual interest rate.
