Ambu had a solid start to 2025/26, with Q1 revenue increasing to DKK 1,558m from DKK 1,510m. Organic growth was 8.6%, down from 19.5% in the same quarter last year, while reported growth was 3.2% compared with 20.4%. Endoscopy Solutions remained the main growth engine, delivering 14.4% organic growth despite high comparables. Urology, ENT, & GI grew 21.0%, primarily driven by continued adoption of the aScope 4 portfolio and strong momentum for aScope 5 Uretero, while Respiratory grew 8.3%, supported by bronchoscopy solutions, aScope 5 Broncho adoption, and the launch of SureSight Connect. Anesthesia & Patient Monitoring was broadly unchanged, with organic growth of -0.1% compared with 17.8% last year, reflecting an exceptionally high prior-year revenue base.

Growth was solid in North America and Europe, which recorded organic growth of 9.8% and 9.7%, respectively. Rest of World declined 1.6% organically due to order fluctuations and high comparable revenue from the prior year. Currency effects reduced total reported growth by 5.4%.

Profitability declined compared with Q1 2024/25. EBIT before special items fell to DKK 164m from DKK 243m, and the EBIT margin decreased to 10.5% from 16.1%. EBITDA before special items declined to DKK 261m from DKK 331m. Gross profit increased to DKK 947m, but the gross margin fell slightly to 60.8% from 61.3%, while the operating expense ratio increased to 50.3% from 45.2%. Management attributed the lower EBIT margin primarily to tariff costs of more than DKK 50m and foreign-exchange headwinds. Excluding these external effects, the EBIT margin was 15.2%, reflecting continued underlying operating leverage. Profit before tax decreased to DKK 153m from DKK 237m, and net profit fell to DKK 118m from DKK 183m.

Free cash flow before acquisitions declined to DKK 13m from DKK 69m. The reduction was mainly associated with lower EBITDA and elevated net working capital, maintained to mitigate geopolitical uncertainties and support growth; FX effects and tariff costs also weighed on cash flow. No acquisitions were made during the quarter. Ambu paid dividends and initiated its share buyback program, purchasing DKK 38m of treasury shares.

The company launched its ZOOM AHEAD strategy and introduced SureSight Mobile, a portable video laryngoscope expanding its Respiratory offering. Ambu maintained its full-year guidance for 10–13% organic revenue growth, a 12–14% EBIT margin before special items, and cash conversion above 40%, with growth and margin expected to be higher in the second half of the year.
