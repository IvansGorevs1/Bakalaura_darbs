Amber Grid AB reported unaudited results for the three months ended 31 March 2026. Revenue increased 58% to EUR 32.821 million from EUR 20.763 million in the corresponding 2025 period. Revenue from natural gas transmission and related services rose 58%, supported by a 35% increase in transmitted gas volumes and higher transmission prices set for 2026. In contrast, revenue from system balancing products fell 99%, following the signing of a new transit agreement with Kaliningrad, which significantly reduced the need for balancing gas.

Profitability improved substantially. EBITDA increased to EUR 23.304 million from EUR 8.918 million, while operating profit rose to EUR 19.627 million from EUR 5.185 million. Profit before tax reached EUR 19.001 million, compared with EUR 4.987 million, and net profit increased to EUR 15.763 million from EUR 4.239 million. Earnings per share rose to EUR 0.09 from EUR 0.02. The improvement was supported by lower operating expenses, which declined to EUR 9.570 million from EUR 11.867 million. Natural gas purchases fell 68% to EUR 1.477 million, mainly because balancing gas costs decreased 99% after the Kaliningrad agreement, although gas costs for technological needs increased 118% due to a 2.7-fold rise in demand.

Adjusted EBITDA was broadly stable at EUR 8.285 million, compared with EUR 8.260 million in the prior-year period. The difference between reported and adjusted performance reflected management adjustments for temporary regulatory differences of EUR 15.019 million in 2026, compared with EUR 0.658 million in 2025.

Operating cash flow increased to EUR 22.927 million from EUR 8.264 million. Investing cash outflows rose to EUR 3.570 million from EUR 1.308 million, mainly reflecting higher additions to property, plant and equipment and intangible assets. Financing cash outflows increased to EUR 20.467 million from EUR 6.981 million, including a EUR 17.845 million reduction in overdraft. Consequently, cash and cash equivalents declined from EUR 1.942 million at year-end 2025 to EUR 0.832 million at 31 March 2026.

Financial position changes were also affected by debt restructuring. Total borrowings decreased to EUR 104.174 million from EUR 123.666 million at 31 December 2025, while non-current borrowings increased as liabilities were reclassified following the expiry of the Nordic Investment Bank agreement. On 25 February 2026, EPSO-G assumed the remaining EUR 9.783 million NIB loan, and Amber Grid was no longer subject to those financial covenants. Equity increased to EUR 182.039 million from EUR 166.276 million, primarily through the period’s net profit. After the reporting period, shareholders approved EUR 9.989 million of dividends for 2025.
