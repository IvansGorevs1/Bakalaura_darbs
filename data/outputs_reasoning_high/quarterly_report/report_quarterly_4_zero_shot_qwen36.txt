Ambu A/S reported a solid start to its 2025/26 fiscal year, with first-quarter revenue reaching DKK 1,558 million. The company achieved 8.6 percent organic revenue growth against a high comparative base, resulting in 3.2 percent reported growth after a 5.4 percent foreign exchange headwind. Endoscopy Solutions led expansion with 14.4 percent organic growth to DKK 986 million. The Urology, ENT, and GI division surged 21.0 percent organically, while Respiratory grew 8.3 percent, supported by aScope 5 Broncho adoption. Anesthesia and Patient Monitoring posted flat organic growth of negative 0.1 percent, weighed down by exceptionally strong prior-year revenues. North America and Europe each expanded organically by roughly 9.7 to 9.8 percent, whereas Rest of World declined 1.6 percent due to order volatility.

Profitability faced near-term external pressures. EBIT before special items stood at DKK 164 million, yielding a 10.5 percent margin compared to 16.1 percent last year. The contraction was directly caused by tariff costs exceeding DKK 50 million and foreign exchange losses. After adjusting for these factors, the underlying EBIT margin reached 15.2 percent, demonstrating continued operating leverage. Gross margin decreased to 60.8 percent from 61.3 percent, primarily due to a stronger comparable period last year. Net profit declined to DKK 118 million, or DKK 0.44 per share. Cash generation weakened, with free cash flow before acquisitions falling to DKK 13 million from DKK 69 million. This reduction was driven by elevated net working capital, as inventory was increased to buffer geopolitical uncertainties and fund growth.

Operational developments centered on the ZOOM AHEAD strategy implementation and the launch of the SureSight Mobile video laryngoscope. Sustainability efforts advanced with the Recircle program extension and a 33 percent drop in carbon emissions per tonne of finished goods. The company returned capital through a DKK 90 million dividend and DKK 38 million in treasury share purchases.

Management maintains full-year guidance, projecting 10 to 13 percent organic revenue growth and a 12 to 14 percent EBIT margin before special items. Excluding an assumed two-percentage-point tariff impact, the margin target rises to 14 to 16 percent. Cash conversion is forecast above 40 percent, with leadership anticipating stronger growth and profitability in the second half of the fiscal year.
