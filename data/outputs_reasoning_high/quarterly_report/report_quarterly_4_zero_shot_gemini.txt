Ambu delivered a solid start to the 2025/26 financial year with continued strong growth momentum. During the first quarter ending December 31, 2025, total revenue reached 1,558 million kroner, representing a reported growth of 3.2 percent and organic growth of 8.6 percent compared to 1,510 million kroner in the same period of the previous year. 

Endoscopy Solutions remained the primary growth engine, contributing 63 percent of total revenue with revenue of 986 million kroner and organic growth of 14.4 percent. The Urology, ENT, and GI business group posted organic growth of 21.0 percent and revenue of 499 million kroner, driven by the aScope 4 portfolio, aScope 5 Uretero adoption, and order timings. The Respiratory business group achieved 8.3 percent organic growth with revenue of 487 million kroner, supported by aScope 5 Broncho adoption and the SureSight Connect launch. Meanwhile, Anesthesia and Patient Monitoring generated flat organic growth of minus 0.1 percent and revenue of 572 million kroner, representing 37 percent of total revenue. This flat performance reflected extraordinarily high comparable revenue last year, with Anesthesia declining by 1.2 percent and Patient Monitoring growing by 1.1 percent. 

Geographically, organic revenue growth reached 9.8 percent in North America with revenue of 764 million kroner and 9.7 percent in Europe with revenue of 653 million kroner. The Rest of the World declined organically by 1.6 percent to 141 million kroner due to order fluctuations and high comparative baselines. 

Operating profit before special items was 164 million kroner, down from 243 million kroner in the prior-year period. The EBIT margin before special items decreased to 10.5 percent from 16.1 percent, negatively impacted by tariff costs exceeding 50 million kroner and foreign exchange headwinds. Adjusting for these external factors, the underlying EBIT margin reached 15.2 percent. Net profit for the period totaled 118 million kroner compared to 183 million kroner last year, resulting in earnings per share of 0.44 kroner against 0.68 kroner previously. 

Cash flow from operating activities was 106 million kroner, down from 144 million kroner, while free cash flow before acquisitions dropped to 13 million kroner from 69 million kroner, affected by net working capital changes, foreign exchange headwinds, and tariffs. Ambu paid out 90 million kroner in dividends and deployed 38 million kroner to acquire treasury shares under its share buyback program. Management maintained its full-year 2025/26 financial guidance, continuing to expect organic revenue growth of 10 to 13 percent and an EBIT margin before special items of 12 to 14 percent.
