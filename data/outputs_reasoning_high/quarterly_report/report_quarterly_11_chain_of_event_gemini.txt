Kvika banki hf. reported its financial results for the second quarter of 2026, showing a pre-tax profit of ISK 1,545 million, a decrease of 23.7 percent compared to ISK 2,025 million in the second quarter of 2025. Profit after tax for Q2 2026 stood at ISK 903 million, down from ISK 1,439 million year-on-year. For the first six months of 2026, pre-tax profit from continuing operations increased by 23.0 percent to ISK 3,354 million, supported by solid net interest income growth and cost management.

Net interest income for Q2 2026 reached ISK 3,013 million, a 1.7 percent increase year-on-year, though it decreased sequentially from ISK 3,266 million in the first quarter of 2026 due to lower inflation impacting Kvika's positive inflation balance and an ISK 10.2 billion dividend payment in June. For the six-month period, net interest income rose by 6.8 percent to ISK 6,279 million. Conversely, net fee and commission income fell by 26.8 percent year-on-year to ISK 1,416 million, reflecting subdued capital market activity. Other net operating income improved to ISK 327 million from ISK 231 million, bringing total net operating income for Q2 2026 to ISK 4,756 million, down 7.3 percent year-on-year.

Administrative expenses amounted to ISK 2,947 million in Q2 2026, marking a 1.1 percent decrease year-on-year despite a one-off restructuring charge of ISK 112 million from cost efficiency measures implemented in June. These measures are expected to lower the annual cost base by ISK 300 to 400 million. Net impairment losses rose to ISK 269 million compared to ISK 122 million in Q2 2025.

Total assets grew by 9.5 percent from year-end 2025 to ISK 375.7 billion at the end of June 2026. Loans to customers increased by 7.6 percent to ISK 223.3 billion, driven by domestic corporate banking and vehicle and equipment lending. Total deposits surpassed ISK 200 billion for the first time, rising 16.5 percent from year-end 2025 to ISK 201.2 billion, supported by retail and public entity inflows. Market funding increased to ISK 101.7 billion following senior unsecured and additional tier one bond issuances, including a EUR 150 million senior preferred issuance in June.

The bank maintained strong liquidity and capital metrics, with a liquidity coverage ratio of 276 percent and a net stable funding ratio of 137 percent at the end of June 2026. The capital adequacy ratio closed the quarter at 23.0 percent following the June special dividend payout, while the total capital requirement decreased to 17.7 percent. Additionally, the sale process for Straumur greiðslumiðlun hf. is in progress with due diligence underway, and Chief Executive Officer Ármann Þorvaldsson announced plans to step down by the end of 2026.
