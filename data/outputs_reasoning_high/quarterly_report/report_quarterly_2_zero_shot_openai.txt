Amber Grid AB reported a strong improvement in financial performance for the three months ended 31 March 2026, although the interim statements are unaudited. Revenue increased to EUR 32.821 million from EUR 20.763 million in the corresponding period of 2025, while revenue and other income rose to EUR 32.874 million from EUR 20.785 million. Revenue from natural gas transmission and related services increased by 58%, supported by a 35% increase in transmitted gas volumes and higher transmission prices set for 2026.

Revenue from system balancing products declined by 99%, following the signing of a new transit agreement to Kaliningrad that significantly reduced the need for balancing gas. This was accompanied by a 68% reduction in natural gas costs, as balancing product costs fell by 99%. In contrast, gas costs for technological needs increased by 118%, reflecting a 2.7-fold increase in demand for those needs.

Operating expenses excluding depreciation, impairment and write-offs decreased to EUR 9.570 million from EUR 11.867 million. As a result, EBITDA rose to EUR 23.304 million from EUR 8.918 million, while EBIT increased to EUR 19.627 million from EUR 5.185 million. However, adjusted EBITDA was broadly unchanged at EUR 8.285 million, compared with EUR 8.260 million in the prior-year period. The difference reflects management adjustments for temporary regulatory differences, which had a negative overall effect of EUR 15.019 million in 2026 compared with EUR 0.658 million in 2025.

Net finance costs increased to EUR 0.626 million from EUR 0.408 million, and income tax expense rose to EUR 3.238 million from EUR 0.748 million. Profit before tax nevertheless increased to EUR 19.001 million from EUR 4.987 million, and net profit rose to EUR 15.763 million from EUR 4.239 million. Basic and diluted earnings per share increased to EUR 0.09 from EUR 0.02.

Operating cash flow strengthened to EUR 22.927 million from EUR 8.264 million. Investing cash outflow increased to EUR 3.570 million, mainly reflecting EUR 2.837 million of additions to property, plant and equipment and intangible assets. Financing cash outflow was EUR 20.467 million, including a EUR 17.845 million change in overdraft, leaving cash of EUR 0.832 million at 31 March 2026, compared with EUR 1.942 million at year-end 2025.

Total assets remained broadly stable at EUR 324.064 million, while equity increased to EUR 182.039 million. Net debt was EUR 106.610 million, higher than EUR 76.052 million at 31 March 2025. A EUR 5.815 million provision for potential repayment of GIPL guarantee funds remained unchanged. After the reporting period, shareholders approved EUR 9.989 million of dividends.
