During the twelve-month financial year ended June 30, 2025, SAF Tehnika reported an unaudited consolidated net turnover of EUR 26.11 million, representing a 4% decline compared with EUR 27.09 million in the previous financial year. Despite the decrease in revenue, the company significantly reduced its bottom-line losses. The Group closed the reporting period with a net loss of EUR 820 thousand, which is an improvement compared with the net loss of EUR 2.37 million recorded for the 2023/2024 financial year. Basic earnings per share improved to negative EUR 0.28 from negative EUR 0.80 in the prior year.

For the fourth quarter of the 2024/2025 financial year, net turnover reached EUR 5.18 million, marking a 13% decrease compared with the corresponding period of the previous year. The fourth-quarter net loss widened to EUR 913 thousand from a loss of EUR 498 thousand in the fourth quarter of the preceding financial year. Management noted that quarterly turnover fluctuations are frequently influenced by the completion timing, scope, and complexity of individual projects, along with production durations and material procurement lead times.

Geographically, performance varied across regions during the twelve-month period. Revenues in the North and Latin America region declined by 14% to EUR 14.28 million, accounting for 55% of total turnover. Conversely, European revenues increased by 17% to EUR 10.04 million, representing 38% of total sales. The Asia, Africa, and Middle East region generated EUR 1.78 million, reflecting a 5% decrease and comprising 7% of total revenue.

Throughout the period, the Group continued to invest in new product development, product enhancements, and sales promotion across existing and new market segments. Operations were previously impacted by global shortages of electronic components, prompting the company to accumulate material reserves to ensure competitive delivery times, a strategy that has helped delivery timelines improve recently. Total assets increased to EUR 25.92 million as of June 30, 2025, compared with EUR 23.02 million at the end of the previous financial year. Total owners equity stood at EUR 15.58 million at the end of the period.

The Group maintained a strong liquidity position, generating a net cash flow of EUR 2.6 million over the twelve-month period and closing the year with a cash and bank balance of EUR 4.8 million. Customer prepayments for goods and services reached EUR 3.9 million. To further support liquidity, the parent company maintained an active, unutilized credit line agreement with Luminor Bank AS for EUR 4.95 million. Total headcount rose slightly to 273 employees at the end of the reporting period, compared with 265 employees at the close of the prior financial year. Management remains cautious regarding specific forecasts while actively focusing on product diversification and stabilizing turnover to achieve long-term profitability.
