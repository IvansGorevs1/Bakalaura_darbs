Infortar Group reported total revenue of EUR 1,837.0 million for the twelve months ended December 31, 2025, rising substantially from EUR 1,371.8 million in the prior year. EBITDA increased by approximately 60 percent to EUR 232.6 million, and operating profit grew to EUR 111.1 million. Headline net profit declined to EUR 72.0 million from EUR 193.7 million, a decrease the document explicitly attributes to one-off financial effects and fair value adjustments stemming from the initial consolidation of Tallink Grupp in 2024. Total assets reached EUR 2,587.8 million while total equity stood at EUR 1,177.5 million, raising the equity ratio to 45.5 percent from 43.0 percent. Interest-bearing liabilities decreased to EUR 1,060.5 million from EUR 1,223.3 million due to systematic debt repayment across group entities.

The marked improvement in core operating metrics was primarily driven by consolidating Tallink Grupp results for a full twelve months in 2025, compared to partial consolidation starting in August 2024. The energy segment also demonstrated resilience, with Elenger Grupp increasing gas and electricity sales volumes by seven percent in the fourth quarter to 4.8 terawatt-hours despite a two percent decline in regional natural gas consumption. This volume growth enabled the company to maintain a twenty-one percent market share in the Finnish-Baltic region. Conversely, the maritime transportation segment faced notable headwinds over the full year. Tallink transported 5.53 million passengers, marking a slight year-on-year decrease, and saw a sharper drop in cargo volumes. Full-year maritime revenue fell to EUR 765.3 million and EBITDA contracted to EUR 130.1 million. The report identifies mounting geopolitical tensions, subdued domestic confidence, the sale of the cargo vessel Sailor, and large dividend distributions as primary factors impacting maritime profitability. Despite the annual decline, the segment posted a quarterly recovery, generating EUR 12.2 million in net profit during the fourth quarter compared to a loss the previous year.

Strategic portfolio expansion continued through the acquisition of OÜ Oisu Biogaas and ongoing investments in agricultural and biomethane production, diversifying revenue streams away from cyclical sectors. Capital deployment prioritized maintenance upgrades for cruise ferries, technical electronic enhancements, and infrastructure projects including a EUR 67.2 million contract for the Rail Baltica mainline. Operating cash flow remained strong at EUR 388.6 million, reflecting disciplined inventory management and normalized working capital following the absence of extensive acquisition-related accounting adjustments in 2025. The group maintained adequate liquidity reserves of EUR 219.8 million and reinforced its balance sheet positioning, allowing management to uphold its dividend policy while navigating persistent macroeconomic and energy market volatility.
