AB Pieno Žvaigždės reported a substantial improvement in profitability in the first three months of 2026, despite slightly lower revenue. Revenue was EUR 52.064 million, compared with EUR 52.324 million in the same period of 2025. Cost of sales decreased from EUR 42.530 million to EUR 38.078 million, increasing gross profit from EUR 9.794 million to EUR 13.986 million. Other operating income also rose to EUR 363 thousand from EUR 28 thousand. Sales and administrative expenses increased from EUR 9.287 million to EUR 10.983 million, but operating profit before finance costs still rose significantly, from EUR 535 thousand to EUR 3.366 million.

Net finance expenses improved modestly, from EUR 308 thousand to EUR 298 thousand. As a result, profit before tax and net profit increased from EUR 227 thousand in the first quarter of 2025 to EUR 3.068 million in the first quarter of 2026. No corporate income tax was reported in either period. Earnings per share increased from EUR 0.01 to EUR 0.07, while EBITDA rose from EUR 2.001 million to EUR 4.730 million. The statements show that the stronger result was achieved through the lower cost of sales and higher other operating income, although the report does not identify specific operational or market causes for these changes.

Cash generation also improved. Net cash flow from operating activities changed from an outflow of EUR 183 thousand in the prior-year period to an inflow of EUR 1.625 million. Cash flows from ordinary activities before working-capital changes increased to EUR 4.336 million from EUR 1.367 million. Investing cash outflow declined to EUR 551 thousand from EUR 1.058 million, while financing activities used EUR 723 thousand, compared with a EUR 4.133 million inflow in 2025, when the company received EUR 8.5 million in loans. Cash and cash equivalents rose by EUR 351 thousand during the quarter to EUR 2.157 million.

At 31 March 2026, total assets were EUR 85.305 million, up from EUR 83.475 million at year-end 2025. Equity increased to EUR 41.164 million from EUR 38.238 million, mainly reflecting the quarter’s net profit. Total liabilities declined to EUR 44.141 million from EUR 45.237 million. The current ratio improved from 1.27 to 1.51, and book value per share increased from EUR 0.89 to EUR 0.91. Sales remained concentrated in Lithuania, the EU and non-EU markets, with fresh milk products continuing to account for the majority of sales. No material post-balance-sheet events were reported.
