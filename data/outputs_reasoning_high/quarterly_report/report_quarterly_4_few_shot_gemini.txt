Ambu A/S delivered a solid start to the 2025/26 financial year, characterized by continued growth momentum across its core business areas. Total revenue for the first quarter reached 1,558 million Danish kroner, representing organic growth of 8.6% and reported growth of 3.2%, which was tempered by negative foreign exchange impacts of 5.4%. 

The primary growth engine remained Endoscopy Solutions, generating revenue of 986 million Danish kroner, accounting for 63% of total sales, and achieving organic growth of 14.4%. This performance was led by the Urology, ENT, and GI business group with 21.0% organic growth, driven by strong momentum in urology and ongoing adoption of the aScope portfolio. The Respiratory business group contributed 8.3% organic growth, supported by steady adoption of bronchoscopy solutions and the introduction of new video laryngoscopes such as SureSight Mobile. In contrast, the Anesthesia and Patient Monitoring segment posted flat organic growth of -0.1%, yielding 572 million Danish kroner in revenue, largely due to exceptionally high comparable sales from the previous year. 

Geographically, growth was robust in core markets, with North America and Europe posting organic revenue increases of 9.8% and 9.7%, respectively, while the Rest of World region declined organically by 1.6%. 

Profitability metrics experienced headwinds compared to the previous year. Operating profit, or EBIT before special items, dropped to 164 million Danish kroner from 243 million Danish kroner, yielding an EBIT margin before special items of 10.5% compared to 16.1% previously. This margin compression was driven by tariff costs exceeding 50 million Danish kroner and adverse foreign exchange developments. Adjusted for these external factors, the underlying EBIT margin reached 15.2%, reflecting continued operational leverage. Gross profit stood at 947 million Danish kroner with a margin of 60.8%, slightly down from 61.3%. Net profit declined to 118 million Danish kroner from 183 million Danish kroner, and earnings per share decreased to 0.44 Danish kroner from 0.68 Danish kroner. 

Free cash flow before acquisitions fell to 13 million Danish kroner from 69 million Danish kroner, weighed down by changes in net working capital, which stood at 1,321 million Danish kroner to mitigate global supply chain uncertainties. During the quarter, the company distributed 90 million Danish kroner in dividends and deployed 38 million Danish kroner toward share buybacks. 

Looking ahead, Ambu maintained its full-year 2025/26 financial guidance, projecting total organic revenue growth between 10% and 13% and an EBIT margin before special items of 12% to 14%.
