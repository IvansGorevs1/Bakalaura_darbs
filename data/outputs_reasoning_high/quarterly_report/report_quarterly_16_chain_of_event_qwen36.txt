Nordic Fibreboard AS reported consolidated net revenue of €1,861 thousand for the first quarter of 2026, a 7.3% increase from €1,735 thousand in the prior year period. This growth was driven by an increase in orders from Finnish DIY stores, which contributed €241 thousand. Revenue from fibreboard sales formed the core of the Group's activity. Sales within the European Union rose to €1,781 thousand from €1,639 thousand, while revenues from Africa declined to €62 thousand from €87 thousand. The Group experienced a substantial deterioration in profitability, recording a consolidated EBITDA loss of €395 thousand compared to a loss of €85 thousand in the first quarter of 2025. The EBITDA margin contracted to negative twenty-one percent from negative five percent. Cost of goods sold rose sharply to €1,966 thousand from €1,608 thousand, resulting in a gross loss of €105 thousand versus a gross profit of €127 thousand previously. Management attributed the negative results to the scheduled shutdown of the factory in early January for the transition to a new boiler house, where calibration and start-up operations extended over approximately two months. Additionally, cold weather increased energy consumption. Crucially, the United States military attack on Iran in late February triggered a sharp surge in energy prices, causing the market price of gas to nearly double within the month. Although prices subsequently declined, gas rates remained approximately fifty percent higher than at the beginning of the year. Consequently, electricity, heat, and water expenses escalated from €725 thousand to €1,048 thousand. Labour costs including taxes decreased to €411 thousand from €471 thousand, aligning with a reduction in employee headcount to fifty-seven from sixty-nine. The consolidated net loss expanded to €570 thousand against a loss of €228 thousand in the comparative period. Despite the accounting losses, operating cash flow improved to a generation of €13 thousand from a net cash usage of €528 thousand, influenced significantly by increases in trade payables. Total assets stood at €9,694 thousand with total liabilities at €5,802 thousand. In its real estate segment, the Group advanced the Admirali Quarter project with design completion and permit issuance, and entered a preliminary agreement to sell ten properties by mid-2026.
