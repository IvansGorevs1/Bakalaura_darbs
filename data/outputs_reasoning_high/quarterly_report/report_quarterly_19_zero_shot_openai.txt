AS Pro Kapital Grupp reported a substantial improvement in performance in 2025. Revenue increased to EUR 53.2 million from EUR 18.2 million in 2024, while fourth-quarter revenue rose to EUR 18.9 million from EUR 7.6 million. The increase was primarily driven by the handover of completed apartments in Tallinn’s Kalaranna and Uus-Kindrali developments and in the City Villas project in Vilnius. Real estate sales revenue reached EUR 45.4 million, compared with EUR 10.7 million a year earlier.

Gross profit rose to EUR 18.8 million from EUR 5.4 million, with the gross margin improving from 30% to 35%. Operating profit increased to EUR 14.7 million from EUR 0.1 million, and the operating margin rose from 1% to 28%. Net profit was EUR 12.0 million, compared with a EUR 3.9 million loss in 2024, resulting in earnings per share of EUR 0.22 versus a loss per share of EUR 0.06. Fourth-quarter net profit was EUR 6.0 million, compared with EUR 0.2 million in the corresponding period.

Other operating income included a EUR 3.5 million fair-value gain on investment property, compared with EUR 1.1 million in 2024. This was partly offset by a EUR 0.9 million impairment of goodwill related to the Italian Preatoni Nuda Proprietà business. Finance costs declined to EUR 2.6 million from EUR 4.3 million; the 2024 figure included a EUR 1.4 million loss from modification of a financial liability.

Estonia accounted for 75% of total revenue, compared with 27% in 2024, reflecting the increased apartment handovers. Latvia’s share fell to 0.3% from 25% as its remaining inventory was largely sold, while Lithuania contributed 12% of revenue and Germany 11%. Hotel revenue was broadly stable at EUR 6.1 million, but occupancy declined to 65% from 67% and the hotel’s net operating profit decreased to EUR 0.4 million from EUR 0.5 million. Italian operations faced subdued real-estate market activity and reported a loss.

Operating cash flow improved to EUR 10.8 million from cash outflow of EUR 10.4 million in 2024. Cash increased to EUR 5.1 million. Total equity rose to EUR 63.3 million from EUR 51.2 million, liabilities declined to EUR 61.2 million from EUR 67.5 million, and the debt-to-equity ratio improved to 0.97 from 1.32. Net asset value per share increased to EUR 1.12 from EUR 0.89.

During the year, Uus-Kindrali’s first building and Kalaranna were completed or substantially completed, Blue Marine in Riga entered construction, and the Group repaid EUR 9.4 million of secured bonds. It also acquired an additional 10% of Preatoni Nuda Proprietà, increasing its ownership to 77.5%.
