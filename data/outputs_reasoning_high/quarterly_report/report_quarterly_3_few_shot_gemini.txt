During the first three months of 2026, AS Amber Latvijas balzams experienced a significant deterioration in financial performance, driven by severe external challenges, geopolitical pressures, and disruptions within its affiliated corporate group. In January 2026, the commencement of bankruptcy proceedings for related group company Stoli Group under Chapter 11 in the United States caused the Company to lose control over operations within that jurisdiction, directly impacting the solvency of its largest customer and its own capacity to meet creditor obligations. Additional headwinds included adverse actions by Russian authorities against group entities following public support for Ukraine, alongside a broader downturn in the global spirits market. These combined pressures severely strained cash flow and resulted in overdue excise tax liabilities, prompting the State Revenue Service to freeze the Company bank accounts. To ensure business continuity and protect creditors, the Company filed for legal protection proceedings, which were approved by the Riga City Court in February 2026 to facilitate liability restructuring and operational stabilization.

Financial results for the reporting period reflected sharp declines across key indicators. Total sales volume dropped by 36.1% to 517,600 nine-liter cases, down from 810,079 cases in the corresponding period of 2025. Unaudited net turnover declined by 29.6% to EUR 11.1 million, compared with EUR 15.8 million in the prior-year period, with the decrease attributed to lower sales volumes and a high comparison base from the previous year that had benefited from contractual revenue adjustments. Gross profit fell by 66.5% to EUR 1.2 million, down from EUR 3.5 million, driven by unfavorable changes in product mix and pricing methodology adjustments. Consequently, the Company reported an operating loss of EUR 1.6 million for the first three months of 2026, contrasting sharply with an operating profit of EUR 0.6 million in the first quarter of 2025, while the operating profit margin dropped from 3.7% to negative 14.5%. Net results similarly worsened, shifting from a net profit of EUR 0.9 million in the prior year to a net loss of EUR 1.4 million for the reporting period. Basic earnings per share worsened to negative EUR 0.19 from positive EUR 0.11.

In response to these challenging conditions, management continued to implement cost-reduction measures and initiatives aimed at improving production efficiency and commercial performance. Furthermore, subsequent events included judicial reorganization proceedings initiated by the principal shareholder in Luxembourg, alongside notices regarding secured debt obligations from creditors. Management remains focused on stabilizing operations, navigating ongoing restructuring frameworks, and strengthening financial governance to secure sustainable long-term development.
