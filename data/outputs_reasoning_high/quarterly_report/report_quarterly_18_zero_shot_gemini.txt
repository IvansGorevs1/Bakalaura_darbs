During the first three months of 2026, AB Pieno Žvaigždės recorded total revenue of 52,064 thousand euros, showing a slight decrease compared to 52,324 thousand euros reported in the corresponding period of 2025. Despite the minor drop in sales, the company achieved a substantial improvement in profitability. Cost of sales decreased significantly to 38,078 thousand euros from 42,530 thousand euros in the previous year, which drove a sharp rise in gross profit to 13,986 thousand euros compared to 9,794 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2025. Other operating income, net, stood at 363 thousand euros, up from 28 thousand euros, while sales and administrative expenses increased to 10,983 thousand euros from 9,287 thousand euros. Consequently, the operating profit before finance costs surged to 3,366 thousand euros, compared to 535 thousand euros during the same period in 2025. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization reached 4,730 thousand euros, compared to 2,001 thousand euros previously. Net financial expenses remained relatively stable at 298 thousand euros compared to 308 thousand euros. As a result, the company reported a net profit of 3,068 thousand euros for the three months ended March 31, 2026, marking a dramatic increase from the net profit of 227 thousand euros recorded in the first three months of 2025. Net profit per share correspondingly rose to 0.07 euros from 0.01 euros, and the book value per share at the end of the period was 0.91 euros compared to 0.89 euros at the end of March 2025. Regarding the financial position, total assets increased to 85,305 thousand euros as of March 31, 2026, from 83,475 thousand euros at the end of December 2025. Total equity grew to 41,164 thousand euros from 38,238 thousand euros, driven by an increase in retained earnings to 18,614 thousand euros from 15,688 thousand euros. Total liabilities decreased to 44,141 thousand euros from 45,237 thousand euros over the same timeframe. Cash flows also showed a positive development, with net cash flow from operating activities turning positive at 1,625 thousand euros compared to a negative 183 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2025. Net cash flow used in investing activities was 551 thousand euros, down from 1,058 thousand euros, while net cash used in financing activities was 723 thousand euros compared to a positive 4,133 thousand euros previously. An analysis of sales by geographical markets shows that domestic sales in Lithuania were 31,405 thousand euros compared to 32,112 thousand euros in the prior year period, sales in other European Union countries rose to 11,341 thousand euros from 10,801 thousand euros, and sales in other non-European Union countries were 9,318 thousand euros compared to 9,410 thousand euros. By product groups, fresh milk products generated 42,497 thousand euros compared to 42,598 thousand euros, cheese sales reached 5,276 thousand euros compared to 5,192 thousand euros, dry milk products yielded 1,397 thousand euros compared to 1,912 thousand euros, and other products contributed 2,894 thousand euros compared to 2,621 thousand euros. The report confirms that there are no material post-statement events.
