AB KN Energies’ unaudited consolidated revenue for the three months ended 31 March 2026 was EUR 29.823 million, up 17.8% from EUR 25.326 million a year earlier. Gross profit increased 31.8% to EUR 12.907 million despite an 8.9% rise in cost of sales to EUR 16.916 million. Operating profit rose 34.5% to EUR 9.608 million, while profit before income tax increased 64.5% to EUR 8.824 million. Net profit reached EUR 7.247 million, 63.8% above the EUR 4.426 million reported for the first quarter of 2025, and earnings per share increased from EUR 0.01 to EUR 0.02.

Revenue growth was led by LNG-related and service activities. Revenue from LNGT regasification services collected directly from users increased to EUR 19.162 million from EUR 14.593 million, while liquid energy products transshipment revenue rose to EUR 7.596 million from EUR 6.615 million and consulting revenue to EUR 2.749 million from EUR 1.932 million. These increases more than offset lower revenue from other LNG terminal activities and other transshipment services. The report states that LNGT tariffs are regulated by the National Energy Regulatory Council and adjusted annually. It also states that the reduction in LNG security supplement income was affected by the proportionate repayment of excess returns from regulated activities in 2022–2023.

Segment performance was mixed. LNGT revenue increased to EUR 18.687 million from EUR 14.989 million, and segment net profit rose to EUR 4.529 million from EUR 1.328 million. Commercial LNG revenue increased 35.8% to EUR 3.357 million, with net profit rising to EUR 1.128 million. In contrast, the liquid energy terminals segment reported revenue of EUR 7.779 million, slightly below EUR 7.864 million, while its net profit declined to EUR 1.590 million from EUR 2.332 million. Group EBITDA increased 18.8% to EUR 16.153 million.

Profitability also benefited from improved net finance costs, which narrowed to EUR 0.784 million from EUR 1.777 million. Finance income increased to EUR 1.076 million, including a EUR 458 thousand net gain on derivatives, while finance costs declined to EUR 1.860 million, partly due to lower interest expenses on borrowings. Income tax expense nevertheless increased 68.1% to EUR 1.577 million.

Operating cash flow more than doubled to EUR 27.917 million, supported particularly by a EUR 7.010 million decrease in receivables. After investing and financing outflows, cash increased to EUR 17.986 million from EUR 12.361 million at year-end, although it was below EUR 25.623 million at 31 March 2025. Borrowings declined to EUR 302.069 million from EUR 311.194 million at year-end. After the reporting period, a new six-member Board was elected, replacing the previous two-tier governance structure.
