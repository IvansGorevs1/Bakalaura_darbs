Arco Vara AS reported improved financial performance during the first quarter of 2025, driven by a moderate recovery in the Estonian real estate market. The company’s sales revenue increased significantly to 1,706 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2025, compared to 990 thousand euros in the same period of the previous year. This revenue growth was primarily supported by higher proceeds from the sale of the group’s own real estate, which rose to 1,589 thousand euros from 927 thousand euros, alongside increases in service and franchise revenues.

Profitability also showed a positive turnaround compared with the previous year. The group recorded an operating profit of 259 thousand euros for the first quarter of 2025, recovering from an operating loss of 208 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2024. Net profit for the reporting period reached 103 thousand euros, representing a substantial improvement from the net loss of 349 thousand euros reported in the corresponding period of 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share both stood at 0.01 euros, compared to -0.03 euros in the prior-year period.

The broader economic environment during the quarter was characterized by a stable real estate sector supported by declining Euribor rates and stabilizing inflation, which contributed to increased consumer interest and transaction activity. Operational developments included the sale of four apartments during the first quarter of 2025, bringing total units sold in the Rannakalda project to 91 out of 113. Pre-sales activities continued across other residential projects, including Soodi 6 and Botanica Lozen in Bulgaria, where the first phase neared completion. Additionally, the company acquired a new development property on Spordi Street in Tallinn during the quarter to expand its future project portfolio.

Regarding balance sheet and financial indicators, total assets increased to 42,728 thousand euros as of 31 March 2025, up from 35,079 thousand euros at the end of the first quarter of 2024. Equity at the end of the reporting period stood at 19,842 thousand euros, compared to 20,668 thousand euros a year earlier. The group’s debt burden, represented by net loans, reached 15,455 thousand euros as of 31 March 2025, reflecting ongoing investments in real estate development projects. Interest-bearing liabilities totaled 16,568 thousand euros at the end of the period, with a weighted average annual interest rate of 8.2 percent, marking a decrease of 0.5 percentage points compared to the end of December 2024 due to declining benchmark interest rates. Cash and cash equivalents amounted to 1,113 thousand euros at the end of March 2025, reflecting net cash flows of -607 thousand euros for the quarter driven by cash usage in operating activities, which was partially offset by financing activities. Overall, the first quarter of 2025 reflected operational stability, preparation for upcoming construction stages, and a return to profitability amid recovering market conditions.
