AB KN Energies reported consolidated revenue of EUR 29,823 thousand for the three months ended 31 March 2026, an increase of 17.8 percent compared to EUR 25,326 thousand in the same period of 2025. Revenue growth was driven by the LNG terminal segment, which rose 24.7 percent to EUR 18,687 thousand, and the commercial LNG segment, which grew 35.7 percent to EUR 3,357 thousand. The liquid energy terminals segment experienced a marginal decline of 1.1 percent to EUR 7,779 thousand. Revenue composition includes deductions for regulatory security supplement repayments of EUR 905 thousand. Gross profit expanded by 31.8 percent to EUR 12,907 thousand from EUR 9,793 thousand, resulting from revenue gains outpacing a rise in cost of sales to EUR 16,916 thousand. Higher costs were primarily attributed to increased environmental taxes of EUR 2,225 thousand against EUR 1,783 thousand in 2025, along with elevated repair and maintenance expenses and wage costs. Compensation expenses grew across operations, with LNG terminal wages increasing to EUR 1,434 thousand and other segment wages also rising.

Operating profit increased by 34.5 percent to EUR 9,608 thousand, up from EUR 7,141 thousand. This improvement was supported by the LNG terminal segment's net profit rising sharply to EUR 4,529 thousand from EUR 1,328 thousand, and the commercial LNG segment achieving net profit of EUR 1,128 thousand. Distribution costs and administrative expenses rose by 10.5 percent to EUR 3,316 thousand. Other gains decreased to EUR 17 thousand from EUR 350 thousand. Financial performance benefited significantly from a 55.9 percent reduction in net finance costs, which fell to EUR 784 thousand from EUR 1,777 thousand. Net finance cost decline stemmed from an 89.4 percent increase in finance income to EUR 1,076 thousand and a 20.7 percent drop in finance costs to EUR 1,860 thousand, as interest expenses on borrowings decreased to EUR 1,729 thousand from EUR 2,160 thousand. Profit before tax surged by 64.5 percent to EUR 8,824 thousand. Following income tax expenses of EUR 1,577 thousand, net profit reached EUR 7,247 thousand, a 63.7 percent increase over EUR 4,426 thousand, with basic earnings per share improving to EUR 0.02 from EUR 0.01.

Operating cash flows more than doubled to EUR 27,917 thousand from EUR 13,758 thousand, aided by working capital improvements including the recovery of an EUR 9,642 thousand excess payment to the lending bank recognized in January 2026. Total borrowings declined from EUR 311,194 thousand to EUR 302,069 thousand, supporting the lower financing costs. Investment activity intensified with asset acquisitions reaching EUR 4,906 thousand compared to EUR 3,237 thousand in the prior year. Equity increased to EUR 179,866 thousand from EUR 172,592 thousand due to retained earnings. The management confirmed no impairment indicators for the Klaipėda terminal assets during the reporting period.
