AS LHV Group reported a consolidated net profit of EUR 24.7 million for the second quarter of 2026, representing an increase compared to the previous quarter's net profit of EUR 19.7 million, but a decrease compared to EUR 30.8 million in the second quarter of 2025. For the first six months of 2026, the group achieved a cumulative net profit of EUR 44.4 million, down from EUR 59.9 million in the same period of the previous year. Net profit attributable to the owners of the parent company amounted to EUR 24.1 million for the quarter, yielding basic and diluted earnings per share of EUR 0.07. The return on equity attributable to shareholders stood at 13.1% for the quarter, improving from 10.7% in the first quarter of 2026 while declining from 17.4% in the corresponding period of 2025. Capital adequacy at the end of the quarter was 20.8%. 

Total consolidated net income for the second quarter reached EUR 79.0 million, reflecting growth of 7.3% compared to EUR 73.7 million in the first quarter of 2026 and a slight increase from EUR 73.9 million in the second quarter of 2025. Net interest income grew to EUR 62.5 million, up 5% quarter-over-quarter and 8% year-over-year, driven by stronger-than-expected interest income and favorable market interest rates. Conversely, net fee and commission income remained relatively flat at EUR 14.6 million compared to the previous quarter, though it declined 6% year-over-year largely due to lower client activity affecting service fees. Operating expenses for the quarter decreased to EUR 43.6 million from EUR 45.6 million in the first quarter, but increased compared to EUR 40.5 million in the second quarter of the previous year. Loan and bond provisions increased significantly by EUR 5.8 million during the quarter, reflecting model-based, forward-looking impairment charges applied for specific clients despite overall portfolio quality remaining robust. 

The group’s total loan volume expanded to EUR 5,768 million by the end of the second quarter, marking a 3% increase over the quarter and a 15% rise year-over-year, supported primarily by growth in corporate and home lending segments. Total customer deposits decreased by EUR 245 million during the quarter to EUR 7,557 million, impacted by a reduction in platform deposits and financial intermediaries' deposits, whereas deposits from ordinary clients increased. Looking at individual business units, AS LHV Pank recorded a net profit of EUR 24.3 million for the quarter, while LHV Bank Limited earned EUR 1.4 million, AS LHV Varahaldus generated EUR 0.4 million, and AS LHV Kindlustus posted a net profit of EUR 0.8 million. Conversely, LHV Paytech reported a net loss of EUR 0.3 million. During the quarter, AS LHV Pank completed a major strategic milestone by successfully migrating its entire core banking system to the Amazon Web Services cloud environment and received a crypto-assets service provider license under the MiCA Regulation. Overall, despite higher expenses and increased provisioning, the company demonstrated solid business expansion, robust interest income generation, and key technological advancements during the reporting period.
