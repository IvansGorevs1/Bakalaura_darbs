AS Silvano Fashion Group’s revenue for the first six months of 2026 increased 0.6% to EUR 28.590 million from EUR 28.428 million in the comparable period. Gross profit rose 5.3% to EUR 15.892 million, while gross margin improved from 53.1% to 55.6%, supported by a 4.8% reduction in cost of goods sold. However, operating profit declined 3.5% to EUR 5.751 million and EBITDA fell 0.8% to EUR 7.508 million. Operating and EBITDA margins decreased to 20.1% and 26.3%, respectively, from 21.0% and 26.6%.

Profit for the period fell 31.2% to EUR 4.321 million, while profit attributable to the parent company’s shareholders decreased 31.5% to EUR 3.961 million. Earnings per share declined from EUR 0.16 to EUR 0.11. The income statement shows that net finance income decreased to EUR 444 thousand from EUR 1.652 million, including a reduction in currency exchange income to EUR 152 thousand from EUR 1.438 million. Income tax expense also increased to EUR 1.870 million from EUR 1.322 million. Distribution expenses rose to EUR 6.912 million from EUR 6.230 million and administrative expenses to EUR 3.084 million from EUR 2.672 million.

Revenue performance reflected a shift from wholesale to retail. Wholesale revenue declined 9.1% to EUR 14.934 million, reducing its share of sales from 57.8% to 52.2%, while retail revenue increased 13.6% to EUR 13.548 million and represented 47.4% of sales. Russia remained the largest market, generating EUR 15.767 million, down 0.6% in euros and 7.8% in local currency. Belarus sales increased 6.4% to EUR 9.046 million, or 2.7% in local currency. Russia and Belarus together accounted for 86.7% of total sales. The Group operated 135 own stores and, with franchise partners, 547 stores at period-end, compared with 137 own stores at the end of 2025. Management stated that it would focus on improving retail profitability and continue store expansion where reasonably priced locations were available.

Operating cash flow increased 96.6% to EUR 6.842 million. Cash and cash equivalents rose 87.4% to EUR 12.443 million, while short-term deposits remained broadly stable at EUR 38.707 million. Total assets increased 5.1% to EUR 98.603 million and equity attributable to parent-company shareholders rose 6.1% to EUR 81.479 million. At the annual meeting, shareholders approved retaining the 2025 net profit of EUR 9.229 million without distributing dividends. The interim report is unaudited.
