During the 2025 financial year, AS Tallink Grupp faced a challenging operating environment characterized by economic uncertainty, low consumer and business confidence in core markets such as Finland, and ongoing geopolitical tensions. Consequently, the group reported a contraction in overall financial performance for the twelve-month period ended December 31, 2025. Unaudited consolidated revenue decreased by 2.6 percent to EUR 765.3 million compared to EUR 785.8 million in the previous financial year. The total number of passengers carried across operations edged down by 0.9 percent to 5,531,132, while transported cargo units dropped significantly by 19.2 percent to 245,004. Passenger vehicles decreased by 2.2 percent to 760,473.

Profitability metrics declined across the board for the full year. Gross profit fell to EUR 143.2 million from EUR 153.6 million, and EBITDA dropped to EUR 130.1 million compared to EUR 175.2 million in 2024. Unaudited net profit for the twelve months decreased to EUR 17.3 million from EUR 40.3 million in the prior year, resulting in earnings per share of EUR 0.023 compared to EUR 0.054 previously. Profitability was partially cushioned by a reduction in amortization and depreciation expenses, which decreased by EUR 18.0 million to EUR 79.8 million. This decrease was driven by the alignment of the estimated useful lives of cruise and passenger vessels to 45 years and the sale of three vessels during the year. Net finance costs also improved, declining by EUR 6.8 million to EUR 21.4 million due to lower outstanding loan balances and decreasing interest rates.

During the reporting period, the group actively managed its fleet and debt structure, completing the sales of the passenger vessel Star I and the cargo vessels Regal Star and Sailor. Capital investments totaled EUR 33.0 million, and planned maintenance works accounted for 73 operating days. Net debt stood at EUR 432.4 million at the end of the year, down from EUR 537.7 million at the end of 2024, with the net debt to EBITDA ratio registering at 3.3. Total loan repayments and interest payments reached EUR 113.5 million. The company paid total dividends of EUR 44.6 million, or EUR 0.06 per share, and the management board intends to propose a dividend of EUR 0.06 per share for the upcoming year. Despite full-year headwinds, performance recovered in the fourth quarter of 2025. Revenue for the final quarter grew by 2.4 percent year-on-year to EUR 188.0 million, EBITDA rose to EUR 27.6 million from EUR 25.7 million, and net profit rebounded to EUR 12.2 million compared to a net loss of EUR 5.2 million in the fourth quarter of 2024.
