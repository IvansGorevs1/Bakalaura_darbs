During the nine months of 2025, AB KN Energies and its subsidiaries reported an overall improvement in financial performance and profitability compared with the same period of the previous year. Group revenue from contracts with customers increased to EUR 76,664 thousand from EUR 67,977 thousand in the first nine months of 2024. Company revenue grew to EUR 72,848 thousand from EUR 65,009 thousand. This revenue expansion was primarily supported by higher income from liquefied natural gas terminal regasification services collected directly from users, which reached EUR 43,640 thousand compared with EUR 31,401 thousand previously, along with increases in liquid energy products transshipment services and consulting services. Results from the previous year were additionally impacted by the terminal being out of operation for one and a half months for dry-dock repairs. Revenue growth was partially offset by lower sales from unused allocated capacities and a reduction in income from LNG security supplements due to the repayment of excess returns from regulated activities.

Group cost of sales increased to EUR 45,115 thousand from EUR 38,740 thousand, driven by higher depreciation expenses following the reclassification of the floating storage and regasification unit from right-of-use assets to property, plant and equipment after its acquisition in December 2024, as well as higher employee wages, maintenance costs, and environmental taxes. Despite higher costs, group gross profit improved to EUR 31,549 thousand from EUR 29,237 thousand. Group operating expenses rose moderately to EUR 10,640 thousand from EUR 10,419 thousand, largely reflecting increased personnel expenses.

Operating profit for the group increased to EUR 21,282 thousand from EUR 18,942 thousand. Finance income declined to EUR 1,520 thousand from EUR 2,644 thousand, while finance costs remained stable at EUR 6,616 thousand compared with EUR 6,692 thousand, as higher loan interest expenses were offset by lower losses from financial derivatives. Group profit before tax rose to EUR 16,186 thousand from EUR 14,910 thousand. After income tax expenses of EUR 3,028 thousand, the group net profit for the reporting period reached EUR 13,158 thousand, up from EUR 11,862 thousand in the first nine months of 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share increased to EUR 0.035 from EUR 0.031. The company reported a net profit of EUR 11,613 thousand, compared with EUR 10,702 thousand in the previous year.

As of 30 September 2025, total group assets stood at EUR 530,364 thousand, compared with EUR 535,174 thousand at the end of December 2024. Total equity increased to EUR 167,721 thousand from EUR 163,128 thousand, driven by the period's net profit and offset by declared dividends of EUR 8,622 thousand. Cash and cash equivalents rose significantly to EUR 54,398 thousand from EUR 17,203 thousand, supported by strong net cash flows from operating activities of EUR 76,331 thousand. Overall, the reporting period was characterized by revenue growth, enhanced operating profitability, and a robust cash position.
