During the second quarter of 2026, Infortar Group experienced a decrease in consolidated revenue alongside a notable improvement in core profitability indicators. Total revenue for the second quarter reached 440.9 million euros, declining from 504.5 million euros in the same period of the previous year. For the first six months of the financial year, revenue stood at 945.9 million euros, compared with 951.9 million euros a year earlier. The quarterly revenue contraction was primarily driven by lower sales volumes within the energy segment, where wholesale activities saw a reduction. 

Despite the lower top-line figures, the group demonstrated stronger operational profitability. Gross profit for the second quarter rose to 59.4 million euros from 55.7 million euros in the prior-year period. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization for the second quarter increased to 64.2 million euros compared to 57.4 million euros a year ago, with the EBITDA margin expanding to 14.6 percent from 11.4 percent. For the six-month period, EBITDA increased significantly by 31 percent to 111.2 million euros. Operating profit for the quarter grew to 29.7 million euros from 27.0 million euros, while six-month operating profit nearly doubled to 51.1 million euros compared to 26.4 million euros in the first half of 2025. 

Net profit for the second quarter was 1.9 million euros, improving from 150 thousand euros a year earlier. Net profit attributable to the owners of the parent reached 2.3 million euros for the quarter and 15.2 million euros for the first six months, recovering from a net loss of 2.5 million euros in the corresponding period of 2025. The profit improvement was supported by stronger operational performance across business segments and a significant turnaround in supporting business activities, which swung from an operating loss to a profit. 

The maritime transport segment maintained stable revenue of 207.0 million euros for the quarter, while reporting a net loss of 2.5 million euros, matching the prior year. Maritime results were impacted by heightened geopolitical tensions that increased fuel costs by 8.4 million euros, an expanded emissions trading system compliance requirement, and a 12.5 million euro income tax expense on dividends, alongside a 2.2 percent decrease in passenger volumes to approximately 1.45 million passengers. 

As of 30 June 2026, Infortar Group reported total assets of 2.71 billion euros, total equity of 1.20 billion euros, and cash and cash equivalents of 253.2 million euros. Interest-bearing liabilities stood at 1.08 billion euros at the end of the reporting period. Total investments for the first six months amounted to approximately 50 million euros. The group maintained a diversified business model and a stable capital structure to navigate ongoing macroeconomic and geopolitical volatility while pursuing its long-term strategic objectives.
