Infortar Group’s first-half 2026 performance improved substantially despite broadly unchanged revenue. Six-month revenue was EUR 945.9 million, compared with EUR 951.9 million a year earlier, while gross profit increased to EUR 114.1 million from EUR 81.7 million. EBITDA rose 31% to EUR 111.2 million, lifting the EBITDA margin from 8.9% to 11.8%, and EBIT increased 94% to EUR 51.1 million from EUR 26.4 million. Profit before tax reached EUR 27.2 million, versus EUR 2.7 million in the prior-year period. Net profit was EUR 7.2 million, compared with a loss of EUR 14.4 million, while profit attributable to the parent’s owners improved to EUR 15.2 million from a loss of EUR 2.5 million. Basic earnings per share rose to EUR 0.74 from negative EUR 0.12.

In the second quarter, revenue declined to EUR 440.9 million from EUR 504.5 million, mainly because of lower energy-segment revenue and reduced wholesale sales. Nevertheless, EBITDA increased to EUR 64.2 million from EUR 57.4 million, EBIT to EUR 29.7 million from EUR 27.0 million, and net profit to EUR 1.9 million from EUR 0.2 million. Profit attributable to parent-company owners was EUR 2.3 million, compared with EUR 1.9 million. Second-quarter results included EUR 12.5 million of income tax on dividends.

The energy segment remained the largest contributor to first-half EBITDA, which increased to EUR 59.9 million from EUR 51.7 million, despite a 34% decline in gas and electricity sales volumes to 3.3 TWh, primarily due to lower wholesale sales. Maritime transport EBITDA rose to EUR 39.3 million from EUR 33.3 million for the first half, and its operating loss narrowed from EUR 12.9 million to EUR 3.0 million. In the second quarter, however, Tallink’s EBITDA fell to EUR 34.3 million from EUR 37.4 million. Passenger numbers decreased 2.2%, while fuel costs increased by EUR 8.4 million and EU ETS surrender requirements rose to 100% of emissions from 70% in 2025. Supporting businesses delivered a significant turnaround, moving from a EUR 4.4 million EBITDA loss to EUR 5.6 million of EBITDA, while real-estate EBITDA increased to EUR 6.5 million from EUR 4.4 million.

The Group completed approximately EUR 50 million of investments, including the Halinga biomethane plant and ongoing construction and real-estate projects. Infortar also increased its Tallink ownership to 68.76%; no new business combinations were completed in the quarter.

At 30 June, total assets were EUR 2.714 billion and equity EUR 1.201 billion. Cash increased to EUR 253.2 million from EUR 219.8 million at year-end, while net debt declined to EUR 829.9 million and net debt/EBITDA improved to 3.3 from 3.7.
