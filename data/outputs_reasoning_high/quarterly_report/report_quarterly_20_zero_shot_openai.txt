For the 12 months ended June 30, 2025, SAF Tehnika’s unaudited consolidated net sales were EUR 26.11 million, down 4% from EUR 27.09 million in the previous financial year. Revenue performance differed significantly by region: sales in North and Latin America declined 14% to EUR 14.28 million and represented 55% of total turnover, while European sales increased 17% to EUR 10.04 million, accounting for 38%. Sales in Asia, Africa and the Middle East fell 5% and represented 7% of turnover.

Profitability improved substantially over the full year, although the Group remained loss-making. EBIT was a loss of EUR 754 thousand, compared with a EUR 2.26 million loss in 2023/2024. The net loss narrowed to EUR 820 thousand from EUR 2.37 million. Total operating expenses decreased to EUR 27.56 million from EUR 30.27 million, while direct costs of goods sold fell to EUR 9.33 million from EUR 13.12 million. However, salaries, bonuses and related social expenses increased 8% to EUR 12.41 million. Financial items resulted in a EUR 62 thousand loss, compared with a EUR 90 thousand loss previously, while the foreign-exchange result changed from a EUR 45 thousand gain to a EUR 47 thousand loss.

The fourth quarter was weaker than the corresponding quarter of the prior year. Net sales declined 13% to EUR 5.18 million, with North and Latin American turnover falling 40% to EUR 2.5 million. European sales increased 24% to EUR 1.9 million, and turnover in Asia, Africa and the Middle East doubled to EUR 846 thousand. The Group recorded a Q4 net loss of EUR 913 thousand, compared with EUR 498 thousand in Q4 2023/2024; Q4 EBIT worsened to a loss of EUR 863 thousand from EUR 495 thousand. The quarterly financial result was also affected by a EUR 47 thousand foreign-exchange loss, compared with a small gain in the prior-year quarter.

Operations continued to be affected by shortages of electronic components, although delivery times had recently improved. The Group maintained material reserves to support order fulfillment and continued investing in product development, enhancements and sales promotion. The microwave radio market showed no major changes, while the Group monitored global trade conditions, including potential US tariff adjustments.

Cash generation improved: total cash flow was EUR 2.60 million, versus an outflow of EUR 1.25 million previously, increasing cash and bank balances to EUR 4.81 million. Customer advances rose to EUR 3.92 million, and the EUR 4.95 million Luminor credit line remained unused. Total assets increased to EUR 25.92 million, while owners’ equity declined to EUR 15.58 million.
