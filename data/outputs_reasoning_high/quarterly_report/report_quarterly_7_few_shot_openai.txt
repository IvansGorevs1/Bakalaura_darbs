AS Silvano Fashion Group’s performance in the six months ended 30 June 2026 was characterized by largely stable sales, improved gross profitability, but lower operating and net profit. Revenue increased 0.6% to EUR 28.590 million, while gross profit rose 5.3% to EUR 15.892 million. The gross margin improved to 55.6% from 53.1%, supported by a 4.8% reduction in the cost of goods sold. However, higher distribution and administrative expenses reduced operating profit by 3.5% to EUR 5.751 million, and the operating margin declined to 20.1% from 21.0%. EBITDA was broadly stable, decreasing 0.8% to EUR 7.508 million, with the margin edging down to 26.3%.

Net profit declined substantially to EUR 4.321 million from EUR 6.279 million, while profit attributable to the parent company’s equity holders fell 31.5% to EUR 3.961 million. Earnings per share decreased to EUR 0.11 from EUR 0.16. The decline in profitability below the operating level was affected by lower net finance income of EUR 444 thousand, compared with EUR 1.652 million in the prior-year period, including a significant reduction in currency exchange income. Income tax expense also increased to EUR 1.870 million from EUR 1.322 million. In the second quarter alone, revenue increased to EUR 16.774 million from EUR 16.412 million, while operating profit fell to EUR 4.060 million from EUR 4.559 million and net profit was broadly stable at EUR 3.275 million.

Performance reflected a shift toward retail. Retail revenue increased 13.6% to EUR 13.548 million and represented 47.4% of total sales, compared with 41.9% a year earlier. Wholesale revenue declined 9.1% to EUR 14.934 million. Russia remained the largest market, contributing EUR 15.767 million, down 0.6% in euros and 7.8% in local currency. Belarusian sales increased 6.4% to EUR 9.046 million, partly offsetting weaker Russian sales; together, the two markets generated 86.7% of total revenue. The Group and its franchise partners operated 547 stores, including 135 directly operated stores, compared with 137 own stores at year-end.

Cash generation and the financial position strengthened. Operating cash flow almost doubled to EUR 6.842 million from EUR 3.481 million. Total assets increased 5.1% to EUR 98.603 million, cash and cash equivalents rose 87.4% to EUR 12.443 million, and equity attributable to parent-company shareholders increased 6.1% to EUR 81.479 million. Inventories and current liabilities decreased. The Group employed 1,594 people, down from 1,639, although salaries and related taxes increased to EUR 8.838 million. Shareholders approved retaining the 2025 profit of EUR 9.229 million, with no dividends declared.
