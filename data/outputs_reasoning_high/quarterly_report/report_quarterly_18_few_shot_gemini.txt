During the first three months of 2026, AB Pieno Žvaigždės experienced a significant improvement in profitability despite a slight decline in overall revenue compared with the same period in 2025. Total revenue for the reporting period reached 52,064 thousand euros, representing a minor decrease from 52,324 thousand euros reported in the first quarter of the previous year. Looking at regional performance, sales in Lithuania amounted to 31,405 thousand euros, while sales in other European Union countries reached 11,341 thousand euros, and sales in non-European Union countries stood at 9,318 thousand euros. Regarding product segments, fresh milk products generated 42,497 thousand euros, cheese sales reached 5,276 thousand euros, dry milk products contributed 1,397 thousand euros, and other products accounted to 2,894 thousand euros.

Despite the flat revenue development, the company achieved a dramatic reduction in the cost of sales, which dropped to 38,078 thousand euros from 42,530 thousand euros in the prior year period. This cost reduction drove a substantial expansion in gross profit, which surged to 13,986 thousand euros compared with 9,794 thousand euros a year earlier. Net other operating income also increased to 363 thousand euros from 28 thousand euros, although sales and administrative expenses rose to 10,983 thousand euros from 9,287 thousand euros. Consequently, operating profit before finance costs climbed sharply to 3,366 thousand euros, up from 535 thousand euros in the first three months of 2025. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization reached 4,730 thousand euros, compared to 2,001 thousand euros previously.

Net financial expenses remained relatively stable, totaling 298 thousand euros compared with 308 thousand euros in the comparative period. Driven by the strong operational performance, the company reported a net profit of 3,068 thousand euros for the first three months of 2026, representing a massive increase from the 227 thousand euros recorded in the corresponding period of 2025. Earnings per share rose accordingly to 0.07 euros, up from 0.01 euros in the previous year.

The company's financial position strengthened by the end of the reporting period. Total assets increased to 85,305 thousand euros as of March 31, 2026, compared with 83,475 thousand euros at the end of December 2025. Total equity grew to 41,164 thousand euros from 38,238 thousand euros, supported by an increase in retained earnings to 18,614 thousand euros. Total liabilities decreased to 44,141 thousand euros from 45,237 thousand euros over the same timeframe. Net cash flow from operating activities improved significantly, generating 1,625 thousand euros compared with a negative cash flow of 183 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2025. Net cash used in investing activities amounted to 551 thousand euros, while net cash used in financing activities totaled 723 thousand euros. Cash and cash equivalents stood at 2,157 thousand euros at the end of March 2026, showing an increase from 1,806 thousand euros at the start of the year.
