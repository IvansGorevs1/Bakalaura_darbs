AB Pieno Žvaigždės delivered a marked improvement in financial performance for the first quarter of 2026, characterized by significantly expanded profit margins following a substantial reduction in costs, even as sales volumes remained broadly stable. Total revenue amounted to 52,064 thousand euros, a decrease of less than one percent from 52,324 thousand euros in the corresponding period of the previous year. Sales within the domestic Lithuanian market declined modestly to 31,405 thousand euros from 32,112 thousand euros, while shipments to other European Union countries rose to 11,341 thousand euros from 10,801 thousand euros. Exports to non-EU countries edged down slightly to 9,318 thousand euros. Product-wise, sales of fresh milk products held steady at 42,497 thousand euros, dominating the portfolio, whereas dry milk sales contracted noticeably to 1,397 thousand euros; however, cheese and other product lines showed modest growth, contributing to a stable top-line environment across diverse market segments.

The most material factor driving profitability was a steep decline in the cost of sales, which fell to 38,078 thousand euros from 42,530 thousand euros. This efficiency gain propelled gross profit to 13,986 thousand euros, a 43 percent increase compared to 9,794 thousand euros in the prior year. While sales and administrative expenses climbed to 10,983 thousand euros from 9,287 thousand euros, indicating some pressure on overheads, the company recorded an operating profit before finance costs of 3,366 thousand euros, a dramatic recovery from 535 thousand euros the previous year. EBITDA more than doubled, reaching 4,730 thousand euros versus 2,001 thousand euros. After accounting for finance expenses netting 298 thousand euros, comparable to the prior period, net profit surged to 3,068 thousand euros, yielding earnings per share of 0.07 euros compared to 0.01 euros previously.

The company's balance sheet position strengthened during the period. Total assets grew to 85,305 thousand euros from 83,475 thousand euros at the end of 2025, comprising non-current assets of 49,653 thousand euros and current assets of 35,651 thousand euros. Retained earnings increased to 18,614 thousand euros, bringing total equity to 41,164 thousand euros. Long-term borrowings stood at 16,548 thousand euros, while current liabilities, primarily trade payables of 21,418 thousand euros, decreased slightly, supporting an improved current ratio of 1.51 against 1.27 in the prior year quarter. Cash flow from operating activities generated a net inflow of 1,625 thousand euros, a sharp turnaround from a 183 thousand euro outflow previously. This improvement was supported by depreciation of 1,427 thousand euros and favorable working capital movements, notably a cash release from inventory reductions of 998 thousand euros, partially offset by an increase in receivables of 2,536 thousand euros. The company maintained disciplined investment spending with net cash used in investing activities at 551 thousand euros, and financing activities resulted in a 723 thousand euro outflow, driven by borrowings repayments of 469 thousand euros and finance lease payments, signaling a shift toward deleveraging and return of capital amidst the improved cash generation profile. Overall, the reporting period demonstrated the company's ability to convert stable revenues into much stronger earnings through effective cost management and working capital optimization.
