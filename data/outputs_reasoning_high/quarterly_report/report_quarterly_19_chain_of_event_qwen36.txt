AS Pro Kapital Grupp reported a significant turnaround in its financial performance for the twelve months ended 31 December 2025 compared to the prior year. Total revenue surged to EUR 53.2 million from EUR 18.2 million, while gross profit increased to EUR 18.8 million from EUR 5.4 million, raising the gross profit margin to 35 percent. Operating profit improved drastically to EUR 14.7 million from a negligible EUR 0.1 million, resulting in an operating margin of 28 percent. The Group shifted from a net loss of EUR 3.9 million to a net profit of EUR 12.0 million, with earnings per share rising to EUR 0.22 from a deficit of EUR 0.06.

The substantial growth in revenue and profitability was primarily driven by the completion and handover of residential units in key development projects. In Tallinn, sales resulted from apartment handovers in the Kalaranna District and the white building of the Uus-Kindrali development. Additionally, revenue recognition occurred in Vilnius following the handover of units in the City Villas project. These completions caused the Estonian segment's contribution to total revenue to jump to 75 percent from 27 percent the previous year. Conversely, the Latvian segment's revenue share fell to 0.3 percent as remaining inventory was sold, and the German segment's share decreased to 11 percent. Although hotel operations in Germany showed encouraging momentum earlier in the period, occupancy softened toward the end of the year. Italian operations faced subdued market conditions, leading to a full impairment loss of EUR 0.863 million on goodwill acquired during the acquisition of Preatoni Nuda Proprietà S.r.l., where the Group also increased its ownership interest to 77.5 percent.

Financial position strengthened considerably. Net assets per share increased to EUR 1.12 from EUR 0.89, and the debt-to-equity ratio improved to 0.97 from 1.32. Return on equity turned positive at 21.0 percent versus minus 7.0 percent previously. Cash generation from operating activities totaled EUR 10.8 million, reversing a cash use of EUR 10.4 million in 2024. During the reporting period, the Group redeemed EUR 9.405 million in nominal amount of its senior secured callable fixed rate bonds, reducing outstanding secured bonds. Furthermore, fair value gains of EUR 3.47 million were recognized on investment properties, contributing to other operating income. Despite these strong results, the Chairman noted that the hotel performance remained slightly below budget, and market uncertainties persisted in Italy, though the Group maintained a focused execution strategy across its Baltic residential developments.
