SAF Tehnika’s performance improved substantially for the full 2024/2025 financial year, although the Group remained unprofitable and the fourth quarter weakened. Annual net sales declined by 4% to EUR 26.11 million from EUR 27.09 million, reflecting lower revenue in the Americas, while European sales increased. Despite the lower turnover, the net loss narrowed to EUR 820 thousand from EUR 2.37 million in the previous financial year. The operating loss improved to EUR 754 thousand from EUR 2.26 million, supported by a significant reduction in direct production costs and lower total operating expenses. Financial items also remained a relatively small expense at EUR 62 thousand, compared with EUR 90 thousand previously.

The regional sales mix changed materially. Revenue from North and Latin America fell 14% to EUR 14.28 million and represented 55% of annual turnover. European revenue increased 17% to EUR 10.04 million, accounting for 38% of sales, while revenue from Asia, Africa and the Middle East decreased 5% to EUR 1.78 million. In the fourth quarter, sales declined 13% year on year to EUR 5.18 million. North and Latin American turnover fell 40% to approximately EUR 2.5 million, partly offset by a 24% increase in Europe to EUR 1.9 million and a doubling of sales in Asia, Africa and the Middle East to EUR 846 thousand. Quarterly results were affected by the timing and completion of individual projects, with production, procurement and revenue recognition sometimes extending into later quarters.

Fourth-quarter profitability deteriorated. The Group recorded a net loss of EUR 913 thousand, compared with EUR 498 thousand in the prior-year quarter, while EBIT declined to a loss of EUR 863 thousand from EUR 495 thousand. EBITDA was negative EUR 460 thousand, compared with negative EUR 96 thousand. Higher foreign-exchange losses and increased bonus costs contributed to the quarterly deterioration. For the full year, salaries, bonuses and related social expenses increased 8% to EUR 12.41 million, although total expenses remained within budget. The Group continued investing in product development, product enhancements and sales promotion.

Cash generation strengthened significantly. Operating activities generated EUR 2.13 million, compared with EUR 939 thousand previously, and total cash flow was positive EUR 2.60 million versus negative EUR 1.25 million. Cash increased to EUR 4.81 million, supported also by EUR 3.92 million in customer advances, while the EUR 4.95 million Luminor credit line remained unused. Inventory increased 8% as the Group maintained reserves to address electronic-component shortages and support delivery times, which have recently improved. SAF Tehnika continued developing microwave, spectrum-analyzer and IoT products amid stable market conditions and maintained a cautious outlook because of broader business uncertainty.
