Infortar Group’s unaudited 2025 results showed stronger operating performance but lower net profit than in 2024, when the initial consolidation of Tallink generated significant one-off effects. Consolidated revenue reached EUR 1.837 billion, compared with EUR 1.372 billion in 2024. EBITDA rose to EUR 232.581 million from EUR 145.415 million, and the EBITDA margin improved to 13.4% from 10.6%. Operating profit increased to EUR 111.061 million from EUR 77.024 million. However, net profit declined to EUR 71.969 million from EUR 193.670 million, while profit attributable to the parent’s shareholders decreased to EUR 72.662 million from EUR 191.253 million. Earnings per share fell to EUR 3.54 from EUR 9.36. The report attributes the year-on-year decline in net profit mainly to one-off effects associated with Tallink’s initial consolidation in 2024.

Fourth-quarter performance improved operationally. Revenue was EUR 417.438 million, compared with EUR 446.168 million in Q4 2024, while EBITDA increased to EUR 42.604 million from EUR 27.892 million. Operating profit improved from a EUR 6.792 million loss to a EUR 19.842 million profit, and net profit rose from a EUR 11.988 million loss to EUR 14.141 million. The EBITDA improvement reflected stronger segment performance, although total financial income and expenses declined to a EUR 8.513 million expense from income of EUR 3.452 million in Q4 2024.

Energy was the largest contributor to full-year revenue and EBITDA. Its segment revenue was EUR 1.013 billion and EBITDA EUR 89.323 million, up from EUR 980.791 million and EUR 77.235 million, respectively. In Q4, Elenger’s gas and electricity sales volumes increased 7% to 4.8 TWh despite a 2% decline in regional natural-gas consumption. Maritime transport generated full-year revenue of EUR 765.288 million and EBITDA of EUR 124.187 million, below 2024 levels of EUR 785.822 million and EUR 175.181 million. Tallink carried 5.531 million passengers during the year, down 0.9%, while cargo units fell 19.2%. In Q4, maritime revenue increased 2.4% to EUR 188.0 million and net profit was EUR 12.2 million, compared with a loss of EUR 5.2 million a year earlier. Results were affected by low consumer and business confidence, geopolitical tensions, the sale of the cargo vessel Sailor, and lower financing repayments and interest expense.

The balance sheet strengthened: total equity rose to EUR 1.178 billion, the equity ratio to 45.5%, and net debt fell to EUR 840.682 million from EUR 1.056 billion. Cash increased to EUR 219.771 million, while operating cash flow reached EUR 388.632 million. Key developments included the acquisition of Oisu Biogaas, continued Rail Baltica and Depo construction, a multi-year agreement for 12 LNG cargoes, and the extension of Silja Europa’s charter agreement to January 2027.
