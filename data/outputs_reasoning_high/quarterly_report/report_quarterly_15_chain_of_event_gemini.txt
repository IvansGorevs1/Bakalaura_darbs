Nordecon AS reported its unaudited financial results for the twelve months of 2025, showing a decline in overall revenue and profitability alongside a substantial expansion in its order book. Group revenue for the reporting period decreased by seven percent year on year to 208,281 thousand euros, down from 223,925 thousand euros in 2024. The revenue contraction was primarily driven by a roughly ten percent decline in revenue generated by the Buildings segment, which totaled 168,302 thousand euros, whereas revenue in the Infrastructure segment grew by ten percent to 39,902 thousand euros. The Buildings segment accounted for 81 percent of total group revenue.

The group’s gross profit for 2025 fell to 13,535 thousand euros, resulting in a gross margin of 6.5 percent compared to 7.5 percent in the prior year. Operating profit decreased to 5,651 thousand euros, yielding an operating margin of 2.7 percent down from 3.6 percent in 2024. Administrative expenses decreased by approximately fourteen percent to 6,814 thousand euros, largely due to lower staff costs resulting from reduced provisions for performance-based pay. Net profit for the period dropped to 2,545 thousand euros compared to 5,165 thousand euros in 2024, with net profit attributable to owners of the parent declining to 1,439 thousand euros from 3,827 thousand euros. Profitability was notably affected by a foreign exchange loss of 750 thousand euros, which arose from the translation of euro-denominated loans provided to Ukrainian subsidiaries following a twelve percent weakening of the Ukrainian hryvnia against the euro.

Despite the lower financial results for the past year, the group significantly strengthened its future revenue potential through order book growth. At 31 December 2025, the group order book stood at 273,060 thousand euros, reflecting a thirty percent increase compared to the end of 2024. This growth was propelled by a nearly threefold expansion in the order book of the Infrastructure segment, driven by major project wins including the Rail Baltica main line infrastructure contract and road construction agreements with the Estonian Transport Administration. New contracts secured during 2025 totaled 234,994 thousand euros, surpassing the 181,437 thousand euros secured in 2024. Approximately 69 percent of the current order book is scheduled for execution in 2026.

Operating cash flows produced a net inflow of 1,204 thousand euros in 2025, a slight increase from 1,075 thousand euros in 2024. Cash and cash equivalents stood at 5,266 thousand euros at the end of the period, down from 8,195 thousand euros at the close of 2024. Management noted that the Estonian construction market stabilized during the second half of 2025 amid modest one percent economic growth, while fierce market competition and rising material costs continued to present business risks. Backed by a robust order book and cautious risk management regarding fixed costs and pricing, management expects business volumes to increase in 2026.
