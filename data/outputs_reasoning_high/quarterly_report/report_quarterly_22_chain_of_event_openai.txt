Telia Lietuva reported strong financial performance in the first half of 2026. Revenue increased 7.2% year on year to EUR 259.1 million. Mobile services revenue rose 8.3% to EUR 100.0 million, fixed services revenue increased 5.3% to EUR 112.9 million, and equipment revenue grew 9.6% to EUR 46.1 million. Within fixed services, IT services were particularly strong, increasing 23.9% to EUR 19.8 million. Mobile subscriptions increased 3.3% to 1.731 million, supported by 4.2% growth in post-paid subscriptions, while broadband and TV customer numbers declined modestly.

Profitability improved faster than revenue. EBITDA rose 9.5% to EUR 105.5 million, exceeding EUR 100 million for the first time, and the EBITDA margin increased from 39.9% to 40.7%. Adjusted EBITDA excluding non-recurring items grew 10.2% to EUR 106.9 million, with the margin rising to 41.3%. Operating profit increased 15.1% to EUR 62.4 million, while profit before tax rose 16.6% to EUR 61.3 million. Profit for the period increased 14.7% to EUR 51.3 million, lifting the profit margin from 18.5% to 19.8% and earnings per share from EUR 0.077 to EUR 0.088.

Cost of goods and services increased 2.3%, reflecting higher equipment sales volumes and increased network interconnection and roaming costs. Operating expenses excluding non-recurring items rose 10.2%; energy expenses increased 14.3%, external IT service costs 18.4%, and expenses for common Group functions also increased. Employee-related expenses included EUR 1.4 million of redundancy costs associated with restructuring the Digital & Technology unit. Despite these cost pressures, revenue growth and operating leverage supported the improvement in margins. Income tax expense increased 27.0%, partly reflecting Lithuania’s increase in the profit tax rate from 16% to 17%.

Cash generation strengthened, with operating cash flow up 15.0% to EUR 103.1 million and free cash flow up 12.5% to EUR 64.1 million. Capital investments increased 57.9% to EUR 37.5 million, including EUR 9.7 million for 5G spectrum licenses, EUR 6.1 million for the mobile network and EUR 14.2 million for IT systems and infrastructure. In June, the company acquired two 1,500 MHz frequency blocks for almost EUR 8 million and extended its 2,100 MHz spectrum rights for EUR 2 million. The company cited annual mobile data traffic growth of about 25% as requiring continued network capacity investment.

The company paid EUR 81.6 million in dividends for 2025, or EUR 0.14 per share. Following the payment, shareholders’ equity declined 7.5% from year-end to EUR 372.0 million, while net debt remained negative at EUR 4.3 million and the company had no outstanding loans. The interim financial statements are unaudited.
