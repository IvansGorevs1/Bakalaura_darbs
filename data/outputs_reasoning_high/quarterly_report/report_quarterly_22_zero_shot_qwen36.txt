Telia Lietuva, AB delivered strong financial results for the first half of 2026, characterized by robust growth, expanding margins, and accelerated cash generation. Total revenue rose by 7.2 percent to EUR 259.1 million, driven by a 6.7 percent increase in service revenue and a 7.2 percent uplift in equipment sales. Adjusted EBITDA climbed 10.2 percent to EUR 106.9 million, lifting the adjusted margin to 41.3 percent. Reported EBITDA reached EUR 105.5 million, up 9.5 percent, while operating profit increased by 15.1 percent to EUR 62.4 million. Net profit grew by 14.7 percent to EUR 51.3 million, yielding earnings per share of EUR 0.088. Operating cash flow strengthened by 15.0 percent to EUR 103.1 million, translating into free cash flow of EUR 64.1 million, an increase of 12.5 percent. These flows returned the company to a net cash position of EUR 4.3 million, reversing EUR 27.5 million in net debt from the prior year. Following shareholder approval, a dividend of EUR 81.6 million, or EUR 0.14 per share, was distributed.

Revenue expansion was led by IT services, which surged 23.9 percent, and internet services, up 4.2 percent. Equipment sales buoyed both revenue and cost figures. While mobile billed services grew by 8.5 percent, traditional voice telephony revenue contracted by 1.9 percent. Operating expenses rose by 11.3 percent to EUR 68.8 million, reflecting higher equipment distribution costs, increased interconnection and roaming charges, a 14.3 percent rise in energy expenses, and increased external IT service purchases. A one-time EUR 1.4 million in redundancy payments for the Digital and Technology restructuring temporarily affected the expense baseline. Concurrently, the company streamlined operations, reducing headcount by 86 to 1,601 and full-time equivalents by 93 to 1,524.

Strategic priorities focused on network capacity to handle Lithuania’s mobile data traffic, which expands at roughly 25 percent annually. Capital expenditures surged 57.9 percent to EUR 37.5 million, predominantly directed toward spectrum and digital infrastructure. In June, the company acquired two 1,500 MHz frequency blocks for nearly EUR 8 million, securing 40 MHz dedicated to 5G enhancement, and paid EUR 2 million to extend rights to the 2,100 MHz band. Ongoing investments fund 50 new mobile base stations, modernization of 300 sites, and radio upgrades at approximately 130 locations. By mid-year, the fiber-optic network passed 992 thousand households, covering 56.4 percent of national residential potential. Construction also began on a new Tier III data center near Vilnius, with planned investments of EUR 26 million for initial modules expected by late 2027. Executive appointments, including Andreas Ekström joining the Board and Vaidas Dominauskas assuming the role of Head of Enterprise, aligned leadership structures with these sustained infrastructure and commercial targets.
