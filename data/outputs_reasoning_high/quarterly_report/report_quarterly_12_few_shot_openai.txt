AS LHV Group’s Q2 2026 performance improved significantly from the previous quarter but remained weaker than a year earlier. Consolidated net profit rose 25% quarter on quarter to EUR 24.7 million, including EUR 24.1 million attributable to the parent’s shareholders, while earnings per share increased to EUR 0.07 from EUR 0.06. Compared with Q2 2025, however, net profit declined 20% from EUR 30.8 million and earnings per share fell from EUR 0.09. For the first half of 2026, net profit was EUR 44.4 million, down 26% from EUR 59.9 million a year earlier.

Revenue performance strengthened during the quarter. Net operating income increased 7% from Q1 to EUR 79.0 million and was also 7% higher year on year. Net interest income was the main contributor, rising 5% quarter on quarter to EUR 62.5 million and 8% from Q2 2025, supported by loan growth and a higher interest-rate environment. Net fee and commission income was broadly unchanged at EUR 14.6 million sequentially but declined 6% year on year, reflecting lower client activity. Other financial income also improved, partly because of the revaluation of the bond portfolio. Operating expenses fell 4% from Q1 to EUR 43.6 million, but were 8% higher year on year. As a result, operating profit increased to EUR 35.4 million from EUR 28.1 million in Q1.

Credit-related charges were the main factor limiting profitability. Loan and bond portfolio losses increased to EUR 5.8 million from EUR 1.3 million in Q1, compared with a EUR 4.2 million gain in Q2 2025. Management attributed the charges to forward-looking, model-based provisions for specific clients, while stating that overall loan portfolio quality remained strong. The loan-loss reserve reached EUR 47.4 million at the end of June. Lower income-tax expense of EUR 5.0 million partly offset the higher impairment cost.

Business volumes continued to expand despite a reduction in funding. The Group’s loan portfolio grew 3% during the quarter to EUR 5.77 billion and was 15% above Q2 2025. Customer deposits declined 3% sequentially to EUR 7.56 billion, mainly because of lower platform and financial-intermediary deposits, although ordinary customer deposits increased. LHV Pank generated EUR 24.3 million of Q2 profit, while LHV Bank earned EUR 1.4 million, LHV Varahaldus EUR 0.4 million and LHV Kindlustus EUR 0.8 million. Insurance returned to profit as major losses and loss frequency decreased.

The Group’s headline return on equity was 13.1%, below the 17.4% reported a year earlier, while capital adequacy remained above the internal target at 20.8%. The quarter also included strategic initiatives, notably the migration of LHV Pank’s core systems to Amazon Web Services, adoption of a product-based management model, and receipt of Estonia’s first MiCA crypto-assets service-provider licence.
