In the first quarter of 2026, VILVI Group’s consolidated revenue increased 22.4% year on year to EUR 86.970 million, supported by a 54.2% increase in purchased basic-indicator milk to 155,954 tons. Revenue growth was particularly strong in Lithuania, which rose 72.9% to EUR 14.633 million, and in other countries, which increased 46.6% to EUR 29.818 million. European Union revenue was broadly stable, rising 0.6% to EUR 42.518 million.

The revenue mix changed substantially. Cheese, cheese products and other revenue declined 28.8% to EUR 37.641 million, while fresh milk products increased 73.4% to EUR 12.748 million and dry milk products rose 126.9% to EUR 24.659 million. Canned milk generated EUR 11.923 million in its first period of consolidation from January 1, 2026. The canned milk business was added following the January 16 acquisition of 100% of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB, which operates facilities producing canned milk products, butter and milk powders.

Profitability from underlying operating activity weakened significantly. EBITDA fell 50.2% to EUR 3.003 million, and the EBITDA margin declined from 8.5% to 3.5%. Gross profit decreased from EUR 7.459 million to EUR 5.420 million as cost of sales increased to EUR 81.550 million from EUR 63.613 million. Distribution and administrative expenses also increased. Nevertheless, operating profit rose from EUR 4.715 million to EUR 5.999 million, alongside other operating income of EUR 5.664 million compared with EUR 81,000 a year earlier. The report states that Q1 2026 EBITDA excludes preliminary negative goodwill of EUR 5.5 million related to the Marijampolės pieno konservai acquisition. Net profit increased 25.7% to EUR 5.085 million, while earnings per share rose 26.5% to EUR 0.43. Lower income tax partly supported the profit outcome, although net finance costs increased to EUR 851,000 from EUR 354,000.

The Group began commercial cheese production at its new Baltic Dairy Board factory in Bauska, Latvia, after completing construction, equipment installation and testing. Planned capacity is 18,000 tons annually, with exports to more than 40 countries and more than 100 planned jobs; the report does not quantify its effect on first-quarter results.

Expansion and acquisition activity increased leverage. Net financial debt rose 89.7% year on year to EUR 83.427 million, while total assets increased to EUR 220.311 million from EUR 188.977 million at year-end 2025. Operating cash flow remained negative at EUR 9.633 million, although this improved from negative EUR 12.800 million in Q1 2025. Financing generated EUR 15.836 million, but cash declined to EUR 951,000 at March 31, 2026.
