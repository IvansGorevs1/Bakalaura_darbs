AS Silvano Fashion Group reported stable overall revenue alongside a contraction in net profitability during the first six months of 2026 compared with the same period of the previous year. Consolidated revenue increased slightly by 0.6 percent to reach 28,590 thousand euros, supported by strong performance in the retail segment, which offset a decline in wholesale operations. Retail revenue grew by 13.6 percent to 13,548 thousand euros, representing 47.4 percent of total sales, whereas wholesale revenue decreased by 9.1 percent to 14,934 thousand euros.

Profitability metrics showed mixed results across the reporting period. The group achieved a gross profit of 15,892 thousand euros, representing a 5.3 percent increase due to lower costs of sold goods, pushing the gross margin up to 55.6 percent from 53.1 percent in the prior year. However, consolidated operating profit decreased by 3.5 percent to 5,751 thousand euros, and consolidated EBITDA declined slightly by 0.8 percent to 7,508 thousand euros. Net profit for the period experienced a more pronounced decrease, falling 31.2 percent to 4,321 thousand euros. Net profit attributable to equity holders of the parent company amounted to 3,961 thousand euros, down 31.5 percent compared with 5,781 thousand euros in the first six months of 2025. Consequently, basic and diluted earnings per share dropped to 0.11 euros from 0.16 euros.

Geographically, performance varied across the company's core markets. Sales in Russia, the group's largest market, decreased marginally by 0.6 percent in euros to 15,767 thousand euros, representing a 7.8 percent drop in local currency, with the group operating 67 self-owned stores at the end of the period. Conversely, sales in Belarus expanded by 6.4 percent to 9,046 thousand euros, translating to a 2.7 percent increase in local currency, supported by 65 self-owned stores. Operations in Ukraine and the Baltics reported decreases in revenue during the period.

The group’s financial position strengthened by the end of June 2026. Total assets rose by 5.1 percent to 98,603 thousand euros compared with the end of December 2025. Cash and cash equivalents increased significantly to 12,443 thousand euros, while short-term bank deposits over three months stood at 38,707 thousand euros. Total equity attributable to equity holders of the parent company increased by 6.1 percent to 81,479 thousand euros. Operating cash flow for the six-month period improved substantially, surging by 96.6 percent to 6,842 thousand euros compared with 3,481 thousand euros in the prior year. Total personnel decreased slightly to 1,594 employees by the close of the reporting period. Overall, the first half of 2026 was characterized by resilient retail demand and improved gross margins, weighed down by lower net earnings and reduced wholesale revenues.
