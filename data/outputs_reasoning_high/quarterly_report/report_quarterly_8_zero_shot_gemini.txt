During the 2025 financial year, AS Tallink Grupp faced a challenging operating environment marked by economic uncertainty, low consumer and business confidence in core markets, and geopolitical tensions. Consolidated revenue for 2025 reached EUR 765.3 million, representing a decrease from EUR 785.8 million in 2024. Despite the annual revenue decline, fourth quarter revenue rose by 2.4 percent year-on-year to EUR 188.0 million compared to EUR 183.5 million in the fourth quarter of 2024. Total passenger numbers for the year decreased slightly by 0.9 percent to 5,531,132 passengers, while transported cargo units fell significantly by 19.2 percent to 245,004 units. Passenger vehicles declined by 2.2 percent to 760,473 units.

Profitability indicators showed decreases over the twelve-month period. Unaudited EBITDA for 2025 totaled EUR 130.1 million, down from EUR 175.2 million in 2024, and unaudited net profit dropped to EUR 17.3 million, or EUR 0.023 per share, compared to EUR 40.3 million, or EUR 0.054 per share, in the previous year. Gross profit for the year fell to EUR 143.2 million from EUR 153.6 million. However, fourth quarter performance showed improvement, with EBITDA reaching EUR 27.6 million compared to EUR 25.7 million in the fourth quarter of 2024, and net profit recovering to EUR 12.2 million from a net loss of EUR 5.2 million in the prior year period.

Operating results benefited from a reduction in amortization and depreciation expenses, which fell to EUR 79.8 million from EUR 97.8 million due to the alignment of estimated useful lives of cruise and passenger vessels to 45 years and the sale of three vessels during the year. Net finance costs also declined to EUR 21.4 million from EUR 28.3 million, supported by a decreased outstanding loan balance and declining interest rates. Throughout 2025, the Group completed the sale of three vessels, specifically the passenger vessel Star I, the cargo vessel Regal Star, and the cargo vessel Sailor, while maintaining three chartered-out vessels. Capital expenditures totaled EUR 33.0 million for the year, up from EUR 22.4 million in 2024, driven largely by maintenance and refurbishment works.

As of 31 December 2025, the Group's net debt stood at EUR 432.4 million, reflecting a reduction from EUR 537.7 million at the end of 2024, with a net debt to EBITDA ratio of 3.3. Cash and cash equivalents at year-end were EUR 13.5 million, accompanied by EUR 96.9 million in unused credit lines, forming a total liquidity buffer of EUR 110.4 million. Total loan repayments and interest payments during 2025 amounted to EUR 113.5 million. The company distributed EUR 44.6 million in dividends during the year, paying EUR 0.06 per share across two instalments, and the management board intends to propose a dividend of 6 euro cents per share for 2026.
