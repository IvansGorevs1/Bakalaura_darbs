AB KN Energies Group reported a strong financial performance for the three months ended 31 March 2026, marked by significant revenue growth and substantial improvements in profitability relative to the first quarter of 2025. Consolidated revenue increased by approximately 18% to EUR 29,823 thousand from EUR 25,326 thousand. This upward trajectory was spearheaded by the liquefied natural gas terminal (LNGT) segment, where revenue grew to EUR 18,687 thousand from EUR 14,989 thousand, supported by a rise in regasification service collections to EUR 19,162 thousand. The commercial LNG segment also saw robust growth, with revenue climbing to EUR 3,357 thousand from EUR 2,473 thousand. In contrast, liquid energy terminal revenues stabilized at EUR 7,779 thousand, marginally below EUR 7,864 thousand. Revenue figures were moderated by a EUR 905 thousand deduction corresponding to the proportional repayment of excess returns from regulated activities accumulated in 2022-2023.

Gross profit expanded significantly to EUR 12,907 thousand, compared to EUR 9,793 thousand, indicating healthy margin expansion. Gross margins improved despite cost of sales rising to EUR 16,916 thousand from EUR 15,533 thousand. Notable cost increases included environmental taxes and emission allowances, which rose to EUR 2,225 thousand from EUR 1,783 thousand, as well as higher wages and salaries totaling EUR 4,967 thousand versus EUR 4,479 thousand in the prior period. Other operating expenses such as repair and maintenance increased to EUR 691 thousand from EUR 308 thousand. Operating profit surged by 35% to EUR 9,608 thousand from EUR 7,141 thousand. Distributing this gain, the LNGT segment generated an EBITDA of EUR 11,761 thousand against EUR 8,683 thousand previously, underpinning the group's earnings power. Administrative and distribution costs rose moderately to EUR 3,316 thousand from EUR 3,002 thousand, influenced by higher telecommunications and IT expenses.

A decisive factor enhancing net results was the pronounced reduction in net finance costs. Net finance expenses contracted sharply to EUR 784 thousand from EUR 1,777 thousand. This improvement stemmed from lower interest expenses on borrowings, declining to EUR 1,729 thousand from EUR 2,160 thousand, coupled with the recognition of a EUR 458 thousand net gain on derivatives, whereas the prior period showed no derivative gains. As a result, profit before income tax more than doubled to EUR 8,824 thousand from EUR 5,364 thousand. Income tax expenses increased to EUR 1,577 thousand from EUR 938 thousand. The Group achieved a net profit of EUR 7,247 thousand, up from EUR 4,426 thousand, delivering basic earnings per share of EUR 0.02 compared to EUR 0.01.

Financial resilience was further evidenced by robust cash generation. Net cash flows from operating activities totaled EUR 27,917 thousand, a substantial rise from EUR 13,758 thousand, facilitated by a favorable working capital shift that included a EUR 7,010 thousand release in trade receivables. Capital expenditures increased to EUR 4,906 thousand from EUR 3,237 thousand, reflecting ongoing investment. The Company prioritized debt reduction, repaying EUR 7,728 thousand in borrowings, which brought total borrowings down to EUR 302,069 thousand from EUR 311,194 thousand. Cash and cash equivalents grew to EUR 17,986 thousand, and equity expanded to EUR 179,866 thousand, supported by retained earnings. Total assets remained stable at EUR 556,798 thousand. On the governance front, the Company transitioned to a unified Board of Directors following elections completed in late April 2026.
