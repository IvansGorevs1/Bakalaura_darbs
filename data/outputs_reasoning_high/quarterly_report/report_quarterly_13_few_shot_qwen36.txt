Liven AS reported a recovery in sales demand during the first half of 2026, although temporary profitability compression reflected lower home deliveries and capital-marketing costs. Half-year revenue increased by 22.8% to EUR 11.44 million from EUR 9.32 million in the prior-year period. Second-quarter revenue contracted to EUR 4.50 million from EUR 7.39 million, largely due to fewer completions and handovers. Underlying sales momentum accelerated significantly, with the company signing 111 contracts in the first six months, nearly double the 56 signed a year earlier. The presale portfolio grew to EUR 48.6 million by quarter-end, fueled by new launches in the Regati, Iseära, and Kadakadabra projects. Improved buyer interest drove the weekly sales ratio to a quarterly average of 2.4%, rising to nearly 3.1% by June. Liven secured over 8% of the Greater Tallinn market, supported by stabilizing consumer prices and stronger household purchasing power.

Profitability weakened relative to the previous year due to revenue timing and non-recurring expenses. Half-year profit before tax fell to EUR 186 thousand from EUR 296 thousand, resulting in a net loss of EUR 198 thousand compared to a prior-year net profit of EUR 268 thousand. The second quarter recorded a net loss of EUR 618 thousand, primarily impacted by an EUR 384 thousand income tax charge tied to dividend distributions and approximately EUR 90 thousand in offering-related administrative costs. EBITDA margins compressed to 21.2% from 29.8% in 2025, though operating cash flows remained positive despite elevated construction expenditures.

The group’s balance sheet strengthened considerably following a successful initial public offering in April and May, which raised EUR 4.93 million through the issuance of over 1.05 million shares at EUR 4.68 each. Total equity expanded to EUR 26.7 million, lifting the adjusted equity ratio to 32.0%. Total borrowings reached EUR 57.3 million, with construction loans increasing to EUR 16.6 million to finance active developments. During the quarter, the company acquired two residential plots in Berlin, expanding a development pipeline that encompasses 136,506 square meters and an estimated EUR 497.2 million in future revenue. Post-period, a EUR 10 million central Tallinn property acquisition was finalized. Management emphasized that financial results will concentrate heavily in the fourth quarter as the sales cycle transitions into delivery phases. With nearly 90% of the EUR 59 million annual revenue target now backed by contracts or completions, the company anticipates a swift return to profitability and sustained returns exceeding its 20% equity target as construction scales and financing requirements normalize.
