During the second quarter and first six months of 2026, Liven AS experienced strong residential real estate market activity alongside significant corporate developments, notably the initial public offering and listing of its shares on the Baltic Main List of the Nasdaq Tallinn Stock Exchange on 15 May 2026. Through the offering, the company issued 1,053,936 new shares at EUR 4.68 per share, generating net proceeds of EUR 4,687 thousand out of a total volume of EUR 4,932 thousand. For the six months ended 30 June 2026, the group reported revenue of EUR 11,439 thousand, compared to EUR 9,319 thousand in the same period of 2025. Profit before income tax for the first six months was EUR 186 thousand, compared to EUR 296 thousand a year earlier, while the net result for the six-month period was a loss of EUR 198 thousand following a net profit of EUR 268 thousand in the first half of 2025. The net loss was influenced by corporate income tax expense on dividends and one-off public offering costs. For the second quarter alone, revenue reached EUR 4,503 thousand, down from EUR 7,388 thousand in the second quarter of 2025, and the net loss for the quarter was EUR 618 thousand compared to a net profit of EUR 974 thousand previously. Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, and amortisation for the six months totalled EUR 2,425 thousand with an EBITDA margin of 21.2 percent, compared to EUR 2,777 thousand and 29.8 percent in the first half of 2025. The group handed over 26 homes and commercial units during the first six months, including 12 units in the second quarter, reflecting the cyclical timing of project completions. Despite lower interim revenue recognition in the second quarter, sales momentum remained robust. The group signed 111 sales contracts under the law of obligations during the first six months, nearly double the 56 contracts signed in the first half of 2025, with 57 contracts signed in the second quarter. Liven entered the third quarter with 171 contracts under the law of obligations and a total presale volume of EUR 48.6 million, of which EUR 39.8 million relates to buildings scheduled for completion in 2026. Market demand was supported by recovering household purchasing power, slowing inflation, and wage growth outpacing price increases, pushing Liven's market share in Greater Tallinn to over 8 percent over the six months. Total assets grew to EUR 100,186 thousand as of 30 June 2026, up from EUR 86,457 thousand at the end of 2025, driven largely by continued construction and new property acquisitions. Total loan liabilities reached EUR 57,341 thousand, including construction loans of EUR 16,561 thousand. Total equity rose to EUR 26,746 thousand, resulting in an equity ratio of 26.7 percent and an adjusted equity ratio excluding construction loans of 32.0 percent. During the quarter, the company paid EUR 1,362 thousand in dividends from 2025 profit. Furthermore, Liven expanded its development portfolio by acquiring new properties in Berlin and Tallinn. Management notes that nearly 90 percent of the 2026 revenue target of EUR 59 million is now realized or covered by contracts, with earnings and home handovers heavily concentrated in the final quarter of the year, supporting expectations to exceed the 20 percent return on equity target.
