AS Pro Kapital Grupp reported a substantial recovery in financial performance during the twelve months ended December 2025 compared to the prior year. Revenue surged to EUR 53.2 million from EUR 18.2 million in 2024, driven by the completion and handover of major residential projects including developments in the Kalaranna and Uus-Kinderali districts in Tallinn and the City Villas project in Vilnius. The revenue growth translated into strong profitability improvements. Gross profit rose to EUR 18.8 million with a margin of 35%, up from EUR 5.4 million at 30% margins in 2024. Operating profit strengthened significantly to EUR 14.7 million, a marked improvement over the near-break-even operating result of EUR 0.1 million recorded previously. Consequently, net profit turned positive at EUR 12.0 million, contrasting with a net loss of EUR 3.9 million in the comparable period, resulting in earnings per share of EUR 0.22 versus a loss of EUR 0.06. Return on equity improved to 21.0% from negative levels last year.

The Estonian segment became the dominant contributor, accounting for 75% of total revenue compared to 27% in 2024, reflecting successful project completions there. Conversely, the Latvian segment's contribution declined sharply as remaining inventory was liquidated. Cash generated from operating activities reversed to a positive EUR 10.8 million from an outflow of EUR 10.4 million in the previous year. Net assets per share increased to EUR 1.12 from EUR 0.89. Operational progress was notable across key markets. In Riga, construction commenced on the Blue Marine project within the Kliversala Quarter, supported by new financing arrangements. Business development advanced in Tallinn with permits obtained for historical buildings and continued marketing for larger phases. International segments faced mixed conditions; hotel operations in Germany yielded a net profit of EUR 0.4 million but saw softer occupancy rates. Italian real estate brokerage activities remained subdued amid challenging market dynamics, leading to a full impairment loss of EUR 863 thousand against goodwill acquired in the acquisition of Preatoni Nuda Proprietà S.r.l., though the group increased its stake in the subsidiary to 77.5% during the year.

Financial discipline was maintained through conservative funding policies. The group partially redeemed EUR 9.4 million of senior secured callable fixed rate bonds in August 2025, reducing the outstanding nominal amount to approximately EUR 10.5 million. The debt-to-equity ratio improved to 0.97 from 1.32, and total liabilities decreased while equity grew. Management highlighted a robust pipeline of development projects and ongoing focus on delivering high-quality residential assets, reinforcing confidence in sustained growth and value creation.
