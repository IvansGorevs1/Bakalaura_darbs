Liven’s second-quarter performance weakened year on year because project handovers were limited. Revenue was EUR 4.503 million, down from EUR 7.388 million in Q2 2025, while EBITDA fell to EUR 884 thousand from EUR 2.390 million and operating profit declined to a loss of EUR 177 thousand from a profit of EUR 1.012 million. The group reported a EUR 234 thousand loss before tax, compared with a EUR 1.002 million profit a year earlier. Net loss was EUR 618 thousand, versus net profit of EUR 974 thousand, and basic loss per share was EUR 0.049 compared with earnings of EUR 0.081. The quarter included approximately EUR 90 thousand of one-off public-offering costs and EUR 384 thousand of income tax related to dividends.

For the first six months, revenue increased to EUR 11.439 million from EUR 9.319 million, although profitability declined. EBITDA was EUR 2.425 million, compared with EUR 2.777 million, and the EBITDA margin fell to 21.2% from 29.8%. Operating profit increased modestly to EUR 341 thousand from EUR 325 thousand, but profit before tax declined to EUR 186 thousand from EUR 296 thousand. After the dividend-related tax expense, the group recorded a net loss of EUR 198 thousand, compared with net profit of EUR 268 thousand in the first half of 2025. The lower quarterly revenue and uneven results reflect the timing of construction completions and handovers: Liven handed over 12 homes in Q2 and 26 in the first half, compared with 35 and 41, respectively, in the corresponding 2025 periods.

Sales momentum improved substantially. Liven signed 57 sales contracts in Q2, compared with 31 a year earlier, and 111 in the first half versus 56. The weekly sales ratio reached nearly 3.1% at quarter-end, above the long-term normal range of 1.5–2.0%, while the quarterly average rose to approximately 2.4% from 1.5% in Q1. Presales grew to EUR 48.6 million, including EUR 39.8 million for projects scheduled for completion in 2026. Together with first-half revenue and early third-quarter sales, nearly 90% of the EUR 59 million 2026 revenue target was realized or covered by contracts.

The recovery in demand was supported by improving household purchasing power, slower price growth and a higher tax-free minimum, despite geopolitical uncertainty and a rise in six-month Euribor to 2.57%. Continued construction increased assets by EUR 8.505 million during the quarter to EUR 100.186 million. The IPO raised EUR 4.932 million gross, strengthening capital for new developments; shares began trading on Nasdaq Tallinn’s Main List on 15 May. The development portfolio expanded to 136,506 square metres with forecast revenue of EUR 497.2 million, following new Berlin acquisitions and, after the reporting date, the EUR 10 million acquisition of Tallinn properties at Pärnu maantee. Management expects most 2026 handovers, revenue and profit in the final quarter and forecasts net profit to exceed the 20% return-on-equity target.
