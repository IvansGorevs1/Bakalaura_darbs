During the first six months of 2026, Aktsiaselts Infortar recorded consolidated sales revenue of EUR 945,917 thousand, remaining stable compared to EUR 951,869 thousand in the first half of 2025. Despite the slight revenue decrease, the group achieved substantial profit growth. Gross profit for the six-month period rose to EUR 114,132 thousand from EUR 81,736 thousand, EBITDA increased to EUR 111,228 thousand from EUR 85,051 thousand, and operating profit grew to EUR 51,068 thousand from EUR 26,383 thousand. The group reported a net profit of EUR 7,201 thousand for the first six months of 2026, recovering from a net loss of EUR 14,411 thousand in the prior-year period. Net profit attributable to the owners of the parent reached EUR 15,163 thousand compared to a net loss of EUR 2,549 thousand previously.

For the second quarter of 2026, consolidated revenue stood at EUR 440,921 thousand, down from EUR 504,512 thousand in the second quarter of 2025. Second-quarter EBITDA rose to EUR 64,158 thousand from EUR 57,390 thousand, operating profit reached EUR 29,686 thousand compared to EUR 27,038 thousand, and net profit for the period was EUR 1,883 thousand versus EUR 150 thousand a year earlier.

Performance during the reporting period was shaped by differing sectoral developments. The revenue decrease was primarily driven by the energy segment, where Elenger Group experienced lower gas and electricity sales volumes due to reduced wholesale sales. Total natural gas consumption in the Finnish-Baltic region fell by seven percent to 7.0 terawatt-hours in the second quarter, and Elenger's sales volumes dropped by 34 percent to 3.3 terawatt-hours. Conversely, profitability improved due to stronger operational performance across segments, contributions from new agricultural units, and a major turnaround in supporting businesses, where EBITDA shifted from a loss of EUR 4.4 million to a profit of EUR 5.6 million.

In the maritime transport segment, Tallink Grupp reported second-quarter revenue of EUR 207.0 million and a net loss of EUR 2.5 million, both unchanged year-on-year, while EBITDA decreased to EUR 34.3 million from EUR 37.4 million. Tallink transported 1,454,725 passengers, a 2.2 percent decrease. Operations faced challenges from geopolitical tensions, weaker consumer confidence, increased fuel prices raising fuel costs by EUR 8.4 million, and stricter EU Emissions Trading System requirements mandating the surrender of 100 percent of emitted carbon dioxide equivalents. During the quarter, Infortar increased its ownership in Tallink Grupp to 68.76 percent.

As of 30 June 2026, Infortar Group held total assets of EUR 2,714,309 thousand, compared to EUR 2,596,607 thousand at the end of 2025. Total equity increased to EUR 1,200,533 thousand from EUR 1,175,404 thousand, interest-bearing liabilities stood at EUR 1,083,091 thousand, and cash and cash equivalents totaled EUR 253,187 thousand, maintaining a robust financial position.
