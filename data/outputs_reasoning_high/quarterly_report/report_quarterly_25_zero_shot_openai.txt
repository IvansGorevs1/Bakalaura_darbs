In the first quarter of 2026, VILVI Group’s consolidated revenue increased 22.4% year on year to EUR 86.97 million, compared with EUR 71.07 million in Q1 2025. Growth was accompanied by a substantial increase in purchased milk volumes, which rose 54.2% to 155,954 tons. However, profitability was mixed. EBITDA declined 50.2% to EUR 3.00 million, and the EBITDA margin fell from 8.5% to 3.5%. Net profit increased 25.7% to EUR 5.09 million, while earnings per share rose from EUR 0.34 to EUR 0.43 and the net profit margin edged up from 5.7% to 5.8%.

The income statement shows that gross profit decreased from EUR 7.46 million to EUR 5.42 million, while cost of sales rose to EUR 81.55 million from EUR 63.61 million. Operating profit nevertheless increased to EUR 6.00 million from EUR 4.72 million, reflecting other operating income of EUR 5.66 million compared with EUR 0.08 million in the previous year. Finance costs also increased, reaching EUR 0.87 million versus EUR 0.45 million. Q1 2026 EBITDA excludes preliminary negative goodwill of EUR 5.5 million related to the acquisition of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB.

The Group completed the acquisition of 100% of Marijampolės pieno konservai on 16 January 2026. The acquired company produces canned milk products, butter and milk powders, and its revenue contribution is reflected in the newly reported canned milk segment, which generated EUR 11.92 million in Q1. Dry milk products revenue more than doubled to EUR 24.66 million, fresh milk products revenue rose 73.4% to EUR 12.75 million, while cheese, cheese products and other revenue fell 28.8% to EUR 37.64 million. The Group also began commercial cheese production at its new Baltic Dairy Board factory in Latvia, with planned annual capacity of 18,000 tons and exports to more than 40 countries.

Revenue growth was strongest in Lithuania, up 72.9% to EUR 14.63 million, and in other countries, up 46.6% to EUR 29.82 million. European Union revenue was broadly stable at EUR 42.52 million. The acquisition and ongoing investment increased total assets to EUR 220.31 million from EUR 188.98 million at year-end 2025, while net financial debt rose 89.7% to EUR 83.43 million. Operating cash flow remained negative at EUR 9.63 million, and cash declined to EUR 0.95 million, partly offset by EUR 15.84 million of net financing inflows.
