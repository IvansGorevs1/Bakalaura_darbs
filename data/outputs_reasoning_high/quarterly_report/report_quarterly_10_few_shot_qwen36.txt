Infortar Group delivered strong operational improvements in 2025 despite facing macroeconomic headwinds and geopolitical uncertainty. The group recorded full-year revenue of EUR 1,837.0 million, representing a substantial increase compared with EUR 1,371.8 million in 2024, primarily driven by the full-year consolidation of Tallink Grupp and organic growth in the energy segment. EBITDA expanded significantly to EUR 232.6 million from EUR 145.4 million the prior year, while operating profit rose to EUR 111.1 million. Although net profit decreased to EUR 72.0 million from EUR 191.3 million, management noted this decline was attributable to non-recurring fair value and tax effects related to the initial accounting treatment of the Tallink acquisition in 2024 rather than underlying operational deterioration.

Quarterly performance underscored a clear turnaround. Fourth quarter revenue amounted to EUR 417.4 million, down marginally from EUR 446.2 million in the same period of 2024, but gross profit nearly doubled to EUR 63.3 million. EBITDA surged to EUR 42.6 million with a margin improvement to 10.2 percent, and the division shifted from a fourth-quarter operating loss to a EUR 19.8 million profit. Net profit in the quarter reversed to a EUR 14.1 million gain. The maritime transport segment saw passenger volumes remain relatively stable but experienced a sharp 19.2 percent drop in cargo tonnage. Operational results in the quarter were moderated by subdued domestic consumer confidence, geopolitical tensions, the disposal of a cargo vessel, and substantial debt reduction efforts that included the repayment of EUR 21.8 million in long-term loans alongside associated interest payments. Conversely, the energy division grew market share in the Finnish-Baltic region, increasing sales volumes by 7 percent year-on-year to 4.8 terawatt-hours despite an overall 2 percent contraction in regional natural gas demand. Management also secured a long-term liquefied natural gas supply contract and advanced renewable infrastructure projects, including battery storage installations in Latvia.

The group strengthened its financial position throughout the reporting period. Total equity reached EUR 1,177.5 million, yielding an equity ratio of 45.5 percent. Interest-bearing liabilities declined to EUR 1,060.5 million, and leverage metrics improved, with net debt falling to EUR 840.7 million and the net debt to EBITDA ratio tightening to 3.6 times. Cash reserves rose to EUR 219.8 million, providing robust liquidity. Following these results, the board announced plans to propose a dividend aligned with its long-term shareholder return policy. Strategic initiatives continued across real estate development and agricultural holdings, with the recent full acquisition of a biomethane production entity further broadening the portfolio defensive characteristics. Overall, the company demonstrated enhanced earnings stability, disciplined capital management, and successful execution of its diversified, risk-mitigated business model during a challenging macroeconomic environment.
