AB KN Energies reported improved consolidated results for the nine months ended 30 September 2025. Revenue increased 12.8% to EUR 76.664 million from EUR 67.977 million, while operating profit rose 12.4% to EUR 21.282 million. Profit before tax increased 8.6% to EUR 16.186 million, and net profit grew 10.9% to EUR 13.158 million. Earnings per share rose to EUR 0.035 from EUR 0.031. Gross profit increased 7.9% to EUR 31.549 million, although cost of sales grew faster than revenue, rising 16.5% to EUR 45.115 million and resulting in a somewhat lower gross margin. Operating expenses increased only 2.1% to EUR 10.640 million.

The principal growth driver was LNG terminal activity. Revenue from LNGT services increased to EUR 43.640 million from EUR 31.401 million, supported by higher regasification volumes, customer bonuses for booked capacity, and the fact that the terminal had been out of operation for one and a half months for dry-dock repairs in 2024. Revenue from liquid energy product transshipment rose to EUR 21.375 million from EUR 20.241 million, while consulting revenue increased to EUR 7.507 million from EUR 3.961 million. These gains were partly offset by lower income from other LNG terminal activities and a EUR 2.753 million reduction related to repayment of excess returns from regulated activities. Segment net profit increased in LET and LNGT activities, but declined in commercial LNG activities to EUR 2.411 million from EUR 3.948 million.

Cost growth was mainly influenced by depreciation and amortisation, which increased to EUR 18.335 million from EUR 6.865 million. Following the acquisition of the floating storage and regasification unit in December 2024, EUR 11.369 million of related depreciation was recorded in this category in 2025 rather than as right-of-use depreciation. Wages, FSRU operation and maintenance, and environmental taxes also increased. Finance income fell to EUR 1.520 million from EUR 2.644 million, while finance costs were broadly stable at EUR 6.616 million. In the third quarter alone, revenue was nearly unchanged at EUR 25.581 million, but net profit declined to EUR 4.570 million from EUR 10.403 million.

Cash generation strengthened significantly. Operating cash flow rose to EUR 76.331 million from EUR 35.637 million, partly reflecting the reclassification of EUR 31.900 million of financial assets to cash and cash equivalents. Year-end cash increased to EUR 54.398 million from EUR 17.203 million, while borrowings declined to EUR 311.443 million from EUR 320.352 million. Total equity rose to EUR 167.721 million despite EUR 8.622 million in dividends. The separate company also improved, with revenue rising 12.1% to EUR 72.848 million and net profit increasing 8.5% to EUR 11.613 million.
