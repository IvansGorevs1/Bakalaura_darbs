Tallink Grupp’s 2025 performance weakened overall, although results improved significantly in the fourth quarter. Full-year revenue declined by EUR 20.5 million to EUR 765.3 million, while EBITDA fell to EUR 130.1 million from EUR 175.2 million and net profit decreased to EUR 17.3 million from EUR 40.3 million. EBITDA margin declined from 22.3% to 17.0%, and net profit margin from 5.1% to 2.3%. Gross profit fell to EUR 143.2 million from EUR 153.6 million, while operating profit declined to EUR 50.3 million from EUR 77.4 million.

Demand was subdued by low consumer and business confidence, economic uncertainty in the core markets—particularly Finland—persistent inflation, geopolitical tensions and weaker cargo flows. Passenger volumes decreased by 0.9% to 5.5 million, cargo units fell 19.2% to 245,004, and passenger vehicles declined 2.2% to 760,473. Route operations revenue decreased by EUR 13.7 million to EUR 625.2 million and the segment result fell to EUR 70.5 million from EUR 81.5 million. Estonia-Finland revenue increased by EUR 1.7 million, but its segment result fell by EUR 10.9 million. Finland-Sweden revenue and result also declined, while Estonia-Sweden revenue fell by EUR 12.1 million, although its loss narrowed to EUR 1.2 million from EUR 2.6 million.

The decline in profitability occurred despite lower depreciation and financing costs. Depreciation and amortisation decreased by EUR 18.0 million to EUR 79.8 million after the estimated useful lives of most cruise and passenger vessels were aligned to 45 years and three vessels were sold. Net finance costs fell by EUR 6.8 million to EUR 21.4 million because of lower debt and declining interest rates. However, total operating costs increased 1.4%, including a 5.1% increase in staff costs.

In the fourth quarter, revenue rose 2.4% year-on-year to EUR 188.0 million, EBITDA increased 7.3% to EUR 27.6 million, and the Group generated net profit of EUR 12.2 million compared with a EUR 5.2 million loss in the prior-year quarter. Core route segment results improved to EUR 16.1 million from EUR 9.9 million. The report cites lower depreciation, the sale of the cargo vessel Sailor and the sale of surplus FuelEU Maritime compliance units among the factors affecting fourth-quarter results.

The Group sold three vessels, maintained three vessels on charter and ended the year with no vessels in lay-up. Net debt was broadly unchanged at EUR 432.4 million, while EUR 113.5 million of loan and interest payments were made. Cash declined to EUR 13.5 million, partly because EUR 44.6 million of dividends and EUR 11.3 million of related income tax were paid. The Board intends to propose another dividend of EUR 0.06 per share for 2026.
