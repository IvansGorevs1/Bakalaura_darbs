SAF Tehnika reported a consolidated net turnover of EUR 26.11 million for the twelve months ended June 30, 2025, representing a four percent decline from the previous financial year. The fourth quarter contributed EUR 5.18 million in revenue, marking a thirteen percent decrease year over year. Regional performance shifted notably, with North and Latin American turnover falling by fourteen percent annually and forty percent in the quarter, while European revenue grew seventeen percent and twenty-four percent respectively. Revenue from Asia, Africa, and the Middle East remained relatively stable on an annual basis but doubled during the fourth quarter. The company recorded a net loss of EUR 820,000 for the full year, a substantial improvement compared to the EUR 2.37 million loss posted in the prior year. However, the fourth quarter alone resulted in a net loss of EUR 913,000, nearly double the previous year quarterly figure, driven primarily by project completion cycles that delay revenue recognition and associated costs into later periods.

Operating cash flow strengthened significantly to EUR 2.13 million over the twelve-month period, helping the company build its cash reserve to EUR 4.81 million by period end. This liquidity improvement was supported by customer advances totaling EUR 3.9 million and maintained within a fully available EUR 4.95 million bank credit line. The Group actively managed supply chain constraints related to global electronic component shortages by accumulating strategic material reserves, which successfully shortened delivery times and ensured order fulfillment throughout the reporting period.

Employee headcount increased to 273, contributing to an eight percent rise in salary and bonus expenses, yet overall operating costs remained within budget. Direct costs of goods sold decreased substantially, aiding the narrowing of the annual net loss. Management continues to allocate resources toward new product development, including IoT environmental monitoring solutions and next-generation microwave transmission systems, alongside expanded sales promotion efforts. The board acknowledges persistent macroeconomic uncertainty and monitors potential tariff adjustments but confirms no operational disruptions from regional conflicts. With a clear objective to stabilize recurring turnover and secure long-term profitability, the company maintains a cautious outlook while demonstrating improved cost discipline and working capital efficiency during a transitional period of portfolio diversification and market realignment.
