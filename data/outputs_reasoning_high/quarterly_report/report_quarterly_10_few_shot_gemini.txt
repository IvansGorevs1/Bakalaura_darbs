During the financial year 2025, the Infortar Group demonstrated strong overall financial performance, characterized by stable operational cash flows and a solid balance sheet, despite ongoing economic uncertainty and geopolitical tensions. Consolidated revenue for the twelve-month period reached 1.837 billion euros, compared to 1.372 billion euros in the previous year, with the increase largely reflecting the full-year consolidation of Tallink Grupp following its acquisition in August 2024. Group EBITDA grew significantly to 232.581 million euros, up from 145.415 million euros in 2024, supported by improved profitability in the energy segment and broader business contributions. The EBITDA margin improved to 13.4% from 10.6%. However, net profit for the financial year decreased to 71.969 million euros from 193.670 million euros reported in the previous year. This decline was primarily attributable to one-off effects and initial consolidation gains related to Tallink Grupp recognized during 2024. Net profit attributable to the owners of the parent company amounted to 72.662 million euros, compared to 191.253 million euros a year earlier. Basic earnings per share stood at 3.54 euros, down from 9.36 euros in 2024. For the fourth quarter of 2025 specifically, the group reported revenue of 417.438 million euros, operating profit of 19.842 million euros, and a net profit of 14.141 million euros, recovering from a net loss of 11.988 million euros in the final quarter of 2024. The group's financial position remained robust as of December 31, 2025, with total assets amounting to 2.588 billion euros, down slightly from 2.715 billion euros at the end of 2024. Total equity increased to 1.178 billion euros from 1.166 billion euros, strengthening the equity ratio to 45.5% compared to 43.0% previously. Interest-bearing liabilities decreased notably to 1.060 billion euros from 1.223 billion euros, driven by disciplined loan repayments across several group entities, including Tallink Grupp. Net debt improved to 840.682 million euros from 1.055 billion euros. Cash and cash equivalents rose to 219.771 million euros at year-end, up from 167.579 million euros. Operating cash flows showed a substantial increase, totaling 388.632 million euros for the twelve months compared to 89.288 million euros in 2024, aided by deliberate and market-driven inventory management that reduced natural gas storage levels and by stable operational results without major acquisition-related one-off impacts. The maritime transport segment experienced lower passenger and cargo transport metrics alongside reduced revenue and profitability due to market headwinds, while the energy segment expanded its sales volumes and improved earnings despite volatile European gas prices. Overall, the group successfully diversified its portfolio into areas like agriculture and infrastructure while maintaining strict financial discipline and strong liquidity.
