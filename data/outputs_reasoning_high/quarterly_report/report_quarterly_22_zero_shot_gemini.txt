During the first half of 2026, Telia Lietuva, AB demonstrated strong overall financial performance, marked by solid growth across revenue, profitability, and cash generation. Total revenue for the six-month period ended June 30, 2026, grew by 7.2 per cent to reach EUR 259.1 million compared to EUR 241.7 million in the same period of 2025. This top-line growth was supported by increases across all main revenue streams. Fixed services revenue increased by 5.3 per cent to EUR 112.9 million, mobile services revenue rose by 8.3 per cent to EUR 100.0 million, and equipment sales revenue grew by 9.6 per cent to EUR 46.1 million.

Profitability indicators showed significant improvements. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization, known as EBITDA, increased by 9.5 per cent to EUR 105.5 million, exceeding the EUR 100 million threshold for an interim period for the first time, with the EBITDA margin standing at 40.7 per cent compared to 39.9 per cent a year ago. Adjusted EBITDA excluding non-recurring items grew by 10.2 per cent to EUR 106.9 million, resulting in an adjusted EBITDA margin of 41.3 per cent. Operating profit, or EBIT, rose by 15.1 per cent to EUR 62.4 million, while profit for the period increased by 14.7 per cent to EUR 51.3 million compared to EUR 44.7 million in the previous year. Basic and diluted earnings per share went up by 14.7 per cent to EUR 0.088.

The company's performance was influenced by higher equipment sales volumes, increased network interconnection and roaming costs, and non-recurring redundancy expenses of EUR 1.4 million associated with the restructuring of the digital and technology unit. Operating expenses excluding non-recurring items rose by 10.2 per cent to EUR 67.3 million. Total headcount decreased by 6.9 per cent to 1,601 employees by the end of June 2026, down from 1,720 a year earlier. 

Operating cash flow for the first half of 2026 reached EUR 103.1 million, representing a 15.0 per cent increase. Free cash flow grew by 12.5 per cent to EUR 64.1 million. Capital investments surged by 57.9 per cent to EUR 37.5 million, driven by investments in IT systems and infrastructure, mobile network expansion, and the acquisition of 5G spectrum licenses. Major operational developments during the period included acquiring 40 MHz of 1,500 MHz frequencies for nearly EUR 8 million and extending the right to use the 2,100 MHz frequency band for EUR 2 million to advance 5G network development, alongside commencing the construction of a new data center near Vilnius with planned investments of up to EUR 26 million. Following the annual general meeting decision in May 2026, the company distributed a record EUR 81.6 million in dividends, or EUR 0.14 per share, for the year 2025. Total assets as of June 30, 2026, stood at EUR 623.2 million, with shareholders equity amounting to EUR 372.0 million.
