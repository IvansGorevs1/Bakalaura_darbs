During the second quarter of 2026, AS LHV Group reported a consolidated net profit of EUR 24.7 million, representing an increase of EUR 5.0 million or 25% compared to the EUR 19.7 million recorded in the first quarter of 2026, though lower than the EUR 30.8 million reported in the second quarter of 2025. Net profit attributable to the owners of the parent was EUR 24.1 million in Q2 2026, up from EUR 19.9 million in Q1 2026 and down from EUR 30.0 million in Q2 2025. For the first six months of 2026, total net profit reached EUR 44.4 million, compared to EUR 59.9 million for the same period in 2025. Total net operating income in Q2 2026 amounted to EUR 79.0 million, reflecting a 7.3% increase from EUR 73.7 million in Q1 2026 and a 7% increase from EUR 73.9 million in Q2 2025. Net interest income grew to EUR 62.5 million, up 5% quarter-over-quarter and 8% year-over-year. Net fee and commission income remained stable at EUR 14.6 million. Operating expenses decreased by 4% during the quarter to EUR 43.6 million, compared to EUR 40.5 million in Q2 2025. Loan and bond provisions increased to EUR 5.8 million in Q2 2026, up from EUR 1.3 million in Q1 2026. The return on equity attributable to shareholders rose to 13.1% in Q2 2026 from 10.7% in Q1 2026, though it remained lower than the 17.4% recorded in Q2 2025. Capital adequacy stood at 20.8% at the end of June 2026, compared to 21.7% in the previous quarter and 23.0% in Q2 2025. 

Total assets at the end of the reporting period were EUR 9,652 million. The Group's loan volume expanded by 3% during the quarter to reach EUR 5,768 million, marking a 15% increase compared to Q2 2025. Conversely, total customer deposits decreased by 3% during the quarter to EUR 7,557 million. Business results were influenced by an operating environment marked by persistent geopolitical tensions, fluctuating energy prices, and tighter monetary policy by central banks, including rising Euribor rates. Across individual business units, AS LHV Pank contributed a net profit of EUR 24.3 million, LHV Bank Limited earned EUR 1.4 million, AS LHV Varahaldus posted a net profit of EUR 0.4 million, and AS LHV Kindlustus generated EUR 0.8 million, successfully recovering from a Q1 loss. Key strategic developments during the period included AS LHV Pank successfully migrating its core systems to the Amazon Web Services cloud environment, adopting a new product-based management model, securing a crypto-assets service provider license under the MiCA Regulation, and initiating preparations to change its business name to LHV Bank AS to support international growth. Overall, despite higher loan impairment provisions and a challenging external geopolitical background, the company demonstrated solid underlying revenue growth, driven primarily by strong net interest income performance and successful technological infrastructure upgrades.
