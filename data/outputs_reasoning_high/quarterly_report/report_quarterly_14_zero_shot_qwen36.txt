AS Merko Ehitus Group reported revenues of EUR 149.9 million for the first half of 2026, representing a 10.7 percent decline from EUR 167.9 million in the same period of 2025. Second-quarter revenues reached EUR 92.7 million, up from EUR 82.6 million in Q2 2025. Despite higher quarterly activity, full-half profitability weakened significantly. Net profit attributable to shareholders fell to EUR 12.8 million from EUR 21.7 million in H1 2025, reducing the net profit margin to 8.6 percent from 12.9 percent. Pre-tax profit also declined to EUR 10.8 million, bringing the pre-tax margin to 7.2 percent against 14.0 percent a year earlier. The operating profit amounted to EUR 8.4 million, down from EUR 18.6 million, with an operating margin of 5.6 percent.

Performance varied across segments. The construction services division generated EUR 118.3 million in revenue, a modest 1.5 percent decrease, but its operating profit dropped sharply to EUR 3.0 million with a margin of just 2.6 percent, primarily due to the expiration of historically profitable contracts and a reversion to standard competitive pricing. The real estate development segment saw a steeper revenue contraction of 33.9 percent to EUR 31.6 million, alongside a fall in apartment sales from 222 to 120 units. Nevertheless, the segment maintained strong profitability, delivering an operating profit of EUR 7.0 million and a 22.2 percent margin. The group launched construction and marketing for 204 new apartments while investing EUR 42.6 million in ongoing development projects.

Market conditions continued to be shaped by persistent inflationary pressures, with management noting that anticipated rises in construction input costs materialized in the second quarter. Geopolitical tensions and elevated energy prices are expected to sustain higher price levels through year-end. Demand across Estonia, Latvia, and Lithuania remains highly price-sensitive, driving a shift toward more affordable residential units. Against this backdrop, the company’s secured order book grew substantially, reaching EUR 835.7 million as of 30 June 2026, up 88 percent from EUR 443.8 million a year prior. This expansion was fueled by approximately EUR 486.4 million in newly signed contracts, heavily weighted toward public-sector infrastructure and defence-related projects.

Financially, cash and cash equivalents increased to EUR 39.6 million, though operating cash flows turned negative at EUR 31.6 million due to seasonal inventory buildup in development projects and shifts in working capital. Net debt rose to EUR 29.6 million from a net cash position of EUR 1.1 million the previous year, pushing the debt ratio to 15.8 percent. Total equity stood at EUR 251.1 million, representing 57.4 percent of total assets. Shareholders received dividends of EUR 22.1 million, equivalent to EUR 1.25 per share. Overall, the first half reflected stronger forward-looking contract acquisition and resilient long-term balance sheet metrics, tempered by near-term margin compression and a cyclical slowdown in apartment deliveries amid sustained cost inflation.
