During the three-month period ended 31 March 2026, AB KN Energies and its subsidiaries demonstrated strong financial performance, reporting revenue from contracts with customers of EUR 29,823 thousand for the Group, compared to EUR 25,326 thousand in the corresponding period of 2025. For the parent company alone, revenue reached EUR 28,681 thousand, up from EUR 24,283 thousand. Gross profit for the Group grew to EUR 12,907 thousand from EUR 9,793 thousand, while the company's gross profit improved to EUR 12,150 thousand from EUR 9,050 thousand. Operating profit for the Group advanced to EUR 9,608 thousand compared with EUR 7,141 thousand in the prior year period.

The Group reported a net profit for the period of EUR 7,247 thousand, reflecting an increase from EUR 4,426 thousand reported for the first three months of 2025. The parent company recorded a net profit of EUR 6,762 thousand, compared to EUR 4,016 thousand. Basic and diluted earnings per share for both the Group and the company rose to EUR 0.02, up from EUR 0.01 in the comparative period. Net finance costs improved to a negative EUR 784 thousand compared to a negative EUR 1,777 thousand in the prior year, influenced by lower interest expenses on borrowings and net gains on derivatives. Profit before income tax for the Group reached EUR 8,824 thousand, higher than the EUR 5,364 thousand recorded during the first three months of 2025.

Segment results show that liquefied natural gas terminal activities served as a primary growth driver. Revenue from LNG terminal regasification services collected directly from users amounted to EUR 19,162 thousand, compared to EUR 14,593 thousand previously. Sales of liquid energy products transshipment services generated EUR 7,596 thousand compared to EUR 6,615 thousand. Net cash flows from operating activities for the Group improved significantly to EUR 27,917 thousand compared to EUR 13,758 thousand in the prior year period. Total assets for the Group stood at EUR 556,798 thousand as at 31 March 2026, compared to EUR 554,958 thousand as at 31 December 2025, while total equity increased to EUR 179,866 thousand from EUR 172,592 thousand.

In corporate developments, the term of office of the board expired on 25 April 2026, and a new board was elected on 30 April 2026 for a four-year term, replacing the previous two-tier governance structure with a unified board structure. No other significant events occurred after the reporting date that would require adjustments or disclosures in the financial statements.
