Despite modest revenue contraction driven by reduced energy wholesale sales and higher operational costs, the group delivered substantial profitability expansion in the first half of 2026. Consolidated revenue remained broadly unchanged at 945.9 million euros compared to 951.9 million euros in the prior year period, while second-quarter revenue declined to 440.9 million euros. Nevertheless, earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization increased significantly by 31% to 111.2 million euros, and operating profit rose 94% to 51.1 million euros. Second-quarter EBITDA reached 64.2 million euros against 57.4 million euros a year earlier.

The marked improvement in profitability occurred despite headwinds including an 8.4 million euro year-on-year rise in fuel costs for the maritime segment and increased European Union emissions trading system compliance burdens. Management attributes the positive financial shift primarily to a stronger performance in the energy segment, a substantial turnaround in supporting businesses where operations moved from a 4.4 million euro loss to a 5.6 million euro profit following the closure of unprofitable units and the integration of new agricultural activities, and the recent completion of the Halinga biomethane plant. Revenue decreases were largely concentrated in the energy division, where gas and electricity sales volumes fell 34% due to reduced wholesale activity, although market pricing stabilized after spring peaks driven by regional geopolitical conflicts.

Strategic developments during the period include the successful continuation of a major construction contract for the Rail Baltica mainline, progressive work on several real estate and logistics projects, and the acquisition of additional Tallink Grupp shares to raise ownership to 68.76%. The company also initiated a share repurchase program and secured shareholder approval for a dividend distribution. Financial resilience was reinforced by a strengthened balance sheet, with total equity growing to 1.201 billion euros and net debt decreasing to 830 million euros. Cash balances expanded to 253 million euros, reflecting effective liquidity management and maintained investment capacity amid volatile macroeconomic and geopolitical conditions that continue to influence energy commodity markets and operational expenditures.
