During the first quarter of 2026, Nordic Fibreboard AS reported consolidated sales revenue of 1.86 million euros, representing a 7.3 percent increase compared to 1.73 million euros in the first quarter of 2025. Revenue growth was largely driven by an increase in order volumes from Finnish DIY stores, which rose by 241 thousand euros from 614 thousand euros to 855 thousand euros. Despite top-line growth, profitability declined significantly during the reporting period. Consolidated EBITDA was negative 395 thousand euros, yielding an EBITDA margin of negative 21 percent, worsening from negative 85 thousand euros or negative 5 percent in the first quarter of 2025. The fibreboard production segment recorded an EBITDA of negative 321 thousand euros compared to negative 62 thousand euros previously. Operating loss expanded to 530 thousand euros with an operating margin of negative 28 percent, compared to an operating loss of 217 thousand euros and a negative 13 percent operating margin in the comparative period. Financial expenses decreased slightly to 41 thousand euros from 51 thousand euros. Consequently, the group posted a consolidated net loss of 570 thousand euros for the first quarter of 2026, more than doubling from a net loss of 228 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2025. Basic and diluted earnings per share both stood at negative 0.07 euros. The weaker financial performance was primarily influenced by operational and external market factors. Results were negatively impacted by the scheduled factory shutdown in early January for the transition to a new boiler house, the two-month calibration and start-up period of the new gas boiler house, and cold weather conditions that elevated normal energy consumption. Furthermore, a sharp rise in energy prices in late February, caused by geopolitical tensions involving a US military attack on Iran, nearly doubled gas market prices, which subsequently remained about 50 percent higher than at the start of the year. In the real estate segment, design works for the Admirali Quarter development project at Suur-Jõe 48 in Pärnu were completed, and various building permits were issued. Additionally, on January 8, 2026, the company entered into a preliminary sale-purchase agreement to sell ten properties on Suur-Jõe and Admirali streets, with the final agreement expected by June 30, 2026. As of March 31, 2026, total assets reached 9.69 million euros, compared to 9.32 million euros at the end of 2025 and 8.72 million euros a year earlier. Total liabilities increased to 5.80 million euros, including total borrowings of 3.32 million euros, while total equity stood at 3.89 million euros. The group employed 57 people at the end of the period, down from 69 a year earlier, and total labour expenses decreased to 411 thousand euros from 471 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2025.
