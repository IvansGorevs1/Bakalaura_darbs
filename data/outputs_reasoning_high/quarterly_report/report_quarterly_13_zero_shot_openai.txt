Liven AS reported stronger sales momentum but weaker quarterly profitability in the second quarter of 2026, while first-half revenue increased year on year. The group signed 57 sales contracts during the quarter, compared with 31 in Q2 2025, bringing the six-month total to 111, nearly twice the 56 contracts signed in the first half of 2025. The four-week rolling weekly sales ratio reached nearly 3.1%, while the quarterly average was approximately 2.4%, up from about 1.5% in the first quarter. The presale portfolio grew to EUR 48.6 million at the start of the third quarter, including EUR 39.8 million for projects scheduled for completion in 2026.

Quarterly revenue fell to EUR 4.503 million from EUR 7.388 million a year earlier, primarily reflecting the handover of only 12 homes compared with 35 in Q2 2025. EBITDA decreased to EUR 884 thousand from EUR 2.390 million, and the EBITDA margin declined to 19.6% from 32.3%. Operating profit was a loss of EUR 177 thousand, compared with a profit of EUR 1.012 million. Profit before tax was negative EUR 234 thousand versus a profit of EUR 1.002 million, while net loss was EUR 618 thousand compared with net profit of EUR 974 thousand. The quarterly result included approximately EUR 90 thousand of one-off share-offering costs and EUR 384 thousand of income tax related to dividends.

For the first six months, revenue increased to EUR 11.439 million from EUR 9.319 million, despite fewer handovers, at 26 compared with 41. EBITDA declined to EUR 2.425 million from EUR 2.777 million, but operating profit rose to EUR 341 thousand from EUR 325 thousand. Profit before tax decreased to EUR 186 thousand from EUR 296 thousand, and net result moved to a loss of EUR 198 thousand from a profit of EUR 268 thousand, including the dividend-related tax expense.

Continued construction expanded total assets by EUR 8.505 million during the quarter to EUR 100.186 million. Equity rose to EUR 26.746 million from EUR 18.431 million at 30 June 2025, supported by the initial public offering, which raised EUR 4.932 million gross. Total loan liabilities were EUR 57.341 million, while cash increased to EUR 10.033 million.

Market conditions were supported by recovering household purchasing power, slower inflation and wage growth, although six-month Euribor rose to 2.57% and geopolitical uncertainty persisted. Liven expanded its portfolio through acquisitions in Berlin and, after the reporting date, Tallinn. Management expects nearly 90% of the EUR 59 million 2026 revenue target to be earned or covered by contracts, with most handovers and profit expected in the fourth quarter.
