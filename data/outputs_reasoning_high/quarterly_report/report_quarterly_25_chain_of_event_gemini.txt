During the first three months of 2026, VILVI Group reported consolidated sales revenue of 86,970 thousand euros, representing a 22.4 percent increase compared to the same period in 2025, when revenue stood at 71,072 thousand euros. The revenue growth was accompanied by significant shifts across operating segments, notably the inclusion of canned milk products, which generated 11,923 thousand euros and accounted for 14 percent of total revenue following the acquisition of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB. Dry milk products revenue reached 24,659 thousand euros, marking a 126.9 percent increase, while fresh milk products revenue rose by 73.4 percent to 12,748 thousand euros. Conversely, revenue from cheese, cheese products, and other categories declined by 28.8 percent to 37,641 thousand euros. Regarding geographical markets, sales in Lithuania increased by 72.9 percent to 14,633 thousand euros, sales to other countries grew by 46.6 percent to 29,818 thousand euros, and European Union sales remained relatively stable at 42,518 thousand euros, showing a 0.6 percent increase.

Despite the revenue expansion, the group experienced a decline in core operating profitability metrics. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization dropped by 50.2 percent to 3,003 thousand euros down from 6,031 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2025, causing the EBITDA margin to contract from 8.5 percent to 3.5 percent. The reported EBITDA excludes preliminary negative goodwill of 5,500 thousand euros related to the acquisition of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB. Despite lower operating earnings, net profit for the period increased by 25.7 percent to 5,085 thousand euros compared to 4,045 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2025. Basic and diluted earnings per share rose from 0.34 euros to 0.43 euros, representing a 26.5 percent increase, while the profit margin slightly improved from 5.7 percent to 5.8 percent.

The financial position and operational scale of the group were directly affected by major corporate events during the period. On January 16, 2026, Vilkyškių pieninė AB completed the acquisition of one hundred percent of the shares of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB. Additionally, commercial cheese production officially commenced at the new VILVI Group cheese factory operated by Baltic Dairy Board SIA in Bauska, Latvia. Driven by these business expansions, the group purchased 155,954 tons of basic indicators milk during the first three months of 2026, representing a 54.2 percent increase compared to the first quarter of 2025. Meanwhile, net financial debt increased significantly by 89.7 percent, rising from 43,971 thousand euros as of March 31, 2025, to 83,427 thousand euros as of March 31, 2026, reflecting the capital requirements associated with the group's ongoing strategic acquisitions and investments. Total assets reached 220,311 thousand euros at the end of the reporting period, compared to 188,977 thousand euros at the end of December 2025.
