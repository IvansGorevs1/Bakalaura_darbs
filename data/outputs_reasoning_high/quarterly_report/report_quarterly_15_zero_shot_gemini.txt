Nordecon group reported financial results for the twelve months of 2025 showing a decrease in revenue and profitability compared to the previous year. Total revenue for 2025 was 208,281 thousand euros, representing a decrease of approximately 7 percent from 223,925 thousand euros in 2024. The revenue decline was primarily driven by an approximate 10 percent drop in the buildings segment, whereas revenue in the infrastructure segment grew by 10 percent. The buildings segment accounted for 81 percent of total revenue. Gross profit for 2025 was 13,535 thousand euros, down from 16,770 thousand euros in 2024, resulting in a gross margin of 6.5 percent compared to 7.5 percent in the prior year. Operating profit decreased to 5,651 thousand euros from 8,061 thousand euros, and the operating margin declined to 2.7 percent from 3.6 percent in 2024. EBITDA was 8,250 thousand euros with a 4.0 percent margin, down from 11,025 thousand euros and 4.9 percent in 2024. Administrative expenses decreased by approximately 14 percent to 6,814 thousand euros, primarily due to lower staff costs and reduced provisions for performance-based pay. Net profit for the period was 2,545 thousand euros, compared to 5,165 thousand euros in 2024. Net profit attributable to owners of the parent company was 1,439 thousand euros, down from 3,827 thousand euros, resulting in basic and diluted earnings per share of 0.05 euros compared to 0.12 euros in 2024. Financial performance was negatively impacted by foreign exchange losses of 750 thousand euros resulting from the 12 percent weakening of the Ukrainian hryvnia against the euro, which affected loans provided to Ukrainian subsidiaries. Operating cash flow stood at 1,204 thousand euros, showing a slight increase from 1,075 thousand euros in 2024. Cash and cash equivalents decreased to 5,266 thousand euros at the end of 2025 from 8,195 thousand euros at the end of 2024. Total assets increased to 126,700 thousand euros, and total equity rose to 29,000 thousand euros. A significant positive development during the period was the substantial growth of the company order book. At 31 December 2025, the order book stood at 273,060 thousand euros, representing an increase of approximately 30 percent compared to 209,489 thousand euros at the end of 2024. This growth was driven by a nearly threefold increase in the infrastructure segment order book, bolstered by major contracts such as the Rail Baltica project and road construction assignments. New contracts secured during 2025 totaled 234,994 thousand euros, paving the way for anticipated revenue growth in 2026. The broader Estonian construction market stabilized during the year with a modest 1 percent economic growth and a 1.5 percent increase in the construction price index driven by material prices, while facing fierce competition and heavy reliance on public sector investments.
