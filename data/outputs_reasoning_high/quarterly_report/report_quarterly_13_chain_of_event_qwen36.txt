Liven AS reported consolidated revenue of EUR 11,439 thousand for the first half of 2026, representing growth from EUR 9,319 thousand in the same period last year. Despite the top-line improvement, the group recorded a net loss of EUR 198 thousand for the six-month period and EUR 618 thousand for the second quarter, contrasting sharply with net profits of EUR 268 thousand and EUR 974 thousand, respectively, in the prior year comparative periods. Management explicitly attributes this profitability decline to one-off expenses of approximately EUR 90 thousand related to the initial public offering of shares, which were recognized within distribution and administrative costs rather than as a reduction of share premium. Additionally, income tax expense surged to EUR 384 thousand in the second quarter due to taxes payable on dividends distributed during the period. Core operational performance remained stable, with operating profit of EUR 341 thousand for the half-year and earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation reaching EUR 2,425 thousand.

Sales momentum accelerated significantly throughout the reporting period. The company signed 111 contracts under the law of obligations in the first six months, nearly doubling the fifty-six contracts recorded in the corresponding period of 2025. The quarterly average weekly sales ratio climbed to approximately 2.4 percent, exceeding the historical normal range and peaking near 3.1 percent, driven by strong presales in the Regati phase II, expanded Iseära project, and Kadakadabra phase I. This elevated demand expanded the presale portfolio by EUR 14.9 million since January, bringing the cumulative reservation value to EUR 48.6 million by quarter-end. Combined with revenues already recognized in the first half, these commitments secure nearly ninety percent of the EUR 59 million annual revenue target, allowing management to shift strategic focus toward construction completion and home delivery.

Financial position strengthened substantially following capital markets activity. Total assets increased by EUR 8,505 thousand during the second quarter to EUR 100,186 thousand, primarily funded by new bank construction loans that raised total borrowings to EUR 57,341 thousand. Equity improved to EUR 26,746 thousand and the equity ratio rose to twenty-six point seven percent after the public offering generated net proceeds of EUR 4,687 thousand, with shares commencing trading on the Nasdaq Tallinn Main List on May fifteenth. Cash and cash equivalents likewise grew to EUR 10,033 thousand. Against a macroeconomic backdrop of recovering household purchasing power, moderating inflation, and sustained local demand reflected in an estimated eight percent market share in Greater Tallinn, Liven continued expanding its long-term development pipeline. The company initiated presales for multiple domestic phases and acquired two residential sites in Berlin for EUR 2.9 million to support future volume growth. Management anticipates full-year revenue recovery and profitability normalization in the third and fourth quarters as construction phases conclude and project deliverables translate into recognized earnings.
