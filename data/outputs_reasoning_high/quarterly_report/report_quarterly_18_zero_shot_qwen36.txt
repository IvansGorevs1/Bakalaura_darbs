AB Pieno Žvaigždės delivered a significant turnaround in profitability during the first quarter of 2026, recording net profit of EUR 3,068 thousand compared to EUR 227 thousand in the same period of 2025. Revenue remained largely stable at EUR 52,064 thousand, reflecting a minor decline of 0.5% from EUR 52,324 thousand in Q1 2025. The core driver of financial improvement was a substantial reduction in cost of sales, which fell to EUR 38,078 thousand from EUR 42,530 thousand. This decrease enabled gross profit to expand by 42.8% to EUR 13,986 thousand. Despite sales and administrative expenses rising by 18.3% to EUR 10,983 thousand, the expansion in gross profit resulted in operating profit before finance costs reaching EUR 3,366 thousand, up 529% from EUR 535 thousand. EBITDA also posted strong growth, nearly doubling to EUR 4,730 thousand from EUR 2,001 thousand, an increase of 136.5%. Earnings per share improved from EUR 0.01 to EUR 0.07. Finance expenses remained relatively stable, with net finance costs decreasing marginally to EUR 298 thousand from EUR 308 thousand, fully supporting the surge in pre-tax profit, which matched net profit as corporate income tax provisions were nil in both periods.

The company's financial position strengthened during the reporting period. Total assets increased to EUR 85,305 thousand, while total equity grew to EUR 41,164 thousand, with retained earnings reaching EUR 18,614 thousand after accounting for the current period profit and temporary IFRS 15 adjustments. Liquidity metrics improved, evidenced by a rise in the current ratio to 1.51 at the end of the quarter, compared to 1.27 at the end of Q1 2025. Cash flow performance showed a dramatic reversal; net cash flow from operating activities turned positive at EUR 1,625 thousand, recovering from a cash outflow of EUR 183 thousand in the prior year period. This improvement was aided by working capital developments, including a favorable cash release from reduced inventories, although this was partially offset by higher receivables and lower trade payables. Capital expenditures stood at EUR 900 thousand. On the liability side, the company managed its debt structure effectively, with interest-bearing current liabilities declining from EUR 2,759 thousand at the end of December 2025 to EUR 2,127 thousand, signaling active repayment of short-term obligations, while long-term borrowings increased slightly to EUR 16,548 thousand.

Operational developments indicated mixed trends across markets and product groups. Sales in the domestic Lithuanian market decreased slightly by 2.2% to EUR 31,405 thousand, while exports to other EU countries expanded by 5.0% to EUR 11,341 thousand. Sales of fresh milk products remained resilient at EUR 42,497 thousand, closely following the prior year level. In contrast, sales of dry milk products contracted to EUR 1,397 thousand from EUR 1,912 thousand. Book value per share increased to EUR 0.91 from EUR 0.89, reflecting the accumulation of equity. Overall, the reporting period was characterized by a marked reduction in production costs and improved cash generation capabilities that successfully translated into superior profitability despite stable revenue and modest shifts in geographic sales distribution. There were no material post-balance sheet events affecting the company's position.
