Hepsor’s financial performance weakened in 2025 compared with the previous year, although the Group expanded its development portfolio and increased sales activity. Revenue declined by 7.8% to 35.4 million euros from 38.4 million euros, primarily reflecting fewer apartment handovers: 141 homes were delivered, compared with 194 in 2024. Real estate sales revenue decreased to 33.2 million euros from 36.2 million euros. Revenue from Estonia fell to 26.1 million euros, while revenue from Latvia increased to 9.3 million euros from 5.9 million euros, raising Latvia’s share of total revenue to 26.3%.

Gross profit decreased to 5.6 million euros from 6.8 million euros, and the gross margin fell to 15.9% from 17.6%. Operating profit declined to 3.4 million euros from 4.3 million euros, with the operating margin decreasing to 9.5% from 11.3%. EBITDA fell to 3.5 million euros from 4.5 million euros. Results were affected by lower margins on sold projects and higher marketing expenses, which increased to 1.3 million euros from 0.9 million euros as the number and scale of projects on sale expanded. Finance costs and personnel expenses remained broadly stable, while interest income increased by 0.3 million euros and other operating income included 0.8 million euros from properties reclassified and revalued as investment property.

Net profit declined by 51.3% to 1.0 million euros from 2.1 million euros. Profit attributable to the owners of the parent company was 0.4 million euros, compared with 0.4 million euros in 2024, while earnings per share decreased slightly to 0.10 euros from 0.11 euros. Fourth-quarter revenue fell to 4.5 million euros from 10.5 million euros, and the Group recorded a net loss of 0.5 million euros compared with a net profit of 1.0 million euros in the corresponding quarter.

Despite lower reported earnings, primary sales agreements increased substantially to 185 homes worth 36.1 million euros, compared with 109 homes worth 19.8 million euros in 2024. The number of homes under construction rose to 428 from 192, driven particularly by new projects in Latvia. The Group added six residential projects during the year and ended 2025 with 33 active projects and approximately 196,650 square metres of sellable area. It plans to begin five additional development projects in 2026. In Canada, building rights were secured for the Weston Road project, increasing its planned development volume from 27,000 to 62,000 square metres.

Total assets increased to 91.8 million euros, while inventories declined to 58.9 million euros. Interest-bearing loan liabilities decreased to 47.7 million euros from 54.7 million euros, reducing the debt ratio to 52.0% from 61.6%. Equity increased to 27.1 million euros, partly due to a 5.8 million euro non-cash contribution from a minority shareholder. Cash declined to 3.8 million euros from 6.2 million euros. In November, Hepsor raised 8.0 million euros through a bond issue carrying a fixed annual interest rate of 9.50%, strengthening funding for its development activities.
