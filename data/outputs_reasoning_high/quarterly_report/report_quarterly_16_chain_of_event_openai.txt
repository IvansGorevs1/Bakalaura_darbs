Nordic Fibreboard AS reported unaudited consolidated revenue of €1.861 million for Q1 2026, up 7.3% from €1.735 million in Q1 2025. The increase was mainly driven by Finnish DIY stores, where order volume rose by €241 thousand to €855 thousand. European Union sales increased to €1.781 million from €1.639 million, while sales to Africa declined to €62 thousand from €87 thousand.

Despite the revenue growth, profitability deteriorated substantially. EBITDA was negative €395 thousand compared with negative €85 thousand a year earlier, reducing the EBITDA margin to negative 21% from negative 5%. The Group reported an operating loss of €530 thousand, versus €217 thousand in Q1 2025, and a net loss of €570 thousand compared with €228 thousand. Basic and diluted earnings per share were both negative €0.07 for the quarter, compared with negative €0.05 in Q1 2025.

The main pressure came from production costs. Cost of goods sold increased to €1.966 million from €1.608 million, turning gross profit of €127 thousand into a gross loss of €105 thousand. Electricity, heat and water costs rose to €1.048 million from €725 thousand. The Group states that results were negatively affected by the scheduled factory shutdown in early January during the transition to a new boiler house. Commissioning, calibration and start-up of the new system took approximately two months, while cold weather increased normal energy consumption. In late February, the US military attack on Iran caused gas prices to nearly double; although prices subsequently declined, they remained approximately 50% above the beginning-of-year level.

Distribution costs increased to €261 thousand from €228 thousand and administrative expenses to €154 thousand from €111 thousand, partly offset by lower total labour costs of €411 thousand versus €471 thousand. Financial expenses declined to €41 thousand from €51 thousand, but Q1 2025 included €40 thousand of financial income from the sale of Trigon Property Development shares, which did not recur.

At 31 March 2026, total assets were €9.694 million, up from €8.722 million a year earlier, while total liabilities increased to €5.802 million from €5.334 million. Equity fell from €4.462 million at 31 December 2025 to €3.892 million after the quarterly loss, and the debt-to-equity ratio rose to 60% from 52%. Operating cash flow improved to €13 thousand from negative €528 thousand, but €169 thousand was invested in production assets and cash declined by €4 thousand to only €1 thousand. Financing included a €343 thousand related-party loan. Management expects fibreboard demand to remain stable and continues to invest in Nordic markets, product development and energy-efficient production.
