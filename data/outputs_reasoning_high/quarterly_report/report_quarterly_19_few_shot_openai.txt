AS Pro Kapital Grupp reported a significantly stronger financial performance in 2025, driven primarily by increased real estate handovers. Revenue rose to EUR 53.2 million from EUR 18.2 million in 2024, while fourth-quarter revenue increased to EUR 18.9 million from EUR 7.6 million. Real estate sales generated EUR 45.4 million, compared with EUR 10.7 million a year earlier, mainly reflecting apartment handovers in Tallinn’s Kalaranna District and Uus-Kindrali developments and in the City Villas project in Vilnius.

Profitability improved substantially. Gross profit increased to EUR 18.8 million from EUR 5.4 million, with the gross margin rising to 35% from 30%. Operating profit reached EUR 14.7 million, compared with EUR 0.1 million in 2024, and the operating margin improved to 28% from 1%. Net profit was EUR 12.0 million, reversing the EUR 3.9 million net loss recorded in the previous year. Earnings per share increased to EUR 0.22 from a loss of EUR 0.06. Fourth-quarter net profit was EUR 6.0 million, compared with EUR 0.2 million in the corresponding period of 2024.

The results also benefited from a EUR 3.5 million fair-value gain on investment property, compared with EUR 1.1 million in 2024. Finance costs declined to EUR 2.6 million from EUR 4.3 million, partly because 2024 included a EUR 1.4 million loss related to the modification of financial liabilities. These gains were partly offset by an EUR 863 thousand impairment of goodwill related to the Italian Preatoni Nuda Proprietà business, whose projections reflected delayed profitability and dependence on additional financing.

Cash flow improved materially, with EUR 10.8 million generated from operating activities compared with EUR 10.4 million used in 2024. Year-end cash increased to EUR 5.1 million from EUR 4.3 million. The Group also redeemed EUR 9.4 million of secured bonds, reducing their outstanding nominal amount to EUR 10.5 million. Total liabilities declined to EUR 61.2 million from EUR 67.5 million, while equity increased to EUR 63.3 million from EUR 51.2 million. The debt-to-equity ratio improved to 0.97 from 1.32, and net asset value per share rose to EUR 1.12 from EUR 0.89.

Operationally, Kalaranna was completed, Uus-Kindrali handovers began, and construction of the Blue Marine project in Riga started, supported by a new construction loan. In Vilnius, City Villas reached its final stage and initial units were handed over. Hotel performance remained slightly below budget as occupancy declined to 65% from 67%, while Italian operations faced subdued real estate market activity. Overall, 2025 marked a strong recovery, supported by project completions, higher property sales, improved cash generation and reduced leverage, although market demand, financing conditions and the performance of the Italian business remained important risks.
