VILVI Group achieved robust top-line growth in the first quarter of 2026, with consolidated revenue rising by 22.4% year-on-year to MEUR 87.0. This expansion was driven by significant increases in domestic and international markets; sales in Lithuania grew by 72.9% and revenues in other countries rose by 46.6%, while the European Union market remained relatively stable with a marginal increase of 0.6%. The surge in performance was supported by a substantial 54.2% increase in milk purchases, totalling 156 thousand tons. Revenue mix shifted toward high-growth categories, with fresh milk products posting a 73.4% increase and dry milk products more than doubling to MEUR 24.7. A new canned milk segment contributed MEUR 11.9 following the acquisition completed in January. Conversely, revenue from cheese and related products declined by 28.8%, highlighting a divergence in segment performance.

Despite strong revenue growth, core operational profitability deteriorated. The group recorded an EBITDA of MEUR 3.0, a 50.2% decrease compared to the prior year, with the EBITDA margin compressing to 3.5% from 8.5%. Cost of sales escalated rapidly, reducing gross profit to MEUR 5.4 from MEUR 7.5 in the previous period. Nevertheless, net profit improved by 25.7% to MEUR 5.1. This result was significantly influenced by a preliminary gain from bargain purchase of approximately MEUR 5.5 linked to the acquisition of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB, which offset the decline in operating margins. Excluding this item, underlying profitability was notably weaker. Finance costs also rose sharply to MEUR 0.9, adding to margin pressure.

The period featured key strategic developments, including the full consolidation of Marijampolės pieno konservai UAB, reinforcing the group's status as the sole Baltic producer of canned milk products. Plans are underway to integrate operations and expand the largest regional whey processing line. Commercial production launched at the new cheese factory operated by Baltic Dairy Board SIA in Latvia, boasting an annual capacity of 18 thousand tons and targeting exports to over 40 countries. The facility created over 100 jobs and received finalist status in the Latvian Construction Awards 2025. Additionally, the group discontinued transparent plastic lids for yogurt packaging to support sustainability goals. Financing activities expanded net financial debt by 89.7% to MEUR 83.4, supporting investment in property, plant and equipment and acquisition activities. Total assets reached MEUR 220.3.
