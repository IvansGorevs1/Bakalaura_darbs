The Infortar Group delivered solid operational results for the full year and fourth quarter of 2025, demonstrating improved profitability and a stronger balance sheet despite ongoing macroeconomic uncertainty. Consolidated revenue for the twelve months reached EUR 1,837.0 million, while EBITDA rose to EUR 232.6 million, supported by a gross profit expansion to EUR 240.8 million. The EBITDA margin improved to 13.4 percent from 10.6 percent in 2024. Although net profit decreased to EUR 72.0 million from EUR 193.7 million in the prior year, management clarified that this decline was primarily attributable to one off consolidation effects related to Tallink Grupp in 2024 rather than underlying operational weakness. Quarterly performance showed a pronounced recovery, with the group generating an operating profit of EUR 19.8 million and a net profit of EUR 14.1 million in Q4 2025, reversing a combined operating loss of EUR 6.8 million and net loss of EUR 12.0 million recorded in the same period last year.

The maritime transport segment recorded annual revenue of EUR 765.3 million and EBITDA of EUR 130.1 million. While total passengers carried declined by 0.9 percent and cargo units fell by 19.2 percent, Q4 revenues increased by 2.4 percent to EUR 188.0 million, restoring profitability after a seasonal dip. This recovery followed elevated financial expenses, dividend distributions totaling EUR 76.5 million, and active loan repayments that reduced financing costs. The energy division benefited from strategic supply diversification and disciplined inventory management. Despite a 2 percent drop in regional natural gas consumption, sales volumes grew by 7 percent to 4.8 terawatt hours in Q4, maintaining a 21 percent market share. European gas prices declined sharply to EUR 26.53 per megawatt hour by December, improving trading conditions, while the group advanced renewable initiatives by commissioning its first battery storage systems and signing a multi year contract for 12 liquefied natural gas cargoes.

Financially, the group reinforced its balance sheet through sustained deleveraging. Interest bearing liabilities contracted to EUR 1,060.5 million from EUR 1,223.3 million, lowering the net debt to EBITDA ratio from 4.0 to 3.6 times and lifting the equity ratio to 45.5 percent. Total assets remained robust at EUR 2,587.8 million against total equity of EUR 1,177.5 million. Working capital dynamics and reduced inventories drove operating cash flows to EUR 388.6 million, while cash reserves climbed to EUR 219.8 million. The workforce expanded to 6,466 employees, and the portfolio continued evolving through targeted acquisitions in biogas and agriculture to enhance cyclical resilience. Management emphasized a conservative capital structure and predictable cash generation, aiming to sustain dividends and navigate geopolitical and market volatility through diversified, infrastructure anchored operations.
