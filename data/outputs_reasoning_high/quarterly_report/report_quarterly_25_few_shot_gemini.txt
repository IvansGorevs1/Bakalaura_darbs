During the first three months of 2026, VILVI Group achieved substantial revenue growth and higher net profit, driven largely by major corporate acquisitions and expanded production capabilities, despite a contraction in core operating profitability. Consolidated sales revenue reached 86.97 million euros, representing a 22.4 percent increase compared to 71.07 million euros in the same period of 2025. This top-line expansion was supported by strong growth in dry milk products, which surged by 126.9 percent to 24.66 million euros, and fresh milk products, which rose by 73.4 percent to 12.75 million euros. Additionally, the newly consolidated canned milk segment contributed 11.92 million euros following the acquisition of Marijampolės pieno konservai, offsetting a 28.8 percent decline in the traditional cheese, cheese products, and other segment down to 37.64 million euros. Geographically, sales rose significantly in Lithuania by 72.9 percent to 14.63 million euros and in other foreign countries by 46.6 percent to 29.82 million euros, while European Union sales remained stable at 42.52 million euros.

Despite the strong revenue performance, the group experienced a notable decline in core operating profitability. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization dropped by 50.2 percent to 3.00 million euros compared to 6.03 million euros in the previous year, causing the EBITDA margin to contract from 8.5 percent to 3.5 percent. Conversely, the group's net profit for the period increased by 25.7 percent to 5.09 million euros, up from 4.05 million euros in the first quarter of 2025. This net profit growth was heavily bolstered by other operating income of 5.66 million euros, which included a preliminary negative goodwill gain of 5.54 million euros resulting from the bargain purchase of Marijampolės pieno konservai. Basic earnings per share correspondingly rose by 26.5 percent to 0.43 euros.

The reporting period was defined by major strategic expansions. On January 16, 2026, the group finalized the 100 percent share acquisition of Marijampolės pieno konservai, integrating its canned milk, butter, and milk powder operations. Furthermore, VILVI Group officially commenced commercial cheese production at its new Baltic Dairy Board factory in Bauska, Latvia. Reflecting these expansion activities, the group's purchased basic milk volume increased by 54.2 percent to 155,954 tons. Total assets expanded to 220.31 million euros by the end of March 2026, up from 188.98 million euros at the end of December 2025, while net financial debt rose by 89.7 percent to 83.43 million euros, driven by increased borrowings and financing requirements associated with the corporate developments.
