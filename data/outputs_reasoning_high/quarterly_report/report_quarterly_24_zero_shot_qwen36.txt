AS Trigon Property Development reported a net loss of 99,507 euros for the twelve months ended 31 December 2025, reversing a 167,409 euro profit recorded in the prior year. The deterioration was driven by zero property sales in 2025 compared to a 331,920 euro gain in 2024, coupled with administrative and general expenses rising to 108,021 euros from 63,650 euros. The expense increase originated primarily from newly implemented remuneration for management and supervisory board members totaling 41,452 euros, supplemented by standard professional and compliance fees. This combination produced an operating loss of 108,021 euros versus a 161,649 euro operating profit previously. Earnings per share shifted to minus 0.02212 from plus 0.03721.

Balance sheet metrics reflected the underlying strategic shift. Total assets contracted to 1,804,656 euros from 1,873,680 euros, cushioned by a 91,262 euro addition to inventories following direct capital expenditures in the company’s thirteen-point-two-hectare Pärnu commercial and logistics development. Equity decreased to 1,755,267 euros. Performance ratios adapted accordingly, with return on assets falling to negative 5.51 percent from 8.93 percent and return on equity declining to negative 5.67 percent from 9.03 percent. The debt ratio remained minimal at 2.74 percent, up slightly from 1.01 percent. Operating cash flows reversed sharply, showing a negative 142,945 euro outflow against a positive 194,723 euro inflow in 2024, leaving a closing cash balance of 227,911 euros.

Operational activities centered on advancing the Pärnu project under a revised April 2024 detailed plan that increased commercial plotting flexibility, though management elected to delay land sales during the assessment period. Capital structure and shareholder composition remained concentrated, with OÜ Pärnu Holdings retaining a 39.22 percent controlling stake and Väätsa Agro AS entering as a 17.88 percent holder. Equity trading consisted of 146,813 shares changing hands for 96,814 euros, with the year-end closing price settling at 0.634 euros. Overall, the reporting period characterized a deliberate pivot from portfolio liquidation to active land acquisition and development preparation. While this strategic pause eliminated top-line gains and temporarily suppressed profitability through elevated governance and holding costs, it preserved a solvent, low-leverage balance sheet aligned with long-term site positioning.
