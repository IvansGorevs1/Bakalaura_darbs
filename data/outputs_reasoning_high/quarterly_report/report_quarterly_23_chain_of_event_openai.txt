For the nine months ended 30 September 2025, AB KN Energies’ consolidated revenue increased to EUR 76.664 million from EUR 67.977 million in the corresponding 2024 period. Gross profit rose to EUR 31.549 million from EUR 29.237 million, while operating profit increased to EUR 21.282 million from EUR 18.942 million. Profit before tax reached EUR 16.186 million, compared with EUR 14.910 million previously, and net profit rose to EUR 13.158 million from EUR 11.862 million. Basic and diluted earnings per share increased from EUR 0.031 to EUR 0.035.

The main revenue improvement came from LNG terminal services, which generated EUR 43.640 million compared with EUR 31.401 million in 2024. The report attributes this increase to the terminal’s one-and-a-half-month dry-dock outage in 2024, higher LNG regasification volumes and bonuses from clients for booked capacity. Liquid energy products transshipment revenue also increased to EUR 21.375 million from EUR 20.241 million, while consulting services revenue rose to EUR 7.507 million from EUR 3.961 million. These gains were partly offset by lower revenue from other LNG terminal activities, which declined to EUR 6.383 million from EUR 11.530 million because clients made fewer payments for unused capacity. Revenue was also reduced by EUR 2.753 million from the proportional repayment of excess returns from regulated activities in 2022–2023.

Cost of sales increased to EUR 45.115 million from EUR 38.740 million. Depreciation and amortisation rose to EUR 18.335 million from EUR 6.865 million, reflecting the acquisition of the floating storage and regasification unit in December 2024 and the resulting reclassification of its depreciation from right-of-use assets. Operating expenses were also higher, at EUR 10.640 million compared with EUR 10.419 million. Despite these cost increases, higher revenue supported the improvement in operating profit.

Finance income fell to EUR 1.520 million from EUR 2.644 million, while finance costs were broadly stable at EUR 6.616 million versus EUR 6.692 million. The weaker net finance result limited the increase in profit before tax. In the third quarter alone, net profit declined to EUR 4.570 million from EUR 10.403 million, and operating profit fell to EUR 7.102 million from EUR 8.888 million despite slightly higher revenue.

Operating cash flow more than doubled to EUR 76.331 million from EUR 35.637 million, including EUR 31.900 million transferred from financial assets to cash. Cash and cash equivalents consequently rose to EUR 54.398 million from EUR 17.203 million. Loans declined to EUR 311.443 million from EUR 320.352 million. The company’s standalone net profit also improved, reaching EUR 11.613 million compared with EUR 10.702 million in 2024.
