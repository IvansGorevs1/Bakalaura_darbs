AS Trigon Property Development reported a significant deterioration in financial performance in 2025. Full-year revenue and gross profit from land sales were both zero, compared with gain on sales of €331,920 and gross profit of €220,299 in 2024. The prior-year sales comprised a 0.09-hectare transport land plot sold for €31,920 and a 0.70-hectare property sold for €300,000. With no comparable sales in 2025, the company recorded an operating loss of €108,021, reversing an operating profit of €161,649 in 2024.

The company’s net result changed from a profit of €167,409 in 2024 to a loss of €99,507 in 2025. Fourth-quarter performance also weakened, with a net loss of €23,333 compared with a profit of €154,215 in the fourth quarter of 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share declined from €0.03721 to negative €0.02212. Reported return on assets fell from 8.93% to -5.51%, while return on equity declined from 9.03% to -5.67%.

Administrative and general expenses increased to €108,021 from €63,650 in 2024. The report identifies €41,452 of board remuneration in 2025, compared with none in 2024, as well as higher accounting, stock exchange, land-tax and property-related costs. The company also purchased €12,000 of services from entities controlled by supervisory board members, compared with €3,240 in 2024. Financial income increased to €8,513 from €5,760, but this was insufficient to offset the operating loss.

Operating cash flow turned negative, with €142,945 used in 2025 compared with €194,723 generated in 2024. Cash declined from €370,856 to €227,911. Inventories increased from €1.47 million to €1.56 million because €91,262 was invested in the development project; no inventory write-downs were recorded. Total assets decreased to €1.80 million from €1.87 million, while equity fell to €1.76 million from €1.85 million following the annual loss. Liabilities increased to €49,389 from €18,906, and the debt ratio rose from 1.01% to 2.74%.

The company continues to own one 13.2-hectare Pärnu development project intended for a commercial, industrial and logistics park. Management plans to continue selling the existing land plots and does not plan to start a new business activity next year. The share price increased modestly from €0.620 to €0.634, raising market capitalisation from €2.79 million to €2.85 million, despite the weaker earnings and lower book value per share.
