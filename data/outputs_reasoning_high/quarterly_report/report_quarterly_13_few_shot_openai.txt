Liven AS reported mixed results in the first half of 2026. Revenue increased to EUR 11.439 million from EUR 9.319 million a year earlier, but profitability weakened. EBITDA declined to EUR 2.425 million from EUR 2.777 million, reducing the EBITDA margin to 21.2% from 29.8%. Operating profit was broadly stable at EUR 341 thousand, compared with EUR 325 thousand in the first half of 2025, while profit before tax decreased to EUR 186 thousand from EUR 296 thousand. The group recorded a net loss of EUR 198 thousand, compared with a net profit of EUR 268 thousand a year earlier, and basic loss per share was EUR 0.016.

Second-quarter performance was significantly weaker because of the timing of project handovers. Revenue fell to EUR 4.503 million from EUR 7.388 million, EBITDA declined to EUR 884 thousand from EUR 2.390 million, and operating profit shifted to a loss of EUR 177 thousand from a profit of EUR 1.012 million. The net loss was EUR 618 thousand, compared with net profit of EUR 974 thousand in the second quarter of 2025. There were no new completions during the quarter, and only 12 previously completed homes were handed over, compared with 35 a year earlier. Results were also affected by approximately EUR 90 thousand in one-off costs related to the share offering and EUR 384 thousand of income tax associated with dividends.

Despite the weaker reported earnings, demand improved materially. Liven signed 57 sales contracts in the second quarter, compared with 31 a year earlier, bringing the six-month total to 111 versus 56. The four-week average weekly sales ratio reached nearly 3.1% at quarter-end, while the quarterly average was approximately 2.4%, above the first-quarter average of approximately 1.5%. The group entered the third quarter with 171 contracts and a presale portfolio worth EUR 48.6 million, including EUR 39.8 million for projects scheduled for completion in 2026. Together with first-half revenue, nearly 90% of the EUR 59 million 2026 revenue target was realised or covered by contracts.

The balance sheet expanded as construction activity continued. Assets reached EUR 100.186 million and equity EUR 26.746 million at 30 June, compared with EUR 86.457 million and EUR 23.590 million at year-end 2025. The equity ratio improved to 26.7% from 19.4% a year earlier, while cash increased to EUR 10.033 million. Total borrowings rose to EUR 57.341 million from EUR 46.730 million at year-end, mainly because construction loans increased to EUR 16.561 million.

The initial public offering completed in May raised EUR 4.932 million, and Liven’s shares began trading on Nasdaq Tallinn’s Main List. The development portfolio expanded to 136,506 square metres with forecast revenue of EUR 497.2 million. New properties were acquired in Berlin, while a Tallinn acquisition announced after the reporting date added a planned development of more than 160 homes. Management expects most 2026 handovers, revenue and profit to occur in the final quarter and plans to begin construction on five project phases in the second half of the year.
