Amber Grid AB reported a significant improvement in its financial performance for the three-month period ended 31 March 2026, driven by higher transmission volumes and increased tariffs. Total revenue for the first quarter of 2026 reached 32,821 thousand euros, representing a 58 percent increase compared with 20,763 thousand euros reported in the corresponding period of 2025. This revenue growth was primarily supported by a 35 percent increase in the volume of natural gas transmitted within Lithuania and higher transmission prices established for 2026. Conversely, revenue from system balancing products experienced a sharp decline, dropping 99 percent due to a new transit agreement signed for Kaliningrad that significantly reduced the operational need for balancing gas.

Operating expenses excluding depreciation, write-offs, and impairment decreased by 19 percent to 9,570 thousand euros, down from 11,867 thousand euros in the previous year. This reduction was largely propelled by a 68 percent decrease in natural gas purchase costs, which fell to 1,477 thousand euros primarily because of lower expenses incurred for system balancing products. Meanwhile, wages, salaries, and related expenses increased to 4,367 thousand euros compared with 3,659 thousand euros in the prior-year period.

Reflecting the higher revenue and lower operating costs, the company's profitability surged during the reporting period. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization rose sharply to 23,304 thousand euros, compared with 8,918 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2025. Operating profit reached 19,627 thousand euros, up from 5,185 thousand euros. Net profit for the three months ended 31 March 2026 totaled 15,763 thousand euros, marking a substantial increase from 4,239 thousand euros reported a year earlier. Consequently, basic and diluted earnings per share increased to 0.09 euros from 0.02 euros.

As of 31 March 2026, total assets stood at 324,064 thousand euros, remaining stable compared with 324,739 thousand euros at the end of 2025. Total equity strengthened to 182,039 thousand euros from 166,276 thousand euros, primarily reflecting the strong net profit generated during the quarter. Borrowings underwent notable shifts following loan agreement expirations and refinancing activities, including the assumption of a loan by parent company EPSO-G and the reclassification of other borrowings from current to non-current liabilities. Total non-current borrowings reached 97,707 thousand euros at the end of March 2026, while current borrowings decreased to 6,467 thousand euros. Net debt stood at 106,610 thousand euros.

The company continued to manage legal disputes regarding warranty obligations with a construction contractor concerning the gas pipeline interconnection between Poland and Lithuania, maintaining a provision of 5,815 thousand euros for potential fund repayments. Additionally, after the reporting period, shareholders approved the distribution of 9,989 thousand euros in dividends for the 2025 financial year, equating to 0.0560 euros per share.
