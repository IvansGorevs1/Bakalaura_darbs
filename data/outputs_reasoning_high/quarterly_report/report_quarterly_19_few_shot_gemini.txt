AS Pro Kapital Grupp reported a strong and profitable financial performance for the twelve months of 2025, driven by successful real estate handovers and increased business activity. Total revenue for the year reached 53.2 million euros, representing a substantial increase compared to 18.2 million euros in the previous year. Fourth-quarter revenue also rose significantly to 18.9 million euros from 7.6 million euros in the final quarter of 2024. The revenue growth was primarily powered by real estate sales amounting to 45.4 million euros, up from 10.7 million euros in 2024, as completed apartments were successfully handed over to buyers.

Profitability metrics improved markedly across the period. Gross profit for 2025 climbed to 18.8 million euros with a gross margin of 35 percent, compared with 5.4 million euros and a 30 percent margin in 2024. Operating profit surged to 14.7 million euros, yielding an operating margin of 28 percent, a sharp rise from 0.1 million euros the prior year. The company recorded a net profit of 12.0 million euros for the twelve months of 2025, representing a strong turnaround from the net loss of 3.9 million euros reported in 2024. Basic earnings per share rose accordingly to 0.22 euros for the year, compared with a loss of 0.06 euros in the previous period. Fourth-quarter net profit alone reached 6.0 million euros, compared with 0.2 million euros in the final quarter of 2024.

The financial position of the group strengthened during the reporting period. Total assets increased to 124.5 million euros as of 31 December 2025, up from 118.8 million euros a year earlier, while total liabilities decreased to 61.2 million euros from 67.5 million euros. Total equity grew to 63.3 million euros, lifting the net asset value per share to 1.12 euros from 0.89 euros. The debt-to-equity ratio improved to 0.97 from 1.32 at the end of 2024. Operating cash flow turned positive, generating 10.8 million euros during 2025 compared to a cash outflow of 10.4 million euros in the prior year. Capital structure management included the partial redemption of secured non-convertible bonds by 9.4 million euros, reducing the outstanding nominal bond amount to 10.5 million euros by year-end.

Operationally, the results reflected active project execution and completion cycles across the Baltic capitals. In Tallinn, construction was completed on the white building at the Uus-Kindrali project and the Kalaranna District, directly driving revenue through apartment handovers. In Riga, construction commenced on the Blue Marine residential project within the Klīversala Quarter following the securing of bank financing. In Vilnius, construction neared completion on the final stage of Šaltinių Namai Attico with City Villas, with customer handovers beginning in December 2025. Despite these positive developments, the group recognized a full goodwill impairment loss of 863 thousand euros related to its Italian subsidiary, Preatoni Nuda Proprietà.
