During the nine months of 2025, AB KN Energies reported a positive development in financial performance compared to the same period in 2024. For the reporting period ending September 30, 2025, the Group recorded revenue from contracts with customers amounting to 76,664 thousand euros, up from 67,977 thousand euros in the first nine months of 2024. The Parent Company recorded revenue of 72,848 thousand euros compared to 65,009 thousand euros previously. This revenue growth was driven by higher liquefied natural gas terminal regasification volumes, client bonuses for booked capacity, and the continuous operation of the terminal, whereas in 2024 it was out of operation for one and a half months for dry-dock repairs. These positive factors were partially offset by a reduction in LNG security supplement income due to the proportionally recognized repayment of excess returns from regulated activities during 2022 through 2023.

The Group cost of sales increased to 45,115 thousand euros from 38,740 thousand euros, while gross profit rose to 31,549 thousand euros compared to 29,237 thousand euros in the prior year period. Distribution costs and administrative expenses slightly increased to 10,640 thousand euros from 10,419 thousand euros. Operating profit for the Group improved to 21,282 thousand euros from 18,942 thousand euros. Finance costs were stable at 6,616 thousand euros compared to 6,692 thousand euros, whereas finance income decreased to 1,520 thousand euros from 2,644 thousand euros.

The Group profit before tax reached 16,186 thousand euros, up from 14,910 thousand euros. After income tax expenses of 3,028 thousand euros, the Group net profit for the nine months of 2025 was 13,158 thousand euros, compared to 11,862 thousand euros in the first nine months of 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share attributable to shareholders were 0.035 euros, compared to 0.031 euros in the previous period. The Parent Company reported a net profit of 11,613 thousand euros, up from 10,702 thousand euros.

As of September 30, 2025, the Group total assets stood at 530,364 thousand euros, compared to 535,174 thousand euros at the end of 2024. Total equity increased to 167,721 thousand euros from 163,128 thousand euros, following the net profit generation and 8,622 thousand euros in dividends declared. Total borrowings and related liabilities decreased to 311,443 thousand euros from 320,352 thousand euros. Cash and cash equivalents rose to 54,398 thousand euros from 17,203 thousand euros. Overall, the company demonstrated solid financial performance and operational stability throughout the reporting period.
