The company reported consolidated revenue of EUR 765.3 million for the full year 2025, declining from EUR 785.8 million in 2024. Full-year net profit fell to EUR 17.3 million, or EUR 0.023 per share, compared with EUR 40.3 million previously, while EBITDA contracted to EUR 130.1 million from EUR 175.2 million. Fourth-quarter momentum improved significantly, with revenue increasing 2.4 percent to EUR 188.0 million and net profit returning to EUR 12.2 million after a net loss in the prior year period. Improved annual profitability stemmed from reduced depreciation expenses following the alignment of estimated vessel useful lives to forty-five years, alongside lower finance costs driven by shrinking debt balances and declining interest rates.

Passenger volumes declined 0.9 percent to 5.53 million, while cargo units dropped 19.2 percent to 245 thousand. Core route revenues were constrained by weak Finnish consumer confidence, increased Estonian and Finnish taxes, and geopolitical tensions that suppressed regional trade and tourism. The Estonia-Finland corridor demonstrated relative resilience, generating EUR 314.5 million in revenue, whereas the Finland-Sweden and Estonia-Sweden segments faced sharper contractions due to volume losses and scheduled maintenance downtime. Chartering activities and the sale of surplus emissions allowances sustained the Other segment at EUR 145.5 million in revenue.

To navigate the challenging operating environment, the Group disposed of three vessels to streamline its fleet and optimize costs, while securing extended charter agreements for three additional ferries to stabilize rental income. Capacity discipline on primary routes helped preserve margins despite lower traffic. Net debt remained stable at EUR 432.4 million, reducing the leverage ratio to 3.3 times trailing EBITDA. Total borrowings decreased substantially throughout the year, partly offset by major cash outflows for capital distributions. Shareholders received EUR 44.6 million in 2025 dividends, and the Board intends to propose an identical EUR 0.06 per share payout for 2026. Capital expenditure totaled EUR 33.0 million, directed toward dry docking, technical modernization, and IT infrastructure upgrades.

Overall, strategic asset reallocation, controlled expenditures, and consistent shareholder returns effectively mitigated macroeconomic headwinds. The company delivered a robust balance sheet, managed debt levels, and achieved a definitive earnings recovery in the closing quarter, positioning it for measured growth as regional demand indicators show early signs of stabilization.
