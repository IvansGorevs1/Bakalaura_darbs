AS Silvano Fashion Group reported consolidated revenue of 28,590 thousand euros for the six months ended 30 June 2026, representing a 0.6 percent increase from 28,428 thousand euros in the prior year period. Gross profit rose 5.3 percent to 15,892 thousand euros, with the gross margin expanding to 55.6 percent from 53.1 percent, a development explicitly attributed to a 4.8 percent decrease in the cost of goods sold. Despite margin improvements, consolidated operating profit declined by 3.5 percent to 5,751 thousand euros, and EBITDA decreased marginally by 0.8 percent to 7,508 thousand euros. The sharpest contraction occurred in bottom-line profitability, as reported net profit fell 31.2 percent to 4,321 thousand euros, while net profit attributable to equity holders of the Parent company dropped 31.5 percent to 3,961 thousand euros. Earnings per share correspondingly decreased to 0.11 euros from 0.16 euros. This reduction in net profit aligns with lower net financial income, which contracted to 444 thousand euros from 1,652 thousand euros, alongside increased income tax expenses reaching 1,870 thousand euros. Conversely, operating cash flow improved substantially by 96.6 percent to 6,842 thousand euros, reflecting favorable working capital movements including a 1,535 thousand euro reduction in inventory. 

Sales structure shifted toward direct channels, as wholesale revenue contracted 9.1 percent to 14,934 thousand euros, while retail revenue expanded 13.6 percent to 13,548 thousand euros, now comprising 47.4 percent of total revenue. Geographic performance highlighted Belarus as the primary growth driver with sales increasing 6.4 percent to 9,046 thousand euros, supported by a 2.7 percent rise in local currency volumes. Russian sales remained nearly stable at 15,767 thousand euros despite a 7.8 percent decline in local currency activity, and Ukraine experienced a 58.3 percent volume drop to 15 thousand euros. Management maintained a strategic emphasis on improving retail profitability and selectively expanding its store network contingent on the availability of reasonably priced commercial spaces, reporting 67 owned stores in Russia and 65 in Belarus at period end. On the balance sheet, total assets increased 5.1 percent to 98,603 thousand euros, and equity attributable to parent shareholders grew 6.1 percent to 81,479 thousand euros. Cash and cash equivalents nearly doubled to 12,443 thousand euros. During the reporting window, shareholders resolved to retain the 2025 full-year net profit of 9,229 thousand euros within retained earnings without distributing dividends, and authorized Treetiger OÜ as the group auditor. These developments collectively underscore a transitional period characterized by margin resilience, shifting sales mix toward retail, moderated profit growth, and strengthened liquidity positions.
