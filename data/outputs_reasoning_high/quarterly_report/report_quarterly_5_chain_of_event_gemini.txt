During the first quarter of 2025, Arco Vara AS reported a significant financial improvement compared to the same period in 2024, driven by a moderate recovery in the real estate market, stabilizing inflation, and declining interest rates. The group generated total revenue of 1706 thousand euros in Q1 2025, representing an increase of 716 thousand euros compared to 990 thousand euros reported in Q1 2024. Revenue from the sale of the group's own real estate rose to 1589 thousand euros from 927 thousand euros, while service and franchise revenues contributed 117 thousand euros compared to 63 thousand euros previously. Cost of sales increased to 1108 thousand euros from 817 thousand euros, resulting in a higher gross profit of 598 thousand euros compared to 173 thousand euros in Q1 2024. Operating expenses remained well managed, with marketing and distribution expenses virtually unchanged at 69 thousand euros versus 70 thousand euros, and administrative expenses decreasing to 270 thousand euros from 297 thousand euros. Consequently, the group achieved an operating profit of 259 thousand euros in Q1 2025, recovering from an operating loss of 208 thousand euros in Q1 2024. Financial income and expenses resulted in a net cost of 143 thousand euros, closely matching the 141 thousand euros recorded in the prior year period. After accounting for income tax of 13 thousand euros, Arco Vara posted a net profit of 103 thousand euros for Q1 2025, marking a strong turnaround from a net loss of 349 thousand euros in Q1 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share both improved to 0.01 euros from negative 0.03 euros in Q1 2024. The financial position of the company expanded during the period. Total assets increased to 42728 thousand euros as of 31 March 2025, compared to 39568 thousand euros at the end of December 2024. Inventories rose to 32329 thousand euros from 29170 thousand euros, reflecting ongoing investments in properties under development, including the acquisition of a new development property at Spordi Street in Tallinn during the quarter. Total equity attributable to owners of the parent stood at 19842 thousand euros at the end of March 2025, showing a slight increase from 19739 thousand euros at year end 2024. Interest-bearing liabilities increased to 16568 thousand euros as of 31 March 2025, up from 15215 thousand euros at the end of 2024, driven by new bank loan financing secured for construction activities at Rannakalda and the Spordi project. Net loans reached 15455 thousand euros, while cash and cash equivalents decreased to 1113 thousand euros from 1720 thousand euros. The weighted average annual interest rate of interest-bearing liabilities was 8.2% at the end of Q1 2025, representing a decrease of 0.5 percentage points compared to 31 December 2024, aided by declining Euribor rates. Operating activities used net cash of 2838 thousand euros, while financing activities generated 2231 thousand euros, resulting in a net cash decrease of 607 thousand euros for the quarter. Overall, the first quarter of 2025 demonstrated a solid operational recovery and continued progression across the group's active residential development projects in Estonia and Bulgaria.
