AB KN Energies reported a stronger first quarter of 2026, with consolidated revenue increasing to EUR 29,823 thousand from EUR 25,326 thousand in the first quarter of 2025. Gross profit rose to EUR 12,907 thousand from EUR 9,793 thousand, while operating profit increased to EUR 9,608 thousand from EUR 7,141 thousand. Profit before income tax reached EUR 8,824 thousand, compared with EUR 5,364 thousand previously, and profit for the period rose to EUR 7,247 thousand from EUR 4,426 thousand. Total comprehensive income was EUR 7,274 thousand, compared with EUR 4,495 thousand. Basic and diluted earnings per share increased from EUR 0.01 to EUR 0.02.

The main revenue contribution came from LNG terminal activities. Revenue from LNGT regasification services collected directly from users increased to EUR 19,162 thousand from EUR 14,593 thousand, while liquid energy products transshipment revenue rose to EUR 7,596 thousand from EUR 6,615 thousand. Consulting services revenue increased to EUR 2,749 thousand from EUR 1,932 thousand, and the Group recorded EUR 215 thousand from a new biomethane liquefaction service. These increases were partly offset by lower revenue from other LNG terminal activities and other transshipment services. LNG security supplement income was reduced by the proportional repayment of excess returns from regulated activities in 2022–2023.

By segment, LNGT revenue increased to EUR 18,687 thousand from EUR 14,989 thousand, and segment net profit rose to EUR 4,529 thousand from EUR 1,328 thousand. Commercial LNG revenue and net profit also increased to EUR 3,357 thousand and EUR 1,128 thousand, respectively. The liquid energy terminals segment weakened, with revenue declining to EUR 7,779 thousand from EUR 7,864 thousand and net profit falling to EUR 1,590 thousand from EUR 2,332 thousand.

Cost of sales increased to EUR 16,916 thousand from EUR 15,533 thousand, mainly reflecting higher environmental pollution and emissions allowance taxes, wages, and asset repair and maintenance costs. Distribution and administrative expenses increased to EUR 3,316 thousand from EUR 3,002 thousand. Net finance costs narrowed substantially to EUR 784 thousand from EUR 1,777 thousand, supported by higher finance income, including a EUR 458 thousand net gain on derivatives, and lower borrowing interest expenses.

Operating cash flow more than doubled to EUR 27,917 thousand from EUR 13,758 thousand. However, higher investing and financing outflows, including EUR 4,906 thousand of capital expenditure and EUR 7,728 thousand of borrowings repayments, limited the net cash increase to EUR 5,625 thousand, versus EUR 8,420 thousand previously. Cash and cash equivalents ended at EUR 17,986 thousand. Total assets were EUR 556,798 thousand and equity EUR 179,866 thousand at 31 March 2026. On 30 April 2026, a new six-member Board was elected for a four-year term.
