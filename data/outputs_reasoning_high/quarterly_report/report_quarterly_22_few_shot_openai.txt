Telia Lietuva reported stronger financial performance in the first half of 2026, with growth in revenue, earnings and cash generation. Revenue increased 7.2% year on year to EUR 259.1 million, while second-quarter revenue rose 6.9% to EUR 130.3 million. H1 service revenue grew 6.7% and equipment sales increased 9.6%. Fixed-service revenue rose 5.3% to EUR 112.9 million, supported particularly by IT services, which grew 23.9%. Mobile-service revenue increased 8.3% to EUR 100.0 million, while equipment revenue reached EUR 46.1 million. Mobile subscriptions grew 3.3% to 1.731 million, although broadband connections, TV customers and fixed telephone lines declined modestly.

Profitability improved despite higher operating costs. H1 adjusted EBITDA excluding non-recurring items increased 10.2% to EUR 106.9 million, with the margin rising from 40.1% to 41.3%. Reported EBITDA grew 9.5% to EUR 105.5 million and the EBITDA margin reached 40.7%, exceeding 40% for the first time. Operating profit increased 15.1% to EUR 62.4 million, while profit before tax rose 16.6% to EUR 61.3 million. Net profit increased 14.7% to EUR 51.3 million, lifting the profit margin from 18.5% to 19.8% and earnings per share from EUR 0.077 to EUR 0.088.

Cost growth was concentrated in operating expenses, which increased 11.3% to EUR 68.8 million, while cost of goods and services rose 2.3%. Higher equipment sales and increased network interconnection and roaming costs contributed to the increase in goods and services costs. Energy expenses rose 14.3% and external IT services increased 18.4%. The company also recorded EUR 1.4 million of non-recurring redundancy expenses related to restructuring its Digital & Technology unit. The workforce declined to 1,601 employees from 1,687 a year earlier.

Cash generation remained strong. Net cash from operating activities rose 15.0% to EUR 103.1 million and free cash flow increased 12.5% to EUR 64.1 million. Capital investments grew 57.9% to EUR 37.5 million, including EUR 9.7 million for 5G spectrum licenses and increased spending on mobile networks and IT infrastructure. The company acquired two 1,500 MHz frequency blocks for almost EUR 8 million and extended its 2,100 MHz spectrum rights for EUR 2 million. It also began construction of a new data center near Vilnius, with up to EUR 26 million planned for the initial modules.

At June-end, net debt was negative EUR 4.3 million, compared with EUR 27.5 million of net debt a year earlier. The company paid EUR 81.6 million in dividends for 2025, or EUR 0.14 per share, while continuing investments in network capacity and future infrastructure.
