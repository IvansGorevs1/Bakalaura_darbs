Arco Vara’s Q1 2025 performance improved materially from Q1 2024. Sales revenue increased by €716 thousand to €1.706 million, including €1.589 million from sales of properties in the group’s own developments compared with €927 thousand a year earlier. Service revenue rose to €117 thousand from €63 thousand, supported mainly by higher franchise fees. Gross profit increased to €598 thousand from €173 thousand, while operating performance turned positive: EBIT was €259 thousand compared with an operating loss of €208 thousand in Q1 2024. Net profit was €103 thousand, or €0.01 per share, versus a net loss of €349 thousand and negative earnings per share of €0.03 in the prior-year quarter. Finance costs remained broadly stable at €143 thousand, compared with €141 thousand, while the group recorded €13 thousand of income tax expense.

The improvement was led by the Estonian development segment, which generated €1.589 million of revenue versus €935 thousand and increased operating profit to €320 thousand from a €128 thousand loss. Its net profit was €191 thousand compared with a €190 thousand loss in Q1 2024. The Bulgarian development segment had no revenue and recorded a €77 thousand net loss, broadly similar to the €81 thousand loss a year earlier. Estonian construction recorded a €46 thousand operating loss, an improvement from €70 thousand, while other activities generated €117 thousand of revenue and €61 thousand of operating profit.

Operating cash flow remained negative at €2.838 million, compared with €2.217 million used in Q1 2024, reflecting development-related cash requirements. Financing activities provided €2.231 million, including new borrowing, resulting in a net cash outflow of €607 thousand, an improvement from €738 thousand. Cash and cash equivalents declined from €1.720 million at year-end 2024 to €1.113 million. Total assets rose to €42.728 million from €39.568 million, mainly as inventories increased to €32.329 million from €29.170 million, including a €3.5 million property acquisition. Interest-bearing liabilities increased to €16.568 million from €15.215 million, and net loans reached €15.455 million. The weighted average loan interest rate decreased by 0.5 percentage points from year-end to 8.2%, although interest paid increased to €322 thousand from €231 thousand in Q1 2024. Equity was €19.842 million and the equity ratio was 46.4%.

The company reported a cautious but improving Estonian real estate market, supported by falling Euribor, stabilizing inflation and stronger residential interest. Rannakalda sold four apartments, bringing sales to 91 of 113 units. Soodi 6 had 10 of 66 apartments pre-sold, while Botanica Lozen reached 95% completion and had sold nine of 16 houses. Arco Vara also acquired the Spordi Street development site in Tallinn, planned for 56 apartments with total investment of €15 million. Progress continued on Arcojärve and Kodulahe’s next phases. The group’s focus remains selling completed units, completing Botanica Lozen and preparing new developments for construction.
