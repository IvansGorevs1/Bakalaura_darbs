During the first six months of 2026, AS Silvano Fashion Group achieved consolidated revenue of 28,590 thousand euros, representing a 0.6 percent increase compared to 28,428 thousand euros in the first six months of 2025. The group specializes in women lingerie design, manufacturing, and distribution, with Russia and Belarus remaining its core markets and accounting for 86.7 percent of total sales. Sales in Russia totaled 15,767 thousand euros, down 0.6 percent reported in euros and down 7.8 percent in local currency. Sales in Belarus increased by 6.4 percent to 9,046 thousand euros, representing a 2.7 percent increase in local currency. Wholesale revenue decreased by 9.1 percent to 14,934 thousand euros, while retail revenue grew by 13.6 percent to 13,548 thousand euros. At the end of the period, the group operated 135 own stores, down from 137 at the close of 2025. 

The group reported a gross profit of 15,892 thousand euros, marking a 5.3 percent increase from 15,091 thousand euros. The gross margin improved to 55.6 percent from 53.1 percent due to a decrease in the cost of sold goods. Consolidated operating profit decreased by 3.5 percent to 5,751 thousand euros, and consolidated EBITDA decreased by 0.8 percent to 7,508 thousand euros. Reported consolidated net profit for the six-month period dropped by 31.2 percent to 4,321 thousand euros compared to 6,279 thousand euros in the previous year. Net profit attributable to equity holders of the parent company amounted to 3,961 thousand euros, down 31.5 percent from 5,781 thousand euros. Earnings per share decreased to 0.11 euros from 0.16 euros. 

Operating cash flow showed a significant improvement, rising by 96.6 percent to 6,842 thousand euros from 3,481 thousand euros. Investments in property, plant, and equipment totaled 115 thousand euros, compared to 368 thousand euros in the prior year period. Total assets as of June 30, 2026, increased by 5.1 percent to 98,603 thousand euros compared to 93,847 thousand euros at the end of December 2025. Total equity attributable to equity holders of the parent company increased by 6.1 percent to 81,479 thousand euros. Cash and cash equivalents rose to 12,443 thousand euros from 6,639 thousand euros, accompanied by short-term deposits over three months totaling 38,707 thousand euros. Overall, despite a decrease in net profit, the company demonstrated stable revenue growth, improved gross margins and cash generation, and strengthened its financial position during the reporting period.
