During the first six months of 2026, AS Merko Ehitus reported consolidated revenue of EUR 149.9 million, representing a 10.7% decrease compared to EUR 167.9 million in the first six months of 2025. Revenue for the second quarter of 2026 reached EUR 92.7 million, up from EUR 82.6 million in the second quarter of the previous year. Net profit attributable to shareholders for the six-month period was EUR 12.8 million, down from EUR 21.7 million in the prior-year period. Second-quarter net profit attributable to shareholders was EUR 8.5 million, compared to EUR 11.2 million in the second quarter of 2025. Pre-tax profit for the first six months of 2026 stood at EUR 10.8 million, resulting in a pre-tax profit margin of 7.2%, compared to EUR 23.5 million and a 14.0% margin in the first six months of 2025. The decline in profitability was impacted by the expiration of successful contracts from previous periods and a return to more typical sector margins amid high market competition, alongside first-quarter seasonality effects.

The construction service segment generated EUR 118.3 million in revenue for the first six months, down 1.5% year-over-year, and recorded an operating profit of EUR 3.0 million. The real estate development segment generated EUR 31.6 million in revenue, down 33.9%, with operating profit dropping to EUR 7.0 million from EUR 11.9 million. During the first six months, the group sold 120 apartments and five commercial units, generating EUR 27.6 million from the sale of own-developed apartments, compared to 222 apartments and EUR 44.9 million in the corresponding period of 2025. Despite lower revenue and net profit, the group achieved an all-time high secured order-book of EUR 835.7 million as of June 30, 2026, marking an 88% increase compared to EUR 443.8 million as of June 30, 2025. This growth was largely driven by public sector investments, including Rail Baltica and major defence-related contracts, with group companies signing EUR 486.4 million in new contracts during the first six months of 2026. Private sector clients remained cautious due to weak economic growth, while construction input prices faced continuing upward pressure from energy costs and geopolitical factors.

Cash and cash equivalents stood at EUR 39.6 million at the end of June 2026, compared to EUR 25.9 million a year earlier. Total equity reached EUR 251.1 million, representing 57.4% of total assets. The company paid out EUR 22.1 million in dividends, equivalent to EUR 1.25 per share, during the reporting period.
