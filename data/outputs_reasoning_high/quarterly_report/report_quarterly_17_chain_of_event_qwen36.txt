AB Novaturas reported a twelve-month period ending 31 December 2025 characterized by a deliberate strategic shift from volume expansion to profitability optimization. While top-line metrics contracted significantly due to market conditions and intentional portfolio adjustments, the Group achieved substantial improvements in operational efficiency and earnings quality. Total consolidated revenue declined by 16.1 percent year-on-year to EUR 168 million, accompanied by a 20 percent reduction in the number of served travellers to 191 thousand. This top-line decrease was a direct result of management strategy to cut volumes on unprofitable routes and concentrate resources on higher-margin destinations to enhance overall profitability. Despite the lower sales volume, financial performance showed marked improvement across key profitability indicators. Adjusted EBITDA transitioned from a negative EUR 449 thousand in 2024 to a positive EUR 420 thousand in 2025, representing a 193.6 percent improvement. The annual net loss narrowed by 39 percent to EUR 1.17 million, notably excluding a prior-year non-cash goodwill impairment of EUR 5.7 million that heavily impacted the 2024 baseline. Gross profit margins expanded by 1.3 percentage points to 11.66 percent, driven by stricter supply discipline and optimized route mix.

Operational enhancements underpinned the improved cost structure. Aircraft load factors rose by 1.5 percentage points to 97.2 percent, with particularly strong gains observed in long-haul destinations exceeding 99 percent utilization in the second half of the year and ski routes climbing from 90.5 percent to 97.2 percent. These efficiency gains helped offset the impact of intense industry competition and an oversupply of travel services that emerged during the autumn season, which subsequently pressured pricing dynamics. Furthermore, unexpected disruptions at Vilnius Airport related to illegal cross-border drone activities curtailed last-minute travel demand, contributing to the overall decline in client volumes. Nevertheless, forward-looking indicators remain robust, as bookings for the 2026 summer season reached levels 59 percent higher than the previous year by December 2025, led by strong demand for Greece, Bulgaria, Tunisia, and Turkey. Management plans to sustain this profitability trajectory through continued operational and cost efficiency initiatives, alongside an expanded hotel portfolio designed to maintain competitive pricing. Overall, the reporting period demonstrates a successful execution of a margin-focused restructuring strategy that stabilized core earnings while navigating challenging macroeconomic and operational market conditions.
