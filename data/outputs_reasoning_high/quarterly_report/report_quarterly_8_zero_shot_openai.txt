AS Tallink Grupp had a difficult 2025, with results affected by economic uncertainty, weak consumer and business confidence, reduced cargo demand and geopolitical tensions in its core markets. The Group carried 5.53 million passengers, down 0.9% year on year, while cargo volumes fell 19.2% to 245,004 units and passenger vehicles declined 2.2% to 760,473. Consolidated revenue decreased by EUR 20.5 million to EUR 765.3 million. Gross profit fell to EUR 143.2 million from EUR 153.6 million, while EBITDA declined to EUR 130.1 million from EUR 175.2 million. Operating profit decreased to EUR 50.3 million from EUR 77.4 million, and net profit fell to EUR 17.3 million, or EUR 0.023 per share, compared with EUR 40.3 million, or EUR 0.054 per share, in 2024.

Route operations revenue decreased by EUR 13.7 million to EUR 625.2 million and the segment result declined to EUR 70.5 million from EUR 81.5 million. Estonia-Finland passenger volumes increased 1.8% and revenue rose to EUR 314.5 million, but the segment result fell by EUR 10.9 million to EUR 70.4 million. Finland-Sweden revenue declined to EUR 225.5 million and the segment result fell to EUR 1.3 million; performance was affected by 68 days of maintenance for Baltic Princess and Silja Serenade. Estonia-Sweden revenue decreased to EUR 85.3 million, while its segment loss narrowed to EUR 1.2 million. Other-segment revenue fell to EUR 145.5 million, but its result remained broadly stable at EUR 28.5 million.

Operating costs increased 1.4% despite lower revenue, including a 5.1% increase in staff costs. However, depreciation and amortisation decreased by EUR 18.0 million to EUR 79.8 million following the change in estimated vessel useful lives and the sale of three vessels. Net finance costs also declined by EUR 6.8 million to EUR 21.4 million as a result of lower debt and interest rates. During the year, the Group sold Star I, Regal Star and Sailor, chartered out Romantika and extended the charters of Galaxy I and Silja Europa.

Fourth-quarter performance improved: revenue rose 2.4% to EUR 188.0 million, EBITDA increased to EUR 27.6 million from EUR 25.7 million, and net profit was EUR 12.2 million versus a EUR 5.2 million loss a year earlier. The Group ended the year with net debt of EUR 432.4 million, down from EUR 537.7 million at the end of 2024, after EUR 113.5 million of loan and interest payments. Cash was EUR 13.5 million after EUR 44.6 million of dividends and related tax payments. Management intends to propose another dividend of EUR 0.06 per share in 2026.
