During the twelve-month period ended 31 December 2025, AB Novaturas reported consolidated revenues of EUR 168.48 million, representing a 16.1 percent decline compared to EUR 200.88 million in 2024. The group served 190.9 thousand travellers during the year, which is a 20.3 percent decrease from 239.5 thousand travellers in the previous year. The contraction in volume was an intentional strategic decision by management to prioritize profitability over scale amidst an operating environment characterized by intense competition and excess travel supply. By reducing volumes on loss-making routes and optimising travel programs, the company successfully improved its operational margins and achieved a positive EBITDA of EUR 420 thousand, compared to a negative EBITDA of EUR 6.13 million in 2024 and an adjusted negative EBITDA of EUR 449 thousand for the prior year.

Gross profit for the twelve-month period stood at EUR 19.65 million, decreasing by 5.3 percent compared to EUR 20.74 million in 2024. However, the gross profit margin improved by 1.3 percentage points to 11.66 percent, up from 10.33 percent. Operating efficiency was further reflected in the aircraft load factor, which increased by 1.5 percentage points to 97.2 percent compared to 95.7 percent in 2024, supported by strong performance in Estonian operations, long-haul destinations, and ski holidays. 

The group significantly reduced its net loss for the period to EUR 1.17 million, marking an 84.6 percent improvement compared to a net loss of EUR 7.60 million in 2024, which had included a one-off non-cash impairment of goodwill amounting to EUR 5.7 million. Earnings per share improved to negative EUR 0.15 from negative EUR 0.97 in the prior year. Despite these operational profitability improvements, final financial results were constrained by market-wide supply increases during the autumn season that pressured prices, as well as disruptions at Vilnius Airport related to illegal cross-border activity involving unmanned balloons, which dampened last-minute travel demand. 

Regarding the financial position as of 31 December 2025, total assets reached EUR 43.38 million compared to EUR 39.45 million at the end of 2024. Total equity decreased to EUR 6.86 million from EUR 8.02 million, while total borrowings increased to EUR 12.87 million from EUR 9.25 million. The report indicates that the group complied with certain financial covenants while breaching others. Cash and cash equivalents increased to EUR 1.69 million from EUR 394 thousand, and other current financial assets stood at EUR 4.50 million, consisting of cash deposits placed for travel guarantees. 

Early sales for the 2026 summer season showed positive momentum, with bookings by December 2025 increasing by 59 percent compared to the previous year. Management plans to maintain a similar or slightly higher travel programme level for 2026 while focusing on cost and operational efficiency.
