Aktsiaselts Infortar released its consolidated unaudited financial report for the fourth quarter and full year of 2025, demonstrating overall growth in revenue and operational earnings alongside a decrease in net profit compared to the previous year. For the twelve months ended December 31, 2025, the group reported total revenue of EUR 1,837.0 million, marking an increase from EUR 1,371.8 million recorded in the twelve months of 2024. Operating profit for 2025 reached EUR 111.1 million compared to EUR 77.0 million in 2024. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization rose to EUR 232.6 million in 2025 from EUR 145.4 million in 2024. The document explicitly links the higher 2025 EBITDA to the inclusion of AS Tallink Grupp results for the full twelve-month period, whereas Tallink Grupp results were only consolidated from August onwards in 2024, alongside an approximate EUR 12 million EBITDA improvement in Elenger and contributions from Estonia Farmid and Halinga. Conversely, net profit for the 2025 financial year declined to EUR 72.0 million from EUR 193.7 million in 2024, with profit attributable to owners of the parent dropping to EUR 72.7 million from EUR 191.3 million. The document attributes this decrease in net profit mainly to one-off effects related to the initial consolidation of Tallink Group recognized in 2024. Basic earnings per share for 2025 decreased to EUR 3.54 from EUR 9.36 in the prior year. Total assets stood at EUR 2,587.8 million at the end of December 2025, down from EUR 2,715.2 million at the end of 2024, while total equity increased slightly to EUR 1,177.5 million from EUR 1,166.2 million. Interest-bearing liabilities decreased to EUR 1,060.5 million from EUR 1,223.3 million, a reduction that the report reflects as the result of loan repayments across several group entities, including the Tallink Group level, and financial adjustments aligned with market conditions.

During the 2025 financial year, the maritime transport segment under Tallink carried 5,531,132 passengers, representing a 0.9% decrease year-on-year, while generating revenue of EUR 765.3 million and a net profit of EUR 17.3 million. In the energy segment, Elenger Grupp increased its gas and electricity sales volumes by 7% in the fourth quarter of 2025 to total 4.8 TWh, despite a regional decline in natural gas consumption. Strategic developments during the period included continued infrastructure construction such as the Rail Baltica mainline by INF Infra, further expansion into agriculture through the acquisition of OÜ Oisu Biogaas in November 2025, and portfolio diversification aimed at securing stable cash flows amidst geopolitical tensions and uncertain economic cycles.
