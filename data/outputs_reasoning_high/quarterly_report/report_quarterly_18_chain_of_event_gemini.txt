During the first three months of 2026, AB Pieno Žvaigždės reported total sales revenue of 52,064 thousand euros, showing a slight decrease compared to 52,324 thousand euros reported in the corresponding period of 2025. Despite the minor decline in revenue, the company achieved a substantial improvement in profitability. Cost of sales decreased significantly to 38,078 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2026 from 42,530 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2025. Consequently, gross profit rose markedly to 13,986 thousand euros compared to 9,794 thousand euros in the previous year. Operating profit before finance costs also improved substantially, reaching 3,366 thousand euros up from 535 thousand euros, while EBITDA increased to 4,730 thousand euros compared to 2,001 thousand euros for the same period in 2025. Net profit for the three months ended March 31, 2026, surged to 3,068 thousand euros, reflecting a major increase from 227 thousand euros reported for the first three months of 2025. Earnings per share rose accordingly to 0.07 euros compared to 0.01 euros in the prior year period. Regarding segment performance, sales in Lithuania were 31,405 thousand euros down from 32,112 thousand euros, sales in other European Union countries increased to 11,341 thousand euros from 10,801 thousand euros, and sales in other non-European Union countries stood at 9,318 thousand euros compared to 9,410 thousand euros. Looking at product groups, fresh milk products generated 42,497 thousand euros compared to 42,598 thousand euros, dry milk products amounted to 1,397 thousand euros down from 1,912 thousand euros, cheese sales reached 5,276 thousand euros up from 5,192 thousand euros, and other products contributed 2,894 thousand euros compared to 2,621 thousand euros. The company's financial position as of March 31, 2026, showed total assets of 85,305 thousand euros, increasing from 83,475 thousand euros at the end of December 2025. Total non-current assets stood at 49,653 thousand euros, while total current assets reached 35,651 thousand euros, which included inventories of 20,392 thousand euros, receivables of 13,102 thousand euros, and cash and cash equivalents of 2,157 thousand euros. Total equity increased to 41,164 thousand euros as of March 31, 2026, from 38,238 thousand euros as of December 31, 2025, driven by an increase in retained earnings to 18,614 thousand euros. Total liabilities decreased to 44,141 thousand euros at the end of March 2026 from 45,237 thousand euros at the end of December 2025. Non-current liabilities totaled 20,596 thousand euros, while current liabilities were 23,545 thousand euros. Cash flow performance also showed positive developments, with net cash flow from operating activities turning positive at 1,625 thousand euros for the three months of 2026 compared to a negative 183 thousand euros for the same period in 2025. Net cash flow used in investing activities was 551 thousand euros, and net cash flow used in financing activities was 723 thousand euros. Ending cash and cash equivalents stood at 2,157 thousand euros as of March 31, 2026, compared to 4,902 thousand euros as of March 31, 2025.
