Nordic Fibreboard AS reported consolidated net revenue of 1.86 million euros for the first quarter of 2026, representing a 7.3 percent increase compared to 1.74 million euros in the same period of the previous year. The revenue growth was primarily supported by higher order volumes from Finnish DIY stores within the fibreboard segment, whereas property management revenue remained immaterial following the termination of lease agreements in August 2024. Despite the top-line increase, the company's profitability deteriorated significantly during the reporting period. Gross profit dropped to a negative 105 thousand euros from a positive 127 thousand euros a year earlier. Consolidated EBITDA worsened to a negative 395 thousand euros, resulting in an EBITDA margin of negative 21 percent, compared to a negative 85 thousand euros and a negative 5 percent margin in the first quarter of 2025. The operating loss expanded to 530 thousand euros from 217 thousand euros, leading to a net loss of 570 thousand euros, which represents a substantial increase compared to the net loss of 228 thousand euros recorded in the first quarter of 2025. These negative financial results were heavily impacted by a scheduled factory shutdown in early January related to the transition to a new boiler house, a two-month calibration and start-up period for the new gas boiler house, cold weather conditions that increased normal energy consumption, and a sharp spike in gas prices in late February due to geopolitical events, leaving market prices approximately 50 percent higher than at the beginning of the year. Consequently, electricity, heat, and water costs within the cost of goods sold rose sharply to 1.05 million euros from 725 thousand euros. Total assets increased to 9.69 million euros as of March 31, 2026, compared to 8.72 million euros a year prior. Total liabilities grew to 5.80 million euros, while equity decreased to 3.89 million euros from 4.46 million euros at the end of December 2025. Borrowings stood at 3.32 million euros at the end of March 2026. Operating cash flow improved notably to a negative 13 thousand euros for the three months of 2026, compared to a negative 528 thousand euros in the corresponding period of 2025. Cash outflows from investing activities reached 169 thousand euros due to investments in production assets, while financing activities generated net cash inflows of 152 thousand euros. In real estate developments, design works for the Admirali Quarter project in Pärnu were completed, and building permits were issued for internal roads, utility networks, specific apartment buildings, and the reconstruction of the existing office building. Furthermore, the company entered into a preliminary sale-purchase agreement on January 8, 2026, for ten properties in Pärnu, with the final transaction scheduled for completion no later than June 30, 2026. The group employed 57 people at the end of March 2026, down from 69 people a year earlier, contributing to a decrease in total labour expenses to 411 thousand euros from 471 thousand euros. Looking forward, management anticipates stable demand and sales volumes for fibreboards while focusing on market expansion and energy efficiency improvements.
