Telia Lietuva, AB delivered strong financial performance in the first half of 2026, characterized by solid revenue growth and marked improvement in profitability. Total revenue increased by 7.2 percent to EUR 259.1 million, driven by a 6.7 percent rise in service revenue and a 7.2 percent increase in equipment sales. Growth spanned both consumer and enterprise segments, with mobile services advancing 8.3 percent and fixed services growing 5.3 percent. Profitability expanded sharply, as operating profit climbed 15.1 percent to EUR 62.4 million and profit for the period rose 14.7 percent to EUR 51.3 million. EBITDA increased by 9.5 percent to EUR 105.5 million, supported by pricing discipline and operational efficiencies that pushed the EBITDA margin above 40 percent. These gains held despite a statutory corporate tax rate increase to 17 percent at the beginning of the year.

Operating expenses rose by 11.3 percent, primarily due to EUR 1.4 million in non-recurring restructuring costs within the Digital and Technology unit, alongside higher energy procurement and external IT service fees. Nevertheless, productivity improvements partially neutralized these pressures, as the total headcount decreased by nearly 7 percent to 1,601 employees. The company’s cash generation remained robust, with operating cash flow increasing by 15.0 percent to EUR 103.1 million. Consequently, free cash flow advanced by 12.5 percent to EUR 64.1 million. On the balance sheet, Telia Lietuva transformed its financing position, converting a prior-year net debt balance into a net cash position of minus EUR 4.3 million by late June. This improved liquidity facilitated a record dividend distribution of EUR 81.6 million, or EUR 0.14 per share, covering the full 2025 fiscal year.

Strategic capital expenditure accelerated substantially, surging 57.9 percent to EUR 37.5 million. The majority of investments targeted network modernization and digital infrastructure, including EUR 14.2 million for IT systems and EUR 9.7 million dedicated to spectrum licenses. A focal point of the period was the acquisition of two 1,500 MHz frequency blocks for nearly EUR 8 million in a national regulatory auction, securing 40 MHz of spectrum specifically optimized for high-speed mobile data transmission. The company also secured additional rights to the 2,100 MHz band for EUR 2 million to bolster 5G capacity. Concurrently, fiber-optic coverage expanded to 992,000 households nationwide, while plans were initiated for a new data center project requiring up to EUR 26 million in phased investments through 2027. Sustained mobile data traffic growth of approximately 25 percent annually continues to underpin network utilization. Overall, the reporting period reflected disciplined execution, successful transition to stronger liquidity, and decisive reinvestment in future telecommunications infrastructure, supporting long-term competitive positioning.
