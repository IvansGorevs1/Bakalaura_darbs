Infortar Group’s first-half 2026 performance improved substantially despite broadly unchanged revenue. Six-month revenue was EUR 945.9 million, down 0.6% from EUR 951.9 million, while gross profit increased 39.6% to EUR 114.1 million. EBITDA rose 31% to EUR 111.2 million, lifting the margin from 8.9% to 11.8%, and EBIT nearly doubled, increasing 94% to EUR 51.1 million. Profit before tax rose from EUR 2.7 million to EUR 27.2 million. Net profit was EUR 7.2 million compared with a EUR 14.4 million loss in the prior-year period, while profit attributable to the parent’s owners increased to EUR 15.2 million from a EUR 2.5 million loss. Basic earnings per share improved from negative EUR 0.12 to EUR 0.74.

Second-quarter revenue declined 12.6% to EUR 440.9 million, mainly because lower wholesale sales reduced energy volumes. However, profitability still improved: EBITDA increased 11.8% to EUR 64.2 million, the margin rose to 14.6% from 11.4%, and EBIT increased 9.8% to EUR 29.7 million. Net profit rose to EUR 1.9 million from EUR 0.2 million, although the result was affected by EUR 15.8 million of income tax expense, including EUR 12.5 million of tax on dividends recorded during the quarter.

All segments contributed to the first-half EBITDA improvement. Maritime transport EBITDA rose to EUR 39.3 million from EUR 33.3 million, energy EBITDA to EUR 59.9 million from EUR 51.7 million, real estate EBITDA to EUR 6.5 million from EUR 4.4 million, and supporting-segment EBITDA changed from a EUR 4.4 million loss to a EUR 5.6 million profit. Management attributed the stronger group profitability to improved energy performance, the addition of agricultural units and the closure of unprofitable operations. The completed Halinga biomethane plant further integrated the Group’s agricultural, energy and circular-economy activities.

Tallink’s second-quarter revenue was unchanged at EUR 207.0 million, but EBITDA fell to EUR 34.3 million from EUR 37.4 million and passenger numbers declined 2.2%. Higher fuel costs, up EUR 8.4 million year on year, the increase in EU ETS surrender requirements to 100% from 70%, weak demand and geopolitical-related energy-market volatility affected results. In energy, sales volumes fell 34% to 3.3 TWh as wholesale sales declined, while regional gas consumption fell 7%.

Total assets increased to EUR 2.714 billion and equity to EUR 1.201 billion at June-end. Cash rose to EUR 253.2 million, net debt declined to EUR 829.9 million from EUR 851.6 million, and net debt to EBITDA improved from 3.7 to 3.3. The Group invested approximately EUR 50 million and increased its Tallink ownership to 68.76%; no business combinations were completed in the quarter.
