During the three months ended 31 March 2026, AB KN Energies and its subsidiaries reported significant improvements in overall financial performance compared with the same period of 2025. The group achieved revenue from contracts with customers of 29,823 thousand euros, representing an increase from 25,326 thousand euros reported in the previous year. Gross profit rose to 12,907 thousand euros compared with 9,793 thousand euros, while operating profit increased to 9,608 thousand euros from 7,141 thousand euros. Net profit for the period grew to 7,247 thousand euros, up from 4,426 thousand euros, resulting in basic and diluted earnings per share of 0.02 euros compared to 0.01 euros in the prior year.

The performance expansion was primarily driven by the liquefied natural gas terminal segment, which generated revenues of 18,687 thousand euros compared with 14,989 thousand euros in the prior period, and commercial liquefied natural gas activities, which contributed 3,357 thousand euros compared with 2,473 thousand euros. Conversely, revenues from liquid energy terminals declined slightly to 7,779 thousand euros from 7,864 thousand euros. Cost of sales increased to 16,916 thousand euros from 15,533 thousand euros, influenced by higher taxes on environmental pollution, emission allowances, and increased wages and salaries. Distribution costs and administrative expenses also rose to 3,316 thousand euros from 3,002 thousand euros. However, net finance costs improved significantly, decreasing to a net expense of 784 thousand euros from 1,777 thousand euros, largely due to lower interest expenses on borrowings.

The financial position remained robust, supported by strong operating cash flows. Net cash flows from operating activities reached 27,917 thousand euros for the three months of 2026, marking a substantial increase from 13,758 thousand euros in the comparative period. Cash and cash equivalents increased to 17,986 thousand euros as at 31 March 2026 from 12,361 thousand euros at the end of 2025, and short-term deposits stood at 69,520 thousand euros. Total borrowings decreased to 302,069 thousand euros from 311,194 thousand euros at year-end, while total equity reached 179,866 thousand euros. In corporate developments subsequent to the reporting period, the term of the previous board expired on 25 April 2026, and a new board was elected on 30 April 2026 for a four-year term, replacing the former two-tier governance structure that included an independent supervisory council and a separate board.
