Arco Vara’s unaudited Q1 2025 performance improved significantly compared with the same period of 2024. Revenue increased 72% to €1.706 million from €0.990 million, driven mainly by real estate sales, which rose to €1.589 million from €0.927 million. Service revenue also increased to €117,000 from €63,000, including franchise fees of €65,000 and rental revenue of €44,000. Gross profit grew to €598,000 from €173,000, while operating expenses remained broadly controlled: marketing and distribution expenses were €69,000, compared with €70,000 a year earlier, and administrative expenses fell to €270,000 from €297,000.

The group reported an operating profit of €259,000, reversing an operating loss of €208,000 in Q1 2024. Net profit was €103,000, compared with a net loss of €349,000 a year earlier, and basic and diluted earnings per share improved to €0.01 from negative €0.03. Estonian development was the main contributor to the improvement, generating €1.589 million of revenue and €320,000 of operating profit, compared with an operating loss of €128,000 in the prior-year quarter. Bulgarian development remained loss-making, with an operating loss of €76,000, while Estonian construction recorded a €46,000 operating loss.

The company operated in a cautiously recovering real estate market. Declining Euribor and stabilizing inflation supported transaction activity and consumer interest, although demand remained measured. Arco Vara sold four additional apartments in the Rannakalda development during the quarter, bringing cumulative sales to 91 of 113 units. The Soodi 6 project had 10 of 66 apartments pre-sold by quarter-end, with construction expected to begin at the end of Q2. Botanica Lozen in Bulgaria reached 95% completion, with nine of 16 houses sold, and is expected to be finalized by the end of Q2. Arco Vara also acquired the Spordi Street development site in Tallinn, a planned 56-apartment project requiring total investment of €15 million, with pre-sales scheduled to begin in Q2. The development pipeline’s estimated completion value increased by €15 million to €210 million.

Cash flow remained negative as operating activities used €2.838 million, compared with €2.217 million in Q1 2024. Financing activities provided €2.231 million, resulting in a net cash outflow of €607,000 and cash of €1.113 million at quarter-end. Total interest-bearing liabilities rose to €16.568 million from €15.215 million at year-end, while net loans increased by €1.960 million to €15.455 million. The weighted average interest rate declined to 8.2%, reflecting lower Euribor, but interest paid increased to €322,000. Total assets rose to €42.728 million, primarily alongside higher inventories, while equity was €19.842 million.
