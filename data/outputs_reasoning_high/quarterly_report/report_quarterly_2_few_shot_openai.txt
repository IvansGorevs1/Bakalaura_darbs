The period saw a marked improvement in Amber Grid’s reported financial performance. Revenue increased to EUR 32.821 million from EUR 20.763 million, while total income rose to EUR 32.874 million from EUR 20.785 million. Revenue from natural gas transmission and related services increased by 58%, supported by a 35% rise in transmitted gas volumes and higher transmission prices set for 2026. Revenue from system balancing products declined by 99%, following a new transit agreement to Kaliningrad that significantly reduced the need for balancing gas.

Operating expenses excluding depreciation, write-offs and impairment decreased to EUR 9.570 million from EUR 11.867 million. Natural gas costs fell by 68% to EUR 1.477 million, mainly because expenditure on balancing products declined by 99%, partly offset by a 118% increase in gas costs for technological needs. Wage and related costs increased to EUR 4.367 million from EUR 3.659 million, while other expenses rose to EUR 3.231 million from EUR 3.015 million. EBITDA consequently increased to EUR 23.304 million from EUR 8.918 million, and operating profit rose to EUR 19.627 million from EUR 5.185 million.

Reported IFRS results were significantly affected by regulatory adjustments. Adjusted EBITDA was EUR 8.285 million, compared with EUR 8.260 million in the prior-year period, indicating broadly stable adjusted performance. Management’s adjustments reduced EBITDA by EUR 15.019 million in the current period, compared with EUR 658 thousand in the first quarter of 2025. After depreciation and amortisation of EUR 3.677 million, net finance costs of EUR 626 thousand and income tax of EUR 3.238 million, net profit reached EUR 15.763 million, compared with EUR 4.239 million. Basic and diluted earnings per share increased to EUR 0.09 from EUR 0.02.

Operating cash flow improved to EUR 22.927 million from EUR 8.264 million. Investing cash outflows increased to EUR 3.570 million from EUR 1.308 million, while financing cash outflows rose to EUR 20.467 million from EUR 6.981 million, largely reflecting a EUR 17.845 million reduction in overdraft. Cash declined to EUR 832 thousand from EUR 1.942 million at year-end.

Total assets remained broadly stable at EUR 324.064 million, while equity increased to EUR 182.039 million from EUR 166.276 million, mainly due to current-period profit. Total borrowings decreased to EUR 104.174 million from EUR 123.666 million at year-end, with more debt classified as non-current following loan-transfer and covenant-related developments. Net debt was EUR 106.610 million, compared with EUR 76.052 million at 31 March 2025. A EUR 5.815 million provision remained for the potential repayment of guarantee funds related to the GIPL construction dispute.
