During the first quarter of 2025, Arco Vara AS experienced a continuation of the moderate recovery in Estonia's real estate market, supported by declining Euribor rates and stabilizing inflation, which contributed to increased transaction activity and consumer interest. The group reported a notable improvement in its financial performance compared to the same period in 2024. Total sales revenue for the first quarter of 2025 reached 1,706 thousand euros, representing an increase of 716 thousand euros compared to 990 thousand euros reported in the first quarter of 2024. This growth was largely driven by higher revenue from the sale of the group's own real estate, which amounted to 1,589 thousand euros compared to 927 thousand euros in the previous year's corresponding period. Service and franchise revenue also increased, rising to 117 thousand euros from 63 thousand euros. Cost of sales rose to 1,108 thousand euros from 817 thousand euros, resulting in a gross profit of 598 thousand euros, up from 173 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2024. Operating profit reached 259 thousand euros, marking a turnaround from an operating loss of 208 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2024. After accounting for finance costs and income tax, the net profit for the first quarter of 2025 was 103 thousand euros, reflecting a positive shift from a net loss of 349 thousand euros in the first quarter of 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share both improved to 0.01 euros, compared to -0.03 euros in the first quarter of 2024. Regarding the statement of financial position, total assets stood at 42,728 thousand euros as of 31 March 2025, increasing from 39,568 thousand euros at the end of December 2024 and 35,079 thousand euros at the end of March 2024. Total equity reached 19,842 thousand euros at the end of the reporting period, compared to 19,739 thousand euros at the end of 2024 and 20,668 thousand euros at the end of March 2024. Total liabilities increased to 22,886 thousand euros from 19,829 thousand euros at the end of 2024. Net loans increased to 15,455 thousand euros as of 31 March 2025, compared to 10,559 thousand euros at the end of the first quarter of 2024, while total interest-bearing liabilities reached 16,568 thousand euros, with a weighted average annual interest rate of 8.2%. Cash and cash equivalents decreased to 1,113 thousand euros as of 31 March 2025, down from 1,720 thousand euros at the start of the period. Net cash used in operating activities was 2,838 thousand euros, while net cash from financing activities was 2,231 thousand euros, resulting in a net cash flow of -607 thousand euros for the quarter. Operationally, the group sold four apartments during the first quarter of 2025, matching the four units sold in the first quarter of 2024, and expanded its portfolio by acquiring a new development site on Spordi Street in Tallinn. Overall, the first quarter of 2025 demonstrated financial recovery and stability, underpinned by active project development and favorable market factors in the group's home markets.
