For the unaudited three months ended 31 March 2026, AB KN Energies reported substantially improved consolidated financial performance. Revenue increased to EUR 29,823 thousand from EUR 25,326 thousand in the same period of 2025, an increase of approximately 18%. Growth was led by LNG terminal regasification and reloading services, which generated EUR 19,162 thousand compared with EUR 14,593 thousand, and liquid energy product transshipment services, which rose to EUR 7,596 thousand from EUR 6,615 thousand. Consulting services also increased to EUR 2,749 thousand from EUR 1,932 thousand. These gains more than offset lower revenue from other LNG terminal and transshipment-related activities and the reduction in income from the LNG security supplement associated with the repayment of excess returns from regulated activities.

Cost of sales increased by 9% to EUR 16,916 thousand, while distribution and administrative expenses rose by 10% to EUR 3,316 thousand. Higher costs included wages, environmental pollution and emission allowance taxes, repairs and maintenance, electricity, and other operating expenses. Despite these increases, gross profit expanded by approximately 32% to EUR 12,907 thousand, and operating profit increased by 35% to EUR 9,608 thousand. LNG terminal activities were the main contributor to the improvement: LNGT revenue rose to EUR 18,687 thousand from EUR 14,989 thousand and segment EBITDA increased to EUR 11,761 thousand from EUR 8,683 thousand. LET revenue declined slightly and its EBITDA fell to EUR 3,002 thousand from EUR 3,724 thousand, while commercial LNG revenue and EBITDA increased.

Net finance costs more than halved to EUR 784 thousand from EUR 1,777 thousand, supported by higher finance income, including a EUR 458 thousand net gain on derivatives, and lower interest expenses. As a result, profit before tax increased by approximately 64% to EUR 8,824 thousand. Net profit rose 64% to EUR 7,247 thousand from EUR 4,426 thousand, while earnings per share increased from EUR 0.01 to EUR 0.02. The parent company also improved, with revenue increasing to EUR 28,681 thousand and net profit to EUR 6,762 thousand from EUR 4,016 thousand.

Operating cash flow more than doubled to EUR 27,917 thousand, aided by lower receivables and higher earnings. Investing cash outflow increased to EUR 10,771 thousand, including EUR 4,906 thousand of capital expenditure and additional short-term deposit activity. Financing outflow rose to EUR 11,521 thousand because of higher debt repayments and interest payments. Cash nevertheless increased to EUR 17,986 thousand, while borrowings declined to EUR 302,069 thousand from EUR 311,194 thousand. Total equity increased to EUR 179,866 thousand, primarily reflecting the period’s profit. No impairment indicators were identified.
