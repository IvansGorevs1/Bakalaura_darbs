AS Trigon Property Development’s financial performance weakened significantly in 2025, as the company recorded no real estate sales compared with sales of €331,920 in 2024. Consequently, gross profit fell to zero from €220,299, and the company reported an operating loss of €108,021, compared with an operating profit of €161,649 a year earlier. The fourth quarter also ended in a loss of €23,333, versus a profit of €154,215 in the fourth quarter of 2024.

The absence of sales was the main factor behind the annual net loss of €99,507, compared with net profit of €167,409 in 2024. Financial income increased to €8,513 from €5,760 but was insufficient to offset the operating loss. Basic and diluted earnings per share declined to -€0.02212 from €0.03721.

Administrative and general expenses increased to €108,021 from €63,650. The increase included €41,452 of remuneration paid to members of the Management and Supervisory Boards, compared with no such expenses in 2024, as well as higher accounting, stock exchange, land tax and property-related costs. The company had no employees and no labour costs in either year. It also purchased €12,000 of services from companies controlled by Supervisory Board members, compared with €3,240 in 2024.

Cash flow reflected the weaker operating result. Operating activities used €142,945 during 2025, compared with a positive cash flow of €194,723 in 2024. Cash declined to €227,911 from €370,856 at year-end 2024. Inventories increased by €91,262 to €1.56 million, reflecting investments in the company’s Pärnu development project; no inventory write-downs were recorded. Trade and other receivables fell to €14,245 from €31,586, while total liabilities increased to €49,389 from €18,906.

Total assets decreased to €1.80 million from €1.87 million, and equity declined to €1.76 million from €1.85 million following the annual loss. The debt ratio rose to 2.74% from 1.01%, while return on assets and return on equity fell to -5.51% and -5.67%, respectively, from positive levels in 2024.

The company continued to own one 13.2-hectare development project in Pärnu intended for commercial, industrial and logistics use. Management stated that it would continue seeking buyers for the land plots and did not plan to start a new business activity in the following year. Despite the loss, the share price increased to €0.634 from €0.620, raising market capitalisation to €2.85 million from €2.79 million.
