AS Merko Ehitus’ 2025 revenue fell 42.3% to EUR 310.9 million from EUR 539.0 million in 2024, mainly because several large construction contracts completed during the year were not immediately replaced by equivalent projects. Gross profit declined to EUR 55.9 million from EUR 95.9 million, although gross margin improved slightly to 18.0% from 17.8%. EBITDA decreased to EUR 37.5 million from EUR 75.7 million, while the EBITDA margin fell to 12.0% from 14.0%. Operating profit declined 52.6% to EUR 34.3 million, with the margin decreasing to 11.0% from 13.4%.

Construction services accounted for 76.4% of revenue, down from 88.0%. Segment revenue declined 49.9% to EUR 237.6 million and operating profit fell 56.8% to EUR 25.7 million; the operating margin decreased to 10.8% from 12.5%. The report attributes this to the completion of successful contracts that had supported 2024 results and a return to more typical industry margins in a highly competitive market. Private-sector demand remained weak amid economic uncertainty, while public-sector activity increasingly focused on defence and infrastructure. Renewable-energy investment also declined, and Rail Baltica work was delayed.

Real estate development provided some offset. Revenue increased 13.8% to EUR 73.4 million, including EUR 67.8 million from apartment sales. The Group sold 358 apartments, compared with 323 in 2024, but segment operating profit decreased 18.8% to EUR 13.4 million and the margin fell to 18.2% from 25.5%. The result was affected by project-specific cost and revenue mix and a write-down of a property in Norway. The Group invested EUR 56.2 million in development projects, compared with EUR 41.6 million in 2024, and started more than three times as many new apartments as in the previous year.

Pre-tax profit declined to EUR 44.8 million from EUR 76.4 million, while net profit attributable to the parent fell to EUR 39.9 million from EUR 64.7 million. Net margin nevertheless improved to 12.8% from 12.0%, supported by lower finance costs, the absence of the EUR 5.1 million loss from investments in subsidiaries recorded in 2024, lower corporate income tax expense, and EUR 10.4 million of profit from joint ventures. Connecto-related joint ventures contributed approximately one quarter of consolidated net profit, according to management.

Cash and cash equivalents decreased to EUR 41.4 million from EUR 91.9 million. Operating cash flow moved from a positive EUR 57.8 million to negative EUR 1.7 million, primarily reflecting increased inventories and working-capital movements. Financing cash flow included EUR 33.6 million of dividends paid. Despite weaker earnings and cash generation, equity increased to EUR 260.6 million, the equity ratio rose to 62.8%, and the secured order book grew 37.1% to EUR 466.9 million. New construction contracts worth EUR 362.8 million were signed, with activity improving in the second half of the year. The early-2026 signing of major Rūdninkai defence-campus contracts is expected to contribute to revenue from 2026 onward.
