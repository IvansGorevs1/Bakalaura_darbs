During the financial year ended December 31, 2025, AS Tallinna Sadam Group demonstrated stable overall financial performance, characterized by improved profitability despite a minor decrease in total revenue. Consolidated revenue declined by 0.8 percent compared with the previous year to EUR 118.7 million, primarily driven by lower charter fees for the icebreaker Botnica resulting from fewer operating days. This decrease was partially offset by growth across other revenue streams, including higher vessel dues supported by larger vessel capacities and fee indexing, increased operating lease income, passenger fees, cargo charges, and ferry service revenue. 

Despite the slight contraction in revenue, profitability indicators showed significant improvement. Adjusted EBITDA grew by 6.4 percent to EUR 56.5 million, raising the adjusted EBITDA margin to 47.6 percent from 44.4 percent in the prior year. Operating profit increased by 12.2 percent to EUR 32.7 million, benefiting from a 10 percent reduction in operating expenses down to EUR 36.4 million, largely due to lower non-current asset repair costs and the receipt of an insurance indemnity. Furthermore, net profit rose by 17.3 percent to EUR 22.5 million, supported by a 30 percent decrease in finance costs resulting from lower market interest rates and reduced loan liabilities, which outweighed an increase in income tax expense on dividends due to a higher tax rate. 

Operational results reflected positive developments across core business segments. Total cargo volume grew by 5.1 percent to 13.8 million tonnes, supported by a significant recovery in liquid bulk volumes as well as robust shipments of steel and grain. The number of passengers served across the Group increased by 1.0 percent to 8.3 million, driven by growth on the Tallinn to Helsinki route and a 25 percent increase in cruise passengers. In domestic ferry services, TS Laevad served 2.5 million passengers and 1.2 million vehicles across 23,050 trips, achieving higher vehicle transport volumes despite a slight decrease in total voyages. Conversely, the utilization rate of the icebreaker Botnica fell to 49 percent due to fewer active project charter days, though the vessel successfully participated in a notable rescue operation in northern Canada. 

Capital investments totaled EUR 33.2 million during the reporting period, representing a 14.4 percent decrease compared with the previous year. Major capital allocations included the completion of a new multifunctional quay and back-up area at Paldiski South Harbour, ongoing infrastructure improvements, and preparations for future shore power installations. The Group maintained a strong financial position, closing the year with total assets of EUR 622.1 million, total equity of EUR 380.9 million, and a reduced net debt of EUR 141.7 million. Continuing its commitment to shareholder value, the company paid out EUR 19.2 million in dividends, representing EUR 0.073 per share and 100 percent of the prior year profit. Overall, the reporting period reflected successful strategic execution, solid cost discipline, and resilient operational performance across the Group's diversified business portfolio.
