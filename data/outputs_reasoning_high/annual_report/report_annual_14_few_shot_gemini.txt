Lyko Group AB reported continued top-line growth during the 2025 financial year, alongside a decline in profitability due to major strategic investments and restructuring costs. Total net revenue increased by 11.3 percent to SEK 3,962.6 million, compared to SEK 3,561.7 million in 2024. Growth was primarily driven by the Nordic segment, where net sales rose by 12.0 percent to SEK 3,699.8 million, supported by market share gains and physical store expansion. Notably, annual revenue in Norway surpassed SEK 1 billion for the first time. Conversely, net sales in the European segment decreased by 1.3 percent to SEK 130.7 million, prompting a late-year reorganization that included closing local offices in Germany and the Netherlands to centralize operations at the Nordic headquarters.

Despite the revenue expansion, profitability metrics weakened across the board. Gross profit increased moderately to SEK 1,658.7 million, but the gross profit margin contracted from 44.0 percent in the previous year to 41.9 percent. Operating profit fell to SEK 78.2 million, down from SEK 117.4 million in 2024, resulting in an operating margin of 2.0 percent compared to 3.3 percent previously. The company recorded a net loss of SEK 3.0 million for the year, a decline from a net profit of SEK 21.0 million in 2024, corresponding to earnings per share of SEK -0.20 compared to SEK 1.37 in the prior period.

The financial results were significantly influenced by heavy capital expenditures and strategic infrastructure projects, most notably the commissioning of the new automated warehouse expansion in Vansbro. This largest project in company history required substantial investments in property, plant, and equipment, lifting total investing cash flow outflows to SEK 351.2 million. Consequently, cash flow from operating activities strengthened markedly to SEK 527.5 million, up from SEK 209.7 million in 2024. Cash and cash equivalents rose to SEK 452.5 million at year-end, while net debt excluding IFRS 16 decreased slightly to SEK 292.5 million. Total assets expanded to SEK 3,086.4 million, and equity stood at SEK 456.6 million, lowering the equity ratio to 14.8 percent from 20.3 percent.

During the year, the store network expanded to 34 locations, highlighted by the launch of the first scalable Fab Store concept in Täby Centrum. To prioritize long-term expansion, the Board of Directors proposed no dividend for the 2025 fiscal year. Furthermore, following the reporting period, Lyko announced a cost-savings program in January 2026 expected to generate annual savings of approximately SEK 100 million through workforce reductions affecting around 70 full-time positions, accompanied by SEK 22 million in restructuring costs recorded in the first quarter of 2026.
