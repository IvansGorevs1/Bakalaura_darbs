Eleving Group delivered record financial results in 2024. Revenue increased nearly 15% to EUR 216.6 million from EUR 189.3 million in 2023, while net interest income rose 16.9% to EUR 162.2 million. Interest revenue grew to EUR 203.7 million, outpacing the increase in interest expense to EUR 41.5 million from EUR 37.5 million. Adjusted EBITDA reached a record EUR 89.8 million, up more than 16% from EUR 77.5 million. Profit before tax increased to EUR 37.7 million from EUR 28.5 million, and total net profit rose 21% to an all-time high of EUR 29.6 million. Profit from continuing operations increased to EUR 28.8 million from EUR 21.9 million, although profit from discontinued operations declined to EUR 0.8 million from EUR 2.5 million as the Group completed exits from Belarus and the Balkan vehicle-financing business.

Growth was supported by a 26.8% increase in loan issuance to EUR 368.6 million and a 16% expansion in the net loan and vehicle-rental portfolio to EUR 371.2 million. The largest portfolio increases came from Romania, Uganda, Albania, and the Sub-Saharan markets acquired in 2023. The Group reported that integration of those operations tripled the combined portfolio across its three main regional markets. Digitalization also supported demand and efficiency: the new self-service platform was rolled out across established European vehicle-finance markets, while vehicle-loan applications increased 80.9% year on year and total applications nearly doubled.

Portfolio quality improved alongside expansion. Non-performing loan rates declined from 7.5% to 6.1% in vehicle finance and from 4.5% to 4.3% in consumer finance. The ratio of impairment expense to interest revenue fell by two percentage points despite the larger portfolio, which the Group linked to continued underwriting discipline and risk management. The net foreign-exchange loss also narrowed to EUR 3.7 million from EUR 6.4 million, with the main impact arising from fluctuations in the Kenyan shilling.

A significant negative item was a EUR 3.0 million additional VAT liability assessed by the Romanian Tax Authority for 2017–2022, reducing reported net profit by EUR 2.6 million. Excluding this one-off expense, management reported net profit of EUR 32.2 million, more than 31% above 2023; the assessment is being contested.

Capitalization strengthened materially following the October IPO, which raised EUR 29.0 million and was the largest in Nasdaq Riga’s history. Total equity increased to EUR 108.1 million from EUR 65.4 million, the capitalization ratio improved to 29.3% from 26.1%, and net leverage declined to 3.3 from 3.7. IPO proceeds were used partly to repay subordinated bonds and selected higher-cost funding. In June, Fitch upgraded the Group’s rating from B- to B with a stable outlook.
