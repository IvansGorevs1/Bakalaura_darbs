Impero A/S delivered a resilient financial and operational performance during the financial year ending December 31, 2025, despite geopolitical uncertainty and macroeconomic volatility. Annual recurring revenue reached DKK 46.2 million at the end of 2025, representing a year-on-year growth rate of 19 percent compared to DKK 38.7 million in 2024. Total recognized revenue grew by 19 percent to DKK 42.0 million from DKK 35.4 million in the previous year. The company demonstrated solid SaaS metrics, highlighted by an exceptionally low customer churn rate of 1 percent, down from 2 percent in 2024, and a net revenue retention rate of 106 percent. Average annual recurring revenue per account rose to DKK 228 thousand, reflecting an 8 percent increase from DKK 212 thousand in 2024. Profitability showed significant improvement across the board. EBITDA improved by DKK 5.6 million to negative DKK 3.1 million, compared to negative DKK 8.7 million in 2024. Operating loss improved to negative DKK 6.8 million from negative DKK 11.7 million, and the net loss for the period narrowed to DKK 6.8 million from DKK 11.4 million in the prior year. The financial improvements were driven by steady top-line growth combined with disciplined cost management, despite a 6 percent increase in staff costs and other external expenses due to commercial investments and new hires. Business developments were marked by a challenging start to the year followed by recovered momentum in the second half, which delivered the company's strongest second half ever in net new annual recurring revenue. Impero surpassed the milestone of 200 customers, welcoming 26 new clients during the year, while successfully reducing top 10 customer concentration to 20 percent. Core growth remained anchored in Denmark and the DACH region, which grew annual recurring revenue by 22 percent and 26 percent, respectively. Product milestones included the successful launch of the control testing module in July 2025 and advanced integration of artificial intelligence features to enhance platform efficiency. To strengthen its capital position and support long-term value creation, Impero completed a directed issue raising DKK 15 million in December 2025. Consequently, equity increased to DKK 15.4 million at year-end, up from DKK 6.2 million in 2024, and cash and cash equivalents rose to DKK 18.9 million from DKK 9.8 million. Driven by this improved capital flexibility and strong SaaS metrics, the Board of Directors decided to defer its strategic ambition of achieving positive recurring cash flow before the end of 2026, choosing instead to accelerate commercial investments to drive higher future growth. For the full year 2026, Impero expects annual recurring revenue to reach between DKK 53 million and DKK 57 million, with EBITDA projected to range from negative DKK 2 million to positive DKK 1 million.
