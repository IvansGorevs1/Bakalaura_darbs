Trophy Games reported strong financial results for fiscal year 2025, marked by accelerated revenue growth and record quarterly profitability. Total revenue increased by 40% to DKK 126,523 thousand, up from DKK 90,070 thousand in 2024. EBITDA rose 26% to DKK 31,117 thousand, while profit before tax more than doubled by 109% to DKK 19,013 thousand, yielding an EBT margin of 15%. Profit for the year reached DKK 12,539 thousand, reflecting a 20% return on equity and basic earnings per share of DKK 0.47. The solvency ratio stood at 78%, supported by a debt-free capital structure.

Revenue expansion was primarily driven by the scaling of the Transport Series portfolio. The flagship titles Airline Manager, Truck Manager, and Farm Manager collectively accounted for 67% of total revenue, up from 35% in the prior year. Airline Manager contributed DKK 37,787 thousand, Truck Manager generated DKK 25,181 thousand after its late 2024 launch, and Farm Manager added DKK 22,002 thousand within six months. Revenue from older transport titles declined 46% to DKK 20,955 thousand as marketing resources reallocated toward the core portfolio. Margins benefited from a strategic shift toward in-app advertising, which doubled to DKK 18,476 thousand and avoids standard platform fees. To sustain growth, marketing expenditures increased 76% to DKK 40,486 thousand, targeting titles with rapid payback periods.

Although elevated marketing spend compressed the EBITDA margin to 25% from 27% in 2024, underlying profitability remained stable once the DKK 4,918 thousand in non-recurring impairment charges from 2024 are excluded. The fourth quarter delivered the strongest results in company history, with revenue jumping 106% to DKK 40,959 thousand and profit before tax reaching DKK 10,120 thousand. Additional financial adjustments included a DKK 4.7 million repayment of historical R&D tax credits following a Danish tax reassessment, and the completion of a DKK 4.89 million share buyback program.

Management attributes the performance to disciplined execution in a mature mobile gaming environment, utilizing data-driven user acquisition and refined ad monetization. For 2026, the company guides for revenue between DKK 130 million and DKK 153 million and EBITDA ranging from DKK 28 million to DKK 36 million. This forecast incorporates projected advertising income of DKK 22 million to DKK 27 million, a planned marketing budget of DKK 45 million to DKK 50 million, and sustained development investments in upcoming titles including Train Manager 2 and Space Manager.
