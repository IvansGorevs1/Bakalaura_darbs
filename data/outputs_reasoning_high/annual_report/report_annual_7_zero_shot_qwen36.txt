Heimstaden delivered strong operational and financial performance in 2025, characterized by margin expansion, disciplined capital allocation, and continued deleveraging. Rental income remained essentially flat year-on-year at SEK 15,916 million, as robust like-for-like rental growth of 4.9% and indexation-driven standing asset contributions were offset by the negative impact of the ongoing privatisation programme and portfolio divestments. Property expenses declined by 6.1% to SEK 4,378 million, reflecting successful cost control measures, digitalisation initiatives, and the reduced size of the portfolio following unit sales. Consequently, net operating income increased by 2.6% to SEK 11,475 million, elevating the LTM net operating income margin to 72.1% from 70.3% in 2024.

The group recorded a profit for the period of SEK 13,056 million, significantly higher than the SEK 7,621 million reported in the prior year, primarily driven by unrealised fair value adjustments of SEK 9,498 million on investment properties and realised gains of SEK 2,207 million from property divestments. Fair values across the European portfolio grew by 2.9% to SEK 324,429 million, supported by rising net operating income, value-accretive capital expenditure, and favourable ownership housing market conditions in markets such as Denmark, the Netherlands, and Poland. Real economic occupancy stabilised at 98.5%, matching the previous year, with stronger occupancy trends emerging throughout the latter half of 2025 as newly developed units in the United Kingdom gradually reached stability.

Financing activities remained a focal point, with the group advancing its deleveraging trajectory through targeted privatisation sales and liability management exercises that successfully extended debt maturities. Net loan-to-value decreased to 52.2% from 56.4% a year earlier, while the interest coverage ratio improved to 2.0 times. Total interest-bearing liabilities stood at SEK 175,227 million, with an average interest rate of 3.4% and substantial unutilised credit facilities of SEK 23,449 million providing liquidity headroom. The group completed approximately SEK 59,500 million in refinancing and new funding transactions, including green bond issuances and bank financing, maintaining access to favourable capital market terms despite shifting macroeconomic conditions. During the year, the group also recognised a goodwill impairment loss of SEK 3,707 million, primarily linked to adjusted cash flow projections and increased discount rates associated with specific operating segments.

Business developments were marked by continued progress on sustainability commitments and strategic investor relations. The subsidiary received Sustainalytics Negligible ESG risk for the third consecutive year, while energy intensity improved by 10% on a weather-corrected basis and greenhouse gas emissions fell 14.2% against the 2020 baseline. Privatisation sales accounted for SEK 10,664 million in the period, delivering 2,466 residential units at a 28.9% premium to book value. In legal matters, an arbitral tribunal ruled in favour of the company in the dispute initiated by shareholder Alecta, confirming no breach of the shareholders agreement. Overall, 2025 demonstrated consistent execution of the group’s evergreen investment philosophy, with solid top-line resilience, enhanced profitability, and a strengthened balance sheet positioning the company for sustained long-term value creation.
