For the financial year 2025, Infortar Group reported consolidated revenue of EUR 1,837 million, a significant increase from EUR 1,371.8 million in 2024. This growth was primarily driven by the full twelve-month consolidation of Tallink Grupp following the acquisition of control in August 2024, an approximately EUR 12 million improvement in Elenger’s operating results, and incremental contributions from the consolidated agricultural assets Estonia Farmid and Halinga. The group’s EBITDA rose to EUR 228 million from EUR 145.3 million in the prior year, expanding the EBITDA margin to 12.4 percent from 10.6 percent. Operating profit increased to EUR 109.0 million, while net profit declined to EUR 69.9 million from EUR 193.7 million in 2024. Management indicated that the earnings reduction was mainly attributable to one-time positive effects from Tallink’s initial consolidation and favorable investment valuations recorded in the comparative period. As a result, earnings per share decreased to EUR 3.3 from EUR 9.1.

The group’s balance sheet reflected improved financial flexibility. Total assets stood at EUR 2,597 million, while total equity grew to EUR 1,175 million, raising the equity ratio to 45 percent from 43 percent. Interest-bearing liabilities to equity declined to 91 percent from 104.9 percent, and net debt decreased to EUR 851.6 million from EUR 1,055.7 million. The workforce expanded to 6,466 employees. Consistent with its stated dividend policy, Infortar distributed EUR 3 per share, totaling EUR 62 million in payouts.

Operational performance was shaped by strategic integration and evolving market conditions. The maritime transport segment faced higher compliance costs from EU ETS and FuelEU Maritime regulations. While passenger volumes dipped 0.9 percent and cargo units fell 19.2 percent, partly due to Ukraine-driven demand shifts, extended vessel charters effectively smoothed seasonal revenue patterns. The energy division benefited from the complete annual impact of the Elenger Polska acquisition and the commissioning of a 4.9-megawatt solar park alongside an 8.0-megawatt-hour battery storage facility in Latvia. Regional natural gas consumption contracted 9 percent, but normalized pricing and infrastructure upgrades supported steady trading and distribution. In real estate, the group handed over a 25,000-square-meter logistics center, advanced a 30,000-square-meter commercial project, and secured a EUR 67.2 million civil engineering contract for Rail Baltica. Strategic entry into agriculture via the purchase of a 96.6 percent stake in Estonia Farmid further broadened the portfolio. Against a backdrop of moderate macroeconomic growth, persistent geopolitical uncertainty, and gradually easing inflation, Infortar demonstrated resilient earnings power, strengthened capital structure, and disciplined execution, positioning the company to sustain stable cash flows and shareholder returns despite sector-specific headwinds.
