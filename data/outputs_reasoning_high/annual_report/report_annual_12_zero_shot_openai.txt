Konsolidator delivered stronger financial and operational results in 2025, although the Group remained loss-making and had negative consolidated equity. Revenue increased 25% to DKK 25.3m from DKK 20.3m in 2024. Subscription revenue rose to DKK 20.6m from DKK 18.3m, while onboarding and consulting revenue increased 134% to DKK 4.7m, following higher onboarding prices introduced for new customers. Contribution increased to DKK 23.4m, although the contribution margin declined to 92% from 94%.

Profitability improved substantially. EBITDA loss narrowed to DKK 2.0m from DKK 9.3m, supported by higher revenue, lower staff costs and external expenses, and DKK 2.0m of other operating income. Staff costs after capitalized wages declined to DKK 21.1m from DKK 22.0m, while external expenses fell 11% to DKK 6.3m, mainly because of lower advertising and marketing costs, reduced consultancy use, and lower investor-relations spending. EBIT loss improved to DKK 5.1m from DKK 12.1m, and the net loss decreased to DKK 6.6m from DKK 14.9m. Lower financial expenses and a DKK 1.0m deferred-tax benefit also supported the result.

The second half showed a significant operational improvement: EBITDA was positive at DKK 0.5m compared with a DKK 3.6m loss in H2 2024, and operating cash flow was positive at DKK 0.1m versus negative DKK 3.6m. For the full year, operating cash flow improved to negative DKK 1.0m from negative DKK 9.2m, while net cash flow was positive by DKK 11,000 compared with a DKK 1.5m outflow in 2024.

Recurring revenue indicators strengthened. Contracted ARR increased 15% to DKK 24.4m, new sales rose 45% to DKK 4.5m, and the CAC payback period improved to 23 months from 43 months. The Rule of 40 score improved from negative 40% to 17%, and CLV/CAC increased from 2.7 to 4.1. However, ARR churn rose to 12.6% from 10.5%, while net retention remained 94%.

The company added 52 customers, expanded into four new markets, and made Iberia its third-largest market. It also launched its Financial Data Management solution, developed the FP&A product, and expanded its partner model; 38% of new customers came through partners versus 22% in 2024. Two private placements raised DKK 4.8m, but consolidated equity remained negative at DKK 3.6m, with cash of DKK 0.4m at year-end. For 2026, management guides for CARR and revenue of DKK 27–29m and EBITDA of DKK 1–2m.
