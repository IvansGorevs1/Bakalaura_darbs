Talkpool delivered improved financial performance in 2025 as it completed its restructuring phase and returned to organic growth. Net sales increased 12.5% to EUR 17.756 million from EUR 15.782 million in 2024, marking the first year of double-digit growth since the consolidation phase began. Gross margin was 26.4%.

EBITDA rose to EUR 1.828 million from EUR 1.702 million, although the EBITDA margin decreased to 10.3% from 10.8%. EBIT increased to EUR 1.620 million from EUR 1.527 million, with the EBIT margin declining to 9.1% from 9.7%. Net earnings after tax improved more strongly, reaching EUR 862 thousand compared with EUR 605 thousand in 2024, an increase of 42%; the EAT margin rose to 4.8% from 3.8%. Margins remained relatively stable despite investments in personnel, technology, and future growth.

The company reported that all revenue growth was organic. However, currency movements reduced reported revenue in euros, particularly during the first half of the year, as the US dollar weakened by 11% and the Pakistani rupee by 14%. Talkpool also reported continued balance-sheet improvement: equity reached EUR 2.1 million at year-end, compared with negative EUR 2.5 million in 2021, while net cash improved to EUR 0.3 million from net debt of EUR 5.7 million in 2021.

The Americas remained the largest market and continued to grow despite currency weakness and global uncertainty. The Caribbean performed well, supported by the long-term Digicel relationship, while Talkpool also supported the restoration of Jamaica’s communications infrastructure after Hurricane Melissa. The company re-entered the US market through a MasTec contract, but the US ramp-up was slower than planned and order volumes remained low after the company discontinued fiber rollout work to focus on remote wireless services.

Germany remained a solid contributor, supported by strong fiber-investment activity and Talkpool’s planning work for Deutsche Telekom, although a shift toward homes passed rather than connected temporarily flattened assignment volumes. Pakistan achieved its highest-ever revenue in local currency, improved gross profitability after renegotiating customer terms, and expanded activity across several telecom service areas.

Talkpool shifted from cost reduction toward growth, focusing on technology-enabled remote services, lower-cost delivery hubs, and AI-powered solutions. Initial AI trials and proof-of-concept work were completed during 2025, while management expects temporary pressure on cash flow and margins as investments take effect. The company launched its Vision 2030 plan, targeting EUR 40 million in revenue, a 14% EBITDA margin, and zero net debt by 2030.
