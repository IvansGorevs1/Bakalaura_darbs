During the financial year 2025, AS Tallinna Vesi and its subsidiaries achieved overall financial growth, driven by higher revenues from water services and construction activities, alongside operational efficiencies. Total group sales for 2025 reached 72.48 million euros, representing an 11.4 percent increase compared to 65.08 million euros in 2024. Water services revenue increased by 6.9 percent to 62.31 million euros, largely influenced by tariff changes and increased consumption among private customers in the main service area, where sales rose 18.3 percent to 31.84 million euros. Conversely, sales to business customers declined 2.7 percent to 16.76 million euros due to tariff reductions implemented to harmonize prices between private and business customers. Construction services revenue experienced significant growth, surging 62.0 percent to 8.46 million euros, driven by higher turnover from pipe and external road construction projects. The cost of goods and services sold rose 12.0 percent to 42.96 million euros, impacted by a 74.5 percent increase in construction service costs and higher pollution and chemical charges, though partially offset by a 6.9 percent decrease in electricity costs resulting from internal energy generation at the newly launched combined heat and power plant. Gross profit for 2025 increased by 10.5 percent to 29.52 million euros, compared to 26.71 million euros in the previous year. Operating profit before depreciation and amortisation, or EBITDA, grew to 30.95 million euros from 27.51 million euros. Operating profit rose 11.8 percent to 21.22 million euros, up from 18.98 million euros in 2024. Profit before taxes increased by 18.0 percent to 17.43 million euros, aided by lower net financial expenses of 3.78 million euros, which benefited from a declining Euribor rate. Net profit for the period grew 7.2 percent to 14.24 million euros, compared to 13.28 million euros in 2024, with net profit growth slightly tempered by a higher income tax rate on dividend payments. Earnings per share rose to 0.71 euros from 0.66 euros. The group continued robust capital investments, dedicating 55.74 million euros to fixed assets in 2025 compared to 49.53 million euros in 2024, aimed at infrastructure continuity, environmental protection, and smart water meter installations. Outstanding borrowings increased to 160 million euros from 118 million euros following additional drawdowns under long-term loan agreements, while the year-end cash balance rose to 11.29 million euros from 3.59 million euros. The company distributed a dividend of 0.53 euros per share in June 2025, corresponding to 80 percent of 2024 net profit. Overall, financial performance improved across key profitability and revenue metrics, supported by strategic tariff adjustments, operational cost controls, and expanded construction activity.
