JLT Mobile Computers AB reported improved operating results and sales growth for the full year 2025 despite a challenging macroeconomic and currency environment. Net sales increased by 11.1 percent to 131.5 million Swedish kronor, compared with 118.4 million Swedish kronor in the previous year. The top-line growth was supported by strong commercial progress, including a major order of 22 million Swedish kronor from an American food producer. Total order intake rose significantly by 37.5 percent to 141.6 million Swedish kronor, up from 103.0 million Swedish kronor, leaving the order backlog at 24.1 million Swedish kronor at year-end compared to 13.9 million Swedish kronor previously.

Profitability metrics also showed sequential improvement despite continuing losses. Operating profit strengthened significantly to a loss of 1.9 million Swedish kronor from a loss of 9.7 million Swedish kronor in 2024. EBITDA improved slightly to a loss of 1.7 million Swedish kronor from a loss of 2.1 million Swedish kronor. The net result after tax was a loss of 2.5 million Swedish kronor, representing an improvement compared with the net loss of 7.7 million Swedish kronor reported for the previous year. 

The gross margin decreased from 45.8 percent to 38.3 percent, with gross profit falling to 50.4 million Swedish kronor from 54.2 million Swedish kronor. This margin compression was primarily a temporary effect caused by currency fluctuations, as the US dollar depreciated by nearly 20 percent against the Swedish krona while dollar-denominated sales accounted for 74 percent of turnover. In addition, inventory revaluation based on the weaker dollar rate weighed on gross results. 

Market demand varied across segments, with subdued activity in warehousing and logistics due to geopolitical uncertainty and customer caution, while port operations and mining equipment segments exhibited positive momentum. To counter market headwinds and improve efficiency, the company implemented structural cost-reduction measures, including the discontinuation of a subsidiary and coordination of software development, which successfully lowered overhead expenses. Operating expenses overall decreased to 52.4 million Swedish kronor from 57.3 million Swedish kronor.

The company also focused heavily on balance sheet strength and capital efficiency by reducing excess safety inventories built up after the pandemic. Inventories decreased by 12.7 million Swedish kronor to 25.5 million Swedish kronor at year-end. Cash and cash equivalents rose to 16.9 million Swedish kronor compared with 13.3 million Swedish kronor, and the solidity ratio improved to 59 percent from 54 percent. Total equity stood at 41.8 million Swedish kronor. The Board of Directors proposed that no dividend be paid for the 2025 financial year, consistent with the previous year. Overall, the period demonstrated operational resilience, successful cost control, and a stronger platform for future recovery.
