Tallinna Vesi Group delivered stronger financial results in 2025. Revenue rose 11.4% to €72.48 million, from €65.08 million in 2024. Water-services revenue increased 6.9% to €62.31 million, while construction-services revenue grew 62.0% to €8.46 million, supported by higher pipe-construction and external road-construction activity. Revenue from private customers in the main service area increased 18.3% to €31.84 million, reflecting higher consumption and tariff changes. Business-customer revenue declined 2.7% to €16.76 million, mainly because business tariffs were reduced as part of the statutory harmonisation of private and business prices. Revenue from customers outside the main service area increased 13.8% to €7.71 million. Stormwater and fire-hydrant service revenue fell 25.5% to €4.35 million, largely because revised pricing rules changed the classification of combined-sewer-system revenue.

Cost of goods and services sold increased 12.0% to €42.96 million. Direct production costs were broadly unchanged at €9.90 million: lower purchased electricity costs, resulting partly from the new combined heat and power plant, largely offset higher chemical and pollution-tax expenses. Other costs increased 16.0%, including a 6.8% rise in staff costs and a 74.5% increase in construction-services costs. Despite the cost growth, gross profit increased 10.5% to €29.52 million, EBITDA rose to €30.95 million from €27.51 million, and operating profit increased 11.8% to €21.22 million. The company attributed the operating-profit improvement mainly to electricity-cost optimisation following the launch of the combined heat and power plant.

Net financial expense declined to €3.78 million from €4.20 million, mainly due to lower Euribor rates. Profit before tax increased 18.0% to €17.43 million, while net profit rose 7.2% to €14.24 million. Net profit growth was moderated by the higher income-tax rate on dividend payments. Earnings per share increased from €0.66 to €0.71, although the net profit margin declined from 20.41% to 19.69%.

Investment remained a major feature of the period. The Group invested €55.74 million in fixed assets, above the previous year, and operating cash flow increased to €30.83 million from €26.25 million. Cash at year-end rose to €11.29 million from €3.59 million, supported by €45 million of new borrowing, while total borrowings increased to €161.4 million and the debt-to-assets ratio rose to 66.02% from 61.71%.

Operationally, the company advanced major network, stormwater and treatment-plant projects. It laid or reconstructed 45.1 kilometres of pipes, expanded smart-meter coverage to more than 80% of customers, and commissioned combined heat and power generation that produced all required treatment-plant heat and 43.8% of relevant electricity consumption. Water and wastewater quality remained high, while sewer blockages declined to 363 from 441. The Group’s financial statements received an unmodified auditor’s opinion.
