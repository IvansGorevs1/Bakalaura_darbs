AAC Clyde Space’s 2025 performance weakened financially compared with 2024, primarily because of programme delays, although the company continued to expand its recurring data and services business. Group net sales decreased 16% to SEK 295.3 million from SEK 352.9 million, while total revenue declined to SEK 337.8 million from SEK 408.8 million. EBITDA fell to SEK 17.5 million from SEK 46.7 million, although the company reported a positive EBITDA for the third consecutive year. EBIT deteriorated to a loss of SEK 40.0 million from a loss of SEK 4.0 million, and the loss after tax increased to SEK 46.6 million from SEK 5.7 million. Earnings per share declined to SEK -7.63 from SEK -1.00.

The decline was concentrated in Products & Missions, where sales fell to SEK 245.0 million from SEK 346.0 million and EBITDA to SEK 16.8 million from SEK 51.6 million. Data & Services continued to develop strongly: sales increased 80% to SEK 80.1 million from SEK 44.9 million, and EBITDA rose to SEK 27.8 million from SEK 21.1 million. The company attributed the weaker overall outcome to delays in two programmes. Technical discussions involving an end customer and subcontractor postponed approximately SEK 32 million of milestone revenue into 2026, while political and procurement developments delayed the start of the EPS-Sterna programme. Sedna satellite commissioning also took longer than planned, and VIREON’s initial launch was delayed by the launch provider.

Operating cash flow consequently changed from an inflow of SEK 56.8 million in 2024 to an outflow of SEK 72.6 million in 2025. Cash and cash equivalents decreased to SEK 30.1 million from SEK 49.7 million, but the Group had an undrawn overdraft facility of SEK 50 million, giving total available cash and facilities of SEK 80.1 million. The equity ratio strengthened to 80% from 71%, supported by two directed share issues that raised approximately SEK 64.5 million in June and SEK 50.3 million in December before transaction costs. No dividend was proposed.

Operationally, the Group reorganised into Products & Missions and Data & Services, brought Sedna-1 and Sedna-2 into operation, delivered its first CubeCAT laser terminals and began assembling the VIREON Earth Observation constellation. INFLECION Phase 1 was also contracted with ESA. After year-end, AAC received an EUR 76.3 million order from OHB Sweden for 20 EPS-Sterna radiometers; by the end of March 2026, the company reported an order backlog of approximately SEK 1.1 billion. These developments, together with planned Sedna and VIREON expansion, improved the company’s forward position despite the disappointing 2025 financial results.
