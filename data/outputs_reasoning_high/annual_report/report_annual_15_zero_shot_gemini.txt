During the financial year ended 31 December 2025, AS Merko Ehitus group reported revenue of 310.9 million euros, representing a decrease of 42.3 percent compared to 539.0 million euros in 2024. The decline in revenue was primarily driven by the completion of several large-scale construction contracts initiated in earlier years and a slower pace of signing new contracts during 2024 and the first half of 2025. Gross profit for 2025 stood at 55.9 million euros compared to 95.9 million euros in 2024, with a stable gross margin of 18.0 percent against 17.8 percent previously. Operating profit for the reporting period was 34.3 million euros, down from 72.5 million euros, yielding an operating profit margin of 11.0 percent compared to 13.4 percent. EBITDA decreased to 37.5 million euros from 75.7 million euros, with an EBITDA margin of 12.0 percent. Net profit attributable to equity holders of the parent was 39.9 million euros, down from 64.7 million euros, while the net margin improved to 12.8 percent from 12.0 percent. Return on equity for 2025 was 15.6 percent, compared to 28.4 percent in 2024.

Global geopolitical uncertainty and economic conditions continued to restrain private investments during the period, resulting in major investments originating predominantly from the public sector, largely focused on defence and infrastructure. However, the second half of 2025 showed a clear improvement in preparatory efforts and new contract acquisitions. In the real estate development segment, improving market conditions enabled the group to start construction on more than three times as many new apartments in 2025 compared to 2024, supported by an increase in the pace of pre-sales agreements. Total apartment sales during the year reached 358 units with a sales value of 67.8 million euros. 

The group's secured order book as at 31 December 2025 rose to 466.9 million euros from 340.6 million euros at the end of 2024, providing a stable workload for future periods. Total assets stood at 415.1 million euros compared to 447.1 million euros previously. Total equity increased to 260.6 million euros from 254.3 million euros, with the equity ratio strengthening to 62.8 percent from 56.9 percent at the end of the prior year. Cash, cash equivalents and short-term deposits totalled 59.4 million euros at year-end, down from 101.9 million euros at the end of 2024, largely reflecting operational capital changes and dividend distributions. Reflecting the company's strong financial position and long-term objectives, the Management Board has proposed a dividend payment of 1.25 euros per share, totalling 22.1 million euros, representing a 55 percent dividend rate for the 2025 financial year.
