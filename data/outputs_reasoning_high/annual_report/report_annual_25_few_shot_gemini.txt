WindowMaster International A/S reported its financial results for the 2025 financial year, reflecting a decline in revenue and profitability compared with the previous year amid a challenging business environment. Total revenue for 2025 decreased by 8% to DKK 268.6 million from DKK 294.5 million in 2024. This contraction was primarily driven by a slow start to the year, reduced sales to a key customer in the Nordic region, lower revenue in Climatic, and slower growth in North America. Despite the top-line decline, the company successfully improved its gross profit margin to 48.8% from 46.9% in the prior year, resulting in a gross profit of DKK 131.1 million compared with DKK 137.9 million in 2024. The improved gross margin was attributed to a favorable business mix featuring lower revenue from key accounts and a higher proportion of direct project revenue under the Accelerate Core strategy.

Operating profitability experienced a more pronounced decrease during the period. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization dropped to DKK 28.0 million in 2025 from DKK 37.3 million in 2024, lowering the EBITDA margin to 10.4% from 12.7%. This margin reduction was caused by maintained fixed cost levels required to support future growth alongside extraordinary costs of DKK 2.5 million incurred due to a ransomware attack affecting the company's IT infrastructure in May and June of 2025. Profit before tax declined to DKK 2.9 million compared with DKK 12.2 million in the previous year. Profit after tax fell sharply to DKK 0.9 million down from DKK 10.6 million in 2024, affected by a high effective tax rate resulting from the non-utilization of certain tax assets related to tax loss carryforwards.

Cash flow from operating activities remained relatively stable at DKK 28.5 million compared with DKK 29.4 million in 2024, supported by improvements in net working capital, particularly regarding inventories and work in progress. Cash flow from investing activities amounted to DKK -9.4 million, reflecting ongoing investments in digitalization, software development, process automation, and IT network security upgrades. Net interest-bearing debt increased to DKK 38.7 million at the end of 2025 from DKK 36.7 million at the end of 2024, resulting in a financial gearing ratio of 1.7 measured as net interest-bearing debt to EBITDA, compared with 1.2 in the previous year. Total equity decreased to DKK 32.5 million at year-end, representing a net cash equity ratio of 23.5%. Market conditions showed improvement during the second half of 2025 as interest rates stabilized, boosting project execution in the non-residential segment, although residential demand remained subdued due to low consumer confidence. Looking ahead to 2026, management expects organic revenue growth between 7% and 14%, reaching DKK 290 to 310 million, with EBITDA anticipated to increase to DKK 45 to 55 million.
