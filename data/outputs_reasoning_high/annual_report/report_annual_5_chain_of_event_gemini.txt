Eleving Group S.A. reported its strongest financial performance to date for the twelve-month period ended 31 December 2025, driven by robust portfolio growth, successful geographical expansion, and strategic product diversification. Total revenue for 2025 reached EUR 250.0 million, representing a 15.5 percent increase compared to EUR 216.6 million reported in 2024. The revenue growth was supported by well-balanced contributions across core business lines, including consumer lending, flexible vehicle financing, traditional vehicle financing, and newly introduced device financing products. Adjusted EBITDA reached a record high of EUR 101.9 million, reflecting a 13.5 percent increase year-on-year compared to EUR 89.8 million in 2024. The Group achieved its all-time best twelve-month loan issuances of EUR 458.0 million, marking a 24.3 percent increase over the EUR 368.6 million issued in the previous year. This growth was propelled by strong customer demand, resulting in total loan applications surging by 50.0 percent to 2.7 million. Consequently, the net loan portfolio expanded by 20.2 percent to EUR 446.3 million as of 31 December 2025, up from EUR 371.2 million at the end of 2024. Profit from continuing operations slightly increased to EUR 29.2 million in 2025 from EUR 28.8 million in 2024, while net profit before foreign exchange results and discontinued operations rose by 25.6 percent to EUR 40.8 million compared to EUR 32.5 million in 2024. Total net profit for the period stood at EUR 29.2 million, compared to EUR 29.6 million in the prior year which included contributions from discontinued operations. The financial results were significantly influenced by strategic operational developments, including the successful expansion of installment loan offerings in European markets such as Latvia, Estonia, Romania, and Armenia, which generated EUR 14.7 million in issuances. Furthermore, the Group expanded its footprint in Africa by launching smartphone financing in Kenya and Uganda, scaling that specific portfolio to EUR 13.5 million by year-end, and entering Tanzania with motorcycle financing. Capital management was a defining feature of 2025, highlighted by the first anniversary of the Group’s IPO and two major corporate bond transactions. In March 2025, the Group completed a EUR 40 million bond tap, followed by a landmark refinancing in October 2025, where it successfully issued EUR 275 million in new 5-year senior secured bonds at an annual interest rate of 9.5 percent to refinance existing notes maturing in 2026. The Group's total assets grew to EUR 577.7 million as of 31 December 2025, compared to EUR 476.3 million at the end of 2024, while total borrowings principal increased to EUR 441.6 million. Fulfilling its IPO commitments, the Group distributed EUR 19.65 million in dividends to shareholders during 2025. Key capital adequacy metrics included a capitalization ratio of 23.7 percent, an interest coverage ratio of 2.3, and a net leverage of 3.8, reflecting a strong funding base and solid operational foundations for continued growth in 2026.
