During the financial year ended June 30, 2025, AS SAF Tehnika and its subsidiaries operated as a developer, manufacturer, and distributor of digital microwave transmission equipment, microwave spectrum analyzers, and wireless sensor network solutions. The Group closed the reporting year with a net loss of 643 thousand euros, representing a notable improvement compared to the net loss of 2.37 million euros recorded in the previous financial year. The parent company reported a net loss of 552 thousand euros, compared to a net loss of 1.34 million euros previously. Basic and diluted loss per share for the Group stood at 0.216 euros, down from 0.798 euros in the prior year.

The Group’s net turnover for the 2024 and 2025 financial year was 25.99 million euros, decreasing by 1.1 million euros or 4% compared to 27.09 million euros in the previous financial year. The parent company’s net turnover was 21.92 million euros, down by 1.33 million euros or 6% from 23.26 million euros. International sales outside Latvia accounted for 97% of total Group turnover, reaching 25.24 million euros across 87 countries.

Geographically, the Americas region generated 14.17 million euros, representing 55% of total Group turnover, which reflects a decrease of 2.3 million euros or 14% compared to the prior year. This reduction was primarily influenced by large-scale project realization shifting to subsequent periods and temporary client cautiousness regarding investment decisions and tariff announcements. Conversely, turnover in the European region, including Latvia, increased by 1.4 million euros or 17% to 10.0 million euros, while revenues from Asia, the Middle East, and Africa decreased by 90 thousand euros or 5% to 1.8 million euros.

Global trade tensions between the United States and China continued to affect the electronics market, including a 15% import tariff on Aranet products imported into the United States. To mitigate potential supply chain and customs risks, the Group established a branch in Canada. Ongoing product development and research activities received 570 thousand euros in co-financing from the competence center LEO Pētījumu centrs SIA. Total investments in IT infrastructure, production and research equipment, software, licenses, product certification, and premises renovation amounted to 812 thousand euros during the reporting year.

Group inventories totaled 13.99 million euros at year-end. Although material availability has stabilized, the Group maintains higher material reserves to manage component discontinuation risks, while total slow-moving inventory decreased by 1.24 million euros compared to the previous year-end. The Group’s cash and cash equivalents increased by 2.6 million euros to reach 4.81 million euros at the end of the period. Furthermore, the parent company maintained an unused credit line facility with Luminor Bank AS totaling 4.95 million euros to ensure liquidity. The Board proposes covering the reporting year losses from previous years accumulated profits.
