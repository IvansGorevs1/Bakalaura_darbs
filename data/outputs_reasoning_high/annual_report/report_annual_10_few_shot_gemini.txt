InCoax Networks AB experienced a challenging financial year in 2025, characterized by lower sales volumes and an increased operating loss, alongside a strategic transition from a development-intensive phase toward commercial execution. Net sales for the full year 2025 amounted to SEK 48,055,007, representing a 35 percent decrease compared with SEK 74,497,775 in 2024. The revenue decline was primarily driven by timing shifts in customer orders and a more cautious market environment affecting operators' investment decisions. Despite the lower top-line figures, the gross margin improved to 62 percent from 57 percent in the previous year, with gross profit totaling SEK 29,919,750 compared to SEK 42,179,123 in 2024.

The company’s profitability weakened further during the period. The operating loss expanded to SEK 32,380,175, compared with an operating loss of SEK 19,378,406 in 2024. This earnings development reflected the lower revenue levels combined with continued high investments in product development and strategic market expansion initiatives. Capitalized development costs remained substantial at SEK 25,269,745, compared with SEK 25,649,493 in the prior year, focusing heavily on next-generation platform products such as the D2501 and D2508 units. Consequently, the net result after tax deepened to a loss of SEK 32,559,663, compared with a loss of SEK 19,150,134 in 2024, resulting in earnings per share of SEK -0.25 versus SEK -0.18 previously.

Cash flow from operating activities improved to negative SEK 20,011,617 from negative SEK 27,117,256 in 2024. Total cash flow including financing activities turned positive at SEK 6,516,117, compared with negative SEK 12,758,133 in the previous year, bolstered by financing activities including directed share issues, convertibles, and loan arrangements that provided gross proceeds of approximately SEK 53.5 million before issue costs. Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the period stood at SEK 8,748,046, up from SEK 2,231,929 at the end of 2024. The equity ratio stood at 71.8 percent at year-end, down from 80.8 percent in 2024, with total equity amounting to SEK 109,778,755.

To address financial pressures and support its commercial scale-up, InCoax initiated a structural cost reduction program designed to lower ongoing expenses by more than 40 percent, equating to annual savings of approximately SEK 25 million starting in 2026. The company reached a key milestone with the integration of its technology into Nokia platforms, paving the way for broader commercial rollouts in North America and Europe. However, the auditor’s report highlights material uncertainty regarding the company's ability to continue as a going concern, noting that short-term financing remains dependent on the successful exercise of outstanding warrant programs or securing alternative capital. Overall, 2025 marked a pivotal strategic turning point as the company shifted focus toward converting its technical position and partnerships into commercial success and achieving cash flow neutrality.
