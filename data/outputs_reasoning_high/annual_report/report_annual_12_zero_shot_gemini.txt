During the financial year 2025, Konsolidator A/S completed the first full year of executing its Resilient Growth strategy, achieving significant top-line growth, substantially reduced losses, and improved operational efficiency. Total revenue for 2025 increased by 25 percent to DKK 25.3 million, compared with DKK 20.3 million in 2024. This growth was driven by an increase in subscription fees to DKK 20.6 million from DKK 18.3 million, and a 134 percent surge in onboarding and consultancy fees to DKK 4.7 million from DKK 2.0 million. Contracted Annual Recurring Revenue rose by 15 percent year-over-year to reach DKK 24.4 million at year-end, exceeding previous guidance.

Profitability metrics showed substantial improvements across the period. The EBITDA loss for 2025 narrowed by 77 percent to DKK 2.0 million, compared with a loss of DKK 9.3 million in 2024, aided by positive EBITDA performance in the second half of the year amounting to DKK 0.5 million. The EBIT loss improved to DKK 5.1 million from DKK 12.1 million, and the net loss for the year decreased to DKK 6.6 million from DKK 14.9 million in 2024. Cash flow from operating activities also improved significantly to a negative DKK 1.0 million, compared with a negative DKK 9.2 million in the prior year, supported by proactive working capital management and annual subscription invoicing.

Key SaaS metrics reflected stronger operational unit economics following cost restructuring initiatives initiated in 2024. The Rule of 40 score improved by 57 percentage points to 17 percent at year-end 2025, up from negative 40 percent in 2024. The Customer Acquisition Cost payback period dropped by 50 percent to 23 months from 43 months, while the Customer Lifetime Value to Customer Acquisition Cost ratio strengthened from 2.7 to 4.1. However, the ARR churn rate increased to 12.6 percent from 10.5 percent, and net retention remained stable at 94 percent. Consolidated equity stood at a negative DKK 3.6 million at year-end, impacted by accumulated subsidiary losses, while the parent company maintained a positive equity of DKK 0.7 million, further supported by DKK 4.8 million raised through two private placements during the year.

Business developments during 2025 were characterized by the transition from a standalone consolidation tool to the broader Konsolidator Suite. This expansion included launching a Financial Data Management solution built on Microsoft Fabric and developing a Financial Planning and Analysis tool that secured early customer signings ahead of its official launch. Furthermore, the company scaled its partner-driven growth model, with 38 percent of new customers acquired via partners, notably through an expanded agreement with Exsitec AB. Subsidiary Konsolidator Iberia also expanded rapidly, becoming the third-largest market by customer count. Looking ahead to 2026, Konsolidator guides for Contracted Annual Recurring Revenue between DKK 27 and 29 million, revenue between DKK 27 and 29 million, and a positive EBITDA of DKK 1 to 2 million.
