During the financial year 2025, AAC Clyde Space reported net sales of SEK 295.3 million, representing a 16 percent decrease compared to SEK 352.9 million in 2024. The revenue decline occurred in the Products & Missions segment, where net sales fell to SEK 245.0 million from SEK 346.0 million and segment EBITDA decreased to SEK 17.0 million from SEK 52.0 million. In contrast, the Data & Services segment achieved strong growth, with net sales increasing by 80 percent to SEK 80.1 million from SEK 44.9 million, and segment EBITDA rising to SEK 27.8 million from SEK 21.1 million.

Total Group EBITDA reached SEK 17.5 million, marking the third consecutive year of positive EBITDA, though declining from SEK 46.7 million in 2024. Operating loss (EBIT) widened to SEK -40.0 million from SEK -4.0 million, and the net loss after tax deepened to SEK -46.6 million compared to SEK -5.7 million. Earnings per share declined to SEK -7.63 from SEK -1.00. These financial changes were directly influenced by several operational developments and delays described in the report. Technical discussions between an end customer and a subcontractor delayed milestone recognition, shifting approximately SEK 32 million in revenue into 2026. Additionally, political and procurement developments postponed the EPS-Sterna programme, commissioning of the Sedna satellites took longer than planned, and launch provider rescheduling delayed the launch of the first two VIREON Earth Observation satellites.

Operating cash flow worsened significantly to SEK -72.6 million from SEK +56.8 million in 2024, and cash and cash equivalents decreased to SEK 30.1 million at year-end from SEK 49.7 million. To support strategic investments in satellite constellations and data services, the company completed capital raises, including a directed share issue in December 2025 involving Bonnier Capital as part of an investment agreement of up to SEK 140 million, raising approximately SEK 50.3 million before transaction costs. Supported by these equity transactions, the equity-to-assets ratio improved to 80 percent from 71 percent. The order backlog at year-end stood at SEK 352.0 million, down from SEK 641.0 million in the previous year.

Subsequent events after the close of the reporting period strengthened the company's financial outlook, highlighted by the EUMETSAT approval of the full EPS-Sterna weather constellation and a major contract awarded in March 2026 by OHB Sweden valued at EUR 76.3 million, approximately SEK 820 million, for the delivery of 20 microwave radiometers.
