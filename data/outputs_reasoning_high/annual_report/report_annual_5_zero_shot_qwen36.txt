Eleving Group S.A. delivered its strongest financial performance to date for the year ended 31 December 2025, driven by robust portfolio expansion, strategic product diversification, and successful capital market activities. The company reported record revenue of EUR 250.0 million, representing a 15.5 percent increase compared to 2024. Adjusted EBITDA reached an all-time high of EUR 101.9 million, up 13.5 percent from the prior year, while the net loan portfolio grew by 20.2 percent to EUR 446.3 million. Profitability metrics reflected steady operational execution, with total net profit stabilizing at EUR 29.2 million and net profit before foreign exchange impacts rising by 25.6 percent to EUR 40.8 million. Loan issuance volumes also hit a record EUR 458.0 million, marking a 24.3 percent year-on-year increase fueled by heightened customer demand and new product launches.

Revenue composition shifted notably during the reporting period. Consumer lending products contributed EUR 114.8 million, growing 18.0 percent, while flexible vehicle financing surged by 25.9 percent to EUR 60.3 million. Traditional vehicle financing declined by 6.0 percent to EUR 67.0 million as the Group strategically pivoted toward installment loans in several European markets. Device financing, introduced in 2025, generated EUR 7.9 million in revenue. Geographically, operations in Latvia, Romania, and Kenya led growth, supported by the launch of smartphone financing in Kenya and Uganda, which quickly accumulated a EUR 13.5 million portfolio, and the successful rollout of installment loans in Eastern Europe. The Group expanded its geographic footprint by entering Tanzania with motorcycle financing.

Capital management and liquidity were characterized by decisive actions and improved market confidence. Eleving Group executed two major bond transactions, including a EUR 275 million refinancing and new issuance in October, which represented the largest bond deal in its history. The proceeds, combined with diversified local currency funding initiatives, strengthened the balance sheet and reduced foreign exchange exposure. The Group distributed EUR 19.65 million in dividends, equivalent to a 10 percent cash yield based on the IPO price. Credit metrics adjusted accordingly, with the capitalization ratio declining to 23.7 percent from 29.3 percent due to rapid portfolio scaling, while net leverage rose to 3.8 times from 3.3 times. Disciplined risk controls, conservative underwriting standards, and comprehensive currency hedging maintained asset quality, allowing Fitch Ratings to upgrade the Group outlook to positive while affirming its B credit rating.

Overall, Eleving Group demonstrated resilient and balanced growth throughout 2025. The combination of double-digit top-line expansion, margin stability, successful debt capital market participation, and strategic market entries positioned the company for sustained profitability. Management emphasized continued focus on operational efficiency, technological integration such as AI-driven call center solutions, and responsible lending practices to navigate macroeconomic conditions and support long-term value creation across its presence in seventeen markets.
