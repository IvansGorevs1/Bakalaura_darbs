During the 2024 financial year, AS LHV Group delivered record business volumes and operating profit, driven by robust balance sheet expansion, sustained credit quality, and disciplined operational execution. Total consolidated assets grew 23 percent year-over-year to reach EUR 8.7 billion. The net loan portfolio expanded by EUR 990 million, or 28 percent, to EUR 4.6 billion, while customer deposits increased 21 percent to EUR 6.9 billion. Consolidated net income rose 11 percent to EUR 338.3 million, comprising net interest income of EUR 273.3 million (up 8 percent) and net fee and commission income of EUR 60.3 million (up 24 percent). Operating expenses amounted to EUR 146.9 million, reflecting a 14 percent increase primarily linked to a larger workforce and higher personnel costs. Consequently, pre-tax profit reached EUR 175.1 million, yielding a net profit of EUR 150.3 million, a 7 percent year-over-year increase. Return on equity stood at 24.5 percent, with a cost-to-income ratio of 43.4 percent and return on assets of 1.9 percent.

The Group’s strong financial performance was underpinned by a restrictive interest rate environment that temporarily boosted net interest margins, alongside aggressive lending and deposit acquisition strategies across all customer segments. The number of Estonian bank customers increased by 38,000, or 9 percent, with actively using digital payment users rising 12 percent. In the United Kingdom, LHV Bank Ltd capitalized on its full-year licensed operations, achieving exceptional growth with the SME and retail loan portfolio expanding 338 percent to EUR 348 million and platform deposits surging 388 percent to EUR 416 million. Late in the year, the UK subsidiary launched its retail banking offering, including mobile application services. The Group also acquired a small but highly profitable loan portfolio from AS TBB Pank and successfully issued EUR 20 million of Tier 2 subordinated bonds in November, following the timely redemption of EUR 20 million in Additional Tier 1 instruments earlier in the year.

Macroeconomic conditions presented mixed signals, with Estonian GDP contracting slightly throughout 2024 before stabilization cues emerged heading into 2025. The European Central Bank responded to moderating inflation by implementing four consecutive 25-basis-point rate cuts during the second half of the year, lowering the deposit facility rate to 3 percent by December. This shifting monetary policy prompted LHV to revise its internal financial plan in September, raising profitability targets by 25 percent while acknowledging that declining rates will likely compress net interest margins in the near term. Despite this headwind, the Group maintained strict cost controls, executed targeted organizational restructuring, and preserved an excellent asset quality profile with historically low non-performing loan ratios. All regulatory capital, MREL, and liquidity requirements were comfortably met, evidenced by a Common Equity Tier 1 ratio of 16.89 percent and a liquidity coverage ratio of 187.5 percent. Entering 2025, LHV Group remains strategically positioned for continued expansion, planning to offset transitional margin pressure through accelerated loan growth, deeper cross-subsidiary integration, and ongoing technological efficiency improvements.
