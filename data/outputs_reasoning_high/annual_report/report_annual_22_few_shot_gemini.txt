AS Tallinna Vesi reported positive financial performance for the 2025 financial year, characterized by business expansion, higher revenue, and improved operational profitability. The group generated total sales of 72.48 million euros, representing an increase of 11.4% or 7.40 million euros compared to the previous year. This revenue growth was primarily supported by a 6.9% increase in water services sales, which reached 62.31 million euros, alongside a 62.0% surge in construction services sales to 8.46 million euros. Sales to private customers within the main service area rose by 18.3% to 31.84 million euros, driven by higher consumption and tariff adjustments. Conversely, sales to business customers decreased by 2.7% to 16.76 million euros due to statutory water price harmonisation requirements. Sales to customers outside the main service area grew by 13.8% to 7.71 million euros.

The group’s cost of goods and services sold increased by 12.0% to 42.96 million euros. Total direct production costs remained stable at 9.90 million euros, as lower electricity expenses resulting from self-generation at the newly commissioned combined heat and power plant offset rising chemical costs and pollution taxes. Other costs of goods sold rose by 16.0% to 33.05 million euros, driven by higher staff costs and increased construction service expenses. Despite rising operational costs, gross profit reached 29.52 million euros, up 10.5% from 2024. Operating profit before depreciation and amortisation increased by 12.5% to 30.95 million euros, and operating profit grew by 11.8% to 21.22 million euros. Profit before taxes reached 17.43 million euros, an 18.0% improvement, supported by lower net financial expenses of 3.78 million euros due to declining Euribor rates. Net profit for the period rose by 7.2% to 14.24 million euros, with growth moderated by a higher income tax rate on dividend distributions.

During 2025, the company continued extensive capital investments to ensure service continuity and sustainability, investing 55.74 million euros in fixed assets compared to 49.53 million euros in 2024. Key operational developments included expanding smart water meter deployment, reconstructing network pipes, and operating the combined heat and power plant, which covered the treatment plant's total heat demand and over half of its electricity needs. To finance these activities, outstanding loans increased to 160 million euros at year-end, raising the total debt-to-assets ratio to 66.02% from 61.71%. The provision for potential third-party claims concerning historical tariff disputes remained unchanged at 6.02 million euros against total claims of 14.67 million euros. The company's share price closed the year at 11.00 euros, marking a 7.84% increase from the start of the period. Overall, the financial year demonstrated robust revenue growth, successful cost optimization, and steady operational advancement.
