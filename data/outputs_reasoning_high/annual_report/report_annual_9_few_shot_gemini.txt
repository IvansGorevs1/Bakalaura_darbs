Impero A/S reported its financial results for the full year 2025, demonstrating solid top-line growth and improved profitability despite initial macroeconomic headwinds. Recognized revenue for the period increased by 19% to 42.0 million Danish kroner, up from 35.4 million Danish kroner in 2024. Annual recurring revenue reached 46.2 million Danish kroner at the end of 2025, corresponding to a 19% year-on-year growth rate and landing at the upper end of the company's revised guidance range. Net new annual recurring revenue totaled 7.5 million Danish kroner for the full year.

The company's SaaS metrics remained robust, highlighted by an exceptionally low churn rate of 1% and a net revenue retention rate of 106%. Average annual recurring revenue per account rose by 8% to a record 228 thousand Danish kroner, driven by a successful land-and-expand strategy and a gross uplift of 12%. Total customers surpassed the 200 milestone following the addition of 26 new customers during the year, with growth primarily led by the Danish and DACH core markets.

Profitability showed significant improvement compared with the previous year. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization improved to a negative 3.1 million Danish kroner from a negative 8.7 million Danish kroner in 2024, performing at the better end of the revised guidance range. Operating loss narrowed to 6.8 million Danish kroner from 11.6 million Danish kroner, while the net loss for the period stood at 6.8 million Danish kroner compared with 11.4 million Danish kroner in the prior year. This earnings improvement was supported by the revenue growth, a 16% reduction in the cost of sales to 1.0 million Danish kroner, and higher capitalized own work of 5.2 million Danish kroner, which helped offset a 6% increase in both staff costs and other external expenses.

The company's capital position was strengthened by a directed issue in December 2025 that raised 15.0 million Danish kroner. Consequently, equity increased to 15.4 million Danish kroner at year-end, up from 6.2 million Danish kroner in 2024. Cash and cash equivalents rose to 18.9 million Danish kroner, while cash flow from ordinary operating activities improved to a negative 1.3 million Danish kroner from a negative 4.3 million Danish kroner.

Looking ahead to 2026, Impero expects annual recurring revenue to reach between 53.0 million and 57.0 million Danish kroner, representing growth of approximately 15% to 23%. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization for 2026 are projected to be between a negative 2.0 million and a positive 1.0 million Danish kroner. Backed by its strengthened capital position, the board of directors has elected to accelerate commercial investments and has deferred its ambition of achieving positive cash flow on a recurring basis before the end of 2026 to prioritize long-term shareholder value and higher future growth.
