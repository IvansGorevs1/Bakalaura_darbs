During the financial year ended 31 December 2025, AS Tallink Grupp and its subsidiaries recorded consolidated revenue of EUR 765.3 million, representing a 2.6% decrease compared to EUR 785.8 million in 2024. Revenue from route operations, which form the core of the Group’s business, fell by EUR 13.7 million to EUR 625.2 million. The Group’s operating results showed a decline year-on-year, with EBITDA dropping by 25.7% to EUR 130.1 million compared to EUR 175.2 million in 2024. Net profit for the period stood at EUR 17.3 million, down 57.1% from EUR 40.3 million in the previous financial year. Basic and diluted earnings per share were EUR 0.023, compared to EUR 0.054 in 2024. Gross profit decreased to EUR 143.2 million from EUR 153.6 million. Operational metrics indicated that the Group carried 5.53 million passengers in 2025, a 0.9% decrease from 5.58 million in 2024. Transported cargo units dropped by 19.2% to 245,004 units, while passenger vehicles decreased by 2.2% to 760,473 units. 

The financial performance and demand for travel were adversely affected by low consumer and business confidence, ongoing economic challenges in the Group’s core markets of Finland, Estonia, and Sweden, and heightened global geopolitical tensions. These external conditions resulted in subdued leisure spending and lower cargo volumes. Conversely, profitability was positively supported by a reduction in depreciation and amortisation expenses, which fell by EUR 18.0 million to EUR 79.8 million due to the alignment of the estimated useful lives of cruise and passenger vessels, excluding shuttle vessels, to 45 years, alongside the sale of three vessels during the year. Net finance costs also declined by EUR 6.8 million to EUR 21.4 million, benefiting from a decreased outstanding loan balance and lower interest rates. 

Total interest-bearing liabilities reduced by 19.9% to EUR 445.9 million at the end of 2025, down from EUR 556.4 million at the end of 2024. Net debt decreased to EUR 432.4 million from EUR 537.7 million, while the net debt to EBITDA ratio stood at 3.3, compared to 3.1 in the previous year. The Group’s cash position was influenced by the payment of dividends totaling EUR 44.6 million, equivalent to EUR 0.06 per share, and related income tax expenses of EUR 11.3 million. Total loan repayments and related interest payments during the year amounted to EUR 113.5 million. Capital expenditures totalled EUR 33.0 million, directed toward maintenance and IT system upgrades. As at 31 December 2025, the Group owned 11 vessels, having completed the sales of the passenger vessel Star I, the cargo vessel Regal Star, and the cargo vessel Sailor during the period. The Management Board will propose to the General Meeting of Shareholders the payment of a dividend of EUR 0.06 per share in 2026.
