Impero A/S delivered resilient financial performance throughout 2025, marked by solid revenue expansion and markedly improved profitability metrics despite initial macroeconomic headwinds. Recognized revenue grew by 19% to DKK 42.0 million, driven primarily by sustained demand across core markets in Denmark and the DACH region. The company’s annual recurring revenue increased by 19% year-on-year to DKK 46.2 million, outperforming early forecasts and settling at the upper end of the revised guidance range. This growth was supported by a record average revenue per account of DKK 228 thousand and a gross uplift of 12% within the existing customer base. Customer retention remained exceptionally strong, with a churn rate of just 1% and a net revenue retention rate of 106%.

Operating efficiency improved significantly as the company balanced its investment pace. EBITDA narrowed to a loss of DKK 3.1 million compared to DKK 8.7 million in the prior year, reflecting better cost control and higher capitalized development expenditures of DKK 5.2 million. Operating profit similarly improved to DKK 6.8 million from a loss, benefiting from a 19% revenue increase that outpaced a 6% rise in staff and other external expenses, the latter largely tied to targeted lead-generation campaigns. Cash burn relative to new ARR generation stabilized at an efficient 0.9 multiplier by year-end, while operating cash flow improved to minus DKK 1.3 million from minus DKK 4.3 million.

Strategically, the Board deferred its earlier objective of achieving recurring positive cash flow before the end of 2026 to allocate additional capital toward commercial expansion and product development. This shift was enabled by a DKK 15 million private placement in December 2025, which strengthened equity to DKK 15.4 million and secured a cash balance of DKK 18.9 million without interest-bearing debt. Commercially, the customer base surpassed 200 clients, with concentration among the top ten accounts reduced to 20%. Growth in Denmark and the DACH region contributed 22% and 26% net ARR increases respectively, offsetting a 6% decline in other markets stemming from contractual adjustments in the UK. Product enhancements included the launch of a Control Testing module and the initial deployment of AI-driven compliance features, alongside preparations for an upcoming Entity Management capability. Looking ahead, Impero expects ARR to reach between DKK 53 million and DKK 57 million in 2026, with EBITDA projected to range from minus DKK 2 million to plus DKK 1 million as it scales its commercial footprint. Overall, the period demonstrated disciplined execution, strengthening market positioning while prioritizing sustainable long-term value creation over near-term profitability targets.
