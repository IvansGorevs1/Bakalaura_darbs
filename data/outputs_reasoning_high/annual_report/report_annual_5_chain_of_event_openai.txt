Eleving Group delivered record revenue of EUR 250.0 million in 2025, up 15.4% from EUR 216.6 million in 2024. Adjusted EBITDA rose 13.5% to EUR 101.9 million, while net profit from continuing operations increased modestly to EUR 29.2 million from EUR 28.8 million. Total net profit was EUR 29.2 million, compared with EUR 29.6 million in 2024, when EUR 0.8 million came from discontinued operations. Net profit before foreign exchange and discontinued operations increased 25.6% to EUR 40.8 million. The net foreign exchange gain declined to EUR 3.7 million from EUR 11.7 million, mainly reflecting currency fluctuations in Romania and Africa.

Growth was supported by a 20.2% increase in the net portfolio to EUR 446.3 million and record loan issuances of EUR 458.0 million, up 24.3% year on year. Vehicle and device finance issuances increased 34.0%, while consumer finance issuances grew 15.7%. Consumer lending generated EUR 114.8 million of revenue, up 18.0%; flexible vehicle finance generated EUR 60.3 million, up 25.9%; traditional vehicle finance contributed EUR 67.0 million, down 6.0%; and device financing contributed EUR 7.9 million following its launch in 2025. The Group attributed demand growth to expanded products, customer retention and repeat borrowing, digital channels, and partner networks. Loan applications increased 50.0% to 2.7 million.

The Group introduced installment loans in Latvia, Estonia, Romania, and Armenia, generating EUR 14.7 million of issuances, and launched smartphone financing in Kenya and Uganda. By year-end, smartphone financing had reached a EUR 13.5 million portfolio and 260,000 customers. Tanzania became the Group’s 17th market through motorcycle financing. The Group also piloted AI voice agents at call centers, with capacity of up to 20,000 calls per day, and reported a reduced cost-to-income ratio.

Capital-market activity materially strengthened funding capacity. A EUR 40 million bond tap was completed in March, followed in October by a EUR 275 million five-year senior secured bond issuance that refinanced EUR 150 million of bonds maturing in 2026. Additional local-currency funding was raised, particularly in Kenya. Fitch affirmed the Group’s B rating and upgraded its outlook from stable to positive. Dividends paid to parent-company shareholders totaled EUR 19.65 million, while total dividends, including non-controlling interests, were EUR 24.88 million.

Total assets increased to EUR 577.7 million from EUR 476.3 million, while total equity declined to EUR 105.8 million from EUR 108.1 million after dividends and a EUR 5.0 million other comprehensive loss, primarily from currency translation. The capitalization ratio decreased to 23.7% from 29.3%, and net leverage increased to 3.8 from 3.3. Operating cash flow was negative EUR 52.0 million as portfolio growth required funding, while year-end cash increased to EUR 39.1 million. The auditor issued an unqualified opinion on the consolidated financial statements.
