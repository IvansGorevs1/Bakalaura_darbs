For the fiscal year ending June 30, 2025, A/S SAF Tehnika reported consolidated net revenue of EUR 25.99 million, representing a 4 percent decrease from EUR 27.09 million in the prior year. The parent company alone recorded EUR 21.92 million, down 6 percent. Despite the revenue decline, the group posted a net loss of EUR 643 thousand, a substantial improvement compared to the EUR 2.37 million loss in the previous year. On a standalone basis, the parent company recorded a net loss of EUR 552 thousand versus EUR 1.34 million previously. The reduction in losses was primarily driven by lower cost of goods sold and controlled operating expenditures, which together brought the operating loss down to EUR 1.29 million from EUR 3.22 million a year earlier. Total gross profit actually increased to EUR 9.50 million from EUR 7.53 million due to a sharper contraction in production costs than in sales.

Geographically, the Americas segment accounted for 55 percent of total revenue, generating EUR 14.17 million, a 14 percent decline attributed to client caution following investment reassessments and tariff policy announcements. However, management noted signs of market stabilization and a gradual restoration of activity in North America. European operations, including Latvia, grew by 17 percent to EUR 10.0 million, while the Asia, Middle East, and Africa region saw a slight 5 percent decrease to EUR 1.8 million. Global geopolitical dynamics, including ongoing trade tensions between the United States and China, have not materially disrupted sales volumes. Import duties on microwave equipment into the US remain unchanged, though the Aranet product line faces a 15 percent tariff. To mitigate supply chain and customs risks, the group established a Canadian subsidiary during the reporting period.

Operational adjustments continued throughout the year, with inventory levels stabilizing after multi-year stockpiling aimed at securing component availability. Slow-moving inventory decreased by EUR 1.24 million compared to the prior year-end. Cash reserves strengthened significantly, rising by EUR 2.6 million to EUR 4.81 million, supported by positive operating cash flow and an unused EUR 4.95 million credit facility with Luminor Bank. The company invested EUR 812 thousand in research and development, IT infrastructure, equipment, and facility upgrades, while also securing EUR 570 thousand in project co-financing. Management characterized the current net loss as temporary and maintains a cautious but positive outlook, emphasizing continued focus on niche wireless data transmission technologies and IoT environmental monitoring solutions without providing forward-looking commercial projections.
