During the financial year 2025, Talkpool successfully transitioned from its multi-year restructuring and cost-minimization phase into a period of accelerating organic growth. The company reported net sales of EUR 17,756 thousand, representing a 12.5 percent increase compared to EUR 15,782 thousand in 2024, marking the first year of double-digit revenue growth since the consolidation phase began. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization reached EUR 1,828 thousand, up from EUR 1,702 thousand in the previous year, yielding an EBITDA margin of 10.3 percent compared to 10.8 percent in 2024. Operating profit stood at EUR 1,620 thousand with an EBIT margin of 9.1 percent, compared to EUR 1,527 thousand and 9.7 percent in 2024. Net earnings after tax experienced a substantial increase of 42 percent, rising to EUR 862 thousand from EUR 605 thousand, resulting in a higher net earnings margin of 4.8 percent compared to 3.8 percent in the prior year. The financial position of the group strengthened significantly, with net cash reaching a positive position of EUR 0.3 million by the end of 2025, improving by EUR 6.0 million from a negative EUR 5.7 million in 2021. Equity also improved markedly to EUR 2.1 million from negative EUR 2.5 million in 2021. These financial improvements were accompanied by company auditors no longer expressing uncertainty regarding the ability to continue as a going concern. The positive financial performance was achieved despite currency headwinds during the first half of 2025, which saw the US dollar weaken by 11 percent and Pakistani Rupees by 14 percent, reducing EUR-reported revenues. Regionally, the Americas remained Talkpool's largest revenue market, demonstrating strong business momentum despite the US currency weakness. In Pakistan, operations achieved record local currency revenue and successfully renegotiated commercial terms with major customers, resulting in improved gross profitability. Meanwhile, operations in Germany provided a stable contribution despite a temporary flattening of planning assignment volumes caused by a shift in Deutsche Telekom rollout priorities. Strategic milestones during the year included launching the Vision 2030 strategic growth plan in April, establishing a new Swedish affiliated company for defense sector solutions, and advancing trials of AI-powered technology solutions. Furthermore, an independent valuation by BDO assessed the market value of Talkpool's German subsidiary at approximately EUR 20.5 million. Overall, Talkpool advanced its strategic shift toward technology-enabled remote services, laying a solid operational foundation for future growth while improving profitability, strengthening its balance sheet, and reducing net debt.
