JLT Mobile Computers reported a recovery in sales and order intake in 2025, although the Group remained loss-making. Net sales increased 11.1% to SEK 131.5 million from SEK 118.4 million in 2024. Order intake rose 37.5% to SEK 141.6 million, and the year-end order backlog increased to SEK 24.1 million from SEK 13.9 million, including SEK 15.0 million scheduled for delivery in the first quarter of 2026. A significant contributor to order intake was a SEK 22 million order from an American food producer.

Gross profit declined to SEK 50.4 million from SEK 54.2 million, and the gross margin fell to 38.3% from 45.8%. The report attributes the margin pressure to currency effects: the US dollar weakened by almost 20% against the Swedish krona, while 74% of sales were invoiced in dollars. In addition, uncertainty surrounding US tariffs affected customer demand and increased administrative work. Swedish-made computers remained exempt from US export duties, but certain non-European components, accessories and spare parts were subject to customs duties.

Cost reductions and lower amortization significantly improved the bottom line. Operating expenses fell to SEK 52.4 million from SEK 57.3 million, with overhead and research and development costs decreasing by SEK 6.3 million. The company coordinated software and product development and discontinued JLT Software Solutions AB. Amortization and impairment declined to SEK 1.3 million from SEK 9.0 million, following substantial development-cost write-downs in 2024. EBITDA improved to negative SEK 1.7 million from negative SEK 2.1 million, while the operating loss narrowed to SEK 1.9 million from SEK 9.7 million. Profit before tax was negative SEK 1.8 million, compared with negative SEK 9.6 million, and the net loss decreased to SEK 2.5 million from SEK 7.7 million.

Market conditions remained weak, particularly in warehousing and logistics, as well as transportation and manufacturing. Demand was more positive in ports and mining, supporting the company’s strategy of broadening its market exposure. JLT launched the JLT6015, described as the industry’s first rugged vehicle-mounted computer with a 15-inch Full HD display, and introduced an AI- and machine-learning-based screen-blanking safety solution. The company also validated its VERSO computers for port operations with Navis.

Cash flow was reported at positive SEK 3.7 million, while cash and cash equivalents increased to SEK 16.9 million from SEK 13.3 million. Inventory reduction was a key factor, with inventories falling by SEK 12.7 million to SEK 25.5 million. Equity declined to SEK 41.8 million from SEK 47.6 million, while solidity improved to 59% from 54%. No dividend is proposed for 2025. Overall, JLT delivered stronger commercial performance and substantially reduced its losses, but currency pressure, weak core-market demand and negative operating margins continued to weigh on results.
