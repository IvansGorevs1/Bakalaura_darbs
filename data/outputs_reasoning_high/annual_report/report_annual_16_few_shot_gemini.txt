Nordic Fibreboard AS reported consolidated net sales of 7.33 million euros for the 2025 financial year, representing a 3.4% decrease compared to 7.59 million euros in 2024. The revenue decline was primarily driven by lower sales in non-European Union regions such as Africa and Asia, whereas sales within the European Union remained relatively stable, representing 92% of total turnover. Real estate management revenue dropped significantly to 3 thousand euros from 25 thousand euros following prior rental agreement terminations. 

The company's profitability weakened during the reporting period. Consolidated EBITDA was negative 420 thousand euros, down from negative 25 thousand euros in 2024, resulting in a negative EBITDA margin of 6%. This performance was heavily impacted by underutilized production capacity and a planned factory shutdown in December to transition to a new gas boiler house, during which personnel and maintenance costs continued to accrue. Consequently, the operating loss widened to 940 thousand euros compared to 547 thousand euros in the previous year. With net financial costs remaining substantial, the consolidated net loss for 2025 reached 1.15 million euros, compared to a net loss of 786 thousand euros in 2024. Basic and diluted loss per share stood at 0.14 euros, compared to 0.17 euros in the prior year. 

Total assets as of December 31, 2025, increased to 9.32 million euros from 8.25 million euros, while total liabilities rose to 4.86 million euros from 4.64 million euros. Total borrowings decreased to 3.17 million euros from 3.72 million euros. The Group recorded a negative operating cash flow of 503 thousand euros. Investing cash outflows totaled 989 thousand euros, mainly comprising 1.37 million euros invested into production assets and 161 thousand euros into real estate, offset by 539 thousand euros from the sale of shares in AS Trigon Property Development. Financing activities generated positive cash flow of 1.44 million euros, bolstered by a share capital increase completed in October 2025 that raised 2.0 million euros through the issuance of 4 million new shares, expanding share capital to 850 thousand euros and recording 1.6 million euros in share premium. 

Corporate developments during the year included the merger of subsidiary Pärnu Riverside Development OÜ into the parent company to simplify group structure and consolidate management of the Admirali Quarter real estate project. Design work and building permits for the project were finalized during 2025. Furthermore, after the balance sheet date on January 8, 2026, the company entered into a preliminary agreement to sell the ten properties comprising the Admirali Quarter project, with final execution scheduled no later than June 30, 2026. Despite ending the year with negative net working capital of 2.7 million euros, management anticipates that pending property sales and stable underlying fibreboard demand will support future financial stability.
