OODA AI AB reported a substantial commercial expansion in 2025, with Group revenue rising to SEK 32,058 thousand from SEK 1,061 thousand in 2024. Net sales increased to SEK 31,880 thousand from SEK 1,056 thousand and consisted entirely of consulting services. The company attributed the increase to invoicing several major transactions in line with its previously communicated plan. Sales were generated primarily outside Sweden, including SEK 26,730 thousand in Switzerland, SEK 2,354 thousand elsewhere in the EU and SEK 2,796 thousand in other countries outside the EU.

Operational performance improved significantly. Operating loss narrowed to SEK 29,270 thousand from SEK 51,781 thousand, while EBITDA improved to SEK 2,957 thousand from a negative SEK 28,229 thousand. EBITDA was affected by approximately SEK 10,238 thousand in non-recurring costs related to liquidating older company structures. Excluding these costs, adjusted EBITDA was approximately SEK 13,195 thousand, corresponding to an adjusted EBITDA margin of approximately 32% of revenue. Personnel costs increased to SEK 9,210 thousand from SEK 7,432 thousand, while the Group maintained a low-cost structure and ended the year with four employees.

Despite the improved operating result, the Group reported a net loss of SEK 125,799 thousand, compared with SEK 83,355 thousand in 2024. Financial items deteriorated to negative SEK 95,853 thousand from negative SEK 29,900 thousand, mainly because of impairments and results from associated companies and other financial holdings. Financial assets fell to SEK 8,387 thousand from SEK 96,036 thousand following impairments, divestments and revaluations; several holdings were written down to zero. Earnings per share were negative SEK 35.29, compared with negative SEK 29.00.

The company advanced its commercialization strategy during the year. The Win.Rocks AG relationship reached approximately SEK 21 million in total contract value, followed by a new agreement worth at least SEK 15 million over 24 months in December. OODA AI also signed an AI document-interpretation agreement with Sergel Kredittjänster and communicated an HMM Deutschland agreement estimated at approximately SEK 10 million in 2025 and SEK 30 million in 2026. The platform expanded to more than 150 AI models, while the decentralized AI initiative progressed from concept to an MVP and operational test network.

Operating cash flow improved to negative SEK 16,662 thousand from negative SEK 29,680 thousand, and was positive by SEK 5,098 thousand in the second half. However, cash at year-end declined to SEK 3,335 thousand, and equity fell to SEK 23,859 thousand from SEK 119,371 thousand, reflecting the valuation effects. Capital raisings contributed SEK 44,302 thousand, and the Board considers continued commercialization, cost control and access to additional capital important for ongoing operations.
