During the year ended 31 December 2025, AS Tallink Grupp’s performance weakened compared with 2024. Revenue decreased 2.6% to EUR 765.3 million from EUR 785.8 million, while revenue from core route operations fell by EUR 13.7 million to EUR 625.2 million. Gross profit declined by EUR 10.3 million to EUR 143.2 million, and EBITDA fell by EUR 45.1 million to EUR 130.1 million, reducing the EBITDA margin from 22.3% to 17.0%. EBIT decreased to EUR 50.3 million from EUR 77.4 million, while net profit fell 57.1% to EUR 17.3 million, or EUR 0.023 per share compared with EUR 0.054 in 2024. The net profit margin declined from 5.1% to 2.3%.

Demand was affected by weak consumer and business confidence, economic challenges in the core markets, geopolitical tensions and intense competition. Passenger volumes decreased 0.9% to 5.5 million, cargo units declined 19.2% to 245,004, and passenger vehicles fell 2.2% to 760,473. Estonia-Finland passenger numbers increased 1.8% and revenue rose to EUR 314.5 million, but the segment result declined by EUR 10.9 million to EUR 70.4 million. Finland-Sweden revenue decreased to EUR 225.5 million and the segment result fell to EUR 1.3 million, partly reflecting 68 days of maintenance on two vessels. Estonia-Sweden revenue dropped EUR 12.1 million to EUR 85.3 million, although its loss narrowed to EUR 1.2 million. Other-segment revenue declined to EUR 145.5 million, while its result remained broadly stable at EUR 28.5 million.

Cost pressures included personnel expenses rising 4.3% to EUR 201.0 million and fuel and emission costs increasing to EUR 96.5 million. These effects were partly offset by an EUR 18.0 million reduction in depreciation and amortisation, including EUR 16.7 million from extending the estimated useful lives of most cruise and passenger vessels to 45 years, and by lower net finance costs, which fell to EUR 21.4 million from EUR 28.3 million.

Net operating cash flow remained positive at EUR 127.1 million, although down from EUR 153.4 million. Investing cash flow was positive at EUR 46.1 million, supported by EUR 78.8 million from the sale of three vessels. Capital expenditure increased to EUR 33.0 million. Interest-bearing liabilities declined 19.9% to EUR 445.9 million and net debt fell to EUR 432.4 million, despite the net debt-to-EBITDA ratio increasing to 3.3. The Group paid EUR 44.6 million in dividends and will propose another EUR 0.06 per share for 2026. It maintained a EUR 110.4 million liquidity buffer at year-end and continued to report sufficient liquidity for operations.
