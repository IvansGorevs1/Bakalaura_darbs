During the 2025 financial year, Aktsiaselts Infortar reported consolidated revenue of EUR 1.837 billion, representing a significant increase compared to EUR 1.372 billion in the previous year. This revenue growth was primarily driven by the full twelve-month consolidation of Tallink Grupp, which was previously consolidated only from August 2024 onwards, alongside expansions in the energy and agricultural sectors, including the acquisition of Estonia Farmid. Profitability indicators showed mixed results. Gross profit improved substantially to EUR 239.8 million from EUR 128.6 million, and EBITDA rose to EUR 227.9 million compared to EUR 145.3 million in 2024, with the EBITDA margin improving from 10.6% to 12.4%. Operating profit also increased to EUR 109.0 million from EUR 77.0 million. However, net profit decreased to EUR 69.9 million from EUR 193.7 million in the previous year. Net profit attributable to owners of the parent was EUR 70.6 million compared to EUR 191.3 million in 2024. The reduction in net profit was mainly attributable to one-off effects recorded in 2024, specifically the initial bargain purchase gain recognized upon the initial consolidation of Tallink Grupp.

The Group maintained a robust financial position with total assets amounting to EUR 2.597 billion as of 31 December 2025, compared to EUR 2.715 billion at the end of 2024. Total equity increased to EUR 1.175 billion from EUR 1.166 billion, raising the equity ratio to 45% from 43%. Interest-bearing liabilities decreased to EUR 1.071 billion from EUR 1.223 billion, reflecting loan repayments across several Group entities, including Tallink Grupp, and adjustments aligning with market conditions. Net debt decreased significantly to EUR 852 million from EUR 1.056 million, while the net debt to EBITDA ratio improved from 4.0 to 3.7. Cash and cash equivalents stood at EUR 219.8 million at the end of the year, up from EUR 167.6 million. Basic earnings per share were EUR 3.43 compared to EUR 9.36 in the prior year.

Business operations during the year were influenced by varying sector conditions and strategic developments. In the maritime transport segment, Tallink Grupp carried 5.53 million passengers, representing a 0.9% decrease year-on-year, while the transport of cargo units declined by 19.2% to 245,004. In the energy segment, regional gas consumption in Finland and the Baltic states decreased by 9% to 39.7 TWh amid ongoing market volatility and fluctuating price trends. Strategic highlights included expanding agricultural operations through the acquisition of a 96.6% share in Estonia Farmid OÜ in July 2025, advancing biomethane production, and expanding renewable energy infrastructure such as solar power generation and energy storage in Latvia. Reflecting its commitment to shareholder returns, Infortar paid dividends of EUR 3 per share in 2025, totaling EUR 62 million, and the management proposed a dividend of EUR 3.02 per share for the 2025 financial results. The company's share price closed the year at EUR 45.8, marking a 5.5% annual increase on the Nasdaq Baltic Exchange.
