WindowMaster International A/S reported a full-year 2025 revenue of DKK 268.6 million, representing an eight percent decline compared to DKK 294.5 million in 2024. The contraction was primarily driven by a sluggish first quarter and reduced sales volume to a major Nordic customer, compounded by slower growth in North America. Despite the top-line decrease, the company delivered a gross profit of DKK 131.1 million, successfully expanding its gross margin to 48.8 percent from 46.9 percent in the prior year. This margin expansion reflects a deliberate business mix shift away from lower-margin key accounts toward higher-yield direct project revenues, aligning with the adopted Accelerate Core strategy. Operating profit measured as EBITDA settled at DKK 28.0 million, down from DKK 37.3 million in 2024, compressing the EBITDA margin to 10.4 percent from 12.7 percent. The operating profit decline was largely anchored to maintained fixed cost levels designed to fund future capacity and DKK 2.5 million in extraordinary expenses triggered by a ransomware attack that disrupted IT infrastructure in mid-2025. Earnings before tax fell to DKK 2.9 million, translating into a post-tax profit of merely DKK 0.9 million, with the disproportionately high tax charge stemming from unrealized tax loss carryforwards. Operational cash generation remained resilient at DKK 28.5 million, supported by efficient working capital management, while net interest-bearing debt relative to EBITDA climbed to 1.7 from 1.2, staying comfortably below the internal target threshold of 2.0. Market dynamics during the reporting period featured stabilizing interest rates that revitalized non-residential construction activity, particularly in the second half of the year, while residential demand stayed subdued. Long-term tailwinds persist through strict energy-efficiency mandates across the European Union and North America. For 2026, management guides for organic revenue growth between seven and fourteen percent, targeting DKK 290 million to DKK 310 million, with EBITDA forecast to reach DKK 45 million to DKK 55 million and margins normalized to eight to ten percent. The company plans to hold its fixed cost and investment base steady to capture operating leverage, sustain cash conversion, and progressively deleverage its balance sheet in line with expectations.
