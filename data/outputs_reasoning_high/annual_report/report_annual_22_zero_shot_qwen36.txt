Tallinna Vesi delivered strong financial performance in 2025, marked by robust revenue growth and expanded profitability despite an inflationary cost environment. Total sales reached €72.48 million, representing an 11.4 percent year-on-year increase of €7.40 million. This growth was primarily driven by updated water service tariffs implemented in July 2024, May 2025, and November 2024, which lifted private customer revenues by 18.3 percent to €31.84 million. Conversely, business customer sales declined slightly by 2.7 percent to €16.76 million following statutory price harmonization. Revenue from construction services surged by 62.0 percent to €8.46 million, reflecting heightened activity from network upgrades and external contracts. Sales from clients outside the primary service area also grew by 13.8 percent.

Cost of goods and services sold rose by 12.0 percent to €42.96 million, largely attributable to a 74.5 percent jump in construction-related expenses, a 6.8 percent increase in staff costs, and higher chemical and pollution tax expenditures. These pressures were partially mitigated by a 6.9 percent reduction in electricity costs, enabled by commissioning the new combined heat and power plant at the Paljassaare wastewater facility, which now supplies all thermal energy and over half of the electricity required for treatment operations. Consequently, gross profit improved by 10.5 percent to €29.52 million, while operating profit expanded by 11.8 percent to €21.22 million. Profit before taxes climbed 18.0 percent to €17.43 million, and net profit increased by 7.2 percent to €14.24 million, yielding earnings per share of €0.71. The group maintained its dividend policy by distributing €0.53 per share for 2024 results, with 2025 distributions pending shareholder approval.

Capital expenditure totaled €55.74 million, exceeding prior-year levels and focusing on pipe rehabilitation, smart meter deployment, and separate stormwater infrastructure to enhance service continuity and climate resilience. Operational metrics reflected these investments, with water emergency incidents falling by 23 percent and sewer blockages decreasing by 18 percent. The water loss rate stood at 13.01 percent, meeting internal targets. On the liability side, current obligations increased to €24.89 million, primarily linked to ongoing investment payables, while outstanding bank loans rose to €160 million with a weighted average interest rate of 3.24 percent. Deferred income from connection fees grew by €2.01 million to €52.11 million. The company also continued to account for a €6.02 million provision regarding pending third-party tariff claims, maintaining the reserve level set in 2024 due to ongoing litigation.

Throughout the reporting period, Tallinna Vesi effectively balanced aggressive infrastructure modernization with disciplined cost management and customer service enhancements. Benefiting from a gradually recovering Estonian economic backdrop and favorable interest rate trends that reduced net financial expenses to €3.78 million, the group demonstrated resilient earnings generation and sustained capital allocation toward long-term environmental and operational objectives. The financial trajectory underscores a stable, well-capitalized utility advancing its sustainability agenda while preserving shareholder value through consistent profitability and regulated service delivery.
