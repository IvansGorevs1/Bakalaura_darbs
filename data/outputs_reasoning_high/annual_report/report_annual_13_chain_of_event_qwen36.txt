AS LHV Group delivered record financial performance in 2024, with consolidated net profit reaching EUR 150.3 million, a 7 percent increase compared to EUR 140.9 million in 2023. Total assets expanded by 23 percent to EUR 8.7 billion, driven primarily by a 28 percent rise in the loan portfolio to EUR 4.6 billion and a 21 percent increase in deposits to EUR 6.9 billion. Net interest income grew 8 percent to EUR 273.3 million, benefiting from elevated interest rate levels during much of the year, while net fee and commission income surged 24 percent to EUR 60.3 million, reflecting heightened customer engagement across services. Operating expenses increased by 14 percent to EUR 146.9 million, a development the report explicitly attributes to workforce expansion and strategic investments required to establish retail banking operations in the United Kingdom. Despite these expenditures, the group maintained a manageable cost to income ratio of 43.4 percent, up slightly from 42.3 percent in the prior year. Return on equity stood at 24.5 percent, down from 29.0 percent in 2023, as the equity base grew alongside profitability. Credit quality remained strong, with impairment losses totaling EUR 16.3 million against a healthy allowance, and capital buffers exceeded internal and regulatory requirements, evidenced by a Common Equity Tier 1 ratio of 16.89 percent and a total capital adequacy ratio of 20.68 percent. The pronounced balance sheet growth stemmed from multiple strategic drivers: Estonian corporate and retail lending expanded by 21 percent each, UK subsidiary LHV Bank recorded exceptional SME loan growth exceeding 338 percent, and an acquisition of a profitable loan portfolio accelerated volumes. Management successfully upgraded its annual financial targets by 25 percent following stronger-than-expected mid-year results. Liquidity remained resilient, with liquidity coverage and net stable funding ratios positioned well above regulatory thresholds. While interest rates peaked and began decreasing in the second half of 2024, impacting future margin expectations, the group navigated the shifting rate environment through disciplined liability management, including a deliberate reduction in costly deposit platform inflows while growing stable retail funding. Strategic actions also included issuing EUR 20 million in new Tier 2 bonds and restructuring debt maturities to optimize the capital framework ahead of evolving regulatory mandates. Overall, the consolidated results reflect aggressive yet controlled expansion across established and emerging markets, supported by sustained asset quality and robust capital generation.
