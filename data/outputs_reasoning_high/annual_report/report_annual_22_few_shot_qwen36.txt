The company delivered strong financial results for 2025, with both revenue and profitability expanding meaningfully compared to the prior year. Group sales reached €72.48 million, representing an 11.4 percent increase or €7.40 million, primarily supported by tariff adjustments for private customers, heightened demand for external construction services, and growth in sales outside the core service region. This upward trajectory was slightly tempered by a 2.7 percent decline in business customer revenue due to mandated tariff harmonization and a reclassification of stormwater billing. Operating profit before depreciation and amortisation rose by 12.7 percent to €30.95 million, while net profit increased by 7.2 percent to €14.24 million, lifting earnings per share to €0.71. Total costs of goods and services sold climbed by 12.0 percent to €42.96 million, largely reflecting a 74.5 percent surge in construction-related expenses and a 6.8 percent rise in personnel costs. These cost pressures were partially neutralized by a 6.9 percent drop in electricity spending, which benefited from the full operational capacity of a newly commissioned combined heat and power plant at the wastewater treatment facility. Reduced Euribor rates further lowered net financial expenses to €3.78 million. The company maintained an aggressive investment profile, deploying €55.74 million in fixed assets to modernize critical infrastructure, reduce distribution network losses to 13.01 percent, and advance the rapid rollout of smart water meters across its service area. Operational reliability was reinforced through extensive pipe rehabilitations, the adoption of environmentally sustainable no-dig construction techniques, and the swift resolution of a rare winter ice disruption that temporarily affected district water pressure. The firm continues to manage regulatory transitions, notably maintaining a €6.02 million provision for pending third-party claims stemming from historical tariff disputes, while legally defending its compliance. Overall, the reporting period was marked by consistent earnings growth, strategic capital deployment toward climate-resilient infrastructure, improved service efficiency, and the successful integration of renewable energy generation, positioning the company for continued sustainable expansion.
