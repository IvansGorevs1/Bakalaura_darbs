Trophy Games reported a strongly executing 2025 defined by rapid revenue expansion and successful portfolio scaling. Total revenue increased by 40% to DKK 126.5 million, up from DKK 90.1 million in 2024. Growth was concentrated in the company’s transport and management simulation franchises, with three core titles accounting for 67% of total revenue compared to 35% previously. New releases Truck Manager and Farm Manager successfully transitioned into major commercial pillars, leveraging established codebases to drive high user acquisition and sustained engagement.

Operating profit demonstrated healthy absolute growth alongside intentional margin normalization. EBITDA rose by 26% to DKK 31.1 million, resulting in a 25% EBITDA margin, a slight decline from 27% in 2024. This contraction reflects a calculated 76% expansion of the marketing budget to DKK 40.5 million, deployed to defend and grow market share within a highly competitive mobile segment. Concurrently, the company optimized its monetization mix by doubling in-app advertising revenue to DKK 18.5 million. Because advertising income carries zero platform fees versus the 15% to 30% levied on in-app purchases, this shift directly improved gross margins and direct cost efficiency.

Profit before tax increased by 109% to DKK 19.0 million, achieving a 15% EBT margin. Management clarifies that this steep comparative rise relies heavily on favorable baselines, specifically DKK 4.9 million in non-recurring impairment charges booked in 2024. Adjusted for those historical costs, prior-year EBT stood at DKK 13.9 million, indicating that core profitability remained stable at 15% across both periods. Initial USD volatility in the first half dampened reported early-year figures, but leadership capitalized on lower initial marketing costs to aggressively increase user acquisition budgets. This tactical reallocation fueled exceptional momentum later in the year, producing a historic fourth quarter with revenue growing 106% to DKK 41.0 million and EBT jumping 574% to DKK 10.1 million.

Financial resilience persisted throughout the period, characterized by a debt-free balance sheet and a 78% solvency ratio. Year-end cash settled at DKK 23.0 million due to manageable timing factors, including a substantial VAT receivable, a DKK 4.7 million retroactive tax credit repayment following regulatory reassessment, and an active share buyback program. Operational cash generation quickly restored liquidity to DKK 36.4 million in early January. Supported by framework-driven development efficiencies and predictable recurring revenues, the company advanced 2026 financial guidance targeting revenue between DKK 130 million and DKK 153 million, underpinned by disciplined user acquisition and systematic monetization enhancements across the live portfolio.
