During the financial year 2025, OODA AI AB transitioned from structure and stabilization to commercialization and growth within its core artificial intelligence operations. The group reported a sharp increase in net sales to 31,880 thousand kronor, up from 1,056 thousand kronor in 2024, driven by the invoicing of major commercial transactions. Total group revenue reached 32,058 thousand kronor compared with 1,061 thousand kronor in the previous year. Operating performance improved significantly, with the operating loss narrowing to 29,270 thousand kronor from 51,781 thousand kronor in 2024. The group achieved a positive EBITDA of 2,957 thousand kronor, contrasting with a negative EBITDA of 28,229 thousand kronor in the prior year. When adjusted for approximately 10,238 thousand kronor in non-recurring one-off costs attributable to the liquidation of older company structures, adjusted EBITDA stood at approximately 13,195 thousand kronor, corresponding to an adjusted EBITDA margin of roughly 32 percent of revenue. 

Despite operational progress, the group recorded a net loss for the year of 125,799 thousand kronor, compared to a loss of 83,355 thousand kronor in 2024. Earnings per share were negative 35.29 kronor compared to negative 29.00 kronor per share in the previous year. This negative bottom-line result was primarily driven by financial items totaling negative 95,853 thousand kronor, which were heavily burdened by impairments, revaluations, and results from associated companies and other securities as management continued streamlining operations to focus strictly on the AI core business. Consequently, financial fixed assets decreased to 8,387 thousand kronor at the end of 2025 from 96,036 thousand kronor at the end of 2024, and total group equity declined from 119,371 thousand kronor to 23,859 thousand kronor. The group equity ratio was 55 percent compared to 82 percent in the previous year, and total assets amounted to 43,640 thousand kronor down from 145,033 thousand kronor. 

Cash flow from operating activities was negative 16,662 thousand kronor, an improvement from negative 29,680 thousand kronor in 2024, with the second half of the year generating a positive operating cash flow of 5,098 thousand kronor. Cash flow from investing activities was negative 49,413 thousand kronor, while financing activities contributed 44,302 thousand kronor through share issues. Cash and cash equivalents ended the year at 3,335 thousand kronor compared to 25,108 thousand kronor at the end of 2024. The number of shares increased during the year from 3,385,254 to 3,731,465 following capital acquisitions. Business growth was propelled by major commercial agreements, including expanded contracts with Win.Rocks AG totaling approximately 21 million kronor plus an additional December agreement of at least 15 million kronor over 24 months, a major agreement with HMM Deutschland estimated at 10 million kronor in 2025 and 30 million kronor in 2026, and an agreement with Sergel Kredittjänster, alongside strategic partnerships and the advancement of decentralized AI infrastructure.
