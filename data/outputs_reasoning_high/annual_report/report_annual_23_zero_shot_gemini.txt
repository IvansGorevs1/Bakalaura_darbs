During the 2025 financial year, TKM Grupp AS operated in a challenging economic environment characterized by low consumer confidence, high price sensitivity, and domestic tax changes, most notably the introduction of the motor vehicle tax in Estonia. Consolidated sales revenue for 2025 reached 919.6 million euros, representing a decrease of 2.6 percent compared to 944.6 million euros in 2024. Gross profit amounted to 254.6 million euros, slightly down from 259.8 million euros in the previous year, while the gross margin remained stable at 27.7 percent compared to 27.5 percent in 2024. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization decreased by 14.7 percent to 77.4 million euros from 90.7 million euros. Operating profit declined to 34.9 million euros from 47.6 million euros, and profit before tax fell by 31.4 percent to 24.3 million euros. Consolidated net profit for 2025 decreased by 36.2 percent to 17.5 million euros, down from 27.5 million euros in 2024. Basic and diluted net profit per share decreased to 0.43 euros from 0.67 euros in the prior year. The decline in profitability was primarily driven by a significant contraction in the car trade segment due to the steep drop in the Estonian passenger car market following the motor vehicle tax implementation. Total investments made by the Group during 2025 amounted to 25.5 million euros, compared to 36.6 million euros in 2024, and were directed toward store modernizations, showroom openings in the Baltic States, and real estate development projects. Performance across business segments varied. The supermarkets segment recorded stable external revenue of 611.9 million euros, a minor increase of 0.3 percent year-on-year, though net profit declined by 38.6 percent to 9.5 million euros. The department stores segment experienced a 1.1 percent decrease in revenue to 103.0 million euros, resulting in a net loss of 0.5 million euros. The car trade segment was the most negatively affected, with revenue decreasing by 11.9 percent to 176.9 million euros and net profit dropping by 54.4 percent to 4.3 million euros, heavily impacted by the nearly 50 percent contraction of new car sales in Estonia. The security services segment recorded external revenue of 20.1 million euros, down 8.1 percent, with a net result near zero. Conversely, the real estate segment grew external revenue by 5.1 percent to 7.7 million euros, supported by new rental income from a logistics center, while net profit rose by 20.5 percent to 6.3 million euros. Following the reporting period, in January 2026, the Group's subsidiary TKM Auto OÜ reached an agreement to acquire Škoda distribution and retail rights in Tallinn and Harju County through the acquisition of Rohe Auto AS and SKO Motors OÜ. For the 2025 financial year, the management board proposes a dividend distribution of 0.60 euros per share, amounting to 24.4 million euros, following a dividend payment of 0.65 euros per share in the previous year. Overall, despite volatile macroeconomic conditions and regulatory pressures, the Group maintained financial stability through effective cost control, disciplined inventory planning, and strategic investments designed to support long-term growth.
