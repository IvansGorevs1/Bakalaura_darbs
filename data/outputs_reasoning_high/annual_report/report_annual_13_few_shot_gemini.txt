AS LHV Group reported a successful financial performance in 2024, achieving record-high profitability and significant business growth despite a challenging macroeconomic environment characterized by negative domestic GDP growth and falling base interest rates. Consolidated net profit reached a historically high level of EUR 150.3 million, representing a 7% increase compared to the previous year. This growth was driven by robust asset expansion, effective cost management, and sustained credit quality. Total net income increased by 11% year-over-year to EUR 338.3 million, supported by an 8% rise in net interest income to EUR 273.3 million and a 24% increase in net fee and commission income to EUR 60.3 million. Operating expenses grew by 14% to EUR 146.9 million, primarily due to an expanded employee headcount to support ongoing business scaling. The cost-to-income ratio stood at 43.4%, while the return on equity was robust at 24.5%.

The group's balance sheet experienced substantial expansion, with total consolidated assets reaching EUR 8.7 billion, up 23% from the prior year. The net loan portfolio grew by 28% to EUR 4.6 billion, driven by strong lending activity across all key segments. Estonian corporate loans increased by 21% to EUR 2.3 billion, retail loans rose by 21% to EUR 1.9 billion, and UK SME lending surged by 338% to EUR 348 million following the full operational year under LHV Bank's banking license. Customer deposits expanded by 21% to EUR 6.9 billion, with regular customer deposits in LHV Pank increasing by 14%, largely supported by higher term deposit inflows amid changing interest rate dynamics. Assets under management rose by 3% to EUR 1.56 billion, primarily fueled by positive capital market returns.

Financial results were significantly influenced by macroeconomic shifts, notably the peak and subsequent rapid decline of base interest rates during the second half of the year as the European Central Bank implemented multiple rate cuts. This monetary easing reduced the net interest margin from 3.88% to 3.48%. Credit quality remained strong overall, although expected credit losses increased to EUR 16.3 million from EUR 11.5 million due to a cautious provisioning approach amid economic uncertainty. LHV maintained a strong capital and liquidity profile, with a total capital adequacy ratio of 20.68% and a Common Equity Tier 1 ratio of 16.89%, both comfortably exceeding regulatory minimums. The group’s share price decreased by 7% during the year to EUR 3.245, while the dividend per share was set at EUR 0.13, reflecting management's commitment to delivering shareholder value while preserving required capital buffers for future growth.
