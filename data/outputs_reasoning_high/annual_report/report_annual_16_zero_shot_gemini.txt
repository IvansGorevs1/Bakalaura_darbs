Nordic Fibreboard AS published its consolidated annual report for the financial year 2025, detailing the performance of its fibreboard production and wholesale operations alongside real estate management activities. During 2025, the group generated consolidated net sales of 7.33 million euros, representing a 3.4 percent decrease compared to 7.59 million euros in 2024. Sales of fibreboard production reached 7.33 million euros down from 7.57 million euros in the previous year, with European Union sales remaining relatively flat and accounting for 92 percent of total sales, while shipments to Africa and Asia declined. Real estate management revenue dropped to 3 thousand euros compared to 25 thousand euros in 2024 due to rental agreement terminations. The group reported a consolidated EBITDA of negative 0.42 million euros for 2025, worsening from negative 25 thousand euros in 2024, yielding a negative EBITDA margin of 6 percent. This negative result was driven by underutilized production capacity and a planned factory shutdown in December 2025 for transitioning to a new gas boiler house, which caused continuing personnel, maintenance, and repair expenses during production suspension. Consequently, the operating loss widened to 0.94 million euros from 0.55 million euros in 2024, and the net loss for 2025 expanded to 1.15 million euros compared to a net loss of 0.79 million euros in 2024. Basic and diluted loss per share improved to 0.14 euros from 0.17 euros in 2024. 

As of 31 December 2025, total assets stood at 9.32 million euros, increasing from 8.25 million euros at the end of 2024. Total liabilities increased to 4.86 million euros from 4.64 million euros, while borrowings decreased to 3.17 million euros from 3.72 million euros. Total equity rose to 4.46 million euros compared to 3.62 million euros, supported by a public share issue completed in October 2025 that increased share capital by 400 thousand euros and share premium by 1.60 million euros, issuing 4 million new shares and expanding the total share count to 8,499,061 shares. Operating cash flows were negative at 503 thousand euros, while investing activities resulted in a cash outflow of 989 thousand euros, primarily driven by 1.37 million euros in production asset investments and 161 thousand euros in real estate investments, offset by 539 thousand euros from the sale of Trigon Property Development shares. Financing activities provided a positive cash flow of 1.44 million euros. The group faced a challenging macroeconomic environment characterized by modest Estonian economic growth, weak domestic demand, and high inflation driven by tax increases. In real estate development, Pärnu Riverside Development was merged into Nordic Fibreboard AS in July 2025, uniting ownership of the Admirali quarter project properties. After the balance sheet date on 8 January 2026, the company entered a preliminary sale-purchase agreement for ten properties in the Admirali quarter, with final execution scheduled by 30 June 2026.
