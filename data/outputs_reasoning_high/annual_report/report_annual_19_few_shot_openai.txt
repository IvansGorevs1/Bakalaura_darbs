Talkpool’s financial performance improved in 2025 as the company completed its restructuring phase and returned to organic growth. Net sales increased 12.5% to EUR 17.756 million from EUR 15.782 million in 2024, marking the first year of double-digit growth since the consolidation phase began. Gross margin was 26.4%. EBITDA rose to EUR 1.828 million from EUR 1.702 million, although the EBITDA margin declined slightly to 10.3% from 10.8% as the company began investing in personnel, technology, and future growth. EBIT increased to EUR 1.620 million from EUR 1.527 million, with the margin decreasing to 9.1% from 9.7%.

Net earnings after tax increased 42% to EUR 862 thousand from EUR 605 thousand, and the earnings margin improved to 4.8% from 3.8%. Management described the overall margin development as stable despite substantial investments. It also expects temporary pressure on margins and cash flow as these investments take time to generate revenue.

The Americas remained Talkpool’s largest market, with the Caribbean continuing to be its single largest revenue market and maintaining growth despite weakness in the US dollar. The Haiti operation remained an important contributor despite severe security and humanitarian conditions. The US business established a foothold through network services projects and a new service contract with MasTec, but the ramp-up was slower than planned after the company shifted its focus toward remote wireless services. US revenue included in the annual results amounted to EUR 272,540, with approximately EUR 70,000 in gross margin.

Germany continued to provide a solid and significant contribution, particularly through fiber-network planning and documentation for Deutsche Telekom. However, assignment volumes temporarily flattened as the operator placed greater emphasis on homes passed rather than connected. Pakistan achieved its highest revenue in local currency, improved gross profitability after renegotiating customer terms, and became the Group’s main production base for cost-efficient engineering capacity, although currency depreciation reduced its euro-reported contribution. Currency headwinds in the first half of the year also included an 11% decline in the US dollar and a 14% decline in the Pakistani rupee.

The balance sheet strengthened materially. Equity reached EUR 2.1 million, while net cash improved to EUR 0.3 million at year-end, compared with a net debt position of EUR 5.7 million in 2021. Talkpool launched its Vision 2030 plan, targeting EUR 40 million in revenue, a 14% EBITDA margin, and zero net debt by 2030. After year-end, a EUR 1.65 million share issue was announced, expected to move the company to approximately EUR 2 million of net cash.
