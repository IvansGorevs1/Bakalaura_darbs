In 2024, AS LHV Group achieved the highest business volumes and net profit in its history, reporting a consolidated net profit of EUR 150.3 million, representing a 7 percent increase year-over-year or an increase of EUR 9.4 million. The Group’s total net income rose by 11 percent to EUR 338.3 million, supported by robust asset growth, efficient cost management, and sustained credit quality despite challenging macroeconomic conditions, including negative GDP growth in Estonia and a shifting interest rate environment. Net interest income grew by 8 percent to EUR 273.3 million, while net fee and commission income rose by 24 percent to EUR 60.3 million. Total operating expenses increased by 14 percent to EUR 146.9 million, driven largely by an increased number of employees, resulting in a cost-to-income ratio of 43.4 percent compared to 42.3 percent in the previous year. Impairment losses on financial assets measured at amortised cost increased to EUR 16.3 million from EUR 11.5 million. The Group’s financial position strengthened significantly, with total consolidated assets reaching EUR 8.7 billion, up 23 percent year-over-year. The balance sheet expansion was propelled by a 28 percent growth in the net loan portfolio, which increased by EUR 990 million to EUR 4.6 billion. This growth was distributed across Estonian corporate banking, which grew by 21 percent to EUR 2.3 billion, Estonian retail loans, which increased by 21 percent to EUR 1.9 billion, and UK SME loans, which expanded significantly by 338 percent to EUR 0.3 billion following the full year of operations under a UK banking license. Total customer deposits grew by 21 percent to EUR 6.9 billion, driven by growth across all customer segments, with regular customer deposits in LHV Pank increasing by 14 percent to EUR 4.6 billion, while financial intermediaries deposits reached EUR 1.5 billion. Throughout the year, customer acquisition remained strong, with the number of LHV Pank customers increasing by 9 percent, or 38,000, to reach 456,000. Capital adequacy and liquidity ratios remained robust and well above regulatory and internal targets, with the total capital adequacy ratio at 20.68 percent, the Common Equity Tier 1 ratio at 16.89 percent, the Liquidity Coverage Ratio at 187.5 percent, and the Net Stable Funding Ratio at 154.4 percent. Return on equity stood at 24.5 percent, and return on assets was 1.9 percent. LHV Group successfully executed capital management actions during the year, including calling back EUR 20 million of Additional Tier 1 bonds, issuing EUR 20 million of Tier 2 bonds, and managing MREL-eligible bond issuances. Looking forward, LHV enters 2025 in a strong position, anticipating short-term profit pressures from decreasing base interest rates but planning to offset these impacts through continued business growth and operational efficiencies.
