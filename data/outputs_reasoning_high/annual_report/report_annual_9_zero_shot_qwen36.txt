Impero A/S delivered resilient financial performance in 2025, recovering from initial market headwinds to achieve strong year-end metrics. Annual Recurring Revenue reached DKK 46.2 million, representing a 19 percent year-over-year increase from DKK 38.7 million in 2024, fulfilling the upper bound of revised guidance. Recognized revenue grew 19 percent to DKK 42.0 million, driven by sustained customer expansion and disciplined cost management. The company recorded an EBITDA of negative DKK 3.1 million, marking a substantial improvement of DKK 5.6 million compared to negative DKK 8.7 million in 2024, landing within the updated guidance range. The operating loss narrowed to negative DKK 6.8 million from negative DKK 11.4 million the prior year, resulting in an earnings per share of negative DKK 0.29, up from negative DKK 0.49.

Core software-as-a-service metrics remained robust, underpinned by an exceptionally low annual churn rate of one percent, down from two percent in 2024, with zero churn in the DACH region. Net revenue retention stood at 106 percent, slightly lower than 108 percent in 2024, while gross uplift reached 12 percent. Average revenue per account hit a record DKK 228,000, reflecting successful land-and-expand dynamics, though customer acquisition cost payback extended to 22 months due to targeted lead-generation investments and a modest reduction in new customer intake. Customer concentration risk declined markedly, with the top ten customers accounting for 20 percent of Annual Recurring Revenue at year-end 2025, down from 37 percent in 2020, after adding 26 clients to surpass a total base of 200.

Strategic decisions notably shifted focus from short-term cash flow targets to long-term growth acceleration. Following a DKK 15 million equity placement in December 2025 that strengthened the balance sheet to DKK 18.9 million in cash with zero interest-bearing debt, the Board deferred its ambition to achieve recurring positive cash flow before the end of 2026. This reallocation prioritizes commercial scaling and product innovation, including the July launch of a Control Testing module, expanded artificial intelligence-driven compliance features, and enhanced entity management capabilities. Geographic momentum concentrated in Denmark and the DACH region, which jointly generated 88 percent of year-end Annual Recurring Revenue, while partner network expansions in Germany position the company for future channel-led growth. Looking ahead, 2026 guidance projects Annual Recurring Revenue between DKK 53 million and DKK 57 million, with earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization expected to range from negative DKK 2.0 million to positive DKK 1.0 million, reflecting the anticipated impact of accelerated hiring and commercial investment during the ramp-up phase. Overall, the company demonstrated improved operational leverage, solidified pricing power, and strategic agility amid evolving European regulatory demands.
