Lyko Group AB continued to grow and gain market share during the 2025 financial year despite an industry environment characterized by intense competition and structural shifts. Total net sales for the group increased by 11.3 percent to SEK 3,962.6 million, compared to SEK 3,561.7 million in 2024. Total revenue reached SEK 3,975.7 million, up from SEK 3,579.1 million. Growth was primarily driven by the Nordic operating segment, where net sales rose by 12.0 percent to SEK 3,699.8 million, with Norway surpassing one billion SEK in annual revenue. Conversely, sales in the Europe segment decreased slightly by 1.3 percent to SEK 130.7 million, prompting a fourth quarter reorganization involving the closure of local offices in the Netherlands and Germany to manage operations centrally.

Gross profit for the group increased to SEK 1,658.7 million from SEK 1,568.8 million in the previous year. However, the gross profit margin declined to 41.9 percent from 44.0 percent. Operating profit, or EBIT, decreased to SEK 78.2 million compared to SEK 117.4 million in 2024, lowering the EBIT margin from 3.3 percent to 2.0 percent. The group reported a net loss for the year of SEK 3.0 million, down from a profit of SEK 21.0 million in 2024. Consequently, earnings per share dropped to SEK -0.20 from SEK 1.37.

The company's financial performance and results were influenced by major strategic investments and operational developments during the year. Most notably, Lyko commissioned its largest project in company history, a new automated warehouse expansion in Vansbro designed for a capacity of up to 100,000 orders per day. While this implementation and ramp-up affected results during parts of the year, it established a scalable logistical platform for future volumes. Additional developments included opening the first 500 square meter Fab Store concept in Täby Centrum in October, expanding the total store network to 34 stores across Sweden, Norway, and Finland, and relaunching the iconic Make Up Store. In the fourth quarter alone, Lyko welcomed over 350,000 new customers.

Cash flow from operating activities showed a significant improvement, rising to SEK 527.5 million from SEK 209.7 million in 2024. Cash flow from investing activities amounted to SEK -351.2 million, driven by investments in the Vansbro automation, store openings, and digital development. Interest-bearing liabilities, including lease liabilities, stood at SEK 1,416.3 million, while net debt excluding IFRS 16 decreased to SEK 292.5 million compared to SEK 333.1 million in the prior year. Total equity at year-end was SEK 456.6 million, yielding an equity ratio of 14.8 percent. Reflecting the company's focus on continued expansion, the Board of Directors proposed no dividend for the 2025 financial year, prioritizing generated cash flows for growth initiatives.
