The company reported strong top-line growth during the 2025 fiscal year, while profitability metrics softened compared to the prior period. Net revenue increased by 11.3 percent to SEK 3,962.6 million, primarily driven by robust expansion in the Nordic segment, which grew 12.0 percent to SEK 3,699.8 million. The Norwegian market surpassed SEK 1 billion in annual sales, and the company attracted over 350,000 new customers in the fourth quarter alone. Conversely, European operations posted a marginal decline of 1.3 percent, reflecting a strategic reorganization that centralized management from the Nordic headquarters and reduced regional overhead costs.

Operating profitability narrowed during the reporting period. EBIT fell to SEK 78.2 million from SEK 117.4 million in 2024, corresponding to a drop in EBIT margin from 3.3 percent to 2.0 percent. The gross profit margin also contracted to 41.9 percent, down from 44.0 percent. Consequently, the group recorded a net loss of SEK 3.0 million compared to a profit of SEK 21.0 million the previous year. Despite the lower headline result, operating cash flow strengthened markedly to SEK 527.5 million from SEK 209.7 million, supported by efficient working capital management. Net debt excluding lease liabilities decreased to SEK 292.5 million from SEK 333.1 million.

The financial performance was shaped by several major operational investments and strategic developments. The most significant initiative was the commissioning of a new automated warehouse facility in Vansbro, representing a SEK 500 million investment that expands daily processing capacity to 100,000 orders. While the implementation ramp-up contributed to higher depreciation charges and temporary operational adjustments, it establishes a scalable logistics platform intended to improve long-term cost efficiency. Retail strategy advanced with the opening of the first Fab Store in Täby Centrum, designed as a standardized model for future location rollouts. The company also continued its geographic consolidation within existing markets, bringing the total store count to 34 across Sweden, Norway, and Finland.

Looking ahead, the board has prioritized reinvestment over shareholder distributions, proposing no dividend for the current year. Following the reporting period, management announced a cost-saving program targeting SEK 100 million in annual run-rate savings to strengthen near-term earnings. Overall, the period was characterized by sustained market share gains and infrastructure scaling, alongside deliberate margin normalization as the company transitions into a heavily automated growth phase.
