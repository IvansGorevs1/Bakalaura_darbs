Konsolidator A/S reported its financial results for 2025, marking the first full year of executing its Resilient Growth strategy. The company demonstrated significant improvements across key performance indicators, driven by revenue growth, cost discipline, and a partner-driven expansion model. Total revenue increased by 25 percent to DKK 25.3 million from DKK 20.3 million in 2024. This was supported by an increase in subscription fees to DKK 20.6 million and a 134 percent increase in onboarding and consulting fees to DKK 4.7 million following price adjustments. Contracted Annual Recurring Revenue rose 15 percent year-over-year to DKK 24.4 million, exceeding previous guidance, while active customers reached 283 after adding 52 new customers. Profitability showed substantial improvement as the EBITDA loss narrowed by 77 percent to DKK 2.0 million from DKK 9.3 million, with the company achieving a positive EBITDA of DKK 0.5 million in the second half of the year. The net loss for the year improved to DKK 6.6 million from DKK 14.9 million. Operating cash flow improved to a negative DKK 1.0 million compared to a negative DKK 9.2 million previously, aided by lower operating losses and working capital enhancements from annual customer invoicing and extended vendor payment terms. Key SaaS metrics reflected strengthening operational efficiency. The Rule of 40 score improved to 17 percent from negative 40 percent. The Customer Acquisition Cost payback period shortened from 43 to 23 months, and the Customer Lifetime Value to CAC ratio strengthened from 2.7 to 4.1. These efficiency gains were supported by the full-year effect of marketing restructuring implemented in 2024, contributing to an 11 percent decrease in external expenses to DKK 6.3 million, while staff costs remained stable at DKK 21.0 million with 31 employees at year-end. Product development focused on introducing the Konsolidator Suite, expanding beyond core consolidation to include a Financial Data Management solution built on Microsoft Fabric and a new Financial Planning and Analysis tool. Partner-driven sales expanded, with 38 percent of new customers acquired through partners compared to 22 percent in 2024. The Spanish subsidiary, Konsolidator Iberia, grew its customer count to 30, becoming the third-largest market. Financially, the company secured net proceeds of DKK 4.8 million through two private placements. Consolidated equity stood at a negative DKK 3.6 million at year-end, while the parent company maintained a positive equity of DKK 0.7 million. In July 2025, loan repayments to EIFO and 2L Kapital A/S commenced, with DKK 1.5 million repaid, bringing total borrowings to DKK 14.1 million. For 2026, Konsolidator guided for Contracted Annual Recurring Revenue and revenue both to reach DKK 27 to 29 million, a positive EBITDA of DKK 1 to 2 million, and a decline in ARR churn to between 6 and 9 percent.
