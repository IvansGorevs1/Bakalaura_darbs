Lyko delivered continued top-line growth in 2025, but profitability declined. Net sales increased 11.3% to SEK 3,962.6 million from SEK 3,561.7 million in 2024, while total revenue reached SEK 3,975.7 million. Growth was primarily driven by the Nordic segment, where sales rose 12.0% to SEK 3,699.8 million and represented 93.4% of group sales. Europe remained comparatively weak, with sales declining 1.3% to SEK 130.7 million.

Gross profit increased to SEK 1,658.7 million from SEK 1,568.8 million, but the gross margin fell from 44.0% to 41.9%. EBIT decreased to SEK 78.2 million from SEK 117.4 million, reducing the EBIT margin from 3.3% to 2.0%. EBITDA declined to SEK 252.3 million from SEK 287.4 million. The group reported a loss of SEK 3.0 million, compared with a profit of SEK 21.0 million in 2024, and earnings per share fell to negative SEK 0.20 from SEK 1.37. Net financial items were broadly unchanged at negative SEK 79.1 million, while the tax expense decreased to SEK 2.1 million from SEK 16.6 million.

The report attributes part of the year’s weaker results to the commissioning and ramp-up of the new automated warehouse expansion in Vansbro, which affected performance during parts of the year. The project, Lyko’s largest investment, amounts to approximately SEK 500 million and provides capacity for approximately 100,000 orders per day. It is expected to be fully operational in early 2026 and is intended to support higher volumes, improved product availability and more cost-efficient logistics. The year was also characterized by intense competition, industry restructuring and a challenging market environment, although Lyko states that it continued to gain market share.

Cash generation improved substantially. Cash flow from operating activities rose to SEK 527.5 million from SEK 209.7 million. This supported SEK 351.2 million of investing cash outflow, mainly related to warehouse automation, store openings and digital development, compared with SEK 204.0 million in 2024. Total cash flow increased to SEK 327.3 million from SEK 71.7 million, and cash and cash equivalents reached SEK 452.5 million.

The group expanded its store network to 34 locations through openings in Finland and Norway and launched its first Fab Store in Täby. A reorganization of the Europe segment began in December, including closure of local offices in Germany and the Netherlands. Interest-bearing liabilities including leases increased to SEK 1,416.3 million, but net debt excluding IFRS 16 declined to SEK 292.5 million from SEK 333.1 million. The equity ratio fell to 14.8% from 20.3% as investments and borrowing expanded the balance sheet. No dividend is proposed. After year-end, Lyko announced a cost-savings program expected to generate approximately SEK 100 million annually.
