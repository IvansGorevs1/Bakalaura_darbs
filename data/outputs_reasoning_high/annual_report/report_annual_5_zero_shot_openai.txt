Eleving Group delivered strong growth in 2025, with record revenue, EBITDA and portfolio volumes. Reported revenue reached EUR 250.0 million, up 15.4% from EUR 216.6 million in 2024. Adjusted EBITDA increased 13.5% to EUR 101.9 million. Interest revenue rose 18.6% to EUR 241.6 million, while interest expense increased 10.8% to EUR 46.0 million, resulting in net interest income of EUR 195.6 million, compared with EUR 162.2 million a year earlier. Revenue growth was supported by consumer lending, which generated EUR 114.8 million, up 18.0%, and flexible vehicle finance, which generated EUR 60.3 million, up 25.9%. Traditional vehicle finance revenue declined 6.0% to EUR 67.0 million, while newly launched device financing contributed EUR 7.9 million.

Net portfolio increased by EUR 75.1 million, or 20.2%, to EUR 446.3 million at year-end. Loan issuances reached an all-time high of EUR 458.0 million, up 24.3% from EUR 368.6 million in 2024, with vehicle and device finance issuances growing 34.0% and consumer finance issuances 15.7%. Demand strengthened materially, with loan applications increasing 50.0% to 2.7 million. Latvia, Romania and Kenya were identified as the main contributors to portfolio growth.

Profit before tax rose to EUR 41.9 million from EUR 37.7 million. Profit from continuing operations increased modestly to EUR 29.2 million from EUR 28.8 million, while total profit for the period was EUR 29.2 million versus EUR 29.6 million in 2024, which included EUR 0.8 million from discontinued operations. Net profit before foreign exchange and discontinued operations increased 25.6% to EUR 40.8 million. Bottom-line growth was constrained by a lower net foreign exchange gain of EUR 3.7 million compared with EUR 11.7 million in 2024 and a higher corporate tax charge of EUR 12.7 million versus EUR 8.9 million.

The Group expanded its product and geographic footprint by launching installment loans in several European markets, smartphone financing in Kenya and Uganda, and motorcycle financing in Tanzania, its seventeenth market. Smartphone financing reached a EUR 13.5 million portfolio by year-end. The Group also sold a EUR 12.4 million vehicle-finance portfolio in Moldova and reviewed its Uzbekistan business model.

Capital access strengthened through a EUR 40 million bond tap and a EUR 275 million five-year bond issuance, which refinanced EUR 150 million of maturing debt. Total borrowings principal reached EUR 441.6 million, with EUR 50.4 million due for renewal within 12 months. However, capitalization declined to 23.7% from 29.3%, net leverage rose to 3.8 from 3.3, and operating cash flow was negative EUR 52.0 million compared with positive EUR 18.4 million in 2024. The Group paid EUR 19.65 million in dividends and received a positive outlook revision from Fitch while retaining its B rating.
