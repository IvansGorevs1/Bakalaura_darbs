Eleving Group delivered record financial results in 2024, supported by growth in lending volumes, portfolio expansion, improved profitability and strengthened capitalization. Revenue increased to EUR 216.6 million from EUR 189.3 million in 2023, an increase of nearly 15%. Consumer lending contributed EUR 95.5 million, traditional vehicle financing EUR 73.1 million, and flexible and subscription-based products EUR 48.0 million. Loan issuance rose 26.8% to EUR 368.6 million, compared with EUR 290.6 million a year earlier, while the net loan and pre-owned vehicle rental portfolio increased 16% to EUR 371.2 million.

Adjusted EBITDA reached a record EUR 89.8 million, up from EUR 77.5 million in 2023, an increase of more than 16%. Profit before tax from continuing operations rose to EUR 37.7 million from EUR 28.5 million. Profit from continuing operations increased to EUR 28.8 million from EUR 21.9 million, while total net profit reached an all-time high of EUR 29.6 million, compared with EUR 24.5 million in 2023, an increase of almost 21%. The result included a EUR 2.6 million net profit impact from an additional EUR 3.0 million Romanian VAT liability relating to prior periods. Excluding this one-off expense, net profit was EUR 32.2 million.

The Group’s performance benefited from stable operating conditions, increased demand, expanded sales channels and continued digitalization. Vehicle-finance applications increased 80.9% year over year, and the Group completed the integration of its Sub-Saharan acquisitions, tripling the combined portfolio across its three main regional markets. Portfolio quality also improved: non-performing loan rates declined from 7.5% to 6.1% in vehicle finance and from 4.5% to 4.3% in consumer finance. Net profit before foreign exchange and discontinued operations increased to EUR 32.5 million from EUR 28.3 million, while the reported net foreign exchange loss decreased to EUR 3.7 million from EUR 6.4 million.

A major strategic and capital-markets milestone was the October IPO, described as the largest in Nasdaq Riga’s history. It raised EUR 29.0 million of equity, strengthening the capital base and supporting repayment of subordinated bonds and selected higher-cost funding, as well as future organic growth. Total equity increased to EUR 108.1 million from EUR 65.4 million. The capitalization ratio improved to 29.3% from 26.1%, net leverage fell to 3.3 from 3.7, and the interest coverage ratio rose to 2.4 from 2.3. Fitch also upgraded the Group’s rating from B- to B with a stable outlook. The Group financed more than 2,000 electric motorcycles in East Africa and continued expanding its digital and sustainable mobility offerings, reinforcing its longer-term growth strategy.
