Konsolidator reported substantially improved financial performance in 2025, with stronger revenue growth, lower operating costs, and a significantly reduced loss, although the Group remained unprofitable and financially constrained. Revenue increased 25% to DKK 25.3m from DKK 20.3m in 2024. Subscription revenue rose to DKK 20.6m from DKK 18.3m, while onboarding and consulting revenue increased 134% to DKK 4.7m, following higher onboarding fees introduced for new customers. Contribution increased to DKK 23.4m, although the contribution margin declined to 92% from 94%.

The EBITDA loss narrowed to DKK 2.0m from DKK 9.3m, an improvement of DKK 7.3m. This reflected higher revenue, reduced marketing and external consultancy spending, lower staff costs after capitalization, and DKK 2.0m of other operating income compared with DKK 0.7m in 2024. The EBIT loss improved to DKK 5.1m from DKK 12.1m, while the net loss more than halved to DKK 6.6m from DKK 14.9m. Financial expenses declined modestly to DKK 2.8m from DKK 3.1m. Performance improved particularly in the second half: EBITDA was positive at DKK 0.5m, compared with a loss of DKK 3.6m in the second half of 2024.

Recurring-revenue indicators also strengthened. Contracted annual recurring revenue increased 15% to DKK 24.4m, supported by new sales of DKK 4.5m, up 45%, and higher average customer value. Customer acquisition costs declined 32%, reducing the CAC payback period to 23 months from 43 months, while the CLV/CAC ratio improved to 4.1 from 2.7. However, ARR churn increased to 12.6% from 10.5%, and net retention remained unchanged at 94%, highlighting continuing customer-retention challenges.

The company added 52 customers during the year and served 283 active customers at year-end. Its transition from a standalone consolidation product to the Konsolidator Suite was a major strategic development. The company launched a financial data management solution, continued developing its FP&A tool, and signed its first FP&A customers ahead of the January 2026 launch. Partner-led sales accounted for 38% of new customers, compared with 22% in 2024, supported by agreements including Exsitec in Sweden and Norway. Iberia became the third-largest market, although the subsidiary generated a DKK 2.0m EBIT loss.

Cash flow improved materially but remained negative from operating activities at DKK 1.0m, compared with DKK 9.2m in 2024. After investing and financing flows, net cash flow was positive by DKK 11,000, leaving cash of only DKK 0.4m. The Group’s consolidated equity was negative at DKK 3.6m despite DKK 4.8m raised through private placements. Loan repayments began in 2025, and refinancing is a priority for 2026. Management guides for 2026 CARR and revenue of DKK 27–29m, EBITDA of DKK 1–2m, and churn of 6–9%.
