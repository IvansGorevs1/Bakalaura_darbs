During the 2025 financial year, AS Tallinna Vesi demonstrated solid overall financial and operational performance, achieving growth across key revenue and profit metrics driven by higher water service volumes, tariff adjustments, and expanded construction activity. The group reported total sales of seventy-two point four eight million euros, representing an 11.4 percent increase compared to sixty-five point zero eight million euros in 2024. Water services sales rose by 6.9 percent to sixty-two point three one million euros, largely supported by an 18.3 percent increase in sales to private customers within the main service area, which reached thirty-one point eight four million euros. Conversely, sales to business customers decreased by 2.7 percent to sixteen point seven six million euros due to statutory price harmonization measures. Construction services sales experienced significant growth, surging 62.0 percent to eight point four six million euros, fueled by recovering market activity and pipe construction projects. Gross profit grew by 10.5 percent to twenty-nine point five two million euros, while operating profit before depreciation and amortisation reached thirty point nine five million euros, up 12.5 percent from twenty-seven point five one million euros. Operating profit increased by 11.8 percent to twenty-one point two two million euros compared to eighteen point nine eight million euros in the previous year. Profit before taxes rose by 18.0 percent to seventeen point four three million euros, and net profit increased by 7.2 percent to fourteen point two four million euros, up from thirteen point two eight million euros. Net profit growth was slightly moderated by an increase in the income tax rate applicable to dividend payments. Cost of goods and services sold increased by 12.0 percent to forty-two point nine six million euros, influenced by higher staff costs, increased pollution charges which rose by 21.4 percent, and higher construction service costs. However, direct production costs remained stable at nine point nine zero million euros, as a 6.9 percent decrease in electricity expenses to four point three seven million euros offset higher chemical costs. The reduction in electricity expenses was primarily achieved through the successful commissioning and operation of the combined heat and power plant at the Paljassaare Wastewater Treatment Plant, which generated all required heat and over half of the electricity needed for treatment processes. Financial expenses decreased due to lower Euribor rates, resulting in net financial expenses of three point seven eight million euros. Capital investments in fixed assets expanded significantly, reaching fifty-five point seven four million euros compared to forty-nine point five three million euros in 2024, focusing on infrastructure renewal, smart water meter installation, and environmental protection. Total borrowings increased to one hundred sixty million euros following additional loan drawdowns, bringing the debt-to-assets ratio to 66.02 percent. Overall, the company reinforced its market position and advanced sustainability initiatives.
