During the 2025 financial year, Aktsiaselts Infortar achieved strong overall financial performance across its maritime transport, energy, real estate, and supporting business segments. Consolidated group revenue reached 1,837,000 thousand euros, marking a substantial increase compared to 1,371,775 thousand euros in 2024. Operating profit before depreciation, amortization, and revaluation of non-current assets, or EBITDA, rose significantly to 227,919 thousand euros from 145,275 thousand euros in the previous year. Operating profit for the year improved to 109,008 thousand euros compared to 77,024 thousand euros in 2024. The document explicitly links these increases in revenue, EBITDA, and operating profit to the full twelve-month consolidation of Tallink Grupp results in 2025, whereas Tallink Grupp results were only consolidated from August onwards in 2024, alongside an improvement of approximately 12 million euros in Elenger EBITDA and contributions from newly acquired agricultural entities including Estonia Farmid and Halinga. Conversely, net profit for 2025 decreased to 69,857 thousand euros from 193,670 thousand euros in 2024. The report explicitly attributes this decline in net profit to one-off effects related to the initial consolidation of Tallink Group recognized in 2024, such as bargain purchase gains. Total assets at the end of 2025 stood at 2,596,607 thousand euros, compared to 2,715,234 thousand euros at the end of 2024. Total equity increased to 1,175,404 thousand euros from 1,166,221 thousand euros, reflecting an improved equity ratio of 45 percent compared to 43 percent in the prior year. Interest-bearing liabilities decreased to 1,071,353 thousand euros from 1,223,287 thousand euros, driven by loan repayments across several Group entities, including Tallink Grupp, and adjustments aligning with market conditions. Net debt decreased from 1,055,708 thousand euros to 851,582 thousand euros. Basic earnings per share for the period were 3.43 euros, compared to 9.36 euros in 2024. During 2025, Infortar paid dividends of 3 euros per share, totaling 62 million euros, and management has proposed a dividend of 3 euros and 2 cents per share for the 2025 financial results. The closing price of the company share on the Nasdaq Baltic Exchange increased by 5.5 percent to 45.8 euros at year-end 2025 from 43.4 euros at the end of 2024. Significant business events during 2025 included the acquisition of a 96.57 percent share in Estonia Farmid OÜ in July, the acquisition of Oisu Biogaas OÜ, the ongoing construction of the Halinga biogas plant, and operational expansion in the energy segment where Elenger Polska managed Poland second-largest privately owned natural gas distribution network. In the maritime transport segment, Tallink Grupp carried 5,531,132 passengers during the year, reflecting a 0.9 percent decrease year-on-year, while undertaking vessel maintenance and chartering activities. In the real estate and construction sectors, developments included the completion of the Rimi logistics centre in Laagri, the initiation of construction on a Depo commercial space in Lasnamäe, and the start of Rail Baltica mainline construction by INF Infra under a contract valued at 67.2 million euros.
