Infortar reported strong operating growth in 2025, although net profit declined because the 2024 results included significant one-off gains from the initial consolidation of Tallink. Revenue increased to EUR 1.837 billion from EUR 1.372 billion, while gross profit rose to EUR 239.8 million from EUR 128.6 million. EBITDA reached EUR 227.9 million, compared with EUR 145.3 million in 2024, and the EBITDA margin improved from 10.6% to 12.4%. Operating profit increased to EUR 109.0 million from EUR 77.0 million.

Net profit fell to EUR 69.9 million from EUR 193.7 million, with profit attributable to the parent declining to EUR 70.6 million from EUR 191.3 million. The reduction primarily reflected the absence of the 2024 bargain-purchase and other one-off effects associated with gaining control of Tallink. Earnings per share decreased to EUR 3.43 from EUR 9.36. The Group’s 2025 results also benefited from the full-year consolidation of Tallink, whereas Tallink contributed only from August 2024. Elenger’s EBITDA improved by approximately EUR 12 million, and the results also included contributions from Estonia Farmid and Halinga.

Energy remained the largest revenue contributor, generating EUR 980.2 million compared with EUR 966.2 million in 2024. Maritime transport revenue rose to EUR 761.7 million from EUR 316.8 million as Tallink was consolidated for the full year. Tallink carried 5.5 million passengers, a 0.9% decline year-on-year, while cargo units decreased by 19.2% and passenger vehicles by 2.2%. Vessel chartering helped offset seasonal fluctuations and weaker demand linked to economic uncertainty and geopolitical tensions. Regional gas consumption fell by 9%, but Elenger delivered 18 TWh of LNG cargoes, unchanged from 2024.

The Group expanded and diversified its portfolio during the year. Infortar Agro acquired 96.57% of Estonia Farmid for EUR 37.8 million, acquired full ownership of Oisu Biogaas, and continued developing biomethane production. Elenger commissioned a 4.9 MW solar plant in Latvia and completed an 8.0 MWh battery-storage facility. Real estate activity included completion of the Rimi logistics centre and commencement of a 30,000-square-metre Depo development.

Cash generation strengthened considerably: operating cash flow reached EUR 384.0 million, compared with EUR 89.3 million in 2024. Cash increased to EUR 219.8 million, while interest-bearing liabilities declined to EUR 1.071 billion from EUR 1.223 billion. Net debt fell to EUR 851.6 million, and net debt to EBITDA improved to 3.7 from 4.0. Equity rose to EUR 1.175 billion and the equity ratio increased to 45% from 43%. Infortar paid EUR 62 million in dividends during 2025 and proposed a dividend of EUR 3.02 per share for the 2025 results.
