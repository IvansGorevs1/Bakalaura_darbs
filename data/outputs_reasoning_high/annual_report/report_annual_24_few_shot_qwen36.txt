The company reported robust financial results for fiscal year 2025, characterized by accelerated top-line growth and solid profitability expansion. Revenue increased by 40% to DKK 126.5 million, driven primarily by the successful launch and scaling of its core simulation titles. The three flagship games, Airline Manager, Truck Manager, and Farm Manager, collectively accounted for 67% of total revenue, generating approximately DKK 85 million. Truck Manager emerged as a major growth catalyst, posting a near twenty-fold increase to DKK 25.2 million, while Farm Manager contributed DKK 22.0 million within six months. Revenue from other transport titles declined by 46% as marketing resources were strategically reallocated toward the higher-performing flagship titles.

Operational efficiency and monetization optimization supported strong earnings growth, with EBITDA rising 26% to DKK 31.1 million and profit before tax increasing 109% to DKK 19.0 million. A key contributor was the strategic expansion of in-app advertising, which doubled to DKK 18.5 million and now represents 14.6% of total revenue. Because advertising income carries no platform commissions, the shift improved cost efficiency despite a 34% rise in direct expenses. Marketing expenditures expanded by 76% to DKK 40.5 million to fund user acquisition, which temporarily moderated the EBITDA margin from 27% to 25%. The EBT margin expansion to 15% also benefited from the absence of prior-year impairment charges totaling DKK 4.9 million.

The reporting period featured notable balance sheet movements reflecting disciplined capital allocation and temporary working capital timing. Year-end cash decreased by 9% to DKK 23.0 million due to the record marketing outlay, a substantial receivables buildup including a DKK 14.0 million VAT claim, a DKK 4.9 million share buyback completion, and a one-time DKK 4.7 million repayment of previously received research and development tax credits. Following year-end collections, the cash position rebounded to DKK 36.4 million by the end of January 2026. The company maintained a debt-free capital structure and a healthy solvency ratio of 78%, supporting a 20% return on equity.

Throughout the fiscal year, management progressively raised financial guidance on four separate occasions as execution consistently outperformed initial expectations, culminating in results that materially exceeded original targets. Looking ahead to 2026, the company expects continued profitable scaling, projecting revenue between DKK 130 million and DKK 153 million and EBITDA of DKK 28 million to DKK 36 million. Growth will be anchored by sustained monetization improvements across its established portfolio, targeted user acquisition investments, and the disciplined integration of next-generation titles into its development pipeline.
