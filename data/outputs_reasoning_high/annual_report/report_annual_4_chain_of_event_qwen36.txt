Eleving Group delivered its strongest financial performance to date in 2024, driven by sustained market expansion, operational efficiency, and disciplined risk management. Consolidated revenue reached EUR 216.6 million, representing an increase of nearly 15 percent compared to EUR 189.3 million in the prior year. Total net profit stood at EUR 29.6 million, up almost 21 percent from EUR 24.5 million in 2023. Adjusted for a one-time Romanian VAT liability of EUR 3.0 million that reduced net profit by EUR 2.6 million, underlying net profit amounted to EUR 32.2 million, reflecting year-over-year growth exceeding 31 percent. Operating profitability also advanced, with twelve-month adjusted EBITDA reaching EUR 89.8 million, an increase of over 16 percent from EUR 77.5 million in 2023.

The Group’s net loan and vehicle rental portfolio grew to EUR 371.2 million, marking a 16 percent rise from EUR 320.3 million at the end of 2023. This portfolio expansion occurred alongside measurable improvements in asset quality. Non-performing loan ratios declined to 6.1 percent for vehicle finance and 4.3 percent for consumer finance, down from 7.5 percent and 4.5 percent respectively in the previous year, demonstrating that growth did not come at the expense of underwriting standards. Active customers totaled 326,862. Liquidity and capital metrics strengthened substantially, with total equity increasing to EUR 108.1 million from EUR 65.4 million, primarily due to retained earnings and the successful initial public offering in October 2024. The IPO raised EUR 29.0 million in equity and was promptly utilized to redeem subordinated bonds and reduce higher-cost short-term borrowings, contributing to a decrease in net leverage from 3.7 times to 3.3 times while maintaining an interest coverage ratio of 2.4 times.

Management attributes the revenue and portfolio growth to the successful implementation of a second-generation digital self-service platform across European markets, which more than doubled loan application volumes compared to 2023 and streamlined internal processes. Additional growth drivers included the full integration of Sub-Saharan operations acquired in 2023, expanded customer acquisition in Eastern Europe and Africa, and targeted product pricing adjustments in select markets. The Group also completed its strategic exit from Belarus and the Western Balkans, liquidating or divesting operations in those jurisdictions without negatively impacting overall financial results. Regulatory adaptations were made in response to new consumer protection legislation in Romania, including revised interest rate caps, which were factored into product structures without disrupting growth trajectories.

Overall, the reporting period was characterized by record profitability, balanced top-line and bottom-line expansion, improved portfolio resilience, and a fortified capital structure following the historic listing on Nasdaq Riga and the Frankfurt Stock Exchange. The combination of technological modernization, disciplined credit oversight, and diversified funding sources has positioned the Group for continued scaling while maintaining compliance with bond covenants and regulatory requirements.
