Gigasun continued to expand during 2025, with stronger production and operating profitability, although the Group remained loss-making after financial items and faced a weaker financial position. Total revenue increased 9.8% to SEK 239.7 million from SEK 218.3 million. Net revenue from electricity sales rose 9.9% to SEK 203.2 million and represented 85% of total revenue, while other operating income, mainly subsidies, increased to SEK 36.5 million from SEK 33.5 million. Revenue growth was supported by higher production but constrained by lower average prices, a larger share of electricity sold to the grid, revised tariffs and adverse currency effects, which reduced revenue by SEK 18.0 million.

Operating expenses increased 5.5% to SEK 166.2 million, primarily reflecting the larger installed base, higher depreciation and increased maintenance costs. Gross profit rose to SEK 195.8 million, with the gross margin unchanged at 82%. EBITDA increased 12.3% to SEK 163.7 million and the EBITDA margin improved to 68% from 67%. EBIT increased 21.1% to SEK 73.5 million, raising the operating margin to 31% from 28%. Lower financing costs contributed to the improvement: interest expenses and similar charges declined to SEK 82.0 million from SEK 88.7 million. Nevertheless, the Group reported a loss after tax of SEK 7.2 million, compared with a loss of SEK 32.9 million in 2024. The result included a negative currency effect of SEK 3.8 million and a one-off cost of SEK 3.5 million; the CEO stated that profit adjusted for these items was SEK 0.1 million, compared with negative SEK 34.7 million in 2024.

Operationally, produced electricity increased 30% to 327.8 million kWh, and installed capacity grew 16% to 395.8 MW from 340.1 MW through the addition of 55.7 MW. The portfolio comprised 216 installations at year-end. The company signed 17 contracts representing approximately 41.8 MW of capacity, although signed orders at year-end were reported at 16.4 MW. Electricity tariffs in Jiangsu and Anhui reduced realized prices per kWh by approximately 8% in the second half, with an estimated 3–4% impact on revenue from the existing portfolio. Gigasun stated that lower financing and construction costs were expected to offset this pressure.

Financing remained a central issue. Refinancing agreements totaling approximately SEK 1.242 billion at 3.60% over ten years were initiated, while the SOLT4 bond was extended to 8 November 2026. Interest-bearing debt declined to SEK 1.383 billion from SEK 1.483 billion, but cash fell to SEK 45.1 million from SEK 89.9 million and the equity ratio decreased to 21% from 23%. The Group’s cash flow was negative SEK 34.9 million, as operating cash flow of SEK 101.1 million did not cover investments of SEK 208.1 million. Management stated that failure to secure further financing in 2026 would create significant uncertainty regarding the Group’s ability to continue under its current business plan.
