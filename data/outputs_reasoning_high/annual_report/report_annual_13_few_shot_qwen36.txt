The group delivered record financial performance in 2024, reporting a net profit of EUR 150.3 million, an increase of 7 percent compared to the prior year. This improvement was driven by robust top-line growth, with total net income reaching EUR 338.3 million, an 11 percent rise. Net interest income grew by 8 percent to EUR 273.3 million, while net fee and commission income surged by 24 percent to EUR 60.3 million, reflecting heightened customer activity and broader service adoption. Operating expenses rose by 14 percent to EUR 146.9 million, primarily due to workforce expansion, resulting in a cost-to-income ratio of 43.4 percent.

Balance sheet expansion was substantial, with total assets growing 23 percent to EUR 8.7 billion. The loan portfolio increased by EUR 990 million, or 28 percent, to EUR 4.6 billion, supported by strong corporate lending and retail mortgage growth. Deposit funding expanded by 21 percent to EUR 6.9 billion, bolstered by higher interest rates attracting term deposits and successful customer acquisition that added 38,000 clients. Credit quality remained resilient throughout the year, with non-performing loans maintaining historically low levels despite a contracting domestic economy. Strategic developments significantly shaped the results. The UK subsidiary capitalized on its full year of independent operations, expanding deposits by 195 percent and loans by 338 percent before launching retail services in the fourth quarter. In Estonia, the group captured market share across corporate and private segments, acquiring a highly profitable loan book along the way. These volume gains, combined with sustained elevated base interest rates, underpinned the record operating profit. Management notes that interest rates peaked in early 2024 before entering a rapid decline in the second half, which will pressure future net interest margins. To mitigate this cyclical shift, the group implemented structural cost controls, departmental restructuring, and enhanced automation, updating its financial plan mid-year to reflect 25 percent higher profitability targets. Regulatory capital ratios remained well above minimum requirements, supported by internal earnings generation and strategic bond issuances. Overall, the reporting period was characterized by aggressive scale expansion and solid profitability, positioning the group to navigate an easing monetary environment through disciplined cost management and continued cross-border business growth.
