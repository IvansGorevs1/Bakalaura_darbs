During the 2025 fiscal year, Trophy Games Development A/S achieved accelerated performance, driven by the rapid scaling of its core Transport Series and strategic shifts in monetization. Total revenue reached 126.5 million Danish kroner, representing a 40 percent increase compared to 90.1 million Danish kroner in 2024. This top-line growth was primarily fueled by the company's core titles, Airline Manager, Truck Manager, and Farm Manager, which collectively accounted for 67 percent of total revenue at 85 million Danish kroner. Airline Manager grew 28 percent to 37.8 million Danish kroner, Truck Manager surged to 25.2 million Danish kroner from 1.2 million Danish kroner, and Farm Manager contributed 22.0 million Danish kroner during its first six months following a July 2025 launch. Meanwhile, other transport titles declined 46 percent to 21.0 million Danish kroner as resources were reallocated toward the high-velocity core portfolio.

Operational profitability expanded alongside revenue growth. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization increased by 26 percent to 31.1 million Danish kroner compared to 24.7 million Danish kroner in 2024, yielding an EBITDA margin of 25 percent. Profit before tax rose by 109 percent to 19.0 million Danish kroner, up from 9.1 million Danish kroner, resulting in an improved EBT margin of 15 percent. Net profit reached 12.5 million Danish kroner versus 7.5 million Danish kroner previously. A critical performance driver was the optimization of in-app advertising revenue, which surged 100 percent to 18.5 million Danish kroner, accounting for 14.6 percent of total revenue and enhancing cost efficiency due to zero platform fees.

To scale user acquisition, the company expanded its marketing budget by 76 percent to 40.5 million Danish kroner, utilizing US dollar currency fluctuations to secure favorable marketing spend. Trophy Games maintained a robust financial position with a 78 percent solvency ratio and no interest-bearing external debt, while total assets grew 24 percent to 84.6 million Danish kroner. Shareholder returns included the completion of a share buy-back program totaling 4.9 million Danish kroner. Cash flows were temporarily affected by a 4.7 million Danish kroner repayment of historical tax credits following a Danish tax authority reassessment. Overall, fiscal year 2025 exceeded initial expectations, culminating in a record fourth quarter with revenue increasing 106 percent to 41.0 million Danish kroner, validating the company's data-driven portfolio model and long-term strategy.
