Talkpool reported stronger top-line growth alongside improved bottom-line profitability during the 2025 fiscal year, marking a decisive shift from its prior multi-year restructuring phase into sustained organic expansion. Consolidated net sales reached EUR 17.8 million, representing a 12.5 percent increase over the previous period and delivering the company’s first annual double-digit growth since its consolidation strategy began. This revenue acceleration was driven by stable operations across core markets, including consistent demand for telecom infrastructure planning in Germany, continued strength in Caribbean wireless services, and a major turnaround in Pakistan where local revenues hit record levels and contractual renegotiations significantly boosted gross profitability. Reported figures were partially offset in the first half by currency headwinds, as declines in the US dollar and Pakistani rupee reduced translated earnings. Profitability metrics reflected this transition, with EBITDA rising to EUR 1.83 million and an EBITDA margin settling at 10.3 percent, slightly below the prior year’s 10.8 percent as the firm allocated resources toward future growth initiatives. Earnings after tax advanced markedly to EUR 0.86 million, a 42 percent improvement that lifted the net margin to 4.8 percent from 3.8 percent last year.

Management successfully concluded its intensive cost-minimization program and redirected capital toward hiring, technological development, and market expansion. The company launched its long-term Vision 2030 strategy, establishing targets of EUR 40 million in annual revenue and a 14 percent EBITDA margin by the end of the decade, alongside a commitment to eliminate net debt. Recent initiatives include piloting bespoke artificial intelligence solutions for network monitoring, strengthening partnerships with global equipment vendors, and pursuing re-entry into the US telecommunications services market, though American project ramp-ups proceeded more slowly than initially projected. Financial stability has notably improved, with equity expanding to EUR 2.1 million and the balance sheet transitioning to a net cash position of approximately EUR 0.3 million. External auditors subsequently removed previous going-concern qualifications, confirming the group’s restored operational footing. While initial technology and personnel investments are expected to generate temporary pressure on margins and working capital, management anticipates these expenditures will translate into scalable, high-margin remote service deliveries within the next twelve months, positioning the business for continuous structural improvement.
