Konsolidator A/S reported improved financial performance and significant strategic progress during the 2025 financial year, driven by the execution of its Resilient Growth strategy. Total revenue increased by 25 percent to 25.3 million Danish crowns compared to 20.3 million crowns in 2024. This growth was supported by higher subscription fees of 20.6 million crowns and a 134 percent increase in onboarding and consulting fees to 4.7 million crowns, reflecting pricing changes initiated during the year.

Profitability improved substantially across the period. The EBITDA loss narrowed by 7.3 million crowns to 2.0 million crowns, compared with a loss of 9.3 million crowns in the previous year, supported by a positive EBITDA of 0.5 million crowns in the second half of 2025. The EBIT loss improved to 5.1 million crowns from 12.1 million crowns, and the net loss decreased to 6.6 million crowns from 14.9 million crowns. These results reflect higher revenue combined with reduced external expenses and structural cost savings from marketing restructuring.

Key SaaS metrics showed strengthening commercial traction and unit economics. Contracted Annual Recurring Revenue increased by 15 percent year-over-year to 24.4 million crowns, exceeding guidance. The company added 52 new customers, bringing total active customers to 283. The Rule of 40 score improved to 17 percent from negative 40 percent, the Customer Acquisition Cost payback period shortened to 23 months from 43 months, and the Customer Lifetime Value to Customer Acquisition Cost ratio strengthened to 4.1 from 2.7. Conversely, the ARR churn rate rose to 12.6 percent from 10.5 percent, while net retention remained stable at 94 percent.

Operationally, 2025 marked the transition from a single consolidation tool to the expanded Konsolidator Suite, incorporating new data management and predictive planning capabilities. The company also expanded its partner ecosystem, with 38 percent of new customers acquired through partners, notably through a strategic agreement with Exsitec AB. 

Financial position and cash flow developments included an operating cash flow of negative 1.0 million crowns, improving from negative 9.2 million crowns in 2024 due to working capital optimization and annual invoicing. The company secured 4.8 million crowns in net proceeds from share placements and repaid 1.5 million crowns on loans. Consolidated equity stood at negative 3.6 million crowns at year-end, while the parent company reported positive equity of 0.7 million crowns. Cash and cash equivalents ended the year at 0.4 million crowns.

For 2026, Konsolidator issued financial guidance projecting Contracted Annual Recurring Revenue and revenue of 27 million to 29 million crowns, a positive EBITDA of 1 million to 2 million crowns, and a decline in churn to between 6 and 9 percent.
