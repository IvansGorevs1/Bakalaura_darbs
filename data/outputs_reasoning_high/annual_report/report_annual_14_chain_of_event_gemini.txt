During the financial year 2025, Lyko Group AB reported top-line revenue growth amidst intense market competition, though profitability indicators declined compared to the previous year. Total net revenue increased by 11.3 percent to SEK 3,962.6 million from SEK 3,561.7 million in 2024, while total revenue reached SEK 3,975.7 million compared to SEK 3,579.1 million. Sales growth was primarily driven by the Nordic segment, where net sales grew by 12.0 percent to SEK 3,699.8 million from SEK 3,304.3 million. Conversely, the Europe segment experienced a slight decrease of 1.3 percent to SEK 130.7 million from SEK 132.4 million. Gross profit rose to SEK 1,658.7 million from SEK 1,568.8 million, though the gross margin declined from 44.0 percent to 41.9 percent. Operating profit decreased to SEK 78.2 million from SEK 117.4 million, lowering the EBIT margin to 2.0 percent compared to 3.3 percent in the prior year. The Nordic segment generated an EBIT of SEK 368.3 million compared to SEK 416.7 million, while the Europe segment recorded an operating loss of SEK -26.7 million versus a loss of SEK -33.6 million in 2024. The group posted a net loss for the year of SEK -3.0 million, down from a net profit of SEK 21.0 million, with earnings per share decreasing from SEK 1.37 to SEK -0.20. Cash flow performance improved significantly, with cash flow from operating activities rising to SEK 527.5 million from SEK 209.7 million in 2024. Cash flow from investing activities amounted to SEK -351.2 million, driven by capital expenditures for the new warehouse automation in Vansbro, store openings, and digital development. Cash flow from financing activities was SEK 150.9 million, supported by new loans of SEK 270.3 million and lease amortizations of SEK -119.4 million. Total cash and cash equivalents increased to SEK 452.5 million from SEK 140.5 million. Net debt excluding IFRS 16 decreased to SEK 292.5 million from SEK 333.1 million, and inventory rose to SEK 633.7 million from SEK 512.0 million. The equity ratio declined to 14.8 percent from 20.3 percent following an increase in total assets to SEK 3,086.4 million. Financial performance was affected by implementation costs and major strategic investments, notably the commissioning of the new warehouse automation in Vansbro, which expands total capacity to approximately 100,000 orders per day. Physical store expansion continued with 34 stores operating at year-end, including the opening of the first Fab Store in Täby Centrum, a fifth store in Norway, and a third store in Finland. In December, a reorganization of the Europe segment was initiated to lower overhead costs by closing local offices in Germany and the Netherlands. Reflecting expansion priorities, the Board of Directors proposed no dividend for the 2025 financial year.
