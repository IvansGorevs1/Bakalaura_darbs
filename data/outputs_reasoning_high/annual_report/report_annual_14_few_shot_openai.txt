Lyko reported continued top-line growth in 2025, although profitability weakened. Net sales increased 11.3% to SEK 3,962.6 million from SEK 3,561.7 million, driven mainly by the Nordic segment. Gross profit rose to SEK 1,658.7 million from SEK 1,568.8 million, but the gross margin declined from 44.0% to 41.9%. EBIT fell to SEK 78.2 million from SEK 117.4 million, reducing the EBIT margin from 3.3% to 2.0%. EBITDA declined to SEK 252.3 million from SEK 287.4 million. After net financial expenses of SEK 79.1 million and tax of SEK 2.1 million, the group reported a net loss of SEK 3.0 million, compared with a profit of SEK 21.0 million in 2024. Earnings per share decreased to negative SEK 0.20 from SEK 1.37.

The Nordic business remained the main growth engine. Nordic net sales increased 12.0% to SEK 3,699.8 million and represented 93.4% of group sales. However, Nordic EBIT declined to SEK 368.3 million from SEK 416.7 million. European sales decreased 1.3% to SEK 130.7 million, while the segment’s EBIT loss improved to SEK 26.7 million from SEK 33.6 million. In the fourth quarter, Lyko began reorganizing its European operations, including closing offices in Germany and the Netherlands and centralizing management from the Nordic headquarters.

The company continued investing heavily in capacity and expansion. The new automated warehouse solution in Vansbro, Lyko’s largest project to date, involved an investment of approximately SEK 500 million and reached the ramp-up stage during the year. Once fully operational, it is expected to support capacity of approximately 100,000 orders per day. Lyko also opened stores in Espoo and Sandnes/Stavanger and launched its first 500-square-meter Fab Store in Täby. The warehouse implementation affected results during parts of the year, while the investments are intended to support future scalability and more efficient logistics.

Cash generation improved substantially. Cash flow from operating activities increased to SEK 527.5 million from SEK 209.7 million, while investing cash flow was negative SEK 351.2 million, mainly because of warehouse automation, store openings, and digital development. Total cash flow was SEK 327.3 million, increasing cash and cash equivalents to SEK 452.5 million. Excluding IFRS 16, net debt decreased to SEK 292.5 million from SEK 333.1 million, although total interest-bearing liabilities rose to SEK 1,416.3 million. The equity ratio declined to 14.8% from 20.3%, reflecting a larger balance sheet and increased borrowing.

After year-end, Lyko announced a cost-savings program expected to generate approximately SEK 100 million in annual savings, excluding restructuring costs, and affecting about 70 full-time positions. A SEK 22 million restructuring charge affected the first quarter of 2026. Existing revolving credit facilities were extended to 2028. No dividend is proposed for 2025, as cash flows will be prioritized for continued expansion.
