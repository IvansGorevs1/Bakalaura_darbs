JLT Mobile Computers AB reported net sales of SEK 131.5 million for fiscal year 2025, representing an 11.1 percent increase compared to SEK 118.4 million in 2024. Order intake rose substantially to SEK 141.6 million, a 37.5 percent increase from the prior year. Despite top-line growth, gross margin contracted to 38.3 percent from 45.8 percent in 2024, primarily due to a nearly 20 percent decline in the US dollar relative to the Swedish krona, which impacted invoicing and inventory valuation given that 74 percent of sales are denominated in dollars. Total operating expenses decreased by SEK 6.3 million, reflecting organizational streamlining and the discontinuation of the JLT Software Solutions subsidiary, which lowered overhead costs by approximately SEK 5 million. Consequently, the group reported an operating loss of SEK 1.9 million, a significant improvement from the SEK 9.7 million deficit recorded in 2024. After taxes, the net loss amounted to SEK 2.5 million versus a SEK 7.7 million loss the previous year. Cash flow for the year stood at SEK 3.7 million, supported by a SEK 12.7 million reduction in inventory as the company normalized post-pandemic safety stocks.

The financial results were shaped by a mix of market pressures and strategic execution. Demand in the core warehousing and logistics segment remained subdued amid geopolitical uncertainty and tariff-related caution. However, this weakness was offset by strong commercial momentum in alternative segments, including ports, construction, and mining equipment. A notable SEK 22 million contract with an American food producer further bolstered order intake. On the product front, JLT launched the industry first rugged 15-inch Full HD vehicle-mount computer and an AI-driven screen blanking solution, while its VERSO series received validation for port operations by Navis. Management emphasized that structural cost reductions, portfolio renewal, and broader market exposure have strengthened operational resilience. Although customs uncertainties added administrative expenses, the company successfully insulated its cost base and improved capital efficiency. Overall, JLT navigated a volatile macroeconomic landscape with better profitability and order growth than industry peers, establishing a more flexible operational platform and positioning itself for sustained expansion as customer demand stabilizes in 2026. No dividend was proposed for the year.
