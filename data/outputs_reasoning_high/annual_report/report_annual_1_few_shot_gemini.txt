AAC Clyde Space reported a weaker financial performance for the 2025 financial year, characterized by lower net sales, reduced profitability, and negative operating cash flows, primarily driven by operational programme delays and timing effects. Net sales for the group decreased by 16 percent to SEK 295.3 million, compared with SEK 352.9 million in the previous year. The decline was mainly concentrated in the products and missions segment, where project-based revenues fell due to postponed milestone recognitions and procurement delays. Specifically, technical discussions between an end customer and a subcontractor delayed milestone recognition, shifting approximately SEK 32 million of revenue into 2026, while political and procurement developments postponed the initiation of the EPS-Sterna programme. 

Despite the top-line decline, the company achieved positive EBITDA for the third consecutive year, amounting to SEK 17.5 million, though this was lower than the SEK 46.7 million reported in 2024. The operational structure was reorganized during the year into two primary segments comprising products and missions and data and services. The data and services segment demonstrated strong growth, with net sales increasing to SEK 80.1 million from SEK 44.9 million, supported by the operational deployment of the Sedna-1 and Sedna-2 satellites and expanding recurring maritime data revenues. Conversely, EBITDA for products and missions decreased to SEK 16.8 million from SEK 51.6 million. 

Operating loss widened significantly, with EBIT dropping to negative SEK 40.0 million from negative SEK 4.0 million in 2024. Net financial items deteriorated to negative SEK 10.8 million compared with negative SEK 1.3 million, resulting in a loss after tax of SEK 46.6 million, compared with a loss of SEK 5.7 million in the prior year. Consequently, basic and diluted earnings per share declined to negative SEK 7.63 from negative SEK 1.00. 

Cash flow from operating activities weakened sharply, recording an outflow of SEK 72.6 million compared with an inflow of SEK 56.8 million in the previous period. Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the year stood at SEK 30.1 million, down from SEK 49.7 million, while total available cash including the bank overdraft facility reached SEK 80.1 million. The group strengthened its financial position through capital-raising initiatives, including a directed share issue in June 2025 that raised approximately SEK 64.5 million before transaction costs and a further directed share issue tranche in December 2025 with Bonnier Capital that raised approximately SEK 50.3 million. Reflecting these equity infusions, the group equity ratio improved to 80 percent from 71 percent at the end of 2024. 

The order backlog at year-end was reported at SEK 352 million, compared with SEK 641 million at the close of 2024. Subsequent to the reporting period, the company secured a major order valued at EUR 76.3 million from OHB Sweden for the EPS-Sterna weather constellation, significantly expanding its order backlog and establishing a stable foundation for future growth as the company continues to scale its vertically integrated space data and satellite manufacturing operations.
