AAC Clyde Space’s financial performance weakened in 2025 as programme delays reduced revenue, cash generation and profitability. Group net sales fell 16% to SEK 295.3 million from SEK 352.9 million, while total revenue declined to SEK 337.8 million from SEK 408.8 million. EBITDA decreased to SEK 17.5 million from SEK 46.7 million, although the company reported its third consecutive year of positive EBITDA. Depreciation and impairment increased the operating loss to SEK 40.0 million, compared with SEK 4.0 million in 2024, and the loss after tax widened to SEK 46.6 million from SEK 5.7 million. Earnings per share declined to SEK -7.63 from SEK -1.00.

Performance differed significantly between the two operating segments. Data & Services continued to expand, with revenue increasing 80% to SEK 80.1 million and EBITDA rising to SEK 27.8 million from SEK 21.1 million. Products & Missions remained the larger business but revenue fell to SEK 245.0 million from SEK 346.0 million, while EBITDA declined to SEK 16.8 million from SEK 51.6 million. The Group reorganised its operations into these two segments during the year, reflecting a greater strategic emphasis on recurring data and service revenues.

The principal factors affecting the results were timing disruptions in project deliveries. A technical discussion involving an end customer and subcontractor shifted approximately SEK 32 million of milestone revenue into 2026, while political and procurement developments delayed the start of the EPS-Sterna weather programme. Sedna satellite commissioning also took longer than planned, and VIREON satellite launches were postponed by the launch provider. These developments contributed to a reduction in order backlog to SEK 352 million at year-end from SEK 641 million.

Cash flow deteriorated materially, with operating cash flow of negative SEK 72.6 million compared with positive SEK 56.8 million in 2024. Cash and cash equivalents decreased to SEK 30.1 million from SEK 49.7 million, although the Group had an undrawn SEK 50 million overdraft facility, giving total available liquidity of SEK 80.1 million. The equity ratio improved to 80% from 71%, and bank borrowings were eliminated by year-end. Two directed share issues raised approximately SEK 64.5 million in June and SEK 50.3 million in December before transaction costs.

The company nevertheless advanced its long-term growth initiatives: Sedna-1 and Sedna-2 became operational, VIREON assembly began, and the INFLECION Phase 1 contract was signed. After year-end, AAC received an EUR 76.3 million EPS-Sterna order, launched the first VIREON satellites and increased its order backlog to approximately SEK 1.1 billion by the end of March 2026.
