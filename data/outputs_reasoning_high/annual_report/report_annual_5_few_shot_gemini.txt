Eleving Group S.A. reported a strong financial performance for the year ended December 31, 2025, marked by record-high revenues and continued expansion across its core business lines. Total revenue increased by 15.5% year-on-year to reach EUR 250.0 million, up from EUR 216.6 million in the previous period. This top-line growth was supported by higher market demand, successful execution of the Group strategy, and expansion across existing and new geographic markets, with Latvia, Romania, and Kenya serving as the primary contributors. Total loan issuances reached an all-time high of EUR 458.0 million, representing a 24.3% increase compared to 2024. 

Profitability indicators also demonstrated positive developments. Adjusted EBITDA reached a record EUR 101.9 million, reflecting a 13.5% increase compared with EUR 89.8 million in the prior year. Profit from continuing operations rose to EUR 29.2 million from EUR 28.8 million, while total net profit for the period stood at EUR 29.2 million, compared to EUR 29.6 million in 2024 when including discontinued operations. The net loan portfolio expanded significantly by 20.2% to EUR 446.3 million at the end of 2025, compared with EUR 371.2 million a year earlier. This portfolio growth was driven by robust performance across traditional and flexible vehicle financing, consumer lending, and newly introduced product offerings. 

During the reporting period, the company expanded its product and geographic scope. In European markets, the Group successfully introduced installment loans to address growing customer demand for flexible, unsecured products. In African markets, the Group launched smartphone financing in Kenya and Uganda, building a portfolio of EUR 13.5 million by year-end, and expanded into a new market, Tanzania, by introducing motorcycle financing. Furthermore, the company maintained a disciplined approach to cost efficiency and operational excellence, piloting AI-driven solutions such as voice agents to manage rising operational cost pressures. 

In the capital markets, 2025 was a defining year characterized by major financing activities. The company completed a EUR 40 million bond tap in March and successfully executed the largest bond issuance in its history in October by issuing EUR 275 million in new senior secured bonds to refinance debt maturing in 2026. Fulfilling its IPO commitments, the Group distributed EUR 19.65 million in dividends during the year, delivering a 10% total cash yield based on the initial public offering price. Total assets increased to EUR 577.7 million from EUR 476.3 million, while total equity stood at EUR 105.8 million, supported by robust cash generation and disciplined capital management that prompted Fitch Ratings to upgrade the Group outlook to positive while affirming its B credit rating.
