LHV Group reported a record net profit of EUR 150.3 million for 2024, up 7% from EUR 140.9 million in 2023. Pre-tax profit increased to EUR 175.1 million, while net income rose 11% to EUR 338.3 million. Net interest income grew 8% to EUR 273.3 million and net fee and commission income increased 24% to EUR 60.3 million. These gains were partly offset by a 14% increase in operating expenses to EUR 146.9 million, mainly because of higher employee numbers and related costs, and by expected credit losses rising to EUR 16.3 million from EUR 11.5 million. The cost-to-income ratio increased slightly to 43.4% from 42.3%, while return on equity declined to 24.5% from 29.0% and net interest margin fell to 3.48% from 3.88%.

Growth in lending and deposits was the main business development. Total assets reached EUR 8.7 billion, up 23%, the net loan portfolio increased EUR 990 million, or 28%, to EUR 4.6 billion, and deposits rose 21% to EUR 6.9 billion. Corporate lending in Estonia grew 21% to EUR 2.3 billion, Estonian retail loans rose 21% to EUR 1.9 billion, and UK SME lending expanded 338% to EUR 348 million. Management attributed the Group’s profit growth primarily to strong asset growth, cost management and sustained credit quality. However, the share of loans in Stage 2 and Stage 3 increased, and the Group raised impairment allowances as Estonia’s recession continued, although non-performing loans remained at a low level.

The operating environment became less supportive during the year. The European Central Bank reduced rates four times, and six-month Euribor fell from 3.9% in January to 2.6% in December. LHV stated that falling rates and the repricing cost of deposits negatively affected LHV Pank’s results, while intense competition in both lending and deposits further pressured margins. LHV Pank’s net profit declined 1% to EUR 140.5 million despite 6% income growth, reflecting higher expenses, increased credit losses and a EUR 24.4 million deferred tax expense related to expected future dividends. Its fee income increased 34% due to higher customer activity.

LHV Bank’s net profit rose to EUR 5.8 million from EUR 5.3 million, supported by 338% loan growth and 195% deposit growth, although results fell short of plan because of slower early-year lending and fluctuating financial-intermediary deposits. LHV Kindlustus increased net profit fourfold to EUR 1.2 million as premiums grew 21% and the insurance cost ratio improved. Varahaldus’ profit edged down to EUR 1.6 million, while assets under management rose 3%; no success fee was earned because cumulative fund performance remained below the benchmark. Paytech’s profit increased 40% to EUR 0.7 million.

Capital and liquidity remained strong despite balance-sheet expansion. CET1, Tier 1 and total capital ratios were 16.89%, 17.96% and 20.68%, respectively, all above requirements. The Group issued EUR 300 million of MREL bonds and EUR 20 million of Tier 2 bonds, while redeeming EUR 20 million of Additional Tier 1 bonds. Management expects near-term profit pressure from lower interest rates but plans continued loan growth and greater operating efficiency.
