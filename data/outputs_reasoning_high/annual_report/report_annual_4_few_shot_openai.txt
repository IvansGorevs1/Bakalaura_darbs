Eleving Group delivered record financial results in 2024, supported by continued portfolio growth, higher loan issuance, improved portfolio quality, and stronger capitalisation. Revenue increased by nearly 15% to EUR 216.6 million from EUR 189.3 million in 2023, driven mainly by higher interest revenue, which rose to EUR 203.7 million from EUR 176.3 million. Adjusted EBITDA increased by more than 16% to EUR 89.8 million, compared with EUR 77.5 million a year earlier.

Total net profit reached an all-time high of EUR 29.6 million, up approximately 21% from EUR 24.5 million in 2023. Profit from continuing operations rose to EUR 28.8 million from EUR 21.9 million. Reported earnings were affected by a EUR 3.0 million additional VAT liability assessed by the Romanian Tax Authority for prior periods, which reduced net profit by EUR 2.6 million. Excluding this one-off expense, net profit would have been EUR 32.2 million. A net foreign exchange loss of EUR 3.7 million, largely related to currency movements in Kenya, also reduced reported profitability.

Business volumes expanded significantly. Loan issuance increased 26.8% to EUR 368.6 million, while the net vehicle and consumer financing portfolio grew 16% to EUR 371.2 million from EUR 320.3 million at the end of 2023. Growth was led by Romania, Uganda, Albania, and the Sub-Saharan markets acquired in 2023. The integration of those operations was completed during the year, and the combined portfolio across the three main markets reportedly tripled. Loan applications in the vehicle finance business increased by 80.9%, supported by expanded sales channels and digitalisation. The Group also completed the rollout of its digital self-service platform across established European vehicle finance markets.

Risk indicators improved despite expansion. Non-performing loan rates declined from 7.5% to 6.1% in vehicle finance and from 4.5% to 4.3% in consumer finance. Management attributed this progress to continued underwriting discipline and risk-management improvements.

The Group strengthened its financial position through its October IPO, raising EUR 29.0 million in equity and listing its shares on Nasdaq Riga and the Frankfurt Stock Exchange. IPO proceeds enabled the early repayment of EUR 16.85 million of subordinated bonds and selective repayment of higher-cost funding. Total equity increased to EUR 108.1 million from EUR 65.4 million, while the capitalization ratio improved to 29.3% from 26.1% and net leverage declined to 3.3 from 3.7. Fitch upgraded the Group’s rating from B- to B with a stable outlook. At year-end, cash stood at EUR 34.5 million, compared with EUR 27.5 million in 2023.
