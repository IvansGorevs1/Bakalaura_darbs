During the financial year 2025, InCoax Networks AB experienced a decline in financial performance regarding revenues and profitability, while strengthening its overall cash position through financing activities. Net sales for the full year 2025 amounted to SEK 48,055,007, representing a 35 percent decrease compared to SEK 74,497,775 in 2024. The company stated that this revenue decline was primarily explained by timing shifts in customer orders and a more cautious market environment. Despite the lower sales volume, gross profit was SEK 29,919,750 compared to SEK 42,179,123 in the previous year, while the gross margin improved to 62 percent from 57 percent. Operating loss deteriorated to SEK 32,380,175 compared to an operating loss of SEK 19,378,406 in 2024. This earnings development reflected the lower revenue combined with continued high investments in strategic product development and commercial expansion. Capitalized development costs for the year were SEK 25,269,745, remaining nearly unchanged from SEK 25,649,493 in 2024. Loss after financial items and loss after tax both widened to SEK 32,559,663, compared to SEK 19,150,134 in the previous year. Earnings per share worsened to SEK -0.25 from SEK -0.18. Cash flow from operating activities improved to SEK -20,011,617 from SEK -27,117,256 in 2024. Total cash flow including financing activities turned positive at SEK 6,516,117, compared to SEK -12,758,133 in the preceding year. The positive total cash flow was supported by completed financing activities, including a directed share issue that provided approximately SEK 33.5 million before issue costs, along with loan facilities and credit arrangements. Consequently, cash and bank balances at the end of 2025 increased to SEK 8,748,046 from SEK 2,231,929 at the end of 2024. Total equity at year-end stood at SEK 109,778,755 with an equity ratio of 71.8 percent, compared to SEK 110,540,939 and 80.8 percent in 2024. During the year, the company initiated a structural cost reduction program expected to lower its ongoing cost base by more than 40 percent, representing annual savings of approximately SEK 25 million starting in 2026, aimed at reaching positive cash flow within the coming twelve months. The auditor's report highlights a material uncertainty related to the company's ability to continue as a going concern, noting that short-term financing depends on previously issued warrants being exercised to a sufficient extent or alternative financing being secured.
