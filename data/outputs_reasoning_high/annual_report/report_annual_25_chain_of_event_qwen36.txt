WindowMaster International A/S reported a financial result for the year ending December 31, 2025, characterized by a decline in revenue and profitability driven by specific customer dynamics and market conditions, despite an improvement in gross margins. Total revenue decreased by 8 percent to DKK 269 million compared to DKK 294 million in 2024. This contraction was primarily attributable to a slow start to the year, reduced sales to a key Nordic customer, lower performance in the Climatic business, and weaker growth in North America. Revenue fell short of internal budget expectations by DKK 21 million to DKK 41 million but remained aligned with management guidance.

Gross profit amounted to DKK 131.1 million, yielding a gross margin of 48.8 percent, which represents an improvement over the 46.9 percent margin recorded in 2024. Management attributes this margin expansion to a strategic shift in business mix, characterized by a reduction in key account revenue and a higher proportion of direct project revenue. This development supports the company's Accelerate Core strategy. However, operating earnings were negatively impacted by maintenance of fixed cost levels to support future growth and extraordinary expenses totaling DKK 2.5 million incurred during a ransomware attack that affected the group's IT infrastructure in May and June. Consequently, EBITDA declined by 25 percent to DKK 28.0 million, with an EBITDA margin of 10.4 percent compared to 12.7 percent in the prior year. Operating profit before tax dropped to DKK 8.3 million from DKK 19.7 million. The net result after tax was DKK 0.9 million, significantly down from DKK 10.6 million in 2024, with the effective tax rate elevated at 70 percent due to the non-utilization of tax loss carryforwards.

The overall market environment showed resilience in non-residential segments, aided by stabilized interest rates and regulatory drivers such as the EU Green Deal. In contrast, residential demand remained subdued. Despite global supply chain improvements allowing customers to order closer to execution, enhancing order volatility, the company maintained its local production capacity and mitigated risks related to geopolitical tensions. Cash flow from operating activities remained stable at DKK 28.5 million, supported by working capital optimization in inventories and work in progress. Net interest-bearing debt increased to DKK 38.7 million, and the equity ratio decreased to 23.5 percent, below the internal target of 25 percent.

Looking ahead, management provides guidance for 2026 projecting organic revenue growth of 7 to 14 percent, or DKK 290 million to DKK 310 million, with an expected EBITDA increase to DKK 45 million to DKK 55 million. Growth drivers include price adjustments, higher project volumes, and carry-over effects, while fixed costs are anticipated to remain steady to facilitate scalability.
