OODA AI reported a significant expansion in operating activity during 2025, with Group revenue increasing to SEK 32,058 thousand from SEK 1,061 thousand in 2024. Net sales, consisting entirely of consulting services, rose to SEK 31,880 thousand from SEK 1,056 thousand. Sales were generated primarily in Switzerland, which accounted for SEK 26,730 thousand, followed by other markets outside the EU and the EU. The increase reflected invoicing from several major transactions and the continued commercialization of the company’s AI platform.

Operating performance improved substantially. Operating loss narrowed to SEK 29,270 thousand from SEK 51,781 thousand, while EBITDA improved to a positive SEK 2,957 thousand from negative SEK 28,229 thousand. Results included approximately SEK 10,238 thousand in non-recurring costs associated with liquidating older company structures. Excluding these costs, adjusted EBITDA was approximately SEK 13,195 thousand, corresponding to an adjusted margin of about 32% of revenue. Personnel costs increased to SEK 9,210 thousand from SEK 7,432 thousand, although the company maintained a low cost base and made no major recruitment.

Despite the stronger underlying operating result, the Group reported a net loss of SEK 125,799 thousand, compared with SEK 83,355 thousand in 2024. The deterioration was mainly caused by financial items, which amounted to negative SEK 95,853 thousand versus negative SEK 29,900 thousand. These charges primarily reflected impairments and results related to associated companies and other financial holdings. Financial assets declined to SEK 8,387 thousand from SEK 96,036 thousand as the company wrote down, revalued and divested holdings while concentrating resources on its AI and decentralized infrastructure operations. Earnings per share declined to negative SEK 35.29 from negative SEK 29.00.

The company expanded customer and partner relationships during the year. The Win.Rocks AG collaboration reached a total contract value of approximately SEK 21 million, followed by a new agreement of at least SEK 15 million over 24 months in December. Agreements were also signed with HMM Deutschland and Sergel Kredittjänster, while partnerships with Allgeier Inovar, TIM Solutions and Phala Network supported broader distribution and decentralized confidential AI development. The platform was expanded to more than 150 AI models, and the decentralized AI initiative progressed from concept to an operational MVP and testnet.

Cash flow from operating activities remained negative at SEK 16,662 thousand, although the second half of the year generated positive operating cash flow of SEK 5,098 thousand. Financing activities contributed SEK 44,302 thousand through share issues, while investing activities used SEK 49,413 thousand, including SEK 8,716 thousand for development and AI infrastructure. Cash declined to SEK 3,335 thousand from SEK 25,108 thousand, and equity fell to SEK 23,859 thousand from SEK 119,371 thousand following the impairments. The company completed several capital raisings, reduced debt and decided on an additional approximately SEK 10 million issue after year-end. For 2026, management is prioritizing platform usage, partner distribution, self-service commercialization and improved profitability, with a broader platform launch planned for the second quarter.
