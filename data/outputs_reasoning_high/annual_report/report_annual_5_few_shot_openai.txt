Eleving Group delivered its strongest financial performance to date in 2025, with revenue rising 15.4% to EUR 250.0 million from EUR 216.6 million in 2024. Adjusted EBITDA increased 13.5% to EUR 101.9 million, while profit from continuing operations rose modestly to EUR 29.2 million from EUR 28.8 million. Total net profit was EUR 29.2 million, compared with EUR 29.6 million in the prior year, which included EUR 0.8 million from discontinued operations. Net profit before foreign exchange effects and discontinued operations increased 25.6% to EUR 40.8 million, although a larger foreign-exchange drag limited the improvement in reported profit.

Interest revenue increased to EUR 241.6 million from EUR 203.7 million, supported by substantial portfolio growth. Revenue was diversified across consumer lending of EUR 114.8 million, traditional vehicle finance of EUR 67.0 million, flexible vehicle finance of EUR 60.3 million, and newly launched device financing of EUR 7.9 million. Traditional vehicle finance declined 6.0%, but flexible vehicle finance grew 25.9% and consumer lending increased 18.0%. Administrative expenses fell to EUR 74.7 million from EUR 85.8 million, and selling expenses declined to EUR 7.2 million from EUR 9.6 million. Credit loss activity remained significant: loan write-offs reached EUR 49.5 million, compared with EUR 23.3 million in 2024, although the related release of existing impairment allowances largely offset the income-statement effect.

The net portfolio expanded 20.2% to EUR 446.3 million, including net loans and advances to customers of EUR 445.5 million. Loan issuances reached a record EUR 458.0 million, up 24.3%, reflecting strong demand and growth in both vehicle and device finance and consumer finance. The Group introduced installment loans in several European markets, launched smartphone financing in Kenya and Uganda, and entered Tanzania with motorcycle financing. Smartphone financing reached EUR 13.5 million by year-end and served approximately 260,000 customers.

Total assets increased to EUR 577.7 million from EUR 476.3 million, while borrowings rose to EUR 441.6 million from EUR 339.6 million. Equity declined to EUR 105.8 million from EUR 108.1 million, primarily in the context of EUR 19.65 million in dividends paid to parent-company shareholders and foreign-currency translation losses. The capitalization ratio fell to 23.7% from 29.3%, net leverage increased to 3.8 from 3.3, and interest coverage eased to 2.3 from 2.4.

Operating cash flow was negative EUR 52.0 million as the Group funded portfolio expansion, but financing activities generated EUR 70.9 million and year-end cash increased to EUR 39.1 million. Funding was strengthened through a EUR 40 million bond tap and a EUR 275 million five-year bond issuance that refinanced EUR 150 million of existing debt. Fitch affirmed the B rating and upgraded the outlook to positive. The Group reported compliance with its bond covenants, and the auditor issued an unmodified opinion.
