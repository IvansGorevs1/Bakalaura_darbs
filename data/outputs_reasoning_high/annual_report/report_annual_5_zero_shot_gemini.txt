Eleving Group S.A. achieved record-high financial performance for the twelve-month period ended December 31, 2025, driven by portfolio expansion across European and African markets. Total revenue reached EUR 250.0 million, representing a 15.5 percent increase compared to EUR 216.6 million in 2024. Revenue was generated across core business lines, including EUR 67.0 million from traditional vehicle financing, a 6.0 percent decrease year-on-year; EUR 60.3 million from flexible vehicle financing, a 25.9 percent increase; EUR 7.9 million from new device financing products; and EUR 114.8 million from consumer lending, an 18.0 percent increase.

The Group recorded all-time high twelve-month loan issuances of EUR 458.0 million, a 24.3 percent increase compared to EUR 368.6 million in 2024. Active customers exceeded 620,000. The net loan portfolio increased by 20.2 percent to EUR 446.3 million at the end of 2025, up from EUR 371.2 million in 2024, with Latvia, Romania, and Kenya as the main contributors. Adjusted EBITDA reached EUR 101.9 million, a 13.5 percent increase from EUR 89.8 million in the previous period. Profit from continuing operations was EUR 29.2 million, compared to EUR 28.8 million in 2024, while total net profit was EUR 29.2 million. Net profit before foreign exchange results and discontinued operations rose by 25.6 percent to EUR 40.8 million, up from EUR 32.5 million in 2024.

Capital management was a defining feature of 2025. In March, the Group completed a EUR 40 million bond tap to existing 2023 bonds. In October, Eleving Group successfully refinanced its 2026-maturing bonds and issued new 5-year senior secured bonds raising EUR 275 million at a 9.5 percent annual interest rate, representing the largest bond issuance in the company's history. Fulfilling IPO commitments, the Group distributed EUR 19.65 million in dividends during 2025, yielding a 10 percent cash return based on the IPO price. Fitch Ratings upgraded Eleving Group's outlook from stable to positive while affirming its B credit rating.

Key business developments supporting results included strategic product and geographic diversification. In European markets, the Group introduced installment loans, generating EUR 14.7 million in issuances. In Africa, smartphone financing was launched in Kenya and Uganda, reaching a portfolio of EUR 13.5 million by year-end, and the Group entered a new market, Tanzania, with motorcycle financing. Overall, the company maintained robust operational performance, strong funding access, and profitable growth.
