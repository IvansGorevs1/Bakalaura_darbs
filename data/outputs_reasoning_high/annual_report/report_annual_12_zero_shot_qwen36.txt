Consolidated revenue for Konsolidator A/S reached DKK 25.3 million in 2025, representing a twenty-five percent increase from DKK 20.3 million in 2024. This growth was driven by a DKK 2.3 million rise in subscription fees to DKK 20.6 million and a 134 percent surge in onboarding and consulting income to DKK 4.7 million. Profitability metrics showed substantial improvement, with the EBITDA loss narrowing by seventy-seven percent to DKK 2.0 million from DKK 9.3 million the prior year. The second half of 2025 marked a key milestone with a positive EBITDA of DKK 0.5 million, compared to a DKK 3.6 million loss in the same period of 2024. The net loss for the year decreased to DKK 6.6 million from DKK 14.9 million in 2024, aided by lower external expenses and a favorable change in deferred tax. Operating cash flow also strengthened, moving from a negative DKK 9.2 million in 2024 to a negative DKK 1.0 million in 2025, while primary activity cash flow turned positive.

SaaS performance indicators reflected enhanced operational efficiency and scaling. Contracted annual recurring revenue grew fifteen percent to DKK 24.4 million. Customer acquisition cost payback shortened dramatically from forty-three to twenty-three months, and the customer lifetime value to customer acquisition cost ratio improved to 4.1 from 2.7. Net customer retention held steady at ninety-four percent, though the annual recurring revenue churn rate rose to twelve point six percent from ten point five percent, prompting targeted onboarding process adjustments expected to reduce churn to between six and nine percent in 2026. Fifty-two new clients were added, increasing the average contract value per customer to DKK 87,000 from DKK 70,000. The Rule of Forty score rebounded from negative forty percent to seventeen percent.

Strategic execution centered on launching the Konsolidator Suite, expanding beyond core consolidation software to include financial planning and analysis tools, a data management platform, and an analytics Hub. The product roadmap accelerated with the April 2025 launch of the Microsoft Fabric-based data warehouse and advance sales for the FP&A module scheduled for early 2026. Commercial operations shifted toward a partner-driven model, with thirty-eight percent of new customers sourced through channel partners, up from twenty-two percent in 2024, notably supported by an expanded agreement with Exsitec AB. Geographic expansion solidified Iberia as the company’s third-largest market. Financial stability was reinforced through two private placements raising DKK 4.8 million, resulting in positive parent company equity of DKK 0.7 million despite negative consolidated equity of DKK 3.6 million primarily attributable to the newly established Spanish subsidiary. Management projects continued momentum into 2026, guiding for contracted ARR of DKK 27 to 29 million, full-year revenue of DKK 27 to 29 million, and positive EBITDA of DKK 1 to 2 million.
