During the financial year ended 31 December 2025, the Infortar Group demonstrated robust operational performance and financial stability across its core business sectors of maritime transport, energy, real estate, and agriculture. Consolidated sales revenue reached EUR 1.837 billion, representing a substantial increase compared to EUR 1.372 billion reported in 2024. This top-line growth was primarily driven by the full twelve-month consolidation of AS Tallink Grupp, which was consolidated from August onwards in the previous financial year, alongside positive contributions from Elenger and newly integrated agricultural businesses. Gross profit rose to EUR 239.842 million from EUR 128.628 million, and EBITDA increased to EUR 227.919 million compared to EUR 145.275 million in 2024. Operating profit reached EUR 109.008 million, up from EUR 77.024 million. However, net profit for the financial year decreased to EUR 69.857 million, down from EUR 193.670 million in 2024, with profit attributable to owners of the parent standing at EUR 70.550 million compared to EUR 191.253 million. This decline in net profit was mainly attributable to one-off effects recorded in 2024, specifically the bargain purchase gain of EUR 93.659 million recognized upon the initial consolidation of Tallink Grupp. Basic earnings per share were EUR 3.43, compared to EUR 9.36 in the previous period. Total assets stood at EUR 2.597 billion as at 31 December 2025, compared to EUR 2.715 billion at the end of 2024, while total equity strengthened to EUR 1.175 billion from EUR 1.166 billion. The equity ratio improved to 45 percent from 43 percent, reflecting the Group's enhanced ability to build own funds. Interest-bearing liabilities decreased to EUR 1.071 billion from EUR 1.223 billion due to loan repayments across several Group entities, including Tallink, resulting in a lower net debt of EUR 851.582 million and a net debt to EBITDA ratio of 3.7 compared to 4.0 in 2024. The Group's performance was influenced by several key business events and market factors. In the energy segment, regional gas consumption in Finland and the Baltic States declined by 9 percent, while Elenger expanded its infrastructure and renewable portfolio, commissioning a solar power plant in Olaine and a battery storage facility in Nīca, and operating Elenger Polska. In the agricultural sector, the Group expanded vertically through the acquisition of a 96.57 percent stake in Estonia Farmid OÜ in July 2025 and 100 percent of Oisu Biogaas OÜ, which significantly increased greenhouse gas emissions scope disclosures and added agricultural production lines. In maritime transport, Tallink carried 5,531,132 passengers, representing a minor 0.9 percent decrease year-on-year, impacted by geopolitical uncertainty and regional conflicts. Tallink also extended the estimated useful lives of its cruise and passenger vessels to 45 years, reducing annual depreciation by EUR 16.684 million. In real estate and construction, the Group completed the Rimi logistics centre in Laagri and initiated the Depo commercial space development in Lasnamäe, alongside infrastructure contracts for Rail Baltica. Maintaining its commitment to shareholders, Infortar paid dividends of EUR 3 per share during 2025, totaling EUR 62 million, and proposed a dividend of EUR 3.02 per share for the 2025 financial results, supporting its objective of maintaining a crisis-resilient and dividend-paying portfolio.
