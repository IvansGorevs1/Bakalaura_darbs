Novaturas AB and its subsidiaries reported consolidated revenues of 200.9 million euros for the financial year ended 31 December 2024, representing a 3.6% decrease compared to 208.3 million euros in 2023. During 2024, the group served 239.5 thousand travelers, reflecting a 7.6% decline from 259.3 thousand passengers in the previous year. The overall financial performance deteriorated significantly, primarily driven by an increased competitive environment and an oversupply of travel options in the Baltic States, which triggered intense price competition, particularly in Turkey and the Greek islands. 

Gross profit for 2024 fell by 20.9% to 20.7 million euros compared to 26.2 million euros in 2023, while the gross profit margin decreased from 12.6% to 10.3%. Operating expenses reached 27.5 million euros, marking a 25% increase from the prior year. This increase was heavily influenced by a one-off, non-cash goodwill impairment loss of 5.7 million euros associated with the Estonian cash-generating unit. Excluding this impairment, operating expenses amounted to 21.8 million euros, representing a 1% decrease year-over-year. 

The group recorded a negative EBITDA of 6.1 million euros for 2024, down from a positive 5.2 million euros in 2023. Adjusted EBITDA, which excludes the goodwill impairment, stood at negative 449 thousand euros compared to positive 5.2 million euros in the prior year. Consequently, the group incurred a net loss of 7.6 million euros in 2024, contrasting with a net profit of 3.4 million euros reported for 2023. Excluding the goodwill impairment, the group's net loss was 1.9 million euros. 

Despite weaker performance in the first half of the year, operating results showed a material improvement starting in September 2024. Between September and December 2024, adjusted EBITDA reached 2.3 million euros, aided by effective travel program optimization and reduced sales of last-minute trips. This optimization contributed to a high seat capacity utilization load factor of 98.9% in the fourth quarter of 2024, compared to 96.7% in the final quarter of 2023. 

As of 31 December 2024, the group's total borrowings stood at 9.3 million euros. The group did not comply with certain financial and non-financial covenants under its financing agreements at year-end, though waivers were subsequently obtained from Luminor Bank AS and Pagalbos verslui fondas. Cash and cash equivalents decreased to 394 thousand euros at the end of 2024 from 3.3 million euros at the end of 2023. Subsequent to the reporting period in early 2025, the company secured additional financing, including a 2.5 million euro credit line with SME Bank and loans from a new strategic investor, Neset Kockar. Mr. Kockar acquired a 23.2% stake in the company by April 2025, with plans to increase his holding to 33.19%, marking a new operational phase aimed at strengthening the company position and liquidity.
