InCoax Networks reported net sales of SEK 48.1 million for the 2025 fiscal year, representing a 35 percent decline compared to the previous year's SEK 74.5 million. The revenue contraction resulted mainly from timing shifts in customer orders and a more cautious market climate that extended sales cycles. Despite the drop in top-line growth, the company achieved an improved gross margin of 62 percent, up from 57 percent the prior year, while gross profit decreased to SEK 29.9 million. Operating performance deteriorated, with the operating loss widening to SEK -32.4 million from SEK -19.4 million in the preceding period. The higher loss reflected the lower revenue combined with sustained expenditures on product development and commercial initiatives intended to strengthen the long-term growth platform. The net loss after tax amounted to SEK -32.6 million, equivalent to SEK -0.25 per share.

Cash flow generation improved organically but remained negative; operating cash flow stood at SEK -20.0 million versus a deficit of SEK -27.1 million previously. Total cash flow including financing activities turned positive at SEK 6.5 million, supported by completed financing transactions. These activities raised approximately SEK 53.5 million before costs, including a directed share issue of around SEK 33.5 million, convertible instruments, and new borrowings. At year-end, the company held cash and cash equivalents of SEK 8.7 million, while its credit facility from principal shareholder Saugatuck Invest AB was fully drawn. The equity ratio decreased to 71.8 percent from 80.8 percent due to accumulated losses offset partially by equity raising.

Strategic operations focused on transitioning from development to commercial execution. A key achievement was the launch of the D2501 product integrated into the Nokia Gigabit Connect platform, marking the start of concrete deployments in North America and providing access to a broader operator base. Additionally, the D2508 product was launched through direct channels. To address the revenue shortfall and ensure financial stability, management executed significant structural cost reductions. The company reported lowering ongoing costs by more than 40 percent compared to 2025, yielding anticipated annual savings exceeding SEK 25 million from 2026 onwards. These measures involved reducing development spending and streamlining the organization. The immediate priority is reaching positive cash flow within the coming twelve months and scaling commercial adoption through established partnerships.

The annual report notes that the company operates in a market where fiber and fixed wireless access networks require efficient in-building distribution, a segment where InCoax positions its coaxial reuse technology. However, the auditor identified a material uncertainty related to going concern, noting that liquidity depends heavily on the potential exercise of warrants. If warrant holders do not exercise their rights, the company may face challenges securing necessary financing. Post-balance sheet developments include the issuance of a SEK 12.5 million convertible loan in January 2026 and further warrant programs to bolster capital resources during the critical phase of commercial conversion.
