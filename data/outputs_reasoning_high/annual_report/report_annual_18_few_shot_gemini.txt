During the financial year 2025, OODA AI AB experienced a transitional year shifting from build-up and structural stabilization to commercialization and growth within its core operations in artificial intelligence and scalable software infrastructure. The group achieved a substantial increase in financial revenue, reporting total revenue of SEK 32,058 thousand compared with SEK 1,061 thousand in the previous year, while net sales rose to SEK 31,880 thousand from SEK 1,056 thousand. This sharp top-line expansion was driven by the invoicing of major customer transactions according to plan, featuring key agreements with international partners such as Win.Rocks AG, HMM Deutschland, and Sergel Kredittjänster, alongside the expansion of partner distribution strategies.

Profitability metrics presented a mixed development. Operating profit improved significantly to a loss of SEK 29,270 thousand from a loss of SEK 51,781 thousand in 2024. Furthermore, the group generated a positive EBITDA of SEK 2,957 thousand, recovering from an EBITDA loss of SEK 28,229 thousand in the prior year. This earnings figure was burdened by approximately SEK 10,238 thousand in non-recurring one-off costs related to the liquidation of older corporate structures. When adjusted for these items, EBITDA reached approximately SEK 13,195 thousand, representing an adjusted EBITDA margin of approximately 32 percent of group revenue. Conversely, results from financial items deteriorated sharply to a loss of SEK 95,853 thousand compared with a loss of SEK 29,900 thousand, primarily driven by substantial impairments, write-downs, and results from associated companies as management streamlined operations and evaluated financial holdings. Consequently, the group reported a net loss for the year of SEK 125,799 thousand compared with SEK 83,355 thousand in 2024, with earnings per share worsening to SEK -35.29 from SEK -29.00.

The group's balance sheet underwent significant contraction and restructuring during the period. Total assets decreased to SEK 43,640 thousand at the end of 2025 from SEK 145,033 thousand at the end of 2024, while total equity dropped to SEK 23,859 thousand from SEK 119,371 thousand due to asset impairments and revaluations. The equity ratio stood at 55 percent compared with 82 percent in the previous year. Cash flow from operating activities improved to a negative SEK 16,662 thousand compared with a negative SEK 29,680 thousand, supported by a positive operational cash flow of SEK 5,098 thousand during the second half of the year. Cash flow from investing activities amounted to a negative SEK 49,413 thousand, while financing activities contributed a positive SEK 44,302 thousand through share issues. Cash and cash equivalents closed at SEK 3,335 thousand down from SEK 25,108 thousand. Management and the board addressed liquidity needs through capital injections during the year and subsequent post-closing measures, supporting the going-concern assessment as the company prepares for broader commercial platform scaling in 2026.
