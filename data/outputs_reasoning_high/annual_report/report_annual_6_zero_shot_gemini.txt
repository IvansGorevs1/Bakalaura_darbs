During the 2025 financial year, Gigasun AB demonstrated strong operational performance and improved underlying profitability amidst significant developments in cooperative and renewable energy sectors in China. Total revenue for the group increased by 9.8 percent to SEK 239,655 thousand, compared to SEK 218,264 thousand in 2024. Net revenue from electricity sales rose by 9.9 percent to SEK 203,179 thousand from SEK 184,795 thousand, representing 85 percent of total revenue. Other operating income, primarily consisting of government and regional subsidies, amounted to SEK 36,476 thousand compared to SEK 33,469 thousand. Gross profit improved to SEK 195,829 thousand from SEK 179,219 thousand, maintaining a stable gross margin of 82 percent. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization rose to SEK 163,733 thousand from SEK 145,760 thousand, yielding an EBITDA margin of 68 percent. Operating profit increased by 21.1 percent to SEK 73,478 thousand from SEK 60,677 thousand, with the operating margin improving to 31 percent from 28 percent. The result after tax improved significantly to SEK -7,206 thousand, compared to SEK -32,944 thousand in the preceding year, impacted by a non-cash currency effect of SEK -3,843 thousand and a one-off cost of SEK 3.5 million. Earnings per share after dilution were SEK -0.13, improving from SEK -0.58 in 2024. 

Operational indicators showed continued portfolio expansion. Total installed revenue-generating capacity reached 395.8 MW at year-end, up from 340.1 MW, representing an expansion of 55.7 MW across 216 installations and 197 customers. Produced electricity increased to 327.8 million kWh, reducing carbon dioxide-related emissions by approximately 161,000 tonnes compared to 124,000 tonnes in 2024. The company signed 17 new contracts representing 41.7 MW of installed capacity, leaving 16.4 MW in signed orders at the end of the year. Financially, Gigasun implemented major strategic measures, including agreements with Agricultural Bank Financial Leasing Co., Ltd to refinance approximately SEK 1,242 million in total, extending loan amortization periods to 10 years at a 3.60 percent interest rate to lower financing costs and improve cash flow. The remaining SOLT4 bond was extended to November 8, 2026, with an interest rate of 14.25 percent. Market conditions were affected by regional electricity tariff adjustments in mid-2025 that reduced realized prices by about 8 percent in the second half of the year. Total assets stood at SEK 2,296,782 thousand at year-end, down from SEK 2,528,093 thousand, while equity totaled SEK 488,201 thousand, resulting in an equity ratio of 21 percent compared to 23 percent in 2024. Interest-bearing debt decreased to SEK 1,382,613 thousand from SEK 1,483,061 thousand, and net interest-bearing debt closed at SEK 1,337,465 thousand, with cash and bank balances at SEK 45,149 thousand. Management notes that successful execution of future business plans remains dependent on securing new financing during 2026, creating significant uncertainty if alternative funding options fail.
