Eleving Group S.A. delivered record financial performance for the year ended December 31, 2024, characterized by strong revenue growth, expanded net portfolio, and record profitability supported by strategic and operational milestones. Total revenue for 2024 reached 216.6 million euros, representing an increase of nearly 15 percent compared to 189.3 million euros reported in 2023. The vehicle and consumer financing net portfolio grew by 16 percent to 371.2 million euros from 320.3 million euros in the previous year, driven by organic demand, expansion of sales channels, digitalization efforts, and the integration and scaling of Sub-Saharan operations acquired in 2023. Adjusted EBITDA increased by over 16 percent to 89.8 million euros compared to 77.5 million euros in 2023. The Group achieved an all-time best annual net profit of 29.6 million euros, marking a 21 percent increase from 24.5 million euros in 2023. This net profit includes the impact of an additional VAT liability of 3.0 million euros determined by the Romanian Tax Authority for prior periods 2017 through 2022, which reduced net profit by 2.6 million euros. Adjusted for this one-off tax expense, total net profit amounted to 32.2 million euros. Total assets increased to 476.3 million euros as of December 31, 2024, compared to 421.3 million euros at the end of 2023. Total equity experienced substantial growth, reaching 108.1 million euros compared to 65.4 million euros a year prior, primarily driven by strong profitability and the successful execution of the Group's initial public offering in October 2024 on Nasdaq Riga and the Frankfurt Stock Exchange. The initial public offering secured 29.0 million euros in equity, generating a share premium of 25.5 million euros net of IPO costs. The proceeds were utilized to redeem subordinated bonds, repay higher-cost borrowings, and fund ongoing business growth. Total borrowings principal stood at 339.6 million euros at year-end, with 72.0 million euros due within the next twelve months, comfortably covered by current assets of 232.6 million euros. Operational advancements during the year included the implementation of a next-generation digital customer self-service platform across European vehicle finance markets and significant scaling of electric motorcycle financing in Eastern Africa, where nearly 2,000 electric motorcycles were financed, enabling the Group to reach its 2025 environmental target a year ahead of schedule. Furthermore, Fitch Ratings upgraded the Group's credit rating from B- to B with a stable outlook in June 2024, reflecting improvements in performance, lower leverage, and stable business model execution. Following the balance sheet date, in March 2025, the Group issued additional senior secured bonds worth 40 million euros at a reduced yield of 10 percent to fund future growth and refinance existing liabilities, and expects a tax refund of approximately 1.15 million euros from North Macedonia following a court ruling regarding solidarity tax payments.
