During the financial year ended 31 December 2025, AS Merko Ehitus reported consolidated revenue of EUR 310.9 million, representing a decrease of 42.3% compared to EUR 539.0 million in 2024. The decline in revenue was primarily driven by the completion of several large construction projects that were active in previous years, combined with a slower pace of signing new construction contracts during 2024 and the first half of 2025. Gross profit for 2025 was EUR 55.9 million, compared to EUR 95.9 million in the previous year, while the gross margin remained stable at 18.0% compared to 17.8% in 2024. Operating profit declined to EUR 34.3 million from EUR 72.5 million, and the operating profit margin decreased from 13.4% to 11.0%. Net profit attributable to equity holders of the parent company was EUR 39.9 million in 2025, down from EUR 64.7 million in 2024, resulting in a net margin of 12.8% compared to 12.0% in the prior year. Basic earnings per share were EUR 2.26, down from EUR 3.65 in 2024. The group's financial position remained strong with total assets of EUR 415.1 million as at 31 December 2025, down from EUR 447.1 million at the end of 2024. Equity attributable to equity holders of the parent increased to EUR 260.6 million from EUR 254.3 million. The equity ratio improved to 62.8% from 56.9%, exceeding the long-term objective of at least 40%. Return on equity was 15.6%, compared to 28.4% in 2024, fulfilling the strategic objective of at least 10%. The debt ratio was 8.0%, compared to 7.5% at the end of 2024. Cash, cash equivalents, and short-term deposits totaled EUR 59.4 million at year-end, down from EUR 101.9 million at the end of 2024. Operating cash flows were negative at EUR 1.7 million, compared to positive cash flows of EUR 57.8 million in 2024, influenced by changes in inventories and working capital items. In the real estate development segment, the group sold 358 apartments with a total value of EUR 67.8 million, compared to 323 apartments valued at EUR 58.9 million in 2024. The second half of 2025 showed a clear improvement in signing new major contracts, bringing the group's secured order book to EUR 466.9 million as at 31 December 2025, up from EUR 340.6 million at the end of 2024. For the 2025 financial year, the Management Board, in coordination with the Supervisory Board, proposes to pay shareholders dividends of EUR 1.25 per share, totaling EUR 22.1 million, representing a 55% dividend rate.
