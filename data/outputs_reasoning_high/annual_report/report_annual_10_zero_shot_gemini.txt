During the financial year 2025, InCoax Networks AB experienced a weaker financial performance characterized by lower revenues and an increased operating loss, while completing its most development-intensive phase and pivoting toward commercial execution. Net sales for 2025 amounted to SEK 48,055,007, representing a 35 percent decrease compared to SEK 74,497,775 in 2024. This decline was primarily explained by timing shifts in customer orders and delayed projects. Despite lower revenues, gross profit reached SEK 29,919,750 compared to SEK 42,179,123, while the gross margin improved to 62 percent from 57 percent. 

Operating loss widened to SEK -32,380,175 from SEK -19,378,406 in 2024. This reflects lower sales volumes combined with continued high investments in product development and market expansion. Capitalized development costs for the year were SEK 25,269,745, nearly matching SEK 25,649,493 in the previous year. The net result after tax for 2025 was SEK -32,559,663, compared to SEK -19,150,134 in 2024, resulting in earnings per share of SEK -0.25 versus SEK -0.18. 

Cash flow from operating activities improved to SEK -20,011,617 from SEK -27,117,256. Total cash flow including financing activities turned positive at SEK 6,516,117, compared to SEK -12,758,133 previously, supported by financing activities that strengthened the balance sheet. Cash and cash equivalents at year-end amounted to SEK 8,748,046, up from SEK 2,231,929, and the equity ratio stood at 71.8 percent compared to 80.8 percent. To target positive cash flow within twelve months, the company initiated structural cost reductions aimed at lowering ongoing costs by more than 40 percent, representing annual savings of approximately SEK 25 million starting in 2026. 

Operationally, 2025 marked a strategic transition from development to commercial execution. The company finalized core products like the D2501 and D2508 and advanced its Nokia collaboration, achieving full platform integration and initial installations in North America at the beginning of 2026. While broader market conditions showed long-term growth in fiber and fixed wireless access extensions for multi-dwelling units, performance was affected by cautious operator spending and project delays. Additionally, the auditor highlighted a material uncertainty regarding the going concern assumption, noting dependence on the successful exercise of outstanding warrants or alternative financing.
