For the year ended 30 June 2025, SAF Tehnika Group’s revenue declined 4% to EUR 25.99 million from EUR 27.09 million. The parent company’s revenue decreased 6% to EUR 21.92 million. The decline was concentrated in the Americas, where revenue fell 14% to EUR 14.17 million, representing 55% of Group revenue. Management attributed this mainly to the timing of large projects moving into subsequent periods and temporary customer caution related to investment decisions and tariff announcements. European revenue increased 17% to EUR 10.0 million, while revenue from Asia, the Middle East and Africa decreased 5% to EUR 1.8 million.

Despite lower sales, profitability improved substantially. Gross profit increased to EUR 9.50 million from EUR 7.53 million, as cost of sales fell to EUR 16.49 million from EUR 19.56 million. The Group’s operating loss narrowed to EUR 1.29 million from EUR 3.22 million. Selling and marketing expenses decreased to EUR 7.43 million from EUR 7.84 million, while administrative expenses increased to EUR 3.36 million from EUR 2.91 million. The Group reported a net loss of EUR 642,642, compared with a EUR 2.37 million loss in the previous year; total comprehensive loss was EUR 678,071 versus EUR 2.41 million. The parent company’s net loss improved to EUR 551,789 from EUR 1.34 million.

A significant factor in the cost development was the EUR 1.24 million reduction in the provision for slow-moving inventories. Such inventories totaled EUR 3.9 million at year-end, compared with EUR 5.1 million a year earlier. Total inventories nevertheless increased to EUR 14.0 million from EUR 12.8 million, reflecting continued material reserves and higher work in progress. Research and development activity continued, with related costs of EUR 3.43 million, up from EUR 3.04 million, and EUR 570,000 of project co-financing received.

Cash generation strengthened considerably. Group operating cash flow rose to EUR 3.87 million from EUR 1.71 million, and year-end cash increased by EUR 2.6 million to EUR 4.81 million. The EUR 4.95 million bank credit line remained unused. Investments in IT infrastructure, production and research equipment, software, licences, certification and renovations totaled EUR 812,000.

The Group’s liabilities increased to EUR 10.33 million from EUR 6.59 million, largely reflecting higher customer advances and contract liabilities, which reached EUR 5.15 million from EUR 1.97 million. Equity declined to EUR 15.76 million from EUR 16.44 million because of the annual loss. Management considers the loss situation temporary, remains cautious about future sales and expects market activity to gradually recover. The financial statements received an unmodified audit opinion.
