Gigasun AB experienced continued operational growth and improved underlying profitability during the 2025 financial year amid a shifting market environment in China. Total revenue for the group increased by 9.8 percent to SEK 239.7 million compared with SEK 218.3 million in the previous year. Net revenue, which stems primarily from electricity sales and accounted for 85 percent of total revenue, grew by 9.9 percent to SEK 203.2 million from SEK 184.8 million. Other operating income, largely comprising government and regional subsidies, rose to SEK 36.5 million from SEK 33.5 million. Top-line growth was driven by higher production volumes, though partially dampened by negative currency effects and lower average electricity prices following regional tariff adjustments and a higher share of surplus electricity sold to the grid.

Operational profitability strengthened during the period. Gross profit rose to SEK 195.8 million, maintaining a gross margin of 82 percent. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization increased to SEK 163.7 million, yielding an EBITDA margin of 68 percent compared with 67 percent previously. Operating profit grew by 21.1 percent to SEK 73.5 million from SEK 60.7 million, with the operating margin improving to 31 percent from 28 percent. The group reported a net loss after tax of SEK 7.2 million, representing a substantial improvement compared with the net loss of SEK 32.9 million recorded in the preceding year. Earnings per share improved to SEK -0.13 compared with SEK -0.58.

The company’s portfolio expansion continued, with total installed and revenue-generating capacity reaching 395.8 megawatts across 216 installations at year-end, up from 340.1 megawatts and 200 installations. Total electricity generated during the year increased to 327.8 million kilowatt-hours. Order intake slowed, with the company signing 17 contracts representing 41.7 megawatts of capacity during the year, leaving 16.4 megawatts in signed orders at year-end compared with 38.8 megawatts previously.

Financial position and capital structure developments included significant refinancing activities. The company expanded agreements with Agricultural Bank Financial Leasing Co., Ltd to refinance approximately SEK 1,242 million in total, extending amortization periods to ten years at a 3.60 percent interest rate to replace higher-cost facilities. The remaining SOLT4 bond was successfully extended to November 8, 2026, carrying an interest rate of 14.25 percent. Total interest-bearing debt decreased to SEK 1,382.6 million from SEK 1,483.1 million, and net interest-bearing debt stood at SEK 1,337.5 million. Equity declined to SEK 488.2 million from SEK 577.3 million, resulting in an equity ratio of 21 percent compared with 23 percent at the end of the previous year. Cash and bank balances decreased to SEK 45.1 million from SEK 89.9 million, reflecting a total cash flow of SEK -34.9 million.
