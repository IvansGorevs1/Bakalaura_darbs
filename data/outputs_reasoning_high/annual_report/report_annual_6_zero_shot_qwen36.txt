Gigasun AB reported strong operational growth and improved underlying profitability for the 2025 financial year, driven by expanded production and strategic financing measures. Total revenue increased 9.8 percent to SEK 239,655 thousand, with net revenue rising 9.9 percent to SEK 203,179 thousand. Operating profit rose 21.1 percent to SEK 73,478 thousand, lifting the operating margin to 31 percent from 28 percent in the prior year. EBITDA reached SEK 163,733 thousand, representing a 68 percent margin. The group recognized a post-tax loss of SEK 7,206 thousand compared to a SEK 32,944 thousand loss in 2024. Adjusted for a SEK 3.8 million non-cash currency impact and a SEK 3.5 million one-off charge, profit turned positive at SEK 0.1 million, demonstrating a clear return to operational profitability. The company generated 327.8 million kilowatt hours of electricity, a 30 percent increase year-on-year, supported by an installed capacity that grew 16 percent to 395.8 megawatts across 216 installations.

Several key corporate actions and market developments directly influenced these results. Gigasun advanced a comprehensive refinancing program by securing additional funding totaling approximately SEK 1,242 million through agricultural bank leasing facilities. The new financing carries a 3.60 percent interest rate and extends the amortization period to ten years, significantly lowering interest expenses to SEK 76,944 thousand from SEK 84,165 thousand and strengthening near-term cash flow. On the revenue front, provincial tariff adjustments in Jiangsu and Anhui lowered average electricity prices by roughly 8 percent in the second half of the year. Management assesses the impact on the existing portfolio as manageable, amounting to a 3 to 4 percent revenue reduction that will be offset by declining construction and borrowing costs. Additionally, Swedish krona depreciation relative to the Chinese yuan negatively affected top-line figures by SEK 18.0 million and contributed to the reported foreign exchange loss.

Despite near-term regulatory pricing shifts, Gigasun operated within a highly supportive macroeconomic environment, characterized by Chinese GDP growth of approximately 5 percent and unprecedented national investment in renewable infrastructure. The company maintained its medium-term target of 1,000 megawatts of installed capacity, having executed 17 new contracts delivering 41.7 megawatts during the reporting period. Balance sheet metrics reflect a gradual deleveraging, with total interest-bearing debt decreasing to SEK 1,382,613 thousand from the prior year level, while the equity ratio settled at 21 percent against a stated target range of 20 to 25 percent. The combination of reduced financing costs, stabilized project economics, and sustained order intake positions the business for continued scaled operations, even as management continues to monitor capital requirements to support future expansion.
