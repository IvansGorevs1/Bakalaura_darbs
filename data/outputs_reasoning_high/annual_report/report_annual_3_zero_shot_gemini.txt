During the financial year ended December 31, 2025, Coop Pank AS experienced continued business volume growth alongside a decrease in net profit, driven primarily by a lower-interest economic environment. The group reported a net profit of 28.7 million euros for 2025, representing an 11 percent decrease compared to 32.2 million euros in 2024. Total net operating income decreased from 81.9 million euros in 2024 to 79.4 million euros in 2025. Net interest income declined from 77.6 million euros to 73.3 million euros, while net fee and commission income increased slightly from 4.3 million euros to 4.5 million euros. Operating expenses rose from 40.6 million euros to 41.5 million euros, resulting in a cost-to-income ratio of 52.3 percent compared to 49.6 percent in the previous year. Credit loss allowances improved to 3.3 million euros from 4.6 million euros in 2024, reflecting low-risk credit portfolio quality. Return on equity stood at 12.9 percent, down from 16.2 percent in 2024.

Despite the decline in profitability, Coop Pank achieved robust growth in key business volumes. The net loan portfolio grew by 19 percent year-on-year, exceeding 2 billion euros for the first time in the bank's history to reach 2.11 billion euros at year-end, up from 1.77 billion euros. Business loans experienced the fastest growth, increasing by 27 percent, while the home loan portfolio expanded by 17 percent. Customer deposits increased by 9 percent, reaching 2.05 billion euros compared to 1.88 billion euros at the end of 2024, with demand deposits growing by 11 percent and term deposits by 8 percent. The customer base expanded by 19,000 clients, or 9 percent, reaching a total of 227,000 customers by the end of 2025.

The broader macroeconomic environment was characterized by modest economic growth, receding inflation, and declining central bank interest rates. The European Central Bank lowered deposit facility rates, causing money market rates such as the Euribor to decrease, which subsequently affected the bank's net interest margins and net interest income. To optimize funding and capital, Coop Pank executed several strategic transactions in 2025. In the first quarter, the bank issued 250 million euros of covered bonds on the Irish stock exchange. Additionally, the bank entered into a synthetic securitization transaction with the European Investment Bank Group to provide capital relief and signed a 5 million euro subordinated loan agreement with the European Energy Efficiency Fund.

In accordance with its dividend policy, Coop Pank distributed 7.2 million euros in dividends in May 2025, representing 0.07 euros per share from 2024 earnings. For the 2025 financial year, the management board has proposed a dividend distribution of 0.065 euros per share, totaling 6.7 million euros in net dividends. Shareholders' equity increased from 211.6 million euros at the end of 2024 to 234.5 million euros as of December 31, 2025. Total assets grew from 2.19 billion euros to 2.70 billion euros, and the bank maintained compliance with all regulatory capital requirements, with a total capital adequacy ratio of 18.54 percent.
