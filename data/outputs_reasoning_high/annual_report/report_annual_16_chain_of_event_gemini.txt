In the financial year 2025, Nordic Fibreboard AS reported consolidated net sales of 7.33 million euros, representing a decrease of 3.4 percent compared to 7.59 million euros in 2024. The sales revenue from the production and sale of fibreboards was 7.33 million euros compared to 7.57 million euros in the previous year, while real estate management revenue declined to 3 thousand euros from 25 thousand euros due to the termination of rental agreements in August 2024. Sales within the European Union remained relatively flat at 92 percent of total sales, whereas sales to Africa, Asia, and the Middle East dropped. 

The financial performance of the Group deteriorated during the period, driven by underutilized production capacity and planned factory shutdowns. Consolidated EBITDA for 2025 was negative 420 thousand euros, worsening from negative 25 thousand euros in 2024. The negative EBITDA was partly attributable to a planned factory shutdown in December 2025 to transition to a new gas boiler house, during which personnel, ongoing maintenance, and repair costs continued to accrue, having a negative impact similar to the unplanned force majeure shutdown in December 2024. Operating loss widened to 940 thousand euros from 547 thousand euros in 2024. Net financial costs were 214 thousand euros, resulting in a consolidated net loss of 1.15 million euros for 2025, compared to a net loss of 786 thousand euros in 2024. Basic and diluted loss per share were 0.14 euros, compared to 0.17 euros in the prior year.

Total assets of the Group increased to 9.32 million euros as of December 31, 2025, up from 8.25 million euros at the end of 2024. Total liabilities rose to 4.86 million euros from 4.64 million euros, while borrowings decreased to 3.17 million euros from 3.72 million euros. Total equity increased to 4.46 million euros from 3.62 million euros, primarily supported by a successful share capital increase completed in October 2025, which raised 2.0 million euros through the issuance of 4,000,000 new shares, bringing total share capital to 850 thousand euros across 8,499,061 shares. 

During the year, the Group advanced its real estate and production developments. The former furniture factory property at Suur-Jõe 48 was divided into ten smaller properties in April 2025 under the Admirali quarter development project, and Pärnu Riverside Development was merged into Nordic Fibreboard AS in July 2025. Furthermore, subsequent to the balance sheet date on January 8, 2026, the company entered into a preliminary sale-purchase agreement for the sale of the ten properties in the Admirali quarter, with final execution expected by June 30, 2026. The company also invested in modern energy solutions, including upgrading the boiler system, which was completed in January 2026, aimed at improving production energy efficiency and reliability amidst a challenging economic environment characterized by slow economic growth, high tax-driven inflation, and weak domestic demand in Estonia.
