The company reported a clear stabilization of financial performance during the 2025 reporting period, marked by consistent top-line growth alongside a substantial narrowing of losses. Net sales rose to SEK 131.5 million, representing an 11.1 percent increase over the prior year, buoyed by active pipeline execution and order intake that surged by 37.5 percent to reach SEK 141.6 million. This commercial momentum translated into improved operational leverage, as total operating expenses fell to SEK 52.4 million from SEK 57.3 million. The reduction stemmed from deliberate structural adjustments, notably the integration of software development workflows and the strategic wind-down of the JLT Software Solutions subsidiary, which collectively trimmed overhead and research spending by SEK 6.3 million. As a direct consequence, the operating deficit contracted sharply to minus SEK 1.9 million from minus SEK 9.7 million, while the bottom-line net loss similarly tightened to minus SEK 2.5 million compared to minus SEK 7.7 million previously. Despite these operational improvements, profitability faced downward pressure from currency dynamics, with the gross margin declining to 38.3 percent from 45.8 percent. The contraction was largely attributable to a near 20 percent depreciation of the US dollar against the Swedish krona, a significant headwind given that 74 percent of invoices are priced in American currency. Additionally, unresolved customs regulations increased administrative expenditures and prolonged customer decision-making timelines. Market dynamics reflected a cautious macroeconomic environment, particularly within the traditional warehousing and logistics vertical, where end-users adopted delayed procurement strategies. Conversely, emerging traction in port infrastructure, mining, and heavy construction sectors provided meaningful diversification, highlighted by a secured supply contract valued at SEK 22 million for an American food distribution client. Strategic product investments further reinforced the company’s positioning, including the launch of a pioneering 15-inch high-definition rugged terminal and an integrated artificial intelligence safeguard for industrial vehicle displays. Financial discipline extended to balance sheet management, as targeted depletion of pandemic-era safety stocks reduced inventories by SEK 12.7 million to SEK 25.5 million, thereby unlocking working capital and generating a cash flow of plus SEK 3.7 million. With equity adjusted to SEK 41.8 million and a solidity ratio of 59 percent, the organization concluded the year with a strengthened foundational cost base, expanded regional market penetration, and a robust order backlog of SEK 24.1 million, establishing a consolidated platform for scaling revenues as broader industrial capital expenditure cycles resume.
