During the financial year 2025, JLT Mobile Computers AB reported net sales of 131.5 million Swedish kronor, representing an 11.1 percent increase compared to 118.4 million Swedish kronor in 2024. Order intake showed stronger growth, rising by 37.5 percent to 141.6 million Swedish kronor from 103.0 million Swedish kronor. This top-line growth was supported by major customer contracts, including a 22 million Swedish kronor deal with an American food producer, alongside expansion into new segments such as ports and mining equipment. Despite subdued demand in the core warehousing and logistics segment caused by geopolitical uncertainty, the company successfully increased invoicing volumes and expanded its market exposure.

The financial results showed an improved operating profit of negative 1.9 million Swedish kronor, compared to negative 9.7 million Swedish kronor in the previous year. Profit after tax also improved to negative 2.5 million Swedish kronor from negative 7.7 million Swedish kronor in 2024. This earnings improvement was primarily driven by lower overhead and research and development costs, which decreased by 6.3 million Swedish kronor as a result of structural cost savings implemented following the consolidation of product development and the discontinuation of the subsidiary JLT Software Solutions AB. Furthermore, total depreciations decreased significantly to 1.3 million Swedish kronor compared to 9.0 million Swedish kronor in 2024, when the previous year included large write-downs on capitalized development costs.

However, the gross margin declined to 38.3 percent from 45.8 percent in 2024, causing gross profit to decrease to 50.4 million Swedish kronor from 54.2 million Swedish kronor. The report explicitly connects this margin compression to currency fluctuations, noting that the US dollar lost nearly 20 percent against the Swedish krona during the year while 74 percent of sales were invoiced in dollars. Invoicing occurred at weaker dollar rates than when quotes were given, and inventory was revalued at current purchase rates. Customs and tariff uncertainties also increased administrative burdens and investigation costs.

The company’s balance sheet and cash position strengthened through deliberate capital management. Inventories were reduced by 12.7 million Swedish kronor down to 25.5 million Swedish kronor, successfully drawing down safety stocks built up after the pandemic. Cash and cash equivalents increased to 16.9 million Swedish kronor at year-end compared to 13.3 million Swedish kronor at the end of 2024. Total equity stood at 41.8 million Swedish kronor with a solidity of 59 percent, up from 54 percent in the previous year. The Board of Directors proposed that no dividend be paid for the 2025 financial year, matching the previous year. Entering 2026, JLT maintains an order backlog of 24.1 million Swedish kronor and a lower, more flexible cost base positioned for expected market recovery.
