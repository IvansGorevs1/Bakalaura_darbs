During the 2025 financial year, AS Merko Ehitus experienced a moderation in financial performance following record results in the previous period. Consolidated revenue for the year reached 310.9 million euros, representing a decrease of 42.3 percent compared to 539.0 million euros in 2024. This reduction was primarily driven by lower volume in the construction service segment as several large-scale projects initiated in earlier years were completed, while the pace of signing new construction contracts was slower through 2024 and the first half of 2025. Gross profit declined to 55.9 million euros from 95.9 million euros, with the gross margin remaining stable at 18.0 percent compared to 17.8 percent. Operating profit fell to 34.3 million euros from 72.5 million euros, and the operating profit margin decreased from 13.4 percent to 11.0 percent, reflecting a return to traditional sector margins after the expiration of exceptional contracts completed in 2024. Net profit attributable to equity holders of the parent company decreased to 39.9 million euros from 64.7 million euros, while the net margin improved slightly to 12.8 percent from 12.0 percent.

In the real estate development segment, performance remained robust with revenue increasing by 13.8 percent to 73.4 million euros, supported by higher apartment handovers. The group sold 358 apartments for 67.8 million euros, compared to 323 apartments valued at 58.9 million euros in 2024. Profitability was further reinforced by strong contributions from joint ventures, particularly network companies operating under the Connecto brand. Financial position remained exceptionally strong, underscored by an equity ratio of 62.8 percent at year-end compared to 56.9 percent in 2024, and total assets standing at 415.1 million euros. Return on equity was 15.6 percent, down from 28.4 percent, but remained well above the company's long-term target of 10 percent. Cash and short-term deposits totaled 59.4 million euros at the end of 2025, down from 101.9 million euros, influenced by operational cash flows and dividend payments of 33.6 million euros.

The second half of 2025 demonstrated a clear operational recovery, with successful contract negotiations resulting in major new construction and infrastructure agreements. Consequently, the group’s secured order book rose to 466.9 million euros as of December 31, 2025, compared to 340.6 million euros at the previous year-end, providing a stable workload for future periods. Geopolitical uncertainties and high interest rates continued to restrain private sector investments, shifting market activity primarily toward public infrastructure and national defence. Nonetheless, improving conditions enabled the group to start construction on significantly more new apartments, supporting future development potential. Reflecting the company's stable capitalization and performance, the management board proposed a dividend distribution of 1.25 euros per share, totaling 22.1 million euros.
