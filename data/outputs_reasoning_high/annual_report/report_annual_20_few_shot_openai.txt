AS Tallink Grupp’s financial performance weakened in 2025 amid subdued consumer and business confidence, economic challenges in its core markets and heightened geopolitical uncertainty. Revenue declined by 2.6% to EUR 765.3 million from EUR 785.8 million in 2024, while gross profit fell to EUR 143.2 million from EUR 153.6 million. EBITDA decreased by EUR 45.1 million to EUR 130.1 million, reducing the EBITDA margin from 22.3% to 17.0%. Operating profit declined to EUR 50.3 million from EUR 77.4 million, and net profit fell 57.1% to EUR 17.3 million, or EUR 0.023 per share, compared with EUR 40.3 million, or EUR 0.054 per share, previously. The net profit margin consequently decreased from 5.1% to 2.3%.

Passenger volumes were broadly stable, with 5.5 million passengers carried, down 0.9%, but cargo units decreased 19.2% to 245,004 and passenger vehicles declined 2.2% to 760,473. Revenue from core route operations fell by EUR 13.7 million to EUR 625.2 million, while the segment result declined to EUR 70.5 million from EUR 81.5 million. Estonia-Finland revenue increased modestly to EUR 314.5 million, but its result fell by EUR 10.9 million. Finland-Sweden revenue declined to EUR 225.5 million and its result dropped to EUR 1.3 million, partly affected by 68 days of vessel maintenance. Estonia-Sweden revenue decreased to EUR 85.3 million, although its loss narrowed to EUR 1.2 million. Other segment revenue fell to EUR 145.5 million, while its result remained broadly stable at EUR 28.5 million.

Profitability was supported by cost-efficiency measures, vessel chartering and lower depreciation. Depreciation and amortisation declined by EUR 18.0 million to EUR 79.8 million after the estimated useful lives of most cruise and passenger vessels were extended to 45 years and three vessels were sold. However, personnel expenses rose 4.3% to EUR 201.0 million, while fuel and emission costs increased slightly to EUR 96.5 million. Lower loan balances and interest rates reduced net finance costs to EUR 21.4 million from EUR 28.3 million.

Operating cash flow remained strong at EUR 127.1 million, although below EUR 153.4 million in 2024. Vessel disposals generated EUR 78.8 million, contributing to positive investing cash flow of EUR 46.1 million. After EUR 178.5 million of financing outflows, including EUR 44.6 million in dividends and EUR 97.6 million in loan repayments, cash declined to EUR 13.5 million. Net debt nevertheless fell to EUR 432.4 million from EUR 537.7 million, while the equity ratio improved to 56.4%. Management plans to propose another EUR 0.06-per-share dividend. The 2026 outlook remains exposed to geopolitical instability, energy-price volatility, tax changes and weak travel demand.
