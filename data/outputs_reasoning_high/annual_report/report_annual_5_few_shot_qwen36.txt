Eleving Group reported strong financial growth during the 2025 fiscal year, driven by robust demand across its diversified product portfolio and successful geographic expansion. Consolidated revenue increased by 15.4% to EUR 250.0 million, supported primarily by resilient sales in consumer lending, which rose 18.0% to EUR 114.8 million, and flexible vehicle financing, which grew 25.9% to EUR 60.3 million. The group successfully launched device financing, contributing EUR 7.9 million in its first full year, while traditional vehicle financing saw a moderate decline of 6.0%. Operating profitability improved, with adjusted EBITDA reaching a record EUR 101.9 million, reflecting a 13.5% year-over-year increase. Net profit from continuing operations remained stable at EUR 29.2 million, though underlying earnings before foreign exchange impacts and discontinued items rose by 25.6% to EUR 40.8 million.

The company’s loan book expanded significantly, with the net portfolio growing 20.2% to EUR 446.3 million and total loan issuances hitting an all-time high of EUR 458.0 million, up 24.3% from the previous year. This growth was fueled by strong customer acquisition, exceeding 2.7 million loan applications globally, and higher retention rates. Strategically, Eleving accelerated its market penetration by entering Tanzania with motorcycle financing and scaling smartphone financing in Kenya and Uganda, where the new product segment quickly amassed a EUR 13.5 million portfolio. The introduction of unsecured installment loans in several European markets also provided additional tailwinds. Operational efficiencies were maintained through digital transformation initiatives, including the pilot testing of AI-driven customer service tools, and continued focus on optimizing the cost-to-income ratio.

On the capital front, Eleving demonstrated disciplined treasury management by completing two major debt transactions, most notably a EUR 275 million senior secured bond offering that replaced maturing liabilities. These actions strengthened the group’s funding base and led to a positive credit rating outlook from Fitch Ratings. Shareholders received EUR 19.65 million in dividends, delivering a 10% cash yield relative to the IPO price. Looking ahead, the group remains positioned for sustainable expansion, emphasizing portfolio growth in Africa and Europe, further product diversification, and ongoing technological integration to support its double-digit growth trajectory.
