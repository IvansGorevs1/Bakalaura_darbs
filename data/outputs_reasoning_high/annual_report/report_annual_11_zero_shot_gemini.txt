During the financial year 2025, JLT Mobile Computers AB experienced strong commercial progress and sales growth despite a challenging external market environment characterized by geopolitical uncertainty and weak demand in its largest segment, warehousing and logistics. Consolidated net sales for the full year 2025 rose by 11.1 percent to SEK 131.5 million, compared to SEK 118.4 million in 2024. Order intake showed a robust increase of 37.5 percent, reaching SEK 141.6 million compared to SEK 103.0 million the previous year, supported by strategic deals including a major SEK 22 million order to an American food producer. 

Despite top-line growth, profitability was heavily impacted by currency fluctuations. The US dollar lost nearly 20 percent against the Swedish krona during the year, while dollar-denominated sales accounted for 74 percent of total turnover. Because invoicing occurred at a weaker dollar rate than when quotes were given and inventory was revalued at current purchase rates, the gross margin dropped by 7.5 percentage points to 38.3 percent, resulting in a gross profit of SEK 50.4 million compared to SEK 54.2 million in 2024. 

To counter market pressures and improve scalability, the company implemented structural cost-saving measures, including coordinating software development and discontinuing the subsidiary JLT Software Solutions AB, which reduced overhead costs by SEK 5 million. Consequently, operating expenses decreased, and operating profit significantly strengthened to SEK -1.9 million from SEK -9.7 million the previous year, with the operating margin improving from -8.2 percent to -1.4 percent. EBITDA similarly improved slightly to SEK -1.7 million from SEK -2.1 million. Net financial items resulted in a profit before tax of SEK -1.8 million, compared to SEK -9.6 million in 2024. After tax, the net loss for the year narrowed to SEK -2.5 million from SEK -7.7 million, yielding earnings per share of SEK -0.09 compared to SEK -0.27 previously. 

The company successfully focused on reducing pandemic-era safety stocks, lowering inventories by SEK 12.7 million to SEK 25.5 million at year-end, which enhanced capital efficiency and cash flow. Cash flow from operating activities reached SEK 5.9 million, and total cash and cash equivalents increased to SEK 16.9 million from SEK 13.3 million. The order backlog at the end of 2025 rose to SEK 24.1 million from SEK 13.9 million. 

Market factors also included ongoing tariff and customs concerns, which increased administrative burdens and investigation costs, though Swedish-manufactured computers remained exempt from US export duties. Operationally, JLT expanded its market focus into ports, construction, and mining equipment, and advanced its product portfolio by launching the JLT6015 15-inch Full HD rugged computer and an AI-powered screen blanking safety feature. The Board of Directors proposed that no dividend be paid for the 2025 fiscal year, matching the previous year.
