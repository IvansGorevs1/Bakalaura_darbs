Infortar reported a strong operating year in 2025, with revenue rising to EUR 1.837 billion from EUR 1.372 billion and EBITDA increasing to EUR 227.9 million from EUR 145.3 million. The EBITDA margin improved to 12.4% from 10.6%. Gross profit increased to EUR 239.8 million from EUR 128.6 million, while operating profit rose to EUR 109.0 million from EUR 77.0 million. However, net profit declined substantially to EUR 69.9 million from EUR 193.7 million, and profit attributable to the parent fell to EUR 70.6 million from EUR 191.3 million. Earnings per share decreased to EUR 3.43 from EUR 9.36. Management attributes the lower net profit mainly to one-off effects from the initial consolidation of Tallink in 2024, including a EUR 93.7 million bargain-purchase gain; the 2025 results included no comparable gain.

The main driver of revenue and EBITDA growth was Tallink Grupp, which was consolidated for the full twelve months in 2025 compared with only five months in 2024. Maritime transport revenue increased to EUR 761.7 million from EUR 316.8 million. Tallink carried 5.53 million passengers, down 0.9% year on year, while cargo units declined 19.2% and passenger vehicles 2.2%. Vessel chartering generated EUR 54.9 million and helped reduce seasonal fluctuations. The energy segment remained the largest revenue contributor, with external revenue of EUR 980.2 million compared with EUR 966.2 million, while Elenger’s EBITDA improved by approximately EUR 12 million. Energy deliveries remained stable at 18 TWh despite a 9% decline in regional gas consumption and continued price volatility.

Growth also reflected the full-year inclusion of Elenger Polska and the contribution of agricultural businesses. Infortar acquired 96.6% of Estonia Farmid in July, adding dairy and farming operations that contributed EUR 11.2 million of revenue and EUR 6.4 million of profit attributable to owners during the reporting period. It also acquired Oisu Biogaas in November and continued expanding biomethane, solar generation, and energy-storage activities. Tallink invested EUR 33 million in vessel maintenance and refurbishment, while Group investment in property, plant, equipment, real estate, and intangible assets totaled approximately EUR 80.1 million.

Financial strength improved. Cash increased to EUR 219.8 million from EUR 167.6 million, and operating cash flow rose to EUR 384.0 million from EUR 89.3 million. Interest-bearing liabilities declined to EUR 1.071 billion from EUR 1.223 billion, while net debt fell to EUR 851.6 million from EUR 1.056 billion; net debt to EBITDA improved to 3.7 from 4.0. Total equity increased to EUR 1.175 billion and the equity ratio improved to 45% from 43%. Infortar paid approximately EUR 62 million in dividends for 2024 and proposed a 2025 dividend of EUR 3.02 per share.
