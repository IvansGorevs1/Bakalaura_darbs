During the financial year ended December 31, 2024, Eleving Group S.A. achieved its all-time best annual financial performance, supported by a stable macroeconomic environment and strategic execution. Total revenue for the reporting period reached EUR 216.6 million, representing an increase of nearly 15 percent compared to EUR 189.3 million in 2023. The Group reported an adjusted EBITDA of EUR 89.8 million, marking an increase of over 16 percent compared to EUR 77.5 million in the previous year. Net profit for the period reached an all-time high of EUR 29.6 million, up almost 21 percent from EUR 24.5 million in 2023. Following an audit by the Romanian Tax Authority that determined additional VAT liabilities for prior periods, net profit was reduced by EUR 2.6 million due to a one-off tax expense of EUR 3.0 million. Adjusted for this one-off tax expense, total net profit amounted to EUR 32.2 million, representing growth of over 31 percent year-over-year.

The vehicle and consumer financing net portfolio expanded by 16 percent to EUR 371.2 million as of December 31, 2024, compared to EUR 320.3 million at the end of 2023. Total loan issuance volumes increased by 26.8 percent to EUR 368.6 million, up from EUR 290.6 million in the previous year, driven by organic demand, digital transformation efforts, and expanded sales channels. The total number of active customers reached 326,862. Operationally, the Group completed the integration of Sub-Saharan operations acquired in 2023, tripling the combined portfolio size across its three main markets in the region. Furthermore, the Group advanced its sustainability agenda by financing nearly 2,000 electric motorcycles in Eastern Africa, achieving its 2025 environmental target a full year ahead of schedule. Portfolio asset quality improved year-over-year, evidenced by a reduction in non-performing loan rates to 6.1 percent for vehicle finance and 4.3 percent for consumer finance.

A defining milestone of the reporting period was the successful initial public offering on Nasdaq Riga and the Frankfurt Stock Exchange in October 2024, which secured EUR 29.0 million in equity proceeds and strengthened the Group's capital base. Total equity significantly increased to EUR 108.1 million at the end of 2024, compared to EUR 65.4 million at the end of 2023. Total principal borrowings amounted to EUR 339.6 million at year-end, of which EUR 72.0 million is due for renewal over the following twelve months, comfortably covered by current assets of EUR 232.6 million. The capitalization ratio improved to 29.3 percent from 26.1 percent in 2023, the interest coverage ratio was 2.4 compared to 2.3 in the prior year, and net leverage improved to 3.3 from 3.7. The Group distributed EUR 9.0 million in dividends to equity holders of the parent company during the year, corresponding to EUR 0.09 per share.
