OODA AI AB reported a decisive transition from infrastructure build-up to commercial scaling in its 2025 financial year, fundamentally altering its revenue profile and operational trajectory. Group net sales escalated sharply to SEK 31,880 thousand from SEK 1,056 thousand in 2024, an increase of nearly 2,900 percent. Management explicitly attributes this top-line expansion to the timely invoicing of several pre-negotiated commercial agreements, notably the deepening partnership with Win.Rocks AG, which consolidated a total contract value of approximately SEK 21 million plus a new SEK 15 million arrangement, alongside service deployments for Sergel Kredittjänster and HMM Deutschland. Despite the strong revenue growth, the group posted a wider net loss of SEK 125,799 thousand compared to SEK 83,355 thousand the prior year, with earnings per share declining to SEK -35.29 from SEK -29.00. The Board clarifies that this deterioration in net profitability stems entirely from non-operational factors, specifically large-scale impairments and negative valuations of legacy financial holdings conducted during a strategic review aimed at shedding non-core assets and concentrating resources on the AI platform business. These accounting adjustments drove financial result expenses to SEK -95,853 thousand and reduced total equity to SEK 23,859 thousand from SEK 119,371 thousand.

Underlying operational performance, however, showed clear improvement. The operating loss contracted substantially to SEK -29,270 thousand from a loss of SEK -51,781 thousand in 2024. After excluding one-time restructuring and liquidation costs of approximately SEK 10,238 thousand, the adjusted EBITDA reached roughly SEK 13,195 thousand, reflecting a healthy adjusted margin of approximately thirty-two percent of revenue. The company maintained a lean cost structure with personnel expenses limited to SEK -9,210 thousand and a headcount of four, deliberately avoiding mass recruitment during the scaling phase. Liquidity and operational flexibility improved markedly, as cash flow from operating activities narrowed to a deficit of SEK -16,662 thousand from a broader shortfalls of SEK -29,680 thousand, with the second half of the year generating positive operating cash flow of SEK 5,098 thousand. To finance continued platform advancement, which included capitalizing roughly SEK 8.7 million in development expenditures for the Universal AI Platform and decentralized computing infrastructure, the group completed multiple equity issuances totaling approximately SEK 44.6 million throughout 2025. A further cash raise of approximately SEK 10 million was approved shortly after year-end. Relying on confirmed recurring contracts, an expanding partner distribution network, disciplined expense control, and validated capital funding, the board affirms the company meets going concern criteria and is positioned to scale usage and margin expansion through formal platform commercialization in early 2026.
