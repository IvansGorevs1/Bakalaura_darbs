WindowMaster International’s financial performance weakened in 2025, as lower revenue and continued investment in the organization reduced earnings compared with the previous year. Revenue declined by 8% to DKK 268.6m from DKK 294.5m, following a slow start to the year and lower sales to a major Nordic customer. Revenue was also below the original budget by DKK 21–41m, mainly because of weaker sales in Climatic and lower growth in North America, but it was in line with the latest guidance of DKK 269–270m.

Gross profit fell to DKK 131.1m from DKK 137.9m, reflecting the lower sales level. However, the gross margin improved to 48.8% from 46.9%, supported by a more favorable business mix, including less revenue from key accounts and a greater proportion of direct project revenue. EBITDA decreased to DKK 28.0m from DKK 37.3m, while the EBITDA margin declined to 10.4% from 12.7%. Fixed costs were maintained to support future growth, and included DKK 2.5m of extraordinary costs related to a ransomware attack in May and June, including infrastructure rebuilding, enhanced firewall security and external consultants. Average employee numbers increased to 146 from 136, and staff costs rose to DKK 103.4m from DKK 100.6m.

Operating profit declined to DKK 8.3m from DKK 17.7m, while profit before tax fell to DKK 2.9m from DKK 12.2m. Profit after tax was DKK 0.9m, compared with DKK 10.6m in 2024. The effective tax rate increased to 70% from 13%, largely because tax assets were not utilized. No dividend was proposed for 2025, compared with a DKK 6.0m dividend in the prior year.

Cash generation remained relatively strong despite the lower earnings. Operating cash flow was DKK 28.5m, compared with DKK 29.4m, helped by improved working capital, particularly lower inventories and work in progress. Investing cash outflow decreased to DKK 9.4m from DKK 17.6m, with investments focused on digitalization, software development, process automation and IT infrastructure. Cash increased by DKK 0.6m to DKK 4.0m. Net interest-bearing debt rose to DKK 38.7m from DKK 36.7m, and gearing increased to 1.7 from 1.2 but remained below the target of 2.0. Equity decreased to DKK 32.5m from DKK 36.6m, with the net-cash equity ratio at 23.5%, below the target of 25%.

Market activity improved during the second half as interest rates stabilized, particularly in non-residential construction, while residential demand remained subdued. For 2026, management expects organic revenue growth of 7–14% to DKK 290–310m and EBITDA of DKK 45–55m, with conditions expected to improve gradually despite continued economic, geopolitical and project-timing uncertainty.
