During the financial year ended June 30, 2025, the group comprising SAF Tehnika and its subsidiaries experienced a decrease in net turnover while significantly narrowing its net losses compared with the previous financial year. Group net turnover reached 25.99 million euro, representing a decline of 4 percent or 1.1 million euro from 27.09 million euro in the 2023 and 2024 financial year. The parent company reported a net turnover of 21.92 million euro, which was 6 percent or 1.33 million euro lower than the 23.26 million euro recorded in the prior period. 

Despite the drop in revenue, the group improved its profitability metrics, closing the reporting period with a net loss of 643 thousand euro, which is a notable recovery compared with the net loss of 2.37 million euro reported in the previous financial year. Similarly, the parent company posted a reduced net loss of 552 thousand euro, improving from a net loss of 1.34 million euro previously. Gross profit for the group increased to 9.50 million euro from 7.53 million euro, while operating losses narrowed to 1.29 million euro compared with a loss of 3.22 million euro in the prior year. 

The financial results were influenced by regional market dynamics. Sales in the Americas region, which accounted for 55 percent of total group turnover, decreased by 14 percent or 2.3 million euro to 14.17 million euro. This decline was largely attributed to the scheduling of large-scale project realization into future periods and temporary customer caution driven by reevaluated investment decisions and tariff announcements, although market stabilization and gradual recovery were subsequently observed. Conversely, revenue in the European region, including Latvia, increased by 17 percent or 1.4 million euro, reaching 10.0 million euro, whereas revenues from Asia, the Middle East, and Africa declined by 5 percent or 90 thousand euro to 1.8 million euro. International sales outside Latvia continued to dominate, representing 97 percent of total group turnover across 87 countries. 

The group maintained a solid liquidity and cash position during the period. Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the reporting year stood at 4.81 million euro for the group, marking an increase of 2.6 million euro compared with the end of the previous financial year, supported by a positive net cash flow from operating activities of 3.87 million euro. Total investments in IT infrastructure, production and research equipment, software, licenses, product certification, and premises renovation amounted to 812 thousand euro. Group inventory at the end of the period was valued at 14.00 million euro, reflecting ongoing management of material reserves and component availability, while slow-moving inventory volumes decreased by 1.24 million euro. 

Management proposed covering the reporting year losses of the parent company from previous years retained earnings, and no dividends were distributed for the reporting period.
