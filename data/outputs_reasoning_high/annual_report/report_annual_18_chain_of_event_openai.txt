OODA AI reported a major operational improvement in 2025, with Group revenue rising to SEK 32,058 thousand from SEK 1,061 thousand in 2024. Net sales increased to SEK 31,880 thousand from SEK 1,056 thousand, which the company attributes directly to invoicing several major transactions. Sales consisted entirely of consulting services and were generated mainly outside Sweden, particularly in Switzerland. The growth was supported by expanded customer agreements, including Win.Rocks AG, whose contracts reached approximately SEK 21 million during the year, plus a new agreement of at least SEK 15 million over 24 months signed in December. Agreements with HMM Deutschland and Sergel Kredittjänster also contributed to the commercialization effort.

Operating performance improved substantially. Operating loss narrowed to SEK 29,270 thousand from SEK 51,781 thousand, while EBITDA turned positive at SEK 2,957 thousand compared with a loss of SEK 28,229 thousand in 2024. EBITDA included approximately SEK 10,238 thousand in non-recurring costs related to liquidating older company structures. Excluding these costs, adjusted EBITDA was approximately SEK 13,195 thousand, equivalent to an adjusted EBITDA margin of about 32% of revenue. The company maintained a low cost base and did not undertake major recruitment, although Group personnel costs increased to SEK 9,210 thousand from SEK 7,432 thousand.

Despite the stronger underlying operations, the Group’s net loss widened to SEK 125,799 thousand from SEK 83,355 thousand, and earnings per share declined to SEK -35.29 from SEK -29.00. The deterioration was primarily caused by financial items, which produced a loss of SEK 95,853 thousand compared with SEK 29,900 thousand in 2024. This mainly reflected impairments and results related to associated companies and other financial holdings. The company’s review and streamlining of these holdings reduced financial assets to SEK 8,387 thousand from SEK 96,036 thousand, with several investments written down, in some cases to zero. Interest expense nevertheless fell sharply to SEK 384 thousand from SEK 17,032 thousand, reflecting lower indebtedness and financing costs.

Cash flow from operating activities improved to negative SEK 16,662 thousand from negative SEK 29,680 thousand, and was positive by SEK 5,098 thousand in the second half of the year. However, increased investing activity and lower financing inflows resulted in cash declining to SEK 3,335 thousand from SEK 25,108 thousand. Equity fell to SEK 23,859 thousand from SEK 119,371 thousand, and the equity ratio decreased to 55% from 82%, largely because of the valuation adjustments.

The company completed capital raisings during the year and continued simplifying its legal structure. It also advanced its AI platform, partner program and decentralized AI infrastructure, including an MVP and operational testnet. Management is targeting broader platform commercialization in the second quarter of 2026 and considers continued customer growth, partner distribution, cost control and additional capital availability important to maintaining operations.
