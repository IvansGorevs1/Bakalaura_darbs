The company reported a slight decline in consolidated net sales during the 2025 financial year, with revenue falling by 3.4% to €7.33 million compared to €7.59 million in the prior period. Sales within the European Union remained relatively stable, accounting for 92% of total turnover, while exports to Africa and Asia experienced notable reductions. Revenue from the real estate management segment was minimal, dropping from €25 thousand to €3 thousand following the termination of rental agreements in August 2024.

Profitability deteriorated across all key metrics. The group recorded a negative EBITDA of €0.42 million, compared to a marginal loss of €0.03 million in 2024, pushing the EBITDA margin to negative 6 percent. This decline was primarily driven by underutilized manufacturing capacity, which raised per-unit production costs, and the financial impact of a December factory shutdown. The shutdown, intended as a planned upgrade to install a new gas boiler house, required ongoing personnel and maintenance payments that weighed heavily on operating results. Depreciation charges and increased distribution and administrative expenses further pressured margins, resulting in an operating loss of €0.94 million and a net loss of €1.15 million.

Strategic and structural developments significantly shaped the reporting period. To streamline corporate governance, the company merged its wholly owned subsidiary Pärnu Riverside Development OÜ into the parent entity effective June 2025, bringing the Admirali Quarter real estate project directly under group management. Design work for the project has been completed, and construction permits have been secured for several apartment buildings and infrastructure networks. Following the balance sheet date, a preliminary agreement was reached for the sale of ten developed properties within the quarter. Additionally, the company successfully closed a public share offering in October 2025, raising €2.0 million to support capital adequacy and investment activities. 

Cash flow reflected these operational and strategic movements. Operating activities generated a net cash outflow of €503 thousand, while investing activities consumed €989 thousand due to €1.367 million in production equipment upgrades and €161 thousand in real estate development expenditures. These outflows were offset by a substantial €1.444 million financing inflow, predominantly from the equity issuance. Despite the improved cash position, the working capital deficit widened to €2.7 million, reflecting accumulated losses. Management intends to address liquidity constraints through property divestments outlined in the Admirali Quarter project. Overall, the year was characterized by stable core product sales alongside structural streamlining and asset investments, though persistent operational challenges and a subdued macroeconomic environment constrained financial performance.
