WindowMaster International A/S reported its financial results for the full year 2025. Consolidated revenue for the year amounted to DKK 268,589 thousand, representing an eight percent decrease compared to DKK 294,481 thousand in 2024. Management noted that the decline was primarily explained by a slow start to the year and lower sales to a key customer in the Nordic region. However, market activity improved during the second half of the year as interest rates stabilized, supporting increased project execution in the non-residential segment. Gross profit for 2025 was DKK 131,056 thousand, compared to DKK 137,949 thousand in the previous year. Despite the lower top-line revenue, the gross margin improved to 48.8 percent from 46.9 percent in 2024, which management attributed to a favorable business mix featuring less revenue from key accounts and higher direct project revenue in alignment with the Accelerate Core strategy. Operating profit before depreciation, amortization, and impairment losses, or EBITDA, decreased to DKK 27,969 thousand in 2025 from DKK 37,305 thousand in 2024, resulting in an EBITDA margin of 10.4 percent compared to 12.7 percent in the prior year. The reduction in EBITDA and profitability was linked to maintained fixed cost levels to support future growth alongside extraordinary costs of DKK 2.5 million incurred due to a ransomware attack affecting the company IT infrastructure in May and June 2025. Earnings before tax dropped to DKK 2,907 thousand from DKK 12,184 thousand in 2024. Profit after tax fell significantly to DKK 869 thousand in 2025 compared to DKK 10,601 thousand in the preceding year, with the high effective tax rate influenced by unutilized tax loss carryforwards. Cash flow from operating activities remained strong at DKK 28,460 thousand, down slightly from DKK 29,431 thousand in 2024, supported by improvements in net working capital such as inventories and work in progress. Cash flows from investing activities amounted to DKK -9,374 thousand, reflecting ongoing investments in digitalization, software development, and IT security infrastructure updates. Total equity at the end of December 2025 stood at DKK 32,473 thousand, down from DKK 36,635 thousand at the end of 2024, yielding an equity ratio of 23.5 percent on a net cash basis. Net interest-bearing debt to EBITDA financial gearing increased to 1.7 at the end of 2025 compared to 1.2 in 2024. Looking ahead to 2026, management expects organic revenue growth between 7 percent and 14 percent, translating to total revenue of DKK 290 million to DKK 310 million, while EBITDA is forecasted to increase to between DKK 45 million and DKK 55 million, representing an EBITDA margin of approximately 8 percent to 10 percent, driven by higher project volumes and carry-over effects from 2025 while keeping fixed costs and investments unchanged.
