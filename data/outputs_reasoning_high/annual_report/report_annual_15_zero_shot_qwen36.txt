AS Merko Ehitus Group recorded consolidated revenue of EUR 310.9 million for the financial year 2025, a 42.3% decline from EUR 539.0 million in 2024. The reduction stemmed mainly from the successful completion of multiple large construction contracts, particularly in defence and renewable energy, which caused construction services revenue to fall by 49.9% to EUR 237.6 million. In contrast, real estate development revenue rose by 13.8% to EUR 73.4 million, driven by higher apartment handovers and steady residential demand, especially in Lithuania. Despite the contracted top line, operational efficiency remained strong, yielding an operating profit of EUR 34.3 million and a net profit of EUR 39.9 million. Profitability ratios improved relative to turnover, with net margin expanding to 12.8% from 12.0% and operating margin settling at 11.0%. EBITDA decreased to EUR 37.5 million, while return on equity stood at 15.6%. The group’s balance sheet reinforced its financial resilience, with total assets moderating to EUR 415.1 million and equity growing to EUR 260.6 million. This strengthened the equity ratio to 62.8% from 56.9% and maintained a conservative debt ratio of 8.0%. Cash and cash equivalents declined to EUR 41.4 million, reflecting increased working capital tied to real estate inventories and shareholder returns. The Management Board proposes a dividend of EUR 22.1 million for 2025. Market conditions during the period were marked by prolonged economic uncertainty, elevated borrowing costs, and cautious consumer sentiment across Estonia and Latvia, although Lithuania exhibited greater resilience. Public sector procurement, heavily focused on national defence and critical infrastructure, partially offset subdued private investment. Strategically, the group maintained a selective tendering policy, prioritizing projects with optimal risk-return profiles rather than pursuing volume growth. Contract signings picked up notably in the second half of 2025, bolstering the secured order book to EUR 466.9 million from EUR 340.6 million. Key forthcoming projects include multi-phase defence facilities in Lithuania, which will phase into revenue streams from 2026 onward. Additionally, equity income from joint ventures, particularly network operator Connecto, accounted for approximately one quarter of consolidated net profit. Looking forward, industry forecasts indicate single-digit construction market growth in the Baltics for 2026, supported by residential development, continued defence infrastructure spending, and progressing Rail Baltica sections. The combination of a robust order backlog, prudent capital management, and adaptive segmental strategies positions the group to navigate near-term volatility while capturing emerging public-sector opportunities.
