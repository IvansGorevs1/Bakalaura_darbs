During the financial year ended June 30, 2025, AS SAF Tehnika and its subsidiaries operated as a developer, manufacturer, and distributor of digital microwave transmission equipment, microwave spectrum analyzers, and wireless sensor network solutions. The group closed the 2024/2025 financial year with a consolidated net turnover of 25.99 million euro, representing a decrease of 1.1 million euro or 4 percent compared to the previous financial year when turnover was 27.09 million euro. The parent company reported a net turnover of 21.92 million euro, which is 1.33 million euro or 6 percent lower than the 23.26 million euro recorded in the 2023/2024 financial year. Despite the decline in revenue, the group significantly improved its bottom-line financial results, recording a consolidated net loss of 643 thousand euro or 642,642 euro for the reporting period, which is a substantial improvement compared to the consolidated net loss of 2.37 million euro or 2,369,364 euro reported in the previous financial year. The parent company similarly reduced its net loss to 552 thousand euro or 551,789 euro, down from a net loss of 1.34 million euro or 1,339,281 euro in the prior year. The group achieved a consolidated gross profit of 9.50 million euro, increasing from 7.53 million euro in the previous year. Consolidated losses from economic activity were reduced to 1.29 million euro compared to a loss of 3.22 million euro in the prior financial year. Regionally, the Americas remained the largest market, accounting for 55 percent of total group turnover at 14.17 million euro, which represents a decrease of 2.3 million euro or 14 percent compared to the previous year. This regional decline was largely driven by the realization of large-scale projects in future periods and temporary client caution related to the re-evaluation of investment decisions and tariff announcements. Conversely, turnover in the European region, including Latvia, increased by 1.4 million euro or 17 percent, reaching 10.0 million euro, while revenues in Asia, the Middle East, and Africa decreased by 90 thousand euro or 5 percent to 1.8 million euro. The group maintained a strong liquidity position, closing the financial year with cash and cash equivalents of 4.81 million euro, an increase of 2.6 million euro compared to 2.21 million euro at the end of the previous financial year. Total investments during the period amounted to 812 thousand euro, directed towards IT infrastructure, production and research equipment, software, licenses, product certification, and premises renovation. Total inventories stood at 14.0 million euro, while slow-moving inventory provisions decreased by 1.24 million euro. No dividends were distributed or calculated during the reporting year.
