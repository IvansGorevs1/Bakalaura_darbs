Trophy Games reported a strong financial improvement in 2025, driven by rapid scaling of its Transport Series and increased advertising monetisation. Revenue and other operating income rose 40% to DKK 126.5m from DKK 90.1m, while EBITDA increased 26% to DKK 31.1m. Operating profit more than doubled to DKK 20.5m from DKK 9.2m, and profit before tax increased 109% to DKK 19.0m. Net profit reached DKK 12.5m, compared with DKK 7.5m in 2024. The EBITDA margin declined from 27% to 25% as the company substantially increased marketing investment, while the EBT margin improved from 10% to 15%. Management noted that 2024 included significant non-recurring impairment costs; on an adjusted basis, the 2024 EBT margin was also 15%.

The Transport Series was the primary growth driver. Revenue from the series increased to DKK 106m from DKK 69m, led by the “Big 3” titles—Airline Manager, Truck Manager and Farm Manager—which generated DKK 85m, or 67% of total revenue, compared with DKK 31m and 35% in 2024. Airline Manager revenue rose 28% to DKK 37.8m. Truck Manager, which was launched in late 2024, generated DKK 25.2m versus DKK 1.2m previously. Farm Manager, launched in July 2025 after five million pre-registrations, contributed DKK 22.0m in its first six months. This growth more than offset a 46% decline in other Transport titles, whose revenue fell to DKK 21.0m as marketing resources were redirected toward the higher-growth titles.

The company’s monetisation mix also improved. In-app advertising revenue doubled to DKK 18.5m and represented 14.6% of revenue, compared with 10.2% in 2024. The late-2025 advertising optimisation programme was rolled out across the portfolio and is expected to support further growth in 2026. Marketing expenditure increased 76% to DKK 40.5m to support user acquisition and market-share expansion. Direct costs rose 34% to DKK 30.5m, but advertising revenue carries no platform fees, supporting operating efficiency.

The fourth quarter was the strongest in company history. Revenue increased 106% to DKK 41.0m, EBITDA rose to DKK 13.8m from DKK 7.2m, and EBT increased 574% to DKK 10.1m despite a 296% increase in quarterly marketing expenditure.

Cash flow was affected by higher marketing spending, increased receivables including a DKK 14m VAT receivable, the DKK 4.7m repayment of tax credits, and DKK 4.9m of share buybacks. Operating cash flow was DKK 12.9m, and year-end cash declined 9% to DKK 23.0m, although it had recovered to DKK 36.4m by 31 January 2026. The company remained debt-free, with a 78% solvency ratio and DKK 66.1m of equity. For 2026, management guides for revenue of DKK 130–153m, EBITDA of DKK 28–36m and EBT of DKK 18–24m, excluding potential acquisitions and The Ranchers.
