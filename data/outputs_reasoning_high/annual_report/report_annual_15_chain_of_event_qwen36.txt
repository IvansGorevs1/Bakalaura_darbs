AS Merko Ehitus reported consolidated revenue of EUR 310.9 million for the financial year ended 31 December 2025, representing a 42.3 percent decrease from EUR 539.0 million in 2024. Total assets amounted to EUR 415.1 million, while shareholders equity stood at EUR 260.6 million. Gross profit reached EUR 55.9 million with a gross margin of 18.0 percent, slightly up from 17.8 percent in the prior year. Operating profit declined to EUR 34.3 million from EUR 72.5 million, resulting in an operating margin of 11.0 percent compared to 13.4 percent previously. Net profit attributable to equity holders was EUR 39.9 million, down from EUR 64.7 million in 2024, though the net margin improved to 12.8 percent from 12.0 percent. Return on equity decreased to 15.6 percent, while the equity ratio strengthened to 62.8 percent against a debt ratio of 8.0 percent. Cash and cash equivalents fell to EUR 41.4 million from EUR 91.9 million, reflecting significant dividend payments and working capital movements.

The pronounced revenue contraction was primarily driven by the scheduled completion of several large-scale construction contracts initiated in prior years. The company operated in a market characterized by subdued private sector demand, elevated input prices, and high financing costs, which collectively restrained commercial construction volumes. Public sector procurement, particularly in defence and infrastructure, rose, but did not fully offset the gap left by finished projects. Within the real estate development segment, revenues increased as the group delivered 358 apartments valued at EUR 67.8 million, benefiting from steadier consumer confidence in Lithuania, Estonia, and Latvia. Despite higher delivery volumes, the real estate segment operating margin contracted to 18.2 percent from 25.5 percent, influenced by individual project cost dynamics and a property write-down in Norway. Operating profitability across both segments settled toward industry averages following the unusually favorable results of completed benchmark projects in 2024.

Management reinforced a selective contracting strategy, declining bids that did not meet target risk-return thresholds, which stabilized overall gross margins despite the volume drop. The secured order book expanded by 37.1 percent to EUR 466.9 million, underpinned by newly signed major contracts including phases of the Rudninkai defence campus in Lithuania and Rail Baltica infrastructure works in Estonia. These orders are projected to generate revenue progressively from 2026 onwards, providing medium-term visibility. Profitability was partially supported by joint venture investments, which contributed approximately EUR 10.4 million to the net profit, accounting for roughly one quarter of the total. The board proposes a dividend distribution of EUR 22.1 million, maintaining a payout ratio aligned with long-term shareholder return objectives. The strengthening equity base, manageable leverage, and growing deferred order flow indicate a transition phase where current earnings reflect strategic portfolio renewal rather than deteriorating operational fundamentals.
