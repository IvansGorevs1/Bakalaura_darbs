InCoax Networks’ financial performance weakened in 2025 as customer orders were delayed and the market became more cautious. Net sales declined 35% to SEK 48.1 million from SEK 74.5 million, with product sales accounting for 85% of revenue. North America remained the main market, generating SEK 39.6 million in sales compared with SEK 69.6 million in 2024. Gross profit decreased to SEK 29.9 million from SEK 42.2 million, but the gross margin improved to 62% from 57%.

The lower revenue, together with continued investment in product development, strategic collaborations, and commercial expansion, resulted in a larger operating loss. EBIT was SEK –32.4 million, compared with SEK –19.4 million in the previous year, while the net loss increased to SEK –32.6 million from SEK –19.2 million. Loss per share was SEK –0.25, compared with SEK –0.18. Total operating expenses declined to SEK 105.9 million from SEK 121.2 million, mainly because of lower goods-for-resale and external costs, although personnel expenses increased. Development investments remained high, with SEK 25.3 million capitalized during the year, raising the carrying value of development assets to SEK 118.0 million.

Cash flow from operating activities improved but remained negative at SEK –20.0 million, compared with SEK –27.1 million in 2024. Investing activities used SEK 25.3 million, primarily for capitalized development costs. Financing activities generated SEK 51.8 million through a directed share issue and borrowing, resulting in total cash flow of SEK 6.5 million compared with SEK –12.8 million the previous year. Cash and cash equivalents increased to SEK 8.7 million at year-end, although the company’s SEK 20 million credit facility was fully utilized. The equity ratio fell to 71.8% from 80.8%, while total liabilities increased to SEK 43.1 million from SEK 26.3 million.

The year marked a transition from intensive development and integration toward commercial execution. The D2501 product was completed and subsequently integrated into Nokia’s Gigabit Connect platform, with initial installations carried out in North America through the partnership. InCoax also launched the D2508 through its own channels, with Nokia integration and channel launch planned for 2026. The company reported weaker-than-expected sales because of delayed customer projects and a slower commercial ramp-up within certain partnerships.

Management has initiated structural cost reductions expected to lower ongoing costs by more than 40%, or over SEK 25 million annually, from 2026. The company aims to reach cash-flow neutrality within the coming year, but the auditor highlighted a material uncertainty regarding going concern because short-term financing depends partly on the exercise of warrants or securing alternative funding. A SEK 12.5 million convertible loan was issued in January 2026, alongside warrant programs that could provide additional capital if exercised.
