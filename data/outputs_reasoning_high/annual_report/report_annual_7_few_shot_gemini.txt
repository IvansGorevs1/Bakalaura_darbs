Heimstaden reported stable revenue performance alongside improved operating profitability and stronger bottom-line results for the full year 2025. Rental income remained essentially flat at SEK 15,916 million compared with SEK 15,919 million in the previous year, supported by an annualized like-for-like rental growth of 4.9% and stable real economic occupancy of 98.5%. The flat revenue performance was primarily driven by positive indexation and tenant improvement investments, which were offset by foreign exchange effects, privatisations, and portfolio sales. Property expenses decreased by 6.1% to SEK 4,378 million, benefiting from cost control measures, operational efficiencies, and a shrinking portfolio size. Consequently, net operating income improved to SEK 11,475 million from SEK 11,188 million, leading to an increased net operating income margin of 72.1% compared to 70.3% in 2024.

The company experienced positive property valuation movements, with fair values of investment properties increasing by 2.9%, generating a fair value gain of SEK 9,498 million compared with SEK 8,553 million previously. This growth was driven by net operating income increases, value-accretive capital expenditures, and a strong homeowner market in select European regions. Total investment properties at year-end stood at SEK 324,429 million, down from SEK 335,422 million largely due to active asset divestments. Heimstaden actively pursued its privatisation programme during the year, achieving gross privatisation sales of SEK 10,664 million across 2,466 residential units sold at a 28.9% premium to book value. Realised gains from the divestment of properties increased to SEK 2,207 million from SEK 1,687 million.

Profit before tax rose significantly to SEK 16,012 million from SEK 10,403 million, supported by higher property valuation gains, lower administrative and operational costs, and favourable foreign exchange movements of SEK 3,996 million. Profit for the year increased to SEK 13,056 million compared with SEK 7,621 million in 2024, despite the company recording a goodwill impairment loss of SEK 3,707 million during the period. Income tax expenses increased moderately to SEK 2,955 million.

The company made continued progress in strengthening its balance sheet and credit metrics. Net loan-to-value improved significantly to 52.2% at the end of 2025 compared with 56.4% in 2024, supported by debt repayment funded through cash proceeds from the privatisation programme and asset monetisation. The interest coverage ratio improved slightly to 2.0x from 1.9x, while interest expenses remained stable at SEK 6,536 million. The average interest rate at year-end ended at 3.43% compared with 3.01% in the prior year. Total equity rose to SEK 150,557 million from SEK 147,803 million, driven by comprehensive income for the period. Overall, 2025 was characterized by disciplined capital execution, enhanced operating profitability, strategic portfolio trimming, and balance sheet deleveraging.
