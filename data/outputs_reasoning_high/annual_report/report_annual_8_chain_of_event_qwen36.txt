Consolidated net turnover for the year ended 30 June 2025 amounted to EUR 25.99 million, representing a decline of EUR 1.1 million or 4 percent compared to EUR 27.09 million in the prior year. The parent company recorded net sales of EUR 21.92 million, down 6 percent year-on-year. Despite the revenue contraction, the group delivered a substantially improved financial result, closing the fiscal year with a net loss of EUR 642,642, which marks a significant reduction from the EUR 2.37 million loss reported in the previous period. Correspondingly, basic loss per share improved to EUR 0.216 from EUR 0.798. Gross profit increased to EUR 9.50 million from EUR 7.53 million, driven by a proportional decrease in production costs that outweighed the sales decline.

Regional performance exhibited divergent trends. Turnover in the Americas region, accounting for 55 percent of total sales at EUR 14.17 million, fell by EUR 2.3 million or 14 percent. Management explicitly attributes this contraction to large-scale projects being realized in subsequent periods and temporary client caution stemming from investment reassessments and tariff announcements. Conversely, European revenues grew by 17 percent to EUR 10.0 million, while Asia, the Middle East, and Africa saw a modest 5 percent decline to EUR 1.8 million. Operating expenses adjusted accordingly, with selling and marketing costs decreasing to EUR 7.43 million, while administrative expenses rose to EUR 3.36 million.

The deterioration in the top line did not translate into worsened profitability, as slow-moving inventory balances decreased by EUR 1.24 million compared to the prior year-end. This reduction reflects the progressive digestion of previously accumulated safety stocks amid stabilizing global material availability, although reserve stocks remain elevated relative to historical norms. Cash reserves strengthened considerably, rising by EUR 2.6 million to EUR 4.81 million, underpinned by positive net cash flows generated from core operating activities. The parent company maintained an available credit facility of EUR 4.95 million, which remained entirely undrawn at year-end.

Strategic initiatives continued throughout the reporting period, including expanded research and development focused on Internet of Things environmental monitoring solutions under the Aranet brand, supported by EUR 570,000 in co-financing. To mitigate cross-border supply chain and customs uncertainties, particularly regarding potential tariff implications and trade policy fluctuations between the United States and Canada, the group established a Canadian branch in December 2024. Global geopolitical tensions and Sino-American trade relations continue to characterize the macroeconomic landscape, though management notes they have not yet caused material adverse effects on current sales volumes. Leadership characterizes the reported losses as transitional, citing resilient operational cash generation and steady progress in specialized wireless transmission niches. The board refrains from providing forward-looking sales projections, maintaining a cautious stance amid ongoing market variability.
