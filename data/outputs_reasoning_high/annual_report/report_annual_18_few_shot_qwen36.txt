OODA AI reported a decisive shift from organizational restructuring to commercial expansion during 2025, marked by substantial revenue growth and improved underlying operational efficiency. Group net sales surged to SEK 31,880 thousand from SEK 1,056 thousand the prior year, driven by the invoicing of major customer agreements. Commercial momentum accelerated with expanded contracts with Win.Rocks AG totaling approximately SEK 21 million, plus a new minimum SEK 15 million agreement over 24 months, and service deployments with clients including Sergel Kredittjänster. These results reflected broader adoption across sectors such as banking, healthcare, and staffing, supported by a functioning technical platform and an expanding partner program.

Operational profitability recovered significantly against a difficult comparison period. Operating profit narrowed to minus SEK 29,270 thousand from minus SEK 51,781 thousand, while EBITDA turned positive at SEK 2,957 thousand versus a deficit of SEK 28,229 thousand in 2024. Core performance was notably stronger, as adjusted EBITDA reached approximately SEK 13,195 thousand, equivalent to a margin of roughly 32 percent of revenue, after excluding non-recurring liquidation costs of about SEK 10,238 thousand related to consolidating older company structures. Personnel costs remained tightly controlled at SEK 9,210 thousand, consistent with a lean model and limited recruitment focused on essential functions.

Although operations improved, the consolidated net loss widened to SEK 125,799 thousand from SEK 83,355 thousand due to substantial non-operational charges. The result from financial items amounted to minus SEK 95,853 thousand, burdened by impairments and valuation adjustments stemming from a comprehensive review of the financial holdings portfolio. This strategic streamlining led to write-downs of several investments to near zero, reducing total equity to SEK 23,859 thousand from SEK 119,371 thousand. The board emphasized that these declines reflect the deliberate pruning of non-core assets to concentrate capital on the AI platform and decentralized infrastructure, rather than deterioration in the core business activities.

The group's financial position was reinforced through capital raising and debt reduction. Financing activities contributed a net inflow of SEK 44,302 thousand, bolstered by cash issues completed throughout the year and a further SEK 10 million issue decided shortly after period end. Interest expenses plummeted to SEK 384 thousand from SEK 17,032 thousand, indicating a streamlined capital structure. Cash flow from operating activities improved to minus SEK 16,662 thousand from minus SEK 29,680 thousand, with a critical highlight being the generation of positive operating cash flow totaling SEK 5,098 thousand in the second half of the year, signaling strengthening cash dynamics. Looking forward, the company focuses on scaling its Universal AI Platform, expanding distribution channels, and launching its decentralized infrastructure commercially in early 2026. The board assessed that ongoing operations are viable, supported by growing revenues, improved cash flows, a lower risk profile, and adequate liquidity, ensuring the report can be prepared on a going concern basis.
