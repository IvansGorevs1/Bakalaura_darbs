Talkpool’s 2025 results showed a return to organic growth after several years focused on restructuring, cost reduction, and balance-sheet repair. Net sales increased 12.5% to EUR 17.756 million from EUR 15.782 million in 2024, representing the company’s first year of double-digit growth since the consolidation phase began. EBITDA rose to EUR 1.828 million from EUR 1.702 million, although the EBITDA margin declined to 10.3% from 10.8%. EBIT increased to EUR 1.620 million from EUR 1.527 million, while the EBIT margin decreased to 9.1% from 9.7%. Net earnings after tax grew 42% to EUR 862 thousand from EUR 605 thousand, raising the net earnings margin to 4.8% from 3.8%. The reported gross margin was 26.4%.

Management attributed the lower operating margins to initial investments in personnel, technology, and growth initiatives as Talkpool moved away from its “reduce to the MAX” strategy. The company expects temporary pressure on margins and cash flow while these investments translate into new business. Currency movements also affected reported performance: the US dollar weakened 11% and the Pakistani rupee 14% during the first half of 2025, reducing revenues when translated into euros.

The Americas remained Talkpool’s largest market and continued to grow despite currency weakness, supported by the longstanding Digicel relationship in the Caribbean and continued activity in Haiti. The company re-entered the US market through a MasTec service contract in May, but the US ramp-up was slower than planned and order volumes remained low after fibre rollout work was discontinued in favor of remote wireless services. Germany continued to provide a strong and steady contribution, although Deutsche Telekom’s shift toward passing homes rather than connecting them temporarily flattened planning volumes. In Pakistan, revenue reached a record level in local currency, and renegotiated customer terms in the second half of the year materially improved gross profitability; however, currency depreciation reduced its euro-reported contribution.

Talkpool launched its Vision 2030 strategy in April, targeting EUR 40 million in revenue, a 14.0% EBITDA margin, 16.8% average annual revenue growth, and zero net debt by 2030. The company expanded remote-service capacity, established Pakistan as its main production base, and conducted initial trials of AI-enabled solutions. At year-end, equity had reached EUR 2.1 million and net cash was positive at EUR 0.3 million, compared with negative positions in 2021. Subsequent 2026 financing and planned strategic initiatives are intended to support further expansion.
