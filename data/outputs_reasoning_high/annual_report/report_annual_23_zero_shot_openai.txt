TKM Grupp AS reported a challenging but operationally stable 2025, with profitability weakening as consumer demand remained subdued and the Estonian automotive market contracted sharply. Consolidated revenue was 919.6 million euros, down 2.6% from 944.6 million euros in 2024. Gross profit declined to 254.6 million euros from 259.8 million euros, although the gross margin improved slightly to 27.7% from 27.5%. EBITDA fell 14.7% to 77.4 million euros and the EBITDA margin decreased to 8.4% from 9.6%. Operating profit declined 26.6% to 34.9 million euros, reducing the operating margin from 5% to 4%.

Profit before tax was 24.3 million euros, down 31.4%, while net profit fell 36.1% to 17.5 million euros. Net margin decreased to 2% from 3%, and earnings per share fell to 0.43 euros from 0.67 euros. The decline occurred despite a 1.5 million euro reduction in interest costs and a 1.2 million euro decrease in income tax expense. The 2024 comparative result also included approximately 4.0 million euros of one-off gains related to investment-property revaluation and the sale of two non-core Latvian properties. Labour costs increased 3.7%, while other operating expenses rose only 0.2%, reflecting the Group’s cost-control and supply-chain optimisation measures.

The main pressure came from car trade. Segment revenue decreased 11.9% to 176.9 million euros, profit before tax fell 48.5% to 5.7 million euros, and net profit declined 54.4% to 4.3 million euros. Estonia’s new passenger-car market fell 48.6%, affected by the introduction of the motor vehicle tax and weaker consumer confidence, although stronger markets in Latvia and Lithuania partly offset the decline. Supermarket revenue increased 0.3% to 611.9 million euros, but net profit fell 38.6% to 9.5 million euros amid lower sales volumes, greater promotional activity and costs associated with the Jõgeva Selver refurbishment. Department-store revenue decreased 1.1% to 103.0 million euros and the segment recorded a 0.5 million euro loss. Real-estate revenue increased 5.1% to 7.7 million euros, while net profit rose 20.5% to 6.3 million euros, supported by rental income and lower borrowing costs.

Operating cash flow remained substantial at 65.6 million euros, compared with 73.1 million euros in 2024. Total investment decreased to 25.5 million euros from 36.6 million euros, while year-end cash fell to 29.5 million euros from 45.5 million euros. Working capital was negative at 14.2 million euros, mainly because 32.1 million euros of loans maturing in 2026 were classified as current liabilities; refinancing had begun and management reported no liquidity issues.

The Group opened new KIA and Škoda facilities in Lithuania and a new dealership in Peetri, Estonia, and continued store and digital-system upgrades. In January 2026, it agreed to acquire Škoda-related businesses in Tallinn and Harju County, subject to completion conditions.
