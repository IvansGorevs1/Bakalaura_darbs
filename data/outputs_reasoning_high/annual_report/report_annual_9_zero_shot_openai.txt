Impero A/S delivered stronger financial performance in 2025 despite a difficult start caused by geopolitical and macroeconomic uncertainty. Revenue increased 19% to DKK 42.0 million from DKK 35.4 million in 2024. Cost of sales fell 16% to DKK 1.0 million, raising the contribution margin to 98% from 97%. Other external expenses and staff costs each increased 6%, reflecting continued commercial investment, lead-generation activities, new hires, salary adjustments, and organizational changes.

The improved revenue growth and relatively controlled operating costs significantly reduced losses. EBITDA improved to negative DKK 3.1 million from negative DKK 8.7 million in 2024, while operating loss narrowed to DKK 6.7 million from DKK 11.7 million. The net loss was DKK 6.8 million, compared with DKK 11.4 million in the previous year. Capitalized development costs increased to DKK 5.2 million from DKK 3.6 million and had a positive impact on EBITDA. Intangible assets rose 18% to DKK 11.4 million as the company continued investing in its software platform.

Annual Recurring Revenue reached DKK 46.2 million at year-end, up 19% from DKK 38.7 million. Growth regained momentum in the second half of the year, with fourth-quarter net new ARR of DKK 3.1 million compared with DKK 1.8 million in the corresponding quarter of 2024. Full-year net new ARR was DKK 7.5 million, below DKK 8.3 million in 2024. Customer metrics remained strong: churn declined to 1% from 2%, Net Revenue Retention was 106% compared with 108%, and average ARR per account increased 8% to DKK 228 thousand. However, customer acquisition cost increased to DKK 371 thousand from DKK 222 thousand, and CAC payback lengthened to 22 months from 15 months.

The customer base surpassed 200 customers after 26 additions. ARR growth was particularly strong in the DACH region, at 26%, and Denmark grew 22%; together these markets represented 88% of total ARR. Partner sales were affected by delayed customer decision-making, although the company expanded its German partner network to five revenue-generating partners. Product development included the launch of the Control Testing module and the introduction of initial AI capabilities.

Cash flow from operating activities improved to negative DKK 1.4 million from negative DKK 4.0 million. After DKK 5.6 million of investing cash outflows, the DKK 15 million directed issue in December supported an increase in year-end cash to DKK 18.9 million and equity to DKK 15.4 million. For 2026, Impero expects ARR of DKK 53–57 million and EBITDA between negative DKK 2 million and positive DKK 1 million, while deferring its ambition of recurring positive cash flow to prioritize commercial investment.
