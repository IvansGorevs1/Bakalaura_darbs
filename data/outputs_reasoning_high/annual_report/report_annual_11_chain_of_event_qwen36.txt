JLT Mobile Computers AB reported net sales of SEK 131.5 million for the fiscal year 2025, representing an 11.1 percent increase from SEK 118.4 million in the prior year. This revenue growth was accompanied by a substantial rise in order intake, which climbed 37.5 percent to SEK 141.6 million from SEK 103.0 million. Despite the top-line expansion, the company recorded a gross profit of SEK 50.4 million and a gross margin of 38.3 percent, a decline of 7.5 percentage points compared to 45.8 percent in 2024. The operating loss narrowed significantly to SEK -1.9 million from a loss of SEK -9.7 million in the previous year. Consequently, the net loss for the period amounted to SEK -2.5 million, compared to SEK -7.7 million previously. EBITDA improved to SEK -1.7 million from SEK -2.1 million, while operating cash flow amounted to SEK 3.7 million, down from SEK 6.8 million in 2024.

These financial outcomes were shaped by distinct market dynamics and internal strategic actions. Weaker demand persisted in the warehousing and logistics segment, the company’s largest market, as geopolitical uncertainty and tariff ambiguities prompted end customers to adopt a more cautious purchasing stance. However, this weakness was partially offset by targeted expansion into the ports, construction, and mining equipment sectors, alongside a major SEK 22 million commercial agreement with an American food producer. A primary factor negatively impacting profitability was the nearly 20 percent depreciation of the US dollar against the Swedish krona during the year. Because 74 percent of sales were invoiced in dollars, the exchange rate movement compressed gross margins through both weaker invoicing rates relative to initial quotes and unfavorable inventory revaluations.

In response to these external pressures, management executed structural cost reductions that materially improved the operating result. The decision to discontinue operations of the subsidiary JLT Software Solutions AB and coordinate software development initiatives lowered overhead costs by approximately SEK 5 million and substantially reduced depreciation expenses on development projects, which fell to SEK 0.4 million from SEK 8.1 million the previous year. Overall operating expenses declined to SEK 52.4 million from SEK 57.3 million. Furthermore, the company successfully reduced inventories by SEK 12.7 million to SEK 25.5 million by drawing down safety stocks accumulated during earlier component supply chain disruptions, thereby enhancing cash conversion and capital efficiency. Given the net loss and ongoing market volatility, the board proposed no dividend for the fiscal year. The strengthened order backlog of SEK 24.1 million and recent product launches, including the industry’s first rugged vehicle mounted computer with a fifteen inch full HD display, provide a foundation for the anticipated market recovery expected in 2026.
