Eleving Group reported strong financial growth during the 2024 reporting year, achieving record levels across key metrics. Revenue increased by 15% to EUR 216.6 million, driven by robust loan issuance volumes that grew 26.8% year-over-year to EUR 368.6 million. The net loan portfolio expanded by 16% to reach EUR 371.2 million, supported by successful integration of acquired Sub-Saharan operations and sustained demand across European and East African markets. Total net profit reached an all-time high of EUR 29.6 million, representing a 21% increase compared to 2023. Adjusted for a contested one-off Romanian VAT liability of EUR 2.6 million, net profit would stand at EUR 32.2 million, reflecting over 31% underlying growth. Operating performance was further strengthened by a record EBITDA of EUR 89.8 million, up more than 16% from the prior year, while impairment expenses remained relatively stable despite portfolio expansion, aided by disciplined underwriting and improved non-performing loan ratios.

The period was defined by several strategic milestones, most notably the company’s successful initial public offering in October, which raised EUR 29.0 million in equity through the largest listing in Nasdaq Riga’s history. The capital raised was promptly deployed to redeem expensive subordinated borrowings and reduce funding costs, resulting in a significantly improved balance sheet. The capitalization ratio rose to 29.3%, and net leverage decreased to 3.3 times, enhancing financial flexibility. Operationally, the Group completed the rollout of its next-generation digital self-service platform across Europe, nearly doubling customer applications and strengthening operational efficiency. Concurrently, the subsidiary in Sub-Saharan Africa accelerated sustainable mobility initiatives, financing nearly 2,000 electric motorcycles and meeting long-term environmental targets ahead of schedule. The Group also finalized exits from non-core markets in Belarus and the Balkans, with discontinued operations generating a profit of EUR 0.8 million. Overall, the company delivered resilient top-line and bottom-line expansion, optimized its capital structure, and advanced its digital and sustainability strategies while maintaining strict credit discipline.
