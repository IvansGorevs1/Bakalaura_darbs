WindowMaster International’s 2025 performance weakened compared with 2024, mainly because of a slow start to the year, lower sales to a major Nordic customer, reduced activity in Climatic in the Nordic region, and slower growth in North America. Revenue declined 8% to DKK 268.6 million from DKK 294.5 million, below the original budget by DKK 21–41 million but in line with the latest guidance of DKK 269–270 million. Revenue improved during the second half of the year, which Management expects to continue into 2026.

Gross profit decreased to DKK 131.1 million from DKK 137.9 million, but the gross margin improved to 48.8% from 46.9%. The report links the margin improvement to a changed business mix, with less revenue from key accounts and more direct project revenue, consistent with the Accelerate Core strategy. The major customer generated DKK 30 million of revenue, down from DKK 43 million in 2024.

EBITDA declined to DKK 28.0 million from DKK 37.3 million, reducing the EBITDA margin to 10.4% from 12.7%. Fixed costs were maintained to support future growth, while the result was also reduced by DKK 2.5 million of extraordinary costs following a ransomware attack in May and June. Earnings before tax fell to DKK 2.9 million from DKK 12.2 million, and profit after tax declined to DKK 0.9 million from DKK 10.6 million. The effective tax rate rose to 70% from 13%, partly because tax assets and tax-loss carryforwards were not utilized.

Cash flow remained positive despite the lower earnings. Operating cash flow was DKK 28.5 million, compared with DKK 29.4 million in 2024, supported by improved net working capital, particularly inventories and work in progress. Investing cash outflow decreased to DKK 9.4 million from DKK 17.6 million, although investments continued in digitalization, software, process automation, and IT infrastructure and security. Year-end cash increased to DKK 4.0 million from DKK 3.4 million. Net interest-bearing debt rose to DKK 38.7 million from DKK 36.7 million, and gearing increased to 1.7 from 1.2 but remained below the target of 2.0. Equity declined to DKK 32.5 million from DKK 36.6 million, with the net-cash equity ratio at 23.5%, below the target of 25%.

Market activity improved in the second half as interest rates stabilized, particularly in non-residential construction. Demand continued to benefit from energy-efficiency investments and environmental regulation, although residential demand remained subdued and reduced supply-chain constraints lowered order visibility. For 2026, Management expects organic revenue growth of 7–14% to DKK 290–310 million and EBITDA of DKK 45–55 million, corresponding to an EBITDA margin of approximately 8–10%.
