Konsolidator reported strong revenue growth and marked improvement in profitability during the 2025 fiscal year, driven by execution of its Resilient Growth strategy and an expanded product portfolio. Total revenue increased by 25% to DKK 25.3 million compared with DKK 20.3 million in 2024. This growth was fueled by a 13% rise in subscription fees to DKK 20.6 million and a 134% surge in onboarding and consulting fees to DKK 4.7 million following price adjustments initiated in the first quarter. Profitability metrics also strengthened significantly. The EBITDA loss narrowed sharply to DKK 2.0 million from DKK 9.3 million in the prior year, with the second half of 2025 generating a positive EBITDA of DKK 0.5 million. Consequently, the net loss for the year decreased to DKK 6.6 million from DKK 14.9 million, while operating cash flow improved from negative DKK 9.2 million to negative DKK 1.0 million as working capital management strengthened and customer invoicing shifted toward longer, upfront payment terms.

SaaS unit economics demonstrated substantial progress. Contracted annual recurring revenue grew by 15% to DKK 24.4 million, supported by a 45% increase in new sales to DKK 4.5 million. The company Rule of 40 score recovered markedly to 17% from negative 40% in 2024, reflecting the combined effect of top-line expansion and cost discipline. Customer acquisition cost payback period improved to 23 months from 43 months, driven by reduced marketing spend and higher conversion efficiency. While annual recurring revenue churn rose to 12.6% from 10.5%, net customer retention remained stable at 94%, and the customer lifetime value to acquisition cost ratio climbed from 2.7 to 4.1.

Strategic developments centered on transforming the offering into the Konsolidator Suite, integrating predictive financial planning and analysis capabilities alongside its core consolidation and data management solutions. Go-to-market efforts increasingly relied on channel partnerships, which accounted for 38% of new customers compared with 22% in 2024, including a major agreement with Exsitec AB that expanded coverage in the Nordics. Geographic expansion accelerated, with the Iberian subsidiary establishing itself as the company third-largest market. To sustain operations and fund product development, the company completed two private placements raising DKK 4.8 million. Although consolidated equity remained negative at DKK 3.6 million, the Danish parent company maintained positive equity of DKK 0.7 million. Looking ahead to 2026, management targets contracted ARR and revenue between DKK 27 million and DKK 29 million, with an expected return to positive EBITDA of DKK 1 million to DKK 2 million and a reduction in churn to between 6% and 9%, underscoring a clear progression toward sustainable, profitable growth.
