InCoax Networks AB’s financial performance weakened in 2025, primarily because customer orders were delayed and the market was more cautious. Net sales fell 35% to SEK 48.1 million from SEK 74.5 million in 2024. Product sales declined to SEK 41.0 million from SEK 69.2 million, while service sales increased to SEK 7.0 million from SEK 5.3 million. North America remained the largest market, although sales there decreased to SEK 39.6 million from SEK 69.6 million.

Gross profit declined to SEK 29.9 million from SEK 42.2 million, but the gross margin improved to 62% from 57%. The operating loss nevertheless widened to SEK 32.4 million from SEK 19.4 million. The deterioration reflected lower revenue together with continued investment in product development, strategic collaborations, and commercial expansion. Capitalized development costs amounted to SEK 25.3 million, broadly unchanged from SEK 25.6 million in 2024. Total operating expenses decreased to SEK 105.9 million from SEK 121.2 million, mainly due to lower costs for goods for resale and other external expenses, partly offset by higher personnel costs. The average number of employees remained 31.

The loss after tax increased to SEK 32.6 million from SEK 19.2 million, and loss per share increased to SEK 0.25 from SEK 0.18. Despite the weaker earnings, operating cash flow improved to negative SEK 20.0 million from negative SEK 27.1 million, supported by reductions in inventories and receivables. Investing cash flow was negative SEK 25.3 million, mainly due to capitalized development costs. Financing activities generated SEK 51.8 million, including approximately SEK 33.5 million from a directed share issue and SEK 20 million in borrowings. Consequently, total cash flow was positive at SEK 6.5 million, compared with negative SEK 12.8 million in 2024, and year-end cash increased to SEK 8.7 million from SEK 2.2 million.

The company continued shifting from intensive product development toward commercialization. During the year, it advanced strategic partnerships, developed and launched new broadband solutions, and expanded market activities in Europe and North America. However, delayed customer projects and slower commercial ramp-up within certain partnerships limited revenue growth. Following the year-end, the first product under the Nokia collaboration was integrated into Nokia’s Gigabit Connect platform, and initial North American installations were completed. Management also reported a structural cost reduction of more than 40%, or over SEK 25 million annually, targeted from 2026.

The financial position was strengthened by financing, but the equity ratio declined to 71.8% from 80.8%, while non-current liabilities increased to SEK 23.2 million. The auditor highlighted a material uncertainty regarding going concern because short-term financing depends partly on the exercise of warrants and possible additional financing.
