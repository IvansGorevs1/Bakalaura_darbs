Talkpool AG reported its full-year financial results for the period ending December 31, 2025, marking the conclusion of its multi-year restructuring phase and the transition into organic growth. During 2025, net sales amounted to EUR 17,756 thousand, representing a 12.5 percent increase compared to EUR 15,782 thousand in 2024. This top-line expansion represented the first year of double-digit growth since the company began its consolidation phase. Profitability remained solid, with earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization reaching EUR 1,828 thousand, compared to EUR 1,702 thousand in the previous year, resulting in an EBITDA margin of 10.3 percent versus 10.8 percent in 2024. Earnings before interest and taxes totaled EUR 1,620 thousand, up from EUR 1,527 thousand, with an EBIT margin of 9.1 percent compared to 9.7 percent. Net earnings after tax experienced significant growth, increasing by 42 percent to EUR 862 thousand from EUR 605 thousand, while the net earnings after tax margin improved to 4.8 percent from 3.8 percent.

The company’s financial position strengthened substantially during the reporting period. Net cash reached a positive position of EUR 0.3 million by the end of 2025, representing a total improvement of EUR 6.0 million from a negative EUR 5.7 million position in 2021. Total equity improved to EUR 2.1 million, recovering from negative EUR 2.5 million in 2021. Auditors no longer express uncertainty regarding the company's ability to continue as a going concern.

Several business events and market factors influenced performance during the year. In April 2025, Talkpool launched its Vision 2030 strategic growth plan, targeting 16.8 percent average annual revenue growth, a 14.0 percent EBITDA margin, and zero net debt by 2030. Organic growth reached the 16.8 percent long-term target by year-end, driven by business momentum despite macroeconomic headwinds and currency challenges during the first half of the year, where the US dollar weakened by 11 percent and Pakistani Rupees by 14 percent, reducing euro-reported revenues. Operational developments included re-entering the US market through a service contract with MasTec, partnering with Nokia USA on a 5G testing project, supporting Digicel in Jamaica following Hurricane Melissa, and establishing Talkpool AB in Sweden to serve the defense sector. Germany’s operations maintained a steady contribution despite temporary flat planning assignment volumes due to operator shifts in fiber rollout priorities, and an independent valuation by BDO assessed the fair value of the German subsidiary at EUR 20.5 million. Meanwhile, Talkpool Pakistan achieved its highest-ever local currency revenue and improved gross profitability following commercial contract renegotiations, alongside developing a Vision AI site security and monitoring solution.

Overall, Talkpool demonstrated positive operational performance development in 2025, successfully shifting from cost reduction and risk minimization to strategic investments, remote service scaling, and technology-driven expansion while strengthening its balance sheet and returning to profitable growth.
