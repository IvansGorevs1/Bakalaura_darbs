AS Tallinna Sadam Group’s 2025 performance improved overall, with stronger profitability despite a slight decline in revenue. Revenue decreased by EUR 0.9 million, or 0.8%, to EUR 118.7 million, mainly because of lower charter income from the icebreaker Botnica. This was largely offset by recovery in the passenger and cargo businesses. Cargo volume increased by 5.1% to 13.8 million tonnes, supported particularly by higher liquid bulk volumes, while passenger numbers rose by 1.0% to 8.3 million. Vessel calls were unchanged at 7,127.

Revenue growth was recorded in all operating segments except Other, which includes Botnica. Vessel dues increased by 8% to EUR 34.1 million, while lease income rose by 3% to EUR 14.6 million, passenger fees by 3% to EUR 12.2 million, cargo charges by 3% to EUR 6.9 million, and ferry service revenue by 1% to EUR 36.4 million. Botnica charter fees fell by EUR 4.0 million, or 34%, to EUR 7.9 million, as charter days declined by 42 and utilization decreased to 49%. In the segment results, Cargo harbours produced the strongest improvement: revenue rose 5% to EUR 33.4 million and adjusted EBITDA increased 19% to EUR 16.9 million. Passenger harbours’ adjusted EBITDA rose 5% to EUR 20.8 million, while Ferry adjusted EBITDA declined 2% to EUR 15.2 million.

Cost reductions supported the improvement in margins. Operating expenses fell by EUR 4.0 million, or 10%, to EUR 36.4 million, primarily because of lower maintenance and repair costs, while impairment losses on financial assets declined substantially. Personnel expenses increased by 3% to EUR 26.6 million despite a reduction in average employees from 438 to 425. Operating profit increased by 12.2% to EUR 32.7 million, raising the operating margin from 24.4% to 27.6%. Adjusted EBITDA rose 6.4% to EUR 56.5 million, and the margin improved from 44.4% to 47.6%.

Profit before tax increased by 25% to EUR 27.9 million. Net profit rose 17.3% to EUR 22.5 million, or EUR 0.09 per share, supported by a 30% reduction in finance costs as interest rates and debt declined. Growth was partly limited by higher dividend-related income tax of EUR 5.4 million and a EUR 26 thousand loss from Green Marine, compared with a EUR 0.5 million profit in 2024.

Operating cash flow increased to EUR 53.7 million from EUR 48.7 million. Capital investment decreased 14.4% to EUR 33.2 million, mainly reflecting completion of the multifunctional quay at Paldiski South Harbour. Cash and cash equivalents rose to EUR 32.0 million, while net debt fell 15.5% to EUR 141.7 million. However, current liabilities exceeded current assets by EUR 66.0 million because significant loan and bond maturities fall in 2026; management is considering refinancing or extending maturities. The Group paid EUR 19.2 million in dividends for 2024 and proposed the same EUR 0.073 per share dividend for 2025. For 2026, management expects continued moderate passenger and cargo recovery but anticipates pressure from higher input and financing costs, and therefore expects operating and net profit may decline.
