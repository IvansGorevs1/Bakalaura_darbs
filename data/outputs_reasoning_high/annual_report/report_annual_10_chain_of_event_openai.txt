InCoax Networks’ 2025 financial performance declined compared with 2024. Net sales fell 35% to SEK 48.1 million from SEK 74.5 million, mainly because of delayed customer orders, timing shifts in projects, a more cautious market, and a slower commercial ramp-up in certain partnerships. Gross profit decreased to SEK 29.9 million from SEK 42.2 million, although the gross margin improved to 62% from 57%, partly reflecting the lower proportion of goods-related costs.

The lower revenue, together with continued investment in product development, strategic collaborations, and commercial expansion, resulted in a substantially higher operating loss of SEK 32.4 million, compared with SEK 19.4 million in 2024. Loss after tax increased to SEK 32.6 million from SEK 19.2 million, while earnings per share declined to SEK –0.25 from SEK –0.18. Capitalized development costs remained high at SEK 25.3 million, broadly unchanged from SEK 25.6 million, increasing the carrying value of development assets to SEK 118.0 million at year-end.

Operating expenses decreased to SEK 105.9 million from SEK 121.2 million. Lower costs for goods for resale and other external expenses were partly offset by higher personnel costs. Operating cash flow remained negative at SEK –20.0 million, but improved from SEK –27.1 million due mainly to reductions in inventories and receivables. Investing cash flow was SEK –25.3 million, primarily related to capitalized product-development expenditure. Overall cash flow, including financing activities, was positive at SEK 6.5 million compared with negative SEK 12.8 million in 2024, following financing that provided approximately SEK 53.5 million before issue costs through a directed share issue, borrowing, and convertible financing. Cash and cash equivalents consequently increased to SEK 8.7 million from SEK 2.2 million.

The year marked a transition from intensive development and integration toward commercialization. The company continued developing its MoCA Access platform and launched new solutions, including the D2508, while strategic partnerships and market-development initiatives were expanded. At the beginning of 2026, the D2501 was fully integrated into Nokia’s Gigabit Connect platform, and initial Nokia-related installations were completed in North America. The company also launched D2508 through its own channels and planned its integration into Nokia’s platform.

To address weak earnings and cash generation, InCoax initiated structural cost reductions expected to lower ongoing costs by more than 40%, or over SEK 25 million annually, from 2026. However, the company ended the year with its credit facility fully utilized. The auditor highlighted a material uncertainty regarding going concern because short-term financing depends partly on sufficient exercise of outstanding warrants or obtaining alternative financing.
