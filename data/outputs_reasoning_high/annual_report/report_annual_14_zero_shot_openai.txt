Lyko Group delivered continued revenue growth in 2025 but with lower profitability. Net sales increased 11.3% to SEK 3,962.6 million from SEK 3,561.7 million in 2024, while total revenue reached SEK 3,975.7 million. Growth was driven mainly by the Nordic segment, where revenue rose 12.0% to SEK 3,699.8 million and accounted for 93.4% of group sales. Norway exceeded SEK 1 billion in annual revenue. Europe remained smaller and declined 1.3% to SEK 130.7 million.

Gross profit increased to SEK 1,658.7 million from SEK 1,568.8 million, but the gross margin decreased from 44.0% to 41.9%. EBIT fell to SEK 78.2 million from SEK 117.4 million, reducing the EBIT margin from 3.3% to 2.0%. EBITDA declined to SEK 252.3 million from SEK 287.4 million. After net financial expenses of SEK 79.1 million, compared with SEK 79.7 million in 2024, profit before tax decreased to a loss of SEK 0.9 million from a profit of SEK 37.6 million. The lower tax expense, SEK 2.1 million compared with SEK 16.6 million, was not sufficient to prevent a net loss of SEK 3.0 million, versus a profit of SEK 21.0 million. Earnings per share fell to SEK -0.20 from SEK 1.37.

The company attributed the year’s development to strong Nordic momentum in a more challenging beauty market characterized by intense competition and structural shifts. The commissioning and ramp-up of the new automated warehouse in Vansbro affected results during parts of the year. The project, Lyko’s largest investment at approximately SEK 500 million, increased capacity to approximately 100,000 orders per day and is expected to be fully operational in early 2026. Lyko also opened stores in Espoo and Sandnes/Stavanger, reopened its Täby store as the first 500-square-metre Fab Store, relaunched Make Up Store, launched Scandinavian Soap Factory and began reorganizing its European operations by closing local offices in Germany and the Netherlands.

Cash flow from operating activities strengthened substantially to SEK 527.5 million from SEK 209.7 million. Investing cash flow was SEK -351.2 million, primarily reflecting warehouse, store and digital investments, while total cash flow rose to SEK 327.3 million from SEK 71.7 million. Cash and equivalents increased to SEK 452.5 million, and net debt excluding IFRS 16 declined to SEK 292.5 million from SEK 333.1 million. However, the equity ratio fell from 20.3% to 14.8% as assets and borrowings increased. No dividend was proposed.
