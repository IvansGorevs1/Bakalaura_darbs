Impero A/S presented its annual report for the financial year 2025, demonstrating resilient performance and improved financial results despite initial macroeconomic and geopolitical headwinds. Annual recurring revenue reached 46.2 million Danish kroner at the end of 2025, corresponding to a year-on-year growth rate of 19 percent compared to 38.7 million Danish kroner at the end of 2024. Total recognized revenue for 2025 rose by 19 percent to 42.0 million Danish kroner from 35.4 million Danish kroner in the previous year. 

Profitability metrics also showed significant improvement. EBITDA for 2025 landed at negative 3.1 million Danish kroner, representing an improvement of 5.6 million Danish kroner compared to negative 8.7 million Danish kroner in 2024. Operating loss improved to negative 6.8 million Danish kroner from negative 11.6 million Danish kroner, while the net loss for the period was reduced to 6.8 million Danish kroner from 11.4 million Danish kroner in 2024. This financial enhancement was primarily driven by revenue growth combined with a controlled cost base, where staff costs and other external expenses experienced modest 6 percent increases. 

Impero maintained solid SaaS metrics during the reporting period. The customer churn rate reached an exceptionally low level of 1 percent, down from 2 percent in 2024, while average annual recurring revenue per account increased by 8 percent to 228 thousand Danish kroner. Net revenue retention was 106 percent compared to 108 percent in the prior year. The customer base crossed the milestone of 200 customers, supported by 26 new customer additions, particularly in the Danish and DACH regional markets, which grew annual recurring revenue by 22 percent and 26 percent respectively. 

During the year, Impero achieved key product milestones, including the launch of the control testing module in July 2025 and the advancement of artificial intelligence features and control triggering capabilities. To strengthen its financial flexibility and support future expansion, the company successfully executed a directed share issue raising 15 million Danish kroner in December 2025. Consequently, Impero entered the end of the year with a robust cash position of 18.9 million Danish kroner and total equity of 15.4 million Danish kroner. 

Backed by this improved capital position, the board of directors decided to defer the strategic ambition of achieving positive cash flow on a recurring basis before the end of 2026, choosing instead to accelerate investments in commercial activities and market expansion. Looking ahead to 2026, Impero guides for annual recurring revenue to reach between 53 million and 57 million Danish kroner, reflecting a growth rate of approximately 15 to 23 percent, and expects EBITDA to range between negative 2 million and positive 1 million Danish kroner due to planned commercial acceleration.
