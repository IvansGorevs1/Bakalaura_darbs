Impero delivered stronger financial performance in 2025 despite a difficult start caused by geopolitical and macroeconomic uncertainty. Revenue increased 19% to DKK 42.0M from DKK 35.4M in 2024, supported by continued commercial activity and investment in the platform and its offerings. Cost of sales fell 16%, lifting the contribution margin to 98% from 97%. Other external expenses and staff costs each increased 6%, reflecting continued lead-generation activities, new hires, salary adjustments, and commercial organizational changes.

Profitability improved substantially, although the company remained loss-making. EBITDA improved to negative DKK 3.1M from negative DKK 8.7M, while operating loss narrowed to DKK 6.7M from DKK 11.7M. The loss for the year was DKK 6.8M compared with DKK 11.4M in 2024. Capitalized development costs increased to DKK 5.2M from DKK 3.6M as Impero increased product-development activity. Intangible assets rose 18% to DKK 11.4M, mainly due to investments in new development projects.

Annual Recurring Revenue reached DKK 46.2M at year-end, an increase of 19% from DKK 38.7M and at the upper end of the revised guidance range. Momentum recovered in the second half of the year: fourth-quarter net new ARR was DKK 3.1M, compared with DKK 1.8M in the corresponding quarter of 2024, although full-year net new ARR declined to DKK 7.5M from DKK 8.3M. Customer numbers exceeded 200 after 26 new customer wins. Customer retention remained strong, with churn at 1% versus 2% in 2024 and net revenue retention at 106% versus 108%. Gross uplift from existing customers was 12%, and average ARR per account increased 8% to DKK 228T. However, customer acquisition cost rose to DKK 371T from DKK 222T, and CAC payback increased to 22 months from 15 months, reflecting higher lead-generation spending and fewer new customers.

Growth was led by the DACH region, where ARR grew 26%, and Denmark, where it grew 22%. Each market represented approximately 44% of total ARR at year-end. ARR from other markets declined 6% to DKK 5.5M, primarily because of contraction in the UK. Product development advanced through the launch of the Control Testing module, initial AI features, and expanded German partner coverage.

Cash flow improved operationally, with cash flow from ordinary operating activities improving to negative DKK 1.3M from negative DKK 4.3M. Cash ended at DKK 18.9M after a DKK 15M directed issue and warrant exercises, while equity increased to DKK 15.4M from DKK 6.2M. For 2026, Impero expects ARR of DKK 53–57M and EBITDA between negative DKK 2M and positive DKK 1M. Commercial investment will accelerate, and the ambition of recurring positive cash flow before the end of 2026 has been deferred.
