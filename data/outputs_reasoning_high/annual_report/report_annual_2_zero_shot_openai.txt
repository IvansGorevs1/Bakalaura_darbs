Infortar reported strong operating growth in 2025, although net profit declined because the 2024 result included significant one-off effects from the initial consolidation of Tallink. Group revenue increased from EUR 1,371.8 million to EUR 1,837.0 million, while gross profit rose from EUR 128.6 million to EUR 239.8 million. EBITDA increased from EUR 145.3 million to EUR 227.9 million, and the EBITDA margin improved from 10.6% to 12.4%. Operating profit rose from EUR 77.0 million to EUR 109.0 million.

Net profit fell from EUR 193.7 million to EUR 69.9 million, with profit attributable to the parent’s shareholders decreasing from EUR 191.3 million to EUR 70.6 million. Earnings per share declined from EUR 9.36 to EUR 3.43. The comparison was affected by the 2024 bargain-purchase and other one-off gains related to gaining control of Tallink. In 2025, profit before tax was EUR 87.2 million, compared with EUR 173.8 million in 2024, while financial income and expenses moved from a EUR 73.8 million net gain to a EUR 26.0 million net expense.

The main contributor to revenue growth was the maritime transport segment, whose consolidated revenue increased to EUR 761.7 million from EUR 316.8 million as Tallink was consolidated for the full year in 2025, compared with only five months in 2024. Tallink carried 5.53 million passengers, down 0.9% year on year; cargo units declined 19.2% to 245,004 and passenger vehicles decreased 2.2% to 760,473. Vessel chartering helped offset seasonal fluctuations. The energy segment remained the largest by revenue, increasing to EUR 980.2 million from EUR 966.2 million, while its EBITDA improved to EUR 89.3 million from EUR 77.2 million. Regional gas demand fell 9% to 39.7 TWh amid volatile prices, but Elenger delivered 18 TWh of LNG cargoes, unchanged from 2024.

The Group expanded agriculture and renewable energy through the acquisition of 96.57% of Estonia Farmid, completed in July, the acquisition of Oisu Biogaas, and construction of the Halinga biogas plant. Estonia Farmid contributed EUR 11.2 million of revenue and EUR 6.4 million of profit attributable to owners during the period.

Financial capacity strengthened. Operating cash flow rose to EUR 384.0 million from EUR 89.3 million, year-end cash increased to EUR 219.8 million from EUR 167.6 million, and interest-bearing liabilities declined to EUR 1,071.4 million from EUR 1,223.3 million. Net debt decreased to EUR 851.6 million, and the equity ratio improved to 45% from 43%. The Group paid EUR 3 per share in dividends during 2025 and proposed EUR 3.02 per share for 2025.
