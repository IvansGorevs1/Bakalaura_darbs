The company reported a contraction in total revenue during the fiscal year, while profitability saw a marked improvement compared with the prior period. Consolidated net turnover declined by four percent to twenty-five point nine nine million euros, driven primarily by weaker demand in the Americas market. Despite lower sales volumes, the Group recorded a significantly narrower comprehensive loss of six hundred forty-three thousand euros, reversing from a two point three seven million euro deficit the previous year. The parent entity also posted an improved result, recording a loss of five hundred fifty-two thousand euros against a thirteen hundred thirty-nine thousand euro shortfall previously. 

Revenue shifts across geographical markets shaped the top-line performance. Sales in the Americas region dropped by fourteen percent, representing a two point three million euro decrease, as large multi-year projects deferred to subsequent periods and customers temporarily moderated purchasing decisions amid macroeconomic uncertainty and trade policy adjustments. European operations provided strong counterweight momentum, expanding revenue by seventeen percent to ten million euros. Asian, Middle Eastern, and African markets contracted modestly by five percent. Management noted that ongoing international trade tensions have not yet disrupted order volumes, though a fifteen percent import duty applies to the Aranet environmental monitoring product line. To mitigate cross-border logistics and tariff exposure, the Company established a Canadian branch during the reporting period.

Operational adjustments supported margin resilience despite the revenue dip. Inventory provisions were partially released, reducing slow-moving stock valuations by over one point two million euros as supply chain conditions stabilized following earlier component shortages. Investment in research and development continued, including enhancements to digital micro-wave radios and the Aranet Internet of Things ecosystem, supplemented by five hundred seventy thousand euros in public co-financing. The balance sheet reflects strengthened liquidity, with cash and cash equivalents rising by two point six million euros to four point eight one million euros. An existing credit facility remained entirely undrawn, reinforcing funding flexibility. 

Overall, the reporting period was characterized by strategic positioning amid cyclical demand fluctuations and external economic headwinds. Management considers the current loss phase temporary and emphasized sustained investment in niche wireless technologies and specialized sensor solutions. Positive core operating cash flows, combined with disciplined cost control and inventory optimization, underscored the Group’s underlying financial stability and readiness to capture recovery opportunities as market activity gradually normalizes.
