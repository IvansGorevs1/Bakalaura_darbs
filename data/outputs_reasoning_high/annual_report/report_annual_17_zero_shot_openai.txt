Novaturas Group’s 2024 performance deteriorated compared with 2023, although operating results improved substantially toward year-end. Revenue decreased 3.6% to EUR 200.9 million from EUR 208.3 million, while the number of passengers served fell 7.6% to 239.5 thousand. Gross profit declined 20.9% to EUR 20.7 million, reducing the gross margin to 10.3% from 12.6%. Flight package revenue, the Group’s main product, fell to EUR 170.7 million from EUR 182.8 million, while other sales increased to EUR 25.4 million from EUR 20.9 million.

The Group reported negative EBITDA of EUR 6.1 million, compared with positive EBITDA of EUR 5.2 million in 2023. The result included a one-off, non-cash goodwill impairment charge of EUR 5.7 million related to the Estonian cash-generating unit. Excluding this impairment, adjusted EBITDA was negative EUR 449 thousand, versus positive EUR 5.2 million in 2023. Operating expenses excluding the impairment were EUR 21.8 million, 1% below the prior year, but higher costs relative to gross profit contributed to the weaker result. The Group recorded a loss before tax of EUR 7.8 million, compared with a restated profit of EUR 3.9 million, and a net loss of EUR 7.6 million versus a restated net profit of EUR 3.4 million. Basic loss per share was EUR 0.98, compared with earnings of EUR 0.43 in 2023. Prior-year figures were restated for errors involving overstated prepayments and an unrecognised impairment.

Results were affected by increased competition and oversupply in the Baltic travel market, causing intense price competition, particularly for Turkey and the Greek islands. Adjusted EBITDA was negative EUR 1.6 million in the first half, but improved to positive EUR 2.3 million from September through December as the Group optimized its travel programme and adjusted its offering to customer preferences. Fourth-quarter seat utilization rose to 98.9% from 96.7%, while the proportion of last-minute sales decreased. Demand shifted toward Spain, long-haul destinations and group travel; Spain passenger volumes were reported 149% above the comparison noted in the report, and long-haul customers increased 8%.

Financial flexibility weakened. Year-end cash and cash equivalents fell to EUR 394 thousand from EUR 3.3 million, operating cash flow was negative EUR 1.6 million compared with positive EUR 3.0 million, and current liabilities exceeded current assets by EUR 12.5 million. Equity declined to EUR 8.0 million from EUR 15.7 million, while the debt/equity ratio rose to 121.1% from 58.0%. The Group breached certain financing covenants but obtained waivers. Management expects 2025 EBITDA of EUR 2.0–3.3 million and, after year-end, secured additional financing and a strategic investor who acquired 23.2% of the shares.
