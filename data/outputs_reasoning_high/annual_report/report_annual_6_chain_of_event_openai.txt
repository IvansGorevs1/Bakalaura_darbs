Gigasun reported improved operating performance in 2025 despite lower electricity prices, currency headwinds and continued financing constraints. Group revenue rose 9.8% to SEK 239.7 million from SEK 218.3 million, while net revenue from electricity sales increased 9.9% to SEK 203.2 million. Other operating income, mainly subsidies, amounted to SEK 36.5 million, including SEK 32.7 million in subsidies. Higher electricity production supported revenue growth, although the increase was partly offset by lower average prices, a higher share of electricity sold to the grid, revised tariffs and a weaker currency. Currency effects reduced total revenue by SEK 18.0 million.

Operationally, electricity production increased 30% to 327.8 million kWh, and installed revenue-generating capacity expanded 16% to 395.8 MW from 340.1 MW. The company installed 55.7 MW during the year and signed 17 contracts representing approximately 41.8 MW, compared with 15 contracts and 56.6 MW in 2024. At year-end, signed orders were 16.4 MW, down from 38.8 MW. Production reduced estimated CO2-related emissions by approximately 161,000 tonnes, compared with 124,000 tonnes in 2024.

Profitability strengthened. EBITDA increased to SEK 163.7 million from SEK 145.8 million, with the margin rising to 68% from 67%. EBIT increased 21.1% to SEK 73.5 million, and the EBIT margin improved to 31% from 28%. Operating expenses rose 5.5% to SEK 166.2 million, primarily because the larger installed base increased depreciation and maintenance costs. Interest expenses and similar charges declined to SEK 82.0 million from SEK 88.7 million. Nevertheless, the Group reported a net loss of SEK 7.2 million, substantially improved from SEK 32.9 million in 2024. The result included a negative non-cash currency effect of SEK 3.8 million and a one-off cost of SEK 3.5 million; adjusted for these items, management reported a result of SEK 0.1 million versus SEK -34.7 million.

Refinancing was a major development. Agreements with Agricultural Bank Financial Leasing totaling approximately SEK 1.242 billion carry a 3.60% interest rate and extend amortization from seven to ten years. The refinancing was approximately halfway completed by year-end and is intended to reduce financing costs and improve cash flow. The SOLT4 bond was extended to November 8, 2026, at 14.25% interest.

Cash flow from operations fell to SEK 101.1 million from SEK 133.8 million, while investing outflows decreased to SEK 208.1 million. Total cash flow was negative SEK 34.9 million, reducing cash to SEK 45.1 million. Interest-bearing debt declined to SEK 1.383 billion, but the equity ratio fell to 21% from 23%. Management states that significant uncertainty regarding going concern remains if new financing cannot be secured in 2026.
