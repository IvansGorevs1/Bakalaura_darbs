Gigasun reported strong operational growth and improved profitability during the 2025 financial year, despite broader volatility in the Chinese renewable energy market. Total revenue increased by 9.8 percent to SEK 239.7 million, driven primarily by higher electricity production across an expanding asset base. Net revenue rose by 9.9 percent to SEK 203.2 million, while adverse currency translation effects reduced top-line figures by approximately SEK 18.0 million. Operating profit expanded by 21.1 percent to SEK 73.5 million, reflecting a 3-percentage-point improvement in the EBIT margin to 31 percent. EBITDA grew by 12 percent to SEK 163.7 million, with the EBITDA margin stabilizing at 68 percent. Although the group posted a net loss of SEK 7.2 million, this represented a substantial narrowing compared to SEK 32.9 million in the prior year. The reduced loss was primarily attributable to lower financing costs and stabilized operations, with temporary adjustments including a SEK 3.8 million unfavorable currency impact and a SEK 3.5 million one-off expense excluded from underlying performance metrics.

Operational expansion remained a central driver of results throughout the period. The company increased its installed solar capacity by 16 percent to 395.8 megawatts and generated 327.8 million kilowatt-hours of electricity, marking a 30 percent rise in output. This growth was supported by the signing of 17 new contracts adding 41.7 megawatts of planned capacity, although backlog orders at year-end stood at 16.4 megawatts. The company successfully expanded into previously unprofitable regions such as Chongqing, benefiting from declining installation costs. Conversely, regulatory adjustments and provincial tariff reforms in Jiangsu and Anhui reduced average electricity selling prices by approximately 8 percent in the second half of the year. Management assessed that the negative impact on existing project revenues was contained at roughly 3 to 4 percent and expected to be fully offset by structural improvements in financing and construction expenses.

The company’s financial position was reinforced through deliberate refinancing activities and sustained cash generation from operations. Interest expenses decreased by 8.5 percent to SEK 76.9 million as ongoing conversions from high-cost lease facilities to Agricultural Bank Financial Leasing terms lowered effective borrowing costs to 3.60 percent over extended ten-year periods. Interest-bearing debt declined to SEK 1,382.6 million, down from SEK 1,483.1 million at year-end 2024, while the equity ratio contracted to 21 percent due to retained losses and translation adjustments. Operating cash flow generated SEK 101.1 million, partially funding investments of SEK 169.9 million in new and ongoing solar projects. The company also extended its outstanding SOLT4 bond repayment to November 2026, improving near-term liquidity flexibility. With gross margins holding steady at 82 percent and long-term power purchase agreements providing stable contracted cash flows, the reported period demonstrated a consolidation of core operational profitability amid strategic portfolio scaling and favorable cost trajectories.
