During the financial year ended 31 December 2024, Novaturas Group recorded revenues from contracts with customers of 200.9 million euros, representing a 3.6 percent decrease compared to 208.3 million euros in 2023. The total number of travelers served across Lithuania, Latvia, and Estonia decreased by 7.6 percent to 239.5 thousand travelers, down from 259.3 thousand travelers in the previous year. Gross profit for 2024 reached 20.7 million euros, marking a 20.9 percent decline from 26.2 million euros in 2023, while the gross profit margin dropped from 12.6 percent to 10.3 percent. The group reported a negative EBITDA of 6.1 million euros, down from a positive 5.2 million euros in 2023. Adjusted EBITDA, which excludes a one-off non-cash goodwill impairment, stood at negative 449 thousand euros compared to positive 5.2 million euros in the prior year. Operating profit was negative 6.7 million euros, and the group incurred a net loss of 7.6 million euros for 2024, contrasting with a net profit of 3.4 million euros recorded in 2023. This net result included a one-off, non-cash goodwill impairment of 5.7 million euros related to the Estonian cash-generating unit, excluding which the net loss was 1.9 million euros. Basic and diluted earnings per share dropped to negative 0.97 euros from positive 0.43 euros in 2023.

The financial performance was largely affected by an increased competitive environment and oversupply of travel in the Baltic States, which triggered intense price competition particularly in Turkey and the Greek islands. Despite weaker performance in the first half of the year, operating results showed material improvement starting in September 2024 due to travel program optimization, reduced last-minute sales, and a high fourth-quarter seat load factor of 98.9 percent. The group also expanded alternative destinations, achieving a 149 percent increase in passengers to Spain and growth in exotic long-haul locations, alongside organizing a record corporate group trip for 2.5 thousand employees to Turkey.

Regarding financial position and subsequent events, the group did not comply with certain loan covenants as of 31 December 2024, though required waivers were subsequently received from lenders. Additional credit lines and loans were secured in early 2025, and shareholders entered into agreements for Turkish investor Neset Kockar to acquire a strategic equity stake. Comparative figures for 2023 were restated due to the correction of prepayment overstatement and expense understatement errors. Management concluded that the going concern assumption remains appropriate based on projected profitability recovery, operational efficiencies, and incoming financial backing.
