JLT Mobile Computers improved its commercial performance in 2025, although profitability remained negative. Group net sales increased 11.1% to SEK 131.5 million from SEK 118.4 million, while order intake rose 37.5% to SEK 141.6 million from SEK 103.0 million. The year-end order backlog increased to SEK 24.1 million from SEK 13.9 million, including SEK 15.0 million scheduled for delivery in the first quarter of 2026 compared with SEK 7.5 million previously. A significant contribution to order intake came from a SEK 22 million deal with an American food producer.

Profitability improved from the previous year’s weak level, but the company did not return to profit. EBITDA improved to a loss of SEK 1.7 million from SEK 2.1 million, operating loss narrowed to SEK 1.9 million from SEK 9.7 million, and the net loss decreased to SEK 2.5 million from SEK 7.7 million. The operating margin improved to negative 1.4% from negative 8.2%. Cost reductions supported the improvement: operating expenses fell to SEK 52.4 million from SEK 57.3 million, while organizational and R&D costs declined by SEK 6.3 million. The company attributed these savings to structural measures including consolidating software development and discontinuing JLT Software Solutions AB. Depreciation and amortization also decreased substantially, from SEK 9.0 million to SEK 1.3 million, following development-cost write-downs recorded in 2024.

Gross profitability deteriorated despite higher sales. Gross profit fell to SEK 50.4 million from SEK 54.2 million, and the gross margin declined to 38.3% from 45.8%. The report explicitly attributes this temporary decline to currency effects. The US dollar weakened by almost 20% against the Swedish krona, while 74% of sales were invoiced in dollars; invoicing occurred at weaker exchange rates than those used when quotations were issued, and inventory was revalued using current dollar purchase rates.

Market conditions remained difficult because of geopolitical uncertainty, tariff concerns and weak demand in warehousing and logistics, JLT’s largest segment. Run-rate business also declined in transportation and manufacturing, while ports and mining performed positively. JLT broadened its market focus toward ports, construction and mining equipment, launched the JLT6015 rugged computer and introduced an AI-based screen-blanking safety solution. These initiatives, together with cost savings, are presented as supporting future growth rather than as quantified contributors to 2025 results.

Cash flow was reported at SEK 3.7 million, down from SEK 6.8 million, but cash and equivalents increased to SEK 16.9 million from SEK 13.3 million. Inventory reduction of SEK 12.7 million was a major contributor to improved capital efficiency. No dividend was proposed for 2025.
