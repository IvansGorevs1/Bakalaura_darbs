Konsolidator delivered strong top-line growth and marked improvements in profitability during 2025. Total revenue reached DKK 25.3 million, a 25 percent increase from DKK 20.3 million in 2024. This growth was driven by a rise in subscription fees to DKK 20.6 million and a 134 percent jump in onboarding and consulting fees to DKK 4.7 million, following first-quarter pricing adjustments. Contracted Annual Recurring Revenue grew 15 percent to DKK 24.4 million, supported by 52 new customers and a larger average contract value of DKK 87,000. The strategic transition from a single consolidation tool to the expanded Konsolidator Suite, which adds financial planning and data management capabilities, directly enabled higher customer values and smoother workflow adoption.

Profitability and cash flow metrics improved significantly. The consolidated EBITDA loss narrowed sharply to DKK 2.0 million from DKK 9.3 million in 2024, while the net loss decreased to DKK 6.6 million from DKK 14.9 million. Operating cash flow recovered from a DKK 9.2 million deficit to a DKK 1.0 million deficit. Management links these gains to disciplined cost reduction, specifically the outsourcing of marketing functions which lowered the EBIT loss by DKK 3 million. External expenses fell 11 percent to DKK 6.3 million as advertising and consulting costs were trimmed. Staff costs remained stable at DKK 21.0 million across an average headcount of 32. The second half of 2025 achieved positive EBITDA of DKK 0.5 million, reflecting operational scaling. Cash flow benefited from shifting to annual upfront customer invoicing and extending supplier payment terms. Unit economics also strengthened substantially. Customer Acquisition Cost dropped 32 percent, cutting the payback period from 43 to 23 months and raising the Customer Lifetime Value to Acquisition Cost ratio from 2.7 to 4.1. These improvements stem directly from earlier sales and marketing cost cuts and a scaled partner channel, now responsible for 38 percent of new acquisitions versus 22 percent previously. Conversely, the ARR churn rate climbed to 12.6 percent from 10.5 percent, which the report attributes to competitors offering native consolidation within ERPs and shifting customer expectations toward predictive forecasting. Targeted onboarding refinements were implemented in the second quarter to address this. To fund ongoing development and operations, the company executed two private placements totaling DKK 4.8 million, stabilizing the parent company equity at DKK 0.7 million despite negative consolidated equity of DKK 3.6 million. For 2026, management forecasts revenue of DKK 27 to 29 million, expects EBITDA to turn positive, and anticipates declining churn to between 6 and 9 percent as the suite expands and partner networks mature.
