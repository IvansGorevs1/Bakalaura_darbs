During the 2025 financial year, Gigasun AB achieved solid financial and operational growth, driven by an expanding portfolio of solar energy installations in China. Total revenue for the group rose by 9.8 percent to SEK 239,655 thousand compared to SEK 218,264 thousand in the previous year, while net revenue increased by 9.9 percent to SEK 203,179 thousand from SEK 184,795 thousand. This top-line growth was primarily propelled by a substantial increase in electricity production, which reached 327.8 million kWh, representing a rise of approximately 30 percent compared to 252.6 million kWh generated in 2024. However, revenue growth was partially offset by negative currency effects totaling SEK 18.0 million, lower average electricity prices due to a higher share of grid sales, and revised regional tariffs introduced in the second half of the year. 

Gross profit improved to SEK 195,829 thousand from SEK 179,219 thousand, maintaining a stable gross margin of 82 percent. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization increased to SEK 163,733 thousand compared to SEK 145,760 thousand, yielding an EBITDA margin of 68 percent. Operating profit rose by 21.1 percent to SEK 73,478 thousand compared to SEK 60,677 thousand, reflecting improved underlying profitability from stable operations despite higher operating expenses of SEK 166,177 thousand, which were driven by increased scheduled depreciation and maintenance costs associated with a larger asset base. Profit after tax improved significantly to SEK -7,206 thousand compared to SEK -32,944 thousand in 2024. When adjusted for a non-cash currency effect of SEK -3,843 thousand and a one-off cost of SEK 3,500 thousand, adjusted profit stood at SEK 100 thousand, demonstrating core operational profitability compared to SEK -34,700 thousand previously. 

Operationally, Gigasun expanded its installed revenue-generating capacity by 15.7 MW during the year to reach 395.8 MW across 216 installations, up from 340.1 MW and 200 installations at the end of 2024. The company signed 17 new contracts representing 41.7 MW of capacity, leaving 16.4 MW in signed orders at year-end. To optimize its capital structure, Gigasun advanced a major strategic refinancing initiative during 2025, entering into agreements with Agricultural Bank Financial Leasing Co., Ltd to refinance approximately SEK 1,242 million in total, featuring a lowered interest rate of 3.60 percent and a 10-year amortization period to replace higher-cost sale and leaseback facilities. Additionally, the SOLT4 bond was successfully extended to November 8, 2026, with an interest rate of 14.25 percent. 

Total interest-bearing debt decreased to SEK 1,382,613 thousand from SEK 1,483,061 thousand, and net interest-bearing debt declined to SEK 1,337,465 thousand from SEK 1,393,172 thousand. Total equity stood at SEK 488,201 thousand, resulting in an equity ratio of 21 percent compared to 23 percent at the close of 2024. Cash and bank balances were SEK 45,149 thousand, down from SEK 89,889 thousand. The company notes that securing new financing in 2026 remains essential to executing its ongoing business plan given short-term debt maturities, including the SOLT4 bond and Chinese bank loans.
