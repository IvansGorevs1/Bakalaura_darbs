During the financial year 2025, Heimstaden demonstrated resilient operational performance and continued balance sheet strengthening amidst a volatile macroeconomic environment. Total rental income remained flat at SEK 15,916 million, compared to SEK 15,919 million in 2024, constrained by foreign exchange effects, portfolio sales, and the privatisation program, while supported by indexation and tenant improvements. Annualised like-for-like rental income growth was 4.9 percent, compared to 5.6 percent in the previous year, driven primarily by indexation. Real economic occupancy remained steady at 98.5 percent. Property expenses decreased by 6.1 percent to SEK 4,378 million from SEK 4,661 million, driven by cost control measures, digitalisation, and a shrinking portfolio size. Consequently, net operating income improved to SEK 11,475 million from SEK 11,188 million, pushing the last-twelve-months net operating income margin up to 72.1 percent from 70.3 percent. Fair value changes of investment properties yielded a positive increase of 2.9 percent, or SEK 9,498 million, bringing total investment property fair value to SEK 324,429 million compared to SEK 335,422 million at the end of 2024. Profit before tax rose significantly to SEK 16,012 million from SEK 10,403 million, and profit for the period reached SEK 13,056 million compared to SEK 7,621 million, bolstered by valuation gains, lower deferred taxes from German corporate tax rate reductions, and operational performance, despite a goodwill impairment loss of SEK 3,707 million recognized in the fourth quarter. The company actively pursued its privatisation strategy, generating SEK 10,664 million through the sale of 2,466 residential units at a 28.9 percent premium to book value. Net proceeds and cash flows from these divestments were systematically deployed to reduce outstanding debt, supporting a material improvement in credit metrics. Net loan-to-value improved to 52.2 percent from 56.4 percent, and the interest coverage ratio strengthened to 2.0x from 1.9x. Interest expenses were largely stable at SEK 6,536 million, though the average interest rate rose to 3.43 percent from 3.01 percent. Financing activities remained robust, with subsidiary Heimstaden Bostad executing approximately SEK 59,500 million in total refinancings and new financings across bank and capital markets. Heimstaden also executed liability management exercises, issuing SEK 750 million and EUR 830 million in senior unsecured notes to tender near-term maturities. Additionally, an arbitral tribunal ruled favorably in December 2025 regarding an arbitration case initiated by Alecta, confirming no breach of the Shareholders Agreement. Overall, Heimstaden advanced its strategic focus on cost discipline, deleveraging, and portfolio efficiency, resulting in improved profitability margins and strengthened financial stability during 2025.
