OODA AI AB reported a significant commercial breakthrough in 2025, marking a shift from a buildup phase to active growth. Net sales surged to SEK 31,880 thousand from SEK 1,056 thousand in 2024, driven by the invoicing of major transactions with key clients, including Win.Rocks AG, where the total contract value reached approximately SEK 21 million alongside a new agreement worth at least SEK 15 million over 24 months. Additional contracts were secured with Sergel Kredittjänster and HMM Deutschland, the latter estimated at SEK 10 million for 2025. Revenue was predominantly realized in international markets. This top-line expansion coincided with improved operational performance; operating profit narrowed to minus SEK 29,270 thousand from minus SEK 51,781 thousand in the prior year. EBITDA turned positive, reaching SEK 2,957 thousand compared to minus SEK 28,229 thousand. Adjusted for one-off costs of approximately SEK 10,238 thousand related to the liquidation of older corporate structures, adjusted EBITDA amounted to roughly SEK 13,195 thousand, reflecting an adjusted EBITDA margin of approximately 32 percent. Operational costs remained disciplined, with personnel costs at minus SEK 9,210 thousand and average headcount stable at four employees, while development efforts continued with capitalized costs of approximately SEK 8.7 million.

Despite operational advancements, the group recorded a net loss of minus SEK 125,799 thousand, deteriorating from minus SEK 83,355 thousand in 2024. The widening loss was primarily attributable to impairments and negative results from financial items totaling minus SEK 95,853 thousand, resulting from a comprehensive review of financial holdings that involved writing down certain investments to a book value of zero. Consequently, financial fixed assets decreased from SEK 96,036 thousand to SEK 8,387 thousand, and equity declined to SEK 23,859 thousand from SEK 119,371 thousand. Conversely, the company enhanced its liquidity through multiple capital raises during the year, issuing shares at approximately SEK 162 each to bring the total share count to 3,731,465. Interest expenses dropped substantially to SEK 384 thousand from SEK 17,032 thousand, indicating reduced indebtedness. Cash flow from operating activities improved to minus SEK 16,662 thousand versus minus SEK 29,680 thousand, and notably turned positive in the second half of the year at plus SEK 5,098 thousand. Strategic initiatives advanced with the launch of an MVP and test network for the decentralized AI infrastructure, expansions via partners like Allgeier Inovar GmbH, and the consolidation of subsidiaries into oodash Portfolio AB. The board affirmed going concern viability, citing capital injections, operational cash flow stabilization, and a clear path to scale, with broader platform commercialization scheduled for the second quarter of 2026.
