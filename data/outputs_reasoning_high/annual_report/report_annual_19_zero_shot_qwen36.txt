Talkpool delivered solid financial progress in 2025, concluding its multi year restructuring phase and initiating an organic growth cycle aligned with its Vision 2030 strategic plan. Net sales reached EUR 17,756 thousand, a 12.5 percent increase from EUR 15,782 thousand in 2024. Profitability metrics strengthened correspondingly, with net earnings after tax rising 42 percent to EUR 862 thousand and an earnings after tax margin of 4.8 percent. The company reported an EBITDA of EUR 1,828 thousand with a 10.3 percent margin, down slightly from 10.8 percent in 2024, alongside an EBIT margin of 9.1 percent. Gross margin stood at 26.4 percent. These results occurred despite first half currency headwinds, including an 11 percent weakening of the US dollar and a 14 percent drop in Pakistani Rupees that temporarily reduced EUR reported revenues.

The overall performance demonstrates a successful transition toward sustainable scaling. Equity improved to EUR 2.1 million, and the company moved into a positive net cash position of EUR 0.3 million at year end, recovering from previous deficit periods. Management credited the improved profitability to stabilized operations in Pakistan, which posted record local revenues and enhanced margins after favorable commercial renegotiations, alongside disciplined cost management. Although initial investments for future growth exerted temporary pressure on margins, organic revenue growth accelerated to exceed 20 percent by December, surpassing long term strategic targets ahead of schedule.

Strategic initiatives and market developments fundamentally reshaped the business landscape. Talkpool launched Vision 2030 in April, targeting EUR 40 million in revenues and a 14 percent EBITDA margin by 2030. The Americas maintained its position as the largest market, while the company re entered the US market through a May service agreement with MasTec. Germany provided steady contributions through fiber planning for Deutsche Telekom, supported by an independent valuation placing the German subsidiary at approximately EUR 20.5 million. Operationally, Talkpool pivoted toward technology enabled remote services, conducting its first AI powered proof of concept trials to prepare for commercialization between 2027 and 2029. The group also expanded into Sweden with a new defense sector affiliate and successfully executed a share options program that multiplied participant value fifty fold amid a rising share price. With auditors removing going concern uncertainties and management forecasting near term cash flow normalization as investments convert to revenue, Talkpool has secured a stable platform for capitalizing on structural industry shifts toward AI driven network optimization.
