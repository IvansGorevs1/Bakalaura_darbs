Talkpool reported positive financial results for the 2025 reporting period, marked by a successful transition from a multi-year restructuring phase into organic business expansion. Net sales for the full year increased by 12.5 percent to EUR 17,756 thousand, compared to EUR 15,782 thousand in the previous year. Profitability also improved as the company focused on higher-margin activities and cost efficiency. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization reached EUR 1,828 thousand, compared to EUR 1,702 thousand in 2024, resulting in an EBITDA margin of 10.3 percent versus 10.8 percent previously. Operating profit stood at EUR 1,620 thousand, up from EUR 1,527 thousand, yielding an EBIT margin of 9.1 percent. Net earnings after tax experienced significant growth, increasing by 42 percent to EUR 862 thousand from EUR 605 thousand, lifting the net earnings margin to 4.8 percent from 3.8 percent in the prior year.

The company strengthened its financial position considerably during 2025. Equity improved to EUR 2.1 million, recovering from negative EUR 2.5 million in 2021. Net cash reached a positive position of EUR 0.3 million by year-end, representing a EUR 6.0 million improvement from negative EUR 5.7 million in 2021. An independent valuation by BDO assessed the fair value of Talkpool's German subsidiary at EUR 20.5 million. Furthermore, post-period developments in early 2026 included a EUR 1.65 million share issue that further increased net cash to approximately EUR 2 million, alongside the removal of going-concern uncertainties by company auditors.

Operationally, performance was supported by diverse geographic segments. The Americas remained the company's largest market, delivering steady growth despite currency headwinds, and the company re-entered the United States market through a new service contract with MasTec. In Germany, operations maintained a solid contribution through fixed network planning partnerships with Deutsche Telekom, despite a temporary flattening of assignment volumes. Pakistan served as the group's main production base, achieving record local currency revenues and improving gross profitability following commercial term renegotiations. Additionally, Talkpool launched a new Swedish affiliated company targeting the defense sector and introduced the Vision 2030 strategic growth plan in April, establishing targets such as a 16.8 percent compound annual growth rate and a 14 percent EBITDA margin. To support future expansion, the company also signed an agreement in April 2026 to acquire Netcom Global Partners, which is expected to contribute approximately EUR 1 million in revenue. Overall, 2025 represented a foundational turning point toward stable, profitable, and technology-enabled growth.
