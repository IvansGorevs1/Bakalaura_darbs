Coop Pank reported consolidated net profit of 28.7 million euros for 2025, an eleven percent decrease from 32.2 million euros in the prior year. The decline stemmed primarily from a contracting interest rate environment that compressed margins, reducing net interest income to 73.3 million euros from 77.6 million euros. Total net operating income reached 79.4 million euros, while net fee and commission income rose modestly to 4.5 million euros. Operating expenses increased to 41.5 million euros, lifting the cost to income ratio to 52.3 percent, whereas credit loss allowances fell to 3.3 million euros from 4.6 million euros. Income tax expense was 5.8 million euros. Return on equity declined to 12.9 percent from 16.2 percent, and the common equity tier one capital ratio stood at 14.15 percent, comfortably exceeding regulatory requirements including buffers.

Business volumes expanded substantially despite margin pressures. The loan portfolio grew nineteen percent to 2.11 billion euros, driven by a twenty seven percent increase in business loans and a seventeen percent rise in mortgage lending. Customer deposits increased nine percent to 2.05 billion euros, supported by eleven percent growth in demand deposits. The retail customer base expanded by nine percent, reaching 227,000 individuals and businesses. These developments occurred against a macroeconomic backdrop of receding inflation, steady European Central Bank rate cuts that brought the deposit facility rate to 2.0 percent, and modest domestic GDP growth of 0.7 percent. The lower rate environment reinvigorated lending demand and facilitated economic recovery, though it temporarily constrained profitability.

Strategic funding and capital management actions significantly strengthened the bank during the period. In the first quarter, Coop Pank issued 250 million euros in four year covered bonds on the Irish stock exchange, securing long term liquidity and reducing reliance on expensive term deposits. A synthetic securitization agreement with the European Investment Bank group transferred credit risk on a segment of the loan portfolio, delivering capital relief that enables up to 249 million euros in new targeted SME financing. Additionally, the bank secured a five million euro subordinated loan from the European Energy Efficiency Fund. Moody maintains a Baa2 deposit rating with a positive outlook. Management leadership transitioned with Arko Kurtmann assuming the role of Management Board Chairman. The bank distributed 7.2 million euros in dividends, consistent with its stated policy. Overall, Coop Pank scaled its core lending operations and diversified its funding profile while navigating a transitional rate cycle, establishing a balanced foundation for future margin normalization and sustained volume growth.
