Eleving Group reported strong financial performance and record growth for the twelve-month period ended December 31, 2024. Total revenue reached 216.6 million euros, representing an increase of nearly 15 percent compared with 189.3 million euros in the previous year. This revenue expansion was supported by strong performance across both vehicle and consumer financing segments, driven by higher loan issuance volumes and organic demand. Total loan issuance volumes increased by 26.8 percent to 368.6 million euros, compared with 290.6 million euros in 2023.

Profitability improved significantly during the reporting period. Adjusted EBITDA rose by nearly 16 percent to 89.8 million euros, up from 77.5 million euros in the prior year. Total net profit reached an all-time high of 29.6 million euros, marking an increase of 21 percent compared with 24.5 million euros in 2023. This net profit figure includes a negative impact from a one-off tax expense of 2.6 million euros resulting from additional VAT liabilities determined after a tax audit in Romania for prior periods. Adjusted for this one-off tax expense, total net profit amounted to 32.2 million euros. Net profit before foreign exchange results and discontinued operations rose to 32.5 million euros, compared with 28.3 million euros previously.

The vehicle and consumer financing net portfolio grew by 16 percent to reach 371.2 million euros at the end of 2024, compared with 320.3 million euros at the end of 2023. The Group maintained disciplined underwriting standards, resulting in improved portfolio quality and a decrease in non-performing loan rates. At the same time, operating expenses grew alongside business scaling. Administrative expenses increased to 74.7 million euros from 63.2 million euros, partly due to full-year contributions from subsidiaries acquired in the previous year, while impairment expenses rose to 42.1 million euros from 39.8 million euros.

A major strategic milestone during the reporting period was the successful initial public offering completed in October 2024 on Nasdaq Riga and the Frankfurt Stock Exchange, which secured 29.0 million euros in equity. Proceeds from the IPO, combined with continued operational cash generation, strengthened the Group's capital base. Total equity increased substantially to 108.1 million euros as of December 31, 2024, compared with 65.4 million euros a year earlier. The capitalization ratio strengthened to 29.3 percent from 26.1 percent, while net leverage improved to 3.3 times from 3.7 times. Additionally, the Group received a credit rating upgrade from Fitch Ratings to 'B' with a stable outlook in June 2024 and successfully prepaid its most expensive subordinated debt using IPO proceeds, reinforcing its financial resilience and capital structure.
