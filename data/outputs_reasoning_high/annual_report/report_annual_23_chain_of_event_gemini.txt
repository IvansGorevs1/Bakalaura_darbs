TKM Grupp AS reported its consolidated financial results for the 2025 financial year, reflecting a challenging economic environment characterised by low consumer confidence and intense retail competition in Estonia. Consolidated sales revenue for 2025 was 919.6 million euros, representing a decrease of 2.6 percent compared to 944.6 million euros in 2024. Profit before tax for 2025 declined by 31.4 percent to 24.3 million euros, compared to 35.5 million euros in the previous year. Net profit for the reporting period amounted to 17.5 million euros, marking a 36.1 percent decrease from 27.5 million euros in 2024. Basic earnings per share decreased to 0.43 euros from 0.67 euros in the prior year. The decline in financial performance was primarily driven by a substantial contraction in the car trade segment, influenced by the introduction of the motor vehicle tax in Estonia which led to a sharp drop in new passenger car sales. Total car segment revenue fell by 11.9 percent to 176.9 million euros, and segment net profit decreased by 54.4 percent to 4.3 million euros. Performance in the real estate segment was also affected by comparative base periods that included one-off gains from property sales and revaluations in 2024. Conversely, the supermarket segment demonstrated resilience, with revenue increasing slightly by 0.3 percent to 611.9 million euros, though net profit declined by 38.6 percent to 9.5 million euros. The department stores segment recorded revenue of 103.0 million euros, a 1.1 percent decrease, and a net loss of 0.5 million euros. The security services segment saw external revenue decrease by 8.1 percent to 20.1 million euros, recording a break-even net result. During 2025, the Group continued to execute strategic investments, totaling 25.5 million euros compared to 36.6 million euros in 2024. Key investments included the opening of a new multi-brand car centre in Vilnius, refurbishments of the Jõgeva Selver store and Kaubamaja Children's World, and upgrades to information systems and energy-efficient building solutions. Total assets at the end of 2025 stood at 697.9 million euros, down from 706.7 million euros at the end of 2024, while total equity increased slightly to 263.8 million euros from 261.5 million euros. Cash and cash equivalents decreased to 29.5 million euros from 45.5 million euros. A significant post-balance-sheet development occurred in January 2026, when subsidiary TKM Auto OÜ entered into an agreement to acquire 100 percent of the shares in Rohe Auto AS and SKO Motors OÜ to strengthen its position in the Estonian car market. For the 2025 financial year, the management board proposed a dividend of 0.60 euros per share, totaling 24.4 million euros.
