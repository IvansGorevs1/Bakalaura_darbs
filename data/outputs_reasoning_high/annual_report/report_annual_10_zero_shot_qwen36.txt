InCoax Networks AB reported net sales of SEK 48,055,007 for fiscal year 2025, a 35 percent decrease compared to SEK 74,497,775 in 2024. The revenue decline was primarily attributed to timing shifts in customer orders, delayed projects, and a more cautious market. Gross profit totalled SEK 29,919,750, improving the gross margin to 62 percent from 57 percent previously. Higher operational spending relative to reduced turnover resulted in an operating loss of SEK 32,380,175, widening from the prior year figure of SEK 19,378,406. Net loss after tax reached SEK 32,559,663, equating to a loss per share of SEK 0.25. Continued strategic investment maintained capitalised development costs at SEK 25,269,745.

Operating cash flow registered a negative SEK 20,011,617, showing marginal improvement from the previous years outflow of SEK 27,117,256. The positive consolidated cash flow of SEK 6,516,117 was entirely funded by financing activities, which raised approximately SEK 53.5 million before costs through a directed share issue, convertible debentures, and borrowings. Year end cash balances stood at SEK 8.7 million, while the shareholder credit facility was completely utilised. The equity ratio consequently decreased to 71.8 percent from 80.8 percent, with total equity closing at SEK 109,778,755.

Operationally, 2025 marked a decisive shift from development to commercial execution. The company finalised its updated MoCA Access platform, launching the D2501 product through Nokia Gigabit Connect and rolling out the D2508 unit via direct channels. These solutions address operator demand for cost effective last mile distribution in multi dwelling buildings across North America and Europe. To offset earnings pressure and preserve liquidity, management enacted a structural cost reduction programme expected to lower the ongoing cost base by more than 40 percent, yielding approximately SEK 25 million in annual savings from 2026. While broader fibre and fixed wireless infrastructure rollouts support market relevance, InCoax faces extended procurement cycles and partner dependency risks. The auditor noted a material uncertainty regarding going concern status, as near term financing depends on warrant exercises linked to equity valuation. Looking forward, executive leadership has prioritised strict capital discipline, accelerated pipeline conversion, and achieving positive operating cash flow within twelve months, targeting cash flow neutrality throughout 2026.
