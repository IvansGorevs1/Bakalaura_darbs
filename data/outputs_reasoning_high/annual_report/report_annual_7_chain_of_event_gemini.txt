During the 2025 financial year, Heimstaden AB demonstrated stable operational performance and balance sheet strengthening despite challenging macroeconomic conditions. Total rental income remained virtually flat at SEK 15,916 million, compared to SEK 15,919 million in 2024, as organic growth from indexation and tenant improvement investments was offset by negative foreign exchange effects, privatisations, and portfolio sales. Annualised like-for-like rental income growth reached 4.9 percent, down from 5.6 percent in the previous year, while real economic occupancy remained stable at 98.5 percent. Net operating income improved to SEK 11,475 million from SEK 11,188 million, driven by disciplined cost control, internal digitalisation initiatives, and portfolio reductions, which helped decrease total property expenses by 6.1 percent to SEK 4,378 million from SEK 4,661 million. Consequently, the last twelve months net operating income margin strengthened to 72.1 percent from 70.3 percent.

Investment property fair values increased by 2.9 percent, or SEK 9,498 million, compared to 2.6 percent or SEK 8,553 million in 2024, supported by positive valuation yields and operational net operating income growth. However, the total carrying fair value of investment properties decreased to SEK 324,429 million from SEK 335,422 million due to ongoing divestments and adverse foreign currency translation effects. Realised gains from property divestments rose to SEK 2,207 million from SEK 1,687 million, significantly boosted by the active privatisation programme, which achieved sales of SEK 10,664 million across 2,466 residential units sold at a 28.9 percent premium to book value. Operating profit increased to SEK 22,484 million from SEK 19,608 million, and profit before tax rose to SEK 16,012 million from SEK 10,403 million, despite a goodwill impairment loss of SEK 3,707 million recorded during the year. Overall profit for the period increased to SEK 13,056 million compared to SEK 7,621 million in 2024.

The company achieved notable deleveraging and credit metric improvements throughout 2025. Total interest-bearing liabilities decreased to SEK 175,227 million from SEK 195,816 million, supported by debt repayments funded through privatisation cash proceeds and liability management exercises. Net loan-to-value improved to 52.2 percent from 56.4 percent, while net debt to total assets decreased to 48.0 percent from 51.0 percent. The interest coverage ratio improved slightly to 2.0x from 1.9x, while interest expenses remained steady at SEK 6,536 million compared to SEK 6,554 million, though the average interest rate rose to 3.43 percent from 3.01 percent. Total equity increased to SEK 150,557 million from SEK 147,803 million. These financial results reflect the successful execution of capital recycling, ongoing cost discipline, and strategic balance sheet fortification.
