During the financial year ended 31 December 2025, AS Tallinna Sadam Group reported consolidated revenue of EUR 118,687 thousand, representing a slight decrease of 0.8 percent or EUR 901 thousand compared to EUR 119,587 thousand in 2024. The reduction in overall revenue was primarily driven by lower charter fees in the Other segment due to a decreased number of charter days and lower day rates for the icebreaker Botnica, where charter fee revenue fell by 33.6 percent to EUR 7,911 thousand. In contrast, revenue increased in the passenger harbours, cargo harbours, and ferry segments, supported by indexed fee rates, higher vessel dues from larger vessels, and growth in operational volumes. Specifically, cargo volumes grew by 5.1 percent to 13.799 million tonnes, supported by liquid bulk recovery at Paldiski South Harbour and record shipments of steel and grain at Muuga Harbour. Passenger numbers increased by 1.0 percent to 8.284 million passengers, driven by growth on the Tallinn to Helsinki route and an increase in cruise passengers. 

Despite the minor decline in revenue, the Group achieved improved profitability due to a 9.9 percent reduction in operating expenses, which fell by EUR 4,015 thousand to EUR 36,412 thousand. This expense reduction was mainly attributable to lower technical maintenance and repair costs for non-current assets, lower lease expenses, and reduced fuel costs, alongside the receipt of insurance indemnity for a prior-year technical failure on the icebreaker Botnica. Consequently, operating profit grew by 12.2 percent to EUR 32,704 thousand compared to EUR 29,148 thousand in 2024. Adjusted EBITDA increased by 6.4 percent to EUR 56,510 thousand from EUR 53,134 thousand, with the adjusted EBITDA margin rising from 44.4 percent to 47.6 percent. 

Financial costs decreased by 30.2 percent to EUR 5,773 thousand due to lower market interest rates and reduced loan and debt security balances. Profit before income tax rose by 25.1 percent to EUR 27,878 thousand. Net profit for the period increased by 17.3 percent to EUR 22,463 thousand compared to EUR 19,153 thousand in the previous year, although net profit growth was partially moderated by an increase in income tax expense on dividends due to a higher tax rate. Basic and diluted earnings per share increased from EUR 0.07 to EUR 0.09. 

Capital investments totaled EUR 33,245 thousand, down 14.4 percent from EUR 38,831 thousand in 2024, with major investments including the completion of the multifunctional quay at Paldiski South Harbour. Net debt decreased by 15.5 percent to EUR 141,708 thousand at year-end, while total equity rose by 0.9 percent to EUR 380,877 thousand, resulting in an equity to assets ratio of 61 percent. In 2025, the Group paid dividends of EUR 0.073 per share, totaling EUR 19,199 thousand, and the management board has proposed a dividend of EUR 0.073 per share, amounting to EUR 19,199 thousand, for the 2025 financial year.
