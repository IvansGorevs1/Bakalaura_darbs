Nordic Fibreboard AS reported a weaker financial performance in 2025 despite relatively stable fibreboard sales. Consolidated revenue decreased 3.4% to €7.328 million from €7.592 million in 2024, with fibreboard production and sales contributing €7.325 million. Sales to the European Union, representing 92% of revenue, remained relatively stable, while sales to Africa, Asia and the Middle East declined. Real estate management revenue fell to €3 thousand from €25 thousand following the termination of rental agreements in 2024.

Profitability deteriorated materially. Gross profit declined to €603 thousand from €771 thousand, while EBITDA fell to a negative €420 thousand from a negative €25 thousand, reducing the EBITDA margin from 0% to negative 6%. The operating loss widened to €940 thousand from €547 thousand, and the net loss increased to €1.154 million from €786 thousand. The net margin consequently declined from negative 10% to negative 16%, while basic and diluted loss per share improved from €0.17 to €0.14 because the number of shares nearly doubled.

The fibreboard segment reported negative EBITDA of €296 thousand compared with positive EBITDA of €26 thousand in 2024. Management attributed the deterioration to underutilised production capacity and the planned December factory shutdown for the transition to a new gas boiler house. Personnel, maintenance and repair costs continued during the shutdown, reducing EBITDA by approximately €0.2 million. Distribution costs increased to €982 thousand from €935 thousand and administrative expenses to €502 thousand from €381 thousand. Total labour expenses rose to €1.931 million from €1.630 million, while electricity, heat and water costs increased to €2.699 million from €2.368 million.

Operating cash flow remained negative and worsened to €503 thousand from €174 thousand. Investing cash outflows increased to €989 thousand, including €1.367 million invested in production assets and €161 thousand in real estate. Financing activities generated €1.444 million, including a €2 million share issue completed in October 2025. Consequently, the net cash outflow was €48 thousand and closing cash declined to €5 thousand from €53 thousand.

Total assets increased to €9.323 million from €8.252 million, mainly reflecting higher fixed assets and inventories. Equity rose to €4.462 million from €3.616 million following the share issue, partly offset by the annual loss. Borrowings declined to €3.168 million from €3.724 million, but working capital was negative by €2.7 million compared with €0.7 million in 2024. Management plans to address this through property sales. The Group also merged Pärnu Riverside Development OÜ into Nordic Fibreboard AS and completed the Admirali Quarter project design and permitting. In January 2026, a preliminary agreement was signed for the sale of the ten development properties, while the boiler-house upgrade was completed.
