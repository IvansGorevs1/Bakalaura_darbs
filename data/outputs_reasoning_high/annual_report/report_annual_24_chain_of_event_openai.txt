Trophy Games delivered a substantially stronger performance in 2025, with revenue and other operating income increasing 40% to DKK 126.5 million from DKK 90.1 million. EBITDA rose 26% to DKK 31.1 million, while profit before tax more than doubled, increasing 109% to DKK 19.0 million from DKK 9.1 million. Profit for the year increased to DKK 12.5 million from DKK 7.5 million. The EBT margin improved to 15% from 10%, although the EBITDA margin declined to 25% from 27% as the company increased marketing investment. Management noted that 2024 EBT included DKK 4.9 million of non-recurring impairment costs; excluding these, the 2024 adjusted EBT margin was also 15%.

Growth was primarily driven by the Transport Series and its “Big 3” titles. Airline Manager revenue increased 28% to DKK 37.8 million, while Truck Manager revenue rose from DKK 1.2 million to DKK 25.2 million as the title entered its scaling phase. Farm Manager, launched in July 2025 after five million pre-registrations and prominent app-store featuring, generated DKK 22.0 million in its first six months. Together, Airline Manager, Truck Manager and Farm Manager generated DKK 85 million, or 67% of group revenue, compared with 35% in 2024. In contrast, revenue from other Transport titles declined 46% to DKK 21.0 million as marketing resources were redirected toward the higher-velocity core titles.

The company also materially changed its monetisation mix. In-app advertising revenue doubled to DKK 18.5 million and represented 14.6% of revenue, compared with 10.2% in 2024. The advertising optimisation programme began in late September and was rolled out progressively during the fourth quarter. Management stated that advertising revenue carries no platform fees, compared with fees of up to 30% on in-app purchases, supporting direct-cost efficiency. Marketing expenditure increased 76% to DKK 40.5 million to accelerate user acquisition. The weaker USD affected reported first-half revenue and EBITDA, but management used lower real marketing costs in key territories to increase acquisition spending.

The fourth quarter was the strongest in company history, with revenue up 106% to DKK 41.0 million and EBT up 574% to DKK 10.1 million, despite a 296% increase in quarterly marketing expenditure. Cash declined to DKK 23.0 million, partly reflecting the expanded marketing programme, a DKK 14 million VAT receivable, DKK 4.7 million in repaid tax credits and DKK 4.9 million of share buybacks; cash had recovered to DKK 36.4 million by 31 January 2026. For 2026, management guides for revenue of DKK 130–153 million, EBITDA of DKK 28–36 million and EBT of DKK 18–24 million, while continuing investment in Truck Manager, Farm Manager, Train Manager 2 and Space Manager.
