For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, Trophy Games Development A/S delivered a landmark performance characterized by accelerated revenue growth and strong profitability across its core simulation and manager portfolio. Total revenue and other operating income increased by 40 percent to DKK 126,523 thousand compared to DKK 90,070 thousand in 2024. This expansion was primarily driven by the successful scaling of the Transport Game Series, specifically the Big Three titles consisting of Airline Manager, Truck Manager, and Farm Manager, which collectively generated 67 percent of total revenue amounting to DKK 85 million. Airline Manager contributed DKK 37,787 thousand, reflecting a 28 percent increase. Truck Manager scaled rapidly to DKK 25,181 thousand, while Farm Manager, launched in July 2025 following five million pre-registrations, contributed DKK 22,002 thousand in its first six months. Conversely, revenue from smaller transport titles declined by 46 percent to DKK 20,955 thousand as marketing resources were strategically reallocated toward the high-velocity Big Three.

Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization grew by 26 percent to DKK 31,117 thousand, up from DKK 24,671 thousand in the prior year, yielding an EBITDA-margin of 25 percent. Profit before tax reached DKK 19,013 thousand, representing a 109 percent increase compared to DKK 9,068 thousand in 2024, resulting in an EBT-margin of 15 percent. Profit for the year increased to DKK 12,539 thousand compared to DKK 7,471 thousand, with basic earnings per share rising to DKK 0.47. Profitability was further supported by a surge in in-app advertising revenue, which doubled to DKK 18,476 thousand and accounted for 14.6 percent of total revenue, benefiting from carrying zero platform fees compared to traditional in-app purchases.

Operational execution involved a strategic expansion in marketing expenses, which rose by 76 percent to DKK 40,486 thousand to drive user acquisition. Total assets grew by 24 percent to DKK 84,597 thousand, driven by operational scaling and an increase in receivables, including a DKK 14 million VAT receivable. Total equity strengthened to DKK 66,120 thousand, supported by a solvency ratio of 78 percent and an unleveraged capital structure free of interest-bearing external debt. Cash at bank and in hand ended at DKK 23,049 thousand, down 9 percent due to record marketing investments, a share buy-back program totaling DKK 4,894 thousand, and an extraordinary repayment of DKK 4.7 million in tax credits following a tax authority reassessment. For 2026, management expects revenue between DKK 130 million and DKK 153 million, EBITDA between DKK 28 million and DKK 36 million, and EBT between DKK 18 million and DKK 24 million.
