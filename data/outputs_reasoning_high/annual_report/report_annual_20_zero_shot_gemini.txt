During the financial year ended 31 December 2025, AS Tallink Grupp and its subsidiaries operated in a challenging economic environment shaped by low consumer and business confidence and global geopolitical tensions. The Group carried 5.5 million passengers, representing a decrease of 0.9 percent compared to 2024. Transported cargo units decreased by 19.2 percent to 245,004 units, while passenger vehicles decreased by 2.2 percent to 760,473 units. Total audited consolidated revenue for 2025 amounted to EUR 765.3 million, reflecting a 2.6 percent decrease from EUR 785.8 million in the previous financial year. Revenue from core route operations decreased by EUR 13.7 million to EUR 625.2 million. Restaurant and shop sales onboard and on shore totalled EUR 366.4 million, ticket sales amounted to EUR 222.7 million, cargo transport sales were EUR 77.9 million, and revenue from chartering vessels reached EUR 54.9 million. Gross profit decreased to EUR 143.2 million compared to EUR 153.6 million in 2024. EBITDA declined by EUR 45.1 million to EUR 130.1 million, down from EUR 175.2 million in the prior year. The Group reported an audited net profit of EUR 17.3 million for 2025, which was lower than the EUR 40.3 million recorded in 2024. Basic and diluted earnings per share were EUR 0.023, compared to EUR 0.054 in the previous year. Profitability was positively impacted by a decline in depreciation and amortisation expenses, which dropped by EUR 18.0 million to EUR 79.8 million, largely resulting from aligning the estimated useful lives of cruise and passenger vessels, excluding shuttle vessels, to 45 years, as well as the sale of three vessels during the year. These asset sales included the passenger vessel Star I in April, the cargo vessel Regal Star in August, and the cargo vessel Sailor in October. As of the end of the year, the Group operated a fleet of 11 vessels, comprising two shuttle vessels, six passenger vessels, and three chartered-out vessels. Net finance costs decreased by EUR 6.8 million to EUR 21.4 million due to lower outstanding loan balances and declining interest rates. Net operating cash flow remained positive at EUR 127.1 million. Interest-bearing liabilities decreased by 19.9 percent to EUR 445.9 million as of 31 December 2025, down from EUR 556.4 million a year earlier. Net debt stood at EUR 432.4 million, compared to EUR 537.7 million at the end of 2024, resulting in a net debt to EBITDA ratio of 3.3. The Group's total liquidity buffer, consisting of cash, cash equivalents, and unused credit facilities, improved to EUR 110.4 million at year-end, up from EUR 93.7 million in 2024. During the financial year, the Group paid dividends totalling EUR 44.6 million, or EUR 0.06 per share, along with related income tax of EUR 11.3 million. Total capital expenditures amounted to EUR 33.0 million, primarily directed toward vessel maintenance, dry-docking, and IT system improvements.
