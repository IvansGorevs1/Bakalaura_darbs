WindowMaster International A/S reported a weaker financial performance in 2025 than in 2024, although activity improved during the second half of the year. Revenue declined 8% to DKK 268.6 million from DKK 294.5 million, primarily because of a slow start to the year, lower sales to the major Nordic customer, weaker performance in Climatic and less growth in North America. Revenue was DKK 21–41 million below the original budget but in line with the latest guidance of DKK 269–270 million.

Gross profit decreased to DKK 131.1 million from DKK 137.9 million, while the gross margin improved to 48.8% from 46.9%. Management attributed the margin improvement to the business mix, including less revenue from key accounts and more direct project revenue. EBITDA fell to DKK 28.0 million from DKK 37.3 million, reducing the EBITDA margin to 10.4% from 12.7%. The decline occurred despite the higher gross margin because fixed costs were maintained to support future growth. Fixed costs also included DKK 2.5 million of extraordinary costs related to rebuilding IT infrastructure, strengthening firewall security and external support following a ransomware attack in May and June. No ransom was paid.

Operating profit declined to DKK 8.3 million from DKK 17.7 million. Earnings before tax fell to DKK 2.9 million from DKK 12.2 million, with net finance expenses of DKK 5.3 million compared with DKK 5.5 million in 2024. Profit after tax was DKK 0.9 million, down from DKK 10.6 million. The effective tax rate rose to 70%, which the report relates to the non-utilization of tax assets, including tax loss carryforwards.

Cash generation remained solid. Cash flow from operating activities was DKK 28.5 million, compared with DKK 29.4 million in 2024, supported by improved net working capital, particularly inventories and work in progress. Investing cash outflow decreased to DKK 9.4 million from DKK 17.6 million, with investments focused mainly on digitalization and IT infrastructure. Year-end cash increased to DKK 4.0 million. Net interest-bearing debt rose to DKK 38.7 million from DKK 36.7 million, and gearing increased to 1.7 from 1.2, remaining below the target of 2.0. Equity declined to DKK 32.5 million from DKK 36.6 million, and the net-cash equity ratio was 23.5% versus 24.8%.

Market activity improved as interest rates stabilized, particularly in non-residential construction, while residential demand remained subdued. For 2026, management expects organic revenue growth of 7–14% to DKK 290–310 million and EBITDA of DKK 45–55 million, with continued uncertainty around project timing, economic conditions and geopolitical developments.
