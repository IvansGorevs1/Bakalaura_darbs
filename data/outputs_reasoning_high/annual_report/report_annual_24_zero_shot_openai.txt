Trophy Games delivered a significantly stronger performance in 2025, driven mainly by rapid scaling of its Transport Series. Revenue and other operating income increased 40% to DKK 126.5 million from DKK 90.1 million in 2024. EBITDA rose 26% to DKK 31.1 million, while profit before tax more than doubled by 109% to DKK 19.0 million. Profit for the year increased to DKK 12.5 million from DKK 7.5 million. The EBITDA margin declined to 25% from 27%, reflecting substantially higher marketing investment, while the EBT margin improved to 15% from 10%. Management noted that, after adjusting for significant non-recurring impairments in 2024, the underlying EBT margin was 15% in both years.

The Transport Series was the primary growth engine, generating DKK 106 million in revenue compared with DKK 69 million in 2024. The “Big 3” titles—Airline Manager, Truck Manager and Farm Manager—accounted for 67% of group revenue, or DKK 85 million, compared with 35% in 2024, and generated DKK 20 million of EBITDA versus DKK 9 million. Airline Manager revenue increased 28% to DKK 37.8 million. Truck Manager, launched in late 2024, generated DKK 25.2 million compared with DKK 1.2 million previously, while Farm Manager contributed DKK 22.0 million during its first six months following its July launch. By contrast, revenue from other Transport titles fell 46% to DKK 21.0 million as marketing resources were redirected toward the higher-growth core titles.

The company expanded marketing expenditure 76% to DKK 40.5 million to support user acquisition and market-share growth. In-app advertising revenue doubled to DKK 18.5 million and increased to 14.6% of total revenue from 10.2%, supporting direct-cost efficiency because advertising is not subject to platform fees. The first half was affected by a weakening USD, but the company used the lower real cost of marketing in key territories to increase acquisition spending. User metrics also strengthened, with unique paying users up 69%, installs up 48% and transactions up 58%.

Momentum accelerated in the fourth quarter, which was the strongest in company history. Revenue rose 106% to DKK 41.0 million, EBITDA increased to DKK 13.8 million from DKK 7.2 million, and EBT rose 574% to DKK 10.1 million despite a 296% increase in quarterly marketing expenditure.

Operating cash flow declined to DKK 12.9 million from DKK 19.3 million, and year-end cash fell 9% to DKK 23.0 million, partly due to higher working capital, share buybacks and a DKK 4.7 million repayment of previously received tax credits. The group retained a strong capital structure, with a 78% solvency ratio and no interest-bearing external debt. For 2026, management guides for revenue of DKK 130–153 million, EBITDA of DKK 28–36 million and EBT of DKK 18–24 million.
