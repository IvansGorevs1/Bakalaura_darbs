During 2025, Konsolidator delivered stronger growth and substantially reduced losses. Revenue increased 25% to DKK 25.3 million from DKK 20.3 million in 2024, comprising DKK 20.6 million in subscription fees and DKK 4.7 million in onboarding and consulting fees. Subscription revenue rose from DKK 18.3 million, while onboarding and consulting revenue increased 134% from DKK 2.0 million, following higher onboarding prices introduced for new customers during the first quarter.

The EBITDA loss narrowed to DKK 2.0 million from DKK 9.3 million, and the EBIT loss improved to DKK 5.1 million from DKK 12.1 million. The loss for the year was DKK 6.6 million, compared with DKK 14.9 million in 2024. The improvement reflected higher revenue, lower staff and external costs, and operating efficiency measures. Marketing outsourcing reduced the EBIT loss by approximately DKK 3 million, while external expenses declined 11% to DKK 6.3 million. Other operating income of DKK 2.0 million also positively affected EBITDA and included income associated with a strategic commercial agreement with a sales partner. In the second half, EBITDA was positive at DKK 0.5 million, compared with a loss of DKK 3.6 million in the same period of 2024.

Recurring revenue indicators also improved. Contracted annual recurring revenue increased 15% to DKK 24.4 million, above the company’s guidance of DKK 23–24 million. New sales rose 45% to DKK 4.5 million, 52 new customers were added compared with 45 in 2024, and average contracted revenue per customer increased to DKK 87,000 from DKK 70,000. Lower marketing costs reduced customer acquisition costs by 32%, cutting the CAC payback period from 43 to 23 months and improving the CLV/CAC ratio from 2.7 to 4.1. The Rule of 40 score improved from minus 40% to 17%. However, ARR churn increased to 12.6% from 10.5%, while net retention remained unchanged at 94%. Management attributed higher churn among established customers partly to increased demand for FP&A functionality and ERP systems meeting the needs of smaller groups.

The company’s transition from a consolidation product to the broader Konsolidator Suite was a central development. It introduced financial data management and analytics capabilities, began developing FP&A, and signed its first FP&A customers ahead of the January 2026 launch. Partner-led growth expanded, with 38% of new customers acquired through partners versus 22% in 2024, including the Exsitec partnership covering sales and customer success in Sweden and Norway.

Operating cash flow improved to negative DKK 1.0 million from negative DKK 9.2 million, supported by lower losses, earlier invoicing, annual customer payments, and longer vendor payment terms. Net cash flow was positive by DKK 11,000 after DKK 4.8 million raised through private placements, but year-end cash was only DKK 0.4 million. Consolidated equity remained negative at DKK 3.6 million, although parent-company equity was positive at DKK 0.7 million. For 2026, Konsolidator guides for CARR and revenue of DKK 27–29 million and positive EBITDA of DKK 1–2 million, while prioritizing churn reduction, operational cash generation, and refinancing of its loans.
