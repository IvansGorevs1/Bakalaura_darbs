Trophy Games achieved significant financial and operational growth during the 2025 fiscal year, driven primarily by the rapid scaling of its core Transport Game Series. Total revenue increased by 40 percent to 126.5 million Danish kroner, up from 90.1 million Danish kroner in 2024. This top-line expansion was spearheaded by the company's leading titles, comprising Airline Manager, Truck Manager, and Farm Manager. Together, these three games accounted for 67 percent of total revenue, generating 85 million Danish kroner, and 65 percent of EBITDA, amounting to 20 million Danish kroner. Airline Manager contributed 37.8 million Danish kroner, representing a 28 percent increase. Truck Manager expanded dramatically to 25.2 million Danish kroner following its late 2024 launch, while Farm Manager, introduced in July 2025, contributed 22.0 million Danish kroner during its initial six months. Conversely, revenue from smaller transport titles declined as marketing resources were reallocated toward the faster-growing flagship games.

Profitability metrics improved substantially compared with the previous period. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization increased by 26 percent to 31.1 million Danish kroner, though the EBITDA margin moderated slightly to 25 percent from 27 percent due to increased user acquisition spending. Profit before tax rose by 109 percent to 19.0 million Danish kroner, resulting in an improved EBT margin of 15 percent compared with 10 percent in 2024, when results were affected by non-recurring impairment costs. Net profit reached 12.5 million Danish kroner, up from 7.5 million Danish kroner. A major operational catalyst for margin efficiency was the strategic optimization of in-app advertising, which surged 100 percent to 18.5 million Danish kroner and constituted 14.6 percent of total revenue, carrying zero platform commission fees.

Operating expenses grew significantly as management executed an aggressive growth strategy, with total marketing expenses increasing by 76 percent to 40.5 million Danish kroner. Financial performance was also affected by a one-time tax repayment of 4.7 million Danish kroner, including interest and surcharges, following a regulatory reassessment by Danish authorities regarding game development research and development classifications. 

The company maintained a robust balance sheet, ending the year with a strong solvency ratio of 78 percent and no interest-bearing external debt. Total assets grew by 24 percent to 84.6 million Danish kroner, supported by scaling operations and higher receivables, including a substantial value-added tax receivable. Cash and cash equivalents closed at 23.0 million Danish kroner, down 9 percent due to heavy marketing investments, working capital dynamics, the tax repayment, and a share buyback program totaling 4.9 million Danish kroner. Total equity rose to 66.1 million Danish kroner, yielding a return on equity of 20 percent. Looking ahead to 2026, management issued financial guidance projecting revenue between 130 million and 153 million Danish kroner, EBITDA between 28 million and 36 million Danish kroner, and profit before tax between 18 million and 24 million Danish kroner.
