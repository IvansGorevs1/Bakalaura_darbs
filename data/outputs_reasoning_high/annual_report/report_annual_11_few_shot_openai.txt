JLT Mobile Computers reported improved financial performance in 2025, although the Group remained loss-making. Net sales increased by 11.1% to MSEK 131.5 from MSEK 118.4, while order intake rose 37.5% to MSEK 141.6. The year-end order backlog increased to MSEK 24.1 from MSEK 13.9, including MSEK 15.0 scheduled for delivery in the first quarter of 2026. A significant contributor to order intake was a MSEK 22 deal with an American food producer.

Profitability was affected by a sharp decline in the US dollar, which weakened by almost 20% against the Swedish krona. Since 74% of sales were invoiced in dollars, the exchange-rate movement and inventory revaluation reduced the gross margin to 38.3%, compared with 45.8% in 2024. Gross profit therefore declined to MSEK 50.4 from MSEK 54.2 despite higher sales. Cost savings partly offset the margin pressure: operating expenses decreased to MSEK 52.4 from MSEK 57.3, with organizational and research and development costs down by MSEK 6.3. The discontinuation of JLT Software Solutions and the consolidation of software and product development also reduced overhead costs by approximately MSEK 5.

EBITDA improved to a loss of MSEK 1.7 from MSEK 2.1, while the operating loss narrowed significantly to MSEK 1.9 from MSEK 9.7. Lower depreciation and amortization, which fell to MSEK 1.3 from MSEK 9.0 following substantial development-asset write-downs in 2024, supported this improvement. The loss before tax narrowed to MSEK 1.8 from MSEK 9.6, and the net loss decreased to MSEK 2.5 from MSEK 7.7. The tax expense was MSEK 0.7, compared with a tax benefit of MSEK 1.9 in the prior year.

Market conditions remained challenging, particularly in warehousing, logistics, transportation and manufacturing, where customers adopted a cautious approach amid geopolitical and customs uncertainty. Demand was stronger in ports and mining, encouraging JLT to diversify beyond its largest market segment. The company launched the JLT6015, a rugged vehicle-mounted computer with a 15-inch Full HD display, and introduced an AI- and machine-learning-based screen-blanking safety solution. A defense-market evaluation also generated approximately MSEK 1 in costs.

Cash flow and working-capital management improved. Inventory decreased by MSEK 12.7 to MSEK 25.5, contributing to reported cash flow of MSEK 3.7 and year-end cash of MSEK 16.9, up from MSEK 13.3. Equity declined to MSEK 41.8 from MSEK 47.6, while solidity improved to 59% from 54%. The Board proposed no dividend for 2025.
