During the financial year 2025, WindowMaster International A/S achieved revenue of DKK 268,589 thousand, representing an 8.8 percent decrease compared to DKK 294,481 thousand in 2024. The top-line decline was primarily explained by a slow start to the year and lower sales to a key Nordic customer. Gross profit amounted to DKK 131,056 thousand down from DKK 137,949 thousand in the previous year, although the gross margin improved to 48.8 percent from 46.9 percent due to a favorable business mix with reduced revenue from key accounts and higher direct project revenue. Operating profit, measured as EBITDA, decreased to DKK 27,969 thousand compared to DKK 37,305 thousand in 2024, resulting in an EBITDA margin of 10.4 percent compared to 12.7 percent. The EBITDA performance was impacted by maintained fixed cost levels designed to support future growth and extraordinary costs of DKK 2.5 million incurred due to a ransomware attack on the IT infrastructure in May and June 2025. Earnings before tax dropped to DKK 2,908 thousand from DKK 12,184 thousand, and profit after tax fell sharply to DKK 869 thousand compared to DKK 10,601 thousand in 2024, influenced by high tax charges from unutilized tax loss carryforwards. Cash flow from operating activities remained strong at DKK 28,461 thousand, only slightly down from DKK 29,431 thousand in the prior year, supported by net working capital improvements in inventories and work in progress. Total equity at the end of 2025 stood at DKK 32,473 thousand, corresponding to an equity ratio of 23.5 percent with net cash, compared to DKK 36,635 thousand at the end of 2024. Market conditions during the year were characterized by stabilizing interest rates, which improved activity levels and project execution in the second half of the year, particularly within the non-residential segment, whereas the residential market remained subdued due to low consumer confidence. Furthermore, easing global supply chain constraints led customers to place orders closer to project execution, challenging forward visibility. Despite these challenges, management considers the overall performance satisfactory in light of the business environment. Looking ahead to 2026, WindowMaster expects organic revenue growth in the range of 7 to 14 percent, projecting revenue between DKK 290 million and DKK 310 million, and anticipates an increase in EBITDA to DKK 45 million to DKK 55 million, representing an EBITDA margin of 8 to 10 percent, driven by anticipated market improvements and higher project volumes across markets.
