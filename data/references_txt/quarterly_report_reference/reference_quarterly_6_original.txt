Financial Performance (H1 & Q2 2026)
Operating profit (EBIT): Increased by 94% to EUR 51 million (EUR 51.068 million) in H1 2026. 
EBITDA: Rose by 31% to EUR 111 million (EUR 111.228 million) in H1 2026. 
Revenue: Remained broadly unchanged at EUR 946 million (EUR 945.917 million in H1 2026 vs EUR 951.869 million in H1 2025). 
Net profit: Consolidated net profit for H1 2026 was EUR 7.201 million (compared to a net loss of EUR 14.411 million in H1 2025). 
Investments: Group invested approximately EUR 50 million during H1 2026 (H1 2025: EUR 38 million). 
Debt position: Total loan and lease liabilities amounted to EUR 1,083.091 million; net debt stood at EUR 829.904 million. 
Dividends: Dividend of EUR 3.02 per share approved for the 2025 financial year, with payments on 15 July 2026 and 15 December 2026.

Major Events & Operational Highlights
Maritime transport (Tallink): In Q2, Tallink carried 1,454,725 passengers (-2.2%), 68,986 cargo units (+2.9%), and 199,025 passenger vehicles (-6.5%). 
Energy (Elenger): Opened a new biomethane plant at the Halinga dairy farm complex in June. Q2 gas market share in the Finnish-Baltic market increased to 29%, while sales volumes decreased 34% to 3.3 TWh. 
Real estate & infrastructure: INF Infra continued Rail Baltica mainline construction on the Kangru-Saku section (contract value EUR 67.2 million). Construction of the DEPO DIY store continued, scheduled for completion in autumn 2026.
