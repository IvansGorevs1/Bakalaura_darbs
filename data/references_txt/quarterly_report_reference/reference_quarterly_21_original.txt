Operational Performance & Key Drivers
In Q2 2026, passengers decreased by -1.1%, cargo volumes by -2.3%, and vessel calls by -3.1%. Ferry segment showed growth in trips (+1.2%), passengers (+1.8%), and vehicles (+2.8%).
Icebreaker Botnica was chartered 44% of the time.
Profitability was impacted by rising fuel prices, extraordinary ship repairs due to ice conditions, withdrawal of a vessel from Paldiski–Kapellskär route, decline in cargo volumes, and a one-off land sale gain recorded in Q2 2025.
Revenue growth was supported by the cruise business and Botnica chartering.
