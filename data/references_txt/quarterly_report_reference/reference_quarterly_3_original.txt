Financial Performance (Q1 2026)

Net turnover: EUR 11.1 million, a decrease of 29.6% compared to the corresponding period in 2025.

Sales volume (9Lcs): 36.1% lower than in Q1 2025, related to Stoli Group strategic decisions regarding brand development and supply chain optimization.

Gross profit: EUR 1.2 million (-EUR 2.3 million or -66.5%), negatively affected by declining sales volume and pricing methodology changes, partially offset by reduced manufacturing expenses.

Net result: Loss of EUR 1.4 million (compared to a profit of EUR 0.8 million in Q1 2025). Profit-before-tax-to-turnover ratio was 0.2% (2025: 3.7%).

Legal Protection Proceedings (LPP) & Restructuring

AS “Amber Latvijas balzams” filed for LPP on 30 January 2026 (approved 5 February 2026) to restructure liabilities and stabilize operations.

Causes: Russian authorities confiscating the Group-owned Talvis distillery, global spirits market downturn, and the State Revenue Service (SRS) freezing bank accounts following overdue excise tax liabilities.

Timeline: LPP action plan preparation and approval deadline was extended to 5 October 2026, while the effects of the initiation remain in force until 5 August 2026.

Governance: Conducting an additional assessment of provisions for doubtful receivables established in 2025 and prior reporting periods while strengthening the finance function.
