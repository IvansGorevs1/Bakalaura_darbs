Q2 2025/26 Financial Highlights
Revenue: Increased organically by 7.3% to DKK 1,573m (reported growth 1.2%). 
Endoscopy Solutions: Grew 13.8% organically, driven by Respiratory (+12.2%) and Urology, ENT, & GI (+15.5%). 
Anesthesia & Patient Monitoring (A&PM): Organic growth declined by 2.5%. 
Regional Growth: Organic growth in Europe was 7.9%, Rest of World 10.7%, and North America 6.1%. 
EBIT (b.s.i.): DKK 173m with an EBIT margin of 11.0% (14.5% adjusting for tariff costs and FX). 
Free Cash Flow: DKK 104m before acquisitions. 
Share Buyback: Repurchase shares for up to DKK 300m from 6 May 2026 until no later than 30 Sep. 2026. 

Updated FY 2025/26 Financial Guidance
Organic revenue growth: Updated to 10-12% (previously 10-13%), driven by an updated full-year outlook of low-single-digits for A&PM (previously mid-single-digits). 
EBIT margin b.s.i.: Unchanged at 12-14% (14-16% excluding assumed impacts of ~2%-pts given expected tariffs).
