Unaudited financial results of AB Pieno žvaigždės for the three monthsof 2026

 According to preliminary unaudited data, the sales revenue of AB
 Pieno žvaigždės in the first three months of 2026 was EUR 52.1
 million, or 0.5% less than a year ago (sales revenue for the first
 three months of 2025 was EUR 52.3 million).

 EBITDA in the first three months of 2026 was EUR 4.7 million,
 compared to EBITDA of EUR 2.0 million a year ago.

 In the first three months of 2026, the company generated a profit
 before tax of EUR 3.1 million. In the first three months of 2025, the
 company generated a profit before tax of EUR 0.2 million.
