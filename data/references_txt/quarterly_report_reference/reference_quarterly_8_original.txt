Financial & Operational Highlights (FY 2025)
Transported volumes: 5,531,132 passengers (-0.9%), 245,004 cargo units (-19.2%), and 760,473 passenger vehicles (-2.2%). 
Revenue: EUR 765.3 million (FY 2024: EUR 785.8 million). 
EBITDA: EUR 130.1 million (FY 2024: EUR 175.2 million). 
Net profit: EUR 17.3 million or EUR 0.023 per share (FY 2024: EUR 40.3 million). 
Net debt & Liquidity: Net debt amounted to EUR 432.4 million; Net debt to EBITDA ratio stood at 3.3. Total liquidity buffer was EUR 110.4 million (cash and cash equivalents of EUR 13.5 million and EUR 96.9 million in unused credit lines). 
Capital investments: Totalled EUR 33.0 million. 
Dividends: Paid EUR 0.06 per share in 2025 (EUR 44.6 million); proposal to pay a dividend of 6 cents per share in 2026.

Fleet & Operational Highlights
