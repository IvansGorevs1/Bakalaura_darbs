Compared to the optimistic forecasts at the beginning of the year,
 Estonia’s economy grew modestly by 1%, according to
 preliminary estimates. The construction market stabilised, with a
 slight increase in activity in the second half of the year in both
 the buildings and infrastructure segments. The construction price
 index increased by 1.5% in 2025 compared to 2024, primarily due
 to an upward trend in material prices.
 Nordecon’s revenue for 2025 decreased compared to 2024,
 primarily due to a roughly 10% decline in revenue generated by
 the Buildings segment. Revenue generated by the Infrastructure
 segment grew by 10%. The Buildings segment accounted for 81%
 of the group’s total revenue, with no significant change in the
 revenue breakdown between segments compared to the prior
 year. In 2025, the group substantially increased its order book,
 paving the way for revenue growth in 2026.
 The group’s gross margin was 6.5% and operating margin was
 2.7%. Net profit for the period was primarily affected by the
 foreign exchange loss caused by the weakening of the Ukrainian
 hryvnia against the euro.
 During the year, i.e. compared to 31 December 2024, the group’s
 order book grew by 30%, driven by a nearly threefold increase in
 the order book of the Infrastructure segment. Approximately 69%
 of the group’s order book comprises work scheduled for 2026,
 with the remainder spread across 2027–2028.
