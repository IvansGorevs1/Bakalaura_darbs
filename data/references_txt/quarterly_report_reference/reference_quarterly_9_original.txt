Financial Highlights
Revenue: Consolidated revenue for 2025 was 35.4 million euros (2024: 38.4 million euros); Q4 2025 revenue was 4.5 million euros (Q4 2024: 10.5 million euros). 
Net Profit / Loss: Net profit for 2025 was 1.0 million euros (2024: 2.1 million euros), with 0.4 million euros attributable to owners of the parent company (2024: 0.4 million euros). Q4 2025 recorded a net loss of 0.5 million euros (Q4 2024: net profit of 1.0 million euros). 
Profitability drivers: Impacted by fewer apartments handed over (141 in 2025 vs 194 in 2024), lower profit margins on development projects (16.9% in 2025 vs 17.8% in 2024), and higher marketing expenses of 1.3 million euros (2024: 0.9 million euros). 
Sales & Development Operations
Handed over homes: 141 homes in 2025 (91 in Tallinn, 50 in Riga), including 22 in Q4. 
Primary sales: Concluded agreements for 185 homes valued at 36.1 million euros (2024: 109 homes and 19.8 million euros). 
Under construction: 428 new homes as at 31 December 2025 (227 in Latvia, 201 in Estonia), a 223% increase year-on-year. 
Completed inventory: 10 residential projects on sale; inventory of completed unsold apartments stood at 28 units. 
Financing & Strategic Expansion
Bond programme: Completed on 21 November 2025, volume increased from 6 million to 8 million euros (1.4 times oversubscribed) at a fixed annual interest rate of 9.50%. 
Portfolio expansion: Added three new land plots (~700 apartments, ~2,500 m² commercial space) plus a plot at Manufaktuuri 3, Tallinn (~300 apartments, allowing a high-rise up to 60 floors). 
Canada operations: Toronto City Council approved building rights for the Weston Road project (two residential towers of 35 and 39 storeys; volume increased to 62,000 m²). 
2026 Planned Project Launches
Estonia: V7 residential project (Võistluse 7, 8 timber-frame apartments), Paevälja quarter stage I (93 apartments, 918 m² commercial space), and Peetri business centre (3,551 m² leasable area). 
Latvia: Veidema quarter stage I (Ganību dambis 17a stock-office project) and Starta 17 residential project (255 new homes).
