The year-end report contained unaudited figures, as stated
 therein. In connection with the audit and the subsequent review
 of the consolidated accounts, a number of items have been
 reclassified between lines in the income statement and the
 balance sheet. The items are set out in the consolidated income
 statement and balance sheet with related notes. The adjustments
 concern how the items are reported and do not change the
 picture of the underlying business.

 Summary of the adjustments:
 The line result from shares in associated companies has been
 adjusted by SEK 22.7 million, from SEK -86.0 million to SEK -63.2
 million. The adjustment relates to impairments attributable to
 prior years and the current year and follows the closer review of
 the consolidated accounts carried out in connection with the
 audit.

 An adjustment to depreciation and impairment of SEK 0.8 million
 affects operating profit by a corresponding amount.
 Taken together, these reclassifications change the reported result
 for the year from SEK -149.3 million to SEK -125.8 million, while
 equity at year-end remains unchanged.

 A receivable from associated companies of SEK 8.3 million is
 reclassified from current to non-current assets.

 A reclassification of SEK 1.4 million has been made between
 operating activities and investing activities in the cash flow
 statement, with no effect on total cash flow for the period.
