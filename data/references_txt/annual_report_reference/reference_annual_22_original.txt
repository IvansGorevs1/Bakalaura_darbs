In 2025, AS Tallinna Vesi increased its revenue by 11.4% to €72.48 million and earned the title of "Green Company of the Year," directing €55.74 million toward infrastructure investments. 

Financial Indicators (2025)	
Revenue reached €72.48 million, up 11.4% compared to 2024. 
Operating profit increased by 11.8% to €21.22 million. 
Net profit rose by 7.2% to €14.24 million. 
Capital expenditure (investments in fixed assets) totaled €55.74 million (compared to €49.53 million in 2024). 
Dividend payouts for 2024 were €0.53 per share (80% of net profit). 

Operational and Environmental Achievements
Tap water quality achieved 99.5% compliance with regulatory standards. 
The combined heat and power plant at the Paljassaare wastewater treatment facility fully supplied the site with heat and over 50% of its electricity from self-produced biogas. 
Cleaned 165 km of water networks using trenchless ice-pigging technology. 
The proportion of installed smart water meters among customers exceeded 80%. 
Sewer blockages decreased by 18%, and water pipe bursts dropped by 23%. 

Infrastructure Projects
Built and reconstructed 45.1 km of pipes, 49% of which was completed using trenchless methods. 
Subsidiary Watercom launched a trenchless sewer rehabilitation service using CIPP technology, renewing approximately 6 km of pipes. 
Construction began on Tallinn's first onshore stormwater retention pond in the Lahepea Street area. 

Key Goals for 2026
Completing the full transition of the service area to remote smart water meters by the end of 2026. 
Harmonizing water tariffs for residential and commercial customers by summer 2026. 
Continuing the modernization of the ozonation system at the Ülemiste water treatment plant and the reconstruction of secondary clarifiers in Paljassaare. 
Executing the planned investment volume of up to €60 million.
