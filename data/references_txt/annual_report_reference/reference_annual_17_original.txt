2024 Performance Overview

Novaturas Group reported audited revenues of EUR 201 million for 2024, having served 239 thsnd. travelers across the Baltic States (a 3.6% decline in revenue and an 8% decrease in traveler numbers compared to 2023).

In the fourth quarter, the company returned to profitability with an adjusted EBITDA of EUR 2.3 million.

The adjusted annual EBITDA came in at -EUR 449 thsnd., and the operating net loss was EUR 1.9 million.

The final audited net loss reached EUR 7.6 million due to a one-off, non-cash EUR 5.7 million goodwill impairment, primarily reflecting a more conservative outlook for the Estonian market.

Strategic Investor Partnership

In early 2025, Neset Kockar acquired a 23.2% stake in Novaturas, with the final stage (reaching 33.19%) expected to be completed in the autumn.

Mr. Kockar provided the company with a short-term loan increased to EUR 2 million.

Novaturas has started testing a hybrid travel packaging solution to allow clients access to a wider range of hotel options and pricing.

Travel Trends & Operations

Spain trips (Malaga, Barcelona, Mallorca) increased by 149% compared to 2023.

Long-haul travel sales grew by 8% (Vietnam +367%, Phuket +23%, Zanzibar +21%).

Aircraft load factor increased to 98.9% in the fourth quarter (compared to 96.7% in Q4 2023).
