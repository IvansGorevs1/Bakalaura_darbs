
AAC Clyde Space today publishes its Annual Report for 2025.
 The year marked a third consecutive year of positive EBITDA and continued growth in the Data & Services segment. The
 company continues to invest in its capabilities and operates in a market with strong and growing demand for space systems and space-based data.

 Interest in space-based capabilities continues to grow, supported by both technological development and geopolitical uncertainty.
 Advances in AI are lowering the barriers to analysing data, increasing the importance of access to high-quality data.

 Meeting this demand requires both capacity and long-term investment. Over the past year, AAC Clyde Space has strengthened its position in both areas.

 Bonnier Capital's investment at the end of 2025 has strengthened the ownership base and supports continued investments in the data-driven part of the business.

 In early 2026, EUMETSAT confirmed the next generation of weather satellites, EPS-Sterna, with OHB Sweden as prime contractor. 
 This resulted in the largest single order in the company's history and brought the order backlog to approximately SEK 1.1 billion as of 31 March 2026.

 "As AI tools become more widely available, the value increasingly lies in the data itself. We see a clear increase in demand, and our
 focus is to build the capacity needed to meet that demand overtime," says Luis Gomes, CEO of AAC Clyde Space.
