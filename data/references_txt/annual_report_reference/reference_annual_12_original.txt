 The subsidiary Konsolidator Iberia delivered positive results by
 the end of 2025, making Iberia the third-largest market.

 In 2025, Konsolidator signed 52 new customers, 38% of whom
 were acquired through partners. Furthermore, the average
 customer size increased from TDKK 70 in 2024 to TDKK 87 in
 2025, following the company’s ability to attract larger customers.

 In 2025, Konsolidator secured additional equity through two
 successful private placements, raising a total of DKK 4.8m to
 support operations and enable continued investment in product
 development, primarily the newly launched FP&A tool and a Data
 management tool.

 2025 Financial Highlights

 CARR amounted to DKK 24.4m at the end of 2025 compared
 to DKK 21.3m in 2024, corresponding to an increase of 15%.
 Revenue in 2025 amounted to DKK 25.3m compared to DKK
 20.3m in 2024, an increase of 25%.
 EBITDA amounted to a loss of DKK 2.1m at the end of 2025
 compared to an EBITDA loss of DKK 9.3m in 2024, an
 improvement of 77%
 Consolidated equity amounted to a negative equity of DKK
 3.6m on December 31, 2025; however, Konsolidator A/S has a
 positive equity of DKK 0.7m.

 Guidance 2026
 On January 21, 2026, Konsolidator announced its 2026 guidance
 (Company Announcement 02-2026). The guidance is based on
 the company’s strategic priorities outlined in the 2025–2027
 strategy, Resilient Growth, and reflects progress achieved in
 2025.

 CARR: DKK 27-29m
 Revenue: DKK 27-29m
 EBITDA: DKK 1-2m

 In 2026, Konsolidator expects to deliver positive EBITDA while
 continuing to execute the Resilient Growth strategy with
 discipline.
