Financial Highlights 2025

 Order intake reached DKK 270m (2024: 281m) as a result of
 a slowdown in 1st half followed by a pick-up in 2nd half. The
 positive momentum continued into the beginning of 2026.
 Revenue decreased by 8% to DKK 269m (2024: DKK 294m)
 explained by a slow start to the year and less sale to a
 Nordic key customer.
 EBITDA decreased to DKK 28m (2024: DKK 37m),
 corresponding to an EBITDA margin of 10.4% (2024: 12.7%).
 The decreased profitability is a result of a deliberate
 decision to maintain higher fixed cost levels to support
 future growth.
 Profit after tax decreased to DKK 0.9m from DKK 10.6m in
 2024, adversely impacted by the non-utilisation of tax
 assets and high paid taxes in US.
 Cash flow from operating activities remained strong in
 2025, reaching DKK 28.5m (2024: 29.4m)
 The outlook for 2026 is maintained (ref. company
 announcement no. 068-2025, 16 December 2025) despite
 the increased geopolitical uncertainty. Revenue is expected
 in the range of DKK 290-310m, equivalent to -1% to 6%
 organic growth, while EBITDA is expected to be in the range
 of DKK 45-55m, equivalent to an EBITDA margin of around
 8-10%.
