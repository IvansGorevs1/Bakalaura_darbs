Financial Highlights (2025)

Net sales: EUR 17 756 thousand (EUR 15 782 thousand in 2024), representing an increase of 12.5 percent.

EBITDA: EUR 1 828 thousand with a 10.3 percent margin (EUR 1 702 thousand, 10.8 percent in 2024).

EBIT: EUR 1 620 thousand with a 9.1 percent margin (EUR 1 527 thousand, 9.7 percent in 2024).

Net earnings after tax: EUR 862 thousand, an increase of 42 percent (EUR 605 thousand in 2024).

Equity: improved to EUR 2.1 million from -EUR 2.5 million in 2021.

Net Cash: reached a positive position of EUR 0.3 million from -EUR 5.7 million in 2021.

Key Events & Strategy

Ended the multi-year "reduce to the MAX" restructuring phase and transitioned to organic growth.

Launched the "Vision 2030" Strategic Growth Plan, targeting EUR 40 million in revenues, 16.8% CAGR, a 14.0% EBITDA margin, and zero net debt by 2030.

Established Pakistan as the main production base for cost-efficient engineering capacity.

Re-entered the U.S. market through a service contract with MasTec and announced a partnership with Nokia USA on a 5G EME/MEP testing project.

Launched Talkpool AB in Sweden to deliver communication solutions for the defence sector.

Supported Digicel in restoring Jamaica’s communications infrastructure following Hurricane Melissa.

Independent valuation by BDO assessed Talkpool’s German subsidiary at approximately EUR 20.5 million.

Accelerated remote services ramp-up and conducted trials of AI-powered solutions, with commercialization planned for 2027–2028 and mass deployment from 2029.
